Codex Collection is a growing library of ancient biblical and related writings, translated into clear, everyday
American English from the best manuscript evidence we have.

Explore the texts that became the Bible, the writings that didn't, and the world that shaped them. Every
translation comes with notes on ancient culture, history, manuscript differences and the choices made in
translating. No assumptions about what you should believe: just the writings, the evidence, and the context to
explore them yourself.

## Faithful to the evidence
Each translation starts from the Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek. Where old manuscripts disagree, you see every reading,
which manuscripts have it, and why one was chosen.

## Clear modern English
The translation reads at about an 8th-grade level, without changing what the ancient writer meant to make a hard
idea easier. Hard words get a plain modern paraphrase you can turn off.

## Context without an agenda
History, culture, disputed authorship and competing interpretations are laid out side by side: Jewish and
Christian, traditional and critical. Evidence is kept apart from scholarly guesswork and religious tradition.
