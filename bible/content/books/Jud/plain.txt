## Greeting
1:1 From Jude, a slave of Jesus Christ and a brother of James, to the people who have been called, who are loved in God the Father and kept safe for Jesus Christ:
1:2 Mercy to you, and peace, and love: may they be multiplied.
## Fight for the faith
1:3 Beloved, I was very eager to write to you about the salvation we share. But now I find I must write to urge you to fight hard for the faith that was handed down to the saints once for all.
1:4 Some people have slipped in among you without being noticed. Their judgment was written down long ago. They are ungodly. They turn the grace of our God into shameless self-indulgence, and they disown our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.
## Three warnings from the past
1:5 You already know all this once and for all, but I want to remind you that Jesus saved a people out of the land of Egypt, and later destroyed those who did not believe.
1:6 And the angels who did not keep to their own rank, but deserted the dwelling that was theirs, he has kept in everlasting chains down in the gloom, for the judgment of the great day.
1:7 In the same way, Sodom and Gomorrah and the towns around them, which like these gave themselves to sexual immorality and went after other flesh, are set out as an example: they suffer the punishment of eternal fire.
## Dreamers who slander
1:8 Yet these dreamers, in the same way, stain the flesh, set aside lordship, and slander the glorious ones.
1:9 But when Michael the archangel was arguing with the devil, disputing about the body of Moses, he did not dare to bring a slanderous judgment against him. Instead he said, "The Lord rebuke you!"
1:10 But these people slander everything they know nothing about. And what they grasp by nature, as animals that cannot think do, is what ruins them.
## The road of Cain
1:11 Woe to them! They have walked the road Cain walked. For pay they have thrown themselves into Balaam's error. They have perished in Korah's revolt.
1:12 These people are sunken rocks at your love feasts. They feast with you and, without fear, shepherd themselves. They are waterless clouds swept along by winds; late-autumn trees with no fruit, dead twice over, torn up by the roots;
1:13 wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shameful deeds; wandering stars, for whom the gloom of darkness has been kept forever.
## Enoch's prophecy
1:14 Enoch, the seventh from Adam, also prophesied about these people. He said, "Look! The Lord has come with his holy ones by the tens of thousands,
1:15 to carry out judgment on everyone, and to convict all the ungodly of all their ungodly deeds that they did in their ungodliness, and of all the harsh words ungodly sinners spoke against him."
1:16 These people are grumblers who complain about their lot in life. They live by their own desires. Their mouths speak puffed-up words, and they flatter people to get what they want.
## Remember and keep yourselves
1:17 But you, beloved, must remember the words spoken beforehand by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:18 They kept telling you, "In the last time there will be mockers who live by their own ungodly desires."
1:19 These people draw dividing lines. They are merely natural and lack the Spirit.
1:20 But you, beloved, build yourselves up on your most holy faith. Pray in the Holy Spirit.
1:21 Keep yourselves in God's love while you look forward to the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ, which ends in eternal life.
## Mercy for the wavering
1:22 Have mercy on some, those who waver.
1:23 Rescue others: grab them out of the fire. On still others have mercy, but with fear, and hate even the tunic the flesh has soiled.
## Praise to the God who guards
1:24 Now to the one who is able to guard you from tripping and to set you in front of his glory, without a flaw and shouting for joy,
1:25 to the only God, our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, greatness, might and authority, before every age, and now, and into all the ages. Amen.
