## Greeting
1:1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, in keeping with the promise of the life that is in Christ Jesus,
1:2 to Timothy, a child I love. Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.
## Thanks for Timothy's faith
1:3 I am grateful to God, whom I serve with a clean conscience, as my forebears did. I never stop remembering you when I pray, night and day.
1:4 I remember your tears, and I long to see you, so that joy can fill me.
1:5 I call to mind the faith in you that has no pretense in it. It made its home first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced it lives in you too.
## Do not be ashamed
1:6 So I am reminding you: stir up the fire of God's gift again, the gift that is in you through the laying on of my hands.
1:7 God did not give us a spirit of cowardice. He gave us a spirit of power, of love and of clear thinking.
1:8 So never be ashamed of bearing witness to our Lord, or of me, the prisoner who is his. Take your share of hardship with me for the good news, relying on God's power.
1:9 He saved us and called us with a holy calling. In this he did not go by what we had done, but by his own purpose and grace. That grace was given to us in Christ Jesus before the long ages of time,
1:10 and now it has been brought into the open through the appearing of our Savior, Christ Jesus. He has made death powerless, and through the good news he has brought life and immortality out into the light.
1:11 For this good news I was appointed a herald, an apostle and a teacher.
1:12 That is the reason I suffer as I do. But I am not ashamed, because I know the one I have put my trust in. I am convinced that he has the power to guard my deposit until that day.
1:13 Take the sound words you heard from me as your model, holding to them in faith and in the love that is in Christ Jesus.
1:14 Keep the good deposit safe through the Holy Spirit, who has made his home in us.
## Deserters and a loyal friend
1:15 You know this already: all those in Asia turned their backs on me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes.
1:16 May the Lord give mercy to Onesiphorus' household, because many times he lifted my spirits, and my chain did not shame him.
1:17 No, when he came to Rome he went looking for me eagerly until he found me.
1:18 May the Lord let him find mercy from the Lord on that day! And all the service he gave in Ephesus you know better than anyone.
## A soldier, an athlete, a farmer
2:1 You, then, my child: grow strong in the grace that is ours in Christ Jesus.
2:2 The things you heard from me through many witnesses, entrust to faithful people, people who will be able to teach others too.
2:3 Suffer hard things along with me, as Christ Jesus' good soldier.
2:4 No one serving in the army gets tangled up in the business of everyday life, so that he can please the officer who signed him up.
2:5 In the same way, if anyone competes in the games, he does not win the crown unless he keeps the rules of the contest.
2:6 The farmer who works hard should be the first to take a share of the harvest.
2:7 Think about what I am saying. The Lord will make you understand all of it.
## The word of God is not chained
2:8 Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, from the offspring of David, as my good news says.
2:9 For that good news I am suffering hard things, even chains, like someone who has done evil. But God's word is not in chains.
2:10 That is why I put up with everything for the sake of the people God has chosen, so that they too can reach the salvation found in Christ Jesus, and eternal glory with it.
2:11 This saying is trustworthy: / If we died with him, / we will live with him too;
2:12 if we hold out, / we will also reign with him; / if we deny him, / he will deny us as well;
2:13 if we are unfaithful, / he stays faithful, / for he cannot deny himself.
## A worker approved by God
2:14 Keep reminding people of these things. Before the Lord, solemnly warn them not to wage word battles. That is useless, and it tears down those who are listening.
2:15 Work hard to stand before God as someone he has tested and approved, a worker with nothing to be ashamed of, who cuts the word of truth straight.
2:16 Stay clear of the empty talk of the unholy. People who go in for it will only advance further into ungodliness,
2:17 and what they say will eat its way like gangrene. Hymenaeus and Philetus are two of them.
2:18 They have missed the mark about the truth, claiming that the resurrection has already taken place, and they are upending some people's faith.
2:19 But the solid foundation God laid stands firm, and it carries this seal: "The Lord knows the ones who are his," and, "Everyone who speaks the Lord's name must keep away from wrongdoing."
2:20 A large house has more than vessels of gold and silver. It also has wooden ones and clay ones. Some are for honor and some for dishonor.
2:21 If someone, then, scrubs himself clean of these, he will be a vessel for honor, made holy, of good use to the Master, prepared for any good work.
2:22 Run away from the desires that young people have. Chase after righteousness, faith, love and peace, together with all who call on the Lord with clean hearts.
## The Lord's slave must be gentle
2:23 Turn down silly, uneducated debates. You know that they give birth to fights.
2:24 A slave of the Lord must not be a fighter. He must be gentle toward all, good at teaching, able to bear wrongs,
2:25 and he must correct those who oppose him with a mild spirit. Perhaps God will grant them repentance, so that they come to know the truth,
2:26 and they will sober up and get free of the devil's snare, where he had caught them alive to do his will.
## Hard times in the last days
3:1 Know this: in the last days hard times will set in.
3:2 For people will be in love with themselves and in love with money. They will brag, look down on others and speak abusively. They will not obey their parents. They will be thankless and unholy,
3:3 heartless, refusing to make peace, slanderers, out of control, brutal, haters of good,
3:4 ready to betray, rash, puffed up, loving pleasure more than they love God.
3:5 They hold on to the outward shape of godliness, but they have said no to its power. Turn away from people like that too.
3:6 From among them come the men who slip into households and make captives of easily swayed women, who are buried under sins and driven by desires of every kind,
3:7 women who are always learning and never able to arrive at knowing the truth.
3:8 Jannes and Jambres stood against Moses, and in the same way these people stand against the truth. Their minds are ruined, and as for the faith, they have failed the test.
3:9 But they will not advance much further. Their stupidity will be clear for all to see, just as the stupidity of those two became clear.
## Hold to what you learned
3:10 You, though, have kept close to me: my teaching, how I live, what I aim at, my faith, my patience, my love, my endurance,
3:11 the persecutions, the sufferings that came to me in Antioch, in Iconium, in Lystra. What persecutions I bore! Yet the Lord pulled me out of every one.
3:12 In fact, everyone who wants to live in a godly way in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.
3:13 But wicked people and charlatans will move on to worse, leading others astray and being led astray themselves.
3:14 As for you, remain in the things you learned and became sure of. You know the people you learned them from,
3:15 and that ever since you were a small child you have known the holy writings. They have the power to give you the wisdom that leads to salvation, through the faith that is in Christ Jesus.
3:16 Every Scripture is God-breathed and useful: for teaching, for exposing wrong, for putting things right, for training people in righteousness,
3:17 so that the man of God may be fully fit, equipped for any good work.
## Proclaim the word
4:1 Before God and Christ Jesus, who is about to judge the living and the dead, I solemnly call on you, by his appearing and by his kingdom:
4:2 Proclaim the word. Stand ready when the time is right and when it is not. Expose wrong, rebuke, urge on, with endless patience and with teaching.
4:3 For there will be a time when people refuse to put up with sound teaching. They will follow their own cravings and heap up teachers for themselves, because their ears itch.
4:4 They will stop listening to the truth and veer off into myths.
4:5 But you, stay clear-headed in everything, put up with hardship, do an evangelist's work, and carry your ministry through to the end.
## Paul's farewell
4:6 For I am already a drink offering being poured out, and the time for me to depart is here.
4:7 I have competed in the good contest. I have run the course to the finish. I have kept the faith.
4:8 What is left is the crown of righteousness, laid aside for me. The Lord, the Judge who is righteous, will hand it to me on that day, and not only to me but also to all who have set their love on his appearing.
## Last requests
4:9 Try hard to get here to me quickly.
4:10 For Demas has left me behind. He loved the present age, and he has gone off to Thessalonica. Crescens went to Galatia, and Titus to Dalmatia.
4:11 Luke is the only one with me. Pick up Mark and bring him along, because he is of great use to me in the ministry.
4:12 I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus.
4:13 The cloak I left behind in Troas with Carpus: bring it when you come, and the scrolls too, especially the parchments.
4:14 Alexander the metalworker did many evil things against me. The Lord will pay him back as his works deserve.
4:15 Be on your guard against him too, for he stood fiercely against our words.
4:16 The first time I made my defense, nobody came to my side; they all left me. May it not be held against them.
4:17 Yet the Lord was there beside me and made me strong. He did this so that through me the proclamation would reach its full extent, with all the Gentiles hearing it. And I was pulled from the lion's mouth.
4:18 The Lord will pull me out of every evil work and keep me safe for his kingdom in heaven. To him belongs the glory for ever and ever. Amen.
## Greetings
4:19 Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus.
4:20 Erastus stayed in Corinth. Trophimus I left sick in Miletus.
4:21 Try hard to get here before winter. Greetings to you from Eubulus, from Pudens, Linus and Claudia, and from all the brothers.
4:22 The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you.
