## Scattered for our sins
9:0 Of Solomon. For correction.
9:1 When Israel was taken away as captives to a foreign land, / when they turned away from the Lord who had set them free,
9:2 they were thrown out of the inheritance the Lord had given them. / Israel was scattered among every nation, / just as God had said.
9:3 It happened so that you, God, would be shown to be in the right, / and your righteousness would stand, because of our lawless deeds.
9:4 For you are the righteous judge / of all the earth's peoples.
9:5 No one who does wrong will stay hidden from your knowledge,
9:6 and the right deeds of your devout ones are in front of you, Lord. / Where could anyone hide from your knowledge, God?
9:7 What we do lies in the choice and power of our own souls, / to do right or to do wrong with the work of our hands.
9:8 And in your righteousness you watch over human beings.
9:9 Whoever does what is right stores up life for himself with the Lord, / and whoever does wrong is himself to blame for his own ruin.
9:10 For the Lord judges in righteousness, / each man and each household.
9:11 To whom will you be kind, God, / if not to those who call on the Lord?
9:12 He will wash a soul clean of its sins / when it confesses and owns up to them.
9:13 For we and our faces are covered with shame / because of all these things.
9:14 And whose sins will he forgive / if not the sins of those who have sinned?
9:15 You will bless the righteous and not hold their sins against them, / and your kindness is on those who sin and then are sorry.
## You chose us
9:16 And now, you are God, and we are the people you have loved. / Look at us and take pity, God of Israel, for we belong to you. / Do not take your mercy away from us, / or our enemies will fall on us.
9:17 For you chose Abraham's offspring / above all the nations,
9:18 and you put your name on us, Lord, / and you will not stop, ever.
9:19 You made a covenant with our ancestors about us, / and we will hope in you as our souls turn back to you.
9:20 The Lord's mercy is on Israel's house / forever and ever.
## The discipline that heals
10:0 With hymns. Of Solomon.
10:1 Happy the man whom the Lord keeps in mind with a rebuke, / whom he fences off from the evil road with a whip, / to wash him clean of sin, so that it does not grow.
10:2 Whoever gets his back ready for the whip will be made clean, / for the Lord is good to those who bear his discipline.
10:3 He will make straight the paths of the righteous, / and he will not bend them out of shape by his discipline.
10:4 The mercy of the Lord is on those who love him truly, / and the Lord will remember his servants with mercy.
10:5 For the witness is in the law of the everlasting covenant; / the Lord's witness is on the roads people take, when he visits.
10:6 Our Lord is righteous and loyal in his judgments forever, / and Israel will praise the Lord's name with gladness.
10:7 The devout will give thanks in the assembly of the people, / and God will have mercy on the poor, to Israel's gladness.
10:8 For God is good and merciful forever, / and the assemblies of Israel will give glory to the Lord's name.
10:9 Salvation belongs to the Lord, on the house of Israel, / to bring lasting soundness of mind.
## Jerusalem, look!
11:0 Of Solomon. For waiting in hope.
11:1 Blow the trumpet in Zion, the signal trumpet of the holy ones.
11:2 In Jerusalem call out the voice of one who brings good news: / God has had mercy on Israel and visited them.
11:3 Stand on a height, Jerusalem, and see your children, / gathered all at once from the east and the west by the Lord.
11:4 From the north they come, glad in their God; / from distant islands God has gathered them.
11:5 He has brought high mountains down / to flat ground for them.
11:6 The hills ran away as they came in. / The woods gave them shade as they passed by.
11:7 God made every sweet-smelling tree spring up for them, / so that Israel might pass by / when the glory of their God visits.
11:8 Put on your glorious clothes, Jerusalem. / Get ready the robe of your holiness. / For God has promised good things to Israel forever and ever.
11:9 May the Lord do what he promised for Israel and Jerusalem. / May the Lord raise Israel up by his glorious name. / The mercy of the Lord is on Israel forever and ever.
## A slanderer's tongue
12:0 Of Solomon. On the tongue of law-breakers.
12:1 Lord, rescue me from the law-breaking and evil man, / from the law-breaking, whispering tongue / that speaks lies and tricks.
12:2 The words of an evil man's tongue twist and turn every which way. / Like a fire among a people that sets their beauty ablaze
12:3 is his presence among them. / He fills houses with a lying tongue, / cuts down trees of gladness, / sets law-breakers on fire,
12:4 and throws the houses of law-breakers into confusion by war with whispering lips. / May God keep the lips of law-breakers far from the innocent, and leave them in need. / May the bones of whisperers be scattered far from those who fear the Lord.
12:5 In a flaming fire may the whispering tongue be destroyed, / far from the devout.
12:6 May the Lord guard the quiet soul that hates the unjust. / May the Lord keep on a straight path the man who makes peace at home.
12:7 The salvation of the Lord is on Israel his servant forever.
12:8 May the sinners be destroyed from the Lord's presence once and for all, / and may the Lord's devout receive the promises as their inheritance.
## Spared by the Lord's right hand
13:0 Of Solomon. A psalm. Comfort for the righteous.
13:1 The Lord's right hand sheltered me; / the Lord's right hand spared us.
13:2 The Lord's arm saved us from the sword that swept through, / from famine and from the death that sinners die.
13:3 Wild beasts ran at them, savage ones. / With their teeth they ripped their flesh, / and with their back teeth they crushed their bones. / But from all these the Lord rescued us.
13:4 The ungodly man was shaken because of his offenses, / afraid he would be swept away with the sinners.
13:5 For terrible is the sinner's downfall, / but not one of all these things will touch the righteous.
13:6 For the discipline of the righteous for what they did without knowing / is not like the sinner's downfall.
13:7 The righteous is disciplined in private, / so that the sinner will not gloat over the righteous.
13:8 For he will correct the righteous like a son he loves, / and his discipline is like that of a firstborn.
13:9 For the Lord will spare his devout ones / and wipe out their offenses by discipline. / For the life of the righteous lasts forever.
13:10 But sinners will be carried off to destruction, / and no one will remember them anymore.
13:11 But the mercy of the Lord is on the devout, / and his mercy is on those who fear him.
## The Lord's garden
14:0 A hymn of Solomon.
14:1 The Lord is faithful to those who love him truly, / to those who bear his discipline, / to those who live by the righteousness of his commands, / by the law he commanded us so that we would live.
14:2 The Lord's devout ones will live through it forever. / His devout ones are the Lord's paradise, the trees of life.
14:3 Their planting is rooted for all time. / They will not be torn up all the days of heaven, / for God's share and inheritance is Israel.
14:4 Not so the sinners and law-breakers, / who loved a day spent in partnership with their sin. / Their longing is for a little rot,
14:5 and they did not remember God. / For people's ways are known to him at all times, / and he knows the hidden rooms of the heart before anything happens.
14:6 That is why their inheritance is Hades, darkness and destruction, / and they will not be found on the day when the righteous receive mercy. / But the Lord's devout will inherit life with gladness.
## The mark on the righteous
15:0 A psalm of Solomon. With a song.
15:1 When I was in trouble, I called on the Lord's name. / I hoped for help from the God of Jacob, and I was saved.
15:2 For you, God, are the hope and the safe place of the poor.
15:3 For who is strong, God, / except one who gives you thanks truly?
15:4 And what power does anyone have, / except to give thanks to your name?
15:5 A new psalm with a song from a glad heart, / fruit of the lips from the well-tuned instrument of the tongue, / the first harvest of the lips from a devout and righteous heart:
15:6 whoever does these things will never be shaken by evil. / The flame of fire and the anger meant for the unjust will not touch him
15:7 when it goes out against sinners from the Lord's presence / to destroy everything sinners stand on.
15:8 For God's sign is on the righteous, to save them. / Famine and sword and death stay far from the righteous.
15:9 For they will run from the devout like people chased in war, / but they will chase the sinners and catch them. / Those who do lawless things will not escape the Lord's judgment. / They will be caught as if by enemies trained for war.
15:10 For the sign of destruction is on their foreheads,
15:11 and the inheritance of the sinners is destruction and darkness. / Their lawless deeds will chase them all the way down to Hades.
15:12 Their inheritance will not be found for their children,
15:13 for sins will leave the houses of sinners empty and ruined. / The sinners will be destroyed forever on the day of the Lord's judgment,
15:14 when God visits the earth with his judgment.
15:15 But those who fear the Lord will receive mercy on that day / and will live by the mercy of their God. / And the sinners will be destroyed for all time.
## My soul almost slipped
16:0 A hymn of Solomon. For help for the devout.
16:1 When my soul grew drowsy, away from the Lord, / I nearly slipped, falling heavy with sleep.
16:2 When I was far from God, my soul was nearly poured out into death, / close to the gates of Hades with the sinner,
16:3 when my soul was carried away from the Lord God of Israel, / if the Lord had not taken hold of me with his mercy that lasts forever.
16:4 He jabbed me as a horse is jabbed with a goad, to keep me awake for him. / My Savior and helper at every moment saved me.
16:5 I will give you thanks, God, for you took hold of me to save me, / and you did not count me with the sinners, to be destroyed.
16:6 Do not take your mercy away from me, God, / or the memory of you from my heart, until I die.
16:7 Take control of me, God, to keep me from evil sin / and from every evil woman who trips up a fool.
16:8 Do not let the beauty of a law-breaking woman lead me astray, / or anything that comes from sin that does no good.
16:9 Keep the work of my hands straight in your place, / and guard my steps by keeping you in my mind.
16:10 Wrap my tongue and my lips in words of truth. / Put anger and thoughtless rage far away from me.
16:11 Put grumbling and faint-heartedness in hard times far from me / if I sin while you discipline me to bring me back.
16:12 But steady my soul with your goodwill and with cheerfulness. / When you make my soul strong, what I have been given will be enough for me.
16:13 For if you do not give strength, / who can bear discipline in poverty,
16:14 when a soul is corrected by the hand of its own decay? / You test him in his body and in the hardship of being poor.
16:15 When the righteous man holds out through these things, / he will receive mercy from the Lord.
## The Lord is our King
17:0 A psalm of Solomon. With a song. For the king.
17:1 Lord, you are our King forever and ever, / for in you, our God, our soul will boast.
17:2 How long is a person's life on earth? / As long as his time lasts, so does the hope placed in him.
17:3 But we will hope in God our Savior, / for the might of our God lasts forever, with mercy,
17:4 and the kingdom of our God is over the nations forever.
## David's throne laid waste
17:5 You, Lord, chose David to be king over Israel, / and you swore to him about his offspring forever / that his royal line would never come to an end before you.
17:6 But because of our sins, sinners rose up against us. / They attacked us and pushed us out. / What you had not promised them, they seized with violence.
17:7 They did not give glory to your honored name. / In their glory they set up a royal house to match their high rank.
17:8 They laid waste David's throne in the arrogance of their exchange. / But you, God, will throw them down / and take their offspring away from the earth,
17:9 when a man foreign to our race rises up against them.
17:10 You will pay them back for their sins, God, / so that what they get matches what they did.
17:11 God will not have mercy on them. / He hunted down their offspring and did not let one of them go.
17:12 The Lord is faithful in all the judgments / he carries out on the earth.
17:13 The lawless one emptied our land of the people who lived in it. / They wiped out young and old and their children all together.
17:14 In anger, in his splendor, he sent them away as far as the west, / and the rulers of the land were made a laughingstock. / He did not spare them.
17:15 Being a foreigner, the enemy acted with arrogance, / and his heart was foreign to our God.
17:16 And everything he did in Jerusalem / was just what the nations do for their gods in their cities.
17:17 And the children of the covenant prevailed over them, living among the mixed nations. / Not one of them in Jerusalem / did mercy and truth.
17:18 Those who loved the assemblies of the devout ran away from them. / Like sparrows they flew away from their nests.
17:19 They wandered in deserts to save their lives from harm, / and in the eyes of those living abroad / any life saved from them was precious.
17:20 Those who loved the assemblies of the devout ran away from them. / The lawless scattered them all over the earth. / For the sky held back from dripping rain on the earth.
17:21 The springs that flowed forever from the deeps and from the high mountains were held back. / For not one of them did what was right and just. / From their ruler to the least of the people, they lived in every sin.
17:22 The king was a law-breaker, the judge would not obey, / and the people were in sin.
## Raise up their king
17:23 Look, Lord, and raise up for them their king, the son of David, / at the time you have foreseen, God, / to reign over Israel your servant.
17:24 Wrap strength around him like a belt, / to shatter unjust rulers.
17:25 Clean Jerusalem of the nations / that trample it to destruction, in wisdom, in righteousness,
17:26 to drive the sinners out of the inheritance, / to smash the sinner's arrogance like a potter's jars, / to shatter everything they stand on with an iron rod,
17:27 to destroy law-breaking nations by the word of his mouth, / so that at his threat the nations run from his presence, / and to convict sinners by what their own hearts say.
17:28 He will bring together a holy people / and lead them in righteousness, / and he will judge the tribes of the people / made holy by the Lord his God.
17:29 He will not let wrongdoing camp among them any longer, / and no one who knows evil will live with them.
17:30 For he will know them, that they are all their God's sons, / and he will divide them up by their tribes on the land.
17:31 The resident alien and the foreigner will not settle among them again. / He will judge peoples and nations in the wisdom of his righteousness. Selah
17:32 He will have the peoples of the nations serve him under his yoke, / and he will give glory to the Lord in a place the whole earth can see.
17:33 He will make Jerusalem clean and holy, / as it was in the beginning,
17:34 so that nations will come from the ends of the earth to see his glory, / bringing as gifts her sons who had grown weak,
17:35 and to see the glory of the Lord, with which God has glorified her. / And over them will be a king who is righteous, one whom God has taught.
17:36 In his days no wrong will be done among them, / for all of them will be holy, / and their king will be the Lord Messiah.
17:37 For he will not put his hope in horse and rider and bow, / and he will not pile up gold and silver for himself for war, / or gather his hopes in great numbers for the day of battle.
17:38 The Lord himself is his King, / the hope of the one who is strong in hoping in God, / and he will have mercy on all the nations that stand before him in fear.
17:39 For he will shatter the earth by the word of his mouth forever.
17:40 He will bless the Lord's people with wisdom and gladness.
17:41 He himself will be pure from sin, / so that he can rule great peoples, / convict rulers, and remove sinners by the strength of his word.
17:42 Leaning on his God, he will not grow weak all his days, / for God has made him powerful by a holy spirit / and wise in understanding counsel, with strength and righteousness.
17:43 The Lord's blessing will be with him in strength,
17:44 and his hope in the Lord will not grow weak. / Who can stand against him? / He is strong in what he does and mighty in the fear of God,
17:45 shepherding the Lord's flock faithfully and righteously, / and he will not let any of them grow weak in their pasture.
17:46 He will lead them all as equals, / and there will be no arrogance among them / that lets one of them be oppressed.
17:47 This is the splendor of the king of Israel, whom God knew, / to raise him up over the house of Israel to discipline it.
17:48 His words will be fire-refined, more than the most precious gold. / In the assemblies he will judge the tribes of a people made holy.
17:49 His words will be like the words of holy ones / among peoples made holy.
17:50 Happy are those who live in those days, / to see the good things of Israel / in the gathering of the tribes, which God will bring about.
17:51 May God hurry his mercy to Israel. / He will rescue us from the uncleanness of godless enemies. / The Lord himself is our King forever and ever.
## The day of his Messiah
18:0 A psalm of Solomon. Again, of the Lord's Messiah.
18:1 Lord, your mercy rests on the work of your hands forever.
18:2 Your kindness, with a rich gift, is on Israel. / Your eyes are watching over them, and none of them will go without.
18:3 Your ears listen to the prayer of the poor who hope in you. / Your judgments are over all the earth, with mercy,
18:4 and your love is on Abraham's offspring, the children of Israel. / You discipline us as one disciplines a firstborn, an only son,
18:5 to turn back a willing soul / from the folly that comes from not knowing.
18:6 May God make Israel clean for the day of mercy, with blessing, / for the day he has chosen, when he brings forward his Messiah.
18:7 Happy are those who live in those days! / They will see the good things the Lord / will do for the generation that is coming,
18:8 under the rod of discipline of the Lord's Messiah, / in the fear of his God, / in wisdom of spirit, of righteousness and of strength,
18:9 to guide people in doing what is right by the fear of God, / to set them all before the Lord,
18:10 a good generation living in the fear of God, in the days of mercy. Selah
## The lights keep their path
18:11 How great our God is, and how glorious! / He makes his home in the highest places.
18:12 He set the lights in their courses to mark the times of the hours from day to day, / and they have not left the path you commanded them.
18:13 Their way is in the fear of God every day, / from the day God created them and forever.
18:14 They have not wandered since the day he created them. / From ancient generations they have not left their paths, / except when God commanded them by the order of his servants.
