## The alarm of war
1:1 I cried out to the Lord when trouble pressed me to the limit, / to God when sinners attacked.
1:2 Suddenly a battle cry was heard right in front of me. / He will listen to me, because I was full of righteousness.
1:3 In my heart I counted myself full of righteousness, / because I was doing well and had many children.
1:4 Their wealth spread out over the whole earth, / and their glory to the far end of the earth.
1:5 They were lifted up as high as the stars. / They said they would never fall.
1:6 They grew insolent in their good times, / and they could not bear it.
1:7 Their sins were done in secret places, / and I did not know.
1:8 Their acts of lawlessness went beyond those of the nations before them. / They profaned the Lord's holy things through and through.
## The dragon at the walls
2:0 A psalm of Solomon. About Jerusalem.
2:1 When the sinner grew arrogant, he battered down strong walls with a ram, / and you did not stop him.
2:2 Foreign nations went up onto your altar. / They trampled it in their sandals, in their arrogance.
2:3 It was because the sons of Jerusalem had defiled the Lord's holy things / and kept profaning God's gifts with acts of lawlessness.
2:4 For this reason he said, "Throw them far away from me." / The beauty of his glory brought them no success.
2:5 It was treated with contempt in God's presence; / it was utterly dishonored.
2:6 Sons and daughters went into cruel captivity, / a seal on their necks, a mark to be seen among the nations.
2:7 He dealt with them as their sins deserved, / for he left them in the hands of those who overpowered them.
2:8 For he turned his face away from showing them mercy, / young and old and their children, all at once.
2:9 For all of them together did evil, / refusing to listen.
2:10 And the sky was weighed down with grief, / and the earth was disgusted with them,
2:11 for no one on it had ever done what they did.
2:12 And the earth will know all your righteous judgments, God.
2:13 They put the sons of Jerusalem up to be mocked, / in place of the prostitutes in her. / Everyone who went by went in, in full view of the sun. / They made sport with their lawlessness,
2:14 just as they themselves used to do; in full view of the sun they made a public show of their wrongs. / And the daughters of Jerusalem were made unholy, as your judgment decreed,
2:15 because they had defiled themselves by mixing together in confusion. / My belly and my insides ache over these things.
2:16 I will declare you in the right, God, with an upright heart, / for your righteousness is in your judgments, God.
2:17 For you have paid back the sinners for what they did, / for their sins, which were very evil.
2:18 You exposed their sins, / so that your judgment would be seen.
2:19 You wiped out the memory of them from the earth. / God is a righteous judge, and he will not be swayed by anyone's face.
2:20 For the nations heaped insults on Jerusalem, trampling her down. / He dragged her beauty down from the throne of glory.
2:21 She wrapped herself in sackcloth instead of fine clothes, / a rope around her head instead of a crown.
2:22 She took off the glorious turban that God had placed on her.
2:23 Her beauty was flung to the ground in dishonor.
2:24 And I saw, and I pleaded for the Lord's favor, and I said, / "Let it be enough, Lord, that your hands have been heavy on Israel, / bringing the nations in.
2:25 For they made sport of us and spared no one, / in anger and fury and rage.
2:26 And Israel will be finished off, / unless you, Lord, rebuke the nations in your anger.
2:27 For they did it not out of zeal, / but because their souls craved it,
2:28 to pour out their anger on us in plunder. / Do not wait, God, to pay them back on their own heads,
2:29 so that the dragon's arrogance is told of in dishonor."
2:30 And I did not wait long until God showed me his insolence, / stabbed through on the mountains of Egypt, / held in less regard than the least, on land and sea.
2:31 His body was tossed about on the waves with great insult, / and there was no one to bury him,
2:32 because God had treated him with contempt, in dishonor. / He did not consider that he was a man, / and he did not think about the end.
2:33 He said, "I will be lord of land and sea," / and he did not recognize that God is great, / mighty in his great strength.
2:34 He is king over the heavens, / and he judges kings and rulers.
2:35 He raises me up to glory / and puts the arrogant to sleep, to everlasting destruction, in dishonor, / because they did not know him.
2:36 And now, you great ones of the earth, see the Lord's judgment, / for he is a great and righteous king, judging all that is under heaven.
2:37 Bless God, you who fear the Lord with understanding, / for the Lord's mercy is on those who fear him, with judgment,
2:38 to set apart the righteous and the sinner, / to pay sinners back forever for what they have done,
2:39 and to have mercy on the righteous, freeing him from the sinner's oppression, / and to pay the sinner back for what he did to the righteous.
2:40 For the Lord is kind to those who call on him with endurance, / acting toward his devout in keeping with his mercy, / so that they may stand before him always, in strength.
2:41 Blessed is the Lord forever, in the sight of his servants.
## The righteous and the sinner
3:0 A psalm of Solomon. About the righteous.
3:1 Soul, why are you sleeping and not blessing the Lord?
3:2 Sing a hymn and praise to God, who is worthy of praise. / Sing, and stay awake for his waking, / for a psalm to God from a good heart is good.
3:3 The righteous remember the Lord at all times, / giving thanks and declaring the Lord's judgments right.
3:4 The righteous person does not make light of being disciplined by the Lord; / his goodwill is always before the Lord.
3:5 The righteous person stumbled, and he declared the Lord in the right. / He fell, and he watches for what God will do for him;
3:6 he looks out to see where his salvation will come from.
3:7 The steadiness of the righteous comes from God, their savior. / In the house of the righteous, sin does not settle in on top of sin.
3:8 The righteous person is always looking over his house, / to clear out any wrong he did by slipping.
3:9 He made atonement for sins of ignorance by fasting, / and he humbles his soul.
3:10 And the Lord makes clean every devout man and his house.
3:11 The sinner stumbled, and he curses his life, / the day he was born, and his mother's labor pains.
3:12 He has piled sins on top of sins in his life.
3:13 He fell, for his fall is a bad one, and he will not rise. / The sinner's destruction is forever,
3:14 and he will not be remembered when God visits the righteous.
3:15 This is the portion of sinners forever.
3:16 But those who fear the Lord will rise to eternal life, / and their life will be in the Lord's light, and it will never come to an end.
## Against the people-pleasers
4:0 A conversation of Solomon. To the people-pleasers.
4:1 You unholy man, why do you sit in the devout council, / when your heart has gone far away from the Lord, / and you anger the God of Israel with your law-breaking?
4:2 He goes too far in words, too far in outward show, more than anyone, / the one who is harsh in words when he condemns sinners in court.
4:3 His hand is the first one raised against the condemned, as if in zeal, / yet he himself stands guilty with every kind of sinner, in his lack of self-control.
4:4 His eyes go to every woman, with no line drawn. / His tongue lies when he makes a deal under oath.
4:5 At night and in secret places he sins, as if no one sees him. / With his eyes he talks to every woman, arranging wickedness.
4:6 He is quick to go into every house, all smiles, as if he were innocent.
4:7 May God remove those who live in hypocrisy among the devout, / with his flesh decaying and his life in poverty.
4:8 May God expose the deeds of men who are people-pleasers, / his deeds, to laughter and scorn.
4:9 And may the devout declare the judgment of their God right, / when sinners are removed from the presence of the righteous,
4:10 the people-pleaser who speaks only with deceit.
4:11 And their eyes are on the house of a man who lives in security. / Like a snake, they set out to undo one another's wisdom / with the words of law-breakers.
4:12 His words are tricks, / to carry out what wrongdoers desire.
4:13 He did not let up until he won, scattering them as if they were orphans, / and he laid a house waste because of his law-breaking desire.
4:14 He fooled them with talk that no one sees and no one judges.
4:15 He has had his fill of law-breaking in this house, / and his eyes turn to another house, to destroy it with words that give wings to desire. / His soul, like Hades, is never full with all these things.
4:16 Lord, let his portion be dishonor before you. / Let his going out be with groans, and his coming in with a curse.
4:17 Lord, let his life be pain and poverty and helplessness, / his sleep full of grief, and his waking full of helplessness.
4:18 Let sleep be taken from his temples at night. / May they fall away from every work of his hands.
4:19 And let his house be lacking everything that would satisfy his soul.
4:20 Let his old age be spent alone, without children, until he is taken away.
4:21 Let the flesh of the people-pleasers be scattered by wild animals, / and the bones of the law-breakers lie in full view of the sun, in dishonor.
4:22 Let ravens peck out the eyes of people who play the hypocrite,
4:23 for they have laid many people's houses waste in dishonor / and scattered them in their desire.
4:24 And they did not remember God, / and they did not fear God in all these things.
4:25 And they angered God and provoked him / to remove them from the earth, / because with trickery they played the hypocrite to innocent souls.
4:26 Happy are those who fear the Lord in their innocence.
4:27 And the Lord will rescue them from deceitful people and sinners, / and he will rescue us from every trap the law-breakers set.
4:28 May God remove those who do every kind of wrong in arrogance, / for the Lord our God is a great and mighty judge, in righteousness.
4:29 Lord, let your mercy be on all who love you.
## God feeds the poor
5:0 A psalm of Solomon.
5:1 Lord God, I will praise your name with joy, / among those who know your righteous judgments.
5:2 For the refuge of the poor is kind and merciful.
5:3 When I cry out to you, do not keep silent toward me.
5:4 For no one will take plunder from a strong man.
5:5 And who will take anything from all that you have made, unless you give it?
5:6 For a person and his portion are with you, on the scales. / He will not add anything, to have more than you have decided, God.
5:7 When we are in trouble, we will call on you for help, / and you will not turn our request away, for you are our God.
5:8 Do not make your hand heavy on us, / or we may sin because we are forced to.
5:9 And even if you do not bring us back, we will not stay away. / We will come to you.
5:10 For if I am hungry, I will cry out to you, God, / and you will give to me.
5:11 You feed the birds and the fish, / when you give rain to the deserts so that green grass springs up, / to make fodder ready in the desert for every living thing.
5:12 And if they are hungry, they will lift their faces to you.
5:13 You feed kings and rulers and peoples, God, / and who is the hope of the poor and the needy, if not you, Lord?
5:14 And you will listen. For who is kind and gentle except you? / You gladden the soul of the humble by opening your hand in mercy.
5:15 A person's kindness comes sparingly, and then it is tomorrow. / And if he gives a second time without grumbling, even that would amaze you.
5:16 But your gift is great and rich, with kindness, / and the one whose hope is in you will not be sparing in giving.
5:17 Your mercy is over all the earth, Lord, with kindness.
5:18 Happy is the one whom God remembers with a fitting measure of enough.
5:19 If a person has far too much, he sins.
5:20 A modest share with righteousness is enough, / and here is the Lord's blessing: to be filled up in righteousness.
5:21 Those who fear the Lord rejoiced in good things, / and your kindness is on Israel in your kingdom.
5:22 Blessed is the glory of the Lord, for he is our king.
## Ready to call on the Lord
6:0 In hope. Of Solomon.
6:1 Happy is the man whose heart is ready to call on the name of the Lord.
6:2 When he remembers the name of the Lord, he will be saved.
6:3 His paths are made straight by the Lord, / and the work of his hands is kept safe by the Lord his God.
6:4 He will not be shaken by the sight of his bad dreams.
6:5 His soul will not be terrified when he crosses rivers or when the seas toss.
6:6 He got up from his sleep and blessed the name of the Lord.
6:7 With a steady heart he sang hymns to the name of God, / and he pleaded for the Lord's favor for his whole household.
6:8 And the Lord heard the prayer of everyone who lives in the fear of God, / and the Lord carries out every request of a soul that hopes in him.
6:9 Blessed is the Lord, who shows mercy to those who love him in truth.
## Do not take your tent from us
7:0 Of Solomon. Of turning back.
7:1 Do not take your tent away from us, God, / or those who hated us for no reason will attack us.
7:2 For you have rejected them, God. / Do not let their feet trample the inheritance of your sanctuary.
7:3 Discipline us yourself, as you will, / but do not hand us over to the nations.
7:4 For if you send death, / you yourself give it orders about us. / For you are merciful, / and you will not be angry enough to wipe us out.
7:5 While your name dwells in our midst, we will be shown mercy,
7:6 and no nation will overpower us, / for you are our shield.
7:7 And we will call on you, and you will hear us.
7:8 For you will pity the race of Israel forever, / and you will not reject them. / And we will be under your yoke forever, / and under the whip of your discipline.
7:9 You will set us straight in the time when you help us, / to have mercy on the house of Jacob, for the day that you promised them.
## The war at Jerusalem
8:0 Of Solomon. For victory.
8:1 My ear caught the distress and the sound of war, / the sound of a trumpet blaring slaughter and ruin.
8:2 The sound of a huge crowd, like a very great wind, / like a storm of great fire rushing through the desert.
8:3 And I said to my heart, "Where, then, will God judge him?"
8:4 I heard a sound going into Jerusalem, the city of the sanctuary.
8:5 My back was crushed at what I heard; / my knees went weak.
8:6 My heart was afraid; / my bones shook like flax.
8:7 I said, "They will keep their ways straight in righteousness." / I thought back over God's judgments since the creation of heaven and earth. / I declared God in the right in his judgments, which have stood from long ago.
8:8 God exposed their sins in full view of the sun. / The whole earth knew God's righteous judgments.
8:9 Their law-breaking was done in hidden places underground, to provoke him.
8:10 Son with mother, and father with daughter, they mixed together in confusion.
8:11 They committed adultery, each man with his neighbor's wife. / They made agreements with each other under oath about these things.
8:12 They plundered the holy things of God, / as if there were no heir to redeem them.
8:13 They trod on the Lord's altar fresh from uncleanness of every sort, / and they defiled the sacrifices with menstrual blood, as if it were common meat.
8:14 There was no sin they left undone, sinning worse than the nations.
8:15 For this reason God mixed for them a spirit of going astray. / He made them drink a cup of unmixed wine until they were drunk.
8:16 He brought the one from the far end of the earth, / the one who strikes hard.
8:17 He decreed war against Jerusalem and her land.
8:18 The rulers of the land met him with joy. / They said to him, "Your coming is what we prayed for! / Come, all of you, enter in peace."
8:19 They made the rough roads smooth before his entry. / They opened the gates to Jerusalem, / and they crowned her walls.
8:20 He came in like a father into his sons' house, in peace. / He set his feet down in great safety.
8:21 He seized her towers and the wall of Jerusalem,
8:22 for God led him in safety while they were going astray.
8:23 He did away with their rulers and every wise counselor. / He poured out the blood of Jerusalem's people like unclean water.
8:24 He carried off their sons and daughters, / the children they had fathered in unholiness.
8:25 They did as their uncleanness led them, just as their fathers had.
8:26 He defiled Jerusalem and the things made holy for the name of God.
8:27 God was shown to be in the right in his judgments among the nations of the earth,
8:28 and the devout of God were like lambs in innocence among them.
8:29 Worthy of praise is the Lord, who judges the whole earth in his righteousness.
8:30 Look now, God, you have shown us your judgment in your righteousness.
8:31 Our eyes have seen your judgments, God. / We have declared your honored name right forever,
8:32 for you are the God of righteousness, judging Israel with discipline.
8:33 Turn your mercy toward us, God, and pity us.
8:34 Gather the scattered people of Israel with mercy and kindness,
8:35 for your faithfulness is with us. / And we made our necks stiff, / and you are the one who disciplines us.
8:36 Do not overlook us, our God, / or the nations will swallow us up, as if there were no one to redeem us.
8:37 And you have been our God from the beginning, / and our hope is in you, Lord.
8:38 And we will not keep away from you, / for your judgments on us are kind.
8:39 Your goodwill is on us and our children forever. / Lord, our savior, we will never be shaken again, for all time to come.
8:40 Worthy of praise is the Lord in his judgments, in the mouth of the devout, / and blessed by the Lord is Israel forever.
