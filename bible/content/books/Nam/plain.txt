## An oracle about Nineveh
1:1 An oracle about Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum of Elkosh.
## The LORD who avenges and shelters
1:2 The LORD is a jealous God who takes vengeance. / The LORD takes vengeance, and rage is his to wield. / The LORD takes vengeance on those who oppose him, / and he keeps his anger ready for his enemies.
1:3 The LORD is slow to anger, his power is great, / and he will by no means leave guilt unpunished. / He goes his way in gale and storm, / and the dust his feet stir up is cloud.
1:4 He scolds the sea, and it goes dry. / He drains every river. / Bashan and Carmel wither. / The flower of Lebanon withers too.
1:5 The mountains shake because of him, / and the hills dissolve. / The earth is lifted up in front of him, / the world and everyone who lives in it.
1:6 Who can stand up when he is indignant? / Who can hold out against his burning anger? / His rage pours down like fire, / and the rocks are torn down because of him.
1:7 The LORD is good. / When the day of trouble comes, he is a stronghold, / and he knows each person who shelters in him.
1:8 But with a flood sweeping over, / he will make a full end of her place, / and he will chase his enemies into darkness.
## Turning between Nineveh and Judah
1:9 What are you plotting against the LORD? / He himself will make a full end. / Trouble will not arise twice.
1:10 Though they are tangled together like thorns / and soaked like drunkards in their drink, / they will be eaten up like stubble that is completely dry.
1:11 One who plans evil against the LORD has come out of you, / a worthless adviser.
1:12 The LORD says this: / "They may be sound and very many, / but they will be cut down all the same, and he will pass on. / I have brought you low, but I will not bring you low again.
1:13 Now I will snap the bar of his yoke off you / and tear your chains apart."
1:14 The LORD has issued an order about you: / "Nothing more will be sown from your name. / I will chop off the carved idol and the cast idol / from the temple of your gods. / I will set your grave in place, for you count for nothing."
1:15 Look! On the mountains, / the feet of the one who brings good news, / who makes peace heard! / Hold your festivals, Judah. / Pay your vows. / For the worthless one will never again pass through you. / He is cut off completely.
## The destroyer comes
2:1 Up he comes to face you, the one who scatters. / Guard the fort! Watch the road! / Brace yourselves! Gather every bit of your strength!
2:2 The LORD will bring back the pride of Jacob, / like the pride of Israel. / Those who strip have stripped them bare / and broken off their vine shoots.
2:3 The shields of his warriors are dyed red. / His brave men are dressed in scarlet. / The chariots glitter like fire with their metal / on the day he makes them ready, / and the cypress spears are swung.
2:4 Through the streets the chariots go mad. / They jolt and swerve in the wide squares. / To look at, they are like torches, / like flashes of lightning they race.
2:5 He calls his great men to mind, but they falter on their way. / They rush to her wall, / and the covering is set in place.
2:6 The gates on the rivers swing open, / and the palace caves in.
2:7 It is settled: she is uncovered and carried off. / Her slave girls mourn with the sound of doves / and pound on their chests.
2:8 Nineveh has been like a pool of water ever since she began. / Now they are running away. / "Stop! Stop!" Yet no one looks back.
2:9 Loot the silver! Loot the gold! / The storeroom has no end, / heavy with every costly thing.
2:10 Empty, emptied, wasted away! / Hearts melt, and knees knock together. / Agony grips every waist, / and every face loses its color.
## The lions' den
2:11 Where is the lions' lair now, / the pasture of the young lions, / where lion and lioness prowled, / and the lion's cub, with no one to scare them?
2:12 The lion tore up what his cubs needed / and throttled prey for his lionesses. / He packed his caves with kills / and his dens with mangled meat.
2:13 "Look, I am against you," declares the LORD of Armies. / "In smoke I will burn up her chariots, / and the sword will feed on your young lions. / I will cut your prey off from the earth, / and the voice of your messengers / will never be heard again."
## Woe to the city of bloodshed
3:1 Woe to the city of bloodshed! / It is all lies, full of loot, / and the prey never goes away.
3:2 A whip cracks. Wheels rumble. / Horses gallop, and chariots bounce and leap.
3:3 Horsemen charge up. Swords blaze, and spears flash. / The dead are many, and the corpses lie in heaps. / There is no end to the bodies. / People trip over their bodies.
3:4 All because of the many acts of prostitution of the prostitute, / the charming one, the mistress of sorceries, / who sells nations with her prostitution / and clans with her sorceries.
3:5 "Look, I am against you," declares the LORD of Armies. / "I will uncover your skirts over your face / and make the nations look at your nakedness, / the kingdoms at your shame.
3:6 I will hurl detestable things at you. / I will hold you in scorn / and set you up to be stared at.
3:7 Everyone who sees you will run from you / and say, 'Nineveh is ruined! Who will feel sorry for her?' / Where would I look for comforters for you?"
## Thebes fell too
3:8 Are you any better than Thebes, / who sat among the Nile's streams, / ringed by water, / her outer defense the sea, / and the water her wall?
3:9 Her strength was Cush and Egypt, / with no limit to it. / Put and the Libyans were your helpers.
3:10 Yet she too went into exile. / She went off as a captive. / Her little ones were smashed / where every street begins. / They cast lots for her honored men, / and all her great ones were put in chains.
3:11 You too will be drunk. / You will be hidden away. / You too will look for a stronghold against the enemy.
3:12 Each of your fortresses is a fig tree with early figs. / Shake them, and the figs drop / right into the mouth of the one who eats.
3:13 Look at the people in your midst: they are women! / To your enemies the gates of your land will swing wide open, / and fire will eat up your bars.
## Locusts, shepherds and a wound
3:14 Haul in water for the siege. / Make your fortresses strong. / Climb into the clay and stamp the mortar. / Grip the brick mold.
3:15 There fire will eat you up. / The sword will cut you down. / It will eat you up like the hopping locust. / Multiply like the hopping locust! / Multiply like the locust!
3:16 You have multiplied your merchants beyond the stars of the sky, / but the hopping locust strips everything and takes wing.
3:17 Your court officers are like locusts, / and your scribes like a swarm of locusts / camping along the walls on a cold day. / When the sun comes up, they scatter, / and no one knows where they went. Where are they?
3:18 Your shepherds are sleeping, king of Assyria. / Your great men lie still. / Your people lie scattered across the mountains, / and no one is there to bring them together.
3:19 There is no relief for your fracture. / Your wound is severe. / Everyone who hears the report about you / strikes hands together over you. / For is there anyone your wickedness has not passed over, again and again?
