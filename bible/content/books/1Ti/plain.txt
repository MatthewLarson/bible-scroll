## Greeting
1:1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and of Christ Jesus our hope,
1:2 to Timothy, my true child in the faith. Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.
## Stop the other teaching
1:3 As I urged you when I was setting out for Macedonia, stay on in Ephesus, so that you can order certain people not to teach anything different,
1:4 and not to pay attention to myths and endless genealogies. These bring on speculation rather than God's plan, which works by faith.
1:5 But the goal of this order is love, coming from a pure heart, a good conscience and a faith without pretense.
1:6 Some have missed the mark on these things and wandered off into empty talk.
1:7 They would like to be teachers of the law, yet they understand neither what they say nor the things they so firmly insist on.
1:8 Now we know that the law is a good thing, provided a person uses it lawfully.
1:9 Keep this in mind: law is not set in place for a righteous person. It is for the lawless and the unruly, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and the irreverent, for those who kill their fathers or kill their mothers, for killers,
1:10 for those who commit sexual sin, for men who lie with males, for slave dealers, for liars, for people who swear falsely, and for anything else that stands against sound teaching.
1:11 Such teaching fits the good news of the glory of the blessed God, the good news put in my trust.
## Mercy for the first of sinners
1:12 I am grateful to the one who made me strong, Christ Jesus our Lord. He counted me faithful and placed me in his service,
1:13 though before that I was a man who blasphemed, persecuted and abused. Yet mercy was shown to me, because I did it without knowing, in my unbelief.
1:14 And our Lord's grace flowed over, more than enough, bringing faith and the love found in Christ Jesus.
1:15 This saying is trustworthy and deserves full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, and among them I am the first.
1:16 Yet this is the reason I was shown mercy: so that Christ Jesus could put the whole of his patience on display in me first, as a pattern for those who were going to put their faith in him for eternal life.
1:17 To the King of the ages, who cannot decay and cannot be seen, the only God: honor and glory to him forever and ever. Amen.
## The good campaign
1:18 I am placing this order in your hands, Timothy my child, in line with the prophecies spoken over you earlier, so that with them you may serve in the good campaign as a soldier,
1:19 with faith and a good conscience in your keeping. Some pushed conscience away, and their faith ended in shipwreck.
1:20 Hymenaeus and Alexander are two of them. I have handed them over to Satan, so that discipline will teach them not to blaspheme.
## Prayer for everyone
2:1 So the first thing I urge is this: make requests, prayers, appeals and thanksgivings for every person,
2:2 for kings and for all who hold high office, so that we can spend calm and quiet lives in full godliness and dignity.
2:3 Doing this is good, and God our Savior welcomes it.
2:4 He wants everyone to be saved and to arrive at full knowledge of the truth.
2:5 For God is one, and one also is the mediator between God and humans: Christ Jesus, himself human.
2:6 He gave himself as a ransom on behalf of all, the witness to this given when its time came.
2:7 It is for this that I was made a herald and an apostle. What I say is true; I do not lie. I was made a teacher of the Gentiles, in faith and in truth.
## Men and women at prayer
2:8 My wish, then, is that in every place the men pray with holy hands raised, free of anger and argument.
2:9 Likewise the women: they should make themselves beautiful with decent clothing, with modesty and good sense, not with braids and gold or pearls or costly robes,
2:10 but, as suits women who say they honor God, with good works.
2:11 A woman is to learn in quietness, with complete submission.
2:12 I do not allow a woman to teach or to hold authority over a man; she is to be in quietness.
2:13 For Adam was shaped first, and Eve after him.
2:14 And Adam was not fooled. The woman was the one fooled through and through, and she fell into wrongdoing.
2:15 But she will be saved through the bearing of children, if they stay in faith, love and holiness, along with good sense.
## Overseers
3:1 This saying is trustworthy: if anyone reaches for the office of overseer, he is setting his heart on a fine work.
3:2 The overseer, then, has to be someone no one can fault: the husband of one wife, clear-headed, sensible, orderly, welcoming to strangers, able to teach,
3:3 not a heavy drinker, not a man who hits people, but kind and fair, no fighter, free from the love of money.
3:4 He should be one who leads his own household well, whose children are under his authority, with full respect.
3:5 After all, if someone does not know how to lead his own household, how will he look after a church of God?
3:6 He should not be newly planted in the faith, so that he does not swell with pride and fall into the devil's judgment.
3:7 Those outside should also speak well of him, so that he does not fall into shame and into the devil's snare.
## Deacons
3:8 The deacons too should be people of serious character. They should not say one thing to one person and another to the next, should not be devoted to much wine, and should not chase shameful profit.
3:9 Let them keep the mystery of the faith with a clean conscience.
3:10 These also should be tested first. Then let them serve, if no charge can be brought against them.
3:11 Women likewise should be serious, not given to slander, clear-headed, trustworthy in everything.
3:12 Let deacons be husbands of one wife, who lead well both their children and their own households.
3:13 Those who serve well win for themselves a fine place to stand, and much boldness in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.
## The household of God
3:14 I write you this while hoping to come to you before long.
3:15 But if I am slow in coming, you will know how a person should live in the household of God. That household is the church of the living God, a pillar and a firm base for the truth.
3:16 And by common confession, the mystery of godliness is great: / He was made visible in the flesh, / proven right in the Spirit, / seen by angels, / heralded among the nations, / believed in the world, / taken up in glory.
## Teachers who forbid
4:1 Now the Spirit says plainly: in later times some people will step away from the faith, giving their attention to misleading spirits and to teachings that come from demons.
4:2 These come through the play-acting of people who speak lies, their own consciences burned as if with a branding iron.
4:3 They stop people from marrying and tell them to keep clear of foods. Yet God made those foods to be taken with thanks by believers, who know the truth well.
4:4 Every created thing God made is good. None of it should be thrown out when it is taken with thanks,
4:5 since God's word and prayer make it holy.
## A good servant of Christ Jesus
4:6 Lay these things before the brothers, and you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, fed on the words of the faith and of the fine teaching you have kept close to.
4:7 Refuse the unholy myths, the kind of tales old women tell. Instead, train yourself toward godliness.
4:8 Exercising the body brings a little benefit. Godliness brings benefit in every way, because it carries a promise of life, life now and life in the age to come.
4:9 This saying is trustworthy and deserves full acceptance.
4:10 This is what we toil and struggle for, since our hope rests on the living God. He is Savior of all people, most of all of believers.
4:11 Give these orders, and teach them.
4:12 Let no one treat your youth with contempt. Be a model for the believers instead: in what you say, in how you live, in love, in faith, in purity.
4:13 Till I come, give yourself to the reading aloud, to urging and to teaching.
4:14 Do not leave unused the gift in you. It was given to you through a prophecy, with the laying on of the elders' hands.
4:15 Think hard about these things. Live in them, so that your growth is plain for all to see.
4:16 Pay attention to yourself and to the teaching. Stay with them. If you do this, you will save yourself and the people who listen to you as well.
## Old and young
5:1 Never lash out at an older man. Plead with him as you would with a father. Plead with the younger men as brothers,
5:2 the older women as mothers, the younger women as sisters, keeping yourself entirely pure.
## Widows
5:3 Give honor to the widows who are widows in fact.
5:4 If a widow has children or grandchildren, though, these should learn first to practice godliness at home and to pay back what they owe their parents. That is something God welcomes.
5:5 The widow in fact, the one left on her own, has put her hope in God. She stays with her pleas and prayers night and day.
5:6 But the one who lives in luxury has died while still alive.
5:7 Order these things as well, so that no one can find fault with them.
5:8 If a person does not look after his own people, most of all those in his own home, he has turned his back on the faith. He is worse than someone who does not believe.
5:9 Put a widow on the list only when she is not younger than sixty, has been the wife of one husband,
5:10 and has people who can speak to her good works: she has raised children, taken in strangers, washed the feet of the saints, given relief to people under pressure, and followed after every good work.
5:11 Turn down the younger widows. For when sexual desire sets them at odds with Christ, they wish to marry,
5:12 and they earn a judgment for throwing off their first pledge.
5:13 At the same time they also learn idleness as they make the rounds of people's homes. And they are not just idle; they chatter and meddle, and speak of things that are not fitting.
5:14 My wish, then, is that the younger ones marry, bear children, run their homes, and hand the opponent no opening for abuse.
5:15 Some of them have already wandered off after Satan.
5:16 A believing woman who has widows in her family should give them relief herself. The church should not be weighed down, so that it can give relief to the widows in fact.
## Elders
5:17 Elders who lead well should be counted worthy of a double honor, above all those who toil at the word and at teaching.
5:18 The Scripture says, "You shall not muzzle an ox that is treading out the grain," and, "A worker has earned his pay."
5:19 Do not take up an accusation against an elder except on the word of two or three witnesses.
5:20 Those who go on sinning, correct in front of all, so that the others too will have fear.
5:21 With God, Christ Jesus and the chosen angels as witnesses, I solemnly charge you: keep these things without judging beforehand, and do nothing by leaning to one side.
5:22 Put your hands on no one in haste, and have no part in other people's sins. Keep yourself clean.
5:23 Be a water-drinker no longer. Take a little wine for your stomach and for the illnesses you keep getting.
5:24 Some people's sins are out in the open, running ahead of them to judgment. Other people's sins come along behind.
5:25 Good works are the same: they are out in the open, and the ones that are not still cannot be kept out of sight.
## Slaves and masters
6:1 Those who are slaves under a yoke should count their own masters worthy of full honor, so that no one speaks evil of God's name and of the teaching.
6:2 Those whose masters are believers should not think less of them because they are brothers. They should serve them better still, since the masters who gain from their good work are believers and dearly loved. Teach and press these things.
## False teaching and true gain
6:3 Anyone who teaches something different, and does not come over to the sound words of our Lord Jesus Christ and the teaching in keeping with godliness,
6:4 is swollen with pride and knows nothing. He is sick for arguments and word-fights. Out of these come envy, rivalry, abuse, nasty suspicions,
6:5 and endless rubbing against each other, from people whose minds are ruined and who have been stripped of the truth. They suppose godliness is a source of profit.
6:6 And godliness is in fact a great source of profit, when it comes with being content.
6:7 We carried nothing into the world, because we cannot carry anything out of it either.
6:8 If we have food and covering, we will count that enough.
6:9 People who are set on getting rich fall into temptation and into a snare, and into many senseless, damaging desires that sink people down into ruin and destruction.
6:10 A root of every kind of evil is the love of money. Some who stretched out for it were led astray from the faith and stabbed themselves through with many pains.
## The good contest
6:11 As for you, man of God, run from these things. Chase after righteousness, godliness, faith, love, staying power, a gentle spirit.
6:12 Compete in the good contest of the faith. Grab hold of eternal life. You were called to it, and you made the good confession about it before many witnesses.
6:13 Before God, who keeps all things alive, and before Christ Jesus, who bore witness to the good confession in front of Pontius Pilate, I give you this order:
6:14 keep the command spotless, with nothing to fault, until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ.
6:15 God will show that appearing at its own set times, he who is the blessed one, the only Ruler, the King of those who reign and the Lord of those who rule.
6:16 He alone has life that cannot die. He makes his home in light no one can come near. No human being has seen him, and none can see him. Honor and power belong to him forever. Amen.
## The rich
6:17 Order those who are rich in the present age not to think highly of themselves, and not to pin their hope on riches, which no one can count on. Their hope should be on God, who gives us all things in rich supply for us to enjoy.
6:18 They should do good, be rich in good works, give freely, and share what they have.
6:19 This way they lay away for themselves a fine foundation for what is coming, so that they can grab hold of the life that is life indeed.
## Guard what was entrusted
6:20 Timothy, guard the deposit. Keep away from unholy, empty talk and from the counterclaims of the knowledge that wrongly bears that name.
6:21 Some who have laid claim to it have missed the mark on the faith. Grace be with you all.
