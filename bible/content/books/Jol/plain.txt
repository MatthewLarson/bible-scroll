## The Word to Joel
1:1 The LORD's word came to Joel, son of Pethuel.
## Tell Your Children
1:2 Hear this, you elders. / Pay attention, everyone who lives in the land. / Has this ever happened in your days, / or in the days of your forefathers?
1:3 Tell your children about it. / Let your children tell their children, / and their children the next generation.
1:4 The cutting locust left some behind, and the swarming locust ate it. / The swarming locust left some behind, and the hopping locust ate it. / The hopping locust left some behind, and the stripping locust ate it.
## Wake Up and Weep
1:5 Drunkards, wake up and weep. / Wail, all you who drink wine, / over the sweet wine, / for it has been cut off from your mouths.
1:6 A nation has marched onto my land, / strong, with too many to count. / Its teeth are a lion's teeth, / and the fangs of a lioness are its own.
1:7 It has made my vine a ruin / and my fig tree a stump. / It has stripped them bare and thrown them down, / and their branches have turned white.
1:8 Wail like a young woman dressed in sackcloth / for the husband of her youth.
1:9 From the house of the LORD, grain offering and drink offering are cut off. / The priests have gone into mourning, / those who serve the LORD.
1:10 The field is wrecked. / The ground is in mourning. / The grain is wrecked, / the new wine has gone dry, / and the fresh oil has withered.
1:11 Be ashamed, you farmers. / Wail, you vinedressers, / over the wheat and the barley, / for the harvest of the field is lost.
1:12 Dry is the vine, and the fig tree wilts. / The pomegranate, the palm and the apple, / every tree in the field, is dry, / and joy has dried up / from human beings.
## Priests, Put On Sackcloth
1:13 Tie on sackcloth and mourn, you priests. / Wail, you who serve at the altar. / Come in and lie all night in sackcloth, / you who serve my God, / for grain offering and drink offering / have been kept back from the house of your God.
1:14 Consecrate a fast. / Proclaim a sacred assembly. / Bring in the elders, / everyone who lives in the land, / to the house of the LORD your God. / Then cry out to the LORD.
## The Day Is Near
1:15 Alas for the day! / For the day of the LORD is close. / It comes like havoc from the Almighty.
1:16 Was the food not cut off while we looked on, / and joy and gladness / from the house of our God?
1:17 Seeds lie shriveled beneath their clods of earth. / The storehouses stand bare. / The grain bins have been pulled down, / for the grain has gone dry.
1:18 How the livestock groan! / The cattle herds stumble about, bewildered, / for there is no grass for them. / The sheep flocks also pay the price.
1:19 I call out to you, LORD, / for fire has eaten the grasslands of the wilderness, / and a flame has scorched every tree in the field.
1:20 The animals of the field also gasp for you, / for the channels of water are dry, / and fire has eaten the grasslands of the wilderness.
## Blow the Horn in Zion
2:1 Blow the ram's horn in Zion. / Raise a shout on my holy mountain. / Let everyone in the land shake with fear, / for the day of the LORD is coming. / Yes, it is near.
2:2 A day of darkness and gloom, / a day of cloud and deep darkness. / Like dawn spread over the mountains, / a people, many and mighty. / Nothing like it has happened since long ago, / and nothing like it will happen again / through the years of all generations.
2:3 A fire eats ahead of it, / and a flame blazes behind it. / The land ahead of it is like the garden of Eden; / the land behind it, a desolate wilderness. / There is no escape from it.
2:4 They look like horses, / and like horsemen they run.
2:5 They bound across the peaks of the mountains / with a sound like chariots, / with a sound like a blazing flame / eating straw, / like a mighty people / lined up for war.
2:6 Before it peoples writhe in pain. / Every face loses its color.
2:7 Like warriors they run. / Like men of war they climb a wall. / Each one marches on his own road, / and none turns off his path.
2:8 No one pushes his brother. / Each man marches on his own track. / They hurl themselves through the weapons / and do not stop.
2:9 They swarm over the city. / They race along the wall. / They go up into the houses. / Through the windows they come in, like a thief.
2:10 Before it the earth trembles. / The heavens shake. / The sun and moon grow dark, / and the stars draw back their light.
2:11 The LORD gives out his voice / at the head of his army, / for his camp is very large, / and the one who carries out his word is mighty. / The day of the LORD is great / and very awesome. / Who can bear it?
## Even Now
2:12 Even now, the LORD declares, / turn back to me with your whole heart, / in fasting, in weeping and in mourning.
2:13 Tear your hearts and not your clothes. / Turn back to the LORD your God, / for he is gracious and compassionate, / slow to anger and great in loyal love, / and he changes his mind about the harm.
2:14 Who knows? He may turn back and change his mind, / and leave behind him a blessing, / grain offering and drink offering / for the LORD your God.
## Gather the People
2:15 Blow the ram's horn in Zion. / Consecrate a fast. / Proclaim a sacred assembly.
2:16 Gather the people. / Consecrate the congregation. / Bring the elders together. / Gather the children, / even the babies still nursing. / The bridegroom must come out of his room, / the bride out of her bridal chamber.
2:17 Between the porch and the altar / let the priests weep, those who serve the LORD. / Let them say, "Have pity on your people, LORD, / and do not hand over your inheritance to disgrace, / to nations who would rule over them. / Why should they say among the peoples, / 'Where is their God?'"
## The LORD Answers
2:18 Then the LORD burned with jealousy for his land / and took pity on his people.
2:19 The LORD answered his people: / Look, I am sending you the grain, / the new wine and the fresh oil, / and you will be full of them. / I will never again make you / a disgrace among the nations.
2:20 The northern one I will send far away from you. / I will thrust it into a dry and empty land, / its front into the eastern sea / and its rear into the western sea. / Its stink will go up, / and its rotten smell will go up, / for he has done great things.
2:21 Land, do not be afraid. / Be glad and rejoice, / for the LORD has done great things.
2:22 Animals of the field, do not be afraid, / for the grasslands of the wilderness have turned green. / The trees are bearing their fruit; / the fig tree and the vine give their strength.
2:23 Children of Zion, be glad. / Rejoice in the LORD your God, / for he has given you the early rain to set things right. / He has sent down rain for you, / the early rain and the spring rain, as before.
2:24 The threshing floors will be piled with grain, / and the vats will run over / with new wine and fresh oil.
2:25 I will give you back the years / that the swarming locust ate, / the hopping locust, the stripping locust / and the cutting locust, / my great army that I sent among you.
2:26 You will eat and eat until you are full, / and you will praise the name of the LORD your God, / who has treated you in a wonderful way. / My people will never be ashamed.
2:27 You will know that I am among Israel, / that I am the LORD your God, and there is no other. / My people will never be ashamed.
## I Will Pour Out My Spirit
2:28 After this, I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh. / They will prophesy, your sons and your daughters. / Your old men will have dreams, / and your young men will see visions.
2:29 Even on the male slaves and the female slaves / I will pour out my Spirit in those days.
2:30 I will set wonders in the heavens and on the earth: / blood and fire and pillars of smoke.
2:31 The sun will change into darkness, / the moon into blood, / before the day of the LORD arrives, / that great and awesome day.
2:32 And everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be rescued. / For on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem / there will be survivors, as the LORD has said, / and among those who are left / are the ones the LORD calls.
## The Valley of Jehoshaphat
3:1 For look, in those days and at that time, / when I turn the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem around,
3:2 I will gather all the nations / and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. / There I will bring my case against them, / over my people and my inheritance Israel, / whom they scattered among the nations. / They divided up my land.
3:3 They cast lots for my people. / They gave a boy for a prostitute / and sold a girl for wine, and they drank.
3:4 What are you to me, Tyre and Sidon, / and all the districts of Philistia? / Is it a payback you are paying me? / And if you are acting against me, / I will swiftly, quickly return / your payback on your own heads.
3:5 You took my silver and my gold. / My best and most precious things / you brought into your temples.
3:6 You sold the people of Judah and the people of Jerusalem / to the Greeks, / to send them far from their own country.
3:7 Look, I am about to stir them up / from the place where you sold them, / and I will return your payback on your own heads.
3:8 I will sell your sons and your daughters / into the hands of the people of Judah, / and they will sell them to the people of Sheba, / a nation far away. / For the LORD has spoken.
## Beat Your Plowshares into Swords
3:9 Call this out among the nations: / Consecrate war! / Wake the warriors. / Let every man of war step forward and march up.
3:10 Pound your plowshares into swords / and your pruning knives into spears. / Let even the weakest say, "I am a warrior."
3:11 Hurry and come, all you nations from all around, / and gather there. / LORD, bring down your warriors there.
3:12 Let the nations be stirred up / and march up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat, / for there I will sit to judge / all the nations all around.
3:13 Send in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe. / Come down and tread, for the winepress is full. / The vats are running over, / because their evil is great.
3:14 Crowds upon crowds in the valley of decision! / For the day of the LORD is near / in the valley of decision.
3:15 The sun and moon grow dark, / and the stars draw back their light.
3:16 The LORD roars from Zion / and gives out his voice from Jerusalem. / Heaven and earth shake. / But the LORD is a refuge for his people / and a stronghold for the Israelites.
3:17 Then you will know that I am the LORD your God, / who lives in Zion, my holy mountain. / Jerusalem will be a holy place, / and outsiders will never pass through her again.
## The Mountains Drip Wine
3:18 On that day sweet wine will drip from the mountains, / milk will run down the hills, / and water will run in every streambed of Judah. / A spring will flow out from the house of the LORD / and water the Valley of Shittim.
3:19 Egypt will become a waste, / and Edom a wilderness of ruin, / because of the violence against Judah's people, / for they shed innocent blood in Judah's land.
3:20 But Judah will be lived in forever, / and Jerusalem from generation to generation.
3:21 I will clear their blood, which I had not cleared. / And the LORD lives in Zion.
