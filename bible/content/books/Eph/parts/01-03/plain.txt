## Greeting
1:1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, to the saints who are in Ephesus, faithful in Christ Jesus.
1:2 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
## The great blessing
1:3 Blessed be God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. In Christ he has blessed us with every spiritual blessing, in the heavenly world.
1:4 As part of this, he chose us in Christ before the world was founded, to be holy and without blame before him in love.
1:5 He decided beforehand that we would be adopted as his children through Jesus Christ, to belong to himself. This was what he wanted, and it pleased him.
1:6 The result is praise for the glory of his grace, which he freely gave us in the Beloved.
1:7 In him we have redemption by his blood. This is the forgiveness of our trespasses, and it comes from the wealth of his grace,
1:8 which he made overflow toward us in all wisdom and understanding.
1:9 He made the mystery of his will known to us, as it pleased him. This was the purpose he set out in Christ,
1:10 as a plan for when the times are full: to bring all things together in Christ, the things in the heavens and the things on the earth. All of it is in him.
1:11 In him we were also given an inheritance, since we were decided on beforehand by the purpose of the one who works all things by the plan of his own will.
1:12 So we, who were the first to put our hope in Christ, are meant to bring praise to his glory.
1:13 In him you also heard the word of truth, the good news of your salvation. In him you also believed, and you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise.
1:14 This Spirit is a down payment on our inheritance, until the redemption of the people God has made his own. All of it is to the praise of his glory.
## Paul's prayer for knowing God
1:15 This is why I too, since I heard about the faith among you in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints,
1:16 never stop giving thanks for you. I mention you in my prayers,
1:17 asking that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, would give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation, as you come to know him.
1:18 May the eyes of your heart be lit up, so that you know the hope that comes with his call, the wealth of the glory of his inheritance among the saints,
1:19 and how very great his power is for us who believe. It is the work of the might of his strength,
1:20 which he put to work in Christ when he raised him from the dead and seated him at his right hand in the heavenly world,
1:21 far above every ruler, authority, power and lordship, and above every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the age that is coming.
1:22 God placed all things beneath his feet, and gave him to the church as head over all things.
1:23 The church is his body, the fullness of the one who is filling all things in every way.
## Made alive with Christ
2:1 You were dead, in your trespasses and your sins.
2:2 You once walked in them, following the age of this world. You followed the ruler of the domain of the air, the spirit who is now active among those who refuse to obey.
2:3 We all lived among them once, in the desires of our flesh, doing what the flesh and its thoughts wanted. By nature we were children of anger, like everyone else.
2:4 But God is rich in mercy. He loved us with a great love.
2:5 Even when we were dead in our trespasses, he gave us life together with Christ. It is by grace that you have been saved.
2:6 He raised us up with him and seated us with him in the heavenly world, in Christ Jesus,
2:7 so that in the ages to come he could show how very rich his grace is, by his kindness to us in Christ Jesus.
2:8 It is by grace that you have been saved, through faith. This does not come from you. It is God's gift.
2:9 It does not come from works, so that no one can boast.
2:10 We are what he has made, created in Christ Jesus to do good works. God prepared those works ahead of time, for us to walk in them.
## One new humanity
2:11 So remember: once you were Gentiles in the flesh. The people called "the circumcision," whose circumcision is made in the flesh by human hands, called you "the uncircumcision."
2:12 At that time you were separate from Christ. You were cut off from the commonwealth of Israel and were strangers to the covenants of the promise. You had no hope, and you were without God in the world.
2:13 But now, in Christ Jesus, you who were once far away have come near. The blood of Christ did it.
2:14 For he is himself our peace. He made the two one, and he broke down the middle wall, the wall that fenced us apart.
2:15 In his flesh he ended the hostility. He put out of effect the law of commandments with its rules, to create in him one new humanity out of the two, making peace,
2:16 and to reconcile both to God in one body through the cross. By it he killed the hostility.
2:17 He came with the good news of peace for you who were far away, and peace for those who were near.
2:18 It is through him that both groups have access to the Father, in one Spirit.
2:19 So you are no longer strangers and resident aliens. You are citizens together with the saints, and you belong to God's household.
2:20 You were built on the apostles and prophets as the foundation, with Christ Jesus himself as the cornerstone.
2:21 In him the whole building is fitted together, and it is growing up as a temple that is holy in the Lord.
2:22 In him you too are being built together, to be a home for God in the Spirit.
## The mystery and Paul's task
3:1 For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you Gentiles...
3:2 If you have indeed heard how I was given the task of managing God's grace for you,
3:3 and how the mystery became known to me through a revelation, as I wrote briefly before.
3:4 When you read it, you will be able to see how I understand the mystery of Christ.
3:5 In other generations it was not made known to human beings in the way it has now been unveiled to his holy apostles and prophets, in the Spirit.
3:6 The mystery is that the Gentiles are heirs together, members of the body together, and sharers together in the promise in Christ Jesus through the good news.
3:7 I became a servant of this good news, according to the gift of God's grace, which was given to me as his power went to work.
3:8 To me, less than the least of all the saints, this grace was given: to tell the Gentiles the good news of the riches of Christ, riches no one can trace out,
3:9 and to shine a light for everyone on the plan of the mystery, kept hidden from the ages in God, the creator of all things.
3:10 The purpose is that now, through the church, the richly varied wisdom of God would be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly world.
3:11 This fits the purpose of the ages, which he accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord.
3:12 In him we have freedom of speech and access, and we come with confidence, through faith in him.
3:13 So I ask that you not lose heart over my troubles for you, which are your glory.
## Paul's prayer for love and fullness
3:14 For this reason I kneel before the Father,
3:15 from whom every family in heaven and on earth gets its name.
3:16 I ask that out of the wealth of his glory he would give you power to be made strong, through his Spirit, in your inner self,
3:17 and that Christ would settle in your hearts through faith. May you be rooted and founded in love,
3:18 so that you will be fully able to grasp, together with all the saints, what the width and length and height and depth are,
3:19 and to know the love of Christ, though it goes beyond knowing, so that you may be filled up to all the fullness of God.
3:20 Now to the one who is able to do far beyond all that we ask or think, by the power working in us,
3:21 to him belongs the glory, in the church and in Christ Jesus, for all generations, forever and ever. Amen.
