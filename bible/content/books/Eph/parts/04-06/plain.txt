## One Body and One Hope
4:1 So I appeal to you, I who am a prisoner in the Lord: walk in a way that fits the calling you received.
4:2 Be completely humble and gentle, and be patient. Put up with one another in love.
4:3 Do your best to hold on to the unity that the Spirit gives, with peace as the tie that binds you.
4:4 There is one body and one Spirit. In the same way, you were called into one hope, the hope of your calling.
4:5 One Lord, one faith, one baptism.
4:6 One God and Father of all. He is over all, through all, and in all.
## Christ's Gifts to the Church
4:7 Each one of us was given grace, in the measure of Christ's gift.
4:8 That is why it says: "When he went up on high, he led a crowd of captives, and he gave gifts to people."
4:9 Now what does "he went up" mean, except that he also came down into the lower parts of the earth?
4:10 The one who came down is also the one who went up, higher than all the heavens, so that he might fill everything.
4:11 And he himself gave some as apostles, some as prophets, some as evangelists, and some as shepherds and teachers.
4:12 Their purpose is to get the saints ready, for the work of service, for the building up of the body of Christ,
4:13 until all of us arrive at oneness in the faith and in knowing the Son of God. We are to become a full-grown person, with the height that belongs to the fullness of Christ.
4:14 Then we will no longer be babies, tossed by waves and carried around by every wind of teaching, by the dice games of people, by a craftiness that is set on scheming and deceit.
4:15 Instead, being truthful in love, we will grow in every way into him. He is the head: Christ.
4:16 From him the whole body is fitted together and held firm by every joint that supplies it. As each single part does its work in its own measure, the body grows and builds itself up in love.
## The Old Self and the New
4:17 So I say this, and I insist on it in the Lord: you must no longer walk the way the Gentiles walk, whose mind is stuck in futility.
4:18 Their understanding has gone dark. They are strangers to the life of God, because of the ignorance inside them and the hardening of their hearts.
4:19 Having lost all feeling, they handed themselves over to sensuality, to carry out every kind of impurity, with greed.
4:20 But that is not how you learned Christ,
4:21 if you really did hear him and were taught in him, as the truth is in Jesus.
4:22 You were taught to take off the old self, the one that belongs to your earlier way of life and is being ruined by its deceiving desires,
4:23 to be made new in the spirit of your mind,
4:24 and to put on the new self, created in line with God, in the righteousness and holiness that truth brings.
## Words, Anger and Kindness
4:25 So put away falsehood. "Speak truth, each one of you, with your neighbor," because we are parts of one another.
4:26 "Be angry, but do not sin." Do not let the sun set on your anger,
4:27 and do not give the devil any room.
4:28 Whoever has been stealing must steal no more. He should work hard instead, making something good with his own hands, so that he has something to share with anyone in need.
4:29 Let no rotten word come out of your mouth. Say only what is good for building people up, as the need arises, so that it gives grace to those who hear.
4:30 And do not cause grief to the Holy Spirit of God. In him you were sealed, for the day of redemption.
4:31 Let all bitterness, rage and anger, and all shouting and abusive talk, be lifted away from you, along with every kind of malice.
4:32 Be kind and tenderhearted toward each other, forgiving one another freely, just as God also forgave you in Christ.
## Children of Light
5:1 So become imitators of God, like children who are loved,
5:2 and walk in love, in the way Christ also loved us and handed himself over for us, as an offering and a sacrifice to God, a soothing smell.
5:3 As for sexual immorality and any kind of impurity or greed, let them not even be mentioned among you. That is how it should be among saints.
5:4 Nor should there be shameful speech, silly talk or sly joking, none of which is fitting. Let there be thanksgiving instead.
5:5 You can know this for certain: no one who is sexually immoral, or unclean, or greedy (and greed is idolatry) has an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.
5:6 Let no one fool you with empty words. It is because of these things that God's anger comes on the people who disobey.
5:7 So do not become their partners.
5:8 For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. So walk as children of the light,
5:9 for the fruit of the light is found in every kind of goodness, righteousness and truth.
5:10 Test what pleases the Lord.
5:11 Do not join in the fruitless works of the darkness. Instead, show them up for what they are.
5:12 The things they do in secret are shameful even to talk about.
5:13 But everything that the light shows up is made visible, and whatever is made visible is light.
5:14 That is why it says: "Wake up, you who sleep! Stand up from among the dead, and Christ will shine on you."
## Be Filled with the Spirit
5:15 So watch carefully how you walk: not as unwise people, but as wise,
5:16 buying up the right moment, because the days are evil.
5:17 For this reason, do not be senseless, but understand what the Lord's will is.
5:18 And do not be drunk with wine, for reckless living is in that. Instead, be filled with the Spirit,
5:19 talking to one another in psalms, hymns and songs from the Spirit, singing and making music in your heart to the Lord,
5:20 always giving thanks for everything to God the Father, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,
5:21 submitting yourselves to one another in the fear of Christ.
## Wives and Husbands
5:22 Wives, to your own husbands, as to the Lord,
5:23 because a husband is head of his wife, in the way that Christ is also head of the church. He himself is the Savior of the body.
5:24 But as the church submits to Christ, so also wives to their husbands in everything.
5:25 Husbands, love your wives in the way Christ loved the church, handing himself over for her,
5:26 so that he might make her holy. He cleansed her by the washing of water, with the spoken word,
5:27 so that he himself might present the church to himself in glory, with no spot or wrinkle or anything of that kind, but holy and without fault.
5:28 That is how husbands ought to love their own wives: as their own bodies. A man who loves his wife is loving himself.
5:29 No one has ever hated his own flesh. He feeds it and keeps it warm, as Christ also does for the church,
5:30 because we are parts of his body.
5:31 "Because of this, a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will be one flesh."
5:32 This mystery is a great one. And I am speaking about Christ and about the church.
5:33 Still, you also, each one of you, must love his own wife in this way, as himself. And the wife must have respect for her husband.
## Children and Parents
6:1 Children, obey your parents, in the Lord. That is what is right.
6:2 "Honor your father and your mother." This is the first commandment that comes with a promise:
6:3 "so that it will go well with you, and you will live long on the earth."
6:4 Fathers, do not stir your children to anger. Raise them instead in the Lord's discipline and warning.
## Slaves and Masters
6:5 Slaves, obey your human masters with fear and trembling and a single-minded heart, as you would obey Christ.
6:6 Do not serve only while eyes are on you, like people who want to please others. Serve as slaves of Christ, doing God's will from the heart,
6:7 working with good will, as for the Lord and not for people.
6:8 You know that each person who does something good will get that same thing back from the Lord, slave or free.
6:9 Masters, treat them in the same way. Stop the threats. You know that their Lord and yours is in the heavens, and with him there is no favoritism.
## The Armor of God
6:10 Finally, be made strong in the Lord and in the power of his strength.
6:11 Dress yourselves in all of God's armor, so that you can stand against the schemes of the devil.
6:12 For our wrestling match is not against blood and flesh. It is against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world-rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavenly world.
6:13 For this reason, pick up all of God's armor. Then you will be able to hold your ground on the evil day and, when you have done everything, to remain standing.
6:14 Stand, then, with your waist wrapped in truth, with righteousness put on as your breastplate,
6:15 and with your feet fitted with the readiness of the good news of peace.
6:16 On top of all these, pick up the shield of faith. With it you will be able to put out every burning arrow of the evil one.
6:17 And receive the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is God's spoken word.
6:18 Through every kind of prayer and request, keep praying in the Spirit at every moment. To this end stay alert, with all persistence and with requests for all the saints,
6:19 and for me also: that when I open my mouth, a word may be given to me, so that I can make known with freedom and courage the mystery of the good news.
6:20 It is for this that I am an ambassador in a chain. Pray that in it I will speak freely, as I must.
## Tychicus and the Farewell
6:21 Now, so that you too may know how things stand with me and what I am doing, Tychicus will make everything known to you. He is a beloved brother and a faithful servant in the Lord.
6:22 That is exactly why I have sent him to you: so that you may know how we are, and so that he may put courage in your hearts.
6:23 Peace to the brothers, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
6:24 Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with an undying love.
