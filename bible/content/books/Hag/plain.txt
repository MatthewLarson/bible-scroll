## The word to the leaders
1:1 On the first day of the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest:
1:2 The LORD of Armies says this: This people says, "The time has not come, the time for rebuilding the LORD's house."
1:3 Then the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet:
1:4 Is this a time for you, yes you, to live in houses covered with wood, while this house is desolate?
1:5 So now the LORD of Armies says this: Take your ways to heart.
1:6 You have sown much, but you bring in little. You eat, but never to the point of being full. You drink, but never to the point of being drunk. You put on clothes, but no one is warm. And whoever earns wages earns them for a bag with holes poked in it.
1:7 The LORD of Armies says this: Take your ways to heart.
1:8 Go up into the hill country, bring wood, and build the house. Then I will be pleased with it, and I will be honored, says the LORD.
1:9 You looked for much, and see, it came to little. When you brought it into the house, I blew on it. Why? the LORD of Armies declares. Because of my house, which is desolate, while each of you runs to his own house.
1:10 This is why the heavens over you have held back dew, and the land has held back its produce.
1:11 I have summoned a drought on the land and on the hills, on the grain, the new wine and the fresh oil, on whatever the ground brings out, on people and livestock, and on all that hands labor for.
## The people obey and work
1:12 Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all the remnant of the people listened to the voice of the LORD their God and to the words of Haggai the prophet, since the LORD their God had sent him. And the people were afraid before the LORD.
1:13 Then Haggai, the LORD's messenger, spoke to the people with the LORD's message: "I am with you, the LORD declares."
1:14 The LORD roused the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people. They came and did the work on the house of the LORD of Armies, their God.
1:15 This was on the twenty-fourth day of the month, in the sixth month of the second year of King Darius.
## The first glory and the last
2:1 In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day of the month, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet:
2:2 Say to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and to the remnant of the people:
2:3 Who is left among you who saw this house in its first glory? How do you see it now? Is it not like nothing in your eyes?
2:4 And now, Zerubbabel, be strong, the LORD declares. Joshua son of Jehozadak, high priest, be strong. All you people of the land, be strong, the LORD declares, and work. For I am with you, the LORD of Armies declares.
2:5 As for the word that I cut with you when you came out of Egypt, my spirit is standing among you. Do not be afraid.
2:6 For the LORD of Armies says this: Once more, in a little while, I am about to shake the heavens and the earth, the sea and the dry ground.
2:7 I will shake all the nations. The prized things of all the nations will come, and I will fill this house with glory, says the LORD of Armies.
2:8 The silver belongs to me, and the gold belongs to me, the LORD of Armies declares.
2:9 The last glory of this house will be greater than the first, says the LORD of Armies. In this place I will give peace, the LORD of Armies declares.
## A ruling on holy and unclean
2:10 On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Haggai the prophet:
2:11 The LORD of Armies says this: Ask the priests for a ruling of the law.
2:12 "If someone carries meat that is holy in the fold of his robe, and that fold touches bread, or stew, or wine, or oil, or any other food, does the food become holy?" The priests answered, "No."
2:13 Then Haggai said, "Suppose someone made unclean by a dead person touches any of these. Will it become unclean?" The priests answered, "It will become unclean."
2:14 Then Haggai answered, "So it is with this people, and so with this nation before me, the LORD declares. So it is with all the work of their hands. What they offer there is unclean."
## From this day I will bless
2:15 And now, take it to heart, from this day on back, to before a stone was laid on a stone in the LORD's temple.
2:16 In those days, when someone came to a heap that should have held twenty measures, there were ten. When someone came to the wine vat to draw out fifty measures, there were twenty.
2:17 I struck you with blight, mildew and hail, in all the work of your hands. Yet none of you turned back to me, the LORD declares.
2:18 Take it to heart, from this day on back, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, from the day the LORD's temple was founded. Take it to heart.
2:19 Is the seed still in the barn? Even the vine, the fig tree, the pomegranate and the olive tree have not borne fruit. From this day on I will bless you.
## A seal ring for Zerubbabel
2:20 The word of the LORD came to Haggai a second time, on the twenty-fourth day of the month:
2:21 Say to Zerubbabel, governor of Judah: I am about to shake the heavens and the earth.
2:22 I will overturn the throne of kingdoms and wipe out the strength of the kingdoms of the nations. I will overturn the chariot and those who ride in it. Horses and their riders will go down, each by the sword of his brother.
2:23 On that day, the LORD of Armies declares, I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, my servant, the LORD declares, and I will make you like a seal ring, for I have chosen you, the LORD of Armies declares.
