## Greeting to the church in Smyrna
1:0 Ignatius, also called Theophorus, to the church of God the Father and of the beloved Jesus Christ. It has been shown mercy in every gift, and it is filled with faith and love and lacks no gift. It is most fit for God and a bearer of holy things. To the church that is in Smyrna, in Asia: abundant greetings in a blameless spirit and in the word of God.
## Truly born, truly nailed
1:1 I give glory to Jesus Christ, the God who has made you so wise. For I have seen that you are made complete in a faith that cannot be moved. It is as if you were nailed to the cross of the Lord Jesus Christ in flesh and in spirit, and firmly set in love by the blood of Christ. You are fully convinced about our Lord. He is truly of David's family according to the flesh, and Son of God by the will and power of God. He was truly born of a virgin, and baptized by John so that by him all righteousness might be fulfilled.
1:2 Under Pontius Pilate and Herod the tetrarch he was truly nailed for us in the flesh. We are from that fruit, from his suffering that God has blessed. He did this so that, through his resurrection, he might raise a flag for all ages for his holy and faithful people, whether among Jews or among the nations, in the one body of his church.
## He truly suffered
2:1 For he suffered all these things for our sake, so that we might be saved. And he truly suffered, just as he also truly raised himself. It is not as some unbelievers say, that he suffered only in appearance. They themselves are only in appearance! And it will turn out for them just as they think: they will be bodiless, like demons.
## In the flesh after the resurrection
3:1 For I know and believe that he was in the flesh even after the resurrection.
3:2 When he came to those with Peter, he said to them, "Take hold, feel me and see that I am not a bodiless demon." And at once they touched him and believed, joined to his flesh and his spirit. That is why they looked down on death itself, and were found to be above death.
3:3 After the resurrection he ate and drank with them as a man of flesh, though in spirit he was united with the Father.
## Beasts in human form
4:1 I urge these things on you, loved ones, though I know you see it the same way. I want to protect you in advance from beasts in human form. You must not only refuse to receive them; if you can, do not even meet them. Only pray for them, in case they may somehow repent, which is hard. Jesus Christ, who is our true life, has the power to do it.
4:2 For if these things were done by our Lord only in appearance, then I too am in chains only in appearance. And why have I handed myself over to death, to fire, to the sword, to wild beasts? Because near the sword is near God, and in the midst of wild beasts is in the midst of God. I endure everything only in the name of Jesus Christ, so that I may suffer with him, and he, the perfect human being, makes me strong.
## Those who deny him
5:1 Some, not knowing him, deny him. Or rather, they have been denied by him, since they speak up for death more than for the truth. The prophecies have not persuaded them, nor the law of Moses, nor even the gospel up to now, nor the sufferings each of us has borne.
5:2 For they think the same about us. What good does anyone do me if he praises me but blasphemes my Lord, not confessing that he was flesh-bearing? Whoever says this has denied him completely, and is himself a carrier of a corpse.
5:3 Their names are unbelieving, and I have not thought it right to write them down. In fact, may I not even remember them, until they repent and come back to the suffering, which is our resurrection.
## Faith and love are everything
6:1 Let no one be fooled. Even the things in heaven, the glory of the angels, and the rulers seen and unseen, if they do not believe in the blood of Christ, will face judgment too. Let the one who makes room for this make room for it. Let no one's place puff him up. The whole thing is faith and love, and nothing ranks above them.
6:2 But take note of those who hold other opinions about the grace of Jesus Christ that has come to us, and see how they go against the mind of God. They do not care about love: not for the widow, not for the orphan, not for the oppressed, not for the one in chains or the one set free, not for the hungry or the thirsty.
## Away from the thanksgiving
7:1 They keep away from the thanksgiving and from prayer, because they do not confess that the thanksgiving is the flesh of our Savior Jesus Christ, which suffered for our sins and which the Father raised in his kindness. So those who speak against God's gift die in their arguing. It would be better for them to love, so that they might also rise.
7:2 It is right to keep away from such people, and not to talk about them either in private or in public. Pay attention instead to the prophets, and above all to the gospel. In it the suffering has been made plain to us, and the resurrection has been brought to completion. Run from divisions, because they are the beginning of evils.
## Follow the overseer
8:1 All of you, follow the overseer the way Jesus Christ follows the Father. Follow the council of elders as if it were the apostles. Respect the deacons as God's command. Let no one do anything that belongs to the church without the overseer. Count that thanksgiving valid which is under the overseer, or under someone he entrusts it to.
8:2 Wherever the overseer appears, there let the people be, just as wherever Jesus Christ is, there is the catholic church. It is not lawful to baptize or to hold a love feast without the overseer. But whatever he approves is pleasing to God as well. That way all you do will be safe and valid.
9:1 From now on it makes sense for us to come back to our senses, while there is still time to turn to God in repentance. It is good to know God and the overseer. Whoever honors the overseer has been honored by God. Whoever does anything behind the overseer's back is serving the devil.
9:2 So let everything overflow for you in grace, for you are worthy. You refreshed me in every way, and so may Jesus Christ refresh you. You loved me when I was away and when I was with you. God is your reward. Enduring everything for his sake, you will gain him.
## Philo and Rheus Agathopus
10:1 Philo and Rheus Agathopus followed me for the word of God, and you did well to welcome them as deacons of God. They also give thanks to the Lord for you, because you refreshed them in every way. None of it will ever be lost for you.
10:2 My spirit is a life in exchange for yours, and so are my chains, which you did not look down on and were not ashamed of. Nor will the perfect hope, Jesus Christ, be ashamed of you.
## Send an envoy to Syria
11:1 Your prayer has gone out to the church in Antioch of Syria. I come from there, bound in chains most fit for God, and I greet everyone. I am not worthy to be from there, since I am the last of them. But by God's will I was counted worthy, not from anything in my own conscience, but from the grace of God. I pray that this grace will be given to me in full, so that through your prayer I may reach God.
11:2 So that your work may be complete on earth and in heaven, your church should, for God's honor, elect an envoy of God. He should go to Syria and rejoice with them that they are at peace, that they have gained back their own greatness, and that their own body has been restored to them.
11:3 So it seemed to me a deed worthy of God for one of your people to carry a letter to them, to join in praising the calm that God has given them, and the fact that through your prayer they were already reaching harbor. You are mature, so think mature thoughts as well. For when you want to do good, God is ready to provide it.
## Greetings from Troas
12:1 The love of the brothers in Troas greets you. I am writing to you from there by Burrhus, whom you sent with me together with the Ephesians, your brothers. He has refreshed me in every way. If only everyone would imitate him, since he is a model of service to God. Grace will repay him in every way.
12:2 I greet your overseer, worthy of God, and your council of elders, fit for God, and my fellow slaves the deacons, and all of you, one by one and all together. I greet you in the name of Jesus Christ, in his flesh and blood, and in his suffering and resurrection, of the flesh and of the spirit, in unity with God and with you. Grace to you, mercy, peace and endurance, always.
13:1 I greet my brothers' households, with their wives and children, and the virgins who are called widows. Farewell to you in the power of the Father. Philo, who is with me, greets you.
13:2 I greet the household of Tavia. I pray that she will be firmly set in faith and love, of the flesh and of the spirit. I greet Alce, a name dear to me, and Daphnus, who has no equal, and Eutecnus, and everyone by name. Farewell in the grace of God.
