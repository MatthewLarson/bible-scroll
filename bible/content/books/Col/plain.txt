## Greeting
1:1 Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and Timothy our brother,
1:2 to the saints in Colossae, the faithful brothers in Christ. Grace to you and peace from God our Father.
## Thanks for the good news among them
1:3 Every time we pray for you, we thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:4 We have heard about your faith in Christ Jesus, and about the love you hold toward all the saints.
1:5 You have them because of the hope that is set aside for you in the heavens. You heard of that hope before, in the word of truth, the good news
1:6 that is here with you. All over the world it keeps bearing fruit and growing, and it has done the same among you ever since the day you heard it and came to know God's grace as it truly is.
1:7 It was Epaphras, our beloved fellow slave, who taught you. For our sake he is a faithful servant of Christ,
1:8 and he is the one who let us know of your love in the Spirit.
## A prayer for knowledge and strength
1:9 This is why, since the day we heard, we too keep praying for you without a break. We ask God to fill you with the full knowledge of what he wants, in every kind of wisdom and in spiritual understanding.
1:10 Then you will walk as the Lord deserves, pleasing him in everything. You will bear fruit in every good thing you do, and you will grow in knowing God.
1:11 You will be given power, all the power that comes from the might of his glory, so that you can hold out and stay patient in everything with joy.
1:12 You will give thanks to the Father. He has made you fit to share in the lot of the saints in the light.
1:13 He rescued us out of the authority of darkness and moved us into the kingdom of the Son of his love.
1:14 In him we have redemption. This is the forgiveness of our sins.
## The firstborn of all creation
1:15 He is the image of the God who cannot be seen, / the firstborn of all creation,
1:16 because in him all things were created, / in the heavens and on the earth, / the seen and the unseen, / whether thrones or lordships or rulers or authorities. / Through him and for him all things have been created.
1:17 He himself is before all things, / and all things hold together in him.
1:18 He himself is the head of the body, the church. / He is the beginning, / the firstborn from the dead, / so that he himself would come to be first in all things,
1:19 because in him all the fullness was pleased to make its home,
1:20 and through him to reconcile all things to himself, / making peace through the blood of his cross, / through him, whether the things on the earth or the things in the heavens.
## Reconciled to stand before him
1:21 And you were once strangers to him, hostile in your thinking and in the evil things you did. But now he has reconciled you
1:22 in the body of his flesh, through his death. He did it so that he can present you before him holy and without blame and free of any charge.
1:23 That is, if you stay in the faith, with your foundation laid and standing firm, not shifted away from the hope of the good news you heard. This good news has been announced to every creature under heaven, and I, Paul, became its servant.
## Paul's work for the mystery
1:24 Now I am glad in my sufferings for you. What is still missing from Christ's hardships I am filling up in my own flesh, on behalf of his body, the church.
1:25 I became the church's servant through the task of managing that God gave me for you: to bring God's word to its full extent.
1:26 It is the mystery that was kept hidden from the ages and from the generations, but now has been made plain to his saints.
1:27 It was God's will to show them the wealth of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles. The mystery is Christ in you, the hope of glory.
1:28 He is the one we tell of. We warn every person and teach every person in all wisdom, to bring every person before God complete in Christ.
1:29 For this I labor and strain, by his working, which is at work in me with power.
2:1 I want you to know what a hard fight I am in for you, for those in Laodicea, and for all who have never seen my face in person.
2:2 I want their hearts to take courage, held firm together in love, and to reach all the wealth that comes from understanding with full certainty, so that they know God's mystery, that is, Christ.
2:3 In him all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge lie hidden.
2:4 I say this so that no one will lead you astray with smooth talk.
2:5 I may be absent in the flesh, but in spirit I am there with you. I rejoice as I see you standing in good order, and your faith in Christ standing solid.
## Walk in him
2:6 So walk in him, in the same way you received Christ Jesus, the Lord.
2:7 Be rooted in him and keep being built up in him. Be made firm in the faith as you were taught, and overflow with thanksgiving.
## The fullness of deity and the stripped powers
2:8 Watch out, so that no one carries you off like plunder with philosophy, with empty tricks that follow human tradition and the basic elements of the world, and do not follow Christ.
2:9 Because in him all the fullness of deity makes its home bodily,
2:10 and you have been made full in him. He is the head of every ruler and authority.
2:11 In him you also received circumcision, one not done by human hands. It was the stripping off of the body of the flesh, the circumcision of Christ.
2:12 In baptism you were buried with him. In it you were also raised with him, through faith in God's working, for God raised him from the dead.
2:13 You were dead in your trespasses and in the uncircumcision of your flesh. But God gave you life together with him. He forgave us all our trespasses.
2:14 He wiped away the handwritten bill of debt that was against us, with its decrees. It stood in our way, and he has taken it out of the way by nailing it to the cross.
2:15 He stripped the rulers and the authorities bare and made an open example of them, leading them in his victory parade by it.
## Shadow and body
2:16 So let no one sit in judgment over you in eating or drinking, or over a festival, a new moon or Sabbaths.
2:17 These are the shadow of what is to come, but the body is Christ's.
2:18 Let no one act as umpire and rule you out, someone who delights in self-humbling and in the worship of angels, entering into the things he has seen. He is puffed up for no reason by the mind of his flesh,
2:19 and he does not hold on to the head. From him the whole body, supplied and held firm by its joints and ligaments, grows with the growth that comes from God.
2:20 If you died with Christ and left the basic elements of the world behind, why let yourselves be put under decrees, as though you were still living in the world?
2:21 "Do not take hold! Do not taste! Do not even touch!"
2:22 These are all things that wear out and are gone once they are used. The rules follow the commandments and teachings of people.
2:23 They do have a reputation for wisdom: worship people choose for themselves, self-humbling, and not sparing the body. But they are not worth anything against the flesh's craving to be filled.
## Seek what is above
3:1 So then, since you were raised with Christ, go after what is above. That is where Christ is, sitting at God's right hand.
3:2 Let what is above fill your thoughts, not what is on the earth.
3:3 You died, you see, and your life is kept hidden away with Christ in God.
3:4 When Christ is revealed, he who is your life, then you too will be revealed with him in glory.
## Taking off the old self
3:5 So put to death the parts of you that belong to the earth: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and greed, since greed is idolatry.
3:6 It is because of these things that God's anger comes on the people who disobey.
3:7 You also used to walk in these, back when your life was in them.
3:8 But now you also must take them all off: anger, rage, malice, abusive talk, and foul words out of your mouth.
3:9 Stop lying to one another, since you have taken off the old self and the things it did,
3:10 and have put on the new self, which is being made new in knowledge, to match the image of the one who created it.
3:11 There, no one is Greek or Jew, circumcised or uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave or free. Christ is all things and in all.
## Putting on the new self
3:12 You are God's chosen ones, holy and loved. So put on tenderhearted compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience.
3:13 Put up with one another, and forgive one another freely if anyone has a complaint against someone. Just as the Lord also forgave you, you must do the same.
3:14 On top of all these, put on love, the tie that makes them complete.
3:15 Let the peace of Christ be the umpire in your hearts. You were called to that peace in one body. Keep on being thankful.
3:16 Let the word of Christ live in you richly. Teach and warn one another in all wisdom, in psalms, hymns and songs from the Spirit. Sing to God in your hearts with thanks.
3:17 Let everything you do, by word or by deed, be done in the name of the Lord Jesus, and through him thank God the Father.
## Wives and husbands, children and fathers, slaves and masters
3:18 Wives, submit yourselves to your husbands, as is proper in the Lord.
3:19 Husbands, love your wives, and do not turn bitter against them.
3:20 Children, obey your parents in everything. That is what is pleasing in the Lord.
3:21 Fathers, do not keep irritating your children, so that they do not lose heart.
3:22 Slaves, obey your human masters in everything. Do not serve only while eyes are on you, like people who want to please others, but with a single-minded heart, fearing the Lord.
3:23 Whatever you are doing, do the work from the heart, as for the Lord and not for people.
3:24 You know that you will get your repayment from the Lord: the inheritance. The Lord Christ is the one you serve as slaves.
3:25 For the one who does wrong will get his own wrongdoing back. There is no favoritism.
4:1 Masters, grant your slaves what is just and what is equal. You know that you too have a Lord in heaven.
## Prayer and those outside
4:2 Keep at prayer without giving up. Stay awake in it, with thanksgiving.
4:3 And pray for us as well, asking God to open a door for the word for us, so we can speak the mystery of Christ. It is for this that I am bound,
4:4 so pray that I may make it plain, speaking as I must.
4:5 Be wise in the way you walk toward those outside, buying up the right moment.
4:6 Your words should always come with grace and have salt in them for flavor. Then you will know how you need to answer each one.
## Tychicus and Onesimus
4:7 Tychicus will make everything known to you about how things stand with me. He is a beloved brother, a faithful servant and a fellow slave in the Lord.
4:8 That is exactly why I have sent him to you: so that you may know how we are, and so that he may put courage in your hearts.
4:9 He comes with Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will make known to you everything that is happening here.
## Greetings
4:10 Greetings to you from Aristarchus, my fellow captive, and from Mark, Barnabas's cousin. You have had instructions about Mark: welcome him if he comes to you.
4:11 Jesus, the one called Justus, sends greetings too. These are the only ones from the circumcision who work with me for the kingdom of God. They have become my comfort.
4:12 Greetings also from Epaphras, one of your own, a slave of Christ. He never stops fighting for you in his prayers, asking that you stand complete and fully convinced in everything God wants.
4:13 I testify that he takes great pains for you, and for the people of Laodicea and Hierapolis.
4:14 Luke, the beloved doctor, sends you greetings, and Demas does too.
4:15 Greet the brothers who are in Laodicea, and Nympha, and the church that meets in her house.
4:16 After the letter is read out among you, arrange for it to be read in the Laodiceans' church as well. You, for your part, read the one from Laodicea.
4:17 And say this to Archippus: "Watch over the service you received in the Lord, and carry it out fully."
## Paul's own hand
4:18 The greeting is in my own hand, Paul's. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.
