## The Lord of all things
1:0 The prayer of Manasseh.
1:1 Lord Almighty in heaven, / God of our fathers, / of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob / and of the righteous who came from them:
1:2 you made heaven and earth / with all their array.
1:3 You put the sea in fetters / by the word of your command. / You shut the abyss / and sealed it with your fearsome and glorious name.
1:4 Everything shudders at you / and trembles at the sight of your power.
1:5 For no one can bear the splendor of your glory, / and no one can stand against your anger / when you threaten sinners.
1:6 The mercy you promise / is past measuring and past searching out.
1:7 For you are the Lord Most High, / tender-hearted, slow to anger and great in mercy, / and you change your mind about the harm that comes on human beings.
## Repentance set for a sinner
1:8 So you, Lord, God of the righteous, / did not set repentance for the righteous, / for Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, / who had not sinned against you. / You set repentance for me, the sinner.
1:9 For I have sinned beyond the count of the sand of the sea. / My lawless deeds have piled up, / and I am not fit to fix my eyes / and look at the height of heaven, / because my wrongs are so many.
1:10 A heavy iron chain bends me low, / so that I cannot lift up my head, / and I get no relief. / For I stirred up your anger / and did what is evil in your sight. / I set up abhorrent things / and heaped up things you loathe.
## The knee of the heart
1:11 Now I bend my heart's knee, / begging you for your kindness.
1:12 I have sinned, Lord, I have sinned, / and I myself know my lawless deeds.
1:13 I ask you, I beg you: / let me go free, Lord, let me go free. / Do not destroy me along with my lawless deeds. / Do not stay angry forever and store up harm for me. / Do not condemn me to the lowest parts of the earth. / For you, Lord, are the God of those who repent,
1:14 and in me you will show your goodness. / You will save me, though I am unworthy, / as your mercy is great,
1:15 and I will praise you always, / through the days of my life. / For the whole army of the heavens sings hymns to you, / and yours is the glory forever. Amen.
