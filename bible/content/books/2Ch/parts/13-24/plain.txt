## Abijah king of Judah
13:1 Abijah took the throne over Judah in the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam.
13:2 He reigned in Jerusalem for three years. His mother's name was Micaiah, Uriel's daughter, from Gibeah. There was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.
13:3 Abijah opened the battle with an army of mighty men of war, four hundred thousand chosen men. Jeroboam set his ranks for battle facing him with eight hundred thousand chosen men, mighty men of strength.
## Abijah's speech on Mount Zemaraim
13:4 Abijah took his stand on top of Mount Zemaraim, in Ephraim's hill country. "Listen to me, Jeroboam, and all Israel!" he said.
13:5 "It is yours to know, isn't it, that the LORD, the God of Israel, gave David the kingship over Israel for all time, to him and to his sons, by a covenant of salt?
13:6 But Jeroboam son of Nebat rose up. He was a servant of David's son Solomon, and he revolted against his lord.
13:7 Empty men gathered around him, worthless men, and they proved too strong for Rehoboam son of Solomon. Rehoboam was young and timid of heart, and he could not hold his own against them.
13:8 And now you think you can hold your own against the LORD's kingdom, which is in the hands of David's sons. You are a great crowd, and with you are the calves of gold that Jeroboam made for you as gods.
13:9 Didn't you drive out the LORD's priests, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites? Didn't you make priests for yourselves the way the peoples of the lands do? Anyone who comes with a young bull and seven rams to be installed becomes a priest of no-gods.
13:10 For our part, the LORD is our God, and we have not left him. The priests serving the LORD are Aaron's sons, and the Levites are at their work.
13:11 Morning by morning and evening by evening they make burnt offerings go up in smoke to the LORD, with fragrant incense. The row of bread is on the pure table, and the gold lampstand and its lamps are lit evening by evening. For we keep our duty to the LORD our God, but you have left him.
13:12 Look: God is with us, at our head, and so are his priests with the trumpets of the battle blast, to sound the blast against you. Israelites, don't make war on the LORD, the God of your ancestors. You will not win."
## God strikes Jeroboam
13:13 But Jeroboam had sent the ambush around to get behind them. His men were in front of Judah, and the ambush was at their back.
13:14 Judah turned and saw that they had battle in front of them and behind them. They cried out to the LORD, and the priests were blowing on the trumpets.
13:15 The men of Judah let out a war cry. As the men of Judah gave the cry, God struck Jeroboam and all Israel in front of Abijah and Judah.
13:16 The Israelites fled from Judah, and God put them in Judah's power.
13:17 Abijah and his army struck them hard, with a great slaughter. Of Israel five hundred thousand chosen men fell slain.
13:18 The Israelites were humbled at that time. The men of Judah proved stronger, because they had leaned on the LORD, the God of their ancestors.
13:19 Abijah went in pursuit of Jeroboam and captured towns from him: Bethel and its villages, Jeshanah and its villages, and Ephrain and its villages.
13:20 Jeroboam did not hold on to his power any longer while Abijah lived. The LORD struck him, and he died.
13:21 Abijah, though, kept growing stronger. He took fourteen wives for himself, and fathered twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.
13:22 The rest of Abijah's story, his ways and his words, is written in the commentary of the prophet Iddo.
## Asa seeks the LORD
14:1 Abijah lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him in the City of David. His son Asa became king in his place. In his days the land was quiet for ten years.
14:2 Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God.
14:3 He took away the altars for foreign worship and the high places. He smashed the standing stones and chopped down the Asherah poles.
14:4 He told Judah to seek the LORD, the God of their ancestors, and to do what the law and the command said.
14:5 He removed the high places and the incense altars from all the towns of Judah, and the kingdom was quiet under him.
14:6 He built fortified towns in Judah, since the land was quiet. No one made war on him in those years, because the LORD had given him rest.
14:7 He told Judah, "Let's build up these towns and ring them with walls, with towers, gates and bars. The land still lies open before us, because we sought the LORD our God. We sought him, and he gave us rest on all sides." So they built, and they did well.
14:8 Asa had an army of men who carried large shields and spears: three hundred thousand from Judah. From Benjamin there were two hundred eighty thousand who carried small shields and drew the bow. All these were mighty men of strength.
## Zerah the Cushite
14:9 Zerah the Cushite came out to fight them. He had an army of a thousand thousands and three hundred chariots, and he got as far as Mareshah.
14:10 Asa went out against him. They set their battle lines in the Zephathah Valley at Mareshah.
14:11 Asa called out to the LORD his God. "LORD," he said, "for you there is no difference in helping, whether the side is great or has no strength. Help us, LORD our God, for we lean on you. We have come against this crowd in your name. LORD, you are our God. Do not let a mere man hold out against you."
14:12 The LORD struck the Cushites in front of Asa and Judah, and the Cushites fled.
14:13 Asa and the people who were with him chased them all the way to Gerar. Cushites fell until there was no life left in them, for they were broken before the LORD and before his camp. And Judah's men carried away very much loot.
14:14 They struck all the towns around Gerar, for the dread of the LORD was on them. They looted every town, because the towns were full of loot.
14:15 They also struck down the tents where the herds were kept, and led away sheep in abundance, and camels. Then they went back to Jerusalem.
## Azariah's message and Asa's covenant
15:1 Now Azariah son of Oded had the Spirit of God on him.
15:2 He went out in front of Asa. "Hear me, Asa, all Judah and Benjamin!" he said. "The LORD is with you as long as you are with him. Seek him, and he will let you find him. Leave him, and he will leave you.
15:3 For many days Israel had no God of truth, no priest who taught, and no law.
15:4 But when trouble came, they turned back to the LORD, the God of Israel. They sought him, and he let them find him.
15:5 In those times there was no peace for anyone going out or coming in. All who lived in those lands were in great confusion.
15:6 They were beaten to pieces, nation against nation and city against city, for God troubled them with every trouble.
15:7 But you, be strong. Don't let your hands go limp, for your work will have its reward."
15:8 When Asa heard these words, the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took heart. He removed the disgusting idols from all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and from the towns he had captured in the hill country of Ephraim. He renewed the LORD's altar that stood in front of the LORD's porch.
15:9 He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and the people from Ephraim, Manasseh and Simeon who had settled among them. For many from Israel had come over to him when they saw that the LORD his God was with him.
15:10 It was the fifteenth year of Asa's reign, in the third month, when they gathered in Jerusalem.
15:11 That day they sacrificed to the LORD from the plunder they had brought: seven hundred cattle and seven thousand sheep.
15:12 They came into the covenant: they would seek the LORD, the God of their ancestors, with all their heart and all their being.
15:13 Whoever did not seek the LORD, the God of Israel, would be put to death, from the least to the greatest, from man to woman.
15:14 They gave the LORD their oath with a great voice and a shout, with trumpets and rams' horns blowing.
15:15 All Judah was glad about the oath, because they had sworn it with all their heart and sought him with all their will. He let them find him, and the LORD gave them rest on all sides.
15:16 Even Maacah, mother of King Asa: he took away the rank of queen mother from her, because she had made a horrid thing for Asherah. Asa cut down her horrid thing, crushed it and burned it in the Kidron Valley.
15:17 The high places were not taken away from Israel, but Asa's heart was whole all his days.
15:18 He brought into God's house the things his father had set apart as holy, and his own holy gifts: silver, gold and utensils.
15:19 There was no war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa's reign.
## Asa's treaty with Aram
16:1 In the thirty-sixth year of Asa's reign, Baasha king of Israel came up against Judah and built up Ramah, so that no one could go out or come in for Asa king of Judah.
16:2 Asa brought out silver and gold from the treasuries of the LORD's house and the king's house. He sent it to Ben-hadad king of Aram, who lived in Damascus, with this message:
16:3 "Let a covenant stand between me and you, between my father and your father. Look, I am sending you silver and gold. Go and break your covenant with Baasha king of Israel, so that he will pull back from me."
16:4 Ben-hadad listened to King Asa. He sent the commanders of his armies against Israel's towns, and they struck Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim, and all the store towns of Naphtali.
16:5 As soon as Baasha heard of it, he quit building Ramah and put a stop to his work.
16:6 Then King Asa took all Judah. They hauled away the stones and timber of Ramah that Baasha had built with, and he used them to fortify Geba and Mizpah.
## Hanani the seer
16:7 At that time Hanani the seer came to Asa king of Judah. He told him, "Because you leaned on the king of Aram and did not lean on the LORD your God, that is why the king of Aram's army got away from you.
16:8 The Cushites and the Libyans were an army too, weren't they? A huge crowd, with chariots and horsemen, very many. But since you leaned on the LORD, he put them into your hands.
16:9 For the LORD's eyes roam all over the earth, so that he may show himself strong for those whose hearts are whole toward him. You have acted like a fool in this. From now on there will be wars for you."
16:10 Asa grew angry at the seer and put him in the prison with the stocks, because he raged at him over this. At that same time Asa crushed some of the people.
## Asa's sickness and death
16:11 Asa's story, early and late, is there, written in the scroll of the kings of Judah and Israel.
16:12 Asa fell sick in his feet in the thirty-ninth year of his reign, and his sickness rose higher and higher. Even in his sickness he did not seek the LORD, only the healers.
16:13 Asa lay down with his ancestors. He died in the forty-first year of his reign.
16:14 They buried him in his own tomb, which he had dug for himself in the City of David. They laid him on a couch that he had filled with spices, all kinds of them, mixed with a perfumer's skill. Then they lit a great fire for him, very great.
## Jehoshaphat and the scroll of the law
17:1 His son Jehoshaphat became king in his place, and he made himself strong against Israel.
17:2 He set an army in every fortified town of Judah, and posts of soldiers in the land of Judah and in the Ephraim towns his father Asa had taken.
17:3 The LORD was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the ways his forefather David had walked at first. He did not seek the Baals.
17:4 Instead he sought his father's God and walked in his commands. He did not act the way Israel acted.
17:5 So the LORD made the kingdom secure in his hand. All Judah paid Jehoshaphat tribute, and he had riches and honor in abundance.
17:6 His heart rose high in the LORD's ways. Once more he took away the high places and the Asherah poles from Judah.
17:7 In his third year as king, he sent out his officials to teach in Judah's towns: Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel and Micaiah.
17:8 With them went the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah and Tob-adonijah, all Levites, and with them the priests Elishama and Jehoram.
17:9 They taught in Judah, and they had with them the scroll of the LORD's law. They went around all the towns of Judah and taught among the people.
## Jehoshaphat's power
17:10 The dread of the LORD was on all the kingdoms of the lands around Judah, and they did not make war on Jehoshaphat.
17:11 From the Philistines came tribute for Jehoshaphat, and silver by the load. The Arabs also brought him flocks: rams, seven thousand seven hundred, and male goats, seven thousand seven hundred.
17:12 So Jehoshaphat grew greater and greater. He built forts and store towns in Judah.
17:13 Much property was his in Judah's towns, and he kept soldiers, mighty men of strength, in Jerusalem.
17:14 This is how they were enrolled by their families. From Judah, the commanders of thousands: Adnah the commander, and with him three hundred thousand mighty men of strength.
17:15 Next to him was Jehohanan the commander, and with him two hundred eighty thousand.
17:16 Next to him was Amasiah son of Zichri, who offered himself freely to the LORD, and with him two hundred thousand mighty men of strength.
17:17 From Benjamin: Eliada, a mighty man of strength, and with him two hundred thousand armed with bow and shield.
17:18 Next to him was Jehozabad, and with him one hundred eighty thousand armed for war.
17:19 These served the king. There were others too, whom the king had put in the fortified towns all over Judah.
## Jehoshaphat joins Ahab
18:1 Jehoshaphat had riches and honor in abundance, and he tied himself to Ahab by marriage.
18:2 At the end of some years he went down to Samaria, to Ahab. Ahab slaughtered sheep and cattle in abundance for him and for the people with him, and he coaxed him into going up against Ramoth-gilead.
18:3 Ahab king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, "Will you come with me to Ramoth-gilead?" He said to him, "I am one with you, my people one with your people, and we will be with you in the battle."
## The prophets and Micaiah
18:4 But Jehoshaphat also told the king of Israel, "Please, today, ask what the LORD has to say."
18:5 The king of Israel gathered the prophets, four hundred men. "Shall we march on Ramoth-gilead to fight," he asked them, "or shall I let it be?" "Go up," they said. "God will hand it over to the king."
18:6 Jehoshaphat said, "Isn't there still a prophet of the LORD around here? Let us ask him too."
18:7 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is still one man through whom we could seek the LORD, but I hate him. He does not prophesy good things about me, but evil all his days. He is Micaiah son of Imlah." Jehoshaphat said, "The king shouldn't talk like that."
18:8 The king of Israel called a court official over. "Hurry, get Micaiah son of Imlah," he said.
18:9 The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were each sitting on his throne, wearing robes. They were sitting on the threshing floor at the opening of Samaria's gate. All the prophets were prophesying in front of them.
18:10 Zedekiah son of Kenaanah had made himself horns of iron. He said, "The LORD says this: 'With these you will butt Aram until you finish them off.'"
18:11 All the prophets were prophesying the same. "Go up to Ramoth-gilead and win," they said. "The LORD will hand it over to the king."
18:12 The messenger who went to call Micaiah told him, "Look, the prophets speak with one mouth, all good for the king. Please let your word be like one of theirs. Speak good."
18:13 But Micaiah said, "By the life of the LORD, what my God tells me is what I will speak."
18:14 He came to the king, and the king asked him, "Micah, shall we march on Ramoth-gilead to fight, or shall I let it be?" "Go up and win," he said. "They will be handed over to you."
18:15 The king said to him, "How often do I have to put you under oath to speak only the truth to me in the LORD's name?"
18:16 Micaiah said, "I saw all Israel scattered on the mountains like a flock with no shepherd. And the LORD said, 'These have no masters. Let each of them go home in peace.'"
18:17 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Didn't I tell you? He does not prophesy good things about me, never anything but evil."
## Micaiah's vision
18:18 Micaiah said, "So listen to the LORD's word. I saw the LORD sitting on his throne, and all the army of heaven standing on his right and his left.
18:19 The LORD said, 'Who will lure Ahab king of Israel into going up, so that he falls at Ramoth-gilead?' One said one thing, and another said another.
18:20 Then the spirit came out and stood in front of the LORD. 'I will lure him,' it said. 'How?' the LORD asked it.
18:21 'I will go out,' it said, 'and I will be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' 'You will lure him,' he said, 'and you will win, too. Go out and do it.'
18:22 So now, look: the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of your prophets here. The LORD has spoken disaster against you."
18:23 Zedekiah son of Kenaanah came up and struck Micaiah on the cheek. "Just which way did the LORD's Spirit take when it crossed over from me to talk to you?" he said.
18:24 Micaiah said, "Look, you will see on that day, when you go into a room inside a room to hide."
18:25 The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and bring him back to Amon, the commander of the city, and to Joash, the king's son.
18:26 Tell them, 'The king says this: Put this man in the prison house. Feed him the bread of hardship and the water of hardship until I come back in peace.'"
18:27 Micaiah said, "If you really do come back in peace, the LORD has not spoken through me." And he said, "Listen, peoples, all of you!"
## Ahab dies at Ramoth-gilead
18:28 The king of Israel went up to Ramoth-gilead, and Jehoshaphat king of Judah with him.
18:29 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and go into the battle. But you, put on your own robes." So the king of Israel disguised himself, and they went into the battle.
18:30 The king of Aram had given orders to his chariot commanders: "Don't fight anyone, small or great. Fight only the king of Israel, him alone."
18:31 When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they said, "That is the king of Israel!" They closed in around him to attack, and Jehoshaphat cried out. The LORD came to his aid; God coaxed them away from him.
18:32 When the chariot commanders saw that he was not the king of Israel, they turned back from chasing him.
18:33 But one man pulled back his bow with no special aim, and he hit the king of Israel between the joints of his armor and the breastplate. The king called to the man at the reins, "Swing the chariot around and drive me out of the battle lines. I've been hit."
18:34 The fighting rose higher that day. The king of Israel held himself up in the chariot, facing Aram, until evening, and he died at the time the sun went down.
## Jehu the visionary rebukes Jehoshaphat
19:1 Jehoshaphat king of Judah came back in peace to his house in Jerusalem.
19:2 Jehu son of Hanani the visionary went out to meet him. He said to King Jehoshaphat, "Is it the wicked you should be helping? Is it those who hate the LORD you should love? For this, wrath from the LORD's presence is on you.
19:3 But good things have been found in you. You cleared the Asherah poles out of the land and made your heart ready to seek God."
## Judges for Judah
19:4 Jehoshaphat made his home in Jerusalem. But once more he went out among the people, all the way from Beersheba to Ephraim's hill country, and brought them back to the LORD, their ancestors' God.
19:5 He set judges in the land, in each of Judah's fortified towns, town by town.
19:6 "Look at what you are doing," he told the judges. "It is not for people that you judge, but for the LORD. He is with you in the matter of judgment.
19:7 So now let the dread of the LORD be on you. Be careful what you do, for with the LORD our God there is no crookedness, no playing favorites, no taking a bribe."
19:8 In Jerusalem, too, Jehoshaphat appointed some of the Levites and priests and some of the heads of Israel's families, to give the LORD's judgment and to settle disputes. Then they returned to Jerusalem.
19:9 He gave them this order: "This is how you must act: in the fear of the LORD, faithfully, and with a whole heart.
19:10 Whenever a case comes to you from your brothers who live in their towns, whether it is between one kind of bloodshed and another, or about law and command, rules and rulings, warn them. Then they will not become guilty before the LORD, and wrath will not come on you and your brothers. Act this way, and you will not be guilty.
19:11 Look, Amariah the head priest is over you in every matter of the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, the leader of the house of Judah, in every matter of the king. The Levites will serve you as officials. Be strong and get to work, and may the LORD be with the good."
## The armies from beyond the Sea
20:1 After this the Moabites and the Ammonites, and some of the Meunites with them, came against Jehoshaphat to make war.
20:2 Some men came and told Jehoshaphat, "A great crowd is coming against you from beyond the Sea, from Aram. They are already in Hazazon-tamar," that is, En-gedi.
20:3 Jehoshaphat was afraid, and he turned his face to seeking the LORD. He called for a fast throughout Judah.
20:4 Judah came together to ask the LORD for help. People came out of every town of Judah to seek the LORD.
## Jehoshaphat's prayer
20:5 Jehoshaphat stood up in the assembly of Judah and Jerusalem in the LORD's house, in front of the new courtyard.
20:6 "LORD, God of our ancestors," he said, "you are the God in the heavens, aren't you? You are ruler over every kingdom of the nations. In your hand are strength and power, and no one can hold his ground against you.
20:7 Our God, it was you who drove out those living in this land before your people Israel, wasn't it? You gave it for all time to the offspring of your friend Abraham.
20:8 They settled in it. In it they built you a holy place for your name, and said,
20:9 'Should evil come on us, whether sword, judgment, plague or famine, we will take our stand before this house, and before you, since your name is in this house. Out of our trouble we will cry to you, and you will hear and save.'
20:10 And now, see the Ammonites, Moab and Mount Seir! When Israel came out of the land of Egypt, you did not let them go into these lands. Israel went around them and did not wipe them out.
20:11 Look at the way they pay us back! They are coming to drive us out of your property, the land you gave us to take possession of.
20:12 O our God, won't you bring them to judgment? There is no strength in us to stand up to this huge crowd coming against us. What we should do, we do not know. Our eyes are on you."
20:13 All Judah was standing in the LORD's presence, with their small children, their wives and their sons too.
## "The battle belongs to God"
20:14 There in the middle of the assembly, Jahaziel had the Spirit of the LORD come on him. He was son of Zechariah son of Benaiah son of Jeiel son of Mattaniah, a Levite, one of Asaph's sons.
20:15 He said, "Listen, all Judah, you who live in Jerusalem, and King Jehoshaphat! The LORD says this to you: Don't be afraid, and don't panic at this great crowd. The battle does not belong to you. It belongs to God.
20:16 Go down at them tomorrow. Look, they are coming up the Ascent of Ziz. You will find them at the end of the wadi, on the edge of the wilderness of Jeruel.
20:17 It is not for you to fight this time. Take your stand, stand still, and watch the LORD save you, Judah and Jerusalem. Don't be afraid, and don't panic. Tomorrow go out in front of them, and the LORD will be with you."
20:18 Jehoshaphat bent low, face to the ground. All Judah and those living in Jerusalem fell down before the LORD to bow down to the LORD.
20:19 Levites from the sons of the Kohathites and the sons of the Korahites rose to praise the LORD, the God of Israel, at the top of their voices.
## The battle won by singing
20:20 They got up early in the morning and headed out into the Tekoa wilderness. On their way out, Jehoshaphat stood up and said, "Listen to me, Judah, and you who live in Jerusalem! Hold firm to the LORD your God, and you will be held firm. Hold firm to his prophets, and you will succeed."
20:21 After he had consulted with the people, he appointed singers for the LORD, to praise in holy splendor as they went out ahead of the armed men. They were saying, "Give thanks to the LORD, for his loyal love is forever."
20:22 As soon as they started the glad shout and the praise, the LORD put men in ambush against the Ammonites, Moab and Mount Seir, who had come to Judah. And they were beaten.
20:23 The Ammonites and Moab rose up against those who lived on Mount Seir, to put them under the ban and wipe them out. When they were done with the people of Seir, each one helped to destroy his neighbor.
20:24 Judah reached the watchtower of the wilderness. They turned to look at the crowd, and there they were, corpses fallen to the ground. Not one had escaped.
20:25 Jehoshaphat and his people came to take their plunder. They found plenty among them: goods, dead bodies, and costly things. They stripped off more than they could carry. For three days they were taking the plunder, there was so much of it.
20:26 They came together on the fourth day in the Valley of Beracah, since that is where they blessed the LORD. That is why the place is still named the Valley of Beracah to this day.
20:27 Every man of Judah and Jerusalem turned back, with Jehoshaphat leading them, to go home to Jerusalem in gladness. The LORD had made them glad over their enemies.
20:28 They came to Jerusalem with harps, lyres and trumpets, to the LORD's house.
20:29 The dread of God was on all the kingdoms of the lands when they heard that the LORD had fought against Israel's enemies.
20:30 Jehoshaphat's kingdom had quiet, and his God gave him rest on all sides.
## The end of Jehoshaphat's reign
20:31 Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah. He was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he ruled for twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother was named Azubah daughter of Shilhi.
20:32 He walked in the road of his father Asa and did not turn aside from it. He did what was right in the LORD's eyes.
20:33 Only, the high places were not removed. The people still had not made their hearts ready for the God of their ancestors.
20:34 The rest of Jehoshaphat's story, early and late, is there, written in the words of Jehu son of Hanani, which were taken up into the scroll of the kings of Israel.
20:35 Later on, Jehoshaphat king of Judah made a partnership with Ahaziah king of Israel. He did wickedly in doing so.
20:36 The two were partners in making ships to sail to Tarshish. They made the ships at Ezion-geber.
20:37 Then Eliezer son of Dodavahu, from Mareshah, prophesied against Jehoshaphat: "Since you have partnered with Ahaziah, the LORD has broken apart your work." The ships were wrecked, and they never managed to sail for Tarshish.
## Jehoram kills his brothers
21:1 Jehoshaphat lay down with his ancestors and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. His son Jehoram became king in his place.
21:2 He had brothers, sons of Jehoshaphat: Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariahu, Michael and Shephatiah. All these were sons of Jehoshaphat king of Israel.
21:3 Their father had handed them large gifts, silver and gold and fine things, with fortified towns in Judah. The kingdom, though, he handed to Jehoram, since he was the firstborn.
21:4 Jehoram rose up over his father's kingdom and made himself strong. Then he killed all his brothers with the sword, and some of Israel's officials too.
21:5 Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he ruled for eight years in Jerusalem.
21:6 He walked in the road of Israel's kings, just as Ahab's house did, because his wife was a daughter of Ahab. He did what the LORD sees as evil.
21:7 But because of the covenant he had made with David, the LORD did not want to wipe out David's house. He had said he would give him a lamp, and to his sons, for all time.
## Edom and Libnah revolt
21:8 During his reign Edom revolted out from under Judah's hand and made itself a king.
21:9 Jehoram went across with his commanders, and all the chariots went with him. In the night he rose and struck Edom, which had surrounded him, and the chariot commanders.
21:10 Edom has stayed in revolt, out from under Judah's hand, down to this day. Then Libnah also revolted, in that same period, out from under his hand, because he had left the LORD, the God of his ancestors.
21:11 He even made high places on Judah's mountains. He led the people of Jerusalem into prostitution, and he drove Judah off the path.
## Elijah's letter
21:12 Then a writing came to him from Elijah the prophet. It said: "The LORD, the God of your forefather David, says this: You have not walked in the ways of your father Jehoshaphat, or in the ways of Asa king of Judah.
21:13 You have walked in the road of Israel's kings. You have led Judah and the people of Jerusalem into prostitution, the way Ahab's house led Israel into it. You have even killed your own brothers, your father's family, who were better than you.
21:14 Look, the LORD is going to strike a great blow on your people, on your sons and wives, and on all your goods.
21:15 And you will have great sickness, sickness in your bowels, until day after day your bowels come out from the disease."
## Jehoram's end
21:16 The LORD stirred up against Jehoram the spirit of the Philistines and of the Arabs who were next to the Cushites.
21:17 They marched up into Judah and split it open. They took away everything of value they found belonging to the king's house, and his sons and wives as well. He was left with no son except Jehoahaz, the youngest of his sons.
21:18 And after all that, the LORD struck his bowels with sickness, for which there was no cure.
21:19 Day followed day, and about the time two years ran out, his bowels came out with his sickness. He died of cruel diseases. His people lit no fire for him like the fire for his ancestors.
21:20 He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he ruled for eight years in Jerusalem. He passed on with no one missing him. They buried him in the City of David, but not in the kings' tombs.
## Ahaziah king of Judah
22:1 Those who lived in Jerusalem crowned his youngest son, Ahaziah, in his place. All the older ones had been killed by the war band that had come into the camp with the Arabs. So Ahaziah son of Jehoram became king of Judah.
22:2 Ahaziah was forty-two years old when he became king, and he ruled for one year in Jerusalem. His mother was named Athaliah daughter of Omri.
22:3 He too walked in the roads of Ahab's house, for his mother advised him to act wickedly.
22:4 He did what the LORD sees as evil, as Ahab's house did, because after his father's death they were his advisers, to his ruin.
22:5 He also walked in their advice. Together with Jehoram son of Ahab king of Israel, he went to fight Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead, and the Arameans wounded Joram.
22:6 He returned to Jezreel to get well from the wounds they had dealt him at Ramah in his fight with Hazael king of Aram. Azariah son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to Jezreel to visit Jehoram son of Ahab, since he was laid up.
## Jehu kills Ahaziah
22:7 It was God's doing that Ahaziah was brought down by coming to Joram. When he came, he went out with Jehoram against Jehu son of Nimshi, whom the LORD had anointed to cut off Ahab's house.
22:8 When Jehu was carrying out judgment on Ahab's house, he found Judah's officials, and the sons of Ahaziah's brothers who waited on Ahaziah, and he killed them.
22:9 Jehu went looking for Ahaziah. He was caught hiding in Samaria, and they brought him to Jehu and put him to death. They buried him, because they said, "He is Jehoshaphat's son, and Jehoshaphat sought the LORD with all his heart." And Ahaziah's house had no one left with the strength to keep the kingdom.
## Athaliah seizes the throne
22:10 Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son had died. She rose up and destroyed all the royal offspring of the house of Judah.
22:11 Jehoshabeath, though, the king's daughter, took Ahaziah's son Joash. She stole him out from among the king's sons who were being put to death, and she put him and his nurse in the room where the beds were kept. So Jehoshabeath, daughter of King Jehoram and wife of Jehoiada the priest, hid him from Athaliah, since she was Ahaziah's sister, and Athaliah did not put him to death.
22:12 For six years he was with them in God's house, kept in hiding, while Athaliah reigned over the land.
## Jehoiada makes Joash king
23:1 In the seventh year Jehoiada took heart. He brought the officers over the hundreds into a covenant with him: Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri.
23:2 They traveled all over Judah and gathered the Levites from all Judah's towns and the heads of Israel's families, and they came to Jerusalem.
23:3 There in God's house the whole assembly cut a covenant with the king. Jehoiada said to them, "Here is the king's son! He will reign, just as the LORD said of David's sons.
23:4 Here is what you are to do. A third of you, the ones who come in on the Sabbath, the priests and the Levites, will keep the thresholds.
23:5 A third will be at the king's house, and a third at the Foundation Gate. All the people will be in the courtyards of the LORD's house.
23:6 Let no one come into the LORD's house except the priests and the Levites who serve. They may come in, because they are holy. All the people must keep what the LORD has charged them to keep.
23:7 The Levites are to form a ring around the king, every man with his weapons in hand. Whoever comes into the house is to be put to death. Be with the king when he comes in and when he goes out."
23:8 The Levites and all Judah did just what Jehoiada the priest ordered. Each took his men, those coming in on the Sabbath along with those going out on the Sabbath, for Jehoiada the priest had not let the divisions go.
23:9 Jehoiada the priest handed the officers over the hundreds the spears, the shields and the bucklers that had been King David's, which were kept in God's house.
23:10 He stationed all the people, every man with his weapon in hand, from the south side of the house to the north side, near the altar and the house, around the king.
23:11 Then they brought out the king's son and set the crown on him, and the testimony. They made him king, and Jehoiada and his sons anointed him. And they cried, "Long live the king!"
## Athaliah is killed
23:12 Athaliah heard the sound of the people running and praising the king, and she came to the people in the LORD's house.
23:13 She looked, and there stood the king by his pillar at the entrance. With the king were the officers and the trumpets, and all the people of the land were glad and sounding the trumpets, and the singers with their instruments of song were leading the praise. Athaliah ripped her clothes. "Conspiracy!" she said. "Conspiracy!"
23:14 Jehoiada the priest brought out the officers over the hundreds, the men set over the army. "Take her outside, between the ranks," he said to them, "and whoever comes after her is to be put to death with the sword." For the priest had said, "Do not kill her in the LORD's house."
23:15 They laid hands on her. She went to the entrance of the Horse Gate of the king's house, and there they put her to death.
## Jehoiada's covenant and reforms
23:16 Jehoiada made a covenant between himself and all the people and the king, that they would be a people belonging to the LORD.
23:17 Then all the people went into the house of Baal and pulled it down. Its altars and its images they broke to pieces, and Mattan, Baal's priest, they killed in front of the altars.
23:18 Jehoiada placed the posts of oversight for the LORD's house in the hands of the Levite priests. David had assigned them their divisions over the LORD's house, to offer up the LORD's burnt offerings as the law of Moses has it written, with joy and with song, by David's direction.
23:19 He set gatekeepers at the gates of the LORD's house. No one who was unclean for any reason was to come in.
23:20 He took the officers over the hundreds, the nobles, the rulers of the people and all the people of the land, and he brought the king down from the LORD's house. They came through the Upper Gate to the king's house, and they seated the king on the throne of the kingdom.
23:21 The people of the land, all of them, were glad, and the city had rest. Athaliah they had killed with the sword.
## Joash repairs the Temple
24:1 Joash was seven years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem forty years. His mother was named Zibiah. She came from Beersheba.
24:2 Joash did what was right in the LORD's eyes all the days of Jehoiada the priest.
24:3 Jehoiada took two wives for him, and he fathered sons and daughters.
24:4 After this it came into Joash's heart to renew the LORD's house.
24:5 He called the priests and Levites together. "Go out into the towns of Judah," he told them, "and collect money from all Israel, yearly, to make the house of your God strong. Hurry with this matter." The Levites did not hurry.
24:6 So the king called Jehoiada, the head. "Why haven't you required the Levites to bring in Moses' tax from Judah and Jerusalem?" he said. "Moses, the LORD's servant, and the assembly of Israel set it for the tent of the testimony."
24:7 For Athaliah's sons, sons of that wicked woman, had broken into God's house, and they had even made over all the holy things of the LORD's house to the Baals.
24:8 The king gave the word. They made a chest and put it at the gate of the LORD's house, on the outside.
24:9 They sent out word in Judah and in Jerusalem: people were to bring the LORD the tax that Moses, God's servant, put on Israel in the wilderness.
24:10 All the officials and all the people rejoiced, and they came and threw money into the chest until they were done.
24:11 From time to time the Levites would bring the chest to the king's overseers. When they saw the money was plenty, the king's scribe came with the head priest's officer. They emptied the chest, then picked it up and returned it to its place. They did so day after day and collected money in abundance.
24:12 The king and Jehoiada handed it over to the men doing the work for the service of the LORD's house. These hired stonecutters and craftsmen to make the LORD's house new again, and also smiths in iron and bronze to make the LORD's house strong.
24:13 So the workmen worked, and under their hands the mending went forward. They stood God's house up again to its right measure and made it firm.
24:14 When they had finished, they brought the rest of the money to the king and Jehoiada. It was made into utensils for the LORD's house: things for the service and the offerings, dishes, and other gold and silver utensils. All Jehoiada's days, burnt offerings went up in the LORD's house without a break.
## Jehoiada's death and Judah's turn
24:15 Jehoiada aged and had his fill of days, and he died. He was a hundred thirty years old at his death.
24:16 They laid him in a grave among the kings in the City of David. He had done good in Israel, for God and for God's house.
24:17 After Jehoiada died, Judah's officials came and bowed down to the king. Then the king listened to them.
24:18 They walked away from the house of the LORD, their ancestors' God, and became servants of the Asherah poles and the idols. For this guilt, wrath fell on Judah and Jerusalem.
24:19 He sent prophets among them to turn them back to the LORD, and the prophets bore witness against them. Still the people would not listen.
## The stoning of Zechariah
24:20 God's Spirit put on Zechariah son of Jehoiada the priest like clothing. He stood up higher than the people and told them, "God says this: Why are you going against the LORD's commands? You will not succeed. You have left the LORD, so he has left you."
24:21 They formed a plot against him. On the king's order they pelted him with stones in the courtyard of the LORD's house.
24:22 So King Joash did not remember the loyal love Jehoiada, Zechariah's father, had shown him, and he killed his son. As Zechariah was dying he said, "May the LORD see it, and may he seek it out."
## Aram's army and Joash's murder
24:23 When the year came around, Aram's army marched up against him. They entered Judah and Jerusalem and wiped out all the people's officials from among the people. All the loot they took they sent to the king of Damascus.
24:24 Aram's army had come with a handful of men, but the LORD put a very large army into their hands, because the people had left the LORD, the God of their ancestors. So they carried out judgments on Joash.
24:25 As they went away from him, leaving him with many wounds, his own servants plotted against him over the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest. They killed him on his bed, and he died. They buried him in the City of David, but they did not bury him in the kings' tombs.
24:26 These are the men who plotted against him: Zabad son of Shimeath the Ammonite woman, and Jehozabad son of Shimrith the Moabite woman.
24:27 His sons, the great oracle against him, and the rebuilding of God's house are there, written in the commentary on the scroll of the kings. Amaziah his son became king in his place.
