## Solomon asks for wisdom
1:1 Solomon son of David took firm hold of his kingdom. The LORD his God was with him and made him greater and greater.
1:2 Solomon called on all Israel: the commanders over thousands and over hundreds, the judges, and every leader in all Israel, the family heads.
1:3 Solomon went with the whole assembly to the high place at Gibeon, because God's tent of meeting was there, the one Moses the servant of the LORD had made in the wilderness.
1:4 But David had already brought the ark of God up from Kiriath-jearim. He brought it to the place he had made ready for it, for he had set up a tent for it in Jerusalem.
1:5 The bronze altar that Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, had made, he had set in front of the LORD's tabernacle. Solomon and the assembly sought it.
1:6 There, before the LORD, Solomon went up to the bronze altar that belonged to the tent of meeting. He offered up a thousand burnt offerings on it.
1:7 That night God showed himself to Solomon and said to him, "Ask. What should I give you?"
1:8 Solomon said to God, "You showed great loyal love to my father David, and you have made me king in his place.
1:9 Now, LORD God, may your word to David my father hold true, since you have made me king over a people that is like the dust of the earth in number.
1:10 So now give me wisdom and knowledge, so I can go out in front of this people and come back in. For who can judge this great people of yours?"
1:11 God said to Solomon, "This is what was in your heart. You did not ask for riches, treasure or glory, or for the lives of those who hate you. You did not ask to live many days either. What you asked for yourself was wisdom and knowledge, to judge my people, over whom I made you king.
1:12 The wisdom and the knowledge are given to you. And riches, treasure and glory I will give you too. No king before you had so much, and none after you will."
1:13 Then from the high place at Gibeon, from in front of the tent of meeting, Solomon came back to Jerusalem. And he reigned over Israel.
## Solomon's chariots and wealth
1:14 Solomon built up a force of chariots and horsemen, one thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen. He based them in the chariot towns and with the king in Jerusalem.
1:15 In Jerusalem the king made silver and gold like the stones, and he made cedars like the wild fig trees of the Shephelah, there were so many.
1:16 The horses Solomon had came out of Egypt and out of Que. The king's dealers would buy them from Que for a price.
1:17 They would bring up a chariot out of Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for one hundred and fifty. In the same way the dealers brought them out to all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.
## Solomon prepares to build
2:1 Solomon made up his mind to build a house for the name of the LORD, and a house for his own royal rule.
2:2 Solomon counted out seventy thousand men to carry loads, eighty thousand to cut stone in the hill country, and three thousand six hundred to oversee them.
2:3 Solomon sent word to Huram king of Tyre. He said, "Treat me as you treated David my father. You sent him cedars so he could build a house to live in.
2:4 I am about to build a house for the name of the LORD my God and set it apart for him. In it sweet spices will burn as incense in his presence, the rows of bread will always be laid out, and burnt offerings will be made morning and evening, on Sabbaths and new moons, and at the set feasts of the LORD our God. This is Israel's duty for all time.
2:5 The house I am about to build is a great one, for our God is great, above all the gods.
2:6 Yet who has the strength to build him a house? The heavens, the heavens above the heavens, are not big enough for him. Who am I, then, that I should build him a house? It can only be a place to burn offerings before him.
2:7 So now send me a skilled man who knows how to work in gold, silver, bronze and iron, and in purple, crimson and blue-violet cloth, and who knows how to engrave. He will work with the skilled men I have in Judah and Jerusalem, the ones David my father made ready.
2:8 Send me logs of cedar, cypress and algum from Lebanon too. I am aware that your servants are skilled at cutting the trees of Lebanon. My servants will work with your servants
2:9 to get a great deal of timber ready for me, for the house I am building is to be great and a wonder.
2:10 For your servants, the woodcutters who fell the trees, I will give twenty thousand cors of threshed wheat, twenty thousand cors of barley, twenty thousand baths of wine and twenty thousand baths of oil."
## Huram's answer
2:11 Huram king of Tyre answered in a letter that he sent to Solomon: "It is because of the LORD's love for his people that he has put you over them as king."
2:12 Huram went on, "Blessed is the LORD, the God of Israel, who made the heavens and the earth! He has given a wise son to King David, gifted with good sense and understanding, who will build a house for the LORD and a house for his own royal rule.
2:13 Now I am sending you a wise man who has understanding: Huram-abi.
2:14 His mother came from the daughters of Dan, and his father was a man from Tyre. He knows how to work in gold and silver, bronze and iron, stone and wood, in purple and blue-violet, in fine linen and crimson. He can engrave any engraving and carry out any design given to him. He will work with your skilled men and with the skilled men of my lord David your father.
2:15 Now let my lord send his servants the wheat and the barley, the oil and the wine, that he spoke of.
2:16 And we will cut timber from Lebanon, as much as you need. We will bring it to you on the sea, tied in rafts, to Joppa, and you can carry it up to Jerusalem."
2:17 Solomon counted all the foreigners living in the land of Israel, after the count his father David had made of them. They came to one hundred and fifty-three thousand six hundred.
2:18 He made seventy thousand of them carry loads and eighty thousand cut stone in the hill country, and three thousand six hundred were overseers to keep the people working.
## Building the Temple
3:1 Solomon began building the LORD's house in Jerusalem. It stood on Mount Moriah, where he had shown himself to David his father, at the place David had made ready: the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.
3:2 The building began in the second month, on its second day, in the fourth year of his reign.
3:3 These are the measurements Solomon laid down for building God's house. Its length was sixty cubits, by the old measure, and its width was twenty cubits.
3:4 The porch across the front was twenty cubits long, matching the width of the house, and it was a hundred and twenty cubits high. Inside, he covered it with pure gold.
3:5 The great room he lined with cypress wood, and over that he laid fine gold. On it he set palm trees and chains.
3:6 He covered the house with precious stones to make it beautiful, and the gold was Parvaim gold.
3:7 He covered the house in gold, the beams and the thresholds, the walls and the doors, and on the walls he engraved cherubim.
3:8 He made the room for the most holy place. It was twenty cubits long, matching the width of the house, and twenty cubits wide. He lined it with fine gold, six hundred talents of it.
3:9 The gold for the nails weighed fifty shekels. And the upper rooms he lined with gold.
3:10 Inside the most holy place he made two cherubim, a sculpted work, and they covered them with gold.
3:11 The cherubim's wings measured twenty cubits from end to end. A wing of the one, five cubits long, reached to the wall of the house. Its second wing, five cubits, reached to the wing of the other cherub.
3:12 A wing of the other cherub, five cubits, reached to the wall of the house on the other side, and its second wing, five cubits, held fast to the wing of the first cherub.
3:13 These cherubim spread their wings twenty cubits wide. They stood on their feet, with their faces turned toward the house.
3:14 The curtain he made of blue-violet, purple and crimson cloth and of fine linen, and he set cherubim on it.
## The two pillars
3:15 In front of the house he made two pillars, thirty-five cubits in length. The capital on top of each was five cubits.
3:16 He made chains in the inner room and put them on the tops of the pillars. He made a hundred pomegranates and put them on the chains.
3:17 He stood the pillars up in front of the great hall, one on the south side and the other on the north. He called the name of the south one Jakin, and the name of the north one Boaz.
## The bronze furnishings
4:1 He made a bronze altar. It was twenty cubits in length, twenty in width and ten cubits in height.
4:2 He made the Sea out of cast metal. From one lip to the other it was ten cubits, and it was round all the way around. It stood five cubits high, and a cord thirty cubits long would go around it.
4:3 Figures of oxen were under it, all around, ringing it, ten to every cubit, going right around the Sea. There were two rows of oxen, cast as part of it when it was cast.
4:4 It stood on twelve oxen. Three looked toward the north, three toward the west, three toward the south and three toward the east. The Sea sat up on top of them, and their back parts all pointed inward.
4:5 Its metal was as thick as a hand is wide. Its lip was shaped like the lip of a cup, like a lily flower opening. It could take in three thousand baths; that much it would hold.
4:6 He made ten basins and put five on the south and five on the north, for washing. In them they would rinse the things that went into the burnt offering. The Sea, though, was where the priests washed.
4:7 He made the gold lampstands, ten of them, in the way they were meant to be made. He placed them in the great hall, five at the south and five at the north.
4:8 He also made ten tables and set them down in the great hall, five at the south and five at the north. He made a hundred bowls of gold as well.
4:9 He made the courtyard of the priests and the great enclosure, and doors for the enclosure. Their doors he covered with bronze.
4:10 The Sea he placed at the south side, toward the southeast.
4:11 Huram made the pots too, and the shovels and the bowls. So Huram completed the work that he did for King Solomon in God's house:
4:12 the two pillars; the bowls and the capitals on top of the two pillars; the two networks to cover the two bowls of the capitals on top of the pillars;
4:13 the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, two rows of pomegranates for each network, to cover the two bowls of the capitals on the pillars;
4:14 the stands he made, and the basins he made on the stands;
4:15 the one Sea, with the twelve oxen under it;
4:16 and the pots, the shovels and the forks. All their objects Huram his father made for King Solomon, for the LORD's house, of polished bronze.
4:17 In the Jordan valley the king cast them, in molds of clay earth, between Succoth and Zeredah.
4:18 Solomon made all these objects, a very great number of them. No one ever knew how much the bronze weighed.
## The gold furnishings
4:19 Solomon made all the objects for God's house as well: the altar of gold; the tables with the Presence Bread on them;
4:20 the lampstands and their lamps, of refined gold, to burn as prescribed before the inner room;
4:21 and the flower work, the lamps and the tongs, of gold, the purest gold;
4:22 the wick trimmers, bowls, ladles and fire pans, of refined gold; and the entrance of the house, its inner doors to the most holy place and the doors of the house to the great hall, of gold.
5:1 Now all the work Solomon did for the LORD's house was complete. Solomon brought in the holy gifts of David his father, the silver and the gold and all the objects, and stored them in the treasure rooms of God's house.
## The ark comes to the Temple
5:2 Then Solomon called together to Jerusalem the elders of Israel and all the tribal heads, the leaders of the Israelite families, to bring the ark of the LORD's covenant up out of the City of David, that is, Zion.
5:3 Every man in Israel assembled before the king at the feast. That is the seventh month.
5:4 All Israel's elders came, and the Levites picked up the ark.
5:5 They carried up the ark, with the tent of meeting and all the holy objects that were in the tent. It was the Levite priests who carried them up.
5:6 In front of the ark stood King Solomon and all the community of Israel who had gathered to him. They were sacrificing sheep and cattle, so many that no one could count them or number them.
5:7 The priests carried the ark of the LORD's covenant in to where it belonged: the inner room of the house, the most holy place, beneath the cherubim's wings.
5:8 The cherubim's wings were spread out over the spot where the ark was, and from above, the cherubim covered the ark and its poles.
5:9 The poles were long, and the tips of the poles showed from the ark in front of the inner room, but they did not show outside. It has stayed there until today.
5:10 Nothing was inside the ark but the two tablets. Moses had placed them in it at Horeb, when the LORD made his covenant with the Israelites as they came out of Egypt.
## The cloud fills the house
5:11 As the priests came out from the holy place, every priest who was there had made himself holy, whatever his division.
5:12 All the Levites who were singers stood east of the altar: Asaph, Heman and Jeduthun, with their sons and their brothers. They were dressed in fine linen and held cymbals, harps and lyres. With them were a hundred and twenty priests blowing trumpets.
5:13 The trumpeters and the singers were as one, making one sound heard as they praised and thanked the LORD. They raised the sound with trumpets, cymbals and instruments of music, praising the LORD: "For he is good, for his loyal love is forever." Then the house was filled with a cloud, the LORD's house.
5:14 The priests were not able to stand and serve, because of the cloud. The LORD's glory filled God's house.
## Solomon blesses the people
6:1 Then Solomon said, "The LORD has said / he will live in the thick dark cloud.
6:2 As for me, I have built you a princely house, / a settled place for you to live forever."
6:3 The king turned to face them and blessed all the assembly of Israel. All Israel's assembly stood there.
6:4 "Blessed is the LORD, the God of Israel," he said. "His own mouth spoke to David my father, and his own hands have made it happen. He said,
6:5 'From the day I brought my people out of the land of Egypt, I have chosen no city out of all Israel's tribes as the place to build a house where my name would be. Nor did I choose any man to be ruler over my people Israel.
6:6 But I chose Jerusalem for my name to be there, and I chose David to be over my people Israel.'
6:7 Building a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, was something David my father had set his heart on.
6:8 But the LORD told David my father, 'You set your heart on building a house for my name. It was good that you set your heart on it.
6:9 But you are not going to build the house. Instead your son, who will come out of your own body, will build the house for my name.'
6:10 Now the LORD has made his word come true, the word he spoke. I have stood up in place of David my father. I sit on Israel's throne, just as the LORD said, and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.
6:11 And I have put the ark there. In it lies the LORD's covenant, the one he made with the Israelites."
## Solomon's prayer
6:12 He took his place before the LORD's altar, facing all the assembly of Israel, and lifted his open palms.
6:13 For Solomon had made a bronze platform and put it in the middle of the courtyard. It was five cubits long, five cubits wide and three cubits high. He stood on it. Then he went down on his knees in front of all the assembly of Israel and lifted his open palms to the heavens.
6:14 He said, "LORD, God of Israel, not in the heavens and not on the earth is there any God like you. You keep covenant and loyal love with your servants who walk in your presence with their whole heart.
6:15 For your servant David my father you have kept what you told him. Your mouth said it, and your hand has made it happen, as it stands this day.
6:16 So now, LORD, God of Israel, keep for your servant David my father what you told him: 'A man of yours will never be cut off from before me, from sitting on Israel's throne, if only your sons guard their way and walk in my law as you walked before me.'
6:17 And now, LORD, God of Israel, make your word stand firm, the word you spoke to your servant David.
6:18 But can God truly make his home with human beings on earth? Look: the heavens, the heavens above the heavens, are not big enough for you. Then how could this house I built be enough!
6:19 All the same, LORD my God, turn toward your servant's prayer and his plea for grace. Hear the cry, the prayer your servant prays in front of you.
6:20 Day and night keep your eyes open toward this house, the place where you said you would put your name. Listen to the prayer your servant prays toward this place.
6:21 Listen to the pleas of your servant and of Israel your people when they pray toward this place. Yes, listen from the place where you live, from the heavens. Hear, and forgive.
6:22 "If a man sins against his neighbor, and a curse oath is laid on him to make him swear, and he comes and swears before your altar in this house,
6:23 then you, from the heavens, hear and act. Be judge for your servants. Pay back the guilty man and bring his own ways down on his head. Declare the one in the right to be in the right, and give him what his rightness deserves.
6:24 "If your people Israel go down in defeat before an enemy because they sin against you, and they turn back and acknowledge your name, and pray and plead for grace before you in this house,
6:25 then you, from the heavens, hear. Forgive your people Israel their sin, and bring them home to the land you gave to them and to their ancestors.
6:26 "Say the sky is locked shut and no rain falls, because they sinned against you. If they pray toward this place, and acknowledge your name, and turn back from their sin because you are pressing them down,
6:27 then you, in the heavens, hear. Forgive the sin of your servants, your people Israel. Yes, teach them the good road to walk on, and give rain on your land, the land you gave your people as their inheritance.
6:28 "Say there is hunger in the land, or deadly disease, or crops scorched or moldy, or swarms of locusts of every kind. Say their enemies lay siege to them in the land where their towns are. Whatever the blow, whatever the sickness,
6:29 every prayer, every plea for grace that comes from any person, from any of your people Israel, when each one knows his own wound and his own pain and spreads his palms toward this house,
6:30 then you, from the heavens, the place where you live, hear. Forgive. Give each person what fits all his ways, for you know his heart. You and no one else know the hearts of human beings.
6:31 So they will fear you and walk in your ways every day they are alive on the face of the ground you gave our ancestors.
6:32 "And the foreigner too, the one who is not from your people Israel: say he comes from a land far away for the sake of your great name and your strong hand and your outstretched arm. If they come and pray toward this house,
6:33 then you, from the heavens, from the place where you live, hear. Do all that the foreigner calls to you for. So all the earth's peoples will know your name and fear you, just like your people Israel, and they will know that your name has been called over this house I built.
6:34 "Say your people march out to fight their enemies, on whatever road you send them. If they pray to you facing this city you chose and the house I built for your name,
6:35 then from the heavens hear their prayer and their plea for grace, and do justice for them.
6:36 "Say they sin against you, since there is no human being who never sins, and you grow angry with them and hand them over to an enemy. Say their captors carry them off as prisoners to a land far away or near.
6:37 If, in the land where they were taken as prisoners, they take it to heart and turn back and plead for grace with you there in the land of their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned. We have gone wrong. We have done evil,'
6:38 and they come back to you with all their heart and all their being in the land of their captivity, where they were carried away, and they pray facing their land, the land you gave their ancestors, and the city you chose, and the house I built for your name,
6:39 then from the heavens, the place where you live, hear their prayer and their pleas for grace, and do justice for them. Forgive your people who sinned against you.
## Rise up, LORD
6:40 "Now, my God, please keep your eyes open, and let your ears pay attention to prayer from this place.
6:41 And now, up, LORD God, to your place of rest, / you and the ark of your strength! / May your priests, LORD God, wear salvation like clothes, / and your faithful ones be glad in good things.
6:42 LORD God, do not turn your anointed one away. / Remember the loyal love shown to David your servant."
## Fire from heaven
7:1 Solomon had just finished praying when fire came down out of the heavens. It devoured the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the LORD's glory filled the house.
7:2 The priests were not able to go into the LORD's house, because the LORD's glory filled the LORD's house.
7:3 All the Israelites were watching as the fire came down and the LORD's glory rested on the house. They knelt down on the stone pavement, faces to the ground. They bowed down and gave thanks to the LORD: "For he is good, for his loyal love is forever."
7:4 The king and all the people were offering sacrifices in the LORD's presence.
7:5 King Solomon sacrificed twenty-two thousand cattle and a hundred and twenty thousand sheep. This is how the king and all the people dedicated God's house.
7:6 The priests were standing at their stations, and so were the Levites with the LORD's instruments of music. King David had made these for thanking the LORD, "for his loyal love is forever," when David gave praise through them. Across from them the priests blew trumpets, and all Israel was standing.
7:7 Solomon set apart as holy the center of the courtyard in front of the LORD's house. He offered up the burnt offerings and the fat of the peace offerings there, because the bronze altar Solomon had made could not take them all: the burnt offering, the grain offering and the fat.
7:8 At that time Solomon kept the feast, and all Israel with him, for seven days. It was a truly huge gathering, from Lebo-hamath down to the Brook of Egypt.
7:9 On the eighth day they held a closing gathering. They had kept the altar's dedication for seven days and the feast for seven days.
7:10 On the twenty-third day of the seventh month he let the people go off to their tents. They were happy, their hearts were full, because of the good the LORD had done for David and for Solomon and for Israel his people.
## God answers Solomon
7:11 Solomon finished the LORD's house and the king's house. Everything that came into Solomon's heart to do in the LORD's house and in his own house, he carried out with success.
7:12 The LORD appeared to Solomon in the night. "I have heard your prayer," he told him. "This place I have chosen for myself, to be a house of sacrifice.
7:13 If I lock the sky shut and no rain falls, or if I command the locusts to eat up the land, or if I send deadly disease among my people,
7:14 and my people, over whom my name has been called, humble themselves and pray, seeking my face and turning back from their evil ways, then I myself will hear from the heavens. I will forgive their sin and heal their land.
7:15 Now my eyes will be open, and my ears will pay attention to prayer from this place.
7:16 Now I have chosen and made this house holy, so that my name will be there forever. My eyes and my heart will be there all the days to come.
7:17 As for you, if you walk before me as your father David walked, doing all I have commanded you and keeping my rules and my rulings,
7:18 then I will make the throne of your kingship stand. That is the covenant I made with your father David when I said, 'A man of yours will never be cut off from ruling over Israel.'
7:19 But if you all turn away and leave my rules and my commands that I set before you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them,
7:20 then I will pull them up by the roots from my land that I gave them. This house that I made holy for my name I will throw away out of my sight. I will make it an example in people's sayings and a target of their jokes among all the peoples.
7:21 As for this house, which was so high, everyone who passes by it will be stunned. He will say, 'Why has the LORD done this to this land and to this house?'
7:22 And the answer will be, 'Because they left the LORD, the God of their ancestors, who brought them out of the land of Egypt. They took hold of other gods, bowed down to them and served them. That is why he brought this whole disaster upon them.'"
## Solomon's other works
8:1 Twenty years went by while Solomon built the LORD's house and his own house.
8:2 After that, the towns Huram had given to Solomon, Solomon rebuilt, and he settled Israelites in them.
8:3 Solomon went to Hamath-zobah and overpowered it.
8:4 He built Tadmor in the wilderness, and all the storage towns he built in Hamath.
8:5 He built Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon as towns of defense, with walls, gates and bars,
8:6 and Baalath, and all of Solomon's storage towns, all the towns for his chariots and the towns for his horsemen, and whatever Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon and in all the land he ruled.
8:7 There were still people left from the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, who were not from Israel.
8:8 Their descendants were left after them in the land, because the Israelites had not finished them off. Solomon drafted them as forced labor, and so it is to this day.
8:9 But Solomon made no one from the Israelites a slave for his work. They were fighting men, chiefs of his officers, and commanders of his chariots and his horsemen.
8:10 These were King Solomon's chief overseers: two hundred and fifty of them, giving orders to the people.
8:11 As for Pharaoh's daughter, Solomon moved her up from the City of David to the house he had built for her. For he said, "No wife of mine will live in the house of David king of Israel, because the places the LORD's ark has come into are holy."
## Worship in the Temple
8:12 Solomon then offered burnt offerings up to the LORD on the LORD's altar, the one he had built in front of the porch.
8:13 He offered them up as each day required, as Moses commanded, on the Sabbaths, at the new moons, and at the set feasts three times a year: the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks and the Feast of Booths.
8:14 He set the priests in their divisions for their service, following the rule of David his father, and the Levites at their posts, to praise and to serve beside the priests, as each day required. The gatekeepers too he set by their divisions at each gate, for that was the command of David the man of God.
8:15 They did not turn aside from the king's command concerning the priests and the Levites in any matter, or concerning the treasuries.
8:16 Everything Solomon did was set in order, up to the day the LORD's house was founded and until it was finished. The LORD's house was complete.
## Ships to Ophir
8:17 Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Eloth, on the shore of the sea in the land of Edom.
8:18 Huram sent him ships by his servants, and servants who knew the sea. They reached Ophir with Solomon's servants, took from there four hundred and fifty talents of gold, and carried it to King Solomon.
## The queen of Sheba
9:1 The queen of Sheba heard the news about Solomon. So she came to Jerusalem to put Solomon to the test with riddles. She arrived with a very large company, and camels loaded with spices, a great deal of gold and precious stones. She came to Solomon and put to him everything she had in her heart.
9:2 Solomon answered every question she raised. Nothing she brought up was a secret to Solomon, and he left nothing unexplained.
9:3 The queen of Sheba saw Solomon's wisdom and the house he had built.
9:4 She saw the food on his table, the seating of his officials, the way his attendants stood and how they were dressed, his cupbearers and how they were dressed, and the stairway by which he went up to the LORD's house. It left her breathless.
9:5 She said to the king, "What I heard back home about your sayings and your wisdom was the truth.
9:6 But I did not trust their words until I came here and my eyes saw for themselves. Why, they did not tell me the half of your great wisdom! You have gone beyond the report I heard.
9:7 Your men are fortunate! Fortunate are these servants of yours, who stand in your presence day after day and listen to your wisdom!
9:8 May the LORD your God be blessed! He took delight in you and seated you on his own throne, as king for the LORD your God. Your God loves Israel and will make it stand forever. That is why he made you king over them, to do what is just and right."
9:9 She gave the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, a huge amount of spices, and precious stones. There were never spices like those the queen of Sheba gave King Solomon.
9:10 Besides this, Huram's servants and Solomon's servants, who brought gold from Ophir, brought algum wood and precious stones.
9:11 From the algum wood the king made steps for the LORD's house and for the king's house, and harps and lyres for the singers. Nothing like them had been seen before in the land of Judah.
9:12 Whatever the queen of Sheba wanted and asked for, King Solomon gave her, besides what she had brought to the king. Then she and her servants turned and went home to her own land.
## Solomon's gold
9:13 Each year Solomon took in gold weighing six hundred and sixty-six talents.
9:14 That was apart from what the traders and the dealers brought in. All the Arabian kings and the governors of the land also brought gold and silver to Solomon.
9:15 King Solomon had two hundred body-length shields made of hammered gold. He used six hundred shekels of hammered gold on each one.
9:16 He made three hundred round shields of hammered gold too, with three hundred shekels of gold to each shield. The king placed them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon.
9:17 The king made a great throne of ivory as well, and covered it with pure gold.
9:18 Six steps led up to the throne, and a footstool of gold was fastened to the throne. The seat had an arm on each side, and two lions stood beside the arms.
9:19 Twelve lions stood there on the six steps, on this side and that. No kingdom ever had one made like it.
9:20 Every cup King Solomon drank from was gold, and every utensil in the House of the Forest of Lebanon was fine gold. None was silver. In Solomon's time silver was not thought worth anything.
9:21 For the king had ships going to Tarshish with Huram's servants. One time in three years the Tarshish ships would arrive, bringing gold and silver, ivory, monkeys and apes.
9:22 So King Solomon became greater than any king on earth, in riches and in wisdom.
9:23 All the kings of the earth came looking to see Solomon's face and hear his wisdom, the wisdom God had placed in his heart.
9:24 Each one brought a gift: things of silver and things of gold, robes, weapons, spices, horses and mules, a set amount every year.
9:25 Solomon kept four thousand stalls for horses and chariots, and he had twelve thousand horsemen. He based them in the chariot towns and with the king in Jerusalem.
9:26 He was ruler over all the kings from the River as far as the Philistines' land and to Egypt's border.
9:27 In Jerusalem the king made silver like the stones, and he made cedars like the wild fig trees of the Shephelah, there were so many.
9:28 Horses were brought out of Egypt for Solomon, and out of all the lands.
## Solomon's death
9:29 The rest of Solomon's story, the early and the late, is written in the accounts of Nathan the prophet, in the prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Iddo the visionary about Jeroboam son of Nebat, is it not?
9:30 For forty years Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel.
9:31 Solomon lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him in the city of his father David. Rehoboam his son became king in his place.
## The kingdom splits
10:1 Rehoboam went to Shechem. All Israel had come to Shechem to make him king.
10:2 When Jeroboam son of Nebat heard of it, he was in Egypt. He had run away there from King Solomon, and Jeroboam came back from Egypt.
10:3 So they sent for him and called him, and Jeroboam came with all Israel. They said to Rehoboam,
10:4 "Your father made our yoke a hard one. So now, make lighter the hard service your father demanded and the heavy yoke he set on us, and we will serve you."
10:5 He told them, "Three days more. Then return to me." So the people went.
10:6 King Rehoboam talked it over with the elders who used to stand before his father Solomon in his lifetime. "What answer do you counsel me to give these people?" he said.
10:7 They told him, "Be kind to this people, show them favor, and speak good words to them, and they will be your servants for all time."
10:8 But he set aside the counsel the elders had given him. Instead he talked it over with the boys he had grown up with, who stood in attendance on him.
10:9 He asked them, "What is your counsel? What word should we send back to these people? They have told me, 'Make lighter the yoke your father set on us.'"
10:10 The boys he had grown up with told him, "The people said to you, 'Your father made our yoke heavy, but you lighten it for us.' Here is what you say back to them: 'My smallest part is thicker than my father's hips.
10:11 My father loaded you with a heavy yoke, and I will make your yoke heavier still. My father punished you with whips, but I with scorpions.'"
10:12 On the third day Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam, just as the king had told them: "Return to me on the third day."
10:13 The king gave them a hard answer. King Rehoboam set aside the counsel of the elders,
10:14 and spoke to them the way the boys had counseled: "My father made your yoke heavy, and I will add to it. My father punished you with whips, but I with scorpions."
10:15 The king would not listen to the people. God had brought about this turn, so that the LORD could make good the word he had spoken to Jeroboam son of Nebat by means of Ahijah the Shilonite.
10:16 All Israel found that the king would not listen to them. So the people sent the king this answer: "What is our share in David? / We have no inheritance in Jesse's son. / Every man to your tents, Israel! / Now see to your own house, David!" So all Israel went off to their tents.
10:17 The Israelites who lived in Judah's towns, though, had Rehoboam as their king.
10:18 Then King Rehoboam sent out Hadoram, the man over the forced labor, and the Israelites stoned him with stones until he died. King Rehoboam hurried to climb into his chariot and fled to Jerusalem.
10:19 Israel rose up in revolt against the house of David, and so it stands to this day.
## No war with Israel
11:1 Rehoboam came to Jerusalem and called up the house of Judah and Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand picked warriors. They were to fight Israel and bring the kingdom back to Rehoboam.
11:2 But the word of the LORD came to Shemaiah, the man of God:
11:3 "Speak to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin. Tell them,
11:4 'The LORD says this: You must not march up and fight with your kinsmen. Each of you, return to his own house, because this thing has come from me.'" So they listened to the LORD's words, and they turned back from going against Jeroboam.
## Rehoboam's fortress towns
11:5 Rehoboam stayed in Jerusalem. He built up towns in Judah to defend it.
11:6 He built up Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,
11:7 Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam,
11:8 Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,
11:9 Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,
11:10 Zorah, Aijalon and Hebron. These fortified towns were in Judah and in Benjamin.
11:11 He strengthened the strongholds, placed commanders in them, and stocked them with food, oil and wine.
11:12 In every town he put body-length shields and spears, and he made them very strong. So Judah and Benjamin were his.
## Priests and Levites come south
11:13 The priests and the Levites who were in all Israel came and stood with him, from all their territory.
11:14 The Levites had left behind their pasturelands and what they owned, and gone to Judah and Jerusalem. Jeroboam and his sons had thrown them out of their work as priests of the LORD.
11:15 Jeroboam set up priests of his own for the high places, for the goat idols and for the calves he had made.
11:16 Behind the Levites came people from all the tribes of Israel, those who had made up their minds to seek the LORD, the God of Israel. They came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to the LORD, the God of their ancestors.
11:17 They made the kingdom of Judah strong, and for three years they backed Rehoboam son of Solomon, because for three years they walked in the way of David and Solomon.
## Rehoboam's family
11:18 Rehoboam took as his wife Mahalath, daughter of Jerimoth son of David, and of Abihail daughter of Eliab son of Jesse.
11:19 She bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah and Zaham.
11:20 After her he took Maacah daughter of Absalom, and she bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza and Shelomith.
11:21 Rehoboam loved Maacah daughter of Absalom more than all his other wives and concubines. He had taken eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.
11:22 Rehoboam made Abijah son of Maacah the head, the leader among his brothers, for he meant to make him king.
11:23 He was shrewd. He scattered some of his sons across all the regions of Judah and Benjamin, in all the fortified towns. He gave them plenty of food, and he asked for many wives for them.
## Shishak invades
12:1 Once Rehoboam's kingdom was firmly set and he had become strong, he left the LORD's law, and all Israel did too.
12:2 In King Rehoboam's fifth year, Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem, because they had acted unfaithfully toward the LORD.
12:3 He came with one thousand two hundred chariots and sixty thousand horsemen, and the people who came with him from Egypt were past counting: Libyans, Sukkites and Cushites.
12:4 He captured the fortified towns that belonged to Judah and came as far as Jerusalem.
12:5 Then Shemaiah the prophet came to Rehoboam and to Judah's leaders. They had gathered at Jerusalem in fear of Shishak. He told them, "The LORD says this: 'You have left me, so I in turn have left you in Shishak's hand.'"
12:6 The leaders of Israel and the king humbled themselves. "The LORD is in the right," they said.
12:7 The LORD saw that they had humbled themselves, and his word came to Shemaiah: "They have humbled themselves, so I will not destroy them. I will give them a little escape, and my fury will not pour out on Jerusalem by Shishak's hand.
12:8 Still, they will be his servants. Then they will know what it is to serve me, and what it is to serve the kingdoms of the lands."
12:9 Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem. He took the treasures of the LORD's house and the treasures of the king's house. He took the lot. He even took the gold shields Solomon had made.
12:10 In their place King Rehoboam made shields of bronze, and he put them in the care of the officers of the runners, who guarded the doorway of the king's house.
12:11 Each time the king went to the LORD's house, the runners would come and carry them, and then bring them back to the runners' guardroom.
12:12 When he humbled himself, the LORD's anger turned back from him, and it did not destroy him to the end. Judah, too, still had good things in it.
## Rehoboam's reign summed up
12:13 King Rehoboam made himself strong in Jerusalem and went on reigning. Rehoboam was forty-one years old at the start of his reign, and for seventeen years he reigned in Jerusalem, the city the LORD chose from all the tribes of Israel as the place to set his name. His mother was Naamah the Ammonite.
12:14 He did evil. He had not made his heart ready to seek the LORD.
12:15 The things Rehoboam did, the early and the late, are written in the accounts of Shemaiah the prophet and of Iddo the visionary, in the family records, are they not? There were wars between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all their days.
12:16 Rehoboam lay down with his ancestors and was buried in the City of David. His son Abijah became king in his place.
