## Amaziah king of Judah
25:1 Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem twenty-nine years. His mother was named Jehoaddan. She came from Jerusalem.
25:2 He did what was right in the LORD's eyes, only not with an undivided heart.
25:3 Once the kingdom was firm under him, he killed those servants of his who had struck down the king, his father.
25:4 But their sons he did not put to death. He acted by what is written in the law, in the book of Moses, where the LORD gave this command: "Fathers must not die because of their children, and children must not die because of their fathers. Each one is to die for his own sin."
## Hired troops sent home
25:5 Amaziah gathered Judah and set them up by their ancestors' houses, under commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, for all Judah and Benjamin. He enrolled them from twenty years old and up, and found three hundred thousand picked men, ready to go out to war, who could wield spear and large shield.
25:6 From Israel he hired a hundred thousand strong fighting men, paying a hundred talents of silver.
25:7 A man of God came to him, though. "King," he said, "Israel's army must not go with you. The LORD is not with Israel, nor with any son of Ephraim.
25:8 If you go anyway, then act! Be strong for battle! God will make you stumble before the enemy. With God is the strength to help or to make stumble."
25:9 Amaziah said to the man of God, "And what is to be done about the hundred talents I gave to Israel's troop?" The man of God said, "It is in the LORD's power to give you far beyond this."
25:10 So Amaziah set apart the troops that had come to him from Ephraim and sent them home. Their anger burned hot against Judah, and they went home in a blazing rage.
## Victory over Edom, and Edom's gods
25:11 Amaziah grew bold. He led his people out and made for the Valley of Salt. There he struck down ten thousand of the men of Seir.
25:12 Ten thousand more the men of Judah took alive. They brought them to the top of the Rock and threw them off the top of the Rock, and every one of them burst open.
25:13 As for the men of the troop that Amaziah sent back from going with him to battle, they raided the towns of Judah from Samaria to Beth-horon. They struck down three thousand of them and took much plunder.
25:14 When Amaziah came home from striking down the Edomites, he brought the gods of the people of Seir with him and stood them up to be his gods. He bowed down before them and sent up smoke to them.
25:15 The LORD's anger burned against Amaziah, and he sent him a prophet, who said to him, "Why go seeking the gods of this people? They could not rescue their own people from your hand."
25:16 He was still speaking when the king cut in: "Is it the king's counselor we have made you? Leave off, for your own good. Why should anyone strike you down?" The prophet left off, but he said, "I know that God's counsel is to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my counsel."
## Joash of Israel defeats Amaziah
25:17 Amaziah king of Judah took counsel, then sent word to Joash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel: "Come on, let us look each other in the face."
25:18 Joash king of Israel sent back to Amaziah king of Judah: "The thornbush in Lebanon sent this to the cedar in Lebanon: 'Give your daughter to my son for a wife.' But a wild animal of Lebanon passed by and stamped the thornbush flat.
25:19 You say, 'Look, I have struck Edom,' and it has gone to your head, so that you want still more glory. Now sit at home. Why pick a fight with disaster, so that you fall, and Judah falls with you?"
25:20 Amaziah did not listen. It came from God, so that he could hand them over, because they had sought the gods of Edom.
25:21 So Joash king of Israel marched up, and they looked each other in the face, he and Amaziah king of Judah, at Beth-shemesh, which is in Judah.
25:22 Israel beat Judah, and they ran away, every man to his tent.
25:23 Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash son of Jehoahaz, was taken prisoner by Joash king of Israel at Beth-shemesh. He brought him to Jerusalem and tore a gap in Jerusalem's wall four hundred cubits wide, from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate.
25:24 He took all the gold and the silver, every article to be found in God's house in Obed-edom's care, the storerooms of the king's house, and people given as pledges, and went back to Samaria.
## Amaziah's end
25:25 Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.
25:26 The rest of Amaziah's story, from first to last, is written in the scroll of the kings of Judah and Israel, is it not?
25:27 From the time Amaziah turned away from following the LORD, in Jerusalem a plot was formed against him, and he ran to Lachish. They sent after him to Lachish and put him to death there.
25:28 They carried him on horses and buried him with his ancestors in the city of Judah.
## Uzziah king of Judah
26:1 The whole people of Judah took Uzziah, a boy of sixteen, and made him king to follow his father Amaziah.
26:2 It was he who built up Eloth and brought it back to Judah, after the king had lain down with his ancestors.
26:3 Uzziah was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem fifty-two years. His mother was named Jecoliah. She came from Jerusalem.
26:4 He did what was right in the LORD's eyes, all that his father Amaziah had done.
26:5 He kept seeking God in the days of Zechariah, a man who understood the visions of God. And in the days when he sought the LORD, God made things go well for him.
## Uzziah's strength
26:6 He went out and fought the Philistines. He broke through the walls of Gath, Jabneh and Ashdod, and he built towns in the region of Ashdod and among the Philistines.
26:7 God helped him against the Philistines, against the Arabs who lived in Gur-baal, and against the Meunites.
26:8 The Ammonites paid tribute to Uzziah. His name reached as far as the way into Egypt, because his strength rose higher and higher.
26:9 In Jerusalem Uzziah built towers by the Corner Gate, by the Valley Gate and by the bend in the wall, and he made them strong.
26:10 He built towers in the wilderness and dug many cisterns, because he had a great many herds, in the lowland and on the plain. He had farmers and vinedressers in the hills and in the garden lands, for he was a man who loved farmland.
26:11 Uzziah had an army ready for battle, going out to war in units by the count of their muster. Jeiel the secretary and Maaseiah the official made the count, under the hand of Hananiah, one of the king's commanders.
26:12 The full number of the heads of families over these strong fighting men was two thousand six hundred.
26:13 Under them was a fighting force of three hundred seven thousand five hundred, who made war with mighty strength, to help the king against the enemy.
26:14 For the whole army Uzziah supplied shields, spears, helmets, coats of armor, bows and sling stones.
26:15 In Jerusalem he made machines, inventions of an inventor, to stand on the towers and on the corners, to shoot arrows and large stones. His name reached distant places, for help came to him in wonderful ways, until he was strong.
## Uzziah's pride and skin disease
26:16 But once he was strong, his heart rose up high, and it brought him to ruin. He acted unfaithfully against the LORD his God. He went into the LORD's temple to burn incense on the altar of incense.
26:17 After him came Azariah the priest, and with him eighty priests of the LORD, strong, brave men.
26:18 They stood up against King Uzziah and said to him, "Uzziah, it is not your place to burn incense to the LORD. That belongs to the priests, Aaron's descendants, who are set apart to burn incense. Get out of the sanctuary, for you have acted unfaithfully. There is no honor in this for you from the LORD God."
26:19 Uzziah grew angry, with a censer in his hand to burn incense. In his anger at the priests, the skin disease shone out on his forehead, in front of the priests in the LORD's house, beside the altar of incense.
26:20 Azariah the chief priest and all the priests turned toward him, and there it was: he had skin disease on his forehead. They hurried him out from there. He too was in a rush to leave, for the LORD had struck him.
26:21 King Uzziah had a skin disease until his dying day. He lived in a house set apart, with his skin disease, for he was cut off from the LORD's house. Jotham his son was over the king's house and judged the people of the land.
26:22 The rest of Uzziah's story, from first to last, the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz wrote down.
26:23 Uzziah lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him with his ancestors in the kings' burial field, for they said, "He has a skin disease." His son Jotham became king in his place.
## Jotham king of Judah
27:1 Jotham was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years. His mother was named Jerushah daughter of Zadok.
27:2 He did what was right in the LORD's eyes, all that his father Uzziah had done, only he did not go into the LORD's temple. But the people still went on acting corruptly.
27:3 It was he who built the upper gate of the LORD's house, and he did a great deal of building on the wall of the Ophel.
27:4 Towns he built in Judah's hill country, and in the wooded hills he built forts and towers.
27:5 He went to war with the king of the Ammonites and overpowered them. That year the Ammonites gave him a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand cors of wheat and ten thousand of barley. They paid this again in the second year and the third.
27:6 Jotham grew strong, because he kept his ways steady before the LORD his God.
27:7 The rest of Jotham's story, all his wars and his ways, is written in the scroll of the kings of Israel and Judah.
27:8 He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years.
27:9 Jotham lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him in the City of David. His son Ahaz became king in his place.
## Ahaz king of Judah
28:1 Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD, the way David his forefather did.
28:2 He walked in the ways of the kings of Israel. He even made cast images for the Baals.
28:3 He burned offerings in the Valley of Ben-hinnom, and he burned his sons in the fire, like the abhorrent things done by the nations whom the LORD drove out from in front of the Israelites.
28:4 He sacrificed and burned offerings at the high places, on the hills and under every leafy tree.
## Defeat by Aram and Israel
28:5 So the LORD his God gave him into the hand of Aram's king. They struck him and carried off a great crowd of captives from him to Damascus. Into the hand of Israel's king, too, he was given, and Israel struck him a great blow.
28:6 Pekah son of Remaliah killed a hundred twenty thousand in Judah in one day, all of them strong fighting men, because they had left the LORD, the God of their ancestors.
28:7 Zichri, a warrior of Ephraim, killed Maaseiah the king's son, Azrikam the officer over the palace, and Elkanah, who was second to the king.
28:8 From their own relatives the Israelites carried off two hundred thousand captives, women, sons and daughters, and a great deal of plunder besides, which they took to Samaria.
## Oded and the captives sent home
28:9 There was a prophet of the LORD there, Oded by name. He went out in front of the army as it came into Samaria and told them, "Look! In the burning anger of the LORD, the God of your ancestors, against Judah, he gave them into your hand. And you have killed them in a fury, and it has reached the heavens.
28:10 And now you are talking of crushing the people of Judah and Jerusalem into slaves for yourselves, men and women. But what about you? Don't you carry guilt of your own before the LORD your God?
28:11 So listen to me. Return the captives you have taken from your own relatives, because the heat of the LORD's anger is on you."
28:12 Then some of the leaders of the Ephraimites stood up against those coming back from the war: Azariah son of Jehohanan, Berechiah son of Meshillemoth, Jehizkiah son of Shallum and Amasa son of Hadlai.
28:13 They said to them, "Don't bring the captives in here. What you are saying will bring the LORD's guilt on us, adding to our sins and our guilt. We already carry a great load of guilt, and burning anger hangs over Israel."
28:14 The soldiers then gave up the captives and the plunder, there before the commanders and all the assembly.
28:15 The men just named got up and took the captives in hand. Everyone among them who was naked, they dressed from the plunder. They gave them clothes and sandals, food and drink, and rubbed them with oil. Anyone too weak to walk they carried on donkeys. They brought them to Jericho, the City of Palms, to their relatives, and went back to Samaria.
## Ahaz turns to Assyria and to other gods
28:16 It was then that King Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria, asking them to help him.
28:17 Once again the Edomites had come, struck Judah and carried off captives.
28:18 The Philistines had raided the towns of the lowland and of Judah's Negev. They had captured Beth-shemesh, Aijalon and Gederoth, Soco with its villages, Timnah with its villages and Gimzo with its villages, and they settled there.
28:19 For the LORD brought Judah low because of Ahaz king of Israel, who had let Judah run wild and had acted very unfaithfully against the LORD.
28:20 Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria came against him. He pressed him hard and did not strengthen him.
28:21 Ahaz had taken a share from the LORD's house, from the king's house and from the officials, and given it to the king of Assyria, but it brought him no help.
28:22 While he was hard pressed, he added to his unfaithfulness against the LORD. That was King Ahaz.
28:23 He sacrificed to the gods of Damascus, who had struck him. "The gods of Aram's kings help them," he said. "To them I will sacrifice, and they will help me." Instead they made him stumble, him and all Israel.
28:24 Ahaz collected the articles of God's house and chopped up the articles of God's house. He closed the doors of the LORD's house, and at every corner in Jerusalem he made altars for himself.
28:25 Town after town in Judah, he made high places for sending up smoke to other gods. He provoked the LORD, the God of his ancestors.
28:26 The rest of his story and all his ways, from first to last, are written in the scroll of the kings of Judah and Israel.
28:27 Ahaz lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him in the city, in Jerusalem. They did not bring him into the tombs of Israel's kings. His son Hezekiah became king in his place.
## Hezekiah king of Judah
29:1 Hezekiah became king when he was twenty-five years old, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Abijah, daughter of Zechariah.
29:2 He did what was right in the LORD's eyes, in everything just as his forefather David had done.
## The temple doors opened
29:3 It was he who, in the first year of his reign, in the first month, opened the doors of the LORD's house and made them strong again.
29:4 He had the priests and the Levites come in, and gathered them in the open square on the east side.
29:5 He said to them, "Listen to me, Levites! Now make yourselves holy, and make holy the house of the LORD, the God of your ancestors. Take everything unclean out of the holy place.
29:6 Our fathers acted unfaithfully. They did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD our God and left him. From the LORD's tabernacle they turned their faces, and gave it their backs.
29:7 They even shut the doors of the porch and put out the lamps. Incense they did not burn, and burnt offerings they did not send up in the holy place to Israel's God.
29:8 So fury from the LORD came upon Judah and Jerusalem. He has made them something to shudder at, a horror, a thing people hiss at, as you see with your own eyes.
29:9 Look: our fathers fell by the sword, and for this our sons, our daughters and our wives are captives.
29:10 Now my heart is set on making a covenant with the LORD, the God of Israel, so that the heat of his anger turns back from us.
29:11 My sons, don't hold back now. It is you the LORD chose, to stand in front of him and wait on him, to be his servants and to send up incense smoke."
## The Levites cleanse the temple
29:12 Then the Levites got up: from the Kohathites, Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah; from the Merarites, Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel; from the Gershonites, Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah;
29:13 from the sons of Elizaphan, Shimri and Jeiel; from the sons of Asaph, Zechariah and Mattaniah;
29:14 from the sons of Heman, Jehiel and Shimei; and from the sons of Jeduthun, Shemaiah and Uzziel.
29:15 They gathered their brothers and made themselves holy. Then they came in, as the king had ordered by the LORD's words, to make the LORD's house clean.
29:16 The priests went inside the LORD's house to make it clean. Everything unclean that they found in the LORD's temple they brought out to the courtyard of the LORD's house, and the Levites received it and hauled it outside to the Kidron Wadi.
29:17 They began making it holy on the first day of the first month, and on the eighth day of the month they reached the LORD's porch. For eight days they made the LORD's house holy, and on the sixteenth day of the first month they finished.
29:18 Then they came inside to King Hezekiah and said, "We have made the whole house of the LORD clean: the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the table for the rows of bread with all its utensils.
29:19 All the utensils that King Ahaz threw out in his unfaithfulness while he reigned, we have set ready and made holy. There they are, set out before the LORD's altar."
## Atonement and worship
29:20 King Hezekiah got up early. He called together the city's officials and went up to the LORD's house.
29:21 They brought seven of each, bulls, rams, lambs and male goats, as a sin offering for the kingdom, for the sanctuary and for Judah. He ordered Aaron's descendants, the priests, to send them up on the LORD's altar.
29:22 They slaughtered the cattle, and the priests took the blood and splashed it on the altar. They slaughtered the rams and splashed the blood on the altar. They slaughtered the lambs and splashed the blood on the altar.
29:23 Then they brought the goats for the sin offering before the king and the assembly, and they leaned their hands on them.
29:24 The priests slaughtered them and put their blood on the altar as a sin offering, to make atonement for all Israel. For it was for all Israel, the king had said, that the burnt offering and the sin offering were to be made.
29:25 He stationed the Levites in the LORD's house with cymbals, harps and lyres, by the command of David, of Gad the king's visionary and of Nathan the prophet. For the command was by the LORD's hand, by the hand of his prophets.
29:26 The Levites stood ready with David's instruments, and the priests with the trumpets.
29:27 Hezekiah gave the order to send up the burnt offering on the altar. The moment the burnt offering began, the song of the LORD began too, with the trumpets and with the instruments of David king of Israel.
29:28 The whole assembly was bowing down, the song was being sung, the trumpets were sounding, all of it until the burnt offering was complete.
29:29 As they finished sending up the offering, the king and all who were there with him went down on their knees and bowed low.
29:30 King Hezekiah and the officials told the Levites to praise the LORD with the words of David and of Asaph the visionary. They praised with joy, and they bent low and bowed down.
## The people's offerings
29:31 Then Hezekiah spoke up: "Now you have given yourselves to the LORD's service. Come near and bring sacrifices and thank offerings to the LORD's house." So the assembly brought sacrifices and thank offerings, and everyone whose heart was willing brought burnt offerings.
29:32 The burnt offerings the assembly brought numbered seventy cattle, a hundred rams and two hundred lambs. All of them went up as a burnt offering to the LORD.
29:33 The holy offerings came to six hundred cattle and three thousand sheep.
29:34 But there were too few priests, and they could not skin all the burnt offerings. So the Levites, their brothers, backed them up until the work was done and until the priests had made themselves holy. The Levites' hearts had been more honest than the priests' about making themselves holy.
29:35 There were also burnt offerings in great number, along with the fat of the peace offerings and the drink offerings for the burnt offerings. So the service of the LORD's house was set in order again.
29:36 Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced over what God had made ready for the people, for it had all come about in a moment.
## Letters to all Israel
30:1 Hezekiah sent to all Israel and Judah. He even wrote letters to Ephraim and Manasseh, calling them to come to the LORD's house in Jerusalem and hold a Passover for the LORD, the God of Israel.
30:2 Taking counsel together, the king, his officials and all the assembly in Jerusalem agreed to hold the Passover in the second month.
30:3 At the proper time they could not hold it: too few priests had made themselves holy, and the people had not come together in Jerusalem.
30:4 In the eyes of the king and of the whole assembly, the thing was right.
30:5 So they made it a decree, to send a call through all Israel, from Beersheba as far as Dan, to come and hold a Passover for the LORD, the God of Israel, in Jerusalem. For they had not kept it as it is written, with the people gathered in a great crowd.
30:6 The runners went out with the letters from the hand of the king and his officials through all Israel and Judah, by the king's command. They said, "Israelites, return to the LORD, the God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel, so that he may come back to those of you who got away, who are left from the hand of Assyria's kings.
30:7 Don't be like your fathers and your brothers, who acted unfaithfully against the LORD, the God of their ancestors. He made them a horror, as you can see.
30:8 Now do not stiffen your necks like your fathers. Give your hand to the LORD. Come to his sanctuary, which he has made holy forever, and serve the LORD your God, so that the heat of his anger turns back from you.
30:9 For if you return to the LORD, your brothers and your children will find pity before their captors and come back to this land. For the LORD your God is gracious and compassionate. He will not turn his face aside from you, if you turn back to him."
30:10 So the runners were passing from town to town through the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, as far as Zebulun. But people laughed at them and made fun of them.
30:11 Yet there were men from Asher, Manasseh and Zebulun who humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem.
30:12 In Judah too God's hand was at work. He made them of one heart, to carry out the order of the king and the officials, given by the LORD's word.
## The great Passover
30:13 A great crowd of people gathered in Jerusalem to keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread in the second month, a very large assembly.
30:14 They got up and took away the altars in Jerusalem. The incense altars too, every one, they took away and threw into the Kidron Wadi.
30:15 The Passover was slaughtered on the fourteenth day of the second month. The priests and the Levites were ashamed, and they made themselves holy and brought burnt offerings to the LORD's house.
30:16 They took their places by their rule, as the law of Moses the man of God says, the priests splashing the blood handed to them by the Levites.
30:17 Many in the assembly had not made themselves holy, so the Levites were in charge of slaughtering the Passover animals for each person who was unclean, to make them holy to the LORD.
30:18 A large part of the people, many from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar and Zebulun, had not made themselves clean, and still they ate the Passover, not as it is written. For Hezekiah had prayed over them: "May the good LORD make atonement for
30:19 everyone whose heart is set on seeking God, the LORD, the God of his ancestors, even without the cleansing the holy place requires."
30:20 The LORD listened to Hezekiah and healed the people.
30:21 All the Israelites to be found in Jerusalem kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days with great joy. Day after day the Levites and the priests praised the LORD with mighty instruments for the LORD.
30:22 Hezekiah spoke to the heart of all the Levites, who showed good skill for the LORD. For the seven days they ate the festival meals, sacrificing peace offerings and giving thanks to the LORD, the God of their ancestors.
## Seven more days of joy
30:23 The whole assembly took counsel and decided to keep another seven days. So they kept seven days more, full of joy.
30:24 For Hezekiah king of Judah set aside for the assembly a thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep, and the officials set aside for them a thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep. Priests made themselves holy in large numbers.
30:25 The whole assembly of Judah rejoiced, with the priests and the Levites, and the whole assembly that came from Israel, and the foreigners who came from the land of Israel and those who lived in Judah.
30:26 Jerusalem was filled with great joy. Not since the days of Solomon son of David, king of Israel, had Jerusalem seen anything like it.
30:27 The Levite priests rose and blessed the people. Their voice was heard, and their prayer went up to his holy home, to heaven.
## The high places torn down
31:1 When all this was finished, all Israel to be found there went out to the towns of Judah. They broke the standing stones to pieces and chopped down the Asherah poles. They pulled down the high places and the altars throughout Judah and Benjamin, and in Ephraim and Manasseh, until they had made an end of them. Then all the Israelites returned to their towns, each to his own holding.
## Gifts for the priests and Levites
31:2 Hezekiah set up the divisions of the priests and the Levites, division by division. Each man had his service, priest or Levite: burnt offerings and peace offerings, ministering, giving thanks and praising in the gates of the LORD's camps.
31:3 The king's share from his own property was for the burnt offerings. It covered the morning and evening burnt offerings, and the burnt offerings for the Sabbaths, the New Moons and the set feasts, as it is written in the LORD's law.
31:4 He told the people, those who lived in Jerusalem, to give the priests and the Levites their share, so that they might stay firm in the LORD's law.
31:5 When the word got around, the Israelites gave more and more of the firstfruits of the grain, the new wine, the fresh oil, the honey and every crop of the field. They brought in the tithe of everything, heaps of it.
31:6 As for the Israelites and Judeans living in Judah's towns, they too brought the tithe of cattle and sheep, and the tithe of the holy gifts made holy to the LORD their God. They stacked them up, heaps and heaps.
31:7 They started laying the heaps' foundations in the third month, and finished in the seventh.
31:8 Hezekiah and the officials came and looked at the heaps. They blessed the LORD, and they blessed his people Israel.
31:9 Hezekiah asked the priests and the Levites about the heaps.
31:10 Azariah the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, answered him. "Since the gifts began to come into the LORD's house, we have eaten and been full, and much is left over. The LORD has blessed his people. What is left is this great heap."
31:11 Hezekiah said to get rooms ready in the LORD's house, and they got them ready.
31:12 They faithfully brought in the gifts, the tithe and the holy things. Konaniah the Levite was the officer in charge of them, and his brother Shimei was second.
31:13 Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath and Benaiah were overseers under Konaniah and his brother Shimei, by the appointment of King Hezekiah and of Azariah, the officer in charge of God's house.
31:14 Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the gatekeeper on the east side, had charge of the freewill gifts to God, to give out the gifts for the LORD and the most holy things.
31:15 Under him, in the towns of the priests, Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah and Shecaniah faithfully gave out shares to their brothers by divisions, to great and small alike.
31:16 This was apart from the males listed in the family records from three years old and up. They were all who came into the LORD's house for each day's duty, for their service in their posts, by their divisions.
31:17 The priests were listed in the family records by their ancestors' houses, and the Levites from twenty years old and up, by their posts and their divisions.
31:18 They were listed with all their little ones, their wives, their sons and their daughters, for the whole assembly, for in their faithfulness they kept themselves holy.
31:19 As for Aaron's descendants, the priests, in the pasture fields of their towns: in every town there were men named one by one. They gave out shares to every male among the priests and to everyone listed among the Levites.
31:20 Hezekiah did this throughout Judah. He did what was good, right and true before the LORD his God.
31:21 In every work he began in the service of God's house, and in the law and the command, to seek his God, he acted with all his heart, and things went well for him.
## Sennacherib invades
32:1 After these things and this faithfulness, Sennacherib king of Assyria came. He came into Judah and camped against the walled towns. He meant to break them open and take them.
32:2 Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib had come, his face set on war against Jerusalem.
32:3 He took counsel with his officials and his warriors about stopping up the water of the springs outside the city, and they helped him.
32:4 A large crowd gathered, and they stopped up all the springs and the stream that ran through the middle of the land. "Why let Assyria's kings come and find water in plenty?" they said.
32:5 He set to work with vigor. He rebuilt the whole wall where it was broken and built it up as high as the towers, with another wall outside it. He strengthened the Millo in the City of David and made great quantities of weapons and shields.
32:6 He put commanders of war over the people and gathered them to himself in the open square at the city gate. He put heart into them with these words:
32:7 "Be strong and bold. Do not be afraid and do not be shaken because of the king of Assyria and all the crowd that is with him. For the one with us is greater than the one with him.
32:8 He has an arm of flesh. We have the LORD our God, to help us and fight our battles for us." And the people leaned on the words of Hezekiah king of Judah.
## Sennacherib's threats
32:9 After this, while Sennacherib king of Assyria was at Lachish with all his royal might, he sent his servants to Jerusalem, to Hezekiah king of Judah and to all Judah that was in Jerusalem, with this message:
32:10 "Sennacherib king of Assyria says this: What are you trusting in, sitting under siege in Jerusalem?
32:11 Isn't Hezekiah leading you astray, to give you up to die of hunger and thirst, when he says, 'The LORD our God will rescue us from the king of Assyria's grip'?
32:12 Isn't Hezekiah the very one who has done away with his high places and his altars? He has told Judah and Jerusalem, 'You must bow down in front of one altar, and on it you must send up smoke.'
32:13 Surely you know what I have done, I and my fathers, to all the peoples of the lands. Could the gods of the lands' nations, any of them, rescue their land from my hand?
32:14 Which one of all the gods of these nations, which my fathers put under the ban, was able to rescue his people from my hand, that your God should be able to rescue you from my hand?
32:15 So now, don't let Hezekiah fool you, and don't let him lead you astray like this. Don't believe him. For not one god of any nation or kingdom could rescue its people from my hand, or from my fathers' hand. Your gods, then, will surely not rescue you from my hand!"
32:16 Sennacherib's servants kept on talking against the LORD God and against Hezekiah his servant.
32:17 He also wrote letters to insult the LORD, the God of Israel, and to speak against him: "The gods of the lands' nations did not rescue their people from my hand. In the same way, Hezekiah's God will not rescue his people from my hand."
32:18 They shouted at the top of their voices in Judean to the people of Jerusalem on the wall, to scare them and throw them into panic, so they could take the city.
32:19 They spoke of the God of Jerusalem as they spoke of the gods of the peoples of the earth, things that human hands had made.
## The LORD rescues Jerusalem
32:20 Hezekiah the king and Isaiah son of Amoz, the prophet, prayed about this and cried out to the heavens.
32:21 Then the LORD sent an angel. He wiped out every strong fighting man, every leader and commander, in the camp of the king of Assyria. So Sennacherib went back to his own land, red-faced with shame. He went into the house of his god, and there some who had come from his own body cut him down with the sword.
32:22 In this way the LORD saved Hezekiah and Jerusalem's people from the hand of Sennacherib king of Assyria and from every other hand. He guided them on every side.
32:23 Many people kept bringing gifts for the LORD to Jerusalem, and costly presents for Hezekiah king of Judah. After this, all the nations looked up to him.
## Hezekiah's illness, pride and wealth
32:24 In those days Hezekiah was sick and dying. He prayed to the LORD, and the LORD spoke to him and gave him a sign.
32:25 But Hezekiah did not pay back the good done to him, because his heart rose up high. So wrath was on him, and on Judah and Jerusalem.
32:26 Then Hezekiah humbled himself for the pride of his heart, he and the people living in Jerusalem. So the LORD's wrath did not come on them in Hezekiah's days.
32:27 Hezekiah had wealth and honor, a great deal of both. He made himself treasuries for silver and gold, for precious stones and spices, for shields and for every kind of fine object,
32:28 storehouses for the yield of grain, new wine and fresh oil, and stalls for every kind of beast, and flocks for the sheepfolds.
32:29 He built towns for himself, and he had flocks and herds in great numbers, for God had given him a great, great deal of property.
32:30 This same Hezekiah stopped up the upper outlet of the waters of Gihon and led them straight down, westward, to the City of David. Hezekiah did well in all he did.
32:31 And so it was with the envoys of Babylon's officials, who were sent to him to ask about the sign that had happened in the land. God left him, to test him and to know all that was in his heart.
32:32 The rest of Hezekiah's story, and his acts of loyal love, are written in the vision of the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz, in the scroll of the kings of Judah and Israel.
32:33 Hezekiah lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him on the rise where the tombs of David's descendants are. All Judah and the people of Jerusalem honored him at his death. His son Manasseh became king in his place.
## Manasseh king of Judah
33:1 Manasseh was twelve years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem.
33:2 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He copied the abhorrent ways of the nations that the LORD drove out ahead of the Israelites.
33:3 The high places that his father Hezekiah had torn down, he built again. He put up altars for the Baals and made Asherah poles. He bowed down to all the army of the heavens and served them.
33:4 He even built altars inside the LORD's house, the very place the LORD had spoken of when he said, "In Jerusalem my name will be forever."
33:5 In both courtyards of the LORD's house he built altars to all the army of the heavens.
33:6 He made his sons pass through the fire in the Valley of Ben-hinnom. He read signs, looked for omens and practiced sorcery. He appointed people to deal with ghosts of the dead and spirits that know things. He did a great deal of what was evil in the LORD's eyes, to provoke him.
33:7 He took the carved image of the figure he had made and placed it in God's house. Of this house God had said to David and his son Solomon, "In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I chose out of all Israel's tribes, I will put my name for all time.
33:8 Never again will I remove Israel's feet from the soil I appointed for your ancestors, as long as they take care to do everything I commanded them: the whole law, the rules and the rulings given through Moses."
33:9 Manasseh lured Judah and the people of Jerusalem off course, and they did worse evil than the nations the LORD wiped out ahead of the Israelites.
## Manasseh's captivity and return
33:10 The LORD spoke to Manasseh and his people, but they would not listen.
33:11 So the LORD let loose on them the officers of the king of Assyria's army. They caught Manasseh with hooks, bound him in bronze fetters and led him off to Babylon.
33:12 Hard pressed, he pleaded with the LORD his God. Before the God of his ancestors he humbled himself deeply.
33:13 He prayed to him, and God was entreated by him. He heard his pleading and brought him back to Jerusalem, to his kingship. Then Manasseh came to know that the LORD, he is God.
33:14 After this he built an outside wall to the City of David, on the west side of Gihon, in the wadi, up to the entrance at the Fish Gate. It went around the Ophel, and he made it very high. He put army commanders in all the walled towns in Judah.
33:15 He removed the foreign gods and the figure from the LORD's house, and every altar he had built on the hill of the LORD's house and in Jerusalem. He threw them outside the city.
33:16 He rebuilt the LORD's altar and sacrificed on it peace offerings and thank offerings. He told Judah to serve the LORD, the God of Israel.
33:17 Still, the people went on sacrificing at the high places, though only to the LORD their God.
33:18 The rest of Manasseh's story, his prayer to his God, and the words of the visionaries who spoke to him in the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, are there in the records of the kings of Israel.
33:19 His prayer, and how God was entreated by him, and all his sin and his unfaithfulness are written in the records of Hozai. So are the places where he built high places and set up the Asherah poles and the carved idols before he humbled himself.
33:20 Manasseh lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him at his house. His son Amon became king in his place.
## Amon
33:21 Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem.
33:22 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes, as his father Manasseh had done. To all the carved idols his father Manasseh had made Amon sacrificed, and he served them.
33:23 Before the LORD he did not humble himself, the way his father Manasseh had humbled himself. This Amon only made his guilt greater.
33:24 His servants plotted against him and killed him in his own house.
33:25 Then the people of the land killed everyone in the plot against King Amon. And the people of the land put his son Josiah on the throne after him.
## Josiah king of Judah
34:1 Josiah was eight years old when he became king, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem.
34:2 He did what was right in the LORD's eyes. He walked in the ways of his forefather David, swerving neither right nor left.
## The land made clean
34:3 When he had reigned eight years, and was still a young man, he began to seek the God of his forefather David. In the twelfth year he started making Judah and Jerusalem clean of the high places, the Asherah poles, the carved idols and the cast images.
34:4 In front of him they pulled down the altars of the Baals, and the incense altars high above them he chopped down. He broke the Asherah poles, the carved idols and the cast images to pieces and ground them to dust. He scattered it on top of the graves of the people who used to sacrifice to them.
34:5 Priests' bones he burned on their altars. So he purified Judah and Jerusalem.
34:6 In the towns of Manasseh, Ephraim and Simeon, as far as Naphtali, and in the ruined places around them,
34:7 he pulled down the altars and the Asherah poles and crushed the carved idols to powder, and all through the land of Israel he chopped down every incense altar. Then he went back to Jerusalem.
## Repairs and the scroll found
34:8 In the eighteenth year of his reign, to purify the land and the house, he sent Shaphan son of Azaliah, Maaseiah the city commander and Joah son of Joahaz the recorder. They were to repair the house of the LORD his God.
34:9 They came to Hilkiah the high priest and handed over the silver brought into God's house. The Levites who guarded the doorway had gathered it from the hands of Manasseh and Ephraim and all the rest of Israel, from all Judah and Benjamin, and the people of Jerusalem.
34:10 They put it into the hands of the work supervisors who oversee the LORD's house, and they paid it out to the workmen working in the LORD's house, to fix and repair the house.
34:11 They gave it to the woodworkers and the builders, for buying cut stone, and lumber for the joints and for roofing the buildings that the kings of Judah had ruined.
34:12 The men worked at the job in good faith. Placed over them to lead the work were Jahath and Obadiah, Levites of the sons of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam, of the sons of the Kohathites. And the Levites, every one who knew how to play instruments of song,
34:13 had charge of the porters and led on everyone doing work, one task after another. Others of the Levites were secretaries, officials and gatekeepers.
34:14 When they took out the silver brought into the LORD's house, Hilkiah the priest came upon the scroll of the LORD's law, given by the hand of Moses.
34:15 Hilkiah spoke up and said to Shaphan the secretary, "The scroll of the law! I have found it in the LORD's house." Hilkiah gave the scroll to Shaphan.
34:16 Shaphan brought the scroll to the king, and he also reported back to the king. "All that was put in your servants' hands, they are doing," he said.
34:17 "They have emptied out the silver found in the LORD's house and put it into the hands of the supervisors and into the hands of the workmen."
34:18 Shaphan the secretary went on to tell the king, "There's a scroll Hilkiah the priest gave me." Then Shaphan read from it in front of the king.
34:19 The king heard the words of the law, and he tore his clothes.
## Huldah the prophet
34:20 The king gave orders to Hilkiah, Ahikam son of Shaphan, Abdon son of Micah, Shaphan the secretary and Asaiah the king's servant:
34:21 "Go and seek the LORD for me, and for those left in Israel and in Judah, about the words of the scroll that was found. The LORD's anger is great. It has poured out on us, because our ancestors did not keep the LORD's word and do everything written in this scroll."
34:22 So Hilkiah and those the king named went to Huldah, a woman prophet. She was the wife of Shallum son of Tokhath, son of Hasrah, the one in charge of the robes, and she lived in Jerusalem in the second district. They talked with her about this.
34:23 She told them, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'Tell the man who sent you to me:
34:24 The LORD says this: Look, I am bringing disaster on this place and on those who live here, all the curses written in the scroll that they read in front of the king of Judah.
34:25 They left me. They sent up smoke to other gods, to anger me with everything their hands made. So my anger is pouring out on this place, and no one will put it out.'
34:26 And to the king of Judah, who sent you to seek the LORD, say this: 'The LORD, the God of Israel, says this about the words you heard:
34:27 Your heart grew soft. You humbled yourself before God when you heard his words about this place and its people. You humbled yourself before me, tore your clothes and wept in front of me. So I, for my part, have heard, the LORD declares.
34:28 Look, I am going to gather you in with your ancestors. You will be gathered into your grave in peace, and your eyes will not have to see all the disaster I am bringing on this place and on those who live here.'" The men reported this to the king.
## The covenant renewed
34:29 The king sent out a call and gathered all the elders of Judah and of Jerusalem.
34:30 The king went up to the LORD's house, and all the men of Judah, those who lived in Jerusalem, the priests and the Levites, all the people, great and small alike. Into their ears he read every word of the covenant scroll that had been found in the LORD's house.
34:31 The king stood in his place and made the covenant before the LORD. He pledged to walk after the LORD and keep his commands, his decrees and his rules with all his heart and all his being. He would do the words of the covenant written in this scroll.
34:32 He made everyone found in Jerusalem and Benjamin take their stand. And the people who lived in Jerusalem acted by the covenant of God, the God of their ancestors.
34:33 Josiah removed all the abhorrent things from all the lands that belonged to the Israelites, and he made all who were found in Israel serve the LORD their God. While he lived, they never left off following the LORD, the God of their ancestors.
## Josiah's Passover
35:1 Josiah held a Passover for the LORD in Jerusalem. The Passover was slaughtered on the fourteenth day of the first month.
35:2 He put the priests in their posts and strengthened them for the work of the LORD's house.
35:3 He said to the Levites, who taught all Israel and were holy to the LORD, "Set the ark of holiness in the house that Solomon son of David, king of Israel, built. You have no load to carry on your shoulders. Serve the LORD your God now, and his people Israel.
35:4 Get yourselves ready by your ancestors' houses, in your divisions, as David king of Israel wrote and as his son Solomon wrote.
35:5 Take your stand in the holy place, so that for each group of ancestors' houses of your brothers, the people, there is a part of a Levite family.
35:6 Slaughter the Passover, make yourselves holy, and get things ready for your brothers, to do what the LORD said by the hand of Moses."
35:7 For the people Josiah gave flock animals, lambs and young goats, all of them for Passover offerings, for everyone who was there. They came to thirty thousand, with three thousand cattle. These were from the king's property.
35:8 His officials set aside a freewill gift for the people, the priests and the Levites. Hilkiah, Zechariah and Jehiel, the officers in charge of God's house, gave the priests two thousand six hundred Passover offerings and three hundred cattle.
35:9 Konaniah, with Shemaiah and Nethanel his brothers, and Hashabiah, Jeiel and Jozabad, the leaders of the Levites, set aside for the Levites five thousand Passover offerings and five hundred cattle.
35:10 So the service was set in order. The priests took their stand in their places and the Levites by their divisions, by the king's command.
35:11 They slaughtered the Passover, and the priests splashed the blood they took from their hands, while the Levites did the skinning.
35:12 They took away the burnt offering portions to give them to the groups of ancestors' houses of the people, for them to present to the LORD, as it is written in the scroll of Moses. And so with the cattle.
35:13 They cooked the Passover in the fire, by the rule. The holy offerings they cooked in pots, kettles and pans, and they hurried them out to all the people.
35:14 Afterward they made things ready for themselves and for the priests. The priests, Aaron's descendants, were sending up burnt offerings and fat until nightfall, so the Levites got things ready for themselves and for the priests, Aaron's descendants.
35:15 The singers, Asaph's descendants, stood at their posts by the command of David, Asaph, Heman and Jeduthun the king's visionary, and the gatekeepers stood gate by gate. None of them had to step away from his service, for their brothers the Levites made things ready for them.
35:16 So the whole service of the LORD was set in order that day, to hold the Passover and to send up burnt offerings on the LORD's altar, by King Josiah's command.
35:17 The Israelites to be found there kept the Passover at that time, and the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days.
35:18 No Passover like it had been held in Israel since the days of Samuel the prophet. None of Israel's kings had held a Passover like the one Josiah held, with the priests and the Levites, all Judah and the Israel that was present, and the people who lived in Jerusalem.
35:19 It was in the eighteenth year of Josiah's reign that this Passover was held.
## Josiah dies at Megiddo
35:20 After all this, when Josiah had prepared the house, up came Neco king of Egypt to make war at Carchemish on the Euphrates. Josiah went out to meet him.
35:21 But Neco sent messengers to him: "What do I have to do with you, king of Judah? Not against you, you yourself, today: I come against the house I am at war with. God bade me hurry. Leave God alone, who is with me, or he will destroy you."
35:22 But Josiah would not turn his face away from him. He disguised himself to fight him, and he did not listen to Neco's words from God's mouth. He came to fight in the valley of Megiddo.
35:23 The shooters shot at King Josiah, and the king said to his servants, "Get me away from here. I am hurt very badly."
35:24 His servants lifted him from the chariot, set him in the second chariot he had and drove him to Jerusalem. There he died, and he was buried in his ancestors' graves. All Judah and Jerusalem were mourning over Josiah.
35:25 Jeremiah sang a lament over Josiah. To this day every singer, man and woman, speaks of Josiah in the laments. They made these laments a fixed custom in Israel, and you will find them written in the Laments.
35:26 The rest of Josiah's story, and his acts of loyal love in keeping with what is written in the LORD's law,
35:27 his story from first to last, is written in the scroll of the kings of Israel and Judah.
## Jehoahaz
36:1 The people of the land took Josiah's son Jehoahaz and made him king to succeed his father in Jerusalem.
36:2 Joahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem.
36:3 The king of Egypt removed him from the throne in Jerusalem, and he fined the land one hundred talents of silver and one talent of gold.
## Jehoiakim
36:4 The king of Egypt made Eliakim, his brother, king over Judah and Jerusalem, and turned his name into Jehoiakim. Joahaz his brother, Neco took away and brought to Egypt.
36:5 Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD his God.
36:6 Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against him and bound him in bronze fetters to take him to Babylon.
36:7 Some of the articles of the LORD's house Nebuchadnezzar brought to Babylon, and he put them in his temple in Babylon.
36:8 The rest of Jehoiakim's story, the abhorrent things he did and what was found against him, is written in the scroll of the kings of Israel and Judah. His son Jehoiachin became king in his place.
## Jehoiachin
36:9 Jehoiachin was eight years old when he became king, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem. He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes.
36:10 At the turn of the year King Nebuchadnezzar sent and brought him to Babylon, along with the precious articles of the LORD's house. He made Zedekiah, his brother, king over Judah and Jerusalem.
## Zedekiah and the fall of Jerusalem
36:11 Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.
36:12 He did what was evil in the eyes of the LORD his God. Before Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke for the LORD, he would not humble himself.
36:13 Against King Nebuchadnezzar, too, he rebelled, though Nebuchadnezzar had put him under oath by God. He made his neck stiff and his heart firm against turning back to the LORD, the God of Israel.
36:14 All the leaders of the priests and the people, too, added unfaithfulness to unfaithfulness, like all the abhorrent things of the nations. They made the LORD's house unclean, the house he had set apart in Jerusalem.
36:15 The LORD, the God of their ancestors, sent to them by his messengers, sending early and sending often, for he felt pity for his people and for the place where he lived.
36:16 They, though, made fun of God's messengers. They looked down on his words and jeered at his prophets, till the LORD's fury came up against his people, till there was no cure.
36:17 So he raised up the king of the Chaldeans against them. In their holy house that king put their young men to the sword. He spared no one, young man or young woman, old man or the bent with age. God put them all into his hand.
36:18 He carried everything to Babylon: every article of God's house, big and small, the treasures of the LORD's house, and the treasures of the king and of his officials.
36:19 They set God's house on fire and tore down Jerusalem's wall. All its fortified palaces they burned with fire, and they wrecked every precious thing in it.
## The seventy years
36:20 Those who escaped the sword he took into exile in Babylon, and they became slaves to him and to his sons until Persia's kingdom began to reign.
36:21 This was to fulfill the LORD's word by Jeremiah's mouth: until the land had made up for its Sabbaths. All the days it lay desolate it rested, to fill up seventy years.
## Cyrus's decree
36:22 In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, to fulfill the LORD's word by Jeremiah's mouth, the LORD roused the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia. He sent a proclamation through his whole kingdom, and in writing as well:
36:23 "Cyrus king of Persia says this: All the kingdoms of the earth the LORD, the God of the heavens, has given me. He has appointed me to build him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Who among you is from all his people? The LORD his God be with him, and let him go up."
