## Isaac in Gerar
26:1 There was a famine in the land, another one besides the first famine that came in the days of Abraham, and Isaac went to Abimelech king of the Philistines, to Gerar.
26:2 The LORD appeared to him and said, "Do not go down to Egypt. Live in whatever land I tell you.
26:3 Stay as a foreigner in this land, and I will be with you and bless you, because to you and to your offspring I will give all these lands. I will make good the oath that I swore to Abraham your father.
26:4 I will make your offspring as many as the stars of the sky, and I will give your offspring all these lands, and all the nations of the earth will bless themselves by your offspring,
26:5 because Abraham listened to my voice and kept what I required of him: my commands, my rules and my laws."
26:6 So Isaac lived in Gerar.
26:7 When the men of the place asked about his wife, he said, "She is my sister." He was afraid to say "my wife," thinking, "The men of the place might kill me over Rebekah, because she is beautiful to look at."
26:8 After he had been there a long time, Abimelech king of the Philistines looked down through the window, and there was Isaac playing with Rebekah his wife.
26:9 Abimelech called Isaac in and said, "Look, she must be your wife! How could you say, 'She is my sister'?" Isaac said to him, "I said to myself, 'Or else I may die because of her.'"
26:10 Abimelech said, "What is this you have done to us? It would have taken little for one of the people to lie with your wife, and you would have brought guilt on us."
26:11 Abimelech then commanded all the people, "Anyone who touches this man or his wife will be put to death without fail."
26:12 Isaac sowed seed in that land, and that year he reaped a hundred times what he sowed. The LORD blessed him.
26:13 The man grew great. He kept on growing greater until he was very great indeed.
26:14 He had flocks of sheep and goats, herds of cattle and a large household of servants, and the Philistines envied him.
26:15 All the wells that his father's servants had dug in the days of Abraham his father, the Philistines had stopped up and filled with dirt.
26:16 Abimelech said to Isaac, "Go away from us. You have grown far too strong for us."
## The quarrel over the wells
26:17 So Isaac left there, camped in the Gerar valley, and settled there.
26:18 Isaac dug out again the wells of water that had been dug in the days of Abraham his father, which the Philistines had stopped up after Abraham died, and he called them by the same names his father had called them.
26:19 Isaac's servants dug in the valley and found there a well of living water.
26:20 But Gerar's herdsmen argued with Isaac's herdsmen, "The water belongs to us!" So he called the well Esek, because they had picked a quarrel with him.
26:21 They dug another well, and they fought over that one too, so he named it Sitnah.
26:22 He moved away from there and dug another well, and over this one they did not quarrel. He called it Rehoboth and said, "For now the LORD has given us room, and we will bear fruit in the land."
## The LORD appears at Beersheba
26:23 From there he went up to Beersheba.
26:24 That night the LORD appeared to him and said, "I am the God of Abraham your father. Do not be afraid, because I am with you. I will bless you and make your offspring many, for the sake of Abraham my servant."
26:25 There he built an altar and called on the LORD's name. He pitched his tent there, and there Isaac's servants dug a well.
## A treaty with Abimelech
26:26 Then Abimelech went to him from Gerar, along with Ahuzzath his friend and Phicol, the commander of his army.
26:27 Isaac said to them, "Why have you come to me, when you hate me and sent me away from you?"
26:28 They said, "We have seen with our own eyes that the LORD has been with you. So we said, let there be an oath between us, between us and you, and let us make a covenant with you,
26:29 that you will do us no harm, just as we have not touched you, and just as we have done you nothing but good and sent you away in peace. You are now the LORD's blessed one."
26:30 He made them a feast, and they ate and drank.
26:31 They rose early in the morning and swore, each man to his brother. Isaac sent them off, and they went from him in peace.
26:32 It happened on that day: Isaac's servants came and told him about the well they had been digging, and they said to him, "We have found water."
26:33 He called it Shibah. That is why the name of the city is Beersheba to this day.
## Esau's Hittite wives
26:34 At forty years of age Esau took two wives: Judith, Beeri the Hittite's daughter, and Basemath, Elon the Hittite's daughter.
26:35 They were a bitterness of spirit to Isaac and to Rebekah.
## Isaac sends Esau to hunt
27:1 When Isaac was old and his eyes had grown too dim to see, he called Esau, his older son, and said to him, "My son." He answered, "Here I am."
27:2 Isaac said, "Look, I have grown old. I do not know the day I will die.
27:3 So now, take up your gear, your quiver and your bow, and go out into the open country and hunt some game for me.
27:4 Then make me a savory meal, the kind I love, and bring it to me, and let me eat, so that my soul may bless you before I die."
## Rebekah's plan
27:5 Rebekah was listening while Isaac spoke to Esau his son. Esau went off to the open country to hunt game and bring it back.
27:6 Rebekah said to Jacob her son, "Listen, I heard your father talking to Esau your brother. He said,
27:7 'Bring me game and make me a savory meal, so I can eat and bless you before the LORD before I die.'
27:8 So now, my son, listen to my voice, and do what I command you.
27:9 Go to the flock, please, and fetch me two fine young goats from there, and I will cook them into a savory meal for your father, the way he likes it.
27:10 Then you will bring it to your father and he will eat it, so that he will bless you before his death."
27:11 Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "But Esau my brother is a hairy man, and I am a smooth man.
27:12 What if my father feels me? In his eyes I will be a man playing a trick on him, and I will bring a curse on myself, not a blessing."
27:13 His mother told him, "Let your curse fall on me, my son. Only listen to my voice. Go and get them for me."
27:14 So he went and got them and brought them to his mother, and his mother made a savory meal, the kind his father loved.
27:15 Then Rebekah took the best clothes of Esau her older son, which she had with her in the house, and she dressed Jacob her younger son in them.
27:16 And with the skins of the young goats she covered his hands and the smooth part of his neck.
27:17 Then she put the savory meal and the bread she had made into the hand of Jacob her son.
## Jacob takes the blessing
27:18 He went in to his father and said, "My father." He said, "Here I am. Who are you, my son?"
27:19 Jacob said to his father, "It is I, Esau, your firstborn. I did as you told me. Up, please, sit and eat from my game, so that your soul may bless me."
27:20 Isaac said to his son, "How did you find it so fast, my son?" He said, "Because the LORD your God made it come my way."
27:21 Isaac said to Jacob, "Come close, please, so I can feel you, my son, and know whether you really are my son Esau or not."
27:22 Jacob came close to Isaac his father, and Isaac felt him and said, "The voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are Esau's hands."
27:23 He did not know who he was, for his hands were hairy like the hands of Esau his brother; and he blessed him.
27:24 He said, "Are you really my son Esau?" He said, "I am."
27:25 Then he said, "Bring it close to me, and let me eat of my son's game, so that my soul may bless you." Jacob brought it close to him and he ate, and he brought him wine and he drank.
27:26 Then his father Isaac said to him, "Come close, please, and kiss me, my son."
27:27 He went close and kissed him, and Isaac caught the smell of his clothes. Then he blessed him and said, "See, my son's smell / is like the smell of a field / the LORD has blessed.
27:28 May God give you / some of the dew of the sky / and some of the richness of the earth, / plenty of grain and new wine.
27:29 May peoples serve you, / and nations bow down to you. / Be master over your brothers, / and may your mother's sons bow down to you. / Whoever curses you, cursed! / Whoever blesses you, blessed!"
## Esau comes too late
27:30 Isaac had just finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob had barely gone out from the presence of Isaac his father, when Esau his brother came in from his hunting.
27:31 He too made a savory meal and brought it to his father. He said to his father, "Let my father sit up and eat some of his son's game, so that your soul may bless me."
27:32 Isaac his father said to him, "Who are you?" He said, "I am your son, your firstborn, Esau."
27:33 Isaac shook with a great trembling, past all measure, and said, "Who then was it who hunted game and brought it to me? I ate from all of it before you came, and I blessed him. Yes, and blessed he will be."
27:34 When Esau heard his father's words, he cried out with a cry, a great and bitter cry beyond measure, and said to his father, "Bless me as well, me too, my father!"
27:35 But he said, "Your brother came with trickery and took your blessing."
27:36 Esau said, "Isn't that why he is named Jacob? He has grabbed me by the heel these two times. He took my birthright, and look, now he has taken my blessing." Then he said, "Have you not kept back a blessing for me?"
27:37 Isaac answered Esau, "Look, I have made him master over you. I have given him all his brothers as servants, and I have supplied him with grain and new wine. So what can I do for you, my son?"
27:38 Esau said to his father, "Is that the only blessing you have, my father? Bless me, me too, my father!" And Esau raised his voice and wept.
27:39 Then Isaac his father answered him, "Look, away from the richness of the earth / your home will be, / and away from the dew of the sky above.
27:40 By your sword you will live, / and your brother you will serve. / But when you break loose, / you will tear his yoke off your neck."
## Esau's grudge
27:41 So Esau bore a grudge against Jacob over the blessing with which his father had blessed him. Esau said in his heart, "The days of mourning for my father are drawing near. Then I will kill Jacob my brother."
27:42 Someone told Rebekah what Esau her older son had said. She sent for Jacob her younger son and said to him, "Listen, Esau your brother is comforting himself with the thought of killing you.
27:43 So now, my son, listen to my voice. Get up and run away to Laban my brother in Haran.
27:44 Stay with him a few days, until your brother's rage cools,
27:45 until your brother's anger turns away from you and he forgets what you have done to him. Then I will send and fetch you from there. Why should I be robbed of you both in a single day?"
27:46 Then Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am sick of living because of the daughters of Heth. If Jacob marries one of the daughters of Heth like these, one of the women of this land, what good will life be to me?"
## Isaac sends Jacob away
28:1 So Isaac called Jacob and blessed him. He gave him this order: "Do not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan.
28:2 Get up, go to Paddan-aram, to the house of Bethuel your mother's father, and take a wife for yourself there, from the daughters of Laban your mother's brother.
28:3 May God Almighty bless you, and make you bear fruit and grow many, until you become an assembly of peoples.
28:4 May he give you Abraham's blessing, you and your offspring with you, so you may own the land where you are a foreigner, which God gave to Abraham."
28:5 So Isaac sent Jacob away. He went to Paddan-aram, to Laban son of Bethuel the Aramean, whose sister Rebekah was the mother of Jacob and Esau.
## Esau marries again
28:6 Esau saw that Isaac had blessed Jacob and sent him off to Paddan-aram to take a wife from there. He saw that when Isaac blessed him he ordered him, "Do not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan,"
28:7 and that Jacob had heeded his father and his mother and had gone off to Paddan-aram.
28:8 Esau saw that the daughters of Canaan were displeasing in the eyes of Isaac his father.
28:9 So Esau went to Ishmael and took Mahalath daughter of Ishmael, Abraham's son, the sister of Nebaioth, as a wife, on top of the wives he had.
## Jacob's dream at Bethel
28:10 Jacob went out from Beersheba and headed toward Haran.
28:11 He came upon the place and stopped there for the night, because the sun had gone down. He took one of the stones of the place, put it by his head, and lay down in that place.
28:12 He dreamed, and look: a stairway set up on the earth, its top reaching the sky, and look: angels of God going up and going down on it.
28:13 And look: the LORD stood above it and said, "I am the LORD, the God of Abraham your father and the God of Isaac. The land you are lying on I will give to you and to your offspring.
28:14 Your offspring will be like the dust of the earth, and you will break out westward and eastward, northward and southward. In you and in your offspring all the clans of the earth will be blessed.
28:15 Look, I am with you. I will guard you everywhere you go and bring you back to this soil. I will not let go of you until I have done what I have spoken to you."
28:16 Jacob woke from his sleep and said, "The LORD really is in this place, and I did not know it."
28:17 Fear came over him, and he said, "How fearsome this place is! This is nothing but the house of God, and this is heaven's gate."
28:18 Early in the morning Jacob got up, took the stone he had put by his head, set it up as a standing stone, and poured oil on its top.
28:19 He named that place Bethel, though the city was called Luz before that.
28:20 Then Jacob made a vow: "If God will be with me and guard me on this road I am traveling, and give me bread to eat and clothes to wear,
28:21 and I return in peace to my father's house, then the LORD will be my God,
28:22 and this stone, which I have set up as a standing stone, will become a house of God, and of everything you give me I will give a tenth, a tenth to you."
## Jacob meets Rachel
29:1 Jacob picked up his feet and went on to the land of the people of the east.
29:2 He looked, and there was a well in the open field, and three flocks of sheep lying beside it, because the flocks were watered from that well. The stone over the mouth of the well was large.
29:3 When all the flocks had gathered there, the shepherds would roll the stone off the mouth of the well and water the sheep, and then put the stone back in its place over the well's mouth.
29:4 Jacob said to them, "My brothers, where are you from?" They said, "We are from Haran."
29:5 He said to them, "Do you know Laban, Nahor's son?" They said, "We know him."
29:6 He said to them, "Is it well with him?" They said, "It is well. And look, here comes Rachel his daughter with the sheep."
29:7 He said, "Look, the day is still long. It is not time to bring the animals in. Water the sheep, then go and let them graze."
29:8 But they said, "We can't, not until all the flocks are gathered in and they roll the stone off the well's mouth. Then we water the sheep."
29:9 He was still speaking with them when Rachel came with her father's sheep, because she was the one who tended them.
29:10 When Jacob saw Rachel, the daughter of Laban his mother's brother, and the sheep of Laban his mother's brother, Jacob stepped up. He rolled the stone off the mouth of the well, and watered the flock of Laban his mother's brother.
29:11 Then Jacob kissed Rachel, and he raised his voice and wept.
29:12 Jacob told Rachel that he was her father's kinsman and that he was Rebekah's son, and she ran and told her father.
29:13 When Laban heard the news about Jacob, his sister's son, he ran to meet him, hugged him, kissed him, and brought him to his house. Jacob told Laban everything that had happened.
29:14 Laban said to him, "You really are my bone and my flesh." Jacob stayed with him a full month.
## Seven years for Rachel
29:15 Then Laban said to Jacob, "Should you work for me for nothing just because you are my kinsman? Tell me what your wages should be."
29:16 Laban had two daughters. The older one's name was Leah; the younger one's name was Rachel.
29:17 Leah's eyes were soft, but Rachel was lovely in form and lovely to look at.
29:18 Jacob loved Rachel. He said, "I will work for you seven years for Rachel, your younger daughter."
29:19 Laban said, "Better that I give her to you than give her to some other man. Stay with me."
29:20 So Jacob worked seven years for Rachel, and in his eyes they were like a few days, because he loved her.
29:21 Then Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife, because my time is complete. Let me go in to her."
29:22 Laban gathered all the people of the place and made a feast.
29:23 But in the evening he took Leah his daughter and brought her to Jacob, and Jacob went in to her.
29:24 (Laban had given his slave woman Zilpah to Leah his daughter as her slave woman.)
29:25 In the morning, there she was: Leah! Jacob said to Laban, "What is this you have done to me? Was it not for Rachel that I worked for you? Why have you tricked me?"
29:26 Laban said, "It is not done this way in our place, giving the younger before the firstborn.
29:27 Finish this one's wedding week, and we will give you the other one too, for the work you will do for me for another seven years."
29:28 Jacob did so. He finished that one's week, and Laban gave him Rachel his daughter as his wife.
29:29 (Laban had given his slave woman Bilhah to Rachel his daughter as her slave woman.)
29:30 He went in to Rachel as well, and he loved Rachel, too, more than Leah. And he served Laban seven more years.
## Leah's sons
29:31 The LORD saw that Leah was the unloved one, and he opened her womb. Rachel was barren.
29:32 Leah conceived and bore a son and called his name Reuben, for she said, "The LORD has looked on my misery; now my husband will love me."
29:33 She conceived once more and bore a son, and said, "The LORD heard that I was unloved, and so he gave me this one too." She named him Simeon.
29:34 Again she conceived and bore a son, and said, "This time, now, my husband will join himself to me, because I have given him three sons." That is why his name was called Levi.
29:35 She conceived yet again and bore a son, and she said, "This time I will praise the LORD." That is why she named him Judah. Then she stopped bearing.
## Rachel and Leah compete for children
30:1 When Rachel saw that she had borne Jacob no children, Rachel became jealous of her sister. She said to Jacob, "Give me children! If you don't, I am a dead woman."
30:2 Jacob's anger flared against Rachel, and he said, "Am I in the place of God, who has kept the fruit of the womb from you?"
30:3 She said, "Here is my slave woman Bilhah. Go in to her, so that she can give birth on my knees, and I too can be built up through her."
30:4 So she gave him Bilhah her slave woman as a wife, and Jacob went in to her.
30:5 Bilhah conceived and bore Jacob a son.
30:6 Then Rachel said, "God has judged for me. Yes, he has heard my voice and given me a son." That is why she named him Dan.
30:7 Bilhah, Rachel's slave woman, conceived again and bore Jacob a second son.
30:8 Rachel said, "I have wrestled God's wrestlings with my sister, and I have won." So she named him Naphtali.
30:9 When Leah saw that she had stopped having children, she took Zilpah her slave woman and gave her to Jacob as a wife.
30:10 Zilpah, Leah's slave woman, bore Jacob a son.
30:11 Leah said, "Good fortune has come!" So she named him Gad.
30:12 Zilpah, Leah's slave woman, bore Jacob a second son.
30:13 And Leah said, "Happy me! For daughters will call me happy." So she named him Asher.
## The mandrakes
30:14 Reuben went out in the days of the wheat harvest and came upon mandrakes in the open field, and he brought them to Leah his mother. Then Rachel said to Leah, "Give me some of your son's mandrakes, please."
30:15 But Leah said to her, "Is it a small thing that you took my husband? Will you take my son's mandrakes too?" Rachel said, "All right, he can lie with you tonight in return for your son's mandrakes."
30:16 In the evening, when Jacob came from the field, Leah went out to meet him. "It is me you are coming in to," she said, "because I have hired you outright with my son's mandrakes." And he lay with her that night.
30:17 And God heard Leah. She conceived and bore Jacob a fifth son.
30:18 Leah said, "God has paid me my wages, because I gave my slave woman to my husband." So she named him Issachar.
30:19 Leah conceived again and bore Jacob a sixth son.
30:20 Leah said, "God has given me a fine present. Now my husband will honor me, since I have borne him six sons." So she called him Zebulun.
30:21 Afterward she bore a daughter and named her Dinah.
30:22 And God remembered Rachel; God paid heed to her and opened her womb.
30:23 She conceived and bore a son, and said, "God has taken away my disgrace."
30:24 She named him Joseph, saying, "May the LORD add another son for me."
## Jacob's wages
30:25 After Rachel had given birth to Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, "Send me off, so that I can go to my own place and my own country.
30:26 Give me my wives and my children, for whom I have worked for you, and let me go. You yourself know the work I have done for you."
30:27 Laban said to him, "Please, if I have found favor in your eyes, stay. I have read the signs: the LORD has blessed me on your account."
30:28 He went on, "Fix the wage you want from me, and I will pay it."
30:29 Jacob said to him, "You know how I have worked for you, and how your livestock has fared with me.
30:30 The little you had before I came has burst out into a great deal, and the LORD has blessed you wherever I set my foot. But now, when will I do something for my own household too?"
30:31 Laban said, "What shall I give you?" Jacob said, "Give me nothing at all. If you will do this one thing for me, I will go back to grazing and guarding your flock.
30:32 Let me go through your whole flock today and take out of it every speckled and spotted sheep, every dark lamb, and the spotted and speckled goats. They will be my wages.
30:33 My honesty will answer for me later, when you come to look over my wages. Any goat that is not speckled or spotted, and any lamb that is not dark, if found with me, will count as stolen."
30:34 Laban said, "Good. Let it be as you say."
30:35 But that same day Laban removed the streaked and spotted male goats and all the speckled and spotted female goats, every one with white on it. He also took every dark one among the lambs, and he put them in the care of his sons.
30:36 And he set three days' travel between himself and Jacob, while Jacob tended what was left of Laban's flock.
## Jacob's striped branches
30:37 Then Jacob took fresh-cut branches of poplar, almond and plane trees and peeled white stripes in them, laying bare the white of the branches.
30:38 He set the peeled branches in the troughs, in the water channels where the flocks came to drink, right in front of the flocks. They mated when they came to drink.
30:39 So the flocks mated in front of the branches, and they gave birth to streaked, speckled and spotted young.
30:40 Jacob separated the lambs, and he made the flock face the streaked and all the dark animals in Laban's flock. He set his own herds apart by themselves and did not put them with Laban's flock.
30:41 Whenever the strong females were in heat, Jacob put the branches in the troughs in front of the flock's eyes, so that they would mate among the branches.
30:42 With the weaker animals, he did not set them out. So the feeble ones became Laban's, and the strong ones Jacob's.
30:43 The man grew very, very rich. He had large flocks, female and male slaves, camels and donkeys.
## Jacob decides to leave
31:1 Jacob heard what Laban's sons were saying: "Jacob has taken everything that was our father's. Out of what belonged to our father he has made all this wealth."
31:2 Jacob looked at Laban's face, and it was not toward him as it had been before.
31:3 Then the LORD told Jacob, "Return to the land of your fathers, to your own kin, and I will be with you."
31:4 Jacob sent for Rachel and Leah to come out to the field where his flock was.
31:5 He said to them, "I can see that your father's face is not toward me as before. But the God of my father has been with me.
31:6 You know that with all my strength I have served your father.
31:7 Yet your father has cheated me and changed my wages ten times. But God did not let him do me harm.
31:8 If he said, 'The speckled ones will be your wages,' then the whole flock gave birth to speckled ones. And if he said, 'The streaked ones will be your wages,' then the whole flock gave birth to streaked ones.
31:9 God has stripped your father of his livestock and given it to me.
31:10 Once, at the time when the flock was mating, I looked up and saw in a dream that the male goats mounting the flock were streaked, speckled and mottled.
31:11 The angel of God said to me in the dream, 'Jacob.' I said, 'Here I am.'
31:12 He said, 'Look up and see: all the male goats mounting the flock are streaked, speckled and mottled, because I have seen everything Laban is doing to you.
31:13 I am the God of Bethel, where you anointed a standing stone and made a vow to me. Now get up, leave this land, and go back to the land of your birth.'"
31:14 Rachel and Leah answered him, "Is there any share or inheritance left for us in our father's house?
31:15 Doesn't he think of us as foreigners? He sold us, and then he ate up our money completely.
31:16 For all the riches God has stripped from our father are ours and our children's. So now, whatever God has said to you, do it."
## Jacob runs away
31:17 Jacob rose and lifted his children and his wives up onto the camels.
31:18 He drove off all his livestock and all the goods he had gained, the livestock he had acquired in Paddan-aram, to go to Isaac his father in the land of Canaan.
31:19 Laban had gone to shear his sheep, and Rachel stole the household gods that belonged to her father.
31:20 And Jacob stole the heart of Laban the Aramean: he did not tell him that he was running away.
31:21 He ran away with all he had. He got up, crossed the River, and headed for the hill country of Gilead.
## Laban catches up
31:22 On the third day Laban was told that Jacob had run away.
31:23 He took his relatives with him and chased after him for seven days, and caught up with him in the hill country of Gilead.
31:24 But God came to Laban the Aramean in a dream at night and said to him, "Watch yourself. Do not speak with Jacob, whether good or bad."
31:25 Laban caught up with Jacob. Jacob had pitched his tent in the hill country, and Laban and his relatives pitched camp in the hill country of Gilead.
31:26 Laban said to Jacob, "What have you done? You stole my heart and carried my daughters off like prisoners taken by the sword.
31:27 Why did you run away in secret, and steal away from me, and not tell me? I would have sent you off with joy and songs, with drums and lyres.
31:28 You did not even let me kiss my sons and my daughters. Now you have acted like a fool.
31:29 It is in my power to do you harm. But the God of your father spoke to me last night and said, 'Watch yourself. Do not speak with Jacob, whether good or bad.'
31:30 And now, you just had to go, because you were homesick for your father's house. But why did you steal my gods?"
31:31 Jacob answered Laban, "Because I was afraid. I thought you would tear your daughters away from me.
31:32 Whoever you find with your gods will not live. In front of our relatives, search for whatever of yours is with me, and take it." Jacob did not know that Rachel had stolen them.
31:33 So Laban went into Jacob's tent, and Leah's tent, and the tent of the two slave women, but he did not find them. He came out of Leah's tent and went into Rachel's tent.
31:34 Rachel had taken the household gods and put them in the camel's saddle, and she was sitting on them. Laban felt through the whole tent, but he did not find them.
31:35 She said to her father, "Do not be angry, my lord, that I cannot stand up in front of you. The way of women is upon me." He searched but did not find the household gods.
31:36 Then Jacob grew angry and took Laban to task. Jacob said to Laban, "What is my crime? What is my sin, that you have come chasing after me so hotly?
31:37 You have felt through all my things. What have you found of all your household goods? Set it here in front of my relatives and your relatives, and let them decide between the two of us.
31:38 These twenty years I have been with you. Your ewes and your female goats have not lost their young, and I have not eaten the rams of your flock.
31:39 What wild animals tore I never brought to you. I took the loss myself. You demanded it from my hand, whether stolen by day or stolen by night.
31:40 This is how I was: by day the heat ate me up, and by night the frost, and sleep ran away from my eyes.
31:41 Twenty years I have had in your house. I served you fourteen years for your two daughters and six years for your flock, and you changed my wages ten times.
31:42 If the God of my father, the God of Abraham and the Fear of Isaac, had not been on my side, you would have sent me away empty-handed by now. God saw my misery and the hard work of my hands, and last night he gave his verdict."
## The covenant at Galeed
31:43 Laban answered Jacob, "The daughters are my daughters, the children are my children, and the flock is my flock. All that you see is mine. But what can I do today about these daughters of mine, or about the children they have borne?
31:44 Now come, let us cut a covenant, I and you, and it will stand as a witness between me and you."
31:45 So Jacob took a stone and set it up as a standing stone.
31:46 Jacob said to his relatives, "Gather stones." They took stones and made a heap, and they ate there by the heap.
31:47 Laban named it Jegar-sahadutha; Jacob named it Galeed.
31:48 Laban said, "This heap is a witness between me and you today." That is why it was named Galeed,
31:49 and also Mizpah, because he said, "May the LORD keep watch between me and you when we are out of each other's sight.
31:50 If you mistreat my daughters, or if you take wives besides my daughters, though no one is with us, see: God is the witness between me and you."
31:51 Laban said to Jacob, "Here is this heap, and here is the standing stone that I have set up between me and you.
31:52 This heap is a witness and the standing stone is a witness, that I will not pass beyond this heap to you, and you will not pass beyond this heap and this standing stone to me, to do harm.
31:53 The God of Abraham and the God of Nahor, may they judge between us, the God of their father." So Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.
31:54 Jacob offered a sacrifice on the mountain and called his relatives to eat bread. They ate bread and spent the night on the mountain.
31:55 Early in the morning Laban got up, kissed his sons and his daughters, and blessed them. Then Laban went and returned to his place.
## The camp of God
32:1 As Jacob went on his way, angels of God met him.
32:2 Jacob said when he saw them, "This is a camp of God!" And he called the name of that place Mahanaim.
## Jacob sends word to Esau
32:3 Jacob sent messengers before him to Esau his brother in the land of Seir, Edom's open country.
32:4 He gave them this order: "This is what you will say to my lord Esau: 'Your servant Jacob says this: I have lived as a guest with Laban and stayed on until now.
32:5 I have oxen and donkeys, flocks, male and female slaves. I have sent to tell my lord, hoping to find favor in your eyes.'"
32:6 The messengers returned to Jacob and said, "We came to your brother Esau. He is on his way to meet you, and four hundred men with him."
32:7 Jacob was very afraid and in anguish. He split the people with him, and the flocks, the herds and the camels, into two camps.
32:8 He thought, "If Esau comes to one camp and strikes it, the camp that is left can get away."
## Jacob's prayer
32:9 Then Jacob said, "God of my father Abraham and God of my father Isaac, LORD, you who told me, 'Go back to your land and your relatives, and I will do you good,'
32:10 I am too small for all the faithful love and all the faithfulness you have shown your servant. With only my staff I crossed this Jordan, and now I have become two camps.
32:11 Rescue me, please, from the hand of my brother, from the hand of Esau, because I am afraid of him. He may come and strike me down, mothers and children alike.
32:12 But you yourself said, 'I will certainly do you good and make your offspring like the sand of the sea, too many to count.'"
## A gift for Esau
32:13 He spent that night there. From what he had with him he chose a gift for Esau his brother:
32:14 two hundred female goats and twenty male goats, two hundred ewes and twenty rams,
32:15 thirty nursing camels with their young, forty cows and ten bulls, twenty female donkeys and ten male donkeys.
32:16 He handed them over to his servants, each herd by itself, and told his servants, "Go on ahead of me, and leave a gap between one herd and the next."
32:17 He ordered the first one, "When Esau my brother meets you and asks you, 'Whose are you? Where are you going? Whose are these animals in front of you?'
32:18 you will say, 'They are your servant Jacob's. It is a gift sent to my lord Esau. And look, he himself is behind us.'"
32:19 He gave the same order to the second and the third and to all who walked behind the herds: "You will speak to Esau in just this way when you find him.
32:20 And you will say, 'Look, your servant Jacob is behind us.'" For he thought, "I will cover his face with the gift that goes ahead of me, and afterward I will see his face. Maybe he will lift up my face."
32:21 So the gift went over ahead of him, while he himself spent that night in the camp.
## Wrestling at the Jabbok
32:22 He rose that night and took his two wives, his two slave women and his eleven children, and crossed at the ford of the Jabbok.
32:23 He took them and sent them across the stream, and he sent across everything he had.
32:24 Jacob was left alone. And a man wrestled with him until the dawn came up.
32:25 When the man saw that he could not overcome him, he touched the socket of his hip, and the socket of Jacob's hip came out of joint as he wrestled with him.
32:26 The man said, "Let me go, because the dawn has come up." But Jacob said, "I will not let you go unless you bless me."
32:27 He said to him, "What is your name?" He said, "Jacob."
32:28 He said, "No longer will your name be called Jacob, but Israel, for you have struggled with God and with humans and have won."
32:29 Then Jacob asked him, "Tell me your name, please." He said, "Why is it that you ask my name?" And he blessed him there.
32:30 Jacob called the name of the place Peniel: "For I have seen God face to face, and still my life was rescued."
32:31 As he crossed over Penuel the sun rose on him, and he limped because of his hip.
32:32 That is why, to this day, the people of Israel do not eat the sinew of the thigh that is on the hip socket, because he touched Jacob's hip socket at the thigh sinew.
## Jacob meets Esau
33:1 Jacob lifted his eyes and looked, and there was Esau coming, and four hundred men with him. So he split up the children among Leah, Rachel and the two slave women.
33:2 He put the slave women and their children in front, Leah and her children behind them, and Rachel and Joseph last.
33:3 He himself went on ahead of them and bowed down to the ground seven times, until he came near his brother.
33:4 But Esau ran to meet him, hugged him, fell on his neck and kissed him, and they wept.
33:5 Then Esau lifted his eyes and saw the women and children. "Who are these to you?" he asked. Jacob said, "The children with whom God has favored your servant."
33:6 Then the slave women came near, they and their children, and they bowed down.
33:7 Leah also came near with her children, and they bowed down. Last of all Joseph and Rachel came near, and they bowed down.
33:8 Esau said, "What do you mean by all this camp I met?" He said, "To find favor in the eyes of my lord."
33:9 Esau said, "I have plenty, my brother. Keep what is yours for yourself."
33:10 Jacob said, "No, please. If I have found favor in your eyes, take my gift from my hand. For truly, seeing your face is like seeing the face of God, now that you have received me kindly.
33:11 Please take my blessing that has been brought to you, because God has been gracious to me, and I have everything." He kept urging him, and Esau took it.
33:12 Then Esau said, "Let us set out and go. I will go alongside you."
33:13 But Jacob said to him, "My lord knows that the children are frail, and the sheep and cattle that are nursing are in my care. If they are driven hard for even one day, the whole flock will die.
33:14 Let my lord go on ahead of his servant, and I will move along slowly, at the pace of the animals in front of me and the pace of the children, until I come to my lord in Seir."
33:15 Esau said, "Then let me leave with you some of the people who are with me." But Jacob said, "Why do that? Just let me find favor in the eyes of my lord."
33:16 So that day Esau went back on his way to Seir.
## Succoth and Shechem
33:17 But Jacob set out for Succoth. He built himself a house there and made shelters for his livestock. That is why the place is named Succoth.
33:18 Jacob came safely to the city of Shechem in the land of Canaan, on his way from Paddan-aram, and he camped in front of the city.
33:19 He bought the piece of land where he had pitched his tent from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for a hundred qesitahs.
33:20 He set up an altar there and named it El-Elohe-Israel.
## The violation of Dinah
34:1 Dinah, Leah's daughter, whom she had borne to Jacob, went out to look at the daughters of the land.
34:2 Shechem, the son of Hamor the Hivite, who ruled the region, saw her. He took her and lay with her and dishonored her.
34:3 His soul clung to Dinah, Jacob's daughter. He loved the girl and spoke to the girl's heart.
34:4 Shechem said to Hamor his father, "Get me this girl as my wife."
34:5 Jacob heard that he had defiled Dinah his daughter. His sons were with his livestock in the field, so Jacob kept quiet until they came.
## Hamor's offer
34:6 Hamor, Shechem's father, went out to Jacob to speak with him.
34:7 Jacob's sons came in from the field when they heard it. The men were hurt and furious, because Shechem had done a disgraceful thing in Israel: he had lain with Jacob's daughter, and such a thing is not done.
34:8 Hamor spoke with them: "My son Shechem's soul is set on your daughter. Please give her to him as a wife.
34:9 Become in-laws with us. Your daughters you will give to us, and our daughters you will take for yourselves.
34:10 Live with us. The land will lie open before you. Live in it, move about in it, and get property in it."
34:11 Shechem said to her father and her brothers, "Let me find favor in your eyes, and whatever you tell me, I will give.
34:12 Make the bride price and gift as high as you like, and I will pay whatever you tell me. Only give me the girl as my wife."
## The brothers' trick
34:13 Jacob's sons answered Shechem and Hamor his father with trickery, speaking as they did because he had defiled Dinah their sister.
34:14 They said to them, "We cannot do this thing, give our sister to a man who has a foreskin. That would be a disgrace for us.
34:15 Only on this condition will we agree with you: that you become like us, and every male among you is circumcised.
34:16 Then our daughters we will give to you, and your daughters we will take for ourselves. We will live with you, and we will become one people.
34:17 But if you will not listen to us and be circumcised, we will take our daughter and go."
34:18 Their words seemed good to Hamor and to Shechem, Hamor's son.
34:19 The young man did not wait to do the thing, because he wanted Jacob's daughter. And he was the most honored of all his father's household.
34:20 So Hamor and Shechem his son came to the gate of their town and spoke to its men:
34:21 "These men are friendly toward us. Let them live in the land and move about in it. Look, the land is wide on both sides, with room for them. Let us take their daughters as wives and give them our daughters.
34:22 But only on this condition will the men agree to live with us and become one people: that every male among us be circumcised, as they are circumcised.
34:23 Their livestock, their goods and all their animals, will they not be ours? Only let us agree with them, and they will live with us."
34:24 All who went out of the city gate listened to Hamor and Shechem his son, and every male was circumcised, all who went out of the gate of his city.
## Simeon and Levi's revenge
34:25 On the third day, while they were in pain, two of Jacob's sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah's brothers, each took his sword. They came upon the city while it felt secure and killed every male.
34:26 They killed Hamor and Shechem his son with the edge of the sword. They took Dinah from Shechem's house and left.
34:27 Jacob's sons came upon the slain and looted the city, because they had defiled their sister.
34:28 They took their flocks and their herds and their donkeys, whatever was in the city and whatever was in the field.
34:29 All their wealth, all their little children and their wives they carried off as captives, and they looted everything in the houses.
34:30 Then Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, "You have stirred up trouble for me. You have made me stink among the people of the land, the Canaanites and the Perizzites. I have only a few men. If they gather against me and attack me, I will be destroyed, I and my household."
34:31 But they said, "Like a prostitute, is that how he should treat our sister?"
## Back to Bethel
35:1 God said to Jacob, "Get up, go up to Bethel, and live there. Make an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you were running away from Esau your brother."
35:2 Jacob told his household and everyone with him, "Put away the foreign gods in your midst. Make yourselves clean and change your clothes.
35:3 Let us get up and go up to Bethel. I will make an altar there to the God who answered me on the day of my trouble and has been with me on the road I have traveled."
35:4 They handed Jacob all the foreign gods in their hands, and the rings in their ears, and Jacob hid them under the oak that is by Shechem.
35:5 Then they set out, and a terror from God fell on the cities around them, so that no one chased the sons of Jacob.
35:6 Jacob came to Luz in the land of Canaan, that is, Bethel, he and all the people with him.
35:7 He built an altar there and named the place El-bethel, because it was there that God had revealed himself to him when he was running away from his brother.
35:8 Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died, and she was buried below Bethel under the oak. So it was named Allon-bacuth.
## God renames Jacob again
35:9 God appeared to Jacob again when he came from Paddan-aram, and blessed him.
35:10 God said to him, "Your name is Jacob. Your name will no longer be called Jacob. Israel will be your name." So he named him Israel.
35:11 God said to him, "I am God Almighty. Be fruitful and become many. A nation, and a gathering of nations, will come from you, and kings will come out of your loins.
35:12 The land I gave to Abraham and to Isaac I will give to you, and to your offspring after you I will give the land."
35:13 God went up and away from him, at the place where he had spoken with him.
35:14 Jacob set up a standing stone at the place where he had spoken with him, a standing stone of stone. He poured a drink offering on it, and he poured oil on it.
35:15 To the place where God had spoken with him, Jacob gave the name Bethel.
## Rachel dies giving birth
35:16 Then they set out from Bethel. While there was still some distance to go to Ephrath, Rachel went into labor, and her labor was hard.
35:17 While her labor was at its hardest, the midwife said to her, "Do not be afraid. This one too is a son for you."
35:18 As her life was going out of her, for she was dying, she named him Ben-oni. But his father called him Benjamin.
35:19 Rachel died, and they buried her on the way to Ephrath, which is Bethlehem.
35:20 Jacob set up a standing stone over her grave. It is the standing stone of Rachel's grave to this day.
## Reuben's sin and Jacob's twelve sons
35:21 Israel set out again and pitched his tent beyond Migdal-eder.
35:22 It was while Israel was living in that land: Reuben went and lay with Bilhah, his father's concubine, and Israel heard. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve.
35:23 Leah's sons: Reuben, the firstborn of Jacob, and Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar and Zebulun.
35:24 The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin.
35:25 The sons of Bilhah, Rachel's slave woman: Dan and Naphtali.
35:26 The sons of Zilpah, Leah's slave woman: Gad and Asher. These are the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Paddan-aram.
## Isaac dies
35:27 Jacob came to Isaac his father at Mamre, at Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac had lived as foreigners.
35:28 Isaac lived a hundred and eighty years.
35:29 Then Isaac breathed his last. He died and was gathered to his people, old and satisfied with days, and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
## Esau's family
36:1 This is the family history of Esau, that is, Edom.
36:2 Esau took his wives from the daughters of Canaan: Adah daughter of Elon the Hittite, and Oholibamah daughter of Anah, daughter of Zibeon the Hivite,
36:3 and Basemath daughter of Ishmael, sister of Nebaioth.
36:4 Adah bore Eliphaz to Esau, and Basemath bore Reuel.
36:5 Oholibamah bore Jeush, Jalam and Korah. These are Esau's sons who were born to him in the land of Canaan.
36:6 Esau took his wives, his sons and daughters, all the people of his household, his livestock, all his animals and all the goods he had gained in the land of Canaan, and he went to a land away from Jacob his brother.
36:7 Their possessions were too great for them to live together. The land where they were living as foreigners could not carry them both because of their livestock.
36:8 So Esau settled in the hill country of Seir. Esau is Edom.
## Esau's descendants in Seir
36:9 This is the family history of Esau, the father of Edom, in the hill country of Seir.
36:10 These are the names of Esau's sons: Eliphaz, son of Adah, Esau's wife; Reuel, son of Basemath, Esau's wife.
36:11 The sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam and Kenaz.
36:12 Timna was a concubine of Eliphaz, Esau's son, and she bore Amalek to Eliphaz. These are the descendants of Adah, Esau's wife.
36:13 These are the sons of Reuel: Nahath and Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah. These were the descendants of Basemath, Esau's wife.
36:14 These were the sons of Oholibamah, Esau's wife, daughter of Anah, daughter of Zibeon. She bore to Esau Jeush, Jalam and Korah.
## The chiefs of Edom
36:15 These are the clan chiefs among Esau's sons. The sons of Eliphaz, Esau's firstborn: Chief Teman, Chief Omar, Chief Zepho, Chief Kenaz,
36:16 Chief Korah, Chief Gatam, Chief Amalek. These are the chiefs of Eliphaz in the land of Edom. These are the descendants of Adah.
36:17 These are the sons of Reuel, Esau's son: Chief Nahath, Chief Zerah, Chief Shammah, Chief Mizzah. These are the chiefs of Reuel in the land of Edom. These are the descendants of Basemath, Esau's wife.
36:18 These are the sons of Oholibamah, Esau's wife: Chief Jeush, Chief Jalam, Chief Korah. These are the chiefs born of Oholibamah daughter of Anah, Esau's wife.
36:19 These are the sons of Esau, and these are their chiefs. He is Edom.
## The Horites of Seir
36:20 These are the sons of Seir the Horite, the people who lived in the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,
36:21 Dishon, Ezer and Dishan. These are the chiefs of the Horites, the sons of Seir, in the land of Edom.
36:22 The sons of Lotan were Hori and Hemam, and Lotan's sister was Timna.
36:23 These are the sons of Shobal: Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho and Onam.
36:24 These are the sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. This is the Anah who found the hot springs in the wilderness while he was grazing the donkeys of Zibeon his father.
36:25 These are the children of Anah: Dishon, and Oholibamah daughter of Anah.
36:26 These are the sons of Dishon: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran and Cheran.
36:27 These are the sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan and Akan.
36:28 These are the sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran.
36:29 These are the chiefs of the Horites: Chief Lotan, Chief Shobal, Chief Zibeon, Chief Anah,
36:30 Chief Dishon, Chief Ezer, Chief Dishan. These are the Horite chiefs, chief by chief, in the land of Seir.
## The kings of Edom
36:31 These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the people of Israel.
36:32 Bela son of Beor reigned in Edom. The name of his city was Dinhabah.
36:33 Bela died, and Jobab son of Zerah, from Bozrah, reigned in his place.
36:34 Jobab died, and Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.
36:35 Husham died, and Hadad son of Bedad, who struck down Midian in the country of Moab, reigned in his place. The name of his city was Avith.
36:36 Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place.
36:37 Samlah died, and Shaul from Rehoboth on the River reigned in his place.
36:38 Shaul died, and Baal-hanan son of Achbor reigned in his place.
36:39 Baal-hanan son of Achbor died, and Hadar reigned in his place. The name of his city was Pau, and his wife's name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, daughter of Me-zahab.
## The clan chiefs of Esau
36:40 These are the names of the chiefs who came from Esau, by their clans and their places, name by name: Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jetheth,
36:41 Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon,
36:42 Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,
36:43 Chief Magdiel, Chief Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom, by their settlements in the land they held. This is Esau, the father of Edom.
