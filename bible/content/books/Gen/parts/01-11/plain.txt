## The six days of creation
1:1 In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.
1:2 The earth was shapeless and empty. Darkness lay on the face of the deep, and God's Spirit fluttered over the face of the waters.
1:3 Then God said, "Let there be light." And light came to be.
1:4 God saw the light, that it was good, and God divided the light from the darkness.
1:5 God named the light Day and named the darkness Night. Evening came, and morning came: day one.
1:6 Then God said, "Let there be a dome in the middle of the waters, and let it divide waters from waters."
1:7 So God made the dome, and he divided the waters under the dome from the waters above the dome. And so it was.
1:8 God named the dome Sky. Evening came, and morning came: a second day.
1:9 Then God said, "Let the waters under the sky be gathered to one place, and let dry ground be seen." And so it was.
1:10 God named the dry ground Land, and the gathering of the waters he named Seas. And God saw that it was good.
1:11 Then God said, "Let the land sprout green growth: plants that make seed, and fruit trees on the land that bear fruit, each by its kind, with its seed inside it." And so it was.
1:12 The land brought out green growth: plants that make seed, each by its kind, and trees that bear fruit with their seed inside them, each by its kind. And God saw that it was good.
1:13 Evening came, and morning came: a third day.
1:14 Then God said, "Let lights appear in the sky's dome to keep the day apart from the night. They will be signs, and will mark the appointed times, the days and the years.
1:15 They will shine from the sky's dome to give light to the earth." And so it was.
1:16 God made the two big lights, the bigger light to govern the day and the smaller light to govern the night, and the stars.
1:17 God set them in the sky's dome to shine on the earth,
1:18 to govern by day and by night, and to keep the light apart from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.
1:19 Evening came, and morning came: a fourth day.
1:20 Then God said, "Let the waters swarm with swarming things that live, and let birds fly above the earth, over the face of the sky's dome."
1:21 So God created the great sea monsters, and all the living things that creep and swarm in the waters, each by its kind, and every bird with wings by its kind. And God saw that it was good.
1:22 God blessed them, saying, "Be fruitful, grow many, fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds grow many on the land."
1:23 Evening came, and morning came: a fifth day.
1:24 Then God said, "Let the land bring out living creatures, each by its kind: herd animals, creeping things and wild animals of the land, each by its kind." And so it was.
1:25 God made the wild animals of the land by their kind, the herd animals by their kind, and every creeping thing of the ground by its kind. And God saw that it was good.
1:26 Then God said, "Let us make humankind in our image, as our likeness. Let them rule over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the sky, over the herd animals and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creeps on the earth."
1:27 So God created the human in his image, / in God's image he created him, / male and female he created them.
1:28 God blessed them, and God said to them, "Be fruitful and grow many. Fill the earth and bring it under control. Rule over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the sky, and over every living thing that creeps on the earth."
1:29 Then God said, "Look, I give you every plant that makes seed on the face of the whole earth, and every tree that has seed-bearing fruit. They will be your food.
1:30 And to every wild animal of the earth, to every bird of the sky, and to everything that creeps on the earth, everything that has the breath of life in it, I give all the green plants for food." And so it was.
1:31 God looked at everything he had made, and there it was, very good. Evening came, and morning came: the sixth day.
## The seventh day
2:1 So the heavens and the earth were finished, and all their crowded host.
2:2 On the seventh day God finished the work he had been doing, and on the seventh day he rested from all the work he had been doing.
2:3 God blessed the seventh day and set it apart as holy, because on it he rested from all his work, all that God had created and made.
## The man in the garden
2:4 This is the family history of the heavens and the earth when they were created. On the day the LORD God made earth and heaven,
2:5 no bush of the field was yet on the earth, and no plant of the field had yet sprouted, because the LORD God had not sent rain on the earth, and there was no human to work the ground.
2:6 But a flow of water would rise from the earth and water the whole face of the ground.
2:7 Then the LORD God shaped the man from dust of the ground and blew into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living being.
2:8 The LORD God planted a garden in Eden, toward the east, and put there the man he had shaped.
2:9 Out of the ground the LORD God made every tree grow that is lovely to look at and good to eat, with the tree of life in the middle of the garden, and the tree of knowing good and evil.
2:10 A river goes out from Eden to water the garden, and from there it divides and becomes four streams.
2:11 The first is named Pishon. It circles the whole land of Havilah, where gold is found.
2:12 The gold of that land is good. Bdellium and onyx stone are there too.
2:13 The second river is named Gihon. It circles the whole land of Cush.
2:14 The third river is named Tigris. It flows east of Assyria. The fourth river is the Euphrates.
2:15 The LORD God took the man and settled him in the garden of Eden to work it and to guard it.
2:16 The LORD God gave the man an order: "You may eat, yes eat, from every tree of the garden.
2:17 But from the tree of knowing good and evil you must not eat, because on the day you eat from it you will die, yes die."
## A partner for the man
2:18 Then the LORD God said, "It is not good for the man to be by himself. I will make him a helper who is his match."
2:19 So out of the ground the LORD God shaped every wild animal of the field and every bird of the sky, and he brought them to the man to see what he would call each one. Whatever the man called each living creature, that became its name.
2:20 The man gave names to all the herd animals and to the birds of the sky and to every wild animal of the field. But for Adam no helper was found who was his match.
2:21 So the LORD God made a deep sleep fall on the man, and he slept. Then he took one of his ribs and closed up the flesh in its place.
2:22 The LORD God built the rib he had taken from the man into a woman, and he brought her to the man.
2:23 Then the man said, / "This one at last is bone from my bones / and flesh from my flesh. / This one will be called Woman, / because from Man this one was taken."
2:24 So a man leaves his father and his mother and sticks close to his wife, and they become one flesh.
2:25 The two of them, the man and his wife, were naked, and they felt no shame.
## The snake and the tree
3:1 Now the snake was shrewder than every wild animal of the field that the LORD God had made. He said to the woman, "Is it really true that God said, 'You must not eat from any tree of the garden'?"
3:2 The woman told the snake, "From the fruit of the garden's trees we may eat.
3:3 But from the fruit of the tree in the middle of the garden, God said, 'Do not eat from it, and do not touch it, so that you will not die.'"
3:4 But the snake said to the woman, "You will not die, not you!
3:5 No, God knows that on the day you eat from it your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God, knowing both good and evil."
3:6 The woman saw that the tree was good to eat, that it was a delight to the eyes, and that the tree was attractive for making a person wise. So she took some of its fruit and ate it, and she gave some to her husband too, who was there with her, and he ate.
3:7 The eyes of the two of them were opened, and they realized they were naked. They sewed fig leaves together and made themselves loincloths.
## Hiding from God
3:8 They heard the sound of the LORD God walking about in the garden in the breezy part of the day, and the man and his wife hid from the face of the LORD God among the trees of the garden.
3:9 The LORD God called to the man and said to him, "Where are you?"
3:10 He said, "I heard the sound of you in the garden, and I was afraid because I was naked, so I hid."
3:11 He said, "Who told you you were naked? The tree I commanded you not to eat from: have you eaten from it?"
3:12 The man said, "The woman! You set her at my side, and she is the one who gave me some from the tree, so I ate."
3:13 Then the LORD God said to the woman, "What have you done here?" The woman answered, "The snake fooled me, and I ate."
## The curses and the promise
3:14 The LORD God said to the snake, / "Because you have done this, / cursed are you more than all the herd animals / and more than all the wild animals of the field. / On your belly you will crawl, / and dust you will eat / all the days of your life.
3:15 I will put hostility between you and the woman, / and between your offspring and her offspring. / He will strike you on the head, / and you will strike him on the heel."
3:16 To the woman he said, / "I will make your pain and your pregnancies very great; / in pain you will give birth to children. / Your desire will be for your husband, / and he will rule over you."
3:17 To Adam he said, "Since you obeyed your wife's voice and ate from the tree about which I commanded you, 'Do not eat from it,' / the ground is cursed on your account. / In pain you will eat its yield / all the days of your life.
3:18 Thorn and thistle it will sprout for you, / and you will eat the plants of the field.
3:19 By the sweat of your face you will eat bread, / until you go back to the ground, / for you were taken from it. / For you are dust, / and to dust you will go back."
## Outside the garden
3:20 The man named his wife Eve, because she was the mother of everyone living.
3:21 The LORD God made Adam and his wife clothes out of skin and dressed them.
3:22 Then the LORD God said, "The man has now become like one of us, knowing good and evil. What if he stretches out his hand now, takes from the tree of life too, eats, and lives forever?"
3:23 So the LORD God sent him out of the garden of Eden to work the ground he had been taken from.
3:24 He drove the man out, and east of the garden of Eden he stationed the cherubim, and the flame of a sword that turned every way, to guard the way to the tree of life.
## Cain and Abel
4:1 The man knew Eve his wife, and she became pregnant and gave birth to Cain. She said, "I have gained a man, with the LORD."
4:2 Then she gave birth again, to his brother Abel. Abel became a shepherd of flocks, and Cain became a worker of the ground.
4:3 After some time, Cain brought some of the fruit of the ground as a gift to the LORD.
4:4 Abel for his part brought some of the firstborn of his flock and of their fat. The LORD looked kindly at Abel and at his gift,
4:5 but at Cain and his gift he did not look. Cain's anger burned hot, and his face fell.
4:6 The LORD said to Cain, "Why are you burning with anger? Why has your face fallen?
4:7 If you do well, will there not be a lifting up? But if you do not do well, sin is crouching at the door. Its desire is for you, but you must rule over it."
4:8 Cain said to Abel his brother, "Come, let's go out into the open country." And once they were out in the country, Cain turned on Abel his brother and killed him.
4:9 Then the LORD said to Cain, "Where is your brother Abel?" He said, "I don't know. Am I my brother's guard?"
4:10 The LORD said, "What have you done? Your brother's blood has a voice, and it is crying to me from the ground.
4:11 And now, cursed are you, away from the ground, which opened its mouth to take in your brother's blood from your hand.
4:12 When you work the ground, it will no longer give you its strength. You will be a homeless wanderer on the earth."
4:13 Cain said to the LORD, "My punishment is too heavy to carry.
4:14 Look, today you are driving me off the face of the ground, and I will be hidden from your face. I will be a homeless wanderer on the earth, and anyone who finds me will kill me."
4:15 Then the LORD said to him, "Therefore: anyone who kills Cain, sevenfold vengeance will follow." And the LORD set a sign for Cain, so that no one who came upon him would strike him.
4:16 Cain went out from before the LORD and lived in the land of Nod, east of Eden.
## Cain's family
4:17 Cain knew his wife; she conceived and bore Enoch. Cain was then building a city, and he called the city by his son's name, Enoch.
4:18 To Enoch was born Irad. Irad fathered Mehujael, Mehujael fathered Methushael, and Methushael fathered Lamech.
4:19 Lamech took two wives. One was named Adah, and the other was named Zillah.
4:20 Adah gave birth to Jabal. He was the ancestor of those who live in tents and keep herds.
4:21 His brother's name was Jubal. He was the ancestor of everyone who plays the lyre and the pipe.
4:22 Zillah, too, gave birth, to Tubal-cain, who hammered out every kind of tool from bronze and iron. Tubal-cain's sister was Naamah.
4:23 Lamech said to his wives, / "Adah and Zillah, listen to my voice; / you wives of Lamech, hear what I say: / a man I have killed for wounding me, / a boy for striking me.
4:24 For Cain will be avenged sevenfold, / but Lamech seventy and seven."
## Seth
4:25 Adam knew his wife again, and she gave birth to a son and named him Seth. "Because," she said, "God has set in place for me another offspring in place of Abel, since Cain killed him."
4:26 Seth too had a son born to him, and he named him Enosh. That was when people began to call on the LORD's name.
## From Adam to Noah
5:1 This is the book of the family history of Adam. On the day God created humankind, it was in God's likeness that he made them.
5:2 He created them male and female. He blessed them, and on the day they were created he gave them the name Humankind.
5:3 Adam lived 130 years and fathered a son in his likeness, by his image, and named him Seth.
5:4 After he fathered Seth, Adam's days were 800 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:5 All of Adam's days that he lived came to 930 years, and he died.
5:6 Seth lived 105 years and fathered Enosh.
5:7 After he fathered Enosh, Seth lived 807 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:8 All of Seth's days came to 912 years, and he died.
5:9 Enosh lived 90 years and fathered Kenan.
5:10 After he fathered Kenan, Enosh lived 815 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:11 All of Enosh's days came to 905 years, and he died.
5:12 Kenan lived 70 years and fathered Mahalalel.
5:13 After he fathered Mahalalel, Kenan lived 840 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:14 All of Kenan's days came to 910 years, and he died.
5:15 Mahalalel lived 65 years and fathered Jared.
5:16 After he fathered Jared, Mahalalel lived 830 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:17 All of Mahalalel's days came to 895 years, and he died.
5:18 Jared lived 162 years and fathered Enoch.
5:19 After he fathered Enoch, Jared lived 800 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:20 All of Jared's days came to 962 years, and he died.
5:21 Enoch lived 65 years and fathered Methuselah.
5:22 After he fathered Methuselah, Enoch walked with God 300 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:23 All of Enoch's days came to 365 years.
5:24 Enoch walked with God. Then he was gone, for God took him.
5:25 Methuselah lived 187 years and fathered Lamech.
5:26 After he fathered Lamech, Methuselah lived 782 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:27 All of Methuselah's days came to 969 years, and he died.
5:28 Lamech lived 182 years and fathered a son.
5:29 He named him Noah, saying, "This one will bring us relief from our work and from the painful labor of our hands, from the ground that the LORD cursed."
5:30 After he fathered Noah, Lamech lived 595 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
5:31 All of Lamech's days came to 777 years, and he died.
5:32 When Noah was 500 years old, Noah fathered Shem, Ham and Japheth.
## The sons of God and the daughters of humankind
6:1 When humankind began to grow many on the face of the ground, and daughters were born to them,
6:2 the sons of God saw that the daughters of humankind were good to look at, and they took wives for themselves from all of them, whomever they chose.
6:3 Then the LORD said, "My spirit will not stay in humans forever, since they are flesh. Their days will be 120 years."
6:4 In those days the Nephilim were on the earth, and later as well, whenever the sons of God went in to the daughters of humankind and they bore children to them. They were the heroes of ancient times, men with a name.
## The LORD's grief
6:5 The LORD saw how great the evil of humankind was on the earth, and that every plan their hearts shaped was nothing but evil all day long.
6:6 The LORD was sorry that he had made humankind on the earth, and it pained him to his heart.
6:7 So the LORD said, "I will wipe out the humankind I created from the face of the ground: people and animals, creeping things and birds of the sky, because I am sorry that I made them."
6:8 But Noah found favor in the LORD's eyes.
## Noah builds the ark
6:9 This is the family history of Noah. Noah was a righteous man; he was blameless in his generations. Noah walked with God.
6:10 Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham and Japheth.
6:11 The earth had gone to ruin in God's sight, and the earth was full of violence.
6:12 God looked at the earth, and there it was, ruined, because all flesh had ruined its way on the earth.
6:13 God said to Noah, "The end of all flesh is coming before me, because the earth is full of violence on account of them. Look, I am going to ruin them along with the earth.
6:14 Make yourself an ark of gopher wood. Make the ark with rooms in it, and coat it inside and outside with pitch.
6:15 This is how you will make it: the ark's length 300 cubits, its width 50 cubits, its height 30 cubits.
6:16 Give the ark a roof, finishing it off a cubit above. Set the ark's door in its side. Build it with three decks: lower, second and third.
6:17 As for me, I am going to bring the flood, water over the earth, to ruin all flesh that has the breath of life in it under the sky. Everything on the earth will die.
6:18 But with you I will set up my covenant. Into the ark you will go, you and your sons, your wife and your sons' wives with you.
6:19 And from every living thing, from all flesh, bring two of each kind into the ark, to keep them alive with you. They are to be male and female.
6:20 From the birds by their kind, from the herd animals by their kind, from every creeping thing of the ground by its kind, two of each will come to you to be kept alive.
6:21 Gather some of every food that is eaten and keep it by you; it will be food for you and for them."
6:22 Noah did this. He did everything just as God commanded him.
## Into the ark
7:1 The LORD said to Noah, "Come into the ark, you and your whole house, because you are the one I have seen to be righteous before me in this generation.
7:2 From every clean animal take seven and seven, a male and its mate, and from the animals that are not clean, two, a male and its mate.
7:3 From the birds of the sky too take seven and seven, male and female, to keep their offspring alive on the face of the whole earth.
7:4 For after seven more days I will bring rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights, and wipe every existing thing that I made off the face of the ground."
7:5 Noah did everything the LORD commanded him.
7:6 Noah was 600 years old when the flood came, water over the earth.
7:7 Noah went into the ark, and his sons and his wife and his sons' wives with him, because of the waters of the flood.
7:8 From the clean animals and from the animals that are not clean, from the birds and from everything that creeps on the ground,
7:9 two by two they came to Noah, into the ark, male and female, as God had commanded Noah.
## The flood comes
7:10 After the seven days, the waters of the flood came over the earth.
7:11 In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on that very day all the springs of the great deep burst open, and the windows of the sky were opened.
7:12 The rain poured on the earth forty days and forty nights.
7:13 On that same day Noah went into the ark, with Shem, Ham and Japheth, Noah's sons, and Noah's wife, and his sons' three wives with them.
7:14 They went in, and with them every wild animal by its kind, every herd animal by its kind, every creeping thing that creeps on the earth by its kind, and every bird by its kind, every bird, every winged thing.
7:15 They came to Noah, into the ark, two by two from all flesh that has the breath of life in it.
7:16 Those that went in were male and female from all flesh, as God had commanded him. Then the LORD shut the door behind him.
7:17 The flood lasted forty days on the earth. The waters grew and lifted the ark, and it rose high above the earth.
7:18 The waters swelled and grew very great on the earth, and the ark drifted on the face of the waters.
7:19 The waters swelled more and more over the earth, until all the high mountains under the whole sky were covered.
7:20 Fifteen cubits above them the waters swelled, and the mountains were covered.
7:21 All flesh that moved on the earth died: birds, herd animals, wild animals, all the swarming things that swarm on the earth, and all humankind.
7:22 Everything on dry land that had in its nostrils the breath of the spirit of life died.
7:23 He wiped out every existing thing on the face of the ground, from humans to animals, creeping things and birds of the sky; they were wiped off the earth. Only Noah remained, with whoever was with him in the ark.
7:24 The waters held the upper hand over the earth for 150 days.
## The waters go down
8:1 Then God remembered Noah, and all the wild animals and all the herd animals that were with him in the ark. God made a wind blow over the earth, and the waters calmed.
8:2 The springs of the deep and the windows of the sky were shut, and the rain from the sky was held back.
8:3 Little by little the waters drew back from the earth, and by the end of 150 days the waters had gone down.
8:4 In the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, the ark came to rest on the mountains of Ararat.
8:5 The waters kept going down until the tenth month. In the tenth month, on the first of the month, the tops of the mountains could be seen.
8:6 When forty days had passed, Noah opened the window he had made in the ark.
8:7 He sent out the raven, and it went out, going and coming back, until the waters were dried up off the earth.
8:8 Then he sent the dove out from him, to see whether the waters had shrunk from the face of the ground.
8:9 But the dove found no place to rest the sole of its foot, and it came back to him in the ark, because there was water on the face of the whole earth. He reached out his hand, took it, and brought it back to him into the ark.
8:10 He waited another seven days, and again he sent the dove out from the ark.
8:11 When the dove came back to him toward evening, there was a fresh-torn olive leaf in its mouth. So Noah knew the waters had dropped away from the earth.
8:12 He waited another seven days and sent out the dove, and it did not come back to him anymore.
8:13 In the six hundred and first year, in the first month, on the first of the month, the waters had dried from the earth. Noah took the covering off the ark and looked, and there was the face of the ground, drying.
8:14 In the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry.
## Out of the ark
8:15 Then God spoke to Noah:
8:16 "Go out of the ark, you and your wife, and your sons and your sons' wives with you.
8:17 Every living creature with you, of all flesh, birds and herd animals and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth, bring them out with you. Let them swarm over the earth and be fruitful and grow many on the earth."
8:18 Noah went out, and his sons, his wife and his sons' wives with him.
8:19 All the living creatures, all the creeping things, all the birds, all that moves on the earth, by their families they went out of the ark.
8:20 Noah built an altar to the LORD. He took some of every clean animal and every clean bird and offered whole burnt offerings on the altar.
8:21 The LORD smelled the soothing smell, and the LORD said in his heart, "I will not curse the ground any more because of humankind, though what the human heart shapes is evil from youth on. And I will not strike every living thing any more, as I have done.
8:22 From now on, as long as the earth lasts, / seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, / summer and winter, day and night / will not stop."
## God's covenant with Noah
9:1 God blessed Noah and his sons. He told them, "Be fruitful, grow many, fill the earth.
9:2 The fear and terror of you will be on every wild animal of the earth and every bird of the sky, on everything that creeps on the ground and all the fish of the sea. Into your hand they are given.
9:3 Everything that creeps and lives is yours for food. Just as with the green plants, I give you all of it.
9:4 Only, flesh with its life, its blood, you must not eat.
9:5 And for your own blood, your lives, I will demand a reckoning. From every animal I will demand it. And from humankind, from each person for the life of his brother, I will demand a reckoning for a human life.
9:6 Whoever spills a human's blood, / by a human his blood will be spilled, / for in God's image / he made humankind.
9:7 As for you, be fruitful and grow many. Swarm on the earth and grow many on it."
9:8 God spoke to Noah, and to his sons with him, saying,
9:9 "As for me, look, I am setting up my covenant with you and with your offspring after you,
9:10 and with every living creature that is with you, the birds, the herd animals and every wild animal of the earth with you, all that came out of the ark, every animal of the earth.
9:11 I will set up my covenant with you. All flesh will not again be cut off by the waters of the flood, and there will not again be a flood to ruin the earth."
9:12 God said, "Here is the sign of the covenant that I am putting between me and you and every living creature with you, for generations without end:
9:13 my bow I have put in the clouds, and it will be the sign of a covenant between me and the earth.
9:14 When I bring clouds over the earth and the bow is seen in the clouds,
9:15 then I will remember my covenant, which is between me and you and every living creature of all flesh, and the waters will never again become a flood to ruin all flesh.
9:16 When the bow is in the clouds, I will see it and remember the covenant that lasts forever between God and every living creature of all flesh on the earth."
9:17 God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant I have set up between me and all flesh on the earth."
## Noah's sons
9:18 Noah's sons who came out of the ark were Shem, Ham and Japheth; and Ham is the father of Canaan.
9:19 These three were the sons of Noah, and it was from them that the whole earth was peopled far and wide.
9:20 Noah, a man of the ground, was the first to plant a vineyard.
9:21 He drank some of the wine, became drunk, and uncovered himself in the middle of his tent.
9:22 Ham, Canaan's father, saw the nakedness of his father and told it to his two brothers outside.
9:23 Then Shem and Japheth took the cloak, put it on both their shoulders, and walked in backward. They covered their father's nakedness with their faces turned away, and they did not see their father's nakedness.
9:24 Noah woke up from his wine and found out what his youngest son had done to him.
9:25 He said, / "A curse on Canaan! / A slave of slaves / he will be to his brothers."
9:26 He also said, / "Blessed be the LORD, the God of Shem! / Let Canaan be his slave.
9:27 May God make room for Japheth, / and may he live in the tents of Shem. / Let Canaan be his slave."
9:28 Noah lived 350 years after the flood.
9:29 All of Noah's days came to 950 years, and he died.
## The nations
10:1 This is the family history of Noah's sons, Shem, Ham and Japheth. Sons were born to them after the flood.
10:2 Japheth's sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech and Tiras.
10:3 Gomer's sons were Ashkenaz, Riphath and Togarmah.
10:4 Javan's sons were Elishah and Tarshish, the Kittim and the Dodanim.
10:5 From these the coastland peoples branched out into their own lands, each with its own language, by their clans, in their nations.
10:6 Ham's sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put and Canaan.
10:7 Cush's sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah and Sabteca. Raamah's sons were Sheba and Dedan.
10:8 Cush fathered Nimrod. He was the first to become a hero on the earth.
10:9 He was a hero of the hunt in the LORD's sight. So people say, "A hero of the hunt in the LORD's sight, like Nimrod."
10:10 His kingdom started with Babel, Erech, Akkad and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.

## The tower of Babel
11:1 The whole earth had one language and one set of words.
11:2 As they journeyed from the east, people came on a plain in the land of Shinar and made their home there.
11:3 They said to one another, "Come on, let's mold bricks and bake them through." They had brick to use as stone and bitumen to use as mortar.
11:4 They said, "Come on, let's build ourselves a city, and a tower with its head in the heavens. We will win a name for ourselves, and not be scattered across the face of all the earth."
11:5 The LORD came down to see the city and the tower that the human race had built.
11:6 The LORD said, "Here they are, one people with one language for all of them, and this is what they have begun to do. From now on nothing they set out to do will be held back from them.
11:7 Come, let us go down and mix up their language there, so that no one will understand his neighbor's language."
11:8 The LORD scattered them from there across the face of all the earth, and they quit building the city.
11:9 That is how the place got the name Babel, because it was there that the LORD mixed up the speech of all the earth, and from there that the LORD scattered them over the face of all the earth.
## From Shem to Abram
11:10 This is the family history of Shem. Shem was 100 years old when he fathered Arpachshad, two years after the flood.
11:11 After he fathered Arpachshad, Shem lived 500 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
11:12 Arpachshad lived 35 years and fathered Shelah.
11:13 After he fathered Shelah, Arpachshad lived 403 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
11:14 Shelah lived 30 years and fathered Eber.
11:15 After he fathered Eber, Shelah lived 403 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
11:16 Eber lived 34 years and fathered Peleg.
11:17 After he fathered Peleg, Eber lived 430 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
11:18 Peleg lived 30 years and fathered Reu.
11:19 After he fathered Reu, Peleg lived 209 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
11:20 Reu lived 32 years and fathered Serug.
11:21 After he fathered Serug, Reu lived 207 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
11:22 Serug lived 30 years and fathered Nahor.
11:23 After he fathered Nahor, Serug lived 200 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
11:24 Nahor lived 29 years and fathered Terah.
11:25 After he fathered Terah, Nahor lived 119 years, and he fathered sons and daughters.
11:26 Terah lived 70 years and fathered Abram, Nahor and Haran.
## Terah's family
11:27 This is the family history of Terah. Terah fathered Abram, Nahor and Haran, and Haran fathered Lot.
11:28 Haran died while his father Terah was still alive, in the land where he was born, in Ur of the Chaldeans.
11:29 Abram and Nahor married. Abram's wife's name was Sarai, and Nahor's wife's name was Milcah daughter of Haran, father of Milcah and Iscah.
11:30 Now Sarai was barren: there was no child for her.
11:31 Terah took Abram his son, and Lot son of Haran, his grandson, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, the wife of Abram his son, and they went out with them from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan. They came as far as Haran and settled there.
11:32 Terah's days came to 205 years, and Terah died in Haran.
