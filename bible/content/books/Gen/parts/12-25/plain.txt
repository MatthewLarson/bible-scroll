## The call of Abram
12:1 The LORD said to Abram, "Go on your way, out of your land, away from your kin and from your father's house, to the land that I will show you.
12:2 I will make you a great nation. I will bless you and make your name great. And be a blessing!
12:3 I will bless those who bless you, and whoever treats you with contempt I will curse. All the clans of the earth will find blessing in you."
12:4 So Abram went, as the LORD had told him, and Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran.
12:5 Abram took his wife Sarai and his brother's son Lot, with all the property they had gathered and the people they had gained in Haran, and they set out for the land of Canaan. And they came to the land of Canaan.
## Altars across the land
12:6 Abram passed through the land as far as the place at Shechem, the great tree of Moreh. At that time the Canaanites were in the land.
12:7 The LORD appeared to Abram and said, "To your offspring I will give this land." So Abram built an altar there to the LORD, who had appeared to him.
12:8 From there he moved on to the hills east of Bethel and set up his tent, with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east. He built an altar to the LORD there and called on the name of the LORD.
12:9 Then Abram moved on, camp by camp, toward the Negev.
## Sarai in Pharaoh's house
12:10 There was a famine in the land, and Abram went down to Egypt to live there as a foreigner, because the famine lay heavy on the land.
12:11 As he came near to entering Egypt, he said to his wife Sarai, "Listen. I know that you are a woman beautiful to look at.
12:12 When the Egyptians see you, they will say, 'This is his wife.' Then they will kill me, but they will keep you alive.
12:13 Please say you are my sister, so that things will go well for me because of you, and I will stay alive thanks to you."
12:14 When Abram came into Egypt, the Egyptians saw that the woman was very beautiful.
12:15 Pharaoh's officials saw her and sang her praises to Pharaoh, and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's house.
12:16 Pharaoh treated Abram well for her sake. Abram came to own sheep and cattle, male donkeys, male and female slaves, female donkeys and camels.
12:17 But the LORD struck Pharaoh and his household with great plagues over the matter of Sarai, Abram's wife.
12:18 Pharaoh called for Abram and said, "What is this you have done to me? Why didn't you tell me she was your wife?
12:19 Why did you say, 'She is my sister,' so that I took her to be my wife? Now, here is your wife. Take her and go!"
12:20 Pharaoh gave his men orders about him, and they sent him on his way with his wife and everything he had.
## Back to Bethel
13:1 So Abram went up from Egypt into the Negev, he and his wife and everything he had, and Lot with him.
13:2 Abram was very heavy with wealth: livestock, silver and gold.
13:3 He traveled on by stages from the Negev as far as Bethel, to the place between Bethel and Ai where his tent had stood at the beginning,
13:4 the place of the altar he had made there at first. There Abram called on the name of the LORD.
## Abram and Lot part ways
13:5 Lot, who was traveling with Abram, also had flocks and herds and tents.
13:6 The land could not carry the two of them living together. Their property was so great that they could not stay together.
13:7 So quarrels broke out between the herders of Abram's livestock and the herders of Lot's livestock. The Canaanites and the Perizzites were living in the land at that time.
13:8 Abram said to Lot, "Please, let there be no fighting between me and you, or between my herders and your herders, for we are brothers.
13:9 Isn't the whole land in front of you? Please part from me. If you go left, I will go right. If you go right, I will go left."
13:10 Lot raised his eyes and saw that the whole Plain of the Jordan was well watered all the way to Zoar, like the garden of the LORD, like the land of Egypt. This was before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.
13:11 So Lot chose for himself the whole Plain of the Jordan, and Lot set out eastward. They parted from each other.
13:12 Abram settled in the land of Canaan, while Lot settled among the cities of the plain and moved his tent as far as Sodom.
13:13 Now the men of Sodom were evil, great sinners against the LORD.
## Look and walk
13:14 After Lot had parted from him, the LORD said to Abram, "Raise your eyes and look out from the place where you are, north and south, east and west.
13:15 All the land that you see I will give to you and to your offspring for all time.
13:16 I will make your offspring like the dust of the earth. If anyone could count the dust of the earth, then your offspring could be counted too.
13:17 Get up and walk through the land, its length and its width, for I will give it to you."
13:18 So Abram moved his tent and came to live by the great trees of Mamre, which are at Hebron. There he built an altar to the LORD.
## War of the kings
14:1 In the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam and Tidal king of Goiim,
14:2 these kings went to war against Bera king of Sodom, Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, that is, Zoar.
14:3 All these joined forces in the Valley of Siddim, which is the Salt Sea.
14:4 For twelve years they had served Chedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.
14:5 In the fourteenth year Chedorlaomer and the kings who were with him came and struck down the Rephaim in Ashteroth-karnaim, the Zuzim in Ham, the Emim in Shaveh-kiriathaim,
14:6 and the Horites in their hill country of Seir, as far as El-paran, which is by the wilderness.
14:7 Then they turned back and came to En-mishpat, that is, Kadesh, and struck down the whole country of the Amalekites, and also the Amorites who lived in Hazazon-tamar.
14:8 Then the king of Sodom, the king of Gomorrah, the king of Admah, the king of Zeboiim and the king of Bela, that is, Zoar, marched out and lined up for battle against them in the Valley of Siddim,
14:9 against Chedorlaomer king of Elam, Tidal king of Goiim, Amraphel king of Shinar and Arioch king of Ellasar: four kings against the five.
14:10 The Valley of Siddim was full of tar pits, pit after pit. When the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled, they fell there, and the rest fled to the hills.
14:11 The enemy took all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah and all their food, and went away.
14:12 They also took Lot, the son of Abram's brother, and his goods, and went away, for Lot was living in Sodom.
## Abram the Hebrew to the rescue
14:13 A man who had escaped came and told Abram the Hebrew. Abram was living by the great trees of Mamre the Amorite, the brother of Eshcol and of Aner, and these men were bound by treaty to Abram.
14:14 When Abram heard that his brother had been taken captive, he called out his trained men, born in his household, three hundred and eighteen of them, and chased the enemy as far as Dan.
14:15 He split up his forces against them by night, he and his servants, struck them, and chased them as far as Hobah, north of Damascus.
14:16 He brought back all the goods, and he also brought back his brother Lot and his goods, and the women and the people too.
## Melchizedek and the king of Sodom
14:17 After Abram came back from defeating Chedorlaomer and the kings who were with him, the king of Sodom came out to meet him at the Valley of Shaveh, that is, the King's Valley.
14:18 And Melchizedek king of Salem brought out bread and wine. He was a priest of God Most High.
14:19 He blessed Abram and said, "Blessed be Abram by God Most High, maker of heaven and earth.
14:20 And blessed be God Most High, who has handed your enemies over to you." And Abram gave him a tenth of everything.
14:21 The king of Sodom said to Abram, "Give me the people, and take the goods for yourself."
14:22 But Abram said to the king of Sodom, "I have raised my hand to the LORD, God Most High, maker of heaven and earth:
14:23 not a thread, not a sandal strap, not anything that is yours will I take, so that you will never say, 'I made Abram rich.'
14:24 Nothing for me. Only what the young men have eaten, and the share of the men who went with me, Aner, Eshcol and Mamre. Let them take their share."
## Count the stars
15:1 After these things, the word of the LORD came to Abram in a vision: "Do not be afraid, Abram. I am a shield for you. Your reward will be very great."
15:2 But Abram said, "Lord GOD, what will you give me? I go on childless, and the one who will own my household is Eliezer of Damascus."
15:3 And Abram said, "Look, you have given me no offspring, so a servant born in my house will be my heir."
15:4 Then the word of the LORD came to him: "This man will not be your heir. Someone who comes out of your own body, he will be your heir."
15:5 He brought him outside and said, "Look toward the sky and number the stars, if you can number them." Then he said to him, "So will your offspring be."
15:6 Abram put his trust in the LORD, and he reckoned it to him as righteousness.
## A covenant cut in the dark
15:7 He said to him, "I am the LORD, who brought you out from Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land to possess."
15:8 But Abram said, "Lord GOD, how will I know that I will possess it?"
15:9 He said to him, "Bring me a three-year-old heifer, a three-year-old female goat, a three-year-old ram, a turtledove and a young pigeon."
15:10 Abram brought him all these, cut them in two down the middle, and laid each half opposite the other. But he did not cut the birds in two.
15:11 Birds of prey came down on the carcasses, and Abram drove them away.
15:12 As the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell on Abram, and suddenly a terror, a great darkness, fell on him.
15:13 Then the LORD said to Abram, "Know for certain that your offspring will be foreigners in a land that is not theirs. They will serve its people, and they will be oppressed for four hundred years.
15:14 But I will judge the nation they serve, and afterward they will come out with great wealth.
15:15 But you yourself will go in peace to your fathers; you will be buried at a good old age.
15:16 In the fourth generation they will come back here, for the guilt of the Amorites is not yet full."
15:17 When the sun had gone down and it was dark, a smoking oven and a blazing torch passed between these pieces.
15:18 On that day the LORD cut a covenant with Abram, saying, "To your offspring I have given this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates:
15:19 the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites,
15:20 the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim,
15:21 the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites and the Jebusites."
## Sarai's plan
16:1 Sarai, Abram's wife, had borne him no children. But she owned a slave woman from Egypt named Hagar.
16:2 Sarai said to Abram, "Look, the LORD has held me back from having children. Go in to my slave woman. Maybe I can be built into a family through her." And Abram listened to Sarai.
16:3 So after Abram had lived ten years in the land of Canaan, Sarai, Abram's wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her slave woman, and gave her to her husband Abram as a wife.
16:4 He went in to Hagar, and she conceived. When she saw that she had conceived, her mistress became small in her eyes.
16:5 Sarai said to Abram, "This wrong done to me is on you! I myself laid my slave woman in your arms. Now she sees she has conceived, and I have become small in her eyes. Let the LORD judge between me and you."
16:6 Abram answered Sarai, "Look, your slave woman is in your hands. Do to her whatever looks good to you." So Sarai treated her harshly, and Hagar ran away from her.
## The God who sees
16:7 The angel of the LORD found her by a spring of water out in the desert, the spring beside the road to Shur.
16:8 He said, "Hagar, slave woman of Sarai, where have you come from, and where are you going?" She said, "I am running away from my mistress Sarai."
16:9 The angel of the LORD said to her, "Go back to your mistress, and bow yourself under her hand."
16:10 The angel of the LORD also said to her, "I will multiply and multiply your offspring, until they are too many to count."
16:11 Then the angel of the LORD said to her, "Look, you are with child, and you will bear a son. Call him Ishmael, because the LORD has listened to your suffering.
16:12 He will be a wild donkey of a man. His hand will be against all, and the hand of all against him, and he will live in the face of all his brothers."
16:13 She gave a name to the LORD who had spoken to her: "You are El-roi, God who sees." For she said, "Have I really seen here, after the One who sees me?"
16:14 That is why the well is called Beer-lahai-roi. It lies between Kadesh and Bered.
16:15 So Hagar bore Abram a son, and Abram gave the son Hagar bore him the name Ishmael.
16:16 Abram was eighty-six when Hagar bore Ishmael to him.
## God Almighty's covenant
17:1 When Abram was ninety-nine years old, the LORD appeared to him and said, "I am God Almighty. Go about your life in my sight, and be whole.
17:2 I will set my covenant between me and you, and I will make you very, very many."
17:3 Abram fell on his face, and God spoke with him. He said,
17:4 "For my part, look: my covenant is with you. You will become the father of a multitude of nations.
17:5 No longer will you be called Abram. Abraham will be your name, because I have made you father of a multitude of nations.
17:6 I will make you fruitful beyond measure. I will turn you into nations, and kings will come from you.
17:7 I will keep my covenant in force between me and you and your offspring after you, generation after generation, as a covenant that lasts forever: I will be God to you and to your offspring after you.
17:8 To you and your offspring after you I will give the land where you now live as a foreigner, all the land of Canaan, to hold forever. And I will be their God."
## The sign in the flesh
17:9 God said to Abraham, "As for you, you must keep my covenant, you and your offspring after you, generation after generation.
17:10 This is my covenant that you are to keep, between me and you and your offspring after you: every male among you is to be circumcised.
17:11 You are to cut away the flesh of your foreskin, and that will be the sign of the covenant between me and you.
17:12 Every male among you, generation after generation, is to be circumcised at eight days old. This includes the slave born in your household and the one bought with money from any foreigner who is not of your offspring.
17:13 Both the one born in your house and the one bought with your money must be circumcised. So my covenant will be in your flesh, a covenant that lasts forever.
17:14 Any uncircumcised male, whose foreskin has not been cut, will be cut off from his people. He has broken my covenant."
## Sarai becomes Sarah
17:15 God also said to Abraham, "As for Sarai your wife, do not call her Sarai. Sarah will be her name.
17:16 I will bless her, and from her too I will give you a son. I will bless her, and she will become nations; kings of peoples will come from her."
17:17 Abraham fell on his face and laughed. He said to himself, "Can a man a hundred years old have a son? Can Sarah, a woman of ninety, give birth?"
17:18 And Abraham said to God, "If only Ishmael could live on in your presence!"
17:19 God said, "No, your wife Sarah will bear you a son, and you will name him Isaac. I will keep my covenant in force with him, a covenant that lasts forever, for his offspring after him.
17:20 As for Ishmael, I have heard you. Look, I will bless him. I will make him fruitful and give him a great many descendants. He will father twelve chieftains, and I will make him a great nation.
17:21 But my covenant I will keep in force with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this season next year."
17:22 When he had finished speaking with him, God went up from Abraham.
## Circumcised that same day
17:23 That very day Abraham took his son Ishmael and all the slaves born in his household and all those bought with his money, every male among the men of Abraham's household, and he cut away the flesh of their foreskins, as God had told him.
17:24 Abraham was ninety-nine years old when the flesh of his foreskin was cut away,
17:25 and his son Ishmael was thirteen years old when the flesh of his foreskin was cut away.
17:26 On that very same day Abraham and his son Ishmael were circumcised.
17:27 All the men of his household, those born in the household and those bought with money from foreigners, were circumcised with him.
## Three visitors at noon
18:1 The LORD appeared to Abraham by the great trees of Mamre while he sat at the door of his tent in the heat of the day.
18:2 He raised his eyes and looked, and there were three men standing near him. As soon as he saw them, he ran from the door of the tent to meet them and bowed low to the ground.
18:3 He said, "My lord, if I have found favor with you, please do not pass your servant by.
18:4 Let a little water be brought, and wash your feet, and lean back under the tree.
18:5 Let me fetch a bit of bread so you can refresh your hearts. Then you can go on your way, since you have come by your servant." They said, "Do as you have said."
18:6 Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said, "Quick! Three measures of the best flour. Knead it and make round loaves."
18:7 Then Abraham ran to the herd, picked out a fine, tender calf and gave it to a young servant, who hurried to get it ready.
18:8 He brought curds and milk and the calf that had been prepared, and he set it all in front of them. He stood beside them under the tree while they ate.
## Sarah laughs
18:9 They asked him, "Where is Sarah your wife?" He said, "There, in the tent."
18:10 One of them said, "I will surely come back to you when the season comes round again, and Sarah your wife will have a son." Sarah was listening at the door of the tent, which was behind him.
18:11 Abraham and Sarah were old, far along in years, and Sarah no longer had the monthly periods of women.
18:12 So Sarah laughed inside herself. She thought, "After I am worn out, will I have pleasure, and my lord old too?"
18:13 The LORD said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh and say, 'Can I truly bear a child, when I am this old?'
18:14 Is anything too wonderful for the LORD? At the set time I will come back to you, when the season comes round, and Sarah will have a son."
18:15 But Sarah denied it. "I did not laugh," she said, because she was frightened. He said, "No. You laughed."
## Abraham pleads for Sodom
18:16 Then the men got up from there and looked out over Sodom, and Abraham walked along with them to see them off.
18:17 The LORD said, "Shall I hide from Abraham what I am about to do?
18:18 Abraham will surely become a great and strong nation, and all the nations of the earth will find blessing in him.
18:19 I have chosen him so that he will command his children and his household after him to keep the LORD's way by doing what is right and fair. Then the LORD can bring about for Abraham all that he has said about him."
18:20 Then the LORD said, "The outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great indeed, and their sin weighs very heavy.
18:21 I will go down and see whether their deeds are as bad as the outcry that has reached me. If not, I will know."
18:22 The men turned away from there and went toward Sodom, but Abraham was still standing before the LORD.
18:23 Abraham came near and said, "Will you really sweep away the righteous along with the wicked?
18:24 What if there are fifty righteous people inside the city? Will you really sweep it away? Will you not spare the place for the sake of the fifty righteous people in it?
18:25 You would never do such a thing, putting the righteous to death with the wicked, so that righteous and wicked fare alike. Never! Will the Judge of all the earth not do what is just?"
18:26 The LORD said, "If I find fifty righteous people inside the city of Sodom, I will spare the whole place for their sake."
18:27 Abraham spoke again: "Here I am, daring to speak to the Lord, though I am only dust and ashes.
18:28 Suppose the fifty righteous lack five. Will you wipe out the whole city because of five?" He said, "If I find forty-five there, I will not wipe it out."
18:29 Once more Abraham spoke to him: "Suppose forty are found there." He answered, "I will not do it, because of the forty."
18:30 Then he said, "Please do not let the Lord be angry if I speak. Suppose thirty are found there." He answered, "I will not do it if I find thirty there."
18:31 He said, "Here I am, daring to speak to the Lord. Suppose twenty are found there." He answered, "Because of the twenty I will not wipe it out."
18:32 He said, "Please do not let the Lord be angry if I speak just this once more. Suppose ten are found there." He answered, "Because of the ten I will not wipe it out."
18:33 When the LORD had finished speaking with Abraham, he went his way, and Abraham went back home.
## Two guests in Sodom
19:1 The two angels reached Sodom in the evening. Lot was sitting in the gate of Sodom. When he saw them, he got up to meet them and bowed with his face to the ground.
19:2 He said, "Please, my lords, turn aside to your servant's house. Spend the night and wash your feet. Then you can get up early and go on your way." "No," they said, "we will spend the night in the town square."
19:3 But he pressed them hard, so they turned aside with him and came into his house. He made them a feast and baked bread without yeast, and they ate.
19:4 Before they had gone to bed, the men of the city, the men of Sodom, young and old, all the people from every quarter, surrounded the house.
19:5 They shouted to Lot, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us so we can know them."
19:6 Lot went out to them at the doorway and shut the door behind him.
19:7 He said, "Please, my brothers, do not do this evil.
19:8 Look, I have two daughters who have not known a man. Let me bring them out to you, and you can do with them whatever looks good to you. Only do nothing to these men, since they have come under the shelter of my roof."
19:9 They said, "Get out of the way!" And they said, "This one came here to live as a foreigner, and now he keeps playing judge! Now we will treat you worse than them." They pushed hard against the man, against Lot, and came close to breaking down the door.
19:10 The men inside put out their hands, brought Lot in to them and closed the door.
19:11 Then they struck the men at the doorway of the house, from the smallest to the greatest, with blindness, so that they wore themselves out trying to find the door.
## Get out of this place
19:12 The men said to Lot, "Who else do you have here? A son-in-law, your sons, your daughters, anyone you have in the city, bring them out of this place.
19:13 We are about to destroy this place, because the outcry against its people has grown great before the LORD, and the LORD has sent us to destroy it."
19:14 So Lot went out and said to his sons-in-law, the men who were to marry his daughters, "Up! Leave this place, for the LORD is about to destroy the city." But his sons-in-law thought he was joking.
19:15 As dawn was breaking, the angels urged Lot on. "Up! Take your wife and the two daughters who are here, or you will be swept away when the city is punished."
19:16 But he lingered. So the men took hold of his hand and the hands of his wife and his two daughters, because the LORD had pity on him, and they led him out and left him outside the city.
19:17 As soon as they had brought them out, one of them said, "Run for your life! Do not look behind you, and do not stop anywhere in the plain. Run to the hills, or you will be swept away."
19:18 But Lot said to them, "Oh no, my lord, please!
19:19 Look, your servant has found favor with you, and you have shown me great kindness in saving my life. But I cannot escape to the hills. The disaster will catch me, and I will die.
19:20 Look, that town is near enough to run to, and it is only a small one. Please let me escape there. It is small, isn't it? Then my life will be saved."
19:21 He said to him, "Very well, I grant you this request too. I will not overthrow the town you speak of.
19:22 Hurry, run there, for I can do nothing until you get there." That is why the town was named Zoar.
## Fire from the sky
19:23 The sun had risen over the land when Lot reached Zoar.
19:24 Then the LORD rained sulfur and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah, from the LORD, out of the sky.
19:25 He overthrew those cities and the whole plain, with all who lived in the cities and everything that grew from the ground.
19:26 His wife, coming behind him, looked back, and she turned into a pillar of salt.
19:27 Early the next morning Abraham went to the place where he had stood before the LORD.
19:28 He looked down toward Sodom and Gomorrah and over all the land of the plain, and he saw smoke rising from the land like the smoke of a kiln.
19:29 So when God destroyed the cities of the plain, he remembered Abraham. He sent Lot out from the middle of the ruin as he overturned the cities where Lot had been living.
## Lot's daughters
19:30 Lot went up from Zoar and settled in the hills with his two daughters, because he was afraid to stay in Zoar. He lived in a cave, he and his two daughters.
19:31 The older daughter said to the younger, "Our father is old, and there is no man in the land to come in to us, as is the way of all the earth.
19:32 Come, let us get our father to drink wine, and let us lie with him, so that we can keep our father's family line alive."
19:33 That night they got their father to drink wine, and the older one went in and lay with her father. He did not know when she lay down or when she got up.
19:34 The next day the older said to the younger, "Look, last night I lay with my father. Let us get him to drink wine tonight as well. Then you go in and lie with him, so that we can keep our father's family line alive."
19:35 So that night too they got their father to drink wine, and the younger one went and lay with him. He did not know when she lay down or when she got up.
19:36 So the two daughters of Lot conceived by their father.
19:37 The older bore a son and named him Moab. He is the ancestor of the Moabites to this day.
19:38 The younger also bore a son and named him Ben-ammi. He is the ancestor of the Ammonites to this day.
## Sarah and Abimelech
20:1 Abraham moved on from there into the land of the Negev and lived between Kadesh and Shur. While he was staying as a foreigner in Gerar,
20:2 Abraham said of his wife Sarah, "She is my sister." So Abimelech king of Gerar sent for Sarah and took her.
20:3 But God came to Abimelech in a dream by night and said to him, "You are a dead man because of the woman you have taken. She is another man's wife."
20:4 Abimelech had not come near her. He said, "Lord, will you kill a nation even if it is innocent?
20:5 Did he not tell me himself, 'She is my sister'? And she too said, 'He is my brother.' I did this with a clear heart and clean hands."
20:6 God said to him in the dream, "Yes, I know you did this with a clear heart. In fact it was I who held you back from sinning against me. That is why I did not allow you to touch her.
20:7 Now give the man his wife back. He is a prophet, and he will pray for you, and you will live. But if you do not give her back, know that you will surely die, you and everyone who belongs to you."
20:8 Early in the morning Abimelech called all his officials and told them everything that had happened, and the men were terrified.
20:9 Then Abimelech called Abraham and said to him, "What have you done to us? How did I wrong you, that you have brought such great guilt on me and my kingdom? You have treated me in ways no one should ever be treated."
20:10 And Abimelech asked Abraham, "What were you thinking when you did this?"
20:11 Abraham said, "I told myself, 'There is no fear of God at all in this place, and they will kill me to get my wife.'
20:12 And anyway, she is in fact my sister, my father's daughter though not my mother's, and she became my wife.
20:13 When God sent me wandering from my father's house, I said to her, 'This is the loyalty you can show me: wherever we go, say of me, "He is my brother."'"
20:14 Then Abimelech took sheep and cattle, and male and female slaves, and gave them to Abraham, and he returned his wife Sarah to him.
20:15 Abimelech said, "Look, my land lies open before you. Live wherever you like."
20:16 To Sarah he said, "Look, I am giving your brother a thousand pieces of silver. Let it be a covering of the eyes for you before everyone who is with you. With everyone, you are set right."
20:17 Then Abraham prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, his wife and his slave women, and they bore children.
20:18 For the LORD had closed up every womb in Abimelech's household because of Sarah, Abraham's wife.
## Isaac is born
21:1 The LORD took note of Sarah as he had said, and the LORD did for Sarah what he had promised.
21:2 Sarah conceived and bore Abraham a son in his old age, at the very time God had told him.
21:3 Abraham named the son born to him, the son Sarah bore him, Isaac.
21:4 When his son Isaac was eight days old, Abraham circumcised him, as God had commanded him.
21:5 Abraham was a hundred years old when his son Isaac was born to him.
21:6 Sarah said, "God has made laughter for me. Everyone who hears will laugh with me."
21:7 And she said, "Who would have told Abraham that Sarah would nurse children? Yet here I am, having borne him a son in his old age."
## Hagar and Ishmael sent away
21:8 The child grew and was weaned, and on the day Isaac was weaned Abraham held a great feast.
21:9 Sarah saw that the son Hagar the Egyptian had borne to Abraham was laughing.
21:10 So she said to Abraham, "Throw out this slave woman and her son. The son of this slave woman will not share the inheritance with my son, with Isaac."
21:11 This troubled Abraham deeply, because it concerned his son.
21:12 But God said to Abraham, "Do not be troubled about the boy and your slave woman. Whatever Sarah tells you, listen to her, for it is through Isaac that offspring will be named for you.
21:13 Yet I will make a nation of the slave woman's son as well, because he is your offspring."
21:14 Abraham got up early in the morning. He took bread and a skin of water and gave them to Hagar, putting them on her shoulder, along with the boy, and sent her away. She went off and wandered in the desert of Beersheba.
21:15 When the skin had no water left, she cast the boy down under one of the bushes.
21:16 Then she went and sat down by herself about a bowshot away, for she said, "I cannot watch the boy die." As she sat there, she lifted up her voice and wept.
21:17 God heard the boy's voice, and the angel of God called to Hagar from the sky. "What is wrong, Hagar?" he said. "Do not be afraid, for God has heard the boy's voice where he is.
21:18 Get up, lift the boy, and hold him firmly by the hand, for I will make him a great nation."
21:19 God opened her eyes, and she caught sight of a well. She went, filled the skin and let the boy drink.
21:20 God was with the boy as he grew up. He lived in the desert and became a bowman.
21:21 He lived in the desert of Paran, and his mother found him a wife from the land of Egypt.
## The oath at Beersheba
21:22 About that time Abimelech, with Phicol his army commander, spoke to Abraham: "God is with you in all you do.
21:23 So swear to me here by God that you will not betray me or my children or my descendants. Deal with me and with the land where you are living as a foreigner with the same loyalty I have dealt to you."
21:24 Abraham said, "I swear it."
21:25 Then Abraham took Abimelech to task about a well of water that Abimelech's servants had taken by force.
21:26 Abimelech said, "I do not know who did this. You never told me, and I have not heard of it until today."
21:27 So Abraham took sheep and cattle and gave them to Abimelech, and the two of them made a covenant.
21:28 Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock apart by themselves.
21:29 Abimelech asked Abraham, "What do these seven ewe lambs mean, that you have set apart by themselves?"
21:30 He said, "Take these seven ewe lambs from my hand as a witness for me that I dug this well."
21:31 That is why the place was called Beersheba, because there the two of them swore an oath.
21:32 So they made a covenant at Beersheba. Then Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, got up and went back to the land of the Philistines.
21:33 Abraham planted a tamarisk tree at Beersheba, and there he called on the name of the LORD, the Everlasting God.
21:34 And Abraham lived as a foreigner in the land of the Philistines for a long time.
## God tests Abraham
22:1 Some time after these things, God put Abraham to the test. He said to him, "Abraham!" And he answered, "Here I am."
22:2 God said, "Take your son, please, your only one, whom you love, Isaac, and go to the land of Moriah. There offer him up as a burnt offering on one of the mountains that I will point out to you."
22:3 So Abraham got up early in the morning and saddled his donkey. He took two of his young servants with him, and his son Isaac. He split wood for the burnt offering, and then he set out for the place God had told him about.
22:4 On the third day Abraham raised his eyes and saw the place far off.
22:5 Abraham said to his servants, "Stay here, you two, with the donkey. The boy and I will go over there. We will worship, and then we will come back to you."
22:6 Abraham took the wood for the burnt offering and laid it on his son Isaac, and in his own hand he took the fire and the knife. And the two of them walked on together.
22:7 Isaac spoke to his father Abraham: "Father!" He answered, "Here I am, my son." Isaac said, "Here are the fire and the wood, but where is the lamb for a burnt offering?"
22:8 Abraham said, "God will see to the lamb for a burnt offering himself, my son." And the two of them walked on together.
## The binding of Isaac
22:9 They came to the place God had told him about. There Abraham built the altar and laid the wood in order. He bound his son Isaac and put him on the altar, on top of the wood.
22:10 Abraham stretched out his hand and took hold of the knife to slaughter his son.
22:11 But the angel of the LORD called to him from the sky: "Abraham! Abraham!" He answered, "Here I am."
22:12 He said, "Do not lay your hand on the boy. Do nothing to him. Now I know that you fear God, since you have not held back your son, your only one, from me."
22:13 Abraham raised his eyes and looked, and there was a ram behind him, its horns caught in the thicket. Abraham went and took the ram and offered it up as a burnt offering in place of his son.
22:14 Abraham named that place "The LORD Will See." So it is said to this day, "On the mountain of the LORD it will be seen."
## The oath
22:15 The angel of the LORD called to Abraham a second time from the sky
22:16 and said, "By myself I swear, says the LORD: because you have done this and have not held back your son, your only one,
22:17 I will bless you richly and make your offspring as many as the stars in the sky and the sand on the seashore. Your offspring will take over the gate of his enemies.
22:18 And all the nations of the earth will bless themselves by your offspring, because you have listened to my voice."
22:19 Then Abraham went back to his servants, and they set off together for Beersheba. And Abraham lived at Beersheba.
## Nahor's family
22:20 Some time after these things, Abraham was told, "Milcah too has borne children to your brother Nahor:
22:21 Uz his firstborn, Buz his brother, Kemuel the father of Aram,
22:22 Kesed, Hazo, Pildash, Jidlaph and Bethuel."
22:23 Bethuel became the father of Rebekah. Milcah bore these eight to Nahor, Abraham's brother.
22:24 His concubine, whose name was Reumah, also bore children: Tebah, Gaham, Tahash and Maacah.
## Sarah dies
23:1 The life of Sarah came to a hundred and twenty-seven years. These were the years of Sarah's life.
23:2 Sarah died at Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan, and Abraham came to mourn for Sarah and to weep for her.
## A grave bought at full price
23:3 Then Abraham rose from beside his dead and spoke to the sons of Heth:
23:4 "I am a foreigner living among you as a settler. Sell me a piece of ground among you to own as a grave, so that I can bury my dead out of my sight."
23:5 The sons of Heth answered Abraham,
23:6 "Hear us, my lord. You are a prince of God among us. Bury your dead in the best of our graves. Not one of us will refuse you his grave for burying your dead."
23:7 Abraham rose and bowed low to the people of the land, the sons of Heth.
23:8 He said to them, "If you are willing for me to bury my dead out of my sight, hear me and put in a word for me with Ephron son of Zohar.
23:9 Let him sell me the cave of Machpelah that belongs to him, at the edge of his field. Let him sell it to me among you at its full price in silver, as a burial ground of my own."
23:10 Ephron was sitting there among the sons of Heth. Ephron the Hittite answered Abraham in the hearing of the sons of Heth, of all who went in at the gate of his city:
23:11 "No, my lord, hear me. The field I give to you, and the cave in it I give to you too. Before the eyes of my people I give it to you. Bury your dead."
23:12 Abraham bowed low before the people of the land.
23:13 He spoke to Ephron in the hearing of the people of the land: "If only you would hear me! I am paying the price of the field. Accept it from me, so that I may bury my dead there."
23:14 Ephron answered Abraham,
23:15 "My lord, hear me. Land worth four hundred shekels of silver, what is that between you and me? Bury your dead."
23:16 Abraham agreed with Ephron. He weighed out for Ephron the amount of silver he had named, so that the sons of Heth heard it: four hundred shekels of silver, at the merchants' standard weight.
23:17 So Ephron's field at Machpelah, facing Mamre, passed into Abraham's hands: the field and the cave in it, and every tree in the field within its borders all around.
23:18 It became Abraham's property before the eyes of the sons of Heth, of all who went in at the gate of his city.
23:19 After that Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the cave of the field of Machpelah facing Mamre, that is, Hebron, in the land of Canaan.
23:20 So the field and the cave in it passed from the sons of Heth to Abraham, as his own burial ground.
## A wife for Isaac
24:1 Abraham was now old, well on in years, and the LORD had blessed him in every way.
24:2 Abraham said to his servant, the oldest of his household, who was in charge of everything he owned, "Put your hand under my thigh.
24:3 I want you to swear by the LORD, God of the heavens and God of the earth, that you will not take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites I live among.
24:4 Instead, go to my own land and my own relatives and take a wife for my son Isaac."
24:5 The servant asked him, "What if the woman does not want to come back with me to this land? Should I then take your son back to the land you came from?"
24:6 Abraham told him, "Be careful never to take my son back there.
24:7 The LORD, God of the heavens, who took me from my father's house and the land of my birth, who spoke to me and swore to me, 'To your offspring I will give this land,' he will send his angel ahead of you, and from there you will take a wife for my son.
24:8 If the woman does not want to come with you, you will be free of this oath of mine. Only do not take my son back there."
24:9 So the servant put his hand under the thigh of Abraham his master and swore to him about this matter.
## At the well
24:10 The servant took ten of his master's camels and set off, with all kinds of good things from his master in his hand. He traveled to Aram-naharaim, to the town of Nahor.
24:11 He made the camels kneel by the well outside the town. It was evening, the hour when the women come out to draw water.
24:12 He prayed, "LORD, God of my master Abraham, let it happen for me today. Show loyal love to my master Abraham.
24:13 Here I stand by the spring, and the daughters of the townspeople are coming out to draw water.
24:14 I will say to a young woman, 'Tip your jar, please, so I can drink.' If she answers, 'Drink, and I will water your camels as well,' let her be the one you have picked out for your servant Isaac. By this I will know that you have shown loyal love to my master."
24:15 Before he had even finished speaking, out came Rebekah with her jar on her shoulder. She was the daughter of Bethuel son of Milcah, the wife of Abraham's brother Nahor.
24:16 The young woman was very beautiful to look at, a virgin whom no man had been with. She went down to the spring, filled her jar and climbed back up.
24:17 The servant hurried toward her. "Please," he said, "let me sip a little water from your jar."
24:18 "Drink, my lord," she said. Quickly she lowered her jar onto her hand and gave him a drink.
24:19 When he had drunk all he wanted, she said, "I will draw for your camels as well, until they have had enough."
24:20 She quickly emptied her jar into the trough and ran back to the well to draw more, and she drew water for all his camels.
24:21 The man stood staring at her without a word, waiting to learn whether the LORD had made his journey succeed or not.
24:22 When the camels had finished drinking, the man brought out a gold ring weighing a beka, half a shekel, and two gold bracelets for her wrists weighing ten shekels.
24:23 He asked, "Tell me, please, whose daughter you are. Does your father's house have space for us to stay the night?"
24:24 She told him, "My father is Bethuel, whom Milcah bore to Nahor."
24:25 She added, "We have plenty of straw and feed, and space to stay the night too."
24:26 The man bowed his head and worshiped the LORD.
24:27 He said, "Blessed be the LORD, God of my master Abraham, who has not stopped showing his loyal love and faithfulness to my master. As for me, the LORD has led me on the road to the house of my master's relatives."
## In Laban's house
24:28 The young woman ran and told her mother's household all about it.
24:29 Rebekah had a brother named Laban. Laban ran out to the man at the spring.
24:30 As soon as he had seen the ring and the bracelets on his sister's wrists, and had heard his sister Rebekah say, "This is what the man said to me," he went out to the man. There he was, standing by the camels at the spring.
24:31 Laban said, "Come in, you whom the LORD has blessed! Why stand out here? I have cleared the house and made room for the camels."
24:32 So the man came to the house. The camels' loads were taken off, and straw and feed were brought for them, and water for him and the men with him to wash their feet.
24:33 Food was set before him, but he said, "I will not eat until I have told my errand." Laban said, "Tell it."
24:34 He said, "I am Abraham's servant.
24:35 The LORD has blessed my master richly, and he has grown rich. The LORD has given him flocks and herds, silver and gold, male and female slaves, camels and donkeys.
24:36 Sarah my master's wife bore my master a son when she was old, and he has given that son everything he owns.
24:37 My master put me under oath. He said, 'You are not to take a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites whose land I live in.
24:38 No, go to my father's house and my own family, and take a wife for my son there.'
24:39 I said to my master, 'Perhaps the woman will not follow me.'
24:40 He told me, 'The LORD, in whose sight I have lived, will send his angel along with you and give you success on the road. You will take a wife for my son from my family and my father's house.
24:41 Then you will be free of my oath, once you have gone to my family. If they will not give her to you, you will be free of my oath.'
24:42 Today I came to the spring and prayed, 'LORD, God of my master Abraham, if you will make the road I am traveling succeed,
24:43 here I stand by the spring. Let the young woman who comes out to draw water, to whom I say, "Please give me a little water from your jar to drink,"
24:44 and who says to me, "Drink, and I will draw for your camels as well," be the woman the LORD has picked out for my master's son.'
24:45 I had not yet finished saying this in my heart when out came Rebekah with her jar on her shoulder. She went down to the spring and drew water, and I asked her, 'Please give me a drink.'
24:46 At once she lowered her jar from her shoulder and said, 'Drink, and I will give your camels a drink as well.' I drank, and she watered the camels as well.
24:47 Then I asked her, 'Whose daughter are you?' She said, 'Bethuel's, the son Milcah bore to Nahor.' So I put the ring on her nose and the bracelets on her wrists.
24:48 I bowed my head and worshiped the LORD. I blessed the LORD, God of my master Abraham, who had guided me by the right road to take the daughter of my master's relative for his son.
24:49 So now, if you will show loyal love and faithfulness to my master, tell me. If not, tell me, so that I know which way to turn."
24:50 Laban and Bethuel answered, "This has come from the LORD. We can say nothing to you, bad or good.
24:51 Here is Rebekah in front of you. Take her and go, and let her become the wife of your master's son, as the LORD has said."
24:52 When Abraham's servant heard what they said, he bowed down to the ground before the LORD.
24:53 Then the servant brought out silver and gold jewelry and clothes and gave them to Rebekah. To her brother and her mother he gave rich presents.
## "I will go"
24:54 Then he and the men with him ate and drank and stayed the night. When they got up in the morning, he said, "Send me back to my master."
24:55 But her brother and her mother said, "Let the girl stay with us some days, say ten. Then she can go."
24:56 He said to them, "Do not hold me back, now that the LORD has made my journey succeed. Send me off, so I can go to my master."
24:57 They said, "Let us call the girl and ask her what she says."
24:58 They called Rebekah and asked her, "Will you go with this man?" She said, "I will go."
24:59 So they sent off their sister Rebekah with her nurse, and Abraham's servant and his men.
24:60 They blessed Rebekah and said to her, "Our sister, may you become / thousands of ten thousands, / and may your offspring take over / the gate of those who hate him!"
24:61 Then Rebekah and her young women got up, mounted the camels and followed the man. So the servant took Rebekah and left.
## Isaac meets Rebekah
24:62 Now Isaac had come from the way to Beer-lahai-roi, for he was living in the land of the Negev.
24:63 Isaac had gone out toward evening to muse in the field. He looked up and saw camels coming.
24:64 Rebekah looked up too, and when she saw Isaac she slipped down from the camel.
24:65 She asked the servant, "Who is that man coming across the field toward us?" The servant said, "That is my master." So she took her veil and covered her face.
24:66 The servant gave Isaac a full account of everything he had done.
24:67 Then Isaac brought her into the tent of his mother Sarah. He took Rebekah, and she became his wife, and he loved her. So Isaac was comforted after his mother.
## The sons of Keturah
25:1 Abraham took another wife, and her name was Keturah.
25:2 She bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak and Shuah.
25:3 Jokshan fathered Sheba and Dedan. The sons of Dedan were the Asshurim, the Letushim and the Leummim.
25:4 The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida and Eldaah. All these were the descendants of Keturah.
25:5 Abraham handed over all he had to Isaac.
25:6 To the sons of his concubines Abraham made gifts. Then, while he was still living, he sent them off east, away from his son Isaac, to the land of the East.
## The death of Abraham
25:7 The days of the years of Abraham's life came to a hundred and seventy-five years.
25:8 Then Abraham breathed his last and died at a good old age, old and satisfied, and he was gathered to his people.
25:9 His sons Isaac and Ishmael laid him to rest in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron son of Zohar the Hittite, which faces Mamre.
25:10 It was the field Abraham had bought from the sons of Heth. Abraham and his wife Sarah were buried there.
25:11 When Abraham was dead, God blessed Isaac his son. Isaac made his home by Beer-lahai-roi.
## The line of Ishmael
25:12 This is the family history of Ishmael, Abraham's son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah's slave woman, bore to Abraham.
25:13 These are the names of the sons of Ishmael, by their names in the order of their births: Nebaioth, Ishmael's firstborn; then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,
25:14 Mishma, Dumah, Massa,
25:15 Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish and Kedemah.
25:16 These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names by their villages and their camps: twelve chieftains by their clans.
25:17 These are the years of Ishmael's life: a hundred and thirty-seven years. He breathed his last and died, and was gathered to his people.
25:18 They lived from Havilah to Shur, which faces Egypt, in the direction of Asshur. He settled in the face of all his brothers.
## The birth of Esau and Jacob
25:19 This is the family history of Isaac, Abraham's son. Abraham fathered Isaac.
25:20 Isaac was forty years old when he took Rebekah as his wife, the daughter of Bethuel the Aramean of Paddan-aram, the sister of Laban the Aramean.
25:21 Isaac pleaded with the LORD for his wife, because she was barren. The LORD answered his plea, and his wife Rebekah conceived.
25:22 The children crushed against each other inside her, and she said, "If it is like this, why am I alive?" So she went to ask the LORD.
25:23 The LORD said to her, "Two nations are in your womb, / and two peoples will divide from your body. / One people will be stronger than the other, / and the elder will serve the younger."
25:24 When her days to give birth were full, there were twins in her womb.
25:25 The first one out was red, covered all over like a cloak of hair, and they named him Esau.
25:26 Next his brother came out with his hand gripping Esau's heel, and he was named Jacob. Isaac was sixty years old when she bore them.
## The birthright sold
25:27 The boys grew up. Esau knew how to hunt and was a man of the wild, while Jacob was a quiet man who stayed among the tents.
25:28 Isaac loved Esau, because he had a taste for game, but Rebekah loved Jacob.
25:29 One day Jacob was boiling a stew when Esau came in from the wild, worn out.
25:30 Esau said to Jacob, "Let me gulp down some of that red stuff, this red stuff, for I am worn out." That is why he was called Edom.
25:31 Jacob said, "Sell me your birthright first, today."
25:32 Esau said, "Here I am, about to die. What good to me is a birthright?"
25:33 Jacob said, "Swear to me first, today." So he swore to him and sold his birthright to Jacob.
25:34 Then Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. He ate and drank, got up and went his way. So Esau despised the birthright.
