## Judith the widow
8:1 In those days Judith heard about it. She was the daughter of Merari son of Ox son of Joseph son of Oziel son of Elkiah son of Elihu son of Hilkiah son of Eliab son of Nathanael son of Salamiel son of Sarasadai son of Israel.
8:2 Her husband Manasseh was of her own tribe and clan. He had died in the days of the barley harvest.
8:3 He was standing over the men who were binding sheaves in the field, and the burning heat came down on his head. He fell onto his bed and died in his town of Bethulia. They buried him with his ancestors in the field between Dothan and Balamon.
8:4 Judith had lived as a widow in her house for three years and four months.
8:5 She had made herself a tent on the roof of her house. She put sackcloth around her waist and wore her widow's clothes.
8:6 She fasted every day of her widowhood, except the days before the Sabbaths and the Sabbaths, the days before the new moons and the new moons, and the feasts and joyful days of the house of Israel.
8:7 She was beautiful, and very lovely to look at. Her husband Manasseh had left her gold and silver, male and female slaves, livestock and fields, and she stayed on and managed them.
8:8 No one spoke a bad word against her, for she feared God greatly.
## Judith calls the elders to account
8:9 Judith heard the hostile words the people had spoken against their ruler, because they had lost heart for lack of water. She also heard everything Uzziah had said to them, how he had sworn to them that he would hand the town over to the Assyrians after five days.
8:10 So she sent her maid, who was in charge of everything she owned, and called Chabris and Charmis, the elders of her town.
8:11 They came to her, and she said to them, "Listen to me, you rulers of the people of Bethulia. What you said to the people today was not right. You set up this oath between God and yourselves, and you said you would hand the town over to our enemies unless the Lord turns and helps you within those days.
8:12 Now who are you, to put God to the test today, and to stand in God's place among human beings?
8:13 Now you are cross-examining the Lord Almighty, and you will never find out anything, ever!
8:14 You cannot find the bottom of a human heart, and you cannot grasp the thoughts of a person's mind. How will you search out God, who made all these things? How will you know his mind or understand his thinking? No, my brothers, do not make the Lord our God angry.
8:15 If he does not choose to help us within the five days, he has the power to shelter us on any days he wants, or even to destroy us in front of our enemies.
8:16 Do not demand a pledge for the plans of the Lord our God. For God is not like a man, to be threatened, or like a son of man, to be bargained with.
8:17 So as we wait for him to save us, let us call on him for help. He will listen to us, if he is pleased to.
8:18 For in our generations there has not been, and there is not today, any tribe or clan or district or town of ours that bows down to gods made by hands, as there was in former days.
8:19 That was why our ancestors were handed over to the sword and to plunder, and fell with a great fall before our enemies.
8:20 But we know no other god but him. So we hope that he will not look down on us or on any of our people.
8:21 For if we are captured, all Judea will fall too, and our sanctuary will be plundered, and he will demand our blood for its desecration.
8:22 The killing of our brothers, the captivity of the land and the ruin of our inheritance he will bring down on our heads among the nations, wherever we are made slaves. We will be something offensive and shameful to those who own us.
8:23 For our slavery will not turn out to our favor. The Lord our God will turn it to our dishonor.
8:24 So now, brothers, let us set an example for our brothers, for their lives hang on us, and the sanctuary, the house and the altar rest on us.
8:25 Even so, let us thank the Lord our God. He is testing us, just as he tested our ancestors.
8:26 Remember all that he did with Abraham, and how he tested Isaac, and all that happened to Jacob in Mesopotamia of Syria, while he was keeping the sheep of Laban, his mother's brother.
8:27 For he has not put us through the fire as he did them, to search their hearts, and he has not taken vengeance on us. The Lord whips those who come near him, to warn them."
8:28 Uzziah said to her, "Everything you have said, you have spoken with a good heart, and no one can stand against your words.
8:29 This is not the first day your wisdom has been plain to see. From the start of your life all the people have known your understanding, for the shape of your heart is good.
8:30 But the people were terribly thirsty. They forced us to do as we promised them, and to bring an oath on ourselves that we will not break.
8:31 So now, pray for us, for you are a devout woman. Then the Lord will send the rain to fill our cisterns, and we will not faint anymore."
8:32 Judith said to them, "Listen to me. I am going to do a deed that will be told to the children of our people down through all generations.
8:33 Stand at the town gate tonight. I will go out with my maid. Before the days are up after which you said you would hand the town over to our enemies, the Lord will come to the help of Israel by my hand.
8:34 But do not search into what I am doing. I will not tell you until it is done."
8:35 Uzziah and the rulers said to her, "Go in peace, and may the Lord God go ahead of you to take vengeance on our enemies."
8:36 They went back from the tent and returned to their posts.
## Judith's prayer
9:1 Then Judith fell on her face, put ashes on her head and uncovered the sackcloth she had on. At that very time the incense of that evening was being offered in Jerusalem, in the house of God. Judith cried out to the Lord with a loud voice and said:
9:2 "Lord, God of my ancestor Simeon! You put a sword in his hand to take vengeance on the foreigners who violated a virgin's womb to defile her. They stripped her thigh to shame her and profaned her womb to disgrace her. For you had said, 'It must not be done,' and yet they did it.
9:3 So you gave their rulers over to be killed, and their bed, which was ashamed of their deceit, was itself deceived and stained with blood. You struck down slaves along with princes, and princes on their thrones.
9:4 You gave their wives over to be seized, and their daughters to captivity, and all their goods to be divided among the sons you loved. They burned with zeal for you, they were horrified at the defilement of their blood, and they called on you for help. O God, my God, hear me too, a widow.
9:5 For you made what came before those things, and those things, and what came after. You planned what is happening now and what is still to come, and the things you had in mind came to be.
9:6 The things you decided stood before you and said, 'Here we are!' For all your ways are prepared, and your judgment is made with foreknowledge.
9:7 Look, the Assyrians are many in their might. They are proud of their horses and riders. They boast in the strength of their foot soldiers. They trust in shield and javelin, in bow and sling. They do not know that you are the Lord who shatters wars.
9:8 'The Lord' is your name. Smash their strength with your power, and break their might in your anger. For they have planned to profane your sanctuary, to defile the tent where your glorious name rests, and to hack off the horn of your altar with iron.
9:9 Look at their arrogance. Send your anger down on their heads. Give into my hand, a widow's hand, the strength I have planned for.
9:10 Strike down the slave with the ruler, and the ruler with his servant, by the deceit of my lips. Break their pride by a woman's hand.
9:11 For your strength does not lie in numbers, and your rule does not rest on the mighty. You are the God of the lowly. You are the helper of the least, the protector of the weak, the shelter of those given up for lost, the savior of those without hope.
9:12 Yes, yes, God of my father, God of Israel's inheritance, Master of heaven and earth, Creator of the waters, King of all your creation, listen to my plea!
9:13 Make my words and my deceit wound and bruise those who have planned cruel things against your covenant and your holy house, against the summit of Zion and the house your children possess.
9:14 Give your whole nation and every tribe the knowledge that you are the God of all power and strength, and that no one but you shields the race of Israel."
## Judith goes down to the enemy
10:1 When she had stopped crying out to the God of Israel and had finished all these words,
10:2 she got up from where she lay on the ground. She called her maid and went down into the house where she used to stay on the Sabbath days and on her feast days.
10:3 She took off the sackcloth she had been wearing and put off her widow's clothes. She bathed her body with water and anointed herself with rich perfume. She arranged the hair of her head and put a headdress on it, and she put on the festive clothes she used to wear while her husband Manasseh was alive.
10:4 She put sandals on her feet and put on her anklets, bracelets, rings, earrings and all her jewelry. She made herself very beautiful, to catch the eye of any man who might see her.
10:5 She gave her maid a skin of wine and a flask of oil. She filled a bag with roasted grain, a cake of dried figs and loaves of pure bread. She wrapped up all her dishes and put them on her maid to carry.
10:6 They went out to the gate of the town of Bethulia and found Uzziah standing there with the town elders, Chabris and Charmis.
10:7 When they saw her, with her face changed and her clothing different, they were amazed at her beauty, very greatly, and they said to her,
10:8 "May the God of our ancestors give you favor and bring your plans to completion, for the delight of the Israelites and the honor of Jerusalem." She bowed down to God,
10:9 and she said to them, "Order the gate of the town opened for me, and I will go out to carry out the things you talked about with me." So they ordered the young men to open it for her, as they had said.
10:10 They did so. Judith went out, she and her slave girl with her. The men of the town kept watching her until she had gone down the mountain and through the valley, and they could see her no longer.
10:11 The women were walking straight ahead through the valley when an Assyrian patrol met her.
10:12 They took her and asked, "Whose are you? Where do you come from, and where are you going?" She said, "I am a daughter of the Hebrews, and I am running away from them, for they are about to be handed over to you to be devoured.
10:13 I am on my way to see Holofernes, the commander in chief of your army, to give him true information. I will show him a way he can go and take control of the whole hill country, without losing one of his men, body or breath."
10:14 When the men heard her words and studied her face, she looked wonderfully beautiful to them. They said to her,
10:15 "You have saved your life by hurrying to come down to our lord's presence. Now go to his tent. Some of us will escort you until they hand you over to him.
10:16 When you stand in front of him, do not be afraid in your heart. Tell him what you have told us, and he will treat you well."
10:17 They chose a hundred of their men to go with her and her maid, and they brought them to the tent of Holofernes.
10:18 People came running from all over the camp, for news of her arrival spread from tent to tent. They came and stood around her as she waited outside Holofernes' tent until they told him about her.
10:19 They marveled at her beauty and marveled at the Israelites because of her. They said to one another, "Who can look down on this people, when they have women like this among them? It would be a mistake to leave even one of their men alive. If they are let go, they will be able to outwit the whole world."
10:20 Then the men who slept near Holofernes and all his servants came out and brought her into the tent.
10:21 Holofernes was resting on his bed under the canopy, which was woven of purple and gold, emeralds and precious stones.
10:22 They told him about her, and he came out to the front of the tent, with silver lamps carried before him.
10:23 When Judith came before him and his servants, they all marveled at the beauty of her face. She fell on her face and bowed down to him, and his slaves lifted her up.
## Judith's story to Holofernes
11:1 Holofernes said to her, "Courage, woman! Have no fear in your heart. I have never done harm to anyone who chose to be a servant of Nebuchadnezzar, king of the whole earth.
11:2 Even now, if your people who live in the hill country had not treated me with contempt, I would not have raised my spear against them. They brought this on themselves.
11:3 Now tell me why you ran away from them and came to us. You have come to safety. Take courage! You will live tonight and from now on.
11:4 No one will do you wrong. Everyone will treat you well, as they do the servants of my lord King Nebuchadnezzar."
11:5 Judith said to him, "Receive what your slave has to say, and let your servant girl speak before you. I will not tell my lord a lie tonight.
11:6 If you follow the words of your servant, God will bring this matter to a full end with you, and my lord will not fail in his plans.
11:7 As Nebuchadnezzar, king of the whole earth, lives, and as his power lives, who sent you to set every living thing in order: because of you, not only do human beings serve him, but the wild animals, the livestock and the birds of the sky will live, through your strength, for Nebuchadnezzar and all his house.
11:8 For we have heard of your wisdom and your clever schemes. The whole earth has been told that you alone are the best in the whole kingdom, able in knowledge and amazing in the campaigns of war.
11:9 Now as for what Achior said in your council, we have heard his words, for the men of Bethulia rescued him, and he told them everything he had said to you.
11:10 So, my lord and master, do not brush his words aside. Keep them in your heart, for they are true. Our people are not punished, and the sword does not overpower them, unless they sin against their God.
11:11 But now, so that my lord will not be thwarted and fail, death is about to fall on them. A sin has caught hold of them, by which they will anger their God whenever they do what is not right.
11:12 Since their food has run out and all their water is scarce, they have decided to lay hands on their animals. They are resolved to use up everything God commanded them in his laws not to eat.
11:13 They have decided to consume the first part of the grain and the tenth of the wine and oil, which they had set apart as holy for the priests who serve in Jerusalem before the face of our God. None of the common people should even touch these things with their hands.
11:14 They have sent men to Jerusalem to bring back permission from the council of elders, because the people living there have done this too.
11:15 When word reaches them and they do it, on that very day they will be handed over to you to be destroyed.
11:16 So when I, your slave, learned all this, I ran away from them. God has sent me to do things with you that will astonish the whole earth, everyone who hears of them.
11:17 For your slave is a God-fearing woman. Night and day she serves the God of heaven. Now I will stay with you, my lord, and your slave will go out each night into the ravine and pray to God. He will tell me when they have committed their sins.
11:18 Then I will come and report it to you, and you can go out with your whole army. Not one of them will be able to stand against you.
11:19 I will lead you through the middle of Judea until you come in front of Jerusalem. I will set your seat in the middle of it. You will drive them like sheep without a shepherd, and not a dog will even mutter with its tongue at you. These things were told to me, by my foreknowledge. They were revealed to me, and I have been sent to tell you."
11:20 Her words pleased Holofernes and all his servants. They marveled at her wisdom and said,
11:21 "There is no woman like her from one end of the earth to the other, for a beautiful face and wise words!"
11:22 Holofernes said to her, "God did well to send you ahead of the people, to put strength in our hands and destruction on those who treated my lord with contempt.
11:23 Now, you are lovely to look at and good in your words. If you do as you have said, your God will be my God, and you will live in the house of King Nebuchadnezzar and be famous throughout the whole earth."
## Three days in the camp
12:1 He ordered them to bring her in where his silver dishes were kept. He gave orders to set a table for her with some of his own fine food and to give her some of his wine to drink.
12:2 But Judith said, "I will not eat any of it, so that it will not become a stumbling block. I will be supplied from the things I brought with me."
12:3 Holofernes said to her, "If what you have with you runs out, where will we get more like it to give you? None of your people are with us."
12:4 Judith said to him, "As your soul lives, my lord, your slave will not use up what I have with me before he has done by my hand what he has planned."
12:5 Holofernes' servants brought her into the tent, and she slept until midnight. Then she got up toward the morning watch
12:6 and sent word to Holofernes: "Let my lord please give orders to allow your slave to go out to pray."
12:7 Holofernes ordered his bodyguards not to stop her. She stayed in the camp three days. Each night she went out to the ravine of Bethulia and bathed at the spring in the camp.
12:8 When she came up, she prayed to the Lord God of Israel to guide her way for the rescue of the children of his people.
12:9 She came back in pure and stayed in the tent until she ate her food toward evening.
## The banquet
12:10 On the fourth day Holofernes gave a banquet for his own slaves only. He did not invite any of the officers on duty.
12:11 He said to Bagoas, the eunuch who was in charge of all his affairs, "Go and persuade the Hebrew woman who is in your care to come to us and eat and drink with us.
12:12 It would be a disgrace for us to let a woman like this go without having been with her. If we do not draw her in, she will laugh at us."
12:13 Bagoas left Holofernes' presence and went in to her. He said, "Do not let this lovely girl hesitate to come to my lord, to be honored in his presence, to drink wine with us and be merry, and to become today like one of the Assyrian women who serve in the house of Nebuchadnezzar."
12:14 Judith said to him, "Who am I to speak against my lord? Anything that is pleasing in his eyes I will hurry to do, and it will be my gladness until the day of my death."
12:15 She got up and dressed herself in her clothes and all her woman's finery. Her slave went ahead and spread on the ground, facing Holofernes, the fleeces she had received from Bagoas for her daily use, so that she could recline on them to eat.
12:16 Judith came in and reclined. Holofernes' heart was carried away by her, and his soul was shaken. He burned with desire to sleep with her. Ever since the day he saw her, he had been looking for a time to seduce her.
12:17 Holofernes said to her, "Drink now, and be merry with us!"
12:18 Judith said, "I will drink, my lord, for today my life means more to me than on any day since I was born."
12:19 She took and ate and drank in front of him what her slave had prepared.
12:20 Holofernes was delighted with her, and he drank very much wine, more than he had drunk on a single day in his whole life.
## The head of Holofernes
13:1 When it grew late, his slaves hurried away. Bagoas shut the tent from outside and sent the attendants away from his lord's presence. They went off to their beds, for they were all worn out because the banquet had gone on so long.
13:2 Judith was left alone in the tent, with Holofernes sprawled on his bed, for he was soaked in wine.
13:3 Judith had told her slave to stand outside the bedroom and wait for her to come out, as she did every day. She had said she would be going out to pray, and she had told Bagoas the same thing.
13:4 Everyone went out from his presence, and no one, small or great, was left in the bedroom. Judith stood beside his bed and said in her heart, "Lord, God of all power, at this hour look upon what my hands are doing, to bring honor to Jerusalem.
13:5 For now is the time to help your inheritance and to carry out my plan to crush the enemies who have risen against us."
13:6 She went to the bedpost near Holofernes' head and took down his sword from it.
13:7 She came close to the bed, took hold of the hair of his head and said, "Make me strong today, God of Israel!"
13:8 Then with all her strength she struck him twice in the neck and took off his head.
13:9 She rolled his body off the bed and pulled the canopy down from the posts. A moment later she went out and gave Holofernes' head to her maid,
13:10 who put it in her food bag. Then the two of them went out together, as they always did. They passed through the camp, went around the ravine, climbed the mountain to Bethulia and came to its gates.
## Judith comes home
13:11 From far off Judith called to the guards at the gates, "Open up, please open the gate! God, our own God, is with us, to act again with strength in Israel and with might against our enemies, as he has done today!"
13:12 When the men of her town heard her voice, they hurried down to the gate of their town and called the elders of the town.
13:13 Everyone, small and great, came running, because they could hardly believe she had come back. They opened the gate and welcomed them. They lit a fire for light and gathered around them.
13:14 She said to them in a loud voice, "Praise God! Praise him! Praise the God who has not taken his mercy back from the house of Israel, but has shattered our enemies through my hand tonight!"
13:15 She took the head out of the bag and showed it to them. "Look, the head of Holofernes, the commander in chief of the army of Assyria! And look, the canopy he lay under in his drunken bouts! The Lord has struck him down by a woman's hand.
13:16 As the Lord lives, who guarded me on the road I took: my face deceived him and brought him to ruin, but he did no sin with me to make me unclean and ashamed."
13:17 All the people were utterly amazed. They bowed down and worshiped God, and said with one voice, "Blessed are you, our God, who today have brought the enemies of your people to nothing!"
13:18 Uzziah said to her, "Daughter, you are blessed by God Most High above all the women on earth! And blessed be the Lord God, who created the heavens and the earth, who led you to wound the head of our enemies' chief.
13:19 Your hope will never leave the hearts of those who remember God's strength, forever.
13:20 May God make these things an everlasting height of honor for you, and visit you with good things, because you did not hold back your own life when our people were humbled. You went out against our downfall, walking a straight path before our God." And all the people said, "So be it! So be it!"
## The head on the wall
14:1 Judith said to them, "Listen to me, my brothers. Take this head and hang it on the battlements of your wall.
14:2 As soon as dawn breaks and the sun comes up on the earth, every one of you take up your weapons. Let every able man go out of the town. Put a commander over them, as if you were going down to the plain against the Assyrian patrol. But do not go down.
14:3 The Assyrians will take up their armor and go to their camp and wake up the generals of the Assyrian army. They will run to Holofernes' tent and will not find him. Then panic will fall on them, and they will run away from you.
14:4 Then you and everyone who lives anywhere in Israel's territory must chase them and cut them down where they run.
## Achior believes
14:5 But before you do this, call Achior the Ammonite to me. Let him see and recognize the man who treated the house of Israel with contempt and sent him to us as if to his death."
14:6 So they called Achior from Uzziah's house. When he came and saw the head of Holofernes in the hand of one of the men in the gathering of the people, he fell on his face, and his spirit left him.
14:7 When they lifted him up, he threw himself at Judith's feet and bowed down before her. He said, "Blessed are you in every tent of Judah, and in every nation! Those who hear your name will tremble.
14:8 Now tell me everything you have done these days." Judith told him, in front of all the people, everything she had done from the day she went out until the moment she was speaking to them.
14:9 When she stopped speaking, the people raised a great shout and filled their town with sounds of joy.
14:10 When Achior saw all that the God of Israel had done, he believed in God completely. He had the flesh of his foreskin circumcised, and he was joined to the house of Israel, to this day.
## Panic in the Assyrian camp
14:11 As soon as dawn came, they hung the head of Holofernes from the wall. Every man of Israel took up his weapons, and they went out in companies toward the mountain passes.
14:12 When the Assyrians saw them, they sent word to their leaders, and those leaders went to the generals, the commanders of thousands and all their officers.
14:13 They came to Holofernes' tent and said to the one in charge of all his affairs, "Wake up our lord! The slaves have dared to come down to fight us, to be wiped out completely."
14:14 So Bagoas went in and rapped on the curtain of the tent, for he thought Holofernes was sleeping with Judith.
14:15 When no one answered, he opened the curtains and went into the bedroom. He found him thrown down on the floor, dead, with his head cut off.
14:16 He cried out in a loud voice, with weeping and groaning and a great shout, and tore his clothes.
14:17 He went into the tent where Judith had been staying and did not find her. He ran out to the troops and shouted,
14:18 "The slaves have broken faith! One Hebrew woman has brought shame on the house of King Nebuchadnezzar! Look, Holofernes is on the ground, and his head is not on him!"
14:19 When the commanders of the Assyrian army heard these words, they tore their tunics. They were badly shaken, and their shouting and loud cries rose up in the middle of the camp.
## The rout
15:1 When the men in the tents heard it, they were stunned at what had happened.
15:2 Trembling and fear fell on them. No one stayed beside the next man any longer. They poured out all together and fled by every path across the plain and the hill country.
15:3 Those who had camped in the hills around Bethulia also turned and ran. Then the Israelites, every fighting man among them, poured out against them.
15:4 Uzziah sent messengers to Betomasthaim, Choba and Chola and all through Israel's territory to tell what had happened, so that everyone would pour out against the enemy to destroy them.
15:5 When the Israelites heard it, they all fell on them together and cut them down as far as Choba. In the same way, men came from Jerusalem and from all the hill country, for they had been told what had happened in their enemies' camp. Those in Gilead and in Galilee outflanked them with a great slaughter, until they were past Damascus and its borders.
15:6 The rest of the people of Bethulia fell on the Assyrian camp and plundered it, and they became very rich.
15:7 The Israelites came back from the slaughter and took over what was left. The villages and farms in the hill country and the plain gained a great deal of plunder, for there was a huge amount of it.
## Jerusalem blesses Judith
15:8 Joakim the high priest and the council of elders of the Israelites who lived in Jerusalem came to see the good things the Lord had done for Israel, and to see Judith and greet her.
15:9 When they came to her, they all blessed her together and said to her, "You are Jerusalem's honor! You are Israel's great delight! You are our people's great boast!
15:10 You have done all this with your own hand. You have done good things for Israel, and may God be pleased with them. May you be blessed by the Lord Almighty for all time!" And all the people said, "So be it!"
15:11 All the people plundered the camp for thirty days. They gave Judith Holofernes' tent and all his silver dishes, his beds, his bowls and all his furniture. She took them and loaded her mule, hitched up her wagons and piled them on.
15:12 All the women of Israel came running to see her. They blessed her, and some of them performed a dance in her honor. She took leafy wands in her hands and gave them to the women who were with her.
15:13 She and the women with her crowned themselves with olive leaves. She went in front of all the people in the dance, leading all the women, and all the men of Israel followed, armed and wearing garlands, with songs on their lips.
15:14 Judith began this song of thanks in front of all Israel, and all the people sang this praise after her.
## Judith's song
16:1 Judith said: "Strike up for my God with tambourines! / Sing to the Lord with cymbals! / Tune a psalm and a song of praise to him! / Lift him high and call on his name!
16:2 For the Lord is a God who shatters wars. / Into his camp, among the people, / he rescued me from the hands of those who chased me.
16:3 Assyria came down from the mountains of the north. / He came with tens of thousands of his troops. / Their numbers choked the streams, / and their horses covered the hills.
16:4 He said he would set my mountains on fire, / kill my young men with the sword, / throw my nursing babies to the ground, / take my little children as plunder / and carry off my young women as spoil.
16:5 But the Lord Almighty brought them to nothing / by a woman's hand.
16:6 For their mighty one did not fall to young men, / no sons of Titans struck him down, / and no towering giants attacked him. / But Judith, Merari's daughter, / unstrung him with the beauty of her face.
16:7 For she put off her widow's clothes / to lift up those who suffered in Israel. / She anointed her face with perfume,
16:8 tied back her hair with a headdress / and put on a linen gown to deceive him.
16:9 Her sandal caught his eye, / her beauty took his soul captive, / and the sword went through his neck.
16:10 The Persians shuddered at her daring, / and the Medes were shaken by her boldness.
16:11 Then my lowly ones raised the war cry, / and my weak ones were afraid and trembled. / They raised their voices, and the enemy was routed.
16:12 Sons of young girls stabbed them through; / they wounded them like the children of runaways. / They perished before the battle line of my Lord.
16:13 I will sing a new song to my God. / Lord, you are great and full of glory, / marvelous in strength, past all surpassing.
16:14 Let all your creation serve you, / for you spoke, and they came to be. / You sent out your spirit, and it built them. / No one can stand against your voice.
16:15 Mountains will be shaken from their foundations, together with the waters; / rocks will melt like wax at your presence. / Yet you are kind to those who fear you.

## Judith's last years
16:18 When they came to Jerusalem, they worshiped God. As soon as the people were purified, they offered their burnt offerings, their freewill offerings and their gifts.
16:19 Judith dedicated to God all the things of Holofernes that the people had given her. The canopy she had taken for herself from his bedroom she gave to God as a dedicated offering.
16:20 The people went on celebrating in Jerusalem before the sanctuary for three months, and Judith stayed with them.
16:21 After those days each of them went home to their own inheritance. Judith went back to Bethulia and stayed on her estate. In her time she became famous throughout the whole land.
16:22 Many men desired her, but no man slept with her all the days of her life, from the day her husband Manasseh died and was gathered to his people.
16:23 She grew greater and greater. She grew old in her husband's house and lived to be a hundred and five. She set her maid free. She died in Bethulia, and they buried her in the cave of her husband Manasseh.
16:24 The house of Israel mourned for her seven days. Before she died, she divided her property among all those closest to her husband Manasseh and those closest to her own family.
16:25 No one made the Israelites afraid again in Judith's lifetime, or for a long time after her death. Amen.
