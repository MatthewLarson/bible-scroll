## One place for sacrifice
17:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
17:2 "Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites. Tell them, 'This is the thing the LORD has commanded:
17:3 Any man of the house of Israel who slaughters an ox or a lamb or a goat inside the camp, or who slaughters it outside the camp,
17:4 and does not bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting to present it as an offering to the LORD in front of the LORD's dwelling, will have blood counted against him. That man has shed blood, and he will be cut off from among his people.
17:5 This is so that the Israelites will bring their sacrifices, which they now sacrifice out on the face of the field. They are to bring them to the LORD, to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and sacrifice them to the LORD as sacrifices of peace offerings.
17:6 The priest will splash the blood on the LORD's altar at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and turn the fat into smoke, a soothing smell for the LORD.
17:7 They must no longer sacrifice their sacrifices to the goat idols, which they go after, prostituting themselves. This will be a rule for all time for them, through all their generations.'
## Blood is life
17:8 "Say to them also: 'Any man of the house of Israel, or any foreigner living among them, who offers up a burnt offering or a sacrifice
17:9 and does not bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting to offer it to the LORD, that man will be cut off from his kin.
17:10 Any man of the house of Israel, or any foreigner living among them, who eats any blood at all: I will set my face against that person who eats the blood, and I will cut him off from among his people.
17:11 For a body's life is in its blood, and I myself have given it to you on the altar, to make atonement for your lives. It is the blood that makes atonement, by means of the life in it.
17:12 That is why I have told the Israelites: None of you may eat blood, and the foreigner who lives among you may not eat blood either.
17:13 Any Israelite man, or any foreigner living among them, who hunts down a wild animal or a bird that may be eaten must pour out its blood and cover it with dirt.
17:14 For as for the life of every living body, its blood is its life. So I have told the Israelites: You must not eat the blood of any body, because the life of every body is its blood. Anyone who eats it will be cut off.
17:15 Any person, whether born in the land or a foreigner, who eats an animal that died on its own or was torn by a wild beast must wash his clothes and bathe in water. He will be unclean until evening, and then he will be clean.
17:16 But if he does not wash his clothes or bathe his body, he will carry his guilt.'"
## Not like Egypt, not like Canaan
18:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
18:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: I am the LORD your God.
18:3 Do not do what is done in the land of Egypt, where you used to live, and do not do what is done in the land of Canaan, where I am bringing you. Do not walk by their rules.
18:4 Carry out my rulings and keep my rules, and walk in them. I am the LORD your God.
18:5 Keep my rules and my rulings. If a human being does them, he will live by them. I am the LORD.
## Close relatives
18:6 No man among you may come near any close relative of his own flesh to expose nakedness. I am the LORD.
18:7 Do not expose the nakedness of your father and the nakedness of your mother. She is your mother; do not expose her nakedness.
18:8 Do not expose the nakedness of your father's wife. It is your father's nakedness.
18:9 The nakedness of your sister, your father's daughter or your mother's daughter, whether she was born in the household or born outside it: do not expose their nakedness.
18:10 The nakedness of your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter: do not expose their nakedness, because their nakedness is your own.
18:11 The nakedness of the daughter of your father's wife, born to your father: she is your sister. Do not expose her nakedness.
18:12 Do not expose the nakedness of your father's sister. She is your father's own flesh.
18:13 Do not expose the nakedness of your mother's sister, for she is your mother's own flesh.
18:14 Do not expose the nakedness of your father's brother: do not go near his wife. She is your aunt.
18:15 Do not expose the nakedness of your daughter-in-law. She is your son's wife; do not expose her nakedness.
18:16 Do not expose the nakedness of your brother's wife. It is your brother's nakedness.
18:17 Do not expose the nakedness of a woman and also of her daughter. Do not take her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter to expose her nakedness. They are her own flesh. It is a vile scheme.
18:18 Do not take a woman as a rival to her sister, exposing her nakedness alongside her sister's while her sister is still alive.
## Other forbidden acts
18:19 Do not go near a woman to expose her nakedness while she is unclean with her monthly flow.
18:20 Do not give your seed in bed to your fellow Israelite's wife, making yourself unclean with her.
18:21 Do not hand over any of your children to make them pass over to Molech, and do not treat the name of your God as common. I am the LORD.
18:22 With a male do not lie down in the ways of lying down with a woman. It is abhorrent.
18:23 Do not give your seed in bed to any animal, making yourself unclean with it. And a woman must not stand in front of an animal to mate with it. It is a wrongful mixing.
## The land that vomits
18:24 Do not make yourselves unclean by any of these things, for by all of these the nations that I am driving out ahead of you made themselves unclean.
18:25 The land became unclean, so I called it to account for its guilt, and the land vomited out the people living in it.
18:26 But you, keep my rules and my rulings, and do none of these abhorrent things, neither the one born in the land nor the foreigner who lives among you.
18:27 For the people who lived in the land before you did all these abhorrent things, and the land became unclean.
18:28 Then the land will not vomit you out for making it unclean, the way it vomited out the nation that was there before you.
18:29 Anyone who does any of these abhorrent things, the persons who do them will be cut off from among their people.
18:30 So keep what I have placed in your charge, and do not follow any of the abhorrent customs that were practiced before you came, and do not make yourselves unclean by them. I am the LORD your God."
## Be holy
19:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
19:2 "Speak to the whole community of the Israelites and say to them: You will be holy, because I am holy, I the LORD your God.
19:3 Each of you is to fear his mother and his father, and to keep my Sabbaths. I am the LORD your God.
19:4 Do not turn to the no-gods, and do not make yourselves gods out of poured metal. I am the LORD your God.
19:5 When you sacrifice a peace offering to the LORD, sacrifice it in a way that will win you acceptance.
19:6 It is to be eaten on the day you sacrifice it or on the next day. Whatever is left until the third day must be burned up in fire.
19:7 If any of it is eaten at all on the third day, it is rotten meat. It will not be accepted.
19:8 Whoever eats it will carry his guilt, because he has treated as common what is holy to the LORD. That person will be cut off from his kin.
## Leave some for the poor
19:9 When you harvest the crops of your land, do not harvest all the way to the edge of your field, and do not gather up what is left behind after your harvest.
19:10 Do not pick your vineyard clean, and do not gather the fallen grapes of your vineyard. Leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I am the LORD your God.
## Your neighbor
19:11 Do not steal. Do not deceive. Do not deal falsely, any of you, with his fellow citizen.
19:12 Do not swear by my name to a lie, and so treat the name of your God as common. I am the LORD.
19:13 Do not extort from your neighbor, and do not rob him. The wages of a hired worker must not stay with you overnight until morning.
19:14 Do not curse a deaf person, and do not set something to trip over in front of a blind person. Fear your God. I am the LORD.
19:15 Do no wrong in judgment. Do not lift up the face of the poor man, and do not honor the face of the great man. Judge your fellow citizen in what is right.
19:16 Do not go about as a slanderer among your kin. Do not stand by over your neighbor's blood. I am the LORD.
19:17 Do not hate your brother in your heart. Be sure to correct your fellow citizen, so that you do not carry sin because of him.
19:18 Do not take vengeance, and do not keep a grudge against the children of your people. You are to love your neighbor as yourself. I am the LORD.
## Mixtures and boundaries
19:19 Keep my rules. Do not let two kinds of your animals mate. Do not sow your field with two kinds of seed. Do not let a garment of two kinds of thread woven together come upon your body.
19:20 If a man lies with a woman and has sex with her, and she is a slave woman promised to another man, but she has not been bought back and has not been given her freedom, damages are due. They are not to be put to death, because she had not been set free.
19:21 He must bring his guilt offering to the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting, a ram as a guilt offering.
19:22 With the ram of the guilt offering the priest will make atonement for him before the LORD, for the sin he committed, and he will be forgiven for the sin he committed.
19:23 When you come into the land and plant any kind of tree for food, count its fruit as its foreskin. For three years it will be uncircumcised for you. It must not be eaten.
19:24 In the fourth year all its fruit will be holy, a gift of praise to the LORD.
19:25 In the fifth year you may eat its fruit, so that it will yield you more and more. I am the LORD your God.
19:26 Do not eat anything with the blood. Do not look for omens, and do not read signs.
19:27 Do not cut your hair round at the sides of your head, and do not spoil the edges of your beard.
19:28 Do not slash your bodies for someone who has died, and do not put tattooed writing on yourselves. I am the LORD.
19:29 Do not make your daughter common by turning her into a prostitute, or the land will turn to prostitution and the land will fill up with vile schemes.
19:30 Keep my Sabbaths and fear my holy place. I am the LORD.
19:31 Do not turn to the ghosts of the dead or to the spirits that know things. Do not go looking for them and become unclean through them. I am the LORD your God.
19:32 Rise up before gray hair, and honor the face of the old. Fear your God. I am the LORD.
## The foreigner
19:33 When a foreigner lives with you in your land, do not mistreat him.
19:34 The foreigner who lives with you is to be to you like one born among you. You are to love him as yourself, because you were foreigners in the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.
19:35 Do not do wrong in a court, or with a ruler, a scale or a measure.
19:36 Have scales that are right, weights that are right, an ephah that is right and a hin that is right. I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.
19:37 Keep all my rules and all my rulings, and do them. I am the LORD."
## Molech and the ghosts
20:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
20:2 "Say to the Israelites: Any Israelite, or any of the foreigners living in Israel, who gives any of his children to Molech must be put to death. The people of the land are to stone him with stones.
20:3 I myself will set my face against that man and cut him off from among his people, because he gave one of his children to Molech, and so made my holy place unclean and treated my holy name as common.
20:4 And if the people of the land look the other way from that man when he gives one of his children to Molech, and do not put him to death,
20:5 then I myself will set my face against that man and against his clan. I will cut him off from among their people, him and all who go after him to prostitute themselves after Molech.
20:6 The person who turns to the ghosts of the dead and to the spirits that know things, chasing after them like a prostitute: I will set my face against that person and cut him off from among his people.
20:7 Make yourselves holy, and be holy, for I am the LORD your God.
20:8 Keep my rules and do them. I am the LORD, who makes you holy.
## The penalties
20:9 Anyone who curses his father or his mother must be put to death. He has cursed his father or his mother; his blood is on him.
20:10 A man who commits adultery with another man's wife, who commits adultery with his neighbor's wife, must be put to death, the man and the woman who commit adultery.
20:11 A man who lies with his father's wife has exposed his father's nakedness. The two of them must be put to death. Their blood is on them.
20:12 A man who lies with his daughter-in-law: the two of them must be put to death. They have done a wrongful mixing. Their blood is on them.
20:13 A man who lies down with a male in the ways of lying down with a woman: the two of them have done something abhorrent. They must be put to death. Their blood is on them.
20:14 A man who takes a woman and her mother: it is a vile scheme. They are to burn him and them in fire, so that there will be no vile scheme among you.
20:15 A man who gives his seed in bed to an animal must be put to death, and you are to kill the animal.
20:16 A woman who goes near any animal to mate with it: you are to kill the woman and the animal. They must be put to death. Their blood is on them.
20:17 A man who takes his sister, his father's daughter or his mother's daughter, so that he sees her nakedness and she sees his nakedness: it is a disgrace. They are to be cut off before the eyes of their people. He has exposed his sister's nakedness; he will carry his guilt.
20:18 A man who lies with a woman during her monthly sickness and exposes her nakedness has laid bare her fountain, and she has uncovered the fountain of her blood. Both of them will be cut off from among their people.
20:19 Do not expose the nakedness of your mother's sister or your father's sister, for that lays bare one's own flesh. They will carry their guilt.
20:20 A man who lies with his uncle's wife has exposed his uncle's nakedness. They will carry their sin. They will die childless.
20:21 A man who takes his brother's wife: it is an unclean thing. He has exposed his brother's nakedness. They will be childless.
## Set apart from the peoples
20:22 Keep all my rules and all my rulings, and do them, so that the land where I am bringing you to live will not vomit you out.
20:23 Do not walk by the rules of the nation that I am driving out ahead of you. They did all these things, and I came to loathe them.
20:24 But I have told you: You will take their soil as your own, and I will give it to you to own, a land flowing with milk and honey. I am the LORD your God, who has set you apart from the peoples.
20:25 So you must set apart the clean animal from the unclean, and the unclean bird from the clean. Do not make yourselves loathsome by animal or bird or anything that creeps on the ground, things I have set apart for you as unclean.
20:26 You will be holy for me, because I am holy, I the LORD, and I have set you apart from the peoples to be mine.
20:27 A man or a woman who has a ghost or a knowing spirit in them must be put to death. They are to stone them with stones. Their blood is on them."
## Priests and the dead
21:1 The LORD said to Moses, "Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say to them: A priest must not make himself unclean for a dead person among his kin,
21:2 except for a relative who is near to him: for his mother and his father, for his son and his daughter, and for his brother,
21:3 and for his sister, a young woman who is near to him and has not belonged to a husband. For her he may make himself unclean.
21:4 He must not make himself unclean as a husband among his kin, and so make himself common.
21:5 Priests must not make their heads bald, or shave the corners of their beards, or make cuts in their bodies.
21:6 They are to be holy to their God, and they must not treat the name of their God as common, for they present the LORD's offerings by fire, the food of their God. So they must be holy.
21:7 They must not marry a woman who is a prostitute or who has been made common, and they must not marry a woman divorced from her husband, for the priest is holy to his God.
21:8 You are to treat him as holy, because he presents the food of your God. He is to be holy to you, because I, the LORD who makes you holy, am holy.
21:9 If a priest's daughter makes herself common by prostitution, she makes her father common. She is to be burned in fire.
## The high priest
21:10 The priest who is greater than his brothers, on whose head the anointing oil has been poured, and who has been ordained to wear the robes, must not let his hair hang loose or tear his clothes.
21:11 He must not go in where any dead body lies. He must not make himself unclean even for his father or his mother.
21:12 He must not go out of the holy place, and he must not treat the holy place of his God as common, because the crown of his God's anointing oil is on him. I am the LORD.
21:13 He is to marry a woman who is a virgin.
21:14 A widow, a divorced woman, or a woman made common by prostitution: these he must not marry. Only a virgin from his own kin may he take as his wife,
21:15 so that he does not make his children common among his kin. For I am the LORD, who makes him holy."
## Priests with a defect
21:16 The LORD spoke to Moses:
21:17 "Speak to Aaron and say: No man among your descendants, in any generation, who has a defect may come near to present the food of his God.
21:18 No man with a defect may come near: no man who is blind or lame, or who has a slit nose or a limb too long,
21:19 or who has a broken foot or a broken hand,
21:20 or who is a hunchback or withered, or has a spot in his eye, or a scaly rash or a crusted sore, or crushed testicles.
21:21 No man among the descendants of Aaron the priest who has a defect may come near to present the LORD's offerings by fire. He has a defect; he must not come near to present the food of his God.
21:22 He may eat the food of his God, from the most holy things and from the holy things.
21:23 But he must not go in to the curtain or come near the altar, because he has a defect, so that he does not make my holy places common. For I am the LORD, who makes them holy."
21:24 So Moses told this to Aaron and his sons and all the Israelites.
## Who may eat holy food
22:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
22:2 "Tell Aaron and his sons to keep themselves apart from the holy gifts of the Israelites, which they set apart as holy for me, so that they do not treat my holy name as common. I am the LORD.
22:3 Say to them: In all your generations, if any man among your descendants comes near the holy gifts that the Israelites set apart for the LORD while his uncleanness is on him, that person will be cut off from my presence. I am the LORD.
22:4 No man among Aaron's descendants who has a skin disease or a discharge may eat any of the holy gifts until he is clean. The same goes for anyone who touches something made unclean by a dead body, or a man who has had an emission of seed,
22:5 or anyone who touches any swarming creature that makes him unclean, or a person who makes him unclean, whatever his uncleanness is.
22:6 The person who touches such a thing will be unclean until evening. He must not eat any of the holy gifts unless he has bathed his body in water.
22:7 When the sun goes down he will be clean, and after that he may eat from the holy gifts, for they are his food.
22:8 He must not eat an animal that died on its own or was torn by a wild beast, and so make himself unclean by it. I am the LORD.
22:9 They must keep what I have placed in their charge, so that they do not carry sin because of it and die for it when they treat it as common. I am the LORD, who makes them holy.
22:10 No outsider may eat a holy gift. A lodger staying with a priest, or a hired worker, may not eat a holy gift.
22:11 But if a priest buys a person with his money, that person may eat of it, and those born in his household may eat of his food.
22:12 If a priest's daughter marries an outsider, she may not eat from the holy gifts that are set aside.
22:13 But if a priest's daughter is widowed or divorced and has no child, and she returns to her father's house as in her girlhood, she may eat from her father's food. No outsider may eat of it.
22:14 If someone eats a holy gift by mistake, he must add a fifth to it and give the holy gift to the priest.
22:15 The priests must not treat as common the holy gifts of the Israelites, which they set aside for the LORD,
22:16 and so load them with guilt that must be paid for, when they eat their holy gifts. For I am the LORD, who makes them holy."
## Animals fit for the altar
22:17 The LORD spoke to Moses:
22:18 "Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israelites and say to them: Any man of the house of Israel, or of the foreigners in Israel, who presents his offering, whether for any of their vows or for any of their freewill gifts that they present to the LORD as a burnt offering,
22:19 must bring a flawless male from the cattle, the sheep or the goats, if it is to be accepted for you.
22:20 Do not present anything that has a defect, because it will not be accepted for you.
22:21 When anyone presents a sacrifice of peace offering to the LORD to keep a vow, or as a freewill gift, from the herd or the flock, it must be flawless to be accepted. There must be no defect in it at all.
22:22 Do not present to the LORD anything blind, or injured, or maimed, or with a wart, or a scaly rash or a crusted sore. Do not put any of them on the altar as an offering by fire to the LORD.
22:23 An ox or a sheep with a limb too long or too short you may offer as a freewill gift, but it will not be accepted for a vow.
22:24 Do not present to the LORD an animal whose testicles are bruised, crushed, torn or cut. Do not do this in your land.
22:25 And do not present any of these as the food of your God when they come from the hand of a foreigner. They are damaged, there is a defect in them, and they will not be accepted for you."
22:26 The LORD spoke to Moses:
22:27 "When a calf or a lamb or a kid is born, it is to stay seven days with its mother. From the eighth day on, it will be accepted as an offering by fire to the LORD.
22:28 Whether cow or ewe, do not slaughter the mother and her young on one day.
22:29 When you sacrifice a thank offering to the LORD, sacrifice it in a way that will win you acceptance.
22:30 It must be eaten on that same day. Leave none of it until morning. I am the LORD.
22:31 Keep my commands and do them. I am the LORD.
22:32 Do not treat my holy name as common, so that I will be shown to be holy among the Israelites. I am the LORD, who makes you holy,
22:33 who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD."
## The LORD's set times
23:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
23:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: These are the LORD's set times, which you are to proclaim as holy gatherings. These are my set times.
23:3 Work may be done for six days, but the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, a holy gathering. Do no work at all. It is a Sabbath for the LORD wherever you live.
## Passover and Unleavened Bread
23:4 These are the LORD's set times, the holy gatherings that you are to proclaim, each at its set time.
23:5 In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, between the two evenings, is the LORD's Passover.
23:6 On the fifteenth day of this month is the LORD's Feast of Unleavened Bread. For seven days you are to eat unleavened bread.
23:7 On the first day you will have a holy gathering. Do no daily work.
23:8 For seven days present an offering by fire to the LORD. On the seventh day there is a holy gathering. Do no daily work."
## The first sheaf
23:9 The LORD spoke to Moses:
23:10 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you come into the land I am giving you and reap its harvest, bring the priest a sheaf from the first of your harvest.
23:11 He will wave the sheaf before the LORD so that you will be accepted. The priest is to wave it on the day after the Sabbath.
23:12 On the day you wave the sheaf, offer a flawless male lamb in its first year as a burnt offering to the LORD,
23:13 with its grain offering, two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, an offering by fire to the LORD, a soothing smell, and its drink offering, a quarter of a hin of wine.
23:14 Do not eat bread, or roasted grain, or fresh ears of grain until this very day, until you have brought the offering of your God. This is a rule for all time, through all your generations, wherever you live.
## The Feast of Weeks
23:15 Then count for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath, from the day you bring the sheaf that is waved: seven Sabbaths. They must be full.
23:16 Count fifty days, up to the day after the seventh Sabbath. Then present a new grain offering to the LORD.
23:17 From wherever you live, bring two loaves of bread to be waved, each made of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour and baked with leaven, as first fruits for the LORD.
23:18 Along with the bread, present seven flawless male lambs in their first year, one young bull, and two rams. They will be a burnt offering to the LORD, with their grain offering and their drink offerings, an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
23:19 Offer one male goat as a sin offering, and two male lambs in their first year as a sacrifice of peace offering.
23:20 The priest will wave them, with the bread of the first fruits, as a wave offering before the LORD, along with the two lambs. They will be holy to the LORD, for the priest.
23:21 On that very day you are to make a proclamation. It will be a holy gathering for you. Do no daily work. This is a rule for all time, wherever you live, through all your generations.
23:22 When you harvest the crops of your land, do not finish off the edge of your field as you harvest, and do not gather what is left behind after your harvest. Leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I am the LORD your God."
## The day of blasts
23:23 The LORD spoke to Moses:
23:24 "Speak to the Israelites: In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you are to have a day of rest, a reminder made with loud blasts, a holy gathering.
23:25 Do no daily work, and present an offering by fire to the LORD."
## The Day of Atonement
23:26 The LORD spoke to Moses:
23:27 "Mark this: the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of Atonement. It will be a holy gathering for you. You are to humble yourselves and present an offering by fire to the LORD.
23:28 Do no work at all on that very day, because it is a day of atonement, to make atonement for you before the LORD your God.
23:29 Any person who does not humble himself on that very day will be cut off from his kin.
23:30 And any person who does any work at all on that very day, I will wipe out that person from among his people.
23:31 Do no work at all. This is a rule for all time, through all your generations, wherever you live.
23:32 It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you are to humble yourselves. On the ninth day of the month, in the evening, from evening to evening, you are to keep your Sabbath."
## The Feast of Booths
23:33 The LORD spoke to Moses:
23:34 "Speak to the Israelites: On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the Feast of Booths, seven days for the LORD.
23:35 On the first day there is a holy gathering. Do no daily work.
23:36 For seven days present an offering by fire to the LORD. On the eighth day hold a holy gathering, and present an offering by fire to the LORD. It is the closing assembly. Do no daily work.
23:37 These are the LORD's set times, which you are to proclaim as holy gatherings, for presenting offerings by fire to the LORD: burnt offering and grain offering, sacrifice and drink offerings, what belongs to each day on its own day.
23:38 These are besides the LORD's Sabbaths, besides your gifts, besides all your vows, and besides all your freewill gifts that you give to the LORD.
23:39 Mark this: on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have gathered in what the land produces, celebrate the LORD's feast for seven days. The first day is a day of rest, and the eighth day is a day of rest.
23:40 On the first day take for yourselves the fruit of beautiful trees, palm fronds, branches of thick-leaved trees and poplars of the valley stream. Rejoice before the LORD your God for seven days.
23:41 Celebrate it as a feast for the LORD seven days in the year. This is a rule for all time, through all your generations. Celebrate it in the seventh month.
23:42 Live in booths for seven days. Everyone born in Israel is to live in booths,
23:43 so that your descendants will know that I made the Israelites live in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God."
23:44 So Moses told the Israelites the LORD's set times.

## The man who cursed the Name
24:10 Now the son of an Israelite woman, whose father was an Egyptian, went out among the Israelites. In the camp, a fight broke out between the Israelite woman's son and a man of Israel.
24:11 The Israelite woman's son spoke the Name with contempt and cursed, so they brought him to Moses. His mother's name was Shelomith daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.
24:12 They kept him under guard, so that the LORD's own word would make the case clear to them.
24:13 The LORD spoke to Moses:
24:14 "Take the one who cursed outside the camp. All who heard him are to lean their hands on his head, and the whole community is to stone him.
24:15 And speak to the Israelites: Anyone who curses his God will carry his sin.
24:16 Whoever speaks the name of the LORD with contempt must be put to death. The whole community must stone him. Foreigner or native-born, when he speaks the Name with contempt, he must be put to death.
## Life for life
24:17 Anyone who strikes down any human life must be put to death.
24:18 Whoever strikes down an animal's life must make it good, life for life.
24:19 If anyone puts a defect on his fellow citizen, as he has done, so it will be done to him:
24:20 broken bone for broken bone, eye for eye, tooth for tooth. As he put a defect on a person, so it will be put on him.
24:21 Whoever strikes down an animal must make it good, but whoever strikes down a person must be put to death.
24:22 You are to have one law for foreigner and native-born alike, for I am the LORD your God."
24:23 Then Moses spoke to the Israelites, and they took the one who had cursed outside the camp and stoned him with stones. The Israelites did as the LORD had commanded Moses.
## A Sabbath for the land
25:1 The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai:
25:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you come into the land that I am giving you, the land is to rest, a Sabbath for the LORD.
25:3 For six years sow your field, and for six years prune your vineyard and gather in what it yields.
25:4 But in the seventh year the land is to have a Sabbath of complete rest, a Sabbath for the LORD. Do not sow your field, and do not prune your vineyard.
25:5 Do not reap what springs up on its own from your last harvest, and do not pick the grapes of your untrimmed vines. It is to be a year of complete rest for the land.
25:6 What the land gives in its Sabbath will be food for all of you: for you, your slave and your slave woman, your hired worker and the lodger who lives with you,
25:7 and for your livestock and the wild animals in your land. All that it yields may be eaten.
## The Jubilee
25:8 Count off seven Sabbaths of years, seven years seven times, so that the time of the seven Sabbaths of years comes to forty-nine years.
25:9 Then send the blast of the ram's horn through the land in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month. On the Day of Atonement send the horn through all your land.
25:10 Make the fiftieth year holy, and proclaim release in the land for all who live in it. It will be a Jubilee for you. Each of you will go back to his own holding, and each of you will go back to his clan.
25:11 That fiftieth year will be a Jubilee for you. Do not sow, and do not reap what springs up on its own, and do not pick the grapes of its untrimmed vines.
25:12 For it is a Jubilee. It is to be holy to you. Eat what the field gives straight from the field.
25:13 In this Year of Jubilee each of you is to go back to his own holding.
## Fair dealing
25:14 When you sell something to your fellow citizen, or buy from his hand, do not wrong one another.
25:15 Buy from your fellow citizen by counting the years since the Jubilee, and he is to sell to you by counting the years of crops.
25:16 When the years are many, raise the price, and when the years are few, lower the price, because what he is selling you is a number of crops.
25:17 Do not wrong one another. Fear your God, for I am the LORD your God.
25:18 Do my rules, and keep my rulings and do them, and you will live in the land safely.
25:19 The land will give its fruit, and you will eat your fill and live on it safely.
25:20 And if you ask, 'What will we eat in the seventh year, if we may not sow or gather in our crops?'
25:21 I will order my blessing for you in the sixth year, and it will produce crops enough for three years.
25:22 When you sow in the eighth year, you will still be eating from the old crops. Until the ninth year, until its crops come in, you will eat the old.
## The land is mine
25:23 The land must not be sold for good, because the land is mine. You are foreigners and lodgers living with me.
25:24 Throughout the land you hold, you must allow the land to be bought back.
25:25 If your brother becomes poor and sells some of his holding, his closest family redeemer is to come and buy back what his brother sold.
25:26 If a man has no one to redeem it, but he himself prospers and finds enough to buy it back,
25:27 he is to count the years since the sale, pay back what remains to the man he sold it to, and go back to his holding.
25:28 But if he cannot find enough to pay him back, what he sold will stay in the hands of the buyer until the Year of Jubilee. In the Jubilee it will go free, and he will go back to his holding.
## Houses in towns and villages
25:29 If a man sells a house to live in, in a walled city, his right to buy it back lasts until a full year after he sold it. For a year he has the right to buy it back.
25:30 If it is not bought back before a full year is over, the house in the city that has a wall will belong for good to the one who bought it, and to his descendants. It will not go free in the Jubilee.
25:31 But houses in villages that have no wall around them are to be counted as part of the open fields of the land. They may be bought back, and they go free in the Jubilee.
25:32 As for the cities of the Levites, the houses in the cities they hold, the Levites will always have the right to buy them back.
25:33 Whatever someone buys from the Levites, a house sold in a city they hold, will go free in the Jubilee, because the houses in the Levites' cities are their holding among the Israelites.
25:34 But the pastureland around their cities must not be sold, because it is their holding for all time.
## When your brother grows poor
25:35 If your brother becomes poor and his hand slips while he is with you, hold him up. Like a foreigner or a lodger he is to live on with you.
25:36 Do not take interest or profit from him. Fear your God, and let your brother live on with you.
25:37 Do not lend him your silver at interest, and do not give him your food for profit.
25:38 I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to give you the land of Canaan, to be your God.
25:39 If your brother becomes poor while with you and sells himself to you, do not make him work the work of a slave.
25:40 He is to be with you like a hired worker or a lodger. He will work for you until the Year of Jubilee.
25:41 Then he will go out from you, he and his children with him. He will go back to his clan, and to the holding of his fathers he will go back.
25:42 For they are my servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt. They must not be sold the way a slave is sold.
25:43 Do not rule over him with crushing cruelty. Fear your God.
25:44 As for the slaves you may have, men and women: from the nations around you, from them you may buy slaves, men and women.
25:45 You may also buy from the children of the lodgers who live among you, and from their clans who are with you, whom they have fathered in your land. They may become your property.
25:46 You may pass them on to your children after you, to inherit as property. You may make them work as slaves for good. But over your brothers, the Israelites, no one may rule over his brother with crushing cruelty.
25:47 If a foreigner or lodger among you prospers, and your brother beside him becomes poor and sells himself to the foreigner or lodger among you, or to a member of the foreigner's clan,
25:48 after he has sold himself he still has the right to be bought back. One of his brothers may redeem him,
25:49 or his uncle or his uncle's son may redeem him, or any relative of his own flesh from his clan may redeem him. Or if he prospers, he may redeem himself.
25:50 He and the one who bought him will count from the year he sold himself to him until the Year of Jubilee. The price of his sale is to match the number of years; his time with the buyer will be counted like a hired worker's days.
25:51 If many years still remain, he is to pay back for his redemption a matching share of the price he was bought for.
25:52 If only a few years remain until the Year of Jubilee, he is to count them, and pay back for his redemption in keeping with his years.
25:53 He is to be with him like a worker hired year by year. The buyer must not rule over him with crushing cruelty before your eyes.
25:54 If he is not redeemed in any of these ways, he will go out in the Year of Jubilee, he and his children with him.
25:55 For the Israelites are servants to me. They are my servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.
## No idols
26:1 Do not make no-gods for yourselves. Do not set up a carved image or a standing stone for yourselves, and do not place a stone carved with figures in your land to bow down to it. For I am the LORD your God.
26:2 Keep my Sabbaths and fear my holy place. I am the LORD.
## If you walk in my rules
26:3 If you walk in my rules, and keep my commands and do them,
26:4 I will give you your rains at their proper time. The land will give its crops, and the trees of the field will give their fruit.
26:5 Your threshing will last until the grape harvest, and the grape harvest until sowing time. You will eat your bread and have plenty, and you will live safe in your land.
26:6 I will give peace in the land. You will lie down with no one to frighten you. I will rid the land of evil beasts, and no sword will pass through your land.
26:7 You will chase your enemies, and they will fall by the sword in front of you.
26:8 Five of you will chase a hundred, and a hundred of you will chase ten thousand. Your enemies will fall by the sword in front of you.
26:9 I will turn toward you. I will make you bear fruit and grow many, and I will uphold my covenant with you.
26:10 You will eat old grain long stored, and you will have to bring out the old to make way for the new.
26:11 I will set my dwelling among you, and my soul will not loathe you.
26:12 I will walk about among you. I will be your God, and you will be my people.
26:13 I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt so that you would not be their slaves. I broke the bars of your yoke and made you walk upright.
## If you will not listen
26:14 But if you will not listen to me, and do not do all these commands,
26:15 if you reject my rules, and your soul loathes my rulings, so that you do not do all my commands, and you break my covenant,
26:16 then I in turn will do this to you: I will bring on you sudden terror, wasting disease and fever, which make the eyes fail and the life drain away. You will sow your seed for nothing, because your enemies will eat it.
26:17 I will set my face against you, and you will be beaten in front of your enemies. Those who hate you will be your masters, and you will flee when no one is after you.
26:18 And if even after this you will not listen to me, I will go on to discipline you seven times over for your sins.
26:19 I will break your proud strength. I will make your sky like iron and your ground like bronze.
26:20 Your strength will be used up for nothing. Your land will not give its crops, and the trees of the land will not give their fruit.
26:21 If you go on walking against me and are not willing to listen to me, I will add more blows on you, seven times over, to match your sins.
26:22 I will send the wild animals among you, and they will leave you childless, wipe out your livestock, and make you few, so that your roads lie empty.
26:23 If even after these things you will not let me correct you, and you go on walking against me,
26:24 then I too will walk against you. I myself will strike you seven times over for your sins.
26:25 I will bring a sword on you that takes the revenge of the covenant. When you crowd into your cities, I will send plague among you, and you will be handed over to the enemy.
26:26 When I break your staff of bread, ten women will bake your bread in one oven and give it back to you by weight. You will eat, but you will not be satisfied.
26:27 And if in spite of this you will not listen to me, and you go on walking against me,
26:28 then I will walk against you in burning anger, and I myself will discipline you seven times over for your sins.
26:29 You will eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters you will eat.
26:30 I will wipe out your high places and cut down your incense stands. I will throw your corpses on the corpses of your idols, and my soul will loathe you.
26:31 I will turn your cities into ruins and make your holy places desolate, and I will not smell your soothing smells.
26:32 I myself will make the land desolate, so that your enemies who come to live in it will be stunned at it.
26:33 And you I will scatter among the nations, and I will draw out the sword after you. Your land will become a desolation, and your cities will become ruins.
## The land gets its Sabbaths
26:34 Then the land will make up for its Sabbaths, all the days it lies desolate while you are in your enemies' land. Then the land will rest and make up for its Sabbaths.
26:35 All the days it lies desolate it will rest, the rest it did not get in your Sabbaths when you lived on it.
26:36 Into the hearts of those of you who remain I will bring despair, in the lands of their enemies. The sound of a leaf blown about will chase them. They will flee as one flees a sword, and they will fall with no one after them.
26:37 They will stumble over one another as if before a sword, with no one chasing. You will have no power to stand before your enemies.
26:38 You will perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies will eat you up.
26:39 And those of you who remain will rot away for their guilt in the lands of your enemies, and because of their fathers' guilt as well they will rot away with them.
## I will remember my covenant
26:40 Then they will confess their guilt and their fathers' guilt, the unfaithfulness they showed me, and also how they walked against me,
26:41 and that I too walked against them and brought them into the land of their enemies. And when their uncircumcised heart is brought low at last, and they make up for their guilt,
26:42 then I will remember my covenant with Jacob. Also my covenant with Isaac, and also my covenant with Abraham, I will remember, and the land I will remember.
26:43 For the land will be left empty of them and will make up for its Sabbaths while it lies desolate without them, and they will make up for their guilt, because, yes, because they rejected my rulings and their soul loathed my rules.
26:44 Yet even so, even while they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them, and I will not loathe them so as to finish them off and break my covenant with them. For I am the LORD their God.
26:45 For their sake I will remember the covenant with the first generation, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt before the eyes of the nations, to be their God. I am the LORD."
26:46 These are the rules, the rulings and the laws that the LORD set between himself and the Israelites on Mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.
## Vowing a person
27:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
27:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When someone makes an extraordinary vow to the LORD, pledging the value of persons,
27:3 the value for a male from twenty to sixty years old will be fifty shekels of silver, by the shekel of the holy place.
27:4 If it is a female, the value will be thirty shekels.
27:5 If the person is from five to twenty years old, the value will be twenty shekels for a male and ten shekels for a female.
27:6 If the person is from a month to five years old, the value will be five shekels of silver for a male, and for a female the value will be three shekels of silver.
27:7 If the person is sixty years old or more, the value will be fifteen shekels for a male and ten shekels for a female.
27:8 If he is too poor to pay the value you set, he is to bring the person to stand before the priest, and the priest will value him. The priest will value him by what the one who vowed can afford.
## Vowing animals, houses and fields
27:9 If it is an animal of the kind that people present as an offering to the LORD, anything someone gives of it to the LORD becomes holy.
27:10 He must not replace it or exchange it, good for bad or bad for good. If he does exchange one animal for another, then both it and the animal put in its place will be holy.
27:11 If it is any unclean animal, of a kind that people may not present as an offering to the LORD, he is to stand the animal before the priest,
27:12 and the priest will set its value, whether it is good or bad. Whatever value the priest sets, so it will be.
27:13 If he wants to buy it back, he must add a fifth to its value.
27:14 When someone sets apart his house as holy to the LORD, the priest will set its value, whether it is good or bad. Whatever value the priest sets, so it will stand.
27:15 If the one who set apart his house wants to buy it back, he must add a fifth to the silver of its value, and it will be his.
27:16 If someone sets apart for the LORD part of a field from his family holding, its value will be set by the seed it takes: a homer of barley seed at fifty shekels of silver.
27:17 If he sets apart his field from the Year of Jubilee on, the value will stand as set.
27:18 But if he sets apart his field after the Jubilee, the priest will figure the silver for him by the years left until the Year of Jubilee, and it will be taken off the value.
27:19 If the one who set apart the field wants to buy it back, he must add a fifth to the silver of its value, and it will stand as his.
27:20 But if he does not buy the field back, or if he has sold the field to another man, it can never be bought back.
27:21 When the field goes out in the Jubilee, it will be holy to the LORD, like a field under the ban. It will be the priest's holding.
27:22 If someone sets apart for the LORD a field he has bought, which is not part of his family holding,
27:23 the priest will figure for him the amount of the value up to the Year of Jubilee, and he will pay the value on that day, as a holy thing for the LORD.
27:24 In the Year of Jubilee the field will go back to the one he bought it from, the one whose holding in the land it was.
27:25 Every value is to be set by the shekel of the holy place: twenty gerahs to the shekel.
## What cannot be vowed
27:26 But a firstborn among the livestock, born first and so the LORD's, no one may set apart as holy. Ox or sheep, it already belongs to the LORD.
27:27 If it is one of the unclean animals, he may buy it back at its value, adding a fifth to it. If it is not bought back, it is to be sold at its value.
27:28 But anything a man puts under the ban for the LORD, from all that is his, whether a human being, an animal, or a field of his family holding, may not be sold and may not be bought back. Everything under the ban is especially holy to the LORD.
27:29 No human being who has been put under the ban may be ransomed. He must be put to death.
## Tithes
27:30 All the tithe of the land, from the seed of the land or the fruit of the trees, belongs to the LORD. It is holy to the LORD.
27:31 If anyone wants to buy back some of his tithe, he must add a fifth to it.
27:32 All the tithe of herd and flock, every tenth one of all that pass under the rod, will be holy to the LORD.
27:33 He must not pick and choose between good and bad, and he must not trade it. If he does trade it, both it and the one traded for it will be holy. It cannot be bought back."
27:34 These are the commands that the LORD gave Moses for the Israelites on Mount Sinai.
