## The burnt offering
1:1 The LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the tent of meeting. He said,
1:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When any one of you presents an offering to the LORD, present your offering from the livestock, from the herd or from the flock.
1:3 "If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, he is to present a flawless male. He is to present it at the entrance of the tent of meeting, so that it wins him acceptance before the LORD.
1:4 He is to lean his hand on the head of the burnt offering. Then it will be accepted for him, to make atonement for him.
1:5 He is to slaughter the young bull before the LORD. Aaron's sons, the priests, are to present the blood and splash it all around the altar that is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
1:6 He is to skin the burnt offering and cut it into pieces.
1:7 The sons of Aaron the priest are to put fire on the altar and lay wood in order on the fire.
1:8 Then Aaron's sons, the priests, are to lay the pieces, the head and the suet in order on the wood burning on the altar.
1:9 He is to wash the insides and the legs with water. Then the priest is to make all of it go up in smoke on the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
1:10 "If his offering for a burnt offering is from the flock, from the sheep or from the goats, he is to present a flawless male.
1:11 He is to slaughter it on the north side of the altar before the LORD. Aaron's sons, the priests, are to splash its blood all around the altar.
1:12 He is to cut it into pieces, with its head and its suet. The priest is to lay them in order on the wood burning on the altar.
1:13 He is to wash the insides and the legs with water. The priest is to present all of it and make it go up in smoke on the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
1:14 "If his offering to the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, he is to present turtledoves or young doves.
1:15 The priest is to bring it to the altar, nip off its head and make it go up in smoke on the altar. Its blood is to be drained out on the side of the altar.
1:16 He is to remove its crop with its feathers and throw it beside the altar on the east side, where the fatty ashes are.
1:17 He is to tear it open by its wings, but not split it in two. Then the priest is to make it go up in smoke on the altar, on the wood that is burning. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
## The grain offering
2:1 "When anyone presents a grain offering to the LORD, his offering is to be fine flour. He is to pour oil on it and put frankincense on it.
2:2 He is to bring it to Aaron's sons, the priests. The priest is to take from it a full handful of the fine flour and oil, with all the frankincense. He is to make this token portion go up in smoke on the altar. It is an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
2:3 The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the LORD's offerings by fire.
2:4 "When you present a grain offering baked in an oven, it is to be made of fine flour: round loaves without yeast mixed with oil, or thin wafers without yeast spread with oil.
2:5 If your offering is a grain offering cooked on the griddle, it is to be fine flour mixed with oil, without yeast.
2:6 Break it into pieces and pour oil on it. It is a grain offering.
2:7 If your offering is a grain offering cooked in the pan, it is to be made of fine flour with oil.
2:8 Bring to the LORD the grain offering made in any of these ways. It is to be presented to the priest, and he is to bring it to the altar.
2:9 The priest is to lift the token portion from the grain offering and make it go up in smoke on the altar. It is an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
2:10 The rest of the grain offering belongs to Aaron and his sons. It is a most holy part of the LORD's offerings by fire.
## No yeast, no honey, always salt
2:11 "No grain offering that you present to the LORD is to be made with yeast. You must not make any yeast or any honey go up in smoke as an offering by fire to the LORD.
2:12 You may present them to the LORD as an offering of first things, but they must not go up on the altar as a soothing smell.
2:13 Season every grain offering you bring with salt. Do not leave the salt of your God's covenant out of your grain offering. With all your offerings, present salt.
2:14 "If you present to the LORD a grain offering of firstfruits, present young ears of grain roasted in the fire, crushed kernels of new grain. This is the grain offering of your firstfruits.
2:15 Put oil on it and place frankincense on it. It is a grain offering.
2:16 The priest is to make its token portion go up in smoke: some of the crushed grain and some of the oil, with all the frankincense. It is an offering by fire to the LORD.
## The peace offering
3:1 "If his offering is a sacrifice of peace offering, and he presents an animal from the herd, male or female, he is to present a flawless one before the LORD.
3:2 He is to lean his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of meeting. Aaron's sons, the priests, are to splash the blood all around the altar.
3:3 From the sacrifice of peace offering he is to present an offering by fire to the LORD: the fat that covers the insides and all the fat on the insides,
3:4 the two kidneys with the fat on them by the lower back, and the lobe on the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.
3:5 Aaron's sons are to make it go up in smoke on the altar, on top of the burnt offering that lies on the burning wood. It is an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
3:6 "If his offering for a sacrifice of peace offering to the LORD is from the flock, male or female, he is to present a flawless one.
3:7 If he presents a sheep as his offering, he is to present it before the LORD.
3:8 He is to lean his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting. Aaron's sons are to splash its blood all around the altar.
3:9 From the sacrifice of peace offering he is to present its fat as an offering by fire to the LORD. This is the whole fat tail, which he is to remove close to the backbone, the fat that covers the insides and all the fat on the insides,
3:10 the two kidneys with the fat on them by the lower back, and the lobe on the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.
3:11 The priest is to make it go up in smoke on the altar. It is food, an offering by fire to the LORD.
3:12 "If his offering is a goat, he is to present it before the LORD.
3:13 He is to lean his hand on its head and slaughter it in front of the tent of meeting. Aaron's sons are to splash its blood all around the altar.
3:14 From it he is to present his offering, an offering by fire to the LORD: the fat that covers the insides and all the fat on the insides,
3:15 the two kidneys with the fat on them by the lower back, and the lobe on the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.
3:16 The priest is to make them go up in smoke on the altar. It is food, an offering by fire, a soothing smell. All the fat belongs to the LORD.
3:17 This is a rule for all time, for all your generations, wherever you live: you must not eat any fat or any blood."
## The sin offering
4:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
4:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say: Someone may sin by mistake and do any one of the things the LORD commanded not to be done.
## When the anointed priest sins
4:3 If the anointed priest sins and brings guilt on the people, he is to present to the LORD a flawless young bull as a sin offering for the sin he has committed.
4:4 He is to bring the bull to the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD. He is to lean his hand on the bull's head and slaughter it before the LORD.
4:5 Then the anointed priest is to take some of the bull's blood and bring it into the tent of meeting.
4:6 The priest is to dip his finger in the blood and spatter some of it seven times before the LORD, in front of the veil of the holy place.
4:7 The priest is to put some of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense, which stands before the LORD in the tent of meeting. He is to pour out all the rest of the bull's blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
4:8 He is to remove all the fat from the bull of the sin offering: the fat that covers the insides and all the fat on the insides,
4:9 the two kidneys with the fat on them by the lower back, and the lobe on the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.
4:10 It is removed just as it is from the ox of the peace offering. The priest is to make it go up in smoke on the altar of burnt offering.
4:11 But the bull's hide and all its meat, with its head, its legs, its insides and its dung,
4:12 all the rest of the bull, he is to take outside the camp to a clean place, where the fatty ashes are poured out. There he is to burn it on a wood fire, on the ash heap.
## When the whole community sins
4:13 "The whole community of Israel may go astray without the assembly knowing it. They may do one of the things the LORD commanded not to be done, and become guilty.
4:14 When the sin they committed becomes known, the assembly is to present a young bull as a sin offering. They are to bring it in front of the tent of meeting.
4:15 The elders of the community are to lean their hands on the bull's head before the LORD, and the bull is to be slaughtered before the LORD.
4:16 The anointed priest is to bring some of the bull's blood into the tent of meeting.
4:17 The priest is to dip his finger in the blood and spatter it seven times before the LORD, in front of the veil.
4:18 He is to put some of the blood on the horns of the altar that stands before the LORD in the tent of meeting. He is to pour out all the rest of the blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
4:19 He is to remove all its fat and make it go up in smoke on the altar.
4:20 He is to do with this bull just what he did with the bull of the sin offering. The priest will make atonement for them, and they will be forgiven.
4:21 He is to take the bull outside the camp and burn it, as he burned the first bull. It is the sin offering for the assembly.
## When a leader sins
4:22 "A leader may sin by mistake and do one of the things the LORD his God commanded not to be done, and become guilty.
4:23 When he is told of the sin he has committed, he is to bring as his offering a flawless male goat.
4:24 He is to lean his hand on the goat's head and slaughter it before the LORD, in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered. It is a sin offering.
4:25 The priest is to take some of the blood of the sin offering on his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He is to pour out the rest of its blood at the base of the altar of burnt offering.
4:26 He is to make all its fat go up in smoke on the altar, like the fat of the peace offering. The priest will make atonement for him for his sin, and he will be forgiven.
## When one of the common people sins
4:27 "One of the common people of the land may sin by mistake and do one of the things the LORD commanded not to be done, and become guilty.
4:28 When he is told of the sin he has committed, he is to bring as his offering a flawless female goat for that sin.
4:29 He is to lean his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it in the place of the burnt offering.
4:30 The priest is to take some of its blood on his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He is to pour out all the rest of its blood at the base of the altar.
4:31 He is to remove all its fat, just as the fat is removed from the peace offering. The priest is to make it go up in smoke on the altar as a soothing smell to the LORD. The priest will make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.
4:32 "If he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, he is to bring a flawless female.
4:33 He is to lean his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it as a sin offering, in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered.
4:34 The priest is to take some of the blood of the sin offering on his finger and put it on the horns of the altar of burnt offering. He is to pour out all the rest of its blood at the base of the altar.
4:35 He is to remove all its fat, just as the fat of the sheep is removed from the peace offering. The priest is to make it go up in smoke on the altar, on top of the LORD's offerings by fire. The priest will make atonement for him for the sin he has committed, and he will be forgiven.
## Sins of silence, touch and rash oaths
5:1 "Someone may sin in this way: he hears the public curse calling for witnesses, and he is a witness because he saw or knew something, but he does not tell. Then he will carry his guilt.
5:2 Or someone may touch an unclean thing, such as the dead body of an unclean wild animal, of unclean livestock or of an unclean swarming thing. Even if he did not know it, he is unclean and has become guilty.
5:3 Or he may touch human uncleanness, any kind by which a person becomes unclean. He did not know it, but when he finds out, he has become guilty.
5:4 Or someone may swear an oath, speaking rashly with his lips about doing harm or doing good, in any matter where people swear rashly. He did not realize it, but when he finds out, he has become guilty in one of these.
5:5 When he becomes guilty in one of these, he is to confess the sin he has committed.
5:6 He is to bring to the LORD his guilt payment for the sin he committed. It is to be a female from the flock, a lamb or a she-goat, as a sin offering. The priest will make atonement for him for his sin.
## Offerings for the poor
5:7 "If he cannot afford a sheep, he is to bring to the LORD two turtledoves or two young doves as his guilt payment for his sin. One is for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering.
5:8 He is to bring them to the priest. The priest is to present first the one for the sin offering. He is to nip off its head at the front of its neck, but not pull it apart.
5:9 He is to spatter some of the blood of the sin offering on the side of the altar. The rest of the blood is to be drained out at the base of the altar. It is a sin offering.
5:10 He is to offer the second as a burnt offering, following the rule. The priest will make atonement for him for the sin he committed, and he will be forgiven.
5:11 "If he cannot afford two turtledoves or two young doves, he is to bring as his offering for his sin a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a sin offering. He is not to put oil or frankincense on it, because it is a sin offering.
5:12 He is to bring it to the priest. The priest is to take a full handful of it as its token portion and make it go up in smoke on the altar, on top of the LORD's offerings by fire. It is a sin offering.
5:13 The priest will make atonement for him for the sin he committed in any of these matters, and he will be forgiven. The rest will belong to the priest, as with the grain offering."
## The guilt offering
5:14 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
5:15 "Someone may act unfaithfully and sin by mistake against the LORD's holy things. He is to bring to the LORD as his guilt offering a flawless ram from the flock. Its value is set in silver shekels, by the shekel of the sanctuary.
5:16 He is to repay what he took from the holy thing, add a fifth to it, and give it to the priest. The priest will make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and he will be forgiven.
5:17 "Someone may sin and do one of the things the LORD commanded not to be done, without knowing it. He has still become guilty and will carry his guilt.
5:18 He is to bring the priest a flawless ram from the flock, at the set value, as a guilt offering. The priest will make atonement for him for the mistake he made without knowing it, and he will be forgiven.
5:19 It is a guilt offering. He has surely become guilty before the LORD."
## Wrongs against a neighbor
6:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
6:2 "Someone may sin and act unfaithfully against the LORD by cheating his neighbor. He may deny holding something left in his care or given as a pledge, or he may rob or oppress his neighbor.
6:3 Or he may find something lost and deny it. He may swear falsely about any of the things people do that are sins.
6:4 When he has sinned in this way and become guilty, he must give it back. He is to return what he robbed, or what he got by oppression, or what was left in his care, or the lost thing he found,
6:5 or anything he swore falsely about. He is to repay it in full and add a fifth to it. He is to give it to its owner on the day he admits his guilt.
6:6 Then he is to bring to the priest his guilt offering to the LORD, a flawless ram from the flock at the set value.
6:7 The priest will make atonement for him before the LORD, and he will be forgiven for any of the things he did that made him guilty."
## Instructions for the priests: the burnt offering
6:8 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
6:9 "Command Aaron and his sons: This is the procedure for the burnt offering. The burnt offering is to stay on the altar's hearth all night until morning, and the altar's fire is to keep burning on it.
6:10 The priest is to put on his linen garment, with linen shorts on his body. He is to lift up the fatty ashes left by the fire that burned the burnt offering on the altar, and place them beside the altar.
6:11 Then he is to take off these garments and put on others, and carry the fatty ashes outside the camp to a clean place.
6:12 The fire on the altar is to keep burning. It must not go out. Every morning the priest is to add wood to it, lay the burnt offering in order on it, and make the fat of the peace offerings go up in smoke on it.
6:13 Fire is to keep burning on the altar without a break. It is not to go out.
## The grain offering
6:14 "This is the procedure for the grain offering. Aaron's sons are to present it before the LORD, in front of the altar.
6:15 A priest is to take a handful of the fine flour and oil of the grain offering, with all its frankincense. He is to make it go up in smoke on the altar as its token portion for the LORD, a soothing smell.
6:16 Aaron and his sons are to eat the rest of it. It is to be eaten without yeast in a holy place. They are to eat it in the courtyard of the tent of meeting.
6:17 It must not be baked with yeast. I have given it to them as their share of my offerings by fire. It is most holy, like the sin offering and the guilt offering.
6:18 Any male among Aaron's descendants may eat it. It is their set portion for all time, through your generations, from the LORD's offerings by fire. Whatever touches them will become holy."
## The grain offering of the priests
6:19 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
6:20 "This is the offering that Aaron and his sons are to present to the LORD on the day he is anointed. It is a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a regular grain offering, half in the morning and half in the evening.
6:21 It is to be made with oil on a griddle. Bring it well mixed, and present it broken in pieces as a grain offering, a soothing smell for the LORD.
6:22 The priest from among his sons who is anointed to take his place is to prepare it. It is the LORD's set portion for all time, and all of it is to go up in smoke.
6:23 Every grain offering of a priest is to be wholly burned. It must not be eaten."
## The sin offering
6:24 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
6:25 "Speak to Aaron and his sons and say: This is the procedure for the sin offering. The sin offering is to be slaughtered before the LORD in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered. It is most holy.
6:26 The priest who offers it for sin is to eat it. It is to be eaten in a holy place, in the courtyard of the tent of meeting.
6:27 Whatever touches its meat will become holy. If any of its blood is spattered on a garment, you are to wash the spattered part in a holy place.
6:28 The clay pot it is boiled in is to be broken. If it is boiled in a bronze pot, the pot is to be scoured and rinsed with water.
6:29 Any male among the priests may eat it. It is most holy.
6:30 But if any of a sin offering's blood is brought into the tent of meeting to make atonement in the holy place, that offering must not be eaten. It is to be burned with fire.
## The guilt offering
7:1 "This is the procedure for the guilt offering. It is most holy.
7:2 The guilt offering is to be slaughtered in the place where the burnt offering is slaughtered, and its blood is to be splashed all around the altar.
7:3 All its fat is to be presented: the fat tail, the fat that covers the insides,
7:4 the two kidneys with the fat on them by the lower back, and the lobe on the liver, which he is to remove with the kidneys.
7:5 The priest is to make them go up in smoke on the altar as an offering by fire to the LORD. It is a guilt offering.
7:6 Any male among the priests may eat it. It is to be eaten in a holy place. It is most holy.
## What belongs to the priest
7:7 The guilt offering is like the sin offering. The same procedure applies to both. It belongs to the priest who makes atonement with it.
7:8 When a priest presents someone's burnt offering, the hide of that burnt offering belongs to him.
7:9 Every grain offering baked in the oven, and every one made in the pan or on the griddle, belongs to the priest who presents it.
7:10 But every grain offering mixed with oil, or dry, belongs to all Aaron's sons, to each one equally.
## The peace offering
7:11 "This is the procedure for the sacrifice of peace offering that someone presents to the LORD.
7:12 If he presents it to give thanks, he is to present bread along with the thanks sacrifice. It is to be round loaves without yeast mixed with oil, thin wafers without yeast spread with oil, and round loaves of fine flour well mixed with oil.
7:13 Along with his thanks peace offering he is to present round loaves of bread made with yeast.
7:14 From each kind of offering he is to present one loaf as a contribution to the LORD. It belongs to the priest who splashes the blood of the peace offering.
7:15 The meat of his thanks peace offering is to be eaten on the day it is offered. He must not leave any of it until morning.
7:16 "If his sacrifice is for a vow or is a freewill offering, it is to be eaten on the day he presents it, and what is left may be eaten the next day.
7:17 Whatever meat of the sacrifice is left on the third day is to be burned with fire.
7:18 If any meat of his peace offering is eaten on the third day, the one who presented it will not be accepted. It will not be credited to him. It will be foul meat, and whoever eats any of it will carry his guilt.
7:19 "Meat that touches anything unclean must not be eaten. It is to be burned with fire. Anyone who is clean may eat the other meat.
7:20 But if a person eats meat from the LORD's peace offering while he is unclean, that person is to be cut off from his people.
7:21 Someone may touch anything unclean: human uncleanness, an unclean animal or any unclean detestable thing. If he then eats meat from the LORD's peace offering, that person is to be cut off from his people."
## No fat and no blood
7:22 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
7:23 "Speak to the Israelites and say: You must not eat any fat of an ox, a sheep or a goat.
7:24 The fat of an animal that died or was torn by wild animals may be used for any other purpose, but you must never eat it.
7:25 Whoever eats the fat of an animal from which an offering by fire is presented to the LORD is to be cut off from his people.
7:26 Wherever you live, you must not eat any blood, whether of bird or of animal.
7:27 Anyone who eats any blood is to be cut off from his people."
## The priests' portion of the peace offering
7:28 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
7:29 "Speak to the Israelites and say: Whoever presents his peace offering to the LORD is to bring part of it to the LORD as his offering.
7:30 He is to bring the LORD's offerings by fire with his own hands. He is to bring the fat with the breast, and wave the breast as a wave offering before the LORD.
7:31 The priest is to make the fat go up in smoke on the altar, and the breast will belong to Aaron and his sons.
7:32 The right thigh you are to give to the priest as a contribution from your peace offerings.
7:33 The right thigh will belong as a portion to the son of Aaron who presents the blood and the fat of the peace offering.
7:34 From the Israelites' peace offerings I have taken the breast that is waved and the thigh that is contributed. I have given them to Aaron the priest and his sons as their set portion from the Israelites for all time."
## Summary of the offerings
7:35 This is the portion of Aaron and his sons from the LORD's offerings by fire, from the day they were presented to serve the LORD as priests.
7:36 On the day he anointed them, the LORD commanded the Israelites to give it to them. It is a set portion for all time, through their generations.
7:37 This is the procedure for the burnt offering, the grain offering, the sin offering, the guilt offering, the ordination offerings and the sacrifice of peace offering.
7:38 The LORD commanded it to Moses on Mount Sinai, on the day he commanded the Israelites to present their offerings to the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai.
## Moses ordains Aaron and his sons
8:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
8:2 "Take Aaron and his sons with him. Take the garments, the anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams and the basket of bread without yeast.
8:3 Then gather the whole community at the entrance of the tent of meeting."
8:4 Moses did just as the LORD commanded him, and the community gathered at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
8:5 Moses said to the community, "This is what the LORD has commanded to be done."
8:6 Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.
8:7 He put the tunic on Aaron and tied the sash around him. He dressed him in the robe and put the ephod on him. Then he tied the woven band of the ephod around him, fastening the ephod to him.
8:8 He placed the breastpiece on him and put the Urim and the Thummim into it.
8:9 He placed the turban on his head. On the front of the turban he placed the gold plate, the holy crown, just as the LORD commanded Moses.
8:10 Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tabernacle and everything in it, and so made them holy.
8:11 He spattered some of the oil on the altar seven times. He anointed the altar and all its tools, and the basin and its stand, to make them holy.
8:12 He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron's head and anointed him, to make him holy.
8:13 Then Moses brought Aaron's sons forward. He dressed them in tunics, tied sashes around them and fastened caps on them, just as the LORD commanded Moses.
## The sin offering and the burnt offering
8:14 He brought forward the bull for the sin offering. Aaron and his sons leaned their hands on its head.
8:15 He slaughtered it. Moses took the blood and with his finger put it on the horns all around the altar, and so cleansed the altar from sin. He poured out the blood at the base of the altar. So he made it holy, to make atonement on it.
8:16 He took all the fat on the insides, the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys with their fat. Moses made them go up in smoke on the altar.
8:17 He burned the rest of the bull, its hide, its meat and its dung, with fire outside the camp, just as the LORD commanded Moses.
8:18 Then he brought forward the ram for the burnt offering. Aaron and his sons leaned their hands on the ram's head.
8:19 He slaughtered it, and Moses splashed the blood all around the altar.
8:20 He cut the ram into pieces, and Moses made the head, the pieces and the suet go up in smoke.
8:21 He washed the insides and the legs with water, and Moses made the whole ram go up in smoke on the altar. It was a burnt offering, a soothing smell, an offering by fire to the LORD, just as the LORD commanded Moses.
## The ram of ordination
8:22 Then he brought forward the second ram, the ram of ordination. Aaron and his sons leaned their hands on the ram's head.
8:23 He slaughtered it. Moses took some of its blood and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear, on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot.
8:24 He brought Aaron's sons forward. Moses put some of the blood on the lobes of their right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands and on the big toes of their right feet. Then Moses splashed the blood all around the altar.
8:25 He took the fat, the fat tail, all the fat on the insides, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with their fat, and the right thigh.
8:26 From the basket of bread without yeast that was before the LORD, he took one round loaf without yeast, one round loaf made with oil, and one wafer. He placed them on the fat and on the right thigh.
8:27 He put all of it in the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved it as a wave offering before the LORD.
8:28 Then Moses took it from their hands and made it go up in smoke on the altar, on top of the burnt offering. It was an ordination offering, a soothing smell, an offering by fire to the LORD.
8:29 Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before the LORD. It was Moses' portion of the ram of ordination, just as the LORD commanded Moses.
8:30 Moses took some of the anointing oil and some of the blood on the altar. He spattered it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and their garments. So he made Aaron and his garments holy, and his sons and their garments with him.
## Seven days at the entrance
8:31 Moses said to Aaron and his sons, "Boil the meat at the entrance of the tent of meeting. Eat it there with the bread in the basket of ordination, just as I commanded, saying, 'Aaron and his sons are to eat it.'
8:32 Burn with fire whatever is left of the meat and the bread.
8:33 Do not leave the entrance of the tent of meeting for seven days, until your days of ordination are over. For it will take seven days to fill your hand.
8:34 The LORD has commanded that what was done today be done to make atonement for you.
8:35 Stay at the entrance of the tent of meeting day and night for seven days. Keep the LORD's charge, so that you do not die. For this is what I have been commanded."
8:36 Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD commanded through Moses.
## The eighth day
9:1 On the eighth day Moses called Aaron, his sons and the elders of Israel.
9:2 He said to Aaron, "Take a calf for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering, both flawless, and present them before the LORD.
9:3 Then tell the Israelites, 'Take a male goat for a sin offering, and a calf and a lamb, each a year old and flawless, for a burnt offering.

9:6 Moses said, "This is what the LORD has commanded you to do, so that the glory of the LORD may appear to you."

9:17 He presented the grain offering. He took a handful of it and made it go up in smoke on the altar, in addition to the morning burnt offering.
9:18 He slaughtered the ox and the ram as the people's peace offerings. Aaron's sons handed him the blood, and he splashed it all around the altar.
9:19 They took the fat from the ox and the ram: the fat tail, the covering fat, the kidneys and the lobe of the liver.

9:21 Aaron waved the breasts and the right thigh as a wave offering before the LORD, just as Moses commanded.

9:22 Aaron lifted his hands toward the people and blessed them. Then he came down from offering the sin offering, the burnt offering and the peace offerings.
9:23 Moses and Aaron went into the tent of meeting. When they came out, they blessed the people, and the glory of the LORD appeared to all the people.
9:24 Fire came out from before the LORD and ate up the burnt offering and the fat on the altar. When all the people saw it, they shouted for joy and fell on their faces.
## Nadab and Abihu
10:1 Aaron's sons Nadab and Abihu each took his fire pan, put fire in it and placed incense on it. They presented before the LORD strange fire, which he had not commanded them.
10:2 Fire came out from before the LORD and ate them up, and they died before the LORD.
10:3 Moses said to Aaron, "This is what the LORD meant when he said, 'In those who come near me I will show myself holy; / in the sight of all the people I will gain glory.'" And Aaron was silent.
10:4 Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Aaron's uncle Uzziel. He said to them, "Come here. Carry your relatives away from the front of the sanctuary to a place outside the camp."
10:5 They came and carried them, still in their tunics, outside the camp, just as Moses had said.
10:6 Moses said to Aaron and his sons Eleazar and Ithamar, "Do not let your hair hang loose or tear your clothes. Otherwise you will die, and the LORD will be angry with the whole community. But your relatives, the whole house of Israel, may weep over the burning the LORD has caused.
10:7 Do not leave the entrance of the tent of meeting, or you will die, for the LORD's anointing oil is on you." They did as Moses said.
## No wine in the tent
10:8 The LORD spoke to Aaron. He said,
10:9 "You and your sons are not to drink wine or beer when you go into the tent of meeting, so that you do not die. This is a rule for all time, through your generations.
10:10 You must separate the holy from the ordinary, and the unclean from the clean.
10:11 You must teach the Israelites all the rules the LORD has given them through Moses."
## The priests' portions after the deaths
10:12 Moses spoke to Aaron and his remaining sons, Eleazar and Ithamar. "Take the grain offering that is left over from the LORD's offerings by fire. Eat it without yeast beside the altar, for it is most holy.
10:13 Eat it in a holy place. It is your set portion and your sons' portion of the LORD's offerings by fire, for this is what I have been commanded.
10:14 But you and your sons and daughters may eat the breast that is waved and the thigh that is contributed in any clean place. They have been given to you and your children as your portion of the Israelites' peace offerings.
10:15 The thigh that is contributed and the breast that is waved are to be brought with the fat for the offerings by fire, and waved as a wave offering before the LORD. They will belong to you and your sons as a set portion for all time, just as the LORD has commanded."
10:16 Then Moses searched hard for the goat of the sin offering and found that it had been burned. He was angry with Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's remaining sons. He said,
10:17 "Why did you not eat the sin offering in the holy place? It is most holy. The LORD gave it to you to carry the guilt of the community, to make atonement for them before the LORD.
10:18 Look, its blood was not brought inside the holy place. You should certainly have eaten it in the holy place, just as I commanded."
10:19 Aaron said to Moses, "Look, today they offered their sin offering and their burnt offering before the LORD, and yet such things as these have happened to me. If I had eaten the sin offering today, would the LORD have been pleased?"
10:20 When Moses heard this, he was satisfied.
## Land animals
11:1 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said to them,
11:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say: Of all the animals on the land, these are the creatures you may eat.
11:3 You may eat any animal that has a split hoof, divided all the way through, and that chews the cud.
11:4 But some animals only chew the cud or only have a split hoof, and you must not eat them. The camel chews the cud but does not have a split hoof. It is unclean for you.
11:5 The rock hyrax chews the cud but does not have a split hoof. It is unclean for you.
11:6 The hare chews the cud but does not have a split hoof. It is unclean for you.
11:7 The pig has a split hoof, divided all the way through, but it does not chew the cud. It is unclean for you.
11:8 You must not eat their meat or touch their dead bodies. They are unclean for you.
## Creatures of the water
11:9 "Of all the creatures in the water, you may eat these: anything in the seas or the streams that has fins and scales.
11:10 But anything in the seas or the streams that does not have fins and scales is detestable to you. This includes all the swarming things of the water and every other living creature in it.
11:11 They are to be detestable to you. You must not eat their meat, and you are to treat their dead bodies as detestable.
11:12 Anything in the water that does not have fins and scales is detestable to you.
## Birds
11:13 "Among the birds, you are to treat these as detestable. They must not be eaten, for they are detestable: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,
11:14 the kite, any kind of falcon,
11:15 any kind of raven,
11:16 the ostrich, the nighthawk, the gull, any kind of hawk,
11:17 the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl,
11:18 the barn owl, the desert owl, the carrion vulture,
11:19 the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe and the bat.
## Swarming things with wings
11:20 "Every winged swarming thing that walks on all fours is detestable to you.
11:21 But among the winged swarming things that walk on all fours, you may eat those that have jointed legs above their feet for leaping on the ground.
11:22 Of these you may eat any kind of locust, bald locust, cricket or grasshopper.
11:23 Every other winged swarming thing that has four feet is detestable to you.
## Touching dead animals
11:24 "These will make you unclean. Whoever touches their dead bodies will be unclean until evening.
11:25 Whoever carries any of their dead bodies must wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening.
11:26 Every animal that has a hoof not split all the way through, or that does not chew the cud, is unclean for you. Whoever touches them will be unclean.
11:27 Every four-footed animal that walks on its paws is unclean for you. Whoever touches their dead bodies will be unclean until evening.
11:28 Whoever carries their dead bodies must wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening. They are unclean for you.
## Swarming things on the ground
11:29 "Among the swarming things that swarm on the land, these are unclean for you: the weasel, the mouse, any kind of great lizard,
11:30 the gecko, the monitor lizard, the wall lizard, the skink and the chameleon.
11:31 Of all the swarming things, these are unclean for you. Whoever touches them when they are dead will be unclean until evening.
11:32 When one of them dies and falls on something, that thing will be unclean. This applies to anything made of wood, cloth, leather or sackcloth, any object used for work. It must be put into water, and it will be unclean until evening. Then it will be clean.
11:33 If one of them falls into a clay pot, everything inside it will be unclean, and you must break the pot.
11:34 Any food that has had water on it will be unclean, and so will any drink in such a pot.
11:35 Anything that one of their dead bodies falls on will be unclean. An oven or a stove must be broken up. They are unclean, and you must treat them as unclean.
11:36 But a spring or a cistern that collects water will stay clean. Still, whoever touches their dead bodies will be unclean.
11:37 If one of their dead bodies falls on seed for sowing, the seed stays clean.
11:38 But if water has been put on the seed and one of their dead bodies falls on it, it is unclean for you.
## Animals that die
11:39 "If one of the animals you may eat dies, whoever touches its dead body will be unclean until evening.
11:40 Whoever eats any of its dead body must wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening. Whoever carries its dead body must wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening.
## Be holy, for I am holy
11:41 "Every swarming thing that swarms on the land is detestable. It must not be eaten.
11:42 You must not eat any of the swarming things on the land, whether it crawls on its belly, walks on all fours or has many feet. They are detestable.
11:43 Do not make yourselves detestable with any swarming thing. Do not make yourselves unclean with them and so become unclean.
11:44 For I am the LORD your God. Keep yourselves holy, and be holy, for I am holy. Do not make yourselves unclean with any swarming thing that creeps on the land.
11:45 For I am the LORD, who brought you up from the land of Egypt to be your God. So be holy, for I am holy.
11:46 "This is the procedure for land animals, birds, every living creature that moves in the water, and every creature that swarms on the land.
11:47 By it you are to separate the unclean from the clean, and the creatures that may be eaten from those that may not be eaten."
## After childbirth
12:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
12:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say: When a woman yields seed and bears a male, she will be unclean seven days. She will be unclean as she is during her monthly separation.
12:3 On the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin is to be circumcised.
12:4 Then for thirty-three days she is to stay in her blood purifying. She must not touch anything holy or come into the sanctuary until her days of purifying are over.
12:5 If she bears a female child, she will be unclean two weeks, as during her separation. Then for sixty-six days she is to stay in her blood purifying.
12:6 "When her days of purifying are over, for a son or a daughter, she is to bring a year-old lamb for a burnt offering, and a young dove or a turtledove for a sin offering. She is to bring them to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
12:7 He is to present it before the LORD and make atonement for her, and she will be clean from her flow of blood. This is the procedure for a woman who gives birth to a son or a daughter.
12:8 If she cannot afford a sheep, she is to take two turtledoves or two young doves, one for a burnt offering and one for a sin offering. The priest will make atonement for her, and she will be clean."
## Marks on the skin
13:1 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,
13:2 "Someone may have a swelling, flaking skin or a bright spot on his skin that turns into a mark of skin disease. He is to be brought to Aaron the priest or to one of his sons, the priests.
13:3 The priest is to look at the mark on the skin. If the hair in the mark has turned white, and the mark looks deeper than the skin, it is a mark of skin disease. When the priest has looked at him, he is to declare him unclean.
13:4 But the bright spot on his skin may be white without looking deeper than the skin, and its hair may not have turned white. Then the priest is to shut the person with the mark away for seven days.
13:5 On the seventh day the priest is to look at him. If he sees that the mark is unchanged and has not spread in the skin, he is to shut him away for seven more days.
13:6 On the seventh day the priest is to look at him again. If the mark has faded and has not spread in the skin, the priest is to declare him clean. It is only a rash. He is to wash his clothes, and he will be clean.
13:7 But if the rash spreads in the skin after he has shown himself to the priest to be declared clean, he is to show himself to the priest again.
13:8 The priest is to look. If the rash has spread in the skin, the priest is to declare him unclean. It is skin disease.
## Raw flesh
13:9 "When anyone has a mark of skin disease, he is to be brought to the priest.
13:10 The priest is to look. There may be a white swelling in the skin that has turned the hair white, with raw flesh in the swelling.
13:11 Then it is a long-standing skin disease in his skin, and the priest is to declare him unclean. He is not to shut him away, for he is already unclean.
13:12 But the skin disease may break out all over the skin, covering the person from head to foot, as far as the priest can see.
13:13 The priest is to look. If the skin disease has covered his whole body, he is to declare the person clean. He has turned all white, and he is clean.
13:14 But whenever raw flesh appears on him, he will be unclean.
13:15 The priest is to look at the raw flesh and declare him unclean. Raw flesh is unclean. It is skin disease.
13:16 But if the raw flesh changes and turns white again, he is to come to the priest.
13:17 The priest is to look at him. If the mark has turned white, the priest is to declare the person clean. He is clean.
## Boils and burns
13:18 "Someone may have a boil on his skin that has healed.
13:19 In the place of the boil there may be a white swelling or a reddish-white bright spot. He is to show himself to the priest.
13:20 The priest is to look. If it looks deeper than the skin and its hair has turned white, the priest is to declare him unclean. It is a mark of skin disease that has broken out in the boil.
13:21 But the priest may look and find no white hair in it, and it is not deeper than the skin, and it has faded. Then the priest is to shut him away for seven days.
13:22 If it spreads in the skin, the priest is to declare him unclean. It is a mark.
13:23 But if the bright spot stays where it is and does not spread, it is only the scar of the boil. The priest is to declare him clean.
13:24 "Or someone may have a burn on his skin, and the raw place of the burn becomes a reddish-white or white bright spot.
13:25 The priest is to look at it. If the hair in the bright spot has turned white, and it looks deeper than the skin, it is skin disease that has broken out in the burn. The priest is to declare him unclean. It is a mark of skin disease.
13:26 But the priest may look and find no white hair in the bright spot, and it is not deeper than the skin, and it has faded. Then the priest is to shut him away for seven days.
13:27 On the seventh day the priest is to look at him. If it has spread in the skin, the priest is to declare him unclean. It is a mark of skin disease.
13:28 But if the bright spot stays where it is, has not spread in the skin and has faded, it is the swelling from the burn. The priest is to declare him clean, for it is only the scar of the burn.
## The head and the beard
13:29 "A man or a woman may have a mark on the head or in the beard.
13:30 The priest is to look at the mark. If it looks deeper than the skin, and there is thin yellow hair in it, the priest is to declare the person unclean. It is a scaly patch, a skin disease of the head or the beard.
13:31 But the priest may look at the scaly patch and find that it does not look deeper than the skin, and there is no black hair in it. Then the priest is to shut the person with the scaly patch away for seven days.
13:32 On the seventh day the priest is to look at the mark. The scaly patch may not have spread, with no yellow hair in it, and it may not look deeper than the skin.
13:33 Then the person is to shave, but not shave the scaly patch. The priest is to shut him away for seven more days.
13:34 On the seventh day the priest is to look at the scaly patch. If it has not spread in the skin and does not look deeper than the skin, the priest is to declare him clean. He is to wash his clothes, and he will be clean.
13:35 But the scaly patch may spread in the skin after he has been declared clean.
13:36 The priest is to look at him. If the scaly patch has spread in the skin, the priest does not need to look for yellow hair. He is unclean.
13:37 But if he sees that the scaly patch is unchanged and black hair has grown in it, the scaly patch has healed. He is clean, and the priest is to declare him clean.
## Harmless spots and baldness
13:38 "A man or a woman may have bright spots on the skin, white bright spots.
13:39 The priest is to look. If the bright spots on the skin are a faded white, it is a harmless rash that has broken out on the skin. The person is clean.
13:40 "If a man loses the hair on his head, he is bald. He is clean.
13:41 If he loses the hair at the front of his head, he is bald at the forehead. He is clean.
13:42 But a reddish-white mark may appear on the bald head or the bald forehead. Then it is skin disease breaking out on his head or forehead.
13:43 The priest is to look at him. The swelling of the mark on his head or forehead may be reddish-white, looking like skin disease on the body.
13:44 Then he is a man with skin disease. He is unclean. The priest must declare him unclean, because of the mark on his head.
## Living outside the camp
13:45 "The person who has the mark of skin disease is to wear torn clothes and let his hair hang loose. He is to cover his upper lip and call out, 'Unclean! Unclean!'
13:46 He will be unclean as long as he has the mark. He is unclean. He is to live alone, and his home is to be outside the camp.
## Mold in cloth and leather
13:47 "A garment of wool or linen may have a mark of mold in it.
13:48 The mark may be in the woven or knitted fabric of linen or wool, or in leather or anything made of leather.
13:49 If the mark in the garment, the leather, the woven or knitted fabric, or the leather object is greenish or reddish, it is a mark of mold. It is to be shown to the priest.
13:50 The priest is to look at the mark and shut the object away for seven days.
13:51 On the seventh day he is to look at the mark. If it has spread in the garment, the fabric or the leather, whatever its use, the mark is a malignant mold. It is unclean.
13:52 He is to burn the garment, the woven or knitted fabric of wool or linen, or the leather object that has the mark. For it is a malignant mold, and it must be burned with fire.
13:53 "But the priest may look and find that the mark has not spread in the garment, the fabric or the leather object.
13:54 Then the priest is to order them to wash the object with the mark, and he is to shut it away for seven more days.
13:55 After it has been washed, the priest is to look at the mark. If it has not changed its color, even though it has not spread, it is unclean. You are to burn it with fire. It is a rot, whether on the back side or the front side.
13:56 But if the priest looks and the mark has faded after washing, he is to tear it out of the garment, the leather or the fabric.
13:57 If it appears again in the garment, the fabric or the leather object, it is spreading. You are to burn with fire whatever has the mark.
13:58 But if you wash the garment, the fabric or the leather object and the mark disappears, it is to be washed a second time, and it will be clean."
13:59 This is the procedure for a mark of mold in a garment of wool or linen, in woven or knitted fabric, or in any leather object, for declaring it clean or unclean.
## Cleansing after skin disease
14:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
14:2 "This is the procedure for a person with skin disease on the day he is made clean. He is to be brought to the priest.
14:3 The priest is to go outside the camp and examine him. The mark of skin disease may have healed.
14:4 Then the priest is to order two clean live birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn and hyssop to be brought for the person being cleansed.
14:5 The priest is to order one of the birds to be slaughtered over fresh water in a clay pot.
14:6 He is to take the live bird with the cedar wood, the scarlet yarn and the hyssop. He is to dip them all, with the live bird, in the blood of the bird slaughtered over the fresh water.
14:7 He is to spatter the blood seven times on the person being cleansed from skin disease and declare him clean. Then he is to set the live bird free over the open field.
14:8 "The person being cleansed is to wash his clothes, shave off all his hair and bathe in water. Then he will be clean. After that he may come into the camp, but he must stay outside his tent for seven days.
14:9 On the seventh day he is to shave off all his hair: his head, his beard and his eyebrows. He must shave off all his hair. He is to wash his clothes and bathe his body in water, and he will be clean.
## The offerings of the eighth day
14:10 "On the eighth day he is to take two flawless male lambs and one flawless year-old ewe lamb. He is also to take three tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, and one log of oil.
14:11 The priest who declares him clean is to stand him and these offerings before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
14:12 The priest is to take one of the male lambs and present it as a guilt offering, with the log of oil. He is to wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.
14:13 He is to slaughter the lamb in the holy area, where the sin offering and the burnt offering are slaughtered. Like the sin offering, the guilt offering belongs to the priest. It is most holy.
14:14 The priest is to take some of the blood of the guilt offering. He is to put it on the lobe of the right ear of the person being cleansed, on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot.
14:15 The priest is to take some of the log of oil and pour it into the palm of his own left hand.
14:16 He is to dip his right finger in the oil in his left palm, and with his finger spatter some of the oil seven times before the LORD.
14:17 The priest is to put some of the oil left in his palm on the person's right earlobe, right thumb and right big toe, on top of the blood of the guilt offering.
14:18 He is to put the rest of the oil in his palm on the head of the person being cleansed. So the priest will make atonement for him before the LORD.
14:19 "The priest is to offer the sin offering and make atonement for the person being cleansed from his uncleanness. After that he is to slaughter the burnt offering.
14:20 The priest is to offer the burnt offering and the grain offering on the altar. So the priest will make atonement for him, and he will be clean.
## If he is poor
14:21 "If he is poor and cannot afford these, he is to take one male lamb as a guilt offering to be waved, to make atonement for him. He is also to take one tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering, and a log of oil.
14:22 He is to take two turtledoves or two young doves, whichever he can afford, one for a sin offering and the other for a burnt offering.
14:23 On the eighth day he is to bring them for his cleansing to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting, before the LORD.
14:24 The priest is to take the lamb for the guilt offering and the log of oil, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.
14:25 He is to slaughter the lamb for the guilt offering. The priest is to take some of its blood and put it on the person's right earlobe, right thumb and right big toe.
14:26 The priest is to pour some of the oil into the palm of his own left hand.
14:27 With his right finger he is to spatter some of the oil in his left palm seven times before the LORD.
14:28 He is to put some of the oil in his palm on the person's right earlobe, right thumb and right big toe, on the same places as the blood of the guilt offering.
14:29 He is to put the rest of the oil in his palm on the head of the person being cleansed, to make atonement for him before the LORD.
14:30 Then he is to offer one of the turtledoves or young doves, whichever the person could afford,
14:31 one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, with the grain offering. So the priest will make atonement before the LORD for the person being cleansed.
14:32 This is the procedure for someone who has had a mark of skin disease and cannot afford the usual offerings for his cleansing."
## Mold in a house
14:33 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,
14:34 "You are going into the land of Canaan, which I am giving you to hold. There I may put a mark of mold in a house in the land you hold.
14:35 The owner of the house is to come and tell the priest, 'I have seen something like a mark in my house.'
14:36 The priest is to order the house to be emptied before he goes in to look at the mark, so that nothing in the house becomes unclean. After that the priest is to go in to look at the house.
14:37 He is to look at the mark. The mark on the walls may be greenish or reddish hollows that look deeper than the wall.
14:38 Then the priest is to go out of the house to its doorway and shut the house up for seven days.
14:39 On the seventh day the priest is to come back and look. The mark may have spread on the walls of the house.
14:40 Then the priest is to order the stones with the mark on them to be pulled out and thrown into an unclean place outside the city.
14:41 He is to have the inside of the house scraped all around. The plaster they scrape off is to be dumped in an unclean place outside the city.
14:42 They are to take other stones to replace these, and he is to take new plaster and plaster the house.
14:43 "The mark may come back and break out in the house after the stones have been pulled out and the house has been scraped and plastered.
14:44 Then the priest is to come and look. If the mark has spread in the house, it is a malignant mold in the house. It is unclean.
14:45 The house is to be torn down, its stones, its timbers and all its plaster, and taken out of the city to an unclean place.
14:46 Whoever goes into the house while it is shut up will be unclean until evening.
14:47 Whoever sleeps or eats in the house must wash his clothes.
14:48 "But the priest may come and look and find that the mark has not spread after the house was plastered. Then the priest is to declare the house clean, for the mark has healed.
14:49 To cleanse the house from sin, he is to take two birds, cedar wood, scarlet yarn and hyssop.
14:50 He is to slaughter one of the birds over fresh water in a clay pot.
14:51 He is to take the cedar wood, the hyssop, the scarlet yarn and the live bird. He is to dip them in the blood of the slaughtered bird and in the fresh water, and spatter the house seven times.
14:52 So he will cleanse the house from sin with the bird's blood, the fresh water, the live bird, the cedar wood, the hyssop and the scarlet yarn.
14:53 Then he is to set the live bird free over the open field outside the city. So he will make atonement for the house, and it will be clean."

## A man's discharge
15:1 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,
15:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When any man has a discharge from his body, the discharge is unclean.
15:3 This is how his discharge makes him unclean: whether his body runs with the discharge or is blocked by it, it is his uncleanness.
15:4 Any bed the man with the discharge lies on will be unclean, and anything he sits on will be unclean.
15:5 Whoever touches his bed must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:6 Whoever sits on anything the man with the discharge sat on must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:7 Whoever touches the body of the man with the discharge must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:8 If the man with the discharge spits on someone who is clean, that person must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:9 Any saddle the man with the discharge rides on will be unclean.
15:10 Whoever touches anything that was under him will be unclean until evening. Whoever carries such things must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:11 If the man with the discharge touches anyone without first rinsing his hands in water, that person must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:12 A clay pot that the man with the discharge touches must be broken, and any wooden object must be rinsed in water.
15:13 "When the man is healed of his discharge, he is to count off seven days for his cleansing. He is to wash his clothes and bathe his body in fresh water, and he will be clean.
15:14 On the eighth day he is to take two turtledoves or two young doves and come before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting. He is to give them to the priest.
15:15 The priest is to offer them, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. So the priest will make atonement for him before the LORD for his discharge.
## Seed
15:16 "When a man has an emission of seed, he is to bathe his whole body in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:17 Any clothing or leather with an emission of seed on it must be washed in water, and it will be unclean until evening.
15:18 When a man lies with a woman and there is an emission of seed, both of them are to bathe in water, and they will be unclean until evening.
## A woman's monthly flow
15:19 "When a woman has a discharge, and the discharge from her body is blood, she is to be in her separation for seven days. Whoever touches her will be unclean until evening.
15:20 Anything she lies on during her separation will be unclean, and anything she sits on will be unclean.
15:21 Whoever touches her bed must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:22 Whoever touches anything she sits on must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:23 Whether it is the bed or the seat she is sitting on, whoever touches it will be unclean until evening.
15:24 If a man lies with her and her separation comes on him, he will be unclean for seven days, and any bed he lies on will be unclean.
## A flow outside her time
15:25 "A woman may have a flow of blood for many days outside the time of her separation, or a flow that lasts beyond her separation. As long as the unclean flow lasts, she is unclean, as she is during her separation.
15:26 Any bed she lies on while her flow lasts will be like her bed during her separation. Anything she sits on will be unclean, as during her separation.
15:27 Whoever touches these things will be unclean. He must wash his clothes and bathe in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
15:28 When she is healed of her flow, she is to count off seven days, and after that she will be clean.
15:29 On the eighth day she is to take two turtledoves or two young doves and bring them to the priest at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
15:30 The priest is to offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. So the priest will make atonement for her before the LORD for her unclean flow.
## Why it matters
15:31 "You are to keep the Israelites separate from their uncleanness, so that they do not die in their uncleanness by making my tabernacle, which is among them, unclean."
15:32 This is the procedure for a man with a discharge and for a man who has an emission of seed and becomes unclean by it.
15:33 It is also for a woman in her monthly separation, for anyone with a discharge, male or female, and for a man who lies with an unclean woman.
## Not at just any time
16:1 The LORD spoke to Moses after the death of Aaron's two sons, when they drew near before the LORD and died.
16:2 The LORD said to Moses, "Tell your brother Aaron that he must not come at just any time into the holy place behind the veil. He must not come before the atonement lid on the ark, or he will die. For I will appear in the cloud over the atonement lid.
16:3 This is how Aaron is to come into the holy place: with a young bull for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering.
16:4 He is to put on a holy linen tunic, with linen shorts on his body. He is to tie a linen sash around him and wrap a linen turban around his head. These are holy garments. He is to bathe his body in water and then put them on.
16:5 From the community of the Israelites he is to take two male goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering.
## Two goats
16:6 "Aaron is to present the bull for his own sin offering and make atonement for himself and his household.
16:7 He is to take the two goats and stand them before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
16:8 Aaron is to cast lots for the two goats, one lot for the LORD and the other lot for Azazel.
16:9 Aaron is to present the goat chosen by lot for the LORD and offer it as a sin offering.
16:10 But the goat chosen by lot for Azazel is to be stood alive before the LORD, to make atonement on it by sending it away into the wilderness to Azazel.
## Blood inside the veil
16:11 "Aaron is to present the bull for his own sin offering and make atonement for himself and his household. He is to slaughter the bull for his own sin offering.
16:12 He is to take a fire pan full of burning coals from the altar before the LORD and two handfuls of finely ground fragrant incense, and bring them behind the veil.
16:13 He is to put the incense on the fire before the LORD. The cloud of incense will cover the atonement lid over the testimony, so that he does not die.
16:14 He is to take some of the bull's blood and spatter it with his finger on the front of the atonement lid, on the east side. He is also to spatter some of the blood with his finger seven times in front of the atonement lid.
16:15 "Then he is to slaughter the goat for the people's sin offering and bring its blood behind the veil. He is to do with its blood just as he did with the bull's blood, spattering it on the atonement lid and in front of it.
16:16 So he will make atonement for the holy place, because of the uncleanness of the Israelites and their rebellions, all their sins. He is to do the same for the tent of meeting, which stays with them in the middle of their uncleanness.
16:17 No one is to be in the tent of meeting from the time he goes in to make atonement in the holy place until he comes out. He will make atonement for himself, his household and the whole assembly of Israel.
16:18 "Then he is to go out to the altar that is before the LORD and make atonement for it. He is to take some of the bull's blood and some of the goat's blood and put it on all the horns of the altar.
16:19 He is to spatter some of the blood on it with his finger seven times. So he will make it clean and holy again from the uncleanness of the Israelites.
## The goat for Azazel
16:20 "When he has finished making atonement for the holy place, the tent of meeting and the altar, he is to present the live goat.
16:21 Aaron is to lean both his hands on the head of the live goat and confess over it all the guilty deeds and rebellions of the Israelites, all their sins. He is to put them on the goat's head and send it off into the wilderness in the care of a man standing ready.
16:22 The goat will carry all their guilty deeds on itself to a land cut off. The man is to let the goat go in the wilderness.
16:23 "Then Aaron is to come into the tent of meeting. He is to take off the linen garments he put on when he went into the holy place, and leave them there.
16:24 He is to bathe his body in water in a holy place and put on his own garments. Then he is to come out and offer his burnt offering and the people's burnt offering, and make atonement for himself and for the people.
16:25 He is to make the fat of the sin offering go up in smoke on the altar.
16:26 "The man who let the goat go to Azazel must wash his clothes and bathe his body in water. After that he may come into the camp.
16:27 The bull and the goat for the sin offerings, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the holy place, are to be taken outside the camp. Their hides, their meat and their dung are to be burned with fire.
16:28 The man who burns them must wash his clothes and bathe his body in water. After that he may come into the camp.
## A rule for all time
16:29 "This is to be a rule for you for all time. On the tenth day of the seventh month you are to humble yourselves and do no work at all. This applies to the native-born and to the foreigner who lives among you.
16:30 For on this day atonement will be made for you, to make you clean. You will be clean from all your sins before the LORD.
16:31 It is a Sabbath of complete rest for you, and you are to humble yourselves. It is a rule for all time.
16:32 The priest who is anointed and whose hand is filled to serve as priest in his father's place is to make atonement. He is to put on the linen garments, the holy garments.
16:33 He is to make atonement for the holy sanctuary, for the tent of meeting and for the altar. He is also to make atonement for the priests and for all the people of the assembly.
16:34 This is to be a rule for you for all time: atonement is to be made once a year for the Israelites for all their sins." And he did just as the LORD commanded Moses.
