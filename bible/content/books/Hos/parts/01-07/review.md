# Hostile review: Hosea 1-7 (`parts/01-07`), book.json and four topic cores

Reviewer: separate agent. Checked: plain.txt against `node tools/literal.js Hos 1..7`, Swete (`tools/lxx.py`), the DSS printout (`tools/dss.py`, run with PYTHONUTF8=1), all 101 notes, 20 variants, 44 glosses, 7 chapter pages, 3 traditions cards, book.json, and the cores hosea, hosea-and-gomer, book-of-the-twelve, knowledge-of-god. `node tools/build.js --check Hos/01-07` gives 0 errors, 0 warnings. All note anchors occur in their verses. No em-dashes, no "from memory", no websites in reader text.

## Verdict

This is a careful, honest part. The Plain English Translation is close to the Hebrew, the "prostitution" rule is kept in every zenunim and zanah place, the marriage and feminist-critique material is fair and does not soften 2:3 and 2:13, the Plain English Translation follows the Greek only at 6:5 as claimed, and the Targum, Talmud, haftarah, Maimonides, tefillin, Avot de-Rabbi Natan and Genesis Rabbah claims I could test hold up. The problems are in the details. (a) A Dead Sea scroll is cited as a witness where the printout shows the words are wholly restored (7:14) or only partly visible (6:3, 4:15, chapter 6 "6:3-4"). (b) Two real 4Q82 omissions (2:23, 3:3) are missing from the variants. (c) 6:2 "Two days from now" mistranslates "after two days" and contradicts the note's own heading and "on the third day". (d) Several factual slips in chapter pages and topics (Judah "going" to Assyria at 5:13, "on that day" counted five times, Bethel "not named" by Hosea although 10:15 and 12:4 name it, Pekahiah's death date). (e) A few verses sit too close to NIV/ESV wording. (f) Source depth is thin where the brief asked for named sources (Orthodox cards; Rashi, Ibn Ezra, Kimhi, Calvin on 6:2). Nothing invented that I could prove; several claims are unverified (listed at the end). No critical findings.

Counts: critical 0, major 6, minor 24.

## Findings

### Major

1. **Major. plain.txt 6:2 and notes 6:2 (word).** The Plain English Translation says "Two days from now he will give us life again. On the third day..." The Hebrew `miyyomayim` is "after two days" (Swete has `μετὰ δύο ἡμέρας`). "Two days from now" puts the revival on day 2 and then says "on the third day", which is incoherent in English. The note's own heading says "After two days", and the gloss/anchor uses the Plain English Translation's wording. Fix: "After two days he will give us life again. On the third day..."; update the note anchor and text ("the Plain English Translation says 'after two days'"). Also chapter 6 summary ("In two days").
2. **Major. variants.json 7:14.** The variant says the scroll 4Q82 "has the Masoretic word, but the transcription restores most of it." The printout shows `[יתגוררו]` entirely in brackets: no scroll preserves the word. dss.py's rule: never cite a restored word as the scroll's reading. Fix: delete the claim; say only that 4Q82 preserves the neighboring words and the verse's other parts, and that this word is restored.
3. **Major. variants.json 4:15 and chapter 4 views C.** Variant says 4Q78 "has 'Beth' and 'on'". In the printout `[בית]` is restored; only `און` is visible. Also the note that `awen` and `On` "are written with the same consonants" is shaky: Genesis 41:45 writes `אֹן` without waw, though Numbers 16:1 `אוֹן` is the same as `אוון`. Say the Greek read the name On and give the Genesis spelling honestly. Chapter 4 C also says 4Q78 "preserves 4:1 to 4:19 almost completely"; 4:1, 4:11-13 and much else are mostly restored. Fix: "preserves parts of 4:1-19 (many words restored)".
4. **Major. variants.json (missing entries): 2:23, 3:3 in 4Q82; also 1:7, 4:7, 6:9, 7:4-5.** (a) The printout for 4Q82 at 2:23 reads `ורחמתי ואמרתי ללא עמי`, with no `את לא רחמה` and no brackets; at 3:3 it reads `תשבי ולא תהיי לאיש`, lacking `לי לא תזני`. These are the Romans 9:25 verse and the "must not prostitute yourself" clause. They may be haplography or damage; DJD XV must be checked, but they need a variant or at least a note. (b) The brief named 1:7 (Greek "sons"), 4:7, 6:9 and 7:4-5 as variant entries; they appear only in notes (1:7 and 6:9) or not at all (4:7 Greek `θήσομαι` "I will set", 7:4-5 Greek differs substantially). Fix: add entries or a stated reason. (c) The brief asks to mention every scroll that preserves a place; 4Q79 at 1:7-9 and 4Q82 at 1:10-11, 2:1-2:23 are mentioned only in chapter views, not in notes/variants, which is acceptable only if the chapter views are explicit (they are for 1:6-11 but not for 2:2, where 4Q79 and 4Q82 differ in spelling).
5. **Major. notes 4:7 (echo).** States that the MT, the Greek and 4Q78 "all read 'I will exchange'". Swete reads `τὴν δόξαν αὐτῶν εἰς ἀτιμίαν θήσομαι` "I will set their glory to dishonor", not "exchange". The brief told the writer to confirm the rabbinic scribal-correction (tiqqune soferim) tradition with Tov before saying more. The note alludes to "some scholars suspect" but never says that rabbinic tradition lists this verse or does not. Fix: say what the Greek has; either cite Tov (TCHB, scribal corrections section) or drop the claim that a correction is suspected.
6. **Major. topics.json `bethel` and notes/4:15.** The topic says "Hosea does not name Bethel". Part 08-14's own Plain English Translation has "Bethel" at 10:15 and 12:4. Fix: "Hosea mostly says Beth-aven for the shrine; Bethel itself is named at 10:15 and 12:4 (Jacob)."

### Minor

7. **Minor. plain.txt 4:15.** "Israel, you may go on prostituting yourself, but Judah must not become guilty." Hebrew `'im zoneh 'attah Yisra'el` is concessive "Though you, Israel, prostitute yourself"; "you may go on" reads as permission. Fix: "Israel, even if you go on prostituting yourself, Judah must not become guilty."
8. **Minor. plain.txt 2:10, notes 2:10.** "her shame" for `nablutah` is softer than the usual "lewdness" and uses the same English as `bosheth`/`qalon` (4:7, 4:18, 4:19), so the reader cannot see the difference. The brief says not to soften 2:3-13. The note itself says "genitals" and "lewdness" are the competing views. Fix: "uncover her lewdness" or "her naked folly", and keep the note.
9. **Minor. plain.txt 3:3 and note 3:3.** "and I will be that way toward you too" resolves the verbless `vegam 'ani 'elayikh` to the mutual-faithfulness reading (the same resolution NIV makes), yet the note says the Plain English Translation "leaves the verb as general as the Hebrew." Fix: "and I also [will be] to you" in a bracketed or looser form, or say the Plain English Translation chose reading (1).
10. **Minor. drift watch, verses that sit close to NIV/ESV and should be rechecked.** 5:9 "On the day of reckoning" (NIV's phrase; Hebrew `yom tokhehah` is "day of rebuke") and "something certain"; 4:3 "swept away" and "Even the fish of the sea"; 5:2 "gone deep into slaughter" (ESV's wording of an uncertain line, kept); 6:3 "He will come to us like the rain, like the spring rain that waters the land" (shape of ESV/NIV, kept); 3:5 "come trembling to the LORD" with "In the last days" (NIV's wording); 2:10 "no one will rescue her from my hand" (ESV, kept); 7:13 "I would redeem them" (ESV/NJPS). I do not reproduce their text. Most are Hebrew-forced but the audit log records only "run forced by Hebrew order", which is not true for 5:9 "day of reckoning" and 4:3. Fix: reword 5:9 ("on the day of rebuke"), 4:3, 5:2.
11. **Minor. plain.txt 5:12, 5:14.** The Hebrew has similes (`ke'ash`, `kashachal`, `kakefir`: "like a moth", "like a lion"). The Plain English Translation says "I am a moth", "I will be a lion", turning simile into metaphor (2:3 keeps "like a dry land"). Fix: "I am like a moth", "like a lion".
12. **Minor. plain.txt 6:11.** "for when I turn my people's fortunes around" adds "for"; the Hebrew has no `ki`. Fix: "when I turn my people's fortunes around".
13. **Minor. glosses.json 1:6 vs notes/chapters.** Gloss and brief say "Not Pitied"; the 1:6 note, 2:1 gloss, chapter 1 deep table and 2:23 note say "Not Shown Compassion". Choose one (brief: "Not Pitied"). The chapter 1 summary uses "Not Pitied", the table uses the other.
14. **Minor. chapters.json ch 2 deep.** "2:16-23 is the speech of 'on that day', five times." The Hebrew `bayyom hahu` occurs at 2:16, 2:18, 2:21: three times. Fix: "three times".
15. **Minor. chapters.json ch 5 (readings, sum, deep) and topics.json `assyria`.** They say both Ephraim and Judah (or "both nations") went to Assyria at 5:13. The text says only Ephraim went and sent to Jareb; Judah "saw his sore". (Alt's war reading brings in Ahaz's appeal, but that is Alt, not 5:13.) Fix: correct all three places.
16. **Minor. chapters.json ch 7 H.** "Pekah killed Pekahiah (about 735)". Pekahiah's two years put his death about 740 (2 Kings 15:23-25; check `regnal-chronology.json`). Fix the date or give the dating range.
17. **Minor. chapters.json ch 6 C.** "4Q82 preserves 6:3-4": the printout has one restored letter at 6:4. Say 6:3 only. Also in the 6:3 variant, "4Q82 has the same consonants" rests partly on a restored word (`נכון`); say the visible `כשחר ... מוצאו`.
18. **Minor. notes 4:3.** "only Zephaniah 1:3 widens the judgment as far" overlooks Ezekiel 38:20 (fish of the sea shaken) and Genesis 9:2. Fix: "few passages".
19. **Minor. notes 4:2 (echo).** "Exodus 20:13-15" does not contain lying witness (20:16); fix to 20:13-16 in note and chapter 4 echoes.
20. **Minor. notes 6:3.** "the gifts that the Canaanites gave to Baal" is wrong English for the idea (dawn and rain were thought to be Baal's gifts to them, or Shachar's). Reword.
21. **Minor. notes 7:4.** "(here a word for betrayal and treachery as in chapter 1)": chapter 1 uses zenunim, not the root `na'af`. Remove or correct.
22. **Minor. notes 7:6 and others.** "see the printout at 7:6" is reader-facing and the reader has no printout. Name the edition (Swete) and give the Greek words, as other notes do.
23. **Minor. notes 6:6.** "Jesus quotes the Greek form": Swete reads `ἔλεος θέλω ἢ θυσίαν` (with `ἢ`); Matthew 9:13 has `καὶ οὐ`. Say Matthew's word is `eleos`, same as the Greek, with his own conjunction. Likewise notes 2:23: Paul's Romans 9:25 uses `καλέσω` where Swete has `ἐρῶ`; "he follows the Greek" is too strong ("he adapts the Greek").
24. **Minor. notes 6:2 (views).** "Others see ... Baal-style dying and rising" is unattributed; name a scholar (e.g., Kaufmann's opponents, Hvidberg, or leave to Wolff) or mark it as a view in the critical literature. The Greek (`ὑγιάσει ἡμᾶς`, "he will heal us", and `ἀναστησόμεθα`, "we will rise") is not mentioned and is relevant to the resurrection debate. Add. Same for 6:1, where Swete introduces the song with `Ἐν θλίψει αὐτῶν ὀρθριοῦσι`.
25. **Minor. notes 3:4, 2:15, 5:12, 1:4.** Unmentioned Greek divergences: 3:4 (`θυσιαστηρίου`, `ἱερατίας`, `δήλων` for pillar, ephod, teraphim); 2:15 (`διανοῖξαι σύνεσιν` for "door of hope"); 5:12 (`ταραχή`, `κέντρον` for "moth, rot"); 1:4 (Swete reads "house of Judah", probably Ἰού corrupted). Add one-line mentions or variants.
26. **Minor. notes 1:2 (views) and chapter 1 readings.** The Jewish range is thin: Maimonides and the Targum only. Rashi follows the Talmud (real), Ibn Ezra and Kimhi are usually reported as taking it as a vision; name them only after checking a printed source (see Unverified). The brief's list (Garrett, Macintosh, Hubbard, Wellhausen-Harper, Calvin, Jerome) is only partly named; "some Christian writers" is vague. Name them.
27. **Minor. notes 1:4.** "Jehu, at Elisha's word" is loose: Elisha sent a prophet's disciple (2 Kings 9:1-3). Fix. Chapter 1 H says 1:5 and 1:7 "look ahead to" Tiglath-pileser and 701 as fact; the notes properly say the Plain English Translation does not name a fulfillment. Align the H lens ("are often read as").
28. **Minor. chapters.json traditions cards.** (a) The Orthodox cards (church-israel, resurrection, sacrifice) all rest on one general book, Ware's *The Orthodox Church*, and assert what "the Fathers" read without naming one; give Chrysostom (Romans) or the Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom, or the Paschal liturgy.
