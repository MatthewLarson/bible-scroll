## The Word Comes to Hosea
1:1 The word of the LORD that came to Hosea son of Beeri in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel.
## Hosea's Wife and Three Children
1:2 This is how the LORD began to speak through Hosea. The LORD said to Hosea, "Go. Take a wife of prostitution for yourself, and children of prostitution, because the land is prostituting itself again and again, away from following the LORD."
1:3 So Hosea went and took Gomer, Diblaim's daughter. She conceived and bore him a son.
1:4 The LORD said to him, "Name him Jezreel. In a very little while I will bring the blood shed at Jezreel down on the house of Jehu, and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel.
1:5 On that day I will break Israel's bow in the Valley of Jezreel."
1:6 She conceived again and bore a daughter. The LORD said to him, "Name her Lo-ruhamah, for I will never again have compassion on the house of Israel. I will not forgive them at all.
1:7 As for the house of Judah, I will have compassion on them and save them by the LORD their God. I will not save them with bow, sword or battle, with horses or riders."
1:8 After she weaned Lo-ruhamah, she conceived and bore a son.
1:9 Then the LORD said, "Name him Lo-ammi, for you are not my people, and I will not be yours."
## Not My People Becomes Children of God
1:10 The Israelites will be so many that no one can measure or count them, like the sand of the sea. And in the very place where they were told, "You are not my people," they will be told, "Children of the living God!"
1:11 The people of Judah and the people of Israel will be gathered together. They will set one head over themselves and go up out of the land, for the day of Jezreel will be great.
## Take Your Mother to Court
2:1 Say to your brothers, "Ammi," and to your sisters, "Ruhamah."
2:2 Bring a case against your mother, bring a case, / for she is not my wife, and I am not her husband. / Let her take her prostitution away from her face / and her adulteries from between her breasts.
2:3 Otherwise I will strip her bare and put her on show as on the day she was born. / I will turn her into a wilderness and make her like a dry land, / and I will kill her with thirst.
2:4 I will not have compassion on her children either, / for they are children of prostitution.
2:5 Their mother has prostituted herself. / The one who conceived them has done shameful things. / She said, "I am going after my lovers. / They are the ones who give me my food and water, / my wool and flax, my oil and drinks."
2:6 So look, I am going to fence your road in with thorns, / and I will wall her in, / and she will not find her paths.
2:7 She will run after her lovers and not catch them. / She will look for them and not find them. / Then she will say, "Let me go back to my first husband, / for it went well for me then, better than now."
2:8 She never realized that I am the one who gave her grain, new wine and fresh oil. / I made her silver pile up, and gold, / and they made it into Baal.
2:9 So I will come back and take my grain when it ripens / and my new wine in its season. / I will tear away my wool and my flax, / meant to cover her nakedness.
2:10 Now I will uncover her naked folly / in front of her lovers' eyes, / and no one will free her from my hand.
2:11 I will stop all her celebrating: / her festivals, her new moons, her sabbaths, / and every one of her appointed feasts.
2:12 I will ruin her vines and her fig trees, / the ones she called "my pay, the gifts of my lovers." / I will make them a thicket, / and the wild animals will eat them.
2:13 I will call her to account for the days of the Baals, / when she sent smoke up to them, / put on her ring and her jewelry, / and went after her lovers. / But me she forgot, the LORD declares.
## The Wilderness and the Door of Hope
2:14 So look, I am going to coax her. / I will lead her out to the wilderness / and speak to her heart.
2:15 From there I will give her her vineyards, / and the Valley of Achor as a door of hope. / There she will answer, as in the days of her youth, / as on the day she came up from the land of Egypt.
2:16 The LORD declares: On that day you will call me "My husband," / and "My Baal" you will call me no more.
2:17 I will take the names of the Baals out of her mouth, / and they will not be remembered by name again.
2:18 On that day I will make a covenant for them: / with the wild animals, with the birds of the sky, / and with the creeping things of the ground. / I will break the bow, the sword and war, and clear them out of the land, / and I will make them lie down in safety.
2:19 I will betroth you to myself forever. / I will betroth you to myself with righteousness and justice, / with loyal love and compassion.
2:20 I will betroth you to myself with faithfulness, / and you will know the LORD.
2:21 On that day I will answer, the LORD declares. / I will answer the heavens, / and they will answer the earth,
2:22 and the earth will answer the grain, the new wine and the fresh oil, / and they will answer Jezreel.
2:23 I will sow her for myself in the land. / I will have compassion on Lo-ruhamah, / and I will say to Lo-ammi, "You are my people," / and he will say, "My God."
## Hosea Buys Back the Woman
3:1 Again the LORD spoke to me: "Go and love a woman who is loved by a companion and keeps committing adultery. Love her as the LORD loves the Israelites, who keep turning to other gods and loving raisin cakes."
3:2 So I bought her for myself: fifteen shekels of silver, a homer of barley and a lethech of barley.
3:3 I said to her, "You will stay with me for many days. You must not prostitute yourself or belong to any man, and I also toward you."
3:4 For many days the Israelites will stay with no king and no prince, no sacrifice and no pillar, no ephod and no teraphim.
3:5 After that, the Israelites will turn back and look for the LORD their God and David their king. In the last days they will come in fear to the LORD and to his goodness.
## The LORD Has a Case Against the Land
4:1 Listen to the word of the LORD, Israelites, / for the LORD has a case against the people who live in the land. / Nowhere in the land is there faithfulness or loyal love or knowledge of God.
4:2 Cursing and lying, murder, stealing and adultery: / they break out, and blood touches blood.
4:3 That is why the land mourns, and everyone who lives in it withers, / with the wild animals and the birds of the sky. / The fish of the sea too are taken away.
## Like People, Like Priest
4:4 Yet let no one bring a case, and let no one correct anyone, / for your people are like those who bring a case against a priest.
4:5 In the daytime you will stumble, and at night a prophet will stumble with you. / I will destroy your mother.
4:6 My people are destroyed because they have no knowledge. / You are the one who rejected knowledge, / so I reject you as my priest. / You forgot the teaching of your God, / so I, too, will forget your children.
4:7 As they grew in number, so they sinned against me. / I will trade their glory for shame.
4:8 They feed on the sin of my people / and hunger for their guilt.
4:9 Like people, like priest: / I will bring their ways down on him / and pay him back for his deeds.
4:10 They will eat but never have enough. / They will prostitute themselves but never increase, / because they have left the LORD to keep up
## Prostitution Under the Trees
4:11 prostitution. / Wine and new wine take away the mind.
4:12 My people ask a piece of wood for advice, / and their stick gives them the answer. / A spirit of prostitution has led them astray, / and they have prostituted themselves, slipping out from under their God.
4:13 On mountaintops they slaughter offerings, and on hills they let smoke rise, / beneath oak and poplar and terebinth, for the shade there is good. / That is why your daughters prostitute themselves / and your brides commit adultery.
4:14 I will not hold your daughters to account when they prostitute themselves, / or your brides when they commit adultery, / for the men themselves go off with prostitutes / and sacrifice with the shrine women. / A people without understanding is thrown down.
## Do Not Go to Gilgal
4:15 Israel, even if you go on prostituting yourself, Judah must not become guilty. / Do not enter Gilgal. / Do not go up to Beth-aven. / Do not swear, "As the LORD lives."
4:16 Israel has been stubborn, as stubborn as a heifer. / Can the LORD now pasture them like a lamb in open country?
4:17 Ephraim is bound up with idols. / Let him be.
4:18 Their drink has run out, yet they prostitute themselves again and again. / Her shields love shame, yes, love it.
4:19 A wind has bound her up in its wings, / and they will be ashamed of their sacrifices.
## Hear This, Priests and King
5:1 Hear this, you priests! / Pay attention, house of Israel! / Listen, house of the king! / For the judgment falls on you, / because you have turned into a trap at Mizpah, / a net spread out over Tabor.
5:2 The rebels have made their killing run deep, / but I am a discipline to all of them.
5:3 Ephraim I know, / and from me Israel is not hidden. / For now, Ephraim, you have prostituted yourself; / Israel has made itself unclean.
5:4 Nothing they do lets them turn back to their God. / A spirit of prostitution lives inside them, / and the LORD they do not know.
5:5 Israel's pride speaks against him to his face. / Israel and Ephraim will stumble over their guilt, / and Judah will stumble with them.
5:6 They will go to look for the LORD with sheep and cattle, / but they will not find him. / He has pulled away from them.
5:7 They have betrayed the LORD, / for they have borne foreign children. / Now a new moon will devour them along with their fields.
## Sound the Alarm
5:8 Blow the ram's horn in Gibeah, / the trumpet in Ramah. / Raise the war cry at Beth-aven: / "Behind you, Benjamin!"
5:9 Ephraim will become a wasteland on the day of rebuke. / Among the tribes of Israel I make known what can be trusted.
5:10 Judah's princes act like people who shift a boundary marker. / I will pour out my wrath on them like water.
5:11 Ephraim is under oppression, crushed by the verdict, / because he insisted on walking after a command.
5:12 To Ephraim I am like a moth; / to the house of Judah, like rot.
5:13 Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his sore. / Then Ephraim went off to Assyria / and sent word to King Jareb. / But he is not able to heal you; / the wound will not leave you.
5:14 I will be like a lion to Ephraim / and like a young lion to the house of Judah. / I myself will tear them to pieces and walk away; / I will carry them off, and there is no one to pull them free.
5:15 I will go away and go back to my place / until they pay for their guilt and seek my face. / When they are in trouble, they will look for me early and eagerly.
## Come, Let Us Turn Back
6:1 Come, let us turn back to the LORD, / for it was he who tore us apart, and he will heal us. / He struck us, and he will bandage us.
6:2 After two days he will give us life again. / On the third day he will raise us up, / and we will live before him.
6:3 Let us know; let us chase after knowing the LORD. / His coming out is as sure as the dawn. / He will come to us like the rain, / like the spring rain that soaks the earth.
## What Can I Do With You?
6:4 What can I do with you, Ephraim? / What can I do with you, Judah? / Your loyal love is like a morning cloud, / like the dew that rises early and is gone.
6:5 So I have hacked at them through the prophets; / I have killed them with the words of my mouth, / and my judgment goes out like light.
6:6 Loyal love is what I want, not sacrifice, / and knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.
6:7 But they, like Adam, overstepped the covenant. / There they betrayed me.
6:8 Gilead, a town of men who do wrong, / is marked with the tracks of blood.
6:9 Like a man lying in ambush are the gangs, / and a band of priests murders along the road to Shechem. / They have committed a shameful crime.
6:10 I have seen something horrible in the house of Israel: / prostitution belongs to Ephraim there, / and Israel has made itself unclean.
6:11 A harvest is set for you too, Judah, / when I turn my people's fortunes around.
## Ephraim's Wickedness Uncovered
7:1 Each time I try to heal Israel, / Ephraim's guilt comes to light, and so do Samaria's evil deeds, / for they do what is false. / A thief breaks in, / and a raiding band strips people in the street.
7:2 They never tell themselves / that I remember all their evil. / Now their own deeds have them surrounded; / they stand before my face.
7:3 By their evil they gladden the king, / and by their lies the princes.
7:4 All of them are adulterers, / like an oven the baker keeps burning, / who stops stirring the fire / from kneading the dough until it has risen.
7:5 On our king's day the princes make themselves ill with the heat of wine, / and he stretches out his hand along with the mockers.
7:6 For their hearts come near like an oven as they lie in wait. / All night their baker sleeps; / in the morning it burns like a flaming fire.
7:7 They all burn like an oven and eat up their own rulers. / Every one of their kings has fallen, / and not one among them calls to me.
## A Flat Loaf and a Silly Dove
7:8 Among the peoples Ephraim mixes himself in. / Ephraim has become a flat loaf, not turned over.
7:9 Foreigners have consumed his strength, and he has not noticed. / Gray hairs have sprinkled over him, and he has not noticed.
7:10 Israel's pride speaks against him to his face, / yet they have not turned back to the LORD their God, / and for all this they have not looked for him.
7:11 Ephraim has turned into a dove: silly, no mind of its own. / They have called to Egypt; they have gone off to Assyria.
7:12 While they are on their way, I will throw my net over them. / Like birds of the sky I will bring them down, / and I will correct them as the warning to their assembly said.
7:13 Woe to them! They have fled from me. / Destruction is theirs! They have rebelled against me. / I would redeem them, / but they tell lies about me.
7:14 Their hearts do not cry out to me, / though they wail on their beds. / They huddle together over grain and new wine, / and they turn away from me.
7:15 It was I who taught them discipline and gave strength to their arms, / yet they scheme evil against me.
7:16 They turn, but not upward. / They are like a slack bow. / Their princes will fall by the sword / because of the rage of their tongues. / In the land of Egypt this will be how they are mocked.
