## The Horn and the Calf
8:1 Put the ram's horn to your mouth! / Like an eagle, he swoops on the house of the LORD, / because they broke my covenant / and rebelled against my law.
8:2 They cry out to me, / "My God, we know you, we of Israel!"
8:3 Israel has cast off the good; / an enemy will chase him.
8:4 They crowned kings, but the choice was not mine. / They put princes in power, and I did not know about it. / From their silver and gold they made idols for themselves, / and so they will be cut off.
8:5 Your calf, Samaria, he has thrown away. / My anger is hot against them. / How long will they be unable to be clean?
8:6 For it comes from Israel! / A craftsman made it, and it is no god. / The calf of Samaria will turn to splinters.
8:7 They sow wind, / so a storm wind is what they will reap. / Standing grain with no sprout / makes no flour. / And if it did make some, / strangers would swallow it down.
8:8 Israel is swallowed up. / Now they are among the nations / like a jar that no one wants.
8:9 For they themselves went up to Assyria, / like a wild donkey off by itself. / Ephraim has hired lovers.
## Altars and Egypt
8:10 Even if they hire lovers among the nations, / now I will gather them up. / In a little while they will start to writhe / under the load of the king of princes.
8:11 Ephraim has built altar after altar for sin. / They have turned into altars for sin for him.
8:12 Though I write for him the many points of my law, / they are counted as something foreign.
8:13 They offer sacrifices as gifts to me. / They offer meat, and they eat it, / but the LORD is not pleased with them. / Now he will recall their guilt / and call their sins to account. / They will go back to Egypt.
8:14 Israel forgot his Maker / and built palaces. / Judah piled up walled cities. / But I will send fire on his cities, / and it will eat up his fortresses.
## No Joy at the Harvest
9:1 Do not rejoice, Israel, / do not shout for joy like the other peoples, / for you have prostituted yourself away from your God. / You have loved the pay of a prostitute / on every threshing floor of grain.
9:2 Neither threshing floor nor winepress will feed them, / and the new wine will betray them.
9:3 They will not stay in the LORD's land. / Ephraim will go back to Egypt, / and in Assyria they will eat what is unclean.
9:4 They will pour no wine out to the LORD, / and their sacrifices will never be pleasing to him. / Such food is like the bread of mourners: / everyone who eats it becomes unclean. / Their bread is just for their own appetite; / it cannot come into the house of the LORD.
9:5 What will you do on the set festival day, / on the day of the LORD's feast?
9:6 Look, they are leaving because of the devastation. / Egypt will gather them, / Memphis will bury them. / Nettles will take over their treasures of silver, / and thorns will fill their tents.
## The Days of Punishment
9:7 The days of punishment have arrived. / The days of payback have arrived. / Let Israel know it: / the prophet is a fool, / the man of the spirit is mad, / all because your guilt is so great, / and so great the hostility.
9:8 A watchman for Ephraim, along with my God, is the prophet. / Yet a bird-catcher's trap lies on all his paths, / and hostility waits in the house of his God.
9:9 They have gone deep in their corruption, / as in the days of Gibeah. / He will recall their guilt / and call their sins to account.
## Grapes in the Wilderness
9:10 I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness. / I saw your ancestors like the first ripe fig when the tree first bore. / But they went to the Baal of Peor / and gave themselves over to the Shameful Thing, / and they became as detestable as what they loved.
9:11 Ephraim: their glory will fly away like a bird, / away from birth, away from the womb, away from conception.
9:12 Even if they raise their children, / I will make them childless, and no one will be left. / Yes, woe to them when I walk away from them!
9:13 As I have seen it, Ephraim was like Tyre, / set out in a green pasture. / Yet Ephraim will bring his sons out to the one who kills.
9:14 Give them, LORD. What will you give? / Give them a womb that miscarries / and breasts that dry up.
9:15 All their evil is at Gilgal. / There I came to hate them. / Out of my house I will drive them / because of the wickedness of their deeds. / I will love them no more. / All their leaders are rebels.
9:16 Ephraim has been hit hard. / Their root has withered; fruit they will not make. / Even if they give birth, / I will put to death the treasured ones of their womb.
9:17 My God will cast them off, / because they did not listen to him, / and they will wander among the nations.
## A Luxuriant Vine
10:1 Israel is a luxuriant vine; / it bears fruit for itself. / When its fruit was plentiful, it piled up altars. / When its land was good, they made good pillars.
10:2 Their heart is slippery. / Now they must bear their guilt. / He himself will break the necks of their altars / and wreck their pillars.
10:3 Very soon they will say, / "We have no king. We did not fear the LORD, / and what could a king do for us?"
10:4 They speak empty words, / swear false oaths, and cut covenants. / So justice sprouts like a poison weed / in the furrows of a field.
10:5 Those who live in Samaria will fear for the heifers of Beth-aven. / Its people will mourn over it, / and its idol priests will shake over it, / over its glory, because it has gone into exile from them.
10:6 It too will be carried to Assyria / as tribute for King Jareb. / Ephraim will be seized by shame, / and Israel will be ashamed of its own plan.
10:7 Samaria will be destroyed, / and her king will be like a twig on the water's surface.
10:8 The high places of wickedness, Israel's sin, will be wiped out. / Thorn and thistle will grow up over their altars. / Then they will say to the mountains, "Cover us!" / and to the hills, "Fall on us!"
10:9 Since the days of Gibeah, Israel, you have sinned. / There they took their stand. / Will war not catch up with them at Gibeah, / these people of injustice?
10:10 I will discipline them whenever I want. / Nations will be gathered against them / when they are bound to their two guilts.
## Sow Righteousness
10:11 Ephraim was a trained young cow that loved to thresh grain. / I myself passed over her fine neck. / I will make Ephraim pull; / Judah will plow, / Jacob will break up the ground for himself.
10:12 Sow for yourselves in righteousness; / reap by the measure of loyal love. / Plow new ground for yourselves, / for it is time to look for the LORD / until he comes and rains righteousness on you.
10:13 You plowed wickedness. / You harvested injustice. / You ate the fruit of lying, / for you put your trust in your own way, / in your crowd of fighting men.
10:14 An uproar will rise among your people, / and all your fortresses will be plundered, / as Shalman plundered Beth-arbel on a day of war, / when a mother was smashed with her children.
10:15 This is what Bethel has done to you, / because your evil is so great. / At dawn the king of Israel / will be completely cut off.
## A Boy Called Out of Egypt
11:1 When Israel was a youth, I loved him, / and I called my son out of Egypt.
11:2 They called to them; they walked away from them. / They kept sacrificing to the Baals / and burning incense to carved idols.
11:3 Yet I myself taught Ephraim to walk. / I held them up by their arms, / and they did not know that I healed them.
11:4 I led them with human cords, / with ropes of love. / I was to them like those who lift the yoke off their jaws, / and I bent down to him and fed him.
11:5 To the land of Egypt they will not return, / but Assyria himself will be their king, / because they refused to turn back.
11:6 A sword will whirl through their cities, / break the bars of their gates, / and devour them because of their own schemes.
11:7 My people hang on to their turning away from me. / They call to One Above, / but none of them will be lifted up.
## How Can I Give You Away?
11:8 How can I give you away, Ephraim? / How can I surrender you, Israel? / How could I make you like Admah, / or set you like Zeboiim? / My heart turns over inside me, / and all my pity grows warm.
11:9 I will not act on the heat of my anger. / I will not come back to wreck Ephraim, / for I am God, not a human being, / the Holy One among you, / and I will not enter in rage.
11:10 They will follow the LORD. / He roars like a lion, / and when he roars, / his children come shaking from the west.
11:11 They will tremble like a bird as they come out of Egypt, / like a dove out of the land of Assyria. / I will settle them in their own houses, / the LORD declares.
11:12 Ephraim has ringed me round with lying, / and the house of Israel with deceit. / But Judah still roams with God, with the Holy One who is faithful.
## Jacob and Ephraim
12:1 Ephraim herds the wind / and runs after the scorching east wind all day. / Lies and violence pile up with him. / He cuts a covenant with Assyria, / and olive oil is taken to Egypt.
12:2 The LORD also has a case against Judah. / He will call Jacob to account for his ways / and repay him for what he has done.
12:3 While still in the womb, he caught his brother's heel, / and in his full strength he struggled with God.
12:4 He struggled against the angel and overcame him. / He wept and pleaded with him for his favor. / At Bethel he found him, / and there he spoke with us.
12:5 The LORD, the God of Armies, / the LORD is his name to be remembered.
12:6 But you must turn back to your God. / Keep loyal love and justice, / and keep waiting for your God, always.
12:7 Canaan has scales of deceit in his hand. / He loves to cheat.
12:8 Ephraim says, "I have become rich. / I have found wealth for myself. / No one will find in all my earnings / any guilt in me that counts as sin."
## The Prophet and the Bulls
12:9 But I have been the LORD your God since the land of Egypt. / Again I will settle you in tents, / as on festival days.
12:10 I spoke to the prophets. / I gave many visions, / and through the prophets I spoke in comparisons.
12:11 If Gilead is wicked, / they are surely worthless. / In Gilgal they sacrifice bulls, / and their altars are like heaps of stones / in the furrows of a field.
12:12 Jacob fled to the country of Aram. / Israel served for a wife, / and for a wife he stood watch.
12:13 Yet by a prophet the LORD brought Israel up out of Egypt, / and by a prophet he was watched over.
12:14 Ephraim has made him bitterly angry. / His Lord will leave the blood on him / and turn his contempt back on him.
## Like the Morning Mist
13:1 Whenever Ephraim spoke, there was a shudder. / He held himself high in Israel. / Then he fell into guilt through Baal, and he died.
13:2 Now they keep adding sin to sin. / From their silver they have poured out an image, / idols formed with their own skill, / every one the work of a craftsman. / Of these they say, / "Those who sacrifice human beings kiss calves."
13:3 So they will be like the morning cloud, / like the dew that rises early and is gone, / like chaff blown off a threshing floor, / like smoke from a window.
13:4 But I have been the LORD your God since the land of Egypt. / You are to know no god but me, / and there is no savior besides me.
13:5 In the wilderness I knew you, / in a land of scorching drought.
13:6 As they grazed, they had their fill. / Full, they let their hearts swell with pride. / So they forgot me.
## A Lion, a Leopard, a Bear
13:7 I will become a lion for them. / Like a leopard I will watch beside the road.
13:8 I will meet them like a bear whose cubs were taken / and tear open the chest that shuts in their heart. / There I will devour them like a lioness; / a wild animal of the field will rip them apart.
13:9 This is your destruction, Israel: / you are against me, against your helper.
13:10 Where is that king of yours? Let him save you in all your cities. / Where are your rulers, the ones you asked for / when you said, "Give me a king and princes"?
13:11 In my anger I gave you a king, / and in my fury I take him away.
13:12 Ephraim's guilt is wrapped up in a bundle; / his sin is locked away in storage.
13:13 Labor pains will come for him, / but he is not a wise son. / When the time comes, he does not step out / at the opening of the womb.
## Death, Where Are Your Plagues?
13:14 Should I ransom them from the hand of Sheol? / Should I redeem them from death? / Where are your plagues, Death? / Where is your destruction, Sheol? / Pity is hidden from my eyes.
13:15 Though he bears fruit among his brothers, / an east wind will come, / the wind of the LORD rising from the wilderness. / His spring will dry up, / and his well will run dry. / That wind will plunder the treasury / of every precious thing.
13:16 Samaria will be held guilty, / for she rebelled against her God. / By the sword they will fall. / Their little ones will be smashed, / and their pregnant women split open.
## Turn Back
14:1 Turn back, Israel, to the LORD your God, / for you have stumbled over your guilt.
14:2 Bring words with you / and turn back to the LORD. / Say to him, / "Forgive all our guilt and accept what is good, / and let us pay you the bulls of our lips.
14:3 Assyria will not rescue us. / We will not ride on horses. / We will no longer call the work of our hands / 'Our gods!' / For in you the child with no father is shown compassion."
## I Will Heal
14:4 I will heal their turning away. / I will love them freely. / My anger has turned away from him.
14:5 To Israel I will be like the dew. / He will bloom like a lily / and put down roots like Lebanon.
14:6 His branches will reach out, / his glory will be like an olive tree's / and his scent like Lebanon's.
14:7 Those who live in his shade will return. / They will grow grain / and blossom like the vine. / Their fame will be like the wine of Lebanon.
14:8 Ephraim, what more do I have to do with idols? / I have answered him, and I will watch over him. / I am like a luxuriant cypress; / your fruit comes from me.
## Who Is Wise?
14:9 Who is wise enough to understand these things? / Who has the insight to know them? / The LORD's ways are straight. / The righteous walk on them, / but rebels stumble over them.
