## Greeting
1:1 From Paul, a slave of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, for the faith of God's chosen ones and for the knowledge of the truth that goes with godliness.
1:2 It rests on the hope of eternal life. The God who does not lie promised that life before the long ages of time.
1:3 At the times he set, he brought his word into the open in the preaching that was put in my trust, as God our Savior commanded.
1:4 To Titus, my true-born child in the faith we hold in common: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.
## Elders for the towns of Crete
1:5 This is why I left you behind in Crete: so that you would put right what was still lacking and appoint elders town by town, as I instructed you.
1:6 Each one should be a man no one can accuse: the husband of one wife, with children who are faithful, not open to a charge of wild living or of being unruly.
1:7 Since he is God's household manager, the overseer has to be beyond any charge. He must not be stubborn, not hot-tempered, not a heavy drinker, not a man who hits people, not greedy for shameful profit.
1:8 Instead he must welcome strangers and love what is good. He must be sensible, righteous, devout and self-controlled.
1:9 He must keep a tight grip on the word that can be trusted, the word that matches what was taught. Then he will be able both to encourage others with sound teaching and to correct those who argue against it.
## Teachers who must be silenced
1:10 For there are many who are unruly, who talk nonsense and lead minds astray, especially those from the circumcision.
1:11 Their mouths must be stopped. They are turning whole households upside down by teaching what they should not, for the sake of shameful profit.
1:12 One of them, a prophet of their very own, said, "Cretans are always liars, wicked beasts, lazy bellies."
1:13 What he testified is true. For this reason, correct them sharply, so that their faith will be sound,
1:14 and they will stop giving their attention to Jewish myths and to the commands of people who turn their backs on the truth.
1:15 All things are clean to those who are clean. But to those who are stained and do not believe, nothing is clean. Their very minds and consciences are stained.
1:16 They say openly that they know God, but their actions deny him. They are detestable and disobedient, and they fail the test for any good work.
## Teaching for every household
2:1 But you must speak what fits sound teaching.
2:2 The older men should be clear-headed, dignified and sensible, sound in faith, love and endurance.
2:3 In the same way, the older women should carry themselves as people who serve in a holy place, not slanderers, not enslaved to a lot of wine. They should be teachers of what is good,
2:4 so that they can teach good sense to the younger women, who should love their husbands and love their children,
2:5 and be sensible and pure, workers in the home, good-hearted, submitting to their own husbands, so that the word of God is not insulted.
2:6 In the same way, urge good sense on the younger men.
2:7 In every way, offer yourself as a pattern of good works. Your teaching must be free of corruption and serious,
2:8 a sound message that no one can fault, so that anyone on the other side will be put to shame, unable to say anything bad about us.
2:9 Slaves should submit in everything to their own masters and do what pleases them, without talking back,
2:10 and not skim anything off for themselves. Their good faith should be complete and plain to see, so that in all things they make the teaching of God our Savior beautiful.
## The grace that has appeared
2:11 For God's grace has appeared, with salvation for all people.
2:12 It trains us to deny ungodliness and the desires of this world, and to live in this present age sensibly, rightly and in a godly way,
2:13 waiting to welcome the blessed hope and the appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ.
2:14 He gave himself up on our behalf, so that he could redeem us out of every kind of lawlessness, and make clean for himself a people that is his own private treasure, eager for good works.
2:15 Say these things. Urge and correct with full right to command. Let no one brush you aside.
## Good citizens, once lost, now saved
3:1 Remind them to submit to rulers and authorities and obey them, and to stand ready for any good work.
3:2 They should insult no one and stay out of fights. They should be forbearing and show every kind of gentleness to everyone.
3:3 There was a time when we too were without sense, disobedient, wandering off course, slaves to desires and pleasures of every kind. We spent our lives in malice and envy. We were hateful, and we hated each other.
3:4 But when the kindness of God our Savior and his love for humankind appeared,
3:5 he saved us. It was not on the basis of works we ourselves had done in righteousness, but in keeping with his mercy. He did it through the bath of new birth and the renewing by the Holy Spirit.
3:6 God poured out this Spirit on us in rich measure through Jesus Christ our Savior.
3:7 He did this so that we, justified by that grace of his, would become heirs, in keeping with the hope of eternal life.
## What to insist on, and what to avoid
3:8 This saying is trustworthy. I want you to speak firmly about these things, so that those who have come to believe in God will make it their concern to give themselves to good works. These things are good and useful for people.
3:9 But keep away from foolish debates, genealogies, quarrels and fights about the law, for they do no good and lead nowhere.
3:10 As for a person who causes splits, give him a first and a second warning, and then turn him away.
3:11 You know that someone like that is twisted and keeps on sinning. He stands condemned by his own judgment.
## Plans and greetings
3:12 When I send Artemas to you, or Tychicus, hurry to come to me at Nicopolis, for that is where I have made up my mind to spend the winter.
3:13 Send Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their journey with care, so that they are short of nothing.
3:14 Our own people too must learn to give themselves to good works, to meet pressing needs, so that they will not be without fruit.
3:15 Everyone with me greets you. Greet those who love us in the faith. Grace be with all of you.
