## The Prophets Stir the Builders
6:1 In the second year of Darius's reign, Haggai and Zechariah son of Iddo, the prophets, brought prophecies to the Jews in Judea and Jerusalem, in the name of the Lord, the God of Israel, who was over them.
6:2 Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak got up and began to build the Lord's house in Jerusalem. The Lord's prophets were with them, helping them.
## Sisinnes Questions the Builders
6:3 At that time Sisinnes, governor of Syria and Phoenicia, and Sathrabuzanes and their colleagues came to them and said to them,
6:4 "Who issued you an order to build this house and this roof, and to complete all the rest? And who are the builders doing this work?"
6:5 But the elders of the Jews were shown favor, because the Lord had come to watch over the captives.
6:6 No one stopped them from building while a report went to Darius about them, until an answer came back.
## The Letter to Darius
6:7 Here is a copy of the letter that Sisinnes, governor of Syria and Phoenicia, and Sathrabuzanes and their colleagues, the officials in Syria and Phoenicia, wrote and sent to Darius: "To King Darius, greetings.
6:8 Let our lord the king know everything. We went into the country of Judea and to the city of Jerusalem. There in the city of Jerusalem we found the elders of the Jews, of the captivity, building a house for the Lord, great and new, of costly cut stone, with beams being set in the walls.
6:9 Those works are being done with speed. The work is going well in their hands, and it is being finished in full glory and with diligence.
6:10 Then we questioned these elders. We asked them, 'Who issued you an order to build this house and to lay the foundations of these works?'
6:11 We questioned them so that we could let you know and write down for you the men who lead them, and we asked them for the names of their chief men in writing.
6:12 This is the answer they gave us: 'We are servants of the Lord who created heaven and earth.
6:13 A great and strong king of Israel built this house many years ago, and it was completed.
6:14 But because our ancestors angered him and sinned against the Lord of Israel in heaven, he handed them over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, king of the Chaldeans.
6:15 They tore down the house and set it on fire, and they took the people captive to Babylon.
6:16 Yet in the first year of Cyrus's reign over the land of Babylonia, King Cyrus wrote an order to build this house.
6:17 King Cyrus also took out again from the temple in Babylonia the holy vessels of gold and silver, which Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the house in Jerusalem and stored in his own temple. They were given to Zerubbabel and to Sanabassar the governor.
6:18 He was given orders, and he carried all these vessels away to store them in the temple in Jerusalem, and to have the Lord's temple built on its site.
6:19 Then Sanabassar came. He laid the foundations of the Lord's house in Jerusalem, and from then until now it has been under construction. It is still not finished.'
6:20 Now, O king, if you think it good, let a search be made in the royal archives of our lord the king in Babylon.
6:21 If it is found that the building for the Lord in Jerusalem was begun with King Cyrus's consent, and if our lord the king so decides, let him send us word about these things."
## Cyrus's Scroll Found
6:22 Then King Darius ordered a search in the archives kept in Babylon. In Ecbatana, the fortress in the land of Media, a scroll was found, and this was recorded in it:
6:23 "In the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus gave an order to build the Lord's house in Jerusalem, where they offer sacrifices with fire that never goes out.
6:24 It is to be sixty cubits high and sixty cubits wide, with three courses of cut stone and one course of new timber from the land. The cost is to be given out of the house of King Cyrus.
6:25 Also, the holy vessels of the Lord's house, the gold and the silver, which Nebuchadnezzar took from the house in Jerusalem and carried away to Babylon, are to be given back to the house in Jerusalem where they were kept, to be set there."
## Darius Orders the Work Done
6:26 He also ordered Sisinnes, governor of Syria and Phoenicia, and Sathrabuzanes and their colleagues, and the officials appointed in Syria and Phoenicia, to take care to keep away from there. They were to let Zerubbabel, the Lord's servant and deputy governor of Judea, and the elders of the Jews build that house of the Lord on its site.
6:27 "I also have given orders to build it completely, and that they make sure to help the people of the exile from Judea until the Lord's house is finished.
6:28 From the tribute of Coele-Syria and Phoenicia, an allowance is to be given with care to these men for sacrifices to the Lord, to Zerubbabel the governor, for bulls, rams and lambs,
6:29 and likewise wheat, salt, wine and oil, steadily year by year, without question, as the priests in Jerusalem say is needed for each day,
6:30 so that drink offerings can be brought to the Most High God on behalf of the king and his sons, and so that they may pray for their lives.
6:31 I also order that anyone who breaks or cancels any of the things written here is to have a beam taken from his own house and be hanged on it, and his property is to go to the king.
6:32 For this reason, may the Lord, whose name is called on there, wipe out every king and nation that reaches out a hand to block or harm that house of the Lord in Jerusalem.
6:33 I, King Darius, have decreed it. Let it be done with care, just as written."
## The House Finished
7:1 Then Sisinnes, governor of Coele-Syria and Phoenicia, and Sathrabuzanes and their colleagues, following what King Darius had ordered,
7:2 oversaw the sacred work all the more carefully, and worked with the elders of the Jews and the temple overseers.
7:3 And the sacred work went well, as Haggai and Zechariah the prophets prophesied.
7:4 They finished it by the order of the Lord, the God of Israel, and with the approval of Cyrus, Darius and Artaxerxes king of Persia.
7:5 The house was completed by the twenty-third day of the month of Adar, in the sixth year of King Darius.
7:6 The Israelites, the priests, the Levites and the rest of the people of the exile who had joined them acted as the book of Moses directs.
7:7 For the dedication of the Lord's temple they offered 100 bulls, 200 rams, 400 lambs,
7:8 and 12 male goats as a sin offering for all Israel, to match the number of Israel's tribal leaders, twelve.
7:9 The priests and the Levites, dressed in their robes, stood by tribes over the service of the Lord, the God of Israel, according to the book of Moses, and the gatekeepers stood at every gate.
## Passover Kept
7:10 The Israelites of the exile kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month, when the priests and the Levites had been made pure together,
7:11 along with all the people of the exile, because they had been made pure. For the Levites had all been made pure together,
7:12 and they slaughtered the Passover for all the people of the exile, for their brothers the priests, and for themselves.
7:13 The Israelites who had come back from the exile ate it, all who had separated themselves from the abhorrent practices of the nations of the land, seeking the LORD.
7:14 With joy they kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days before the LORD,
7:15 for he had turned the Assyrian king's will toward them, to give their hands strength for the work of the Lord, the God of Israel.
## Ezra Comes from Babylon
8:1 After these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, there came Ezra son of Azaraias, son of Zechrias, son of Helkias, son of Salemus,
8:2 son of Saddulukus, son of Ahitub, son of Amartheus, son of Ozeias, son of Bokkas, son of Abisai, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the first priest.
8:3 This Ezra went up from Babylon. He was a scribe, gifted in Moses' law, given by the God of Israel.
8:4 The king honored him, for Ezra found favor with him in everything he asked.
8:5 Some of the Israelites went up to Jerusalem with him too, along with some of the priests, the Levites, the temple singers, the gatekeepers and the temple servants.
8:6 This was in the seventh year of Artaxerxes' reign, in the fifth month. That year was the king's seventh. They had set out from Babylon on the first day of the first month, and they reached Jerusalem by the good journey the Lord gave them because of him.
8:7 For Ezra had great knowledge. He left nothing out of the LORD's law and its commandments when he taught all Israel the rules and rulings.
## Artaxerxes' Letter to Ezra
8:8 Word came from King Artaxerxes to Ezra, the priest and reader of the LORD's law. A copy of it follows:
8:9 "King Artaxerxes to Ezra, priest and reader of the Lord's law: greetings.
8:10 I have decided on acts of kindness. I have ordered that any of the Jewish nation, of the priests, of the Levites, and of those here in our kingdom who wish and choose to do so, may go with you to Jerusalem.
8:11 So all who are minded to go may set out with you, as I and my seven Friends, my counselors, have decided,
8:12 so that they may inspect Judea and Jerusalem by the measure of the Lord's law.
8:13 They may also take the gifts I and my Friends vowed to the Lord and bring them to Jerusalem, with any gold and silver for the Lord in Jerusalem that turns up in the land of Babylonia, and with the gifts the nation has made to their Lord's temple in Jerusalem.
8:14 The gold and silver are to be collected for bulls, rams and lambs and the things that go with them,
8:15 and so sacrifices may be brought to their Lord's altar in Jerusalem.
8:16 Whatever you and your brothers want to do with the gold and silver, carry it out as your God wills.
8:17 So also with the holy vessels given to you for the service of your God's temple in Jerusalem. And anything else that comes up as a need for the service of your God's temple,
8:18 you will pay for out of the royal treasury.
8:19 I myself, King Artaxerxes, have ordered the treasurers of Syria and Phoenicia to give Ezra the priest, the reader of the law of the Most High God, whatever he sends for, carefully, up to 100 talents of silver,
8:20 and likewise up to 100 kors of wheat and 100 measures of wine.
8:21 Let everything required by God's law be carried out for the Most High God, so that anger does not come on the kingdom of the king and his sons.
8:22 We also make known to you that no one is to lay any tax or other charge on the priests, the Levites, the temple singers, the gatekeepers, the temple servants or the workers of this temple. No one has the authority to impose anything on them.
8:23 As for you, Ezra, use God's wisdom to appoint judges and magistrates. They are to judge, in all Syria and Phoenicia, everyone who knows your God's law. Anyone who does not know it, you are to teach.
8:24 Whoever breaks the law of your God or the king's law is to be punished strictly, with death or with some other penalty, with a fine or with prison."
## Ezra Blesses the Lord
8:25 Blessed is the Lord alone, who put these things into my heart, the king's heart, to bring glory to his house in Jerusalem.
8:26 He has honored me in front of the rulers and all the king's Friends and nobles.
8:27 The LORD my God helped me, and I took courage. I gathered men out of Israel to go up with me.
## Those Who Went Up with Ezra
8:28 Here are the leaders, by their ancestral families and their divisions, who went up with me from Babylon in King Artaxerxes' reign:
8:29 Of the descendants of Phoros, Tarosotomus. Of the descendants of Ietamarus, Gamelus. Of the descendants of David,
8:30 Phares: Zechariah, and with him 150 men enrolled in the register.
8:31 Of the descendants of Maath-moab, Elialonias son of Zaraias, and with him 200 men.
8:32 Of the descendants of Zathoes, Jechonias son of Jethelus, and with him 200 men. Of the descendants of Adin, Uben son of Jonathan, and with him 250 men.
8:33 Of the descendants of Lam, Esias son of Gotholias, and with him 70 men.
8:34 Of the descendants of Sophotias, Zaraias son of Michael, and with him 70 men.
8:35 Of the descendants of Joab, Abadias son of Jezelus, and with him 212 men.
8:36 Of the descendants of Banias, Salimoth son of Josaphias, and with him 160 men.
8:37 Of the descendants of Baier, Zechariah son of Bemai, and with him 28 men.
8:38 Of the descendants of Astath, Joannes son of Akatan, and with him 110 men.
8:39 Of the descendants of Adonikam, the last of them, and these are their names: Eliphala son of Jeuel, and Samaias, and with them 70 men.
8:40 Of the descendants of Banai, Uthi son of Istakalkus, and with him 70 men.
## Levites Sent For
8:41 I gathered them at the place called the River. We stayed in camp there for three days, and I studied them closely.
8:42 Finding none of the priests or the Levites there,
8:43 I sent word to Eleazar, Iduel, Maasmas, Enaatan, Samaias, Joribus, Nathan, Ennatan, Zechariah and Mesolabon, the leaders and men of learning.
8:44 I told them to go to Laadaeus, the head man at the Treasury.
8:45 I instructed them to speak to Lodaeus, his brothers and the treasurers there, and ask them to send us priests for our Lord's house:
8:46 a man of learning from the descendants of Mooli son of Levi, son of Israel, Asebebias, with his sons and brothers, ten;
8:47 and those of the descendants of Chanunaeus, with their sons, 20 men;
8:48 And of the temple servants, whom David and the leaders had given to help the Levites in their work, 220. Every one of them was listed by name.
## A Fast at the River
8:49 There, before our Lord, I vowed a fast for the young men,
8:50 to ask him for a safe journey for us, our children and our animals.
8:51 I was ashamed to ask for horsemen and foot soldiers to go with us and guard us against our enemies.
8:52 We had told the king, "The strength of our Lord will be with those who seek him, to set everything right for them."
8:53 Again we pleaded with our Lord about all this, and we found him ready to be gracious.
## Treasure Entrusted to the Priests
8:54 Next I chose out twelve men from the chief priests, and Eserebias and Assamias, and with them ten men from their brothers.
8:55 I weighed out to them the silver, the gold and the holy vessels of our Lord's house. This is what the king, his counselors, his nobles and all Israel had given.
8:56 I weighed out and handed over to them 650 talents of silver, 100 talents in silver vessels, 100 talents of gold, 20 gold bowls, and ten vessels of fine, gleaming bronze.
8:57 Then I told them, "You also are holy to the LORD, and so are the holy vessels. The silver and the gold are a vowed gift to the LORD, the Lord of our ancestors.
8:58 Stay awake and guard them until you hand them over to the chief priests, the Levites and the family heads of Israel in Jerusalem, in the storerooms of our Lord's house."
8:59 The priests and the Levites who took charge of the silver, the gold and the vessels for Jerusalem brought them into the Lord's temple.
## Arrival in Jerusalem
8:60 We broke camp at the place called Thera on the twelfth day of the first month and went on until we came to Jerusalem. The strong hand of our Lord was on us. He saved us from every enemy on the way in, and we came to Jerusalem.
8:61 On the third day there, the silver and the gold were weighed and handed over in the Lord's house to Marmoth son of Urias, the priest.
8:62 Eleazar son of Phinehas was with him, and with them were the Levites Josabees, Jeshua, and Moeth son of Sabannus. All was done by count and by weight, and the whole weight was written down then and there.
8:63 The people who had come back from the captivity offered sacrifices to the God of Israel, the Lord: 12 bulls for all Israel, 96 rams, 72 lambs, and 12 male goats as a peace offering. All of it was a sacrifice to the LORD.
8:64 They also handed the king's decrees to the royal managers and the governors of Syria and Phoenicia, and these honored the nation and the Lord's temple.
## News of Mixed Marriages
8:65 Once these things were done, the leaders came up to me and said,
8:66 "The rulers, the priests and the Levites have failed to keep apart from the foreign peoples of the land and their uncleanness: the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, the Moabites, the Egyptians and the Edomites.
8:67 They have taken some of their daughters as wives, both they and their sons. So the holy seed has mingled with the foreign peoples of the land, and the leaders and nobles have had a share in this lawlessness from the start of the affair."
8:68 The moment I heard this, I ripped my garments and my holy robe and tore out hair from my head and my beard, and I sat down, troubled and full of grief.
8:69 All who were stirred by the word of the Lord of Israel gathered to me while I mourned over the lawlessness, and I sat full of grief until the evening sacrifice.
## Ezra's Prayer
8:70 Then I got up from my fasting, my garments and holy robe still torn. I sank to my knees, stretched out my hands to the LORD, and said,
8:71 "Lord, I am ashamed. I am humiliated in your presence.
8:72 Our sins have piled up over our heads, and our errors have risen to the heavens,
8:73 ever since our ancestors' time, and we are in great sin right up to today.
8:74 Because of our sins and our ancestors' sins we were handed over, with our brothers, our kings and our priests, to the kings of the earth: to the sword, to captivity and to looting, with shame, right up to this day.
8:75 Now, for a little while, the LORD, the Lord, has shown us mercy. He has left us a root and a name in this holy place.
8:76 He has uncovered our lamp in our Lord's house and given us food in the time of our slavery. Even while we were slaves, our Lord did not leave us alone.
8:77 Instead, he made the kings of Persia look on us with favor, so that they gave us food,
8:78 honored our temple and raised ruined Zion up again, and gave us a firm footing in Judea and Jerusalem.
8:79 So now, Lord, with all this given us, what are we to say? They have broken the commands you laid down through your servants the prophets, when you said,
8:80 'The land you go in to take possession of is a land defiled by the defilement of the foreigners of the land, and they have filled it with their uncleanness.
8:81 So now, your daughters you must not give to their sons, and their daughters you must not take for your sons.
8:82 Never seek to be at peace with them, for all time. Then you will grow strong, you will eat the good things of the land, and you will pass it on to your sons to own forever.'
8:83 Everything that comes on us comes for our evil deeds and our great sins. Yet you, Lord, made our sins weigh less
8:84 and gave us a root like this one. Yet again we have turned aside to break your law and to mix ourselves with the unclean ways of the nations of the land.
8:85 Would you not be angry with us and destroy us, until no root, no seed and no name of ours was left?
8:86 LORD of Israel, you are true. We have been left as a root, as we are today.
8:87 Look at us here in front of you, in our lawless deeds. Because of these things, no one can stand in your presence any longer."
## Jechonias Proposes an Oath
8:88 Ezra was praying and making confession, weeping and lying face down in front of the temple. As he did, a very large crowd gathered to him from Jerusalem: men and women and young people. There was great weeping in the crowd.
8:89 Then Jechonias son of Jeelus, one of the Israelites, called out to Ezra, "We have sinned against the Lord. Foreign women from the nations of the land have come to live with us. But now all Israel is lifted up.
8:90 In this, let us bind ourselves by oath before the Lord to send away all our wives who are from the foreign peoples, with their children, as you have judged it, you and all who obey the Lord's law.
8:91 Get up and carry it out. The matter rests on you. We stand with you, to act with strength."
8:92 Ezra rose. He made the chief priests and the Levites of all Israel swear to act on these words, and they swore it.
## The Assembly in Winter
9:1 Then Ezra rose and left the court of the temple, and went to the room of Jonas son of Nasibus.
9:2 He spent the night there, but he tasted no bread and drank no water, because he was grieving over the great lawless deeds of the people.
9:3 A call went out through all Judea and Jerusalem to all the people of the exile: they were to assemble at Jerusalem.
9:4 Whoever did not come within two or three days, as the presiding elders had decided, would have his livestock given over to the temple, and he himself would be shut out from the assembly of the exile.
9:5 The men of the tribe of Judah and Benjamin gathered at Jerusalem within the three days. It was the ninth month, on the twentieth day of the month.
9:6 The whole people sat in the open space of the temple, shivering because winter had set in.
9:7 Ezra stood up and spoke to them. "You have acted lawlessly," he said. "You have married foreign wives and so added to Israel's sin.
9:8 So now, give the LORD, the God of our ancestors, your confession and your praise.
9:9 Do his will. Set yourselves apart from the nations of the land and from the foreigners."
9:10 The whole people shouted back in a loud voice, "Just as you have said, so we will do.
9:11 But there are a great many people, and it is the season of winter. We have no strength to stay standing out in the open, and we have found no way. Besides, the work will not take one day or two, since we have sinned greatly in these things.
9:12 Let the leaders of the people take their stand, and let every man from our settlements who has a foreign wife come when his time is set,
9:13 with the elders and the judges of each place, until the Lord's anger over this matter is lifted from us."
9:14 Jonathan son of Azael and Ezias son of Thocanus took the matter on, and Mosollamus, Levis and Sabbataeus sat with them as umpires.
9:15 So the people of the exile did all this.
## The Cases Examined
9:16 Ezra the priest picked out for himself men who were heads of their ancestral families, all of them by name. They shut themselves in to meet on the first day of the tenth month to examine the matter.
9:17 They finished the cases of the men who had taken foreign wives by the first day of the first month.
9:18 Among the priests, these were found to have married foreign wives:
9:19 from the descendants of Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers, Maeelas, Eleazar, Joribus and Jodanus.
9:20 They gave their hand on it that they would send out their wives, and they offered rams to make atonement for their error.
9:21 Of the descendants of Emer: Ananias, Zabdaeus, Manes, Thamaeus, Jereel and Azarias.
9:22 Of the descendants of Phaesur: Elionais, Asseas, Ismael, Nathanaeus, Okailedus and Salthas.
9:23 Of the Levites: Jozabdus, Senseis, Konus (that is, Kalitas), Pathaeus, Ooudas and Joanas.
9:24 Of the temple singers: Eliasebus and Bacchurus.
9:25 Of the gatekeepers: Sallumus and Tolbanes.
9:26 Of Israel: of the descendants of Phoros, Jerma, Jezias, Melchias, Milelus, Eleazar, Asebias and Bannaias.
9:27 Of the descendants of Ela: Matan, Zechariah, Jezoriclus, Oabdius, Jeremoth and Aedias.
9:28 Of the descendants of Zamoth: Eliadas, Eliasimus, Othonias, Jarimoth, Sabathus and Zeralias.
9:29 Of the descendants of Bebai: Joannes, Ananias, Zabdus and Emathis.
9:30 Of the descendants of Mani: Olamus, Mamuchus, Jedaeus, Jasubus, Asaelus and Jeremoth.
9:31 Of the descendants of Addin: Lathus, Moosseias, Lakkunus, Naidus, Beskaspasmys, Sesthel, Balnus and Manasseas.
9:32 Of the descendants of Annan: Eliodas, Asaeas, Melchias, Sabbaeas and Simon Chosamus.
9:33 Of the descendants of Asom: Maltannaeus, Mattathias, Sabannaeus, Eliphalat, Manasseh and Semei.
9:34 Of the descendants of Baani: Jeremias, Momdius, Maerus, Juna, Mamdai, Pedias, Anos, Carabasion, Enasibus, Mamtanaemus, Eliasis, Bannus, Edialis, Someis, Selemias and Nathanias. And of the descendants of Ezora: Sesis, Ezril, Azaelus, Samatus, Zambri and Phosepus.
9:35 Of the descendants of Ooma: Zeitias, Zabadaeas, Edus, Uel and Banaeas.
9:36 All these had taken foreign wives, and they sent them away with their children.
## Ezra Reads the Law
9:37 On the first day of the seventh month the priests, the Levites and the people of Israel were living in Jerusalem and in the countryside, and the Israelites were in their settlements.
9:38 All the people gathered as one in the open square in front of the temple's east gate.
9:39 They called on Ezra the priest and reader to bring Moses' law, which was handed down by the God of Israel.
9:40 So Ezra the high priest brought the law before all the people, men and women alike, and before all the priests, so they could hear the law. It was the first day of the seventh month.
9:41 In the open square in front of the temple gate he read it aloud from first light until noon, before the men and the women. They gave their whole minds to the law.
9:42 Ezra the priest, the reader of the law, stood on a wooden platform built for the occasion.
9:43 Next to him, at his right hand, stood Mattathias, Sammus, Ananias, Azarias, Urias, Ezekias and Baalsamus.
9:44 At his left hand were Phaladaeus, Misael, Melchias, Lothasubus, Nabarias and Zechariah.
9:45 Ezra took up the scroll before all the people, since he was seated in honor at the front, before them all.
9:46 As he unrolled the law, they all rose to their feet. Then Azarias blessed the Most High God, the Almighty.
9:47 All the people answered, "Amen, amen." Raising their hands high, they fell to the ground and bowed down before God.
9:48 Jeshua, Anniuth, Sarabias, Jadinus, Jarsubus, Abtaeus, Autaeas, Maiannas, Kalitas, Azarias, Katethzabdus, Hannias and Phalias, the Levites, taught the Lord's law. They read the Lord's law to the people, breathing life into the reading as they went.
## A Day Holy to the Lord
9:49 Then Attarates said to Ezra, the high priest and reader, and to the Levites, the teachers of the people, in front of them all:
9:50 "This day is holy to the LORD." For they were all crying as they heard the law.
9:51 "So go and eat rich food, and send shares to those who have nothing.
9:52 For the day is holy to the Lord. Don't be downcast, because the LORD will give you glory."
9:53 And the Levites were giving orders to all the people. "This day is holy," they said. "Don't be downcast."
9:54 So they all went off to eat and drink and be glad, to send shares to those who had nothing, and to make great joy,
9:55 for the words they had been taught had breathed life into them. And they gathered together.
