## Darius's Great Feast
3:1 King Darius gave a great feast for all who served under him, for everyone born into his household, and for all the great men of Media and Persia,
3:2 and for all the satraps, the generals and the local governors under him, from India to Ethiopia, in the 127 satrapies.
3:3 They ate and drank their fill and went home. King Darius went off to his bedroom and slept, and then he woke up.
## The Three Guards' Contest
3:4 Then the king's three young bodyguards, the men who guarded his person, said to each other,
3:5 "Each of us should name one thing that is strongest. Whoever's saying seems wiser than the others', King Darius will give him great gifts and great prizes for the win:
3:6 to be dressed in purple, to drink from gold and to sleep on gold, a gold-bridled chariot, a fine linen turban and a chain around his neck.
3:7 He will sit second only to Darius because of his wisdom, and he will be called Darius's kinsman."
3:8 Then each one wrote his own saying, sealed it and put it under King Darius's pillow. They said,
3:9 "When the king gets up, someone will hand him what we wrote. The king and the three great men of Persia will judge whose saying is wiser, and the victory will go to him, as it is written."
3:10 The first wrote, "Wine is strongest."
3:11 The second wrote, "The king is strongest."
3:12 The third wrote, "Women are strongest, but truth wins over all things."
3:13 When the king was awake, they took what was written and handed it to him, and he read it.
3:14 He sent out and called all the great men of Persia and Media, the satraps, the generals, the local governors and the chief officers. He sat down in the hall of audience, and what was written was read aloud before them.
3:15 He said, "Summon the young men. Let them explain their own sayings." They were summoned and came in.
3:16 They said to them, "Tell us about what you have written." The first, the one whose saying was about the strength of wine, began, and he said this:
## Wine Is Strongest
3:17 "Gentlemen, this is how wine is strongest. It sends the mind of everyone who drinks it wandering.
3:18 It makes the mind of the king and the mind of the orphan one and the same, the slave's and the free man's, the poor man's and the rich man's.
3:19 It changes every mind into party mood and merriment, so that it remembers no grief and no debt.
3:20 It makes every heart rich, so that it remembers neither king nor satrap, and it makes everyone talk in talents.
3:21 When they drink, the mind does not remember to be a friend to friends and brothers, and a little later they pull out swords.
3:22 When they rise up from the wine, they have no memory of what they did.
3:23 Gentlemen, wine makes people do all this. Is it not strongest?" Having spoken this way, he was silent.
## The King Is Strongest
4:1 Then the second began, the one whose saying was about the king's strength:
4:2 "Gentlemen, human beings are strongest, aren't they? They hold power over the earth and the sea and everything in them.
4:3 But the king is strongest of all. He lords it over them and masters them, and they listen to anything he tells them.
4:4 If he orders them to fight one another, they do. If he sends them off against the enemy, they march, and they overcome mountains, walls and towers.
4:5 They kill and are killed, and they never step over the king's word. If they conquer, everything goes to the king: whatever they plunder and all the rest.
4:6 Then there are those who do not soldier or go to war but farm the ground. Each time they sow and then reap, they carry the crop up to the king. They press one another to carry their taxes to the king.
4:7 And he is just one man! If he says, 'Kill,' they kill. He says, 'Let go,' and they let go.
4:8 He says, 'Strike,' and they beat. He says, 'Lay it waste,' and they lay waste. He says, 'Build,' and they build.
4:9 He says, 'Cut it down,' and they cut down. He says, 'Plant,' and they plant.
4:10 All his people and his armies obey him. On top of that, he lies back at the table, eats and drinks, and sleeps,
4:11 while they keep watch in a circle around him. None of them can go off and do his own work, and none of them fails to listen to him.
4:12 Gentlemen, how can the king not be strongest, when he is obeyed like this?" And he fell silent.
## Women Are Strongest
4:13 Next the third began to speak. He was the one whose saying was about women and truth, and this was Zerubbabel.
4:14 "Gentlemen, the king is great, men are many, and wine is strong, aren't they? So who is master of them? Who is lord over them? Isn't it women?
4:15 The king was born from women, and so was every people that is lord of the sea and the land.
4:16 They came out of women. Women raised the men who plant the vineyards where wine is made.
4:17 Women make the clothing people wear, and women give men their glory. Without women, men cannot be.
4:18 Suppose men gather gold and silver and every fine thing, and then they see one woman who is lovely to look at and beautiful.
4:19 They drop it all and gawk at her. Their mouths hang open as they look at her, and every one of them picks her over the gold and the silver and every fine thing.
4:20 A man walks away from his own father, who raised him, and from his own country, and he sticks close to his own wife.
4:21 With his wife he lets go of his life, and he remembers neither father nor mother nor country.
4:22 This should show you that women are your masters. Don't you sweat and slave, and then hand it all over and carry it to women?
4:23 A man picks up his sword and heads out on the road to raid and to rob and to steal, to sail over the sea and the rivers.
4:24 He stares down the lion and goes about in the dark. After he has stolen, snatched and stripped people bare, he takes the loot to his beloved.
4:25 A man loves his own wife far more than father and mother.
4:26 Women have driven many out of their own minds, and because of women many have ended up as slaves.
4:27 Many have been destroyed, have fallen and have sinned on account of women.
4:28 So now, will you not believe me? Isn't the king great in his authority? Aren't all the lands afraid to lay a hand on him?
4:29 Yet I used to watch him and Apame, the king's concubine, daughter of the admired Bartacus, seated on the king's right.
4:30 She would lift the crown from the king's head and set it on herself, and she would smack the king with her left hand.
4:31 And on top of this the king gazed at her with his mouth hanging open. If she laughs at him, he laughs. If she gets angry at him, he sweet-talks her so she will make up with him.
4:32 Gentlemen, how can women not be strong, since they act this way?"
## Truth Is Strongest of All
4:33 The king and the great men exchanged looks. Then he started speaking about truth:
4:34 "Gentlemen, are women not strong? Great is the earth, high is the sky, and the sun runs fast. It wheels around the ring of the sky and hurries back to its own place in one day.
4:35 Isn't the one who does these things great? Yet truth is great, and it is stronger than everything.
4:36 All the earth calls out to truth, and the sky blesses it. Every work shakes and trembles, and nothing unjust is with him.
4:37 Wine is unjust, the king is unjust, women are unjust, all human beings are unjust, and all their works are unjust, all such things. There is no truth in them, and in their injustice they will perish.
4:38 But truth stays, and its strength lasts forever. It lives and holds power for ever and ever.
4:39 With truth there is no playing favorites and no bribe. It does what is just, and keeps away from everything unjust and evil, and all are pleased with what it does.
4:40 In its judging, nothing is unjust. It has the strength, the royal power, the authority and the majesty of all the ages. Blessed be the God of truth!"
4:41 With that he was silent. Then all the people cried out, and then they said, "Great is truth, and it is strongest!"
## The Winner's Request
4:42 The king said to him, "Ask for what you would like, more than what was written down, and we will grant it, because you have proved the wiser. You will sit beside me and be called my kinsman."
4:43 Then he said to the king, "Remember the vow you made to build Jerusalem, on the day you took over your kingdom,
4:44 and to return every vessel carried off from Jerusalem, the ones Cyrus set aside when he vowed to cut Babylon down. He vowed to send them back there.
4:45 And you made a vow to build the temple. The Edomites set it on fire when the Chaldeans left Judea a wasteland.
4:46 So now, lord king, this is what I ask and what I request of you. This is the generous act that would come from you: I beg you to carry out the vow your own mouth made to the King of heaven."
## Darius's Letters
4:47 King Darius rose and kissed him. He wrote him letters to all the stewards, local governors, generals and satraps, to escort him and everyone going up with him to build Jerusalem.
4:48 To all the local governors in Coele-Syria and Phoenicia and to those in Lebanon he wrote letters too: they were to carry cedar logs from Lebanon to Jerusalem and to build the city with him.
4:49 For all the Jews who went up out of his kingdom to Judea, he wrote about their freedom. No man of power, no satrap, local governor or steward was to come against their doors.
4:50 Any land they took over was to pay no tax, and the Chaldeans were to let go of the Jewish villages they still held.
4:51 Every year twenty talents were to go toward building the temple until the building was done,
4:52 and to have burnt offerings burned on the altar each day, as they are commanded to offer seventeen, ten more talents every year.
4:53 Everyone coming over from Babylonia to found the city was to be free, both they and their children, and all the priests coming over too.
4:54 He also wrote out their allowance, and the priests' robes they would wear to serve.
4:55 He wrote that the Levites were to get their allowance until the day the house was completed and Jerusalem built up.
4:56 He wrote that all who stood guard over the city were to get plots of land and pay.
4:57 He sent off every vessel Cyrus had set aside from Babylon, and whatever Cyrus had said should be done, he too gave orders to do it and send it to Jerusalem.
## Zerubbabel's Prayer
4:58 As the young man went out, he raised his face to heaven, facing Jerusalem, and blessed the King of heaven:
4:59 "Victory is from you, wisdom is from you, the glory is yours, and I am your household servant.
4:60 Blessed are you, for you gave me wisdom. I give thanks to you, Master of the fathers."
4:61 He took the letters and went out to Babylon, and he told all his brothers.
4:62 They blessed the God of their fathers, because he had given them relief and release
4:63 to go up and build Jerusalem and the temple that bears his name. And they held a drinking feast with music and joy for seven days.
## The Return Under Darius
5:1 Then leaders of families, tribe by tribe, were picked to go up, along with their wives, sons and daughters, their male and female slaves, and their animals.
5:2 Darius sent along a thousand horsemen to escort them until they were settled again in Jerusalem in peace, with music, drums and flutes.
5:3 All their brothers were celebrating, and he had them go up together with the others.
5:4 Here are the names of the men going up, family by family in their tribes, under their group leaders.
5:5 The priests, the sons of Phinehas son of Aaron: Jeshua son of Jozadak son of Seraiah, and Joakim son of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, from the house of David, of the line of Perez, of the tribe of Judah,
5:6 He was the one who spoke wise words in front of Darius king of Persia, in Nisan, the first month, in the second year of his reign.
## Those Who Came Back
5:7 These are the people from Judea who came up out of captivity, from where they had lived as strangers. Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried them off to Babylon.
5:8 They came back to Jerusalem and the rest of Judea, each to his own town. They came with Zerubbabel and Jeshua, Nehemiah, Zaraias, Resaias, Enenius, Mordecai, Beelsarus, Aspharasus, Borolius, Roimus and Baana, their leaders.
5:9 The number of those from the nation, with their leaders: descendants of Phoros, 2,172. Descendants of Saphat, 472.
5:10 Descendants of Ares: 756.
5:11 Descendants of Phthalimoab, through the line of Jeshua and Roboab: 2,802.
5:12 Descendants of Iolamus: 2. Descendants of Zaton: 970. Descendants of Chorbe: 705. Descendants of Bani: 648.
5:13 Descendants of Bebai: 633. Descendants of Argai: 1,322.
5:14 Descendants of Adonikam: 37. Descendants of Bosai: 2,606. Descendants of Asilius: 454.
5:15 Descendants of Azer, of Ezekias. Descendants of Kilan and Azetas: 67. Descendants of Azarus: 432.
5:16 Descendants of Annis: 101. Descendants of Arom. Descendants of Bassai: 323. Descendants of Arsiphurith.
5:17 Descendants of Baiterus: 3,005. Descendants from Ragethlomon: 123.
5:18 Those from Netebas: 55. Those from Enatus: 158. Those from Baitasmon Zammoth.
5:19 Those from Kartathiarius: 25. Those from Piras and Berog: 700.
5:20 The Chadiasai and the Ammidioi: 422. Those from Kirama Kabbes: 621.
5:21 Those from Makalon: 122. Those from Betolo: 52. Niphis: 156.
5:22 Descendants of Kalamokalus and Onus: 725. Descendants of Jerechus: 245.
5:23 Descendants of Sama: 3,301.
## Priests, Levites and Temple Servants
5:24 The priests: descendants of Jeddu son of Jeshua, among the descendants of Sanabis: 872. Descendants of Ermerus: 252.
5:25 Descendants of Phassorus: 1,247. Descendants of Charue: 217.
5:26 The Levites: descendants of Jesuis, Kodoelus, Bannus and Sudius: 74.
5:27 The temple singers: descendants of Asaph: 128.
5:28 The gatekeepers: descendants of Salum, Atar, Tolman, Dakubi, Ateta and Sabi, 139 in all.
5:29 The temple servants: the families of Esau, Tasipha, Tabaoth, Keras, Sua, Phalaeus, Labana,
5:30 Akud, Uta, Ketab, Hakkaba, Subai, Hanan, Kua, Keddur,
5:31 Jaeirus, Daisan, Noeba, Chaseba, Kazera, Ozius, Phinoe, Asara, Basthai, Assana, Mani, Naphisi, Akuph, Achiba, Asur, Pharakem, Basalem,
5:32 Dedda, Bachus, Serar, Thomthi, Nasi and Hatepha.
5:33 The descendants of Solomon's servants: the families of Hassaphioth, Pharida, Jeieli, Lozon, Isdael, Saphui,
5:34 Hagia, Phakareth-sabie, Sarothi, Misaias, Gas, Addus, Subas, Apherra, Barodis, Saphag and Allon.
5:35 All the temple servants and the descendants of Solomon's servants: 372.
## Families Without Proof
5:36 These came up from Thermeleth and Thelersas. Their leaders were Charaathalan and Allar.
5:37 They could not tell their families or their line, to show that they were from Israel: descendants of Asan son of Baenan, and descendants of Nekodan: 652.
5:38 And from the sons of priests, those who claimed the priesthood but were not found: descendants of Obbia, Akbos and Jaddus. Jaddus had married Augia, one of the daughters of Phaezeldaeus, and was called by his name.
5:39 Their family record was searched for in the list and could not be found, so they were removed from the priesthood.
5:40 Namias and Attharias told them not to have any share of the holy things until a priest arose clothed in the Showing and the Truth.
## The Whole Assembly
5:41 All of them, Israel from twelve years old and up, not counting male and female slaves, were 42,360. Their male and female slaves were 7,337. Harpists and singers: 245.
5:42 Camels: 435. Horses: 7,036. Mules: 245. Pack animals: 5,525.
5:43 When they reached the temple of God in Jerusalem, some of the heads of families vowed to set the house up again on its site, as they were able,
5:44 and to give to the temple treasury for the work 1,000 minas of gold, 5,000 minas of silver and 100 robes for priests.
5:45 The priests, the Levites and some of the people made their homes in Jerusalem and the countryside. The temple singers, the gatekeepers and all Israel lived in their villages.
## The Altar Rebuilt
5:46 The seventh month arrived, and the Israelites were each at their own places. They gathered all together as one in the open square of the first gate, the one facing east.
5:47 Then Jeshua son of Jozadak rose up with his brothers the priests, and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel with his brothers. They made ready the altar of Israel's God,
5:48 so that they could offer burnt offerings on it, following what is laid down in the book of Moses the man of God.
5:49 People from the other nations of the land gathered to them, and they set the altar up on its site, for they were hostile to them, and all the nations of the earth were stronger than they were. They brought sacrifices in their season, and burnt offerings to the LORD, those of morning and evening.
5:50 They held the Feast of Booths as the law commands, and they offered sacrifices every day, as was fitting.
5:51 After this came the regular offerings, and the sacrifices for the Sabbaths, the new moons and all the holy feasts.
5:52 Those who had vowed a vow to God since the new moon of the first month began to bring sacrifices to God, even though God's temple was not built yet.
5:53 They gave money to the stonecutters and the woodworkers, and drink, food and good cheer to the people of Sidon and Tyre, to bring cedar wood from Lebanon and float it on rafts to Joppa's harbor, by the written order Cyrus king of Persia had given them.
## The Foundation Laid
5:54 In the second year after he came to the temple of God in Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak made a start, with their brothers, the Levite priests and all who had come from the captivity to Jerusalem.
5:55 On the new moon of the second month, in the second year after their arrival in Judea and Jerusalem, they founded God's temple.
5:56 They appointed the Levites twenty years old and up over the work of the LORD. Jeshua stood up with his sons and his brothers, and Damadiel his brother, and the sons of Jeshua Emadabun, and the sons of Judah son of Iliadun, with their sons and brothers. All the Levites, as one, oversaw the work, doing the work on the house of the Lord. And the builders built the LORD's temple.
5:57 The priests stood, dressed in their robes, with music and trumpets, and the Levites of Asaph's family had cymbals, singing hymns to the LORD and blessing him, as David king of Israel had arranged.
5:58 They lifted their voices in hymns, blessing the LORD: "For his kindness and glory last forever, for all Israel."
5:59 The whole people blew trumpets and gave a great shout, singing hymns to the LORD because the LORD's house was rising.
5:60 Old men came from among the Levite priests and the heads of families, men who had seen the earlier house. They came to this house as it was being built, crying out and weeping aloud,
5:61 and many others with trumpets and with voices full of joy,
5:62 so that the people could not hear the trumpets over the people's weeping. Yet the crowd blew the trumpets so hard that the sound carried far away.
## Builders Turned Away
5:63 Hearing this, the enemies of the tribe of Judah and Benjamin came to learn what the trumpet blasts were.
5:64 They found out that those from the captivity were building the temple for the LORD, the God of Israel.
5:65 So they came to Zerubbabel and Jeshua and to the heads of families, and said to them, "We will build with you.
5:66 Like you, we heed your Lord. We have offered sacrifices to him from the days of Asbakaphath king of Assyria, who brought us over here."
5:67 But Zerubbabel, Jeshua and the heads of families of Israel told them, "It is not for you to build the house for the Lord our God.
5:68 We alone will build it for the LORD of Israel, as Cyrus king of Persia commanded us."
5:69 But the nations of the land lay heavy on the people in Judea. They besieged them and blocked the building.
5:70 With schemes and rabble-rousing and plots, they stopped the building from being completed the whole time King Cyrus was alive. For two years, until Darius became king, they were shut out of the building work.
