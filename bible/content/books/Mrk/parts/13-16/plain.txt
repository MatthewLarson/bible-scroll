## The temple will fall
13:1 As he was coming out of the temple, one of his disciples said to him, "Teacher, look! Such stones! Such buildings!"
13:2 Jesus said to him, "Are you looking at these great buildings? Here not one stone will be left on a stone. Every one will be knocked down."
## The start of the labor pains
13:3 He was sitting on the Mount of Olives, across from the temple, when Peter, James, John and Andrew questioned him by themselves.
13:4 "Tell us, when will this happen? And what sign will show that all these things are about to be completed?"
13:5 Jesus began to tell them, "Be on your guard, so that no one leads you astray.
13:6 Many will come using my name. They will say, 'I am he,' and they will lead many people astray.
13:7 When you hear of wars and reports of wars, do not panic. This has to happen, but the end is not here yet.
13:8 One nation will rise up against another, and one kingdom against another. There will be earthquakes in place after place. There will be famines. These things are the start of the labor pains.
13:9 "But you, be on your guard for yourselves. They will hand you over to councils, and in synagogues you will be beaten. Because of me you will stand before governors and kings, as evidence to them.
13:10 And first the good news must be proclaimed to all the nations.
13:11 When they lead you away to hand you over, do not worry ahead of time about what to say. Whatever words you are given when the hour comes, speak those. It is not you who will be doing the talking, but the Holy Spirit.
13:12 One brother will hand another over to be killed, and a father will hand over his child. Children will rise up against their parents and have them executed.
13:13 Everyone will hate you because of my name. But whoever holds out to the end, that person will be saved.
## The abomination and the great suffering
13:14 "When you see 'the abomination of desolation' standing where he must not stand (the one who reads this should think about what it means), then those who are in Judea must run for the hills.
13:15 Whoever is up on the roof must not go down, or go inside to get anything out of his house.
13:16 Whoever is out in the field must not go back home for his coat.
13:17 In those days, woe to women who are expecting, and to mothers nursing babies!
13:18 Pray that it will not come in winter.
13:19 For those days will be a time of suffering. Nothing like it has happened from the start of the creation God made until now, and nothing like it ever will.
13:20 If the Lord had not made those days shorter, no one at all would be saved. But for the sake of the chosen, the ones he chose, he has made the days shorter.
13:21 "And then if someone tells you, 'See, the Messiah is here!' or 'See, he is there!' do not believe them.
13:22 For false messiahs and false prophets will rise up. They will give signs and wonders to lead the chosen astray, if that were possible.
13:23 But you, be on your guard. I have told you everything in advance.
## The Son of Man comes
13:24 "But in those days, after that suffering, 'the sun will turn dark, and the moon will not shed its glow.
13:25 The stars will be falling from the sky, and the powers in the heavens will be shaken.'
13:26 Then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds, with much power and glory.
13:27 Then he will send out the angels and gather together his chosen from the four winds, from the farthest edge of the earth to the farthest edge of the sky.
## The lesson of the fig tree
13:28 "Learn this parable from the fig tree. When its branch is already soft and it pushes out its leaves, you know that summer is close.
13:29 In the same way, when you see these things happening, know that it is near, right outside the doors.
13:30 Amen, I tell you, this generation will not be gone before all these things happen.
13:31 Heaven and earth will be gone, but my words will never be gone.
## No one knows the day
13:32 "As for that day or hour, no one knows it. The angels in heaven do not know, and the Son does not know. Only the Father knows.
13:33 Be on your guard! Stay alert! You do not know when the appointed time will be.
13:34 It is like a man away from home on a trip. He has left his house and given his slaves authority, each one his own task, and he has ordered the doorkeeper to stay awake.
13:35 So stay awake. You do not know when the house's master is coming: late in the evening, at midnight, at rooster crow, or at dawn.
13:36 He might arrive without warning and find you sleeping.
13:37 And what I tell you, I tell everyone: stay awake!"
## The plot against Jesus
14:1 In two days it would be the Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread. The chief priests and the scribes were looking for a way to seize Jesus by a trick and kill him.
14:2 For they kept saying, "Not at the festival, or there will be an uproar among the people."
## A woman pours oil on Jesus at Bethany
14:3 He was in Bethany, in the house of Simon, the man with the skin disease. While he lay at the table, a woman came holding an alabaster jar of perfumed oil, real nard that cost a great deal. She broke the jar open and poured the oil over his head.
14:4 Some of those there grew angry and said to one another, "What was this waste of the oil for?
14:5 Someone could have sold this oil for over three hundred denarii and given that to the poor." And they turned on her angrily.
14:6 But Jesus said, "Leave her alone. Why are you making trouble for her? What she has done for me is a beautiful work.
14:7 For you have the poor among you at all times, and you are able to help them whenever you wish. Me, though, you do not have at all times.
14:8 She has done what was in her power. She has come ahead of time to put oil on my body for its burial.
14:9 Amen, I tell you, wherever the good news is proclaimed in the whole world, people will also tell the story of what she did, so that she is remembered."
## Judas agrees to hand Jesus over
14:10 And Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve, left and went to the chief priests, intending to hand Jesus over to them.
14:11 They were glad when they heard it, and they promised him silver. From then on he looked for the right moment to hand him over.
## The Passover meal
14:12 On the first day of the Feast of Unleavened Bread, when the Passover lambs were sacrificed, his disciples said to him, "Where would you like us to go and get the Passover ready for you to eat?"
14:13 He sent off two of his disciples and told them, "Go into the city. You will meet a man carrying a clay jar of water. Go after him.
14:14 Wherever he goes in, say to the owner of the house, 'The Teacher says: Where is the room for me to stay, where I will eat the Passover with my disciples?'
14:15 He himself will show you a big room upstairs, laid out with couches and ready. Make it ready for us there."
14:16 The disciples set off and came into the city. Everything was as he had said, and they made the Passover ready.
14:17 As evening fell, he came with the Twelve.
14:18 As they lay at the table eating, Jesus said, "Amen, I tell you, one of you will hand me over, a man eating with me."
14:19 They began to be deeply hurt and to say to him, one by one, "It isn't me, is it?"
14:20 He told them, "One of the Twelve. He is dipping into the same bowl as I am.
14:21 For the Son of Man goes his way, just as it stands written about him. But woe to the man through whom the Son of Man is handed over! Better for that man never to have been born."
## Bread and cup
14:22 As they ate, he took a loaf of bread and said the blessing. He broke it and handed it to them. "Take it," he said. "This is my body."
14:23 He also took a cup and gave thanks. He passed it to them, and every one of them drank from it.
14:24 He said to them, "This is my blood of the covenant. It is being poured out for many.
14:25 Amen, I tell you, I will never again drink the produce of the vine until that day, the day I drink it new in the kingdom of God."
14:26 After singing the hymn, they left for the Mount of Olives.
## Jesus predicts Peter's denial
14:27 Jesus said to them, "Every one of you will be tripped up. For Scripture says, 'I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.'
14:28 But after I have been raised, I will get to Galilee ahead of you."
14:29 Peter said to him, "Even if everyone else is tripped up, I will not be."
14:30 Jesus said to him, "Amen, I tell you: today, this very night, before a rooster crows twice, you yourself will deny me three times."
14:31 But he kept saying it all the more strongly: "I will never deny you, not even if I have to die with you!" And all of them talked the same way.
## Praying in Gethsemane
14:32 They came to a spot named Gethsemane. He told his disciples, "Stay seated here until I have prayed."
14:33 He brought Peter, James and John with him. He began to be deeply alarmed and distressed.
14:34 He told them, "My soul is crushed with grief, enough to kill me. Wait here and stay awake."
14:35 He went on a short way and threw himself on the ground. He prayed that, if it could be done, the hour might go past him.
14:36 "Abba, Father," he was saying, "for you anything is possible. Make this cup pass me by. Even so, not what I want, but what you want."
14:37 He came back and found them asleep. He said to Peter, "Simon, are you sleeping? Were you not strong enough to keep awake for one hour?
14:38 Stay awake and pray that you do not come into testing. The spirit is eager, the flesh weak."
14:39 He went away again and prayed, and he said the same thing.
14:40 He returned once more and found them asleep, because their eyes were weighed down. They had no idea how to answer him.
14:41 He came the third time and said to them, "Go on, sleep now and rest. It is enough. The hour has come. Look, the Son of Man is being handed over into the hands of sinners.
14:42 Get up, let's go. Look, the man handing me over has come close."
## The arrest
14:43 Right away, while he was still talking, Judas arrived, one of the Twelve. With him was a crowd carrying swords and clubs. They came from the chief priests, the scribes and the elders.
14:44 The man handing him over had given them a signal. "Whoever I kiss, that's him," he said. "Grab him, and keep him secure as you take him away."
14:45 As soon as he arrived, he went straight up to Jesus. "Rabbi!" he said, and he kissed him.
14:46 Then they put their hands on him and held him fast.
14:47 But someone among those standing by pulled out his sword. He struck the high priest's slave, slicing off his ear.
14:48 Jesus said to them in reply, "Am I a bandit, that you come out with swords and clubs to take me?
14:49 Day by day I was among you in the temple, teaching, and you did not lay a hand on me. But it is so that the Scriptures would be fulfilled."
14:50 Then they all abandoned him and ran off.
## The young man who ran away
14:51 A certain young man had been following along with him, wearing a linen cloth over his naked body. They grabbed him,
14:52 but he left the cloth in their hands and fled naked.
## Before the Sanhedrin
14:53 They took Jesus off to the high priest, and all the chief priests, elders and scribes gathered.
14:54 Peter had followed him from far back, as far as inside the high priest's courtyard. He was sitting with the officers and warming himself at the fire's light.
14:55 The chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin were looking for testimony against Jesus that would let them put him to death, but they were not finding any.
14:56 Many were testifying falsely against him, but their testimonies did not match.
14:57 Some got up and testified falsely against him:
14:58 "We heard him say, 'I will tear down this sanctuary that human hands made, and within three days I will build another that no hands made.'"
14:59 Even so, what they said did not match.
14:60 The high priest stood up in the middle and questioned Jesus. "You answer nothing?" he said. "What are these men testifying against you?"
14:61 But he stayed silent and answered nothing. Again the high priest questioned him. "Are you the Messiah," he asked, "the Son of the Blessed One?"
14:62 Jesus said, "I am. And you will see the Son of Man sitting at the Power's right hand and coming with the clouds of the sky."
14:63 The high priest ripped his tunics. "Why should we need witnesses anymore?" he said.
14:64 "You heard the blasphemy. What is your opinion?" And all of them judged him guilty and liable to the death penalty.
14:65 Some of them started spitting on him. They covered his face, punched him and said to him, "Be a prophet!" And the officers took him and slapped him.
## Peter denies Jesus three times
14:66 Peter was below in the courtyard. One of the high priest's slave girls came along.
14:67 She noticed Peter warming himself. She stared at him and said, "You too were with the Nazarene, with Jesus."
14:68 He denied it. "I have no idea, and I do not understand, what you mean," he said. He went outside to the forecourt, and a rooster crowed.
14:69 The slave girl caught sight of him and started telling the bystanders again, "This man is one of them."
14:70 Again he denied it. A little later the bystanders said to Peter again, "You really are one of them. You are a Galilean too."
14:71 But he began to curse and to swear, "I do not know this man you mean!"
14:72 Right then a rooster crowed a second time. Peter remembered what Jesus had said to him: "Before a rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times." And he burst into tears.
## Handed over to Pilate
15:1 At first light the chief priests, with the elders, the scribes and the whole Sanhedrin, reached a decision. They tied Jesus up, took him away and handed him over to Pilate.
## Jesus before Pilate
15:2 Pilate questioned him: "So you are the King of the Jews?" "You are the one saying it," Jesus answered him.
15:3 The chief priests brought many charges against him.
15:4 Pilate questioned him again. "You answer nothing?" he said. "Look how much they are accusing you of!"
15:5 But Jesus answered nothing more, and Pilate was very surprised.
15:6 At the festival he used to set one prisoner free for them, whichever one they asked for.
15:7 There was a man called Barabbas, held in chains with the rebels who had killed people during the uprising.
15:8 The crowd went up and started asking him to do as he used to do for them.
15:9 Pilate answered them, "Do you want me to set the King of the Jews free for you?"
15:10 For he knew it was jealousy that made the chief priests hand him over.
15:11 The chief priests, though, worked the crowd up so that he would free Barabbas for them instead.
15:12 Pilate spoke to them again: "So what do you want me to do with this 'King of the Jews,' as you call him?"
15:13 They shouted back, "Crucify him!"
15:14 Pilate said to them, "What evil has he done, then?" But they only screamed louder, "Crucify him!"
15:15 Pilate wished to keep the crowd happy, so he freed Barabbas for them. Jesus he had whipped, and then he handed him over for crucifixion.
## The soldiers mock the King
15:16 The soldiers led him away into the courtyard, that is, the Praetorium, and called the whole cohort together.
15:17 They dressed him in purple. They wove thorny branches into a crown and placed it on him.
15:18 They started to greet him: "Hail, King of the Jews!"
15:19 They kept hitting him on the head with a reed and spitting on him. They bent their knees and bowed down before him.
15:20 When they were done making fun of him, they pulled the purple off him and dressed him in his own clothes. Then they marched him out to crucify him.
## The crucifixion
15:21 They drafted a man passing by to carry Jesus' cross. He was Simon of Cyrene, the father of Alexander and Rufus, who was coming in from the fields.
15:22 They brought Jesus to the spot Golgotha, which, translated, is Skull Place.
15:23 They kept trying to give him myrrh-flavored wine, but he would not accept it.
15:24 Then they crucified him and shared out his clothes, throwing lots for them to see who would take what.
15:25 It was the third hour, and they crucified him.
15:26 The inscription with the charge was written up: "The King of the Jews."
15:27 Along with him they crucified two bandits, one on his right and one on his left.
15:28 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
15:29 Those walking past kept reviling him. They shook their heads and said, "Ha! You who tear down the sanctuary and build it in three days,
15:30 save yourself by coming down from the cross!"
15:31 The chief priests made fun of him in the same way among themselves, along with the scribes. They said, "Others he saved. Himself he cannot save.
15:32 The Messiah, the King of Israel, should come down off the cross right now, so that we can see and believe!" Even the men crucified with him insulted him.
## The death of Jesus
15:33 When the sixth hour came, darkness lay over the whole land until the ninth hour.
15:34 At the ninth hour Jesus shouted with a great cry, "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?" which means, "My God, my God, why have you abandoned me?"
15:35 Hearing it, some of those standing there said, "See, he's calling for Elijah!"
15:36 Someone ran and soaked a sponge with sour wine. He put it on a reed and held it up for him to drink. "Hold on!" he said. "Let's see whether Elijah comes to lift him down."
15:37 But Jesus gave a loud cry and breathed out his life.
15:38 And the sanctuary curtain was torn in two from the top down to the bottom.
15:39 The centurion, standing there opposite him, saw the way he breathed out his life, and said, "This man really was God's Son!"
15:40 There were women also watching from far off. Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of the younger James and of Joses, and Salome.
15:41 These women had followed him and served him when he was in Galilee. Many other women were there too, who had come up with him to Jerusalem.
## The burial
15:42 It was already evening, and it was the Day of Preparation, which is the day before the Sabbath.
15:43 Joseph from Arimathea came. He was a council member of high standing, and he too was looking for the kingdom of God. He dared to go in to Pilate and ask for Jesus' body.
15:44 Pilate wondered whether he could be dead already. He summoned the centurion and asked him whether he had died some time ago.
15:45 Once the centurion told him, he gave Joseph the corpse as a gift.
15:46 Joseph bought a linen cloth. He took Jesus down, wrapped him in the linen and laid him in a tomb carved out of the rock. Then he rolled a stone up against the door of the tomb.
15:47 Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses watched to see where he had been put.
## He has been raised
16:1 Once the Sabbath had passed, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome bought fragrant spices. They meant to come and anoint him.
16:2 Very early on the first day of the week, with the sun now up, they came to the tomb.
16:3 They kept asking one another, "Who is going to roll the stone back from the tomb's door for us?"
16:4 When they looked up, they could see the stone had already been rolled back. And it was enormous.
16:5 When they went inside the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right, clothed in a long white robe. They were utterly alarmed.
16:6 He said to them, "Do not be alarmed. You are looking for Jesus the Nazarene, the one who was crucified. He has been raised. He isn't here. Look, here is the place where they put him.
16:7 But go and tell his disciples, and Peter, 'He is going on ahead of you to Galilee. That is where you will see him, as he told you.'"
16:8 They went out and fled from the tomb, because shaking and shock had taken hold of them. And they told no one anything, for they were afraid.
## A later ending: Jesus appears
16:9 Having risen early on the first day of the week, Jesus showed himself first to Mary Magdalene. He had driven seven demons out of her.
16:10 She went and brought the news to his companions, who were grieving and weeping.
16:11 They heard that he was living and had been seen by her, yet they refused to believe it.
16:12 Later, two of them were walking out into the countryside, and he showed himself to them in a different form.
16:13 Those two also went off and told the rest. The rest did not believe them either.
16:14 Later he showed himself to the Eleven themselves as they lay at the table. He rebuked their unbelief and hard-heartedness, because they had not believed those who saw him raised.
16:15 He said to them, "Go out into the whole world and proclaim the good news to every creature.
16:16 The one who has believed and been baptized will be saved. The one who has refused to believe will be condemned.
16:17 And signs like these will follow those who have believed. Using my name they will drive out demons. They will speak in new tongues.
16:18 They will take up snakes, and if they drink some deadly thing, it will do them no harm at all. They will put their hands on people who are sick, and these will be well."
16:19 When the Lord Jesus had finished speaking with them, he was taken up into heaven, and he sat down at God's right hand.
16:20 And they went out and proclaimed it everywhere. The Lord kept working with them, and he backed up the word through the signs that followed.
