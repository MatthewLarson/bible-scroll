## A question about divorce
10:1 He got up and left that place, and he came into the territory of Judea and the far side of the Jordan. Crowds came along to him again, and he taught them again, the way he usually did.
10:2 Some Pharisees came up and asked him whether a man is permitted to divorce a wife. They were testing him.
10:3 He answered, "What command did Moses give you?"
10:4 They said, "Moses let a man write out a divorce document and send her away."
10:5 Jesus said to them, "It was in view of your hard-heartedness that he wrote you this command.
10:6 But from the start of creation, 'male and female he made them.'
10:7 'That is why a man will leave his father and his mother and stick close to his wife,
10:8 and the two will become one flesh.' So they are no longer two, but one flesh.
10:9 What God has yoked together, then, no human being should separate."
10:10 When they had gone into the house, the disciples questioned him about it again.
10:11 He told them, "Anyone who divorces his wife and marries someone else commits adultery against her.
10:12 And if she herself divorces her husband and marries another man, she commits adultery."
## Jesus and the children
10:13 Now people kept bringing little children to him, so that he would touch them. The disciples, though, scolded them.
10:14 Jesus saw it and grew angry. "Let the children come to me," he told them. "Do not stop them. The kingdom of God is for people like them.
10:15 Amen, I tell you: anyone who does not welcome the kingdom of God the way a child does will not get into it at all."
10:16 He gathered the children into his arms and kept blessing them, with his hands resting on them.
## The rich man
10:17 Jesus was heading out onto the road when a man ran up to him and dropped to his knees. "Good teacher," he asked him, "what should I do so that I will inherit eternal life?"
10:18 Jesus said to him, "Why do you call me good? No one is good except one: God.
10:19 You know the commandments: 'You must not murder. You must not commit adultery. You must not steal. You must not give false testimony. You must not cheat. Honor your father and mother.'"
10:20 He said to him, "Teacher, I have obeyed every one of these ever since I was young."
10:21 Jesus fixed his eyes on him and loved him. "One thing is missing for you," he said. "Go and sell whatever you have and give it to the poor. You will have treasure in heaven. And come, follow me."
10:22 But at that word his face clouded over, and he left in grief. He owned a great deal of property.
10:23 Jesus looked around him and told his disciples, "With what difficulty will people who have money get into the kingdom of God!"
10:24 His words left the disciples astounded. Jesus spoke to them once more: "Children, getting into the kingdom of God is so hard!
10:25 A camel could get through a needle's eye more easily than a rich person could get into the kingdom of God."
10:26 They were stunned all the more. "Who can be saved, then?" they said to one another.
10:27 Jesus looked straight at them and said, "From the human side it is impossible, but not with God. With God, anything is possible."
10:28 Peter started to say to him, "Look at us. We left it all behind and have followed you."
10:29 Jesus said, "Amen, I tell you: no one has left home, brothers, sisters, mother, father, children or farms because of me and because of the good news
10:30 without getting back a hundred times as much now, in this present time: homes, brothers, sisters, mothers, children and farms, along with persecutions. And in the age that is coming he will get eternal life.
10:31 But many of the first will be last, and the last will be first."
## Jesus speaks of his death a third time
10:32 Now they were on the road up to Jerusalem. Jesus was going on ahead of them; they were astounded, and the ones following behind were afraid. He took the Twelve aside again and began to tell them the things that were going to happen to him.
10:33 "Listen. We are on our way up to Jerusalem. The Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and the scribes. They will sentence him to death and turn him over to the Gentiles,
10:34 who will mock him, spit on him, whip him and kill him. And he will rise after three days."
## James and John make a request
10:35 Zebedee's sons James and John came up to him. "Teacher," they said, "whatever we ask you for, we want you to do it for us."
10:36 He said to them, "What would you like me to do for you?"
10:37 "Give us this," they told him: "in your glory, let one of us sit on your right and one on your left."
10:38 Jesus said to them, "You have no idea what you are asking for. Can you drink the cup that I am drinking, or be baptized with the baptism that I am being baptized with?"
10:39 "We can," they told him. Jesus told them, "The cup I am drinking, you will drink. The baptism I am being baptized with, you will be baptized with.
10:40 But the seats at my right or my left are not mine to hand out. They go to the ones they have been prepared for."
10:41 When the ten heard about it, they began to be annoyed at James and John.
10:42 Jesus called them over and said to them, "You know how it is with the nations: those who are thought of as their rulers act as lords over them, and their important men use their power over them.
10:43 That is not how it is with you. No, if anyone among you wishes to become great, he will be your servant.
10:44 And if anyone among you wishes to be first, he will be everyone's slave.
10:45 The Son of Man, too, did not come to have people serve him. He came to serve, and to give his life as a ransom in place of many."
## Blind Bartimaeus
10:46 Then they reached Jericho. He was on his way out of Jericho, along with his disciples and a big crowd, and there by the road sat Timaeus' son Bartimaeus, a blind beggar.
10:47 When he heard that it was Jesus the Nazarene, he began to cry out, "Son of David, Jesus, show me mercy!"
10:48 Many sternly told him to hush. But he cried out all the more, "Son of David, show me mercy!"
10:49 Jesus came to a stop. "Call him over," he said. So they called to the blind man. "Courage!" they told him. "Up on your feet. He is calling for you."
10:50 He flung away his cloak, leaped up and came over to Jesus.
10:51 "What would you like me to do for you?" Jesus asked him. The blind man said to him, "Rabbouni, I want to see again."
10:52 Jesus said to him, "Go. Your faith has saved you." At once he could see again, and he began following Jesus on the road.
## The king comes to Jerusalem
11:1 When they were getting close to Jerusalem, to Bethphage and Bethany by the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent off two of his disciples
11:2 and said to them, "Go to the village facing you. As soon as you go into it you will find a colt tied up, one that no person has ever sat on yet. Set it loose and bring it.
11:3 Should anyone say to you, 'Why are you doing this?' tell him, 'The Lord has need of it. He will send it straight back here.'"
11:4 Off they went, and they found a colt tied up beside a doorway, out on the street. They set it loose.
11:5 Some of the bystanders asked them, "What do you mean by setting the colt loose?"
11:6 They spoke to them the way Jesus had said, and the men let them be.
11:7 So they led the colt back to Jesus and laid their cloaks over it, and he took his seat on it.
11:8 Many laid their own cloaks down on the road, and others laid down greenery they had cut in the fields.
11:9 The people walking in front and the ones coming behind were crying out, "Hosanna! Blessed is the one who comes in the Lord's name!
11:10 Blessed is our father David's kingdom, which is coming! Hosanna in the highest!"
11:11 He came into Jerusalem and into the temple, and took a look around at everything. The hour was already late, so he went out to Bethany with the Twelve.
## The fig tree
11:12 On the following day, after they had left Bethany, he felt hungry.
11:13 From far off he spotted a fig tree that had leaves on it, and he went over to see if perhaps he would find something on it. When he reached it he found nothing there, only leaves. It was not the time for figs.
11:14 Then he spoke to the tree: "Never again, for all time, may anyone eat fruit from you!" And his disciples were listening.
## Jesus in the temple
11:15 They came into Jerusalem. He went into the temple and began to throw out the people who were selling and buying in the temple. He turned over the money changers' tables and the chairs of the dove sellers.
11:16 He would not let anyone carry a container through the temple.
11:17 He taught them, saying, "Does Scripture not say, 'My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations'? But you have made it a cave for bandits."
11:18 When the chief priests and the scribes heard of it, they tried to find how they might do away with him. They feared him, since his teaching left the whole crowd stunned.
11:19 Each time it grew late, they went outside the city.
## The fig tree dried up
11:20 Early in the morning, as they walked past, they saw the fig tree dried up from the roots.
11:21 Peter remembered. "Rabbi," he said to him, "look: the fig tree that you cursed is all dried up."
11:22 Jesus answered them, "Have faith in God.
11:23 Amen, I tell you: whoever tells this mountain, 'Be lifted up and hurled into the sea,' and does not waver in his heart, but believes that what he says is happening, he will have it.
11:24 That is why I tell you: everything you pray and ask for, believe that you received it, and you will have it.
11:25 And whenever you are standing in prayer and you have a complaint against anyone, forgive him, so that your Father who is in heaven will also forgive you your wrongs."
11:26 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
## By what authority?
11:27 Once more they came into Jerusalem. While he was walking around in the temple, up came the chief priests, the scribes and the elders.
11:28 "On what authority do you do these things?" they asked him. "Or who handed you this authority to do these things?"
11:29 Jesus said to them, "I have one question to put to you. Answer me, and I will tell you on what authority I do these things.
11:30 The baptism of John: was it from heaven, or from human beings? Answer me."
11:31 They talked it over among themselves. "Suppose we say, 'From heaven.' He will say, 'So why did you not believe him?'
11:32 But suppose we say, 'From human beings'..." They were afraid of the crowd, for all the people considered John to have been a true prophet.
11:33 So their answer to Jesus was, "We do not know." And Jesus told them, "Then I am not telling you either on what authority I do these things."
## The tenants of the vineyard
12:1 Then he began to speak to them in parables. "A man planted a vineyard. He set a hedge around it, dug a trough below the winepress and put up a tower. Then he leased it out to farmers and left the country.
12:2 When the season came, he sent a slave to the farmers, to receive from them a share of the vineyard's fruit.
12:3 They took hold of him, beat him and sent him away with nothing.
12:4 Again he sent another slave to them. This one they hit over the head and humiliated.
12:5 He sent another, and this one they killed. And so with many others: some they beat, and some they killed.
12:6 He still had one left, a dearly loved son. Last of all he sent him to them. 'My son they will respect,' he said.
12:7 But those farmers said among themselves, 'Here is the heir! Come on, let us kill him, and the inheritance will belong to us.'
12:8 They grabbed him and killed him, and cast him outside the vineyard.
12:9 What will the vineyard's master do? He will come and destroy the farmers, and give the vineyard to others.
12:10 Have you not even read this Scripture: 'A stone that the builders threw out has become the head of the corner.
12:11 From the Lord this came about, and in our eyes it is a marvel'?"
12:12 They were looking for a way to seize him, because they could tell the parable was aimed at them. But they feared the crowd. So they left him alone and went off.
## Taxes for Caesar
12:13 Then they sent some of the Pharisees and the Herodians to him, to catch him out with his own words.
12:14 They came and said to him, "Teacher, we know you are honest, and you do not care what anyone thinks, because you are not impressed by anyone's position. You teach God's way truthfully. Is it permitted to pay the head tax to Caesar, or not? Should we pay, or should we not pay?"
12:15 But he saw through their hypocrisy. "Why do you put me to the test?" he said to them. "Bring me a denarius to look at."
12:16 They brought one, and he asked them, "Whose image is this, and whose name is written here?" "Caesar's," they told him.
12:17 Jesus said to them, "Pay back to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God." And they were utterly amazed at him.
## A question about the resurrection
12:18 Then some Sadducees came to him. They say there is no resurrection. They put a question to him:
12:19 "Teacher, Moses wrote down for us: if someone's brother dies, leaving a wife but no child, then his brother must take the wife and raise up descendants for his brother.
12:20 Now, there were seven brothers. The first married, and he died without leaving descendants.
12:21 The second took her, and he too died with no descendants. So did the third.
12:22 None of the seven left descendants. Last of all the woman died too.
12:23 At the resurrection, when they rise, which of them will she be wife to? All seven were married to her."
12:24 Jesus said to them, "Is this not why you have gone astray: you do not know the Scriptures, or God's power?
12:25 When they rise from the dead, men do not take wives and women are not given as wives. They are like angels in the heavens.
12:26 As to the dead being raised: in the book of Moses, at the bush, have you not read how God told him, 'I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob'?
12:27 He is not God of dead people but of living ones. You have gone far astray."
## The first commandment
12:28 One of the scribes had come up and heard them debating. Seeing that Jesus had answered them well, he asked him, "Which commandment is the first of all?"
12:29 Jesus answered, "The first is: 'Listen, Israel: The Lord is our God. The Lord is one.
12:30 And love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your being, with all your mind and with all your strength.'
12:31 This is the second: 'You are to love your neighbor as yourself.' No other commandment ranks above these."
12:32 "Good, Teacher," the scribe said to him. "What you said is true: he is one, and apart from him there is no other.
12:33 Loving him with all the heart, all the understanding and all the strength, and loving your neighbor as yourself: that counts for more than all the burnt offerings and sacrifices."
12:34 Jesus, seeing that he had answered thoughtfully, told him, "You are not far from the kingdom of God." And no one dared to question him any longer.
## Whose son is the Messiah?
12:35 Jesus responded as he was teaching in the temple: "In what way do the scribes say the Messiah is David's son?
12:36 David himself said, in the Holy Spirit, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand, until I set your enemies down beneath your feet."'
12:37 David himself calls him 'Lord.' So in what sense is he his son?" And the big crowd enjoyed listening to him.
## Beware of the scribes
12:38 As part of his teaching he said, "Be on guard against the scribes who like to go about in long robes, and like being greeted in the market squares,
12:39 and the front seats in the synagogues and the top places at dinners.
12:40 They eat up widows' houses, and for show they make long prayers. These men will receive a heavier sentence."
## The widow's offering
12:41 He sat down across from the treasury and watched the crowd dropping money into the treasury. Many rich people were dropping in a lot.
12:42 Then a poor widow came and dropped in two small coins called lepta, which add up to a quadrans.
12:43 He called his disciples over and said to them, "Amen, I tell you: this poor widow has dropped in more than all the others who are dropping money into the treasury.
12:44 They all gave out of what they had left over. But she, out of her need, dropped in everything she had, her whole living."
