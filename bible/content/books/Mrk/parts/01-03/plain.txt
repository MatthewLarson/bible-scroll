## The beginning of the good news
1:1 The beginning of the good news of Jesus Christ, God's Son.
1:2 Just as it stands written in Isaiah the prophet: "Look, I am sending my messenger before your face. He will make your road ready.
1:3 A voice is shouting in the wilderness: / 'Get the Lord's road ready. / Make the paths for him straight.'"
1:4 John the Baptizer came on the scene in the wilderness. He proclaimed a baptism of repentance, for sins to be forgiven.
1:5 All the Judean region kept streaming out to him, and the people of Jerusalem, every one of them. As they confessed their sins out loud, he baptized them in the Jordan River.
1:6 John was dressed in camel hair, with a leather belt tied around his waist. He lived on locusts and wild honey.
1:7 He kept preaching: "The one stronger than I am is coming after me. I am not good enough to bend down and untie the strap of his sandals.
1:8 I have baptized you in water. He himself, though, will baptize you in the Holy Spirit."
## Jesus is baptized
1:9 It was in those days that Jesus came from Nazareth, in Galilee, and John baptized him in the Jordan.
1:10 Right away, as he was stepping up out of the water, he saw the heavens tearing open and the Spirit coming down like a dove into him.
1:11 And a voice came out of the heavens: "You are my dearly loved Son; in you I take delight."
## Tested in the wilderness
1:12 Right away the Spirit threw him out into the wilderness.
1:13 He stayed in the wilderness forty days while Satan put him to the test. He was out among the wild animals, and the angels kept taking care of him.
## Jesus begins in Galilee
1:14 After John had been handed over, Jesus came into Galilee, announcing God's good news.
1:15 His words were, "The time has been fulfilled, and the kingdom of God has drawn near. Repent, and believe in the good news."
## The first disciples
1:16 Passing along beside the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Simon's brother Andrew throwing a net out in the sea, since they fished for a living.
1:17 Jesus told them, "Come after me, and I will make you become fishermen who catch people."
1:18 Right away they dropped their nets and followed him.
1:19 He went on a little way and saw James son of Zebedee and John his brother. They too were in the boat, fixing the nets.
1:20 Right away he called them. Leaving their father Zebedee behind in the boat with the hired workers, they went off after him.
## Authority in the synagogue
1:21 They came into Capernaum. As soon as the Sabbath came, he was in the synagogue teaching.
1:22 People were astounded at his teaching. For he taught them as one who held authority, and not as the scribes did.
1:23 Just then, in their synagogue, there was a man in the grip of an unclean spirit. He shrieked,
1:24 "What is there between us and you, Jesus of Nazareth? Did you come to destroy us? I know who you are: the Holy One of God!"
1:25 Jesus gave it a strict order: "Be muzzled, and come out of him!"
1:26 The unclean spirit shook the man in a fit, let out a great shout, and came out of him.
1:27 Everyone was stunned. They kept asking one another, "What is this? A new teaching! With authority he orders even the unclean spirits, and they do what he says!"
1:28 Word about him went out right away in every direction, through the whole countryside of Galilee.
## Healing at Simon's house
1:29 Right after leaving the synagogue, they came to Simon and Andrew's house, and James and John came too.
1:30 Simon's mother-in-law was laid up in bed and feverish, and right away they spoke to Jesus about her.
1:31 He came up to her, grasped her hand and raised her up. The fever let go of her, and she began serving them.
1:32 As evening came on, when the sun went down, people kept bringing him all who were ill and those who had demons in them.
1:33 The entire town had crowded together at the door.
1:34 He healed many who were ill with all sorts of diseases, and he threw out many demons. He would not let the demons speak, because they knew him.
## Prayer before dawn
1:35 Long before daylight, while it was still night, he got up and went out. He went off to a deserted place and was praying there.
1:36 Simon and the others with him went hunting for him.
1:37 When they found him, they told him, "Everybody is searching for you."
1:38 He said to them, "Let's head elsewhere, into the neighboring towns, so I can preach there as well. This is what I came out for."
1:39 He went into their synagogues all over Galilee, preaching and throwing out the demons.
## A man with a skin disease made clean
1:40 A man with a skin disease came to him, pleading with him and falling on his knees. "If you want to," he said to him, "you are able to make me clean."
1:41 Jesus grew angry, and he stretched out his hand and touched him. "I want to," he said to him. "Be made clean."
1:42 At once the skin disease went away from him, and he was clean.
1:43 Jesus spoke to him harshly and drove him away at once.
1:44 He said to him, "Be careful not to say anything to anyone. Instead, go and let the priest see you. Then bring for your cleansing what Moses ordered, as evidence to them."
1:45 But the man went out and started preaching it far and wide and spreading the message around. As a result, Jesus was no longer able to go openly into a town. He stayed outside, out in deserted places, and people kept coming to him from all around.
## A paralyzed man forgiven
2:1 After some days he came into Capernaum again, and word got around that he was at home.
2:2 So many people crowded in that there was no space anymore, even by the door, and he spoke the message to them.
2:3 Then people arrived with a paralyzed man for him. Four of them were carrying him.
2:4 The crowd kept them from bringing him near, so they unroofed the roof over the spot where Jesus was. When they had dug an opening, they let down the mat with the paralyzed man lying on it.
2:5 Jesus saw their faith and told the paralyzed man, "Child, your sins are forgiven."
2:6 Sitting there were some of the scribes, and they were reasoning it out in their hearts:
2:7 "What does this man mean by talking like this? He is blaspheming! Who is able to forgive sins except the one God?"
2:8 At once Jesus sensed in his spirit that they were reasoning this way inside themselves. He said to them, "Why are you reasoning about these things in your hearts?
2:9 Which is the easier thing to say to the paralyzed man: 'Your sins are forgiven,' or 'Get up, pick up your mat, and walk around'?
2:10 But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority to forgive sins here on earth..." He said to the paralyzed man,
2:11 "I am telling you: get up, pick up your mat, and go to your house."
2:12 The man got up, and right away he picked up his mat and walked out in front of them all. Everyone was beside themselves. They praised God and said, "We have never seen such a thing!"
## Jesus calls Levi
2:13 Jesus went out again along the shore of the sea. All the crowd kept coming to him, and he taught them.
2:14 As he passed along, he saw Levi son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax office. He told him, "Follow me," and he stood up and followed him.
2:15 Then, as Jesus lay at the table in his house, many tax collectors and sinners were eating with Jesus and his disciples. There were many of them, and they followed him.
2:16 The scribes who belonged to the Pharisees saw that he was eating with the sinners and tax collectors, and they kept asking his disciples, "Why is he eating with the tax collectors and sinners?"
2:17 Jesus heard it and said to them, "People who are strong do not need a doctor. Sick people do. I did not come to call righteous people. I came to call sinners."
## A question about fasting
2:18 Once, John's disciples and the Pharisees were keeping a fast. People came to him and said, "Why is it that John's disciples and the Pharisees' disciples fast, while your disciples do not fast?"
2:19 Jesus said to them, "The groom's friends cannot fast while the groom is with them, can they? As long as they have the groom with them, they cannot fast.
2:20 But days are coming when the groom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast, on that day.
2:21 Nobody sews a patch of new cloth that has not been shrunk onto an old coat. Otherwise the patch pulls away from it, the new from the old, and the tear gets worse.
2:22 And nobody puts new wine in old wineskins. Otherwise the wine will burst the skins, and the wine is lost, and the skins too. No: new wine into fresh skins!"
## Lord of the Sabbath
2:23 It happened that on a Sabbath he was passing through fields of grain. As his disciples went making their way, they started pulling off heads of grain.
2:24 The Pharisees said to him, "See here! Why are they doing something on the Sabbath that is not allowed?"
2:25 He said to them, "Have you never once read about David, when he was in need and he and his men were hungry? What did he do?
2:26 He went into God's house while Abiathar was high priest, and he ate the Presence Bread, which no one is allowed to eat except the priests. He even gave some to the men with him."
2:27 He went on to say to them, "The Sabbath came into being for the sake of human beings, and not human beings for the sake of the Sabbath.
2:28 So the Son of Man is Lord even over the Sabbath."
## A withered hand on the Sabbath
3:1 Once again he went into a synagogue, and there was a man there whose hand had withered.
3:2 People were keeping a close eye on Jesus. Would he heal the man on the Sabbath? Then they could bring a charge against him.
3:3 He told the man whose hand was withered, "Get up and stand in the middle."
3:4 Then he asked them, "Is it allowed on the Sabbath to do something good, or something bad? To save a life, or to kill?" But they said nothing.
3:5 His eyes swept over them in anger. He was grieved that their hearts had turned so hard. He said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." He stretched it out, and his hand was made well again.
3:6 The Pharisees left and right away met with the Herodians to plan against him, looking for a way to destroy him.
## Crowds by the sea
3:7 Jesus withdrew with his disciples to the sea. A great mass of people from Galilee followed him. From Judea too,
3:8 and from Jerusalem, from Idumea, from across the Jordan, and from around Tyre and Sidon, a great mass of people came to him, because they heard about all he was doing.
3:9 Because of the crowd, he asked his disciples to keep a little boat standing by for him, so the people would not crush him.
3:10 He had healed so many that everyone with an affliction was falling all over him to touch him.
3:11 The unclean spirits, whenever they caught sight of him, would throw themselves down in front of him and scream, "You are the Son of God!"
3:12 He kept giving them strict orders not to make him public.
## The Twelve
3:13 He went up the mountain and summoned the ones he himself wanted. They left and came to him.
3:14 He formed a group of twelve, to be with him and to be sent out by him to preach,
3:15 and to have authority to throw out the demons.
3:16 So he formed the Twelve: Simon, on whom he put the name Peter;
3:17 James son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James (he put on them the name Boanerges, which means Sons of Thunder);
3:18 Andrew, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James son of Alphaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Cananaean,
3:19 and Judas Iscariot, the one who also handed him over. Then he came home.
## Jesus and Beelzebul
3:20 The crowd came together again, and they were not able even to eat a meal.
3:21 When the people close to him heard, they set out to take hold of him, for they were saying, "He is beside himself."
3:22 Scribes had come down from Jerusalem, and they were saying, "He has Beelzebul in him," and, "He throws out the demons with the help of the ruler of the demons."
3:23 Jesus summoned them and began talking to them in parables. "How is Satan able to throw Satan out?
3:24 If a kingdom is split against itself, that kingdom is not able to last.
3:25 And if a family is split against itself, that family will not be able to last.
3:26 So if Satan has revolted against himself and is split, he is not able to last. He is finished.
3:27 But no one can go into a strong man's house and rob him of his goods without first tying the strong man up. Only then will he rob his house.
3:28 Amen, I tell you: everything will be forgiven to human beings, their sins and all the blasphemies they speak, however many.
3:29 But anyone who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit has no forgiveness, not ever. Instead, that person is held guilty of a sin that is eternal."
3:30 Jesus spoke this way because their claim was, "He has an unclean spirit."
## Jesus' true family
3:31 His mother and his brothers came. Standing outside, they sent a message in to him, asking for him.
3:32 A crowd sat around him. They told him, "Look! Your mother and your brothers are out there, searching for you."
3:33 He answered them, "Who is my mother, or who are my brothers?"
3:34 His eyes swept over the people sitting in a circle around him, and he said, "Look! My mother and my brothers!
3:35 For anyone who does what God wants, that person is my brother and sister and mother."
