## Gifts for a holy place
25:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
25:2 "Speak to the Israelites, and let them set aside a gift for me. Accept my gift from each person whose heart urges him on.
25:3 This is the gift you are to accept from them: gold, silver and bronze;
25:4 violet, purple and scarlet yarn, fine linen, and goat hair;
25:5 rams' skins dyed red, fine leather, and acacia wood;
25:6 oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil and for the sweet incense;
25:7 onyx stones, and stones to be set in the ephod and the breastpiece.
25:8 They are to make me a holy place, and I will live in their midst.
25:9 Make it just like everything I am about to show you, the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all its furnishings. Make it that way.
## The ark
25:10 "They are to make an ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide and one and a half cubits high.
25:11 Cover it with pure gold, inside and outside, and put a gold molding all the way around it.
25:12 Cast four gold rings for it and fix them on its four feet, two rings on one side and two rings on the other.
25:13 Make poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold.
25:14 Slide the poles into the rings on the ark's sides, so the ark can be carried by them.
25:15 The poles must stay in the rings of the ark. They are never to be taken out.
25:16 Put into the ark the testimony that I will give you.
## The atonement lid and the cherubim
25:17 "Make an atonement lid of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide.
25:18 Make two cherubim of gold. Hammer them out at the two ends of the lid,
25:19 one cherub at this end and one cherub at that end. Make the cherubim rise out of the lid itself, at its two ends.
25:20 The cherubim will hold their wings spread upward, sheltering the lid with their wings. They will face each other, their faces turned toward the lid.
25:21 Set the lid on top of the ark, and put inside the ark the testimony that I will give you.
25:22 There I will meet you. From above the lid, from between the two cherubim on the ark of the testimony, I will tell you everything I command you for the Israelites.
## The table
25:23 "Make a table of acacia wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide and one and a half cubits high.
25:24 Cover it with pure gold and put a gold molding all the way around it.
25:25 Around it make a rim a hand's width high, and put a gold molding around the rim.
25:26 Make four gold rings for it and fix the rings at the four corners, by its four legs.
25:27 The rings are to sit close to the rim, as holders for the poles that carry the table.
25:28 Make the poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold. The table will be carried with them.
25:29 Make its plates and its pans, its jugs and its bowls for pouring drink offerings. Make them of pure gold.
25:30 On the table put the Presence Bread, to be in front of me always.
## The lampstand
25:31 "Make a lampstand of pure gold. Make the lampstand of hammered work. Its base and its shaft, its cups, its buds and its flowers are to grow out of it.
25:32 Six branches will come out from its sides, three branches of the lampstand from one side and three from the other.
25:33 On one branch put three cups shaped like almond flowers, each with a bud and a flower, and three almond cups on the next branch, each with a bud and a flower. So it is for all six branches that come out of the lampstand.
25:34 On the lampstand itself put four almond cups, with their buds and flowers.
25:35 Put a bud under the first pair of branches that come out of it, a bud under the second pair, and a bud under the third pair. So it is for the six branches that come out of the lampstand.
25:36 Their buds and branches will grow out of it. The whole of it is to be hammered from one piece of pure gold.
25:37 Make its seven lamps. Set the lamps up so that they give light on the side it faces.
25:38 Its tongs and its trays are to be of pure gold.
25:39 It is to be made, with all these tools, from a talent of pure gold.
25:40 See to it that you make them by their pattern, the one you are being shown on the mountain.
## The tabernacle's curtains
26:1 "Make the tabernacle out of ten curtains of twisted fine linen and violet, purple and scarlet yarn, with cherubim worked into them, the work of a skilled designer.
26:2 Each curtain is to be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide. All the curtains are to have one size.
26:3 Five of the curtains are to be joined to each other, and the other five curtains joined to each other.
26:4 Along the edge of the last curtain in the first set, sew loops of violet yarn. Do it the same way on the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set.
26:5 Put fifty loops on the one curtain and fifty loops on the end of the curtain in the second set, so that the loops line up, each with its partner.
26:6 Make fifty gold hooks and join the curtains to each other with the hooks. So the tabernacle will be one.
## The tent over it
26:7 "Make curtains of goat hair for a tent over the tabernacle. Make eleven of these curtains.
26:8 Each curtain is to be thirty cubits long and four cubits wide. The eleven curtains are to have one size.
26:9 Sew five of the curtains together by themselves, and six by themselves. The sixth curtain is to be doubled over at the front of the tent.
26:10 Put fifty loops on the edge of the outer curtain in the first set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain of the second set.
26:11 Make fifty bronze hooks, put the hooks through the loops, and join the tent together, so that it will be one.
26:12 The part of the tent curtains that is left over, the extra half curtain, will hang down over the back of the tabernacle.
26:13 The cubit left over on each side, in the length of the tent curtains, will hang down over both sides of the tabernacle to cover it.
26:14 Make a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and on top of that a covering of fine leather.
## The frames
26:15 "For the tabernacle make frames of acacia wood that stand upright.
26:16 Each frame is to be ten cubits tall and a cubit and a half wide.
26:17 Each frame will have two pegs, joined one to the other. Do this for all the frames of the tabernacle.
26:18 Make the frames for the tabernacle like this: twenty frames for the south side, facing south.
26:19 Under the twenty frames make forty silver bases, two bases under each frame for its two pegs.
26:20 For the other side of the tabernacle, the north side, make twenty frames
26:21 and their forty silver bases, two bases under each frame.
26:22 For the back of the tabernacle, toward the west, make six frames.
26:23 Make two frames for the corners of the tabernacle at the back.
26:24 They are to be doubled from the bottom, and fit together at the top into the first ring. Both of them are to be made this way. They will form the two corners.
26:25 So there will be eight frames with their silver bases, sixteen bases, two under each frame.
26:26 "Make bars of acacia wood, five of them for the frames of the first side of the tabernacle,
26:27 five bars for the frames of the second side, and five bars for the frames of the back of the tabernacle, toward the west.
26:28 The middle bar is to run through the middle of the frames, from one end to the other.
26:29 Cover the frames with gold, and make their rings of gold, to hold the bars. Cover the bars with gold too.
26:30 Then set up the tabernacle the way it was planned, as you have been shown on the mountain.
## The veil and the screen
26:31 "Make a veil of violet, purple and scarlet yarn and twisted fine linen. Have it made with cherubim worked into it, the work of a skilled designer.
26:32 Hang it on four posts of acacia wood covered with gold, with gold hooks, set in four silver bases.
26:33 Hang the veil from the hooks, and bring the ark of the testimony in behind the veil. The veil will be a dividing line for you between the holy place and the most holy place.
26:34 Put the atonement lid on the ark of the testimony in the most holy place.
26:35 Set the table outside the veil, and the lampstand across from the table on the south side of the tabernacle. Put the table on the north side.
26:36 For the entrance of the tent make a screen of violet, purple and scarlet yarn and twisted fine linen, the work of a weaver in colors.
26:37 For the screen make five posts of acacia and cover them with gold, with gold hooks, and cast five bronze bases for them.
## The altar
27:1 "Build the altar of acacia wood. It is to be square, five cubits by five cubits, and three cubits high.
27:2 Make horns for it at its four corners, growing out of the altar itself, and cover it with bronze.
27:3 Make its pots for clearing away the fatty ashes, and its shovels, bowls, forks and fire pans. Make all its tools of bronze.
27:4 Make a grating for it, a mesh of bronze, and on the mesh make four bronze rings at its four corners.
27:5 Put the grating below the altar's ledge, so that the mesh reaches halfway up the altar.
27:6 Make poles for the altar out of acacia wood, and cover them with bronze.
27:7 The poles will be slid through the rings, so they are on both sides of the altar whenever it is carried.
27:8 Make it hollow, out of boards. They are to make it just as he showed you on the mountain.
## The courtyard
27:9 "Make the courtyard of the tabernacle. On the south side, facing south, the courtyard will have hangings of twisted fine linen, a hundred cubits long for that one side,
27:10 with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. The hooks on the posts and their bands will be silver.
27:11 The north side is to be the same: hangings a hundred cubits long, twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, with silver hooks and bands on the posts.
27:12 Across the west end the courtyard will be fifty cubits wide, with fifty cubits of hangings, ten posts and ten bases.
27:13 The east end, toward the sunrise, is also fifty cubits wide.
27:14 There will be fifteen cubits of hangings on one side, with three posts and three bases,
27:15 and fifteen cubits of hangings on the other side, with three posts and three bases.
27:16 For the courtyard gate there will be a screen twenty cubits long, of violet, purple and scarlet yarn and twisted fine linen, the work of a weaver in colors. It will have four posts and four bases.
27:17 Every post around the courtyard is to have silver bands and silver hooks, and bronze bases.
27:18 The courtyard is to be a hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide, all the way along, with hangings of twisted fine linen five cubits high, and bronze bases.
27:19 All the tools for every use in the tabernacle, and all its tent pegs and all the pegs of the courtyard, are to be bronze.
## Oil for the lamp
27:20 "As for you, command the Israelites to bring you pure oil pressed from olives for the light, so that a lamp can be lit to burn always.
27:21 Aaron and his sons are to keep it in order in the tent of meeting, outside the veil that hangs in front of the testimony, from evening until morning before the LORD. This is a rule for the Israelites for all time, through their generations.
## Clothes for the priests
28:1 "Now bring your brother Aaron near you, with his sons, out from among the Israelites, to be my priests: Aaron, and Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron's sons.
28:2 Make holy clothes for your brother Aaron, for glory and for beauty.
28:3 Speak to all who are skilled, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, and they will make Aaron's clothes, to set him apart as my priest.
28:4 These are the clothes they will make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a tunic of checkered weave, a turban and a sash. They will make holy clothes for your brother Aaron and his sons, for them to be my priests.
28:5 They are to use the gold, the violet, purple and scarlet yarn, and the fine linen.
## The ephod
28:6 "They will make the ephod of gold, of violet, purple and scarlet yarn, and of twisted fine linen, the work of a skilled designer.
28:7 It will have two shoulder straps fastened to its two edges, so it can be joined together.
28:8 Its woven waistband, which goes on it, is to be made the same way and of one piece with it, of gold, of violet, purple and scarlet yarn, and of twisted fine linen.
28:9 "Take two onyx stones and engrave on them the names of Israel's sons,
28:10 six of their names on the one stone and the six that remain on the other, in the order of their birth.
28:11 As a stonecutter engraves a seal, engrave the two stones with the names of Israel's sons. Mount them in gold settings.
28:12 Put the two stones on the shoulder straps of the ephod, as stones that bring Israel's sons to mind. Aaron will carry their names before the LORD on his two shoulders, to bring them to mind.
28:13 Make settings of gold filigree,
28:14 and two chains of pure gold, braided like rope. Fasten the braided chains to the settings.
## The breastpiece
28:15 "Make a breastpiece for decisions, the work of a skilled designer. Make it the same way as the ephod: make it of gold, of violet, purple and scarlet yarn, and of twisted fine linen.
28:16 It is to be square and doubled over, a span long and a span wide.
28:17 Fill it with mounted stones, four rows of stones. The first row: carnelian, topaz and emerald.
28:18 The second row: turquoise, lapis lazuli and quartz.
28:19 The third row: jacinth, agate and amethyst.
28:20 The fourth row: chrysolite, onyx and jasper. Mount them in gold settings.
28:21 There are to be twelve stones for the names of Israel's sons, matching their names. Each is to be engraved like a seal with its own name, for the twelve tribes.
28:22 "On the breastpiece make braided chains of pure gold, worked like rope.
28:23 On the breastpiece make two gold rings, and put the two rings on two ends of the breastpiece.
28:24 Put the two gold ropes through the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece.
28:25 Fasten the other two ends of the two ropes to the two settings, and so attach them to the shoulder straps of the ephod, on its front.
28:26 Make two gold rings and put them on the other two ends of the breastpiece, on its inner edge, the side facing the ephod.
28:27 Make two more gold rings and put them low down on the front of the two shoulder straps, where they join the ephod, above its waistband.
28:28 Tie the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a violet cord, so that it sits on the ephod's waistband. Then the breastpiece will not come away from the ephod.
28:29 "Aaron will carry the names of Israel's sons on the breastpiece for decisions, over his heart, when he goes into the holy place, to keep them always in mind before the LORD.
28:30 Put the Urim and the Thummim into the breastpiece for decisions. They will be over Aaron's heart whenever he goes in before the LORD. So Aaron will always carry the decisions for the Israelites over his heart before the LORD.
## The robe, the gold plate and the rest
28:31 "Make the robe that goes under the ephod all of violet.
28:32 It is to have an opening for the head in the middle, with a woven edge around the opening, like the neck of a coat of mail, so that it will not tear.
28:33 On its hem, all the way around, make pomegranates of violet, purple and scarlet yarn, with gold bells between them all around:
28:34 a gold bell and a pomegranate, a gold bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe.
28:35 Aaron must wear it when he serves. Its sound will be heard when he goes into the holy place before the LORD and when he comes out, and so he will not die.
28:36 "Make a plate of pure gold. Engrave on it, as a seal is engraved, 'Holy to the LORD.'
28:37 Tie it on a violet cord. It is to be on the turban, on the front of the turban.
28:38 It will be on Aaron's forehead, and Aaron will carry the guilt of the holy things that the Israelites set apart, all their holy gifts. It will always be on his forehead, so that they will be accepted before the LORD.
28:39 "Weave the tunic in a checkered pattern of fine linen. Make a turban of fine linen, and make a sash, the work of a weaver in colors.
28:40 For Aaron's sons make tunics. Make sashes for them, and make headbands for them, for glory and for beauty.
28:41 Put these clothes on your brother Aaron and on his sons with him. Anoint them, fill their hands, and set them apart, so that they can be my priests.
28:42 Make linen undershorts for them to cover their naked bodies. They are to reach from the waist to the thighs.
28:43 Aaron and his sons are to wear them whenever they go into the tent of meeting, or come near the altar to serve in the holy place, so that they will not bring guilt on themselves and die. This is a rule for all time for him and for his descendants after him.
## Making the priests holy
29:1 "Here is what you will do to them to set them apart as my priests. Take a young bull from the herd and two rams with nothing wrong with them,
29:2 and bread with no yeast: loaves with no yeast mixed with oil, and thin wafers with no yeast spread with oil. Make them of fine wheat flour.
29:3 Put them in one basket and bring them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.
29:4 Bring Aaron and his sons up to the doorway of the tent of meeting, and wash them there with water.
29:5 Take the clothes. Dress Aaron in the tunic, the robe that goes under the ephod, the ephod and the breastpiece, and tie the ephod on him with its woven waistband.
29:6 Place the turban on his head and set the holy crown on the turban.
29:7 Take the anointing oil, pour it on his head, and anoint him.
29:8 Then bring his sons up and put tunics on them.
29:9 Tie sashes around them, Aaron and his sons, and wrap headbands on them. The priesthood will be theirs by a rule for all time. This is how you will fill the hands of Aaron and of his sons.
## The bull and the first ram
29:10 "Bring the bull up in front of the tent of meeting. Aaron and his sons are to lay their hands on the bull's head.
29:11 Slaughter the bull before the LORD, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
29:12 Take some of the bull's blood and put it with your finger on the altar's horns. Pour out all the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.
29:13 Take all the fat that covers the insides, the lobe on the liver, and the two kidneys with the fat on them, and turn them to smoke on the altar.
29:14 But burn the bull's meat, its hide and its dung in a fire outside the camp. It is a sin offering.
29:15 "Take one of the rams. Aaron and his sons are to lay their hands on the ram's head.
29:16 Slaughter the ram, take its blood, and throw it against the altar on every side.
29:17 Cut the ram into its pieces. Wash its insides and its legs and lay them with its pieces and its head.
29:18 Turn the whole ram to smoke on the altar. It is a burnt offering for the LORD, a soothing smell, a gift by fire for the LORD.
## The ram for ordination
29:19 "Take the second ram. Aaron and his sons are to lay their hands on the ram's head.
29:20 Slaughter the ram and take some of its blood. Put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear and the lobes of his sons' right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands and the big toes of their right feet. Throw the rest of the blood against the altar on every side.
29:21 Take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil, and spatter it on Aaron and his clothes, and on his sons and their clothes. So he and his clothes will be holy, and his sons and their clothes with him.
29:22 "Take from the ram the fat, the fat tail, the fat that covers the insides, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys and the fat on them, and the right thigh, since it is a ram of ordination.
29:23 From the basket of bread with no yeast that is before the LORD, take also one round loaf, one loaf of bread made with oil, and one wafer.
29:24 Put all of it in the hands of Aaron and in the hands of his sons, and wave them before the LORD as a wave offering.
29:25 Then take them back from their hands and turn them to smoke on the altar, on top of the burnt offering, as a soothing smell before the LORD. It is a gift by fire for the LORD.
29:26 Take the breast of Aaron's ram of ordination and wave it before the LORD as a wave offering. It will be your share.
29:27 Set apart the breast that was waved and the thigh that was lifted up, the parts of the ram of ordination that were waved and lifted, from what belongs to Aaron and his sons.
29:28 They will belong to Aaron and his sons as their share from the Israelites for all time. For it is a gift lifted up, and it will be the Israelites' lifted gift from their peace offerings, their gift lifted up to the LORD.
29:29 "Aaron's holy clothes will go to his sons after him. They will be anointed in them and have their hands filled in them.
29:30 The son who becomes priest in his place, and comes into the tent of meeting to serve in the holy place, is to wear them for seven days.
29:31 "Take the ram of ordination and boil its meat in a holy place.
29:32 At the entrance of the tent of meeting, Aaron and his sons will eat the ram's meat and the bread in the basket.
29:33 They are to eat the things by which atonement was made for them when their hands were filled and they were set apart. No outsider may eat them, because they are holy.
29:34 If any of the ordination meat or any of the bread is left until morning, burn what is left in the fire. It must not be eaten, because it is holy.
29:35 "Do this for Aaron and his sons, exactly as I have commanded you. Take seven days to fill their hands.
29:36 Every day offer a bull as a sin offering for atonement. Cleanse the altar from sin when you make atonement for it, and anoint it to make it holy.
29:37 For seven days make atonement for the altar and make it holy. The altar will become most holy. Anything that touches the altar will become holy.
## The daily offering
29:38 "This is what you are to offer on the altar: two yearling lambs, every day, without fail.
29:39 Offer one lamb in the morning and offer the other lamb at dusk.
29:40 With the first lamb offer a tenth of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin of pressed olive oil, and a quarter of a hin of wine as a drink offering.
29:41 Offer the second lamb at dusk. With it offer the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, as a soothing smell, a gift by fire for the LORD.
29:42 It will be a burnt offering made without fail through your generations, at the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD. There I will meet with you, to speak to you there.
29:43 There I will meet with the Israelites, and the place will be made holy by my glory.
29:44 I will make the tent of meeting and the altar holy. I will set Aaron and his sons apart to be my priests.
29:45 I will live among the Israelites, and I will be their God.
29:46 Then they will know that I am the LORD their God, the one who led them out of Egypt's land to live in their midst. I am the LORD their God.
## The incense altar
30:1 "Make an altar for burning incense. Make it of acacia wood.
30:2 It is to be a cubit long and a cubit wide, square, and two cubits high. Its horns will grow out of it.
30:3 Cover its top, its sides all around, and its horns with pure gold, and put a gold molding all the way around it.
30:4 Make two gold rings for it below the molding. Put them on its two sides, on opposite sides, to hold the poles it is carried by.
30:5 Make the poles of acacia wood and cover them with gold.
30:6 Place the altar in front of the veil that hangs before the ark of the testimony, in front of the atonement lid that is over the testimony, where I will meet you.
30:7 Aaron will burn sweet incense on it. He will burn it each morning when he trims the lamps.
30:8 And when Aaron lights the lamps at dusk, he will burn it again. It is to be incense burned before the LORD without fail, through your generations.
30:9 On this altar you must not offer any strange incense, or a burnt offering or a grain offering. You must not pour a drink offering on it.
30:10 Once a year Aaron will make atonement on its horns. He will make atonement for it once a year with blood from the sin offering of atonement, through your generations. It is most holy to the LORD."
## The atonement money
30:11 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
30:12 "When you take a count of the Israelites, of those to be enrolled, each one will give the LORD a ransom for his life when you enroll them, so that no plague will strike them as you enroll them.
30:13 Everyone who crosses over to join those enrolled will give this: half a shekel, by the holy place's shekel, which is twenty gerahs. The half shekel is a gift for the LORD.
30:14 Everyone who crosses over to join those enrolled, from twenty years old and up, will give the LORD's gift.
30:15 The rich are not to pay more than half a shekel, and the poor are not to pay less, when you give the LORD's gift to make atonement for your lives.
30:16 Take the atonement money from the Israelites and put it toward the service of the tent of meeting. It will keep the Israelites in mind before the LORD, to make atonement for your lives."
## The bronze basin
30:17 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
30:18 "For washing, make a basin of bronze with a bronze stand. Place it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and fill it with water.
30:19 From it Aaron and his sons will wash their hands and their feet.
30:20 Before they enter the tent of meeting they must wash with water, or they will die. The same holds when they come near the altar to serve by burning a gift by fire for the LORD.
30:21 They will wash their hands and their feet, so that they will not die. This will be a rule for all time for them, for Aaron and his descendants through their generations."
## The anointing oil
30:22 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
30:23 "Take the finest spices: five hundred shekels of liquid myrrh; half that much sweet cinnamon, two hundred and fifty; two hundred and fifty of sweet cane;
30:24 five hundred of cassia, all by the holy place's shekel; and a hin of olive oil.
30:25 From these make a holy anointing oil, a blend mixed by the perfumer's art. It will be a holy anointing oil.
30:26 With it anoint the tent of meeting, the ark of the testimony,
30:27 the table and all its tools, the lampstand and its tools, the altar of incense,
30:28 the altar of burnt offering and all its tools, and the basin with its stand.
30:29 Make them holy, and they will be most holy. Anything that touches them will become holy.
30:30 Anoint Aaron and his sons and set them apart to be my priests.
30:31 Then tell the Israelites, 'This will be my holy anointing oil through your generations.
30:32 It must not be poured on anyone's body, and you must not make anything like it with the same mixture. It is holy, and you are to treat it as holy.
30:33 Whoever mixes any like it, or puts any of it on an outsider, will be cut off from his people.'"
## The incense
30:34 The LORD said to Moses, "Take spices: stacte, onycha and galbanum, spices mixed with pure frankincense, an equal part of each.
30:35 Make it into incense, a blend mixed by the perfumer's art, salted, pure and holy.
30:36 Grind some of it to fine powder, and put some of it in front of the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet you. It will be most holy to you.
30:37 The incense you make, you must not make with the same mixture for yourselves. You are to treat it as holy to the LORD.
30:38 Whoever makes any like it to enjoy its smell will be cut off from his people."
## The craftsmen
31:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
31:2 "Look, I have called by name Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.
31:3 I have filled him with the Spirit of God, with skill, with understanding, with know-how, and with every kind of craft,
31:4 to think up designs and carry them out in gold, silver and bronze,
31:5 to cut stones for setting, to carve wood, and to work at every craft.
31:6 And I myself have given him Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, to work with him. I have put skill in the heart of everyone who is skilled at heart, and they will make everything I have commanded you:
31:7 the tent of meeting, the ark for the testimony with the atonement lid on it, and all the furnishings of the tent;
31:8 the table and its tools, the pure gold lampstand with all its tools, the altar of incense,
31:9 the altar of burnt offering with all its tools, and the basin with its stand;
31:10 the woven clothes, the holy clothes for Aaron the priest, and the clothes his sons wear to serve as priests;
31:11 and the anointing oil and the sweet incense for the holy place. They will make them just the way I have commanded you."
## The Sabbath sign
31:12 The LORD said to Moses,
31:13 "Speak to the Israelites and say, 'Make sure, though, that you keep my Sabbaths. For this is a sign between me and you through your generations, so that you will know that I, the LORD, set you apart as holy.
31:14 Keep the Sabbath, because it is holy for you. Anyone who treats it as common must be put to death. Whoever does any work on it, that person will be cut off from among his people.
31:15 For six days work can be done, but the seventh day is a Sabbath, a day of total rest, holy to the LORD. Anyone who does work on the Sabbath day must be put to death.
31:16 The Israelites are to keep the Sabbath, celebrating the Sabbath through their generations as a covenant for all time.
31:17 Between me and the Israelites it is a sign forever. For in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day he stopped and caught his breath.'"
## The tablets
31:18 When he finished speaking with Moses on Mount Sinai, he handed him the two tablets of the testimony, stone tablets written by God's own finger.
## The golden calf
32:1 The people saw that Moses was slow to come down from the mountain. They gathered around Aaron and told him, "Come on, make us gods that will go ahead of us. This Moses, the man who brought us up from the land of Egypt, we have no idea what has happened to him."
32:2 Aaron told them, "Pull off the gold rings in the ears of your wives, your sons and your daughters, and bring them to me."
32:3 So all the people pulled off the gold rings in their ears and brought them to Aaron.
32:4 He took the gold from them, shaped it with a tool, and made it into a calf of cast metal. And they said, "These are your gods, Israel, that brought you up from the land of Egypt!"
32:5 When Aaron saw this, he built an altar in front of it. Aaron called out, "Tomorrow is a festival for the LORD!"
32:6 Early the next day they got up, offered burnt offerings and brought peace offerings. The people sat down to eat and drink, and they got up to play.
## Moses pleads for the people
32:7 The LORD said to Moses, "Go down at once! Your people, whom you brought up from the land of Egypt, have ruined themselves.
32:8 They have been quick to leave the path I commanded them. They have made themselves a calf of cast metal. They have bowed down to it, sacrificed to it, and said, 'These are your gods, Israel, that brought you up from the land of Egypt.'"
32:9 The LORD said to Moses, "I have seen this people. Look, they are a stiff-necked people.
32:10 So now leave me alone, so that my anger can burn against them and I can finish them off. Then I will make you into a great nation."
32:11 But Moses begged the LORD his God to soften. He said, "LORD, why does your anger burn against your people? You brought them out from the land of Egypt with great strength and a strong hand.
32:12 Why let the Egyptians say, 'He brought them out to do them harm, to kill them in the mountains and wipe them off the face of the ground'? Turn from your blazing anger. Change your mind about this harm to your people.
32:13 Remember Abraham, Isaac and Israel, your servants. You swore to them by your own self and said, 'I will make your descendants as many as the stars in the sky. All this land I have spoken of I will give to your descendants, and it will be theirs forever.'"
32:14 So the LORD changed his mind about the harm he had said he would do to his people.
## The broken tablets
32:15 Moses turned and went down from the mountain, the two tablets of the testimony in his hand, tablets written on both sides. They had writing on the one side and on the other.
32:16 The tablets were God's work, and the writing was God's writing, cut into the tablets.
32:17 Joshua heard the people's noise as they shouted. He said to Moses, "That is the sound of battle in the camp."
32:18 But Moses said, "It is not the sound of singing for victory, / and not the sound of singing for defeat. / It is the sound of singing that I hear."
32:19 As soon as he came near the camp, he saw the calf and the dancing. Moses' anger burned. He threw the tablets down from his hands and smashed them at the foot of the mountain.
32:20 He took the calf they had made and burned it in the fire. He ground it until it was fine dust, scattered it on the water, and made the Israelites drink it.
32:21 Moses said to Aaron, "What did this people do to you, that you brought such a great sin on them?"
32:22 Aaron said, "Do not let my lord's anger burn. You yourself know the people, how bent on evil they are.
32:23 They said to me, 'Make us gods that will go ahead of us. This Moses, the man who brought us up from the land of Egypt, we have no idea what has happened to him.'
32:24 So I said to them, 'Who has gold? Pull it off.' They gave it to me. I threw it in the fire, and out came this calf!"
## The Levites and the plague
32:25 Moses saw that the people were running wild. Aaron had let them run wild, so that their enemies would laugh at them.
32:26 Moses stood at the gate of the camp and said, "Whoever is on the LORD's side, to me!" All the Levites gathered around him.
32:27 He said to them, "Here is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Each of you strap his sword on his thigh. Go through the camp and back again, from gate to gate, and kill, each of you, his brother, his friend and his neighbor.'"
32:28 The Levites did as Moses said. About three thousand of the people fell that day.
32:29 Moses said, "Fill your hands today for the LORD, each of you at the cost of his son and his brother, so that he may give you a blessing today."
32:30 The next day Moses told the people, "What you have done is a great sin. But now I will go up to the LORD. It may be that I can make atonement for your sin."
32:31 So Moses went back to the LORD and said, "Oh, what a great sin this people has done! They have made gods of gold for themselves.
32:32 And now, if you will forgive their sin... But if not, wipe me out, please, from your book that you have written."
32:33 The LORD said to Moses, "The one who has sinned against me is the one I will wipe out of my book.
32:34 Now go. Lead the people to the place I told you about. Look, my angel will go ahead of you. But on the day I come to call them to account, I will hold them to account for their sin."
32:35 And the LORD struck the people, because they had made the calf. Aaron was the one who made it.
## "I will not go up among you"
33:1 The LORD said to Moses, "Go on up from here, you and the people you brought up from the land of Egypt, to the land I promised with an oath to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, when I said, 'I will give it to your descendants.'
33:2 I will send an angel ahead of you, and I will drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites.
33:3 Go up to a land flowing with milk and honey. But I will not go up in your midst, since you are a stiff-necked people, or I might finish you off on the road."
33:4 When the people heard this hard word, they mourned. No one put on any of his jewelry.
33:5 For the LORD had told Moses, "Say to the Israelites, 'You are a stiff-necked people. If I went up in your midst for a single moment, I would finish you off. Now take your jewelry off, and I will decide what to do with you.'"
33:6 So the Israelites stripped off their jewelry, from Mount Horeb on.
## The tent outside the camp
33:7 Moses used to take the tent and pitch it outside the camp, at a distance from the camp. He called it the tent of meeting. Anyone who wanted to seek the LORD went out to the tent of meeting outside the camp.
33:8 Each time Moses went out to the tent, the whole people would get up and stand at their own tent doors, their eyes following Moses until he went inside.
33:9 As Moses went into the tent, the pillar of cloud came down and stood at the tent's door, and the LORD spoke with Moses.
33:10 All the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the tent's door. Then all the people rose and bowed down, each at the door of his own tent.
33:11 The LORD spoke to Moses face to face, as anyone talks with a close companion. Then Moses went back to the camp. But his servant Joshua son of Nun, a young man, never left the inside of the tent.
## "Show me your glory"
33:12 Moses said to the LORD, "Look, you keep telling me, 'Lead this people up,' but you have not let me know who you will send with me. Yet you have said, 'I know you by name, and you have found favor in my eyes.'
33:13 Now, please, if I have found favor in your eyes, let me know your ways, so that I may know you and go on finding favor in your eyes. And keep in mind that this nation is your people."
33:14 He said, "My face will go, and I will give you rest."
33:15 Moses said to him, "If your face is not going, do not bring us up from here.
33:16 How else will anyone know that I have found favor in your eyes, I and your people? Is it not by your going with us? That is what will set us apart, I and your people, from every people on the face of the earth."
33:17 The LORD said to Moses, "This thing you have spoken of, I will do it too. For you have found favor in my eyes, and I know you by name."
33:18 Moses said, "Please let me see your glory."
33:19 He said, "I will make all my goodness pass in front of you, and I will call out the name of the LORD before you. I will show grace to whoever I show grace, and pity whoever I pity."
33:20 But he said, "You cannot see my face, for no human being can see me and stay alive."
33:21 The LORD said, "Look, there is a place beside me. You will stand on the rock.
33:22 As my glory goes by, I will set you in a crack in the rock and shield you with my hand until I have gone past.
33:23 Then I will take my hand away. You will see my back, but my face will not be seen."
## New tablets
34:1 The LORD said to Moses, "Cut out two tablets of stone for yourself, like the first ones, and I will write on them the same words that were on the first tablets, the ones you smashed.
34:2 Be ready by morning. In the morning come up Mount Sinai and present yourself to me there on top of the mountain.
34:3 Let no one come up with you, and let no one be seen anywhere on the mountain. Do not let even sheep or cattle graze facing that mountain."
34:4 Moses cut two stone tablets like the first ones. Early in the morning he climbed Mount Sinai, as the LORD had commanded him, taking the two stone tablets in his hand.
## The name of the LORD
34:5 The LORD came down in the cloud and stood with him there, and he called out the name of the LORD.
34:6 The LORD passed in front of him and called out, "The LORD, the LORD, a God compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, great in loyal love and faithfulness,
34:7 keeping loyal love to thousands, forgiving guilt, rebellion and sin. Yet he will by no means leave guilt unpunished. He brings the fathers' guilt home to the children and the children's children, to the third and the fourth generation."
34:8 Moses quickly bowed to the ground and worshiped.
34:9 He said, "If I have found favor in your eyes, Lord, please let the Lord go in our midst. Yes, this is a stiff-necked people, but forgive our guilt and our sin, and take us as your own."
## The covenant renewed
34:10 He said, "Here I am, making a covenant. In front of all your people I will do wonders that have never been created anywhere on earth or in any nation. All the people you live among will see the LORD's work, for what I am about to do with you is fearsome.
34:11 Keep what I command you today. Here I am, about to drive out ahead of you the Amorites, Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites.
34:12 Be on your guard. Do not make a covenant with those who live in the land you are going into, or they will become a trap in your midst.
34:13 Instead, tear down their altars, smash their standing stones, and cut down their Asherah poles.
34:14 For you must not bow down to another god, because the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God.
34:15 Do not make a covenant with those who live in the land. When they go chasing after their gods like prostitutes and sacrifice to their gods, someone will invite you and you will eat from his sacrifice.
34:16 You will take some of their daughters for your sons. Then their daughters will chase after their gods like prostitutes and lead your sons to chase after their gods too.
34:17 Do not make yourself gods of cast metal.
34:18 "Keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. For seven days eat flat bread made with no yeast, as I commanded you, at the time set in the month of Abib, for in the month of Abib you came out of Egypt.
34:19 Every first to open the womb is mine, every male among your livestock, the firstborn of cattle and sheep.
34:20 Ransom the firstborn of a donkey with a lamb. If you do not ransom it, break its neck. Ransom every firstborn among your sons. No one is to appear before my face with empty hands.
34:21 For six days do your work, but on the seventh day stop. Even at plowing time and at harvest you are to stop.
34:22 Hold the Feast of Weeks, with the first crops of the wheat harvest, and the Feast of Ingathering as the year comes round.
34:23 Three times a year every male among you is to appear before the Lord, the LORD, the God of Israel.
34:24 I will drive nations out ahead of you and stretch your borders wide. When you go up to appear before the LORD your God three times a year, no one will covet your land.
34:25 Do not sacrifice the blood of my sacrifice with anything that has risen with yeast. The sacrifice of the Passover feast must not be left until morning.
34:26 The first and best of the early crops of your ground you are to bring to the house of the LORD your God. Do not boil a young goat in the milk of its mother."
34:27 The LORD said to Moses, "Write these words down for yourself. For on the basis of these words I have made a covenant with you and with Israel."
34:28 Moses was there with the LORD forty days and forty nights. Bread he did not eat, and water he did not drink. And he wrote on the tablets the words of the covenant, the Ten Words.
## The shining face
34:29 Moses came down from Mount Sinai with the two tablets of the testimony in his hand. As he was coming down from the mountain, the skin of his face was shining from his speaking with him, and Moses did not know it.
34:30 Aaron and all the Israelites looked at Moses. There was the skin of his face, shining, and they were too afraid to come near him.
34:31 Moses called out to them, and Aaron and all the community's leaders returned to him. Then Moses talked with them.
34:32 After that all the Israelites came near, and he gave them as commands all that the LORD had spoken with him on Mount Sinai.
34:33 When Moses finished speaking with them, he put a veil over his face.
34:34 But whenever Moses went in before the LORD to speak with him, he took the veil off until he came out. Then he would come out and tell the Israelites what he had been commanded.
34:35 The Israelites would see Moses' face, how the skin of his face shone. And Moses would put the veil over his face again until he went in to speak with him.
## Sabbath first
35:1 Moses gathered the whole community of the Israelites and said to them, "Here are the things the LORD has commanded you to do.
35:2 For six days work can be done, but the seventh day is to be holy for you, a Sabbath, a day of total rest for the LORD. Anyone who does work on it must be put to death.
35:3 On the Sabbath day you must not kindle a fire in any of your homes."
## The people's gifts
35:4 Moses said to the whole community of the Israelites, "This is what the LORD has commanded.
35:5 From what you have, set aside a gift for the LORD. Everyone who is willing at heart is to bring it as the LORD's gift: gold, silver and bronze;
35:6 violet, purple and scarlet yarn, fine linen, and goat hair;
35:7 rams' skins dyed red, fine leather, and acacia wood;
35:8 oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil and for the sweet incense;
35:9 onyx stones, and stones to be set in the ephod and the breastpiece.
35:10 "Let all among you who are skilled at heart come and make everything the LORD has commanded:
35:11 the tabernacle, with its tent and its covering, its hooks, its frames, its bars, its posts and its bases;
35:12 the ark with its poles, the atonement lid, and the veil that screens it off;
35:13 the table with its poles and all its tools, and the Presence Bread;
35:14 the lampstand for the light, with its tools and its lamps, and the oil for the light;
35:15 the incense altar with its poles, the anointing oil and the sweet incense, and the screen for the doorway at the entrance of the tabernacle;
35:16 the altar of burnt offering with its bronze grating, its poles and all its tools, and the basin with its stand;
35:17 the hangings of the courtyard, its posts and its bases, and the screen for the courtyard gate;
35:18 the tent pegs of the tabernacle and of the courtyard, and their ropes;
35:19 the woven clothes for serving in the holy place, the holy clothes for Aaron the priest, and the clothes his sons wear to serve as priests."
35:20 Then the whole community of the Israelites went out from Moses' presence.
35:21 They came, every person whose heart lifted him and whose spirit urged him on, and brought the LORD's gift for the work on the tent of meeting, for all the service in it, and for the holy clothes.
35:22 Men came along with the women, all whose hearts were willing. They brought nose rings, earrings, finger rings and necklaces, every kind of gold object. Each one brought gold as a wave offering for the LORD.
35:23 Everyone who had violet, purple or scarlet yarn, fine linen or goat hair, rams' skins dyed red or fine leather, brought it.
35:24 All who could give a gift of silver or bronze brought it as the LORD's gift. All who had acacia wood that could be used for any of the work brought it.
35:25 Every skilled woman spun with her own hands and brought what she had spun: violet, purple and scarlet yarn and fine linen.
35:26 And all the women whose hearts lifted them with skill spun the goat hair.
35:27 The leaders brought onyx stones and stones to be set in the ephod and the breastpiece,
35:28 and the spice and the oil for the light, for the anointing oil and for the sweet incense.
35:29 So the Israelites brought a freewill gift to the LORD. Every man and woman whose heart urged them on brought something for all the work the LORD had commanded through Moses to be done.
## Bezalel and Oholiab
35:30 Moses said to the Israelites, "Look, the LORD has called by name Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.
35:31 He has filled him with the Spirit of God, with skill, with understanding, with know-how, and with every kind of craft,
35:32 to think up designs and carry them out in gold, silver and bronze,
35:33 to cut stones for setting, to carve wood, and to work at every kind of skilled design.
35:34 And he has given him the gift of teaching, both him and Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.
35:35 He has filled them with skill to do every kind of work: the work of the engraver and the designer, of the weaver in colors who works in violet, purple and scarlet yarn and fine linen, and of the weaver. They can do every kind of work and think up designs.
36:1 So Bezalel and Oholiab will work, with everyone who is skilled at heart, in whom the LORD has put skill and understanding to know how to do all the work for the service of the holy place, just as the LORD has commanded."
## More than enough
36:2 Moses called Bezalel and Oholiab and everyone skilled at heart, in whose heart the LORD had put skill, everyone whose heart lifted him to come forward to the work and do it.
36:3 From Moses they received the whole gift the Israelites had brought to do the work for the service of the holy place. But the people kept on bringing him freewill gifts, morning after morning.
36:4 Then the skilled workers came, all who were busy on the work of the holy place, each from his own task,
36:5 and they told Moses, "The people are bringing too much, more than the service needs for the work the LORD has commanded us to do."
36:6 Moses gave an order, and they had it called out through the camp: "Let no one, man or woman, do any more work on gifts for the holy place." So the people stopped bringing.
36:7 What they had was enough for all the work to be done, and more than enough.
## The curtains and the tent
36:8 All the skilled at heart among those doing the work made the tabernacle out of ten curtains of twisted fine linen and violet, purple and scarlet yarn, with cherubim worked into them, the work of a skilled designer.
36:9 Each curtain was twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide. All the curtains had one size.
36:10 He joined five of the curtains to each other, and the other five curtains he joined to each other.
36:11 Along the edge of the last curtain in the first set he sewed loops of violet yarn. He did it the same way on the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set.
36:12 He put fifty loops on the one curtain and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set, so that the loops lined up, each with its partner.
36:13 He made fifty gold hooks and joined the curtains to each other with the hooks. So the tabernacle was one.
36:14 He made curtains of goat hair for a tent over the tabernacle. He made eleven of these curtains.
36:15 Each curtain was thirty cubits long and four cubits wide. The eleven curtains had one size.
36:16 He sewed five of the curtains together by themselves, and six by themselves.
36:17 He put fifty loops on the edge of the outer curtain in the first set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain of the second set.
36:18 He made fifty bronze hooks to join the tent together, so that it would be one.
36:19 He made a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and on top of that a covering of fine leather.
## The frames
36:20 For the tabernacle he made frames of acacia wood that stood upright.
36:21 Each frame was ten cubits tall and a cubit and a half wide.
36:22 Each frame had two pegs, joined one to the other. He did this for all the frames of the tabernacle.
36:23 He made the frames for the tabernacle like this: twenty frames for the south side, facing south.
36:24 Under the twenty frames he made forty silver bases, two bases under each frame for its two pegs.
36:25 For the other side of the tabernacle, the north side, he made twenty frames
36:26 and their forty silver bases, two bases under each frame.
36:27 For the back of the tabernacle, toward the west, he made six frames.
36:28 He made two frames for the corners of the tabernacle at the back.
36:29 They were doubled from the bottom, and fit together at the top into the first ring. He made both of them this way, for the two corners.
36:30 So there were eight frames with their silver bases, sixteen bases, two under each frame.
36:31 He made bars of acacia wood, five of them for the frames of the first side of the tabernacle,
36:32 five bars for the frames of the second side, and five bars for the frames of the back of the tabernacle, toward the west.
36:33 He made the middle bar to run through the middle of the frames, from one end to the other.
36:34 He covered the frames with gold, and made their rings of gold, to hold the bars. He covered the bars with gold too.
## The veil and the screen
36:35 He made the veil of violet, purple and scarlet yarn and twisted fine linen. He made it with cherubim worked into it, the work of a skilled designer.
36:36 For it he made four posts of acacia and covered them with gold, with gold hooks, and he cast four silver bases for them.
36:37 For the entrance of the tent he made a screen of violet, purple and scarlet yarn and twisted fine linen, the work of a weaver in colors,
36:38 with its five posts and their hooks. He covered the tops of the posts and their bands with gold. Their five bases were bronze.
## The ark and the lid
37:1 Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, one and a half cubits wide and one and a half cubits high.
37:2 He covered it with pure gold, inside and outside, and put a gold molding all the way around it.
37:3 He cast four gold rings for it, for its four feet, two rings on one side and two rings on the other.
37:4 He made poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold.
37:5 He slid the poles into the rings on the ark's sides, for carrying the ark.
37:6 He made an atonement lid of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and one and a half cubits wide.
37:7 He made two cherubim of gold. He hammered them out at the two ends of the lid,
37:8 one cherub at this end and one cherub at that end. He made the cherubim rise out of the lid itself, at its two ends.
37:9 The cherubim held their wings spread upward, sheltering the lid with their wings. They faced each other, their faces turned toward the lid.
## The table and the lampstand
37:10 He made the table of acacia wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide and one and a half cubits high.
37:11 He covered it with pure gold and put a gold molding all the way around it.
37:12 Around it he made a rim a hand's width high, and put a gold molding around the rim.
37:13 He cast four gold rings for it and fixed the rings at the four corners, by its four legs.
37:14 The rings sat close to the rim, as holders for the poles that carry the table.
37:15 He made the poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold, for carrying the table.
37:16 He made the things that go on the table, its plates and its pans, its bowls and the jugs for pouring drink offerings, of pure gold.
37:17 He made the lampstand of pure gold. He made the lampstand of hammered work. Its base and its shaft, its cups, its buds and its flowers grew out of it.
37:18 Six branches came out from its sides, three branches of the lampstand from one side and three from the other.
37:19 On one branch were three cups shaped like almond flowers, each with a bud and a flower, and three almond cups on the next branch, each with a bud and a flower. So it was for all six branches that come out of the lampstand.
37:20 On the lampstand itself were four almond cups, with their buds and flowers.
37:21 There was a bud under the first pair of branches that come out of it, a bud under the second pair, and a bud under the third pair. So it was for the six branches that come out of it.
37:22 Their buds and branches grew out of it. The whole of it was hammered from one piece of pure gold.
37:23 He made its seven lamps, and its tongs and its trays, of pure gold.
37:24 He made it, with all its tools, from a talent of pure gold.
## The incense altar, oil and incense
37:25 He made the altar of incense of acacia wood. It was a cubit long and a cubit wide, square, and two cubits high. Its horns grew out of it.
37:26 He covered its top, its sides all around, and its horns with pure gold, and put a gold molding all the way around it.
37:27 He made two gold rings for it below the molding. He put them on its two sides, on opposite sides, to hold the poles it is carried by.
37:28 He made the poles of acacia wood and covered them with gold.
37:29 He made the holy anointing oil and the pure sweet incense, mixed by the perfumer's art.
## The altar and the basin
38:1 He built the altar of burnt offering of acacia wood. It was square, five cubits by five cubits, and three cubits high.
38:2 He made horns for it at its four corners, growing out of the altar itself, and covered it with bronze.
38:3 He made all the altar's tools: the pots, the shovels, the bowls, the forks and the fire pans. He made all its tools of bronze.
38:4 He made a grating for the altar, a mesh of bronze, below its ledge, reaching halfway up.
38:5 He cast four rings for the four corners of the bronze grating, as holders for the poles.
38:6 He made the poles of acacia wood and covered them with bronze.
38:7 He slid the poles through the rings on the sides of the altar, to carry it by them. He made it hollow, out of boards.
38:8 He made the bronze basin and its bronze stand from the mirrors of the women who served at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
## The courtyard
38:9 He made the courtyard. On the south side, facing south, the hangings of the courtyard were of twisted fine linen, a hundred cubits long,
38:10 with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases. The hooks on the posts and their bands were silver.
38:11 On the north side the hangings were a hundred cubits long, with twenty posts and twenty bronze bases, and silver hooks and bands on the posts.
38:12 On the west side there were fifty cubits of hangings, with ten posts and ten bases, and silver hooks and bands on the posts.
38:13 The east side, toward the sunrise, was fifty cubits wide.
38:14 There were fifteen cubits of hangings on one side, with three posts and three bases,
38:15 and on the other side, so on both sides of the courtyard gate, fifteen cubits of hangings, with three posts and three bases.
38:16 All the hangings around the courtyard were of twisted fine linen.
38:17 The bases for the posts were bronze. The hooks on the posts and their bands were silver, and their tops were covered with silver. All the posts of the courtyard had silver bands.
38:18 The screen for the courtyard gate was the work of a weaver in colors, of violet, purple and scarlet yarn and twisted fine linen. It was twenty cubits long, and five cubits high along its width, to match the hangings of the courtyard.
38:19 It had four posts and four bronze bases. Their hooks were silver, and their tops were covered with silver, and their bands were silver.
38:20 Every tent peg for the tabernacle and for the courtyard around it was bronze.
## What it cost
38:21 Here is the account of the tabernacle, the tabernacle of the testimony. It was drawn up at Moses' order. It was the Levites' task, under Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.
38:22 Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, made everything the LORD had commanded Moses.
38:23 With him was Oholiab son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan. He was an engraver and a designer, and a weaver in colors who worked in violet, purple and scarlet yarn and fine linen.
38:24 All the gold that went into the work, in all the work on the holy place, the gold that was waved as a gift, came to twenty-nine talents and 730 shekels, by the holy place's shekel.
38:25 The silver from those of the community who were enrolled came to a hundred talents and 1,775 shekels, by the holy place's shekel.
38:26 That was one beka for each person, half a shekel by the holy place's shekel, from everyone who crossed over to join those enrolled, from twenty years old and up: 603,550 men.
38:27 The hundred talents of silver went to cast the bases of the holy place and the bases of the veil, a hundred bases from the hundred talents, a talent for each base.
38:28 From the 1,775 shekels he made hooks for the posts, covered their tops, and made bands for them.
38:29 The bronze that was waved as a gift came to seventy talents and 2,400 shekels.
38:30 With it he made the bases for the entrance of the tent of meeting, the bronze altar and its bronze grating, and all the altar's tools,
38:31 the bases all around the courtyard, the bases of the courtyard gate, all the tent pegs of the tabernacle, and all the tent pegs around the courtyard.
## The priests' clothes are made
39:1 From the violet, purple and scarlet yarn they made woven clothes for serving in the holy place. They made Aaron's holy clothes, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
39:2 He made the ephod of gold, of violet, purple and scarlet yarn, and of twisted fine linen.
39:3 They beat the gold into thin sheets, and he cut them into threads to work in among the violet, the purple, the scarlet yarn and the fine linen, the work of a skilled designer.
39:4 They made shoulder straps for it, fastened at its two edges, so it was joined together.
39:5 Its woven waistband, which went on it, was made the same way and of one piece with it, of gold, of violet, purple and scarlet yarn, and of twisted fine linen, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
39:6 They prepared the onyx stones, mounted in gold filigree settings, engraved as a seal is engraved with the names of Israel's sons.
39:7 He put them on the shoulder straps of the ephod, as stones that bring Israel's sons to mind, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
39:8 He made the breastpiece, the work of a skilled designer, the same way as the ephod: of gold, of violet, purple and scarlet yarn, and of twisted fine linen.
39:9 It was square. They made the breastpiece doubled over, a span long and a span wide, doubled.
39:10 They filled it with four rows of stones. The first row: carnelian, topaz and emerald.
39:11 The second row: turquoise, lapis lazuli and quartz.
39:12 The third row: jacinth, agate and amethyst.
39:13 The fourth row: chrysolite, onyx and jasper. They were mounted in gold filigree settings.
39:14 There were twelve stones for the names of Israel's sons, matching their names. Each was engraved like a seal with its own name, for the twelve tribes.
39:15 On the breastpiece they made braided chains of pure gold, worked like rope.
39:16 They made two gold filigree settings and two gold rings, and put the two rings on two ends of the breastpiece.
39:17 They put the two gold ropes through the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece.
39:18 They fastened the other two ends of the two ropes to the two settings, and so attached them to the shoulder straps of the ephod, on its front.
39:19 They made two gold rings and put them on the other two ends of the breastpiece, on its inner edge, the side facing the ephod.
39:20 They made two more gold rings and put them low down on the front of the two shoulder straps, where they join the ephod, above its waistband.
39:21 They tied the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a violet cord, so that it sat on the ephod's waistband and the breastpiece would not come away from the ephod, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
39:22 He made the robe that goes under the ephod, woven work, all of violet.
39:23 The opening of the robe was in the middle, like the neck of a coat of mail, with an edge around the opening so that it would not tear.
39:24 On the hem of the robe they made pomegranates of twisted violet, purple and scarlet yarn.
39:25 They made bells of pure gold and put the bells between the pomegranates, all around the hem of the robe, between the pomegranates:
39:26 a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe, for serving, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
39:27 They made the tunics of fine linen, woven work, for Aaron and his sons,
39:28 the turban of fine linen, the fine linen headbands, and the linen undershorts of twisted fine linen,
39:29 and the sash of twisted fine linen and violet, purple and scarlet yarn, the work of a weaver in colors, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
39:30 They made the plate, the holy crown, of pure gold, and wrote on it, as a seal is engraved, "Holy to the LORD."
39:31 They tied a violet cord to it to fix it on the turban at the top, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
## Moses sees the work
39:32 So all the work for the tabernacle of the tent of meeting was finished. The Israelites acted in every way just as the LORD had commanded Moses. That is how they did it.
39:33 They brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent and all its furnishings, its hooks, its frames, its bars, its posts and its bases;
39:34 the covering of rams' skins dyed red, the covering of fine leather, and the veil that screens it off;
39:35 the ark of the testimony with its poles, and the atonement lid;
39:36 the table with all its tools, and the Presence Bread;
39:37 the pure gold lampstand with its lamps set in a row, all its tools, and the oil for the light;
39:38 the gold altar, the anointing oil, the sweet incense, and the screen for the entrance of the tent;
39:39 the bronze altar with its bronze grating, its poles and all its tools, and the basin with its stand;
39:40 the hangings of the courtyard, its posts and its bases, the screen for the courtyard gate, its ropes and its tent pegs, and all the tools for the service of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting;
39:41 the woven clothes for serving in the holy place, the holy clothes for Aaron the priest, and the clothes his sons wear to serve as priests.
39:42 In everything, just as the LORD had commanded Moses, so the Israelites had done all the labor.
39:43 Moses saw all the work, and there it was: they had done it. Just as the LORD had commanded, so they had done it. And Moses blessed them.
## Setting it up
40:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
40:2 "On the first day of the first month, set up the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.
40:3 Put the ark of the testimony inside, and screen it off with the veil.
40:4 Bring in the table and lay out the things that are set on it. Bring in the lampstand and set up its lamps.
40:5 Place the gold altar for incense in front of the ark of the testimony, and hang the screen at the entrance of the tabernacle.
40:6 Place the altar of burnt offering in front of the doorway of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.
40:7 Set the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and fill it with water.
40:8 Set up the courtyard all around, and hang the screen at the courtyard gate.
40:9 "Take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and everything in it. Make it and all its furnishings holy, and it will be holy.
40:10 Anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its tools. Make the altar holy, and the altar will be most holy.
40:11 Anoint the basin and its stand, and make it holy.
40:12 "Bring Aaron and his sons up to the doorway of the tent of meeting, and wash them there with water.
40:13 Dress Aaron in the holy clothes, anoint him, and set him apart, so that he can be my priest.
40:14 Bring his sons up and put tunics on them.
40:15 Anoint them as you anointed their father, so that they can be my priests. Their anointing will make them a priesthood for all time, through their generations."
40:16 Moses did it. He did everything just as the LORD had commanded him.
40:17 So in the first month of the second year, on the first day of the month, the tabernacle was set up.
40:18 Moses set up the tabernacle. He laid its bases, stood up its frames, put in its bars and raised its posts.
40:19 He spread the tent over the tabernacle and put the tent's covering on top of it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
40:20 He took the testimony and placed it in the ark. He put the poles on the ark and set the atonement lid on top of the ark.
40:21 He brought the ark into the tabernacle and hung the veil that screens it off, and so screened off the ark of the testimony, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
40:22 In the tent of meeting he placed the table, on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the veil.
40:23 On it he laid out the bread in rows before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
40:24 He placed the lampstand in the tent of meeting, across from the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.
40:25 He set up the lamps before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
40:26 He placed the gold altar in the tent of meeting, in front of the veil,
40:27 and he burned sweet incense on it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
40:28 He hung the screen at the entrance of the tabernacle.
40:29 At the doorway of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, he set the altar of burnt offering, and on it he offered the burnt offering and the grain offering, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
40:30 Between the tent of meeting and the altar he set the basin, and he put water in it for washing.
40:31 From it Moses, Aaron and his sons washed their hands and their feet.
40:32 Whenever they went into the tent of meeting, or came near the altar, they washed, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
40:33 He set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and the altar, and hung the screen at the courtyard gate. So Moses finished the work.
## The glory fills the tabernacle
40:34 The cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the LORD's glory filled the tabernacle.
40:35 Moses could not go into the tent of meeting, because the cloud had settled on it and the LORD's glory filled the tabernacle.
40:36 Whenever the cloud lifted from over the tabernacle, the Israelites would set out, through all their journeys.
40:37 But if the cloud did not lift, they did not set out until the day it lifted.
40:38 For the LORD's cloud was over the tabernacle by day, and fire was in it by night, before the eyes of the whole house of Israel, through all their journeys.
