## Greeting to the church in Tralles
1:0 Ignatius, also called Theophorus, to the holy church that is in Tralles in Asia. It is loved by God, the Father of Jesus Christ. It is chosen and worthy of God, and it has peace in flesh and spirit through the suffering of Jesus Christ, who is our hope in our resurrection into him. I greet it in the fullness, in the apostolic manner, and I send it abundant greetings.
## Polybius at Smyrna
1:1 I have learned that your mind is blameless and unwavering in endurance, not as a habit but by nature. Polybius, your overseer, made this plain to me. By the will of God and of Jesus Christ he came to Smyrna, and he shared my joy so fully, as I am in chains in Christ Jesus, that in him I saw your whole congregation.
1:2 So through him I received your godly goodwill, and I gave glory to God, for I found you, as I had learned, to be imitators of God.
## Subject to the overseer
2:1 For when you are subject to the overseer as to Jesus Christ, you seem to me to be living not in a human way, but in the way of Jesus Christ, who died for us, so that by believing in his death you may escape dying.
2:2 So it is necessary, as you already do, to do nothing without the overseer. Be subject also to the council of elders as to the apostles of Jesus Christ, our hope, for if we live in him, we will be found.
2:3 The deacons, too, who are deacons of the mysteries of Jesus Christ, must please everyone in every way. For they are not deacons of food and drink, but assistants of the church of God. So they must guard against blame as they would against fire.
## Without these it is not called a church
3:1 In the same way, let everyone respect the deacons as Jesus Christ, just as they respect the overseer, who is a type of the Father, and the elders as God's council and as the band of the apostles. Without these, it is not called a church.
3:2 I am sure that you see these things this way. For I have received the model of your love, and I have it with me, in your overseer. His very bearing is a great lesson, and his gentleness is power. I think even the godless respect him.
3:3 Because I love you, I spare you, though I could write more sharply about this. I did not think I should go as far as this: that I, a condemned man, should give you orders like an apostle.
## Not puffed up
4:1 I have many thoughts in God, but I take my own measure, so that I do not perish by boasting. For now I must be more afraid, and not listen to those who puff me up. Those who talk to me that way are whipping me.
4:2 For I do love to suffer, but I do not know if I am worthy. For the jealousy is not seen by many, but it makes war on me all the more. So I need gentleness, by which the ruler of this age is brought down.
## Heavenly things for infants
5:1 Am I not able to write to you about heavenly things? I am, but I am afraid that I might do you harm, since you are infants. Forgive me, in case you are not able to take it in and are choked.
5:2 For even I, though I am in chains and can understand heavenly things, the places of the angels and the gatherings of the rulers, seen and unseen, am not already a disciple because of that. For much is still lacking in us, so that we may not lack God.
## Poison in honeyed wine
6:1 So I urge you, not I but the love of Jesus Christ: use only Christian food, and keep away from the foreign weed, which is heresy.
6:2 These people weave Jesus Christ in with themselves to win trust. They are like people who give a deadly poison mixed with honeyed wine. Someone who does not know takes it gladly, and with evil pleasure takes his death.
## Within the altar
7:1 So guard yourselves against such people. You will do this if you are not puffed up and stay inseparable from God Jesus Christ, and from the overseer and the ordinances of the apostles.
7:2 Whoever is within the altar is pure, but whoever is outside the altar is not pure. That is, whoever does anything without the overseer, the council of elders and the deacons is not pure in his conscience.
## Faith the flesh, love the blood
8:1 It is not that I know of anything of this kind among you, but I am guarding you in advance, since you are my loved ones, for I see the snares of the devil ahead. So take up a gentle spirit and recover your strength in faith, which is the flesh of the Lord, and in love, which is the blood of Jesus Christ.
8:2 Let none of you hold anything against his neighbor. Do not give the Gentiles an excuse, so that the congregation that is in God is not insulted because of a few fools. For "Woe to the one through whom my name is insulted for no reason among some."
## Truly born, truly crucified, truly raised
9:1 So be deaf when anyone speaks to you apart from Jesus Christ. He was of David's family and of Mary. He was truly born, and he ate and drank. He was truly persecuted under Pontius Pilate. He was truly crucified and died, in the sight of those in heaven, on earth and under the earth.
9:2 He was also truly raised from the dead, when his Father raised him. In the same way his Father will also raise us who believe in him, in Christ Jesus, without whom we do not have true life.
## Then I die for nothing
10:1 But if, as some godless people say (that is, unbelievers), he suffered only in appearance (they themselves are only in appearance!), why am I in chains? Why do I even pray to fight wild beasts? Then I am dying for nothing. Then I am telling lies about the Lord.
## Branches of the cross
11:1 So run from the bad offshoots that bear deadly fruit. If anyone tastes it, he dies on the spot. For these people are not the Father's planting.
11:2 If they were, they would show themselves as branches of the cross, and their fruit would be incorruptible. By the cross, through his suffering, he calls you, since you are his members. So a head cannot be born apart, without members, since God promises union, which is himself.
## Greetings from Smyrna
12:1 I greet you from Smyrna, together with the churches of God that are here with me. They have refreshed me in every way, in flesh and in spirit.
12:2 My chains urge you on, the chains I carry around for the sake of Jesus Christ as I ask to reach God. Stay in your harmony and in prayer with one another. For it is right for each of you, and above all for the elders, to refresh the overseer, to the honor of the Father, of Jesus Christ and of the apostles.
12:3 I pray that you will listen to me in love, so that my letter does not stand as a witness against you. And pray for me too. I need your love in the mercy of God, so that I may be counted worthy of the lot I am set to reach, and not be found disqualified.
## Farewell
13:1 The love of the Smyrnaeans and Ephesians greets you. Remember in your prayers the church in Syria. I am not worthy to be called one of its people, since I am the last of them.
13:2 Farewell in Jesus Christ. Be subject to the overseer as to the commandment, and likewise to the council of elders. And each of you, love one another with an undivided heart.
13:3 My spirit gives itself as an offering for you, not only now but also when I reach God. For I am still in danger. But the Father is faithful in Jesus Christ to fulfill my request and yours. In him may you be found blameless.
