## Judas agrees to hand Jesus over
22:1 The Feast of Unleavened Bread, the festival called Passover, was coming near.
22:2 The chief priests and the scribes were looking for a way to do away with Jesus, because the people made them afraid.
22:3 Then Satan entered Judas, the one called Iscariot, who was counted among the Twelve.
22:4 He went off and talked with the chief priests and the commanders about how he could hand Jesus over to them.
22:5 They were glad, and they made a deal to pay him in silver.
22:6 He said yes, and from then on he looked for a good chance to hand him over to them away from any crowd.
## The Passover meal
22:7 The day of Unleavened Bread came, the day when they were required to sacrifice the Passover lamb.
22:8 So Jesus sent off Peter and John. "Go and get the Passover ready for us," he said, "so that we can eat it."
22:9 They said to him, "Where would you like us to get it ready?"
22:10 He told them, "Look, as you go into the city, you will meet a man carrying a clay jar of water. Go after him into the house he enters.
22:11 Say to the owner of the house, 'The Teacher says to you: Where is the guest room, where I will eat the Passover with my disciples?'
22:12 That man will show you a big room upstairs, laid out with couches. Make it ready there."
22:13 They went off and found things exactly as he had said to them. And they made the Passover ready.
## Bread and cup
22:14 When the hour came, he lay down at the table, and so did the apostles.
22:15 He said to them, "How I have longed to eat this Passover with you before I suffer!
22:16 For I tell you, I will not eat it until it comes to its fulfillment in the kingdom of God."
22:17 He was handed a cup, and he gave thanks. "Take this," he said, "and share it out among yourselves.
22:18 For I tell you, I will not drink the produce of the vine from this time on, until the kingdom of God has come."
22:19 He took a loaf of bread and gave thanks. He broke it and handed it to them, saying, "This is my body, which is being given for you. Do this to remember me."
22:20 And he took the cup in the same way when the meal was over. He said, "This cup is the new covenant, made in my blood. It is being poured out for you.
## The one who hands him over
22:21 But look: the man handing me over has his hand here with mine on the table.
22:22 For the Son of Man goes his way, as it has been marked out. But woe to that man through whom he is handed over!"
22:23 They started to ask each other who among them could be the one about to do this.
## Who is the greatest?
22:24 They also fell to quarreling over which of them counted as the greatest.
22:25 He said to them, "The kings of the nations act as lords over them, and those who use their power over them are given the title 'Benefactor.'
22:26 With you, though, it is not to be that way. The greater among you must become like the younger, and the one in charge like the one who waits on tables.
22:27 Who is greater, the one lying at the table or the one waiting on him? Isn't it the one at the table? But I, in your company, am like the one who waits on tables.
22:28 You are the ones who have remained at my side through my times of testing.
22:29 And I am making over to you a kingdom, just as my Father made one over to me,
22:30 so that at my table, in my kingdom, you may eat and drink. And you will sit on thrones and judge the twelve tribes of Israel.
## Jesus predicts Peter's denial
22:31 Simon, Simon, listen: Satan has asked to have all of you, to shake you in a sieve like wheat.
22:32 But I have pleaded for you, that your faith would not fail. And you, once you have turned around, must make your brothers strong."
22:33 "Lord," Peter said to him, "with you I am prepared to go to prison, and to death too."
22:34 He answered, "Peter, I tell you: today a rooster will not crow until you have three times denied knowing me."
## Purse, bag and sword
22:35 He said to them, "When I sent you out without a money purse, a bag or sandals, were you short of anything?" "Nothing," they said.
22:36 He said to them, "But now, whoever has a money purse should take it, and a bag as well. And whoever has none must sell his cloak and buy a sword.
22:37 For I tell you, this scripture must be completed in me: 'And he was counted with the lawless.' Indeed, what concerns me is coming to its end."
22:38 They said, "Lord, look, here are two swords." He said to them, "It is enough."
## Praying on the Mount of Olives
22:39 He went out and made his way, as he usually did, to the Mount of Olives. His disciples came along after him too.
22:40 When he reached the place, he said to them, "Pray not to come into testing."
22:41 He tore himself away from them, about as far as a man can throw a stone. Kneeling, he began to pray:
22:42 "Father, if you are willing, make this cup pass me by. Even so, may your will be done, not mine."
22:43 Then an angel appeared to him from heaven and gave him strength.
22:44 Caught in a struggle, he prayed harder still. His sweat came like clots of blood dripping onto the ground.
22:45 He got up from praying and came to the disciples. He found them asleep, worn out by grief.
22:46 He said to them, "Why are you sleeping? Stand up and pray that you do not come into testing."
## The arrest
22:47 He was still talking when, look, a crowd came. Judas, as he was called, one of the Twelve, was walking at their head. He came up close to Jesus to kiss him.
22:48 But Jesus said to him, "Judas, with a kiss you hand over the Son of Man?"
22:49 Those around him saw what was about to happen. "Lord," they said, "are we to strike with the sword?"
22:50 Then one of them struck the high priest's slave, slicing off his right ear.
22:51 But Jesus answered, "Leave it at this!" Touching the ear, he healed him.
22:52 Then Jesus spoke to the ones who had come out against him, the chief priests, the commanders of the temple and the elders: "Am I a bandit, that you come out with swords and clubs?
22:53 Day by day I was there with you in the temple, and you did not lift a hand against me. But this hour belongs to you, and to the authority of darkness."
## Peter denies Jesus three times
22:54 They arrested him and marched him off, and brought him inside the high priest's home. Peter kept following from far back.
22:55 They had lit a fire in the center of the courtyard and sat down around it together, and Peter was sitting in the middle of them.
22:56 A certain slave girl caught sight of him as he sat facing the light. She stared at him and said, "This man too was with him."
22:57 But he denied it. "I do not know him, woman," he said.
22:58 Shortly after, another man noticed him. "You too are one of them," he said. "Man, I am not," said Peter.
22:59 About an hour went by, and another man kept insisting, "It's the truth: this man was with him too. He is a Galilean, after all."
22:60 Peter said, "Man, I have no idea what you mean." Right then, while he was still talking, a rooster crowed.
22:61 The Lord turned around and fixed his eyes on Peter. And Peter remembered what the Lord had said to him: "Before any rooster crows today, you will deny me three times."
22:62 He went outside and cried bitterly.
## Mocked by the guards
22:63 The men keeping Jesus under guard kept making fun of him and hitting him.
22:64 They covered his eyes and kept asking him, "Be a prophet! Who struck you?"
22:65 Many other things too they said to him, reviling him.
## Before the Sanhedrin
22:66 When day came, the people's council of elders gathered, chief priests and scribes alike. They took him off to their Sanhedrin
22:67 and said, "Tell us, if you are the Messiah." He answered them, "If I tell you, you will never believe it,
22:68 and if I ask you, you will never answer.
22:69 But from this time on, the Son of Man will be sitting at the right hand of God's Power."
22:70 Then all of them said, "So you are the Son of God?" He told them, "It is you who say that I am."
22:71 They said, "Why should we need testimony anymore? We ourselves have heard it straight from his mouth."
## Jesus before Pilate
23:1 Then their whole assembly got up and took him to Pilate.
23:2 They started bringing charges against him. "We found this man leading our nation astray," they said. "He hinders people from paying taxes to Caesar, and he says that he himself is Messiah, a king."
23:3 Pilate questioned him: "So you are the King of the Jews?" "You are the one saying it," he answered him.
23:4 Then Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, "I find nothing to charge this man with."
23:5 But they kept pressing. "He is getting the people worked up," they said, "with his teaching all through Judea. Galilee is where he began, and now he has reached this place."
## Before Herod
23:6 Pilate heard it and asked, "Is the man a Galilean?"
23:7 Finding out that he came from the territory Herod ruled, he sent him on to Herod. Herod too was in Jerusalem in those days.
23:8 Herod was delighted to see Jesus. For a long time now he had wished to see him, because of the reports about him, and he was hoping to watch him do some sign.
23:9 He asked him question after question, but Jesus answered him nothing.
23:10 The chief priests and the scribes stood there, hurling accusations at him.
23:11 Herod and his troops treated him as nothing and made fun of him. Herod put a shining robe on him and returned him to Pilate.
23:12 That very day Herod and Pilate made friends with each other. Before, there had been bad blood between them.
## Pilate gives in
23:13 Pilate called a meeting of the chief priests and the rulers, and of the people,
23:14 and said to them, "You brought this man to me as one who leads the people off course. Look: I have questioned him here in front of you, and I have found nothing in him to support the charges you bring against him.
23:15 Herod has found nothing either, since he sent him back to us. Look: nothing he has done deserves death.
23:16 So I will teach him a lesson and set him free."
23:17 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
23:18 But the whole crowd screamed as one, "Take this man away! Set Barabbas free for us!"
23:19 Barabbas had been put in prison over an uprising that had happened in the city, and over a killing.
23:20 Pilate spoke to them again, because he wanted to set Jesus free.
23:21 But they shouted back over him, "Crucify, crucify him!"
23:22 A third time he said to them, "What evil has this man done, then? I have found nothing in him that deserves death. So I will teach him a lesson and set him free."
23:23 But they kept at him with loud shouts, asking for him to be crucified, and their voices won out.
23:24 Pilate gave his ruling: their demand would be carried out.
23:25 He set free the man they asked for, the one who had been put in prison over an uprising and a killing. Jesus he handed over to what they wanted.
## Daughters of Jerusalem
23:26 As they marched him off, they grabbed a man named Simon, from Cyrene, who was coming in from the fields. They laid the cross on him to carry behind Jesus.
23:27 A great crowd of the people kept following him, and women too, who beat their breasts and sang laments for him.
23:28 Jesus turned to them. "Daughters of Jerusalem," he said, "stop crying over me. Cry instead over yourselves and over your children.
23:29 For look, days are coming when people will say, 'Blessed are the barren, the wombs that never gave birth and breasts that never fed a baby.'
23:30 That is when they will start telling the mountains, 'Fall on us,' and the hills, 'Cover us.'
23:31 If this is what they do to green wood, what will happen to the dry?"
## The crucifixion
23:32 Two others, both criminals, were also being taken out with him to be killed.
23:33 When they reached the place called Skull, there they crucified him, and the criminals too, one on his right and one on his left.
23:34 And Jesus kept saying, "Father, forgive them, because they do not know what they are doing." They shared out his clothes by throwing lots.
23:35 The people stood by, watching. The rulers too turned up their noses at him. "Others he saved," they said. "He should save himself, if this man is God's Messiah, the Chosen One."
23:36 The soldiers made fun of him too. They came up and held out sour wine to him
23:37 and said, "If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself!"
23:38 There was a notice over him too: "The King of the Jews, this man."
## The two criminals
23:39 One of the criminals hanging there was reviling him. "You are the Messiah, aren't you?" he said. "Save yourself, and us!"
23:40 The other answered him with a rebuke: "Don't you even fear God? The same sentence has fallen on you.
23:41 For us it is fair. What we are getting back fits what we did. This man, though, has done nothing out of place."
23:42 And he was saying, "Jesus, remember me when you come in your kingdom."
23:43 Jesus said to him, "Amen, I tell you: today you will be with me in paradise."
## The death of Jesus
23:44 It was already about the sixth hour, and darkness lay over the whole land until the ninth hour,
23:45 because the sun had failed. And the sanctuary curtain was torn down the middle.
23:46 Jesus cried out with a great cry, "Father, into your hands I place my spirit." And with these words he breathed out his life.
23:47 The centurion saw what had happened, and he gave glory to God. "This man really was righteous," he said.
23:48 All the crowds that had come together to watch this sight saw what happened, and they went back beating their chests.
23:49 All who knew him stood a long way off, and so did the women who had followed along with him from Galilee. They saw these things.
## The burial
23:50 And look: a man named Joseph, a council member. He was a good man and a righteous one.
23:51 He had not voted for their plan or for what they did. He was from Arimathea, a town of the Jews, and he was looking for the kingdom of God.
23:52 This man went to Pilate with a request for the body of Jesus.
23:53 He took it down and wound linen around it. Then he laid him in a tomb carved out of the rock, where nobody had been laid yet.
23:54 That day was the Day of Preparation, and the Sabbath was beginning to dawn.
23:55 The women who had come out of Galilee with him followed after. They took a look at the tomb and at how his body was placed.
23:56 They went back and got fragrant spices and perfumed oils ready. Then, as the commandment says, they kept the Sabbath rest.
## He has been raised
24:1 On the first day of the week, at first light, they came to the tomb, carrying the fragrant spices they had gotten ready.
24:2 The stone, they found, had been rolled back from the tomb.
24:3 They went inside, but they could not find the body of the Lord Jesus.
24:4 While they were at a loss about this, look: two men stood there beside them, in clothing that flashed like lightning.
24:5 The women were afraid and bent their faces toward the ground. The men said to them, "Why look for the one who lives among the dead?
24:6 He isn't here, but he has been raised. Remember how he spoke to you back when he was still in Galilee.
24:7 He said that the Son of Man must be handed over into the hands of men who are sinners, be crucified, and rise on the third day."
24:8 And they remembered what he had said.
24:9 On their return from the tomb they reported all this to the Eleven and to everyone else.
24:10 These were Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the other women with them. They kept telling the apostles about these things.
24:11 To the apostles their report looked like silly talk, and they refused to believe the women.
24:12 Peter, though, got up and ran to the tomb. He bent down to peer in and saw the linen wrappings, and nothing else. Then he went off home, wondering at what had happened.
## On the road to Emmaus
24:13 On that same day, look, two of the group were traveling to a village sixty stadia from Jerusalem, named Emmaus.
24:14 They were discussing with one another all that had taken place.
24:15 As they talked and debated, Jesus himself came close and began to walk with them.
24:16 Their eyes, though, were held so that they did not recognize him.
24:17 He said to them, "What are these words you are trading back and forth as you walk?" They stopped, their faces gloomy.
24:18 One of them, a man named Cleopas, answered him, "Are you the one stranger in Jerusalem who has not heard what has gone on there in these days?"
24:19 "What sort of things?" he said to them. "About Jesus the Nazarene," they told him. "He was a prophet, a man of power in what he did and what he said, in God's sight and in the sight of all the people.
24:20 Our chief priests and rulers handed him over for a death sentence, and they crucified him.
24:21 We had pinned our hopes on him as the man who would redeem Israel. And on top of it all, this day is the third since it all took place.
24:22 Some women from among us have even left us beside ourselves. They went to the tomb at dawn
24:23 and did not find his body. They came back and told us they had even seen angels in a vision, who say he is alive.
24:24 Some of our people went off to the tomb. They found things exactly as the women had told it. But they did not see him."
24:25 Then he said to them, "Oh, you people without sense! How slow your hearts are to trust everything the prophets said!
24:26 The Messiah had to suffer all this and so go into his glory, didn't he?"
24:27 Starting from Moses and from all the prophets, he explained to them the things about himself in all the Scriptures.
24:28 They came near the village they were heading for, and he made as though he would go on farther.
24:29 They pressed him hard. "Stay with us," they said. "Evening is coming on, and the day has already gone down." So he went inside and stayed with them.
24:30 While he lay at the table with them, he took the bread and said the blessing. He broke it and began handing it to them.
24:31 Their eyes were opened wide, and they knew him. And he was gone from them, out of sight.
24:32 They said to one another, "Our hearts were on fire inside us, weren't they, as he spoke with us on the road, as he laid open the Scriptures for us?"
24:33 That very hour they got up and went back to Jerusalem. They found the Eleven gathered there with the others who were with them,
24:34 who were saying, "The Lord really has been raised! He has appeared to Simon."
24:35 The two of them told about what happened on the road, and how they had come to know him in the breaking of the bread.
## Jesus appears to the disciples
24:36 As they were saying these things, he himself stood in the middle of them and said to them, "Peace to you."
24:37 But they were shaken and full of fear, and they thought they were seeing a spirit.
24:38 He said to them, "What has stirred you up so? And why are these questions coming up in your heart?
24:39 Look at my hands and my feet, and see that it is really me. Feel me and look: a spirit has no flesh and bones, and you can see that I have them."
24:40 With these words he showed them his hands and his feet.
24:41 Their joy was so great that they still could not believe it, and they were full of wonder. So he said to them, "Have you got any food here?"
24:42 They handed him a piece of grilled fish.
24:43 He took it and ate it with them watching.
24:44 Then he said to them, "These are the words I spoke to you back when I was still with you: all that has been written about me in the Law of Moses, in the Prophets and in the Psalms must come true."
24:45 Then he opened up their minds to grasp the Scriptures.
24:46 He told them, "So it stands written: the Messiah would suffer, and on the third day he would rise from among the dead,
24:47 and in his name repentance and the forgiveness of sins would be proclaimed to all the nations, with you starting from Jerusalem.
24:48 You are the witnesses to all this.
24:49 And look: I am sending out my Father's promise upon you. As for you, remain in the city until you are dressed in power from on high."
## Taken up into heaven
24:50 He led them out until they were near Bethany. He lifted his hands and blessed them.
24:51 And as he was blessing them, he stepped away from them and was being carried up into heaven.
24:52 They bowed down before him, and they went back to Jerusalem with great joy.
24:53 And they were in the temple all the time, blessing God.
