## Healing at a Pharisee's table
14:1 On a Sabbath Jesus went into the house of one of the leaders of the Pharisees for a meal, and they were keeping a close eye on him.
14:2 Right there in front of him was a man swollen with dropsy.
14:3 Jesus spoke up and asked the lawyers and Pharisees, "Is it allowed to heal on the Sabbath, or not?"
14:4 They kept quiet. Then he took hold of the man, healed him and let him go.
14:5 And he said to them, "Suppose one of you has a son, or an ox, that falls down a well. Won't he haul him up at once, even on the Sabbath day?"
14:6 And they were unable to answer him back on this.
## The top places at the table
14:7 Then he told the invited guests a parable, because he had noticed how they were choosing the top places. He said to them,
14:8 "When anyone invites you to a wedding, do not recline in the top place. He may have invited someone more highly honored than you.
14:9 Then the one who invited you and him will come and tell you, 'Give this man the place.' And then, ashamed, you will start to take the last place.
14:10 No, when you are invited, go and recline in the last place, so that when the man who invited you comes, he will tell you, 'Friend, come up higher.' Then you will have honor before everyone who is reclining at the table with you.
14:11 For everyone who raises himself up will be brought low, and the one who brings himself low will be raised up."
14:12 Jesus also spoke to his host. "When you put on a midday meal or a supper," he said, "don't call in your friends or your brothers or your relatives or rich neighbors. They might invite you back in turn, and that would be your repayment.
14:13 Instead, when you hold a feast, call in people who are poor, crippled, lame or blind.
14:14 Then you will be blessed, since they have no way to pay you back. You will be paid back at the resurrection of the righteous."
## The great supper
14:15 One of the other guests at the table heard this and said to him, "How blessed is whoever eats bread in the kingdom of God!"
14:16 Jesus answered him, "Someone was putting on a big supper, and he invited a lot of people.
14:17 At the hour of the supper he sent his slave to tell the invited guests, 'Come along, it's ready now.'
14:18 But as one, they all began to beg off. The first told him, 'I bought a field, and I really need to go out and look at it. I'm asking you, count me excused.'
14:19 Another said, 'I bought five pairs of oxen, and I'm going to test them. I'm asking you, count me excused.'
14:20 Another said, 'I've taken a wife, and so I am not able to come.'
14:21 When the slave got back, he told his master all this. The householder grew angry and said to his slave, 'Hurry out into the squares and lanes of the town. Bring the poor in here, and the crippled, the blind and the lame.'
14:22 The slave said, 'Master, what you told me to do is done, and there's still space.'
14:23 So the master told the slave, 'Go out to the roads and the hedgerows, and make them come in, so that my house will be full.
14:24 For I tell you that not one of those invited men will taste my supper.'"
## What it costs to follow
14:25 Big crowds were walking along with him. He turned around and told them,
14:26 "Anyone who comes to me but does not hate his father and mother, his wife and children, his brothers and sisters, and even his own life too, cannot be a disciple of mine.
14:27 Anyone who does not carry his own cross and come behind me cannot be a disciple of mine.
14:28 If one of you wanted to build a tower, wouldn't he sit down first and add up the expense, to see whether he could afford to finish it?
14:29 If he didn't, he might lay a foundation and then be unable to complete the job, and all the onlookers would begin to laugh at him.
14:30 'This fellow started building,' they would say, 'and he couldn't see it through.'
14:31 Or what king, marching out to clash with another king in battle, does not first sit down and think it over? Can he, with ten thousand, meet the one who is coming at him with twenty thousand?
14:32 If he can't, then while the other is still a long way off he sends envoys and asks what peace will take.
14:33 So then, none of you can be a disciple of mine if he does not say goodbye to all his possessions.
14:34 "Salt, then, is a good thing. But if even salt goes flat, what will it be seasoned with?
14:35 It is no good for the land, and no good for the dung heap. They throw it out. Let anyone who has ears to hear listen."
## The lost sheep and the lost coin
15:1 The tax collectors and sinners, every one of them, kept coming close to hear him.
15:2 Both the Pharisees and the scribes were complaining under their breath: "This fellow takes sinners in, and he eats with them!"
15:3 So he spoke this parable to them:
15:4 "Suppose one of you has a hundred sheep, and he loses one. Won't he leave the ninety-nine out in the wilderness and walk after the lost one until he finds it?
15:5 When he has found it, he lifts it onto his shoulders, full of joy,
15:6 and once he gets home he invites his friends and neighbors over and says to them, 'Be glad with me! I've found my sheep, the one that was lost.'
15:7 I tell you, it will be the same in heaven. There will be more joy there over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous people who have no need of repentance.
15:8 "Or think of a woman with ten drachmas. If she loses one drachma, she lights a lamp, doesn't she, and sweeps out the house, and hunts carefully until she finds it?
15:9 And when she has found it, she gathers her women friends and neighbors. 'Be glad with me,' she says, 'because I have found the drachma that I lost.'
15:10 That is how it is, I tell you: joy breaks out before God's angels over one sinner who repents."
## The father and his two sons
15:11 He went on: "A man had two sons.
15:12 The younger of them said to the father, 'Father, give me the part of the estate that falls to me.' So the father split his living between them.
15:13 A few days later the younger son gathered up everything and went off abroad to a faraway country. There he scattered his estate, living a wasteful life.
15:14 Just when he had used up everything, a hard famine struck that whole country, and he started to run short.
15:15 So he went and latched on to one of the citizens of that country, who sent him out into his fields to tend pigs.
15:16 He wished he could eat his fill from the carob pods the pigs were eating, but no one would give him anything.
15:17 At last he came back to himself. 'How many of my father's hired men have bread and to spare,' he said, 'and I am out here dying of hunger!
15:18 I'll get up and go to my father, and I'll tell him, "Father, I have sinned against heaven and in your sight.
15:19 I don't deserve to be called your son anymore. Make me like one of your hired men."'
15:20 He got up and came to his own father. While he was still far away, his father caught sight of him, and his heart went out to him. He ran, fell on his son's neck and kissed him again and again.
15:21 The son said to him, 'Father, I have sinned against heaven and in your sight. I don't deserve to be called your son anymore.'
15:22 But the father told his slaves, 'Hurry, bring out a robe, the best one, and dress him in it. Give him a ring for his hand and sandals for his feet.
15:23 Fetch the calf we've been fattening and slaughter it, and let's eat and be merry.
15:24 This son of mine was dead, and he has come back to life. He was lost, and he has been found.' And the merrymaking began.
15:25 "His elder son was out in the field. As he was coming in and drew close to the house, he heard the sound of music and dancing.
15:26 He called one of the serving boys over and kept asking what all this could be.
15:27 'Your brother is here,' he told him, 'and your father has slaughtered the fattened calf, since he has got him back in good health.'
15:28 That made him angry, and he would not go in. His father came outside and kept urging him.
15:29 He answered his father, 'See here: I have worked like a slave for you for so many years, and not once did I go against an order of yours. And you never once gave me a goat kid to have a party with my friends.
15:30 But when this son of yours turned up, the one who has gobbled up your living with prostitutes, you slaughtered the fattened calf for him!'
15:31 He said to him, 'My child, you are with me all the time, and all I have belongs to you.
15:32 It was right to be merry and glad, because this brother of yours was dead and has come to life. He was lost, and he has been found.'"
## The shrewd manager
16:1 He said to the disciples as well: "A rich man had a manager, and this manager was reported to him for squandering his property.
16:2 He summoned him. 'What's this I'm hearing about you?' he said. 'Hand over the books of your management. You can't be manager any longer.'
16:3 The manager thought it over: 'My master is taking my position away from me. What am I to do? Digging is too hard for me, and begging would shame me.
16:4 I've got it! I know what to do, so that when I am removed from my post, people will take me into their own homes.'
16:5 Then he sent for every one of his master's debtors, one at a time. To the first he said, 'What do you owe my master?'
16:6 'A hundred baths of olive oil,' he answered. He told him, 'Here are your papers. Sit down, quick, and write fifty.'
16:7 Next he asked another, 'And what about you? What do you owe?' 'A hundred cors of wheat,' he said. 'Here are your papers,' he told him. 'Write eighty.'
16:8 And the master praised the crooked manager for acting so shrewdly. For the children of this age are shrewder than the children of light toward their own kind.
16:9 And I myself tell you: make yourselves friends out of the wealth of dishonesty, so that when it runs out, they will take you into the everlasting tents.
16:10 "Someone who is faithful in the smallest things is faithful in big things as well, and someone who is dishonest in the smallest things is dishonest in big things as well.
16:11 So if you have not proved faithful with dishonest wealth, who will trust you with the real thing?
16:12 And if you have not proved faithful with what belongs to another, who will hand you what is yours?
16:13 No household slave can be a slave to two masters. He will either hate the first and love the second, or stick by the first and look down on the second. You cannot be a slave both to God and to wealth."
## The Law and the kingdom
16:14 The Pharisees were listening to all this, and since they were fond of money, they turned up their noses at him.
16:15 He told them, "You justify yourselves in people's eyes, but God knows your hearts. Whatever stands high among humans is abhorrent before God.
16:16 "The Law and the Prophets lasted until John. Since then the good news of the kingdom of God is being told, and everyone is pressing their way into it.
16:17 Yet heaven and earth could pass away more easily than one tiny hook of a letter could fall from the Law.
16:18 "Everyone who divorces his wife and marries someone else commits adultery. And the man who marries a woman divorced from her husband commits adultery.
## The rich man and Lazarus
16:19 "A certain man was rich. He dressed in purple and fine linen, and he made merry in splendor every day.
16:20 And a poor man named Lazarus had been left lying at his gateway, his body full of sores.
16:21 He longed to eat his fill from what dropped off the rich man's table. Not only that, the dogs would come and lick his sores.
16:22 It happened that the poor man died, and he was carried off by the angels to Abraham's embrace. The rich man died too, and was buried.
16:23 From Hades, in the middle of his torments, he raised his eyes and saw Abraham a long way off, and Lazarus in his embrace.
16:24 He called out, 'Father Abraham, show me mercy! Send Lazarus to wet the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue, because this flame is causing me pain.'
16:25 Abraham answered, 'My child, call to mind that you got your good things in full during your life, and Lazarus likewise got the bad ones. Now he is comforted here, while you suffer pain.
16:26 On top of all that, a huge gulf has been set firm between us and you, so that those who wish to go across from here to you are not able to, and no one may get over from there to us.'
16:27 He said, 'In that case, father, I ask this of you: send him to my father's household.
16:28 I have five brothers. He can give them solemn warning, so that they don't end up in this place of torment as well.'
16:29 Abraham said, 'They have Moses and the Prophets. They should listen to those.'
16:30 He said, 'No, father Abraham! But if somebody from among the dead were to go to them, they would repent.'
16:31 He told him, 'If Moses and the Prophets get no hearing from them, then not even if someone rises from the dead will they be persuaded.'"
## Snares, forgiveness and faith
17:1 Jesus said to his disciples, "There is no way that snares will not come. Yet woe to the person through whom they come!
17:2 He would be better off if a millstone were put around his neck and he were hurled into the sea, than that he should trip up one of these little ones.
17:3 Keep watch over yourselves. If your brother sins, take him to task, and if he repents, forgive him.
17:4 And if seven times in one day he sins against you, and seven times turns back to you and says, 'I repent,' you are to forgive him."
17:5 The apostles said to the Lord, "Give us more faith!"
17:6 The Lord said, "If your faith were the size of a mustard seed, you would say to this mulberry tree, 'Pull yourself up by the roots and plant yourself in the sea,' and it would do what you say.
17:7 "Which of you, with a slave out plowing or minding the flock, will tell him when he comes in from the field, 'Go straight in and recline at the table'?
17:8 No, he will say to him, 'Fix something for my supper. Tie up your robe and serve me while I eat and drink. Then you can eat and drink yourself.'
17:9 He doesn't owe the slave thanks for carrying out his orders, does he?
17:10 It is the same with you. When you have done all you were ordered to do, say, 'We are useless slaves. What we were bound to do, we have done.'"
## Ten men made clean
17:11 While he was making his way to Jerusalem, he went on through the country between Samaria and Galilee.
17:12 He was entering a certain village when ten men with skin disease came to meet him. They kept their distance
17:13 and raised their voices: "Jesus, Master, show us mercy!"
17:14 Seeing them, he told them, "Go and let the priests see you." And while they were going, they were made clean.
17:15 One of them saw that he had been healed. He turned back, giving glory to God at the top of his voice,
17:16 and dropped face down at Jesus' feet, giving him thanks. The man was a Samaritan.
17:17 Jesus responded, "Weren't ten made clean? And the nine, where are they?
17:18 Were none of them found coming back to give glory to God, except this foreigner?"
17:19 And he told him, "Get up and go on your way. Your faith has saved you."
## The day of the Son of Man
17:20 Some Pharisees asked him when the kingdom of God was coming. He answered them, "The kingdom of God does not come so that people can watch closely for it.
17:21 People will not say, 'See, it's here!' or 'Over there!' Because, see, the kingdom of God is among you."
17:22 He said to the disciples, "Days will come when you will long for one of the days of the Son of Man, just to see it, and you will not.
17:23 People will tell you, 'See, he's there!' 'See, he's here!' Don't go off, and don't run after them.
17:24 When lightning flashes, it shines from one side under the sky to the other side under the sky. The Son of Man will be like that in his day.
17:25 But first he has to suffer much and be thrown out by this generation.
17:26 "What happened in Noah's days will happen again in the days of the Son of Man.
17:27 They were eating and drinking. Men were taking wives and parents were giving their daughters in marriage, right up to the day Noah went into the ark. Then the flood came and wiped them all out.
17:28 Likewise in the days of Lot: they ate, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they built.
17:29 But on the day Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulfur from heaven and wiped them all out.
17:30 The day the Son of Man is unveiled will go the same way.
17:31 On that day, whoever is up on the roof, with his things in the house, must not go down to get them. And whoever is out in the field, likewise, must not go back for what is behind.
17:32 Remember Lot's wife!
17:33 Anyone who tries to hold on to his life will lose it, but anyone who loses it will keep it alive.
17:34 I tell you, on that night there will be two in one bed. One will be taken along, and the other will be left behind.
17:35 There will be two women grinding grain together. One will be taken along, and the other will be left behind."
17:36 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
17:37 They responded by asking him, "Where, Lord?" He told them, "Where the body is, that is where the vultures will flock together too."
