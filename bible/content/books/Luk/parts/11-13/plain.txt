## Lord, teach us to pray
11:1 Once Jesus was in a certain place praying. When he stopped, one of his disciples said to him, "Lord, teach us to pray, the way John also taught his disciples."
11:2 He said to them, "When you pray, say: 'Father, may your name be honored as holy. May your kingdom come.
11:3 Keep giving us each day our bread for the coming day.
11:4 And forgive us our sins, for we ourselves also forgive everyone who owes us. And do not bring us into testing.'"
## The friend at midnight
11:5 He said to them, "Which of you has a friend, and goes to him at midnight and tells him, 'Friend, lend me three loaves of bread.
11:6 A friend of mine has come to me off the road, and I have nothing to set before him.'
11:7 Would the one inside answer, 'Stop bothering me. The door is already locked, and my children are in bed with me. I can't get up and give you anything'?
11:8 I tell you, even if he will not get up and give him anything because he is his friend, he will get up because of his shamelessness and give him whatever he needs.
11:9 And I tell you: keep asking, and it will be given to you. Keep looking, and you will find. Keep knocking, and it will be opened for you.
11:10 All who ask receive, those who look find, and for the one who knocks it will be opened.
11:11 Is there a father among you who would hand his son a snake in place of a fish, when the boy asks for a fish?
11:12 Or if he asks for an egg, would he hand him a scorpion?
11:13 You are evil, and yet you know how to give your children good gifts. So think how much more the Father from heaven will give the Holy Spirit to the people who ask him!"
## By God's finger
11:14 Jesus was throwing out a demon that left a man unable to speak. Once the demon had gone out, the speechless man spoke, and the crowds were amazed.
11:15 But some of them said, "He throws out the demons with the help of Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons."
11:16 Others were putting him to the test and kept demanding from him a sign out of heaven.
11:17 But he knew what they were thinking, and he said to them, "Any kingdom split against itself is turned into ruins, and house falls on house.
11:18 And if Satan too is split against himself, how can his kingdom last? I say this because you claim that I throw out the demons with Beelzebul's help.
11:19 And if I throw demons out with Beelzebul's help, with whose help do your own sons throw them out? That is why they themselves will be your judges.
11:20 But if it is by God's finger that I throw out demons, then God's kingdom has already reached you.
11:21 As long as the strong man stands guard over his own courtyard in full armor, what he owns is left in peace.
11:22 But if a stronger man comes against him and beats him, he strips off the full armor the man relied on, and he hands out the loot.
11:23 Anyone who is not on my side is against me. Anyone who does not help me gather is scattering.
11:24 "When the unclean spirit leaves a person, it roams through dry country, searching for a place to rest. When it finds none, it says, 'I'll go back to my house, the one I came out of.'
11:25 It arrives and finds the place swept and tidied.
11:26 So it goes off and gathers seven more spirits, worse than itself, and they move in and make their home there. In the end that person is worse off than at the beginning."
## Who is blessed
11:27 As he was saying this, a woman lifted up her voice from the crowd and said to him, "Blessed is the womb that carried you, and the breasts you drank from!"
11:28 But he said, "Rather, blessed are the people who hear the word of God and keep it."
## The sign of Jonah
11:29 As the crowds kept packing in, he began to say, "This generation is an evil generation. It keeps hunting for a sign, and it will be given no sign except the sign of Jonah.
11:30 For Jonah became a sign for the people of Nineveh, and the Son of Man will be one for this generation in the same way.
11:31 At the judgment the queen from the south will be raised alongside the people of this generation and condemn them, because she came from the far edges of the earth to hear how wise Solomon was. And look, here is something greater than Solomon.
11:32 At the judgment the people of Nineveh will stand up alongside this generation and condemn it, because when Jonah preached they repented. And look, here is something greater than Jonah.
## The lamp of the body
11:33 "Nobody lights a lamp only to set it in a hidden corner or under the grain basket. It goes on the lampstand, so that those coming in may see its glow.
11:34 Your body's lamp is your eye. When your eye is sound, your whole body is full of light as well. But when it is evil, your body is full of darkness as well.
11:35 So check whether the light inside you is really darkness.
11:36 If, then, your whole body is bright, without a single dark part, it will be bright all through, just as when a lamp lights you up with its blaze."
## Woes at a Pharisee's table
11:37 As he was speaking, a Pharisee invited him to a midday meal at his house. Jesus went in and reclined for the meal.
11:38 The Pharisee noticed that Jesus had not washed himself first, before the meal, and it surprised him.
11:39 But the Lord said to him, "You Pharisees, now, wash the outside of the cup and the plate clean, but your own inside is filled with what you have grabbed and with wickedness.
11:40 Fools! The one who made the outside, didn't he make the inside as well?
11:41 Rather, give what is inside as alms. Then, look, everything is clean for you.
11:42 "But woe to you Pharisees! You pay a tithe on mint and rue and every garden plant, but you walk right past justice and the love of God. These you should have done, and not neglected the others.
11:43 Woe to you Pharisees! You love the front seat in the synagogues and being greeted in the market squares.
11:44 Woe to you! You are like graves that no one can see, and people walking on top of them do not know they are there."
11:45 One of the lawyers spoke up. "Teacher," he said, "by saying this you insult us too."
11:46 He said, "Woe to you lawyers as well! You weigh people down with loads too heavy to carry, and you yourselves won't put even one of your fingers to those loads.
11:47 Woe to you! You put up tombs for the prophets, but your fathers were the ones who killed them.
11:48 That makes you witnesses who agree with your fathers' deeds: they did the killing, and you do the building.
11:49 This is why God's Wisdom said too: 'I will send prophets and apostles to them. Some of them they will kill and persecute.'
11:50 The result will be that this generation is called to account for the blood of every prophet poured out since the world was founded,
11:51 from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah, who perished between the altar and the house. Yes, I tell you, this generation will be called to account for it.
11:52 Woe to you lawyers! You have carried off the key to knowledge. You did not go in yourselves, and you stood in the way of those who were going in."
11:53 As he left that place, the scribes and the Pharisees turned fiercely hostile and began to draw him out with questions about many things.
11:54 They were setting an ambush for him, to hunt down some word from his own mouth.
## The yeast of the Pharisees
12:1 Meanwhile the crowd had gathered by the tens of thousands, so many that they were trampling one another. Jesus began to speak to his disciples first: "Keep yourselves clear of the yeast of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.
12:2 Nothing is covered up that will not be uncovered, and nothing is secret that will not become known.
12:3 That means whatever you said in the dark will be heard in the daylight. What you spoke into someone's ear behind closed doors will be shouted from the rooftops.
## Whom to fear
12:4 "My friends, I tell you this: do not fear the people who kill the body. After that there is nothing more they can do.
12:5 I will point out to you the one you should fear. Fear the One who has authority, once the killing is done, to throw people into Gehenna. Yes, I tell you, he is the one to fear.
12:6 Five sparrows sell for two small coins, don't they? Yet in God's sight not one of them is forgotten.
12:7 Why, even the hairs on your head have all been counted. Stop being afraid. You matter more than a great many sparrows.
12:8 "I tell you, everyone who openly declares for me in front of people, the Son of Man too will declare for that person in front of God's angels.
12:9 But whoever denies me in front of people will be denied in front of God's angels.
12:10 Anyone who says something against the Son of Man will be forgiven. But the one who blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven.
12:11 When they haul you in before synagogues and rulers and authorities, do not worry about how or with what words to defend yourselves, or what to say.
12:12 When the hour comes, the Holy Spirit will teach you the words you must say."
## The rich fool
12:13 Someone from the crowd said to him, "Teacher, tell my brother to split our inheritance with me."
12:14 But Jesus answered him, "Man, who put me in charge as judge or divider over you?"
12:15 Then he told them, "Look out, and guard yourselves against every kind of greed. Even when someone has more than enough, his life does not come from the things he owns."
12:16 And he told them a parable. "There was a rich man whose fields bore a huge crop.
12:17 So he argued it out with himself. 'What am I going to do? There's nowhere for me to gather in my harvest.'
12:18 Then he said, 'Here's my plan. I'll knock down my barns and put up larger ones, and in them I'll gather all my wheat and my goods.
12:19 Then I'll tell my soul, "Soul, you have lots of good things stored up, enough for many years. Rest now. Eat, drink, celebrate."'
12:20 But God said to him, 'Fool! On this very night they demand your soul back from you. And what you have made ready, who will get it?'
12:21 So it goes for the person who piles up treasure for himself and is not rich toward God."
## Do not worry
12:22 Then he said to his disciples, "So I say to you: stop worrying about your life, what you will eat, or about your body, what you will put on.
12:23 Life means more than food, and the body more than clothing.
12:24 Think about the ravens. They do no sowing and no reaping. They have no storeroom and no barn, yet God feeds them. How much more you matter than the birds!
12:25 Who among you can add a cubit to the length of your life by worrying?
12:26 If you can't manage even the smallest thing, why are you worrying about everything else?
12:27 Think about the lilies, how they grow. They do no work, and they spin no thread. Yet I tell you, Solomon with all his glory never wore anything to match one of them.
12:28 God dresses the grass in the field like that, grass that is here today and tossed into the oven tomorrow. How much more will he clothe you, you people with so little faith!
12:29 As for you, do not keep seeking what you will eat and what you will drink, and do not be left up in the air.
12:30 The nations of the world go hunting for all these things. But your Father knows you need them.
12:31 Instead, seek his kingdom, and you will receive these things in addition.
12:32 "Stop being afraid, little flock. Your Father has delighted to give you the kingdom.
12:33 Sell your property and give alms. Make for yourselves pouches that never get old, an endless treasure in the heavens. There no thief comes close and no moth spoils anything.
12:34 Wherever your treasure is, that is where your heart will be.
## Ready for the master's return
12:35 "Have your robes tucked into your belts and your lamps lit.
12:36 Be like people watching for their master's return from the wedding celebration, ready to open up for him the moment he arrives and knocks.
12:37 Those slaves are blessed whose master finds them keeping watch when he comes. Amen, I tell you, he will tie up his own robe, have them recline for the meal, and come over and serve them.
12:38 He may come in the second watch of the night, or even the third. If he finds them like this, they are blessed.
12:39 You can be sure of this: if the master of the house had known at what hour the thief was coming, he would not have let him dig his way into his house.
12:40 You also need to be ready. The Son of Man comes at an hour you are not counting on."
12:41 Peter asked, "Lord, is this parable of yours meant for us, or for everyone as well?"
12:42 And the Lord said, "Who, then, is the manager who is faithful and sensible? His master will put him over his household staff, to hand out their portion of food when it is time.
12:43 That slave is blessed whose master finds him doing this when he comes.
12:44 I tell you truly, he will put him over everything he owns.
12:45 But what if that slave tells himself, 'My master's coming is delayed,' and he starts beating the serving men and women, and eating and drinking and getting drunk?
12:46 That slave's master will show up on a day he is not looking for him, at an hour he does not know. He will cut him in two and give him his share with the unfaithful.
12:47 The slave who knew what his master wanted, yet neither made ready nor acted on his master's wishes, will get a heavy beating.
12:48 But the slave who did not know, and did things that earned the lash, will get only a few strokes. When someone is given a lot, a lot will be looked for from him. And when people have put a lot in someone's care, they will ask him for even more.
## Fire and division
12:49 "I came to cast fire upon the earth, and how I wish it were already burning!
12:50 But there is a baptism I must be baptized with, and how hard pressed I am until it is carried out!
12:51 Do you imagine I have come to give peace on the earth? No, I tell you: division instead.
12:52 From now on a household of five will be split: three against two, and two against three.
12:53 They will be split, father against son and son against father, mother against daughter and daughter against mother, mother-in-law against her daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law."
## Reading the times
12:54 He was also saying to the crowds, "Whenever you see a cloud coming up over the west, you say right away, 'Rain is on its way,' and so it is.
12:55 When the wind is from the south, you say, 'A heat wave is coming,' and it comes.
12:56 Hypocrites! You can read the face of the earth and the sky. Why can't you read this moment?
12:57 "And why don't you decide on your own what is right?
12:58 As you go with the man who is suing you before a ruler, do your best while you are still on the road to be rid of his claim. Otherwise he may drag you off to the judge, the judge will turn you over to the bailiff, and the bailiff will throw you into prison.
12:59 I tell you, you will not come out of there until you have paid off even the last tiny coin."
## Repent or perish
13:1 Just then some people who were there told him about the Galileans. Pilate had mixed their blood with their sacrifices.
13:2 He answered them, "Because they suffered this, do you suppose these Galileans were greater sinners than every other Galilean?
13:3 Not at all, I tell you. If you do not repent, you will all perish the same way.
13:4 Or take the eighteen people the tower at Siloam fell on and killed. Do you suppose they were bigger debtors than everyone else who lives in Jerusalem?
13:5 Not at all, I tell you. If you do not repent, you will all perish just as they did."
## The fig tree given one more year
13:6 He went on to tell this parable. "Someone had a fig tree that had been planted in his vineyard. He came hoping to find fruit on it, and there was none.
13:7 He said to the man who worked the vineyard, 'See here: three years running I have come looking for fruit on this fig tree, and I find nothing. Chop it down. Why should it waste the ground as well?'
13:8 But the man answered, 'Master, give it this one year more. Let me dig around it and spread manure.
13:9 If it bears fruit in the future, good. If not, you can chop it down.'"
## A daughter of Abraham set free
13:10 One Sabbath Jesus was teaching in a synagogue.
13:11 A woman was there with a spirit of weakness that she had carried for eighteen years. She was bent forward and could not lift herself upright at all.
13:12 Jesus saw her and called out to her. "Woman," he said, "you have been released from your weakness."
13:13 Then he laid his hands on her. Instantly she was straight again, and she kept giving glory to God.
13:14 The synagogue leader was angry that Jesus had healed on the Sabbath. He kept telling the crowd, "Six days are given us when work must be done. Come during them and get your healing, and not on the Sabbath day."
13:15 The Lord replied to him, "Hypocrites! On the Sabbath doesn't every one of you set his ox or donkey loose from the manger and lead it off to drink?
13:16 Then this woman, a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan has kept tied up for eighteen years, think of it! Didn't she have to be untied from this bond on the Sabbath day?"
13:17 As he said this, everyone who opposed him was put to shame, and the whole crowd rejoiced at every glorious thing that was happening through him.
## Mustard seed and yeast
13:18 So he said, "What does the kingdom of God resemble? What can I liken it to?
13:19 It is like a seed of mustard. A man took it and threw it into his own garden. It grew and turned into a tree, and the birds of the sky settled in its branches."
13:20 Again he said, "What can I liken the kingdom of God to?
13:21 It is like yeast. A woman took it and hid it away in three measures of flour, until all of it had risen."
## The narrow door
13:22 He kept traveling from town to town and village to village, teaching, and pressing on with his journey to Jerusalem.
13:23 Someone said to him, "Lord, are those being saved few in number?" He told them,
13:24 "Fight your way in through the narrow door. I tell you, many will look for a way in and will not have the strength.
13:25 When the master of the house gets up and locks the door, you will find yourselves standing outside, knocking on the door and calling, 'Lord, open up for us!' And he will answer you, 'I don't know where you are from.'
13:26 Then you will begin to protest, 'We ate and drank in front of you! You taught in our town squares!'
13:27 And he will tell you, 'I don't know where you are from. Leave me, all you workers of wrong!'
13:28 There people will weep and grind their teeth, when you see Abraham, Isaac and Jacob and all the prophets in the kingdom of God, and yourselves being thrown out.
13:29 And people will come from the sunrise and the sunset, from the north and the south, and lie down to eat in the kingdom of God.
13:30 Listen: some now last will be first, and some now first will be last."
## That fox, and Jerusalem
13:31 At that very hour some Pharisees came up to him. "Leave," they said. "Go on away from here, because Herod wants to kill you."
13:32 He told them, "Go and say to this fox, 'Listen: today and tomorrow I am throwing out demons and finishing my healings, and on the third day I am brought to completion.'
13:33 All the same, today and tomorrow and the day after I have to travel on. It won't do for a prophet to perish outside Jerusalem.
13:34 Jerusalem, Jerusalem, killer of the prophets, stoner of those sent to her! How many times I wanted to gather your children close, the way a bird gathers her brood under her wings. And you would not.
13:35 Look, your house is abandoned to you. I tell you, you will never see me until the time comes when you say, 'Blessed is the one who comes in the Lord's name!'"
