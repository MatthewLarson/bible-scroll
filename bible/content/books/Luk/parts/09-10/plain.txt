## The Twelve sent out
9:1 He called the Twelve together and gave them power and authority over all the demons, and to cure diseases.
9:2 He sent them out to announce the kingdom of God and to heal the sick.
9:3 He told them, "Take nothing for the road: no staff, no bag, no bread, no money, and no one is to have two tunics.
9:4 Whatever house you go into, stay there, and set out again from there.
9:5 As for those who do not welcome you: when you leave that town, shake your feet free of its dust as evidence against them."
9:6 They went out and made their way through the villages, telling the good news and curing people everywhere.
## Herod wonders who Jesus is
9:7 Herod the tetrarch heard of everything that was taking place, and he could not make sense of it, because some people were saying, "John has been raised from the dead."
9:8 Others said, "Elijah has appeared," and still others, "One of the ancient prophets has risen."
9:9 But Herod said, "I beheaded John myself. So who is this man I keep hearing these things about?" And he kept looking for a chance to see him.
## Bread for five thousand
9:10 The apostles came back and gave him a full account of all they had done. He took them with him and slipped away privately to a town called Bethsaida.
9:11 The crowds found out, though, and followed him. He gave them a welcome and went on speaking to them about the kingdom of God, and those in need of treatment he healed.
9:12 The daylight was starting to fade. The Twelve came to him and said, "Dismiss the crowd, so they can go off to the villages and farms all around, put up for the night and find food, because here we are in a lonely spot."
9:13 But he said to them, "Feed them yourselves." "Five loaves and two fish are all we have," they said, "unless we ourselves go and buy food for every one of these people."
9:14 (The men there numbered about five thousand.) He told his disciples, "Have them recline in dining groups, about fifty to a group."
9:15 They did as he said and got everyone reclining.
9:16 He took the five loaves and the two fish, raised his eyes toward heaven and blessed them. He broke them and kept handing them to the disciples to set in front of the crowd.
9:17 Everyone ate until they were full, and the pieces they had left over were picked up: twelve baskets.
## Peter's confession
9:18 One day Jesus was praying by himself, and the disciples were with him. He put a question to them: "Who are the crowds saying I am?"
9:19 They answered, "John the Baptist. Others say Elijah. Still others say that one of the ancient prophets has risen."
9:20 He said to them, "But you yourselves, who do you say I am?" Peter gave the answer: "God's Messiah."
9:21 Then he rebuked them and ordered them to say this to no one.
9:22 He told them, "The Son of Man has to suffer much. He has to be thrown out by the elders, the chief priests and the scribes, and be killed, and on the third day be raised."
## The way of the cross
9:23 He said to them all, "Anyone who wishes to come behind me must say no to himself, pick up his cross every day and follow me.
9:24 For anyone set on saving his life will lose it. But anyone who loses his life because of me, that person will save it.
9:25 What good does it do a person to win the whole world, if he loses himself, or pays for it with himself?
9:26 Suppose anyone is ashamed of me and my words. Then the Son of Man will be ashamed of that person, when he comes in his own glory, and his Father's, and the holy angels'.
9:27 I tell you truly: among those standing right here are some who will never taste death before they see the kingdom of God."
## The transfiguration
9:28 Some eight days after these words, Jesus took Peter, John and James along and climbed the mountain to pray.
9:29 While he was praying, his face took on a different look, and his clothes turned white, flashing like lightning.
9:30 And look, there were two men in conversation with him. They were Moses and Elijah.
9:31 They appeared in glory and were speaking about his exodus, the one he was going to complete in Jerusalem.
9:32 Peter and the men with him had been weighed down with sleep. But they stayed awake, and they saw his glory and the two men who stood there with him.
9:33 As the men were parting from him, Peter said to Jesus, "Master, it's a fine thing that we are here! Let's put up three booths, one for you, one for Moses and one for Elijah." He had no idea what he was saying.
9:34 While he was still saying this, a cloud came and began to cast its shadow over them. Fear came over them as they went into the cloud.
9:35 A voice came out of the cloud: "This is my Son, the Chosen One. Listen to him!"
9:36 When the voice was over, Jesus was found to be alone. They said nothing. In those days they did not report to anyone any of the things they had seen.
## A boy set free
9:37 On the following day they came down from the mountain, and a big crowd came to meet him.
9:38 A man in the crowd shouted out, "Teacher, please, take a look at my son. He is the only child I have.
9:39 Look: a spirit takes hold of him, and all at once he cries out. It shakes him violently till he foams, and it will barely let go of him; it is crushing him.
9:40 I begged your disciples to throw it out, and they were not able."
9:41 Jesus replied, "Oh, faithless and warped generation! How long must I be with you and endure you? Bring your son here."
9:42 Even as the boy was coming, the demon dashed him down and shook him violently. Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit. He healed the boy and handed him back to his father.
9:43 All of them were stunned at God's grandeur. Amid everyone's wonder at all he was doing, he said to his disciples,
9:44 "You, put these words away in your ears: the Son of Man is about to be handed over into human hands."
9:45 The saying made no sense to them. It had been covered over from them, so that they could not perceive it, and they were too frightened to question him about the saying.
## Who ranks highest
9:46 A dispute came up among them: which of them would rank highest?
9:47 Jesus, who knew the debate going on in their hearts, took hold of a little child and had the child stand at his side.
9:48 He said to them, "Anyone who takes in this child for my name's sake is taking me in. And anyone who takes me in is taking in the one who sent me. For whoever is the smallest among all of you, that one is great."
## The man who was not one of us
9:49 John spoke up: "Master, we saw a man throwing out demons in your name. We kept trying to hinder him, since he does not follow along with us."
9:50 Jesus said to him, "Stop hindering him. Anyone who is not against you is on your side."
## The road to Jerusalem: a Samaritan village
9:51 As the days were coming to completion before he would be taken up, he himself fixed his face firmly on going to Jerusalem.
9:52 He sent messengers before his face. They went and entered a village of Samaritans to prepare for his coming.
9:53 But they would not receive him, since his face was going toward Jerusalem.
9:54 The disciples James and John saw it. "Lord," they said, "do you want us to order fire to come down out of the sky and consume them?"
9:55 He turned around and rebuked them.
9:56 So they traveled on to a different village.
## The cost of following
9:57 On the road, as they went along, someone said to him, "Wherever you go, I will follow you."
9:58 Jesus said to him, "Foxes have their holes, and the birds in the sky have places to roost, but the Son of Man does not have anywhere to rest his head."
9:59 He said to another man, "Follow me." But he said, "Lord, allow me first to go off and bury my father."
9:60 He told him, "Let the dead bury their own dead. But you, go off and spread the news of the kingdom of God."
9:61 Another one said, "Lord, I will follow you. But allow me first to say goodbye to those back at my house."
9:62 Jesus said, "Nobody who has set his hand on a plow and then looks at what lies behind him is suited to the kingdom of God."
## The seventy-two sent out
10:1 After these things the Lord named seventy-two others as well. He sent them out in pairs before his face, to every town and place he himself was going to come to.
10:2 He told them, "There is a big harvest, but there are only a few workers. So plead with the Lord of the harvest to send workers out into his harvest.
10:3 Go! Look, I am sending you like lambs into the middle of a pack of wolves.
10:4 Do not carry a money purse, or a bag, or sandals, and do not greet anybody along the way.
10:5 Whatever house you go into, say this first: 'Peace on this household!'
10:6 If anyone there is a son of peace, your peace will settle on him. Otherwise it will come back to you.
10:7 Stay in that same house, taking your food and drink from them, for a worker has earned his pay. Do not keep changing from one house to another.
10:8 And when you go into any town and they welcome you, eat what they put in front of you.
10:9 Cure those in it who are ill, and tell them, 'The kingdom of God has drawn near to you.'
10:10 But when you go into a town and they do not welcome you, go out into its squares and say,
10:11 'We are wiping off on you even the dust from your town that has stuck to our feet. All the same, understand this: the kingdom of God has drawn near.'
10:12 I tell you, on that day Sodom will find it easier to bear than that town.
## Woe to the towns of Galilee
10:13 "Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! Suppose the acts of power that happened in you had happened in Tyre and Sidon. They would have repented long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.
10:14 But at the judgment Tyre and Sidon will find it easier to bear than you will.
10:15 And you, Capernaum: will you be raised high, as far as heaven? You will be brought down as far as Hades!
10:16 "Whoever hears you hears me. Whoever sets you aside sets me aside, and whoever sets me aside sets aside the one who sent me."
## The seventy-two return
10:17 The seventy-two came back full of joy. "Lord," they said, "even the demons are under our command in your name!"
10:18 He said to them, "I was watching Satan fall like lightning out of heaven.
10:19 Look, I have given you the authority to walk on top of snakes and scorpions, and authority over every power the enemy has. Nothing at all will do you harm.
10:20 Still, do not be glad about this, that the spirits are under your command. Be glad that your names stand written in heaven."
## Jesus' joy and thanks
10:21 At that very hour he burst into joy in the Holy Spirit and said, "Father, Lord of heaven and earth, I openly thank you. You hid these things from wise and clever people, and you uncovered them for little children. Yes, Father, because that is what seemed good in your sight.
10:22 Everything has been handed over to me by my Father. Only the Father knows who the Son is. And only the Son knows who the Father is, along with anyone the Son wants to show him to."
10:23 Turning to the disciples, he said to them by themselves, "How blessed are the eyes that are seeing what you see!
10:24 For I tell you, many prophets and kings wanted to see what you are seeing, and they did not see it. They wanted to hear what you are hearing, and they did not hear it."
## The great commandment
10:25 Just then a lawyer stood up and put a question to him as a test: "Teacher, what should I do so that I will inherit eternal life?"
10:26 He said to him, "In the law, what is written? How do you read it?"
10:27 He answered, "'Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your being, with all your strength and with all your mind,' and 'your neighbor as yourself.'"
10:28 "That is the right answer," Jesus told him. "Keep doing this, and you will live."
## The good Samaritan
10:29 He wished to justify himself, though, and said to Jesus, "And just who is my neighbor?"
10:30 Jesus took the question up. "A certain man was on his way down from Jerusalem to Jericho, and he ran into bandits. They stripped him, laid blows on him and went off, leaving him half dead.
10:31 By coincidence a priest was going down by that road. He saw the man and went past him on the far side.
10:32 In the same way a Levite too came to the spot, saw him and went past on the far side.
10:33 But a Samaritan on a journey came upon him, and at the sight of him his heart went out to him.
10:34 He came close, poured oil and wine on his wounds and bandaged them. He lifted him up onto his own mount, led him to an inn and looked after him.
10:35 In the morning he pulled out two denarii and handed them to the innkeeper. 'Look after him,' he told him. 'Anything extra you spend, I myself will repay you on my way back.'
10:36 In your view, which of these three became a neighbor to the man who ran into the bandits?"
10:37 "The one who acted with mercy toward him," he said. Jesus told him, "Go on your way, and do the same yourself."
## Martha and Mary
10:38 As they traveled on, he went into a village, where a woman named Martha took him in as her guest.
10:39 This woman had a sister named Mary. Mary sat down at Jesus' feet and kept on listening to his word.
10:40 Martha, meanwhile, was being pulled every which way by all the serving there was to do. She stepped up and said, "Lord, does it not matter to you that my sister has left me to do the serving on my own? So tell her to lend me a hand."
10:41 The Lord answered her, "Martha, Martha, you are worried and in a turmoil over many things.
10:42 There is need of only one thing. For Mary has picked out the good share, and it will not be taken from her."
