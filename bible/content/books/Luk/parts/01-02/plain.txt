## A word to Theophilus
1:1 Many have already set their hand to arranging an account of the events that have been carried through to completion among us,
1:2 following what was passed on to us by the people who saw it with their own eyes from the beginning and became servants of the message.
1:3 So I too have decided to write it out for you in its proper order, most excellent Theophilus, since I have kept careful track of all these things from the start.
1:4 I do this so that you may recognize how sure and firm the teaching you received really is.
## The angel and the priest
1:5 Back when Herod was king of Judea, there was a certain priest named Zechariah, of Abijah's division. His wife was one of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.
1:6 The two of them were righteous in God's eyes. They walked in all the Lord's commands and rules, and no one could fault them.
1:7 Yet no child had been born to them, since Elizabeth was barren, and the two of them were well along in their days.
1:8 Now it happened that Zechariah was doing his work as a priest before God, when his division's turn came round.
1:9 As the priests' custom was, lots were cast, and it fell to him to go into the Lord's sanctuary and burn the incense.
1:10 At the incense hour the whole crowd of the people was outside, praying.
1:11 Then an angel of the Lord showed himself to him, standing to the right of the incense altar.
1:12 Seeing him, Zechariah was shaken, and fear came down on him.
1:13 But the angel said to him, "Stop being afraid, Zechariah. Your plea has been heard. Elizabeth your wife will give birth to a son for you. Name him John.
1:14 Joy will be yours, and gladness, and many will rejoice that he was born,
1:15 because in the Lord's eyes he will be great. Wine and strong drink he must never touch, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit starting in his mother's womb.
1:16 He will turn many Israelites back to the Lord their God.
1:17 He himself will walk ahead of the Lord with Elijah's spirit and power. He will turn fathers' hearts back to their children, and bring the disobedient around to the good sense of the righteous. So he will have a people fitted out and ready for the Lord."
1:18 "How am I to know this?" Zechariah asked the angel. "I am an old man, and my wife has lived many years too."
1:19 The angel answered, "I am Gabriel, the one who stands before God. I was sent to talk with you and to give you the good news of these things.
1:20 Look, you will go silent and lose the power to speak until the day all this happens. That is because you did not trust my words. They will be fulfilled when their season comes."
1:21 The people kept looking for Zechariah to come out. They were surprised that he was spending so much time in the sanctuary.
1:22 He did come out, but he could not speak to them. They understood that he had seen a vision inside the sanctuary. He kept motioning to them with his hands, and he stayed mute.
1:23 Once the days of his priestly service were complete, he left for his own house.
1:24 Some time after this, Elizabeth his wife conceived. She hid herself away for five months, and she kept saying,
1:25 "This is how the Lord has treated me in these days. He has looked my way, to take away my disgrace among people."
## The angel and Mary
1:26 Then, in the sixth month, God sent Gabriel the angel to a Galilean town by the name of Nazareth,
1:27 to a virgin who was promised in marriage to a man named Joseph, of David's house. The virgin was called Mary.
1:28 The angel went in to her and said, "Greetings, you who have been shown favor! The Lord is with you."
1:29 His words shook her deeply. She kept turning over in her mind what sort of greeting this was.
1:30 "Mary, stop being afraid," the angel told her. "You have found favor with God.
1:31 Look, you will become pregnant and have a son. Name him Jesus.
1:32 He will be great, and he will be called Son of the Most High. The Lord God will hand him the throne of David his father,
1:33 and he will be king over Jacob's house for all the ages. His kingdom will have no end."
1:34 "How is this going to happen," Mary asked the angel, "since I do not know a man?"
1:35 The angel answered, "The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the Most High's power will cast its shadow over you. For that reason the one being born will be called holy, God's Son.
1:36 Look at Elizabeth, your relative. She too has a son in her womb, old as she is. People called her barren, yet this is her sixth month.
1:37 Because no word that comes from God will be powerless."
1:38 Mary said, "Here is the Lord's slave. May it be done to me just as you have said." And the angel went away from her.
## Mary visits Elizabeth
1:39 During those days Mary set out and traveled in a hurry into the hills, to a town of Judah.
1:40 There she entered Zechariah's house and said her greeting to Elizabeth.
1:41 As soon as Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby in her womb leaped. Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit,
1:42 and she called out with a great cry, "Blessed are you among women, and the fruit of your womb is blessed!
1:43 How does this happen to me, that my Lord's mother comes to me?
1:44 Listen: as your greeting sounded in my ears, the baby inside me leaped with gladness.
1:45 Blessed is the woman who believed that what the Lord told her would be carried out."
## Mary's song
1:46 And Mary said: / My soul declares how great the Lord is,
1:47 and my spirit has found its gladness in God my Savior,
1:48 for he has looked with care on the lowliness of his slave. / Yes, from this time on, every generation will count me blessed,
1:49 since the Mighty One has worked great things for me, / and his name is holy.
1:50 His mercy is for generation after generation / of those who fear him.
1:51 He has used the strength of his arm; / the arrogant, with all their heart's schemes, he has scattered.
1:52 He has pulled rulers down from thrones / and raised the lowly high.
1:53 The hungry he has loaded with good things; / the rich he has sent off with empty hands.
1:54 He has taken hold of Israel, his servant, to help him, / and so remembered mercy,
1:55 as he told our fathers he would, / to Abraham and his offspring for all time.
1:56 Mary remained with her for around three months; then she went back to her own house.
## John is born
1:57 The time for Elizabeth's delivery arrived, and she gave birth to a son.
1:58 Those who lived around her and her relatives heard how the Lord had made his mercy great toward her, and they were glad along with her.
1:59 When the eighth day came, they arrived to circumcise the boy, and they started calling him Zechariah, his father's name.
1:60 His mother answered, "Not that. His name will be John."
1:61 They told her, "Not one person in your family goes by that name."
1:62 So they motioned to his father, asking what he wanted him to be named.
1:63 He asked for a small tablet and wrote these words: "John is his name." They were all astonished.
1:64 That very moment his mouth opened, and his tongue too, and he started talking, blessing God.
1:65 All who lived around them were seized by fear, and in every part of Judea's hill country these events were the talk of the people.
1:66 All who heard stored it away in their hearts. "So what will this child turn out to be?" they said. And indeed the hand of the Lord was with him.
## Zechariah's song
1:67 Then Zechariah, the boy's father, was filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke as a prophet. He said:
1:68 Blessed is the Lord, the God of Israel, / for he has visited his people / and worked their redemption.
1:69 In the house of David his servant / he has raised up for us a horn of salvation,
1:70 just as he spoke from the earliest ages / through the mouths of his holy prophets:
1:71 salvation from our enemies, / out of the grip of all who hate us,
1:72 dealing in mercy with our fathers, / calling to mind his holy covenant,
1:73 the oath he swore to Abraham our father: / to grant us
1:74 that, pulled out of our enemies' grip, / we would worship him with nothing to fear,
1:75 in holiness and righteousness in his presence / all the days we have.
1:76 And you, little child, will be called a prophet of the Most High, / for you will walk ahead of the Lord / to make his roads ready,
1:77 to let his people know salvation / by having their sins forgiven,
1:78 all from the heartfelt mercy of our God. / Through it the Dawn from on high will visit us,
1:79 to bring light to people sitting in the dark, in death's shadow, / and to steer our feet onto the road of peace.
1:80 As for the boy, he kept growing and getting stronger in spirit. He stayed in the wilderness until the day he was presented to Israel.
## Jesus is born in Bethlehem
2:1 It was in those days that an order came out from Caesar Augustus: the whole inhabited world was to be registered.
2:2 (It was the first such registration, carried out while Quirinius governed Syria.)
2:3 So all the people were on the move to get registered, each one going to his own town.
2:4 Joseph too made the trip up out of Galilee, leaving the town of Nazareth for Judea. He went to David's town, which is called Bethlehem, since he belonged to David's house and clan.
2:5 He went to be put on the register along with Mary, the woman promised to him in marriage, who was expecting a child.
2:6 While they were staying there, her days were completed, and the birth was due.
2:7 She gave birth to her son, her firstborn. She bound him snugly in strips of cloth and laid him down in a manger, since there was no space for them in the guest room.
## Shepherds and angels
2:8 In that same countryside some shepherds were staying out in the open, taking turns guarding their flock through the night.
2:9 An angel of the Lord came and stood by them, and the Lord's glory blazed all around them. They were overcome with great fear.
2:10 The angel told them, "Stop being afraid. Listen, I am telling you good news of a great joy that will belong to the whole people.
2:11 For today a Savior was born for you in David's town. He is Messiah and Lord.
2:12 Here is your sign: you will find a newborn bound snugly in strips of cloth, lying in a manger."
2:13 All at once a crowd of heaven's army appeared alongside the angel, praising God with these words:
2:14 Glory to God in the heights above, / and on earth peace among people he delights in!
2:15 As soon as the angels had left them and gone back into heaven, the shepherds kept saying to one another, "Let's go on over to Bethlehem, then, and see this word that has come true, which the Lord has made known to us."
2:16 They came in a hurry and tracked down Mary, Joseph and the newborn, who was lying in the manger.
2:17 Having seen him, they passed on the message they had been given about this child.
2:18 Everyone who listened marveled at what the shepherds were telling them.
2:19 Mary, though, held on to every one of these words, turning them over in her heart.
2:20 The shepherds headed back, giving glory and praise to God for all they had heard and seen. It had all been just as they were told.
## Named and presented in the temple
2:21 Eight days went by, and it was time to circumcise him. He was named Jesus, as the angel had called him before his mother conceived him.
2:22 When the days of their purifying under the law of Moses were complete, they took him up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord.
2:23 This followed what stands written in the law of the Lord: "Every male that opens a womb will be called holy, belonging to the Lord."
2:24 They also came to give a sacrifice, as the law of the Lord says: "a pair of turtledoves or two young doves."
## Simeon's song
2:25 Look, there was a man in Jerusalem by the name of Simeon. He was righteous and careful to honor God, and he was looking forward to the comforting of Israel. The Holy Spirit rested on him.
2:26 The Holy Spirit had made known to him that he would not see death until his eyes had seen the Lord's Messiah.
2:27 In the Spirit he came into the temple. The parents were bringing in the little boy Jesus, to carry out for him the law's custom,
2:28 and Simeon received him into his arms. He blessed God and said:
2:29 Master, now you are setting your slave free / to go in peace, just as you said,
2:30 because with my own eyes I have seen your salvation,
2:31 which you made ready in front of all the peoples:
2:32 a light to bring revelation to the nations, / and glory to Israel your people.
2:33 The boy's father and his mother stood there wondering at the things being said about him.
2:34 Simeon blessed them, and to Mary, the child's mother, he said, "Listen. This one is set here for many in Israel to fall and to rise, and to be a sign people speak against.
2:35 And as for you, a sword will run right through your soul. All this is so that what many people think in their hearts will be brought into the open."
## Anna
2:36 A prophetess was there too: Anna, Phanuel's daughter, from Asher's tribe. She had lived a great many years. From the time she was a young bride, she had seven years with her husband,
2:37 and then she was a widow until the age of eighty-four. She did not leave the temple; night and day she served God by fasting and praying.
2:38 At that same hour she stepped up and began thanking God. She kept talking about the boy to everyone who was looking for Jerusalem to be redeemed.
2:39 Once they had carried out all that the law of the Lord called for, they went back to Galilee, to Nazareth, their own town.
2:40 The boy kept growing and getting stronger. He was being filled with wisdom, and God's grace rested on him.
## The boy in his Father's house
2:41 Year by year his parents would travel to Jerusalem for the Passover festival.
2:42 When he turned twelve, they made the trip up, as the festival's custom was.
2:43 When the days were over and they were heading home, the boy Jesus stayed on in Jerusalem, and his parents did not realize it.
2:44 They assumed he was somewhere in the traveling group, so they went a full day's distance before they started searching for him among their relatives and the people they knew.
2:45 Not finding him, they turned back to Jerusalem, searching for him all the way.
2:46 Three days later they found him in the temple, sitting in the middle of the teachers, hearing what they said and putting questions to them.
2:47 All his listeners were astounded at how much he understood and at the answers he gave.
2:48 Seeing him there, they were stunned. "Child," his mother said, "why did you do this to us? Look, your father and I have been in agony, looking for you."
2:49 "Why is it that you were looking for me?" he said to them. "Didn't you know that I must be at my Father's?"
2:50 They did not grasp the meaning of what he told them.
2:51 He went down with them, came to Nazareth, and lived under their authority. His mother kept all these words close in her heart.
2:52 And Jesus kept moving forward in wisdom and in maturity, and in favor with God and with people.
