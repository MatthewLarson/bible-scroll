## The widow and the judge
18:1 He told them a parable about how they needed to pray at all times and not grow weary.
18:2 "There was a judge in one town," he said, "a man who did not fear God and had no regard for people.
18:3 A widow lived in the same town, and she kept coming to him. 'Defend my rights against my opponent at law,' she would say.
18:4 For some time he would not. But afterward he said to himself, 'I may have no fear of God and no regard for people,
18:5 yet this widow is giving me so much trouble that I will see she gets justice, so that she does not finally come and give me a black eye.'"
18:6 Then the Lord said, "Pay attention to what the crooked judge says.
18:7 Will God, then, not get justice for his chosen, who call out to him day and night? Is he slow to act for them?
18:8 I tell you, he will give them justice without delay. But the Son of Man, when he comes, will he find faith on the earth at all?"
## The Pharisee and the tax collector
18:9 Some people felt sure in themselves that they were righteous, and they counted everyone else as nothing. He told this parable to them as well.
18:10 "Two men went up into the temple to pray. One of them was a Pharisee; the other was a tax collector.
18:11 The Pharisee took his stand and prayed these words to himself: 'God, I thank you that I am not like the rest of humanity, the swindlers, the crooks, the adulterers, or even like this tax collector.
18:12 Twice a week I fast. On everything I acquire, I pay the tithe.'
18:13 The tax collector stood a long way back and would not even raise his eyes toward heaven. He kept striking his chest and saying, 'God, be gracious to me, the sinner.'
18:14 I tell you, it was this man, not the other, who went down to his home justified. Everyone who raises himself up will be brought low, and the one who brings himself low will be raised up."
## Jesus and the children
18:15 People kept bringing even their babies to him, so that he would touch them. The disciples saw it and began to scold them.
18:16 But Jesus called the children to him. "Let the children come to me," he said. "Do not stop them. The kingdom of God is for people like them.
18:17 Amen, I tell you: anyone who does not welcome the kingdom of God the way a child does will not get into it at all."
## The rich leader
18:18 A leader put a question to him: "Good teacher, what should I do so that I will inherit eternal life?"
18:19 Jesus said to him, "Why do you call me good? No one is good except one: God.
18:20 You know the commandments: 'You must not commit adultery. You must not murder. You must not steal. You must not give false testimony. Honor your father and mother.'"
18:21 He said, "I have obeyed every one of these ever since I was young."
18:22 When Jesus heard that, he said to him, "One thing is still missing for you. Sell everything you have and share it out among the poor. You will have treasure in the heavens. And come, follow me."
18:23 At these words he grew deeply sad. He was a very rich man.
18:24 Jesus looked at him and said, "With what difficulty do people who have money get into the kingdom of God!
18:25 Indeed, a camel could get through a needle's eye more easily than a rich person could get into the kingdom of God."
18:26 The people who heard it said, "Who can be saved, then?"
18:27 He said, "What is impossible from the human side is possible with God."
18:28 Peter said, "Look at us. We left what was ours and followed you."
18:29 He said to them, "Amen, I tell you: no one has left home, wife, brothers, parents or children because of the kingdom of God
18:30 without getting back many times as much in this present time, and eternal life in the age that is coming."
## Jesus speaks of his death a third time
18:31 He took the Twelve aside and said to them, "Listen. We are on our way up to Jerusalem, and everything the prophets wrote about the Son of Man will be carried out.
18:32 He will be handed over to the Gentiles. They will mock him, insult him and spit on him.
18:33 They will whip him and kill him, and on the third day he will rise."
18:34 But they took in none of it. What he said was kept hidden from them, and they could not grasp the things he was telling them.
## A blind man near Jericho
18:35 When he was getting close to Jericho, a certain blind man sat beside the road and begged.
18:36 Hearing a crowd pass along, he kept asking what it could be.
18:37 They told him, "Jesus the Nazarene is going by."
18:38 And he shouted, "Jesus, Son of David, show me mercy!"
18:39 The people walking in front sternly told him to hush. But he cried out all the more, "Son of David, show me mercy!"
18:40 Jesus came to a stop and ordered the man brought to him. When he came near, Jesus asked him,
18:41 "What would you like me to do for you?" He said, "Lord, I want to see again."
18:42 Jesus said to him, "See again. Your faith has saved you."
18:43 At once he could see again, and he began following Jesus, giving glory to God. And all the people saw it and gave praise to God.
## Zacchaeus
19:1 He went into Jericho and was passing through the town.
19:2 And look: there was a man whose name was Zacchaeus. He was the head of the tax collectors, and a rich man.
19:3 He kept trying to get a look at Jesus, to see who he was. But the crowd was in the way, and he was a short man.
19:4 He raced on ahead and climbed up into a fig-mulberry tree to get a view of him, for Jesus would soon be passing along that road.
19:5 When Jesus reached the spot, he looked up and said to him, "Zacchaeus, be quick and come down. Today I have to stay in your house."
19:6 He was quick to come down, and he welcomed Jesus, full of joy.
19:7 Everyone saw it, and they all started grumbling. "He has gone to stay at a sinful man's house!" they said.
19:8 But Zacchaeus stood there and said to the Lord, "Look, Lord: half of what I own I give to the poor. And if I have squeezed anything out of anyone by false charges, I pay it back four times over."
19:9 Jesus said to him, "Today salvation has come to this household, for he too is a son of Abraham.
19:10 The Son of Man came to look for what was lost and to save it."
## The ten minas
19:11 They were still listening to these words when he added a parable. The reason was that he was close to Jerusalem, and they supposed the kingdom of God was about to show itself at any moment.
19:12 So he said, "A man from a noble family set out for a distant land, to have the kingship given to him and then come back.
19:13 He called in ten of his own slaves and gave them ten minas. 'Trade with these until I come back,' he told them.
19:14 But his fellow citizens hated him, and they sent envoys behind him with a message: 'We do not want this man to rule as king over us.'
19:15 He received the kingship and came back. Then he gave orders to call the slaves he had given the silver to, so that he could find out what they had made by trading.
19:16 The first one came up and said, 'Master, your one mina has made ten minas besides.'
19:17 'Well done, good slave!' he said to him. 'You were faithful over a very little, so you will have authority over ten towns.'
19:18 The second came and said, 'Your mina, master, has made five minas.'
19:19 To this one too he said, 'And you are to be over five towns.'
19:20 Then the other one came and said, 'Master, here is your mina. I have kept it stored away, wrapped in a cloth.
19:21 I was afraid of you. You are an exacting man: you pick up what you never set down, and you harvest what you never planted.'
19:22 'Out of your own mouth I will judge you, you evil slave,' he told him. 'So you knew I was an exacting man, who picks up what he never set down and harvests what he never planted?
19:23 Then why did you not put my silver on the bankers' table? When I came back, I would have collected it with interest.'
19:24 Then he said to those standing by, 'Take the mina away from him and give it to the one who has the ten minas.'
19:25 'Master,' they said to him, 'he has ten minas!'
19:26 'I tell you, everyone who has something will be given more. But the one who has nothing will lose even what he has.
19:27 But these enemies of mine, the ones who refused to have me as their king: bring them here and slaughter them in front of me.'"
## The king comes to Jerusalem
19:28 With these words he walked on in front, climbing the road up to Jerusalem.
19:29 When he was getting close to Bethphage and Bethany, by the Mount of Olives, as it is called, he sent off two of the disciples
19:30 with these words: "Go to the village facing you. As you go into it you will find a colt tied up, one that no person has ever yet sat on. Set it loose and bring it.
19:31 Should anyone ask you, 'Why are you setting it loose?' say this: 'The Lord has need of it.'"
19:32 The ones he sent went off and found it just as he had told them.
19:33 As they were setting the colt loose, its owners said to them, "Why are you setting the colt loose?"
19:34 They said, "The Lord has need of it."
19:35 They led it to Jesus, threw their cloaks over the colt, and set Jesus on it.
19:36 As he moved on, they kept spreading their own cloaks under him on the road.
19:37 Now he was coming near, already at the slope that runs down from the Mount of Olives, and the whole throng of disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the acts of power they had seen.
19:38 "Blessed is the King who comes in the Lord's name!" they said. "In heaven, peace! And glory in the heights above!"
19:39 Some Pharisees from the crowd said to him, "Teacher, scold your disciples!"
19:40 He answered, "I tell you, if these fall silent, the stones will shout it out."
## Jesus weeps over Jerusalem
19:41 When he got near and caught sight of the city, he broke into tears over it.
19:42 "If only you too had recognized on this day what leads to peace!" he said. "As it is, it has been hidden from your sight.
19:43 Days are coming on you when your enemies will put up a palisade of stakes against you. They will ring you round and press in on you from every side.
19:44 They will smash you flat, you and your children inside you, and they will not leave stone on stone within you. For you did not recognize the season when you were visited."
## Jesus in the temple
19:45 He went into the temple and began to throw out the people who were selling,
19:46 and he said to them, "Scripture says, 'My house will be a house of prayer.' But you have made it a cave for bandits."
19:47 Day after day he was teaching in the temple. The chief priests and the scribes, and the leading men of the people too, were looking for a way to destroy him.
19:48 But they could not see what they could do, because the people, every one of them, hung on him as they listened.
## By what authority?
20:1 One day, as he was teaching the people in the temple and telling the good news, up came the chief priests and the scribes, together with the elders.
20:2 They spoke to him: "Tell us, on what authority do you do these things? Or who is it that handed you this authority?"
20:3 He answered them, "I too have a question to put to you. Tell me:
20:4 The baptism of John: was it from heaven, or from human beings?"
20:5 They talked it over among themselves. "Suppose we say, 'From heaven.' He will say, 'Why did you not believe him?'
20:6 But suppose we say, 'From human beings.' The whole people will stone us, for they are convinced that John was a prophet."
20:7 So they answered that they did not know where it came from.
20:8 And Jesus told them, "Then I am not telling you either on what authority I do these things."
## The tenants of the vineyard
20:9 Then he began to tell the people this parable: "A man planted a vineyard. He leased it out to farmers and left the country for a long time.
20:10 When the season came, he sent a slave to the farmers, so that they would give him a share of the vineyard's fruit. But the farmers beat him and sent him away with nothing.
20:11 He went on to send another slave. That one too they beat and humiliated, and sent him away with nothing.
20:12 He went on to send a third. This one too they wounded and threw out.
20:13 Then the vineyard's master said, 'What am I to do? I will send my dearly loved son. Perhaps they will respect him.'
20:14 But when the farmers saw him, they talked it over with one another. 'Here is the heir,' they said. 'Let us kill him, so that the inheritance will be ours.'
20:15 So they cast him outside the vineyard and killed him. What, then, will the vineyard's master do to them?
20:16 He will come and destroy these farmers, and give the vineyard to others." When they heard it, they said, "That must never happen!"
20:17 Jesus looked straight at them and said, "Then what does this Scripture mean: 'A stone that the builders threw out has become the head of the corner'?
20:18 Anyone who falls on that stone will be shattered, and anyone it falls on, it will scatter like chaff."
20:19 The scribes and the chief priests tried to lay hands on him that very hour, but they feared the people. They could tell he had aimed this parable at them.
## Taxes for Caesar
20:20 So they watched him closely and sent agents who acted as if they were righteous men, to seize on something he said, so that they could hand him over to the governor's rule and authority.
20:21 They asked him, "Teacher, we know that what you say and teach is right, and you do not play favorites. You teach God's way truthfully.
20:22 Is it permitted for us to pay tribute to Caesar, or not?"
20:23 But he saw through their trickery and said to them,
20:24 "Show me a denarius. Whose image and name does it have on it?" "Caesar's," they said.
20:25 He said to them, "Then pay back to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God."
20:26 They could not seize on anything he said in front of the people. Amazed at his answer, they fell silent.
## A question about the resurrection
20:27 Then some of the Sadducees came to him, the ones who deny there is any resurrection, and they put a question to him:
20:28 "Teacher, Moses wrote down for us: if someone's brother dies, leaving a wife, and he is childless, then his brother must take the wife and raise up descendants for his brother.
20:29 Now, there were seven brothers. The first took a wife and died childless.
20:30 Then the second,
20:31 and the third took her, and so did all seven. They left no children and died.
20:32 Last, the woman died too.
20:33 So at the resurrection, which of them will the woman be wife to? All seven had her as their wife."
20:34 Jesus said to them, "The children of this age take wives and are given as wives.
20:35 But those who are found worthy to reach that age and the resurrection from the dead neither take wives nor are given as wives.
20:36 For they can never die again. They are the equals of angels, and they are children of God, being children of the resurrection.
20:37 That the dead are raised, even Moses made known, in the passage about the bush, where he calls the Lord 'the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob.'
20:38 He is not God of dead people but of living ones, for to him all are alive."
20:39 Some of the scribes responded, "That was well said, Teacher."
20:40 For they no longer dared to question him about anything.
## Whose son is the Messiah?
20:41 Then he said to them, "In what way do people say the Messiah is David's son?
20:42 David himself says in the book of Psalms, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand,
20:43 until I set your enemies down as a footstool for your feet."'
20:44 David calls him 'Lord,' then. So in what sense is he his son?"
## Beware of the scribes
20:45 With all the people listening, he said to his disciples,
20:46 "Watch out for the scribes. They like to go about in long robes, and they love being greeted in the market squares and having the front seats in the synagogues and the top places at dinners.
20:47 They eat up widows' houses, and for show they make long prayers. These men will receive a heavier sentence."
## The widow's offering
21:1 He looked up and saw rich people dropping their gifts into the treasury.
21:2 He also saw a widow, a needy one, drop in two lepta.
21:3 And he said, "I tell you truly, this poor widow has dropped in more than all of them.
21:4 All these people put their gifts into the offerings out of what they had left over. But she, out of her lack, dropped in her whole living, all she had."
## The temple will fall
21:5 Some people were talking about the temple, how it was decked out with fine stones and dedicated gifts. He said,
21:6 "As for these things you are looking at, days will come when not one stone will be left on a stone. Every one will be knocked down."
## The start of the troubles
21:7 They asked him, "Teacher, when, then, will this happen? And what sign will show that these things are about to take place?"
21:8 He said, "Be on your guard, so that you are not led astray. Many will come using my name. They will say, 'I am he,' and, 'The time has drawn near.' Do not go after them.
21:9 When you hear of wars and uprisings, do not be terrified. These things have to happen first, but the end will not come right away."
21:10 Then he told them, "One nation will rise up against another, and one kingdom against another.
21:11 There will be great earthquakes, and famines and plagues in place after place. There will be terrors, and great signs from the sky.
21:12 "Ahead of all these things, though, people will lay hands on you and persecute you. They will hand you over to synagogues and prisons, and drag you off before kings and governors because of my name.
21:13 This will turn out for you as a chance to testify.
21:14 So fix it firmly in your hearts that you will not practice your defense in advance.
21:15 I myself will give you a mouth and wisdom, and all who oppose you will be unable to stand against it or talk back to it.
21:16 You will be handed over even by parents, brothers, relatives and friends, and they will have some of you executed.
21:17 Everyone will hate you because of my name.
21:18 Yet not one hair from your head will be lost.
21:19 Win your lives by holding out.
## Jerusalem surrounded
21:20 "When you see Jerusalem being encircled by army camps, then know that her desolation has drawn near.
21:21 Then those who are in Judea must run for the hills. Those inside the city must get out, and those out in the country must not go into it,
21:22 for these are days of retribution, so that all that is written may be fulfilled.
21:23 In those days, woe to women who are expecting, and to mothers nursing babies! For there will be great distress on the land, and wrath on this people.
21:24 They will be cut down by the sword and carried off as prisoners into all the nations. And Jerusalem will be trampled by Gentiles until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled.
## The Son of Man comes
21:25 "And there will be signs in the sun, the moon and the stars. On the earth the nations will be gripped with dread, not knowing where to turn at the roar of the sea and the heaving waves.
21:26 People will faint with fear and dread of what is about to overtake the inhabited world, for the powers in the heavens will be shaken.
21:27 And then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud, with power and much glory.
21:28 As these things start to happen, stand tall and raise your heads, for your redemption is getting close."
## The lesson of the fig tree
21:29 Then he told them a parable. "See the fig tree, and every tree.
21:30 When they are already putting out leaves, you see it for yourselves and know that summer is already close.
21:31 In the same way, when you see these things happening, know that the kingdom of God is near.
21:32 Amen, I tell you, this generation will not be gone before it all happens.
21:33 Heaven and earth will be gone, but my words will never be gone.
## Stay alert
21:34 "Keep watch over yourselves, so that your hearts do not grow heavy with wild partying and drinking and the worries of making a living, and that day does not spring on you without warning,
21:35 like a snare. For it will burst in on everyone settled on the face of all the earth.
21:36 Stay alert all the time. Keep praying for the strength to get away from all these things that are about to happen, and to take your stand before the Son of Man."
21:37 During the days he taught in the temple. At night he went out and camped on the Mount of Olives, as it is called.
21:38 And all the people would get up at dawn to come to him in the temple and listen to him.
