## Lord over the Sabbath
6:1 It happened that on a Sabbath he was passing through fields of grain. His disciples were pulling off heads of grain, rubbing them in their hands and eating them.
6:2 Some of the Pharisees said, "Why are you doing something on the Sabbath that is not allowed?"
6:3 Jesus answered them, "Have you not even read about what David did, when he and the men with him were hungry?
6:4 He went into God's house, took the Presence Bread and ate it, and gave some to the men with him. Yet no one is allowed to eat it except the priests alone."
6:5 Then he told them, "The Son of Man is Lord over the Sabbath."
## A withered hand on the Sabbath
6:6 It happened on another Sabbath that he entered the synagogue and taught. There was a man there whose right hand had withered.
6:7 The scribes and the Pharisees were keeping a close eye on him. Would he heal on the Sabbath? They wanted to find a way to bring a charge against him.
6:8 He knew their thoughts, though. He told the man with the withered hand, "Get up and stand in the middle." He rose and stood.
6:9 Then Jesus said to them, "I ask you: is it allowed on the Sabbath to do something good, or something bad? To save a life, or to destroy it?"
6:10 His eyes swept over them all, and he said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." He did, and his hand was made well again.
6:11 But they were filled with mindless fury. They began talking it over among themselves: what could they do to Jesus?
## The twelve apostles
6:12 It was during those days that he went off to the mountain to pray. He spent the whole night there in prayer to God.
6:13 At daybreak he summoned his disciples. From among them he picked out twelve, and he gave them the name apostles:
6:14 Simon, to whom he also gave the name Peter, and Andrew his brother; James and John; Philip and Bartholomew;
6:15 Matthew and Thomas; James son of Alphaeus, and Simon, who was called the Zealot;
6:16 Judas son of James, and Judas Iscariot, the one who turned traitor.
## Crowds on the level ground
6:17 Coming down the mountain with them, he stopped on a stretch of flat ground. A big crowd of his disciples was with him. A great mass of people had come too, from the whole of Judea and Jerusalem and from the coast around Tyre and Sidon. They came to listen to him and to have their diseases healed.
6:18 People tormented by unclean spirits were being cured as well.
6:19 The whole crowd kept trying to touch him, because power was going out of him and healing everyone.
## Blessings and woes
6:20 He raised his eyes toward his disciples and said: "Blessed are the poor, because the kingdom of God is yours.
6:21 Blessed are those who are hungry now, because you will be given your fill. Blessed are those who weep now, because you will laugh.
6:22 You are blessed when people hate you. You are blessed when they cut you off, heap insults on you, and toss out your name as something evil because of the Son of Man.
6:23 Be glad on that day and leap for joy, because, look, your reward in heaven is great. Their fathers did the very same things to the prophets.
6:24 "But woe to you who are rich, because your comfort is already paid to you in full.
6:25 Woe to you who have your fill now, because you will be hungry. Woe, you who are laughing now, because you will grieve and weep.
6:26 Woe, when all people speak well of you! Their fathers did the very same things to the false prophets.
## Love your enemies
6:27 "But I tell you who are listening: keep loving your enemies. Treat well the people who hate you.
6:28 Bless the ones who curse you. Pray for the ones who abuse you.
6:29 When someone hits you on the cheek, offer him the other one too. If someone takes away your outer robe, do not hinder him from taking your tunic too.
6:30 Keep giving to anyone who asks you. When someone carries off your things, do not ask for them back.
6:31 Just as you want people to do for you, do the same for them.
6:32 "If you love the people who love you, what thanks do you have coming for that? Even sinners love the people who love them.
6:33 And if you do good to the people who do good to you, what thanks do you have coming? Sinners do the same.
6:34 And if you lend to people you expect to get something back from, what thanks do you have coming? Sinners, too, lend to sinners, so as to get back as much as they lent.
6:35 Instead, keep loving your enemies, doing good, and lending without hoping for anything back. A great reward will be yours, and you will be sons of the Most High, since he himself is kind to people who are ungrateful and evil.
6:36 Become compassionate, the way your Father is compassionate.
## Judging others
6:37 "Do not judge, and you will never be judged. Do not condemn, and you will never be condemned. Release, and you will be released.
6:38 Keep giving, and gifts will come to you. They will put a good measure into the fold of your robe, packed down, shaken and overflowing. For the measure you use is the one that will be used to measure out to you."
6:39 He gave them a parable as well: "Is a blind man able to guide a blind man? Won't both of them fall into a ditch?
6:40 A disciple does not rank above the teacher. But every disciple who is fully equipped will be like his teacher.
6:41 Why do you stare at the splinter in your brother's eye, while you do not notice the beam in your own eye?
6:42 How can you tell your brother, 'Brother, let me pull out the splinter in your eye,' when you cannot even see the beam in your own eye? Hypocrite! Get the beam out of your own eye first. Then you will see well enough to pull out the splinter in your brother's eye.
## Trees and their fruit
6:43 "No good tree produces rotten fruit, and on the other hand, no rotten tree produces good fruit.
6:44 You can tell every tree by its own fruit. No one gathers figs from thorns or harvests grapes off a bramble.
6:45 A good person brings good out of the good storeroom of his heart, and an evil person brings evil out of evil. For what overflows from the heart is what his mouth speaks.
## Two builders
6:46 "Why do you call me 'Lord, Lord,' and not do what I say?
6:47 Everyone who comes to me, listens to my words and acts on them: I will show you whom he resembles.
6:48 He is like a man who was building a house. He dug, went deep, and set the foundation on bedrock. A flood came, and the river burst against that house, but it did not have the strength to shake it, because the house was well built.
6:49 But the one who has listened and not acted is like someone who put up a house on bare ground, without a foundation. The river burst against it, and it caved in at once. That house fell, and it was a great wreck."
## A centurion's faith
7:1 Jesus completed all he had to say in the people's hearing. Then he went into Capernaum.
7:2 There was a centurion whose slave was ill and close to death. The centurion prized him highly.
7:3 He had heard about Jesus, so he sent some Jewish elders to him. They were to ask him to come and make his slave completely well.
7:4 They came to Jesus and begged him hard. "He is a man who is worthy of this favor from you," they said.
7:5 "He loves our nation. In fact, he built the synagogue for us himself."
7:6 Jesus set out with them. He was already close to the house when the centurion sent some friends with a message for him: "Lord, do not put yourself to trouble. I am not good enough for you to come in under my roof.
7:7 So I did not count myself worthy even to come to you. Instead, say a word, and let my servant get well.
7:8 I am a man placed under someone's authority myself, and I have soldiers under me. I say 'Go!' to this one, and off he goes. I say 'Come!' to another, and he comes. I say to my slave, 'Do this,' and he does it."
7:9 Jesus was amazed at him when he heard these words. He turned to the crowd that was following him and said, "I tell you, I have not met faith this great even in Israel."
7:10 The messengers went back to the house and found the slave healthy.
## A widow's son at Nain
7:11 It happened a little later that he traveled to a town named Nain, with his disciples and a big crowd going along with him.
7:12 Just as he got near the town's gate, look, a dead man was being carried out. He was his mother's only son, and she was a widow. A good-sized crowd of townspeople was with her.
7:13 At the sight of her, the Lord's heart went out to her, and he said to her, "Stop weeping."
7:14 He came up and laid his hand on the open coffin, and the bearers halted. "Young man," he said, "I tell you, get up."
7:15 The man who had been dead sat up and started speaking, and Jesus gave him to his mother.
7:16 Fear took hold of them all, and they gave glory to God. "A great prophet has been raised up among us!" they said. "God has visited his people!"
7:17 This account of him spread all over Judea and the whole region around it.
## John the Baptist sends a question
7:18 John's disciples brought him a report of all these events.
7:19 So John summoned two of his disciples and sent them to the Lord to ask, "Are you the Coming One, or should we wait for someone else?"
7:20 The men came to him and said, "We have been sent to you by John the Baptist. He asks, 'Are you the Coming One, or should we wait for someone else?'"
7:21 In that very hour he cured many people of diseases, torments and evil spirits, and to many blind people he granted the gift of sight.
7:22 He answered them, "Go and give John a report of what you have seen and heard. The blind see again, the lame walk, people with skin disease are made clean, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, the poor are told the good news.
7:23 And blessed is anyone who is not tripped up because of me."
## What Jesus says about John
7:24 When John's messengers had gone, Jesus began talking to the crowds about John. "When you went out into the wilderness, what did you go to gaze at? A reed that the wind shakes?
7:25 No? So what was it you went out to see? A man wrapped in soft garments? Look, the people who wear fine clothes and live pampered lives are in the royal palaces.
7:26 What did you go out for, then? To see a prophet? Yes, I tell you, you saw one who is even more than a prophet.
7:27 It is about him that it is written: 'Look, I am sending my messenger before your face. He will make your road ready ahead of you.'
7:28 I tell you, no one born of a woman is greater than John. But the smallest one in the kingdom of God is greater than he is."
7:29 When all the people heard him, even the tax collectors, they declared that God was in the right, for they had been baptized with John's baptism.
7:30 But the Pharisees and the lawyers set aside God's plan for themselves, since they had not let him baptize them.
7:31 "What, then, shall I liken the people of this generation to? What are they like?
7:32 They are like little children who sit in the town square and shout to one another, 'We piped a tune for you, but you would not dance. We wailed a funeral song, but you would not weep.'
7:33 For John the Baptist has come not eating bread and not drinking wine, and you say, 'There's a demon in him.'
7:34 The Son of Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, 'Look at the man! He overeats and he is a heavy wine drinker, a friend to tax collectors and sinners!'
7:35 And wisdom is proved right by all her children."
## Forgiven much, loving much
7:36 A Pharisee asked Jesus to dine with him. Jesus entered the Pharisee's house and lay down at the table.
7:37 Now look: a woman of that town, a sinner, found out that he was lying at the table in the Pharisee's house. She brought an alabaster jar of perfumed oil.
7:38 She took her place behind him, by his feet, crying, and her tears started to soak his feet. She dried them with the hair of her head, kept kissing his feet, and anointed them with the perfumed oil.
7:39 The Pharisee who had invited him saw it and thought to himself, "This man, if he really were a prophet, would know this woman who keeps touching him: who she is, and what she is like. She's a sinner!"
7:40 Jesus spoke up and said to him, "Simon, there is something I have to say to you." "Teacher, say it," he replied.
7:41 "There were two men in debt to a certain lender. One owed five hundred denarii, the other fifty.
7:42 Since they had nothing to pay with, he freely canceled the debts of both. So which of them will love him more?"
7:43 Simon answered, "I take it the one who had more canceled." Jesus told him, "Your judgment is right."
7:44 Then he turned to the woman, though he spoke to Simon: "You see this woman? When I came into your house, you offered me no water for my feet. She, though, has soaked my feet with her tears and dried them with her hair.
7:45 You did not greet me with a kiss. Yet ever since I came in, she has kept on kissing my feet.
7:46 My head you did not anoint with ordinary oil. But she has anointed my feet with perfume.
7:47 So I tell you, her sins, many as they are, have been forgiven, for she has loved much. But the person forgiven only a little loves only a little."
7:48 Then he said to her, "Your sins have been forgiven."
7:49 The others lying at the table began saying to themselves, "Who is this man, that he even forgives sins?"
7:50 But he said to the woman, "Your faith has saved you. Go in peace."
## Women who traveled with him
8:1 It happened soon afterward that he went on a journey through one town and village after another. He was preaching and telling the good news of the kingdom of God. With him were the Twelve,
8:2 and certain women, cured now of evil spirits and sicknesses: Mary, known as Magdalene, out of whom seven demons had gone;
8:3 Joanna, the wife of Chuza, Herod's steward; Susanna; and many other women. They were serving them out of what they owned.
## The sower
8:4 A big crowd was coming together, as people from one town after another made their way to him. He told them a parable:
8:5 "There was a sower who went out to scatter his seed. While he was scattering it, some seed landed beside the road. It was trampled, and the birds of the sky gobbled it up.
8:6 Other seed fell on the rock. When it came up, it dried out, for lack of moisture.
8:7 Other seed landed in among the thornbushes. The thornbushes came up with it and choked it.
8:8 And other seed landed on the good earth. It sprang up and bore fruit, a hundred times as much." Saying this, he raised his voice: "Let anyone who has ears to hear listen."
## Why Jesus speaks in parables
8:9 His disciples questioned him: what could this parable mean?
8:10 He said, "You have been given the gift of knowing the mysteries of the kingdom of God. But for the rest it comes in parables, so that 'though they look, they may not see, and though they listen, they may not understand.'
## The sower explained
8:11 "This is what the parable means. The seed is the word of God.
8:12 The ones beside the road are people who have heard. But then the devil comes and carries the word off from their hearts, to keep them from believing and being saved.
8:13 The ones on the rock are people who gladly welcome the word whenever they hear it. Yet there is no root in them. They believe for a season, and in a season of testing they turn away.
8:14 What fell into the thornbushes: these are people who have heard. But as they travel on, they are choked by worries and wealth and the pleasures of life, and they bring nothing to ripeness.
8:15 And what fell on the good earth: these are people who hear the word with a fine and good heart, hold it fast, and bear fruit by endurance.
## A lamp on a lampstand
8:16 "Nobody lights a lamp only to cover it with a pot or set it beneath a bed. He sets it on a lampstand, so that those coming in may see its light.
8:17 There is nothing hidden that will not become plain to see, and nothing secret that will not become known and come out into the open.
8:18 So watch how you listen. For anyone who has something will be given more. But anyone who does not have will lose even what he seems to have."
## Jesus' true family
8:19 Then his mother and his brothers arrived, but the crowd kept them from reaching him.
8:20 He was told, "Your mother and your brothers have been standing outside, wanting to see you."
8:21 But he answered them, "The people who hear the word of God and do it: they are my mother and my brothers."
## Jesus calms a storm
8:22 It happened one day that he climbed into a boat, and his disciples with him. He told them, "Let's cross over to the far shore of the lake," and they put out from land.
8:23 While they were sailing, he dropped off to sleep. A gale of wind swept down onto the lake. They were taking on water, and their lives were at risk.
8:24 They came and woke him up, crying, "Master, Master, we are going down!" He got up and spoke sternly to the wind and the surging water. They died down, and a calm came.
8:25 He said to them, "Where is your faith?" Frightened and astonished, they said to one another, "Who can this be, then? He gives orders even to the winds and the water, and they do what he says!"
## The man called Legion
8:26 They sailed down to the land of the Gerasenes, which is across from Galilee.
8:27 When he stepped out on land, a man from the town came to meet him, a man who had demons. He had gone without clothes for a long time. He stayed among the tombs, not in a house.
8:28 At the sight of Jesus he shrieked and dropped down in front of him. With a loud voice he said, "What is there between me and you, Jesus, Son of God Most High? I beg you, do not torment me!"
8:29 For Jesus had been ordering the unclean spirit to leave the man. Over and over it had taken hold of him. People put him under guard and fastened him with chains and shackles, but he would rip the restraints apart. Then the demon would drive him out into the deserted places.
8:30 Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" He said, "Legion," for a great many demons had entered him.
8:31 They kept pleading with him not to order them off into the Abyss.
8:32 There a big herd of pigs was grazing on the hill. The demons pleaded with him to let them enter those pigs, and he let them.
8:33 Leaving the man, the demons went into the pigs, and the herd charged down the cliff into the lake and drowned.
8:34 The herdsmen saw what had happened. They ran away and spread the news in the town and around the farms.
8:35 People came out to look at what had taken place. They came to Jesus and found the man the demons had left sitting at Jesus' feet, with clothes on and his mind sound. And fear took hold of them.
8:36 Those who had seen it told them how the man who had had demons had been saved.
8:37 The whole population of the Gerasene countryside asked him to go away from them, because a great fear had them in its grip. So he climbed into a boat and turned back.
8:38 The man out of whom the demons had gone kept pleading with Jesus to let him stay with him. But Jesus sent him off and said,
8:39 "Go back to your home, and tell all that God has done for you." So he went away, announcing throughout the whole town all that Jesus had done for him.
## Jairus' daughter and a woman who touched his cloak
8:40 On Jesus' return the crowd welcomed him. They had all been watching for him.
8:41 Just then a man came whose name was Jairus. He was a leader of the synagogue. He dropped down at Jesus' feet and kept pleading with him to come into his house.
8:42 His daughter was dying: his only daughter, about twelve years old. As he went, the crowds pressed in until they all but choked him.
8:43 One woman had had a flow of blood for twelve years. No one had been able to cure her, though she had poured her whole livelihood into doctors.
8:44 She came up from behind and touched the tassel of his cloak, and at once her flow of blood stopped.
8:45 Jesus said, "Who is the one who touched me?" They all said it wasn't them, and Peter said, "Master, the crowds are hemming you in and crushing against you."
8:46 But Jesus said, "Someone did touch me. I could tell that power had gone out of me."
8:47 The woman realized she had not escaped notice. She came trembling and threw herself down before him. Then, with all the people listening, she explained the reason she had touched him, and how she had been healed on the spot.
8:48 He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has saved you. Go in peace."
8:49 He was still talking when someone came from the synagogue leader's house. "Your daughter has died," he said. "Do not bother the teacher any longer."
8:50 Jesus heard this and answered him, "Stop being afraid. Just believe, and she will be saved."
8:51 Arriving at the house, he let no one go in with him but Peter, John and James, and the child's father and mother.
8:52 They were all weeping and beating their chests for her. But he said, "Stop weeping. She has not died. She is asleep."
8:53 They kept laughing at him, since they knew she had died.
8:54 But he took hold of her hand and called out, "Child, get up."
8:55 Her spirit came back, and she rose at once. He gave orders for her to be given something to eat.
8:56 Her parents were beside themselves. But he instructed them to tell no one what had taken place.
