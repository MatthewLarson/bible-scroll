## A family leaves Bethlehem
1:1 In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. So a man from Bethlehem in Judah went to live as a foreigner in the fields of Moab, he and his wife and his two sons.
1:2 The man was named Elimelech, his wife was named Naomi, and his two sons were named Mahlon and Chilion. They were Ephrathites, from Bethlehem in Judah. They came into the fields of Moab and remained there.
1:3 Then Elimelech, the husband of Naomi, died, and she was left, she and her two sons.
1:4 They married Moabite women. One was named Orpah, and the other was named Ruth. They lived there about ten years.
1:5 And the two of them, Mahlon and Chilion, died too, and the woman was left behind, without her two boys and without her husband.
## The road back
1:6 Then she got up with her daughters-in-law to go back from the fields of Moab, because she had heard in the fields of Moab that the LORD had come to help his people by giving them bread.
1:7 She left the place where she had been living, and her two daughters-in-law went with her. They set out on the road to go back to the land of Judah.
1:8 Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, "Go on, return, each woman to her mother's house. May the LORD deal with you in faithful love, the way you have dealt with the dead and with me.
1:9 May the LORD grant that each of you finds rest in the house of a husband." Then she kissed them, and they lifted up their voices and wept.
1:10 They said to her, "No, we will go back with you to your people."
1:11 But Naomi said, "Turn back, my daughters. Why go with me? Are there still sons inside me who could become husbands for you?
1:12 Go back, my daughters, go. I am too old to have a husband. Even if I said, 'There is hope for me,' even if I had a husband tonight and then gave birth to sons,
1:13 would you wait for them until they grew up? Would you shut yourselves away and not marry for their sake? No, my daughters. It is far more bitter for me than for you, because the hand of the LORD has gone out against me."
1:14 Again they lifted up their voices and wept. And Orpah kissed her mother-in-law, but Ruth clung to her.
## Where you go, I will go
1:15 Naomi said, "Look, your sister-in-law has gone back to her people and to her gods. Go back after your sister-in-law."
1:16 But Ruth said, "Don't press me to abandon you, to turn back from following you. For where you go, I will go, and where you spend the night, I will spend the night. Your people are my people, and your God is my God.
1:17 Where you die, I will die, and there I will be buried. Thus may the LORD do to me, and more besides, if anything but death comes between me and you."
1:18 Naomi saw that Ruth was set on going with her, and she said no more to her.
## A bitter homecoming
1:19 So the two of them went on until they came to Bethlehem. When they came to Bethlehem, the whole town was stirred up because of them. The women said, "Can this be Naomi?"
1:20 She said to them, "Don't call me Naomi. Call me Mara, because the Almighty has made my life very bitter.
1:21 Full I went out, and empty the LORD has brought me back. Why call me Naomi, when the LORD has testified against me and the Almighty has done me harm?"
1:22 So Naomi came back, and with her came Ruth the Moabite, her daughter-in-law, who came back from the fields of Moab. They arrived in Bethlehem at the beginning of the barley harvest.
## Ruth goes out to glean
2:1 Now Naomi had a relative on her husband's side, a man of great worth from Elimelech's clan. His name was Boaz.
2:2 Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, "Please let me go to the field and glean among the ears of grain behind someone in whose eyes I may find favor." Naomi said to her, "Go, my daughter."
2:3 So she went. She came and gleaned in the field behind the harvesters. As chance would have it, she happened to come to the part of the field that belonged to Boaz, who was from Elimelech's clan.
2:4 And look, Boaz was just coming from Bethlehem. He said to the harvesters, "The LORD be with you!" They answered him, "The LORD bless you!"
2:5 Boaz asked his young man who was in charge of the harvesters, "Whose young woman is this?"
2:6 The young man in charge of the harvesters answered, "She is a young Moabite woman, the one who came back with Naomi from the fields of Moab.
2:7 She said, 'Let me glean, please, and gather among the sheaves behind the harvesters.' So she came, and she has stood here from the morning until now. She has sat in the shelter only a short while."
## Boaz meets Ruth
2:8 Then Boaz said to Ruth, "Listen, my daughter. Don't go to glean in another field, and don't leave this one. Stick close to my young women here.
2:9 Keep your eyes on the field they are harvesting, and follow behind them. I have ordered the young men not to touch you. When you are thirsty, go to the jars and drink from what the young men have drawn."
2:10 She dropped face down and bowed to the earth. "Why," she asked him, "have I found favor in your eyes, for you to notice me? I am a foreigner."
2:11 Boaz answered her, "I have been told everything you have done for your mother-in-law since your husband died. You left your father and your mother and the land where you were born, and you came to a people you did not know before.
2:12 May the LORD pay you back for your work, and may your wages be complete from the LORD, the God of Israel, under whose wings you have come for refuge."
2:13 She said, "May I keep finding favor in your eyes, my lord, because you have comforted me and spoken to the heart of your servant girl, though I am not even like one of your servant girls."
## Bread, roasted grain and a full day's gleaning
2:14 At mealtime Boaz said to her, "Come over here. Eat some of the bread and dip your piece in the vinegar." So she sat beside the harvesters, and he handed her some roasted grain. She ate until she was full, and she had some left over.
2:15 When she got up to glean, Boaz gave orders to his young men: "Let her glean even among the sheaves, and don't shame her.
2:16 Even pull out some stalks from the bundles for her and leave them for her to glean. Don't scold her."
2:17 She went on gleaning in the field until evening, then beat out her gleanings, and there was about an ephah of barley.
2:18 She picked it up and went into the town, and her mother-in-law saw what she had gleaned. Then Ruth brought out what she had left over after eating her fill, and gave it to her.
## "One of our redeemers"
2:19 Her mother-in-law said to her, "Where did you glean today? Where did you work? Blessed be the man who noticed you!" So she told her mother-in-law about the man she had worked with. She said, "The name of the man I worked with today is Boaz."
2:20 Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, "May he be blessed by the LORD, who has not abandoned his faithful love for the living and the dead!" Naomi also said to her, "The man is a close relative of ours. He is one of our family redeemers."
2:21 Then Ruth the Moabite said, "He even said to me, 'Stick close to the young men who work for me until they finish all of my harvest.'"
2:22 Naomi said to Ruth, her daughter-in-law, "It is good, my daughter, that you go out with his young women, so that no one harms you in another field."
2:23 So she stuck close to Boaz's young women to glean until the end of the barley harvest and the wheat harvest. And she lived with her mother-in-law.
## Naomi's plan
3:1 Then Naomi her mother-in-law said to her, "My daughter, shouldn't I look for a place of rest for you, where things will go well for you?
3:2 Now, isn't Boaz our relative, the one whose young women you have been with? Look, tonight he is winnowing barley at the threshing floor.
3:3 So bathe, rub on scented oil, put your cloak on, and go down to the threshing floor. Don't make yourself known to the man until he has finished eating and drinking.
3:4 And when he lies down, mark the spot where he sleeps. Go in, uncover his feet, and lie down there. He will tell you what to do."
3:5 Ruth said to her, "I will do everything you tell me."
## Midnight at the threshing floor
3:6 So she went down to the threshing floor and carried out all that her mother-in-law had commanded.
3:7 Boaz ate and drank, and he was in a good mood. He went to lie down at the far end of the grain pile. Then she came quietly, uncovered his feet, and lay down.
3:8 In the middle of the night the man was startled. He turned over, and there was a woman lying at his feet!
3:9 He said, "Who are you?" She said, "I am Ruth, your servant. Spread the wing of your cloak over your servant, because you are a family redeemer."
3:10 He said, "May the LORD bless you, my daughter! This last act of faithful love is even greater than the first, because you have not gone after the young men, whether poor or rich.
3:11 Now, my daughter, don't be afraid. I will do for you everything you ask, because the whole town knows that you are a woman of worth.
3:12 As for now: yes, I am indeed a family redeemer. But there is a redeemer nearer than I am, too.
3:13 Spend the night here. In the morning, if he will redeem you, good, let him redeem. But if he is not willing to redeem you, then I will redeem you myself, as the LORD lives. Lie down until morning."
3:14 She lay at his feet till morning, then rose before anyone could tell one person from another. He said, "It must not be known that the woman came to the threshing floor."
3:15 Then he said, "Hand over the shawl you have on, and hold it open." She held it, and he measured out six measures of barley and laid it on her. Then he went into the town.
3:16 She came to her mother-in-law, who said, "Who are you, my daughter?" Then Ruth told her everything the man had done for her.
3:17 She said, "These six measures of barley he gave me, for he said to me, 'Do not come to your mother-in-law empty.'"
3:18 Naomi said, "Sit still, my daughter, until you find out how the matter turns out. The man will not rest until he has settled the matter today."
## At the town gate
4:1 Meanwhile Boaz went up to the town gate and sat down there. And look, the family redeemer Boaz had spoken about was passing by. Boaz said, "Come over here and sit down, So-and-so." So he came over and sat down.
4:2 Then Boaz took ten men from the elders of the town and said, "Sit down here." So they sat down.
4:3 Then he said to the family redeemer, "There is the piece of field that was our brother Elimelech's. Naomi, who came back from the fields of Moab, is selling it.
4:4 I thought I should uncover your ear about it and say, 'Buy it in front of those sitting here and in front of the elders of my people.' If you will redeem it, redeem it. But if you will not redeem it, tell me so that I will know. No one has the right to redeem it except you, and I am next after you." He said, "I will redeem it."
4:5 Then Boaz said, "On the day you buy the field from Naomi, you also acquire Ruth the Moabite, the dead man's wife, in order to raise up the dead man's name on his inheritance."
4:6 The family redeemer said, "I cannot redeem it for myself, or I will damage my own inheritance. You redeem it for yourself with my right of redemption, because I cannot redeem it."
## The sandal
4:7 Now this was the custom in Israel in earlier times for redeeming and exchanging, to make any deal final: a man pulled off his sandal and gave it to the other man. This was how a deal was witnessed in Israel.
4:8 So the family redeemer said to Boaz, "Buy it for yourself," and he pulled off his sandal.
4:9 Then Boaz told the elders and all the people, "Today you are witnesses that from Naomi's hand I am acquiring everything of Elimelech's and everything of Chilion's and Mahlon's.
4:10 I am also acquiring Ruth the Moabite, Mahlon's wife, to be my wife, to raise up the dead man's name on his inheritance, so that the dead man's name will not be cut off from among his relatives or from the gate of his hometown. You are witnesses today."
4:11 All the people at the gate and the elders said, "We are witnesses. May the LORD make the woman who is coming into your house like Rachel and Leah, the two who built the house of Israel. May you do worthy things in Ephrathah and make a name in Bethlehem.
4:12 Through the children the LORD will give you by this young woman, may your house be like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah."
## A son for Naomi
4:13 So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife. He went in to her, the LORD let her become pregnant, and she bore a son.
4:14 The women said to Naomi, "Blessed be the LORD, who has not let you lack a family redeemer this day! May his name be proclaimed in Israel!
4:15 He will bring back your life and take care of you when your hair is gray. Your daughter-in-law who loves you has given birth to him, and she is worth more to you than seven sons."
4:16 Then Naomi took the child, laid him against her chest, and became his nurse.
4:17 The women who lived nearby gave him a name. "A son is born to Naomi!" they said, and they called him Obed. He was the father of Jesse, the father of David.
## The family line of David
4:18 Now these are the generations of Perez: Perez fathered Hezron,
4:19 Hezron fathered Ram, Ram fathered Amminadab,
4:20 Amminadab fathered Nahshon, Nahshon fathered Salmon,
4:21 Salmon fathered Boaz, Boaz fathered Obed,
4:22 Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David.
