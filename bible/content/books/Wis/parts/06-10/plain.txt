## Kings, learn wisdom
6:1 Listen, then, you kings, and understand. / Learn, you who judge the ends of the earth.
6:2 Pay attention, you who rule over multitudes / and take pride in your crowds of nations.
6:3 For your power was given to you by the Lord, / and your rule came from the Most High. / He will examine what you do / and probe what you plan.
6:4 You were servants of his kingdom, yet you did not judge rightly. / You did not keep the law / or walk by the plan of God.
6:5 He will come on you swiftly and terribly, / for judgment is harsh for those who stand high.
6:6 The smallest is one whom mercy forgives, / but the powerful will be powerfully examined.
6:7 The Master of all will not draw back before anyone's face / or show regard for greatness. / Small and great alike are his own making, / and he cares for all of them the same.
6:8 But a severe search stands ready for the strong.
6:9 My words, then, are for you who rule alone, / so you can learn wisdom and keep from falling.
6:10 Those who keep holy things in a holy way will be made holy, / and whoever learns them will have an answer ready.
6:11 Desire my words, then. / Yearn for them, and they will teach you.
## Wisdom goes looking
6:12 Wisdom shines and never fades. / Those who love her catch sight of her easily.
6:13 She gets there first, making herself known / to anyone who longs for her.
6:14 Whoever gets up early to look for her will not wear himself out, / for he will find her sitting at his gates.
6:15 To think about her is understanding made complete, / and whoever loses sleep over her will soon be free of worry.
6:16 For she goes around looking for those worthy of her. / She shows herself to them kindly on the roads / and comes to meet them in all their thinking.
## The steps to a kingdom
6:17 The very start of her is a true longing for instruction.
6:18 Care for instruction is love. / Love is keeping her laws. / Paying attention to her laws is the guarantee of incorruption,
6:19 and incorruption puts a person close to God.
6:20 Longing for wisdom, then, lifts a person to a kingdom.
6:21 If thrones and scepters are your delight, you who rule peoples alone, / give wisdom honor, and you will reign for all time.
## I will tell you what she is
6:22 I will report what Wisdom is and how she came into being. / I will not hide mysteries from you. / I will track her from the first moment she came to be / and bring the knowledge of her out into the open. / I will not skip past the truth.
6:23 I will not travel along with envy that eats a person away, / for envy has no share in wisdom.
6:24 A great number of wise people is the world's safety, / and a king with good sense keeps a people steady.
6:25 So let my words instruct you, and you will be the better for it.
## Born like everyone else
7:1 I too am a mortal, the equal of everyone, / descended from the first-formed man, who was made from the earth. / In my mother's womb I was carved into flesh
7:2 over a time of ten months, set firm in blood / from a man's seed and the pleasure that goes with sleep.
7:3 When I was born, I too drew in the air we all share / and fell onto the same earth that all of us endure. / The first noise I made was crying, just as it is for all.
7:4 I was brought up wrapped in swaddling bands and surrounded by cares.
7:5 Not one king began life any other way.
7:6 Everyone enters life by one door and leaves it by the same.
## Wisdom above all riches
7:7 So I prayed, and I was given understanding. / I called for help, and a spirit of wisdom came to me.
7:8 I chose her over scepters and thrones / and thought wealth worth nothing next to her.
7:9 I did not compare a priceless gem to her, / for all the gold there is, set beside her, is a little sand, / and next to her silver is reckoned as mud.
7:10 Health and good looks I loved less than her. / I would rather have her than the light, / for the brightness that comes from her never goes to sleep.
7:11 With her, all good things arrived together, / and in her hands was wealth too great to count.
7:12 I was glad about all of them, because Wisdom goes before them, / but I was unaware that she is where they come from.
7:13 I learned without deceit, and I share without envy. / I keep none of her riches hidden.
7:14 For she is a treasure for people that never runs out. / Those who make use of it win friendship with God, / recommended to him by what instruction gives.
## Knowledge of all that exists
7:15 God grant that I speak as I ought / and think thoughts worthy of what has been given, / for he himself guides wisdom / and puts the wise right.
7:16 His hand holds us and our words, / with every kind of understanding and know-how in the trades.
7:17 He himself gave me true knowledge of all that exists: / to know how the world is put together and how the elements work,
7:18 when the times begin, end and reach their middle, / how the sun turns back at the solstices, how the seasons change,
7:19 how the years come round, where the stars are set,
7:20 what living creatures are like, how wild beasts rage, / the force of the winds and the arguments in human minds, / the many kinds of plants and the powers in roots.
7:21 I came to know all things, both hidden and plain to see,
## Wisdom's nature
7:22 for Wisdom, who crafted all things, taught me. / For she has in her a spirit that thinks, that is holy, / one of a kind, many-sided, fine, / quick-moving, clear, unstained, / distinct, beyond harm, a lover of good, sharp, / that nothing can stop, that does good,
7:23 kind to people, firm, secure, free from worry, / all-powerful, watching over all, / making its way through every spirit / that thinks, that is pure, that is finest of all.
7:24 For Wisdom moves more freely than any motion. / Because she is so pure, she spreads through all things and passes through them.
7:25 She is a vapor rising from God's power, / a clean outflow of the Almighty's glory. / So nothing defiled can slip into her.
7:26 She is a radiance of eternal light, / a stainless mirror of God's working, / an image of his goodness.
7:27 She is one, yet she can do all things. / She stays as she is, yet she makes all things new. / Age after age she crosses over into holy souls / and shapes them into friends of God and prophets.
7:28 For God loves nothing except the one who makes his home with Wisdom.
7:29 She is lovelier than the sun / and above every pattern of the stars. / Set beside the light, she is found to come first,
7:30 for night takes the place of daylight, / but evil cannot hold out against Wisdom.
8:1 She stretches out in strength from one end to the other, / and she governs all things with kindness.
## Wisdom as a bride
8:2 I loved her and searched for her from the time I was young. / I wanted to bring her home as my bride, / and I fell in love with her beauty.
8:3 Her life shared with God makes her high birth shine, / and the Master of all has loved her.
8:4 She shares the secret knowledge of God, / and she chooses his works.
8:5 If wealth is a thing worth wanting in life, / what is richer than Wisdom, who brings all things about?
8:6 And if understanding gets things done, / who in all that exists is a greater craftswoman than she?
8:7 If anyone loves righteousness, / the fruits of her labor are the virtues. / She teaches self-control and understanding, / righteousness and courage. / For people in this life there is nothing more useful.
8:8 If anyone wants to have seen and learned much, / she knows what happened long ago and can reckon what is coming. / She understands the twists of clever speech / and the answers to riddles. / She knows signs and wonders before they happen, / and how seasons and times will turn out.
8:9 So I decided to bring her home to share my life, / knowing that she would advise me in what is good / and encourage me in worry and grief.
8:10 Through her I will win glory with the crowds / and honor from the elders, young as I am.
8:11 I will be found sharp when I judge, / and the powerful will marvel at me when they see me.
8:12 When I am silent, they will wait for me. / When I speak, they will pay attention. / When I talk on, they will put their hands over their mouths.
8:13 Through her I will gain immortality / and leave a memory that lasts forever for those who follow me.
8:14 I will manage the affairs of peoples, / and nations will be placed under me.
8:15 Dreaded absolute rulers will hear of me and be afraid. / Among the people I will prove good, / and brave in battle.
8:16 Coming home, I will rest beside her, / for being with her holds no bitterness / and living with her no pain, / but gladness and joy.
## Seeking her as a gift
8:17 I thought these things over within myself / and turned them over in my heart: / that in being joined to Wisdom there is immortality,
8:18 in her friendship there is real delight, / in the work of her hands, wealth that never runs out, / in practice at talking with her, understanding, / and in sharing her words, a good name. / So I went around looking for a way to take her home with me.
8:19 I was a child with fine natural gifts, / and a good soul was given me as my share.
8:20 Or better, since I was good, I came into a body without stain.
8:21 But I knew that I would not win her any other way, unless God gave her, / and knowing whose gift she is was itself a sign of understanding. / So I went to the Lord and begged him. / From my whole heart I said:
## Solomon's prayer for wisdom
9:1 "God of my fathers, Lord of your mercy, / by your word you made all things,
9:2 and by your wisdom you formed the human being / to be master of the creatures that came into being through you,
9:3 to manage the world with holiness and righteousness / and to judge with an upright soul.
9:4 Give me Wisdom, who sits beside you on your thrones, / and do not turn me away from among your children.
9:5 For I am your slave, the son of your servant girl, / a weak man whose life is short, / falling short in grasping justice and laws.
9:6 Even if someone among the children of men were perfect, / if the wisdom from you is missing, / he will count for nothing.
## The king and the temple
9:7 You picked me out as king of your people, / to judge your sons and your daughters.
9:8 You told me to build a sanctuary on your holy mountain / and an altar in the city where you pitched your tent, / modeled on the holy tent you made ready at the beginning.
9:9 Wisdom is at your side. She knows your works, / and she was present when you were making the world. / She understands what you find pleasing / and what your commands count as right.
9:10 Send her out from the holy heavens. / Send her from your glorious throne, / so that she may be at my side and labor with me, / and I may come to know what you find pleasing.
9:11 For she knows and understands all things. / She will guide me with good sense in what I do / and keep me safe by her glory.
9:12 Then what I do will be accepted. / I will judge your people fairly / and be worthy of my father's thrones.
## Who can know God's plan?
9:13 For what human being can know God's plan? / Who can work out what the Lord wants?
9:14 The reasonings of mortals are timid, / and our schemes are shaky.
9:15 For a perishable body is a weight on the soul, / and this earthly tent presses on a mind full of cares.
9:16 We can hardly make out what is on earth, / and even what is close at hand we find only with hard work. / So who has tracked down what is in the heavens?
9:17 Who has known your plan, unless you gave wisdom / and sent your Holy Spirit down from the heights?
9:18 That is how those on earth had their paths made straight. / Human beings learned what you find pleasing, / and wisdom saved them."
## From Adam to Noah
10:1 She guarded the first-formed father of the world, / created all alone, / and pulled him out of his own trespass,
10:2 and she gave him the power to master all things.
10:3 But when an unrighteous man left her in his anger, / he was destroyed together with the fury in which he killed his brother.
10:4 When the earth was flooded because of him, / Wisdom saved it again. / She piloted the righteous man on a worthless bit of wood.
## Abraham and Lot
10:5 When the nations were thrown into confusion / for their agreement in wickedness, / she found the righteous man and kept him without blame in God's eyes. / She gave him strength over his deep love for his child.
10:6 She rescued a righteous man while the ungodly were being destroyed, / as he ran from the fire falling from above on the Five Cities.
10:7 Their wickedness still has a witness: / a barren land that sends up smoke, / plants whose fruit comes at the wrong season and never matures, / and a standing pillar of salt, / the monument of a soul that would not believe.
10:8 For they passed Wisdom by, / and so they were not only kept from knowing what is good. / They also left the world a memorial of their foolishness, / so that the places where they went wrong could not even stay hidden.
10:9 But those who served Wisdom, she pulled out of their hardships.
## Jacob and Joseph
10:10 A righteous man was running from his brother's anger, / and she led him along straight paths. / She let him see God's kingdom / and taught him about holy things. / She gave him success in his heavy work / and made his labors multiply.
10:11 When those who overpowered him were greedy, / she was there at his side and gave him wealth.
10:12 She guarded him against his enemies / and made him secure against ambush. / She was the judge of his hard match and gave him the prize, / to teach him that godliness has more power than anything.
10:13 A righteous man was sold, but she did not leave him on his own. / She pulled him out of sin.
10:14 She went down with him into the pit / and did not leave him in chains, / until she brought him a kingdom's scepters / and power over those who had lorded it over him. / She showed that those who had blamed him were liars, / and she gave him everlasting glory.
## Out of Egypt
10:15 She rescued a holy people, a blameless offspring, / from a nation that crushed them.
10:16 She went into the soul of the Lord's servant / and stood up to fearsome kings with wonders and signs.
10:17 She paid the holy ones the wages of their labor. / She led them on an astonishing road. / She was a covering for them in the daytime / and a flame of starlight at night.
10:18 She took them across the Red Sea / and brought them through a great deal of water.
10:19 Their enemies, though, she drowned, / and she threw them up out of the depths of the abyss.
10:20 So the righteous stripped the ungodly of their spoils. / They sang hymns to your holy name, Lord, / and with one voice they praised your hand that fought for them,
10:21 for Wisdom opened the mouths of those who could not speak / and gave clear speech to the tongues of infants.
