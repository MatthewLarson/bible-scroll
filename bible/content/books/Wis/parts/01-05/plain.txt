## Love Righteousness, You Who Judge
1:1 Love righteousness, you who judge the earth. / Turn your thoughts to the Lord in goodness, / and seek him with a simple, honest heart.
1:2 Those who do not test him find him; / he makes himself known to those who keep trusting him.
1:3 Twisted thinking cuts people off from God, / and his power, once it is tested, shows fools up for what they are.
1:4 Wisdom will not come into a soul that schemes evil, / or live in a body deep in debt to sin.
1:5 For the holy spirit of discipline runs from deceit. / It gets up and leaves thoughts that make no sense, / and it is put to shame when wrongdoing comes in.
1:6 Wisdom is a spirit that loves people, / but it will not let a blasphemer off for what his lips say. / For God is a witness of his innermost parts, / a true overseer of his heart, / and he hears his tongue.
1:7 For the Spirit of the Lord has filled the world, / and what holds all things together knows every voice.
1:8 So no one who says wicked things can stay hidden, / and Justice, pressing her charge, will not let him slip past.
1:9 The plans of the ungodly will be examined. / A report of what he says will reach the Lord, / and his lawless acts will be proven against him.
1:10 For a jealous ear hears everything, / and even the low hum of grumbling is not hidden.
1:11 So watch out for grumbling that does no good, / and spare your tongue the work of running others down. / No word whispered in secret goes off empty, / and a mouth that tells lies kills the soul.
## God Did Not Make Death
1:12 Do not go chasing after death by the wrong way you live, / and do not pull ruin down on yourselves by what your hands do.
1:13 For God did not make death, / and he gets no joy when living things are destroyed.
1:14 He created everything to exist. / The things that came to be in the world bring health, / and there is no deadly poison in them. / Hades has no royal throne on the earth.
1:15 For righteousness is immortal.
1:16 But the ungodly called death to them by what they did and said. / They thought of it as a friend and pined for it. / They made a pact with it, / for they deserve to belong to its side.
## What the Ungodly Say
2:1 For they talked it over among themselves, and their reckoning was crooked: / "Life is short for us, and it hurts. / Death comes, and there is no cure for it. / No one is known who ever got loose from Hades.
2:2 We came to be by accident, / and later on we will be like people who never existed. / The breath in our noses is smoke, / and our mind is a spark that flickers while our heart keeps moving.
2:3 When that goes out, the body will become ash, / and the breath will spread out like loose air.
2:4 In time our name will be forgotten, / and no one will remember what we did. / Our life will pass like the last wisps of a cloud. / It will scatter like fog / that the sun's rays chase off and its heat beats down.
2:5 Our life is a shadow going by, / and when our end comes, there is no turning it back. / It has been sealed shut, and no one comes back.
2:6 So come, let's make the most of the good things around us, / and use the world hungrily, the way the young do.
2:7 Let's fill up on expensive wine and perfume, / and let no spring blossom get by us.
2:8 Let's wear crowns of rosebuds before they wilt.
2:9 Let none of us miss his share of our wild partying. / Let's leave signs of our good times everywhere, / because this is what we get, and this is our lot.
## The Plot against the Righteous Man
2:10 Let's crush the poor man who does right. / Let's show no mercy to the widow, / and pay no respect to an old man's gray hair and his many years.
2:11 Let our strength be our law of righteousness, / for weakness is shown to be good for nothing.
2:12 Let's set a trap for the righteous man, because he is a nuisance to us. / He stands against what we do. / He throws our sins against the Law in our faces / and charges us with failing the training we were given.
2:13 He says he knows God, / and gives himself the name 'child of the Lord.'
2:14 He has become a living charge against our thoughts. / Just seeing him is hard for us to bear,
2:15 for his life is not like other people's, / and he walks in ways all his own.
2:16 He thinks of us as fake coins. / He keeps away from our ways as if they were filth. / He says the righteous are happy in the end, / and he brags of having God for his father.
2:17 Let's see whether what he says is true. / Let's put to the test what comes of him when he dies.
2:18 If the righteous man is God's son, God will take his side / and pull him out of the grip of those who stand against him.
2:19 Let's question him with insults and torture. / Then we will learn how gentle he is, / and we can judge how much wrong he can bear.
2:20 Let's sentence him to a shameful death, / for by what he says, a visitation will come for him."
## Why They Were Wrong
2:21 That is how they reasoned, but they went astray, / for their own evil made them blind.
2:22 They did not know his mysteries. / They did not hope that holiness would be paid its wages, / or see that blameless souls win a prize.
2:23 For God created the human being for incorruption / and made him an image of his own eternity.
2:24 But death came into the world through the devil's envy, / and those who take his side find out what it is.
## The Souls of the Righteous
3:1 But the souls of the righteous are in God's hand, / and no torment will touch them at all.
3:2 To foolish eyes they seemed to die. / Their going out was counted as suffering,
3:3 and their road away from us a shattering. / Yet they are in peace.
3:4 Even if people saw them punished, / their hope is full of immortality.
3:5 Their discipline was small; the kindness they will get is great. / God put them to the test and found that they were fit for himself.
3:6 He proved them like gold in a smelter's fire / and took them to himself like a sacrifice burned whole.
3:7 When their visitation comes, they will flash out / and dart through the straw like sparks.
3:8 They will judge nations and rule peoples, / and the Lord will be their king forever.
3:9 People who rely on him will come to understand truth, / and the faithful will wait by his side in love, / because grace and mercy are what his chosen receive.
## The Fate of the Ungodly
3:10 But the ungodly will be punished in line with the way they reasoned. / They paid no heed to the righteous / and turned their backs on the Lord.
3:11 Anyone who treats wisdom and discipline as nothing is wretched. / Their hope is empty, their hard work brings no gain, / and what they do is good for nothing.
3:12 Their wives are fools, their children are wicked, / and their family line is under a curse.
## The Barren Woman and the Eunuch
3:13 For the barren woman who stays undefiled is blessed, / she who has never known a sinful bed. / She will have fruit at the visitation of souls.
3:14 So is the eunuch who has done nothing lawless with his hands / and has plotted no evil against the Lord. / For his faithfulness he will be given a special grace / and a share in the temple of the Lord, all the more to his heart's delight.
3:15 Good work bears fruit that is honored, / and the root of understanding never falls away.
3:16 But adulterers' children will never reach full growth, / and offspring of an unlawful bed will vanish.
3:17 They may have long lives, but they will count for nothing, / and when the end comes, no one will honor their old age.
3:18 And if death comes for them quickly, they have no hope, / and on the day of the verdict, no comfort.
3:19 Harsh are the ends that wait for an unrighteous generation.
4:1 It is better to have no children and have virtue. / Virtue is remembered, and in that memory there is immortality, / for God and people both know it.
4:2 People copy it while it is here / and miss it once it has left. / Forever it wears a crown and leads the victory parade, / having won the contest where the prizes are pure.
4:3 But the ungodly, with all their swarm of children, will come to nothing. / Grown from illegitimate cuttings, they will not send roots down deep / or set themselves on a firm base.
4:4 They may put out branches and bloom for a while, / but since they stand on shaky ground, the wind will rock them, / and strong winds will tear them out by the roots.
4:5 Their young shoots will snap off before they are grown. / The fruit they bear is worthless, too green to eat, fit for nothing.
4:6 For children conceived in lawless beds are witnesses / to their parents' wickedness when the parents are examined.
## The Righteous Who Die Young
4:7 But a righteous man who dies before his time / will be in rest.
4:8 For old age that deserves honor does not come from a long life, / and it is not measured by counting years.
4:9 Understanding is a person's gray hair, / and a spotless life is the full height of old age.
4:10 One man pleased God, and God loved him. / He lived among sinners, and he was carried off.
4:11 He was snatched away, so that evil would not twist his mind / and trickery would not lead his soul astray.
4:12 For the spell cast by worthless things dims what is good, / and restless desire digs away at a mind that knows no evil.
4:13 He reached his goal in a short while / and so filled up many years.
4:14 His soul was pleasing to the Lord, / and so it hurried out from the midst of evil. / The peoples watched this happen without understanding it, / and did not set their minds on this:
4:15 that grace and mercy are with his chosen, / and his visitation is with his holy ones.
4:16 The righteous man who has died will condemn the ungodly still alive, / and a young life quickly completed will condemn the unrighteous man's many years of old age.
4:17 They will see the wise man's end, / and not understand what the Lord intended for him / or why the Lord kept him safe.
4:18 They will see and look down on him, / but the Lord will laugh at them.
4:19 After this they will become corpses no one honors, / an object of scorn among the dead forever. / For he will smash them face down, unable to speak, / and shake them loose from their foundations. / They will be left a barren waste to the very end. / They will be in pain, / and no one will remember them.
4:20 When their sins are counted up, they will come trembling, / and their own lawless deeds will stand up to their face and convict them.
## The Righteous Man Vindicated
5:1 Then the righteous man will take his stand, very bold, / face to face with the people who crushed him / and brushed aside all his toil.
5:2 When they see him, they will shake with dreadful fear. / They will be astonished at a salvation no one expected.
5:3 Repenting, they will speak among themselves, / groaning in the distress of their spirit: / "This is the man we once laughed at, / the one we made a byword and an insult,
5:4 we fools! We counted his life as madness / and his death a disgrace.
5:5 How did he come to be counted among the sons of God? / Why is his share among the holy ones?
5:6 So we wandered off the way of truth. / No light of righteousness shone for us, / and no sun rose for us.
5:7 We went down the paths of lawlessness and ruin until we were stuffed full. / We crossed deserts where no road runs, / but we never knew the way of the Lord.
5:8 What good did our pride do us? / What did our riches and our bragging gain for us?
5:9 All of that has gone by like a shadow, / like a piece of news that rushes past,
5:10 like a ship going across the heaving water: / once it has passed, you cannot find a trace of it / or the track its keel made in the waves.
5:11 Or like a bird flying through the air: / no proof of its path is found. / The light air, lashed by the blows of its wings / and split by the force of its rushing, / is crossed as the wings beat, / and afterward no mark of its passing is found there.
5:12 Or like an arrow shot at a target: / the air it cuts closes up again at once, / so that no one can tell where it went through.
5:13 That is how it was with us. We were born, and right away we were gone. / We had no mark of virtue we could show, / but were eaten up by our own wickedness."
5:14 For the hope of the ungodly is like chaff the wind carries off, / like thin frost chased off by a storm. / It drifts away like smoke in the wind / and is gone like the memory of a guest who stayed only one day.
## The Lord Arms Himself
5:15 But the righteous live forever. / Their reward is in the Lord's keeping, / and their care rests with the Most High.
5:16 So they will receive the splendid kingdom / and the beautiful crown from the Lord's hand. / He will cover them with his right hand / and shield them with his arm.
5:17 His zeal will be the full armor he takes up, / and he will turn the created world into a weapon to drive off his enemies.
5:18 He will put on righteousness as a breastplate / and set honest judgment on his head for a helmet.
5:19 He will take up holiness as a shield no one can beat.
5:20 He will sharpen his stern anger into a sword, / and the world will fight at his side against people who have lost their minds.
5:21 Bolts of lightning will fly straight to the mark. / From the clouds, as if from a bow bent into a full curve, / they will leap to the target.
5:22 Hailstones full of fury will be flung as if from a siege machine. / Seawater will boil with anger against them, / and rivers will sweep over them without mercy.
5:23 A wind of power will stand up against them / and blow them away like a whirlwind. / Lawlessness will turn the whole earth into a desert, / and wrongdoing will tip over the thrones of the mighty.
