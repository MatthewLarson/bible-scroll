## Ezra Reads the Law
8:1 All the people gathered as one person in the open square facing the Water Gate. They called on Ezra the scribe to bring the scroll of Moses' law, which the LORD had commanded Israel.
8:2 So Ezra the priest brought the law before the assembly: men and women alike, and everyone able to listen with understanding. It was the first day of the seventh month.
8:3 With his face toward the open square by the Water Gate, he read aloud from it from first light until noon. The men, the women and all who could understand were there in front of him, and every ear among the people was turned to the scroll of the law.
8:4 Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden tower built for the occasion. Next to him, at his right hand, stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah and Maaseiah. At his left hand were Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah and Meshullam.
8:5 Ezra opened the scroll before the eyes of all the people, for he stood higher than any of them. As he opened it, all the people rose to their feet.
8:6 Ezra blessed the LORD, the great God. With their hands raised, all the people answered, "Amen, amen." They bent low and bowed down before the LORD with their faces to the earth.
8:7 Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the Levites were giving the people understanding of the law, while the people stayed in their places.
8:8 They read aloud from the scroll of God's law, explaining it as they went. They set out its meaning, and the people grasped what was read.
## "This Day Is Holy"
8:9 Then Nehemiah, who was the governor, Ezra the priest and scribe, and the Levites who were helping the people understand spoke to all the people: "Today is holy to the LORD your God. Don't go into mourning, and don't cry." For everyone had been crying as they listened to what the law said.
8:10 He told them, "Go and eat rich food, drink sweet drinks, and send shares to those who have nothing ready. This day is holy to our Lord. Don't be downcast, because the joy of the LORD is your stronghold."
8:11 And the Levites were quieting all the people. "Hush," they said, "this day is holy. Don't be downcast."
8:12 So all the people went to eat and drink, to send out shares and to make great joy. They had grasped the words that had been taught to them.
## The Feast of Booths
8:13 The next day the family heads of all the people, the priests and the Levites gathered to Ezra the scribe to gain insight into the words of the law.
8:14 What they found written in the law, which the LORD had commanded by Moses' hand, was this: the Israelites were to live in booths at the feast in the seventh month,
8:15 and they were to spread a proclamation through all their towns and Jerusalem: "Go out into the hill country. Bring back leafy boughs of olive, of oil tree, of myrtle, of palm and of thick-leaved trees, to make booths as it is written."
8:16 The people went out and brought them in. They made themselves booths, each man on his roof, in their yards, in the yards of God's house, and in the open squares at the Water Gate and the Gate of Ephraim.
8:17 All the assembly, those who had returned from the captivity, built booths and lived in them. Not since the days of Jeshua son of Nun had the Israelites done it this way, right up to that day. The joy was very great.
8:18 He read aloud from the scroll of God's law every day, from the first day to the last. They held the feast for seven days, and on the eighth there was a closing assembly, by the rule for it.
## A Day of Fasting and Confession
9:1 This same month, on its twenty-fourth day, the Israelites came together. They were fasting and dressed in sackcloth, with soil on them.
9:2 Israel's offspring kept themselves apart from everyone of foreign birth. They stood up and confessed their sins and their ancestors' wrongdoings.
9:3 Standing where they were, they read from the scroll of the LORD their God's law for a fourth of the day. For another fourth they were confessing and bowing down to the LORD their God.
9:4 Up on the Levites' stairway stood Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani and Kenani. They raised a great cry to the LORD their God.
9:5 Then the Levites, Jeshua, Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah and Pethahiah, said, "Get up! Bless the LORD your God from age to age! May people bless the name of your glory, raised high above every blessing and praise.
## The Great Prayer
9:6 It is you who are the LORD, and you alone. You made the heavens, the heavens of the heavens and all their army, the earth and everything on it, the seas and everything in them. You give life to them all, and the army of the heavens bows low to you.
9:7 It is you, LORD God, who chose Abram, who brought him out from Ur of the Chaldeans and made his name Abraham.
9:8 You found his heart faithful toward you, and you cut the covenant with him to give the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites and the Girgashites, to give it to his offspring. You have made your words come true, for you are in the right.
9:9 You saw how our ancestors were suffering in Egypt, and you heard their cry at the Sea of Reeds.
9:10 You gave signs and wonders against Pharaoh, against all his servants and all the people of his land, because you knew they had been insolent toward them. You made yourself a name that is still yours today.
9:11 You split the sea open before them, and they passed through the middle of the sea on dry ground. Their pursuers you flung into the deeps, like a stone into mighty waters.
9:12 In a pillar of cloud you led them by day, and in a pillar of fire at night, to light up for them the road on which they walked.
9:13 Onto Mount Sinai you came down, and from the heavens you spoke with them. You gave them right rulings and true laws, good rules and commands.
9:14 You let them know your holy Sabbath. By the hand of your servant Moses you laid down for them commands, rules and law.
9:15 Bread from the heavens you gave them when they were hungry, and water from a rock you brought out for them when they were thirsty. You told them to enter and take possession of the land that you had raised your hand to give them.
## Rebellion and Compassion
9:16 Yet they, our ancestors, were insolent. They made their necks stiff and would not listen to your commands.
9:17 They refused to listen. They forgot the wonders you had worked among them. They made their necks stiff, and in their rebellion they appointed a leader to go back to their slavery. But you are a God of forgiveness, gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and great in loyal love. You did not desert them.
9:18 They even made themselves a calf of cast metal and said, 'This is your god, who brought you up from Egypt.' They committed great acts of contempt.
9:19 Even then, because your compassion is great, you did not desert them in the wilderness. The pillar of cloud did not turn away from over them by day, leading them on the road, nor the pillar of fire by night, lighting up the road on which they walked.
9:20 To give them insight you gave your good Spirit. Your manna you did not keep back from their mouths, and you gave them water when they were thirsty.
9:21 Forty years you provided for them in the wilderness, and they were short of nothing. Their clothes did not wear out, and their feet did not swell.
9:22 You gave them kingdoms and peoples, and you shared those out to them, corner by corner. They took possession of Sihon's land, the land of the king of Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.
9:23 You made their sons many, like the stars of the heavens, and you brought them into the land you had spoken of to their ancestors, for them to go in and take possession of it.
9:24 The sons went in and took possession of the land. Before them you humbled the land's inhabitants, the Canaanites. You put them in their hands, both their kings and the peoples of the land, to do with them whatever they chose.
9:25 They captured fortified cities and fertile soil. They took possession of houses full of all kinds of good things, wells cut in the rock, vineyards, olive trees and fruit trees in plenty. They ate and were full and grew fat, and they lived in luxury on your great goodness.
9:26 Still they defied you and rose in rebellion against you. They threw your law behind their backs. Your prophets, who testified against them to turn them back to you, they killed. They committed great acts of contempt.
9:27 So you put them in the power of their foes, who pressed them hard. When they were hard pressed, they cried out to you, and you listened from the heavens. Because your compassion is great, you gave them rescuers, and they rescued them from their foes' power.
9:28 Once they had rest, they again did evil in front of you, and you deserted them, leaving them in their enemies' power, and their enemies ruled them. Then they cried to you again, and you listened from the heavens. Time and again you rescued them, because of your compassion.
9:29 You testified against them to turn them back to your law. Yet they were insolent and would not listen to your commands. Against your rulings they sinned, the ones that, if a human being does them, he will live by them. They gave you a defiant shoulder, made their necks stiff and would not listen.
9:30 You stretched out your patience over them for many years. You testified against them by your Spirit, by the hand of your prophets, but they did not lend an ear. So you put them in the power of the peoples of the lands.
9:31 Yet because your compassion is great, you did not wipe them out, and you did not desert them. You are a God gracious and compassionate.
## Slaves in Our Own Land
9:32 So now, our God, the great, mighty and fearsome God, who keeps the covenant and loyal love: let none of the hardship that has found us seem small in your sight. It has fallen on our kings, our officials, our priests, our prophets, our ancestors and all your people, since the days of the kings of Assyria until this day.
9:33 Yet you are in the right in all that has come on us. You have done what is faithful, and we have done what is wicked.
9:34 As for our kings, our officials, our priests and our ancestors, they did not do your law. They paid no heed to your commands, or to your decrees, which you testified to them.
9:35 They had their own kingdom. They had your great goodness, which you gave them, and the wide, rich land you put before them. Yet they did not serve you, and they did not turn back from their evil deeds.
9:36 Here we are today: slaves! The land you gave our ancestors, so that they could eat its fruit and its good things, here we are on it, slaves.
9:37 Its plentiful yield goes to the kings you have put over us for our sins. Our bodies are theirs to rule, and our cattle too, as they like. We are in deep trouble.
## A Firm Agreement
9:38 With all this in mind, we are making a firm agreement and writing it down. On the sealed copy stand our officials, our Levites and our priests."

## The Terms of the Agreement
10:28 As for the rest of the people, the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants, and everyone who had kept apart from the peoples of the lands for God's law, with their wives, their sons and their daughters, all who could know and understand.
10:29 They now stand firmly with their brothers, their leading men. They take on themselves a curse and an oath: to walk by God's law, which was given by the hand of Moses, God's servant, and to take care to do all the commands of the LORD our Lord, his rulings and his rules.
10:30 And this: our daughters we will not give to the peoples of the land, and their daughters we will not take for our sons.
10:31 If the peoples of the land bring their wares and all kinds of grain to sell on the Sabbath day, we will take nothing from them on a Sabbath or a holy day. We will let the seventh year go, and let go of every loan.
10:32 We have set ourselves commands: to put on ourselves a third of a shekel a year for the work of our God's house,
10:33 for the bread set in rows, the regular grain offering and the regular burnt offering, the Sabbaths, the new moons, the set feasts, the holy gifts and the sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and all the work of our God's house.
10:34 As for the wood offering, we, the priests, the Levites and the people, have cast lots. Each family in turn will bring it to our God's house at set times year by year, to be burned on the altar of the LORD our God, as it is written in the law.
10:35 We will also bring to the LORD's house each year the first of the crops of our ground and the first of the fruit of every tree.
10:36 As it is written in the law, we will bring the firstborn of our sons and of our cattle, and the firstborn of our herds and our flocks, to our God's house, to the priests who serve in our God's house.
10:37 The first of our dough, our gifts, the fruit of every tree, the new wine and the olive oil we will bring to the priests, into the storerooms of our God's house. The tithe of our ground we will bring to the Levites. They, the Levites, take the tithes in all our farming towns.
10:38 When the Levites take the tithes, a priest of Aaron's line is to be with them. The Levites are to carry up a tenth of the tithe to our God's house, into the storerooms of the treasure house.
10:39 For the Israelites and the descendants of Levi are to bring the gift of grain, new wine and olive oil into the storerooms. The vessels of the holy place are kept there, and the priests on duty, the gatekeepers and the singers are there. We will not desert our God's house.

12:46 For long ago, in the days of David and Asaph, there were heads of the singers, and songs of praise and thanks to God.

## Ammonites and Moabites Kept Out
13:1 That day, as the scroll of Moses was read in the people's hearing, they found written in it that no Ammonite or Moabite may enter the assembly of God, ever.
13:2 For they did not come to meet the Israelites with bread and water, and he hired Balaam against them to curse them. But our God turned the curse into a blessing.
13:3 When they heard the law, they set apart from Israel all the mixed crowd.
## Tobiah Thrown Out
13:4 Earlier, Eliashib the priest had been given charge of the storeroom of our God's house. He was close to Tobiah,
13:5 and he fitted out a big room for him. Before, people had kept there the grain offering, the frankincense and the vessels, and the tithe of the grain, the new wine and the olive oil, the due of the Levites, the singers and the gatekeepers by command. The priests' contributions were kept there too.
13:6 Through all of this I was away from Jerusalem. In the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes, king of Babylon, I had gone back to the king. At the end of a stretch of days I asked the king to let me go,
13:7 and I came to Jerusalem. Then I saw clearly what a wrong Eliashib had done for Tobiah's sake, fitting out a room for him in the courtyards of God's house.
13:8 I took it very badly. I threw all of Tobiah's household goods outside, out of the room.
13:9 At my word they cleansed the rooms. I put the vessels of God's house back in them, along with the grain offering and the frankincense.
## The Levites' Shares
13:10 I found out too that no one had been handing the Levites their shares. Each of them, the Levites and the singers who carried out the work, had run off to his own field.
13:11 I had it out with the deputies. "Why is God's house deserted?" I said. I gathered them and set them back in their places.
13:12 All Judah now brought in the tithe of grain, new wine and olive oil to the storehouses.
13:13 Over the storehouses I made treasurers: Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe and Pedaiah from the Levites. Next to them was Hanan son of Zaccur, son of Mattaniah. These men were counted trustworthy, and it fell to them to share out to their brothers.
13:14 My God, remember me for this. Do not wipe away the loyal love I have shown to the house of my God and its duties.
## The Sabbath Kept Holy
13:15 In those days I saw men in Judah treading winepresses on the Sabbath. Others were carrying in heaps of grain and loading them on donkeys, and wine too, with grapes, figs and loads of every kind, and bringing them into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. I spoke out against them on the day they sold their food.
13:16 The Tyrians living there were bringing fish and every kind of merchandise and selling it to the Judeans on the Sabbath, and in Jerusalem at that.
13:17 I had it out with the nobles of Judah. I said to them, "What evil thing is this that you are doing? You are profaning the Sabbath day!
13:18 Isn't this how your ancestors acted? Then our God brought all this harm down on us and on this city. And you are heaping up more fury on Israel by profaning the Sabbath!"
13:19 When the gates of Jerusalem grew dim before the Sabbath, I spoke, and the doors were shut. I said they were not to open them until the Sabbath was past. I posted some of my servants at the gates, so that no load would come in on the Sabbath day.
13:20 The dealers and the sellers of every kind of ware camped for the night outside Jerusalem, once and then a second time.
13:21 I testified against them. "Why are you camping overnight in front of the wall?" I said to them. "Do it again and I will use force on you!" From that time they stayed away on the Sabbath.
13:22 I told the Levites to make themselves clean and to come and stand guard at the gates, to keep the Sabbath day set apart as holy. This too, my God, remember for me, and have pity on me as your great loyal love allows.
## Marriages with Foreign Women
13:23 In those days, too, I saw Jews who had taken Ashdodite, Ammonite and Moabite women into their homes.
13:24 As for their children, half of them spoke Ashdodite. They could not speak Judean; they spoke the tongue of this people or that.
13:25 I had it out with them. I cursed them, hit some of the men and tore at their hair. I put them under oath by God: "Do not give your daughters to their sons, and do not take any of their daughters for your sons or for yourselves.
13:26 Isn't this the very thing Solomon king of Israel sinned over? Among the many nations no king was his equal. He was dear to his God, and God set him as king over all Israel. Him too the foreign women led into sin.
13:27 And is it to be heard of you that you are doing all this great evil, acting unfaithfully toward our God by taking foreign women into your homes?"
13:28 Even one of the sons of Joiada son of Eliashib the high priest had become son-in-law to Sanballat the Horonite. I chased him away from me.
13:29 My God, remember against them how they stained the priesthood, and the covenant of the priesthood and of the Levites.
13:30 So I made them clean of everything foreign. I set up the duties of the priests and the Levites, each man at his own work.
13:31 I also made provision for the wood offering at its set times, and for the first fruits. My God, remember me for good.
