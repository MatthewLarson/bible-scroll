## An Oracle for Israel
1:1 An oracle. The word of the LORD to Israel through Malachi.
1:2 I have loved you, says the LORD. But you say, "How have you loved us?" Esau was Jacob's brother, wasn't he? the LORD declares. Jacob I loved.
1:3 But Esau I hated. I made his mountains a waste and gave his inheritance to the jackals of the wilderness.
1:4 Though Edom says, "We have been shattered, but we will come back and rebuild the ruins," this is what the LORD of Armies says: They will build, and I will tear down. People will call them the Territory of Wickedness, the people the LORD is angry with forever.
1:5 You will see it with your own eyes, and you will say, "The LORD is great, even beyond Israel's border."
## To the Priests
1:6 Sons honor their fathers, and servants honor their masters. If I am a father, where is my honor? If I am a master, where is the fear of me? says the LORD of Armies to you, priests, who despise my name. But you say, "How have we despised your name?"
1:7 Defiled food is what you bring to my altar. But you say, "How have we defiled you?" By saying, "The LORD's table is despicable."
1:8 You bring a blind animal to sacrifice. Is that not evil? You bring one that is lame or sick. Is that not evil? Go ahead, bring it to your governor. Will he accept you? Will he receive you kindly? says the LORD of Armies.
1:9 So now, try to win God over, so that he will be kind to us. This has come from your hands. Will he receive any of you kindly? says the LORD of Armies.
1:10 I wish someone among you would shut the doors, so that you would stop lighting fire on my altar for nothing! I take no pleasure in you, says the LORD of Armies. An offering from your hands I will not accept.
1:11 From where the sun rises to where it sets, my name is great among the nations. In every place incense is being burned and brought to my name, and a pure offering, for my name is great among the nations, says the LORD of Armies.
1:12 But you profane it by saying, "The Lord's table is defiled, and its produce, its food, is despicable."
1:13 And you say, "What a tiresome chore!" and you turn up your nose at it, says the LORD of Armies. You bring what was stolen, and the lame, and the sick. You bring that as your offering! Am I to accept that from your hands? says the LORD.
1:14 A curse on the swindler! He has a male in his flock and has made a vow, yet he sacrifices a spoiled animal to the Lord. For I am a great king, says the LORD of Armies, and my name is feared among the nations.
## The Covenant with Levi
2:1 And now, priests, this command comes to you.
2:2 If you refuse to listen, if you refuse to take it to heart and give honor to my name, says the LORD of Armies, I will send the curse on you, and I will put a curse on your blessings. Indeed, I have put a curse on them already, because you do not take it to heart.
2:3 Look, I am about to rebuke your offspring. I will smear dung on your faces, the dung of your festivals, and you will be carried off with it.
2:4 Then you will know that I sent you this command, so that my covenant with Levi may hold, says the LORD of Armies.
2:5 Life and peace were my covenant with him, and I gave them to him, with fear besides, and he feared me. He trembled before my name.
2:6 True teaching was in his mouth, and no injustice was found on his lips. He walked with me, at peace and on a straight path, and he turned many back from guilt.
2:7 The lips of a priest keep knowledge safe, and people look to his mouth for teaching, for he is the messenger of the LORD of Armies.
2:8 But as for you, you have turned aside from the way. You have made many stumble by your teaching. You have ruined the covenant of Levi, says the LORD of Armies.
2:9 So I, for my part, have made you despised and low before all the people, since you do not keep my ways but play favorites when you teach.
## Betrayal in Judah
2:10 Is there not one father for all of us? Did not one God create us? Why, then, does each man betray his brother and profane the covenant of our ancestors?
2:11 Judah has betrayed. Something abhorrent has been done in Israel and in Jerusalem. Judah has profaned what is holy to the LORD, what he loves, and has become the husband of the daughter of a foreign god.
2:12 From the tents of Jacob may the LORD cut off any man who does this, the one who wakes and the one who answers, even if he brings an offering to the LORD of Armies.
2:13 You do a second thing as well: you cover the altar of the LORD with tears, with crying and moaning, because he no longer turns to the offering or takes it gladly from your hands.
2:14 But you say, "On what grounds?" The grounds are these: the LORD has stood as witness between you and the wife of your youth. You betrayed her, though she is your companion, the wife bound to you by covenant.
2:15 Did he not make one? A remnant of spirit was his. And what was the one seeking? Offspring from God. So take care in your spirit, and let none of you betray the wife of his youth.
2:16 For he hates sending away, says the LORD, the God of Israel, and covering one's garment with violence, says the LORD of Armies. So take care in your spirit, and do not betray.
## Where Is the God of Justice?
2:17 You have made the LORD weary with your words. But you say, "How have we made him weary?" By saying, "Anyone who does evil is good in the LORD's eyes, and he is happy with such people," or, "Where is the God of justice?"
3:1 Look, I am about to send my messenger, and he will clear a road before me. Then all at once the Lord you are looking for will come to his temple. The messenger of the covenant, whom you delight in, look, he is coming, says the LORD of Armies.
3:2 But who can live through the day he comes? Who can remain standing when he appears? For he is like the fire of a metal refiner and like the soap of those who wash clothes.
3:3 He will sit down like one who melts and cleans silver. He will cleanse the descendants of Levi and refine them like gold and like silver, and they will bring offerings to the LORD in righteousness.
3:4 Then the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will please the LORD, as in the days of long ago and as in years gone by.
3:5 I will come near to you for judgment, and I will be a quick witness against sorcerers and adulterers, against people who swear to a lie, against those who cheat a hired worker of his pay, who wrong the widow and the fatherless, who push aside the foreigner living among you, and who have no fear of me, says the LORD of Armies.
## Return to Me
3:6 For I am the LORD, and I do not change. And you, sons of Jacob, have not come to an end.
3:7 You have been turning aside from my rules since the days of your ancestors, and you have not kept them. Return to me, and I will return to you, says the LORD of Armies. But you say, "How are we to return?"
3:8 Can a person rob God? Yet you are robbing me. But you say, "How have we robbed you?" In the tithe and the contribution.
3:9 You are cursed with the curse, and still you rob me, the whole nation of you.
3:10 Bring the entire tithe into the storehouse, so that there is food in my house. Test me by doing this, says the LORD of Armies: will I not open the windows of the heavens for you, and empty out a blessing on you until it is more than enough?
3:11 For your sake I will rebuke the devourer, so that it does not destroy the produce of your ground, and your vine in the field will not go without fruit, says the LORD of Armies.
3:12 All the nations will call you happy, for you yourselves will be a land of delight, says the LORD of Armies.
## Hard Words and a Scroll
3:13 Your words against me have been hard, says the LORD. But you say, "What have we said to each other against you?"
3:14 You have said, "Serving God is worthless. What did we gain by keeping his charge, and by walking about in gloom before the LORD of Armies?
3:15 So now we call the proud happy. Evildoers are built up, and those who test God get away with it."
3:16 Then those who fear the LORD spoke with each other, each with his neighbor. The LORD listened closely, and he heard, and a scroll of remembrance was written before him for those who fear the LORD and who give thought to his name.
3:17 On the day when I act, says the LORD of Armies, they will be mine, my own private treasure. I will have compassion on them as a man has compassion on his son who serves him.
3:18 You will turn and see the difference between a righteous person and a wicked one, between the person who serves God and the person who did not serve him.
## The Day Is Coming
4:1 For look, the day is coming, burning like an oven. All the proud and everyone who does evil will be stubble, and the coming day will burn them up, says the LORD of Armies, so that nothing is left of them, no root and no branch.
4:2 And for you who fear my name a sun of righteousness will rise, with healing in its wings. You will go out and frisk like calves released from the stall.
4:3 On the day when I act, says the LORD of Armies, you will trample the wicked, and under the soles of your feet they will be ashes.
## Moses and Elijah
4:4 Remember the law of Moses my servant. At Horeb I gave him commands for all Israel, rules and rulings.
4:5 Look, I am about to send you Elijah the prophet before the day of the LORD comes, the great and awesome day.
4:6 He will turn the hearts of fathers back to their sons and the hearts of sons back to their fathers. Otherwise I will come and strike the land and put it under the ban.
