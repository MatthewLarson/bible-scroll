## Amos of Tekoa
1:1 The words of Amos, one of the sheep breeders of Tekoa, which he saw concerning Israel. He saw them in the days of Uzziah king of Judah and in the days of Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel, two years before the earthquake.
## The LORD Roars from Zion
1:2 He said: / The LORD roars from Zion / and gives out his voice from Jerusalem. / The shepherds' meadows go dry, / and Carmel's summit withers.
## Damascus
1:3 The LORD says this: / For three rebellions of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn it back. / They threshed Gilead with sledges of iron.
1:4 So I will send a fire into the house of Hazael, / and it will eat up the citadels of Ben-hadad.
1:5 I will break the gate bar of Damascus. / I will cut off those who live in the Valley of Aven, / and the one who holds the scepter from Beth-eden, / and the people of Aram will go into exile to Kir, / says the LORD.
## Gaza and Tyre
1:6 The LORD says this: / For three rebellions of Gaza, and for four, I will not turn it back. / They carried off whole communities of exiles to hand them over to Edom.
1:7 So I will send a fire onto the wall of Gaza, / and it will eat up her citadels.
1:8 I will cut off those who live in Ashdod, / and the one who holds the scepter from Ashkelon. / I will turn my hand against Ekron, / and the remnant of the Philistines will perish, / says the Lord GOD.
1:9 The LORD says this: / For three rebellions of Tyre, and for four, I will not turn it back. / They handed over whole communities of exiles to Edom / and did not remember the brothers' covenant.
1:10 So I will send a fire onto the wall of Tyre, / and it will eat up her citadels.
## Edom and Ammon
1:11 The LORD says this: / For three rebellions of Edom, and for four, I will not turn it back. / He chased down his brother with the sword / and destroyed his compassion. / His anger tore on and on, / and his fury he kept without end.
1:12 So I will send a fire onto Teman, / and it will eat up the citadels of Bozrah.
1:13 The LORD says this: / For three rebellions of the Ammonites, and for four, I will not turn it back. / They ripped Gilead's pregnant women open / to make their own land larger.
1:14 So I will kindle a fire on the wall of Rabbah, / and it will eat up her citadels, / with a battle cry on the day of fighting, / with a gale on the day of the whirlwind.
1:15 Their king will go into exile, / he and his officials together, / says the LORD.
## Moab and Judah
2:1 The LORD says this: / For three rebellions of Moab, and for four, I will not turn it back. / He burned the bones of the king of Edom into lime.
2:2 So I will send a fire onto Moab, / and it will eat up the citadels of Kerioth. / Moab will die in an uproar, / with a battle cry and the blast of the ram's horn.
2:3 I will cut off the ruler from her midst / and kill all her officials with him, / says the LORD.
2:4 The LORD says this: / For three rebellions of Judah, and for four, I will not turn it back. / They have rejected the LORD's teaching / and have not kept his rules. / Their lies made them wander, / the lies their ancestors followed.
2:5 So I will send a fire onto Judah, / and it will eat up the citadels of Jerusalem.
## Israel's Turn
2:6 The LORD says this: / For three rebellions of Israel, and for four, I will not turn it back. / They sell a righteous person for silver / and a needy person for a pair of sandals.
2:7 They stamp the heads of the poor down to the dust of the ground / and push the humble off their road. / A man and his father go in to the same young woman, / so that they profane my holy name.
2:8 They lie down beside every altar / on clothes taken as pledges, / and in the house of their God they drink / wine paid in fines.
2:9 But I was the one who wiped out the Amorites ahead of them. / They were as tall as cedars and as tough as oaks. / I wiped out their fruit on top and their roots underneath.
2:10 And I was the one who brought you up from the land of Egypt / and led you through the wilderness for forty years, / so that you could take possession of the land of the Amorites.
2:11 I raised up some of your sons to be prophets / and some of your young men to be Nazirites. / Is that not so, Israelites? / says the LORD.
2:12 But you gave the Nazirites wine to drink, / and you gave the prophets this order: "You must not prophesy."
2:13 Look, I will press you down where you stand, / as a cart presses down / when it is loaded with cut grain.
2:14 The fast runner will have no place to flee to. / The strong man will not make his strength hold. / The warrior will not save his life.
2:15 The one who handles the bow will not stand. / The one who is quick on his feet will not escape. / The one who rides the horse will not save his life.
2:16 Even the bravest of the warriors / will run away naked on that day, / says the LORD.
## You Only Have I Known
3:1 Hear this word that the LORD has spoken against you, Israelites, / against the whole clan that I brought up from the land of Egypt.
3:2 You are the only ones I have known / among all the clans of the earth. / That is why I will call you to account / for all your guilt.
3:3 Do two people walk along together / unless they have made an appointment to meet?
3:4 Does a lion roar in the woods / unless it has prey? / Does a young lion give out his voice from his den / unless he has caught something?
3:5 Will a bird fall into a trap on the ground / if no snare is set for it? / Will the trap spring up from the soil / if it has caught nothing at all?
3:6 Is a ram's horn blown in a city / and the people do not shake with fear? / Does disaster fall on a city / and the LORD has not done it?
3:7 The Lord GOD does nothing at all / unless he has shown his secret counsel / to his servants the prophets.
3:8 A lion has roared. / Who is not afraid? / The Lord GOD has spoken. / Who will not prophesy?
## Samaria's Fortresses
3:9 Make it heard in the citadels of Ashdod / and in the citadels of the land of Egypt. / Say: Gather on the mountains of Samaria, / and see the great uproar inside her / and the oppression in her midst.
3:10 They do not know how to act straight, declares the LORD. / They store up violence and plunder in their citadels.
3:11 So the Lord GOD says this: / An enemy, all around the land! / He will pull your strength down from you, / and your citadels will be plundered.
3:12 The LORD says this: / As a shepherd rescues from a lion's mouth / two legs or a scrap of an ear, / so the Israelites who sit in Samaria will be rescued, / with only the corner of a bed / and a scrap of cloth from a couch.
3:13 Hear, and testify against the house of Jacob, / declares the Lord GOD, the God of Armies.
3:14 On the day I call Israel to account for its rebellions, / I will call the altars of Bethel to account as well. / The horns of the altar will be cut off / and fall to the ground.
3:15 I will strike the winter house together with the summer house. / The ivory houses will be lost, / and many houses will be swept away, / declares the LORD.
## Cows of Bashan
4:1 Hear this word, you cows of Bashan / on Samaria's mountain, / who squeeze the poor and crush the needy, / who tell your masters, "Bring something, so we can drink!"
4:2 The Lord GOD has sworn by his holiness: / Look, days are coming upon you / when they will haul you away with hooks, / and what is left of you with fishhooks.
4:3 You will go out through the breaches in the wall, / each woman straight ahead, / and you will be flung toward Harmon, / declares the LORD.
## Go to Bethel
4:4 Go to Bethel, and rebel. / Go to Gilgal, and rebel even more. / Bring your sacrifices in the morning, / and your tithes every three days.
4:5 Send up in smoke a thank offering of leavened bread. / Shout out your freewill offerings and make them heard. / For this is what you love, Israelites, / declares the Lord GOD.
## You Did Not Turn Back
4:6 I myself gave you clean teeth in all your cities / and a shortage of bread in all your places, / yet you did not turn back to me, / declares the LORD.
4:7 I myself held back the rain from you / three months before the harvest. / I made it rain on one city / and not on another city. / One field had rain, / and the field with no rain dried up.
4:8 People from two or three cities staggered to one city to drink water, / and they were not satisfied, / yet you did not turn back to me, / declares the LORD.
4:9 I struck you with blight and mildew. / The cutting locust ate your many gardens and vineyards, / your fig trees and your olive trees, / yet you did not turn back to me, / declares the LORD.
4:10 I sent a plague among you, the kind Egypt had. / I killed your young men with the sword, / along with the capture of your horses. / I made the stink of your camps rise into your nostrils, / yet you did not turn back to me, / declares the LORD.
4:11 I overturned some of you / the way God overturned Sodom and Gomorrah, / and you were like a piece of firewood snatched from the blaze, / yet you did not turn back to me, / declares the LORD.
4:12 So this is what I will do to you, Israel. / And because I will do this to you, / get ready to meet your God, Israel.
4:13 For look: the one who forms mountains and creates wind, / who lets people know what he is thinking, / who makes the dawn into darkness / and walks on the high places of the earth, / the LORD, the God of Armies, is his name.
## A Lament for Israel
5:1 Hear this word that I lift up over you, a lament, house of Israel:
5:2 She has fallen, and she will not rise again, / the virgin Israel. / She lies deserted on her own ground, / with no one to help her up.
5:3 For the Lord GOD says this: / The city that marched out with a thousand / will have a hundred left. / The one that marched out with a hundred / will have ten left, / out of the house of Israel.
## Seek Me and Live
5:4 For the LORD says this to the house of Israel: / Seek me and live.
5:5 Do not seek Bethel. / Do not go to Gilgal / or cross over to Beersheba. / For Gilgal will surely go into exile, / and Bethel will become Aven.
5:6 Seek the LORD and live, / or he will rush on the house of Joseph like fire, / and it will burn, / and Bethel will have no one to put it out.
5:7 You who turn justice into wormwood / and leave righteousness lying on the ground!
5:8 The one who made the Pleiades and Orion, / who changes deep shadow into dawn / and darkens the day into night, / who summons the sea's waters / and pours them out over the face of the earth: / the LORD is his name.
5:9 He makes ruin flash out on the strong, / so that ruin comes on the fortress.
5:10 They hate the one who calls out wrong at the gate, / and they loathe the one who speaks honestly.
5:11 So, because you trample the poor / and take from him a portion of grain, / you built houses of cut stone, / but you will never live in them. / You set out vineyards of delight, / but you will never drink their wine.
5:12 For I know how many your rebellions are / and how mighty your sins: / you who hem in the righteous, who take a bribe, / and shove the needy aside at the gate.
5:13 That is why the one with insight says nothing at that time, / for it is an evil time.
5:14 Seek what is good, not what is evil, / so that you may live, / and so the LORD, the God of Armies, may be with you, / just as you say he is.
5:15 Hate what is evil and love what is good. / Make justice stand firm at the gate. / Perhaps the LORD, the God of Armies, will be gracious / to the remnant of Joseph.
5:16 So the LORD, the God of Armies, the Lord, says this: / Wailing will fill all the open squares, / and in every street they will cry, "Alas! Alas!" / They will summon the farmer to mourn / and those who know how to lament to wail.
5:17 In all the vineyards there will be wailing, / for I will pass through your midst, / says the LORD.
## Woe to Those Who Long for the Day
5:18 Woe to you who long for the day of the LORD! / What will the day of the LORD be to you? / It is darkness, and not light.
5:19 It is as when a man runs from a lion, / and a bear comes at him, / or as when he gets home, rests his hand against the wall, / and a snake bites him.
5:20 Will the day of the LORD not be dark and not bright, / black as night, with no shine in it?
## I Hate Your Festivals
5:21 I hate your festivals, I reject them, / and I will not enjoy the smell of your sacred gatherings.
5:22 Even if you offer burnt offerings up to me, with your grain offerings, / I will not be pleased with them. / Your fattened animals for peace offerings: / I will not look at them.
5:23 Get the racket of your songs away from me! / I will not hear the strumming of your harps.
5:24 Let justice roll like water, / and righteousness like a stream that never runs dry.
5:25 Was it to me that you brought sacrifices and grain offerings, / house of Israel, in the wilderness those forty years?
5:26 You will carry Sikkuth your king / and Kiyyun your images, / the star of your gods, / which you made for yourselves.
5:27 So I will send you into exile beyond Damascus, / says the LORD; / the God of Armies is his name.
## Woe to the Comfortable
6:1 Woe to you who live at ease in Zion / and trust in Samaria's mountain, / you famous men of the leading nation, / the ones the house of Israel comes to!
6:2 Cross over to Calneh and look. / Go from there to Hamath-rabbah, / then go down to Philistine Gath. / Are they better than these kingdoms here? / Or is their land larger than yours?
6:3 You push the evil day away, / and you bring the seat of violence near.
6:4 Lying on beds of ivory, / sprawled across their couches, / they eat lambs from the flock / and calves from the pen.
6:5 They make up songs to the plucking of the harp, / and like David they design musical instruments of their own.
6:6 They drink wine by the bowlful / and smear themselves with the best oils, / but the ruin of Joseph does not make them sick.
6:7 That is why they will go into exile now, at the head of the exiles, / and the revelry of the sprawlers will come to an end.
6:8 The Lord GOD has sworn by his own self, / declares the LORD, the God of Armies: / I loathe the pride of Jacob. / I hate his citadels, / and I will hand over the city and all that fills it.
6:9 And if ten men are left in one house, they will die.
6:10 A relative of each, the one who burns the body, will lift it up to carry the bones out of the house. He will say to someone in the innermost part of the house, "Is anyone still with you?" and the answer will be, "No." Then he will say, "Hush! For the name of the LORD must not be mentioned."
6:11 For look, the LORD gives the command, / and the big house is struck into pieces, / and the small house into splinters.
6:12 Do horses race on a rocky cliff? / Does anyone plow there with oxen? / Yet you have turned justice into poison / and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood.
6:13 You who rejoice over Lo-debar, / who say, "With our own strength we took Karnaim for ourselves, did we not?"
6:14 For look, I am raising up a nation against you, / house of Israel, declares the LORD, the God of Armies, / and they will squeeze you / from Lebo-hamath to the wadi of the Arabah.
## Locusts and Fire
7:1 The Lord GOD showed me this. Look, he was forming a swarm of locusts when the late crop began to come up, the late crop that grows after the king's cutting.
7:2 When they were about to finish eating all the green plants of the land, I said, "Lord GOD, please forgive! Small as he is, how will Jacob get up?"
7:3 The LORD changed his mind about this. "It will not happen," the LORD said.
7:4 The Lord GOD showed me this. Look, the Lord GOD was calling out to argue the case with fire. It ate up the great deep, and it ate up the portion of land.
7:5 I said, "Lord GOD, please stop! Small as he is, how will Jacob get up?"
7:6 The LORD changed his mind about this too. "This will not happen either," said the Lord GOD.
## The Plumb Line
7:7 He showed me this. Look, the Lord was standing on a wall checked with a plumb line, and a plumb line was in his hand.
7:8 The LORD said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" I said, "A plumb line." Then the Lord said, "Look, I am setting a plumb line in the middle of my people Israel. I will not overlook them any more."
7:9 The high places of Isaac will be left empty, / Israel's holy places will lie in ruins, / and I will get up against the house of Jeroboam with a sword.
## Amaziah and Amos
7:10 Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent word to Jeroboam king of Israel: "Amos is plotting against you right inside the house of Israel. The land cannot contain all his words.
7:11 For this is what Amos has said: 'Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will surely go into exile away from its land.'"
7:12 Then Amaziah said to Amos, "Seer, get out! Run off to the land of Judah, eat your bread there, and do your prophesying there.
7:13 But never prophesy again at Bethel, for it is the king's sanctuary, the temple of the kingdom."
7:14 Amos answered Amaziah, "I am no prophet, and I am no prophet's son. I am a herdsman, and I tend sycamore figs.
7:15 But the LORD took me from walking behind the flock, and the LORD said to me, 'Go, prophesy to my people Israel.'
7:16 So now hear the word of the LORD. You are saying, 'Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not rant against the house of Isaac.'
7:17 So this is what the LORD says: 'Your wife will become a prostitute in the city. Your sons and daughters will fall by the sword. Your land will be parceled out by a measuring line, and you yourself will die on unclean ground. And Israel will surely go into exile away from its land.'"
## Summer Fruit
8:1 The Lord GOD showed me this: a basket of summer fruit.
8:2 He said, "What do you see, Amos?" I said, "A basket of summer fruit." Then the LORD said to me, "The end has come for my people Israel. I will not overlook them any more."
8:3 On that day the temple songs will turn to wailing, / declares the Lord GOD. / There will be many corpses, thrown out in every place. / Hush!
## Buying the Poor
8:4 Hear this, you who trample the needy / and finish off the poor of the land,
8:5 who say, "When will the New Moon be over, so we can sell grain? / When will the Sabbath be over, so we can open up the wheat? / We will make the ephah small and the shekel large, / and cheat with scales that lie,
8:6 so we can buy the poor for silver / and the needy for the price of a pair of sandals, / and sell even the sweepings of the wheat."
8:7 The LORD has sworn by the pride of Jacob: / Not one of their deeds will I ever forget.
8:8 Because of this, will the land not shake, / and everyone who lives in it mourn? / All of it will swell like the Nile, / heave up, and settle back like the Nile of Egypt.
8:9 On that day, declares the Lord GOD, / I will make the sun set at midday / and bring darkness over the land while the day is bright.
8:10 I will change your festivals into mourning / and all your songs into funeral cries. / I will wrap every waist in sackcloth / and leave every head bald. / I will make it like the grief over an only son, / and the end of it a bitter day.
## A Famine of Hearing
8:11 Look, days are coming, declares the Lord GOD, / when I will send a famine into the land. / It will not be a famine for bread / or a thirst for water, / but for hearing the words of the LORD.
8:12 They will stagger from sea to sea, / and from the north around to the east / they will roam, looking for the word of the LORD, / and they will not find it.
8:13 On that day the beautiful young women / and the young men will faint from thirst.
8:14 Those who take an oath by the guilt of Samaria / and say, "By the life of your god, Dan!" / and "By the life of the way to Beersheba!" / will fall and never get up again.
## No One Escapes
9:1 I saw the Lord standing over the altar. He said: / Strike the capitals so that the thresholds shake. / Break them off on the heads of all of them, / and I will kill the last of them with the sword. / None of them will get away. / None of them who runs will escape.
9:2 Even if they tunnel down to Sheol, / my hand will pull them out from there. / Even if they climb to the heavens, / I will bring them down from there.
9:3 If they hide on the top of Carmel, / from there I will hunt them down and take them. / If they conceal themselves from my sight on the sea floor, / from there I will command the serpent, and it will bite them.
9:4 If they walk as captives in front of their enemies, / from there I will command the sword, and it will kill them. / I will turn my eye on them / for harm and not for good.
9:5 The Lord, the LORD of Armies, / touches the earth, and it melts, / and all who live in it mourn. / All of it swells like the Nile / and settles back like the Nile of Egypt.
9:6 He builds his stairway in the heavens / and has founded his vault on the earth. / He summons the sea's waters / and pours them out over the face of the earth: / the LORD is his name.
## The Cushites, the Philistines and the Arameans
9:7 Are you Israelites not like the Cushites to me? / declares the LORD. / Did I not bring Israel up from the land of Egypt, / and the Philistines from Caphtor, / and the Arameans from Kir?
9:8 The eyes of the Lord GOD are on the sinful kingdom, / and I will wipe it off the face of the ground. / Yet I will not wipe out the house of Jacob completely, / declares the LORD.
9:9 For look, I am giving the command, / and I will shake the house of Israel among all the nations / as grain is shaken in a sieve, / and not one pebble will fall to the ground.
9:10 By the sword they will die, / all the sinners of my people, / who say, "Disaster will not come near us or reach us."
## The Fallen Booth
9:11 On that day I will set up again the fallen booth of David. / I will wall up its gaps, / set up its ruins again, / and build it the way it was in ancient days,
9:12 so that they may take possession of the remnant of Edom / and all the nations over whom my name has been called, / declares the LORD, who is about to do this.
9:13 Look, days are coming, declares the LORD, / when the plowman will catch up with the reaper / and the grape treader with the one who sows the seed. / The mountains will drip with sweet wine, / and all the hills will flow with it.
9:14 I will turn the fortunes of my people Israel around. / They will rebuild the cities that lie ruined and settle in them. / They will plant vineyards and drink their wine, / lay out gardens and eat what they produce.
9:15 I will plant them in their own soil, / and never again will they be torn up / from the land I gave them, / says the LORD your God.
