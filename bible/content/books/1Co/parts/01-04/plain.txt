## Greeting and thanks
1:1 From Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus, called through the will of God, and from our brother Sosthenes.
1:2 To the church of God that is in Corinth, those made holy in Christ Jesus, called as saints, together with all who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, theirs and ours.
1:3 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
1:4 I always thank my God for you, because of the grace of God that was given to you in Christ Jesus.
1:5 In him you were made rich in every way, in all speech and all knowledge,
1:6 just as the testimony about Christ was made firm among you.
1:7 So you are not lacking in any gift as you eagerly wait for the revealing of our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:8 He will also keep you firm to the end, free of blame on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:9 God is faithful, and through him you were called into partnership with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
## Quarrels in the church
1:10 I appeal to you, brothers, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ: all of you say the same thing, let there be no divisions among you, and be knit together in one mind and one view.
1:11 For some of Chloe's people have shown me, my brothers, that there are quarrels among you.
1:12 Here is what I mean. Each of you is saying, "I belong to Paul," or "I belong to Apollos," or "I belong to Cephas," or "I belong to Christ."
1:13 Has Christ been split into parts? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptized into the name of Paul?
1:14 I give thanks that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius,
1:15 so that no one can say you were baptized into my name.
1:16 (I also baptized the household of Stephanas. Beyond that, I do not know whether I baptized anyone else.)
1:17 Christ did not send me to baptize but to tell the good news, and not in the wisdom of words, so that the cross of Christ would not be made empty.
## The word of the cross
1:18 For those who are perishing, the word of the cross is foolishness. For us who are being saved, it is the power of God.
1:19 For it is written: "I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and the understanding of those who understand I will set aside."
1:20 Where is the wise person? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has God not made the wisdom of the world foolish?
1:21 For since the world, in the wisdom of God, did not come to know God through its wisdom, God was pleased to save those who believe through the foolishness of what we preach.
1:22 Jews ask for signs, and Greeks look for wisdom,
1:23 but we preach Christ crucified: a stumbling block to Jews and foolishness to Gentiles,
1:24 but to those who are called, Jews and Greeks alike, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God.
1:25 For God's foolishness is wiser than human beings, and God's weakness is stronger than human beings.
## Look at who you are
1:26 Look at your own calling, brothers. Not many of you were wise according to the flesh, not many were powerful, not many were of noble birth.
1:27 But God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise, and God chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong.
1:28 God chose the low-born things of the world and the things looked down on, the things that are not, to bring to nothing the things that are,
1:29 so that no flesh may boast before God.
1:30 It is from him that you are in Christ Jesus, who became wisdom for us from God, and righteousness and holiness and redemption,
1:31 so that, as it is written, "Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord."
## Preaching in weakness
2:1 When I came to you, brothers, I did not come with lofty speech or wisdom to announce to you the testimony of God.
2:2 For I decided that among you I would know nothing except Jesus Christ, and him as the one who was crucified.
2:3 I was with you in weakness and in fear and in much trembling.
2:4 My word and my preaching were not in persuasive wisdom, but in the proof of the Spirit and of power,
2:5 so that your faith would rest not on human wisdom but on the power of God.
## God's wisdom, given by the Spirit
2:6 Yet we do speak wisdom among the mature, though not the wisdom of this age or of the rulers of this age, who are coming to nothing.
2:7 We speak God's wisdom in a mystery, the hidden wisdom that God decided beforehand, before the ages, for our glory.
2:8 None of the rulers of this age has known it. For if they had known, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.
2:9 But as it is written: "What no eye has seen and no ear has heard, and what has not come up into a human heart, all that God has prepared for those who love him."
2:10 God revealed these things to us through the Spirit. For the Spirit explores everything, even the depths of God.
2:11 For who among people knows what belongs to a person, except the person's own spirit within him? In the same way, no one has known what belongs to God except the Spirit of God.
2:12 Now we have received not the spirit of the world but the Spirit that is from God, so that we may know what God has freely given us.
2:13 We speak of these things too, not in words taught by human wisdom but in words taught by the Spirit, putting spiritual things into spiritual words.
2:14 A natural person does not welcome the things of the Spirit of God. They are foolishness to him, and he cannot understand them, because they are examined spiritually.
2:15 The spiritual person examines everything, yet is examined by no one.
2:16 For "who has come to know the Lord's mind, so as to instruct him?" But we have the mind of Christ.
## Still infants
3:1 And I, brothers, could not speak to you as spiritual people, but as people made of flesh, as infants in Christ.
3:2 I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, because you were not yet able. In fact, you are still not able,
3:3 for you are still fleshly. Since there is jealousy and strife among you, are you not fleshly, and are you not living by human ways?
3:4 For when one says, "I belong to Paul," and another, "I belong to Apollos," are you not merely human?
3:5 What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you came to believe, as the Lord gave to each one.
3:6 I planted, Apollos watered, but God made it grow.
3:7 So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God, who makes it grow.
3:8 The one who plants and the one who waters are one, and each will receive his own reward for his own labor.
3:9 For we are God's fellow workers. You are God's field, God's building.
## The builders and the fire
3:10 By the grace of God given to me, like a wise master builder I laid a foundation, and another builds on it. Let each one take care how he builds on it.
3:11 For no one can lay any other foundation than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.
3:12 If anyone builds on this foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay or straw,
3:13 each person's work will become plain. For the Day will make it clear, because it is revealed with fire, and the fire itself will test what kind of work each one has done.
3:14 If the work someone built on the foundation remains, he will receive a reward.
3:15 If someone's work is burned up, he will forfeit it. He himself will be saved, yet like one who comes through fire.
## God's temple and false wisdom
3:16 Do you not know that you are God's temple, and that the Spirit of God lives in you?
3:17 If anyone ruins God's temple, God will ruin him. For God's temple is holy, and you are that temple.
3:18 Let no one fool himself. If anyone among you thinks he is wise in this age, let him become foolish, so that he may become wise.
3:19 For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. It is written: "He catches the wise in their cunning."
3:20 And again: "The Lord knows that the reasonings of the wise are futile."
3:21 So let no one boast about people. For all things are yours,
3:22 whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas, or the world or life or death, or things present or things to come. All are yours,
3:23 and you are Christ's, and Christ is God's.
## Servants and stewards
4:1 So let a person count us as assistants of Christ and stewards of God's mysteries.
4:2 Here, moreover, what is asked of stewards is that each one be found faithful.
4:3 To me it is a very small matter to be examined by you or by any human court. I do not even examine myself.
4:4 I am aware of nothing against myself, but I am not justified by that. The one who examines me is the Lord.
4:5 So stop judging before the right time, until the Lord comes. He will bring the hidden things of darkness to light and make plain the motives of the heart. Then praise will come to each person from God.
## The apostles on display
4:6 I have applied these things to myself and Apollos for your sake, brothers, so that you may learn from us the saying, "Not beyond what is written," and so that none of you will be puffed up for one person against another.
4:7 Who makes you different from anyone else? What do you have that you did not receive? And if you did receive it, why do you boast as if you had not received it?
4:8 You are already full! You are already rich! You have begun to reign without us! I wish you really did reign, so that we could reign with you!
4:9 For I think God has put us apostles on show last of all, like people sentenced to death, because we have become a spectacle to the world, to angels and to human beings.
4:10 We are fools because of Christ, but you are wise in Christ. We are weak, but you are strong. You are honored, but we are dishonored.
4:11 Up to this very hour we go hungry and thirsty, we have poor clothes, we are beaten with fists, and we have no home.
4:12 We wear ourselves out working with our own hands. When people insult us, we bless them. When they persecute us, we put up with it.
4:13 When we are slandered, we appeal to them. We have become like the scum of the world, the garbage of everything, even now.
## A father's appeal
4:14 I do not write these things to make you ashamed, but to correct you, as my beloved children.
4:15 For you may have ten thousand guardians in Christ, but you do not have many fathers. It was in Christ Jesus, through the good news, that I fathered you.
4:16 So I urge you: become imitators of me.
4:17 For this reason I sent Timothy to you, my beloved and faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you of my ways in Christ Jesus, just as I teach everywhere in every church.
4:18 Some of you have become puffed up, as if I were not coming to you.
4:19 But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will find out not the talk of those who are puffed up but their power.
4:20 For the kingdom of God is not in talk but in power.
4:21 Which will it be? Shall I come to you with a rod, or with love and a gentle spirit?
