## The Good News Paul Received
15:1 Now I am making known to you, brothers, the good news that I told you, which you also received and in which you also stand.
15:2 Through it you are also being saved, if you hold fast to the word I used when I told you the good news, unless you believed for no purpose.
15:3 Among the first things, I handed on to you what I also received: that Christ died for our sins, in line with the Scriptures,
15:4 that he was buried, that he has been raised on the third day, in line with the Scriptures,
15:5 and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the Twelve.
15:6 After that he appeared to more than five hundred brothers at one time. Most of them are still alive, though some have fallen asleep.
15:7 After that he appeared to James, then to all the apostles.
15:8 Last of all, as to one born at the wrong time, he appeared to me also.
15:9 For I am the least of the apostles. I am not fit to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.
15:10 It is by God's grace that I am what I am, and the grace he gave me has not proved empty. I toiled more than all of them. But it was not I; it was the grace of God that is with me.
15:11 So, whether it was I or they, this is how we preach, and this is how you came to believe.
## If Christ Has Not Been Raised
15:12 Now, if the message about Christ is that he has been raised from the dead, how is it that some among you say no resurrection of the dead exists?
15:13 But if no resurrection of the dead exists, then not even Christ has been raised.
15:14 And if Christ has not been raised, then what we preach is empty, and your faith is empty too.
15:15 We are also found to be false witnesses about God: we testified of God that he raised Christ, when he did not raise him, if the dead are really not raised.
15:16 For if the dead are not raised, then not even Christ has been raised.
15:17 And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is pointless, and you are still in your sins.
15:18 Then those who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished as well.
15:19 If we have a hope in Christ that covers this life only, we deserve more pity than anyone else.
## Christ the Firstfruits
15:20 But now Christ has been raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.
15:21 Since death came by a man, the resurrection of the dead has come by a man also.
15:22 Just as all die in Adam, so all will be made alive in Christ.
15:23 But each group has its own place in the order: Christ, the firstfruits; then, at his coming, those who belong to Christ.
15:24 Then comes the end. He hands the kingdom over to God the Father, when he has brought to an end every rule and every authority and power.
15:25 For he has to be king until he places all the enemies under his feet.
15:26 The last enemy to be brought to an end is death.
15:27 For "he put all things under his feet." But when it says, "All things have been put under him," it is clear that the one who put all things under him is not included.
15:28 And when all things have been put under him, then the Son himself will also be put under the One who put all things under him, so that God may be all in all.
## Why We Live as We Do
15:29 Otherwise, what will they do, those who are baptized for the dead? If the dead are not raised at all, why else are they baptized for them?
15:30 And we ourselves, why are we in danger every hour?
15:31 I die every day. I swear it by the pride I have in you, brothers, in Christ Jesus our Lord.
15:32 As a mere man I fought with wild beasts at Ephesus. What is the profit for me? If the dead are not raised: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die."
15:33 Do not let yourselves be led astray: "Bad company ruins good habits."
15:34 Become sober, and rightly so, and quit sinning. Some of you are ignorant of God. I speak to make you ashamed.
## The Body That Is Raised
15:35 But someone will say: "How do the dead get raised? And what sort of body do they come in?"
15:36 Fool! What you sow does not come alive unless it dies.
15:37 And when you sow, you are not sowing the body that is going to be. You sow a bare grain, say of wheat or of some other kind.
15:38 God gives it a body just as he willed, and to each of the seeds a body of its own.
15:39 Not every kind of flesh is the same. Human beings have one kind, beasts a second, birds a third, and fish a fourth.
15:40 There are also heavenly bodies and earthly bodies. But the glory of the heavenly is of one kind, and the glory of the earthly another.
15:41 The sun has its glory, the moon has another glory, and the stars another. Even one star differs from another in glory.
15:42 So it is with the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in decay and raised in what cannot decay.
15:43 It is sown in dishonor and raised in glory. It is sown in weakness and raised in power.
15:44 It is sown as a natural body and raised as a spiritual body. If there is such a thing as a natural body, there is a spiritual one too.
15:45 So it is also written: "The first man, Adam, became a living being." The last Adam became a spirit that gives life.
15:46 But the spiritual did not come first. The natural came first, and then the spiritual.
15:47 The first man came from the earth and was made of dust. The second man is from heaven.
15:48 Like the one made of dust, so are those made of dust; and like the heavenly one, so are those who are heavenly.
15:49 We have worn the image of the one made of dust, and in the same way we will wear the image of the heavenly one.
## The Last Trumpet
15:50 This is what I am saying, brothers: flesh and blood is not able to inherit the kingdom of God. Decay does not inherit what cannot decay.
15:51 Look, I am telling you a mystery: not all of us will sleep, but all of us will be changed,
15:52 in an instant, in the flick of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised free of decay, and we ourselves will be changed.
15:53 This thing that decays has to put on what cannot decay, and this thing that dies has to put on immortality.
15:54 When this thing that decays has put on what cannot decay, and this thing that dies has put on immortality, then the word that is written will come true: "Death has been swallowed up in victory."
15:55 "Death, where is your victory? Death, where is your sting?"
15:56 The sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law.
15:57 But thanks be to God, who is giving us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.
15:58 So then, my dear brothers, be steady, unmovable, always overflowing in the work of the Lord, knowing that in the Lord your toil is not empty.
## The Collection for Jerusalem
16:1 Now about the collection for the saints. Do just what I directed the churches of Galatia to do.
16:2 On the first day of the week, each of you is to put something aside at home, storing up whatever he has had success earning, so that no collections have to be made when I come.
16:3 When I arrive, I will send whoever you approve, with letters, to carry your gift to Jerusalem.
16:4 And if it seems worthwhile for me to go as well, they will go with me.
## Travel Plans
16:5 I will come to you after I pass through Macedonia, for I am planning to pass through Macedonia.
16:6 Perhaps I will stay on with you, or even spend the winter, so that you can send me on my way, wherever I may go.
16:7 I do not want to see you now just in passing. I hope to remain with you for some time, if the Lord permits.
16:8 But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost,
16:9 for a door has opened to me, a great one and a productive one, and there are many opponents.
## Timothy and Apollos
16:10 If Timothy comes, see that he has nothing to fear while he is with you, for he is doing the Lord's work, as I am too.
16:11 Let no one treat him with contempt. Help him on his journey in peace, so that he can come to me, for I am waiting for him with the brothers.
16:12 As for our brother Apollos, I urged him many times to go to you with the brothers. It was not at all his wish to go now, but he will come when he has the chance.
## Last Words
16:13 Keep watch. Stand firm in the faith. Show courage. Be strong.
16:14 Whatever you do should be done in love.
16:15 Brothers, I urge you: you know that the household of Stephanas was the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have devoted themselves to serving the saints.
16:16 I urge you to put yourselves, too, under people like these, and under everyone who joins in the work and labors.
16:17 I am glad about the arrival of Stephanas, Fortunatus and Achaicus, because these men have filled up what was missing on your side.
16:18 They gave rest to my spirit, and to yours. So give recognition to people like these.
## Greetings
16:19 The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca, together with the church that meets in their house, send you many greetings in the Lord.
16:20 All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.
16:21 The greeting is in my own hand, Paul's.
16:22 If anyone does not love the Lord, that person is to be accursed. Marana tha!
16:23 The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.
16:24 My love goes with all of you in Christ Jesus.
