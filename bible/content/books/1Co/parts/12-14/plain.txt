## One Spirit, Many Gifts
12:1 Now about spiritual matters, brothers: I do not want you to be uninformed.
12:2 You know that when you were Gentiles, you were led off to idols that cannot speak, however you were led.
12:3 So I am telling you: no one who speaks by the Spirit of God says, "Jesus is under a curse," and no one can say, "Jesus is Lord," except by the Holy Spirit.
12:4 Gifts are of many kinds, yet the Spirit is the same.
12:5 Services are of many kinds, and the Lord is the same.
12:6 Workings are of many kinds, but it is the same God who works every one of them in everyone.
12:7 Each person is given a way of showing the Spirit, so that all may benefit.
12:8 Through the Spirit one person receives a message of wisdom, and another a message of knowledge, by that same Spirit.
12:9 To another, faith, by the same Spirit. To another, gifts of healing, by the one Spirit.
12:10 To another, works of power. To another, prophecy. To another, telling spirits apart. To another, different kinds of tongues. To another, the interpreting of tongues.
12:11 One and the same Spirit works all these things, handing them out to each person as he decides.
## One Body, Many Parts
12:12 Think of the body. It is one, yet it has many parts, and all the parts of the body, many as they are, make up one body. So also is the Christ.
12:13 In one Spirit, in fact, all of us were baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, slaves or free people, and all of us were given a drink of one Spirit.
12:14 The body is not one part but many.
12:15 Suppose the foot says, "I am not a hand, so I do not belong to the body." That does not make it any less a part of the body.
12:16 And suppose the ear says, "I am not an eye, so I do not belong to the body." That does not make it any less a part of the body.
12:17 If the whole body were an eye, where would hearing be? If the whole body were hearing, where would the sense of smell be?
12:18 But as it is, God has set each one of the parts in the body, just as he wanted.
12:19 If everything were one part, where would the body be?
12:20 As it is, there are many parts, but one body.
12:21 An eye cannot tell a hand, "I can do without you," or a head tell the feet, "I can do without you."
12:22 No, it is the other way around: the body's weaker-looking parts are the ones we cannot do without.
12:23 The parts of the body we think are less honorable, we wrap in extra honor. The parts we would not show get extra modesty,
12:24 while our presentable parts need nothing. But God blended the body together and gave extra honor to the part that lacked it,
12:25 so that there would be no split in the body, and the parts would care for one another in the same way.
12:26 When one part hurts, every part hurts along with it. When one part is honored, every part celebrates with it.
12:27 Now you are the body of Christ, and each of you is a part of it.
12:28 God has placed these in the church: first, apostles; second, prophets; third, teachers; then works of power; then gifts of healing, acts of help, acts of steering, and different kinds of tongues.
12:29 Not everyone is an apostle, is he? Not everyone is a prophet? Not everyone a teacher? Not everyone a worker of powerful deeds?
12:30 Not everyone has gifts of healing, do they? Not everyone speaks in tongues? Not everyone interprets?
12:31 Be eager for the greater gifts. And besides this, I will show you a way beyond measure.
## The Way of Love
13:1 I may speak the languages of humans and of angels. If I have no love, I have become ringing bronze, a clashing cymbal.
13:2 I may have prophecy, and understand all the mysteries and all knowledge. I may have all faith, enough to move mountains. If I have no love, I am nothing.
13:3 I may give away everything I own, and hand over my body so that I can boast. If I have no love, it does me no good.
13:4 Love is slow to anger and acts with kindness. Love is not jealous. Love does not show off and is not puffed up.
13:5 It does not act shamefully, does not look after its own interests, is not easily stirred to anger, and does not count up the harm done to it.
13:6 It takes no pleasure in injustice but shares the joy of the truth.
13:7 It covers all things, trusts in all things, hopes in all things, and holds firm through all things.
## Love Outlasts the Gifts
13:8 Love never collapses. But prophecies will be set aside, tongues will stop, and knowledge will be set aside.
13:9 Our knowledge is partial, and our prophecy is partial.
13:10 But when the complete comes, the partial will be set aside.
13:11 As a child I talked like a child, thought like a child and reasoned like a child. Now that I have become a man, I have put away the things of a child.
13:12 For now we look through a mirror, and what we see is a riddle. But then we will see face to face. For now I know only in part. Then I will know fully, just as I have been fully known.
13:13 But now these three last: faith, hope and love. The greatest of them is love.
## Prophecy and Tongues
14:1 Run after love, and be eager for the gifts of the Spirit, above all that you may prophesy.
14:2 The one who speaks in a tongue speaks not to people but to God. No one catches his meaning, yet in the Spirit he speaks mysteries.
14:3 The one who prophesies brings people building up, encouragement and comfort by what he says.
14:4 The one who speaks in a tongue builds up himself. The one who prophesies builds up the church.
14:5 I wish that all of you spoke in tongues, but even more that you prophesied. The one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks in tongues, unless that person also interprets, so that the church gets built up.
14:6 Brothers, suppose I come to you speaking in tongues. What good will I do you, unless what I say to you brings a revelation, or knowledge, or prophecy, or teaching?
14:7 Take lifeless things that make a sound, a flute or a lyre. If they do not give distinct notes, how can anyone know what is being piped or plucked?
14:8 And if a trumpet gives an unclear call, who will get ready for battle?
14:9 It is the same with you. Unless your tongue gives clear speech, how will anyone know what is said? You will be talking into the air.
14:10 There may be any number of kinds of speech in the world, and none of them is without a voice.
14:11 So if I do not know the force of the sound, then to the person speaking I will be a foreigner, and the speaker will be a foreigner to me.
14:12 It is the same with you. Since you are so eager for what the Spirit gives, aim to overflow with the things that build up the church.
14:13 For this reason, the one who speaks in a tongue should pray to be able to interpret.
14:14 If I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind produces nothing.
14:15 What does this mean for me? I will pray with my spirit, and with my mind as well. I will sing praise with my spirit, and with my mind as well.
14:16 Otherwise, if you give a blessing in the spirit, how can the person in the place of the uninstructed say "Amen" when you give thanks? He does not know what you are saying.
14:17 You may give thanks well enough, but the other person is not built up.
14:18 More than any of you, I speak in tongues, and I thank God for it.
14:19 But in the church gathering I prefer five words spoken with my mind, so that I can teach others too, over ten thousand words in a tongue.
14:20 Brothers, stop being children in how you think. Be little children when it comes to evil, but when it comes to thinking, be fully grown.
14:21 It is written in the law: "With other tongues and with the lips of others I will speak to this people, and even then they will not listen to me," says the Lord.
14:22 So tongues are a sign, not for those who believe but for those who do not. Prophecy is not for those who do not believe but for those who do.
14:23 Suppose the whole church meets in one place and everyone speaks in tongues, and people who are uninstructed or who do not believe come in. Will they not say you are out of your minds?
14:24 But suppose everyone prophesies, and an unbeliever or an uninstructed person comes in. He is convicted by all of them, he is examined by all of them,
14:25 and the secrets of his heart come into the open. Then he will fall on his face and bow down to God, announcing, "God is truly among you!"
## Order When You Meet
14:26 What follows, brothers? When you come together, each of you has a psalm, a teaching, a revelation, a tongue, an interpretation. Everything must aim at building up.
14:27 If someone speaks in a tongue, let it be two at most, or three, each in turn, and let one person interpret.
14:28 But if there is no interpreter, let that person keep silent in the church and speak to himself and to God.
14:29 Let two or three prophets speak, and let the others weigh what is said.
14:30 If a revelation comes to someone else who is sitting there, let the first speaker be silent.
14:31 You can all prophesy, one at a time, so that everyone may learn and everyone may be encouraged.
14:32 The prophets' spirits submit to the prophets.
14:33 For God is not a God of disorder but of peace, as in all the churches of the saints.
## The Women in the Churches
14:34 In the churches, the women are to be silent. They are not given permission to speak. Instead, let them submit, as the law also says.
14:35 If they want to learn anything, their own husbands are the ones to question at home, since it is shameful for a woman to speak in church.
14:36 Or did God's word go out from you? Or did it reach only you?
## Final Words on the Gifts
14:37 If anyone thinks he is a prophet, or spiritual, he must recognize that what I am writing to you is the Lord's command.
14:38 If anyone does not recognize this, he is not recognized.
14:39 So, my brothers, keep being eager to prophesy, and do not stop anyone from speaking in tongues.
14:40 But let everything be done in a proper way, with order.
