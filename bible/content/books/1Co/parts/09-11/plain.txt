## Paul Gives Up His Rights
9:1 Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Have I not seen Jesus our Lord? Are you not my work in the Lord?
9:2 If I am not an apostle to others, at least I am one to you. You yourselves are the seal of my apostleship in the Lord.
9:3 This is my defense to those who question me.
9:4 Do we not have the right to eat and drink?
9:5 Do we not have the right to take along a wife who is a believer, as the other apostles do, and the Lord's brothers, and Cephas?
9:6 Or is it only Barnabas and I who have no right to stop working for a living?
9:7 What soldier ever serves at his own expense? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat its fruit? Who shepherds a flock and does not drink the milk?
9:8 Am I saying this only from a human point of view? Does the law not say the same thing?
9:9 For in the law of Moses it is written, "You shall not muzzle an ox that is treading out the grain." Is it oxen that God cares about?
9:10 Or is he speaking entirely for our sake? Yes, it was written for our sake. The one who plows should plow in hope, and the one who threshes should thresh in hope of a share.
9:11 We planted spiritual things among you. Is it a great thing if we harvest material things from you?
9:12 If others have a claim on you, do we not have a stronger one? But we have not used this right. We put up with everything instead, so that we place no obstacle in front of the good news of Christ.
9:13 Do you not know that people who work at the sacred things eat what comes from the temple, and that those who attend the altar have their share from the altar?
9:14 In the same way the Lord gave an order that those who announce the good news are to live from the good news.
9:15 But I have used none of these rights. I am not writing this so that it will be done that way for me. I would rather die than have anyone make my boast empty.
9:16 If I tell the good news, I have nothing to boast about, because a necessity is laid on me. Woe to me if I do not tell the good news!
9:17 If I do this willingly, I have a reward. If I do it unwillingly, I have still been trusted with a stewardship.
9:18 What, then, is my reward? That when I tell the good news I give it away free, so that I do not use up my right in the good news.
## Becoming Everything to Win Some
9:19 Though I am free from everyone, I made myself everyone's slave, so that I might win more of them.
9:20 To the Jews I became like a Jew, to win Jews. To those under the law I became like one under the law, though I am not myself under the law, to win those under the law.
9:21 To those outside the law I became like one outside the law, though I am not outside God's law but under Christ's law, to win those outside the law.
9:22 To the weak I became weak, to win the weak. I have become all things to everyone, so that in every possible way I might save some.
9:23 I do everything for the sake of the good news, so that I may share in it with them.
## Running to Win
9:24 Surely you know that on a race course everyone runs, but only one gets the prize. Run that way, so that you take hold of it.
9:25 Everyone who competes controls himself in everything. They do it to receive a crown that fades. We do it for one that never fades.
9:26 So I run, not like someone who has no goal. I box, not like someone beating the air.
9:27 But I batter my body and lead it off as a slave, in case I proclaim to others and then am disqualified myself.
## Warnings from the Wilderness
10:1 Brothers, I want you to know this: our fathers were all under the cloud, and all went through the sea.
10:2 All of them were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea.
10:3 All of them ate the same spiritual food,
10:4 and all of them drank the same spiritual drink. They were drinking from a spiritual rock that went along with them, and that rock was the Christ.
10:5 Yet God was not pleased with most of them, for they were struck down and left lying in the wilderness.
10:6 These things became examples for us, so that we would not crave evil things as they craved them.
10:7 Do not become idol worshipers, as some of them did. As it is written, "The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play."
10:8 Let us not commit sexual immorality, as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in one day.
10:9 Let us not put the Christ to the test, as some of them did, and were destroyed by snakes.
10:10 And do not grumble, as some of them grumbled, and were destroyed by the Destroyer.
10:11 These things kept happening to them as examples, and they were written down as a warning for us, on whom the ends of the ages have come.
10:12 So let the one who thinks he is standing take care that he does not fall.
10:13 You have faced only the kind of testing that people face. And God is faithful. He will not allow you to be tested beyond your strength. Along with the test he will provide the way out, so that you are able to stand up under it.
## The Lord's Table and the Table of Demons
10:14 So, my loved ones, run away from the worship of idols.
10:15 I am speaking to sensible people. Judge for yourselves what I say.
10:16 The cup of blessing that we bless: is it not a sharing in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break: is it not a sharing in the body of Christ?
10:17 Because there is one loaf, we who are many are one body. For we all share in the one loaf.
10:18 Look at Israel according to the flesh. Do those who eat the sacrifices not share in the altar?
10:19 What am I saying, then? That food offered to an idol is anything, or that an idol is anything?
10:20 No, I am saying that what they sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God. I do not want you to become partners with demons.
10:21 You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons. You cannot share in the table of the Lord and the table of demons.
10:22 Or are we making the Lord jealous? Are we stronger than he is?
## Do Everything for God's Glory
10:23 "Everything is lawful," but not everything is helpful. "Everything is lawful," but not everything builds up.
10:24 No one should look for his own good, but for the good of the other person.
10:25 Eat anything that is sold in the meat market, asking no questions because of conscience.
10:26 For the earth and all that fills it belong to the Lord.
10:27 If an unbeliever invites you and you want to go, eat whatever is set before you, asking no questions because of conscience.
10:28 But if someone says to you, "This is from a sacrifice," do not eat it, for the sake of the one who told you, and because of conscience.
10:29 I mean the other person's conscience, not your own. For why is my freedom judged by someone else's conscience?
10:30 If I eat with thanks, why am I spoken against over something I give thanks for?
10:31 So whether you eat or drink or do anything else, do everything for the glory of God.
10:32 Give no cause for offense to Jews or to Greeks or to the church of God,
10:33 just as I also try to please everyone in everything. I am not looking for my own benefit but for that of the many, so that they may be saved.
11:1 Be imitators of me, as I also am of Christ.
## Heads and Hair in Worship
11:2 I praise you that you remember me in everything, and that you hold on to the traditions just as I handed them on to you.
11:3 But I want you to know that the head of every man is Christ, the head of a woman is the man, and the head of Christ is God.
11:4 Every man who prays or prophesies with something hanging down from his head dishonors his head.
11:5 But every woman who prays or prophesies with her head uncovered dishonors her head. It is one and the same as having her head shaved.
11:6 If a woman will not cover herself, let her also have her hair cropped. But since it is shameful for a woman to be cropped or shaved, let her cover herself.
11:7 A man ought not to cover his head, since he is the image and glory of God. But the woman is the glory of man.
11:8 For man does not come from woman, but woman from man.
11:9 Also, man was not created for the sake of woman, but woman for the sake of man.
11:10 For this reason a woman ought to have authority on her head, because of the angels.
11:11 Still, in the Lord, woman is not apart from man, and man is not apart from woman.
11:12 For just as woman came from man, so man comes through woman. And everything comes from God.
11:13 Judge for yourselves: is it fitting for a woman to pray to God uncovered?
11:14 Does not even nature itself teach you that it is a dishonor for a man to wear his hair long,
11:15 but for a woman to wear it long is her glory? For her hair has been given to her in place of a covering.
11:16 If anyone seems to want an argument, we have no such custom, and neither do the churches of God.
## The Lord's Supper
11:17 In giving this instruction, I cannot praise you, because you come together not for the better but for the worse.
11:18 First, I hear that when you come together as a church there are divisions among you, and I partly believe it.
11:19 There have to be factions among you, so that the approved ones may become clear among you.
11:20 So when you meet together in one place, what you are eating is not the Lord's Supper.
11:21 For as you eat, each person takes his own supper ahead of the rest. One is hungry, and another is drunk.
11:22 Do you not have houses to eat and drink in? Or do you look down on the church of God and shame those who have nothing? What can I say to you? Shall I praise you? In this I do not praise you!
11:23 For I received from the Lord what I also handed on to you: that the Lord Jesus, on the night he was handed over, took bread,
11:24 he gave thanks, broke it, and said, "This is my body, which is for you. Do this to remember me."
11:25 In the same way he took the cup after the meal, saying, "This cup is the new covenant, made in my blood. Do this, as often as you drink it, to remember me."
11:26 You announce the Lord's death whenever you eat this bread and drink the cup, until he comes.
11:27 So whoever eats the bread or drinks the Lord's cup in an unworthy way will be held answerable for the body and blood of the Lord.
11:28 A person should examine himself, and only then eat of the bread and drink of the cup.
11:29 For the one who eats and drinks eats and drinks judgment on himself if he does not discern the body.
11:30 That is why many among you are weak and sick, and quite a few have fallen asleep.
11:31 But if we judged ourselves, we would not come under judgment.
11:32 But when we are judged by the Lord, we are being disciplined, so that we will not be condemned along with the world.
11:33 Therefore, my brothers, when you meet to eat, wait for each other.
11:34 If anyone is hungry, he should eat at home, so that you do not come together for judgment. I will set the other matters in order when I come.
