## A Man with His Father's Wife
5:1 It is actually reported that there is sexual sin among you, a kind not even found among the Gentiles: a man has his father's wife.
5:2 And you are puffed up! Would you not rather have mourned, so that the man who did this deed would be taken out from among you?
5:3 As for me, though I am away in body, I am with you in spirit, and as if I were there I have already passed judgment on the man who has done this.
5:4 When you are gathered in the name of our Lord Jesus, and my spirit is with you, with the power of our Lord Jesus,
5:5 hand such a man over to Satan, for the ruin of his flesh, so that on the day of the Lord his spirit may be saved.
5:6 Boasting like yours is not good. Do you not know that a little yeast spreads through the whole batch of dough?
5:7 Sweep out the old yeast, so that you can be fresh dough, since you are already unleavened bread. For our Passover, Christ, has been slaughtered.
5:8 So then, let us keep the feast. Let us not use old yeast, the yeast of malice and wickedness, but the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.
## Judging Those Inside
5:9 In my letter I wrote to you not to mix with people who commit sexual sin.
5:10 I did not mean the people of this world who commit sexual sin, or the greedy and the swindlers, or the idol worshipers. Then you would have to go out of the world altogether!
5:11 But what I am writing to you now is this: do not mix with anyone who is called a brother and who is a sexual sinner, or greedy, or an idol worshiper, or a slanderer, or a drunkard, or a swindler. With such a person you must not even eat.
5:12 Why should it be my job to judge outsiders? You judge those inside, do you not?
5:13 God judges those outside. "Remove the evil person from among yourselves."
## Lawsuits among Believers
6:1 If any of you has a matter against another, how dare you have it judged by the unrighteous and not by the saints?
6:2 Or do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if the world is to be judged by you, are you unfit to decide the smallest cases?
6:3 Do you not know that we will judge angels, let alone matters of everyday life?
6:4 So if you have cases about everyday matters, do you set up as judges the very people who count for nothing in the church?
6:5 I say this to make you ashamed. Is there really not one wise person among you who can decide between brother and brother?
6:6 Instead, one brother takes another to court, and before unbelievers!
6:7 It is already a complete loss for you that you have cases against each other at all. Why do you not put up with the wrong instead? Why do you not let yourselves be cheated?
6:8 No, you are the ones who do wrong and cheat, and to your own brothers!
## Who Will Not Inherit the Kingdom
6:9 Or do you not know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived. Neither those who commit sexual sin, nor idol worshipers, nor adulterers, nor soft men, nor men who lie with males,
6:10 nor thieves, nor the greedy, nor drunkards, nor slanderers, nor swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God.
6:11 And some of you were like this. But you were washed. But you were made holy. But you were justified, in the name of the Lord Jesus and in the Spirit of our God.
## The Body Is for the Lord
6:12 "Everything is permitted for me." But not everything helps. "Everything is permitted for me." But I will not come under the authority of anything.
6:13 "Foods are for the stomach, and the stomach is for foods." But God will bring an end to both. The body, however, is not for sexual sin but for the Lord, and the Lord is for the body.
6:14 God both raised the Lord and will raise us up through his power.
6:15 Do you not know that your bodies are parts of Christ? Shall I then take the parts of Christ and make them parts of a prostitute? By no means!
6:16 Or do you not know that the man who joins himself to a prostitute is one body with her? For it says, "The two will become one flesh."
6:17 But the one who joins himself to the Lord is one spirit with him.
6:18 Run away from sexual sin! Every sin that anyone does lies outside the body. But the person who sins sexually is sinning against his own body.
6:19 Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from God? You are not your own,
6:20 for you were bought, and a price was paid. So bring glory to God in your body.
## Questions about Marriage
7:1 Now about the matters you wrote about. "It is good for a man not to touch a woman."
7:2 But because of the sexual sins that go on, each man should have his own wife, and each woman her own husband.
7:3 The husband should give his wife what he owes her, and the wife should do the same for her husband.
7:4 A wife has no authority over her own body; her husband has it. In the same way a husband has no authority over his own body; his wife has it.
7:5 Do not hold back from each other, unless you both agree, and only for a time, so that you are free to give yourselves to prayer. Then come together again, so that Satan does not test you because you cannot control yourselves.
7:6 I say this as something I allow, not as an order.
7:7 I would like everyone to be as I am. But each person has a gift of his own from God, one this kind, another that kind.
## The Unmarried and the Widows
7:8 To the unmarried and to the widows I say: it is good for them if they stay as I am.
7:9 But if they cannot control themselves, they should marry. Marrying is better than burning.
## The Married and Divorce
7:10 To the married I give this order, and it is not mine but the Lord's: a wife must not separate from her husband.
7:11 But if she does separate, she is to stay single, or else be reconciled with her husband. And a husband must not divorce his wife.
7:12 To the rest I say this, I and not the Lord: if any brother has a wife who does not believe, and she agrees to go on living with him, he must not divorce her.
7:13 And if a woman has a husband who does not believe, and he agrees to go on living with her, she must not divorce her husband.
7:14 For the husband who does not believe has been made holy in his wife, and the wife who does not believe has been made holy in the brother. If not, your children are unclean. But now they are holy.
7:15 But if the one who does not believe leaves, let that person leave. The brother or sister is not enslaved in cases like this. God has called us to live in peace.
7:16 For how do you know, wife, whether you will save your husband? Or how do you know, husband, whether you will save your wife?
## Stay as You Were Called
7:17 Except this: each person should live the life the Lord has given him, the one he was in when God called him. This is what I order in all the churches.
7:18 Was anyone already circumcised when called? He should not try to undo it. Was anyone uncircumcised when called? He should not be circumcised.
7:19 Being circumcised is nothing, and being uncircumcised is nothing. What matters is keeping God's commands.
7:20 Each person should stay in the situation in which he was called.
7:21 Were you a slave when you were called? Do not let it worry you. But if you are able to become free, make use of it instead.
7:22 For a slave who was called in the Lord is the Lord's freed person. In the same way, a free person who was called is Christ's slave.
7:23 You were bought for a price. Do not become slaves of other people.
7:24 Brothers, each person should stay in the situation he was called in, and stay there with God.
## Concerning Virgins
7:25 Now about the virgins. I have no command from the Lord, but I give my judgment, as someone who has received mercy from the Lord and so can be trusted.
7:26 I think, then, that this is good because of the pressure of the present time: it is good for a person to stay as he is.
7:27 Are you tied to a wife? Do not try to be set free. Are you free of a wife? Do not look for one.
7:28 But if you do marry, you have not sinned. And if a virgin marries, she has not sinned. Still, such people will have trouble in the flesh, and I want to spare you.
7:29 What I mean, brothers, is this: the time has been cut short. From now on, those who have wives should be as people who do not have them,
7:30 those who weep as though they did not weep, those who rejoice as though they did not rejoice, those who buy as though they kept nothing,
7:31 and those who use the world as though they did not use it to the full. For the shape of this world is passing away.
7:32 I want you to be free of cares. The unmarried man is concerned with the things of the Lord, how he can please the Lord.
7:33 But the married man is concerned with the things of the world, how he can please his wife,
7:34 and he is divided. And the unmarried woman, the virgin, is concerned with the things of the Lord, so that she may be holy in body and in spirit. But the married woman is concerned with the things of the world, how she can please her husband.
7:35 I am saying this for your own good, not to throw a noose over you, but for what is fitting and for devotion to the Lord without distraction.
## The Man and His Virgin
7:36 But if anyone thinks he is behaving improperly toward his virgin, if she is past the bloom of youth and it must be so, let him do what he wishes. He is not sinning. Let them marry.
7:37 But the man who stands firm in his heart, who is under no pressure and has control over his own will, and who has decided in his own heart to keep his virgin, will do well.
7:38 So then, the one who sees to the marriage of his virgin does well, and the one who does not see to it will do better.
## Widows
7:39 A wife is bound as long as her husband lives. But if the husband dies, she is free to marry the man she chooses, but only in the Lord.
7:40 In my judgment she is more blessed if she stays as she is. For I think I also have God's Spirit.
## Food Sacrificed to Idols
8:1 Now about food sacrificed to idols. We know that "all of us have knowledge." Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.
8:2 If anyone thinks he has come to know something, he has not yet known it the way it must be known.
8:3 But if anyone loves God, that person is known by him.
8:4 So about eating food sacrificed to idols, we know that "an idol is nothing in the world," and that "there is no God but one."
8:5 For even if there are so-called gods, in heaven or on earth (and there are many gods and many lords),
8:6 yet for us there is one God, the Father. All things are from him, and we are for him. And there is one Lord, Jesus Christ. All things are through him, and we are through him.
8:7 But not everyone has this knowledge. Some, because they have been used to idols until now, eat the food as food really sacrificed to an idol, and their conscience, being weak, is stained.
8:8 Food will not bring us before God. If we eat, we do not have more. If we do not eat, we do not have less.
8:9 But watch out, so that this right of yours does not become something for the weak to trip over.
8:10 Suppose someone sees you, who have this knowledge, sitting at a meal in an idol's temple. Will his conscience, since he is weak, not be "built up" to eat food sacrificed to idols?
8:11 So the weak person is being destroyed by your knowledge, a brother for whom Christ died.
8:12 In sinning like this against brothers and striking their weak conscience, you sin against Christ.
8:13 So if food causes my brother to stumble, I will never eat meat at all, so that I do not cause my brother to stumble.
