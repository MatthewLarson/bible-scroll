## Greeting
1:1 From Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, and from Timothy our brother. To the church of God in Corinth, together with all the saints throughout Achaia.
1:2 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
## Praise to the God of Comfort
1:3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. He is the Father of compassion and the God of all comfort.
1:4 He is the one who comforts us whenever trouble comes to us. This is so that we can comfort people in every kind of trouble, using the comfort God has given us.
1:5 For as the sufferings of Christ overflow toward us, so through Christ our comfort overflows too.
1:6 If we are in trouble, it is for your comfort and salvation. If we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which does its work in you as you patiently endure the very sufferings we also suffer.
1:7 Our hope for you is firm. We know that as you share in the sufferings, so you share in the comfort.
## Trouble in Asia
1:8 We do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of the trouble that came to us in Asia. We were weighed down far beyond our power, so much that we gave up hope even of living.
1:9 In fact, inside ourselves we had received the verdict of death. This was so that we would not trust in ourselves, but in God, who raises the dead.
1:10 He rescued us from so great a death, and he will rescue us. We have put our hope in him, that he will rescue us again,
1:11 while you join in helping us by your prayer. Then the gift given to us through the help of many people will bring thanks from many faces on our behalf.
## Why Paul Changed His Plans
1:12 This is what we boast about, and our conscience bears witness to it: that we have lived in the world, and most of all toward you, in a holiness and sincerity that come from God. It was not by the wisdom of the flesh but by the grace of God.
1:13 We write you nothing except what you can read and also recognize. I hope you will recognize it right to the end,
1:14 just as you have already recognized us in part: that we are something for you to boast about, as you are for us, on the day of our Lord Jesus.
1:15 Because I was sure of this, I wanted to come to you first, so that you would have a second gift of grace.
1:16 I meant to pass by way of you into Macedonia, to come to you again from Macedonia, and then to be sent on my way to Judea by you.
1:17 Since this was my wish, did I act lightly? Or do I make my plans by the flesh, so that with me it is "Yes, yes" and "No, no" at the same time?
1:18 As God is faithful, our word to you is not "Yes" and "No."
1:19 For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was proclaimed among you through us, through me and Silvanus and Timothy, did not become "Yes" and "No." In him it has always been "Yes."
1:20 For however many promises God has made, in him is the "Yes." That is why the "Amen" also goes up to God through him, bringing glory through us.
1:21 It is God who makes us firm, together with you, in Christ. He is the one who anointed us,
1:22 who also stamped us with his seal and put the Spirit in our hearts as a down payment.
1:23 I call God as my witness, with my own life at stake: it was to spare you that I did not come again to Corinth.
1:24 It is not that we rule over your faith. We are working with you for your joy, because you stand firm by faith.
## The Painful Visit and Letter
2:1 For I decided this for myself: I would not come to you again in grief.
2:2 For if I grieve you, who is there to make me glad, except the one I have grieved?
2:3 I wrote what I did so that I would not arrive and meet grief from the very people who ought to make me glad. I trusted all of you: my joy is the joy of every one of you.
2:4 My trouble was great and my heart was squeezed tight when I wrote to you, and I wrote through many tears. I did not mean to grieve you. I wanted you to know how very much I love you.
## Forgiving the Offender
2:5 If anyone has caused grief, he has grieved not me but, in part (I say "in part" so as not to burden you all), all of you.
2:6 This punishment from the majority is enough for such a person.
2:7 So now you should do the opposite: forgive him and comfort him, or he may be swallowed up by too much grief.
2:8 So I urge you to confirm your love for him.
2:9 I also wrote for this reason, to learn how you stand the test: are you obedient in everything?
2:10 Anyone you forgive anything, I forgive too. And what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, I have done for your sake, before the face of Christ,
2:11 so that Satan will not take advantage of us. For we are not ignorant of what he has in mind.
## From Troas to Macedonia
2:12 When I came to Troas for the good news of Christ, a door was opened for me in the Lord.
2:13 But I could not settle in my spirit, because I did not find my brother Titus. I took leave of them and set out for Macedonia.
## The Victory Parade
2:14 But thanks be to God, who always leads us in his victory parade in Christ, and through us spreads the scent of knowing him in every place.
2:15 For we are the sweet perfume of Christ to God, among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing.
2:16 To the one group we are a smell that comes from death and leads to death. To the other we are a scent that comes from life and leads to life. And who is up to this task?
2:17 For we are not like the many who peddle God's word. We speak from sincerity, from God, and before God, in Christ.
## A Letter Written on Hearts
3:1 Are we starting again to vouch for ourselves? Or do we need, like certain others, letters of introduction to you or from you?
3:2 You yourselves are our letter, inscribed on our hearts, known and read by every person.
3:3 It is plain that you are a letter of Christ, delivered through our ministry, written not in ink but by the Spirit of the living God, not on stone tablets but on tablets that are hearts of flesh.
## Two Ministries, Two Glories
3:4 Such is the confidence we have toward God through Christ.
3:5 It is not that we are able, by ourselves, to reckon anything as coming from us. Our ability comes from God.
3:6 He has made us able to be servants of a new covenant, not of the letter but of the Spirit. For the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.
3:7 Now if the ministry of death, engraved in letters on stones, came with glory, so that the Israelites could not look steadily at the face of Moses because of the glory of his face, a glory that was being set aside,
3:8 how much more glory will the ministry of the Spirit have?
3:9 For if the ministry of condemnation had glory, the ministry of righteousness overflows with much more glory.
3:10 Indeed, the thing that was glorified has not been glorified at all in this respect, since the glory beyond it is so great.
3:11 For if what was being set aside came with glory, much more glory belongs to what remains.
## The Veil
3:12 So, since we have such a hope, we speak with great freedom,
3:13 and not like Moses, who put a veil over his face so that the Israelites would not look steadily at the end of what was being set aside.
3:14 But their minds were hardened. For to this very day, when the old covenant is read, the same veil stays in place and is not lifted, because in Christ it is being set aside.
3:15 Even today, whenever Moses is read aloud, a veil covers their heart.
3:16 But whenever someone turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away.
3:17 Now, the Lord is the Spirit. And where the Lord's Spirit is, there is freedom.
3:18 All of us, our faces uncovered, gaze at the glory of the Lord as in a mirror. We are being changed into that same image, from glory to glory. The change is from the Lord, who is the Spirit.
## An Open Ministry
4:1 So, since we have this ministry, as people who have received mercy, we do not lose heart.
4:2 We have given up the shameful things people hide. We do not walk in trickery or falsify the word of God. By making the truth plain, we commend ourselves to the conscience of every person, in God's sight.
4:3 And if our good news is still covered by a veil, it is covered for those who are perishing.
4:4 The god of this age has blinded the minds of these unbelievers, so that the light of the good news about the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, cannot shine on them.
4:5 For we do not preach ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, and ourselves as your slaves for Jesus' sake.
4:6 For God, who said, "Out of darkness light will shine," is the one who shone in our hearts, so that the knowledge of God's glory, seen in the face of Christ, would give light.
## Treasure in Clay Pots
4:7 But we have this treasure in clay pots, so that the surpassing power will be God's and not ours.
4:8 In every way we are squeezed, but not crushed; at a loss, but not in despair;
4:9 hunted down, but not abandoned; knocked down, but not destroyed.
4:10 We always carry around in the body the dying of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be shown in our body.
4:11 For we who are alive are always being handed over to death because of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be shown in our mortal flesh.
4:12 So death works in us, and life in you.
4:13 We have the same spirit of faith as in what is written: "I believed, and so I spoke." We too believe, and so we too speak,
4:14 knowing that the one who raised the Lord Jesus will raise us also with Jesus, and will present us together with you.
4:15 All this is for your sake, so that grace, as it reaches more and more people, may make thanksgiving increase, to the glory of God.
## Seeing the Unseen
4:16 So we do not lose heart. Even if our outer person is falling to ruin, our inner person is made new, day after day.
4:17 Our trouble is slight and lasts only a moment. It is producing for us a weight of glory beyond all measure, lasting forever.
4:18 We keep our eyes not on the things we can see but on the things we cannot see. What we see lasts only a while; what we cannot see lasts forever.
## The Tent and the Building
5:1 We know that if the earthly house we live in, this tent, is torn down, we have from God a building, a house made by no human hands, lasting forever in the heavens.
5:2 Yes, in this tent we groan, longing to be dressed over with our dwelling from heaven.
5:3 If we are indeed dressed, we will not be found naked.
5:4 While we are in this tent we groan under the weight. It is not that we want to be undressed, but to be dressed over, so that life may swallow up what is mortal.
5:5 God is the one who made us ready for this, and he gave us the Spirit as a down payment.
5:6 So we always take heart, knowing that as long as we live at home in the body, we are away from the Lord.
5:7 (For we walk by faith, not by what we see.)
5:8 We are confident, and we would much prefer to be away from the body and at home with the Lord.
5:9 So our ambition, whether at home or away, is to please him.
5:10 For all of us must be shown openly before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may receive what is due for the things done through the body, according to what he did, whether good or bad.
## Persuaded by Love
5:11 So, knowing the fear of the Lord, we try to persuade people. We are plain to God, and I hope we are plain to your consciences too.
5:12 Again, we are not vouching for ourselves to you. We are giving you a reason to be proud of us, so that you can answer those who boast about appearances and not about the heart.
5:13 If we are out of our minds, it is for God. If we are thinking clearly, it is for you.
5:14 The love of Christ holds us in its grip, because we have reached this conclusion: one died for all, and so all died.
5:15 He died for all so that the living would live no longer for themselves, but for the one who died for them and was raised.
## A New Creation
5:16 So from now on we know no one by the flesh. Even if we once knew Christ by the flesh, we no longer know him that way.
5:17 So if anyone is in Christ: new creation! The old things have passed away. Look, new things have come into being.
## The Ministry of Reconciliation
5:18 Everything comes from God. Through Christ he reconciled us to himself, and he gave us the ministry of reconciliation.
5:19 What I mean is this: God was in Christ, reconciling the world to himself. He did not reckon their trespasses to them, and he placed in us the word of reconciliation.
5:20 So we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were pleading through us. We beg you on behalf of Christ: be reconciled to God.
5:21 God made the one who did not know sin to be sin for us, so that in him we could become the righteousness of God.
