## Now Is the Time of Favor
6:1 As fellow workers, we also urge you not to receive the grace of God for nothing.
6:2 For he says, "At a time of favor I listened to you, and on a day of salvation I helped you." Look, now is the time of favor! Look, now is the day of salvation!
## Servants Who Prove Themselves
6:3 We lay nothing in anyone's path to trip them, so that nobody can point to a blemish on our ministry.
6:4 Instead, in all we do we show ourselves to be God's servants: in great endurance, in troubles, in hardships, in distresses,
6:5 in beatings, in prisons, in uproars, in toil, in nights without sleep, in going without food,
6:6 in purity, in knowledge, in patience, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in love that is real,
6:7 in the word of truth, in the power of God, and with the weapons of righteousness, right-handed and left-handed,
6:8 through glory and dishonor, through bad reports and good reports. We are called impostors, and yet we are true.
6:9 We are unknown, and yet recognized. We are dying, and look, we live on. We are punished, and yet not put to death.
6:10 We are grieving, yet always glad. We are poor, yet we make many rich. We own nothing, yet we hold everything.
## Open Your Hearts
6:11 Our mouth is open to you, Corinthians. Our heart has grown wide.
6:12 You are not cramped by us. You are cramped in your own feelings.
6:13 Pay us back in kind. I am speaking to you as to my children: open your hearts wide too.
## Do Not Be Mismatched
6:14 Do not hitch yourselves to unbelievers in a yoke that does not match. What share do righteousness and lawlessness have? Or what does light have in common with darkness?
6:15 How can Christ be in tune with Beliar? Or what part does a believer have with an unbeliever?
6:16 How can God's temple agree with idols? For it is we who are the temple of the living God. As God said, "I will live in them and walk about among them. I will be their God, and they will be my people."
6:17 "Therefore get out from the middle of them and keep yourselves apart," says the Lord. "Touch nothing unclean, and I will take you in.
6:18 I will be your father, and you will be my sons and my daughters. So says the Lord Almighty."
7:1 Since these promises are ours, dear friends, let us wash ourselves clean of everything that soils flesh and spirit. Let us carry holiness through to the end, living in the fear of God.
## Make Room for Us
7:2 Give us room. We have wronged no one, ruined no one, taken advantage of no one.
7:3 My words are not meant as a condemnation. As I said before, you are in our hearts, so that we die together and live together.
7:4 I speak to you very frankly. I boast about you a great deal. I am full of comfort. In all our trouble I overflow with joy.
## Titus Brings Comfort
7:5 For even after we came into Macedonia, our flesh found no rest. We were pressed on every side: conflicts outside, fears inside.
7:6 But God, who comforts those who are brought low, comforted us by the arrival of Titus,
7:7 and not only by his coming but also by the comfort he received from you. He reported your longing, your mourning and your zeal on my behalf, and so I rejoiced all the more.
## Grief That Leads to Repentance
7:8 Even if I grieved you by my letter, I do not regret it. Even if I was regretting it, I see that this letter grieved you, if only for an hour.
7:9 Now I rejoice, not because you were grieved, but because your grief brought you to repentance. For you were grieved in God's way, so that you lost nothing through us.
7:10 For grief in God's way produces repentance, and it leads to a salvation no one regrets. But the grief of the world produces death.
7:11 Look at what this very thing, being grieved in God's way, has produced in you: what eagerness! Yes, a defense of yourselves, anger, fear, longing, zeal, and justice carried out! In every way you have shown yourselves clean in this matter.
7:12 So even though I wrote to you, I did not write because of the wrongdoer, or because of the one wronged. I wrote so that your eagerness for us would be made plain to you in the sight of God.
## Joy Over Titus
7:13 This is why we have been comforted. And on top of our comfort, we rejoiced even more at Titus's joy, since his spirit has been set at rest by all of you.
7:14 If I boasted to him about you at all, I was not put to shame. Everything we told you was true, and so too the boasting we did to Titus has turned out true.
7:15 His love for you overflows even more as he remembers how all of you obeyed, and how you took him in with fear and trembling.
7:16 I rejoice that in everything I can be confident about you.
## The Churches of Macedonia
8:1 Brothers, we are letting you know about the grace that God gave among the churches of Macedonia.
8:2 While they were being tested by great trouble, their overflowing joy and their deep poverty poured out into rich generosity.
8:3 I testify that they gave what they could, and beyond what they could. They chose it on their own,
8:4 and with much urging they begged us for the grace of sharing in the service to the saints.
8:5 It went beyond all we had hoped. First they gave themselves to the Lord, and then, through the will of God, to us.
8:6 That is why we urged Titus: as he made the start, so he should bring this grace to completion among you too.
8:7 Just as you overflow in everything, in faith, in speech, in knowledge, in every kind of eagerness, and in the love that is in you from us, see that you overflow in this grace too.
## Christ Became Poor
8:8 I am not giving an order. I am using the eagerness of others to test whether your love is genuine too.
8:9 You know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ. He was rich, and yet he became poor for your sake, so that you would be made rich by his poverty.
8:10 My advice in this matter is for your good: a year ago you were the first not only to do something but to want to.
8:11 So now bring the doing to its end. Your readiness to want it should have a partner in finishing it, out of what you have.
8:12 Where the willingness is present, the gift is welcome, and it is measured by what a person has, not by what he lacks.
8:13 This is not meant to give others ease and you trouble,
8:14 but equality. For now, your surplus meets their need, so that their surplus may also meet your need. In this way there will be equality.
8:15 As it is written: "The one who had much had no extra, and the one who had little did not lack."
## Titus and the Brothers Are Sent
8:16 Thanks be to God, who put the same eagerness for you into the heart of Titus.
8:17 He did accept our urging. But he is so eager himself that he is setting out to you by his own choice.
8:18 We are sending along with him the brother whose praise in the good news is told in all the churches.
8:19 More than that, the churches voted for him to be our traveling companion in carrying out this grace, for the glory of the Lord himself and to show our readiness.
8:20 We are guarding against this: that anyone could blame us over the large gift we are handling.
8:21 We plan ahead for what is good, not only before the Lord but also before people.
8:22 And we are sending with them our brother, whom we have tested in many things, many times, and found eager. Now he is much more eager, because he has such great confidence in you.
8:23 As for Titus, he is my partner and my fellow worker among you. As for our brothers, they are messengers sent by the churches, and a glory to Christ.
8:24 So then, in the presence of the churches, give them the proof of your love and of the boasting we have done about you.
## Ready to Give
9:1 It would be needless for me to write to you about the service for the saints.
9:2 I know how ready you are. I have been bragging about it to the Macedonians: "Achaia has been prepared since last year!" And your zeal has spurred on most of them.
9:3 But I have sent the brothers so that our boasting about you in this matter does not prove empty, and so that you are prepared, as I kept saying you were.
9:4 If Macedonians come with me and find you unready, we would be put to shame (not to speak of you) over this very confidence.
9:5 That is why I thought it necessary to ask the brothers to travel to you ahead of me and set in order beforehand the gift you promised. It should be ready as a blessing and not as greed.
## The Cheerful Giver
9:6 Here is the point: the one who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and the one who sows with blessings will also reap with blessings.
9:7 Each one should give what he has decided in his heart, not out of sorrow and not by force. For God loves a glad giver.
9:8 God has the power to make every grace overflow to you, so that in every way and at all times you have all you need, and still have plenty for every good work.
9:9 As it is written: "He has scattered abroad. He has given to the poor. His righteousness remains forever."
## Thanks to God for His Gift
9:10 The One who provides seed for the sower and bread for food will provide the seed you sow and multiply it, and will make the fruits of your righteousness grow.
9:11 In every way you are being made rich, so that you can be generous in all things. Through us, that generosity brings thanks to God.
9:12 The work of this service does more than supply what the saints are lacking. It also overflows into many thanks to God.
9:13 Through the proof this service gives, they will honor God because of your obedience, which goes with your confession of the good news of Christ, and because of the generosity of your sharing with them and with everyone.
9:14 And as they pray for you, they will long for you, because of the extraordinary grace God has given you.
9:15 Thanks to God for his gift that is beyond telling!
