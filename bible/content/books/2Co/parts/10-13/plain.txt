## Paul Answers His Critics
10:1 Now I myself, Paul, appeal to you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ. I am the one who looks "humble" when I am among you, but is bold toward you when I am away.
10:2 I am begging you: when I come, do not force me to be bold with the confidence I expect to use against some people. They think that we are walking "according to the flesh."
10:3 We do live in the flesh. But we do not wage our war by the standards of the flesh.
10:4 The weapons of our war are not weapons of flesh. Before God they have the power to tear down strongholds.
10:5 We overthrow reasonings, and every lofty thing that lifts itself up against the knowledge of God. We lead every thought away as a prisoner, so that it obeys Christ.
10:6 We are also ready to give every kind of disobedience its punishment, once your own obedience has been made full.
10:7 You are looking at things as they appear on the surface. If anyone is sure in his own mind that he belongs to Christ, let him think this over once more: he belongs to Christ, and so do we.
10:8 Suppose I boast somewhat more about our authority. The Lord gave it to us to build you up, not to tear you down. I will not be ashamed of it.
10:9 I do not want it to look as if I am trying to scare you with my letters.
10:10 "His letters are weighty and strong," someone says, "but when he stands in front of you he is weak, and his speaking is nothing at all."
10:11 A person like that should reckon with this: the men we are in our words, in letters while we are away, are the same men we will be in action when we are there.
## Boasting Within Limits
10:12 We would never dare to put ourselves on the same list as certain people who recommend themselves, or to measure ourselves against them. They take themselves as the ruler, and what they hold it up to is themselves. That shows how little they understand.
10:13 We will not boast about things beyond our limits. We will boast only within the limit of the field that God has measured out to us, and that field reaches as far as you.
10:14 We are not stretching ourselves too far, as if we had not reached you. We did reach you, with the good news of Christ.
10:15 We do not boast beyond our limits, in work that other people did. Our hope is this: as your faith grows, our own field among you will grow much larger, still within its measure.
10:16 Then we can announce the good news in the lands beyond you, and we will not boast about work already done in another person's field.
10:17 "Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord."
10:18 The person who puts in a good word for himself is not the one who passes the test. The one the Lord speaks well of is the one who passes.
## Jealous for a Pure Bride
11:1 If only you would bear with me in a little foolishness! Actually, you do bear with me.
11:2 I am jealous for you with a jealousy that is God's own. I arranged for you to be engaged to one husband, so that I could present you to Christ as a pure virgin.
11:3 But I am afraid that, just as the snake fooled Eve with his cunning, your thoughts may be spoiled and led away from the simple, pure loyalty owed to Christ.
11:4 If a man comes and announces another Jesus, one we never announced, or you take in a different spirit from the one you received, or a different good news from the one you welcomed, you put up with it wonderfully well!
11:5 I do not think I have fallen short of those "super-apostles" in anything.
11:6 Maybe I am untrained in speaking, but I am not untrained in knowledge. We have made that plain to you in every way, in everything.
11:7 Or did I commit a sin when I made myself low so that you could be lifted up, because I announced God's good news to you free of charge?
11:8 I robbed other churches by taking wages from them, so that I could serve you.
11:9 When I was with you and ran short, I was a weight on no one. The brothers who came from Macedonia fully supplied what I lacked. In everything I kept myself from being a weight on you, and I will keep doing so.
11:10 As surely as the truth of Christ is in me, this boast of mine will not be shut up anywhere in the regions of Achaia.
11:11 Why do I say this? Is it because I do not love you? God knows that I do.
## False Apostles
11:12 And what I am doing, I will keep on doing. I will take away every chance from those who are looking for a chance to be found equal to us in what they boast about.
11:13 People like that are false apostles. They are workers who cheat, disguising themselves as apostles of Christ.
11:14 That is no surprise. Satan himself takes on the look of an angel of light.
11:15 So it is hardly strange if his servants also disguise themselves as servants of righteousness. In the end they will get what their deeds call for.
## A Fool's Boast
11:16 I say it again: no one should take me for a fool. But if you do, then take me as a fool, so that I too can boast a little.
11:17 What I say in this boasting I do not say as the Lord would say it. I say it like a fool, in this confident boasting.
11:18 Many people boast by the standards of the flesh, so I will boast too.
11:19 You gladly put up with fools, you who are so wise!
11:20 Yes, you put up with it if someone makes slaves of you, if someone eats you up, if someone grabs what is yours, if someone lifts himself high over you, if someone slaps you in the face.
11:21 I say it to my shame, as if we had been too weak to do such things! But whatever anyone else dares to claim (I am speaking like a fool), I dare to claim it too.
11:22 Are they Hebrews? So am I. Are they Israelites? So am I. Are they offspring of Abraham? So am I.
## A List of Sufferings
11:23 Are they servants of Christ? I speak like a man out of his mind: I am more. I have done more labor, been in more prisons, taken beatings past all measure, and been near death again and again.
11:24 Five times I received from the Jews forty blows less one.
11:25 Three times I was beaten with rods. Once I was stoned. Three times I was shipwrecked, and once I spent a night and a day in the open sea.
11:26 I have been on the road many times. I have faced dangers from rivers, dangers from robbers, dangers from my own people, dangers from Gentiles, dangers in the city, dangers in the wilderness, dangers on the sea, and dangers from false brothers.
11:27 I have known labor and toil, many sleepless nights, hunger and thirst, many fasts, cold and having no clothes.
11:28 Apart from everything else, there is the pressure on me every day: my worry for all the churches.
11:29 Who is weak, and I do not feel weak? Who is made to stumble, and I do not burn inside?
11:30 If I have to boast, I will boast about the things that show how weak I am.
11:31 The God and Father of the Lord Jesus, who is blessed forever, knows that I am not lying.
11:32 In Damascus, the governor under King Aretas had guards watching the city of the Damascenes, so that he could seize me.
11:33 But through a window in the wall I was lowered in a basket, and I slipped out of his hands.
## Caught Up to Paradise
12:1 I have to boast. Nothing good comes of it, but I will go on to visions and revelations from the Lord.
12:2 I know a man in Christ. Fourteen years ago (whether in the body I do not know, or outside the body I do not know; God knows) this man was snatched up as far as the third heaven.
12:3 And I know that this same man (whether in the body or apart from the body, I do not know; God knows)
12:4 was snatched away into Paradise. There he heard unspeakable words, words no human being is allowed to say.
12:5 About a man like that I will boast. About myself I will not boast, except about my weaknesses.
12:6 If I decide to boast, I will not be a fool, since I will be telling the truth. But I hold back, so that no one will think more of me than what he sees in me or hears from me,
## The Thorn
12:7 and because of the extraordinary greatness of the revelations. So, to keep me from lifting myself too high, a thorn was given to me in the flesh, a messenger of Satan, to beat me, so that I would not lift myself too high.
12:8 About this, three times I begged the Lord that it would leave me.
12:9 But he has said to me, "My grace is enough for you. Power is made complete in weakness." So I will boast most gladly about my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may set up its tent over me.
12:10 That is why I am content with weaknesses, with insults, with times of need, with persecutions and with tight corners, all for Christ. For when I am weak, then I am strong.
## Not a Burden
12:11 I have become a fool! You forced me to it. You were the ones who should have been speaking well of me. I was not behind those "super-apostles" in anything, even if I am nothing.
12:12 The signs that mark an apostle were done among you, with complete endurance: signs, wonders and acts of power.
12:13 How were you treated worse than the rest of the churches? Only in this: I myself was no weight on you. Forgive me this wrong!
12:14 Look: this is the third time I am ready to come to you, and I will not weigh you down. I am not after your things, but after you. Children are not expected to put money aside for their parents, but parents for their children.
12:15 For my part, I will gladly spend all I have, and be used up myself, for your souls. If I love you so much more, am I to be loved less?
12:16 Fine, then. I did not weigh you down. But, some say, I am a crafty man, and I caught you with a trick.
12:17 Did I get something out of you by using any one of the men I sent to you?
12:18 I urged Titus to go, and I sent the brother along with him. Did Titus make anything off you? Did we not walk in the same spirit? Did we not walk in the same footsteps?
## Fears About the Visit
12:19 Have you been thinking all this time that we are defending ourselves to you? We are speaking in Christ, in the sight of God. Everything we do, dear friends, is to build you up.
12:20 I am afraid that when I come I may find you not as I want you, and that you may find me not as you want me. I am afraid there may be strife, jealousy, tempers, rivalry, backbiting, whispering, swelled heads and unrest.
12:21 I am afraid that when I come again my God will humble me in front of you, and that I will mourn over many who sinned before and have not turned away from the uncleanness, the sexual immorality and the shameless living they practiced.
## A Third Visit
13:1 This is the third time I am coming to you. "Every matter will stand on the word of two witnesses or of three."
13:2 I have already warned you, and I warn you ahead of time, as I did on my second visit. Now I am away, and I say it to those who sinned before and to all the rest: if I come back, I will not spare anyone.
13:3 You are asking for proof that Christ speaks in me. He is not weak toward you. He is strong among you.
13:4 For he was crucified out of weakness, but he lives by God's power. We also are weak in him, but we will live with him by God's power, in our dealings with you.
## Test Yourselves
13:5 Check yourselves to see whether you are in the faith. Put yourselves to the test. Or do you not recognize that Jesus Christ is in you? Unless, of course, you fail the test.
13:6 I hope you will recognize that we do not fail the test.
13:7 We pray to God that you do nothing wrong. We do not pray this so that we may look as if we pass the test, but so that you do what is right, even if we seem to fail.
13:8 We have no power to do anything against the truth, only for it.
13:9 We are glad whenever we are weak and you are strong. This too is our prayer: your restoration.
13:10 That is why I am writing this while I am away: so that when I am with you I will not have to deal sharply with you, using the authority the Lord gave me for building up and not for tearing down.
## Last Words
13:11 Finally, brothers: be glad. Be restored. Take comfort. Think the same way. Live in peace. And the God of love and peace will be with you.
13:12 Greet one another with a holy kiss.
13:13 All the saints send you greetings.
13:14 The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with all of you.
