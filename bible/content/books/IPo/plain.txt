## Greeting to Polycarp
1:0 Ignatius, also called Theophorus, to Polycarp, overseer of the church of the Smyrnaeans, or rather, one overseen by God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: warmest greetings.
## Stand on the rock
1:1 I welcome your mind that is set in God, firmly settled as on an immovable rock, and I give glory beyond measure that I was counted worthy to see your blameless face. May I have the benefit of it in God.
1:2 I urge you, by the grace you have put on, to put more into your race and to urge everyone to be saved. Do justice to your place with every care, of the flesh and of the spirit. Take thought for union: nothing is better. Carry everyone, as the Lord carries you. Put up with everyone in love, as in fact you do.
1:3 Make time for prayer without a break. Ask for understanding beyond what you have. Stay awake, with a spirit that never sleeps. Speak to people one by one, the way God does. Carry everyone's diseases, as a perfect athlete. Where the toil is greater, the gain is great.
## Not every wound
2:1 If you love good disciples, that earns you no thanks. Rather, bring the more troublesome ones under control with gentleness. The same plaster does not heal every wound. Ease fits of pain with soothing compresses.
2:2 Be sharp like the snake in all things, and pure like the dove always. That is why you are made of flesh and of spirit: so that you may handle gently what appears before your face. But ask that what is unseen be shown to you, so that you lack nothing and have every gift in abundance.
2:3 The time demands you, as pilots demand winds and as a storm-tossed sailor demands a harbor, so that you may reach God. Keep a clear head, as God's athlete. The prize is incorruption and eternal life, and you too are convinced of it. In every way I am a life in exchange for you, I and my chains, which you loved.
## Like a struck anvil
3:1 Do not let those who seem trustworthy but teach something different shake you. Stand firm like a struck anvil. A great athlete takes the blows and wins. Above all, for God's sake we must endure everything, so that he too may endure us.
3:2 Be more eager than you are. Learn to read the times. Wait for the one who is above time: the timeless, the invisible, visible for our sake; the untouchable, beyond suffering, able to suffer for our sake; who endured in every way for our sake.
## Widows and slaves
4:1 The widows must not be neglected. After the Lord, you must be the one who cares for them. Let nothing be done without your approval, and do nothing yourself without God, as indeed you do not. Stand steady.
4:2 Let gatherings be held more often. Seek out everyone by name.
4:3 Do not look down on male and female slaves. But they must not be puffed up either. For the glory of God let them serve all the more, so that from God they may gain a better freedom. They should not long to be set free from the common fund, so that they are not found to be slaves of desire.
## Husbands, wives and the single life
5:1 Run from the evil arts, but more than that, preach about them. Speak to my sisters, that they should love the Lord and be content with their husbands, in flesh and in spirit. In the same way charge my brothers in the name of Jesus Christ that they should love their wives as the Lord loves the church.
5:2 If anyone is able to remain in purity for the honor of the Lord's flesh, let him remain so without boasting. Should he boast, he is lost; and if it becomes known beyond the overseer, he is ruined. Men and women who marry should make their union with the overseer's approval, so that the marriage may follow the Lord and not desire. Let everything be done for the honor of God.
## To the church: God's soldiers
6:1 Pay attention to the overseer, so that God may pay attention to you. I am a life in exchange for those who submit to the overseer, the elders and the deacons. May my share be with them in God. Toil together with one another, compete together, run together, suffer together, rest together, rise together, as God's stewards, those who sit at his side, and his assistants.
6:2 Please the one you serve as soldiers, from whom you also receive your pay. Let none of you be found a deserter. Let your baptism remain as your weapons, your faith as a helmet, your love as a spear, your endurance as full armor. Let your deeds be your deposits, so that you may receive the back pay you have earned. So be patient with one another in gentleness, as God is with you. May I have the benefit of you always.
## God's runner for Syria
7:1 The church in Antioch of Syria is at peace, as has been made known to me, because of your prayer. So I too have grown more cheerful, in the freedom from care that God gives, if only through suffering I may reach God, so that I may be found your disciple at the resurrection.
7:2 It is fitting, Polycarp, most blessed by God, to call together a council most fit for God and to elect someone whom you hold very dear and who is tireless, who can be called God's runner. Count him worthy of this, so that he may go to Syria and give glory to your tireless love, for the glory of God.
7:3 No Christian has authority over himself: he spends his time on God. This work belongs to God and to you, when you complete it. For I trust in the grace that you are ready for a good deed that belongs to God. Since I know how earnest you are for the truth, I have urged you in these few lines.
## Write to the churches ahead
8:1 My sudden sailing from Troas to Neapolis, as the will of God requires, has kept me from writing to all the churches. So you must write to the churches ahead, as one who has the mind of God, so that they too may do the same (those who can, by sending messengers, and the others by sending letters through those you send, so that you may be glorified by a deed that lasts forever), as you are worthy.
8:2 I greet everyone by name, and the wife of the procurator, with her whole household and her children. I greet Attalus, my beloved. I greet the one who will be counted worthy to go to Syria. Grace will be with him always, and with Polycarp who sends him.
8:3 I pray that you may always fare well in our God Jesus Christ. Remain in him in the unity and the oversight of God. I greet Alce, a name dear to me. Farewell in the Lord.
