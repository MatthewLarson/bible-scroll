## The unveiling
1:1 This is the unveiling of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show his slaves what must happen soon. He sent it through his angel and made it known to his slave John,
1:2 who testified to the word of God and to the testimony of Jesus Christ, everything he saw.
1:3 Blessed is the one who reads the words of this prophecy out loud, and so are those who hear them and keep what is written in it, because the time is near.
## A greeting to seven churches
1:4 From John, to the seven churches in Asia: Grace to you and peace from the one who is, who was, and who is coming, and from the seven Spirits in front of his throne,
1:5 and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To the one who loves us, who set us free from our sins with his blood,
1:6 and made us a kingdom, priests to his God and Father: to him be the glory and the might forever and ever. Amen.
1:7 Look! He is coming with the clouds. Every eye will see him, and so will those who pierced him. All the tribes of the earth will mourn over him. Yes! Amen.
1:8 "I am the Alpha and the Omega," says the Lord God, "the one who is, who was, and who is coming, the Almighty."
## The vision on Patmos
1:9 I am John, your brother. In Jesus I share with you in the suffering, the kingdom, and the endurance. I was on the island called Patmos because of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus.
1:10 It was the Lord's day, and I was in the Spirit. Then I heard a loud voice behind me, like a trumpet.
1:11 It said, "Put what you are seeing into writing on a scroll, and send it to the seven churches: to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea."
1:12 I turned to see the voice that was talking with me. As I turned, I saw seven gold lampstands.
1:13 In the middle of the lampstands was one like a son of man. He wore a robe that came down to his feet and had a gold sash tied at his chest.
1:14 His head and his hair were white like white wool, white as snow, and his eyes were like a flame of fire.
1:15 His feet were like polished bronze, as if it had been fired in a furnace, and his voice was like the roar of many waters.
1:16 He had seven stars in his right hand, and a sharp two-edged sword was coming out of his mouth. His face was like the sun as it shines in its power.
1:17 When I saw him, I fell down at his feet like a dead man. He laid his right hand on me and said, "Don't be afraid. I am the First and the Last,
1:18 and the one who lives. I died, and look, I am alive forever and ever. I have the keys of Death and of Hades.
1:19 So write what you saw, what is, and what is going to happen after this.
1:20 As for the hidden meaning of the seven stars you saw on my right hand, and of the seven gold lampstands: the seven stars are angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are seven churches."
## To Ephesus
2:1 "To the angel of the church in Ephesus write: This is what the one says who holds the seven stars firmly in his right hand, the one who walks among the seven gold lampstands:
2:2 I know what you do: your hard labor and your endurance. I know you cannot bear evil people. You have put to the test those who call themselves apostles and are not, and you found them to be liars.
2:3 You have endurance. You have borne up for the sake of my name, and you have not worn out.
2:4 But I have this against you: you have let your first love go.
2:5 So remember where you have fallen from. Repent, and do again the works you did at the start. If you don't, I am coming to you, and I will move your lampstand out of its place, unless you repent.
2:6 Still, this is to your credit: you hate the works of the Nicolaitans, and I hate them too.
2:7 Whoever has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who wins the victory, I will give this: to eat from the tree of life, which is in the paradise of God."
## To Smyrna
2:8 "To the angel of the church in Smyrna write: This is what the First and the Last says, the one who died and came back to life:
2:9 I know your suffering and your poverty, yet you are rich. I know the slander that comes from those who say they are Jews and are not. Instead, they are a synagogue of Satan.
2:10 Have no fear at all of what you are going to suffer. Look, the devil is going to throw some of you into prison so that you will be put to the test, and you will have ten days of suffering. Stay faithful all the way to death, and I will give you the crown of life.
2:11 Whoever has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who wins the victory will never be harmed by the second death."
## To Pergamum
2:12 "To the angel of the church in Pergamum write: This is what the one says who has the sharp two-edged sword:
2:13 I know where you live: where Satan's throne is. You hold on to my name, and you did not deny the faith you have in me, even in the days of Antipas, my witness, my faithful one, who was killed among you, where Satan lives.
2:14 But I have a few things against you. You have people there who cling to the teaching of Balaam. He taught Balak to set a trap in front of the Israelites, so that they would eat food sacrificed to idols and commit sexual sin.
2:15 In the same way, you too have some who cling to the teaching of the Nicolaitans.
2:16 So repent. If you don't, I am coming to you soon, and I will wage war on them with the sword of my mouth.
2:17 Whoever has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who wins the victory, I will give some of the hidden manna. I will give him a white stone too, and written on the stone is a new name no one knows but the one who gets it."
## To Thyatira
2:18 "To the angel of the church in Thyatira write: This is what the Son of God says, whose eyes are like a flame of fire and whose feet are like polished bronze:
2:19 I know what you do: your love, your faith, your service, and your endurance. And your latest works are greater than your first.
2:20 But I have this against you: you leave the woman Jezebel alone, who calls herself a prophet. She teaches my slaves and leads them astray, into sexual sin and into eating food sacrificed to idols.
2:21 I gave her time to repent, but she does not want to repent of her sexual sin.
2:22 Look, I am throwing her onto a bed, and those who commit adultery with her into great suffering, unless they repent of her works.
2:23 I will put her children to death. Then all the churches will know that I am the one who searches kidneys and hearts, and I will give to each of you according to your works.
2:24 But to the rest of you in Thyatira, all who do not hold this teaching and have not come to know 'the deep things of Satan,' as they call them, I say this: I am not putting any other burden on you.
2:25 Just keep a firm grip on what you have, until I come.
2:26 As for the one who wins the victory and keeps my works to the very end, I will give him authority over the nations,
2:27 and he will shepherd them with an iron rod, as clay jars are smashed to pieces,
2:28 just as I myself have received it from my Father. And I will give him the morning star.
2:29 Whoever has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."
## To Sardis
3:1 "To the angel of the church in Sardis write: This is what the one says who has the seven Spirits of God and the seven stars: I know what you do. You have a name for being alive, but you are dead.
3:2 Be on the watch, and strengthen what remains, which was about to die. I have not found your works finished in the eyes of my God.
3:3 So remember how you received and heard, and keep it, and repent. If you do not keep watch, I will come like a thief, and you will have no idea at what hour I will come upon you.
3:4 But you have a few names in Sardis who have not dirtied their clothes. They will walk beside me dressed in white, since they are worthy.
3:5 The one who wins the victory will likewise be dressed in white clothes. I will never erase his name from the book of life, and I will declare his name before my Father and before his angels.
3:6 Whoever has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."
## To Philadelphia
3:7 "To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: This is what the Holy One says, the True One, who has the key of David. When he opens, no one will shut. When he shuts, no one opens:
3:8 I know what you do. Look, I have set an open door in front of you, one that no one is able to shut. You have only a little power, but you kept my word and did not deny my name.
3:9 Look, I will give you some from the synagogue of Satan, who say they are Jews and are not, but are lying. Look, I will make them come and bow down before your feet, and they will learn that I have loved you.
3:10 Because you kept the word of my endurance, I in turn will keep you from the hour of testing that is about to come upon the whole inhabited world, to test those who live on the earth.
3:11 I am coming soon. Keep a firm grip on what you have, so that no one takes your crown.
3:12 As for the one who wins the victory, I will make him a pillar in the temple of my God, and he will never go out of it again. On him I will write the name of my God and the name of my God's city, the new Jerusalem that comes down out of heaven from my God, and my new name.
3:13 Whoever has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."
## To Laodicea
3:14 "To the angel of the church in Laodicea write: This is what the Amen says, the witness who is faithful and true, the beginning of God's creation:
3:15 I know what you do: you are neither cold nor hot. If only you were cold or hot!
3:16 So, because you are lukewarm, neither hot nor cold, I am going to vomit you out of my mouth.
3:17 You say, 'I am rich. I have grown wealthy, and I need nothing.' But you do not know that you are the one who is wretched and pitiful, poor, blind, and naked.
3:18 My advice to you is this: buy from me gold refined by fire, and be rich. Buy white clothes and put them on, so the shame of your nakedness will not be exposed. And buy eye salve to rub on your eyes, so that you may see.
3:19 All those I love, I correct and discipline. So be eager, and repent.
3:20 Look! I am standing at the door, knocking. If someone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and have dinner with him, and he with me.
3:21 To the one who wins the victory, I will give a place to sit with me on my throne, just as I too won the victory and took my seat with my Father on his throne.
3:22 Whoever has an ear should listen to what the Spirit says to the churches."
## A door open in heaven
4:1 After this I looked, and there was an open door in heaven. The first voice, the one I had heard speaking with me like a trumpet, said, "Come up here, and I will show you what must happen after this."
4:2 Right away I was in the Spirit, and I saw a throne standing in heaven, with someone sitting on the throne.
4:3 The one sitting there looked like jasper stone and carnelian, and around the throne was a rainbow that looked like emerald.
4:4 In a circle around the throne were twenty-four thrones. Twenty-four elders sat on them, wearing white clothes and gold crowns on their heads.
4:5 Flashes of lightning, loud sounds, and thunder come out from the throne. Seven torches of fire burn in front of the throne. They are the seven Spirits of God.
4:6 Before the throne there was something like a sea of glass, like crystal. In the middle of the throne and all around it were four living creatures, full of eyes in front and behind.
4:7 The first living creature looked like a lion. The second looked like an ox. The third had a face like a human's. The fourth looked like an eagle in flight.
4:8 The four living creatures each have six wings, and they are full of eyes all around and inside. Day and night they take no rest, but say, / "Holy, holy, holy / is the Lord God, the Almighty, / who was, who is, and who is coming."
4:9 And every time the living creatures give glory and honor and thanks to the one seated on the throne, the one who lives forever and ever,
4:10 the twenty-four elders fall down before the one seated on the throne and worship the one who lives forever and ever. They throw their crowns down before the throne and say,
4:11 "You are worthy, our Lord and God, / to receive the glory and the honor and the power, / because you created all things, / and because of your will they were, and were created."
## The scroll and the Lamb
5:1 Then I saw a scroll on the right hand of the one seated on the throne. It had writing inside and on the back, and it was sealed with seven seals.
5:2 I also saw a strong angel shouting in a loud voice, "Who is worthy to open the scroll and to break its seals?"
5:3 No one was able to open the scroll or look at it, no one in heaven, on earth, or under the earth.
5:4 I kept on weeping hard, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or look at it.
5:5 Then one of the elders said to me, "Stop weeping. Look, the Lion from the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has won the victory, so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals."
5:6 Then, in the middle of the throne and of the four living creatures, and in the middle of the elders, I saw a Lamb standing. He looked as though he had been slaughtered. He had seven horns and seven eyes. These are the seven Spirits of God, sent out into the whole earth.
5:7 He came and took it from the right hand of the one seated on the throne.
5:8 As soon as he took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down in front of the Lamb. Each of them had a harp, along with gold bowls full of incense. These are the prayers of the saints.
5:9 They sing a new song: / "You are worthy to take the scroll / and to open its seals, / because you were slaughtered, / and with your blood you bought people for God / from every tribe and language and people and nation.
5:10 You made them a kingdom and priests for our God, / and they will rule on the earth."
5:11 Then I looked, and I heard the voice of many angels around the throne, the living creatures, and the elders. Their number was ten thousand times ten thousand, and thousands times thousands.
5:12 They said in a loud voice, / "Worthy is the Lamb who was slaughtered / to receive power and wealth and wisdom and strength / and honor and glory and blessing!"
5:13 Then every creature in heaven, on the earth, under the earth, and on the sea, everything in them: I heard them saying, / "To the one seated on the throne and to the Lamb / be blessing and honor and glory and might / forever and ever!"
5:14 The four living creatures kept saying, "Amen!" And the elders fell down and worshiped.
## The first six seals
6:1 Then I watched as the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures say, in a voice like thunder, "Come!"
6:2 I looked, and there was a white horse. The one sitting on it had a bow, and a crown was given to him. He rode out winning, and in order to win.
6:3 When the Lamb opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, "Come!"
6:4 And another horse came out, bright red. The one riding it was allowed to take peace away from the earth, so that people would slaughter one another, and he was given a huge sword.
6:5 When the Lamb opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, "Come!" I looked, and there was a black horse, and the one sitting on it held a balance scale in his hand.
6:6 And I heard something like a voice among the four living creatures. It said, "A quart of wheat for a denarius, and three quarts of barley for a denarius. But leave the oil and the wine unharmed."
6:7 When the Lamb opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, "Come!"
6:8 I looked, and there was a pale green horse. The one sitting on it had the name Death, and Hades was following with him. Authority was given to them over a quarter of the earth, to kill with the sword, with hunger, with disease, and by the wild animals of the earth.
6:9 When the Lamb opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been slaughtered because of the word of God and because of the testimony they held.
6:10 They cried out with a loud voice, "How long, Master, holy and true? Will you not judge the people who live on the earth and avenge our blood on them?"
6:11 Each of them was given a white robe, and they were told to rest a little while longer, until their fellow slaves and their brothers and sisters, who were going to be killed just as they had been, were complete in number too.
6:12 And I watched as the Lamb opened the sixth seal, and a great earthquake struck. The sun became black like sackcloth made of hair, and all of the moon became like blood.
6:13 The stars of the sky fell to the earth, the way a fig tree drops its unripe figs when a strong wind shakes it.
6:14 The sky pulled away like a scroll as it rolls up, and every mountain and every island was moved from where it stood.
6:15 And the kings of the earth and the great men and the commanders and the rich and the strong, and every slave and free person, hid themselves in the caves and in the rocky cliffs of the mountains.
6:16 They were saying to the mountains and the rocks, "Fall on us! Hide us from the face of the one who sits on the throne, and from the anger of the Lamb,
6:17 because the great day of their anger is here, and who is able to stand?"
## The sealing of God's slaves
7:1 After this I saw four angels standing at the four corners of the earth. They were keeping a firm hold on the four winds of the earth, so that no wind would blow on the land or on the sea or on any tree.
7:2 And I saw another angel rising from where the sun comes up, with the seal of the living God. He shouted with a loud voice to the four angels who had been allowed to harm the land and the sea:
7:3 "Do not harm the land, or the sea, or the trees, until we have sealed the slaves of our God on their foreheads."
7:4 And I heard how many had been sealed: 144,000, sealed out of every tribe of the Israelites.
7:5 Out of the tribe of Judah, 12,000 were sealed; out of the tribe of Reuben, 12,000; out of the tribe of Gad, 12,000;
7:6 out of the tribe of Asher, 12,000; out of the tribe of Naphtali, 12,000; out of the tribe of Manasseh, 12,000;
7:7 out of the tribe of Simeon, 12,000; out of the tribe of Levi, 12,000; out of the tribe of Issachar, 12,000;
7:8 out of the tribe of Zebulun, 12,000; out of the tribe of Joseph, 12,000; out of the tribe of Benjamin, 12,000 were sealed.
## The crowd no one could count
7:9 After these things I looked, and I saw a crowd so large that no one could count it, from every nation and tribe and people and language. They stood before the throne and before the Lamb, dressed in white robes, with palm branches in their hands.
7:10 They were crying out with a loud voice: / "Salvation is our God's, / who sits on the throne, / and the Lamb's!"
7:11 And all the angels stood in a ring around the throne and around the elders and the four living creatures. They threw themselves face down in front of the throne and worshiped God,
7:12 saying: / "Amen! / Blessing and glory and wisdom and thanks / and honor and power and strength / are our God's forever and ever. / Amen!"
7:13 Then one of the elders spoke up and said to me, "These who are dressed in the white robes: who are they, and where have they come from?"
7:14 I said to him, "My lord, you know." And he said to me, "These are the ones who are coming out of the great suffering. They have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.
7:15 For this reason they are before the throne of God / and serve him day and night in his temple, / and the one who sits on the throne / will spread his tent over them.
7:16 They will never go hungry again, / and never thirsty again; / the sun will not fall on them, / nor any burning heat,
7:17 because the Lamb in the middle of the throne / will shepherd them / and guide them to springs of the waters of life, / and God will wipe every tear from their eyes."
## The seventh seal and the incense
8:1 When the Lamb opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven for about half an hour.
8:2 Then I saw the seven angels who stand in God's presence. They were handed seven trumpets.
8:3 Another angel came and stood at the altar, holding a gold incense pan. Much incense was given to him, to add to the prayers of all the saints on the gold altar that stands before the throne.
8:4 From the angel's hand the smoke of the incense went up before God, along with the prayers of the saints.
8:5 Then the angel took the incense pan, filled it with fire from the altar, and threw it onto the earth. And there came thunder and loud sounds and flashes of lightning and an earthquake.
## The first four trumpets
8:6 And the seven angels who had the seven trumpets got themselves ready to blow them.
8:7 The first angel blew his trumpet. Hail and fire came, mixed with blood, and were thrown down to the earth. And a third of the earth burned up, and a third of the trees burned up, and every bit of green grass burned up.
8:8 The second angel blew his trumpet, and what looked like a great burning mountain was thrown into the sea. A third of the sea became blood,
8:9 and a third of the creatures in the sea, the ones with life in them, died, and a third of the ships were wrecked.
8:10 The third angel blew his trumpet. Out of the sky fell a great star, burning like a torch, and it came down on a third of the rivers and on the springs of water.
8:11 The star is called Wormwood. A third of the waters turned into wormwood, and many people died from those waters, because they had been made bitter.
8:12 The fourth angel blew his trumpet, and a third of the sun was struck, and a third of the moon, and a third of the stars, so that a third of them went dark. The day lost a third of its light, and so did the night.
8:13 Then I looked, and I heard an eagle flying high overhead, crying out with a loud voice: "Woe, woe, woe to the people who live on the earth, at the sound of the trumpets still to come from the three angels who are about to blow them!"
## The fifth trumpet: locusts from the abyss
9:1 The fifth angel blew his trumpet, and I saw a star that had fallen out of the sky onto the earth. The key to the pit of the abyss was given to him.
9:2 He opened the pit of the abyss, and smoke poured up out of the pit like smoke from a huge furnace, and the smoke from the pit made the sun and the air go dark.
9:3 Out of the smoke locusts came down onto the earth, and they were given power, the kind of power that scorpions on the earth have.
9:4 They were ordered not to harm the grass of the earth, or any green plant, or any tree. They could harm only the people who do not have God's seal on their foreheads.
9:5 They were not permitted to kill these people, but to torment them for five months, and their torment was like the torment of a scorpion when it strikes someone.
9:6 In those days people will look for death, and they will not find it at all. They will want to die, and death keeps running away from them.
9:7 In shape the locusts were like horses made ready for war. On their heads were things like crowns that looked like gold, and their faces were like human faces.
9:8 They had hair like women's hair, and their teeth were like a lion's.
9:9 They had breastplates that seemed to be made of iron, and their wings made a noise like many horse-drawn chariots charging into battle.
9:10 Their tails are like scorpions' tails, with stingers, and their power to harm people for five months is in those tails.
9:11 They have a king over them, the angel of the abyss. In Hebrew his name is Abaddon, and in Greek he has the name Apollyon.
9:12 The first woe has passed. Look, two more woes are still coming after this.
## The sixth trumpet: the army from the Euphrates
9:13 The sixth angel blew his trumpet, and I heard a single voice from the horns of the gold altar that stands before God.
9:14 It told the sixth angel, who held the trumpet, "Untie the four angels who are tied up at the great river, the Euphrates."
9:15 And the four angels were untied. They had been made ready for the hour and day and month and year, to kill a third of the human race.
9:16 The cavalry armies numbered two hundred million; I heard how many there were.
9:17 This is how I saw the horses in the vision, and those who rode them: they had breastplates that were fire-red, hyacinth-blue, and sulfur-yellow. The horses' heads were like lions' heads, and fire and smoke and sulfur come out of their mouths.
9:18 By these three plagues a third of the human race was killed: by the fire and the smoke and the sulfur coming out of their mouths.
9:19 The horses' power is in their mouths and in their tails. Their tails are like snakes and have heads, and they use them to do harm.
9:20 The rest of the human race, who were not killed by these plagues, did not even repent of what their hands had made and give up worshiping demons and idols of gold, silver, bronze, stone, and wood, which are not able to see or hear or walk.
9:21 And they did not repent of their murders, or their magic potions, or their sexual immorality, or their thefts.
## The angel and the little scroll
10:1 And I saw another mighty angel coming down out of heaven, dressed in a cloud, and the rainbow was over his head. His face was like the sun, and his feet were like pillars of fire.
10:2 In his hand he held a little scroll that had been opened. He planted his right foot on the sea and his left on the land,
10:3 and he cried out with a great voice, the way a lion roars. And when he cried out, the seven thunders spoke out with their own voices.
10:4 When the seven thunders spoke, I was going to write. Then I heard a voice from heaven say, "Keep under seal what the seven thunders said, and do not write it down."
10:5 Then the angel I saw standing on sea and land lifted his right hand up toward heaven
10:6 and swore by the one who lives forever and ever, who created heaven and the things in it, the earth and the things in it, and the sea and the things in it: "There will be no more waiting!
10:7 Instead, in the days when the seventh angel's voice is heard, when he is about to blow his trumpet, the mystery of God is finished, just as he told the good news to his own slaves the prophets."
10:8 And the voice I had heard from heaven was speaking with me again. It said, "Go and take the open scroll from the hand of the angel standing on the sea and on the land."
10:9 I went off to the angel and asked him for the little scroll. He told me, "Take it and eat it up. It will make your stomach bitter, but in your mouth it will taste as sweet as honey."
10:10 So I took the little scroll out of the angel's hand and ate it up. It was as sweet as honey in my mouth, but once I had eaten it, my stomach turned bitter.
10:11 And they said to me, "Once more you must prophesy about many peoples and nations and languages and kings."
## The two witnesses
11:1 Then I was given a reed like a staff, and was told, "Get up and measure the temple of God and the altar, and the people who worship in it.
11:2 But the courtyard outside the temple, leave it out, and do not measure it, because it has been handed over to the nations. They will trample the holy city for 42 months.
11:3 And I will let my two witnesses prophesy for 1,260 days, dressed in sackcloth."
11:4 These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands standing in the presence of the Lord of the earth.
11:5 If anyone wants to harm them, fire comes out of their mouths and eats up their enemies. If anyone wants to harm them, this is how that person must be killed.
11:6 These have authority to shut the sky so that no rain falls while they are prophesying. They also have authority over the waters, to turn them into blood, and to strike the earth with any plague, as often as they choose.
11:7 When they have completed their testimony, the beast that comes up from the abyss will go to war with them, win the victory over them, and kill them.
11:8 Their corpse will lie in the street of the great city, which is symbolically called Sodom and Egypt, where their Lord too was crucified.
11:9 For three and a half days, people from the peoples and tribes and languages and nations look at their corpse, and they do not let their corpses be put in a grave.
11:10 The people who live on the earth rejoice over them and celebrate, and they will send gifts to one another, because these two prophets tormented the people who live on the earth.
11:11 After the three and a half days, a breath of life from God went into them, and they rose to their feet. Great fear came over those who were watching them.
11:12 And they heard a great voice from heaven say to them, "Come up here!" So they went up into heaven in the cloud, as their enemies looked on.
11:13 In that hour a great earthquake struck, and a tenth of the city fell. Seven thousand people were killed in the earthquake, and the rest were filled with fear and gave glory to the God of heaven.
11:14 The second woe has passed. Look, the third woe is coming soon.
## The seventh trumpet
11:15 Then the seventh angel blew his trumpet, and there were loud voices in heaven, saying: / "The kingdom of the world / has become our Lord's and his Messiah's, / and he will reign forever and ever."
11:16 Then the twenty-four elders who sit on their thrones in God's presence threw themselves face down and worshiped God,
11:17 saying: / "We thank you, Lord God, the Almighty, / who is and who was, / for you have taken hold of your great power / and have taken up your reign.
11:18 The nations were furious, / and your fury has come, / and the time for the dead to be judged, / and for giving the reward to your slaves the prophets / and to the saints and to those who fear your name, / the small and the great, / and for destroying those who destroy the earth."
11:19 And the temple of God in heaven was opened, and the ark of his covenant appeared inside his temple. There were flashes of lightning, loud sounds, thunder, an earthquake, and great hail.
## The woman and the dragon
12:1 A great sign was seen in heaven: a woman wrapped in the sun, with the moon beneath her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars.
12:2 She was pregnant, and she was crying out in her labor pains, in torment to give birth.
12:3 Then another sign was seen in heaven: look, a great fiery-red dragon with seven heads and ten horns, and on its heads seven royal crowns.
12:4 Its tail dragged away a third of the stars of heaven and threw them down to the earth. The dragon took its stand in front of the woman who was about to give birth, so that when she gave birth it could devour her child.

22:12 "Look, I am coming soon, and my reward is with me, to pay each person back for the work they have done.
22:13 I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End.
22:14 Blessed are those who wash their robes, so that they will have the right to the tree of life and may enter the city by its gates.
22:15 Outside are the dogs, and those who practice magic, and the sexually immoral, the murderers, the idol worshipers, and everyone who loves a lie and lives it.
22:16 I, Jesus, sent my angel to bear witness to you about these things for the churches. I am the Root and the Descendant of David, the bright Morning Star."
22:17 The Spirit and the bride say, "Come!" And let the one who hears say, "Come!" Let the one who is thirsty come. Let whoever wants it take the water of life as a free gift.
22:18 I bear witness to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this book: if anyone puts anything onto them, God will put onto that person the plagues written in this book.
22:19 And if anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God will take away that person's share in the tree of life and in the holy city, which are written about in this book.
22:20 The one who testifies to these things says, "Yes, I am coming soon." Amen! Come, Lord Jesus!
22:21 The grace of the Lord Jesus be with all.
