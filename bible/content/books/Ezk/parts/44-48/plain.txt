## The shut gate
44:1 Then he brought me back by the way of the outer gate of the sanctuary, the one that faces east. It was shut.
44:2 The LORD said to me, "This gate is to be shut. It will not be opened, and nobody will walk through it. The LORD, the God of Israel, has entered by it, so it will stay shut.
44:3 As for the leader, he is the leader, and he may sit in it to eat bread before the LORD. He will come in by the way of the porch of the gate, and he will leave the same way he came."
## Glory fills the house
44:4 He led me along the north gate road until I stood facing the house. I looked, and there! The glory of the LORD had filled the house of the LORD. I fell on my face.
44:5 The LORD said to me, "Son of man, pay close attention. Use your eyes to see and your ears to hear everything I am telling you about all the rules of the LORD's house and all its laws. Pay close attention to who may enter the house, and to every exit of the sanctuary.
## Foreigners kept out
44:6 Say to the rebellious one, to the house of Israel: The Lord GOD says this: You have gone too far with all your abhorrent things, house of Israel!
44:7 You brought in the sons of foreigners, people uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh, so that they were in my sanctuary and profaned my own house, while you presented my food, the fat and the blood. They broke my covenant, besides all your abhorrent things.
44:8 You did not keep your charge over my holy things. You appointed others, for yourselves, to keep my charge in my sanctuary.
44:9 The Lord GOD says this: No foreigner who is uncircumcised in heart and uncircumcised in flesh will enter my sanctuary, not one of the foreigners who live among the Israelites.
## The Levites lose their place
44:10 But the Levites who moved far from me when Israel went astray, who went astray from me after their filthy idols, will bear their guilt.
44:11 They will work in my sanctuary as gatekeepers for the house and as its servants. They will be the ones to slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice for the people, and to stand before the people to serve them.
44:12 They served the people in front of their filthy idols, and they became a stumbling block that led the house of Israel into guilt. So I have raised my hand in an oath against them, the Lord GOD declares, and they will bear their guilt.
44:13 They will not come near me to serve me as priests. They will not come near any of my holy things, the most holy things. They will bear their disgrace and the abhorrent things they have done.
44:14 I will make them the keepers of the charge of the house, for all its service and for everything that is done in it.
## The sons of Zadok
44:15 But the Levite priests, the sons of Zadok, kept their charge at my sanctuary while the Israelites went astray from me. They are the ones who will come near me to serve me. They will stand before me and bring me the fat and the blood, the Lord GOD declares.
44:16 They are the ones who will enter my sanctuary, and they are the ones who will draw near to my table to serve me. They will keep my charge.
## Rules for the priests
44:17 Whenever they come in through the gates of the inner courtyard, they must wear linen clothes. Nothing woolen is to touch them while they serve at those gates or farther in.
44:18 Their headdresses will be linen, and linen undershorts will cover their hips. Nothing they wear around the waist may make them sweat.
44:19 When they leave for the outer courtyard, where the people are, they must take off the clothes they served in and leave them in the holy rooms. They will dress in other clothes, so that their clothes do not pass holiness to the people.
44:20 Priests must not shave their heads bare or leave their hair untrimmed and wild. They must keep the hair of their heads trimmed.
44:21 When a priest comes into the inner courtyard, he must not drink wine.
44:22 A priest must not take a widow or a divorced woman as his wife. His wife must be a virgin from the descendants of the house of Israel, or else the widow of another priest.
44:23 They will instruct my people about what is holy and what is common, and make known to them what is unclean and what is clean.
44:24 When there is a dispute, they will stand ready to judge, and they will settle it by my rulings. At all my appointed times they will observe my laws and my rules, and they will keep my Sabbaths holy.
44:25 A priest may not go in where there is a dead person, for that would make him unclean. The only exceptions are his father, his mother, his son, his daughter, his brother, and a sister who has never had a husband. For these he may make himself unclean.
44:26 After he is cleansed, seven days will be counted for him.
44:27 On the day he goes into the holy place, into the inner courtyard, to serve in the holy place, he will bring his sin offering, the Lord GOD declares.
44:28 They will have an inheritance: I am their inheritance. You must give them no possession in Israel. I am their possession.
44:29 Their food will be the grain offering, the sin offering and the guilt offering. Whatever in Israel is devoted to the LORD will be theirs.
44:30 The finest of all the firstfruits of everything, and every contribution of every kind from all your contributions, belongs to the priests. You are also to give the priest the finest of your dough, to bring a blessing to rest on your household.
44:31 The priests may not eat any bird or animal that was found dead or torn.
## The holy district
45:1 When you divide the land as an inheritance, you will set aside a contribution for the LORD, a holy portion of the land. It will be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 wide. All of it, in its whole territory all around, will be holy.
45:2 Inside it the sanctuary will take a square plot, 500 cubits on each side, and a strip of open land 50 cubits wide will run all around that.
45:3 Out of this area measure off a section 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 wide. The sanctuary, the most holy place, will stand in it.
45:4 This holy portion of the land belongs to the priests, the servants of the sanctuary, who come near to serve the LORD. Their houses will stand on it, and it will be holy ground for the sanctuary.
45:5 Another 25,000 cubits in length and 10,000 in width will belong to the Levites who serve at the house. It will be theirs as a possession, with twenty rooms.
45:6 For the city's possession, give a strip 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 long, next to the holy contribution. It is for the whole house of Israel.
45:7 The leader will have land on both sides of the holy contribution and the city's possession, in front of them, one part to the west and one to the east. Together with them, the length matches one of the tribal portions, from the western border to the eastern border.
45:8 This land will be his as a possession in Israel. My leaders will never again oppress my people. They will give the rest of the land to the house of Israel, by their tribes.
45:9 The Lord GOD says this: Enough, leaders of Israel! Put an end to violence and plunder. Practice justice and righteousness. Take your evictions off my people, the Lord GOD declares.
## Honest weights
45:10 You will have scales that are right, an ephah that is right and a bath that is right.
45:11 The ephah and the bath are to be the same size. A bath holds a tenth of a homer, and an ephah is also a tenth of a homer. The homer is what fixes their size.
45:12 A shekel is twenty gerahs. Your mina will be made up of twenty shekels, twenty-five shekels and fifteen shekels.
## The people's gifts
45:13 Here is the gift you must set aside: one sixth of an ephah out of every homer of wheat, and one sixth of an ephah out of every homer of barley.
45:14 For oil, the set share is figured by the bath: a tenth of a bath from every cor. A cor is a homer, since ten baths make a homer.
45:15 From each flock of 200 in the well-watered land of Israel, one sheep is to be given. These are for the grain offering, the burnt offering and the peace offerings, to make atonement for them, the Lord GOD declares.
45:16 All the people of the land are bound to give this contribution to the leader in Israel.
45:17 The leader is responsible for the burnt offerings, the grain offerings and the drink offerings at the festivals, the new moons and the Sabbaths, at every appointed time of the house of Israel. He himself will supply the sin offering, the grain offering, the burnt offering and the peace offerings that make atonement for the house of Israel.
## Purifying the sanctuary
45:18 The Lord GOD says this: In the first month, on the first day of the month, you will take a young bull, a flawless one from the herd, and you will cleanse the sanctuary from sin.
45:19 The priest will take blood from the sin offering and smear it on the doorpost of the house, on the four corners of the altar's ledge, and on the posts of the gate of the inner courtyard.
45:20 Do this again on the seventh day of the month, for people who go wrong by accident and for the simple. So you will make atonement for the house.
## Passover and the autumn festival
45:21 On the fourteenth day of the first month you will keep the Passover. It is a festival of seven days, and unleavened bread will be eaten.
45:22 On that day the leader will provide a young bull as a sin offering for himself and for all the people of the land.
45:23 On each of the seven days of the festival he will give the LORD a burnt offering of seven flawless bulls and seven flawless rams, and every day a male goat as a sin offering.
45:24 With each bull and each ram he will provide a grain offering of one ephah, and a hin of oil for every ephah.
45:25 At the festival in the seventh month, from the fifteenth day of the month, he will make the same provision for seven days: the sin offering, the burnt offering, the grain offering and the oil.
## The gate and the leader
46:1 The Lord GOD says this: The gate of the inner courtyard that faces east is to stay shut for the six days of work. It is opened on the Sabbath day and on the day of the new moon.
46:2 The leader will enter from outside by way of the gate's porch and stand beside the gatepost. The priests will offer his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he will bow down at the gate's threshold. Then he will go out, but the gate will not be shut until evening.
46:3 On the Sabbaths and the new moons the people of the land will bow down before the LORD at the doorway of that same gate.
46:4 On the Sabbath day the leader will bring the LORD a burnt offering of six flawless lambs and one flawless ram.
46:5 With the ram he will give an ephah of grain, and with the lambs whatever he is able to give, along with a hin of oil for each ephah.
46:6 On the day of the new moon the offering will be a flawless young bull, six lambs and a ram. All of them will be flawless.
46:7 He will provide an ephah of grain with the bull and an ephah with the ram, and with the lambs whatever he can afford, plus a hin of oil for each ephah.
46:8 The leader will go in through the porch of the gate, and he will leave by the same road.
46:9 When the people of the land come before the LORD at the appointed times, whoever enters by the north gate to bow down must leave by the south gate, and whoever enters by the south gate must leave by the north gate. No one may turn back the way he came in. He must go out straight across, by the gate opposite.
46:10 The leader will be in their midst. He goes in as they go in, and he goes out as they go out.
46:11 At the festivals and the appointed times, the grain is an ephah with a bull, an ephah with a ram, and with the lambs whatever he is able to give, plus a hin of oil for each ephah.
46:12 When the leader gives something to the LORD of his own free will, whether a burnt offering or peace offerings, someone will open the east-facing gate for him. He will present his burnt offering and his peace offerings just as he does on the Sabbath day. Then he will leave, and someone will shut the gate behind him.
## The daily offering
46:13 Day by day a flawless lamb in its first year is to be offered to the LORD as a burnt offering. Offer it morning by morning.
46:14 Every morning you will also provide the grain that goes with it: a sixth of an ephah, and a third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour. It is a grain offering to the LORD, a rule for all time, to be kept without a break.
46:15 So the lamb, the grain and the oil will be offered every morning, a burnt offering that never stops.
## The leader's land
46:16 The Lord GOD says this: If the leader makes a gift to one of his sons, it comes out of his inheritance and goes to his sons. It is their possession, theirs by inheritance.
46:17 If he gives a servant of his a piece of his inheritance, the servant keeps it only until the year of release. Then it goes back to the leader. His inheritance passes only to his sons, and it belongs to them.
46:18 The leader may not seize any of the people's inheritance or drive them from their possessions. What he gives his sons must come out of his own possession, so that none of my people is scattered away from his land.
## The kitchens
46:19 Next he took me in by the entrance beside the gate, into the priests' holy rooms that face north. At the far western end of them there was a place.
46:20 He told me, "Here is where the priests will boil the guilt offering and the sin offering, and bake the grain offering. Doing it here keeps them from carrying the offerings out into the outer courtyard and passing holiness to the people."
46:21 He took me out to the outer courtyard and had me walk to each of its four corners. In every corner there was a courtyard.
46:22 These corner courtyards were closed in, forty cubits long and thirty wide. All four had the same measurements.
46:23 A row of stones ran around inside each of the four, and boiling places had been built under the rows of stones all around.
46:24 "These are the boiling houses," he told me. "Here those who serve at the house cook the people's sacrifices."
## Water from the threshold
47:1 He took me back to the doorway of the house. Water was flowing out from beneath the threshold, heading east, since the front of the house faced east. The water ran down from beneath the house's south side, south of the altar.
47:2 Then he brought me out by the north gate and led me around on the outside to the outer gate, the one that faces east. There the water was coming out in a thin stream from the south side.
47:3 As the man walked east with a measuring line in his hand, he measured off 1,000 cubits. Then he had me cross the water, and it reached my ankles.
47:4 He measured another 1,000 and had me cross the water, and it reached my knees. He measured another 1,000 and had me cross again, and it reached my hips.
47:5 He measured another 1,000, and it had become a river I could not wade across, because the water had risen. It was water for swimming, a river that cannot be crossed on foot.
47:6 He asked me, "Son of man, do you see this?" Then he took me along and brought me back to the river's bank.
47:7 As I came back, I saw a great many trees growing on the bank of the river, on both sides.
47:8 He told me, "This water is flowing out toward the eastern district and will go down into the Arabah. It runs to the sea, and when it is poured out into the sea, the waters of the sea will be healed.
47:9 Every living creature that swarms will live wherever the two rivers reach. There will be a huge number of fish, because this water has gone there. The waters will be healed, and life will come wherever the river goes.
47:10 From En-gedi to En-eglaim, fishermen will stand beside the sea, and nets will be spread out there. The fish will be of every kind, as many as in the Great Sea.
47:11 But its swamps and marshes will not be healed. They are given over to salt.
47:12 Along the river, on both banks, every kind of tree that gives food will grow up. The leaves will never dry up, and the fruit will never run out. Each month the trees will bear a first crop, because their water flows out from the sanctuary. People will eat the fruit, and the leaves will bring healing."
## The borders of the land
47:13 The Lord GOD says this: These are the borders within which you will give the land out as an inheritance to the twelve tribes of Israel. Joseph will receive two measured portions.
47:14 You will divide it equally, each man the same as his brother, since I raised my hand in an oath to give it to your ancestors. This land will fall to you as an inheritance.
47:15 The border of the land is this. Its northern side runs from the Great Sea by the Hethlon road, past Lebo-hamath to Zedad,
47:16 Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim (which lies between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath), and Hazer-hatticon, which is by the border of Hauran.
47:17 So the border from the sea will be Hazar-enan, at the border of Damascus, with the territory of Hamath to the north. This is the north side.
47:18 The east side lies between Hauran and Damascus, and between Gilead and the land of Israel. The Jordan is the line: from the border to the eastern sea you will measure. This is the east side.
47:19 The south side runs from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, then follows the wadi out to the Great Sea. That is the south side.
47:20 The west side is the Great Sea, from the southern border up to a point across from Lebo-hamath. That is the west side.
47:21 You will divide this land among yourselves by the tribes of Israel.
47:22 You will give it out as an inheritance among yourselves and to the foreigners who live among you and have had children among you. You will count them as native-born among the Israelites. They will be given an inheritance with you in the middle of the tribes of Israel.
47:23 Each foreigner will receive his inheritance in the tribe he lives with, the Lord GOD declares.
## The tribes' portions
48:1 These are the names of the tribes. At the northern end, beside the Hethlon road to Lebo-hamath, to Hazar-enan at the border of Damascus on the north, alongside Hamath, from the east side to the west side: Dan, one portion.
48:2 Beside Dan's border, from the east side to the west side, Asher has one portion.
48:3 Beside Asher's border, from the east side to the west side, Naphtali has one portion.
48:4 Beside Naphtali's border, from the east side to the west side, Manasseh has one portion.
48:5 Beside Manasseh's border, from the east side to the west side, Ephraim has one portion.
48:6 Beside Ephraim's border, from the east side to the west side, Reuben has one portion.
48:7 Beside Reuben's border, from the east side to the west side, Judah has one portion.
## The contribution in the middle
48:8 Beside Judah's border, running from the east side to the west side, is the contribution you will set apart. Its width is 25,000 cubits, and its length from east to west equals that of one tribal portion. The sanctuary will stand in the middle of it.
48:9 The contribution that you set apart for the LORD will be 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 wide.
48:10 The priests will receive this holy contribution. It will measure 25,000 cubits along the north, 10,000 along the west, 10,000 along the east, and 25,000 along the south. The sanctuary of the LORD will be in the middle of it.
48:11 It is for the priests who were made holy, the sons of Zadok. They kept my charge and did not go astray when the Israelites went astray, as the Levites did.
48:12 A special gift out of the land's contribution will be theirs, a most holy piece of land next to the border of the Levites.
48:13 Alongside the priests' border, the Levites will have a strip 25,000 cubits long and 10,000 wide. The whole length is 25,000 and the width 10,000.
48:14 None of it may be sold or traded. The best of the land must never pass into other hands, for it is holy to the LORD.
48:15 The 5,000 cubits left over in width, beside the 25,000, is common ground, meant for the city, for homes and for open land. The city will stand in the middle of it.
48:16 These are the city's measurements: 4,500 cubits on the north side, 4,500 on the south side, 4,500 on the east side, and 4,500 on the west side.
48:17 Around the city there will be open land, a strip of 250 cubits on each side: north, south, east and west.
48:18 Beyond that, what is left of the length beside the holy contribution is 10,000 cubits toward the east and 10,000 toward the west, touching the holy contribution. Its crops will feed the workers of the city.
48:19 The workers of the city, from all the tribes of Israel, will work it.
48:20 The whole contribution is a square, 25,000 cubits by 25,000. You will set it apart, the holy contribution together with the possession of the city.
48:21 The leader gets everything left over, on both sides of the holy contribution and the possession of the city. On the east it reaches from the 25,000 cubits of the contribution to the eastern border, and on the west from the 25,000 cubits to the western border, lying beside the tribal portions. It all belongs to the leader. The holy contribution, with the sanctuary of the house, will be in its middle.
48:22 So the land of the leader lies between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin, with the possession of the Levites and the possession of the city set in the middle of it.
## The southern tribes
48:23 These are the rest of the tribes. From the east side to the west side, Benjamin has one portion.
48:24 Beside Benjamin's border, from the east side to the west side, Simeon has one portion.
48:25 Beside Simeon's border, from the east side to the west side, Issachar has one portion.
48:26 Beside Issachar's border, from the east side to the west side, Zebulun has one portion.
48:27 Beside Zebulun's border, from the east side to the west side, Gad has one portion.
48:28 Gad's border on the south side runs from Tamar to the waters of Meribath-kadesh, then along the wadi to the Great Sea.
48:29 This is the land you will give out by lot as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel. These are their portions, the Lord GOD declares.
## The city and its name
48:30 These are the ways out of the city. The north side measures 4,500 cubits.
48:31 The gates of the city carry the names of the tribes of Israel. On the north side there are three gates: Reuben's gate, Judah's gate and Levi's gate.
48:32 The east side is 4,500 cubits long, and it has three gates: Joseph's gate, Benjamin's gate and Dan's gate.
48:33 The south side measures 4,500 cubits, and it has three gates: Simeon's gate, Issachar's gate and Zebulun's gate.
48:34 The west side is 4,500 cubits long, and it has three gates: Gad's gate, Asher's gate and Naphtali's gate.
48:35 All the way around, the city will measure 18,000 cubits. From that day on, its name will be: The LORD Is There.
