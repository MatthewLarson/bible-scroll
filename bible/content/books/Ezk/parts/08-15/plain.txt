## Carried to Jerusalem
8:1 In the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day of the month, I was sitting in my house, and the elders of Judah were sitting in front of me. There the Lord GOD's hand fell on me.
8:2 I looked, and there was a form that looked like fire. From what looked like his hips down, it was fire. From his hips up, it looked bright, like glowing metal.
8:3 He stretched out the shape of a hand and took me by a lock of the hair of my head. A spirit lifted me up between the earth and the heavens and brought me to Jerusalem in visions of God. It brought me to the entrance of the gate of the inner court that faces north, where the seat of the image of jealousy stood, the image that provokes jealousy.
8:4 And there was the glory of the God of Israel, like the appearance I had seen in the plain.
## The image of jealousy
8:5 He said to me, "Son of man, lift up your eyes now toward the north." So I lifted up my eyes toward the north. North of the altar gate, in the entrance, stood this image of jealousy.
8:6 He said to me, "Son of man, do you see what they are doing? The house of Israel is doing great abhorrent things here, and they are sending me far from my sanctuary. You will see still more great abhorrent things."
## Carvings on the wall
8:7 Then he brought me to the entrance of the court. I looked, and there was a hole in the wall.
8:8 He said to me, "Son of man, dig now in the wall." So I dug in the wall, and there was a doorway.
8:9 He said to me, "Go in and see the wicked abhorrent things they are doing here."
8:10 So I went in and looked, and there, engraved on the wall all around, was every form of crawling creature and beast, all of them detestable, and all the filthy idols of the house of Israel.
8:11 Seventy men from the elders of the house of Israel were standing in front of the carvings, and Jaazaniah son of Shaphan was standing among them. Each man had his censer in his hand, and the smell of a cloud of incense was rising.
8:12 He said to me, "Son of man, have you seen it? In the darkness the elders of the house of Israel are at work, each man in the rooms of his carved image. For they say, 'The LORD does not see us. The LORD has left the land.'"
8:13 He said to me, "You will see still more of the great abhorrent things they are doing."
## Women weeping for Tammuz
8:14 Then he brought me to the entrance of the gate of the house of the LORD that faces north. There women were sitting, weeping for Tammuz.
8:15 He said to me, "Do you see this, son of man? You will see still greater abhorrent things than these."
## Bowing to the sun
8:16 Then he brought me into the inner court of the house of the LORD. There at the entrance of the LORD's temple, between the porch and the altar, were about twenty-five men. They stood with their backs to the LORD's temple, facing east, bowing low to the sun in the east.
8:17 He said to me, "Do you see, son of man? Is it too small a thing for the house of Judah to do the abhorrent things they have done here? They have filled the land with violence and keep turning back to make me angry. Look, they send the branch to their nose!
8:18 So I too will act in rage. My eye will not look with compassion, and I will show no pity. They will cry out in my ears with a loud voice, and I will not listen to them."
## Six men and a man in linen
9:1 Then he called out in my hearing with a loud voice: "The punishments of the city have come near, and each man has his weapon of destruction in his hand!"
9:2 And there were six men coming from the direction of the upper gate that faces north, each with his smashing weapon in his hand. One man among them was clothed in linen, with a scribe's writing case at his hips. They came in and stood beside the bronze altar.
## The mark
9:3 The glory of the God of Israel went up from the cherub where it had been, to the threshold of the house. Then he called to the man clothed in linen, who had the scribe's writing case at his hips.
9:4 The LORD said to him, "Pass through the city, through the middle of Jerusalem, and make a mark on the foreheads of the people who sigh and groan over all the abhorrent things done in it."
## The killing begins
9:5 To the others he said in my hearing, "Pass through the city after him and strike. Let your eye not look with compassion, and show no pity.
9:6 Kill them utterly: old men, young men, young women, little children and women. Do not go near anyone who has the mark. Start at my sanctuary." So they began with the old men who were in front of the house.
9:7 He said to them, "Make the house unclean. Fill the courts with the slain. Go out." They went out and began to strike in the city.
## Ezekiel's cry
9:8 While they were striking, I was left alone. I fell on my face and cried out, "Ah, Lord GOD! Will you destroy all the remnant of Israel as you pour out your rage on Jerusalem?"
9:9 He said to me, "The guilt of the houses of Israel and Judah is very, very great. Bloodshed fills the land, and crookedness fills the city. For they say, 'The LORD has left the land, and the LORD does not see.'
9:10 I too will not look with compassion, and I will show no pity. I will pay back their conduct on their own heads."
## The report
9:11 And there was the man clothed in linen, with the writing case at his hips, bringing back word: "I have done as you commanded me."
## Coals of fire for the city
10:1 I looked, and there on the dome above the heads of the cherubim was a sapphire-like stone, in the likeness of a throne. It appeared above them.
10:2 He said to the man clothed in linen, "Go in between the whirling wheel, to the place under the cherub. Fill your cupped hands with coals of fire from between the cherubim, and sprinkle them over the city." And he went in as I watched.
10:3 The cherubim were standing on the south side of the house when the man went in, and the cloud filled the inner court.
10:4 Then the glory of the LORD rose up from the cherub to the threshold of the house. The house was filled with the cloud, and the court was full of the brightness of the glory of the LORD.
10:5 The sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of God Almighty when he speaks.
10:6 When he commanded the man clothed in linen, "Take fire from between the whirling wheel, from between the cherubim," the man went in and stood beside the wheel.
10:7 Then a cherub stretched out his hand from between the cherubim to the fire that was between them. He lifted some and put it into the cupped hands of the man clothed in linen, who took it and went out.
10:8 Under the wings of the cherubim there could be seen the shape of a human hand.
## The wheels and the faces
10:9 I looked, and there were four wheels beside the cherubim, one wheel beside each cherub. The wheels looked like a stone of Tarshish.
10:10 All four of them looked alike, as if one wheel were in the middle of another wheel.
10:11 When they went, they went toward any of their four sides. They did not turn aside as they went. For they went in the direction the head faced, going after it. They did not turn as they went.
10:12 Their whole bodies, their backs, their hands and their wings were full of eyes, and so were the wheels, all around, the wheels of all four of them.
10:13 The wheels were called "the whirling wheel" in my hearing.
10:14 Each had four faces. The first face was the face of a cherub, the second was the face of a human, the third was the face of a lion, and the fourth was the face of an eagle.
10:15 The cherubim rose up. They were the living creature I had seen at the Kebar canal.
10:16 When the cherubim went, the wheels went beside them. When the cherubim raised their wings to rise up from the earth, the wheels did not turn away from beside them.
10:17 When the cherubim stood still, the wheels stood still, and when the cherubim rose, the wheels rose with them, for the spirit of the living creature was in them.
## The glory leaves the threshold
10:18 Then the glory of the LORD went out from the threshold of the house and stood above the cherubim.
10:19 The cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the earth before my eyes as they went out, and the wheels were beside them. They stopped at the entrance of the east gate of the house of the LORD, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them.
10:20 This was the living creature I had seen under the God of Israel at the Kebar canal, and I knew that they were cherubim.
10:21 Each had four faces and four wings, and the likeness of human hands was under their wings.
10:22 Their faces looked the same as the faces I had seen at the Kebar canal, in appearance and in themselves. Each one went straight ahead.
## Twenty-five men at the east gate
11:1 A spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of the LORD, the one that faces east. There at the entrance of the gate were twenty-five men. Among them I saw Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, officials of the people.
11:2 He said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who plan harm and give evil advice in this city.
11:3 They are saying, 'The time to build houses is not near. This city is the pot, and we are the meat.'
11:4 Therefore prophesy against them. Prophesy, son of man."
11:5 Then the Spirit of the LORD fell on me, and he said to me, "Say: The LORD says this: That is what you have said, house of Israel, and I know what comes up in your mind.
11:6 You have multiplied your slain in this city and filled its streets with the slain.
11:7 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Your slain, whom you have put in the middle of it, are the meat, and this city is the pot. But you I will bring out of the middle of it.
11:8 You are afraid of the sword, and I will bring the sword on you, the Lord GOD declares.
11:9 I will bring you out of the middle of it and give you into the hand of foreigners, and I will carry out judgments on you.
11:10 You will fall by the sword. I will judge you at the border of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
11:11 This city will not be a pot for you, and you will not be meat in the middle of it. I will judge you at the border of Israel.
11:12 Then you will know that I am the LORD, whose rules you have not followed and whose rulings you have not done. Instead you have acted according to the rulings of the nations around you."
11:13 While I was prophesying, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. I fell on my face and cried out with a loud voice, "Ah, Lord GOD! Are you making a complete end of the remnant of Israel?"
## A little sanctuary for the exiles
11:14 The word of the LORD came to me:
11:15 "Son of man, your brothers, your brothers, the men of your redemption, and the whole house of Israel, all of it: these are the ones to whom the people living in Jerusalem have said, 'Stay far from the LORD. This land has been given to us as a possession.'
11:16 Therefore say: The Lord GOD says this: Though I removed them far away among the nations, and though I scattered them among the lands, I have become a little sanctuary for them in the lands where they have gone.
11:17 Therefore say: The Lord GOD says this: I will gather you from the peoples and collect you from the lands where you were scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel.
11:18 They will come there and remove from it all its detestable things and all its abhorrent things.
11:19 I will give them one heart, and I will put a new spirit inside you. I will take the heart of stone out of their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,
11:20 so that they will follow my rules and keep my rulings and do them. Then they will be my people, and I will be their God.
11:21 But as for those whose heart goes after their detestable things and their abhorrent things, I will pay back their conduct on their own heads, the Lord GOD declares."
## The glory leaves the city
11:22 Then the cherubim lifted their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them.
11:23 The glory of the LORD went up from the middle of the city and stood on the mountain east of the city.
11:24 A spirit lifted me up and brought me to the exiles in Chaldea, in the vision by the spirit of God. Then the vision I had seen went up from me.
11:25 And I told the exiles all the words of the LORD that he had shown me.
## An exile's baggage
12:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
12:2 "Son of man, you are living in the middle of a rebellious house. They have eyes to see but do not see, and ears to hear but do not hear, for they are a rebellious house.
12:3 And you, son of man, pack an exile's baggage for yourself and go into exile by day, as they watch. Go from your place to another place as they watch. Perhaps they will see, though they are a rebellious house.
12:4 Bring out your baggage by day, as they watch, like an exile's baggage. Then in the evening, as they watch, go out yourself, like those going out into exile.
12:5 As they watch, dig through the wall for yourself and bring the baggage out through it.
12:6 As they watch, lift it on your shoulder and carry it out in the dark. Cover your face so that you cannot see the land, for I have made you a sign for the house of Israel."
12:7 I did just as I was commanded. I brought out my baggage by day, like an exile's baggage. In the evening I dug through the wall with my hand. I brought the baggage out in the dark and lifted it on my shoulder as they watched.
## What the sign means
12:8 In the morning the word of the LORD came to me:
12:9 "Son of man, did the house of Israel, the rebellious house, not ask you, 'What are you doing?'
12:10 Say to them, 'The Lord GOD says this: This oracle concerns the leader in Jerusalem and the whole house of Israel in their midst.'
12:11 Say, 'I am a sign for you. As I have done, so it will be done to them. They will go into exile, into captivity.'
12:12 The leader who is among them will lift his baggage on his shoulder in the dark and go out. They will dig through the wall to bring him out through it. He will cover his face, so that he cannot see the land with his eye.
12:13 I will spread my net over him, and he will be caught in my hunting net. I will bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, but he will not see it, and there he will die.
12:14 All who are around him, his helpers and all his troops, I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw a sword after them.
12:15 Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I scatter them among the nations and disperse them among the lands.
12:16 But I will leave a few of them alive from the sword, from famine and from plague, so that they can tell about all their abhorrent things among the nations where they go. Then they will know that I am the LORD."
## Food with trembling
12:17 The word of the LORD came to me:
12:18 "Son of man, eat your food with shaking, and drink your water with trembling and with anxiety.
12:19 Say to the people of the land: The Lord GOD says this about those living in Jerusalem, in the land of Israel: They will eat their food in anxiety and drink their water in horror, because their land will be stripped of all that fills it, because of the violence of all who live in it.
12:20 The inhabited cities will be wasted, and the land will become a desolation. Then you will know that I am the LORD."
## The proverb that will end
12:21 The word of the LORD came to me:
12:22 "Son of man, what is this proverb you have about the land of Israel, saying, 'The days drag on, and every vision fails'?
12:23 Therefore say to them: The Lord GOD says this: I will put an end to this proverb, and they will not use it as a proverb in Israel again. But say to them: The days have come near, and so has the word of every vision.
12:24 For there will no longer be any empty vision or smooth divination in the middle of the house of Israel.
12:25 For I am the LORD. I will speak whatever word I speak, and it will be done. It will not be put off any longer. For in your days, rebellious house, I will speak a word and do it, the Lord GOD declares."
## No more delay
12:26 The word of the LORD came to me:
12:27 "Son of man, look, the house of Israel is saying, 'The vision he sees is for many days from now, and he prophesies about distant times.'
12:28 Therefore say to them: The Lord GOD says this: None of my words will be put off any longer. Whatever word I speak will be done, the Lord GOD declares."
## Prophets who follow their own spirit
13:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
13:2 "Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who are prophesying. Say to those who prophesy out of their own hearts: Hear the word of the LORD.
13:3 The Lord GOD says this: Woe to the foolish prophets who follow their own spirit and have seen nothing!
13:4 Your prophets, Israel, have been like foxes among ruins.
13:5 You have not gone up into the breaches or built a wall around the house of Israel, so that it could stand in battle on the day of the LORD.
13:6 They have seen emptiness and lying divination. They say, 'The LORD declares,' when the LORD has not sent them, and they expect their word to come true.
13:7 Have you not seen an empty vision and spoken lying divination, saying, 'The LORD declares,' when I have not spoken?
13:8 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Because you have spoken emptiness and seen lies, therefore I am against you, the Lord GOD declares.
13:9 My hand will be against the prophets who see emptiness and divine lies. They will not be in the council of my people. They will not be written in the register of the house of Israel, and they will not enter the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD.
## Whitewash on a wall
13:10 Precisely because they have led my people astray, saying, 'Peace!' when there is no peace, and because, when a wall is built, they plaster it with whitewash,
13:11 say to those who plaster it with whitewash that it will fall. There will be a flooding rain, and you, hailstones, will fall, and a stormy wind will break it open.
13:12 Look, the wall has fallen! Will people not say to you, 'Where is the whitewash you plastered on?'
13:13 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: I will make a stormy wind break out in my rage. A flooding rain will come in my anger, and hailstones in my rage, to destroy it completely.
13:14 I will tear down the wall that you plastered with whitewash and bring it down to the ground, so that its foundations are uncovered. It will fall, and you will come to an end in the middle of it. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
13:15 I will spend all my rage on the wall and on those who plastered it with whitewash, and I will say to you, 'The wall is gone, and so are those who plastered it,
13:16 the prophets of Israel who prophesied about Jerusalem and saw a vision of peace for it when there was no peace,' the Lord GOD declares."
## The women who tie and veil
13:17 "And you, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people who prophesy out of their own hearts, and prophesy against them.
13:18 Say: The Lord GOD says this: Woe to the women who sew bands on all the joints of my hands and make long veils for the head of people of every height, to hunt lives! Will you hunt the lives of my people and keep lives alive for your own gain?
13:19 You have profaned me to my people for a few handfuls of barley and scraps of bread. You put to death people who should not die and keep alive people who should not live, by lying to my people, who listen to lies.
13:20 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Look, I am against your bands, with which you hunt lives like birds. I will tear them from your arms and set free the lives you hunt, the lives you hunt like birds.
13:21 I will tear off your long veils and rescue my people from your hand. They will no longer be prey in your hand. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
13:22 Because you have made the heart of the righteous sad with lies, though I did not make him sad, and have strengthened the hands of the wicked, so that he does not turn from his evil way and save his life,
13:23 therefore you will no longer see emptiness or practice divination. I will rescue my people from your hand. Then you will know that I am the LORD."
## Idols in the heart
14:1 Some men from the elders of Israel came to me and sat down in front of me.
14:2 The word of the LORD came to me:
14:3 "Son of man, these men have brought their filthy idols up into their hearts, and they have put the stumbling block of their guilt in front of their faces. Should I let myself be consulted by them at all?
14:4 Therefore speak to them and say to them: The Lord GOD says this: Anyone from the house of Israel who brings his filthy idols up into his heart and puts the stumbling block of his guilt in front of his face, and then goes to the prophet: I, the LORD, will give him an answer when he comes, in line with the great number of his idols,
14:5 so that I may catch the house of Israel by their hearts, because all of them have become strangers to me through their filthy idols.
14:6 Therefore say to the house of Israel: The Lord GOD says this: Turn back! Turn away from your filthy idols, and turn your faces away from all your abhorrent things.
14:7 For anyone from the house of Israel, or from the foreigners who live in Israel, who separates himself from following me and brings his filthy idols up into his heart and puts the stumbling block of his guilt in front of his face, and then goes to the prophet to consult me through him: I, the LORD, will answer him myself.
14:8 I will set my face against that man. I will make him a sign and a byword, and I will cut him off from my people. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
## A prophet who is deceived
14:9 As for the prophet, if he is enticed and speaks a word, it is I, the LORD, who enticed that prophet. I will reach out my hand against him and destroy him, cutting him off from my people Israel.
14:10 They will bear their guilt. The guilt of the prophet will be the same as the guilt of the one who asks him,
14:11 so that the house of Israel will no longer wander away from me and no longer make themselves unclean with all their transgressions. They will be my people, and I will be their God, the Lord GOD declares."
## Noah, Daniel and Job
14:12 The word of the LORD came to me:
14:13 "Son of man, if a land sins against me by acting unfaithfully, and I stretch out my hand against it and break its staff of bread and send famine on it and cut off from it people and animals,
14:14 even if these three men were in it, Noah, Daniel and Job, they would save only their own lives by their righteousness, the Lord GOD declares.
14:15 If I were to bring wild animals through the land, and they left it childless, and it became a desolation so that no one could pass through because of the animals,
14:16 and these three men were in it, as I live, the Lord GOD declares, they would not save sons or daughters. They alone would be saved, and the land would be a desolation.
14:17 Or if I were to bring a sword on that land and say, 'Let a sword pass through the land,' and cut off from it people and animals,
14:18 and these three men were in it, as I live, the Lord GOD declares, they would not save sons or daughters. They alone would be saved.
14:19 Or if I were to send a plague on that land and pour out my rage on it with blood, to cut off from it people and animals,
14:20 and Noah, Daniel and Job were in it, as I live, the Lord GOD declares, they would not save a son or a daughter. By their righteousness they would save only their own lives.
## Survivors who come out
14:21 For the Lord GOD says this: How much worse when I send my four terrible judgments against Jerusalem, sword and famine and wild animals and plague, to cut off from it people and animals!
14:22 Yet some will be left in it, survivors who are led out, sons and daughters. Look, they are coming out to you, and you will see their conduct and their deeds. Then you will be comforted about the disaster I brought on Jerusalem, about all that I brought on it.
14:23 They will comfort you when you see their conduct and their deeds, and you will know that I did not do without cause all that I did in it, the Lord GOD declares."
## The useless vine
15:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
15:2 "Son of man, what is the wood of the vine more than any other wood, the vine branch that is among the trees of the forest?
15:3 Can one take timber from it to make some useful thing? Can one take even a peg from it to hang a vessel on?
15:4 Look, it has been handed to the fire to burn. The fire has eaten away both ends, and the middle is blackened. Could it serve any purpose?
15:5 Look, even when it was whole, nothing could be made from it. How much less, now that fire has eaten it and scorched it, could anything be made from it?
15:6 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: As the vine wood among the forest trees is the wood I have handed to the fire for fuel, so I have handed over the people living in Jerusalem.
15:7 I will set my face against them. They have come out of the fire, but the fire will consume them. Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I set my face against them.
15:8 I will make the land a desolation, because they have acted unfaithfully, the Lord GOD declares."
