# Hostile review: Ezekiel 8-15 (part 08-15)

`VERBOSE=1 node tools/build.js --check Ezk/08-15` gives 0 errors, 0 warnings. I read plain.txt, notes, variants, glosses, chapters, topics and sources, rebuilt chapters 8-15 from `literal.js`, and read Swete's Greek (`lxx.py Eze`) and the scroll lines (`dss.py Ezk`, 4Q73 is the only scroll for 10:5-11:11) and `texts/auto/Ezk.json`. No reader-facing text says "from memory".

## Verdict

The Plain English Translation is mostly faithful to the Leningrad text, and the notes are generally careful and honestly hedged. The weak spots are: (1) several verses are close echoes of the ESV or NIV (14:4, 14:9, chapter 15 above all); (2) a hidden Ketiv/Qere crux at 14:4 that changes the sense; (3) one invented-looking explanation (the 8:1 variant misdates the call vision); (4) a few citations that do not say what they are cited for (Lust CBQ 1981 for 12:26-28; Josephus Ant. 10.7.2; Calvin Institutes 1.11.8 as an exegesis of 14:3); (5) the 14:9 note gives no Jewish source and one reading (3) that no one is named as holding; (6) the Orthodox entry of the 14:14 card is unsourced and the 14:9 card shows churches that mostly agree; (7) missing cross-links to existing topics. Items checked and found sound: Hagigah 13b, Shabbat 55a, Rosh Hashanah 31a, Megillah 29a, Mishnah Sukkah 5:4, Mishnah Sanhedrin 11:5 (the false prophet who says what he did not hear; strangulation), Yoma 9b, Josephus War 6.5.3, CCC 311 / 956 / 1477 / 2683, Augsburg 21, Apology 21, WCF 5.4, 5.6, 21.2 (first clause only), Calvin Inst. 1.11.8 wording, Day JBL 98 (1979) 174-184 and Tov ETL 62 (1986) 89-101 as bibliography, P967 lacking 12:26-28 (the fact is right). Every Greek claim about Vaticanus (8:1 fifth month, 8:3 stele "of the one who acquires", 8:11 Jechonias, 8:16 about twenty, 8:17 sneering, 9:2 sapphire belt, 10:5 Saddai, 10:13 Gelgel, 10:14 absent, 10:21 eight wings, 11:1 Phaltias, 11:11-12 absent, 11:15 captivity, 11:19 another heart, 12:10, 13:18 pillows) checks out in Swete.

Findings: 0 critical, 7 major, 15 minor.

## Major

1. **Major. variants.json 8:1 "Sixth month or fifth month?": wrong date for the call vision.** The `why` says the Greek "fifth month" may come from the call vision's date, "which also falls on the fifth day of the fifth month", and that this "would put the second vision exactly a year after the first". Ezekiel 1:1 gives the fourth month, fifth day (1:2 only repeats the fifth day and the fifth year). The explanation is wrong, and the "exactly a year" claim contradicts note 8:1 ("a little over a year"). Also, "The Hebrew text read for this Plain English Translation has only 'sixth'" is odd wording. Fix: delete the invented derivation; say only that the Greek has "fifth month" and that its source is unknown (a scribal slip for "sixth", or a different Hebrew date).

2. **Major. plain.txt 14:4, with notes/variants/chapter page: undeclared Ketiv/Qere crux and NIV echo.** `texts/auto` shows 14:4 word 32: Ketiv `bah` ("in it"), Qere `ba'` ("he who came"). The Plain English Translation, "I, the LORD, will answer him myself, in keeping with his many idols", renders neither: "myself" is imported from 14:7 (`bi`), and "answer ... myself, in keeping with" tracks the NIV's phrasing for this verse. The chapter-page witness paragraph says Ketiv/Qere at 14:4 are "not listed as variants unless they change the sense", but this one does. Fix: rebuild from the Hebrew ("I, the LORD, will answer him [in it/as he comes] with the multitude of his idols"), add a `deb` note and a Ketiv/Qere variant entry, and change the witness paragraph.

3. **Major. plain.txt 14:9: near-verbatim ESV sentence in the verse the brief singles out.** Plain English Translation: "And if the prophet is deceived and speaks a word, I, the LORD, have deceived that prophet. I will stretch out my hand against him and destroy him from among my people Israel." This is the ESV sentence with a period inserted and "midst" changed to "among" (about 25 consecutive words; the NIV has the same frame with "enticed"). "Deceived" is a defensible word for `patah`/`piteiti` (Pual/Piel; compare 1 Kings 22:20-22, Jeremiah 20:7), but the sentence must be rebuilt, for instance "As for the prophet, if he is lured into speaking a word, it is I, the LORD, who have lured that prophet. I will stretch out..." with a note that English versions split between "deceived" (KJV, ESV) and "enticed/prevailed upon" (NIV, NASB). Note 14:9 itself should state the word choice and why.

4. **Major. notes.json 14:9 and chapters.json ch. 14 (J view): readings unsourced, one reading invented, "most" unchecked.** Brief requires Jewish and Christian readings. The note has no Jewish voice (Targum, Rashi, Radak, Talmud); the ch. 14 "J" view says only that the verse "is read, as in the Christian tradition, as a judgment", with no source. Reading (3) "the verb states what the prophet claims, which the LORD proves false" is attributed to no one and I know of no holder; delete or source it. "This is how the Greek and most Christian commentators took it" is a "most" claim with no check; the Greek (Swete) in fact has the protasis active (`ean planese`, "if the prophet leads astray") and the apodosis `peplanēka`, which deserves a line. Fix: name sources (Rashi/Radak on 14:9 via Sefaria; the permissive vs. active-judgment split) or say "reported by"; drop "most".

5. **Major. Plain English Translation chapter 15: ESV sentence shapes.** Checked verse by verse:
   - 15:3 "Is wood taken from it to make anything? Do people take a peg from it to hang any vessel on?" equals the ESV with "men" changed to "people" and the last "it" dropped.
   - 15:4 "it is given to the fire for fuel ... the middle of it is charred. Is it useful for anything?" repeats the ESV's clauses and words.
   - 15:5 "How much less, when fire has consumed it and it is charred, can it be made into anything again?" has the ESV's frame (the Plain English Translation's "made into anything" is the one independent piece).
   - 15:6 "Like the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so I have given the people living in Jerusalem" is the ESV's 25 words almost unchanged.
   The Hebrew is parallel, but the brief asks for sentence shape, and the only departure is a synonym. Rebuild all four from `literal.js` (e.g. 15:3 as a pair of "Can one take ... ? Can one take ... ?" questions; 15:4 as a statement and a closing question with different structure; 15:6 "As the vine wood ... is the one I have handed to the fire for fuel, so I have handed over the people of Jerusalem").

6. **Major. variants.json 12:26-28 (three entries): wrong article cited.** `lust-ezekiel-36-40` (CBQ 43 [1981] 517-533) is about chapters 36-40 and does not report 12:26-28; the line "reported by Tov and by Lust" points to the wrong work. The fact is right (P967 lacks 12:26-28; Filson 1943 first noted it; see Lust's essay in Lust, ed., *Ezekiel and His Book*). Fix: cite Tov (if he lists it in ETL 62, check) and Filson or the Lust chapter that does, or drop to "reported in the apparatus of Ziegler". Also the same three entries repeat one long variant three times; one entry on 12:26 with the range would do, and the "scribe skipped them" explanation should say that a 3-verse omission cannot be a simple homoioteleuton on its own (Lust's argument).

7. **Major. notes.json 12:12: Josephus citation is wrong.** The passage in which Zedekiah thinks Jeremiah and Ezekiel contradict each other and believes neither is Ant. 10.106-107, which is Whiston 10.6.3 (the brief itself says "check the reference before citing"). 10.7.2 is the capture and blinding. The same wrong reference is in chapter 12's J view (also confirm that this is "Jewish tradition": Josephus is a first-century Jewish historian, but not "Jewish interpreters" in the sense of the view's label). Fix both to Ant. 10.6.3 (§§ 104-107), and cite 10.7.2 (§§ 141-142) only for the fulfillment.

## Minor

8. **Minor. Plain English Translation 9:9, 9:10, 8:5-8:11, 9:2, 9:6-9:8, 14:3-14:8, 11:16-17: shape matches ESV/NIV, mostly forced by the Hebrew.** 9:9 ("The guilt of the house of Israel and Judah is very, very great. The land is full of bloodshed, and the city is full of ...  'The LORD has left the land, and the LORD does not see'") follows the ESV/NIV frame closely; 9:6 "And begin at my sanctuary" and 9:7 "So they went out and struck in the city" are ESV words; 9:2 and 9:8 are near the ESV's structure; 11:16-17 and 14:7-8 ("a sign and a byword and cut him off from among my people"; "to consult me through him ... answer him myself") are ESV words. Most are forced, so not required, but rework 9:9-10, 9:6 and 14:8 for variety. 9:10 drops `gam` ("also"), while 8:18 renders the same `gam-ani` "I too"; make them agree.

9. **Minor. plain.txt 13:15: mistranslation.** "I will pour out all my rage on the wall" renders `vekhilleti` (from `kalah`, "finish, use up"); `shafakh` is "pour". Use "I will spend all my rage".

10. **Minor. plain.txt 10:2.** `bo el-bein haggalgal` ("go in between the whirling wheel") loses "between" ("Go in to the whirling wheel"); the Greek has "into the midst of the wheels", and 10:6 keeps "between". Note 10:2 says the verb `zaraq` is "scatter, like seed or chaff"; `zaraq` is "sprinkle, dash" (blood, ashes, Exodus 9:8), and seed/chaff is `zarah`. Correct the note (the literal line already says "sprinkle").

11. **Minor. plain.txt 11:19.** "I will give them one heart, and I will put a new spirit inside you": the Hebrew has the person-shift (`lahem` then `bekirbekhem`), and no note says so. Add a half-line. Same for 11:5 "in your mind" for `ruach`: fine, but say once.

12. **Minor. Missed scroll candidate 4Q73 10:8.** `dss.py` marks `ידי` for MT `יד` ("hands" vs "hand" of a man), like 10:21. Mention it in the 10:21 note or a variant. The 10:14 variant correctly says "cherub" is restored.

13. **Minor. variants.json 11:11-12: the explanation does not work.** "A copyist's eye jumping from 'you will know that I am the LORD' in 11:10 to the same words in 11:12" would leave the rest of 11:12 in the Greek; the Greek lacks all of 11:11-12. Say "the cause is unknown", and give the reading that the Greek may reflect a shorter Hebrew text (the scroll 4Q73 shows 11:11 in Hebrew before the Greek's earliest witness). Also drop "BSB" as a "witness" in 8:2 (a modern translation is not a witness).

14. **Minor. notes.json 14:14 / topics.json `daniel`: "28:3 is written dn'l".** In the text the spelling at 28:3 is `דָּנִיֵּאל` (with yod); only 14:14 and 14:20 are defective (the auto file marks Ketiv `dani'el` at 14:14, 20 only). Fix the `daniel` topic section.

15. **Minor. chapters.json ch. 14 (X view): Calvin.** The text says Calvin "read 14:3 as a charge against the heart" and cites Institutes 1.11.8; that section is on idols in general and does not discuss Ezekiel 14:3. Either cite Calvin's Ezekiel lectures (checked) or say "Calvin's saying about the mind as a factory of idols (Institutes 1.11.8) is often applied to 14:3".

16. **Minor. Dating: "about five years before Jerusalem fell"** (ch. 8, 9, 10, 11 settings; ch. 8 H view "Five years later") vs. "about six years" (ch. 12; note 12:23). September 592 to July/August 586 is almost six years. Make them agree ("almost six").

17. **Minor. chapters.json ch. 12 "Read as a prediction fulfilled".** "...that is what happened, though Ezekiel was in Babylon and never saw it" is garbled (it was Zedekiah who did not see Babylon-as-free-man). Rewrite. H view calls this "one of the most precisely fulfilled predictions in the book": hedge (the blinding is a harmonization, as the deep note says).

18. **Minor. Ch. 14 tradition card "communion-of-saints".** Orthodox entry has no source (brief: source each to the church's own text; e.g. the liturgy of the saints or the Confession of Dositheus, or drop the entry). The Reformed entry's second clause ("the Christian's good works cannot earn anything for another") is not WCF 21.2 (21.2 is worship to God alone); cite WCF 16 or remove. Also missing: Anglican (Article XXII), Baptist; not required but the brief asks to give every major view where churches really differ. The card is a real conflict (invocation of saints); the link to Ezekiel 14 is the Protestant-vs-Catholic use of this text, which the card should say in one clause.

19. **Minor. Ch. 14 tradition card "providence" (14:9): not a real conflict as written.** Reformed, Lutheran, Catholic and Methodist all say God is not the author of sin; the real difference (Westminster 5.4, "not by a bare permission", versus Catechism 311 "permits") is never stated, and the Methodist entry is about predestination, not deception. Either rewrite so the contrast is explicit ("not a bare permission" vs "permits") and cite Formula of Concord SD XI with paragraph numbers (the Article alone is cited; the "hardening ... on those who have already resisted" sentence needs its paragraph), or demote to a `views` note. Add Calvin (Institutes 1.18 on God's use of the wicked and of Ahab's lying spirit) as the Reformed source on 1 Kings 22 / Ezekiel 14:9.

20. **Minor. Missing cross-links to existing topics.** Chapter 12 should list `sign-acts`, `zedekiah` (and `fall-of-jerusalem`), with a contribution; chapter 8 should list `queen-of-heaven` (8:14 beside Jeremiah 44) and chapter 11 `jehoiachin`/`zedekiah` as relevant. Brief asks `sign-acts` for 12.

21. **Minor. Settings.** Chapters 8-11 get one card per chapter, each with the whole-vision `to`; add separate cards for 8:1-4/5-18 (to the elders in the house), 9:1-11, 10, 11:1-13 (already). Chapter 12:21-28 treats two oracles as one; 13:1-16 "views" both take "prophets of Israel" as a place question, but 13:2 "prophets of Israel" is addressed to Ezekiel's exile audience about both, which the "to" says. Fine, minor.

22. **Minor.
