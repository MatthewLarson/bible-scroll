## Against Ammon
25:1 The LORD's word came to me:
25:2 Son of man, fix your face on the Ammonites and prophesy against them.
25:3 Say to the Ammonites: Hear the word of the Lord GOD. The Lord GOD says this: Because you cried "Aha!" over my sanctuary as it was profaned, over the land of Israel as it was laid waste, and over the house of Judah as it went into exile,
25:4 therefore I am about to hand you over to the people of the east as a possession. They will set up camps among you and place their dwellings in your midst. They will eat your fruit. They will drink your milk.
25:5 I will turn Rabbah into grazing land for camels and the Ammonites into a place where flocks lie down. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
25:6 For the Lord GOD says this: Because you clapped your hands and stamped your foot, and rejoiced over the land of Israel with all the scorn that was in you,
25:7 therefore I have stretched out my hand against you. I will give you to the nations as plunder. I will cut you off from the peoples and make you perish from the lands. I will destroy you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
## Against Moab
25:8 The Lord GOD says this: Because Moab and Seir said, "Look, the house of Judah is the same as all the nations,"
25:9 therefore I am about to lay open the flank of Moab, beginning with its cities, the cities at its far edge, the glory of the land: Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon and Kiriathaim.
25:10 I will give it to the people of the east, along with the Ammonites, as a possession, so that the Ammonites will not be remembered among the nations.
25:11 I will carry out judgments on Moab. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## Against Edom
25:12 The Lord GOD says this: Because Edom acted in revenge against the house of Judah, and became deeply guilty by taking revenge on them,
25:13 therefore the Lord GOD says this: I will stretch out my hand against Edom and cut off both human beings and animals from it. I will make it a wasteland. From Teman as far as Dedan they will fall by the sword.
25:14 I will set my vengeance on Edom by the hand of my people Israel. They will deal with Edom as my anger and my rage demand. Then they will know my vengeance, the Lord GOD declares.
## Against the Philistines
25:15 The Lord GOD says this: Because the Philistines acted in revenge, and took their revenge with scorn from deep inside, to destroy out of an old hatred,
25:16 therefore the Lord GOD says this: I am about to stretch out my hand against the Philistines. I will cut off the Kerethites and make the rest of the people on the seacoast perish.
25:17 I will carry out great acts of vengeance on them, with rebukes in rage. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I put my vengeance on them.
## Tyre will be scraped bare
26:1 In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, the LORD's word came to me:
26:2 Son of man, because Tyre said about Jerusalem, "Aha! The doors of the peoples are smashed. It has turned to me. I will be filled, now that she lies waste,"
26:3 therefore the Lord GOD says this: I am against you, Tyre. I will make many nations rise against you, the way the sea makes its waves rise.
26:4 They will wreck the walls of Tyre and pull down its towers. I will scrape the dust off it and make it a bare rock.
26:5 In the middle of the sea it will lie, a place for spreading nets, for I have spoken, the Lord GOD declares. It will become spoil for the nations.
26:6 Its daughter towns on the mainland will be killed by the sword. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## The king from the north
26:7 For the Lord GOD says this: I am about to bring Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon against Tyre from the north. He is the king of kings, and he comes with horses, chariots and horsemen, and with a crowd of soldiers and many people.
26:8 He will kill your daughter towns on the mainland with the sword. He will build a siege wall against you, heap up a ramp against you, and raise a wall of shields against you.
26:9 He will set the blow of his battering ram against your walls and break your towers down with his blades.
26:10 His horses are so many that their dust will cover you. At the noise of horsemen, wheels and chariots your walls will shake, when he enters your gates the way men enter a city that has been broken open.
26:11 With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets. He will kill your people with the sword, and your strong pillars will come down to the ground.
26:12 They will loot your wealth and carry off your merchandise as spoil. They will knock down your walls and tear down your pleasant houses, and your stones, your timber and your dust they will throw into the middle of the water.
26:13 I will silence the din of your songs, and the music of your lyres will never again be heard.
26:14 I will make you a bare rock. You will be a place for spreading nets. You will never be built again, for I the LORD have spoken, the Lord GOD declares.
## The islands tremble
26:15 The Lord GOD says this to Tyre: Will the islands not quake at the crash of your fall, when the mortally wounded cry out, when the slaughter goes on inside you?
26:16 Then all the princes of the sea will step down from their thrones. They will take off their robes and strip away their embroidered clothes. Trembling will be their clothing. They will sit on the ground, shaking every moment, aghast over you.
26:17 Then they will raise a lament over you and say to you: / How you have been lost, / you who were settled from the seas, / the praised city, / who was strong on the sea, / she and those who lived in her, / who gave their terror / to all who lived around her!
26:18 Now the islands tremble / on the day of your fall. / The islands in the sea are terrified / at your departure.
26:19 For the Lord GOD says this: When I make you a ruined city, like cities where no one lives, when I bring the deep up over you, and the many waters cover you,
26:20 then I will send you down with those who go down to the Pit, to the people of long ago. I will make you live in the lowest part of the earth, in ruins from ancient times, with those who go down to the Pit, so that you will not be inhabited. And I will set splendor in the land of the living.
26:21 I will make you a horror, and you will be no more. You will be sought, but you will never be found again, the Lord GOD declares.
## A lament for the ship Tyre
27:1 The LORD's word came to me:
27:2 And you, son of man, raise a lament over Tyre.
27:3 Say to Tyre, who sits at the gateways of the sea, / the dealer of the peoples to many islands: / The Lord GOD says this: / Tyre, you have said, / "I am complete in beauty."
27:4 Your borders were in the heart of the seas; / your builders made your beauty complete.
27:5 They built all your planks of cypress from Senir. / They took a cedar from Lebanon / to make a mast for you.
27:6 They made your oars from the oaks of Bashan. / They made your deck of ivory set in pine / from the islands of Cyprus.
27:7 Linen with colorful weaving, from Egypt, / was your sail and became your banner. / Blue and purple from the islands of Elishah / was your covering.
27:8 The people of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers. / Your wise men, Tyre, were aboard you; / they were your pilots.
27:9 The elders of Gebal and her wise men were in you / as repairers of your leaks. / All the ships of the sea and their sailors were in you / to deal in your goods.
27:10 Men from Persia, Lud and Put served in your army, / your warriors. / They hung up shield and helmet in you; / they gave you your glory.
27:11 The people of Arvad, with your own army, were on your walls all around, / and the Gammadites were in your towers. / They hung up their shields on your walls on every side, / and so made your beauty complete.
## The traders of Tyre
27:12 Tarshish was your trader, drawn by your wealth of every kind: / silver, iron, tin and lead were what they gave for your goods.
27:13 Javan, Tubal and Meshech were your dealers. / They traded human lives and bronze vessels for your goods.
27:14 From Beth-togarmah they gave horses, war horses and mules / in exchange for your goods.
27:15 The people of Dedan were your dealers. / Many islands were the market in your hand; / they brought back horns of ivory and ebony as your payment.
27:16 Aram was your trader because of your many products. / They paid for your goods with turquoise, purple, / embroidered work, fine linen, coral and rubies.
27:17 Judah and the land of Israel were your dealers. / They paid for your goods with wheat from Minnith, / sweet cakes, honey, oil and balm.
27:18 Damascus was your trader because of your many products, / your wealth of every kind: / she traded wine of Helbon and white wool.
27:19 Vedan and Javan from Uzal traded for your goods. / Wrought iron, cassia and sweet cane / were among your merchandise.
27:20 Dedan was your dealer in saddlecloths for riding.
27:21 Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were traders in your service, / dealing with you in lambs, rams and goats.
27:22 The dealers of Sheba and Raamah were your dealers. / They paid for your goods with the best of every spice, / with every precious stone and with gold.
27:23 Haran, Canneh and Eden, / the dealers of Sheba, Asshur and Chilmad / were your dealers.
27:24 They were your dealers in splendid clothing, / in cloaks of blue and embroidery, / in bales of many-colored cloth, tied with cords, / and in cedar chests, / in your marketplace.
## The wreck
27:25 The ships of Tarshish were the carriers of your goods. / So you were full, and you were heavy with glory / in the heart of the seas.
27:26 On the great waters your rowers took you, / and the east wind wrecked you / in the heart of the seas.
27:27 Your wealth, your goods, your merchandise, / your sailors and your pilots, / those who repair your leaks, / those who trade your merchandise, / and all your fighting men who are on board, / with all the crowd in your midst, / will fall into the heart of the seas / on the day of your fall.
27:28 At the sound of your pilots' cry / the open lands shake.
27:29 All who handle the oar, / the sailors and all the pilots of the sea, / will come down from their ships / and stand on the land.
27:30 They will make their voices heard over you / and cry out bitterly. / They will throw dust on their heads / and roll in ashes.
27:31 They will shave their heads bald for you / and put on sackcloth. / They will weep over you in bitterness of heart, / with bitter mourning.
27:32 In their wailing they will raise a lament over you, / they will mourn over you: / "Who was ever like Tyre, / silenced in the middle of the sea?"
27:33 When your goods went out over the seas, / you satisfied many peoples. / With your great wealth and your merchandise / you made the kings of the earth rich.
27:34 Now you are broken by the seas, / in the depths of the water. / Your merchandise and all your company / have fallen in the middle of you.
27:35 All who live on the islands are appalled at you. / Their kings shudder with horror; / their faces are twisted with fear.
27:36 The traders among the peoples hiss at you. / You have become a horror, / and you will be no more, ever.
## A ruler who said, "I am a god"
28:1 The LORD's word came to me:
28:2 Son of man, say to the ruler of Tyre: The Lord GOD says this: Because your heart has grown proud, you have said, "I am a god! I sit in a seat for gods, in the middle of the seas." But you are a human being and not a god, though you make your heart like the heart of a god.
28:3 Look, you are wiser than Daniel! No secret is dark to you!
28:4 Through your wisdom and your understanding you got yourself wealth; gold and silver you gathered into your treasuries.
28:5 By your great skill in trading you have piled up your wealth, and your wealth has made your heart proud.
28:6 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Because you have made your heart like the heart of a god,
28:7 therefore I am about to bring outsiders against you, the most violent of the nations. They will draw their swords against your wisdom's beauty and profane your splendor.
28:8 They will bring you down to the Pit, and you will die as the slain die, in the heart of the seas.
28:9 Will you still say, "I am a god," in front of the one who kills you? You are a human being and not a god, in the hands of those who pierce you.
28:10 You will die the death of the uncircumcised, by the hand of outsiders, for I have spoken, the Lord GOD declares.
## A lament for the king of Tyre
28:11 The LORD's word came to me:
28:12 Son of man, raise a lament over the king of Tyre and say to him: The Lord GOD says this: / You were one who set the seal on the pattern, / full of wisdom / and complete in beauty.
28:13 In Eden, the garden of God, you were. / Every precious stone was your covering: / carnelian, topaz and quartz, / chrysolite, onyx and jasper, / lapis lazuli, turquoise and emerald, / and gold. / The work of your tambourines and your sockets was in you; / on the day you were created they were made ready.
28:14 You were a cherub, outspread, the one who covers, and I appointed you. / You were on the holy mountain of God; / you walked about among stones of fire.
28:15 From the day you were created, / your ways were blameless, / until wrong was found in you.
28:16 Because of your great trading / violence filled your midst, / and you sinned. / So I profaned you, away from the mountain of God, / and I made you perish, covering cherub, / from among the stones of fire.
28:17 Your heart grew proud over your beauty; / you spoiled your wisdom over your splendor. / To the ground I threw you; / before kings I set you, for them to gaze at you.
28:18 With your great crimes, / with the dishonesty of your trading, / you profaned your sanctuaries. / So I drew a fire out of you; / it devoured you. / I made you ash on the ground / before the eyes of all who looked at you.
28:19 All who knew you among the peoples / are appalled at you. / You have become a horror, / and you will be no more, ever.
## Sidon
28:20 The LORD's word came to me:
28:21 Son of man, fix your face on Sidon and prophesy against her.
28:22 Say: The Lord GOD says this: I am against you, Sidon. I will win glory in your midst. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I carry out judgments on her and show myself holy in her.
28:23 I will send a plague into her and blood into her streets. The slain will fall in her midst, by the sword that is against her on every side. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## No more thorns for Israel
28:24 No longer will the house of Israel have a stabbing briar or a painful thorn from any of their neighbors who treated them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.
28:25 The Lord GOD says this: When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they were scattered, and show myself holy among them in the sight of the nations, they will live on their own ground, which I gave to my servant Jacob.
28:26 They will live on it in safety. They will build houses and set out vineyards. They will live in safety once I have carried out judgments on every neighbor who treated them with contempt. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.
