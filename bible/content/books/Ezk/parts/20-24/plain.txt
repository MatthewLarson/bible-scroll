## The elders come to ask
20:1 It was the seventh year, the tenth day of the fifth month. Men from among the elders of Israel came to seek an answer from the LORD, and they took seats in front of me.
20:2 The word of the LORD came to me:
20:3 Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel. Tell them, "The Lord GOD says this: Have you come to ask me for guidance? As I live, I will not let you ask me for guidance, the Lord GOD declares."
20:4 Will you judge them? Will you judge them, son of man? Then show them the abhorrent things their fathers did.
## Rebellion in Egypt
20:5 Tell them, "The Lord GOD says this: When I chose Israel, I raised my hand in an oath to the descendants of Jacob's house, and in Egypt I made myself known to them. With my hand raised I said to them, 'I am the LORD your God.'
20:6 On that day I raised my hand to them: I would take them out of the land of Egypt, into a land I had scouted for them, flowing with milk and honey. It is the Beautiful Land, above all lands.
20:7 I said to them, 'Each of you, throw away the detestable things before your eyes, and do not make yourselves unclean with the filthy idols of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.'
20:8 But they rebelled against me and would not listen to me. Not one of them threw away the detestable things before his eyes, and they did not leave the filthy idols of Egypt. So I said I would pour out my rage on them and spend my anger on them in the middle of the land of Egypt.
20:9 But I acted for the sake of my name, so that it would not be profaned in front of the nations they were living among. Those nations saw me make myself known to Israel by bringing them out of the land of Egypt.
## Rebellion in the wilderness
20:10 I took them out of the land of Egypt and brought them to the wilderness.
20:11 I gave them my rules and showed them my rulings, the ones by which a human being who does them will live.
20:12 I gave them my Sabbaths too, as a sign between me and them. It was so that they would know that I, the LORD, make them holy.
20:13 But the house of Israel rebelled against me in the wilderness. They did not walk by my rules. They rejected my rulings, the ones by which a human being who does them will live. They profaned my Sabbaths terribly. So I said I would pour out my rage on them in the wilderness and put an end to them.
20:14 I acted for the sake of my name, to keep it from being profaned before the eyes of the nations who had watched me bring Israel out.
20:15 Yet I also raised my hand to them in the wilderness, in an oath that I would not bring them into the land I had given them, a land flowing with milk and honey, the Beautiful Land above all lands.
20:16 It was because they rejected my rulings, did not walk by my rules, and profaned my Sabbaths. Their hearts kept walking after their filthy idols.
20:17 Yet my eye took pity on them, and I did not wipe them out. I did not bring total ruin on them in the wilderness.
20:18 I said to their children in the wilderness, 'Do not walk by the rules of your fathers. Do not keep their rulings. Do not make yourselves unclean with their filthy idols.
20:19 I am the LORD your God. Walk by my rules. Keep my rulings and do them.
20:20 Keep my Sabbaths holy, and they will be a sign between me and you, so that you will know that I am the LORD your God.'
20:21 But the children rebelled against me. They did not walk by my rules. They did not keep my rulings and do them, the ones by which a human being who does them will live. They profaned my Sabbaths. So I said I would pour out my rage on them and spend my anger on them in the wilderness.
20:22 But I turned my hand back. I acted for my own name, so that the nations who had watched me bring Israel out would not see it profaned.
20:23 Yet I also raised my hand to them in the wilderness, in an oath that I would scatter them among the nations and drive them apart across the lands,
20:24 because they had not done my rulings. They had rejected my rules and profaned my Sabbaths, and their eyes were on the filthy idols of their fathers.
20:25 And I myself gave them rules that were not good, and rulings they would not live by.
20:26 I made them unclean through their own gifts, when they made every firstborn pass over. I did it to leave them horrified, so that they would know that I am the LORD."
## Rebellion in the land
20:27 So now, son of man, speak to the house of Israel. Tell them, "The Lord GOD says this: Here is another way your fathers reviled me. They acted unfaithfully against me.
20:28 When I brought them into the land that I had raised my hand to give them, they looked at every high hill and every leafy tree. They offered their sacrifices there. They brought their offerings there that angered me. They put out their soothing smell there, and they poured out their drink offerings there.
20:29 I said to them, 'What is this high place you are going to?' Its name has been called Bamah, which means High Place, to this day."
20:30 Therefore tell the house of Israel, "The Lord GOD says this: Are you making yourselves unclean in the way of your fathers? Are you prostituting yourselves after their detestable things?
20:31 When you lift up your gifts and make your children pass through the fire, you are making yourselves unclean with all your filthy idols to this very day. And should I let you ask me for guidance, house of Israel? As I live, the Lord GOD declares, I will not let you ask me for guidance.
## A new exodus
20:32 What comes up in your mind will never happen. You are saying, 'We will be like the nations, like the clans of the lands, serving wood and stone.'
20:33 As I live, the Lord GOD declares, I will surely rule over you with a strong hand and an outstretched arm and with rage poured out.
20:34 With a strong hand and an outstretched arm and with rage poured out, I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands where you were scattered.
20:35 I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples, and there I will put you on trial, face to face.
20:36 As I put your fathers on trial in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will put you on trial, the Lord GOD declares.
20:37 I will make you pass beneath the rod, and I will bring you into the covenant's bond.
20:38 I will clear the rebels out from among you, and those who defy me. I will lead them out of the country where they are living as foreigners, but into the land of Israel they will not come. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
20:39 As for you, house of Israel, the Lord GOD says this: Go, each of you, and serve your filthy idols. But afterward, if you will not listen to me, you will no longer profane my holy name with your gifts and your filthy idols.
20:40 For on my holy mountain, on Israel's high mountain, the Lord GOD declares, the whole house of Israel will serve me there in the land, all of them. There I will welcome them. There I will ask for your contributions and the choicest of your portions, together with all your holy things.
20:41 I will welcome you like a soothing smell, when I bring you out from the peoples and collect you from the lands where you were scattered. And before the eyes of the nations I will show myself holy among you.
20:42 Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I bring you into the land of Israel, into the land I raised my hand to give to your fathers.
20:43 There you will remember your ways, and every act that made you unclean. You will hate the sight of yourselves because of all the evils you committed.
20:44 Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I act toward you for the sake of my name, and not as your wicked ways and your ruined practices deserve, house of Israel, the Lord GOD declares."
## Fire in the southern forest
20:45 The word of the LORD came to me:
20:46 Son of man, turn your face toward the south. Let your word drip toward the south, and prophesy against the forest of the open land in the Negev.
20:47 Say to the forest of the Negev, "Hear the word of the LORD. The Lord GOD says this: Look, I am about to kindle a fire in you. It will eat every green tree in you and every dry tree. Its flaming flame will not go out, and every face from the south to the north will be seared by it.
20:48 All flesh will see that I, the LORD, have kindled it. It will not be put out."
20:49 Then I said, "Alas, Lord GOD! They are saying of me, 'Isn't he always speaking in parables?'"
## The sword drawn from its sheath
21:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
21:2 Son of man, set your face against Jerusalem. Let your word drip against the holy places, and prophesy against the land of Israel.
21:3 Say to the land of Israel, "The LORD says this: Look, I am against you. I will pull my sword from its sheath and cut the righteous and the wicked off from you.
21:4 Since I will cut the righteous and the wicked off from you, my sword will come out of its sheath against all flesh, from the south to the north.
21:5 Then all flesh will know that I, the LORD, have pulled my sword from its sheath. It will not return again."
21:6 As for you, son of man, groan! Groan with your hips breaking and with bitterness, in front of their eyes.
21:7 If they ask you, "What are you groaning about?" tell them, "A report is on its way. Every heart will melt. Every pair of hands will go limp. Every spirit will faint, and all knees will run with water. See, it is coming, and it will happen," the Lord GOD declares.
## The song of the sword
21:8 The word of the LORD came to me:
21:9 Son of man, prophesy and say, "The Lord says this: / A sword, a sword! / It is sharpened, and it is polished too.
21:10 It is sharpened to make a slaughter. / It is polished until it gleams like lightning. / Should we celebrate? The scepter of my son despises every tree.
21:11 Someone has given it out to be polished, to be grasped in the palm. / The sword has been sharpened; it has been polished, / to be placed in the hand of one who slays.
21:12 Shout and howl, son of man, / because it has come on my people. / It has come on every leader of Israel. / They are handed to the sword along with my people, / so slap your thigh.
21:13 For a test has been made. / And what if even the scepter that despises will not be? the Lord GOD declares.
21:14 And you, son of man, prophesy and clap hand to hand. / Let the sword double, let it come a third time. / It is a sword for the slain, / the sword for the great slain, which closes in on them.
21:15 So that hearts melt and the stumbling at all their gates grows great, / I have set the slaughter of the sword. / Ah! It is made for lightning, and wrapped up for slaughter.
21:16 Be sharp! Go to the right! Take your stand! / Go to the left! Wherever your face is set!
21:17 I too will clap hand to hand, and I will let my rage rest. / I, the LORD, have spoken."
## Two roads for the king of Babylon
21:18 The word of the LORD came to me:
21:19 And you, son of man, make two roads for the sword of the king of Babylon to travel. Both begin in the same country. Set up a marker at the start of the road that leads to a city. Set it up there.
21:20 Make one road for the sword to come to Rabbah of the Ammonites, and the other to Judah, to fortified Jerusalem.
21:21 The king of Babylon has halted where the road splits, where the two roads begin, to read the omens. He rattles the arrows. He asks questions of the teraphim. He examines the liver.
21:22 The omen in his right hand says Jerusalem. It says to bring up battering rams, to give the order to kill, to shout the battle cry, to place rams against the gates, to heap up a ramp and to build a siege wall.
21:23 In their eyes it will be a false divination, for they have sworn oaths. But he will bring their guilt to mind, so that they are captured.
21:24 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Because you made your guilt come to mind by exposing your rebellions, so that your sins were seen in all your practices, you have come to mind, and you will be gripped by the hand.
21:25 And you, profane and wicked leader of Israel, whose day has come, in the season when guilt reaches its end,
21:26 the Lord GOD says this: Off with the turban, away with the crown! Nothing will stay as it was. The low will be raised, and the high will be brought down.
21:27 A ruin, a ruin, a ruin: that is what I will make it. And this too will not remain, until he comes whose right it is. To him I will give it.
## A sword against the Ammonites
21:28 And you, son of man, prophesy and say, "The Lord GOD says this about the Ammonites and the scorn they pour out. Say: / A sword, a sword, bare and drawn for the killing! / It is polished to devour, / to gleam like lightning.
21:29 While they see false visions for you / and divine lies for you, / it will lay you on the necks of the wicked who are slain, / whose day has come, in the season when their guilt reaches its end.
21:30 Put it away in its sheath! / Where you were made, / in the land where you began, I will judge you.
21:31 I will pour out my indignation on you / and blow on you with the fire of my fury. / I will give you into the hands of brutish men, / craftsmen in destroying.
21:32 You will become fuel for the fire. / Your blood will lie in the middle of the land. / No one will remember you, / for I, the LORD, have spoken."
## The city of bloodshed
22:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
22:2 And you, son of man, will you judge, will you judge the city of bloodshed? Then make known to her all her abhorrent things.
22:3 Say, "The Lord GOD says this: O city that spills blood within herself, so that her hour arrives, and that makes filthy idols against herself and so becomes unclean!
22:4 Your blood-spilling has made you guilty, and your filthy idols have made you unclean. You have brought your days near and come to the last of your years. That is why I have turned you into a disgrace among the nations and a joke to all the lands.
22:5 Those near and those far from you will mock you, you of the unclean name, you great in confusion.
22:6 Look, each of the leaders of Israel in you has used his arm to shed blood.
22:7 They have made light of father and mother within you. They have squeezed the foreigner with oppression inside you. They have mistreated the fatherless and the widow in you.
22:8 You have despised my holy things and profaned my Sabbaths.
22:9 There are slanderers within you who shed blood. There are people in you who have eaten on the mountains. Within you they have committed depravity.
22:10 In you they have uncovered the nakedness of a father. In you they have dishonored the woman who is unclean in her monthly period.
22:11 In you one man has done an abhorrent thing with his neighbor's wife. Another has defiled his daughter-in-law in depravity. Another has dishonored his sister, his father's daughter.
22:12 Bribes are what they take within you, to shed blood. You have taken interest and increase, and you have gained from your neighbors by oppression. You have forgotten me, the Lord GOD declares.
22:13 See how I clap my hands over the unjust gain you have made and over the blood that has been shed inside you.
22:14 Can your courage stand, or your hands stay strong, in the days when I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will act.
22:15 I will scatter you among the nations and spread you across the lands, and I will finish off the uncleanness in you.
22:16 You will be profaned in yourself in the sight of the nations, and you will know that I am the LORD."
## The furnace
22:17 The word of the LORD came to me:
22:18 Son of man, to me the house of Israel has turned into dross. All of them are copper, tin, iron and lead left inside a furnace. Silver dross is what they have become.
22:19 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Because all of you have become dross, I am about to gather you into the middle of Jerusalem.
22:20 People gather silver, copper, iron, lead and tin into a furnace and blow fire on it to melt it. In the same way I will gather you in my anger and my rage, and I will set you down and melt you.
22:21 I will gather you and blow on you with the fire of my fury, and you will be melted in the middle of it.
22:22 Silver is melted inside a furnace. You will be melted inside it in the same way. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have poured out my rage on you.
## No one in the gap
22:23 The word of the LORD came to me:
22:24 Son of man, say to her, "You are a land that has not been cleansed, a land with no rain in the day of indignation.
22:25 Her prophets plot together within her, like a lion that roars and rips apart its prey. They eat up lives. They take treasure and valuable things again and again. They have made her widows many.
22:26 Her priests have done violence to my law and profaned what is holy to me. They have not told the holy from the common, and they have not taught the difference between the unclean and the clean. They have closed their eyes to my Sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.
22:27 Her officials within her are like wolves ripping at prey. They shed blood and wreck lives to grab unjust gain.
22:28 Her prophets daub whitewash over the cracks for them. They see empty visions and divine lies. They say, 'The Lord GOD says this,' when the LORD has not spoken.
22:29 The people of the land have squeezed others by oppression and carried out robbery. They have mistreated the poor and the needy, and they have cheated the foreigner, denying him justice.
22:30 So I searched among them for one person: someone to build up a wall and take a stand in the gap in front of me for the land's sake, so that I would not destroy it. I found no one.
22:31 So I poured my indignation over them and ended them in the fire of my fury. Their own conduct I have set on their heads, the Lord GOD declares."
## Two sisters
23:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
23:2 Son of man, there were two women, daughters of one mother.
23:3 They prostituted themselves in Egypt. They prostituted themselves in their youth. There their breasts were squeezed, and there their virgin breasts were caressed.
23:4 The older was named Oholah, and her sister Oholibah. They became mine and bore sons and daughters. Their names, in fact: Oholah is Samaria, and Oholibah is Jerusalem.
## Oholah and the Assyrians
23:5 Oholah prostituted herself while she was mine. She lusted for her lovers, the Assyrian warriors
23:6 dressed in blue-violet, governors and deputies, all of them young men to desire, horsemen riding on horses.
23:7 She gave her prostitution to them, all the finest of the Assyrians, and she made herself unclean with the filthy idols of everyone she lusted for.
23:8 She did not give up the prostitution she had brought from Egypt, for in her youth they had lain with her. They had squeezed her virgin breasts and poured out their prostitution on her.
23:9 So I handed her over to her lovers, to the Assyrians she had lusted for.
23:10 They uncovered her nakedness. They took her sons and daughters, and they killed her with the sword. She became a name among women, and they carried out judgments on her.
## Oholibah and the Chaldeans
23:11 Her sister Oholibah saw it. Even so, her lust was more corrupt than Oholah's, and her prostitution went beyond her sister's.
23:12 She lusted for the Assyrians, governors and deputies, warriors in full dress, horsemen riding on horses, all of them young men to desire.
23:13 I saw that she too had made herself unclean. The two of them took the same road.
23:14 But she added to her prostitution. She saw men carved on the wall, images of Chaldeans drawn in red.
23:15 They wore belts at their hips and long, trailing turbans on their heads. Each one looked like a chariot officer, a picture of the sons of Babylon, whose native land is Chaldea.
23:16 As soon as her eyes saw them, she lusted for them and sent messengers to them in Chaldea.
23:17 The Babylonians came to her, to her love bed, and they made her unclean with their prostitution. When she had been made unclean by them, she pulled away from them.
23:18 She laid bare her prostitution and laid bare her nakedness, and I pulled away from her, as I had pulled away from her sister.
23:19 Yet she increased her prostitution, remembering the days of her youth, when she prostituted herself in the land of Egypt.
23:20 She lusted for her paramours, whose flesh was like the flesh of donkeys and whose flow was like the flow of horses.
23:21 You went back in longing to the lewd ways of your youth, to the time when the Egyptians squeezed your nipples because of your young breasts.
## The lovers turn on her
23:22 Therefore, Oholibah, the Lord GOD says this: Look, I am rousing your lovers against you, the ones you pulled away from. I will bring them on you from all around:
23:23 the Babylonians and all the Chaldeans, Pekod and Shoa and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them, young men to desire, governors and deputies, all of them chariot officers and summoned men, every one riding a horse.
23:24 They will come against you with weapons, chariots and wheels, and with a crowd of peoples. They will take their positions against you on every side with large shield, small shield and helmet. I will hand the judgment over to them, and they will judge you by their own judgments.
23:25 I will set my jealousy against you, and they will treat you with rage. They will slice off your nose and your ears, and the last of you will fall by the sword. They will carry off your sons and daughters, and the last of you will be eaten by fire.
23:26 They will strip off your clothes and take away your beautiful things.
23:27 I will bring your lewdness to an end, and the prostitution that began in the land of Egypt. You will not raise your eyes to them or think of Egypt again.
23:28 For the Lord GOD says this: Look, I am about to hand you over to those you hate, to those you pulled away from.
23:29 They will treat you with hate, carry off all that you labored for, and leave you stripped and bare. The nakedness of your prostitution will be uncovered, your lewdness and your prostitution.
23:30 All this will be done to you, because you chased after the nations like a prostitute and made yourself unclean with their filthy idols.
## The cup of her sister
23:31 You have walked the road your sister walked, so I will put her cup into your hand.
23:32 The Lord GOD says this: / The cup of your sister you will drink, / the cup that is deep and wide. / You will be a laughingstock and a mockery, / so much does it hold.
23:33 You will be filled with drunkenness and grief, / the cup of horror and ruin, / the cup of your sister Samaria.
23:34 You will drink it down and drain it, / gnaw on its broken pieces, / and rip at your breasts. / I have spoken it, the Lord GOD declares.
23:35 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Since you have forgotten me and flung me behind you, you will carry your own lewdness and your own prostitution.
## Judgment on both sisters
23:36 The LORD said to me: Son of man, will you pass judgment on Oholah and Oholibah? Then tell them plainly about their abhorrent things.
23:37 For they have committed adultery, and blood is on their hands. They have committed adultery with their filthy idols. And their children, whom they had borne to me, they made pass over to those idols to be eaten.
23:38 They did this to me as well: on that same day they made my sanctuary unclean, and my Sabbaths they profaned.
23:39 On the very day they slaughtered their children for their filthy idols, they came into my sanctuary to profane it. Look, this is what they did in the middle of my house.
23:40 And they went further. They summoned men from far away, sending a messenger to them, and there they were, arriving! For their sake you washed, you outlined your eyes in paint, and you put on ornaments.
23:41 You sat down on a glorious couch. A table stood arranged in front of it, and it was on that table that you laid my incense and my oil.
23:42 The sound of a carefree crowd was with her, and with the men from the mass of humanity there were brought Sabeans from the wilderness. They put bracelets on the women's wrists and beautiful crowns on their heads.
23:43 I said of the woman worn out with adultery: Now they will go on prostituting themselves with her, and she with them.
23:44 A man would come to her as one comes to a prostitute. In the same way they came to Oholah and to Oholibah, the women of lewdness.
23:45 But righteous men will give them the sentence that belongs to adulteresses and to women who shed blood, for adulteresses they are, and blood is on their hands.
23:46 For the Lord GOD says this: Summon a crowd against them, and hand them over to terror and looting.
23:47 The crowd will pelt them with stones and hack them down with swords. Their sons and daughters they will kill, and their houses they will burn with fire.
23:48 So I will end lewdness in the land, and all the women will take warning and not do what you have done in lewdness.
23:49 They will bring your lewdness back on you, and you will bear the sins of your filthy idols. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD.
## The pot on the fire
24:1 On the tenth day of the tenth month, in the ninth year, the word of the LORD came to me:
24:2 Son of man, write down this day's name, this very day. On this very day the king of Babylon has leaned his weight against Jerusalem.
24:3 Tell a parable to the rebellious house. Say to them, "The Lord GOD says this: / Put the pot on, put it on, / and pour water into it too.
24:4 Pack the meat inside it, / each good piece, the thigh and the shoulder, / and fill it up with the finest bones.
24:5 Take the best of the flock. / Stack the bones underneath. / Make it boil hard, / till its bones are cooked inside it.
24:6 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: / Woe to the city of bloodshed, / the pot with rust in it, / whose rust has not come out of it! / Take it out piece by piece, / with no lot cast over it.
24:7 The blood she spilled is still inside her. / She laid it on a bare rock. / She never poured it on the soil, / where dust could cover it.
24:8 To stir up rage and take vengeance, / I have put her blood on bare rock, / so that it will not be covered.
24:9 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: / Woe to the city of bloodshed! / I myself will make the stack of wood high.
24:10 Pile on the wood, light the fire, / cook the meat until it is done, / stir in the spices, / and let the bones scorch.
24:11 Then stand it empty on its coals, / so that it heats up and its bronze glows red. / Its uncleanness will melt inside it, / and its rust will be burned away.
24:12 It has wearied itself with toil, / yet its great rust has not come out of it. / Into the fire with its rust!
24:13 In your uncleanness is lewdness. / I tried to cleanse you, but you were not clean from your uncleanness. / You will not be clean again / until I let my rage rest on you.
24:14 I, the LORD, have spoken. It is coming, and I will act. / I will not let up, I will show no pity, and I will not change my mind. / They will judge you by your ways and by your deeds, / the Lord GOD declares."
## The death of Ezekiel's wife
24:15 The word of the LORD came to me:
24:16 Son of man, look, I am about to take the delight of your eyes from you with a sudden blow. You must not mourn, you must not weep, and you must let no tears come.
24:17 Groan, but make no sound. Do not carry out mourning for the dead. Bind your turban on your head and put your sandals on your feet. Do not cover your mustache, and do not eat the bread of men.
24:18 I had spoken to the people that morning, and that evening my wife died. In the morning I did what I had been told.
24:19 The people asked me, "Won't you tell us what these things you are doing mean for us?"
24:20 I said to them, "The word of the LORD came to me:
24:21 Say to the house of Israel, 'The Lord GOD says this: Look, I am about to profane my sanctuary, the boast of your strength, the delight of your eyes, the love of your being. The sons and daughters you left behind will fall by the sword.
24:22 And you will do as I have done. You will not cover your mustache, and you will not eat the bread of men.
24:23 Your turbans will remain on your heads, and your sandals on your feet. You will not mourn, and you will not weep. You will rot away in your guilt, groaning to each other.
24:24 So Ezekiel will be your sign. All that he did, you will do. When it happens, you will know that I am the Lord GOD.'
24:25 As for you, son of man: on the day I take away their stronghold, their joy and splendor, the delight of their eyes and the longing of their being, along with their sons and daughters,
24:26 on that day a survivor will arrive to bring the news to your ears.
24:27 On that day your mouth will open to the survivor. You will speak, and you will never be silent again. You will be a sign to them, and they will know that I am the LORD."
