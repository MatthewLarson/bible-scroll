## The watchman and the sword
33:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
33:2 Son of man, talk to your own people. Tell them: Suppose I bring a sword against a land, and the people of the land take one man from their far edge and make him their watchman.
33:3 He sees the sword coming against the land. He blows the ram's horn and warns the people.
33:4 The one who hears the blast of the horn and takes no warning, and then the sword comes and carries him off: his blood will rest on his own head.
33:5 The blast of the horn he heard, and he took no warning. His blood is on him. A person who takes warning saves his own life.
33:6 But suppose the watchman sees the sword coming and does not blow the horn, and the people are not warned. The sword comes and takes one of them. He is carried off in his own guilt, but I will demand his blood from the watchman's hand.
33:7 And you, son of man: I have appointed you as watchman over the house of Israel. Whenever a word comes from my mouth, you will hear it and warn them for me.
33:8 When I say to the wicked, "Wicked man, you will surely die," and you do not speak up to warn the wicked man away from his way, that wicked man will die for his guilt, but I will demand his blood from your hand.
33:9 But if you warn the wicked man to turn back from his way and he does not turn back, he will die for his guilt, and you will have saved your own life.
## "How can we live?"
33:10 As for you, son of man, say to the house of Israel: This is what you have been saying: "Our rebellions and our sins weigh on us, and we are wasting away in them. How can we go on living?"
33:11 Say to them: As surely as I live, the Lord GOD declares, I take no pleasure in the death of a wicked man. I take pleasure when a wicked man turns back from his way and lives. Turn back, turn back from your evil ways! Why must you die, house of Israel?
33:12 And you, son of man, tell your people: When a righteous man rebels, his righteousness cannot rescue him on that day. When a wicked man turns back from his wickedness, he does not stumble over it on that day. And a righteous man is not able to live by his righteousness on the day he sins.
33:13 When I tell the righteous, "You will surely live," and he trusts in his own righteousness and does wrong, not one of his righteous deeds will be remembered. He will die for the wrong he has done.
33:14 And when I tell the wicked, "You will surely die," but he turns back from his sin and does justice and righteousness,
33:15 and returns the pledge he took, pays back what he robbed, and follows the life-giving rules without doing wrong, then he will certainly live. He will not die.
33:16 Not one of the sins he committed will be remembered against him. He has done justice and righteousness. He will certainly live.
33:17 Yet your people say, "The way of the Lord is not fair." It is their own way that is not fair.
33:18 When the righteous turn from their righteousness and do wrong, they die for it.
33:19 And when the wicked turn from their wickedness and do justice and righteousness, they live because of it.
33:20 Yet you say, "The way of the Lord is not fair." I will judge each of you by his own ways, house of Israel.
## The survivor from Jerusalem
33:21 In the twelfth year of our exile, on the fifth day of the tenth month, a survivor from Jerusalem came to me and said, "The city has been struck!"
33:22 The evening before the survivor came, the hand of the LORD had been on me. He opened my mouth before the man came to me in the morning. So my mouth was open, and I was silent no more.
## The people in the ruins
33:23 The word of the LORD came to me:
33:24 Son of man, the people who live in these ruins on the soil of Israel say, "There was one Abraham, and he came to own the land. We are many. The land was given to us as our property."
33:25 So tell them: The Lord GOD says this: You eat meat with the blood still in it. You look up to your filthy idols. You shed blood. And you expect to take possession of the land?
33:26 You stand on your swords. You do abhorrent things. Each of you makes his neighbor's wife unclean. And you expect to take possession of the land?
33:27 Tell them this: The Lord GOD says this: As surely as I live, the ones among the ruins will fall to the sword. The one on open ground I have handed to the wild animals as food. And those in the hill forts and in the caves will die of plague.
33:28 I will turn the land into a desolate waste, and its proud strength will cease. The mountains of Israel will be so empty that no one crosses them.
33:29 Then they will know that I am the LORD, once I have turned the land into a waste and a desolation for all the abhorrent things they have done.
## A pleasant song
33:30 As for you, son of man, your people are talking together about you beside the walls and in the doorways of their houses. One says to another, a man to his brother, "Come on, and hear what word is coming out from the LORD."
33:31 They come to you the way a crowd comes. They sit in front of you as my people and hear your words, but they do not do them. Their mouths pour out passion, but their hearts go after their own gain.
33:32 To them you are like a song of passion, sung by a man with a beautiful voice who plays well. They hear your words, but they do not do them.
33:33 When it comes, and it is coming, then they will know that a prophet was in their midst.
## Woe to the shepherds
34:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
34:2 Son of man, prophesy against the shepherds of Israel. Prophesy to them, to the shepherds, and say: The Lord GOD says this: Woe to the shepherds of Israel who have been shepherding themselves! Should shepherds not shepherd the flock?
34:3 You feast on the fat and wear the wool. You slaughter the fattest animals. But you do not shepherd the flock.
34:4 You have not made the weak strong. You have not healed the sick. You have not bandaged the broken. You have not brought back the strays. You have not gone looking for the lost. You have ruled them with force and with crushing cruelty.
34:5 They were scattered for lack of a shepherd, and they turned into food for every wild animal. Yes, they were scattered.
34:6 My flock strays through all the mountains and across every tall hill. My flock was scattered over the entire earth, and nobody was searching, nobody looking.
34:7 Therefore, shepherds, hear the word of the LORD.
34:8 As surely as I live, the Lord GOD declares: my flock has become prey. My flock has become food for every wild animal, because there was no shepherd. My shepherds did not search for my flock. The shepherds shepherded themselves and did not shepherd my flock.
34:9 So, shepherds, hear the word of the LORD.
34:10 The Lord GOD says this: Here I am against the shepherds. I will demand my flock from their hand. I will stop them from shepherding the flock, and the shepherds will not shepherd themselves any more. I will pull my flock out of their mouths, and it will not be food for them.
## The LORD himself will shepherd
34:11 For the Lord GOD says this: Here I am. I will search for my flock myself, and I will look for them.
34:12 As a shepherd looks for his flock on the day he is among his scattered sheep, so I will look for my flock. I will pull them out of every place where they were scattered on a day of cloud and thick darkness.
34:13 I will bring them out from the peoples and gather them in from the countries. I will take them into their own land and shepherd them on the mountains of Israel, along the stream beds, and in every settled place in the land.
34:14 I will lead them out to good pasture. The high mountains of Israel will be their grazing ground. There they will rest in good grass and feed in rich pasture, on the mountains of Israel.
34:15 It is I who will shepherd my flock. It is I who will make them lie down, the Lord GOD declares.
34:16 The lost one I will look for. The stray one I will bring home. The broken one I will bandage. The sick one I will strengthen. But the fat one and the strong one I will destroy. I will shepherd the flock with justice.
## Judge between sheep and sheep
34:17 And you, my flock: the Lord GOD says this. Look, I am the one who will judge between sheep and sheep, between the rams and the male goats.
34:18 Is it too little for you to graze on good pasture, that you trample the rest of your pasture with your feet? Is it too little to drink clear water, that you foul the rest with your feet?
34:19 And must my flock graze on what your feet trampled, and drink what your feet fouled?
34:20 So the Lord GOD says this to them: Here I am. I will be the judge between a fat sheep and a skinny sheep.
34:21 You shove with flank and shoulder and gore every weak animal with your horns, until you have driven them away to the outside.
34:22 I will save my flock, and they will be prey no more. I will judge between one sheep and another.
## My servant David
34:23 I will set one shepherd over them, and he will shepherd them: my servant David. He will shepherd them. He will be their shepherd.
34:24 I, the LORD, will be their God, and my servant David will be leader among them. I, the LORD, have spoken.
## A covenant of peace
34:25 I will make a covenant of peace with them and put an end to wild animals in the land. In the wilderness they will live in safety, and in the woods they will sleep.
34:26 I will turn them and the area around my hill into a blessing. In its season I will send the rain down. Showers of blessing they will be.
34:27 The trees of the field will give their fruit, and the land will give its produce. They will be in their own land in safety. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I break the bars of their yoke and rescue them from the hand of those who made them work.
34:28 They will no longer be plunder for the nations, and the wild animals of the land will not eat them. They will live in safety, and no one will frighten them.
34:29 I will set up for them a planting famous for its name. They will no longer be wasted by famine in the land, and they will no longer bear the insults of the nations.
34:30 Then they will know that I, the LORD their God, am with them, and that the house of Israel is my people, the Lord GOD declares.
34:31 You are my flock, the flock of my pasture. You are human, and I am your God, the Lord GOD declares.
## Against Mount Seir
35:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
35:2 Son of man, turn your face toward Mount Seir and prophesy against it.
35:3 Say to it: The Lord GOD says this: Here I am against you, Mount Seir. I will reach out my hand against you and make you a desolate waste.
35:4 I will make your cities ruins. You will be a desolation, and you will know that I am the LORD.
35:5 You nursed an ancient hatred, and you poured the Israelites out into the grip of the sword in the hour of their disaster, in the hour of their final punishment.
35:6 Therefore, as surely as I live, the Lord GOD declares, I will appoint you for blood, and blood will chase you. Since you did not hate blood, blood will chase you.
35:7 I will make Mount Seir a desolation, a total waste. I will cut off from it anyone who passes through or comes back.
35:8 I will fill its mountains with its dead. Those killed by the sword will fall in all your ravines and valleys and on your hills.
35:9 I will make you a desolation for all time, and your cities will never be lived in again. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
## "They will be mine"
35:10 You said, "Both of these nations and both lands will belong to me. We will seize them." But the LORD was there.
35:11 Therefore, as surely as I live, the Lord GOD declares, I will treat you as your anger and your jealousy treated them, out of the hatred you had for them. I will let myself be known among them when I judge you.
35:12 Then you will know that I, the LORD, have heard all your insults that you spoke against the mountains of Israel. You said, "They are desolate; they were given to us to eat."
35:13 You boasted against me with your mouth and piled up your words against me. I heard it.
35:14 The Lord GOD says this: When the entire earth is celebrating, I will turn you into a wasteland.
35:15 You rejoiced when the inheritance of the house of Israel became desolate. I will do the same to you. You, Mount Seir, will be a desolation, and so will all of Edom, every bit of it. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## To the mountains of Israel
36:1 As for you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel. Say: Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the LORD!
36:2 The Lord GOD says this: The enemy said about you, "Aha! The ancient heights are ours now, our property."
36:3 So prophesy and say: The Lord GOD says this: Because they laid you waste and trampled you from every side, so that you became the property of the remaining nations, and you came up on the lips of gossips and in the whispering of the people,
36:4 therefore, mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD. The Lord GOD says this to the mountains and the hills, to the riverbeds and the valleys, to the desolate ruins and the abandoned cities, which have become loot and a laughingstock to the rest of the nations all around:
36:5 therefore the Lord GOD says this: In the fire of my jealousy I have surely spoken against the rest of the nations and against all of Edom. With joy in every heart and contempt in the soul, they made my land their own possession, so that its pastureland would become loot.
36:6 Therefore prophesy about the land of Israel. Say to the mountains and the hills, to the riverbeds and the valleys: The Lord GOD says this: Here I am. I have spoken in my jealousy and in my rage, because you have borne the insults of the nations.
36:7 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: I have raised my hand and sworn that the nations all around you will bear their own insults.
36:8 But you, mountains of Israel, will grow branches and carry fruit for Israel, my people, because they are close to coming home.
36:9 Look, I am on your side. I will turn my face to you, and you will be plowed and seeded.
36:10 I will put many people on you, the entire house of Israel, every one. The cities will be lived in, and the ruined places will be built up.
36:11 I will multiply people and animals on you. They will increase and be fruitful. I will settle you as you were before, and I will do you more good than at your beginnings. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
36:12 I will make people walk on you, my people Israel. They will take you as theirs, and you will be their inheritance. You will never again leave them childless.
36:13 The Lord GOD says this: Because people say to you, "You devour people, and you have left your nations childless,"
36:14 therefore you will not devour people any more, and you will not leave your nations childless again, the Lord GOD declares.
36:15 Never again will I let you hear the nations' insults. Never again will you carry the scorn of the peoples, and never again will you make your nations stumble, the Lord GOD declares.
## For the sake of my holy name
36:16 The word of the LORD came to me:
36:17 Son of man, the house of Israel was living on its own soil when it made the soil unclean by the way it lived and the things it did. To me their way of life was as unclean as a woman during her monthly period.
36:18 So I poured out my rage on them because of the blood they had poured out on the land, and because they had made it unclean with their filthy idols.
36:19 I scattered them among the nations, and they were spread out over the countries. I judged them by their conduct and their deeds.
36:20 When they came to the nations where they went, they profaned my holy name, since people said about them, "These are the LORD's people, yet they left his land."
36:21 I took pity on my holy name, which the house of Israel had profaned among the nations they came to.
36:22 So tell the house of Israel: The Lord GOD says this: I am not about to act for your sake, house of Israel. I act for my holy name, the one you profaned among the nations you entered.
36:23 I will make my great name holy, the name profaned among the nations, the one you profaned in their midst. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, the Lord GOD declares, when I show myself holy through you in front of them.
## A new heart and a new spirit
36:24 I will pick you out of the nations, round you up from every country, and bring you back to your own soil.
36:25 I will sprinkle pure water over you, and you will be clean. From all your uncleanness and from all your filthy idols I will make you clean.
36:26 I will give you a new heart and place a new spirit inside you. I will take the heart of stone out of your flesh, and I will give you a heart of flesh.
36:27 I will put my own Spirit inside you. I will cause you to walk by my rules, and you will keep my rulings and do them.
36:28 You will live in the land I gave to your ancestors. You will be my people, and I will be your God.
36:29 I will save you from all your uncleanness. I will call to the grain and make it plentiful, and I will not bring famine on you.
36:30 I will pile up the fruit of the trees and the crops of the field, so that you never again carry the nations' scorn over famine.
36:31 Then you will call to mind your evil ways and the things you did that were not good, and you will be sickened at the sight of yourselves because of your guilt and your abhorrent deeds.
36:32 Understand that I do not act for your sake, the Lord GOD declares. Feel shame and disgrace over your ways, house of Israel.
## Like the garden of Eden
36:33 The Lord GOD says this: On the day I make you clean from all your guilt, I will have the cities lived in, and the ruins will be rebuilt.
36:34 The desolate land will be farmed, though it was a desolation in the eyes of everyone who passed by.
36:35 They will say, "This land, once desolate, is now like the garden of Eden. The cities that were ruined, desolate and torn down are fortified and lived in."
36:36 Then the nations left around you will know that I, the LORD, have rebuilt what was torn down and planted what was desolate. I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.
36:37 The Lord GOD says this: Once more I will let the house of Israel ask me for this, so that I act for them: I will make them as many as a flock of people.
36:38 Like the flocks set apart for sacrifice, like the flocks of Jerusalem on her feast days, in the same way the wasted towns will be full of flocks of human beings. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## The valley of dry bones
37:1 The hand of the LORD came on me. By the Spirit of the LORD he carried me out and put me down in the middle of a valley, and the valley was full of bones.
37:2 He led me around them, all around. There were very many of them on the floor of the valley, and they were very dry.
37:3 He said to me, "Son of man, will these bones live?" I said, "Lord GOD, you are the one who knows."
37:4 He said to me: Prophesy to these bones. Tell them: Dry bones, hear the word of the LORD!
37:5 The Lord GOD says this to these bones: Look, I am about to bring breath into you, and you will live.
37:6 I will put sinews on you, make flesh come up over you, and cover you with skin. I will put breath in you, and you will live. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
37:7 I prophesied exactly as I was ordered. As I was prophesying, a sound arose, and there was a rattling. The bones drew near, each bone to its own bone.
37:8 I watched, and sinews lay on them, and flesh rose, and skin spread over them from above. Breath, though, was not in them.
37:9 He said to me: Son of man, prophesy to the breath. Speak to the breath: The Lord GOD says this: O breath, come from the four winds and blow on these who were killed, so that they can live.
37:10 I prophesied as he had ordered me, and the breath entered them. They came alive and rose up on their feet, an enormous army.
37:11 He said to me: Son of man, the whole house of Israel is these bones. Listen to what they are saying: "Our bones have dried up, our hope has vanished, we have been cut away."
37:12 So prophesy, and tell them: The Lord GOD says this: Look, I am opening your graves, and I will lift you out of your graves, my people, and lead you to the land of Israel.
37:13 You will know that I am the LORD when I have opened your graves and lifted you out of them, my people.
37:14 I will place my Spirit in you so that you live, and I will settle you on your own soil. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will act, the LORD declares.
## One stick, one nation
37:15 The word of the LORD came to me:
37:16 And you, son of man, take a single piece of wood and write on it: "Judah's, and that of the Israelites who are his companions." Take a second piece and write on it: "Joseph's, the wood of Ephraim, and that of the whole house of Israel, his companions."
37:17 Hold them against each other to form one piece of wood, and they will be one in your hand.
37:18 And when your people ask you, "Won't you explain to us what these things mean to you?"
37:19 tell them: The Lord GOD says this: Watch: I will take Joseph's wood, the piece in Ephraim's hand, together with the tribes of Israel who are his companions. I will lay Judah's wood against it and make the two a single piece of wood, and in my hand they will be one.
37:20 You will hold the pieces you wrote on in your hand, where they can see them.
37:21 Then tell them: The Lord GOD says this: Watch: I will take the Israelites out from among the nations they went to, collect them from every side, and lead them to their own soil.
37:22 I will make them a single nation in the land, on Israel's mountains. A single king will rule all of them as king. They will never be two nations again or be split into two kingdoms again.
37:23 They will not make themselves unclean any longer with their filthy idols, their detestable things or any of their rebellions. I will rescue them from all the places where they lived and sinned, and I will purify them. They will become my people, and I will become their God.
## My servant David, a covenant of peace
37:24 My servant David will be their king. One shepherd will lead all of them. They will live by my rulings and keep my rules and carry them out.
37:25 They will settle in the land I gave to my servant Jacob, the land where your ancestors lived. They will settle there, they and their children and their grandchildren, without end, and David my servant will be their leader for all time.
37:26 I will make a covenant of peace with them, a covenant for all time. I will establish them, make them many, and place my sanctuary among them forever.
37:27 My dwelling place will be with them. I will be their God, and they will be my people.
37:28 The nations will know that I am the LORD, who sets Israel apart as holy, when my sanctuary stands among them forever.
## Gog of Magog
38:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
38:2 Son of man, fix your face on Gog, of the land of Magog, chief leader of Meshech and Tubal. Prophesy against him
38:3 and say: The Lord GOD says this: Here I am against you, Gog, chief leader of Meshech and Tubal.
38:4 I will turn you around and set hooks in your jaws. I will lead you out, you and your whole army, the horses and the horsemen, all of them dressed in perfect order. It is a vast company, with big shields and round shields, and every man handles a sword.
38:5 With them are Persia, Cush and Put, every man carrying a shield and wearing a helmet.
38:6 Gomer with all its troops, Beth-togarmah from the far north with all its troops, and many peoples with you.
38:7 Take your stand and make every preparation, you and every group that has assembled with you, and be their guard.
38:8 After many days you will be called up. In the last of the years you will go into a land that has been restored from the sword, gathered from many peoples to the mountains of Israel, which had been a constant ruin. It was brought out from the peoples, and all of them are living in safety.
38:9 You will rise up like a storm wind and roll in like a cloud that covers the land, you, all your troops and the many peoples at your side.
## The plan against a peaceful land
38:10 The Lord GOD says this: On that day ideas will rise up in your heart, and you will think up an evil plot.
38:11 You will say: I will go up against a land of open villages. I will come on people at ease, who live in safety. All of them live where there is no wall, and they have neither bar nor gates.
38:12 You mean to seize spoil and carry off plunder, to turn your hand against ruins that are lived in again, against a people gathered in from the nations, who have gained herds and goods and live at the center of the earth.
38:13 Sheba, Dedan and the merchants of Tarshish, with all their strong young men, will say to you: "Is it spoil you have come to seize? Is it plunder you gathered your company to take? Do you mean to carry off silver and gold, to take herds and goods, to seize enormous spoil?"
38:14 So prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog: The Lord GOD says this: Will you not become aware of it, on that day, when my people Israel live in safety?
38:15 You will set out from your home in the far north, you and many peoples with you, every one of them on horseback, a great company, a mighty army.
38:16 You will march up against my people Israel as a cloud spreads over the land. In the last days this will happen: I will bring you against my land, so that the nations will know me when I show my holiness through you before their eyes, Gog.
38:17 The Lord GOD says this: Are you the one I mentioned long ago through my servants the prophets of Israel? They prophesied in those days, year after year, that I would bring you against them.
## Earthquake and sword
38:18 On that day, the day Gog comes against the land of Israel, the Lord GOD declares, my rage will rise up in my anger.
38:19 In my jealousy and in the fire of my fury I have spoken: on that day a great earthquake will strike the land of Israel.
38:20 They will shake before me: the fish of the sea, the birds of the sky, the animals of the field, every creeping thing that creeps on the ground, and all people on the face of the earth. The mountains will be thrown down, the steep places will fall, and every wall will drop to the ground.
38:21 I will summon a sword against him on all my mountains, the Lord GOD declares. The sword of each man will be against his brother.
38:22 I will go to judgment with him, with plague and with bloodshed. Flooding rain, hailstones, fire and sulfur I will rain down on him, on his troops, and on the many peoples who are with him.
38:23 So I will prove myself great and holy, and I will be known to many nations as they watch. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## Gog falls
39:1 As for you, son of man, prophesy against Gog. Say: The Lord GOD says this: Here I am against you, Gog, chief leader of Meshech and Tubal.
39:2 I will swing you around and push you forward, haul you up from the far north and lead you to the mountains of Israel.
39:3 I will strike the bow from your left hand and make your arrows fall out of your right.
39:4 On the mountains of Israel you will drop dead, you, all your troops and the peoples with you. To birds of prey, of every kind of wing, and to the wild animals I will hand you over as food.
39:5 You will fall on the open field, for I have spoken, the Lord GOD declares.
39:6 I will send fire on Magog and on those who live safely on the islands, and they will know that I am the LORD.
39:7 I will make my holy name known among my people Israel. I will never again let it be profaned. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD, who is holy in Israel.
39:8 It is coming! It will take place, the Lord GOD declares. That is the day I have been speaking about.
## Weapons burned, bones buried
39:9 Those who live in the cities of Israel will go out and set fires, and they will burn the weapons: shields, body shields, bows and arrows, hand clubs and spears. For seven years they will keep fires going with them.
39:10 They will not bring wood in from the fields or cut any from the forests, because the weapons will be their fuel. They will rob those who robbed them and strip those who stripped them, the Lord GOD declares.
39:11 When that day comes, I will give Gog a burial place in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers, east of the sea. It will block those who travel through, since Gog and all his horde will be buried there. People will call it the Valley of Hamon-gog.
39:12 The house of Israel will bury them for seven months, to make the land clean.
39:13 The whole people of the land will do the burying, and the day I display my glory will win them a name, the Lord GOD declares.
39:14 They will appoint men to work full time, going through the land. Along with them others will bury those who remain on the surface of the land, to make it clean. At the end of seven months they will search.
39:15 When the searchers walk through the land and one sees a human bone, he puts up a marker next to it. It stays until the gravediggers bury it in the Valley of Hamon-gog.
39:16 A city there will also be called Hamonah. So they will make the land clean.
## The feast for the birds and beasts
39:17 And you, son of man: the Lord GOD says this. Call out to every kind of bird and to all the wild animals: Gather and come! Assemble from every side at the sacrifice I am slaughtering for you, a huge sacrifice on the mountains of Israel. Eat meat and drink blood.
39:18 You will eat the flesh of warriors and drink the blood of the leaders of the earth: rams, lambs, goats, bulls, all of them fattened animals of Bashan.
39:19 You will stuff yourselves with fat and drink blood to drunkenness at the sacrifice I have slaughtered for you.
39:20 You will eat your fill from my table: horses, drivers of chariots, warriors, every sort of fighting man, the Lord GOD declares.
## Why Israel went into exile, and the end of hiding
39:21 I will put my glory among the nations. All the nations will see my judgment that I carried out and my hand that I laid on them.
39:22 From that day on, the house of Israel will know that I am the LORD their God.
39:23 The nations will know that the house of Israel went into exile for their guilt, because they acted unfaithfully against me. I hid my face from them and gave them into the hand of their opponents, and they all fell by the sword.
39:24 I treated them as their uncleanness and their rebellions deserved, and I hid my face from them.
39:25 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Now I will turn Jacob's fortunes around and have compassion on the whole house of Israel. I will be zealous for my holy name.
39:26 They will bear their disgrace and all their unfaithfulness, the unfaithfulness they showed against me, when they live on their own land in safety with no one to frighten them.
39:27 When I return them from the peoples and collect them out of the lands of their enemies, I will show my holiness through them while many nations watch.
39:28 They will know that I am the LORD their God, since I was the one who exiled them to the nations and I am the one who brings them together on their own soil. I will not leave even one of them there.
39:29 My face will not be hidden from them again, for I have poured my Spirit out on the house of Israel, the Lord GOD declares.
