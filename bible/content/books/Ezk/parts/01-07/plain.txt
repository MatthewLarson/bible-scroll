## Visions of God
1:1 In the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, I was among the exiles by the Kebar canal. The heavens opened, and I saw visions of God.
1:2 On the fifth day of the month (it was the fifth year of the exile of King Jehoiachin),
1:3 the word of the LORD came, truly came, to the priest Ezekiel son of Buzi in the land of the Chaldeans, by the Kebar canal. There the hand of the LORD was on him.
## The storm cloud and the four living creatures
1:4 I looked, and there was a storm wind coming from the north: a great cloud, with fire flashing out of itself, and a bright glow all around it. Out of its middle, out of the middle of the fire, shone something like glowing metal.
1:5 And from its middle came the likeness of four living creatures. This was how they looked: they had the likeness of a human.
1:6 Each one had four faces, and each one had four wings.
1:7 Their legs were straight. The sole of each foot was like the sole of a calf's foot, and they gleamed like polished bronze.
1:8 Under their wings, on their four sides, were human hands. The four of them had faces and wings,
1:9 and their wings touched one another. They did not turn as they moved. Each one went straight in the direction of its face.
1:10 As for the likeness of their faces: a human face, and a lion's face on the right side for all four, and an ox's face on the left side for all four, and an eagle's face for all four.
1:11 Such were their faces. Their wings were spread apart above them. Each one had two wings that touched those of the next, and two that covered its body.
1:12 Each one went straight in the direction of its face. Wherever the spirit was about to go, they went. They did not turn as they moved.
1:13 And the likeness of the living creatures: they looked like burning coals of fire, like torches. Fire kept moving about between the living creatures. The fire was bright, and lightning came out of it.
1:14 The living creatures ran out and ran back, looking like flashes of lightning.
## The wheels
1:15 I looked at the living creatures, and there! One wheel was on the ground beside the living creatures, for each of their four faces.
1:16 The wheels and their workmanship looked like the gleam of a gem from Tarshish, and all four had the same likeness. As for how they looked and how they were made, it was as if one wheel were set inside another wheel.
1:17 When they moved, they moved in any of their four directions. They did not turn as they moved.
1:18 Their rims were high, and they were awesome, and the rims of all four were full of eyes all around.
1:19 When the living creatures moved, the wheels moved beside them. When the living creatures were lifted up from the ground, the wheels were lifted up.
1:20 Wherever the spirit was about to go, they went. They went there because the spirit was to go there, and the wheels were lifted up beside them, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.
1:21 When the creatures moved, the wheels moved. When the creatures stood still, the wheels stood still. When the creatures were lifted up from the ground, the wheels were lifted up beside them, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.
## The dome and the throne
1:22 Above the heads of the living creature was the likeness of a dome, shining like awesome ice, spread out over their heads, above.
1:23 Under the dome their wings were stretched out straight, one toward the other. Each one had two wings covering its body on one side, and two covering it on the other.
1:24 And I heard the sound of their wings: like the sound of many waters when they moved, like the voice of the Almighty, a roar like the sound of an army camp. When they stood still, they let their wings drop.
1:25 A sound came from above the dome that was over their heads. When they stood still, they let their wings drop.
1:26 Above the dome that was over their heads was the likeness of a throne, looking like a sapphire-like stone. And on the likeness of the throne, high above, was a likeness that looked like a human.
1:27 I saw something like glowing metal, looking like fire, all around inside it, from what seemed to be his hips and upward. And from what seemed to be his hips and downward I saw something like fire, with a bright glow around him.
1:28 Like the look of the bow that is in the clouds on a day of rain, so was the look of the bright glow all around. That was the look of the likeness of the glory of the LORD. When I saw it, I fell on my face, and I heard a voice speaking.
## Sent to a rebellious house
2:1 He said to me, "Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak with you."
2:2 While he was speaking to me, a spirit came into me and stood me on my feet, and I heard the one who was speaking to me.
2:3 He said to me, "Son of man, I am sending you to the Israelites, to nations of rebels who have rebelled against me. They and their ancestors have defied me, right up to this very day.
2:4 The sons are hard in the face and stubborn in heart. I am sending you to them, and you are to say to them, 'The Lord GOD says this.'
2:5 As for them, whether they listen or not (for they are a rebellious house), they will know that a prophet has been among them.
2:6 And you, son of man, do not be afraid of them. Do not be afraid of their words, though thistles and thorns are with you and you live among scorpions. Do not be afraid of their words, and do not be shaken by the looks on their faces, for they are a rebellious house.
2:7 You are to speak my words to them, whether they listen or not, for they are rebellious.
## Eating the scroll
2:8 And you, son of man, hear what I am saying to you. Do not be rebellious like that rebellious house. Open your mouth and eat what I am giving you."
2:9 I looked, and there was a hand reaching out to me, and in it was a scroll of writing.
2:10 He unrolled it in front of me. It was written on the front and on the back, and what was written on it was laments, sighing and woe.
3:1 He said to me, "Son of man, eat what you find. Eat this scroll, and go, speak to the house of Israel."
3:2 I opened my mouth, and he fed me the scroll.
3:3 He said to me, "Son of man, feed your belly and fill your stomach with this scroll that I am giving you." I ate it, and in my mouth it was as sweet as honey.
## Hard faces and hard foreheads
3:4 He said to me, "Son of man, go to the house of Israel and speak to them with my words.
3:5 You are not being sent to a people of unclear speech and a heavy tongue. You are being sent to the house of Israel,
3:6 not to many peoples of unclear speech and a heavy tongue, whose words you cannot understand. Surely, if I had sent you to them, they would have listened to you.
3:7 But the house of Israel is not willing to listen to you, because they are not willing to listen to me. The whole house of Israel is hard in the forehead and stubborn in heart.
3:8 Look, I am making your face as hard as their faces, and your forehead as hard as their foreheads.
3:9 I am making your forehead like a diamond, harder than flint. Do not fear them, and do not be shaken by the looks on their faces, for they are a rebellious house."
3:10 He said to me, "Son of man, take into your heart all my words that I will speak to you, and hear them with your ears.
3:11 Then go to the exiles, to the children of your people. Speak to them, and say to them, 'The Lord GOD says this,' whether they listen or not."
## Back to the exiles
3:12 A spirit lifted me up, and behind me I heard the sound of a great rumbling: "Blessed be the glory of the LORD from its place!"
3:13 It was the sound of the wings of the living creatures touching one another, and the sound of the wheels beside them, a sound of great rumbling.
3:14 A spirit lifted me up and took me along. I went away bitter, with my spirit boiling, and the hand of the LORD was strong on me.
3:15 I came to the exiles at Tel-abib, who were living by the Kebar canal, and I sat where they were living. I sat there seven days, appalled, among them.
## The watchman
3:16 At the end of seven days, the word of the LORD came to me:
3:17 "Son of man, I have set you as a watchman for the house of Israel. You will hear a word from my mouth, and you will warn them for me.
3:18 When I say to the wicked person, 'You will surely die,' and you do not warn him, and you do not speak out to warn the wicked person from his wicked way so that he may live, that wicked person will die in his guilt, but I will demand his blood from your hand.
3:19 But if you warn the wicked person and he does not turn from his wickedness and from his wicked way, he will die in his guilt, and you will have saved your own life.
3:20 When a righteous person turns from his righteousness and does wrong, and I put a stumbling block in front of him, he will die. If you did not warn him, he will die in his sin, and the righteous deeds he did will not be remembered, but I will demand his blood from your hand.
3:21 But if you warn the righteous person not to sin and he stays away from sin, he will surely live, since he heeded the warning, and you will have saved your own life."
## Shut in and silent
3:22 The hand of the LORD was on me there, and he said to me, "Get up, go out to the plain, and there I will speak with you."
3:23 I got up and went out to the plain, and there the glory of the LORD was standing, like the glory I had seen by the Kebar canal. I fell on my face.
3:24 A spirit came into me and stood me on my feet. He spoke with me and said to me, "Go, shut yourself up inside your house.
3:25 And you, son of man, look: they will put ropes on you and tie you up with them, and you will not go out among them.
3:26 I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth, and you will be mute, and you will not be a man who reproves them, for they are a rebellious house.
3:27 But when I speak with you, I will open your mouth, and you will say to them, 'The Lord GOD says this.' Anyone who will listen may listen, and anyone who will refuse may refuse, for they are a rebellious house."
## The brick and the iron plate
4:1 "And you, son of man, take a brick and set it in front of you. Scratch a city on it: Jerusalem.
4:2 Set up a siege against it. Build a siege wall against it, pile up a ramp against it, station army camps against it, and place battering rams against it all around.
4:3 And you, take an iron baking plate and set it up as an iron wall between you and the city. Aim your face at it. It will be under siege, and you will lay siege to it. This is a sign for the house of Israel.
## Lying on one side, then the other
4:4 And you, lie down on your left side, and lay the guilt of the house of Israel on it. For the number of days you lie on it, you will bear their guilt.
4:5 I have set for you the years of their guilt as a number of days, three hundred and ninety days. So you will bear the guilt of the house of Israel.
4:6 When you have finished these, lie down a second time, on your right side, and bear the guilt of the house of Judah for forty days. A day for each year, a day for each year, I have set it for you.
4:7 Aim your face at the siege of Jerusalem, with your arm bare, and prophesy against it.
4:8 Look, I am putting ropes on you, and you will not turn from one side to the other until you have finished the days of your siege.
## Bread by weight
4:9 And you, take wheat, barley, broad beans, lentils, millet and spelt. Put them in one container and make them into bread for yourself. For the number of days that you are lying on your side, three hundred and ninety days, you will eat it.
4:10 The food you eat will be by weight, twenty shekels a day. From one time to the next you will eat it.
4:11 You will drink water by measure, a sixth of a hin. From one time to the next you will drink.
4:12 You will eat it as a barley cake, and you will bake it on human dung in plain view of them."
4:13 The LORD said, "This is how the Israelites will eat their bread, unclean, among the nations where I will drive them."
4:14 I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! Look, I myself have not been made unclean. Since I was young, right up to now, I have never eaten a carcass or a torn animal, and no foul meat has ever gone into my mouth."
4:15 He said to me, "See, I am giving you cow dung in place of human dung, and you may make your bread on that."
4:16 He said to me, "Son of man, look, I am about to break the staff of bread in Jerusalem. They will eat bread by weight and with worry, and they will drink water by measure and in horror,
4:17 so that they run short of bread and water. They will be appalled, one man and his brother, and they will rot away in their guilt."
## The sword, the razor and the hair
5:1 "And you, son of man, get yourself a sharp sword, the kind a barber uses as a razor, and pass it over your head and over your beard. Then get yourself scales for weighing, and divide the hair.
5:2 Burn a third of it in the fire in the middle of the city when the days of the siege are finished. Take another third and strike it with the sword all around the city. The last third you will scatter to the wind, and I will draw a sword out behind them.
5:3 Take a small number of them from there and tie them up in the hem of your robe.
5:4 Then take some of these again, throw them into the middle of the fire and burn them in the fire. From it a fire will go out to the whole house of Israel.
## Jerusalem in the middle of the nations
5:5 The Lord GOD says this: This is Jerusalem. I put her in the middle of the nations, and lands lie around her.
5:6 She has rebelled against my rulings with more wickedness than the nations, and against my rules more than the lands around her. They have rejected my rulings, and as for my rules, they have not walked by them.
5:7 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Your uproar is greater than that of the nations around you. You have not walked by my rules or kept my rulings, and you have not even done as the rulings of the nations around you require.
5:8 Therefore the Lord GOD says this: Look, I am against you, even I myself. I will carry out rulings in your midst before the eyes of the nations.
5:9 I will do among you what I have never done, and what I will never do again, because of all your abhorrent things.
5:10 Therefore fathers will eat their sons in your midst, and sons will eat their fathers. I will carry out judgments on you, and I will scatter to every wind all of your remnant.
5:11 Therefore, as I live, the Lord GOD declares, because you made my sanctuary unclean with all your detestable things and all your abhorrent things, I myself will shave you down. My eye will show no pity, and I myself will not spare you.
5:12 A third of you will die of plague, and by famine they will come to an end in your midst. A third will fall by the sword all around you. A third I will scatter to every wind, and I will draw out a sword behind them.
5:13 My anger will be spent, and I will let my rage rest on them, and I will feel relief. Then they will know that I, the LORD, have spoken in my jealousy, when I finish my rage on them.
5:14 I will make you a ruin and a disgrace among the nations around you, in the sight of everyone who passes by.
5:15 You will be an insult and a jeer, a lesson and a horror to the nations around you, when I bring my judgments on you in anger and rage and with fierce rebukes. I, the LORD, have spoken.
5:16 When I shoot the evil arrows of famine at them, arrows meant for ruin, which I will shoot to destroy you, I will add famine upon famine against you and break your staff of bread.
5:17 I will send famine on you, and wild animals, and they will leave you childless. Plague and bloodshed will pass through you, and I will bring a sword on you. I, the LORD, have spoken."
## To the mountains of Israel
6:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
6:2 "Son of man, set your face toward the mountains of Israel, and prophesy against them.
6:3 Say, 'Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord GOD!' The Lord GOD says this to the mountains and the hills, to the ravines and the valleys: Look, I am bringing a sword against you, and I will destroy your high places.
6:4 Your altars will be made desolate, and your incense stands will be smashed. I will make your slain fall in front of your filthy idols.
6:5 I will lay the corpses of the Israelites in front of their filthy idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars.
6:6 In all your settlements the cities will be ruins and the high places will be desolate, so that your altars lie ruined and bear the blame, your filthy idols are smashed and finished, your incense stands are cut down, and what you made is wiped out.
6:7 The slain will fall in your midst, and you will know that I am the LORD.
## Survivors will remember
6:8 But I will leave some of you alive. You will have survivors of the sword among the nations, when you are scattered through the lands.
6:9 Those of you who escape will remember me among the nations where they were taken captive. They will remember that I was broken by their heart, which prostituted itself and turned away from me, and by their eyes, which prostituted themselves after their filthy idols. They will be disgusted with themselves for the evils they did, for all their abhorrent things.
6:10 Then they will know that I am the LORD. I did not speak in vain when I said I would bring this disaster on them.
## Strike your hands and say, Alas
6:11 The Lord GOD says this: Strike your hands together, stamp your foot, and say, 'Alas!' over all the evil, abhorrent things of the house of Israel, who will fall by sword, by famine and by plague.
6:12 The one far away will die of plague, and the one near will fall by the sword. The one who is left and under siege will die of famine. So I will finish my rage on them.
6:13 You will know that I am the LORD when their slain lie among their filthy idols, around their altars, on every high hill, on all the mountaintops, under every green tree and under every leafy terebinth, in the place where they offered a soothing aroma to all their filthy idols.
6:14 I will stretch out my hand against them and make the land a desolation and a waste, from the wilderness to Diblah, in all the places where they live. Then they will know that I am the LORD."
## The end has come
7:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
7:2 And you, son of man, the Lord GOD says this to the land of Israel: / An end! The end has come / on the four corners of the land.
7:3 Now the end is on you. / I will send my anger against you / and judge you by your ways, / and I will lay on you all your abhorrent things.
7:4 My eye will not pity you, / and I will not spare you. / I will lay your ways on you, / and your abhorrent things will be in your midst. / Then you will know that I am the LORD.
7:5 The Lord GOD says this: / Disaster! A single disaster! / Look, it is coming.
7:6 An end has come. / The end has come. / It has woken against you. / Look, it is coming.
7:7 Doom has come to you, / you who live in the land. / The time has come, / the day is near: / panic, and no shout of joy on the mountains.
7:8 Very soon I will pour out my rage on you / and finish my anger against you. / I will judge you by your ways / and lay on you all your abhorrent things.
7:9 My eye will not pity, / and I will not spare. / I will lay your ways on you, / and your abhorrent things will be in your midst. / Then you will know that I am the LORD, the one who strikes.
## The day is here
7:10 Look, the day! Look, it is coming! / Doom has gone out. / The rod has blossomed, / arrogance has budded.
7:11 Violence has risen up into a rod of wickedness. / Nothing of them is left, nothing of their crowd, / nothing of their wealth, / and nothing of worth among them.
7:12 The time has come. The day has arrived. / Let the buyer not rejoice, / and the seller not mourn, / for anger is against all her crowd.
7:13 The seller will never get back what he sold, / even if they are both still alive, / for the vision about all her crowd will not be turned back. / And no one living in his guilt will find strength for his life.
7:14 They blow the trumpet and get everything ready, / but no one goes out to the battle, / for my anger is against all her crowd.
7:15 The sword is outside, / and plague and famine are inside. / Whoever is in the open country will die by the sword, / and whoever is in the city, famine and plague will devour him.
7:16 Their survivors will escape and be on the mountains / like doves of the valleys, / all of them moaning, each one for his own guilt.
7:17 All hands will drop, / and all knees will go to water.
7:18 They will put on sackcloth, / and shuddering will cover them. / Shame will be on every face, / and baldness on all their heads.
## Silver in the streets
7:19 They will throw their silver into the streets, / and their gold will become an unclean thing. / Their silver and gold will not be able to save them / on the day of the LORD's fury. / They will not satisfy their appetite / or fill their bellies, / because it became the stumbling block of their guilt.
7:20 The beauty of its ornament was turned into pride, / and with it they made their images of abhorrent things, their detestable things. / So I will make it an unclean thing to them.
7:21 I will hand it over to outsiders as plunder, / and to the wicked of the earth as loot, / and they will profane it.
7:22 I will turn my face away from them, / and they will profane my hidden place. / Violent robbers will enter it / and profane it.
## No peace, no vision
7:23 Make the chain! / For the land is full of bloody judgment, / and the city is full of violence.
7:24 I will bring the worst of the nations, / and they will take over their houses. / I will put an end to the pride of the strong, / and their holy places will be profaned.
7:25 Anguish has come. / They will look for peace, but there will be none.
7:26 Disaster will come on disaster, / and rumor will follow rumor. / They will look for a vision from a prophet, / but teaching will be lost from the priest / and counsel from the elders.
7:27 The king will mourn, / the leader will be dressed in horror, / and the hands of the people of the land will shake with terror. / I will deal with them as their ways deserve, / and by their own judgments I will judge them. / Then they will know that I am the LORD.
