## The Man with the Measuring Reed
40:1 It was the twenty-fifth year since we were taken into exile, the start of the year, the tenth day of the month, and fourteen years after the city had been struck down. On that very day the hand of the LORD came on me, and he brought me there.
40:2 In visions from God he carried me to the land of Israel. There he put me down on a very high mountain. To the south, on it, stood what looked like the buildings of a city.
40:3 He brought me there, and there was a man whose appearance was like bronze. A cord of linen and a measuring reed were in his hand, and he was standing at the gate.
40:4 The man spoke to me: "Son of man, look with your own eyes and listen with your own ears. Pay close attention to everything I am about to show you, for you were brought here so that I could show it to you. Tell the house of Israel everything you are about to see."
## The Outer Wall and the East Gate
40:5 I saw a wall on the outside of the house, going all around it. In the man's hand was the measuring reed, six cubits long, each cubit being a cubit and a handbreadth. He measured the width of the structure, one reed, and its height, one reed.
40:6 He came to a gate whose front faced east and went up its steps. He measured the threshold of the gate: one reed wide. The other threshold was also one reed wide.
40:7 Each chamber was one reed long and one reed wide, with five cubits between one chamber and the next. The threshold of the gate, beside the porch of the gate on the side toward the house, was one reed.
40:8 He measured the porch of the gate on the side toward the house: one reed.
40:9 Then he measured the porch of the gate: eight cubits, and its pillars two cubits. The porch of the gate was on the side toward the house.
40:10 The east gate had three chambers on this side and three on that. The three had one measurement, and the pillars on each side had one measurement.
40:11 He measured the width of the opening of the gate: ten cubits. The length of the gate was thirteen cubits.
40:12 In front of the chambers there was a barrier one cubit wide, and another barrier one cubit wide on the other side. The chambers themselves were six cubits on this side and six on that.
40:13 He measured the gate from the roof of one chamber to the roof of the other: twenty-five cubits across, with doorway facing doorway.
40:14 He made pillars of sixty cubits, and the courtyard went right up to the pillar at the gate, all around.
40:15 From the front of the gate, where a person enters, to the front of the inner porch of the gate was fifty cubits.
40:16 The chambers and their pillars had windows that were closed in, looking inward toward the gate all around, and the porches had the same. Windows ran all around on the inside, and each pillar had palm trees.
## The Outer Courtyard and Its Gates
40:17 He brought me into the outer courtyard. There were rooms there, and a pavement laid for the courtyard all around, with thirty rooms along the pavement.
40:18 The pavement lay alongside the gates and matched their length. This was the lower pavement.
40:19 He measured the width from the front of the lower gate to the front of the inner courtyard, on the outside: a hundred cubits, on the east and on the north.
40:20 Then there was the gate on the north side of the outer courtyard. He measured its length and its width.
40:21 It had three chambers on this side and three on that. Its pillars and its porches were made to the measure of the first gate: fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.
40:22 Its windows, its porches and its palm trees matched those of the gate that faced east. People went up to it by seven steps, with its porches in front of them.
40:23 The inner courtyard had a gate opposite the gate on the north, just as on the east. He measured from gate to gate: a hundred cubits.
40:24 He led me toward the south, and there was a gate on the south side. He measured its pillars and its porches by the same measurements.
40:25 Windows ran all around it and its porches, like the windows of the others. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.
40:26 It had seven steps up to it, and its porches were in front of them. It had palm trees too, one on each side, on its pillars.
40:27 The inner courtyard also had a gate on the south. He measured from gate to gate on the south: a hundred cubits.
## The Inner Courtyard and Its Gates
40:28 Then he brought me into the inner courtyard through the south gate, and he measured the south gate by the same measurements.
40:29 Its chambers, its pillars and its porches were made by the same measurements. Windows ran all around it and its porches. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.
40:30 Porches ran all around, twenty-five cubits long and five cubits wide.
40:31 Its porches opened toward the outer courtyard. Palm trees were on its pillars, and its stairway had eight steps.
40:32 Then he brought me into the inner courtyard on the east side, and he measured the gate by the same measurements.
40:33 Its chambers, its pillars and its porches were made by the same measurements. Windows ran all around it and its porches. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.
40:34 Its porches opened toward the outer courtyard. Palm trees were on its pillars, on this side and on that, and its stairway had eight steps.
40:35 Then he brought me to the north gate and measured it by the same measurements.
40:36 It had its chambers, its pillars and its porches, and windows all around it. It was fifty cubits long and twenty-five cubits wide.
40:37 Its porches opened toward the outer courtyard. Palm trees were on its pillars, on this side and on that, and its stairway had eight steps.
## Where the Offerings Were Prepared
40:38 A room with its own doorway stood by the pillars of the gates. There they rinsed the burnt offering.
40:39 In the porch of the gate there were two tables on one side and two on the other. On them the burnt offering, the sin offering and the guilt offering were slaughtered.
40:40 Outside the porch, at the side as one goes up to the entrance of the north gate, were two tables. On the other side, by the porch of the gate, were two more tables.
40:41 There were four tables on this side and four on that, at the sides of the gate: eight tables on which they slaughtered.
40:42 There were also four tables of cut stone for the burnt offering, each a cubit and a half long, a cubit and a half wide and one cubit high. On them they would lay the tools with which they slaughtered the burnt offering and the sacrifice.
40:43 Hooks a handbreadth long were fixed inside all around, and the flesh of the offering was laid on the tables.
## Rooms for Singers and for Priests
40:44 Outside the inner gate, in the inner courtyard, were rooms for singers. One stood at the side of the north gate, facing south. The other stood at the side of the east gate, facing north.
40:45 He said to me, "This room, the one that faces south, is for the priests who keep watch over the house.
40:46 The room that faces north is for the priests who keep watch over the altar. They are the sons of Zadok, the ones from the sons of Levi who come near to the LORD to serve him."
40:47 He measured the courtyard: a hundred cubits long and a hundred cubits wide, a square. The altar stood in front of the house.
## The Porch of the House
40:48 Then he brought me to the porch of the house and measured the pillars of the porch: five cubits on this side and five on that. The width of the gate was three cubits on this side and three on that.
40:49 The porch was twenty cubits long and eleven cubits wide. It was reached by steps, and there were columns beside the pillars, one on each side.
## The Great Hall and the Most Holy Place
41:1 Then he brought me to the great hall and measured the pillars: six cubits wide on this side and six cubits wide on that. That was the width of the tent.
41:2 The entrance was ten cubits wide, and the sides of the entrance were five cubits on this side and five on that. He measured the length of the hall: forty cubits, and its width: twenty cubits.
41:3 Then he went further in and measured the pillar of the entrance: two cubits. The entrance was six cubits, and the width of the entrance was seven cubits.
41:4 He measured its length, twenty cubits, and its width, twenty cubits, along the front of the great hall. And he said to me, "This is the most holy place."
## The Side Rooms and the Building at the Back
41:5 He measured the wall of the house: six cubits thick. The width of the side rooms all around the house was four cubits.
41:6 The side rooms stood side room above side room, thirty-three times. They went into the wall that belonged to the house, all around, to serve as supports, so that they would not be supports in the wall of the house itself.
41:7 The side rooms grew wider as they went up, because the structure around the house rose in stages, all around. So the house grew wider higher up, and people went up from the lowest level to the highest by way of the middle one.
41:8 I saw that the house had a raised base all around. The foundations of the side rooms were a full reed, six cubits to the joint.
41:9 The outer wall of the side rooms was five cubits thick, and the open space that was left lay between the side rooms that belonged to the house.
41:10 Between the rooms was a width of twenty cubits, all around the house.
41:11 The doorways of the side rooms opened onto the open space, one doorway on the north and one on the south. The open space was five cubits wide all around.
41:12 The building that stood facing the separate area on the west side was seventy cubits wide. The wall of the building was five cubits thick all around, and it was ninety cubits long.
41:13 He measured the house: a hundred cubits long. The separate area and the building with its walls were also a hundred cubits long.
41:14 The width of the front of the house and of the separate area on the east was a hundred cubits.
41:15 He measured the length of the building facing the separate area at the back of it, with its galleries on this side and on that: a hundred cubits. The inner great hall and the porches of the courtyard,
41:16 the thresholds, the closed windows and the galleries all around, all three of them, in front of the threshold, were paneled with wood all around. The floor up to the windows, and the windows themselves, were covered,
41:17 over the entrance, and to the inner house, and outside, and on every wall all around, inside and outside, by measure.
## Cherubim and Palm Trees
41:18 Cherubim and palm trees were made, with a palm tree between one cherub and the next. Each cherub had two faces.
41:19 A human face looked toward the palm tree on one side, and a young lion's face looked toward the palm tree on the other. This was done on the whole house, all around.
41:20 From the floor up to above the entrance, cherubim and palm trees were made, and so on the wall of the great hall.
41:21 The great hall had square doorposts. In front of the holy place, the look of one matched the look of the other.
41:22 There was an altar of wood, three cubits high and two cubits long. It had corners, and its length and its sides were of wood. He said to me, "This is the table that is before the LORD."
41:23 The great hall and the holy place each had two doors.
41:24 Each door had two leaves, leaves that turned on pivots: two leaves for one door and two for the other.
41:25 Cherubim and palm trees were made on the doors of the great hall, just as they were made on the walls. A wooden canopy was in front of the porch, outside.
41:26 There were closed windows and palm trees on this side and on that, along the sides of the porch, on the side rooms of the house, and on the canopies.
## The Priests' Rooms
42:1 Then he brought me out to the outer courtyard, by the way toward the north. He brought me to the rooms that were opposite the separate area and opposite the building on the north.
42:2 Along its front, at the north entrance, the length was a hundred cubits, and the width was fifty cubits.
42:3 Facing the twenty cubits that belonged to the inner courtyard, and facing the pavement that belonged to the outer courtyard, gallery faced gallery on three levels.
42:4 In front of the rooms was a walkway ten cubits wide, leading inward, and a path one cubit wide. Their entrances were on the north.
42:5 The upper rooms were narrower, because the galleries took more space from them than from the lower and middle rooms of the building.
42:6 They stood in three stories and had no columns, as the courtyards had. So the upper rooms were set back more than the lower and the middle ones, starting from the ground.
42:7 A wall on the outside ran beside the rooms, toward the outer courtyard, in front of the rooms. It was fifty cubits long.
42:8 The rooms on the outer courtyard were fifty cubits long, but the rooms facing the great hall ran a hundred cubits.
42:9 Below these rooms was the entrance on the east side, where a person comes in to them from the outer courtyard.
42:10 In the thickness of the wall of the courtyard on the east, facing the separate area and facing the building, there were rooms.
42:11 A passage lay in front of them, like the rooms on the north. They were the same in length and width, with the same exits, the same plans and the same entrances.
42:12 Like the entrances of the rooms on the south, there was an entrance at the head of the passage, the passage in front of the matching wall on the east, where a person comes in to them.
42:13 He said to me, "The north rooms and the south rooms that face the separate area are the holy rooms. There the priests who come near to the LORD will eat the most holy things. There they will lay the most holy things: the grain offering, the sin offering and the guilt offering. For the place is holy.
42:14 When the priests go in, they must not go out from the holy place into the outer courtyard. There they must lay down the clothes they serve in, because those clothes are holy. They must put on other clothes before they go near the area that belongs to the people."
## The Outer Wall Around the Whole Area
42:15 When he had finished measuring the inside of the house, he brought me out by the way of the gate that faced east, and he measured the area all around.
42:16 He measured the east side with the measuring reed: five hundred reeds, by the measuring reed, all around.
42:17 He measured the north side: five hundred reeds, by the measuring reed, all around.
42:18 He measured the south side: five hundred reeds, by the measuring reed.
42:19 He turned to the west side and measured it: five hundred reeds, by the measuring reed.
42:20 He measured it on all four sides. It had a wall around it, five hundred long and five hundred wide, to separate the holy from the ordinary.
## The Glory Comes Back
43:1 Then he led me to the gate, the gate that faced east.
43:2 And there was the glory of the God of Israel, coming from the east. The sound of it was like the sound of many waters, and the earth lit up from his glory.
43:3 What I saw was like the vision I had seen when I came to destroy the city. These visions were like the one I had seen by the Kebar canal. And I fell on my face.
43:4 The glory of the LORD came into the house by the gate that faced east.
43:5 A spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner courtyard, and there the glory of the LORD filled the house.
43:6 I heard someone speaking to me out of the house. The man was standing next to me.
43:7 He said to me, "Son of man, this is the place of my throne and the place for the soles of my feet, where I will live among the people of Israel forever. The house of Israel will never again defile my holy name, neither they nor their kings, by their prostitution and by the corpses of their kings on their high places.
43:8 They set their threshold right at my threshold and their doorpost right at my doorpost, with only a wall between me and them. They defiled my holy name by the abhorrent things they did, so I consumed them in my anger.
43:9 Now let them put their prostitution and the corpses of their kings far away from me, and I will live among them forever.
43:10 And you, son of man, describe the house to the house of Israel, so that they may be ashamed of their guilt. Let them measure its plan.
43:11 If they are ashamed of everything they have done, show them the design of the house: its arrangement, its exits and its entrances, all its forms, all its rules, all its forms and all its laws. Write it down before their eyes, so that they keep its whole design and all its rules and carry them out.
43:12 This is the law of the house: on the mountaintop, every bit of land around it is most holy. That, then, is the law of the house."
## The Altar
43:13 These are the measurements of the altar, in cubits, where a cubit is a cubit and a handbreadth. At its base is a channel a cubit deep and a cubit wide, with a rim of one span along its edge all around. This is the altar's height:
43:14 From the channel in the ground up to the lower ledge is two cubits, and the ledge is one cubit wide. From the smaller ledge up to the larger ledge is four cubits, and the width is a cubit.
43:15 The hearth is four cubits high, and four horns rise from the hearth.
43:16 The hearth is twelve cubits long by twelve cubits wide, square on its four sides.
43:17 The ledge is fourteen cubits long by fourteen cubits wide, with equal sides. Around it is a rim of half a cubit, and around that a channel of one cubit. The steps up to it face east.
## Making the Altar Ready
43:18 He said to me, "Son of man, the Lord GOD says this: These are the rules for the altar, for the day it is made, for offering burnt offerings on it and sprinkling blood on it.
43:19 To the Levite priests who are of the offspring of Zadok, those who come near to me to serve me, declares the Lord GOD, you are to give a young bull from the herd as a sin offering.
43:20 You will take some of its blood and put it on the four horns of the altar, on the four corners of the ledge, and on the rim all around. So you will cleanse it from sin and make atonement for it.
43:21 You will take the bull of the sin offering, and it will be burned at the appointed place belonging to the house, outside the sanctuary.
43:22 On the second day you will present a male goat, flawless, as a sin offering. They will cleanse the altar from sin as they cleansed it with the bull.
43:23 When you finish the cleansing, you will present a young bull from the herd, flawless, and a ram from the flock, flawless.
43:24 You will present them before the LORD. The priests will throw salt on them and offer them up as a burnt offering to the LORD.
43:25 For seven days you will prepare a goat each day as a sin offering. A young bull from the herd and a ram from the flock, both flawless, will also be prepared.
43:26 For seven days they will make atonement for the altar and make it clean. In this way they will consecrate it.
43:27 When these days are complete, then from the eighth day on, the priests will offer your burnt offerings and your peace offerings on the altar, and I will accept you, declares the Lord GOD."
