# Hostile review: Ezekiel 29-32 (Egypt oracles)

## Verdict

The part is sound and honest in its overall shape. The Plain follows the Hebrew closely, the dates and the chronology (597 BC base; 29:1 about January 587; 29:17 April 571) compute correctly, and the facts I could check (Herodotus 2.70, 2.36-37, 2.104, 2.169; Josephus Ant. 10.9.7 and Ap. 1.21 on a 13-year Tyre siege; BM 33041 in ANET; Petrie at Defenneh 1886; Rashi on 29:11, 29:18, 31:3) are real and mostly reported correctly. No reader-facing text says "from memory", and the first chapter page says which witnesses were read and which were not (P967, Ziegler, Targum, Vulgate). Drift against NIV/ESV is light (a few Hebrew-forced matches, listed in finding 14). The serious problems are in the Greek evidence. The variants and chapter page say the Greek "lacks" 32:19 when its content has only moved to Greek 32:21. A major Hebrew/Greek difference at 32:27 (the negation) is missing. The 31:3 variant mishandles the Greek. Plain "cypresses" at 31:8 clashes with the site's "firs" at Isaiah 14:8, and a note misquotes that verse. The Plain follows a Greek-style emendation at 30:18 with no variant entry. The Kitchen source is cited for a period it does not cover. There are no critical findings, 7 major and 12 minor. `VERBOSE=1 node tools/build.js --check Ezk/29-32` gives 0 errors, 0 warnings.

## Findings

### Major

1. **Major. variants.json 32:19; chapters.json ch. 32 views.C. The Greek does not "lack" 32:19.** The variant is titled "A verse the Greek lacks" and puts `plain: ""` for Vaticanus. `python tools/lxx.py Eze 32` shows Swete's 32:19 empty but 32:21 reading `τίνος κρείττων εἶ; καὶ κατάβηθι καὶ κοιμήθητι μετὰ ἀπεριτμήτων ἐν μέσῳ τραυματιῶν μαχαίρας`. That is the content of Hebrew 32:19 ("Whom do you surpass? Go down, be laid with the uncircumcised"), moved and fused with 32:20-21. Greek 32:19-21 are rearranged, not shorter. The chapter page repeats the error ("it lacks 32:19"), and the "why" invents a speculation ("a short verse ... may be a later addition"). Fix: retitle as a displacement; give the Greek wording at 32:21; correct chapter-page C; cut the addition speculation. The same displacement affects 32:20-21 (Greek 32:20 is shorter and "the giants" speak at 32:21). Mention it in the 32:20 and 32:21 notes.

2. **Major. variants.json 32:27 (and note 32:27). The big difference at 32:27 is missing.** The variant records only `me'arelim` vs Greek `ap' aionos`. The Hebrew has a negative, `velo yishkevu` ("they do not lie with the warriors ..."). The Greek has no negative: `καὶ ἐκοιμήθησαν μετὰ τῶν γιγάντων τῶν πεπτωκότων ἀπ' αἰῶνος`, "and they lay with the giants who fell of old". The Plain's whole sense (Meshech and Tubal denied honorable burial) rests on the MT negative. The Greek reverses it. Readers and commentators (Zimmerli, Block) treat this as the crux of the verse. Add a variant reading "they do not lie / they lie", say which the Plain follows and why, and rewrite the "why" and the note, which now present `me'olam` as the only issue. Also note that the Plain makes the honored warriors carry "their guilt on their bones", which confuses the sentence. State who has the guilt (the Hebrew has the fallen heroes; the Greek `anomiai ... ἐξεφόβησαν` is about them too).

3. **Major. glosses.json 31:8; notes 31:15; Plain 31:8. "cypresses" for berosh conflicts with the site.** Isaiah 14:8 on the site reads "The firs too are glad about you". Ezekiel 27:5 (25-28) and Isaiah 41:19 and 60:13 use "cypress" for the same word. The 31:15 note says "Isaiah has the cypresses and the cedars of Lebanon say that no woodcutter has come up", which is not what the site's Isaiah 14:8 says. No terms.json entry fixes berosh. Fix: pick one rendering for berosh site-wide (the lead decides; Ezk 27:5 and Isa 41:19/60:13 already say cypress, so Isa 14:8 should change), and make the 31:15 note quote Isaiah 14:8 exactly as it stands. Add a note at 31:8 on berosh and `armon` (plane tree; Gen 30:37 on the site also says "plane trees") and on the Greek `kuparissoi / pitues / elatai`. The brief asked for this note.

4. **Major. notes 30:18 "go dark"; variants.json (no entry). The Plain follows a reading the MT does not have.** The MT is `chasakh` (sin), "withhold" (literal.js: "it will withhold"). The Plain's "the day will go dark" follows the Greek `συσκοτάσει` and the standard emendation `chashakh`. The note labels it `deb` but gives no variant table with the MT, the Greek, and the follow/why. Under the project's rule that the Plain's departure from the base text is a variant, this needs a variants.json entry (the Plain is departing from the Leningrad text). Alternatively follow the MT ("the day will hold back") with the Greek in the table.

5. **Major. sources / notes 29:2, 29:15 (`kitchen-tip`). Wrong source for the claim.** `kitchen-tip` is Kitchen's *Third Intermediate Period in Egypt (1100-650 BC)*. It cannot support Hophra's reign (589-570), his fall to Amasis, or Egypt after 570. The citation lends authority it does not have. Replace with a source that covers the 26th Dynasty (Redford is already used; Spalinger's survey is already in the part; Kitchen's TIP is fine only for 663 BC and earlier, e.g. the sack of Thebes at 30:14) or remove it from 29:2 and 29:15.

6. **Major. variants.json 31:3 "Assyria, or a pine?"; chapter 31 views.C/J. The Greek evidence is mishandled.** (a) Greek 31:3 is `Ἀσσοὺρ κυπάρισσος ἐν τῷ Λιβάνῳ`. In this translation `κυπάρισσος` renders erez (the writer says so for 31:8). So the Greek reads "Assyria, a cedar" and agrees with the MT. It is not "rendering both the name and the tree", and it gives no support to te'ashur. The variants table should list the Greek as a reading beside the MT. At present it lists the MT and a modern emendation with no witness, which implies a variant with no manuscript. (b) The brief says the Syriac, Targum and Vulgate are reported to read a tree; the writer read none of them, so the entry should say "not read" or "reported by". (c) The Plain's second reading, "a pine, a cedar", confuses two trees; the emendation is "behold, a te'ashur, a cedar", where te'ashur is the tree named in Isaiah 41:19 ("pine" on the site). Keep it, but gloss te'ashur once. The chapter page's "Read as a fable of Pharaoh" reading rests on that emendation alone; mark it as a conjecture (BHS apparatus), not a textual reading.

7. **Major. chapters.json ch. 32 deep "Who lies in the Pit"; settings 32:17-32 `why`; variants 32:32. The disputed Qere is built into the exposition as settled.** Hebrew 32:32 is Ketiv `chittito` (his terror) with Qere `chittiti` (my terror); the Greek has "his". Many commentators read it as Pharaoh's terror. The chapter page and setting then say "the terror the LORD now puts in the same land" and "the LORD's terror is now in the land of the living", in the present tense, though the verb is "I had put". Neither the variant "why" ("the Qere adds a contrast") nor the setting gives any commentator for this. Fix: state the reading as one view, name the holders of the other, and use the past tense.

### Minor

8. **Minor. notes 29:21 (Rashi).** Rashi (verified on Sefaria, Judaica Press): "I have neither heard nor found the explanation of this verse." He ties "that day" to the end of the forty years of 29:13 (the time of Belshazzar) and to the rise of Persia. The note adds "Israel was in exile eight years before Egypt fell" and "Cyrus ... (Isaiah 45:13)". Neither is in Rashi as reported. Cut the added reasoning or attribute it to the writer, and give Rashi's own link (the forty years).

9. **Minor. notes 30:17.** On/Aven: "written with different vowels". The consonants also differ (Gen 41:45 `אֹן`, Ezek 30:17 `אָוֶן`). Say "spelled differently".

10. **Minor. notes 31:10.** "The Greek smooths it to the third person throughout." Greek 31:10 reads `ἐγένου μέγας ... ἔδωκας ... εἶδον ἐν τῷ ὑψωθῆναι αὐτόν`, second person then third. Correct the claim.

11. **Minor. notes 30:14, glosses 30:15.** The Greek renders Sin as `Σάιν` (Sais), not Pelusium, and Thebes (`No`) as Diospolis. The note names Diospolis but misses that the Greek identifies Sin differently. Add a clause.

12. **Minor. Plain 32:7 "like a lamp"; 32:28 "As for you, Pharaoh".** Hebrew `bekhabotkha` is "when I put you out"; "like a lamp" is added. 32:28 has `ve'attah` ("and you") with no name. Both are small additions the Plain does not mark. Remove "like a lamp" or note it. "Pharaoh" in 32:28 can stay if glossed. Also 32:3 "A crowd of many peoples will gather" adds "will gather"; 32:21 "lie still" adds "still"; 29:3 "The Nile is mine" drops the Hebrew "my Nile"; 29:4 "hook" for plural `chachim`.

13. **Minor. Plain 29:9 / note 29:3.** Hebrew 29:9 has third person "he said"; the Greek has "you said". The Plain keeps "he said" (right), but the note's account does not mention the shift. Add a clause. Also the note says Herodotus tells of Apries "no god could remove him"; Herodotus 2.169 does say so (checked), so the claim is fine, but cite 2.169 only, not "2.161-169".

14. **Minor. Drift audit (the writer logged none; I did all 97 verses against Hebrew via `literal.js` and from my reading of NIV/ESV, quoting nothing).** Sentence shape is Hebrew-led almost everywhere. Closer than I like to a familiar English line, though forced by the Hebrew: 29:5 (end), 29:12-13 and the repeated 30:23, 26 ("scatter ... among the nations and disperse them among the lands"), 29:19 (the three-verb "carry off, plunder, spoil ... wages for his army" run), 32:7 ("the moon will not give its light"), 32:24 and 32:30 ("bear their disgrace with those who go down to the Pit"). Rewording of 29:19 and 32:7 would be cheap and safer. No verse needs rebuilding.

15. **Minor. chapters.json settings 29:1-16 views, 29:17-21 views.** "Some think the oracles against the nations were also meant for the other peoples" and "Some critics hold that an editor wrote it" name no holder or source. Name one (e.g. Zimmerli for the reworking of 29:17-21; the "message to the nations" view needs a source or removal).

16. **Minor. chapters.json ch. 32 "Six dates" deep section.** The closing sentence ("one oracle dated before the fall (29:1 to 31:18) ... one just after ... one fifteen years later") counts groups of oracles as "one oracle". Rewrite as "four before the fall, two after, and one fifteen years later" (counting 29:17 apart: that is five, one, and ... recount). Also reconcile the ch. 32 summary ("two weeks later by one count") with the setting (month not given): the fifteenth is two weeks later only if the month is the twelfth.

17. **Minor. notes 32:22.** "Edom and Sidon were alive in 585 BC; Ezekiel counts them dead because his oracles against them have been spoken" and chapter views.H state this as fact. It is one explanation. Say "commonly explained as". Also the note lists "six peoples" while counting Meshech and Tubal as one.

18. **Minor. notes 31:18 and topic `circumcision`.** The brief asked for the "uncircumcised" note to cover 32:19-32; the only note is at 31:18, and none sits at the first use in 32:19. Add a pointer note at 32:19 (or move). The view that the word marks the dishonored, unburied dead (Block, Zimmerli) is given only through "many commentators". Name them. Add the other view (a plain label for non-Israelite outsiders, as in 28:10; 44:7-9). The 30:5 note's "Libya" for Kub: literal.js glosses Kub as Libya and the Greek lists `Λίβυες`; mention it as a candidate.

19. **Minor. progress.md line for 29-32.** It does not say there are no traditions cards or why (the other parts say "none qualified"). The claim is acceptable (nothing here splits the churches on their own teaching), but record the reason. Also `P967` is a known witness at 32:24-26 (reported, e.g., in Lilly's Emory thesis on Papyrus 967); the 32:25 variant should say "Papyrus 967 not read; it is reported to bear on 32:24-26", not leave it unmentioned. Also missing cross-references worth adding: 32:7-8 with Matthew 24:29 and Revelation 6:12-13 (Joel 2:31); 29:3 and 32:2 with Revelation 12:3.

## Checked and found fine

- Dates, years and months of 29:1, 29:17, 30:20, 31:1, 32:1, 32:17 against 597 BC; the "thirteen dates" count; Hophra 589-570; Persia 525; native kings 404-343.
- Nine "know that I am the LORD" verses plus 30:12.
- Jeremiah 46:13, 46:26 quoted exactly as on the site; Jeremiah 9:25-26 consistent with the site; "sea monster" consistent with Genesis 1:21 and Jeremiah 51:34 (the site uses "dragon" for the same Hebrew in Isaiah 27:1; 51:9 and Psalm 74:13, and the note says so).
- Rashi on 29:11, 29:18 (Tyre taken in the 23rd year; sea washed the spoil away), 31:3 ("Behold Assyria which I have already cast down").
- Greek claims at 29:3, 29:9 ("I made them"), 29:7, 30:5, 30:13, 30:17, 31:3, 31:10-11, 32:5-6, 32:25, 32:29-30.
- Sources: all keys defined; `porten-archives-elephantine` correctly renamed away from the Tobit key. No "from memory" in any file. Settings cards cover every oracle. No em-dashes. `--check Ezk/29-32`: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
- Not independently verified by me: that no Qumran or Masada scroll preserves these chapters (`dss.py`'s Python environment was not available here); it matches my knowledge of the Ezekiel scrolls, but the fixer should run `tools/dss.py Ezk 29` to 32 once and confirm. Not verified: Papyrus 967 at 32:25-26 (no source found); the claims should stay as "not read".
