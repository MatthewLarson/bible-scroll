## The great sea monster of the Nile
29:1 In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:
29:2 Son of man, turn your face against Pharaoh king of Egypt. Prophesy against him and against Egypt, all of it.
29:3 Speak, and say: The Lord GOD says this: I am against you, Pharaoh king of Egypt, great sea monster lying among your Nile channels. You have said, 'The Nile is mine, and I made myself.'
29:4 I will put hooks in your jaws. Every fish of your Nile channels will cling to your scales, and I will drag you up out of your channels with all of them clinging to you.
29:5 I will fling you into the wilderness, you and every fish of your channels. You will fall on open country, and no one will pick you up or gather you. I have given you as food to the wild animals of the earth and the birds of the sky.
29:6 Then all who live in Egypt will know that I am the LORD, because they have been a staff of reed for the house of Israel.
29:7 When they grabbed you in the hand, you splintered and ripped open every shoulder of theirs. When they leaned on you, you snapped and left all their hips unsteady.
29:8 So the Lord GOD says this: I am about to bring a sword against you, and I will cut off from you both people and animals.
29:9 The land of Egypt will become a desolation and a ruin. Then they will know that I am the LORD, because he said, 'The Nile is mine, and I made it.'
## Egypt empty for forty years
29:10 So I am against you and against your Nile channels. I will make the land of Egypt a wasteland, a ruin of desolation, from Migdol to Syene, as far as the border of Cush.
29:11 No human foot will pass through it, and no animal's foot will pass through it. No one will live there for forty years.
29:12 I will make the land of Egypt a desolation in the middle of desolate lands, and its cities will be a desolation for forty years, in the middle of cities laid waste. I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them among the lands.
29:13 For the Lord GOD says this: At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples where they were scattered.
29:14 I will restore the fortunes of Egypt and bring them back to the land of Pathros, the land they came from. A lowly kingdom is what they will be there.
29:15 It will be the lowliest of the kingdoms, and it will never again lift itself above the nations. I will make them so small that they will never again rule over the nations.
29:16 It will no longer be something the house of Israel trusts, a reminder of their guilt, when they turn to look to Egypt. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.
## Egypt as Nebuchadnezzar's wages
29:17 In the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:
29:18 Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon made his army do heavy labor against Tyre. Every head was worn bald, and every shoulder was scraped bare. But there were no wages from Tyre for him or for his army, in return for the labor he did against it.
29:19 So the Lord GOD says this: I am about to give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. He will carry off its riches, loot its goods and strip it of its spoil, and that will be the pay for his army.
29:20 As payment for the work he did, I have given him the land of Egypt, because they acted for me, the Lord GOD declares.
29:21 On that day I will make a horn grow for the house of Israel, and I will give you an open mouth among them. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## The day of the LORD for Egypt
30:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
30:2 Son of man, prophesy and say: The Lord GOD says this: / Wail, 'Alas for the day!'
30:3 For a day is near, / yes, the day of the LORD is near. / It will be a day of cloud, / a time of the nations.
30:4 A sword will come against Egypt, and anguish will fall on Cush, when the slain fall in Egypt. They will take away its wealth, and its foundations will be torn down.
30:5 Cush and Put and Lud, all the mixed peoples, Kub, and the people of the covenant land will fall by the sword together with Egypt.
30:6 The LORD says this: Those who prop up Egypt will fall, and the pride of its strength will be brought low. From Migdol to Syene they will fall in it by the sword, the Lord GOD declares.
30:7 They will be desolate in the middle of desolate lands, and its cities will lie in the middle of cities laid waste.
30:8 Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I set fire to Egypt and all its helpers are broken.
30:9 On that day messengers will go out from me in ships to frighten carefree Cush. Anguish will come on them on the day of Egypt, for look, it is coming.
## Nebuchadnezzar and the cities of Egypt
30:10 The Lord GOD says this: I will put an end to the multitude of Egypt by the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.
30:11 He and his people with him, the most ruthless of the nations, will be brought in to destroy the land. They will draw their swords against Egypt and fill the land with the slain.
30:12 I will turn the Nile channels into dry ground and sell the land into the hand of evil men. By the hand of foreigners I will lay waste the land and everything in it. I, the LORD, have spoken.
30:13 The Lord GOD says this: I will destroy the filthy idols and put an end to the worthless idols in Memphis. There will no longer be a leader from the land of Egypt, and I will put fear in the land of Egypt.
30:14 I will lay waste Pathros, set fire to Zoan and carry out judgments on Thebes.
30:15 I will pour out my rage on Sin, the stronghold of Egypt, and cut off the multitude of Thebes.
30:16 I will set fire to Egypt. Sin will writhe in great pain, Thebes will be broken into, and Memphis will be in distress in broad daylight.
30:17 The young men of On and Pi-beseth will fall by the sword, and those cities will go into captivity.
30:18 In Tahpanhes the day will go dark when I break the yoke bars of Egypt there. The pride of its strength will come to an end in it. As for the city, a cloud will cover it, and its daughters will go into captivity.
30:19 I will carry out judgments on Egypt. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## Pharaoh's broken arm
30:20 In the eleventh year, in the first month, on the seventh day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:
30:21 Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt. Look, it has not been bound up. No healing has been given, no bandage put on to wrap it, so that it could grow strong enough to swing a sword.
30:22 So the Lord GOD says this: I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt. I will break his arms, the strong one as well as the one already broken, and I will make the sword fall from his hand.
30:23 I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them among the lands.
30:24 I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and put my sword in his hand. But I will break the arms of Pharaoh, and he will groan before him with the groans of a man fatally wounded.
30:25 I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, but the arms of Pharaoh will fall. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I put my sword in the hand of the king of Babylon and he stretches it out against the land of Egypt.
30:26 I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them among the lands. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
## A cedar in Lebanon
31:1 In the eleventh year, in the third month, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:
31:2 Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his multitude: Whom are you like in your greatness?
31:3 Look, Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon, / beautiful in its branches, a forest giving shade, / and very tall. / Its top was among the dense boughs.
31:4 Waters made it grow. / The deep lifted it high. / Its rivers ran around the place where it was planted, / and it sent its channels / out to all the trees of the field.
31:5 So it stood taller / than all the trees of the field. / Its boughs grew many, / and its branches grew long, / from the many waters as it sent them out.
31:6 In its boughs / all the birds of the sky made nests. / Under its branches / all the wild animals gave birth. / In its shade / all the many nations lived.
31:7 It was beautiful in its greatness, / in the length of its branches, / for its root went down to many waters.
31:8 The cedars in the garden of God could not cast it into shadow. / The cypresses were not like its boughs. / The plane trees were not like its branches. / No tree in the garden of God / was like it in beauty.
31:9 I made it lovely, / with branches in great number, / and all the trees of Eden in the garden of God / were jealous of it.
## The tree cut down
31:10 So the Lord GOD says this: Because you were tall in height, and it set its top among the dense boughs, and its heart grew proud of its height,
31:11 I will give it into the hand of a mighty ruler of nations, and he will surely deal with it as its wickedness deserves. I have driven it out.
31:12 Foreigners, the most ruthless of the nations, cut it down and left it lying. Its branches dropped onto the mountains and into every valley, and its boughs lay snapped in all the ravines of the land. All the peoples of the earth left its shade and walked away from it.
31:13 On its fallen trunk all the birds of the sky settled, and all the wild animals came to its branches.
31:14 This was so that no trees by the water would ever grow tall again, or set their tops among the dense boughs, and so that none of the trees that drink water would stand in their height as mighty ones. For all of them are given over to death, to the earth below, among mortals, with those who go down to the Pit.
31:15 The Lord GOD says this: On the day it went down to Sheol, I caused mourning. I covered the deep over it and held back its rivers, and the many waters were stopped. I made Lebanon go dark with grief over it, and all the trees of the field withered because of it.
31:16 I made the nations shake at the sound of its fall, when I brought it down to Sheol with those who go down to the Pit. And all the trees of Eden, the choicest and best of Lebanon, all that drink water, were comforted in the earth below.
31:17 They too went down with it to Sheol, to those killed by the sword, the ones who were its arm, who lived in its shade among the nations.
31:18 Whom are you like in this way, in splendor and greatness, among the trees of Eden? Yet you will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the earth below. You will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed by the sword. This is Pharaoh and all his multitude, the Lord GOD declares.
## A lament for Pharaoh
32:1 In the twelfth year, in the twelfth month, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:
32:2 Son of man, raise a lament over Pharaoh king of Egypt and say to him: / You were like a young lion among the nations, / yet you are like the sea monster in the seas. / You surge out into your rivers, / cloud the water with your feet / and stir their streams to mud.
32:3 The Lord GOD says this: / I will spread my net over you / with a crowd of many peoples, / and they will pull you up in my net.
32:4 I will leave you on dry land / and hurl you out on the open ground. / I will let every bird of the sky settle on you, / and I will fill the wild animals of the whole earth with you.
32:5 I will lay your flesh on the mountains / and fill the valleys with your remains.
32:6 I will make the land drink your flowing blood / all the way to the mountains, / and the ravines will be full of you.
32:7 When I put you out, I will veil the heavens / and darken their stars. / I will cover the sun with a cloud, / and the moon will give no light.
32:8 Every glowing light in the sky / I will dim above you, / and I will lay darkness on your land, / the Lord GOD declares.
32:9 I will trouble the hearts of many peoples when I bring your ruin among the nations, into lands you have never known.
32:10 I will make many peoples stand appalled at you. Their kings will shudder with horror over you when I brandish my sword before their faces. Every one of them will tremble, moment by moment, for his own life on the day of your fall.
32:11 For the Lord GOD says this: The sword of the king of Babylon will come against you.
32:12 By the swords of warriors I will make your multitude fall, every one of them among the most ruthless of the nations. They will shatter the pride of Egypt, and all its multitude will be destroyed.
32:13 I will destroy all its livestock from beside the many waters. No human foot will muddy them again, and no animal's hooves will muddy them.
32:14 Then I will let their waters settle and make their rivers flow like oil, the Lord GOD declares.
32:15 When I make the land of Egypt a desolation, and the land is stripped of all that fills it, when I strike down everyone who lives in it, then they will know that I am the LORD.
32:16 This is a lament, and people will chant it. The daughters of the nations will chant it. Over Egypt and over all its multitude they will chant it, the Lord GOD declares.
## Down to the Pit
32:17 In the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:
32:18 Son of man, wail over the multitude of Egypt and send it down, Egypt and the daughters of mighty nations, to the earth below, to those who go down to the Pit.
32:19 Are you lovelier than anyone? Go down! Be laid with the uncircumcised!
32:20 They will fall among those killed by the sword. A sword has been handed over; they have dragged her off along with all her multitudes.
32:21 From the middle of Sheol the mightiest of the warriors will speak to him and to his helpers: 'They have come down. They lie, the uncircumcised, killed by the sword.'
32:22 Assyria is there with all its company, its graves all around it, all of them slain, fallen by the sword.
32:23 Its graves were set in the far corners of the Pit, and its company is all around its grave. All of them are slain, fallen by the sword, who had spread terror in the land of the living.
32:24 Elam is there with all its multitude around its grave, all of them slain, fallen by the sword. They went down uncircumcised into the earth below. They had spread terror in the land of the living, and now they bear their disgrace with those who go down to the Pit.
32:25 Among the slain they have made a bed for Elam with all her multitude. Their graves are all around it. All of them are uncircumcised, killed by the sword, for their terror was spread in the land of the living. They bear their disgrace with those who go down to the Pit. She was put among the slain.
32:26 Meshech and Tubal are there with all their multitude, their graves all around them. All of them are uncircumcised, pierced by the sword, because they spread terror in the land of the living.
32:27 They do not lie with the warriors who fell from among the uncircumcised, who went down to Sheol with their weapons of war, with their swords laid under their heads and their guilt on their bones. For the terror of those warriors was in the land of the living.
32:28 As for you, you will be broken among the uncircumcised and lie with those killed by the sword.
32:29 Edom is there, its kings and all its leaders, who for all their strength were laid with those killed by the sword. They lie with the uncircumcised and with those who go down to the Pit.
32:30 The leaders of the north are there, all of them, and all the Sidonians, who went down with the slain, ashamed of the terror they spread by their strength. They lie uncircumcised with those killed by the sword, and they bear their disgrace with those who go down to the Pit.
32:31 Pharaoh will see them, and he will be comforted over all his multitude, those killed by the sword, Pharaoh and all his army, the Lord GOD declares.
32:32 For I had put my terror in the land of the living, and Pharaoh and all his multitude will be laid among the uncircumcised, with those killed by the sword, the Lord GOD declares.
