## The foundling who became a queen
16:1 The LORD's word came to me:
16:2 "Son of man, make Jerusalem know her abhorrent things.
16:3 Say, 'The Lord GOD says this to Jerusalem: Your roots and your birth come from the land of the Canaanites. Your father was Amorite, your mother Hittite.
16:4 As for your birth, on the day you were born your cord was left uncut. You were not washed in water to make you clean. You were not rubbed with salt at all, and you were not wrapped in cloths at all.
16:5 No eye felt pity for you. No one did even one of these things for you out of compassion. You were dumped on the ground in a field, because your life was loathed on the day you were born.
16:6 Then I went past you and saw you kicking about in your blood. I said to you, "In your blood, live!" Yes, I said to you, "In your blood, live!"
16:7 I made you grow by the ten thousand, like the plants of the field. You grew and shot up and reached the age for ornaments. Your breasts were firm, and your hair had sprouted. But you were naked and bare.
16:8 Then I went past you and saw you, and look: your time was a time for love. I spread the wing of my cloak over you and covered your nakedness. I gave you my oath and came into a covenant with you, the Lord GOD declares. You were mine.
16:9 I washed you with water. I rinsed your blood off you, and I rubbed you with oil.
16:10 I dressed you in embroidered cloth and put sandals of fine leather on your feet. I wrapped your head in linen and draped you in silk.
16:11 I decorated you with jewelry. I put bracelets on your wrists and a chain on your neck.
16:12 I set a ring through your nose, hung earrings from your ears, and placed a beautiful crown on your head.
16:13 So you were decorated with gold and silver. You wore embroidered cloth, silk and linen. Your food was the best flour, honey and oil. You became more and more beautiful, and you rose to royalty.
16:14 Your fame went out among the nations because of your beauty. It was perfect, through the splendor I had put on you, the Lord GOD declares.
## The faithless bride
16:15 But you put your trust in your beauty, and because of your fame you acted as a prostitute. You poured out your prostitution on everyone who passed by. It was his to have.
16:16 You took some of your clothes and made colorful high places for yourself, and on them you acted as a prostitute. It was not to come, and it was never to be.
16:17 You took your beautiful things, made from my gold and my silver, which I had given you, and made male images for yourself. With them you acted as a prostitute.
16:18 You took your embroidered clothes and covered the images. You put my oil and my incense down in front of them.
16:19 My food, which I gave you, the fine flour, oil and honey that I fed you, you set in front of them as a soothing smell. So it was, the Lord GOD declares.
16:20 Your sons and your daughters, the ones you bore to me, you took and sacrificed to the images as food. Was your prostitution too small a thing?
16:21 You slaughtered my children and gave them away, making them pass over to the images.
16:22 Through all your abhorrent things and your prostitution, you never remembered your young days, when you were naked and bare and lay kicking in your blood.
16:23 And it happened after all your wickedness. Woe! Woe to you, the Lord GOD declares!
16:24 You built yourself a mound and made yourself a high place in every public square.
16:25 At the head of every road you built your high place. You made your beauty abhorrent. You parted your legs for anyone going by, and you made your prostitution greater.
16:26 You acted as a prostitute with the Egyptians, your neighbors, men of great flesh. You made your prostitution greater to make me angry.
16:27 So look, I stretched out my hand against you. I cut back your allotment, and I handed you over to the greed of those who hate you, the daughters of Philistia, who blushed at your lewd conduct.
16:28 You acted as a prostitute with the Assyrians too, because you could not get enough. You acted as a prostitute with them, and even so you were not satisfied.
16:29 You made your prostitution greater, all the way to the land of traders, Chaldea. Even with that you were not satisfied.
16:30 How feeble is your heart, the Lord GOD declares, to do all these things, the work of a headstrong prostitute.
16:31 You built your mound at the head of every road and made your high place in every public square. But you were not like a prostitute, since you looked down on the fee.
16:32 You adulterous wife! You take strangers in place of your husband.
16:33 Men give a gift to every prostitute, but you gave your gifts to all your lovers. You bribed them to come to you from all around for your prostitution.
16:34 So in your prostitution you were the reverse of other women. No one came after you to hire you. You paid the fee, and no fee was paid to you. You were the reverse.
## The lovers will judge her
16:35 So, prostitute, hear the LORD's word.
16:36 The Lord GOD says this: Because your lust spilled out, and your nakedness was uncovered in your prostitution with your lovers, and because of all your filthy idols, your abhorrent things, and the blood of your children, which you gave to them,
16:37 therefore, look, I am about to gather all your lovers, those you pleased and all those you loved, along with all those you hated. I will gather them against you from all around. I will uncover your nakedness to them, and they will see all your nakedness.
16:38 I will pass on you the sentences given to women who commit adultery and shed blood. I will give you the blood of rage and jealousy.
16:39 I will hand you over to them. They will tear down your mound and break down your high places. They will strip off your clothes and take your beautiful things. They will leave you naked and bare.
16:40 A mob will be called up against you. They will stone you with stones and slash you apart with their swords.
16:41 Your houses will go up in fire, and many women will watch as sentences are carried out against you. I will make you quit prostitution, and you will never again pay a fee.
16:42 Then my rage against you will settle, my jealousy will go away from you, and I will be quiet. I will not be angry again.
16:43 You never remembered your young days, and you raged against me through all of this. So look, I myself will bring your conduct down on your head, the Lord GOD declares. Did you not commit this lewdness on top of all your abhorrent things?
## Sisters: Samaria and Sodom
16:44 Look, everyone who quotes proverbs will quote this one about you: Like mother, like daughter.
16:45 Your mother's daughter you are, she who turned away in disgust from her husband and children. Sister of your sisters you are, who turned from their husbands and children in disgust. A Hittite was your mother, an Amorite your father.
16:46 Samaria is your older sister, she and her daughters, who live on your left hand. Sodom is your younger sister, with her daughters, who live on your right hand.
16:47 Did you not walk in their ways and do their abhorrent things? As if that were too small a thing, you behaved more corruptly than they did in all your ways.
16:48 As I live, the Lord GOD declares, your sister Sodom never did, she or her daughters, what you and your daughters have done.
16:49 Here is the guilt of Sodom, your sister: pride, a full belly of food, and easy, quiet comfort belonged to her and her daughters. But she did not strengthen the hand of the poor and needy.
16:50 They grew haughty and did something abhorrent before my eyes. So I took them away when I saw it.
16:51 Samaria sinned less than half as much as you. You made your abhorrent things more numerous than hers, and by all the abhorrent things you did you made your sisters look righteous.
16:52 You also must bear your disgrace, since you have made your sisters' case look better by your sins, which were more abhorrent than theirs. They are more righteous than you. So be humiliated as well; carry your disgrace, since you have made your sisters seem in the right.
16:53 I will turn their fortunes around: the fortunes of Sodom and her daughters, the fortunes of Samaria and her daughters, and your fortunes among them,
16:54 so that you bear your disgrace and are ashamed of all you have done, when you comfort them.
16:55 Your sisters, Sodom and her daughters, will be restored to what they were before. Samaria and her daughters will be restored to what they were before. And you and your daughters will be restored to what you were before.
16:56 Did you not use the name of your sister Sodom as a saying on your lips, in the days of your pride,
16:57 before your wickedness was uncovered? Now you are a reproach, like her, to the daughters of Aram and all around her, the daughters of the Philistines who despise you from every side.
16:58 You yourself must bear your lewdness and your abhorrent things, the LORD declares.
## The everlasting covenant
16:59 For the Lord GOD says this: I will treat you as you have acted, you who despised an oath and broke a covenant.
16:60 Yet I myself will keep in mind the covenant I made with you when you were young, and I will set up an everlasting covenant for you.
16:61 You will call your ways to mind and feel shame, when you take in your sisters, the older with the younger. I will give them to you as daughters, though not through your covenant.
16:62 I myself will make my covenant stand with you. Then you will know that I am the LORD,
16:63 so that you remember and are ashamed, and have no more reason to open your mouth because of your disgrace, when I atone for you for all you did, the Lord GOD declares.'"
## The riddle of the two eagles
17:1 The LORD's word came to me:
17:2 "Son of man, tell a riddle and speak a parable to the house of Israel.
17:3 Say, 'The Lord GOD says this: The great eagle came to Lebanon. It was wide in wing and long in feather, full of plumage in a patchwork of colors. It took the very top of the cedar.
17:4 It nipped off the top of its young shoots, flew with it to a land of traders, and put it down in a city of merchants.
17:5 Then it took a seedling of the land's own stock and set it in a field made for sowing. It placed it beside many waters, the way one plants a willow.
17:6 It sprouted and became a vine, spreading out but low. Its branches turned toward the eagle, and its roots were under him. So it became a vine. It put out shoots and sent out boughs.
17:7 Then there was another great eagle, with great wings and thick plumage. And look: this vine bent its roots toward him. From the beds where it was planted, it sent its branches out to him to be watered.
17:8 It was planted in a good field by many waters. It was meant to put out branches and bear fruit and become a splendid vine.
17:9 Say, "The Lord GOD says this: Will it prosper? Will he not tear out its roots and strip off its fruit, so that it shrivels, every fresh leaf it sprouted shriveling? No strong arm and no great crowd is needed to lift it away from its roots.
17:10 Look: it is planted. But will it prosper? Will it not wither, wither completely, the moment the east wind touches it? It will wither in the beds where it grew."'"
## The riddle explained
17:11 The LORD's word came to me:
17:12 "Say to the rebellious house: Have you no idea what this means? Say: Look, the king of Babylon marched to Jerusalem. He seized its king and its officials and led them back with him to Babylon.
17:13 He took one of the royal family and made a covenant with him. He made him swear an oath. He also took the powerful men of the land,
17:14 so that the kingdom would stay low and not lift itself up, and would keep his covenant and so continue.
17:15 But he revolted against him and sent envoys to Egypt, asking for horses and a big army. Will he prosper? Will a man who does such things get away? Break a covenant, and still get away?
17:16 As I live, the Lord GOD declares, he will die in Babylon, in the land of the king who made him king, whose oath he despised and whose covenant he broke.
17:17 Not even Pharaoh, with a strong army and a huge company, will do anything for him in the war, once a ramp is piled up and a siege wall is built to cut off many lives.
17:18 He despised the oath and broke the covenant. He had given his hand to seal it, and still he did all these things. He will not escape.
17:19 So the Lord GOD says this: As I live, the oath he despised is my oath, and the covenant he broke is my covenant. I will pay it back on his own head.
17:20 I will throw my net over him, and my snare will catch him. I will haul him to Babylon and put him on trial there for his betrayal of me.
17:21 All his fugitives among all his troops will fall by the sword, and the survivors will be flung toward every wind. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken.'
## The shoot on the high mountain
17:22 The Lord GOD says this: I will take, I myself, a shoot from the high crown of the cedar and set it down. From the tips of its young branches I will snap off a soft one, and it is I who will plant it on a mountain, high and towering.
17:23 On Israel's high mountain I will plant it. It will put out branches and bear fruit and become a majestic cedar. Birds of every wing will live under it. They will live in the shade of its branches.
17:24 Then all the trees of the field will know that I am the LORD. I bring the tall tree low and raise the low tree up. I dry up the moist tree and make the dry tree blossom. I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will act."
## The sour grapes proverb
18:1 The LORD's word came to me:
18:2 "What is it with you, that you keep reciting this proverb over the land of Israel: 'The fathers ate sour grapes, and the children's teeth go numb'?
18:3 As I live, the Lord GOD declares, you will not use this proverb in Israel anymore.
18:4 Look, all lives belong to me. The father's life and the son's life are both mine. The person who sins, that very person will die.
## Three generations
18:5 Suppose a man is righteous and practices justice and righteousness.
18:6 He has not eaten on the mountains or lifted his eyes to the filthy idols of the house of Israel. He has not defiled his neighbor's wife or gone near a woman during her monthly uncleanness.
18:7 He oppresses no one. He returns the pledge a debtor gave him. He commits no robbery. He gives his bread to the hungry and puts clothes on the naked.
18:8 He makes no loan at interest and takes no profit. He pulls his hand back from injustice. His rulings between one man and another are true.
18:9 He has lived by my rules and guarded my rulings, so as to act faithfully. That man is righteous. He will certainly live, the Lord GOD declares.
18:10 Suppose instead he fathers a violent son, a shedder of blood, who does any one of these things,
18:11 though he himself did none of these things. That son has eaten on the mountains and defiled his neighbor's wife.
18:12 He has oppressed the poor and needy. He has committed robbery. He has not returned a pledge. He has lifted his eyes to the filthy idols and done an abhorrent thing.
18:13 He has made loans at interest and taken a profit. Can he live? He cannot live. He did all these abhorrent things, so he will certainly be put to death. His blood will be on him.
18:14 But suppose this man fathers a son who sees all the sins his father has done. He sees them and does not do the same.
18:15 He has not eaten on the mountains or lifted his eyes to the filthy idols of the house of Israel. He has not defiled his neighbor's wife.
18:16 He has oppressed no one. He has taken no pledge and committed no robbery. He gives his bread to the hungry and puts clothes on the naked.
18:17 He keeps his hand from harming the poor. He takes no interest or profit. He keeps my rulings and walks by my rules. He will certainly live; he will not die for his father's guilt.
18:18 As for his father, because he practiced extortion and robbed his brother and did what was not good among his people, look, he will die for his own guilt.
## Turn back and live
18:19 Yet you ask, 'Why does the son not bear the guilt of the father?' The son has practiced justice and righteousness. He has kept all my rules and done them. He will certainly live.
18:20 The person who sins, that very person will die. A son will not bear the guilt of his father, and a father will not bear the guilt of his son. The righteousness of the righteous person rests on him, and the wickedness of the wicked person rests on him.
18:21 As for the wicked person, he may turn away from all the sins he has done. If he keeps all my rules and does justice and righteousness, he will certainly live. He will not die.
18:22 No one will recall against him any of the rebellious acts he did. Because of the righteousness he has done, he will live.
18:23 Is it the death of the wicked that I really take pleasure in, the Lord GOD declares? Do I not take pleasure when he turns away from his ways and lives?
18:24 But when a righteous person turns away from his righteousness and does injustice, doing the same abhorrent things the wicked person does, will he live? None of the righteous deeds he did will be remembered. He will die for the unfaithfulness he showed and the sin he committed.
18:25 Yet you say, 'The Lord's way is not fair.' Listen, please, house of Israel. Is it my way that is unfair? Is it not your ways that are unfair?
18:26 When a righteous person turns away from his righteousness, does injustice and dies, he dies because of the injustice he has done.
18:27 And when a wicked person turns away from the wickedness he has done, and does justice and righteousness, he will keep his own life alive.
18:28 He took notice and turned away from every rebellious act he had done. He will certainly live. He will not die.
18:29 Yet the house of Israel says, 'The Lord's way is not fair.' House of Israel, are my ways the ones that are unfair? Is it not your ways that are unfair?
18:30 So I will judge you, house of Israel, each one by his ways, the Lord GOD declares. Turn back! Turn away from all your rebellious acts, so that guilt does not become a stumbling block for you.
18:31 Throw off all the rebellious acts you have committed. Make yourselves a new heart and a new spirit. Why should you die, house of Israel?
18:32 For I take no pleasure in the death of anyone who dies, the Lord GOD declares. So turn back and live!"
## A lament for the leaders of Israel
19:1 "As for you, sing a lament for the leaders of Israel.
19:2 Say, 'What was your mother? A lioness! / Among lions she lay down; / among young lions she raised her cubs.
19:3 One of her cubs she raised, / and he grew into a young lion. / He learned how to rip apart prey; / he ate human beings.
19:4 The nations were told about him. / In their pit he was caught, / and with hooks they led him / to the land of Egypt.
19:5 She saw that her waiting had come to nothing, / that her hope was destroyed. / So she took another of her cubs / and made him a young lion.
19:6 He walked among lions / and became a young lion himself. / He learned how to rip apart prey; / he ate human beings.
19:7 He knew his widows / and laid waste their cities. / The land and everything in it was stunned / by the sound of his roar.
19:8 Nations lined up against him / from the provinces all around. / They threw their net over him, / and he was taken in their pit.
19:9 They fastened him in a cage with hooks / and took him to the king of Babylon. / They shut him in fortresses, / so that his voice would never reach the mountains of Israel again.
19:10 Your mother was like a vine in your blood, / planted beside water, / bearing fruit and heavy with branches / thanks to the many waters.
19:11 She grew strong branches, / made to be rulers' scepters. / Her stature rose above the leafy boughs, / and she stood out for her height / and her mass of branches.
19:12 But in a rage she was ripped out and flung to the ground, / and the east wind dried up her fruit. / They were torn off and dried up, her strong branch; / fire ate it.
19:13 Now she is transplanted into the desert, / into a land of drought and thirst.
19:14 Fire came out of a branch of her own twigs / and ate up her fruit. / No strong branch is left on her, / no scepter for ruling.' / It is a lament, and it has become a lament."
