## The arrest in the garden
18:1 After he had said these things, Jesus went out with his disciples to the other side of the Kidron ravine, where there was a garden. He entered it, and his disciples with him.
18:2 Judas, who was handing him over, knew the spot too, since Jesus and his disciples had gathered there many times.
18:3 So Judas took the cohort, along with guards sent by the chief priests and the Pharisees, and came there carrying lanterns, torches and weapons.
18:4 Jesus knew everything that was coming on him. So he stepped out and said to them, "Who are you looking for?"
18:5 They answered him, "Jesus the Nazarene." He said to them, "I am." Judas, who was handing him over, was standing there with them too.
18:6 When he said to them, "I am," they stepped backward and fell to the ground.
18:7 So he asked them again, "Who are you looking for?" And they said, "Jesus the Nazarene."
18:8 Jesus answered, "I told you: I am. So if it is me you want, let these men leave."
18:9 It happened so that what he had said would come true: "I did not lose a single one of those you have given me."
18:10 Simon Peter had a sword. He pulled it out and hit the high priest's slave, and cut off his right ear. The slave's name was Malchus.
18:11 Jesus told Peter, "Put the sword back in its case. The cup the Father has handed me, am I not to drink it?"
## Taken to Annas
18:12 So the cohort and its commander, with the guards of the Jews, seized Jesus and tied him up.
18:13 They led him first to Annas. He was Caiaphas's father-in-law, and Caiaphas was high priest in that year.
18:14 This Caiaphas had given the Jews his advice: it is better for one man to die on behalf of the people.
## Peter's first denial
18:15 Following Jesus were Simon Peter and another disciple. The high priest knew that disciple, and he went into the high priest's courtyard along with Jesus.
18:16 Peter, though, was standing by the gate, outside. So the other disciple, the high priest's acquaintance, came out, had a word with the woman who kept the gate, and brought Peter inside.
18:17 The slave girl who kept the gate said to Peter, "Surely you aren't one of this man's disciples as well?" He said, "I am not."
18:18 It was cold, so the slaves and the guards had built a charcoal fire. They stood around it, getting warm. Peter stood there among them, getting warm too.
## The high priest questions Jesus
18:19 The high priest now asked Jesus questions about his disciples and about what he taught.
18:20 Jesus answered him, "I have spoken to the world in the open. My teaching was always in synagogue and in the Temple, where all the Jews gather. I have not said anything in secret.
18:21 Why do you question me? Ask the people who heard what I told them. See, they know what I said."
18:22 As he said this, one of the guards who stood by gave Jesus a slap and said, "You answer the high priest like that?"
18:23 Jesus answered him, "If I spoke badly, give evidence about what was bad. But if I spoke well, why are you beating me?"
18:24 So Annas sent him, tied up, to Caiaphas the high priest.
## Peter denies him again
18:25 Simon Peter was still standing there, getting warm. They said to him, "Surely you aren't one of his disciples as well?" Peter denied it. "I am not," he said.
18:26 One of the high priest's slaves was related to the man Peter had cut the ear off. He said, "I saw you in the garden with him, didn't I?"
18:27 Once more Peter denied it, and right away a rooster crowed.
## Jesus before Pilate
18:28 Next they brought Jesus from Caiaphas to the Praetorium. It was early. They stayed out of the Praetorium themselves, to keep from becoming unclean, so that they could eat the Passover.
18:29 So Pilate came outside to them. "What is your accusation against this man?" he said.
18:30 They answered him, "Unless this fellow were doing evil, we would never have handed him to you."
18:31 Pilate said to them, "Take him yourselves, and try him under your own law." The Jews said to him, "We are not permitted to kill anyone."
18:32 That way the word of Jesus came true, when he pointed to the sort of death he would die.
18:33 So Pilate went into the Praetorium again. He called for Jesus and said to him, "So you are the King of the Jews?"
18:34 Jesus answered, "Are you saying this yourself, or did others tell it to you about me?"
18:35 Pilate answered, "Surely I am not a Jew? It was your nation and the chief priests who handed you to me. What did you do?"
18:36 Jesus answered, "My kingdom does not come from this world. If my kingdom came from this world, my guards would be fighting to keep me from being handed over to the Jews. But in fact my kingdom does not come from here."
18:37 Pilate said to him, "Then you are a king?" Jesus answered, "You are the one saying I am a king. This is why I have been born, and this is why I have come into the world: to be a witness for the truth. All who belong to the truth hear my voice."
18:38 Pilate said to him, "What is truth?" Having said this, Pilate went back out to the Jews. "I do not find a single charge against him," he told them.
18:39 "You have a custom: at the Passover I set one person free for you. So do you want me to free the King of the Jews for you?"
18:40 Then they shouted again, "Not this one, but Barabbas!" Barabbas was a bandit.
## "Look at the man!"
19:1 So at that point Pilate had Jesus taken and whipped.
19:2 The soldiers wove a crown out of thorny branches and set it on his head. They threw a purple cloak around him,
19:3 and they kept coming up to him and saying, "Hail, King of the Jews!" And they kept hitting him in the face.
19:4 Pilate came out once more. "See," he said to them, "I am leading him out here to you, so you will know that I find no charge against him."
19:5 Jesus came out, then, wearing the thorn crown and the purple cloak. Pilate said to them, "Look at the man!"
19:6 The moment the chief priests and the guards saw him, they yelled, "Crucify! Crucify!" Pilate told them, "Take him and crucify him yourselves, for I do not find a charge against him."
19:7 The Jews answered him, "We have a law. Under the law he has to die, because he made himself out to be God's Son."
19:8 Hearing these words, Pilate grew more afraid than ever.
19:9 He went into the Praetorium again and said to Jesus, "Where are you from?" Jesus did not give him an answer.
19:10 Pilate said to him, "Are you not talking to me? Surely you know I have the power to set you free, and the power to crucify you?"
19:11 Jesus answered him, "You would not have any power against me, none, if it had not been granted to you from above. So the sin of the one who handed me over to you is greater."
19:12 Because of this, Pilate kept trying to set him free. But the Jews shouted, "Free this man and you are no Friend of Caesar! Anyone who makes himself a king is speaking against Caesar."
19:13 On hearing this, Pilate led Jesus out. He took his seat on the judge's platform, at the spot called the Stone Pavement, or Gabbatha in Hebrew.
19:14 It was Preparation Day for the Passover. The time was about the sixth hour. Pilate said to the Jews, "Look at your king!"
19:15 But they shouted, "Away with him! Away! Crucify him!" Pilate said to them, "Am I to crucify your king?" The chief priests answered, "We have no king except Caesar."
19:16 So then Pilate handed him to them to be crucified. They took Jesus along.
## The crucifixion
19:17 He went out carrying the cross for himself, to what is called Skull Place, named Golgotha in Hebrew.
19:18 That is where they crucified him. They also crucified two other men with him, one on this side and one on that, and Jesus in the center.
19:19 Pilate wrote a sign as well and had it put on the cross. What was written was: "Jesus the Nazarene, the King of the Jews."
19:20 Lots of Jews read the sign, since the spot where they crucified Jesus was close to the city. And it was in Hebrew, in Latin and in Greek.
19:21 So the Jews' chief priests kept telling Pilate, "Don't write 'The King of the Jews.' Write that he said, 'I am King of the Jews.'"
19:22 Pilate answered, "What I have written stays written."
19:23 After crucifying Jesus, the soldiers took his clothes and made four portions, one portion for each soldier. They took the tunic too. Now the tunic had no seams. It was woven from the top straight through, all in one piece.
19:24 They said to each other, "Let's not rip it. Let's draw lots for it and see who gets it." That way the Scripture came true that says, "They divided up my clothes among themselves, and over my clothing they threw lots." So that is what the soldiers did.
## His mother and the disciple he loved
19:25 Meanwhile, by Jesus' cross stood his mother and his mother's sister, Mary of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene.
19:26 Jesus saw his mother, and the disciple he loved standing beside her. He said to his mother, "Woman, look: your son."
19:27 Then he said to the disciple, "Look: your mother." From that very hour the disciple took her into his own household.
## "It is finished"
19:28 After this, Jesus knew that everything had now been brought to its end. So that the Scripture would be completed, he said, "I am thirsty."
19:29 There was a container sitting there, full of sour wine. They fixed a sponge soaked in the sour wine onto some hyssop and brought it up to his mouth.
19:30 Jesus took the sour wine and said, "It is finished." He bent his head down and handed over his spirit.
19:31 It was Preparation Day, and the Jews did not want the bodies left on the cross over the Sabbath, because that Sabbath was a great day. So they asked Pilate to have the men's legs broken and the bodies removed.
19:32 The soldiers came, then, and broke the first man's legs, and the legs of the other man crucified alongside him.
19:33 Coming to Jesus, though, they saw he had died already, so they did not break his legs.
19:34 Instead, one soldier jabbed his side with a spear, and right away blood and water came out.
19:35 The man who saw this has given testimony, and his testimony can be trusted. He knows that what he says is true. He tells it so that you also may believe.
19:36 For all this took place to make the Scripture come true: "No bone of his will be broken."
19:37 And another Scripture, again, says, "They will look at the one they pierced."
## The burial
19:38 Later Joseph from Arimathea asked Pilate if he could remove Jesus' body. He was a disciple of Jesus, though he kept it hidden because he was afraid of the Jews. Pilate allowed it, so Joseph came and removed the body.
19:39 Nicodemus came too, the man who had first come to Jesus at night. He brought a blend of myrrh and aloes weighing about a hundred Roman pounds.
19:40 They took Jesus' body and tied it up in linen wrappings along with the spices, the way Jews prepare a body for burial.
19:41 In the area where he had been crucified there was a garden. And the garden held a new tomb, where nobody had been placed yet.
19:42 So, because of the Jews' Preparation Day, and because the tomb was close by, that is where they put Jesus.
## The empty tomb
20:1 Mary Magdalene came to the tomb early on the first day of the week. It was still dark. She saw that the stone had been lifted away from the tomb.
20:2 She ran off to Simon Peter and to the other disciple, whom Jesus loved. She told them, "They have carried the Lord away from the tomb, and we have no idea where they laid him."
20:3 Peter set out with the other disciple, and they headed for the tomb.
20:4 The two of them were running side by side. The other disciple sped ahead, faster than Peter, and got to the tomb first.
20:5 He bent down to peer in and saw the linen wrappings lying there. Still, he did not go inside.
20:6 Simon Peter arrived after him and went right into the tomb. He looked at the linen wrappings lying there,
20:7 and also the cloth that had been on his head. It was not lying with the linen wrappings, but rolled up separately in one place.
20:8 At that point the other disciple, the one who got to the tomb first, went in too. He saw, and he believed.
20:9 They did not yet know the Scripture that says he had to rise from the dead.
20:10 So the disciples went off home again.
## Mary Magdalene meets Jesus
20:11 Mary, though, stayed standing by the tomb outside, crying. While she cried, she bent down and peered into the tomb.
20:12 There she saw two angels dressed in white, seated one by the head and one by the feet of the place where Jesus' body had been lying.
20:13 "Woman, why are you crying?" they asked her. She told them, "They have carried my Lord away, and I have no idea where they laid him."
20:14 As soon as she said this, she turned and looked behind her. Jesus was standing there, but she did not know it was Jesus.
20:15 "Woman, why are you crying?" Jesus said to her. "Who are you looking for?" She thought he was the man who kept the garden, so she said to him, "Sir, if you are the one who carried him off, tell me where you laid him, and I will go and get him."
20:16 Jesus said to her, "Mary!" She turned around and said to him in Hebrew, "Rabboni!" The word means "Teacher."
20:17 Jesus said to her, "Stop clinging to me. I have not yet gone up to the Father. Go to my brothers instead, and say to them, 'I am going up to my Father and your Father, my God and your God.'"
20:18 Mary Magdalene came to the disciples bringing the news: "I have seen the Lord!" She told them what he had said to her.
## Jesus sends his disciples
20:19 Evening came on that same day, the first of the week. The doors of the place where the disciples were staying were locked, because they were afraid of the Jews. Jesus came and stood in the middle of them and said, "Peace to you."
20:20 With these words he showed them his hands and his side. Seeing the Lord filled the disciples with joy.
20:21 Jesus said to them again, "Peace to you. Just as the Father has sent me out, I too am sending you."
20:22 Having said this, he breathed on them. "Receive the Holy Spirit," he told them.
20:23 "If you forgive anyone their sins, those sins have been forgiven them. If you hold on to anyone's sins, they have been held on to."
## Thomas
20:24 Thomas, who was one of the Twelve and was known as Didymus, had not been with them when Jesus came.
20:25 The other disciples kept telling him, "We have seen the Lord!" But he said to them, "If I do not see the print of the nails in his hands, and push my finger into the print of the nails, and push my hand into his side, there is no way I will believe."
20:26 After eight days the disciples were indoors again, and this time Thomas was with them. The doors were locked, but Jesus came, stood in the middle of them and said, "Peace to you."
20:27 Then he said to Thomas, "Bring your finger over here and look at my hands. Bring your hand and push it into my side. Do not turn into an unbeliever. Be a believer."
20:28 Thomas said to him in answer, "My Lord and my God!"
20:29 Jesus said to him, "You have believed because you have seen me? Blessed are the ones who did not see, and yet believed."
## Why this book was written
20:30 Jesus did many more signs as well while the disciples looked on, signs not written down in this book.
20:31 These have been written down so that you may believe that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God, and so that as you believe, you will have life in his name.
## Breakfast by the lake
21:1 Some time later Jesus made himself known to the disciples again, at the Sea of Tiberias. This is the way he did it.
21:2 Gathered together were Simon Peter; Thomas, known as Didymus; Nathanael, who came from Cana in Galilee; Zebedee's sons; and two more of his disciples.
21:3 Simon Peter said to them, "I'm off to fish." "We're coming with you too," they told him. Out they went and climbed into the boat. All that night they did not catch a thing.
21:4 When it was already morning, Jesus was standing on the beach. The disciples, though, did not know it was Jesus.
21:5 Jesus called to them, "Boys, you have no fish to eat, do you?" "No," they answered him.
21:6 He told them, "Throw the net over on the boat's right-hand side and you will find fish." They threw it, and then they could not pull it in, there were so many fish.
21:7 So the disciple Jesus loved said to Peter, "That's the Lord!" The moment Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he tied his outer coat around himself, since he had nothing on, and plunged into the sea.
21:8 The rest of the disciples came in the small boat, hauling the net with the fish in it. They were not far from land, only about two hundred cubits away.
21:9 As they stepped onto the land, they saw a charcoal fire burning, with a fish lying on it, and some bread.
21:10 Jesus told them, "Bring a few of the fish you caught just now."
21:11 Simon Peter went up and pulled the net onto the land. It was full of big fish, a hundred and fifty-three. There were that many, and still the net did not tear.
21:12 "Come, eat breakfast," Jesus said to them. Not one of the disciples had the nerve to question him, "Who are you?" because they knew it was the Lord.
21:13 Jesus came over, took the bread and handed it to them, and the fish the same way.
21:14 This made the third time Jesus had shown himself to the disciples since being raised from the dead.
## "Do you love me?"
21:15 Once they had finished eating breakfast, Jesus asked Simon Peter, "Simon son of John, do you love me more than these?" "Yes, Lord," Peter said, "you yourself know that I love you." Jesus told him, "Feed my lambs."
21:16 A second time Jesus asked him, "Simon son of John, do you love me?" "Yes, Lord," Peter said, "you yourself know that I love you." Jesus told him, "Shepherd my sheep."
21:17 Jesus asked him a third time, "Simon son of John, do you love me?" It hurt Peter that he asked him the third time, "Do you love me?" He said to him, "Lord, you know all there is to know. You can tell that I love you." Jesus told him, "Feed my sheep.
21:18 Amen, amen, I tell you: when you were younger, you would put on your own belt and walk around wherever you liked. But when you grow old, you will stretch out your hands, and someone else will tie a belt on you and take you where you do not want to go."
21:19 He said this pointing to the sort of death by which Peter would bring glory to God. And after he had said it, he told him, "Follow me."
## The disciple Jesus loved
21:20 Peter turned around and saw the disciple Jesus loved coming along behind. This was the disciple who had leaned back against Jesus' chest at the supper and asked, "Lord, who is the one handing you over?"
21:21 Seeing him, Peter said to Jesus, "Lord, and what about him?"
21:22 Jesus said to him, "Suppose I want him to stay until I come. What business is that of yours? As for you, follow me."
21:23 So this word went out among the brothers that the disciple was not going to die. But Jesus had not told him that he would not die. He had said, "Suppose I want him to stay until I come. What business is that of yours?"
21:24 This disciple is the one giving testimony about these events, and the one who wrote them down. And we know his testimony is true.
21:25 There are many other things too that Jesus did. If they were written down one by one, I do not think the world itself would have space for the books it would take.
