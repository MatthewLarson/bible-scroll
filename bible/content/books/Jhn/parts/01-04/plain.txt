## In the beginning, the Word
1:1 In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
1:2 In the beginning, this one was with God.
1:3 Through him everything came to exist; apart from him not a single thing came to exist. What came to exist
1:4 in him was life, and the life was humanity's light.
1:5 The light is shining in the dark, and the dark did not get hold of it.
1:6 A man came on the scene, sent from God. John was his name.
1:7 This man came for testimony, to testify about the light, so that through him everyone would believe.
1:8 He was not the light. His task was to testify about the light.
1:9 The real light, which shines on every person, was on its way into the world.
1:10 He was in the world. The world owed its existence to him, and the world did not know him.
1:11 He came to what was his own, and his own people did not welcome him.
1:12 But to as many as received him he gave the authority to become God's children: to those who believe in his name.
1:13 Their birth came not from blood, not from fleshly desire, not from a man's decision, but from God.
1:14 And the Word became flesh and pitched his tent among us. We gazed at his glory, the kind of glory an only son has from his father, full of grace and truth.
1:15 John testifies about him. He cried out, "This is the man I had in mind when I said, 'The one coming after me has gone ahead of me, because he existed before me.'"
1:16 For all of us have taken from his abundance, grace in exchange for grace.
1:17 For it was through Moses that the law was given, while grace and truth arrived through Jesus Christ.
1:18 Nobody has ever seen God. It is the only one, God, the one in the Father's embrace, who has made him known.
## Who are you?
1:19 This is what John testified when the Jews of Jerusalem sent priests and Levites to put a question to him: "Who are you?"
1:20 He spoke openly and did not deny it. Openly he said, "I am not the Messiah."
1:21 "What, then?" they asked him. "Are you Elijah?" He said, "I am not." "Are you the Prophet?" He answered, "No."
1:22 So they said to him, "Who are you? We need an answer for those who sent us. How do you describe yourself?"
1:23 He said, "I am a voice of someone shouting in the wilderness, 'Make the Lord's road straight,' just as Isaiah the prophet said."
1:24 The ones who had been sent were from the Pharisees.
1:25 They asked him further, "If you are not the Messiah, and not Elijah, and not the Prophet, what are you baptizing for?"
1:26 John answered them, "I baptize in water. In the middle of you stands someone whom you people do not know,
1:27 the one who comes after me. I am not fit to untie his sandal strap."
1:28 These things happened in Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John was at work baptizing.
## The Lamb of God
1:29 The following day John saw Jesus coming his way. "There is the Lamb of God," he said, "the one who takes the world's sin away!
1:30 He is the one I was speaking of when I said, 'After me comes a man who has gone ahead of me, because he existed before me.'
1:31 I did not know him either. But this is why I came baptizing in water: so that he would be made known to Israel."
1:32 John also gave testimony: "I have watched the Spirit come down out of heaven like a dove, and it stayed on him.
1:33 I did not know him either. But the one who sent me to baptize in water told me, 'When you see the Spirit coming down and staying on someone, he is the one who baptizes in the Holy Spirit.'
1:34 I have seen it, and I have given testimony that this man is the Chosen One of God."
## Come and see
1:35 The day after that, John was again standing in his place, along with two of his disciples.
1:36 He looked hard at Jesus walking by and said, "There is the Lamb of God!"
1:37 The two disciples heard what he said, and they went after Jesus.
1:38 Jesus turned and saw them following him. "What are you looking for?" he asked them. They said to him, "Rabbi" (the word means Teacher), "where are you staying?"
1:39 "Come," he told them, "and you will see." So they went and saw where he was staying, and they stayed with him the rest of that day. It was about the tenth hour.
1:40 Of the two who had heard John and gone after Jesus, one was Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter.
1:41 Before anything else, Andrew went to find his own brother Simon. He told him, "We have found the Messiah!" (The word, translated, is Christ.)
1:42 Andrew led him to Jesus. Jesus fixed his eyes on him and said, "You are Simon son of John. You will be called Cephas" (in translation, Peter).
## Philip and Nathanael
1:43 The day after, Jesus chose to set out for Galilee, and he came upon Philip. "Follow me," Jesus told him.
1:44 Philip was from Bethsaida, the town Andrew and Peter came from.
1:45 Philip found Nathanael and said to him, "The one Moses wrote about in the law, and the prophets too, we have found him: Jesus, Joseph's son, from Nazareth."
1:46 Nathanael said to him, "Out of Nazareth, can anything good come?" Philip said to him, "Come and see."
1:47 Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward him and said of him, "Look, a real Israelite, with no trickery in him!"
1:48 "Where do you know me from?" Nathanael asked. Jesus answered, "Before Philip called you, while you were under the fig tree, I saw you."
1:49 "Rabbi," Nathanael answered, "you are the Son of God; you are the King of Israel!"
1:50 Jesus answered him, "Is it because I said I saw you under the fig tree that you believe? You will see bigger things than these."
1:51 And he said to him, "Amen, amen, I tell you all: you will see heaven standing open, and God's angels going up and coming down on the Son of Man."
## Water into wine at Cana
2:1 A wedding took place on the third day, at Cana in Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was present there.
2:2 Jesus was invited to the wedding as well, and so were his disciples.
2:3 When the wine was running short, the mother of Jesus said to him, "They are out of wine."
2:4 Jesus said to her, "What does that have to do with me and you, woman? My hour is not here yet."
2:5 His mother told the servants, "Whatever he says to you, do it."
2:6 Six stone water jars stood there, set out for the Jews' purification rites, each one holding two or three measures.
2:7 "Fill the jars with water," Jesus told them, and they filled them up to the top.
2:8 Next he said to them, "Dip some out now, and carry it to the man in charge of the feast." And they carried it.
2:9 The man in charge of the feast tasted the water, now turned into wine, and did not know where it came from (though the servants who had dipped the water knew). So he called the groom
2:10 and said to him, "Everyone sets out the good wine first, and the poorer wine once people are drunk. You have kept the good wine until now."
2:11 This was how Jesus began his signs, in Cana of Galilee. He made his glory visible, and his disciples believed in him.
2:12 After this he went down to Capernaum, together with his mother, his brothers and his disciples, but they did not stay there long.
## Trouble in the temple
2:13 The Jews' Passover drew near, and Jesus traveled up to Jerusalem.
2:14 He found people in the temple grounds who sold oxen, sheep and doves, and money changers seated at their work.
2:15 Making a whip from cords, he drove everyone out of the temple grounds, the sheep and the oxen with them. The money changers' coins he poured out, and their tables he tipped over.
2:16 To the dove sellers he said, "Get these out of here! Stop turning my Father's house into a house for trading!"
2:17 His disciples remembered the words in Scripture, "Zeal for your house will eat me up."
2:18 So the Jews answered him, "What sign are you showing us, since you are doing these things?"
2:19 Jesus answered them, "Tear this temple down, and within three days I will raise it."
2:20 So the Jews said, "Forty-six years this temple was under construction, and you, in three days, will raise it up?"
2:21 But the temple he meant was the temple of his body.
2:22 Later, once he had been raised from among the dead, his disciples remembered he used to say this, and they believed both the Scripture and what Jesus had said.
2:23 While he was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the festival, many believed in his name as they watched the signs he was doing.
2:24 Jesus, for his part, did not trust himself to them, because he knew everyone.
2:25 He had no need of anyone's testimony about a man, since he himself knew what was inside a man.
## Nicodemus comes by night
3:1 One of the Pharisees was a man called Nicodemus, a leader among the Jews.
3:2 He came to Jesus during the night and told him, "Rabbi, we know you are from God, come as a teacher. These signs you do, no one can do them unless God is with him."
3:3 Jesus answered him, "Amen, amen, I tell you: unless someone is born from above, he has no way to see the kingdom of God."
3:4 Nicodemus said to him, "How can a man be born when he is old? He cannot go back into his mother's womb a second time and be born, can he?"
3:5 Jesus answered, "Amen, amen, I tell you: unless someone is born from water and Spirit, he has no way to enter the kingdom of God.
3:6 What is born from the flesh is flesh, and what is born from the Spirit is spirit.
3:7 Do not be amazed that I told you, 'You people must be born from above.'
3:8 The wind blows where it wants to. You hear the sound of it, yet where it comes from and where it is going you do not know. That is how it is with everyone born from the Spirit."
3:9 Nicodemus answered him, "How can these things come about?"
3:10 Jesus answered, "You are the teacher of Israel, and you do not know these things?
3:11 Amen, amen, I tell you: what we know, we speak; what we have seen, we testify to; and our testimony you people do not receive.
3:12 If I have spoken to you about earthly things and you do not believe, how will you believe if I speak to you about heavenly ones?
3:13 And nobody has gone up into heaven except the one who came down out of heaven, the Son of Man.
3:14 As Moses raised the snake high in the wilderness, so the Son of Man has to be lifted up,
3:15 so that each one who believes may have eternal life in him.
3:16 For this is how God loved the world: he gave his only Son, so that everyone who believes in him would not be lost but would have eternal life.
3:17 For God sent the Son into the world, not to pass judgment on the world, but so that through him the world would be saved.
3:18 The one who believes in him is not judged. The one who does not believe has been judged already, for not having believed in the name of God's only Son.
3:19 The judgment is this: light has entered the world, and people preferred the darkness to the light, because what they did was evil.
3:20 For anyone who makes a practice of evil hates the light and does not come to the light, for fear his works will be exposed.
3:21 But the one who practices the truth comes to the light, so that his works may be shown to have been done in God."
## He must grow
3:22 After these things Jesus and his disciples went into the land of Judea, and there he stayed with them, and he was baptizing.
3:23 John also was baptizing, at Aenon near Salim, since water was abundant there; and people kept arriving to be baptized.
3:24 For this was before John was thrown into prison.
3:25 John's disciples then got into a dispute with a Jew about purification.
3:26 So they came to John. "Rabbi," they said, "you remember the one who was with you beyond the Jordan, the one you gave testimony about? Look: he is baptizing, and everyone is going over to him."
3:27 John answered, "No one can receive anything, not one thing, unless heaven has given it to him.
3:28 You yourselves are my witnesses that I said, 'I am not the Messiah; rather, I have been sent in front of him.'
3:29 It is the groom who has the bride. The groom's friend stands there and hears him, and he is filled with joy at the groom's voice. So my joy has now been made full.
3:30 He must keep growing, and I must keep getting smaller."
3:31 The one coming from above stands over everyone. Whoever is from the earth is earthly and speaks from the earth. The one coming from heaven stands over everyone.
3:32 What he has seen and heard, that he testifies to, and his testimony nobody receives.
3:33 Whoever does receive his testimony has put his seal on this: God is true.
3:34 For the one God sent speaks God's own words; he does not give the Spirit by measure.
3:35 The Father loves the Son, and he has given all things into his hand.
3:36 The one who believes in the Son has eternal life. The one who disobeys the Son will not see life; instead God's wrath stays on him.
## The woman at Jacob's well
4:1 Jesus became aware of a report that had reached the Pharisees: "Jesus is making and baptizing more disciples than John."
4:2 (To be exact, Jesus himself was not baptizing; his disciples were.)
4:3 So he left Judea and headed back to Galilee.
4:4 His road had to take him through Samaria.
4:5 So he comes to Sychar, a Samaritan town close to the field Jacob gave to Joseph his son.
4:6 This was where Jacob's spring was. Jesus was tired out from the road, so he sat down there by the spring, just as he was. It was about the sixth hour.
4:7 Then a woman of Samaria arrived to draw water. "Let me have a drink," Jesus said to her.
4:8 His disciples, you see, were away in the town buying food.
4:9 The Samaritan woman answered, "How do you, a Jew, come to ask me for a drink, when I am a woman of Samaria?" (For Jews do not share vessels with Samaritans.)
4:10 Jesus answered her, "If you knew what God is giving, and who it is that says to you, 'Give me a drink,' then you would have been the one to ask him, and he would have given you living water."
4:11 "Sir," said the woman, "you have no bucket, and the well is deep. So where is this living water of yours coming from?
4:12 Surely you are not greater than our father Jacob? This well was his gift to us. He drank from it himself, and so did his sons and his flocks."
4:13 Jesus answered her, "Whoever drinks some of this water will thirst again.
4:14 But whoever drinks from the water that I will give him will never, ever be thirsty. No: the water I will give him will become a spring inside him, of water leaping up into eternal life."
4:15 "Sir," the woman said, "let me have this water! Then I will not get thirsty, and I will not have to come all the way out here to draw water."
4:16 "Go and call your husband," he told her, "and then come back here."
4:17 "I have no husband," the woman answered. Jesus said, "You put that well: 'A husband I do not have.'
4:18 Five husbands you have had, and the one you have now is not your husband. In this you have told the truth."
4:19 "Sir," said the woman, "I see now: you are a prophet.
4:20 It was on this mountain that our fathers worshiped. But you people say Jerusalem is the place where worship has to happen."
4:21 Jesus said to her, "Believe me, woman: an hour is coming when you people will worship the Father neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem.
4:22 You people worship something you do not know. We worship what we know, since salvation comes from the Jews.
4:23 But an hour is coming, and it is now here, when real worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth. Indeed the Father is looking for such people to worship him.
4:24 God is spirit. Those who worship him have to worship in spirit and truth."
4:25 The woman said to him, "I know the Messiah (the one called Christ) is on his way. When he comes, he will announce all things to us."
4:26 Jesus told her, "I am he, I who am speaking to you."
## Fields white for harvest
4:27 At this point his disciples came back, and they wondered that he was talking with a woman. But not one of them said, "What are you looking for?" or "Why are you talking with her?"
4:28 The woman then left her water jar behind, went away into the town, and said to the people there,
4:29 "Come here and look at a man who told me all the things I have done! He couldn't be the Messiah, could he?"
4:30 Out of the town they went, and they were coming toward him.
4:31 In the meantime his disciples kept pressing him: "Rabbi, have something to eat."
4:32 He said to them, "I have food to eat that is unknown to you."
4:33 So the disciples said to one another, "Nobody brought him anything to eat, did they?"
4:34 Jesus told them, "Doing what the one who sent me wants, and bringing his work to completion: that is my food.
4:35 Isn't that what you say: 'Four more months, and then the harvest'? Look here, I tell you: raise your eyes and take in the fields. They are white for harvest.
4:36 Already the one who harvests is getting his pay and gathering fruit for eternal life, so that sower and harvester can share the joy together.
4:37 For here the saying holds true: 'One person sows, and someone else harvests.'
4:38 I sent you to harvest something you have not toiled over. Others toiled, and you have come in on their toil."
4:39 From that town many of the Samaritans believed in him because of the word of the woman who testified, "He told me all the things I have done."
4:40 When the Samaritans reached him, they kept asking him to stay with them. He stayed there for two days.
4:41 And many more believed because of what he himself said.
4:42 They kept telling the woman, "We believe, but no longer because of your talk. We have heard for ourselves, and we know that he is truly the Savior of the world."
## The official's son
4:43 When the two days were over, he went on from there to Galilee.
4:44 For Jesus himself had testified that a prophet does not receive honor in his own homeland.
4:45 So when he came into Galilee, the Galileans received him, since they had seen everything he did in Jerusalem at the festival; they too had gone to the festival.
4:46 He came back, then, to Cana in Galilee, where he had made the water into wine. In Capernaum there was an official of the king whose son was ill.
4:47 This man heard that Jesus had come from Judea into Galilee. He went to him and kept asking him to come down and heal his son, who was going to die.
4:48 Jesus said to him, "If you people do not see signs and wonders, you will not believe at all."
4:49 "Sir," the official pleaded, "come down before my little boy dies!"
4:50 "Go," Jesus told him, "your son is alive." The man believed the word Jesus spoke to him, and he set out.
4:51 He was already on the way down when his slaves met him, saying that his boy was alive.
4:52 So he asked them at what hour the boy had begun to get better. They told him, "Yesterday, at the seventh hour, the fever left him."
4:53 The father then knew that it was at that very hour when Jesus had said to him, "Your son is alive." And he himself believed, along with his whole household.
4:54 Jesus did this, again a second sign, when he had come from Judea into Galilee.
