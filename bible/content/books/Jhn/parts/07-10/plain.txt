## Jesus and his brothers
7:1 After these things Jesus stayed on the move in Galilee. He did not want to travel around Judea, because the Jews were trying to kill him.
7:2 Now a festival of the Jews was coming near: the Feast of Booths.
7:3 So his brothers said to him, "Move on from here and go to Judea, so that your disciples there can also watch you do your works.
7:4 Nobody does things in hiding while he wants to be out in the open. If you are doing these things, let the world see you."
7:5 In fact, not even his brothers were putting their faith in him.
7:6 So Jesus said to them, "The right moment for me has not arrived yet. But for you, the moment is always ready.
7:7 The world is not able to hate you. But me it hates, because I testify about it that the things it does are evil.
7:8 Go up to the festival yourselves. I am not going up to this festival, since my moment is not yet complete."
7:9 He told them this and stayed behind in Galilee.
## Teaching at the Feast of Booths
7:10 His brothers went up to the festival. After that he went up as well, not in plain view but in a hidden way.
7:11 Meanwhile the Jews kept searching for him at the festival. "Where is that man?" they said.
7:12 The crowds were full of murmuring about him. Some said, "He is good." "No," said others, "he is misleading the crowd."
7:13 Still, nobody talked about him in the open, because they were afraid of the Jews.
7:14 The festival was already at its midpoint when Jesus went up to the temple and started teaching.
7:15 The Jews were astonished. "How does this man have such book learning?" they asked. "He has never been anyone's student."
7:16 Jesus gave them this answer: "The teaching I give is not mine. It is the teaching of the one who sent me.
7:17 If someone wants to do what God wills, that person will know about this teaching, whether it has its source in God or I am speaking from myself.
7:18 Someone who speaks from himself is after glory for himself. But the one who wants glory for the one who sent him is true. No wrongdoing is in him.
7:19 Didn't Moses give you the law? And not one of you does what the law says. Why are you out to kill me?"
7:20 The crowd answered, "You have a demon in you! Who wants to kill you?"
7:21 Jesus replied, "I did a single work, and all of you are astonished.
7:22 That is why Moses gave you circumcision (not that it comes from Moses; it comes from the ancestors), and on the Sabbath you circumcise a male.
7:23 If a male receives circumcision on the Sabbath so as not to break the law of Moses, are you furious with me for making a whole man healthy on the Sabbath?
7:24 Do not judge by how things look on the surface. Judge with a judgment that is right."
## Is this the Messiah?
7:25 Then some of the people who lived in Jerusalem said, "This is the man they want to kill, isn't it?
7:26 Look, he speaks out in the open, and they say nothing to him. Could it be that the rulers have actually realized this man is the Messiah?
7:27 But this man, we know his origins. As for the Messiah, when he comes, no one knows where he is from."
7:28 So Jesus, while he was teaching in the temple, called out loudly: "So you know me, and you know where I come from? I have not come on my own. The one who sent me is genuine, and you do not know him.
7:29 I do know him, because I come from his side, and he is the one who sent me."
7:30 So they kept looking for a way to take hold of him. Yet not one person put a hand on him, since his hour had still not come.
7:31 But many people in the crowd came to believe in him. They said, "When the Messiah comes, surely he won't do more signs than this man has done?"
7:32 Word reached the Pharisees that the crowd was whispering such things about him. So the chief priests and the Pharisees sent guards to take him into custody.
7:33 Jesus said, "For a little while longer I am with you. Then I go away to the one who sent me.
7:34 You will search for me, and you will not find me. And where I am, you are not able to come."
7:35 So the Jews said among themselves, "Where is he planning to go, that we won't find him? Surely he isn't going off to the Dispersion in the Greek world, to teach Greeks?
7:36 What does he mean by saying, 'You will search for me, and you will not find me,' and 'Where I am, you are not able to come'?"
## Rivers of living water
7:37 The festival came to its last and great day. Jesus stood there and cried out, "If anyone is thirsty, he should come to me and drink.
7:38 The one who believes in me, as the Scripture has said: 'Out of his belly rivers of living water will flow.'"
7:39 He said this about the Spirit, whom those who had believed in him were going to receive. For the Spirit had not yet come, because Jesus had not yet been glorified.
7:40 Some in the crowd who heard these words said, "This man truly is the Prophet."
7:41 Others said, "This is the Messiah." But some said, "The Messiah doesn't come from Galilee, does he?
7:42 Hasn't the Scripture said that the Messiah comes from David's seed, and from Bethlehem, David's home village?"
7:43 So the crowd split over him.
7:44 Some of them wanted to grab him, but no one put their hands on him.
## The rulers will not believe
7:45 So the guards returned to the chief priests and Pharisees. Those men asked them, "Why didn't you bring him?"
7:46 The guards answered, "Never has any man spoken in this way!"
7:47 The Pharisees answered, "You haven't been misled too, have you?
7:48 Has a single one of the rulers believed in him? Or any of the Pharisees?
7:49 As for this crowd that doesn't know the law, they are cursed!"
7:50 Then Nicodemus spoke up. He was a member of their group, and at an earlier time he had paid a visit to Jesus. He said to them,
7:51 "Our law does not judge a person unless it first hears from him and finds out what he is doing, does it?"
7:52 They answered him, "Are you from Galilee as well? Examine it, and you will see that out of Galilee no prophet is raised up."
## A later addition: the woman caught in adultery
7:53 Then they went away, each one to his own home.
8:1 But Jesus went off to the Mount of Olives.
8:2 At daybreak he was back in the temple, and all the people kept coming to him. He sat down and taught them.
8:3 The scribes and the Pharisees then led in a woman caught in adultery. They stood her where everyone could see her
8:4 and said to him, "Teacher, this woman was caught while she was committing adultery, in the act itself.
8:5 Moses, in the law, ordered us to stone women like this. So what is your word on it?"
8:6 They were saying this to test him, hoping to find a reason to accuse him. But Jesus stooped low and began to write in the dirt with his finger.
8:7 As they went on asking him, he stood up straight and said to them, "Whichever one of you has never sinned, let him throw a stone at her first."
8:8 Then he stooped low once more and went on writing in the dirt.
8:9 When they heard this, they began to leave, one after another, starting with the elders. Jesus was left by himself, and the woman was in the center, where she had been.
8:10 Jesus stood up straight and said to her, "Woman, where are they? Did no one condemn you?"
8:11 "Nobody, sir," she said. Jesus said, "I do not condemn you either. Go, and from now on, do not keep on sinning."
## The light of the world
8:12 Jesus spoke to them again. "I am the light of the world," he said. "The one who follows me will never walk around in the dark. Instead, the light of life will be his."
8:13 The Pharisees said to him, "You are a witness for yourself. What you testify is not true."
8:14 Jesus replied, "Suppose I do act as a witness for myself. My testimony is still true, because I know the place I came from and the place I am going. But you do not know where I come from, or where I am going.
8:15 You judge by the flesh. I am not judging anyone.
8:16 But suppose I do judge. The judgment I give is genuine, because I am not by myself. It is I together with the Father who sent me.
8:17 It is even written in your law that when two people testify, their testimony is true.
8:18 I am the one who testifies about myself, and the Father who sent me testifies about me as well."
8:19 So they asked him, "Where is your father?" Jesus answered, "You don't know me, and you don't know my Father either. Had you known me, you would have known my Father too."
8:20 He said these things in the treasury area, while teaching in the temple. Still no one took hold of him, since his hour had still not come.
## Where I am going, you cannot come
8:21 Again he said to them, "I am going away. You will search for me, and in your sin you will die. Where I am going, you are not able to come."
8:22 So the Jews said, "He isn't going to kill himself, is he? He does say, 'Where I am going, you are not able to come.'"
8:23 He told them, "You belong to what is below. I belong to what is above. You come from this world. I do not come from this world.
8:24 That is why I told you that you will die in your sins. For unless you believe that I am, you will die in your sins."
8:25 So they said to him, "Who are you?" Jesus told them, "Why am I even talking to you?
8:26 I have many things to say about you and to judge. But the one who sent me is true, and the things I heard from him are what I speak to the world."
8:27 They did not realize he was talking to them about the Father.
8:28 So Jesus said, "When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am, and that I do nothing from myself. I say these things just as the Father taught me.
8:29 The one who sent me is with me. He has not left me on my own, because at all times I do what he finds pleasing."
8:30 While he was saying this, many came to believe in him.
## Children of Abraham
8:31 Then Jesus spoke to the Jews who had put their trust in him: "If you remain in my word, you truly are my disciples.
8:32 You will come to know the truth, and the truth will make you free."
8:33 They answered him, "We are Abraham's seed. We have never been anyone's slaves. What do you mean, 'You will become free'?"
8:34 Jesus answered them, "Amen, amen, I tell you: everyone who keeps on sinning is sin's slave.
8:35 The slave does not remain in the house for all time; the son remains for all time.
8:36 So if the Son makes you free, you will really be free.
8:37 I know you are Abraham's seed. Yet you want to kill me, because my word makes no headway in you.
8:38 I speak of the things I have seen at the Father's side. You, in the same way, do the things you heard from your father."
8:39 They answered him, "Our father is Abraham." Jesus said to them, "If you are Abraham's children, the works of Abraham are what you would be doing.
8:40 But now you are trying to kill me, a human being who has spoken the truth to you, truth I heard from God. That is not something Abraham did.
8:41 You are doing what your father does." They said to him, "We are not the children of sexual sin. We have one Father: God."
8:42 Jesus said to them, "If God were your Father, you would love me, because I came out from God, and here I am. I did not come by my own choice; he is the one who sent me.
8:43 Why don't you understand the way I talk? Because you are not able to listen to my word.
8:44 You come from your father, the devil, and the things your father longs for are what you want to do. From the start he was a killer of people. He has not stood firm in the truth, because there is no truth in him. Whenever he tells a lie, he is speaking from what is his own, because he is a liar and the father of falsehood.
8:45 But I tell the truth, and that is why you do not believe me.
8:46 Which of you can show that I have sinned? If what I say is true, why don't you believe me?
8:47 The one who is from God listens to God's words. This is why you do not listen: you are not from God."
## Before Abraham came to be
8:48 The Jews answered him, "Aren't we telling it straight when we say you are a Samaritan and you have a demon?"
8:49 Jesus answered, "I have no demon. I honor my Father, and you take away my honor.
8:50 I am not after my own glory. There is one who is after it, and he judges.
8:51 Amen, amen, I tell you: if someone keeps my word, he will not see death, ever."
8:52 The Jews said to him, "Now we are sure you have a demon. Abraham died, and the prophets died, and you say, 'If someone keeps my word, he will not taste death, ever.'
8:53 You are not greater than our father Abraham, are you? He died. The prophets died too. Who are you claiming to be?"
8:54 Jesus answered, "If I give myself glory, my glory is worth nothing. The one who gives me glory is my Father, the one you say is 'our God.'
8:55 You have not come to know him, but I know him. If I said I don't know him, I would be a liar, the same as you. But I know him, and I keep his word.
8:56 Abraham your father was overjoyed that he would see my day. He saw it, and he was happy."
8:57 So the Jews said to him, "You are not even fifty, and you have seen Abraham?"
8:58 Jesus said to them, "Amen, amen, I tell you: before Abraham came to be, I am."
8:59 So they took up stones to hurl at him. But Jesus was hidden, and he went out of the temple.
## A man born blind sees
9:1 As he was walking along, he noticed a man who had been blind since birth.
9:2 His disciples asked him, "Rabbi, whose sin caused him to be born blind? His own, or his parents'?"
9:3 Jesus answered, "This man did not sin, and his parents did not sin. Instead, it is so that God's works can be shown openly in him.
9:4 We have to do the works of the one who sent me while it is still daytime. The night is on its way, when nobody can work.
9:5 Whenever I am in the world, I am the world's light."
9:6 When he had said this, he spat on the ground and made a paste of clay out of the spit, and smeared the clay on the man's eyes.
9:7 He told him, "Go and wash in the pool of Siloam." (The name means "Sent.") The man went off and washed, and he came back able to see.
9:8 The neighbors, and the people who had known him earlier as a beggar, said, "This is the man who sat begging, isn't it?"
9:9 Some said, "It's him." Others said, "No, it's someone like him." The man himself insisted, "I am he."
9:10 So they asked him, "Then how did your eyes get opened?"
9:11 He answered, "The man named Jesus made clay and smeared it on my eyes. He said to me, 'Go to Siloam and wash.' So I went off, and once I had washed, I could see."
9:12 They said to him, "Where is that man?" "I don't know," he said.
## The Pharisees question the man
9:13 They took the man who had once been blind to the Pharisees.
9:14 It was a Sabbath, the day Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes.
9:15 So the Pharisees also questioned him again about how he had gained his sight. He told them, "He laid clay on my eyes. I washed. And I see."
9:16 Then some of the Pharisees said, "This man is not from God. He doesn't keep the Sabbath." Others said, "How could a sinful man do signs like these?" And they were split.
9:17 So they spoke to the blind man again: "He opened your eyes. What do you say about him?" He said, "He is a prophet."
9:18 The Jews, though, would not believe that he had been blind and had gained his sight, not until they called in the parents of the man who now could see.
9:19 They asked them, "Is this your son, the one you say was born blind? So how is he able to see now?"
9:20 His parents answered, "This is our son, we know that, and we know he was blind when he was born.
9:21 But how he sees now, we do not know. Who opened his eyes, we do not know. Ask him. He is a grown man. He can talk for himself."
9:22 His parents said this because they were afraid of the Jews. The Jews had already agreed that anyone who declared him to be the Messiah would be thrown out of the synagogue.
9:23 That is why his parents said, "He is a grown man. Ask him."
9:24 They summoned the formerly blind man once more and told him, "Give God the glory! We know this man is a sinner."
9:25 He answered, "If he is a sinner, I don't know. This much I know: I was a blind man, and now I can see."
9:26 They said to him, "What did he do with you? In what way did he open your eyes?"
9:27 He answered them, "I told you before, but you paid no attention. What makes you want to hear it once more? You aren't hoping to become his disciples too, are you?"
9:28 They heaped abuse on him and said, "You are that man's disciple. We are disciples of Moses.
9:29 We know God has spoken to Moses. But this one, we do not know where he is from."
9:30 The man answered them, "Well, here is the astonishing part: you do not know where he is from, and he opened my eyes!
9:31 We know that God does not listen to sinners. But if a person honors God and does his will, God listens to that person.
9:32 From the dawn of time, not a single report has been heard of someone opening the eyes of a person born blind.
9:33 Unless this man came from God, he would not be able to do a thing."
9:34 They answered him, "You were born wholly in sins, and you are teaching us?" And they drove him out.
## Who is really blind?
9:35 Word reached Jesus that they had driven him out. He found him and said, "Do you believe in the Son of Man?"
9:36 "Sir, who is he?" the man answered. "I want to believe in him."
9:37 Jesus said to him, "You have seen him. The one talking with you, that is who he is."
9:38 He said, "I believe, Lord." And he bowed down and worshiped him.
9:39 Jesus said, "I came into this world to bring judgment, so that those who do not see will see, and those who see will turn blind."
9:40 Some of the Pharisees who were with him heard this. They said to him, "We are not blind too, are we?"
9:41 Jesus said to them, "If you were blind, you would have no sin. But now you say, 'We see.' So your sin remains."
## The shepherd and the door
10:1 "Amen, amen, I tell you: someone who does not go into the sheep's courtyard through the door, but climbs up and in from somewhere else, is a thief and a bandit.
10:2 The one who goes in through the door is the one who shepherds the sheep.
10:3 The doorkeeper opens up for him, and the sheep pay attention to his voice. He calls out to his own sheep, each by name, and leads them outside.
10:4 Once he has put all his own sheep out, he walks in front of them. The sheep follow him, because his voice is familiar to them.
10:5 A stranger, though, they will not follow at all. Instead they will run away from him, since strangers' voices are not ones they know."
10:6 Jesus told them this word picture, but they did not grasp what he was talking about.
10:7 So Jesus spoke again: "Amen, amen, I tell you, I am the door for the sheep.
10:8 Everyone who came before me is a thief and a bandit, but the sheep did not pay attention to them.
10:9 I am the door. Whoever goes in through me will be rescued. He will come in and go out, and he will find grass to eat.
10:10 A thief comes for no reason except to steal, slaughter and destroy. I came so they could have life, and have more than enough.
## The good shepherd
10:11 I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his own life for the sheep.
10:12 The hired man, who is not a shepherd and whose sheep they are not, sees the wolf coming. He leaves the sheep behind and runs, and the wolf grabs them and scatters them.
10:13 That is because he works for pay, and the sheep do not matter to him.
10:14 I am the good shepherd. I know those that are mine, and those that are mine know me,
10:15 in the same way that the Father knows me and I know the Father. And I lay down my life for the sake of the sheep.
10:16 I also have other sheep, ones that do not belong to this courtyard. I must lead those as well. They will pay attention to my voice, and they will become one flock with one shepherd.
10:17 This is why the Father loves me: I lay down my life so that I may take it back again.
10:18 No one takes it away from me. I lay it down by my own choice. I have the right to lay it down, and I have the right to take it back again. This is the command I received from my Father."
10:19 These words split the Jews again.
10:20 Many of them said, "He has a demon, and he is out of his mind. Why do you pay attention to him?"
10:21 Others said, "These are not the words of someone with a demon in him. A demon can't open blind people's eyes, can it?"
## At the Feast of Dedication
10:22 The Feast of Dedication came around in Jerusalem. It was wintertime,
10:23 and Jesus was walking around in the temple, in Solomon's Porch.
10:24 The Jews surrounded him and said, "How long are you going to leave us hanging? If you are the Messiah, tell us in plain words."
10:25 Jesus answered them, "I told you, and you are not believing. The works I am doing in my Father's name, they are my witnesses.
10:26 But you are not believing, since you do not belong among my sheep.
10:27 My own sheep pay attention to my voice. I know them, and they follow me.
10:28 I give them eternal life. They will not be lost, ever, and nobody will snatch them from my hand.
10:29 My Father, the one who gave them to me, is greater than all. No one can snatch them away from the Father's hand.
10:30 I and the Father are one."
10:31 Again the Jews lifted stones, ready to stone him.
10:32 Jesus answered them, "Many good works from the Father I have shown you. For which of those works are you stoning me?"
10:33 The Jews answered him, "We are not stoning you over a good work. We are stoning you for blasphemy, because you, a human being, are making yourself God."
10:34 Jesus answered them, "Your own law has it written, doesn't it, 'I said, You are gods'?
10:35 So, if he called those people gods, the people the word of God came to, and the Scripture cannot be undone,
10:36 then the one the Father made holy and sent into the world, do you say to him, 'You blaspheme,' because I said, 'I am God's Son'?
10:37 If I am not doing my Father's works, don't believe me.
10:38 But if I am doing them, then even if you do not believe me, believe the works. Then you will come to know, and go on knowing, that the Father is in me and I am in the Father."
10:39 Again they tried to grab him, but he slipped out of their hands.
## Beyond the Jordan
10:40 Then he went back across the Jordan to the place where John had been baptizing in the early days, and he stayed there.
10:41 Many people came to him. They said, "John did not do a single sign, but everything John said about this man has turned out true."
10:42 And there many came to believe in him.
