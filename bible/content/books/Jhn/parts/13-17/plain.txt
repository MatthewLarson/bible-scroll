## The Teacher washes feet
13:1 The Passover festival was about to begin. Jesus knew that his hour had come, the hour to cross over from this world to the Father. He had loved those who were his own in the world, and he loved them all the way to the end.
13:2 Supper was under way, and the devil had already planted the plan in the heart that Judas, son of Simon Iscariot, would hand Jesus over.
13:3 Jesus knew that the Father had placed everything in his hands. He knew that he had come out from God and was on his way to God.
13:4 So he got up from the supper and set aside his outer clothes. Then he took a towel and fastened it around himself.
13:5 Next he poured water into the basin and started washing his disciples' feet, wiping them dry with the towel he had fastened around himself.
13:6 So he came to Simon Peter. Peter said to him, "Lord, you, washing my feet?"
13:7 Jesus answered him, "Right now you do not know what I am doing. After this, you will know."
13:8 Peter said to him, "No, you will not wash my feet, not ever!" Jesus answered him, "Unless I wash you, you will have no share with me."
13:9 "Lord," Simon Peter said to him, "then wash my hands and my head too, not only my feet!"
13:10 Jesus said to him, "Someone who has had a bath has no need to wash, except the feet. He is clean all over. You are clean, but not all of you are."
13:11 He knew who was handing him over. That was why he said that not all of them were clean.
13:12 After he washed their feet, he took his clothes and put them back on. He lay down at the table again and said to them, "Do you see what I have done for you?
13:13 You call me 'the Teacher' and 'the Lord.' You are right to say so, because I am.
13:14 I am the Lord and the Teacher, and I washed your feet. So you too owe it to one another to wash each other's feet.
13:15 I have given you a model. What I did for you, you are to do as well.
13:16 Amen, amen, I tell you, a slave does not rank above his master. One who is sent does not rank above the one who sent him.
13:17 If you know these things, you are blessed if you do them.
13:18 I am not talking about all of you. I know which ones I chose. But this happens so that the Scripture would come true: 'The one who eats my bread has raised his heel against me.'
13:19 From now on I tell you ahead of time, so that when it comes to pass you will believe that I am.
13:20 Amen, amen, I tell you, whoever takes in someone I send takes me in, and whoever takes me in takes in the one who sent me."
## One of you will hand me over
13:21 When he had said this, Jesus was shaken in his spirit. He testified: "Amen, amen, I tell you, one of you will hand me over."
13:22 The disciples kept glancing at each other. They had no idea whom he was talking about.
13:23 One of his disciples was reclining in Jesus' embrace, close against him. He was the one Jesus loved.
13:24 So Simon Peter nodded to this man to find out who it could be that Jesus meant.
13:25 He simply tipped his head back onto Jesus' chest and said to him, "Lord, who is it?"
13:26 Jesus answered, "I will dip this bite of bread and give it to someone. He is the one." So he dipped the bite and gave it to Judas, son of Simon Iscariot.
13:27 After the bite, at that moment, Satan went into him. Jesus said to him, "Whatever you are doing, get it done faster."
13:28 None of those reclining there knew what he meant by saying this to Judas.
13:29 Judas held the money box, so some of them supposed Jesus was saying, "Buy the things we need for the festival." Or they thought he was to give the poor something.
13:30 Judas took the bite and went straight out. It was night.
## The new command
13:31 Once Judas had gone out, Jesus said, "Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and in him God has been glorified.
13:32 And if God has been glorified in him, God himself will glorify the Son in himself, and he will do it right away.
13:33 Little children, I will be with you just a short while more. You will search for me. What I said to the Jews I now say to you too: where I am going, you are not able to come.
13:34 I am giving you a new command: that you love each other. In the same way that I loved you, you too are to love each other.
13:35 Here is how all will know that you are disciples of mine: if you have love among yourselves."
13:36 Simon Peter said to him, "Lord, where are you going?" Jesus answered, "You are not able to follow me now to the place where I am going. But later on you will follow."
13:37 Peter said to him, "Lord, what keeps me from following you right now? For you I will lay down my life."
13:38 Jesus answered, "For me you will lay down your life? Amen, amen, I tell you, a rooster will not crow before you have denied me, three times over."
## The way to the Father
14:1 "Your heart must not be shaken. Put your trust in God, and put your trust in me as well.
14:2 There are many places to stay in my Father's house. Otherwise, would I have said to you that I am on my way to get a place ready for you?
14:3 If I go and get a place ready for you, I am coming again, and I will take you along to myself, so that where I am, there you will be too.
14:4 And you know the way that leads where I am going."
14:5 Thomas said to him, "Lord, we have no idea where you are going. How could we know the way?"
14:6 Jesus said to him, "I am the way, the truth and the life. Nobody comes to the Father unless he comes through me.
14:7 If you knew me, you would know my Father too. Starting now you know him, and you have seen him."
14:8 Philip said to him, "Lord, show us the Father. That is all we need."
14:9 Jesus said to him, "All this time I have been with you, Philip, and you have not come to know me? The one who has seen me has seen the Father. So how is it that you say, 'Show us the Father'?
14:10 Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in me? What I tell you, I do not say from myself. The Father remains in me, and he is the one doing his works.
14:11 Trust what I say: I am in the Father, and the Father is in me. If not, then believe because of the works themselves.
14:12 Amen, amen, I tell you, anyone who believes in me will also do the works that I do. In fact, he will do greater ones than these, because I am on my way to the Father.
14:13 Anything you ask for in my name, I will do it. That way the Father will be glorified in the Son.
14:14 If you ask me for anything in my name, I myself will do it.
## Another Advocate
14:15 If you love me, you will keep the commands I give.
14:16 I will request it of the Father, and he will give you another Advocate to be with you for all time.
14:17 He is the Spirit of truth. The world is not able to receive him, since it does not see him or know him. But you know him, because he remains beside you, and he will be in you.
14:18 I will not abandon you like orphans. I am coming to you.
14:19 Soon the world will not see me anymore, but you will see me. I live, so you too will live.
14:20 When that day comes, you will know that I am in my Father, that you are in me, and that I am in you.
14:21 The person who has my commands and keeps them, that is someone who loves me. My Father will love anyone who loves me. I will love that person too, and I will let him see me clearly."
14:22 Judas, not the Iscariot, said to him, "Lord, what has happened that you will let us see you, but not the world?"
14:23 Jesus answered him, "Anyone who loves me will keep what I say. My Father will love that person. We will come to him and make a place to stay with him.
14:24 The one who does not love me does not keep what I say. And the message you are hearing is not mine. It comes from the Father, the one who sent me.
14:25 I have said these things to you while I remain with you.
14:26 The Advocate, the Holy Spirit, is the one the Father will send in my name. He will teach you everything, and he will bring back to your mind all that I told you.
14:27 I am leaving you peace. My own peace is what I give you. I do not give it to you the way the world gives. Your heart must not be shaken, and it must not shrink back in fear.
14:28 You heard what I told you: I am going away, and I am coming to you. If you loved me, you would be glad that I am on my way to the Father, because the Father is greater than I am.
14:29 I have said it to you now, ahead of time, so that you will believe when it comes to pass.
14:30 I will not talk with you much longer, because the world's ruler is on his way. In me he owns nothing.
14:31 Instead, so that the world will learn that I love the Father, I am doing just what the Father ordered me to do. Get up. Let us leave this place."
## The real vine
15:1 "I am the real vine, and my Father is the farmer.
15:2 Any branch in me that bears no fruit, he takes away. Any branch that bears fruit, he trims it clean, so that it will bear more fruit.
15:3 You are clean already, thanks to the word I have spoken to you.
15:4 Remain in me, and I in you. The branch is not able to bear fruit on its own unless it remains in the vine. You are no different: you cannot unless you remain in me.
15:5 I am the vine. You are the branches. The one who remains in me, with me in him, is the one who bears a lot of fruit. Without me you can do nothing at all.
15:6 Whoever does not remain in me is tossed out like the branch and dries up. People gather the branches and throw them into the fire, and they burn.
15:7 If you remain in me and what I have said remains in you, ask for anything you want, and it will be yours.
15:8 My Father is glorified in this: when you bear a lot of fruit and become disciples of mine.
15:9 I have loved you the same way the Father has loved me. Remain in my love.
15:10 Keeping my commands is how you will remain in my love. That is how it is with me: I have kept the commands of my Father, and I remain in his love.
15:11 I have said these things to you so that you will have my joy in you, and your joy will be filled up.
15:12 Here is my command: that you love each other just as I loved you.
15:13 There is no greater love anyone can have than this: to lay down his life for the sake of his friends.
15:14 You are friends of mine if you do the things I command you.
15:15 I do not call you slaves anymore, since a slave does not know what his master is up to. I have called you friends, because all I heard from my Father I have let you know.
15:16 It was not you who chose me. I chose you, and I put you in place to go out and bear fruit, fruit that remains. Then the Father will give you whatever you ask him for in my name.
15:17 I give you these commands so that you will love each other.
## When the world hates you
15:18 If the world hates you, remember: it has hated me ahead of you.
15:19 If you came from the world, the world would love what belongs to it. But you do not come from the world. I chose you out of it, and that is why the world hates you.
15:20 Remember the word I spoke to you: 'A slave does not rank above his master.' If they hunted me down, they will hunt you down too. If they kept my word, they will keep yours too.
15:21 But they will do all this to you because of my name, since the one who sent me is a stranger to them.
15:22 Had I not come and talked to them, they would have no sin. But as it is, they have no cover for their sin.
15:23 Anyone who hates me also hates my Father.
15:24 Had I not done the works among them that no one else ever did, they would have no sin. But now they have both seen and hated me and my Father alike.
15:25 But it is so that the word written in their law would come true: 'They hated me for no reason.'
15:26 The Advocate will come, the one I will send to you from the Father. He is the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, and he will be a witness about me.
15:27 You too are witnesses, because you have been with me since the start."
16:1 "I have said these things to you so that you will not be tripped up.
16:2 They will throw you out of the synagogue. Yes, an hour is coming when anyone who kills you will think he is offering worship to God.
16:3 They will do this because they never knew the Father, or me either.
16:4 But I have said these things so that, when their hour arrives, you will recall that I spoke to you about them. I did not say this to you at the start, because I was with you.
## What the Advocate will do
16:5 Now, though, I am on my way to the one who sent me, and not one of you asks me, 'Where are you going?'
16:6 Instead, since I have told you these things, grief has taken over your heart.
16:7 But I am telling you the truth: you are better off if I go away. If I do not leave, the Advocate will not come to you. But if I do go, I will send him to you.
16:8 And when he comes, he will expose the world's error about sin, about righteousness and about judgment.
16:9 About sin: they do not believe in me.
16:10 About righteousness: I am going away to the Father, and you no longer see me.
16:11 About judgment: this world's ruler has already been judged.
16:12 There is much more I could tell you, but right now it would be too heavy for you to carry.
16:13 But when that one comes, the Spirit of truth, he will lead you in all the truth. He will not speak from himself. He will speak whatever he hears, and he will announce to you the things that are coming.
16:14 That one will glorify me, because he will take from what is mine and announce it to you.
16:15 Everything the Father has belongs to me. That is why I said he takes from what is mine and will announce it to you.
## A short time
16:16 A short time, and you no longer see me. Then a short time again, and you will see me."
16:17 So some of his disciples asked each other, "What is this he is telling us, 'A short time, and you do not see me, then a short time again, and you will see me,' and, 'Because I am going to the Father'?"
16:18 They kept saying, "What is this 'short time' he talks about? We cannot tell what he means."
16:19 Jesus could tell they wanted to question him, so he said to them, "Are you trying to work out together what I meant when I said, 'A short time, and you do not see me, then a short time again, and you will see me'?
16:20 Amen, amen, I tell you, you will cry and wail, but the world will be glad. You will be full of grief, but your grief will become joy.
16:21 When a woman is in labor, she is in pain, because her hour has come. But once the child is born, she no longer remembers the suffering. She is glad that a person has been born into the world.
16:22 It is the same with you. You have grief now, but I will see you again. Then your heart will be glad, and nobody takes your joy away from you.
16:23 When that day comes, you will not ask me any questions. Amen, amen, I tell you, if you ask the Father for anything, he will give it to you in my name.
16:24 Up to now you have asked for nothing in my name. Keep asking and you will receive, so that your joy will be filled up.
16:25 I have spoken these things to you in word pictures. An hour is coming when I will not speak to you in word pictures anymore. Instead, I will report to you openly about the Father.
16:26 When that day comes, you will make your requests in my name. I do not tell you that I will ask the Father for you,
16:27 because the Father himself loves you. You have loved me, and you have believed that I came out from God.
16:28 I came out from the Father and have come into the world. Now in turn I am leaving the world and going on my way to the Father."
16:29 His disciples say, "Look, now you are speaking openly. You are not using any word picture.
16:30 Now we can tell that you know everything. You have no need for anyone to put questions to you. Because of this we believe you came out from God."
16:31 Jesus answered them, "So now you believe?
16:32 Listen, an hour is coming, and it is already here, when you will scatter, each of you to his own place, and you will leave me by myself. And yet I am not by myself, because the Father is with me.
16:33 I have spoken these things to you so that you will have peace in me. In the world you have hardship. But be brave: I have won the victory over the world."
## The hour has come: Jesus prays
17:1 Jesus spoke these things. Then he raised his eyes toward heaven and said, "Father, the hour has arrived. Glorify your Son, so that the Son will glorify you.
17:2 For you gave him authority over all people, so that he would give eternal life to all that you have given him.
17:3 Eternal life is this: to know you, the only real God, and the one you sent, Jesus Christ.
17:4 I glorified you on the earth by finishing the work you assigned me.
17:5 So now, Father, you glorify me at your side, with the glory I had beside you before there was a world.
## For those you gave me
17:6 I have brought your name into the open for the people you gave me from the world. They belonged to you, and you gave them to me. They have held on to your word.
17:7 Now they understand: all the things you have given me are from you.
17:8 The sayings you gave me, I have given to them. They took them in, and they truly came to know that I came out from you. They believed that it was you who sent me.
17:9 I am asking on their behalf. It is not the world I am asking for, but the ones you have given me, because they belong to you.
17:10 Everything that is mine is yours, and what is yours is mine. And I have been glorified in them.
17:11 I will not be in the world any longer. They are in the world, but I am coming to you. Holy Father, keep them safe in your name, the name you have given me, so that they will be one, the way we are.
17:12 When I was with them, I kept them safe in your name, the name you have given me. I stood guard, and none of them was ruined except the son of ruin, so that the Scripture would come true.
17:13 Now, though, I am coming to you. I speak these things in the world so that they will have my joy inside them, filled to the full.
17:14 I have given them your word, and the world hated them, because they do not come from the world, just as I do not come from the world.
17:15 I am not asking you to remove them from the world. I am asking you to keep them safe from the evil one.
17:16 They are not from the world, as I am not from the world.
17:17 Make them holy in the truth. Your word is the truth.
17:18 You sent me into the world, and in the same way I sent them into the world.
17:19 For their sake I make myself holy, so that they too will be made holy in truth.
## For all who will believe
17:20 My request is not only about these. It is also about those who believe in me through what they say,
17:21 so that all of them will be one. Father, as you are in me and I am in you, let them also be in us. Then the world will believe that you sent me.
17:22 I have passed on to them the glory you have given me, so that they will be one in the same way that we are one.
17:23 I am in them and you are in me, so that they will be made complete as one. Then the world will know that you sent me, and that you loved them just as you loved me.
17:24 Father, what you have given me, I want them to be with me where I am. I want them to look on my glory, the glory you have given me, since you loved me before the world was founded.
17:25 Righteous Father, the world did not know you, but I knew you, and these came to know that you sent me.
17:26 I have let them know your name, and I will go on letting them know it, so that the love you loved me with will be in them, and I will be in them too."
