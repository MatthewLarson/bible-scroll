## Lazarus is sick
11:1 A certain man was sick. His name was Lazarus, and he came from Bethany, the home village of Mary and her sister Martha.
11:2 This was the Mary who anointed the Lord with perfumed oil and dried his feet with her hair. Her brother Lazarus was the one who was sick.
11:3 So the sisters sent him a message: "Lord, look! The man you love is sick."
11:4 When Jesus heard it, he said, "This sickness is not headed for death. It is for God's glory, so that through it the Son of God may be glorified."
11:5 Jesus loved Martha, her sister and Lazarus.
11:6 So once he heard the news of the sickness, he stayed on two more days right where he was.
11:7 After that, he spoke to the disciples: "Come, let's go into Judea again."
11:8 The disciples said, "Rabbi, the Jews were out to stone you only recently, and you are going there again?"
11:9 Jesus replied, "Doesn't the day have twelve hours? People who walk by day do not trip, because they see the light of this world.
11:10 But people who walk by night do trip, since the light is not in them."
11:11 He said this, and then he told them, "Lazarus, our friend, has gone to sleep. But I am on my way to wake him."
11:12 So the disciples said, "Lord, if he has gone to sleep, he will pull through."
11:13 Jesus had meant Lazarus' death, but they supposed he was talking about the rest that sleep gives.
11:14 Then Jesus said it to them openly: "Lazarus is dead.
11:15 I am glad, for your sakes, that I was not there, because now you can come to believe. But come, let's go to him."
11:16 So Thomas, known as the Twin, told the other disciples, "Let's go as well, so we can die with him."
## The resurrection and the life
11:17 Jesus came and found him already four days in the tomb.
11:18 Bethany was close to Jerusalem, roughly fifteen stadia away.
11:19 Many of the Jews had come to be with Martha and Mary and comfort the sisters about their brother.
11:20 Martha heard that Jesus was on his way, and she went to meet him. Mary, though, stayed sitting in the house.
11:21 Martha said to Jesus, "Lord, had you been here, my brother would not have died.
11:22 But even now I know: anything you ask of God, God will give you."
11:23 Jesus told her, "Your brother will rise."
11:24 "I know he will rise," Martha said, "at the resurrection on the last day."
11:25 Jesus told her, "I am the resurrection and the life. Those who believe in me, even if they die, will live.
11:26 And all who are living and believe in me will never die, not ever. Do you believe that?"
11:27 "Yes, Lord," she said. "I have come to believe that you are the Messiah, the Son of God, the one coming into the world."
## Jesus weeps
11:28 With that she left and called her sister Mary without anyone hearing. "The Teacher has come," she said, "and he is calling for you."
11:29 Mary heard and quickly got to her feet, and she set off toward him.
11:30 Jesus had not come into the village yet. He was still at the spot where Martha met him.
11:31 The Jews who had stayed in the house with Mary to console her saw her get up in a hurry and leave. So they went after her. They thought she was heading to the tomb to wail there.
11:32 Mary made her way to Jesus. When she saw him, she threw herself down at his feet. "Lord," she said, "had you been here, my brother would not have died."
11:33 Jesus saw her wailing, and also the Jews who came with her, wailing. Anger stirred deep in his spirit, and he let himself be shaken.
11:34 He said, "Where have you put him?" They answered, "Lord, come and see."
11:35 Jesus wept.
11:36 The Jews were saying, "Look how he loved him!"
11:37 But some of them said, "He opened the blind man's eyes. Couldn't he also have made sure that this man would not die?"
## "Lazarus, come out"
11:38 Again Jesus felt anger rising inside him as he came to the tomb. The tomb was a cave. A stone lay on it.
11:39 "Move the stone away," Jesus said. Martha, the dead man's sister, told him, "Lord, he already stinks. It has been four days."
11:40 Jesus answered her, "Did I not tell you: believe, and you will see God's glory?"
11:41 They moved the stone away. Then Jesus raised his eyes upward and said, "Father, I give you thanks because you heard me.
11:42 I myself knew that you hear me at all times. I said it because of the crowd standing around me, so they may believe it was you who sent me."
11:43 With that he gave a loud shout: "Lazarus, come out here!"
11:44 The man who had died came out. His feet and hands were bound with strips of cloth, and a cloth was tied around his face. Jesus told them, "Untie him and let him go."
## The council decides Jesus must die
11:45 As a result, many of the Jews believed in him: the ones who had come to Mary and watched what he did.
11:46 Some of them, though, went off to the Pharisees and reported to them the things Jesus had done.
11:47 So the chief priests and the Pharisees called the council together. They said, "What are we doing about this? The man is doing many signs.
11:48 If we leave him alone like this, everyone will believe in him. Then the Romans will come and take from us our place and our nation alike."
11:49 One of them, though, Caiaphas, the high priest in that year, told them, "You don't know anything.
11:50 And you don't stop to think: it is to your advantage that one man should die on behalf of the people, rather than the whole nation being destroyed."
11:51 This did not come from himself. Because he was high priest in that year, he spoke a prophecy: Jesus was about to die for the nation,
11:52 and not just for the nation. He would die to bring God's scattered children together into one.
11:53 So from that day on they were set on killing him.
11:54 Because of this, Jesus stopped going about in public among the Jews. He left there for the countryside close to the wilderness, to a town named Ephraim, and there he remained with the disciples.
11:55 The Passover of the Jews was coming soon. Many people from the countryside went up to Jerusalem ahead of the Passover to make themselves pure.
11:56 They went searching for Jesus. Standing in the temple, they said to each other, "What do you think? Surely he won't come to the festival?"
11:57 The chief priests and the Pharisees had issued commands: anyone who learned where he was must tell them, so they could seize him.
## Mary anoints Jesus at Bethany
12:1 Then, six days ahead of the Passover, Jesus came to Bethany. Lazarus was there, the man Jesus had raised from the dead.
12:2 They held a supper there for him. Martha waited on them, and Lazarus was one of the guests lying at the table with him.
12:3 Mary brought a litra of perfumed oil, real nard and worth a great deal. She anointed Jesus' feet and dried his feet with her hair, and the smell of the oil filled the house.
12:4 But Judas Iscariot, one of his disciples, the one who would soon hand him over, spoke up:
12:5 "This oil could have been sold for three hundred denarii and given to the poor. Why wasn't it?"
12:6 His reason for saying this was not concern for the poor. He was a thief. He held the common purse and would take for himself what people put in it.
12:7 Jesus said, "Let her be, so that she can keep it for the day of my burial.
12:8 For you always have poor people among you, but you do not always have me."
## A plot against Lazarus
12:9 A great crowd of the Jews found out he was there and came. They came not just for Jesus. They also wanted to see Lazarus, the man he had raised from the dead.
12:10 So the chief priests decided to kill Lazarus too,
12:11 since because of him many Jews were drifting away and believing in Jesus.
## The king rides into Jerusalem
12:12 On the following day the big festival crowd heard the news that Jesus was approaching Jerusalem.
12:13 They took fronds from the palm trees and went out to welcome him. They were shouting, "Hosanna! Blessed is the one who comes in the Lord's name, yes, the King of Israel!"
12:14 Jesus found a little donkey and sat on it, as Scripture has it:
12:15 "Daughter of Zion, have no fear. Look, your king is coming, sitting on a donkey's foal."
12:16 None of this made sense to his disciples at first. But once Jesus had been glorified, they remembered: these things were written about him, and these things they had done for him.
12:17 So the crowd kept testifying, the crowd that was with him when he summoned Lazarus from the tomb and raised him from the dead.
12:18 This was also why the crowd came out to welcome him: they had heard that he had done this sign.
12:19 The Pharisees then told each other, "Can't you see you are accomplishing nothing? Look! The world has gone off after him!"
## The hour has come
12:20 Some of the people going up to worship at the festival were Greeks.
12:21 They came up to Philip, from Bethsaida in Galilee, and kept asking him, "Sir, we want to see Jesus."
12:22 Philip came and told Andrew. Then the two of them, Andrew and Philip, came and told Jesus.
12:23 Jesus gave them his answer: "The hour is here when the Son of Man will be glorified.
12:24 Amen, amen, I tell you: if a grain of wheat does not fall into the earth and die, it stays a single grain. But if it dies, it produces a lot of fruit.
12:25 People who love their life are losing it, and people who hate their life in this world will guard it safely into eternal life.
12:26 Anyone who serves me should follow me, and my servant will be wherever I am. The Father will honor anyone who serves me.
12:27 "My soul is shaken now. What am I to say? 'Father, rescue me from this hour'? But this is exactly why I have come to this hour.
12:28 Father, glorify your name!" Then a voice came out of heaven: "I have glorified it already, and again I will glorify it."
12:29 The crowd standing by heard it. Some said there had been thunder. Others said, "An angel spoke to him."
12:30 Jesus responded, "That voice did not come for me. It came for you.
12:31 This world's judgment is happening now. Now this world's ruler will be thrown out.
12:32 As for me, if I am lifted up from the earth, I will draw everyone to myself."
12:33 He spoke this way to hint at what sort of death he was going to die.
12:34 The crowd replied, "We have heard the Law say that the Messiah remains forever. Then how can you say the Son of Man has to be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?"
12:35 Jesus told them, "For a short time still, the light is among you. Keep walking while you have the light, so that the darkness does not take hold of you. Someone walking in the dark has no idea where he is headed.
12:36 Believe in the light while it is yours, so that you will become sons of light." Once he had said this, Jesus went away and was hidden from them.
## Why they did not believe
12:37 He had done so many signs right before their eyes, yet they were not believing in him,
12:38 so that the prophet Isaiah's words would come true, the words he spoke: "Lord, who has believed the report we gave? To whom has the Lord's arm been revealed?"
12:39 This is why they were unable to believe: because, as Isaiah said in another place,
12:40 "He has made their eyes blind and their heart hard, so they would not see with their eyes, or understand with their heart, and turn back for me to heal them."
12:41 Isaiah said this because he had seen his glory, and it was about him that he spoke.
12:42 All the same, many even among the rulers did believe in him. But they did not say so openly because of the Pharisees, so they would not be thrown out of the synagogue.
12:43 They loved the glory people give more than the glory God gives.
## Jesus' last public appeal
12:44 Jesus called out loudly, "When people believe in me, it is not in me that they believe, but in the one who sent me.
12:45 And when someone looks at me, he is looking at the one who sent me.
12:46 I have come as light into the world, so that all who believe in me will not stay in the darkness.
12:47 When someone hears my sayings and does not keep them, I do not judge him. I came to save the world, not to judge it.
12:48 Anyone who sets me aside and refuses my sayings has someone to judge him. The word I have spoken is what will judge him on the last day.
12:49 I did not speak from myself. The Father who sent me, he himself, gave me a command about what I should say and what I should speak.
12:50 And I know that what he commands is eternal life. So the things I speak, I speak exactly as the Father has told me to."
