## A man by the pool at Bethesda
5:1 After these things came a festival of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.
5:2 In Jerusalem, beside the Sheep Gate, there is a pool that is called in Hebrew Bethesda. It has five covered porches.
5:3 Under them lay crowds of the sick: the blind, the lame and those with withered limbs.
5:4 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
5:5 There was a man there who had spent thirty-eight years in his sickness.
5:6 Jesus saw this man lying there and knew he had been like this a long time already. He said to him, "Do you want to become healthy?"
5:7 The sick man answered him, "Sir, when the water gets stirred, I have nobody to put me in the pool. As I am coming, another person gets down there first."
5:8 Jesus told him, "Stand up, lift up your mat and walk."
5:9 At once the man became healthy. He lifted up his mat and began to walk. And that day was a Sabbath.
5:10 So the Jews kept saying to the cured man, "It is the Sabbath! You are not allowed to carry the mat."
5:11 But he answered them, "The one who made me healthy, he is the one who told me, 'Lift up your mat and walk.'"
5:12 They questioned him: "Who is this man that told you, 'Lift it up and walk'?"
5:13 But the healed man did not know who it was, because a crowd filled the place and Jesus had moved away.
5:14 Later, Jesus came upon him in the temple. He said to him, "Look, you have become healthy. Do not keep on sinning, so that nothing worse comes on you."
5:15 The man left and reported to the Jews that the one who had made him healthy was Jesus.
## Father and Son at work
5:16 This is the reason the Jews hounded Jesus: he was doing these things on a Sabbath.
5:17 He answered them, "My Father is at work right up to now, and I am at work too."
5:18 Because of this, the Jews were even more set on killing him. He was breaking the Sabbath, and more than that, he was saying that God was his own Father, and so he was making himself equal to God.
5:19 Jesus responded to them: "Amen, amen, I tell you, the Son is not able to do anything on his own. He does only what he sees the Father doing. Whatever that one does, the Son does the same things in the same way.
5:20 The Father loves the Son, you see, and lets him see everything he himself does. He will show him works greater than these, so that you will be astonished.
5:21 The Father raises up the dead and makes them alive, and the Son likewise makes alive anyone he wishes.
5:22 And the Father does not judge anyone. Instead he has handed all judgment over to the Son,
5:23 so that everyone may give honor to the Son as they give honor to the Father. Anyone who fails to honor the Son fails to honor the Father, the one who sent him.
5:24 "Amen, amen, I tell you, anyone who listens to my word and believes the one who sent me has eternal life. He is not coming into judgment. He has already moved out of death and into life.
5:25 Amen, amen, I tell you, an hour is coming, and it has arrived now, when the dead will hear the Son of God's voice. Those who have heard will live.
5:26 The Father has life within himself, and in the same way he gave the Son to have life within himself.
5:27 He also gave him authority to carry out judgment, since he is the Son of Man.
5:28 Do not be astonished at this. An hour is coming in which everyone lying in the tombs will hear his voice,
5:29 and they will come out. Those who did good will go out to a resurrection that brings life. Those who did wicked things will go out to a resurrection that brings judgment.
5:30 "I am not able to do a single thing on my own. I judge the way I hear, and the verdict I give is right, because what I look for is not what I want but what the one who sent me wants.
## The witnesses for the Son
5:31 "If I give testimony about myself, my testimony does not hold true.
5:32 Someone else is testifying about me, and I know that what he testifies about me is true.
5:33 You yourselves sent people to John, and he has given testimony for the truth.
5:34 I do not take my testimony from a human being. I am saying this so you can be saved.
5:35 That man was the lamp that burns and gives off light, and for a short time you were content to rejoice in his light.
5:36 But the testimony I have is bigger than John's. The Father has given me works to complete. Those same works, which I do, are testifying about me that the Father sent me.
5:37 The one who sent me, the Father, has testified about me himself. Not once have you heard his voice. You have not seen his form.
5:38 And his word is not staying in you, since the one he sent is someone you do not believe.
5:39 You dig into the Scriptures because you think you will have eternal life in them. And they are the very ones testifying about me.
5:40 But you are not willing to come to me so that you can have life.
5:41 "Glory from people is not something I take.
5:42 But I have come to know you. You do not have God's love in yourselves.
5:43 I came in the name of my Father, and you do not welcome me. If another man comes in his own name, him you will welcome.
5:44 How are you able to believe? You take glory from each other, but the glory that comes from the only God you do not seek.
5:45 Do not suppose that I am the one who will bring charges against you before the Father. The one bringing charges against you is Moses, the one you have put your hope in.
5:46 If you believed Moses, you would believe me too, because it was about me that he wrote.
5:47 But if you will not believe the things he wrote, how will you believe the things I speak?"
## Five loaves for five thousand
6:1 After these things Jesus went off across the Sea of Galilee, which is also the Sea of Tiberias.
6:2 A big crowd kept following him, because they had been watching the signs he did for those who were sick.
6:3 Jesus climbed up the mountain and was sitting there with his disciples.
6:4 It was close to the Passover, the festival of the Jews.
6:5 Jesus raised his eyes and saw a big crowd coming his way. He said to Philip, "Where can we buy loaves so these people can have something to eat?"
6:6 He was saying this to put Philip to the test. He knew already what he was about to do.
6:7 Philip answered, "Loaves costing two hundred denarii would not be enough for them, even if each one got only a small piece."
6:8 Then Andrew, one of his disciples and the brother of Simon Peter, said to him,
6:9 "A young boy here has five loaves of barley bread and two small fish. But what good are these among so many people?"
6:10 Jesus said, "Make the people recline." The place was grassy, so they reclined, the men numbering about five thousand.
6:11 Jesus took the loaves, and after giving thanks he passed them around to those reclining there. He did likewise with the fish, giving as much as they wished.
6:12 Once they were filled, he said to his disciples, "Collect the extra broken pieces, so that nothing will be lost."
6:13 They collected them and filled twelve baskets with broken pieces. These came from the five barley loaves and were more than the eaters needed.
6:14 The people saw the sign he had done and kept saying, "This man truly is the Prophet, the one coming into the world!"
6:15 Jesus realized they were going to come and grab him to make him king. So he went back again to the mountain, alone.
## Walking on the sea
6:16 In the evening his disciples went down to the water's edge.
6:17 They climbed into a boat and were heading over the sea toward Capernaum. Darkness had already fallen, and Jesus still had not come to them.
6:18 A great wind was blowing, and the sea was being whipped up.
6:19 After they had rowed about three or three and a half miles, they caught sight of Jesus as he walked on the sea and came close to the boat. They were afraid.
6:20 But he said to them, "I am. Stop being afraid."
6:21 So they wanted to take him into the boat, and at once the boat was at the shore they were making for.
## The crowd crosses over
6:22 On the following day the crowd was standing on the far side of the sea. They had seen that no other small boat had been there except one, and that Jesus did not get into the boat along with his disciples. His disciples had left by themselves.
6:23 Small boats from Tiberias had come, though, close to the spot where they ate the bread once the Lord had given thanks.
6:24 When the crowd saw that Jesus was not there, and his disciples were not either, they too climbed into the small boats and came to Capernaum to look for Jesus.
## The bread of life
6:25 They found him across the sea and asked him, "Rabbi, since when have you been here?"
6:26 Jesus answered them, "Amen, amen, I tell you, it is not seeing signs that makes you look for me. It is that you ate from the loaves and were satisfied.
6:27 Do not work for food that goes bad. Work for the food that stays, leading to eternal life. The Son of Man will give it to you, because God the Father has marked him with his seal."
6:28 So they asked him, "What should we be doing so that we carry out the works of God?"
6:29 Jesus answered, "Here is the work of God: that you believe in the one that he sent."
6:30 So they said to him, "Then what sign will you do for us to see, so we can believe you? What work will you do?
6:31 It was manna that our fathers ate in the desert, just as it is written: 'Bread from heaven he gave them to eat.'"
6:32 Jesus said to them, "Amen, amen, I tell you, Moses has not given you the bread from heaven. No, it is my Father who gives you the bread out of heaven, the true bread.
6:33 God's bread is what comes down out of heaven and gives the world life."
6:34 They said to him, "Sir, give us this bread, and keep on giving it."
6:35 Jesus said to them, "I am the bread of life. Anyone coming to me will not go hungry at all, and the one who believes in me will not thirst again, not ever.
6:36 But I said it to you: you have seen me, and yet you are not believing.
6:37 Everything the Father gives me will come to me, and anyone who comes to me I will never throw out.
6:38 Because I have come down from heaven not to carry out my own will. I came to carry out the will of the one who sent me.
6:39 And this is what the one who sent me wills: that out of all he has given me I should not lose any of it, but should raise it up on the last day.
6:40 My Father wills this: everyone who sees the Son and believes in him is to have eternal life, and I myself will raise that person up on the last day."
6:41 So the Jews started complaining about him, since he had said, "I am the bread that has come down out of heaven."
6:42 They kept saying, "This is Jesus, Joseph's son, isn't it? We know his father and mother. So how can he say now, 'I have come down out of heaven'?"
6:43 Jesus answered them, "Stop complaining to one another.
6:44 Nobody is able to come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him. And I will raise that person up on the last day.
6:45 In the Prophets it is written: 'And all of them will be taught by God.' Anyone who has listened to the Father and learned from him comes to me.
6:46 This does not mean that anyone has seen the Father. Only the one who comes from God has seen the Father.
6:47 Amen, amen, I tell you, anyone who believes has eternal life.
6:48 I am the bread of life.
6:49 Your fathers had the manna to eat in the desert, and they died.
6:50 Here is the bread coming down out of heaven, so that anyone may eat some of it and not die.
6:51 I am the bread that is alive, the bread that came down out of heaven. If someone eats from this bread, he will live forever. And the bread I will give is my flesh, for the sake of the world's life."
6:52 So the Jews began quarreling with one another. They said, "How is this man able to give us his flesh to eat?"
6:53 Jesus said to them, "Amen, amen, I tell you, if you do not eat the Son of Man's flesh and drink his blood, you do not have life in yourselves.
6:54 Anyone who is eating my flesh and drinking my blood has eternal life. I myself will raise him up on the last day.
6:55 For my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink.
6:56 Anyone eating my flesh and drinking my blood stays in me, and I stay in him.
6:57 The Father who is alive sent me, and I am alive because of the Father. In the same way, the one who eats me will also be alive because of me.
6:58 Here is the bread that came down out of heaven. It is not like what the fathers ate, and they died. Anyone who eats this bread will live forever."
6:59 He said all this as he taught in a synagogue at Capernaum.
## Words of eternal life
6:60 Many of his disciples heard this and said, "This word is harsh. Who is able to listen to it?"
6:61 Jesus knew within himself that his disciples were complaining about it. He said to them, "Does this make you stumble?
6:62 So what if you see the Son of Man going up to the place he was at first?
6:63 It is the Spirit that makes alive. The flesh is of no use at all. The sayings I have spoken to you are spirit, and they are life.
6:64 But some of you are not believing." Jesus had known from the start who did not believe and which one would hand him over.
6:65 He went on, "That is the reason I said to you that nobody is able to come to me unless the Father has given it to him."
6:66 From this point on, many of his disciples drew back and went away. They did not walk with him anymore.
6:67 Jesus then asked the Twelve, "Surely you are not wanting to leave as well?"
6:68 Simon Peter answered him, "Lord, who would we go to? You have the sayings of eternal life.
6:69 We for our part have believed, and we have come to know, that you are God's Holy One."
6:70 Jesus answered them, "I chose you, the Twelve, did I not? And one of you is a devil."
6:71 He meant Judas, son of Simon Iscariot. This man, one of the Twelve, was going to hand him over.
