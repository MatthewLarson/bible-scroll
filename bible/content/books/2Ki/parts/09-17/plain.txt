## Jehu Is Anointed King
9:1 Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Belt up your clothes. Take this flask of oil in your hand, and be on your way to Ramoth-gilead.
9:2 When you get there, look for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi. Go in and get him up from among his fellow officers, and bring him into a back room.
9:3 Pour the oil from the flask on his head and say, 'The LORD says this: I anoint you as king over Israel.' Then open the door and run. Do not wait around."
9:4 So the young man, the prophet's young man, went to Ramoth-gilead.
9:5 He came, and there were the commanders of the army, sitting together. He said, "Commander, I have something to say to you." Jehu said, "To which of us all?" He said, "To you, commander."
9:6 Jehu got up and went inside. There the young man poured the oil over Jehu's head and told him, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'I anoint you as king over the LORD's people, over Israel.
9:7 You will strike down the house of Ahab your master, and I will call Jezebel to account for the blood of my servants the prophets and the blood of all the LORD's servants.
9:8 All of Ahab's house is going to perish. I will cut off every male of Ahab's, the bound and the free, in Israel.
9:9 I will make Ahab's house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah.
9:10 The dogs will eat Jezebel on the land of Jezreel, and there will be no one to bury her.'" Then he opened the door and fled.
## "Jehu Is King!"
9:11 Jehu came out to his master's servants. Someone said to him, "Is all well? What did that madman want with you?" He said to them, "You know the man and how he babbles."
9:12 "That's a lie," they said. "Tell us, please." So he said, "He told me this and that. He said, 'The LORD says this: I anoint you as king over Israel.'"
9:13 At once each man grabbed his cloak and laid it down under him on the steps themselves. They sounded the ram's horn and cried, "Jehu has become king!"
9:14 That is how Jehu son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi came to plot against Joram. Joram, with all Israel, had been keeping watch at Ramoth-gilead because of Hazael king of Aram.
9:15 But King Jehoram had come back to Jezreel to be healed. The Arameans had wounded him in his battle against Hazael king of Aram. Jehu said, "If you are of a mind to, let no one escape from the town and go to report in Jezreel."
## Joram and Ahaziah Are Killed
9:16 Jehu mounted his chariot and set off for Jezreel, for Joram lay there. And Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to visit Joram.
9:17 In Jezreel a watchman stood on the tower. He saw Jehu's company as it came, and he called, "I see a company!" Jehoram said, "Get a rider and send him to meet them, and let him ask, 'Is it peace?'"
9:18 The rider on horseback went to meet him. "The king asks, 'Is it peace?'" he said. Jehu said, "Peace? What is that to you? Swing around behind me." The watchman sent word: "The messenger got to them, and he has not come back."
9:19 So he sent a second rider on horseback. He came to them and said, "The king asks, 'Peace?'" Jehu said, "Peace? What is that to you? Swing around behind me."
9:20 The watchman sent word again: "He got to them, and he has not come back. And the driving looks like Jehu son of Nimshi's driving. He drives like a madman."
9:21 Jehoram said, "Harness up!" His chariot was harnessed. Out went Jehoram king of Israel with Ahaziah king of Judah, each man in his chariot. They went out toward Jehu and found him on the land of Naboth the Jezreelite.
9:22 When Jehoram saw Jehu, he said, "Is it peace, Jehu?" He said, "Peace? What peace is there, while your mother Jezebel keeps up her prostitution and her many sorceries?"
9:23 Jehoram swung his reins around and fled. "Betrayal, Ahaziah!" he cried.
9:24 Jehu gripped his bow and shot Jehoram between the arms. The arrow came out through his heart, and he sank down in his chariot.
9:25 Jehu told Bidkar, his officer, "Lift him and throw him into the plot of Naboth the Jezreelite's field. Think back: you and I were riding in pairs behind his father Ahab, and the LORD laid this heavy word on him:
9:26 'As surely as I saw the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons yesterday,' says the LORD, 'I will pay you back on this very plot,' says the LORD. So lift him and throw him on the plot, as the LORD's word said."
9:27 Ahaziah king of Judah saw it and ran, taking the road toward Beth-haggan. Jehu went after him and said, "Him too! Strike him down!" And they struck him in his chariot on the climb to Gur, near Ibleam. He fled to Megiddo and died there.
9:28 His servants carried him by chariot to Jerusalem, and they buried him in his own grave beside his ancestors in the City of David.
9:29 Ahaziah had become king over Judah in the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab.
## The Death of Jezebel
9:30 Jehu came on to Jezreel. Jezebel heard, and she put paint around her eyes and dressed her hair. Then she looked down through the window.
9:31 As Jehu came in through the gate, she said, "Is it peace, Zimri, who killed his master?"
9:32 Jehu lifted his face toward the window. "Who is with me?" he said. "Who?" Two or three court officials peered down at him.
9:33 "Let her drop!" he said, and they let her drop. Her blood spattered against the wall and the horses, and Jehu trampled her.
9:34 Jehu went inside, ate and drank, and then gave an order: "Go and see about this woman under a curse. Bury her. She was a king's daughter, after all."
9:35 They went out to bury her. All they found of her was the skull, the feet and the palms of the hands.
9:36 They came back and reported to him. "It is the LORD's word," he said, "the one he spoke by his servant Elijah the Tishbite: 'On the land of Jezreel the dogs will eat Jezebel's flesh.
9:37 Jezebel's corpse will lie like manure spread on the open field on the land of Jezreel. No one will be able to say, "This is Jezebel."'"
## Ahab's Sons Are Killed
10:1 Ahab had seventy sons living in Samaria. Jehu wrote letters and sent them off to Samaria, addressed to the officials of Jezreel, the elders, and those who were bringing up Ahab's sons. They said:
10:2 "Now then, when this letter comes into your hands: you have your master's sons there with you, and the chariots, the horses, a walled town and the weapons.
10:3 Look over your master's sons for the best and most fit of them. Put him on his father's throne, and fight for your master's house."
10:4 They were very, very afraid. "Look, two kings did not hold out against him," they said. "So how will we hold out?"
10:5 So the man over the palace, the man over the city, the elders and those bringing up the sons sent word to Jehu: "We are your servants. Anything you tell us, we will do. We are not making anyone king. You do what looks good to you."
10:6 Jehu wrote them a second letter. It said, "If you belong to me and listen to my voice, take the heads of the men who are your master's sons, and come to me at Jezreel at this hour tomorrow." The king's sons, seventy men, were in the care of the great men of the city, who were bringing them up.
10:7 As soon as the letter reached them, they seized the king's sons and slaughtered them, seventy men. They laid their heads in baskets and sent them off to Jehu at Jezreel.
10:8 The messenger came with the report: "They have brought the heads of the king's sons." "Make two heaps of them by the gate entrance," Jehu said. "Leave them till morning."
10:9 Morning came, and Jehu went out and stood there and spoke to all the people. "You are in the right," he said. "Look: I am the one who plotted against my master. I killed him. But all these, who struck them down?
10:10 Be sure of this: nothing the LORD has spoken against Ahab's house will drop to the ground. What the LORD said by his servant Elijah, he has done."
10:11 And Jehu struck down all who remained of Ahab's house in Jezreel, with all his leading men, his friends and his priests. He left him not one survivor.
## Ahaziah's Relatives Are Killed
10:12 Then Jehu got up and left for Samaria. He was at the shepherds' Beth-eked, on the road,
10:13 when he came upon the brothers of Ahaziah king of Judah. "Who are you?" he said. "Ahaziah's brothers," they said. "We came down to ask after the king's sons and the queen mother's sons."
10:14 "Seize them alive," he said. They seized them alive and then slaughtered them at the pit of Beth-eked, forty-two men. Not one of them did he leave.
## Jehu Takes Jehonadab Along
10:15 From there he went on and found Jehonadab son of Rechab coming to meet him. Jehu greeted him and said to him, "Is your heart straight with mine, the way my heart is with yours?" Jehonadab said, "It is." "If it is, give me your hand." He gave him his hand, and Jehu lifted him up beside him into the chariot.
10:16 "Come along with me," he said, "and watch how zealous I am for the LORD." They had him ride in Jehu's chariot.
10:17 He came into Samaria and struck down everyone of Ahab's left in Samaria, until he had destroyed his line. It happened by the word the LORD had spoken to Elijah.
## Jehu Wipes Out Baal Worship
10:18 Jehu called all the people together. "Ahab served Baal a little," he told them. "Jehu will serve him a great deal.
10:19 So summon to me all of Baal's prophets, all who serve him and all his priests. Not one must be absent, because I have a great sacrifice to offer to Baal. Whoever is absent will not live." Jehu was playing a trick, so that he could destroy those who served Baal.
10:20 Jehu said, "Set apart a festival assembly for Baal," and they announced it.
10:21 Jehu sent through all Israel, and every servant of Baal came. There was not a man left who did not come. They went into the house of Baal, and the house of Baal was packed, wall to wall.
10:22 He told the man over the robe room, "Bring out clothes for everyone who serves Baal," and the man brought the clothes out to them.
10:23 Jehu went into the house of Baal with Jehonadab son of Rechab, and he told Baal's servants, "Search, and see to it that there are no servants of the LORD here among you. It must be Baal's servants alone."
10:24 They went in to present sacrifices and burnt offerings. Jehu had set eighty men outside and told them, "The men I am bringing into your hands: if any one of them gets away, the man who let him go will pay with his life for that man's life."
10:25 When he had finished offering the burnt offering, Jehu told the runners and the officers, "Go in and strike them down. Do not let a man come out." They struck them down with the edge of the sword. The runners and officers threw them aside and went on into the stronghold of the house of Baal.
10:26 They carried the standing stones out of the house of Baal and burned it.
10:27 They smashed Baal's standing stone, and they smashed Baal's house and turned it into toilets. So it is to this day.
10:28 That is how Jehu wiped Baal out of Israel.
10:29 Only, the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he led Israel into sin, Jehu did not turn away from: the gold calves that stood at Bethel and at Dan.
10:30 The LORD told Jehu, "You did a good thing when you did what is right in my eyes. All that was in my heart for Ahab's house, you have done to it. So sons of yours, down to the fourth generation, will sit on Israel's throne."
10:31 Jehu, though, took no care to walk with his whole heart in the law of the LORD, the God of Israel. He did not leave behind the sins of Jeroboam, by which Jeroboam led Israel into sin.
## Hazael Cuts Israel Down
10:32 In those days the LORD began to cut away pieces of Israel. Hazael struck them down along all Israel's borders,
10:33 from the Jordan toward the sunrise: all the land of Gilead, the Gadites, the Reubenites and the Manassites, from Aroer on the Arnon Valley, both Gilead and Bashan.
10:34 The rest of Jehu's story, all he did and all his strength, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
10:35 Jehu lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him in Samaria. His son Jehoahaz became king in his place.
10:36 Jehu's reign over Israel, in Samaria, lasted twenty-eight years.
## Athaliah Seizes the Throne
11:1 Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son had died. She rose up and wiped out all the royal offspring.
11:2 Jehosheba, though, daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah, took Ahaziah's son Joash. She stole him out from among the king's sons who were being put to death, him and his nurse, into the room where the beds were kept. They hid him from Athaliah, and he was not put to death.
11:3 For six years he was with her in the LORD's house, kept in hiding, while Athaliah reigned over the land.
## Joash Is Crowned
11:4 In the seventh year Jehoiada sent for the officers over the hundreds, of the Carites and of the runners, and brought them to himself in the LORD's house. There he cut a covenant with them and made them swear an oath in the LORD's house, and he let them see the king's son.
11:5 He ordered them, "Here is what you are to do. A third of you, the ones who come in on the Sabbath, will stand guard at the king's house,
11:6 a third at the Gate of Sur, and a third at the gate behind the runners. So you will stand guard over the house in shifts.
11:7 And the two divisions of you, all who go out on the Sabbath, will stand guard over the LORD's house around the king.
11:8 Form a ring around the king, every man with his weapons in hand. Whoever comes into the ranks is to be put to death. Be with the king when he goes out and when he comes in."
11:9 The officers over the hundreds did just what Jehoiada the priest ordered. Each took his men, those coming in on the Sabbath along with those going out on the Sabbath, and they came to Jehoiada the priest.
11:10 The priest handed the officers over the hundreds King David's spear and shields, which were kept in the LORD's house.
11:11 The runners took their places, every man with his weapons in hand, from the south side of the house to the north side, near the altar and the house, around the king.
11:12 Then Jehoiada brought out the king's son and set the crown on him, and the testimony. They made him king and anointed him, and they clapped their hands and cried, "Long live the king!"
11:13 Athaliah heard the sound of the runners and the crowd, and she came to the people in the LORD's house.
11:14 She looked, and there stood the king by the pillar, as kings did. With the king were the officers and the trumpets, and all the people of the land were glad and sounding the trumpets. Athaliah ripped her clothes. "Conspiracy!" she called. "Conspiracy!"
11:15 Jehoiada the priest gave orders to the officers over the hundreds, the men set over the army. "Take her outside, between the ranks," he said, "and kill anyone who comes after her with the sword." The priest had said, "Do not let her be killed in the LORD's house."
11:16 They laid hands on her. She went along the road where the horses come into the king's house, and there she was put to death.
## Jehoiada's Covenant
11:17 Jehoiada made the covenant between the LORD and the king and the people, that they would be a people belonging to the LORD, and between the king and the people.
11:18 Then all the people of the land went into the house of Baal and pulled it down. Its altars and its images they broke thoroughly to pieces, and Mattan, Baal's priest, they killed in front of the altars. The priest set overseers over the LORD's house.
11:19 He took the officers over the hundreds, the Carites, the runners and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the LORD's house. They came by the road of the Runners' Gate to the king's house, and he sat down on the throne of the kings.
11:20 The people of the land, all of them, were glad, and the city had rest. Athaliah they had killed with the sword at the king's house.
11:21 Jehoash was seven years old when he became king.
## Joash Repairs the Temple
12:1 Jehoash became king in the seventh year of Jehu, and he reigned in Jerusalem forty years. His mother was named Zibiah. She came from Beersheba.
12:2 Jehoash did what was right in the LORD's eyes all his days, since Jehoiada the priest had taught him.
12:3 Only, the high places were not removed. The people were still sacrificing and burning offerings at the high places.
12:4 Jehoash told the priests, "All the money of the holy gifts that is brought into the LORD's house: the money that passes from each man in the count, the money for persons at the value set on each, and all the money a man's heart moves him to bring into the LORD's house.
12:5 Let the priests take it in, each one from those he knows, and let them fix the breaks in the house, wherever a break is found."
12:6 Yet in the twenty-third year of King Jehoash, the priests had not fixed the breaks in the house.
12:7 King Jehoash summoned Jehoiada the priest and the priests. "Why are you not fixing the breaks in the house?" he asked them. "Now do not take money from those you know anymore. Give it over for the breaks in the house."
12:8 The priests agreed to take no money from the people, and to have nothing to do with fixing the breaks in the house.
12:9 Then Jehoiada the priest took one chest, drilled a hole in its lid and put it next to the altar, at the right as a person comes into the LORD's house. There the priests who kept the threshold would put all the money that was brought to the LORD's house.
12:10 When they saw there was much money in the chest, the king's scribe came up with the high priest. They bagged up the money found in the LORD's house and counted it.
12:11 The money that had been weighed out they put into the hands of those doing the work, the ones placed over the LORD's house. These paid it out to the woodworkers and the builders working on the LORD's house,
12:12 to the masons and the stone cutters, and for buying wood and cut stone to fix the breaks in the LORD's house, and for everything that was spent on the house to fix it.
12:13 Silver basins, snuffers, bowls, trumpets, anything made of gold or of silver, were not made for the LORD's house out of the money brought into the LORD's house.
12:14 It went to those doing the work, and they fixed the LORD's house with it.
12:15 The men who received the money to pay the workers were never asked to settle accounts, for they worked faithfully.
12:16 Money for guilt offerings and money for sin offerings did not go into the LORD's house. That was the priests'.
## Hazael Turns toward Jerusalem
12:17 Then Hazael king of Aram marched up, made war on Gath and took it. And Hazael turned his face to go up against Jerusalem.
12:18 Jehoash king of Judah gathered up all the holy things his fathers Jehoshaphat, Jehoram and Ahaziah, kings of Judah, had set apart, along with his own holy things and all the gold to be found in the storerooms of the LORD's house and the king's house. He sent it all to Hazael king of Aram, and Hazael went away from Jerusalem.
12:19 The rest of Joash's story, and all he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Judah, is it not?
12:20 His servants rose up and formed a plot, and they struck Joash down at Beth-millo, on the way down to Silla.
12:21 It was Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer, his servants, who struck him down. He died, and they buried him with his ancestors in the City of David. Amaziah his son became king in his place.
## Jehoahaz King of Israel
13:1 In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu became king over Israel in Samaria, for seventeen years.
13:2 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He walked after the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he led Israel into sin, and did not turn aside from them.
13:3 The LORD's anger blazed up against Israel. He gave them into the hand of Hazael king of Aram, and into the hand of Ben-hadad son of Hazael, all through those days.
13:4 Jehoahaz pleaded with the LORD, and the LORD heard him. He saw Israel's misery, how the king of Aram crushed them.
13:5 The LORD gave Israel someone to save them, and they came out from under Aram's hand. The Israelites lived in their tents as in days gone by.
13:6 Even so, they did not leave the sins of Jeroboam's house, by which he led Israel into sin. Israel went on walking in that sin. And the Asherah pole still stood in Samaria.
13:7 For the king of Aram had left Jehoahaz no army but fifty horsemen, ten chariots and ten thousand men on foot. He had destroyed them and made them like dust to be trampled.
13:8 The rest of Jehoahaz's story, all he did and his strength, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
13:9 Jehoahaz lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him in Samaria. His son Joash became king in his place.
## Jehoash King of Israel
13:10 In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz became king over Israel in Samaria, for sixteen years.
13:11 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He did not turn aside from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he led Israel into sin. He walked in them.
13:12 The rest of Joash's story, all he did and his strength, and how he fought Amaziah king of Judah, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
13:13 Joash lay down with his ancestors, and Jeroboam sat on his throne. Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.
## Elisha's Last Word
13:14 Elisha fell sick with the sickness that would kill him. Joash king of Israel came down to him and wept over his face. "My father! My father!" he said. "Israel's chariots and its riders!"
13:15 Elisha told him, "Take a bow and arrows." He took a bow and arrows.
13:16 He told the king of Israel, "Set your hand on the bow." The king set his hand there, and Elisha put his own hands over the king's hands.
13:17 "Open the window to the east," he said, and the king opened it. Elisha said, "Shoot," and he shot. Then Elisha said, "An arrow of the LORD's saving help! An arrow of saving help against Aram! You will strike Aram down at Aphek until you have made an end of them."
13:18 "Pick up the arrows," he said, and he picked them up. He told the king of Israel, "Beat them on the ground." The king beat three times and then stopped.
13:19 The man of God grew angry at him. "Five or six beats, that is what it needed!" he said. "Then you would have struck Aram down to the end. Now you will strike Aram three times."
13:20 So Elisha died, and they buried him. Raiding bands of Moab would come into the land as each year came in.
13:21 One time people were burying a man when they caught sight of a raiding band. They threw the man into Elisha's grave. Down he went, and when he touched Elisha's bones he came to life and stood up on his feet.
## Israel Wins Back Its Towns
13:22 Hazael king of Aram had crushed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.
13:23 But the LORD showed them grace and pity. He turned toward them for the sake of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. He did not want to destroy them, and until now he has not flung them away from his face.
13:24 Hazael king of Aram died, and his son Ben-hadad became king in his place.
13:25 Then Jehoash son of Jehoahaz took the towns back out of the hand of Ben-hadad son of Hazael. These were the towns he had taken in battle from the hand of his father Jehoahaz. Three times Joash struck him down, and he brought Israel's towns back.
## Amaziah King of Judah
14:1 In the second year of Joash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel, Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, became king.
14:2 He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem twenty-nine years. His mother was named Jehoaddan. She came from Jerusalem.
14:3 He did what was right in the LORD's eyes, only not like David his forefather. In all things he did as Joash his father had done.
14:4 Only, the high places were not removed. The people were still sacrificing and burning offerings at the high places.
14:5 Once the kingdom was firm in his hand, he struck down his servants who had struck down the king his father.
14:6 Yet the sons of the killers he did not put to death, as it is written in the book of the law of Moses, where the LORD gave this command: "Fathers must not be put to death because of their children, and children must not be put to death because of their fathers. Each one is to be put to death for his own sin."
14:7 It was he who struck down Edom in the Valley of Salt, ten thousand men. He seized Sela in battle and gave it the name Joktheel, which it has to this day.
14:8 Next Amaziah sent messengers to Jehoash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu, king of Israel, with this message: "Come on, let us look each other in the face."
14:9 Jehoash king of Israel sent back to Amaziah king of Judah: "The thornbush in Lebanon sent this to the cedar in Lebanon: 'Give your daughter to my son for a wife.' But a wild animal of Lebanon passed by and stamped the thornbush flat.
14:10 You struck Edom hard, that is true, and it has gone to your head. Take your honors and sit at home. Why pick a fight with disaster, so that you fall, and Judah falls with you?"
14:11 Amaziah did not listen. So Jehoash king of Israel marched up, and they looked each other in the face, he and Amaziah king of Judah, at Beth-shemesh, which is in Judah.
14:12 Israel beat Judah, and they ran away, every man to his tent.
14:13 Amaziah king of Judah, son of Jehoash son of Ahaziah, was taken prisoner by Jehoash king of Israel at Beth-shemesh. He came to Jerusalem and tore a gap in Jerusalem's wall four hundred cubits wide, from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate.
14:14 He carried off all the gold and the silver, every article to be found in the LORD's house and in the storerooms of the king's house, and people given as pledges, and went back to Samaria.
14:15 The rest of Jehoash's story, what he did and his strength, and how he fought Amaziah king of Judah, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
14:16 Jehoash lay down with his ancestors and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel. His son Jeroboam became king in his place.
14:17 Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, lived fifteen years after the death of Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, king of Israel.
14:18 The rest of Amaziah's story is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Judah, is it not?
14:19 In Jerusalem a plot was formed against him, and he ran to Lachish. They sent after him to Lachish and put him to death there.
14:20 They carried him on horses, and he was buried in Jerusalem beside his ancestors in the City of David.
14:21 The whole people of Judah took Azariah, a boy of sixteen, and made him king to follow his father Amaziah.
14:22 It was he who built up Elath and brought it back to Judah, after the king had lain down with his ancestors.
## Jeroboam II King of Israel
14:23 In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash, king of Israel, became king in Samaria, for forty-one years.
14:24 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He did not turn aside from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he led Israel into sin.
14:25 It was he who won back Israel's border, from Lebo-hamath to the Sea of the Arabah. This was the word of the LORD, the God of Israel, which he had spoken by his servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath-hepher.
14:26 The LORD had seen Israel's misery, very bitter. There was no one, bound or free, and no one to help Israel.
14:27 The LORD had never spoken of erasing Israel's name from under the sky. Instead he rescued them through Jeroboam son of Joash.
14:28 The rest of Jeroboam's story, all he did and his strength, how he made war, and how he brought Damascus and Hamath back to Judah in Israel, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
14:29 Jeroboam lay down with his ancestors, with the kings of Israel. His son Zechariah became king in his place.
## Azariah King of Judah
15:1 In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah, king of Judah, became king.
15:2 He was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem fifty-two years. His mother was named Jecoliah. She came from Jerusalem.
15:3 He did what was right in the LORD's eyes, all that his father Amaziah had done.
15:4 Only, the high places were not removed. The people were still sacrificing and burning offerings at the high places.
15:5 The LORD struck the king, and he had a skin disease until his dying day. He lived in a house set apart, while Jotham, the king's son, was over the house and judged the people of the land.
15:6 The rest of Azariah's story, and all he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Judah, is it not?
15:7 Azariah lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him with his ancestors in the City of David. His son Jotham became king in his place.
## Four Kings of Israel in Fourteen Years
15:8 In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah king of Judah, Zechariah son of Jeroboam became king over Israel in Samaria, for six months.
15:9 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes, as his ancestors had done. He did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he led Israel into sin.
15:10 Shallum son of Jabesh plotted against him. He struck him down in front of the people and killed him, and he reigned in his place.
15:11 The rest of Zechariah's story is there, written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel.
15:12 So came true the word the LORD had spoken to Jehu: "Sons of yours, down to the fourth generation, will sit on Israel's throne." That is how it turned out.
15:13 Shallum son of Jabesh became king in the thirty-ninth year of Uzziah king of Judah, and he reigned a month's time in Samaria.
15:14 Menahem son of Gadi came up from Tirzah. He entered Samaria and struck down Shallum son of Jabesh there, killed him, and reigned in his place.
15:15 The rest of Shallum's story, and the plot he carried out, is there, written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel.
15:16 Then Menahem struck Tiphsah, everyone in it and its lands, starting from Tirzah. He struck it because it did not open up to him, and he cut open all its women who were pregnant.
15:17 In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Menahem son of Gadi became king over Israel, for ten years in Samaria.
15:18 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. All his days he did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he led Israel into sin.
15:19 Pul king of Assyria came into the land. Menahem gave Pul a thousand talents of silver, so that Pul's hands would be with him and would make the kingdom firm in Menahem's hand.
15:20 Menahem drew the silver out of Israel, laying it on all the men of means, fifty shekels of silver for each man, to pay the king of Assyria. Then the king of Assyria went home and did not stay in the land.
15:21 The rest of Menahem's story, and all he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
15:22 Menahem lay down with his ancestors, and his son Pekahiah became king in his place.
15:23 In the fiftieth year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem became king over Israel in Samaria, for two years.
15:24 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he led Israel into sin.
15:25 His officer Pekah son of Remaliah plotted against him and struck him down in Samaria, in the citadel of the king's house, with Argob and with Arieh. Fifty men of the Gileadites were with Pekah. He killed him and reigned in his place.
15:26 The rest of Pekahiah's story, and all he did, is there, written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel.
## Pekah and the First Deportations
15:27 In the fifty-second year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah became king over Israel in Samaria, for twenty years.
15:28 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, by which he led Israel into sin.
15:29 In the days of Pekah king of Israel, Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria came and took Ijon, Abel-beth-maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, Gilead and Galilee, all the land of Naphtali. He carried the people off into exile to Assyria.
15:30 Then Hoshea son of Elah formed a plot against Pekah son of Remaliah. He struck him down and killed him, and he reigned in his place, in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah.
15:31 The rest of Pekah's story, and all he did, is there, written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel.
## Jotham King of Judah
15:32 In the second year of Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jotham son of Uzziah, king of Judah, became king.
15:33 He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years. His mother was named Jerusha daughter of Zadok.
15:34 He did what was right in the LORD's eyes. He did all that his father Uzziah had done.
15:35 Only, the high places were not removed. The people were still sacrificing and burning offerings at the high places. It was he who built the upper gate of the LORD's house.
15:36 The rest of Jotham's story, what he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Judah, is it not?
15:37 It was in those days that the LORD started sending Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah into Judah.
15:38 Jotham lay down with his ancestors and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David his forefather. His son Ahaz became king in his place.
## Ahaz King of Judah
16:1 In the seventeenth year of Pekah son of Remaliah, Ahaz son of Jotham, king of Judah, became king.
16:2 Ahaz was twenty years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years. He did not do what was right in the eyes of the LORD his God, the way David his forefather did.
16:3 He walked in the road of the kings of Israel. He even made his son pass through the fire, like the abhorrent things done by the nations whom the LORD drove out from in front of the Israelites.
16:4 He sacrificed and burned offerings at the high places, on the hills and under every leafy tree.
16:5 That was when Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel came up to Jerusalem for battle. They hemmed Ahaz in, but they were not able to win the fight.
16:6 It was then that Rezin king of Aram won Elath back for Aram and cleared the Judeans out of it. Edomites came to Elath, and they live there to this day.
16:7 Ahaz sent messengers to Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria with this word: "I am your servant and your son. Come up! Save me from the grip of the king of Aram and the grip of the king of Israel, who have risen against me."
16:8 Ahaz gathered the silver and the gold that could be found in the LORD's house and in the storerooms of the king's house, and he sent the king of Assyria a bribe.
16:9 The king of Assyria did as he asked. He went up to Damascus and seized it, took its people away captive to Kir, and killed Rezin.
## Ahaz's Altar from Damascus
16:10 King Ahaz went to meet Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria in Damascus. There he saw the altar that stood in Damascus, and King Ahaz sent Uriah the priest a likeness of it and its pattern, with all the work on it.
16:11 Uriah the priest built the altar exactly as King Ahaz had sent from Damascus. That is what Uriah the priest did, before King Ahaz got back from Damascus.
16:12 The king arrived from Damascus, and the king saw the altar. The king went up to the altar and offered on it.
16:13 He sent up the smoke of his burnt offering and his grain offering, poured out his drink offering, and splashed the blood of his own peace offerings on the altar.
16:14 The bronze altar that was before the LORD he moved from the front of the house, from its place between Ahaz's altar and the LORD's house, and he set it at the side of Ahaz's altar, on the north.
16:15 King Ahaz ordered Uriah the priest: "On the big altar, burn the morning burnt offering and the evening grain offering, the king's burnt offering and his grain offering, and the burnt offering of all the people of the land with their grain offering and their drink offerings. Every drop of the burnt offering's blood and every drop of a sacrifice's blood you are to splash on it. As for the bronze altar, that will be for me to inquire by."
16:16 Uriah the priest did all that King Ahaz ordered.
16:17 King Ahaz cut away the frames of the stands and took the basin off each of them. He lowered the Sea from the bronze oxen that held it up and put it on a stone pavement.
16:18 The Sabbath covering that had been built in the house, and the king's outer entrance, he turned away from the LORD's house because of the king of Assyria.
16:19 The rest of Ahaz's story, what he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Judah, is it not?
16:20 Ahaz lay down with his ancestors and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. His son Hezekiah became king in his place.
## Hoshea, the Last King of Israel
17:1 In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king in Samaria over Israel, for nine years.
17:2 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes, only not like the kings of Israel who were before him.
17:3 Shalmaneser king of Assyria marched up against him, and Hoshea became his servant and brought him tribute.
17:4 Then the king of Assyria uncovered a plot by Hoshea. Hoshea had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, and he had stopped bringing up his yearly tribute to the king of Assyria. So the king of Assyria shut him up and bound him in a prison house.
## Samaria Falls
17:5 The king of Assyria came up through the whole land. He went up to Samaria and held it under siege three years.
17:6 In Hoshea's ninth year the king of Assyria took Samaria. He carried Israel away into exile to Assyria and made them live in Halah, by the Habor River of Gozan, and in the towns of Media.
## Why Israel Fell
17:7 It came about because the Israelites sinned against the LORD their God. He had brought them up out of the land of Egypt, out from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, yet they feared other gods.
17:8 They walked by the rules of the nations whom the LORD drove out from in front of the Israelites, and by the ones the kings of Israel made.
17:9 The Israelites did things in secret against the LORD their God, things that were not right. In every one of their towns they built high places for themselves, from the lookout's tower to the walled city.
17:10 They set up standing stones and Asherah poles for themselves on every high hill and under every leafy tree.
17:11 There, at every high place, they made offerings smoke, like the nations the LORD had carried off ahead of them. They did evil things to stir up the LORD's anger.
17:12 They served the idols, of which the LORD had said to them, "You are not to do this thing."
17:13 Yet the LORD had given warning to Israel and to Judah by every prophet and every visionary: "Turn back from your evil roads and keep my commands and my rules, the whole law that I commanded your ancestors and sent you by my servants the prophets."
17:14 They did not listen. They hardened their necks, like their ancestors' necks, the ones who did not trust the LORD their God.
17:15 They threw aside his rules and his covenant, the one he cut with their ancestors, and his decrees, which he had given as a warning to them. They went after vapor and became vapor. They went after the nations around them, the ones the LORD had commanded them not to copy.
17:16 They left all the commands of the LORD their God. They made themselves a cast image, two calves, and they made an Asherah pole. They bowed down to the whole army of the sky, and they served Baal.
17:17 They made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire. They practiced divination and read omens, and they sold themselves to do evil in the LORD's eyes and anger him.
17:18 The LORD's anger at Israel was fierce, and he put them away out of his sight. None were left except the tribe of Judah by itself.
17:19 Even Judah did not keep the commands of the LORD their God. They walked by the rules that Israel had made.
17:20 The LORD threw aside the whole seed of Israel. He humbled them and gave them into the hands of raiders, until he had cast them away from before him.
17:21 For he had torn Israel from David's house, and they had made Jeroboam son of Nebat king. And Jeroboam pushed Israel away from following the LORD and led them into a great sin.
17:22 The Israelites walked in all the sins Jeroboam did and never turned aside from them,
17:23 until the LORD put Israel away out of his sight, as he had spoken by all his servants the prophets. Israel went into exile from its own soil to Assyria, and is there to this day.
## New Peoples in Samaria
17:24 Then the king of Assyria brought in people from Babylon, from Cuthah, from Avva, from Hamath and Sepharvaim. He settled them in Samaria's towns where the Israelites had lived. They took possession of Samaria and made their homes in its towns.
17:25 At the start of their living there they did not fear the LORD, so the LORD sent the lions in among them, and the lions kept killing some of them.
17:26 Word came to the king of Assyria: "The nations you took captive and settled in Samaria's towns do not know the ways of the god of the land. He has sent the lions in among them, and look, they are killing them, since they do not know the ways of the god of the land."
17:27 The king of Assyria gave the order: "Send back there one of the priests you took captive from there. Let him go and live there, and let him teach them the ways of the god of the land."
17:28 One of the priests they had taken captive from Samaria came and settled in Bethel, and he went on teaching them how to fear the LORD.
17:29 Still, every nation went on making gods of its own. They put them in the houses at the high places that the people of Samaria had made, nation by nation, in the towns where they lived.
17:30 The people of Babylon made Succoth-benoth, the people of Cuth made Nergal, and the people of Hamath made Ashima.
17:31 The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak. The Sepharvites burned up their children in the fire for Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.
17:32 They also feared the LORD. They made priests for the high places from among their own number, and these offered for them in the houses at the high places.
17:33 The LORD they feared, and their own gods they served, in the way of the nations from which they had been taken captive.
## "To This Day"
17:34 To this day they keep doing things the old way. They do not fear the LORD. They do not act by their rules and their rulings, or by the law and the command the LORD gave to the sons of Jacob, the one he named Israel.
17:35 The LORD cut a covenant with them, and gave them this command: "You are not to fear other gods. You must not bow down to them or serve them or sacrifice to them.
17:36 Only the LORD, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great strength and with an arm stretched out: him you are to fear, to him you are to bow down, and to him you are to sacrifice.
17:37 The rules, the rulings, the law and the command that he wrote down for you, be careful to do them all your days. Do not fear other gods.
17:38 The covenant I cut with you, do not forget it. Do not fear other gods.
17:39 Only the LORD your God are you to fear, and he will save you out of the hand of all your enemies."
17:40 But they did not listen. They went on doing things their old way.
17:41 These nations feared the LORD and served their carved images too. Their sons and their sons' sons are still doing what their fathers did, to this day.
