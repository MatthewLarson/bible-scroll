## From Adam to Noah's sons
1:1 Adam, Seth, Enosh,
1:2 Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,
1:3 Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,
1:4 Noah, Shem, Ham and Japheth.
## The sons of Japheth and Ham
1:5 Japheth's sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech and Tiras.
1:6 Gomer's sons were Ashkenaz, Diphath and Togarmah.
1:7 Javan's sons were Elishah and Tarshish, the Kittim and the Rodanim.
1:8 Ham's sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put and Canaan.
1:9 Cush's sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah and Sabteca. Raamah's sons were Sheba and Dedan.
1:10 Cush fathered Nimrod. He was the first to become a hero on the earth.
1:11 Mizraim fathered the Ludim, the Anamim, the Lehabim, the Naphtuhim,
1:12 the Pathrusim and the Casluhim, from whom the Philistines came, and the Caphtorim.
1:13 Canaan fathered Sidon, his firstborn, and Heth,
1:14 and the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites,
1:15 the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites,
1:16 the Arvadites, the Zemarites and the Hamathites.
## The sons of Shem
1:17 Shem's sons were Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud and Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether and Meshech.
1:18 Arpachshad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber.
1:19 To Eber two sons were born: the name of one was Peleg, for in his days the earth was split up; and the name of his brother was Joktan.
1:20 Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,
1:21 Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,
1:22 Ebal, Abimael, Sheba,
1:23 Ophir, Havilah and Jobab. All of these were Joktan's sons.
## From Shem to Abraham
1:24 Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah,
1:25 Eber, Peleg, Reu,
1:26 Serug, Nahor, Terah,
1:27 Abram, who is Abraham.
## Abraham's sons
1:28 Abraham's sons were Isaac and Ishmael.
1:29 This is their family history. Ishmael's firstborn was Nebaioth; then came Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,
1:30 Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema,
1:31 Jetur, Naphish and Kedemah. These were Ishmael's sons.
1:32 Keturah, Abraham's concubine, gave birth to Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak and Shuah. Jokshan's sons were Sheba and Dedan.
1:33 The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida and Eldaah. All these were the descendants of Keturah.
1:34 Abraham fathered Isaac. Isaac's sons were Esau and Israel.
## Esau and Seir
1:35 Esau's sons were Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam and Korah.
1:36 Eliphaz's sons were Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna and Amalek.
1:37 Reuel's sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah and Mizzah.
1:38 Seir's sons were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer and Dishan.
1:39 Lotan's sons were Hori and Homam, and Lotan's sister was Timna.
1:40 Shobal's sons were Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi and Onam. Zibeon's sons were Aiah and Anah.
1:41 Anah's son was Dishon. Dishon's sons were Hamran, Eshban, Ithran and Cheran.
1:42 Ezer's sons were Bilhan, Zaavan and Jaakan. Dishon's sons were Uz and Aran.
## The kings and chiefs of Edom
1:43 These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the people of Israel: Bela son of Beor. The name of his city was Dinhabah.
1:44 Bela died, and Jobab son of Zerah, from Bozrah, reigned in his place.
1:45 Jobab died, and Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.
1:46 Husham died, and Hadad son of Bedad, who struck down Midian in the country of Moab, reigned in his place. The name of his city was Avith.
1:47 Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place.
1:48 Samlah died, and Shaul from Rehoboth on the River reigned in his place.
1:49 Shaul died, and Baal-hanan son of Achbor reigned in his place.
1:50 Baal-hanan died, and Hadad reigned in his place. The name of his city was Pai, and his wife's name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, daughter of Me-zahab.
1:51 Then Hadad died. And the chiefs of Edom were Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jetheth,
1:52 Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon,
1:53 Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,
1:54 Chief Magdiel, Chief Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom.
## The twelve sons of Israel
2:1 These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi and Judah, Issachar and Zebulun,
2:2 Dan, Joseph and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad and Asher.
## Judah's sons
2:3 Judah's sons were Er, Onan and Shelah. These three were born to him by Shua's daughter, the Canaanite woman. Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil in the LORD's eyes, and the LORD put him to death.
2:4 Tamar, his daughter-in-law, bore him Perez and Zerah. All of Judah's sons numbered five.
2:5 The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul.
2:6 The sons of Zerah were Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol and Dara. There were five of them.
2:7 Carmi's son was Achar, Israel's troubler, who acted unfaithfully in regard to what was under the ban.
2:8 The son of Ethan was Azariah.
## From Hezron to David
2:9 The sons born to Hezron were Jerahmeel, Ram and Chelubai.
2:10 Ram fathered Amminadab, and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, leader of the people of Judah.
2:11 Nahshon fathered Salma, and Salma fathered Boaz.
2:12 Boaz fathered Obed, and Obed fathered Jesse.
2:13 Jesse fathered his firstborn, Eliab; the second was Abinadab, the third Shimea,
2:14 the fourth Nethanel, the fifth Raddai,
2:15 the sixth Ozem, and the seventh David.
2:16 Zeruiah and Abigail were their sisters. Zeruiah had three sons: Abshai, Joab and Asahel.
2:17 Abigail gave birth to Amasa, and Amasa's father was Jether the Ishmaelite.
## Caleb's family
2:18 Caleb son of Hezron had children by Azubah, his wife, and by Jerioth. Her sons were Jesher, Shobab and Ardon.
2:19 Azubah died, and Caleb took Ephrath as his wife. She bore him Hur.
2:20 Hur fathered Uri, and Uri fathered Bezalel.
2:21 Afterward Hezron lay with the daughter of Machir, Gilead's father. He was sixty years old when he married her. She bore him Segub.
2:22 Segub fathered Jair. Jair owned twenty-three towns in the land of Gilead.
2:23 Then Geshur and Aram seized Havvoth-jair from them, with Kenath and its villages, sixty towns. These all descended from Machir, the father of Gilead.
2:24 After Hezron died in Caleb-ephrathah, Hezron's wife Abijah had his son Ashhur, the father of Tekoa.
## Jerahmeel's family
2:25 The sons of Jerahmeel, Hezron's firstborn, were Ram the firstborn, then Bunah, Oren, Ozem and Ahijah.
2:26 Jerahmeel had one more wife. Her name was Atarah, and she was Onam's mother.
2:27 The sons of Ram, Jerahmeel's firstborn, were Maaz, Jamin and Eker.
2:28 The sons of Onam were Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai were Nadab and Abishur.
2:29 Abishur's wife was named Abihail. Her sons by him were Ahban and Molid.
2:30 The sons of Nadab were Seled and Appaim. Seled died with no sons.
2:31 Appaim's son was Ishi. Ishi's son was Sheshan. Sheshan's son was Ahlai.
2:32 The sons of Jada, Shammai's brother, were Jether and Jonathan. Jether died with no sons.
2:33 The sons of Jonathan were Peleth and Zaza. These were Jerahmeel's descendants.
2:34 Sheshan had daughters but no sons. He did have a slave, an Egyptian named Jarha.
2:35 Sheshan gave Jarha, his slave, his daughter as a wife, and she had Attai by him.
2:36 Attai fathered Nathan, and Nathan fathered Zabad.
2:37 Zabad fathered Ephlal, and Ephlal fathered Obed.
2:38 Obed fathered Jehu, and Jehu fathered Azariah.
2:39 Azariah fathered Helez, and Helez fathered Eleasah.
2:40 Eleasah fathered Sismai, and Sismai fathered Shallum.
2:41 Shallum fathered Jekamiah, and Jekamiah fathered Elishama.
## More of Caleb's family
2:42 The sons of Caleb, Jerahmeel's brother: Mesha his firstborn, who was the father of Ziph; and the sons of Mareshah, the father of Hebron.
2:43 The sons of Hebron were Korah, Tappuah, Rekem and Shema.
2:44 Shema fathered Raham, the father of Jorkeam, and Rekem fathered Shammai.
2:45 Shammai's son was Maon, and Maon was the father of Beth-zur.
2:46 Ephah, Caleb's concubine, gave birth to Haran, Moza and Gazez. Haran fathered Gazez.
2:47 The sons of Jahdai were Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah and Shaaph.
2:48 Maacah, Caleb's concubine, gave birth to Sheber and Tirhanah.
2:49 And she gave birth to Shaaph, father of Madmannah, and to Sheva, father of Machbenah and father of Gibea. Caleb's daughter was Achsah.
2:50 These were Caleb's descendants. Hur was the firstborn of Ephrathah, and Hur's son was Shobal, father of Kiriath-jearim,
2:51 Salma, father of Bethlehem, and Hareph, father of Beth-gader.
2:52 There were sons of Shobal, father of Kiriath-jearim: Haroeh, half of the Menuhoth.
2:53 The clans of Kiriath-jearim were the Ithrites, the Puthites, the Shumathites and the Mishraites. From these went out the Zorathites and the Eshtaolites.
2:54 The sons of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, half of the Manahathites, the Zorites,
2:55 and the clans of scribes living at Jabez: the Tirathites, the Shimeathites and the Sucathites. They are the Kenites who come from Hammath, father of Rechab's family.
## David's sons
3:1 These were David's sons born to him in Hebron. His firstborn was Amnon, whose mother was Ahinoam the Jezreelite. The second was Daniel, whose mother was Abigail the Carmelite.
3:2 The third was Absalom, the son of Maacah, the daughter of King Talmai of Geshur. The fourth was Adonijah, Haggith's son.
3:3 The fifth was Shephatiah, whose mother was Abital, and the sixth was Ithream, born to Eglah his wife.
3:4 In Hebron six were born to him. He reigned there seven years and six months, and in Jerusalem thirty-three years.
3:5 In Jerusalem these were born to him: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan and Solomon. These four were from Bath-shua daughter of Ammiel.
3:6 And Ibhar, Elishama, Eliphalet,
3:7 Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,
3:8 Elishama, Eliada and Eliphelet, nine.
3:9 These were all David's sons, apart from the concubines' sons. Tamar was their sister.
## The kings of Judah
3:10 Solomon's son was Rehoboam; Abijah his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,
3:11 Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,
3:12 Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son,
3:13 Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,
3:14 Amon his son, Josiah his son.
3:15 The sons of Josiah: the firstborn, Johanan; the second, Jehoiakim; the third, Zedekiah; the fourth, Shallum.
3:16 The sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son.
## David's line after the exile
3:17 The sons of Jeconiah, who was taken captive: Shealtiel his son,
3:18 Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama and Nedabiah.
3:19 Pedaiah's sons: Zerubbabel and Shimei. Zerubbabel's sons: Meshullam and Hananiah, with Shelomith their sister,
3:20 also Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah and Jushab-hesed, five.
3:21 The sons of Hananiah: Pelatiah and Jeshaiah; the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shecaniah.
3:22 The sons of Shecaniah: Shemaiah. The sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah and Shaphat, six.
3:23 The sons of Neariah: Elioenai, Hizkiah and Azrikam, three.
3:24 The sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah and Anani, seven.
## More clans of Judah
4:1 The sons of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur and Shobal.
4:2 Reaiah son of Shobal fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These are the Zorathite clans.
4:3 These belonged to the father of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma and Idbash. Their sister's name was Hazzelelponi.
4:4 Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer the father of Hushah. These were Hur's sons. Hur was Ephrathah's firstborn and the father of Bethlehem.
4:5 Ashhur, father of Tekoa, had two wives, named Helah and Naarah.
4:6 Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni and Haahashtari. These were Naarah's sons.
4:7 The sons of Helah were Zereth, Zohar and Ethnan.
4:8 Koz fathered Anub and Zobebah, and the clans of Aharhel son of Harum.
## Jabez
4:9 Jabez had more honor than his brothers. His mother gave him the name Jabez. "I gave birth in pain," she said.
4:10 Jabez called on the God of Israel. "If you will truly bless me and widen my borders," he said, "if your hand is with me, and if you keep harm away so that it does not hurt me..." And God brought about what he asked.
## Caleb's line and the craftsmen of Judah
4:11 Chelub, Shuhah's brother, fathered Mehir. Mehir was Eshton's father.
4:12 Eshton fathered Beth-rapha, Paseah and Tehinnah the father of Ir-nahash. These were Recah's men.
4:13 The sons of Kenaz: Othniel and Seraiah. The sons of Othniel: Hathath.
4:14 Meonothai fathered Ophrah. Seraiah fathered Joab, the father of Ge-harashim, for they were craftsmen.
4:15 The sons of Caleb son of Jephunneh: Iru, Elah and Naam. The sons of Elah: Kenaz.
4:16 The sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria and Asarel.
4:17 The sons of Ezrah: Jether, Mered, Epher and Jalon. She conceived Miriam, Shammai and Ishbah the father of Eshtemoa.
4:18 His Judean wife bore Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Soco and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. These were sons of Bithiah, the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered had taken.
4:19 Hodiah's wife, Naham's sister, had sons: the father of Keilah the Garmite, and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.
4:20 The sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-hanan and Tilon. The sons of Ishi: Zoheth and Ben-zoheth.
4:21 The sons of Shelah son of Judah: Er, father of Lecah; Laadah, father of Mareshah; the clans of the linen workers' house at Beth-ashbea;
4:22 Jokim, the men of Cozeba, and Joash and Saraph, who ruled in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem. These records are old.
4:23 They were the potters, and they lived at Netaim and Gederah. They stayed there with the king, doing his work.
## Simeon
4:24 The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah and Shaul;
4:25 Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.
4:26 The sons of Mishma: Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, Shimei his son.
4:27 Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters. His brothers, though, had few sons, and their clan as a whole never grew to match Judah's numbers.
4:28 They lived in Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar-shual,
4:29 Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad,
4:30 Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag,
4:31 Beth-marcaboth, Hazar-susim, Beth-biri and Shaaraim. These were their towns up to the time David reigned.
4:32 Their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Tochen and Ashan, five towns,
4:33 and all the villages surrounding those towns, out to Baal. Those were where they settled, and they had their own family register.
4:34 Meshobab, Jamlech, Joshah son of Amaziah,
4:35 Joel, Jehu son of Joshibiah, son of Seraiah, son of Asiel,
4:36 Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah,
4:37 and Ziza son of Shiphi, son of Allon, son of Jedaiah, son of Shimri, son of Shemaiah.
4:38 The men listed here by name were leaders of their clans. Their fathers' houses broke out into great numbers.
4:39 They went toward the entrance of Gedor, out to the eastern edge of the valley, looking for pasture for their flocks.
4:40 They found pasture, rich and good, and the land was wide on every side, quiet and at peace. The people who had lived there before were from Ham.
4:41 In the time of Hezekiah king of Judah, these men whose names are written came and struck down the tents of those people and the Meunites they found there. They put them under the ban, as it is to this day, and settled in their place, since their flocks had pasture there.
4:42 Some of the Simeonites went to the hill country of Seir, five hundred men, with Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi, at their head.
4:43 They struck down the survivors who had escaped from Amalek, and they have stayed there to this day.
## Reuben
5:1 The sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn. Reuben was the firstborn, but he made his father's bed unclean. So his birthright went to the sons of Israel's son Joseph, and Reuben is not enrolled in the family register as the firstborn.
5:2 Judah grew strong among his brothers, and a ruler came from him; yet the birthright was Joseph's.
5:3 The sons of Reuben, Israel's firstborn: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron and Carmi.
5:4 The sons of Joel: Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,
5:5 Micah his son, Reaiah his son, Baal his son,
5:6 Beerah his son, whom Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria carried off into exile. He was a leader of the Reubenites.
5:7 His brothers by their clans, as enrolled by their family lines: Jeiel the chief, and Zechariah,
5:8 and Bela son of Azaz, son of Shema, son of Joel. He lived in Aroer and as far as Nebo and Baal-meon.
5:9 Toward the east he lived as far as the edge of the wilderness that stretches from the river Euphrates. For their herds had grown large in the land of Gilead.
5:10 While Saul was king, they made war on the Hagrites, and the Hagrites fell into their power. They settled in the Hagrites' tents across the whole east side of Gilead.
## Gad
5:11 The people of Gad lived across from them, in the land of Bashan out to Salecah:
5:12 Joel the chief, Shapham the second, then Janai and Shaphat in Bashan.
5:13 Their brothers by their fathers' houses: Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia and Eber, seven.
5:14 These were the sons of Abihail son of Huri, son of Jaroah, son of Gilead, son of Michael, son of Jeshishai, son of Jahdo, son of Buz.
5:15 Ahi son of Abdiel, son of Guni, was head of their fathers' house.
5:16 They lived in Gilead, in Bashan and its villages, and throughout Sharon's pasturelands, to the farthest edges.
5:17 All of them were entered in the family register when Jotham was king of Judah and Jeroboam was king of Israel.
## The war with the Hagrites
5:18 Among the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh were strong fighting men, men bearing shields and swords, men who bent the bow, men taught how to fight: 44,760 ready to go out with the army.
5:19 They made war on the Hagrites, on Jetur, Naphish and Nodab.
5:20 They received help against them: the Hagrites and everyone with them were given into their power. It was to God they had shouted in the battle, and he let himself be moved by their pleas, since they had put their trust in him.
5:21 They took the Hagrites' herds as plunder: 50,000 of their camels, 250,000 sheep and 2,000 donkeys, and 100,000 people.
5:22 For many were killed, since the war came from God. Then they lived there instead of them until the exile.
## The half-tribe of Manasseh
5:23 The half-tribe of Manasseh settled in the land from Bashan up to Baal-hermon, Senir and Mount Hermon, and there were many of them.
5:24 The heads of their fathers' houses were Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah and Jahdiel: strong warriors, men with a name, heads of their fathers' houses.
5:25 But they acted unfaithfully toward the God of their ancestors. They went after the gods of the land's peoples like a prostitute, the peoples God had wiped out in front of them.
5:26 Then the God of Israel roused the spirit of Pul king of Assyria, and the spirit of Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria. He carried the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh into exile and brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara and the river of Gozan, and they are there to this day.
## The high priests
6:1 The sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.
6:2 The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.
6:3 The children of Amram: Aaron, Moses and Miriam. The sons of Aaron: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.
6:4 Eleazar fathered Phinehas, and Phinehas fathered Abishua.
6:5 Abishua fathered Bukki, and Bukki fathered Uzzi.
6:6 Uzzi fathered Zerahiah, and Zerahiah fathered Meraioth.
6:7 Meraioth fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub.
6:8 Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Ahimaaz.
6:9 Ahimaaz fathered Azariah, and Azariah fathered Johanan.
6:10 Johanan fathered Azariah. It was this Azariah who was priest in the house Solomon built in Jerusalem.
6:11 Azariah fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub.
6:12 Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Shallum.
6:13 Shallum fathered Hilkiah, and Hilkiah fathered Azariah.
6:14 Azariah fathered Seraiah, and Seraiah fathered Jehozadak.
6:15 Jehozadak went too, when the LORD drove Judah and Jerusalem into exile through Nebuchadnezzar.

6:18 The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.
6:19 The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the Levite clans, by their fathers.
6:20 Of Gershom: Libni his son, Jahath his son, Zimmah his son,
6:21 Joah his son, Iddo his son, Zerah his son, Jeatherai his son.

6:24 Tahath his son, Uriel his son, Uzziah his son, Shaul his son.
6:25 The sons of Elkanah: Amasai and Ahimoth,
6:26 Elkanah. The sons of Elkanah: Zophai his son, Nahath his son,
6:27 Eliab his son, Jeroham his son, Elkanah his son.
6:28 The sons of Samuel: the firstborn, Joel, and the second, Abijah.
6:29 The sons of Merari: Mahli, Libni his son, Shimei his son, Uzzah his son,

## David's singers
6:31 David appointed these men to lead the singing in the LORD's house, once the ark had a place to rest.
6:32 They served with song in front of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, until Solomon built the LORD's house in Jerusalem. They took their places for their work as the rule for them required.
6:33 These are those who stood on duty, they and their sons. From the Kohathites: Heman the singer, son of Joel, son of Samuel,
6:34 son of Elkanah, son of Jeroham, son of Eliel, son of Toah,
6:35 son of Zuph, son of Elkanah, son of Mahath, son of Amasai,
6:36 son of Elkanah, son of Joel, son of Azariah, son of Zephaniah,
6:37 son of Tahath, son of Assir, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah,
6:38 son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, son of Israel.
6:39 His brother Asaph stood at his right hand: Asaph son of Berechiah, son of Shimea,
6:40 son of Michael, son of Baaseiah, son of Malchijah,
6:41 son of Ethni, son of Zerah, son of Adaiah,
6:42 son of Ethan, son of Zimmah, son of Shimei,
6:43 son of Jahath, son of Gershom, son of Levi.
6:44 The sons of Merari, their brothers, were on the left: Ethan son of Kishi, son of Abdi, son of Malluch,
6:45 son of Hashabiah, son of Amaziah, son of Hilkiah,
6:46 son of Amzi, son of Bani, son of Shemer,
6:47 son of Mahli, son of Mushi, son of Merari, son of Levi.
## Aaron's sons and their work
6:48 The other Levites, their brothers, were given over to all the work of the tabernacle, the house of God.
6:49 Aaron and his sons, though, made the offerings go up in smoke on the altar of burnt offering and on the altar of incense. They did all the work of the most holy place and made atonement for Israel, in keeping with all that God's servant Moses had commanded.
6:50 These were the sons of Aaron: Eleazar his son, Phinehas his son, Abishua his son,
6:51 Bukki his son, Uzzi his son, Zerahiah his son,
6:52 Meraioth his son, Amariah his son, Ahitub his son,
6:53 Zadok his son, Ahimaaz his son.
## The towns of the Levites
6:54 These were their places to live, by their encampments within their borders. To the sons of Aaron, of the clan of the Kohathites, for the lot was theirs,
6:55 they gave Hebron in the land of Judah, with the pastureland around it.
6:56 But the fields of the town and its villages they gave to Caleb son of Jephunneh.

## The gatekeepers
9:17 The gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon and Ahiman, and their brother Shallum was the chief.
9:18 To this day he is at the King's Gate, facing east. These were the gatekeepers for the camps of the Levites.
9:19 Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his brothers from his father's house, the Korahites, oversaw the work of the service, as guards of the tent's thresholds. Their fathers had overseen the LORD's camp, as guards of the way in.
9:20 Phinehas son of Eleazar had been the officer over them in earlier times. The LORD was with him.
9:21 Zechariah son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper at the doorway of the tent of meeting.
9:22 All who were picked to guard the thresholds as gatekeepers numbered 212. They were enrolled in the family register in their villages. David and Samuel the seer had put them in their posts because they could be trusted.
9:23 They and their sons kept watch over the gates of the LORD's house, the house of the tent, by shifts.
9:24 The gatekeepers stood toward the four winds: east, west, north and south.
9:25 Their brothers in their villages had to come in for seven days at a time, from time to time, to be with them.
9:26 The four chief gatekeepers held a post of trust. They were Levites, and they had charge of the rooms and the storerooms of the house of God.
9:27 They spent the night around the house of God, because they had the duty of guarding it, and they were in charge of the key, morning after morning.
## Temple tasks

9:30 Some of the sons of the priests mixed the blend of spices.
9:31 Mattithiah, a Levite and the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite, had the trusted task of making the griddle cakes.
9:32 Some of their Kohathite brothers were in charge of the bread that is set out in rows, to have it ready Sabbath by Sabbath.
9:33 These were the singers, heads of fathers' houses of the Levites, living in the rooms and free from other duties, because they were at their work day and night.
9:34 These were heads of fathers' houses of the Levites by their family lines, chiefs. They lived in Jerusalem.
