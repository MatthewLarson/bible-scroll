## Saul dies on Mount Gilboa
10:1 The Philistines fought Israel. Israel's men ran from the Philistines, and their dead lay fallen on Mount Gilboa.
10:2 The Philistines closed in on Saul and his sons. They cut down Saul's sons Jonathan, Abinadab and Malchishua.
10:3 The battle bore down heavily on Saul. The archers with their bows found him, and he was in anguish from the archers.
10:4 "Pull out your sword and stab me with it," Saul told his armor-bearer. "If you don't, these uncircumcised men will come and have their cruel fun with me." But his armor-bearer was too afraid. He would not. So Saul took hold of the sword and fell on it.
10:5 His armor-bearer saw that Saul had died. Then he also threw himself on the sword, and died.
10:6 That is how Saul died, along with his three sons. His whole house died, all together.
10:7 All Israel's men who were in the valley saw that they had fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead. They walked out of their towns and ran away, and the Philistines came in and settled there.
10:8 On the day after, the Philistines went out to plunder the bodies of the slain. They came upon Saul and his sons lying where they fell on Mount Gilboa.
10:9 They stripped him, and they carried off his head and his armor. Then they sent word all around the land of the Philistines, to bring the good news to their idols and to the people.
10:10 His armor they laid in the house of their gods, and his skull they fastened up in the house of Dagon.
10:11 All Jabesh-gilead heard everything the Philistines had done to Saul.
10:12 Every man of courage got up. They carried off Saul's body and the bodies of his sons and brought them to Jabesh. They buried their bones beneath the oak in Jabesh. Then they went without food for seven days.
## Why Saul died
10:13 So Saul died for his unfaithfulness, the unfaithful act he had done against the LORD. He had not kept the LORD's word, and he had even gone to a ghost of the dead to ask for answers.
10:14 He did not ask the LORD. The LORD killed him, and handed the kingship over to David, Jesse's son.
## David king of all Israel
11:1 All Israel gathered to David at Hebron. "Look," they said, "we are your own bone and flesh.
11:2 Even before, even in Saul's time as king, you were the one leading Israel out and bringing them home. And the LORD your God told you, 'You are the one who will shepherd my people Israel. You will be ruler over my people Israel.'"
11:3 So the elders of Israel, all of them, came to the king at Hebron. There in Hebron, in the LORD's presence, David made a covenant with them, and they anointed him as king over Israel. This was what the LORD had said through Samuel.
## Jerusalem taken
11:4 Then David and all Israel marched on Jerusalem, that is, Jebus. The Jebusites who lived in that land were there.
11:5 The people living in Jebus told David, "You will not come in here." Yet David took Zion's fortress. It is the City of David.
11:6 "The first man to strike down a Jebusite will be head and commander," David had said. Joab son of Zeruiah was the first one up, and he became head.
11:7 David made his home in the fortress. That is why people called it the City of David.
11:8 He built the city all around it, from the Millo to the ring around it, and Joab brought the rest of the city back to life.
11:9 David kept on growing greater, and the LORD of Armies was with him.
## David's warriors
11:10 These are the chiefs of David's warriors. Together with all Israel they stood firmly with him in his kingship, to make him king, as the LORD had said about Israel.
11:11 This is the roll of David's warriors. Jashobeam son of Hachmoni was chief of the officers. He swung his spear over three hundred, slain at one time.
11:12 Next in rank was Eleazar son of Dodo the Ahohite. He was one of the Three warriors.
11:13 He was with David at Pas-dammim, where the Philistines had massed for battle. A field there was planted thick with barley, and the troops fled before the Philistines.
11:14 But they planted themselves in the middle of that field and held it, and they cut the Philistines down. The LORD saved them, and it was a great rescue.
11:15 Three of the Thirty headmen came down to the rock where David was, at the cave of Adullam. The Philistine army had made camp in the Valley of Rephaim.
11:16 At that point David was in the fortress, while a Philistine post held Bethlehem.
11:17 David felt a longing. "Who will bring me water to drink from the well of Bethlehem, the one at the gate?" he said.
11:18 The Three forced their way through the Philistine camp. They drew water from Bethlehem's well at the gate, lifted it, and brought it to David. David was not willing to drink it. Instead he poured it out to the LORD.
11:19 "My God, I could never do this!" he said. "Am I to drink the blood of these men, with their lives at stake? With their lives at stake they brought it." So he would not drink it. Those are the deeds the three warriors did.
11:20 Abshai, Joab's brother, was head of the Thirty. He brandished his spear over three hundred slain, and he was famous alongside the Three.
11:21 Of the Thirty he was honored twice over, and he became their captain. But he never came up to the Three.
11:22 Benaiah son of Jehoiada was a fighter's son, a man of great deeds from Kabzeel. He struck down the two lion-men of Moab. He also went down and killed the lion inside the pit on the day of the snow.
11:23 He struck down an Egyptian too, a man of size, five cubits high. The Egyptian had a spear in his hand like a weaver's beam, but Benaiah went down to him with a stick. He wrenched the spear out of the Egyptian's grip and killed him with it, his own spear.
11:24 Those are the things Benaiah son of Jehoiada did. He had a name among the Three warriors.
11:25 Among the Thirty he had more honor than the rest, but he never came up to the Three. David made him head of his guard.
11:26 The warriors of the army were Asahel, Joab's brother; Elhanan son of Dodo, from Bethlehem;
11:27 Shammoth the Harorite; Helez the Pelonite;
11:28 Ira son of Ikkesh, from Tekoa; Abiezer from Anathoth;
11:29 Sibbecai the Hushathite; Ilai the Ahohite;
11:30 Maharai from Netophah; Heled son of Baanah, from Netophah;
11:31 Ithai son of Ribai, from Gibeah of the Benjaminites; Benaiah from Pirathon;
11:32 Hurai from the ravines of Gaash; Abiel the Arbathite;
11:33 Azmaveth the Baharumite; Eliahba the Shaalbonite;
11:34 the sons of Hashem the Gizonite; Jonathan son of Shagee the Hararite;
11:35 Ahiam son of Sachar the Hararite; Eliphal son of Ur;
11:36 Hepher the Mecherathite; Ahijah the Pelonite;
11:37 Hezro the Carmelite; Naarai son of Ezbai;
11:38 Joel brother of Nathan; Mibhar son of Hagri;
11:39 Zelek the Ammonite; Naharai the Berothite, who carried the armor of Joab son of Zeruiah;
11:40 Ira the Ithrite; Gareb the Ithrite;
11:41 Uriah the Hittite; Zabad son of Ahlai;
11:42 Adina son of Shiza the Reubenite, a leader of the Reubenites, who had thirty men with him;
11:43 Hanan son of Maacah; Joshaphat the Mithnite;
11:44 Uzzia the Ashterathite; Shama and Jeiel, sons of Hotham the Aroerite;

## Men who joined David at Ziklag
12:1 These are the men who came to David at Ziklag while he was still shut out by Saul son of Kish. They were some of the warriors who gave help in battle.
12:2 They carried bows, and they could throw stones and shoot arrows with the right hand or the left. They came from Saul's own kin in Benjamin.
12:3 Their chief was Ahiezer, then Joash, sons of Shemaah from Gibeah; Jeziel and Pelet, sons of Azmaveth; Beracah; Jehu from Anathoth;
12:4 Ishmaiah from Gibeon, a warrior among the Thirty and over the Thirty; Jeremiah, Jahaziel, Johanan, and Jozabad from Gederah;
12:5 Eluzai, Jerimoth, Bealiah, Shemariah, and Shephatiah the Haruphite;
12:6 Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer and Jashobeam, the Korahites;
12:7 and Joelah and Zebadiah, sons of Jeroham, from Gedor.
12:8 Some of the Gadites broke away and joined David at the fortress in the wilderness. They were mighty fighting men, ready for war, armed with shield and spear. They had lion faces, and they could run like gazelles across the mountains.
12:9 Ezer was first, Obadiah second, Eliab third,
12:10 Mishmannah fourth, Jeremiah fifth,
12:11 Attai sixth, Eliel seventh,
12:12 Johanan eighth, Elzabad ninth,
12:13 Jeremiah tenth, Machbannai eleventh.
12:14 These Gadites were heads of the army. Even the smallest of them was worth a hundred; the great ones were worth a thousand.
12:15 They were the ones who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when the river was over all its banks. They drove off all the people of the valleys, to the east and to the west.
12:16 Some of the men of Benjamin and Judah also came to David at the fortress.
12:17 David went out in front of them and spoke up. "If you are here in peace, to help me," he told them, "then my heart will be one with yours. But if it is to hand me over to my enemies, though no violence is in my hands, may the God of our ancestors look and pass judgment."
12:18 Then the Spirit put Amasai, chief of the Thirty, on like clothing, and he said: "Yours, David! / With you, son of Jesse! / Peace, peace to you, / and peace to whoever helps you, / for your God has helped you." So David took them in and put them among the leaders of his raiding band.
12:19 Some men of Manasseh also went over to David when he came with the Philistines for the battle with Saul. But David's men did not help the Philistines, because their rulers talked it over and sent him away. "He will go back over to his master Saul," they said, "and our heads will pay for it."
12:20 When he went to Ziklag, these men of Manasseh went over to him: Adnah, Jozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu and Zillethai, chiefs of thousands in Manasseh.
12:21 They stood with David against the raiding band. Every one of them was a strong warrior, and they became commanders in the army.
12:22 Every day more men came to help David, until his camp was huge, like the camp of God.
## The army that made David king
12:23 Here are the counts of the chiefs of the armed men who came to Hebron to David. They came to hand Saul's kingdom over to him, as the LORD had ordered:
12:24 Men of Judah who bore shield and spear: six thousand eight hundred, armed for war.
12:25 From Simeon, strong warriors for war: seven thousand one hundred.
12:26 From the Levites: four thousand six hundred.
12:27 Jehoiada was leader of Aaron's family, and with him were three thousand seven hundred.
12:28 Zadok, a young man and a strong warrior, came with twenty-two commanders from his father's house.
12:29 From Benjamin, Saul's kinsmen: three thousand. Until then most of them had kept faith with Saul's house.
12:30 From Ephraim: twenty thousand eight hundred strong warriors, men with names in their fathers' houses.
12:31 From the half-tribe of Manasseh: eighteen thousand, who were named one by one to come and put David on the throne.
12:32 From Issachar: men who knew how to read the times, and who knew what Israel ought to do. They had two hundred chiefs, and all their kinsmen followed their orders.
12:33 From Zebulun: fifty thousand men ready to go out to war, drawn up for battle with weapons of every kind, ready to keep rank, with no divided heart.
12:34 From Naphtali: a thousand commanders, and with them thirty-seven thousand men with shield and spear.
12:35 From the Danites, prepared for battle: twenty-eight thousand six hundred.
12:36 From Asher, ready to go out to war and to line up for battle: forty thousand.
12:37 From beyond the Jordan, from the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, with every kind of weapon of war: one hundred twenty thousand.
12:38 All these fighting men, ready to take their places in the battle line, came to Hebron with a whole heart, to make David king over all Israel. All the rest of Israel too was of one heart to make David king.
12:39 They were there with David for three days, eating and drinking, because their kinsmen had made provisions for them.
12:40 Their neighbors too, as far away as Issachar, Zebulun and Naphtali, brought food on donkeys, camels, mules and oxen: flour, fig cakes, raisin cakes, wine, oil, cattle and sheep, in great amounts. Israel was full of joy.
## The ark sets out from Kiriath-jearim
13:1 David talked it over with the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, with each one of the leaders.
13:2 Then David said to the whole assembly of Israel, "If you think it good, and if it comes from the LORD our God, let us send word out everywhere to our kinsmen who are left in all the regions of Israel, and with them to the priests and the Levites in their towns with pasturelands, so that they may gather to us.
13:3 Let us bring our God's ark around to us again. In the days of Saul we did not seek it."
13:4 The whole assembly said to do it, because all the people thought the plan was right.
13:5 So David called together all Israel, from Shihor in Egypt to Lebo-hamath, to bring God's ark from Kiriath-jearim.
13:6 David went up with all Israel to Baalah, to Kiriath-jearim in Judah. They went to bring God's ark up from there, the ark of the LORD who sits on the cherubim as his throne, where the Name is called on.
13:7 They loaded God's ark onto a new cart at the house of Abinadab. Uzzah and Ahio were leading the cart.
13:8 David and all Israel made merry before God with all their strength and with songs, with lyres, harps, hand drums, cymbals and trumpets.
## Uzzah
13:9 When they came to Chidon's threshing floor, Uzzah reached his hand out to grab hold of the ark, because the oxen stumbled.
13:10 The LORD's anger burned against Uzzah, and he struck him for putting his hand out to the ark. And there he died, before God.
13:11 It made David angry that the LORD had burst out in a breakout against Uzzah. To this day the place is called Perez-uzzah.
13:12 That day David feared God. "How could I ever have God's ark brought to me?" he asked.
13:13 So David did not bring the ark to his own place in the City of David. He had it turned aside to the house of Obed-edom the Gittite.
13:14 God's ark stayed three months in Obed-edom's house, with his family. The LORD brought blessing on Obed-edom's family and on all that was his.
## David's house and family
14:1 Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers to David. He also sent cedar wood, masons and woodworkers, to build him a house.
14:2 Then David understood. The LORD had set him firmly in place as Israel's king, and his kingdom was raised very high for the good of his people Israel.
14:3 In Jerusalem David took still more wives, and David fathered still more sons and daughters.
14:4 The children born to him in Jerusalem were named Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,
14:5 Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet,
14:6 Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,
14:7 Elishama, Beeliada and Eliphelet.
## Victories over the Philistines
14:8 The Philistines heard that David was now the anointed king of all Israel. Their whole force came up hunting for David. David learned of it and went out to face them.
14:9 The Philistines came in and made raids across the Valley of Rephaim.
14:10 David asked God, "Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will you put them in my power?" "Go up," the LORD told him. "I will put them in your power."
14:11 So they went up to Baal-perazim, and David beat them there. "God has burst through my enemies by my hand," David said, "the way water bursts through." That is why the place was named Baal-perazim.
14:12 There they left their gods behind. David gave the word, and they were burned in the fire.
14:13 The Philistines raided the valley a second time.
14:14 David asked God again, and God told him, "Don't go up after them. Swing around away from them, and come at them from in front of the baka trees.
14:15 The moment you hear a noise of marching in the treetops of the baka trees, go out into battle, for God will have gone out ahead of you to strike down the Philistine camp."
14:16 David did exactly as God had told him, and they cut down the Philistine camp from Gibeon all the way to Gezer.
14:17 David's name went out into all the lands, and the LORD laid the dread of him on every nation.
## The Levites prepare to carry the ark
15:1 In the City of David, David made himself houses. He prepared a place for God's ark and set up a tent for it.
15:2 "The ark of God is not to be carried by anyone but the Levites," David said then. "The LORD chose them to carry the LORD's ark and to serve him for all time."
15:3 David called all Israel together at Jerusalem to bring the LORD's ark up to its place, the one he had made ready for it.
15:4 David gathered Aaron's descendants and the Levites:
15:5 of Kohath's descendants, Uriel their leader, with a hundred twenty kinsmen;
15:6 of Merari's descendants, Asaiah their leader, with two hundred twenty kinsmen;
15:7 of Gershom's descendants, Joel their leader, with a hundred thirty kinsmen;
15:8 of Elizaphan's descendants, Shemaiah their leader, with two hundred kinsmen;
15:9 of Hebron's descendants, Eliel their leader, with eighty kinsmen;
15:10 of Uzziel's descendants, Amminadab their leader, with a hundred twelve kinsmen.
15:11 David called for Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and for the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel and Amminadab.
15:12 "You are the family heads of the Levites," he said to them. "Make yourselves holy, you and your kinsmen, and bring the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel, up to the place I have made ready for it.
15:13 The first time, you were not the ones carrying it, and so the LORD our God burst out against us, because we did not seek him the way the ruling requires."
15:14 So the priests and the Levites made themselves holy to carry up the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel.
15:15 The sons of the Levites carried God's ark the way Moses had commanded, as the LORD had said: on their shoulders, with the poles resting on them.
## Singers and players
15:16 David told the Levite leaders to appoint some of their kinsmen as singers. They were to play musical instruments, lyres, harps and cymbals, sounding them loudly and raising a sound of joy.
15:17 So the Levites appointed Heman son of Joel; from his kinsmen, Asaph son of Berechiah; and from their kinsmen the descendants of Merari, Ethan son of Kushaiah.
15:18 With them were their kinsmen of the second rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, and Obed-edom and Jeiel the gatekeepers.
15:19 The singers Heman, Asaph and Ethan had bronze cymbals, to play them loudly.
15:20 Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah and Benaiah played lyres set to alamoth.
15:21 Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel and Azaziah played harps set to the sheminith, to lead.
15:22 Kenaniah, the Levites' leader for the carrying, directed the carrying, because he knew how.
15:23 Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the ark.
15:24 The priests Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah and Eliezer were to blow the trumpets in front of God's ark. Obed-edom and Jehiah were gatekeepers for the ark.
## The ark comes into the City of David
15:25 So it was David, with Israel's elders and the commanders of thousands, who went with joy to bring the ark of the LORD's covenant up from Obed-edom's house.
15:26 God helped the Levites who carried the ark of the LORD's covenant, and so they sacrificed seven young bulls and seven rams.
15:27 David was wrapped in a robe of fine linen, and so were all the Levites who carried the ark, the singers, and Kenaniah, the leader of the carrying, with the singers. David also had a linen ephod on.
15:28 All Israel was bringing the ark of the LORD's covenant up, with shouts, with the sound of the ram's horn, with trumpets and cymbals, playing loudly on lyres and harps.
15:29 The ark of the LORD's covenant was coming into the City of David when Saul's daughter Michal looked down from the window. She saw King David dancing and making merry, and in her heart she felt contempt for him.
## The ark in its tent
16:1 They carried God's ark in and put it within the tent that David had set up for it. Then they presented burnt offerings and peace offerings in God's presence.
16:2 When David was done offering up the burnt offering and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD.
16:3 To every person of Israel, man and woman alike, he gave each a loaf of bread, a date cake and a raisin cake.
16:4 Before the LORD's ark he set some of the Levites to serve, to call him to mind, to give thanks, and to praise the LORD, the God of Israel.
16:5 Asaph was at the head, with Zechariah next after him, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom and Jeiel, with lyres and harps. Asaph sounded the cymbals,
16:6 and the priests Benaiah and Jahaziel blew the trumpets at all times before the ark of God's covenant.
## David's song of thanks
16:7 It was on that day that David first gave the task of thanking the LORD to Asaph and his kinsmen:
16:8 Give thanks to the LORD; call out his name. / Let the peoples know his deeds.
16:9 Sing to him, make music for him; / speak of every wonder he has done.
16:10 Make your boast in his holy name; / let those who seek the LORD be glad at heart.
16:11 Seek the LORD and his strength; / look for his face at all times.
16:12 Call to mind the wonders he worked, / his marvels, and the rulings from his mouth,
16:13 you seed of Israel, his servant, / you sons of Jacob, whom he chose.
16:14 He, the LORD, is our God; / his rulings reach the whole earth.
16:15 Keep his covenant in mind for all time, / the word he gave as a command to a thousand generations,
16:16 what he agreed with Abraham, / his sworn word to Isaac.
16:17 He set it up for Jacob as a rule, / for Israel as a covenant for all time,
16:18 saying, "I will give you the land of Canaan, / the share of your inheritance."
16:19 When you were men who could be counted, / very few, foreigners living there,
16:20 they went about from one nation to the next, / from a kingdom to a different people.
16:21 He did not let anyone wrong them; / he scolded kings because of them:
16:22 "Hands off my anointed ones! / Don't hurt my prophets."
16:23 Sing to the LORD, all the earth; / bring the good news of his saving help each day.
16:24 Recount his glory to the nations, / his wonders to every people.
16:25 For the LORD is great, and praised to the highest; / he is more to be feared than all the gods.
16:26 Every god of the peoples is a nothing-god, / but the LORD made the heavens.
16:27 Before him go splendor and majesty; / his place is full of strength and joy.
16:28 Give the LORD, you clans of the peoples; / give the LORD glory and strength.
16:29 Give the LORD the glory of his name. / Carry a gift and come into his presence. / Bow low to the LORD in holy splendor.
16:30 All the earth, shake in his presence! / Surely the world is set firm; it will not totter.
16:31 May the heavens be glad, may the earth celebrate, / and may the nations be told, "The LORD is King!"
16:32 Let the sea thunder with everything that fills it; / let the open country cheer, and all that is in it.
16:33 Then the forest trees will shout for joy before the LORD, / because he is coming to judge the earth.
16:34 Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; / his loyal love is forever.
16:35 And say: "Rescue us, God who rescues us! / Gather us and pull us out from the nations, / to thank your holy name / and to boast in praising you."
16:36 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, / from the ages past to the ages to come. And the whole people said, "Amen!" and gave praise to the LORD.
## Worship at Jerusalem and at Gibeon
16:37 So David left Asaph and his kinsmen there before the ark of the LORD's covenant, to serve before the ark at all times, as each day's work required.
16:38 He also left Obed-edom and their sixty-eight kinsmen. Obed-edom son of Jedithun and Hosah were gatekeepers.
16:39 He left Zadok the priest and his kinsmen the priests before the LORD's tabernacle at the high place that was in Gibeon,
16:40 to offer up burnt offerings to the LORD at all times on the altar of burnt offering, morning and evening, and to carry out everything that is written in the LORD's law, which he commanded Israel.
16:41 Heman and Jeduthun were with them, and the others who had been picked and named one by one to thank the LORD, "for his loyal love is forever."
16:42 Heman and Jeduthun had with them trumpets and cymbals for those who played loudly, and instruments for the songs of God. The sons of Jeduthun were at the gate.
16:43 Then all the people went off, each one home, and David turned back to bless his own household.
## God's promise to David
17:1 David had made his home in his house. "Look," David said to Nathan the prophet, "I live in a house made of cedar, and the ark of the LORD's covenant is under curtains."
17:2 Nathan answered David, "Whatever is in your heart, do it. God is with you."
17:3 That night God's word came to Nathan. It said,
17:4 "Go and say to David, my servant, 'The LORD says this: It is not you who will build the house for me to live in.
17:5 From the day I brought Israel up until today, I have never lived in a house. I have moved from tent to tent, and from one tabernacle to another.
17:6 All the time I walked about among all Israel, did I ever say a single word to any of Israel's judges, the ones I ordered to shepherd my people? Did I ever say, "Why have you not built me a cedar house?"'
17:7 Now then, tell my servant David, 'The LORD of Armies says: It was I who took you off the grazing land, from walking behind the flock, to make you ruler over my people Israel.
17:8 Everywhere you went, I was with you. I wiped out all your enemies ahead of you. I will make you a name as famous as those of the great men on the earth.
17:9 I will pick out a place for my people Israel and plant them there. They will live in it and not be shaken anymore. Evil men will not go on wearing them down as they did at first,
17:10 ever since the days when I put judges in charge of my people Israel. I will bring all your enemies low. I also tell you this: it is a house that the LORD will build for you.
17:11 When your life has run its full course and you must go to join your ancestors, I will raise up your offspring to come after you, one who will come from among your sons. I will make his kingdom stand firm.
17:12 He is the one to build me a house, and I will make his throne secure for all time.
17:13 I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to me. I will not take my loyal love away from him, the way I took it from the one who was before you.
17:14 I will make him stand in my house and in my kingdom for all time. His throne will stand firm forever.'"
17:15 Nathan passed on to David all these words, the whole of this vision.
## David's prayer
17:16 King David went in and sat down before the LORD. "LORD God, who am I?" he said. "And what is my house, that you should bring me to where I am now?
17:17 Yet even this seemed too little to you, God. You have spoken about your servant's house far into the future, and you have seen me as one would see a man of high rank, LORD God.
17:18 What else could David add to you about the honor done to your servant? You know your servant.
17:19 LORD, for your servant's sake and because your heart wanted it, you have done this whole great thing, to make all these great things known.
17:20 LORD, no one is like you. No God exists except you. Everything we have ever heard with our ears says so.
17:21 Is there any other nation on earth like your people Israel? God went to redeem them as a people for himself, to win yourself a name by great and awesome deeds, driving out nations ahead of your people, whom you redeemed from Egypt.
17:22 You gave your people Israel to yourself as a people for all time, and you, LORD, became their God.
17:23 So now, LORD, let the word you spoke about your servant and his house be made sure forever. Do just what you said.
17:24 Let it be made sure, and may your name be great for all time, so that people say, 'The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, is God to Israel!' And your servant David's house will stand secure in your presence.
17:25 For you, my God, have opened your servant's ear: you will build him a house. That is why your servant has found the nerve to pray before you.
17:26 Now, LORD, you and no other are God, and you have spoken this good word to your servant.
17:27 Now you have been willing to bless your servant's house so that it lasts forever in your presence. You, LORD, are the one who has blessed it, and it stays blessed forever."
## David's victories
18:1 Some time later David beat the Philistines and humbled them. He took Gath and its villages out of Philistine hands.
18:2 He beat Moab too. Moab became David's servants, bringing him tribute.
18:3 David also beat Hadadezer king of Zobah, near Hamath, as he was on his way to set up his victory marker by the Euphrates River.
18:4 David captured from him a thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen and twenty thousand foot soldiers. He crippled all the chariot horses by cutting their leg tendons, except that he spared enough of them for a hundred chariots.
18:5 Aram of Damascus came to help Hadadezer, king of Zobah. David cut down twenty-two thousand men of Aram.
18:6 David stationed troops in Aram of Damascus, and Aram became David's servants, bringing him tribute. Everywhere David went, the LORD made him win.
18:7 The gold shields carried by Hadadezer's officers David took away and brought to Jerusalem.
18:8 David also carried off huge amounts of bronze from Tibhath and Cun, towns that belonged to Hadadezer. It was what Solomon used to make the bronze Sea, the pillars and the bronze utensils.
18:9 Tou, the king of Hamath, heard that David had beaten all the forces of Hadadezer king of Zobah.
18:10 He sent his son Hadoram to King David to wish him well and to bless him for fighting Hadadezer and beating him. Hadadezer had been Tou's enemy in many wars. He also sent all kinds of things made of gold, silver and bronze.
18:11 These King David set apart as holy for the LORD as well, together with the silver and gold he had carried off from all the nations: from Edom and Moab, from the Ammonites, the Philistines and Amalek.
18:12 Abshai son of Zeruiah struck Edom in the Valley of Salt: eighteen thousand men.
18:13 He stationed troops in Edom, and every Edomite became David's servant. Everywhere David went, the LORD made him win.
## David's officials
18:14 David ruled as king over all Israel. He kept practicing justice and righteousness toward all his people.
18:15 Joab son of Zeruiah commanded the army. Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud kept the records.
18:16 The priests were Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar. The royal scribe was Shavsha.
18:17 Benaiah son of Jehoiada was in charge of the Kerethites and Pelethites. And David's sons were first in rank beside the king.
## Hanun shames David's envoys
19:1 After this, Nahash, the Ammonites' king, died, and his son took his place as king.
19:2 "I will keep loyal love with Hanun son of Nahash," David said, "because his father kept loyal love with me." So David sent messengers to comfort him over his father's death. David's servants arrived in Ammonite country, coming to Hanun to comfort him.
19:3 The Ammonite leaders said to Hanun, "Is David really honoring your father in your eyes by sending you men to comfort you? No, his servants have come to you to look over, tear down and spy out the land."
19:4 Hanun grabbed David's servants. He shaved them and cut their robes off halfway up, as high as their buttocks. Then he sent them off.
19:5 David was told about the men, and he sent men to meet them, since they were badly shamed. "Stay in Jericho," the king said. "When your beards grow back, come home."
## War with Ammon and Aram
19:6 The Ammonites could see that they had made themselves stink to David. So Hanun and the Ammonites sent off a thousand talents of silver, to hire themselves chariots and horsemen out of Aram-naharaim, Aram-maacah and Zobah.
19:7 For themselves they hired thirty-two thousand chariots, and also the king of Maacah and his troops, who came and camped facing Medeba. The Ammonites gathered from their towns and came out to fight.
19:8 David heard of it and sent out Joab with the whole army, the fighting men.
19:9 The Ammonites marched out and formed their battle lines at the city entrance. The kings who had come stood apart by themselves out in the open fields.
19:10 Joab saw that he would have to fight on two fronts, ahead of him and behind him. So out of all Israel's picked men he took some, and he set them in battle lines facing Aram.
19:11 He put the rest of the troops under his brother Abshai, and they formed lines facing the Ammonites.
19:12 "If Aram gets the upper hand on me," Joab said, "then you be my rescue. And if the Ammonites get the upper hand on you, I will save you.
19:13 Be strong. Let's stand firm and be strong for the sake of our people and our God's cities. The LORD will do whatever he sees is good."
19:14 Joab and his troops closed in to fight Aram, and the Arameans ran from him.
19:15 The Ammonites saw Aram running, and they too ran, away from Joab's brother Abshai, and went inside the city. Then Joab went to Jerusalem.
19:16 Aram saw that Israel had beaten them. They sent messengers and called out the Arameans from the far side of the River. Shophach, commander of Hadadezer's army, was at their head.
19:17 The news reached David. He called up all Israel, went across the Jordan, came to them and drew up his lines against them. David drew up his lines facing Aram for battle, and they fought him.
19:18 But Aram ran before Israel. David killed seven thousand of Aram's chariot drivers and forty thousand foot soldiers. And Shophach, the army's commander, he put to death.
19:19 Hadadezer's servants saw that Israel had beaten them. They came to terms with David and served him. After that Aram was not willing to come to the Ammonites' rescue again.
## Rabbah taken
20:1 When the year came around again, at the season when kings march out, Joab led out the army's fighting force. He brought ruin on the Ammonites' land and went and put Rabbah under siege. But David was staying in Jerusalem. Joab struck Rabbah and tore it down.
20:2 David took their king's crown off his head. He found that its weight was a talent of gold, and it held a precious stone. It went onto David's head. He also carried away a very large amount of loot from the city.
20:3 He brought out the people who lived there and cut them with a saw, with iron picks and with saws. That is how David treated all the Ammonite towns. Then David and the whole army went back to Jerusalem.
## Giants of the Philistines
20:4 Some time later there was a battle with the Philistines at Gezer. Then Sibbecai the Hushathite struck down Sippai, one of the offspring of the Rephaim, and the Philistines were brought low.
20:5 Once more there was fighting with the Philistines. Elhanan son of Jair struck down Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite, whose spear shaft was like a weaver's beam.
20:6 There was war again at Gath. A man of huge size was there. His digits came six and six, twenty-four of them. He too had been born to the Rapha.
20:7 He jeered at Israel, but Jonathan son of Shimea, David's brother, struck him down.
20:8 These had been born to the Rapha in Gath. David and his servants brought them down.
