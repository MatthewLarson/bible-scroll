## David counts the people
21:1 Satan stood up against Israel and stirred David up to count Israel.
21:2 David told Joab and the leaders of the people, "Go, count Israel from Beersheba as far as Dan. Bring the count to me. I want to know their number."
21:3 Joab said, "The LORD make his people a hundred times more than they are! My lord the king, do they not all belong to my lord as his servants? Why is my lord seeking this? Why should it become guilt for Israel?"
21:4 But what the king said won out over Joab. Joab set out. He made his way through all Israel and arrived back in Jerusalem.
21:5 Joab handed David the count of the people enrolled. All Israel had a million and a hundred thousand men who could draw a sword. Judah had four hundred and seventy thousand men who could draw a sword.
21:6 Levi and Benjamin he did not enroll with the rest, because Joab found the king's word abhorrent.
21:7 In God's eyes this thing was wrong, and he struck Israel.
21:8 "This thing I have done is a great sin," David said to God. "Now please take away your servant's guilt. I have been a great fool."
## The choice and the plague
21:9 The LORD spoke to Gad, David's visionary:
21:10 "Go and speak to David. 'The LORD says this: I am holding out three things to you. Pick one, and I will do it to you.'"
21:11 Gad came to David. "The LORD says this," he told him. "Accept for yourself:
21:12 three years of hunger; or three months of being carried off before your foes, with your enemies' sword catching up with you; or three days of the LORD's sword, pestilence in the land, with the LORD's angel bringing ruin through all the territory of Israel. Now see what word I should carry back to the one who sent me."
21:13 "I am in a terrible bind," David told Gad. "Please, let me fall into the LORD's hand, for his compassion runs very deep. Into a human hand, let me not fall."
21:14 So the LORD put pestilence on Israel, and of Israel seventy thousand men fell.
21:15 God sent an angel to Jerusalem to ruin it. But as he was ruining it, the LORD saw, and he regretted the harm. He said to the angel who was destroying, "Enough! Now let your hand drop." The LORD's angel was standing close to the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.
21:16 David looked up and saw the LORD's angel standing between earth and sky, his sword drawn in his hand, stretched out over Jerusalem. David and the elders fell on their faces, covered in sackcloth.
21:17 David said to God, "Did I not give the word to count the people? It is I who sinned and did great evil. And these sheep, what did they do? LORD my God, please turn your hand against me and against my father's family, and not against your people with a plague."
## The altar on Ornan's threshing floor
21:18 Then the LORD's angel told Gad to have David go up and put up an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.
21:19 David went up, following the word Gad had spoken in the LORD's name.
21:20 Ornan turned and saw the angel, and his four sons with him went into hiding. Ornan had been threshing wheat.
21:21 David came to Ornan. Ornan looked and saw David, and he came out from the threshing floor and bowed low before David, his face to the earth.
21:22 David said to Ornan, "Give me the place of the threshing floor, so I can build an altar to the LORD on it. Give it to me for its full price in silver, so the plague on the people will be held back."
21:23 "Take it for yourself!" Ornan told David. "My lord the king may do whatever looks good to him. See, I am giving the oxen for the burnt offerings, the threshing sledges for firewood, and the wheat for the grain offering. I give it all."
21:24 But King David said to Ornan, "No. I will surely buy it for its full price in silver. I will not take what belongs to you for the LORD, or offer up a burnt offering I got for free."
21:25 David gave Ornan gold for the place, six hundred shekels by weight.
21:26 There David built the LORD an altar and offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings. He called on the LORD, and the LORD answered him: fire came from heaven onto the altar of burnt offering.
21:27 The LORD gave the angel the word, and he returned his sword to its sheath.
21:28 At that time David sacrificed there, having seen that the LORD had answered him on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.
21:29 At that time the LORD's tabernacle, the one Moses made in the wilderness, stood at the high place in Gibeon, with the altar of burnt offering.
21:30 David could not go there, before it, to seek God. The sword of the LORD's angel had filled him with terror.
22:1 Then David said, "This is the house of the LORD God, and this is the altar of burnt offering for Israel."
## Materials for the house
22:2 David gave orders to gather the foreigners living in the land of Israel. He put stonecutters to work shaping squared stones to build God's house.
22:3 David laid up iron in plenty for nails for the doors of the gates and for clamps, and so much bronze it could not be weighed.
22:4 And there was cedar wood beyond counting, because the people of Sidon and Tyre had brought David great loads of cedar.
22:5 "My son Solomon is young and tender," David said. "The house that is to go up for the LORD must be exceedingly great, so that its fame and its glory reach every land. So let me get things ready for it." David got a great deal ready before he died.
## David's charge to Solomon
22:6 Then he summoned his son Solomon and gave him the command to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel.
22:7 "My son," David said to Solomon, "it was my own heart's wish to build a house for the name of the LORD my God.
22:8 But the LORD's word came to me: 'You have shed blood in plenty and fought great wars. You will not build a house for my name, since you have shed much blood on the ground before me.
22:9 Look, a son will be born to you. He will be a man of rest. From the enemies all around him I will give him rest. Solomon will be his name, and in his days I will give Israel peace and calm.
22:10 He will build a house for my name. He will be a son to me, and I will be a father to him. I will make the throne of his kingdom over Israel secure for all time.'
22:11 Now, my son, may the LORD be with you. May you prosper and build the house of the LORD your God, just as he promised about you.
22:12 Only may the LORD give you good sense and understanding as he sets you over Israel, and may you keep the law of the LORD your God.
22:13 Then you will prosper, if you take care to do the rules and the rulings the LORD commanded Moses about Israel. Be strong and bold. Do not be afraid and do not be shaken.
22:14 Look, in my hard times I have laid up for the LORD's house a hundred thousand talents of gold and a million talents of silver. The bronze and iron cannot be weighed, there is so much. I have laid up wood and stone too, and you will add to them.
22:15 And you have workers in plenty: men to cut stone, craftsmen in stone and wood, and every skilled man for every kind of work.
22:16 Of gold, silver, bronze and iron there is no counting. Up, get to work! And may the LORD be with you."
22:17 David gave orders to all of Israel's leaders: they were to help his son Solomon.
22:18 "Is the LORD your God not with you?" he said. "Hasn't he given you rest on all sides? He has put the people who lived in the land into my hand, and the land has been brought under the LORD and his people.
22:19 Now set your heart and your being to seek the LORD your God. Get up and build the holy place of the LORD God. Then bring the ark of the LORD's covenant and the holy objects of God into the house that is to be built for the LORD's name."
## The Levites counted
23:1 David was old and satisfied with days. He put his son Solomon on Israel's throne as king.
23:2 He called together all Israel's leaders, and the priests and the Levites.
23:3 The Levites from thirty years old and up were counted. Counted head by head, the men numbered thirty-eight thousand.
23:4 "From these," David said, "twenty-four thousand will direct the work of the LORD's house, and six thousand will be officials and judges.
23:5 Four thousand will be gatekeepers, and four thousand will praise the LORD on the instruments I have made for praising him."
23:6 David divided them into divisions by the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.
23:7 Of the Gershonites: Ladan and Shimei.
23:8 The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the first, then Zetham and Joel, three.
23:9 The sons of Shimei: Shelomoth, Haziel and Haran, three. These were the heads of the families of Ladan.
23:10 The sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, Jeush and Beriah. These four were the sons of Shimei.
23:11 Jahath was the first and Zizah the second. Jeush and Beriah had few sons, so together they made up one family, enrolled as one.
23:12 The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel, four.
23:13 The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron was set apart to treat the most holy things as holy. He and his sons were to do this for all time: to send up smoke before the LORD, to serve him, and to bless in his name for all time.
23:14 As for Moses the man of God, his sons were named with the tribe of Levi.
23:15 The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.
23:16 The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the first.
23:17 The sons of Eliezer: Rehabiah the first. There were no other sons for Eliezer, but Rehabiah's sons grew very many.
23:18 The sons of Izhar: Shelomith the first.
23:19 The sons of Hebron: Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third and Jekameam the fourth.
23:20 The sons of Uzziel: Micah the first and Isshiah the second.
23:21 The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.
23:22 Eleazar died and had no sons, only daughters. Kish's sons, their relatives, took them as wives.
23:23 The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder and Jeremoth, three.
## The Levites' work
23:24 These were Levi's descendants by their families: the family heads, enrolled by the number of their names, head by head. They did the work of serving in the LORD's house, from twenty years old and up.
23:25 For as David said, "The LORD, the God of Israel, has let his people rest, and he lives in Jerusalem for all time.
23:26 And as for the Levites, there is no more carrying of the tabernacle and all its gear for its service."
23:27 By David's last words, the count of Levi's descendants was taken from twenty years old and up.
23:28 Their place was at the side of Aaron's descendants for the service of the LORD's house: in charge of the courtyards and the side rooms, the cleansing of every holy thing, and the work of serving in God's house.
23:29 They had charge of the rows of bread, the fine flour for the grain offering, the thin cakes without yeast, what was baked on the griddle and what was mixed, and every measure of volume and length.
23:30 Morning after morning they were to stand and give thanks and praise to the LORD, and so at evening too.
23:31 They served at every burnt offering sent up to the LORD, for the Sabbaths, the New Moons and the set feasts, in the numbers the rule required of them, always before the LORD.
23:32 In this way they kept the charge of the tent of meeting, the charge of the holy place, and the charge of their relatives, Aaron's descendants, for the service of the LORD's house.
## The priests' twenty-four divisions
24:1 As for Aaron's descendants, these were their divisions. Aaron's sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.
24:2 Nadab and Abihu died while their father was still living, and they had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar acted as priests.
24:3 David divided them up, along with Zadok from Eleazar's sons and Ahimelech from Ithamar's sons, by their assigned duties in their service.
24:4 Eleazar's line turned out to have more chief men than Ithamar's line, so they divided them like this: for Eleazar's line, sixteen family heads; for Ithamar's line, eight, by their families.
24:5 The division was made by lot, both lines alike, because there were leaders of the holy place and leaders of God among the descendants of Eleazar and among the descendants of Ithamar.

## The gatekeepers
26:1 The gatekeepers' divisions were these. From the Korahites came Meshelemiah son of Kore, of the sons of Asaph.
26:2 Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,
26:3 Elam the fifth, Jehohanan the sixth, Eliehoenai the seventh.
26:4 Obed-edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sachar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,
26:5 Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth, for God had blessed him.
26:6 Shemaiah his son also had sons, and they held rule in their father's house, for they were brave and able men.
26:7 The sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed and Elzabad. His relatives Elihu and Semachiah were able men too.
26:8 All these were of Obed-edom's descendants. They, their sons and their relatives were able men, with strength for the service: sixty-two belonging to Obed-edom.
26:9 Meshelemiah had sons and relatives, able men, eighteen.
26:10 Hosah, of the descendants of Merari, had sons: Shimri was the head. He was not the firstborn, but his father set him at the head.
26:11 Hilkiah was the second, Tebaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth. All Hosah's sons and relatives came to thirteen.
26:12 These were the gatekeepers' divisions. Through their chief men they had watches, just as their relatives had, to serve in the LORD's house.
26:13 Small and great alike, family by family, they cast lots for each gate.
26:14 The east fell by lot to Shelemiah. For his son Zechariah, a counselor with insight, they also cast lots, and his lot came out for the north.
26:15 For Obed-edom it was the south, and for his sons the storehouse.
26:16 For Shuppim and Hosah it was the west, by the Shallecheth Gate on the road that goes up, guard beside guard.
26:17 On the east were six Levites; on the north, four a day; on the south, four a day; and for the storehouse, two and two.
26:18 At the Parbar on the west there were four at the road and two at the Parbar.
26:19 Such were the gatekeepers' divisions, from the Korahites' descendants and from Merari's.
## Treasurers, officials and judges
26:20 As for the Levites, their relatives were over the treasuries of God's house and the treasuries of the holy gifts.
26:21 The sons of Ladan, the Gershonites belonging to Ladan, heads of the families of Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.
26:22 The sons of Jehieli, Zetham and his brother Joel, were over the treasuries of the LORD's house.
26:23 Of the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites and the Uzzielites:
26:24 Shebuel, from Gershom son of Moses, was the chief officer over the treasuries.
26:25 His relatives through Eliezer: Rehabiah his son, Jeshaiah his son, Joram his son, Zichri his son and Shelomoth his son.
26:26 This Shelomoth and his relatives were over all the treasuries of the holy gifts that King David had set apart, along with the heads of families, the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, and the commanders of the army.
26:27 Out of the wars and out of the plunder they set gifts apart to strengthen the LORD's house.
26:28 Everything that Samuel the seer, Saul son of Kish, Abner son of Ner and Joab son of Zeruiah had set apart, whatever anyone set apart, was under the hand of Shelomith and his relatives.
26:29 From the Izharites, Chenaniah and his sons had the work outside, over Israel, as officials and judges.
26:30 Of the Hebronites: Hashabiah and his relatives, one thousand seven hundred able men, had oversight of Israel west of the Jordan, for all the LORD's work and for the king's service.
26:31 Of the Hebronites, Jeriah was the chief of the Hebronites by their family records. In the fortieth year of David's reign they were searched out, and strong, able men were found among them at Jazer in Gilead.
26:32 His relatives, able men, were two thousand seven hundred heads of families. King David put them over the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh for every matter of God and every matter of the king.

27:2 Over the first division, for the first month, was Jashobeam son of Zabdiel. His division numbered twenty-four thousand.

## David's charge to the assembly
28:1 David gathered to Jerusalem all of Israel's leaders: the leaders of the tribes, the commanders of the divisions who served the king, the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds, the officers over all the goods and herds of the king and his sons, along with the palace officials, the mighty men and every man of strength.
28:2 King David stood up on his feet. "Listen to me, my brothers and my people," he said. "My heart was set on building a resting house for the ark of the LORD's covenant, for the footstool of our God's feet. I got ready to build.
28:3 God told me, though, 'It is not you who will build a house for my name. You are a man of wars, and you have shed blood.'
28:4 Still, out of all my father's family, it was me the LORD, the God of Israel, chose to be king over Israel for all time. He chose Judah to be ruler. Within Judah's house he chose my father's family, and among my father's sons he delighted in me and made me king over all Israel.
28:5 The LORD has given me many sons, and out of them all he has chosen my son Solomon to sit on the throne of the LORD's kingdom over Israel.
28:6 'Your son Solomon,' he told me, 'is the one who will build my house and my courtyards. I have chosen him to be a son to me, and I will be a father to him.
28:7 I will make his kingdom secure for all time, if he holds firm in doing my commands and my rulings, as he does today.'
28:8 So now, with all Israel watching, the LORD's assembly, and with our God listening: keep all the commands of the LORD your God and search them out. Then you will take possession of the good land and pass it on to your children after you as an inheritance for all time.
28:9 And you, Solomon my son, know your father's God. Serve him with a whole heart and a willing being. The LORD searches out every heart, and he discerns every plan the thoughts form. Seek him, and he will let you find him. Leave him, and he will cast you aside forever.
28:10 Look now, the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the holy place. Be strong, and get it done."
## The pattern of the house
28:11 Then David gave his son Solomon the pattern of the porch and its buildings, its treasuries, its upper rooms and its inner rooms, and the room for the atonement lid.
28:12 He gave him the pattern of everything he had in his spirit: for the courtyards of the LORD's house and all the rooms around them, for the treasuries of God's house and the treasuries of the holy gifts,
28:13 for the divisions of the priests and the Levites, for all the work of serving in the LORD's house, and for all the articles used in serving in the LORD's house.
28:14 He gave the gold by weight for all the gold articles for each kind of service, and the silver by weight for all the silver articles for each kind of service.
28:15 He gave the weight for the gold lampstands and their gold lamps, by the weight of each lampstand and its lamps, and for the silver lampstands by weight, each lampstand and its lamps, by the use of each lampstand.
28:16 He gave the gold by weight for the tables for the rows of bread, for each table, and silver for the silver tables.
28:17 He gave pure gold for the forks, the splashing bowls and the jugs; for the gold cups, by the weight of each cup; and for the silver cups, by the weight of each cup.
28:18 For the incense altar he gave refined gold by weight, and gold for the pattern of the chariot: the cherubim, spreading out and covering the ark of the LORD's covenant.
28:19 "All of it is in writing, from the LORD's hand on me," David said. "He gave me insight into all the work of the pattern."
28:20 David said to his son Solomon, "Be strong and bold, and get to work. Do not be afraid and do not be shaken, for the LORD God, my own God, is with you. He will not let you down, and he will not abandon you, until all the work for the service of the LORD's house is finished.
28:21 The divisions of priests and Levites are here, ready for every service of God's house. Every willing, skilled man, whatever his trade, will work beside you. The leaders and all the people will do everything you say."
## Gifts for the house
29:1 "Solomon my son, God's one choice, is young and tender," King David told the whole assembly, "and the work is great. This citadel is not for a mere man. It is for the LORD God.
29:2 With all my strength I have made things ready for the house of my God: the gold for the gold work, the silver for the silver, the bronze for the bronze, the iron for the iron, and wood for the wood; onyx and gems to be set, dark-set stones and stones of many colors, every precious stone, and alabaster in great amounts.
29:3 What is more, because my heart is in the house of my God, I have my own private treasure of gold and silver, and I give it to the house of my God, over and above all I have made ready for the holy house:
29:4 three thousand talents of gold, gold of Ophir, and seven thousand talents of refined silver, to coat the walls of the buildings,
29:5 gold for what is gold and silver for what is silver, and for all the work done by craftsmen's hands. So who will freely give today, filling his hand with a gift for the LORD?"
29:6 Freely they gave: the family heads, the leaders of Israel's tribes, the commanders over thousands and over hundreds, and the men in charge of the king's work.
29:7 For the service of God's house they gave gold: five thousand talents and ten thousand darics. Of silver they gave ten thousand talents, of bronze eighteen thousand talents, and of iron a hundred thousand talents.
29:8 Those who had gems on hand gave them into the hand of Jehiel the Gershonite for the treasury of the LORD's house.
29:9 The people were glad over their own free giving, since they had given to the LORD of their own will, with a whole heart. King David's joy was great as well.
## David's prayer
29:10 Then David blessed the LORD before the eyes of the whole assembly. "Blessed are you, LORD, God of Israel our father," David said, "from the ages past to the ages to come.
29:11 To you, LORD, belong the greatness and the might, the beauty, the victory and the majesty, for everything in the heavens and on the earth is yours. Yours, LORD, is the kingdom, and you are lifted high as head over all.
29:12 Riches and glory have their source in you, and you are ruler over all. Power and might are in your hand, and your hand can make anyone great and make anyone strong.
29:13 And now, our God, we give thanks to you, and we praise your name in all its beauty.
29:14 Yet who am I, and who are my people, that we should have the strength to give freely like this? All of it comes from you. What we have given you came out of your own hand.
29:15 For we are foreigners before you, and lodgers, as all our fathers were. Like a shadow are our days on the earth, and there is no hope.
29:16 LORD our God, this whole store of wealth that we have gathered to build you a house for your holy name, it is from your hand, and everything is yours.
29:17 My God, I know you put the heart to the test, and you take pleasure in honesty. With an honest heart I have given all this freely. Now I have watched your people who are here give freely to you, and gladly.
29:18 LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel, our fathers, keep this always as the plan the thoughts form in your people's hearts, and make their hearts steady toward you.
29:19 And to Solomon my son give a whole heart, to keep your commands, your decrees and your rules, to do them all, and to build the citadel I have made ready for."
## Solomon made king
29:20 "Please bless the LORD your God," David told the whole assembly. And all of them blessed the LORD, the God of their fathers. They bent their heads and bowed down before the LORD and before the king.
29:21 They offered sacrifices to the LORD, and on the day after that they sent up burnt offerings to the LORD: a thousand young bulls, a thousand rams and a thousand lambs, along with their drink offerings, and a great many sacrifices for all Israel.
29:22 They ate and drank in the LORD's presence that day, full of joy. For a second time they made David's son Solomon king. They anointed him for the LORD as ruler, and Zadok as priest.
29:23 Solomon sat on the LORD's throne as king in place of David his father. He did well, and all Israel listened to him.
29:24 All the leaders and the mighty men, and all King David's sons as well, gave their hand in pledge to King Solomon.
29:25 The LORD raised Solomon very high in the eyes of all Israel. He put on him a kingly splendor that no king over Israel before him had worn.
## David's death
29:26 So it was that David son of Jesse ruled as king over all Israel.
29:27 The time he was king over Israel came to forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.
29:28 He died at a good old age, satisfied with days, riches and glory, and Solomon his son became king in his place.
29:29 The things King David did, the early and the late, are written in the accounts of Samuel the seer, the accounts of Nathan the prophet and the accounts of Gad the visionary,
29:30 with all his reign and his might, and the times that swept over him, over Israel, and over all the kingdoms of the lands.
