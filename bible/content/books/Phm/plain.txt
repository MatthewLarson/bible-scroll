## A letter from a prisoner
1:1 From Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and from Timothy our brother. To Philemon, whom we love, our fellow worker,
1:2 to Apphia our sister, to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church that meets in your house.
1:3 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
## Thanks for Philemon's love
1:4 I always give thanks to my God when I speak of you in my prayers,
1:5 for I hear about your love, and about the faith you hold toward the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints.
1:6 My prayer is that your partnership in the faith will be put to work as you come to know fully every good thing that is ours, leading to Christ.
1:7 Because of your love I have had much joy and comfort, for through you, brother, the saints' hearts have been set at rest.
## An appeal for Onesimus
1:8 So then, in Christ I have plenty of boldness to give you orders about your duty.
1:9 Still, because of love, I would rather appeal to you. I do so as the man I am: Paul, who is old, and who is now a prisoner of Christ Jesus as well.
1:10 My appeal is for my own child, whom I fathered in my chains: Onesimus.
1:11 Once he was useless to you. Now he is useful, to you and to me as well.
1:12 I am returning him to you, and he is my own heart.
1:13 I had wanted to hold on to him here, so that he could serve me on your behalf in the chains I wear for the good news.
1:14 But without your agreement I chose to do nothing. I did not want your good deed to look as if it were forced. It should come from your own free will.
1:15 Perhaps the reason he was parted for a short time was this: so that you could have him back for all time,
1:16 no longer as a slave, but as more than a slave: a much-loved brother. He is dear above all to me, so how much dearer to you, in the flesh and in the Lord alike.
## Charge it to me
1:17 So if you count me as your partner, welcome him as if he were me.
1:18 If he has wronged you in any way, or if he is in your debt, put it on my account.
1:19 I, Paul, have written it in my own hand: I myself will repay. I need not tell you that you owe me your own self as well.
1:20 Yes, brother, let me gain something from you in the Lord. Set my heart at rest in Christ.
1:21 I am writing to you because I am sure you will obey. I know you will go beyond what I say.
1:22 Also, have a guest room ready for me. I hope to be given to all of you as a gift, through your prayers.
## Greetings and blessing
1:23 Greetings to you from Epaphras, my fellow captive in Christ Jesus,
1:24 and from Mark, Aristarchus, Demas and Luke, my fellow workers.
1:25 May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
