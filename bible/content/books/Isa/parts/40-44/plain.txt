## Comfort my people
40:1 Comfort, comfort my people, / your God says.
40:2 Speak to the heart of Jerusalem, / and call out to her / that her term of service is over, / that her guilt has been paid off, / that from the LORD's hand she has taken / twice over for all her sins.
40:3 A voice calls out: / In the wilderness clear the way of the LORD! / In the desert plain make straight / a highway for our God.
40:4 Each valley will rise up, / and each mountain and hill sink low. / The steep ground will turn into flat land, / and the ridges into a broad valley.
40:5 Then the LORD's glory will be revealed, / and all flesh will see it, all together, / for the LORD's own mouth has spoken.
40:6 A voice is saying, "Call out!" / I answered, "What am I to call out?" / All flesh is grass, / and all its loyal love is like a wildflower.
40:7 The grass dries up, the flower wilts, / for the LORD's wind has blown on it. / Yes, the people are grass.
40:8 The grass dries up, the flower wilts, / but our God's word will stand forever.
## Here is your God
40:9 Climb a high mountain, / Zion, you messenger of good news! / Lift your voice with power, / Jerusalem, you messenger of good news! / Lift it, have no fear! / Tell the towns of Judah, / "Look, your God!"
40:10 Look, the Lord GOD comes in strength, / his arm ruling for him. / Look, he brings his reward with him, / and his wages go ahead of him.
40:11 Like a shepherd he will graze his flock. / With his arm he will gather up the lambs / and carry them in the fold of his robe. / He will guide the nursing ewes along.
## Who is like God?
40:12 Who has held the waters in his palm to measure them, / or set the size of the heavens by a hand's span? / Who has held all the earth's dust in a third-measure, / or weighed the mountains on a scale / and the hills on balances?
40:13 Who has taken the measure of the LORD's Spirit? / What adviser of his has taught him anything?
40:14 With whom did he talk it over, to gain insight? / Who trained him in the path of justice? / Who taught him what to know, / or made him aware of the road to understanding?
40:15 The nations? They are a drop dripping from a bucket, / no more than dust on a pair of scales. / The islands? He lifts them like specks of powder.
40:16 There is not enough in Lebanon to burn, / not enough in its wild animals for a burnt offering.
40:17 Set beside him, all nations are like nothing. / He reckons them as mere nothing and emptiness.
40:18 So who will you say God is like? / What likeness will you set up to match him?
40:19 The idol is poured in metal by a craftsman. / A smith hammers gold over it / and makes silver chains for it.
40:20 Someone too poor for a gift like that / picks a log that will not rot. / He hunts for a clever craftsman / to set up an idol that will not tip over.
40:21 Don't you know? Don't you hear? / Weren't you told from the very start? / Haven't you grasped how the earth was founded?
40:22 He is seated high over the circle of the earth, / and those who live on it look like grasshoppers. / He stretches the heavens out like a thin veil / and spreads them out like a tent for living in.
40:23 He makes great lords count for nothing / and turns the earth's judges into emptiness.
40:24 They have barely been planted, / barely been sown, / their stalk has barely rooted in the soil, / and he blows on them, and they dry up, / and the storm wind lifts them away like straw.
40:25 Who will you set me beside, / as if I were his equal? / says the Holy One.
40:26 Raise your eyes to the heights and see. / Who created these? / The one who marches their army out by count / and calls each one by name. / So vast is his might, so strong his power, / that not a single one fails to appear.
40:27 Jacob, why do you say, / Israel, why do you insist, / "My road is hidden from the LORD's sight, / and my God has passed my case by"?
40:28 Don't you know? Haven't you heard? / The God of the ages is the LORD, / who created the far edges of the earth. / He never tires, and he never wears out. / There is no getting to the bottom of his understanding.
40:29 To the tired he gives strength, / and for the powerless he multiplies power.
40:30 Boys may tire and wear out, / and young men in their prime may trip and fall flat.
40:31 But those who wait in hope for the LORD / will get fresh strength. / Like eagles, they will climb on their pinions. / They will run and not wear out. / They will walk and not tire.
## The islands summoned to court
41:1 Islands, be silent before me! / Let the peoples get fresh strength. / Let them step up, and then let them speak. / Together let us come to court.
41:2 Who has roused a man from the east / and calls him in righteousness to his service? / He hands nations over to him / and lets him tread kings down. / His sword turns them to dust, / his bow to straw blown by the wind.
41:3 He chases them and goes through unharmed, / on a road his feet have never touched.
41:4 Who has worked this and done it, / summoning the generations from the start? / I, the LORD, am first, / and with the last ones I am he.
41:5 The islands saw and took fright. / The ends of the earth shake. / They come close; they arrive.
41:6 Every man lends his neighbor a hand / and tells his brother, "Be strong!"
41:7 The craftsman cheers on the smith, / the one who smooths with the hammer / cheers the one who pounds the anvil, / saying of the soldering, "It is good." / He makes it strong with nails, so it will not tip over.
## Israel, my servant
41:8 But you, Israel, my servant, / Jacob, whom I have chosen, / offspring of Abraham, who loved me,
41:9 it was you I grasped from the ends of the earth / and called from its most remote parts. / I told you, "You are my servant. / I chose you, and I never rejected you."
41:10 Don't be afraid, because I am with you. / Don't keep glancing around, because I am your God. / I make you strong; yes, I help you; / yes, I hold you up / with the right hand of my righteousness.
41:11 Look, everyone who burns with anger at you / will be shamed and humiliated. / Those who bring charges against you / will become nothing and die out.
41:12 You will search for your opponents / and not find them. / Those who wage war on you / will be like nothing, like nobody.
41:13 I, the LORD your God, grip your right hand. / I am telling you, "Do not be afraid. / I will help you."
41:14 Don't be afraid, Jacob, you worm, / you small band of Israel. / I will help you, the LORD declares. / The Holy One of Israel is your redeemer.
41:15 Look, I am turning you into a threshing board, / brand-new and sharp, edged with teeth. / You will thresh mountains and grind them fine, / and leave the hills like chaff.
41:16 You will winnow them, and the wind will lift them away, / and a gale will scatter them. / But you will be glad in the LORD / and take pride in the Holy One of Israel.
41:17 The poor and the needy hunt for water. / There is none, and their tongues are dried out with thirst. / I, the LORD, will answer them; / as Israel's God, I will not leave them.
41:18 On the bare hilltops I will open up rivers, / and in among the valleys, springs. / I will turn the wilderness into a pond / and the dry ground into fountains of water.
41:19 In the wilderness I will put the cedar, / the acacia, the myrtle and the olive. / In the desert plain I will set the cypress, / the plane tree and the pine together,
41:20 so that all of them will see and know, / take it to heart and grasp it, / that this is the work of the LORD's hand, / that the Holy One of Israel created it.
## The gods on trial
41:21 Put your case forward, the LORD says. / Lay out your strongest proofs, says the King of Jacob.
41:22 Let them come forward and tell us / what will take place. / The earlier things, what were they? Explain them, / so that we can give them our attention / and know where they led. / Or let us hear what is still to come.
41:23 Tell us the things to come in later days, / and we will know that you are gods. / Go on, do something, good or harmful, / so that we stare at each other / and see it all together.
41:24 Look, you come from nothing, / and your work from nothingness. / Anyone who chooses you is abhorrent.
41:25 From the north I have roused a man, and here he comes. / From where the sun rises he will call on my name. / He will walk over deputies as if they were mud, / the way a potter stamps on clay.
41:26 Which of you told it at the start, so that we would know? / Who said it in advance, so that we could say, "Correct"? / Not one of you told it; not one made it known; / not one heard you say a thing.
41:27 First, to Zion: "Look! There they are!" / And for Jerusalem I provide a messenger of good news.
41:28 I look around, and there is nobody. / Not one of these can give advice. / I question them, and they have no word to give back.
41:29 Look at them all: a fraud! / What they do is nothing. / Their cast images are wind and emptiness.
## The servant and the nations
42:1 Look at my servant! I hold him up. / He is my chosen one; my soul is pleased with him. / I have placed my Spirit on him. / He will bring justice out to the nations.
42:2 He will not cry out. He will not lift his voice. / He will not let it be heard out in the street.
42:3 A reed that is crushed he will not snap. / A wick that burns dim he will not put out. / He will truly bring justice out.
42:4 He will not burn dim, and he will not be crushed, / until he sets justice firm on the earth. / The islands are waiting for his teaching.
42:5 God, the LORD, says this. / He created the heavens and stretched them out. / He hammered out the earth and what springs from it. / He gives the people on it breath, / and spirit to those who walk there:
42:6 I am the LORD. In righteousness I have called you. / I grip you by the hand and keep watch over you. / I make you a covenant of the people / and a light to the nations,
42:7 to open blind eyes, / to lead captives out of the pit / and out of the jail those who sit in the dark.
42:8 I am the LORD; that is my name. / I will not hand my glory to another, / nor the praise owed me to carved images.
42:9 The earlier things? Look, they have happened. / Now I am telling of new things. / Before they sprout, I let you hear them.
## A new song
42:10 Sing a new song to the LORD, / sing his praise from the far end of the earth, / you sailors on the sea, and everything in it, / you islands and all who live on them.
42:11 Let the wilderness raise its voice, and its towns, / the camps where Kedar lives. / Let those who live in Sela ring out with joy / and shout from the mountaintops.
42:12 Let them give the LORD glory / and make his praise known in the islands.
42:13 The LORD marches out like a champion fighter. / He works up his passion like a man of war. / He yells, yes, he gives the battle cry. / Over his enemies he proves the stronger.
42:14 I have said nothing for ages. / I kept still; I held myself in. / Like a mother in labor I will moan; / I will breathe hard and gasp together.
42:15 Mountains and hills I will strip bare; / I will wither all their green growth. / I will turn the rivers into islands / and drain the ponds.
42:16 I will walk blind people down a road they do not know. / On paths they do not know I will lead them. / Ahead of them I will make the darkness light / and the crooked places flat. / These things I will do for them, / and I will not leave them.
42:17 Those who rely on a carved idol / and tell cast images, "You are our gods," / will be driven back in deep shame.
## A blind and deaf servant
42:18 You who are deaf, listen! / You who are blind, look, so that you can see!
42:19 Who is blind, if not my servant? / Who is deaf like the messenger I send? / Who is as blind as the one made whole, / as blind as the LORD's servant?
42:20 You see many things, but you keep none of them. / His ears are open, but he hears nothing.
42:21 For his righteousness' sake the LORD was pleased / to make his teaching great and splendid.
42:22 Yet here is a people stripped and looted. / They are all caught in pits / and shut away in jails. / They became plunder, and nobody rescued them; / spoil, and nobody said, "Give them back."
42:23 Which one of you will open your ears to this? / Who will pay attention and listen for what comes later?
42:24 Who handed Jacob over to be looted, / and Israel to the robbers? / Wasn't it the LORD, the one we sinned against? / They refused to walk in his ways, / and they would not listen to his teaching.
42:25 So he poured his burning anger over him, / and the fury of battle. / It blazed around him on every side, and still he did not know. / It burned into him, and he gave it no thought.
## You belong to me
43:1 But now the LORD says this, / your creator, Jacob, / the one who shaped you, Israel: / Don't be afraid. I have redeemed you. / I called you by your name. You belong to me.
43:2 If you cross the water, I am with you. / and the rivers will not wash over you. / If you walk in the fire, you will not get burned, / and the flame will not catch you.
43:3 For I am the LORD, your God, / the Holy One of Israel, the one who saves you. / I hand over Egypt as your ransom, / Cush and Seba to take your place.
43:4 Since you are dear to me, / and I hold you in honor and love you, / I will hand over human beings in your place, / whole peoples for your life.
43:5 Have no fear; I am with you. / From the sunrise I will bring your offspring, / and from the sunset I will gather you.
43:6 I will tell the north, "Let them go!" / and the south, "Don't keep them!" / Bring my sons from far off, / my daughters from the end of the earth,
43:7 all who bear my name, / those I created to show my glory, / those I shaped, yes, those I made.
## You are my witnesses
43:8 Bring out a blind people who have eyes, / deaf people who have ears.
43:9 Let all the nations gather / and the peoples come together. / Which of them can tell of this / or announce the earlier things to us? / Let them bring their witnesses to win their case, / or let them hear and say, "That is true."
43:10 You are my witnesses, the LORD declares, / and my servant, whom I chose, / so that you will know and trust me / and grasp that I am he. / No god was shaped before me, / and none will exist after me.
43:11 I myself, I am the LORD. / There is no one but me who saves.
43:12 It was I who told it, I who saved, I who made it heard, / and no strange god was among you then. / You are my witnesses, the LORD declares: / I am God.
43:13 Since the first day, I am he. / No one can rescue anyone from my hand. / I go to work, and who can stop it?
## A road in the wilderness
43:14 The LORD, your redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, says this: / For your sake I am sending to Babylon. / I will bring them all down as runaways, / the Chaldeans, in the ships they used to cheer.
43:15 I am the LORD, your Holy One, / Israel's creator, your king.
43:16 The LORD says this, / who opened a road in the sea, / a path in the powerful waters,
43:17 who led out chariot and horse, / soldier and strong man together. / Down they lay, and they did not get up. / They went out; like a wick they were put out.
43:18 Stop calling the earlier things to mind. / Stop thinking over what happened long ago.
43:19 Look at me, about to do something new. / Right now it is sprouting. Won't you recognize it? / I am laying a road through the wilderness, / and rivers in the barren land.
43:20 The animals of the field will honor me, / jackals and ostriches, / because I have put water in the wilderness / and rivers in the barren land / for my people, my chosen one, to drink.
43:21 This people I shaped for myself. / They will recount my praise.
## Not your offerings, but your sins
43:22 Yet it was not me you called on, Jacob, / for you were worn out with me, Israel.
43:23 Not to me did you bring the sheep of your burnt offerings. / Not me did you honor with your sacrifices. / I did not make you slave over grain offerings / or wear you out over frankincense.
43:24 You did not buy me sweet cane with your money, / or fill me up with the fat of your sacrifices. / Instead you made me slave over your sins / and wore me out with your guilt.
43:25 I myself, I am the one / who wipes away your rebellions for my own sake. / Your sins I will not remember.
43:26 Jog my memory; let us take this to court together. / Tell your side, so that you can come out in the right.
43:27 Your very first father sinned, / and your go-betweens rebelled against me.
43:28 So I made the leaders of the holy place unholy, / and I handed Jacob over to the ban / and Israel to insults.
## My Spirit on your children
44:1 So now, Jacob my servant, listen, / Israel, the one I chose.
44:2 The LORD says this, your maker, / who shaped you from the womb and will help you: / Have no fear, my servant Jacob, / Jeshurun, the one I chose.
44:3 I will pour out water on the thirsty, / flowing streams over the dry ground. / On your children I will pour out my Spirit, / on those who come from you, my blessing.
44:4 They will spring up in the middle of the grass, / like poplars beside running streams.
44:5 This one will say, "I am the LORD's." / That one will use the name of Jacob. / Another will write on his hand, "Belongs to the LORD," / and take Israel as an honored name.
## No god but me
44:6 The LORD, the King of Israel, says this, / its redeemer, the LORD of Armies: / I am first, and I am last, / and except for me there is no god.
44:7 Who is like me? Let him speak up. / Let him tell it and set it out in order for me, / from the time I set up a people of long ago. / Let them tell what is coming, / the things yet to happen.
44:8 Don't panic; don't be afraid. / Long ago I let you hear it and told you, didn't I? / You are my witnesses. / Is there a God apart from me? / No, there is no rock. I know none.
## The idol maker
44:9 Those who shape idols, every one of them, are emptiness, / and the things they love are no use. / Their own witnesses see nothing and know nothing, / and so they will be shamed.
44:10 Who would shape a god, / or pour an idol, that can do no good?
44:11 Look, all who join him will be put to shame. / The craftsmen are mere humans. / Let them all assemble; let them stand up. / They will shake with fear, ashamed, all of them at once.
44:12 The ironworker sharpens a tool / and works it over the coals. / He shapes it with hammers / and forges it with all his arm's strength. / Then he is hungry, and his strength is gone. / He has drunk no water, and he tires.
44:13 The carpenter stretches out a line / and sketches it with a marker. / He shapes it with chisels, / outlines it with compasses, / and gives it the form of a man, / the good looks of a human being, / to stay in a house.
44:14 He goes to cut down cedars for himself. / He picks a holm oak or a great oak / and lets it grow strong among the trees of the forest. / He plants a pine, and the rain makes it big.
44:15 It is something a person can burn. / He takes some of it and gets warm. / He kindles a fire and bakes bread. / He also makes a god, and he bows down; / he makes it into an idol and lies face down before it.
44:16 Half the wood he burns in the fire. / Over that half he cooks meat to eat. / He roasts his roast and eats his fill. / He warms himself, too, and says, / "Ah, I'm warm; I can see the glow."
44:17 What is left he turns into a god, his idol. / He lies down before it and bows low. / He prays to it, saying, / "Rescue me! You are my god."
44:18 They know nothing and understand nothing. / Their eyes are smeared shut, so they cannot see, / and their hearts, so they cannot think it through.
44:19 None of them thinks it over. / None has the knowledge or the sense to say, / "I burned half of it in the fire, / and I baked bread on its coals too. / I roasted meat and ate. / Am I going to turn what is left into something abhorrent? / Am I going to bow down to a chunk of a tree?"
44:20 He grazes on ashes. / A heart that has been fooled has turned him aside. / He cannot rescue his own life / or say, "This thing in my right hand is a lie, isn't it?"
## Remember this, Jacob
44:21 Keep these things in mind, Jacob, / and Israel, for you are my servant. / I shaped you; you are my own servant. / Israel, you will not be forgotten by me.
44:22 Like a thick cloud I have wiped away your rebellions, / like a cloud, your sins. / Turn back to me. I have redeemed you.
44:23 Ring out, heavens, for the LORD has acted! / Shout, you depths under the earth! / Burst into joyful song, mountains, / forest, and every tree in it! / The LORD has redeemed Jacob / and will display his splendor in Israel.
## Cyrus, my shepherd
44:24 The LORD says this, your redeemer, / the one who shaped you from the womb: / I am the LORD, who makes all things, / who stretched out the heavens alone, / who hammered out the earth (who was with me?),
44:25 who wrecks the omens of the babblers / and makes the fortune-tellers look like fools, / who sends the wise back the way they came / and makes their knowledge foolish,
44:26 who makes his servant's word stand / and carries out the plan his messengers announce, / who says of Jerusalem, "She will have people living in her," / and of the towns of Judah, "They will be rebuilt. / I will raise up their ruins,"
44:27 who says to the deep, "Dry up! / I will dry out your rivers,"
44:28 who says of Cyrus, "My shepherd! / He will do all that I please," / saying of Jerusalem, "She will be rebuilt," / and to the temple, "You will have your foundations laid."
