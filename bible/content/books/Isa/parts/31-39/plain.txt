## Help from Egypt, help from God
31:1 Woe to those going down to Egypt for help, / who lean on horses! / They trust in chariots, because there are many, / and in horsemen, because they are very strong. / But they do not look to the Holy One of Israel, / and they do not seek the LORD.
31:2 Yet he too is wise, and he brings disaster; / he has not set aside his words. / He will stand up against the house of evildoers, / and against the ones who help wrongdoers.
31:3 The Egyptians are human, and not God; / their horses are flesh, and not spirit. / The LORD will stretch out his hand, / and the helper will stumble, / and the one he helps will fall. / All of them will be finished together.
31:4 For the LORD says this to me: / When a lion, a young lion, rumbles over its kill, / and a crowd of shepherds is called out against it, / their voices do not scare it, / and their uproar does not make it cringe. / In the same way the LORD of Armies will come down / to wage war on Mount Zion and on its hill.
31:5 Like birds that fly, / so the LORD of Armies will guard Jerusalem. / He will guard it and rescue it, / pass over it and bring it out safe.
31:6 Return, you Israelites, / to the one they have rebelled against so deeply.
31:7 For on that day each one will reject / his worthless idols of silver and his worthless idols of gold, / the sin your own hands made for you.
31:8 Assyria will fall by a sword, not a man's. / A sword, not a mortal's, will devour it. / It will run away from the sword, / and its young men will end up in forced labor.
31:9 His rock will pass away in terror, / and his officers will lose their nerve at the flag, / the LORD declares, / who has a fire in Zion / and a furnace in Jerusalem.
## A king who rules rightly
32:1 Look! A king will reign for the sake of righteousness, / and officials will rule for the sake of justice.
32:2 Each of them will be like a hideout from the wind, / a cover from the downpour, / like channels of water in dry country, / like the shadow a heavy rock casts on a land worn out.
32:3 The eyes of those who can see will not be pasted shut, / and the ears of those who can hear will pay attention.
32:4 The hasty heart will grasp what is known, / and the stammering tongue will hurry to speak plainly.
32:5 The godless fool will no longer be called noble, / and no one will call the crook a great man.
32:6 For the godless fool speaks outrage, / and his heart works at wickedness, / to act impiously / and to say twisted things about the LORD, / to leave the hungry with empty stomachs / and keep water from the thirsty.
32:7 The crook's tools are evil. / He thinks up schemes / to wreck the poor with false words, / even when the needy man speaks for what is just.
32:8 A noble person's plans are noble, / and noble deeds are the ground he stands on.
## Warning to women at ease
32:9 Women without a care, stand up and hear my voice! / Daughters so sure of yourselves, listen to my words!
32:10 In a year and a few days / you who are so sure will be shaken, / for the vintage will be over / and the fruit-picking will not come.
32:11 Tremble, carefree women! / Be shaken, you who are so sure! / Strip, lay yourselves bare, / and tie a cloth around your hips.
32:12 Mourn, beating your breasts, / for the fields you loved / and for the fruitful vine,
32:13 for my people's ground, / overgrown with thornbush and thorn, / for every house that once rang with joy, / for the town that loved to celebrate.
32:14 The palace will stand forsaken, / the noise of the city will be gone. / The Ophel and the watchtower will become bare caves for all time, / a playground for wild donkeys, / a pasture for flocks,
## When the spirit is poured out
32:15 until a spirit from on high is poured out on us. / Then the wilderness will turn into a garden land, / and the garden land will be thought of as a forest.
32:16 Justice will make its home in the wilderness, / and righteousness will live on in the garden land.
32:17 What righteousness makes will be peace; / what righteousness yields will be calm and safety forever.
32:18 My people will settle in a pasture of peace, / in homes where they feel safe, / in resting places where no one troubles them.
32:19 But hail will fall as the forest falls, / and the city will be laid low, utterly low.
32:20 Happy are you who sow beside every water / and let the ox and the donkey roam free!
## Woe to the destroyer
33:1 Woe to you, plunderer, / you who were never plundered, / you double-crosser, whom no one double-crossed! / When you have plundered all you can, you will be plundered. / When you have finished double-crossing, others will double-cross you.
33:2 LORD, show us grace. / We have hoped in you. / Be their arm morning by morning, / and our rescue when distress comes.
33:3 Peoples run at the sound of the roar; / nations break apart when you rise up.
33:4 Your plunder will be swept up the way caterpillars sweep a field clean; / people will rush at it like a rush of locusts.
33:5 High above all is the LORD, / for he lives on the heights; / Zion he will fill with justice and righteousness.
33:6 He will be the firm footing of your times, / a store of rescue, wisdom and knowledge; / the fear of the LORD, that is his treasure.
33:7 Listen! Their heroes shout from outside; / the messengers who went for peace weep, and bitterly.
33:8 The highways lie deserted; / no one passes along the road. / A treaty has been broken, / cities spurned, / human life counted as nothing.
33:9 The land dries up and wilts; / Lebanon, ashamed, shrivels; / Sharon has become like the desert plain, / and Bashan and Carmel shake themselves bare.
## The LORD rises
33:10 Now I will rise up, says the LORD. / Now I will stand tall. / Now I will lift myself high.
33:11 You conceive dry grass / and bring stubble into the world; / your breath is a fire / that will eat you up.
33:12 Peoples will become lime in the kiln, / cut thornbushes that the fire sets ablaze.
33:13 Hear what I have done, you who live far off! / You who are near, learn how strong I am!
## Who can live with the fire?
33:14 Dread grips the sinners in Zion; / shaking takes hold of the impious. / "Which of us can stay beside a fire that devours? / Which of us can stay beside flames that burn forever?"
33:15 The one who walks in what is right / and speaks what is straight, / who refuses profit wrung out by force, / who waves away a bribe before his hands can close on it, / who plugs his ears to talk of blood / and keeps his eyes shut rather than look at evil:
33:16 he will live on the heights, / with fortresses in the cliffs for his refuge; / his bread will be handed to him, / and his water will hold out.
## The king in his beauty
33:17 A king in his beauty your eyes will see; / they will see a land of far horizons.
33:18 Your heart will think back on the terror: / "Where is the clerk? Where is the man with the scales? / Where is the one who counted the towers?"
33:19 You will not see the insolent people, / a people whose lips are too deep to follow, / whose tongue stammers with no sense in it.
33:20 Gaze at Zion, our festival city! / Your eyes will see Jerusalem / as a quiet pasture, / a tent no one will pack up. / No one will ever pull out its pegs, / and not one of its ropes will snap.
33:21 But there, the LORD will be majestic on our behalf: / a place of rivers, of streams wide on both sides, / where no rowing boat will go / and no majestic ship will pass through.
33:22 For the LORD is our judge, / the LORD is our commander, / the LORD is our king. / He is the one who will save us.
33:23 Your ropes have gone slack. / They cannot steady the foot of their mast; / they cannot spread out a sail. / Then plunder will be shared out, loot in heaps, / and the lame will grab spoil.
33:24 And none who live there will say, "I am sick"; / the people who dwell in it will have their guilt lifted away.
## The LORD's sword over the nations
34:1 Nations, come close and hear! / Peoples, listen carefully! / Earth, hear this, with everything in you, / and the world, with all it brings forth!
34:2 For the LORD has anger for all the nations / and fury for all their armies. / He has put them under the ban; / he has handed them over to slaughter.
34:3 Their dead will be tossed out, / their bodies will send up a stench, / and the mountains will melt away with their blood.
34:4 The whole army of heaven will rot, / and the sky will roll up like a scroll. / Its whole army will wither, / the way a leaf withers and drops from a vine, / the way a shriveled fig drops from the tree.
34:5 For my sword has drunk deep in the heavens. / Look, it will come down on Edom, / on the people under my ban, to judge them.
34:6 The LORD has a sword, and it is full of blood, / greasy with fat, / with the blood of lambs and goats, / with fat from rams' kidneys. / In Bozrah the LORD holds a sacrifice; / in Edom's land, a mass slaughter.
34:7 Wild oxen will go down with them, / young bulls along with mighty bulls. / Their land will drink deep of blood, / and their dust will be made rich with fat.
34:8 For it is the LORD's day of vengeance, / a year of paying back, to win Zion's case.
## Edom left to the wild
34:9 Its wadis will change to tar, / its dust to sulfur, / and its land will become tar on fire.
34:10 It will burn night and day and never be put out; / its smoke will keep going up forever. / Through every generation it will be a wasteland, / and for all time to come no one will travel through it.
34:11 Desert owl and hedgehog will take it over; / ibis and raven will settle in it. / He will stretch over it a measuring line of shapelessness / and plumb stones of emptiness.
34:12 As for its nobles, nothing will be left there that they could call a kingdom, / and all its officials will be gone.
34:13 Thorns will spring up in its palace towers, / nettles and thistles in its forts. / It will be a den for jackals, / a haunt of ostriches.
34:14 Desert creatures will meet up with hyenas, / and a goat-demon will call out to its fellow. / Lilith, too, will settle there / and find herself a place to rest.
34:15 The owl will nest there and lay, / hatch, and gather her chicks in her shade; / the kites will gather there too, / every one with its partner.
34:16 Look it up in the LORD's scroll, and read: / not one of these will be missing, / none will lack its partner. / For my mouth has given the order, / and his spirit has gathered them.
34:17 He is the one who cast the lot for them, / and his hand measured out their shares with the line. / It is theirs to hold for all time; / they will live in it from one generation to the next.
## The desert in bloom
35:1 The wilderness and the thirsty ground will be happy. / The desert plain will be glad / and put out flowers like the crocus.
35:2 Bloom upon bloom will cover it; / it will celebrate with joy and song. / Lebanon's glory is given to it, / and the beauty of Carmel and of Sharon. / These are the ones who will see the LORD's glory, / the beauty of our God.
35:3 Put strength into slack hands, / and steady knees that wobble.
35:4 Tell those whose hearts are pounding, / "Be strong! Don't be afraid! / Look, your God! Vengeance is coming, / God's repayment. / He is coming himself to save you."
35:5 Then blind people's eyes will open, / and deaf people's ears will be unplugged.
35:6 Then a lame man will jump like a deer, / and a mute tongue will sing out. / For water will break through in the wilderness, / and torrents in the desert plain.
35:7 The shimmering heat will turn into a pond, / and parched land into gushing springs. / Where jackals used to lie in their dens, / grass will grow into reeds and papyrus.
## The holy road
35:8 A raised road will be there, a way, / and its name will be the Holy Road. / Nothing unclean will cross it; / it will be theirs. / Those who walk the road will not go astray, even fools.
35:9 There will be no lion there; / no violent animal will climb onto it; / none will be found there. / The redeemed will walk on it.
35:10 The ransomed of the LORD will return / and come to Zion with joyful shouting; / lasting gladness will be on their heads. / Joy and gladness will catch up with them, / and grief and groaning will run away.
## Sennacherib invades
36:1 In King Hezekiah's fourteenth year, Sennacherib king of Assyria marched on all the walled towns of Judah and seized them.
## The Rabshakeh at the wall
36:2 The king of Assyria sent the Rabshakeh from Lachish to Jerusalem, to King Hezekiah, with a huge army. He took his stand by the water channel of the Upper Pool, on the main road to the Launderers' Field.
36:3 Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was over the palace, went out to him, along with Shebna the secretary and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder.
36:4 The Rabshakeh said to them, "Tell Hezekiah, please, 'The great king, the king of Assyria, says this: What is this trust of yours that you trust in?
36:5 I say: a plan and strength for war are mere words from your lips. Now, who are you leaning on, that you have rebelled against me?
36:6 Look, you are relying on Egypt, this crushed reed you use as a walking stick. A man leans on it, and it goes into his palm and pierces it. That is Pharaoh king of Egypt to everyone who relies on him.
36:7 And if you tell me, "We trust in the LORD our God," aren't his high places and altars the very ones Hezekiah has done away with? He has told Judah and Jerusalem, "You must bow down in front of this altar."
36:8 So now, please, place a bet with my master the king of Assyria. Let me give you two thousand horses, if you can find riders to put on them.
36:9 How could you beat back one governor, even one of my master's lowliest servants? And you count on Egypt for chariots and horsemen!
36:10 Now, is it without the LORD that I have marched up against this land to ruin it? It was the LORD who said to me, "March up against this land and ruin it."'"
36:11 Then Eliakim, Shebna and Joah said to the Rabshakeh, "Please, talk to us, your servants, in Aramaic. We can understand it. Don't talk to us in Judean while the people on the wall are listening."
36:12 But the Rabshakeh said, "Is it to your master and to you that my master sent me with this message? No, it is to the men sitting on the wall. They are the ones who, along with you, will eat their own dung and drink their own urine."
36:13 The Rabshakeh stood up and shouted at the top of his voice in Judean. "Hear the words of the great king, the king of Assyria!
36:14 The king says this: 'Don't let Hezekiah fool you, for he cannot rescue you.
36:15 Don't let Hezekiah get you to rely on the LORD with his talk: "The LORD will surely rescue us. This city will not be handed over to the king of Assyria."
36:16 Don't listen to Hezekiah! For the king of Assyria says this: Make a blessing with me and come out to me. Then you can each eat from your own vine and your own fig tree, and drink the water of your own cistern,
36:17 until I come and take you away to a land like your own land. It is a land of grain and new wine, a land of bread and vineyards.
36:18 Don't let Hezekiah lead you astray when he says, "The LORD will rescue us." Did any of the nations' gods rescue his land from the king of Assyria's hand?
36:19 Where are the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where are the gods of Sepharvaim? Did they rescue Samaria from my hand?
36:20 Which one of all the gods of these lands has rescued his land from my hand? And the LORD is going to rescue Jerusalem from my hand?'"
36:21 They stayed silent. Not one of them answered him a word. That was the king's order: "Don't answer him."
36:22 Then Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was over the palace, Shebna the secretary, and Joah son of Asaph, the recorder, came to Hezekiah with their clothes ripped. They reported to him what the Rabshakeh had said.
## Hezekiah sends to Isaiah
37:1 When King Hezekiah heard it, he tore his clothes, wrapped himself in sackcloth, and went into the LORD's house.
37:2 He sent Eliakim, who was over the palace, Shebna the secretary, and the elders of the priests, all wrapped in sackcloth, to the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz.
37:3 They said to him, "Hezekiah says this: 'Today is a day of trouble, of rebuke and of scorn. The children have come right to the mouth of the womb, but there is no strength to push them out.
37:4 Maybe the LORD your God will hear what the Rabshakeh said. His master, the king of Assyria, sent him to insult the living God. Maybe the LORD your God will call him to account for the words the LORD your God has heard. So raise a prayer for the remnant still here.'"
37:5 So King Hezekiah's servants came to Isaiah,
37:6 and Isaiah told them, "Tell your master this: 'The LORD says: Don't be afraid because of what you heard, the words the errand boys of Assyria's king used to revile me.
37:7 I am about to put a spirit into him. A report will reach him, and he will go back to his own land. There, in his land, I will bring him down by the sword.'"
## Sennacherib's letter
37:8 So the Rabshakeh turned back. He had heard that the king had pulled out of Lachish, and he found the king of Assyria at war with Libnah.
37:9 Word reached the king about Tirhakah king of Cush: "He has marched out to fight you." When he heard it, he sent messengers to Hezekiah with this message:
37:10 "Say this to Hezekiah king of Judah: 'Don't let your God, the one you trust, fool you when he says, "Jerusalem will not be handed over to the king of Assyria."
37:11 Look, you yourself have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the lands, how they put them under the ban to wipe them out. And you think you will be rescued?
37:12 My fathers wiped out Gozan, Haran, Rezeph and the people of Eden who were in Telassar. Did the gods of those nations rescue them?
37:13 Where is Hamath's king now? Or Arpad's? Or the king of the town of Sepharvaim, of Hena or of Ivvah?'"
## Hezekiah's prayer
37:14 Hezekiah took the letter from the messengers' hands and read it through. Then he went up to the LORD's house, and there Hezekiah spread it out before the LORD.
37:15 Hezekiah prayed to the LORD. He said,
37:16 "LORD of Armies, God of Israel, seated on the cherubim! You are God, and you alone, over every kingdom on earth. It was you who made the heavens and the earth.
37:17 Bend your ear, LORD, and listen. LORD, open your eyes and look. Listen to the whole message Sennacherib sent to insult the living God.
37:18 LORD, it is true. Assyria's kings have turned all the lands and their territory into ruins.
37:19 They threw the gods of those nations into the fire. Those were no gods at all, only things that human hands had made out of wood and stone. So they destroyed them.
37:20 Now, LORD our God, rescue us out of his hand. Then every kingdom on earth will know that you are the LORD, you alone."
## Isaiah's answer
37:21 Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent this message to Hezekiah: "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Because you prayed to me about Sennacherib king of Assyria,
37:22 this is the word the LORD has spoken against him: / 'She looks down on you, she laughs at you, / the virgin, Daughter Zion. / Behind your back she shakes her head, / Daughter Jerusalem.
37:23 Who is it you have insulted and reviled? / Whom have you raised your voice against, / and lifted your eyes at so high? / The Holy One of Israel!
37:24 Through your servants you have insulted the Lord. / You said, "With my many chariots / I have climbed the mountain heights, / the far slopes of Lebanon. / I chopped down its tall cedars, / its choice cypresses. / I reached its farthest height, / its garden-like forest.
37:25 I dug, and I drank water. / Under my marching feet / I dried up every channel of the Nile."
37:26 Haven't you heard? / I did this long ago. / In ancient days I shaped it, / and now I have made it happen: / you were there to crush walled cities / into piles of rubble.
37:27 Those who lived there had no power left. / They were terrified and ashamed. / They were like the plants of the field, / like fresh green grass, / grass on the rooftops, / dried up before it can stand tall.
37:28 I know when you sit down, / when you go out, when you come in, / and how you storm against me.
37:29 Since you storm against me, / and your arrogance has come up to my ears, / I will put my hook in your nose / and my bridle on your lips. / I will lead you back / by the same road you came.'
37:30 And this will be your sign: This year you will eat the grain that comes up on its own, / and the year after, the grain that sprouts from that. / Then in the third year sow and harvest, / plant vineyards and eat what they bear.
37:31 Those who escape, the house of Judah that remains, / will again send roots down / and put out fruit on top.
37:32 Out of Jerusalem a remnant will go out, / survivors from Mount Zion. / The zeal of the LORD of Armies will make it happen.
37:33 So the LORD says this about the king of Assyria: / He will not get into this city. / He will not shoot a single arrow at it. / No shield of his will face it. / He will not heap up a ramp against it.
37:34 He will go home by the road he came. / Into this city he will not come, / the LORD declares.
37:35 I will cover this city like a shield, to rescue it, / for my own sake, and for David my servant's sake."
## The night of the angel
37:36 The angel of the LORD went out and struck the camp of Assyria: one hundred eighty-five thousand. People rose early in the morning, and there they all were, dead bodies.
37:37 So Sennacherib king of Assyria pulled up stakes and left. He went back home and stayed in Nineveh.
37:38 He was bowing down in the house of Nisroch his god when Adrammelech and Sharezer, his sons, cut him down with the sword. They escaped to the land of Ararat, and his son Esarhaddon became king in his place.
## Hezekiah's sickness
38:1 In those days Hezekiah was sick and dying. The prophet Isaiah son of Amoz came to him and said, "The LORD says this: 'Give orders to your household, for you are about to die. You will not live.'"
38:2 Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to the LORD.
38:3 He said, "Oh, LORD, please remember: I have lived my life before you in truth, with an undivided heart. I have done what is good in your eyes." And Hezekiah cried and cried.
38:4 Then the LORD's word came to Isaiah:
38:5 "Go and say to Hezekiah, 'The LORD, the God of your forefather David, says this: I have heard your prayer. I have seen your tears. Look, I am going to add fifteen years to your days.
38:6 I will rescue you and this city out of the king of Assyria's hand. I will cover this city like a shield.
38:7 Here is the sign the LORD gives you, to show that the LORD will do this thing he has said:
38:8 Look, I am going to turn the shadow back ten steps, the shadow on the steps that has gone down with the sun on the steps of Ahaz.'" So the sun went back ten steps, on the steps where it had gone down.
## Hezekiah's song
38:9 A writing of Hezekiah king of Judah, from the time he fell sick and lived through his illness:
38:10 I said: Halfway through my days / I must walk into the gates of Sheol, / and the years still owed me are withheld.
38:11 I said: Never will I see Yah, / Yah in the land of the living; / never again will I look on a human face / among those who dwell where everything stops.
38:12 My home is pulled up and carried off from me / like a herder's tent. / Like a weaver I have rolled up my life; / he snips me off the loom. / From dawn to dark you make an end of me.
38:13 I composed myself until morning; / like a lion he breaks all my bones. / From dawn to dark you make an end of me.
38:14 I twitter like a swift, like a crane, / and moan as a dove does; / my eyes give out from gazing on high. / Lord, I am hard pressed; / put up the bond for me!
38:15 What can I say? / He has spoken to me, and he has done it. / Softly I will walk through all my years, / remembering the bitterness I tasted.
38:16 Lord, on these things people live; / my spirit's life is in them all. / Restore my health and keep me alive!
38:17 Look, the bitter, bitter pain I had was for my good. / You loved my life away from the pit of nothing, / for you have flung behind your back / all my sins.
38:18 It is not Sheol that thanks you, / not death that praises you; / those who go down into the pit / do not wait in hope for your faithfulness.
38:19 The living, the living: they are the ones who thank you, / like me, today. / A father makes your faithfulness known / to his children.
38:20 The LORD is ready to save me! / So we will play my songs on the strings / every day we live, / at the LORD's house.
38:21 Isaiah said, "Let them take a lump of pressed figs and rub it on the sore, so that he may live."
38:22 And Hezekiah said, "What is the sign that I will go up to the LORD's house?"
## Envoys from Babylon
39:1 At that time Merodach-baladan son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah. He had heard that Hezekiah had been sick and had gotten well.
39:2 Hezekiah was glad to see them, and he gave them a tour of his treasure house: the silver and gold, the spices and fine oil, his whole arsenal, and everything to be found in his storerooms. In his house and in his whole kingdom, Hezekiah left nothing unshown.
39:3 Isaiah the prophet came to King Hezekiah. "What did these men say?" he asked. "And where have they come to you from?" "From a land far away," Hezekiah answered. "They came to me from Babylon."
39:4 He asked, "In your house, what did they see?" Hezekiah said, "Everything in my house, they saw. In my storerooms there was not a thing I didn't show them."
39:5 Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, "Hear the word of the LORD of Armies:
39:6 'Look, the days are coming. Everything in your house, all that your ancestors have piled up until today, will be hauled away to Babylon. Not a thing will remain, the LORD says.
39:7 Some of your own sons, born from your body, sons you will father, they will take. They will serve as eunuchs in the Babylonian king's palace.'"
39:8 Hezekiah answered Isaiah, "This word of the LORD that you have spoken is good." He went on, "For there will be peace and safety in my days."
