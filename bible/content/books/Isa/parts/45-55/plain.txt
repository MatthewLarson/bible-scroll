## Cyrus, the LORD's anointed
45:1 The LORD says this to his anointed, to Cyrus, / whose right hand I have gripped / to beat down nations in front of him / and unfasten the belts of kings, / to open double doors in front of him, / so that gates will not be shut:
45:2 I myself will go ahead of you / and level the swelling hills. / I will shatter doors of bronze / and hack through bars of iron.
45:3 I will give you treasures kept in the dark, / riches hidden away in secret places, / so that you will know that I am the LORD, / the God of Israel, who calls you by your name.
45:4 For the sake of Jacob my servant, / and Israel my chosen one, / I called you by your name. / I gave you a title of honor, though you did not know me.
45:5 I am the LORD, and there is no one else; / apart from me there is no God. / I will fasten your belt on you, though you did not know me,
45:6 so that from where the sun rises to where it sets / people will know that there is nothing except me. / I am the LORD, and there is no one else.
45:7 I shape the light and create the dark; / I make peace and create disaster. / I am the LORD, the maker of all these.
45:8 Heavens, let it drip from above; / clouds, let righteousness stream down. / Let the earth open, and salvation bear fruit; / let it make righteousness spring up with it. / I, the LORD, have created it.
## The clay and the potter
45:9 Woe to the one who argues with the one who formed him, / a piece of pottery among the pieces of pottery on the ground! / Does the clay say to the one who shapes it, "What are you making?" / Does your handiwork say, "He has no hands"?
45:10 Woe to the one who says to a father, "What are you fathering?" / or to a woman, "What are you in labor with?"
45:11 The LORD says this, / the Holy One of Israel, the one who formed him: / Will you question me about what is coming to my children? / Will you give me orders about the work of my hands?
45:12 I am the one who made the earth / and created humankind on it. / My own hands stretched out the heavens, / and I gave orders to all their army.
45:13 I am the one who stirred him up in righteousness, / and I will make all his roads level. / He will build my city / and set my exiles free, / not for a price and not for a payoff, / says the LORD of Armies.
## The nations come over to Zion
45:14 The LORD says this: / The wealth Egypt worked for, the goods Cush traded, / and the Sabeans, men of great height, / will come over to you and be yours. / They will walk behind you; they will come over in chains. / They will bow down to you; / they will plead with you: / "Surely God is among you, and there is no one else; / there are no other gods at all."
45:15 You are indeed a God who keeps himself hidden, / God of Israel, who saves.
45:16 They will be ashamed and disgraced, all of them; / the makers of idols will go off in disgrace together.
45:17 But Israel is saved by the LORD / with a salvation that lasts forever. / You will never be ashamed or disgraced, / not through all the ages.
## Turn to me and be saved
45:18 For the LORD says this, / who created the heavens, who is God, / who formed the earth and made it, / who set it firmly in place. / He did not create it to be shapeless; / he formed it to be lived in. / I am the LORD, and there is no one else.
45:19 I did not speak in secret, / somewhere in a land of darkness. / I did not say to Jacob's offspring, / "Look for me in the void." / I, the LORD, speak rightly; / I announce what is straight.
45:20 Gather and come; / come close, all together, you survivors of the nations! / Those who haul around the wood of their carved idol know nothing; / they pray to a god that does not save.
45:21 Speak up; bring your case forward; / yes, let them consult together. / Who announced this long ago? / Who told it from back then? / Wasn't it I, the LORD? / There is no other god besides me, / a God who is righteous and saves; / there is none except me.
45:22 Turn to me, all you ends of the earth, and be saved, / for I am God; there is no one else.
45:23 I have taken an oath by myself; / a word has gone out of my mouth in righteousness, / and it will not come back: / every knee will bend before me; / every tongue will take its oath.
45:24 "Only in the LORD," they will say of me, / "are saving acts and strength." / People will come to him, / and all who were furious with him will be ashamed.
45:25 Through the LORD all of Israel's offspring / will be declared in the right, and they will be proud.
## Babylon's gods carried off
46:1 Bel has bowed down; Nebo is doubled over. / Their idols have been loaded on animals and livestock. / The images you carried in procession are packed up as freight, / a heavy load for tired animals.
46:2 They are doubled over and bowed down together; / they could not rescue the load, / and they themselves have gone into captivity.
## I have carried you
46:3 Listen to me, house of Jacob, / all who are left of the house of Israel, / you whom I have carried since you were in the belly, / whom I have lifted since you were in the womb.
46:4 Till you grow old, I am he; / till your hair turns gray, I am the one who will carry you. / I made you, and I will bear you; / I will carry you, and I will get you out safe.
46:5 To whom will you compare me and make me equal? / Who will you set beside me, as if we were alike?
46:6 They tip gold out of the bag / and weigh silver on a balance; / they pay a metalworker to make a god of it, / and they kneel before it; they bow to the ground.
46:7 They hoist it on their shoulders and haul it, / and put it down where it belongs, and it stays standing; / it will not budge from its place. / If anyone cries to it, it gives no answer; / it rescues no one from distress.
46:8 Remember this and hold steady; / bring it back to mind, you rebels.
46:9 Remember the first things, from long ago: / I am God, and there is no one else; / I am God, and there is none like me.
46:10 From the beginning I announce the end, / and from ancient times what has not yet been done. / I say, "My plan will hold, / and whatever I want, I will do."
46:11 I call a bird of prey from the east, / from a far-off land the man who carries out my plan. / I have spoken, and I will bring it about; / I have shaped it, and I will do it.
46:12 Listen to me, you stubborn of heart, / you who are far from righteousness.
46:13 I am bringing my righteousness near; it is not far away, / and my salvation will not be late. / I will place salvation in Zion, / my splendor for Israel.
## Babylon comes down from her throne
47:1 Come down and sit in the dust, / virgin Daughter Babylon. / Sit down on the earth; there is no throne for you, / Daughter of the Chaldeans. / For people will never again call you / gentle and dainty.
47:2 Take a hand mill and grind meal. / Lift off your veil; / pull up your skirt; uncover your thighs; / cross the rivers.
47:3 Your nakedness will be exposed; / yes, your disgrace will be seen. / I will take revenge, / and no human being will stand in my way.
47:4 Our redeemer, the LORD of Armies is his name, / the Holy One of Israel.
47:5 Sit in silence and go into the dark, / Daughter of the Chaldeans. / For people will never again call you / the Lady of the kingdoms.
47:6 I was furious with my people; / I treated my own inheritance as common, / and I put them into your hands. / You did not show them any pity; / you laid your yoke very heavy even on the old.
47:7 You said, "I will be the lady forever, for all time." / You did not take these things to heart / or remember how they would end.
## Widowed in one day
47:8 So now, listen to this, you lover of luxury, / who sit there feeling secure, / who say in your heart, / "I am, and besides me there is nothing. / I will never sit as a widow / or know what it is to lose my children."
47:9 In an instant, on one day, / the two will both come to you: / childlessness and widowhood. / They will fall on you with all their weight, / for all your many sorceries, / for all the strength of your spells.
47:10 You trusted your own evil; / you thought, "Nobody can see me." / It was your wisdom and your learning / that turned you aside, / when you told yourself, / "I am, and besides me there is nothing."
47:11 Disaster will come on you, / and you will not know how to charm it away. / Ruin will fall on you, / and you will not be able to buy it off. / Destruction will come on you suddenly, / something you do not see coming.
47:12 Stand up, please, with your charms / and your crowd of sorceries, / the ones you have toiled over since your youth. / Perhaps you can get some use from them; / perhaps you can strike fear.
47:13 You have worn yourself out with all your advisers. / Let them stand up, please, and save you, / the ones who map out the sky, / who stare at the stars, / who announce at every new moon / what is about to come on you.
47:14 Look, they are like straw, / and fire burns them up; / they cannot get their own lives out / of the flame's grip. / This is no glowing coal to warm by, / no fire to sit beside.
47:15 That is all they will be to you, / the ones you have toiled with, / who traded with you since your youth. / Each one wanders off his own way; / there is no one to save you.
## Told ahead, so you could not credit idols
48:1 Listen to this, house of Jacob, / you who are called by the name of Israel, / who came from the waters of Judah, / who swear by the name of the LORD / and call on the God of Israel, / though it is not honest and not right.
48:2 They name themselves after the holy city / and prop themselves on the God of Israel; / the LORD of Armies is his name.
48:3 The first things I announced long ago; / they came out of my mouth, and I made them known. / Then suddenly I acted, and they happened.
48:4 I knew that you are hard; / your neck is a cord of iron, / and your brow is bronze.
48:5 So I announced them to you long ago; / before they happened I let you hear them, / so that you could not say, "My idol did them; / my carved image and my cast image ordered them."
48:6 You have heard it; look at all of it! / Will you not declare it? / From now on I am letting you hear new things, / hidden things that you have not known.
48:7 They are created now, not long ago; / before today you had not heard of them, / so that you could not say, "Yes, I knew them."
48:8 You have not heard and you have not known; / from long ago your ear has not been open. / For I knew that you would surely betray, / and that you were called a rebel from the womb.
48:9 For my name's sake I am slow to anger; / for the sake of my praise I keep myself in check for you, / so that I will not cut you down.
48:10 Look, I have smelted you, though not as silver; / I have chosen you in the furnace of misery.
48:11 For my sake, my own sake, I will do it; / how could I let it be profaned? / I do not hand my glory to anyone else.
## The first and the last calls Cyrus
48:12 Listen to me, Jacob, / and Israel, my called one: / I am he. / I am first; I am last as well.
48:13 My hand, too, set the earth on its foundation, / and my right hand spanned the heavens. / I call to them, / and they stand up together.
48:14 Gather, all of you, and listen! / Which of them announced these things? / The LORD loves him; / he will carry out his will against Babylon, / and his arm will fall on the Chaldeans.
48:15 I, I myself, have spoken; / yes, I have called him. / I have brought him, / and he will succeed on his way.
48:16 Come near to me and hear this: / from the beginning I have not spoken in secret; / from the time it came to be, I have been there. / And now the Lord GOD has sent me, and his Spirit.
## If only you had listened
48:17 The LORD says this, / your redeemer, the Holy One of Israel: / I am the LORD your God, / who teaches you what will help you, / who guides you along the road you are to walk.
48:18 Oh, that you had listened to my commands! / Your peace would have flowed like a river, / your righteousness rolled in like the waves of the sea.
48:19 Your children would have been as many as the sand, / the offspring of your body like its grains; / their name would not be cut off / or destroyed from my presence.
## Go out from Babylon
48:20 Go out from Babylon! / Run from the Chaldeans! / Announce it with a shout of joy; make it heard; / carry it out as far as the earth's end. / Say, "The LORD has redeemed his servant Jacob!"
48:21 When he led them through the wastelands, they did not go thirsty. / He made water trickle from a rock for them; / he split the rock, and out poured the water.
48:22 The wicked have no peace, says the LORD.
## The servant: a light to the nations
49:1 Listen to me, you islands; / pay attention, you peoples far away. / The LORD called me from the womb; / from my mother's body he said my name aloud.
49:2 He made my mouth a sharp sword / and hid me in the shade of his hand. / He made me a sharpened arrow / and tucked me away in his quiver.
49:3 He said to me, "You are my servant, / Israel; through you I will win glory."
49:4 But I said, "I have worked hard for nothing; / I have used up my strength on emptiness and air. / Yet my right is with the LORD, / and my wages are with my God."
49:5 And now the LORD says, / the one who formed me in the womb to be his servant, / to bring Jacob back to him, / so that Israel would be gathered to him. / I am honored in the LORD's sight, / and my God has become my strength.
49:6 He says, "It is not enough that you should be my servant / to set the tribes of Jacob on their feet / and bring home the ones of Israel I have kept safe. / I will give you as a light to the nations, / so that you may be my salvation / to the farthest end of the earth."
49:7 The LORD says this, / the redeemer of Israel, its Holy One, / to one whom people despise, / whom the nation loathes, / a slave of rulers: / Kings will look and rise to their feet; / princes, too, and they will bow low, / for the sake of the LORD, who keeps faith, / the Holy One of Israel; he has chosen you.
## The day of salvation
49:8 The LORD says this: / When the time of favor came, I answered you; / when the day of salvation came, I helped you. / I will keep you safe / and make you a covenant for the people, / to raise the land up again, / to hand back the plots that lie in ruins as their inheritance,
49:9 to tell the prisoners, "Out you come," / and those in darkness, "Step into the open." / They will feed along the roads, / and every bare height will be their grazing land.
49:10 They will not go hungry or thirsty; / no scorching wind or sun will strike them, / for the one who pities them will lead them / and guide them to springs of water.
49:11 I will turn all my mountains into a road, / and my highways will be raised up.
49:12 Look, these will come from far away, / and look, these from the north and from the west, / and these from the land of Syene.
49:13 Heavens, shout with joy! / Earth, be glad! / Mountains, break out into happy cries! / The LORD has comforted his people, / and he takes pity on his poor ones.
## "The LORD has forgotten me"
49:14 Zion said, "The LORD has left me; / the Lord has forgotten me."
49:15 Can a woman forget her nursing baby / and have no pity for the child of her womb? / Yes, these might forget, / but as for me, I will never forget you.
49:16 Look, I have carved you on the palms of my hands; / your walls are in my sight at all times.
49:17 Your children are hurrying home, / while those who wrecked you and laid you waste march out of you.
49:18 Raise your eyes and look around you: / they have all gathered; they are coming to you. / By my life, the LORD declares, / you will put them all on like jewels / and fasten them on as a bride does.
49:19 For your ruins and your empty places / and your wrecked land / will now be too cramped for the people living there, / and those who swallowed you up will be far away.
49:20 The children of the years when you were bereaved / will still say where you can hear, / "There is no room here for me; / move over so that I can live here."
49:21 Then you will think, / "Who gave me these? / I had lost my children and was barren, / an exile, pushed aside. / Who brought these up? / I was left by myself. / These, where have they been?"
## Kings as guardians
49:22 The Lord GOD says this: / Look, I will raise my hand toward the nations / and lift up my flag for the peoples; / they will bring your sons held close to their chests, / and your daughters will ride on their shoulders.
49:23 Kings will be your guardians, / and their princesses your wet nurses. / Faces to the earth, they will bow to you / and lick up the dust of your feet. / And you will know that I am the LORD: / no one who waits for me ends up ashamed.
49:24 Can loot be snatched from a warrior? / Can a tyrant's captives slip away?
49:25 But the LORD says this: / Even a warrior's captive will be taken back, / and the loot of a tyrant will slip away. / I will take on the one who takes you on, / and your children I will rescue.
49:26 I will feed your oppressors their own flesh; / they will drink their own blood like sweet wine until they are drunk. / And all flesh will know / that I am the LORD, your savior, / your redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.
## The mother sent away
50:1 The LORD says this: / Where are the divorce papers / I gave your mother when I sent her away? / Which of my creditors / did I sell you to? / No, it was for your guilt that you were sold, / for your rebellions that your mother was sent off.
50:2 Why, when I came, was nobody there? / Why, when I called, did nobody answer? / Is my hand too short to ransom you? / Do I lack the power to rescue? / Look: I shout, and the sea dries up; / I make rivers into wilderness; / their fish stink because the water is gone, / and they die of thirst.
50:3 I dress the heavens in black / and give them sackcloth for a cloak.
## The servant who listens
50:4 The Lord GOD has given me a learner's tongue, / so that I can know how to hold up / a tired person with a word. / Each morning he wakes me; / he rouses my ear / to listen the way learners do.
50:5 The Lord GOD has opened my ear, / and I did not resist; / I did not pull back.
50:6 My back I gave to the floggers, / my cheeks to those who tore at my beard. / I did not cover my face / against insult and spit.
50:7 The Lord GOD is my help; / that is why I have not been humiliated. / That is why I have made my face hard as flint; / I know I will not end up ashamed.
50:8 Near at hand is the one who declares me in the right. / Who wants to argue with me? Let us stand side by side in court. / Who is my opponent? Let him step up to me.
50:9 Look, the Lord GOD will help me; / who is it that will condemn me? / Look, all of them will fall apart like old clothes, / and the moth will eat them.
50:10 Who among you fears the LORD / and listens to the voice of his servant? / Whoever walks in the dark / with no light at all, / let him trust in the name of the LORD / and lean on his God.
50:11 But look, all of you who light a fire / and arm yourselves with flaming arrows: / walk in the glow of your fire / and among the arrows you have set ablaze. / This is what you will get from my hand: / you will lie down in pain.
## Look to the rock you were cut from
51:1 Listen to me, you who chase after righteousness, / you who seek the LORD: / look at the rock that you were hewn out of, / at the hollow of the pit that you were dug from.
51:2 Look at Abraham, your ancestor, / and at Sarah, who went through labor to bear you. / He was one man alone when I called him; / I blessed him, and I multiplied him.
51:3 For the LORD comforts Zion; / he comforts all her ruins. / He makes her wilderness like Eden, / her desert like the garden of the LORD. / Delight and joy will be found there, / songs of thanks and the sound of music.
51:4 Pay attention to me, my people; / listen to me, my nation. / For teaching will go out from me, / and I will settle my justice as a light for the peoples.
51:5 My righteousness is near; / my salvation has set out, / and my arms will judge the peoples. / The islands wait for me / and hope in my arm.
51:6 Raise your eyes to the heavens / and look down at the earth: / the heavens will blow away like smoke, / the earth wear thin like clothing, / and the people on it die off like gnats. / But my salvation will be there forever; / my righteousness will not be shattered.
51:7 Listen to me, you who know righteousness, / you people who have my teaching in your hearts: / do not fear the scorn of mere mortals; / do not be shaken by their insults.
51:8 The moth will chew them like a garment; / the grub will chew them like wool. / But my righteousness stays forever, / and my salvation from generation to generation.
## Wake up, arm of the LORD
51:9 Wake up, wake up, put on strength, / arm of the LORD! / Wake up as in days long ago, / in generations long past. / Are you not the one that hacked Rahab apart, / that ran the dragon through?
51:10 Are you not the one that dried up the sea, / the waters of the great deep, / that made the depths of the sea a road / for the redeemed to cross?
51:11 The ransomed of the LORD will return / and come to Zion with joyful shouting; / lasting gladness will be on their heads. / Joy and gladness will catch up with them, / and grief and groaning will run away.
## I am the one who comforts you
51:12 It is I, I myself, who comfort you. / Who are you to be afraid of a man who will die, / of a human being who will be made like grass?
51:13 You forget the LORD who made you, / who spread out the heavens and founded the earth, / and all day, every day, you are afraid / of the rage of the oppressor / as he gets ready to destroy. / Where is that oppressor's rage now?
51:14 The one stooped under his load will quickly be let loose; / he will not die and go down to the Pit; / his bread will not run out.
51:15 For I am the LORD your God, / who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar; / the LORD of Armies is his name.
51:16 I have set my words in your mouth / and sheltered you under the shadow of my hand, / so as to plant the heavens and found the earth / and tell Zion, "You are my people."
## The cup that makes you reel
51:17 Rouse yourself, rouse yourself! / Get up, Jerusalem, / you who drank from the LORD's hand / the cup of his fury, / you who drank the bowl, the cup that makes you reel, / and drained it to the bottom.
51:18 No one leads her, / out of all the sons she gave birth to; / no one holds her hand, / out of all the sons she brought up.
51:19 Two things have happened to you, / and who will shake his head with you? / Wreck and ruin, famine and sword: / how can I comfort you?
51:20 Your sons have passed out; / they lie at the top of every street / like an antelope in a net, / filled up with the LORD's fury, / with your God's rebuke.
51:21 So please hear this, you poor thing, / you who are drunk, though not from wine.
51:22 Your Lord, the LORD, says this, / your God who argues his people's case: / Look, I have taken out of your hand / the cup that makes you reel, / the bowl, the cup of my fury; / you will never drink it again.
51:23 I will hand it to those who torment you, / who told you, "Get down on your face so we can cross over you," / so that you laid your back flat like the ground, / like a road for travelers to walk on.
## Wake up, Zion
52:1 Wake up, wake up, put on your strength, Zion! / Put on your beautiful clothes, / Jerusalem, the holy city! / For the uncircumcised and the unclean / will never come into you again.
52:2 Shake the dust off; / rise and sit, Jerusalem. / Loosen the chains from your neck, / captive Daughter Zion.
52:3 For the LORD says this: / For nothing you were sold, / and not with silver will you be bought back.
52:4 For the Lord GOD says this: / At first my people went down to Egypt to live there as foreigners; / then Assyria oppressed them for no reason.
52:5 So what is there for me here now? the LORD declares. / My people have been taken away for nothing. / Their rulers howl, the LORD declares, / and all day long, without stopping, / my name is treated with contempt.
52:6 So my people will know my name; / so on that day they will know / that I am he, the one who speaks: / here I am.
## Your God is King
52:7 How lovely on the mountains / are the feet of the one who brings good news, / who makes peace heard, / who brings news of good things, / who makes salvation heard, / who tells Zion, "Your God is King!"
52:8 The voice of your lookouts! They lift their voices / and cry out for joy together, / for they will see it eye to eye / when the LORD comes back to Zion.
52:9 Ruins of Jerusalem, / burst out together into joyful cries! / The LORD has comforted his people; / he has bought Jerusalem back.
52:10 The LORD has stripped his holy arm bare / in the sight of every nation, / and every far end of the earth / will see how our God saves.
52:11 Go away, go away, get out of there! / Touch nothing unclean. / Get out from the middle of her and keep yourselves pure, / you who carry the LORD's vessels.
52:12 But you will not go out in a rush / or leave running away, / for the LORD will go ahead of you, / and the God of Israel will guard your rear.
## The servant raised high
52:13 Look, my servant will succeed; / he will be high and lifted up, / and very exalted.
52:14 Just as many were horrified at you, / his look was so marred, more than any man's, / and his form, more than any human's,
52:15 so he will startle many nations; / kings will shut their mouths at him. / For they will see what no one had told them, / and understand what they had never heard.
53:1 Who has believed what we have heard? / And the LORD's arm: to whom has it been shown?
53:2 He came up before him like a little shoot, / like a root from parched ground. / He had no stately form, no grandeur, that we should look at him, / no good looks that we should want him.
53:3 He was despised, and people kept away from him, / a man of pains who knew sickness well. / He was like someone people hide their faces from; / he was despised, and we counted him as nothing.
53:4 Yet it was our sicknesses that he carried, / our pains that he bore. / But we thought of him as stricken, / struck down by God and humbled.
53:5 But he was pierced for our rebellions, / crushed for our guilt. / The discipline that made us whole was on him, / and through his welts we have healing.
53:6 We had all strayed like sheep, / every one of us gone off his own way, / and the LORD made the guilt of us all / fall on him.
53:7 He was mistreated, and he bowed under it, / yet he did not open his mouth. / Like a lamb that is taken off to slaughter, / like a ewe that falls silent in front of her shearers, / he did not open his mouth.
53:8 Through arrest and sentence he was taken off, / and who of his generation gave it a thought? / He was cut off from the land of the living; / because my people rebelled, the blow fell on him.
53:9 They gave him a burial place among the wicked, / and with a rich man in his death, / though he had done nothing violent, / and his mouth held no deceit.
53:10 But the LORD desired to crush him / and made him sick. / If he gives his life as a guilt offering, / he will see offspring; he will lengthen his days, / and what the LORD desires will succeed in his hand.
53:11 Out of the anguish of his life he will see light and be satisfied. / By his knowledge the righteous one, my servant, will make the many righteous, / and he will shoulder their guilt himself.
53:12 So I will give him his share with the many, / and with the mighty he will split the spoil, / because he emptied out his life to the point of death / and was counted with the rebels. / He bore the sin of many, / and he will step in for the rebels.
## Sing, barren one
54:1 Sing for joy, you barren one who has borne no child! / Break out into a joyful song and shout, / you who have not gone into labor! / For the children of the desolate one / will outnumber the children of the married one, says the LORD.
54:2 Widen the space of your tent; / let them spread out the curtains of your homes. / Don't hold back. / Make your tent ropes longer / and your tent pegs stronger.
54:3 For you will spread out to the right and to the left; / your offspring will drive out nations / and settle people in the deserted towns.
54:4 Don't be afraid; you will not be put to shame. / Don't hang your head; you will not be disgraced. / The shame of when you were young you will forget, / and the insults of your widowhood you will remember no more.
54:5 For your husband is your Maker; / the LORD of Armies is his name. / The Holy One of Israel is your redeemer; / he is called the God of the whole earth.
54:6 For the LORD has called you / like a wife abandoned and sick at heart, / like a young man's bride who has been rejected, says your God.
54:7 For a little moment I abandoned you, / but with great tenderness I will gather you in.
54:8 When my anger overflowed / I hid my face from you a moment, / but with a loyal love that never ends / I will show you tender mercy, / says the LORD, your redeemer.
54:9 To me this is like the waters of Noah: / as I swore that Noah's waters / would never again sweep over the earth, / so I have sworn not to be angry with you / and not to rebuke you.
54:10 The mountains may move away / and the hills may shake, / but my loyal love will not move away from you, / and my covenant of peace will not be shaken, / says the LORD, the one who pities you.
## A city built of jewels
54:11 You afflicted one, storm-tossed and not comforted, / look, I will set your stones in dark mortar / and lay your foundations with lapis lazuli.
54:12 I will make your battlements of rubies, / your gates of sparkling stones, / and your whole boundary wall of precious stones.
54:13 All your children will be the LORD's learners, / and your children's peace will be great.
54:14 You will be set firm in righteousness. / Stay far from oppression, for you will have nothing to fear, / and from terror, for it will not come near you.
54:15 If anyone stirs up trouble, it is not from me; / whoever stirs up trouble with you will fall because of you.
54:16 Look, I created the metalsmith / who blows on the coals / and brings out a tool for his trade; / and I created the destroyer to ruin.
54:17 No weapon made against you will succeed, / and you will prove wrong every tongue / that rises against you in court. / This is the inheritance of the LORD's servants, / and their vindication comes from me, the LORD declares.
## Come to the water
55:1 Hey! All you who are thirsty, come to the water! / And you who have no money, / come, buy grain and eat! / Come, buy wine and milk / without silver, without a price.
55:2 Why pay out silver for something that is not bread, / your wages for what leaves you hungry? / Listen to me carefully, and eat what is good, / and your appetite will revel in rich food.
55:3 Turn your ear and come to me; / listen, and you will come alive. / I will make you a covenant that lasts forever: / the sure loyal love of David.
55:4 Look, I made him a witness to the peoples, / a leader and commander for the peoples.
55:5 Look, you will call a nation you do not know, / and a nation that does not know you will run to you, / because of the LORD your God, / the Holy One of Israel, for he has given you splendor.
## Seek the LORD while he lets himself be found
55:6 Look for the LORD while he lets himself be found; / call to him while he is close.
55:7 Let the wicked man give up his way, / and the man of trouble his schemes; / let him come back to the LORD, who will show him mercy, / to our God, who forgives over and over.
55:8 For my thoughts are not your thoughts, / and your ways are not my ways, the LORD declares.
55:9 For as high as the heavens are above the earth, / so high are my ways above your ways, / and my thoughts above your thoughts.
55:10 For as the rain and the snow come down from the sky / and do not go back there / until they have watered the earth / and made it give birth and sprout, / giving seed to the one who sows / and bread to the one who eats,
55:11 so will my word be that goes out from my mouth: / it will not come back to me empty, / but it will do what I want / and succeed in what I sent it for.
55:12 You will set out in gladness / and be brought home in peace. / Before you the mountains and hills will break out in shouts of joy, / and every tree in the countryside will clap its hands.
55:13 Instead of the thornbush, the cypress will grow; / instead of the nettle, the myrtle will grow. / This will make a name for the LORD, / a lasting sign that will never be cut down.
