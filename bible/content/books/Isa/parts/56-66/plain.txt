## Foreigners and eunuchs in God's house
56:1 The LORD says this: / "Keep justice and practice righteousness, / for my salvation is close to coming, / and my righteousness to being revealed.
56:2 Happy is the mortal who does this, / the human being who holds tight to it, / who keeps the Sabbath and does not profane it, / and keeps his hand from doing any evil."
56:3 The foreigner who has joined himself to the LORD must not say, / "The LORD is sure to separate me from his people." / And the eunuch must not say, / "Look at me, I am a dried-up tree."
56:4 For the LORD says this: / "To the eunuchs who keep my Sabbaths, / who choose what I take pleasure in / and hold tight to my covenant,
56:5 in my house and inside my walls / I will set up for them a marker and a name / that is better than sons and daughters. / I will give each of them a name for all time, / one that will not be cut off.
56:6 And the foreigners who join themselves to the LORD, / to minister to him, / to love the name of the LORD / and to be his servants, / everyone who keeps the Sabbath and does not profane it / and holds tight to my covenant,
56:7 I will bring them to my holy mountain / and make them glad in my house of prayer. / Their burnt offerings and their sacrifices / will be welcome on my altar, / for my house will be called / a house of prayer for all the peoples."
56:8 The Lord GOD, who gathers the banished of Israel, declares: / "Still more I will gather to him, / to add to his gathered ones."
## Blind watchmen and greedy shepherds
56:9 Every wild animal of the field, come and eat, / every animal in the woods!
56:10 Its watchmen are blind, / all of them, and they know nothing. / They are all dogs without a voice, / unable to bark. / They lie dreaming, stretched out, / fond of their sleep.
56:11 The dogs are fierce in their hunger; / they never know when they have had enough. / And these are the shepherds! / They do not know how to discern. / Every one of them has turned off down his own road, / each after his own profit, to the last man.
56:12 "Come, let me get wine, / and let us swill beer! / Tomorrow will be just like today, / a great day, with plenty and more!"
57:1 The righteous one perishes, / and no one gives it a thought. / Loyal men are taken away, / and no one understands / that the righteous one is taken away / from the presence of evil.
57:2 He goes into peace. / Those who walked straight ahead / rest on their beds.
## Children of a fortune-teller
57:3 But you, come over here, / you sons of a fortune-teller, / offspring of an adulterer and of a woman who plays the prostitute!
57:4 Who is it you laugh at? / Who is it you make faces at, / with your mouths gaping and your tongues hanging out? / Are you not children born of rebellion, / offspring of a lie?
57:5 You heat yourselves with lust among the great trees, / under every leafy tree. / You slaughter children in the ravines, / beneath the clefts in the rocks.
57:6 Among the smooth stones of the ravine is your share; / they, they are your lot. / To them you have poured out a drink offering; / you have brought up a grain offering. / Should I be content with these things?
57:7 You have spread your bed / on a mountain high and towering, / and you climbed up there too / to make sacrifices.
57:8 Behind the door and the doorpost / you have set up your reminder. / For you left me and uncovered your bed. / You climbed into it and made it wide. / You struck a bargain for yourself with them. / You loved their bed; / you gazed on a hand.
57:9 You went down to the king with oil / and put on more and more of your perfumes. / You sent your envoys far away, / and sent them down as deep as Sheol.
57:10 You wore yourself out with all your traveling, / yet you did not say, "It is hopeless." / You found new life in your strength, / so you did not grow weak.
57:11 Whom did you dread and fear, / that you lied to me, / and did not remember me / or give me a thought? / Is it not because I have kept silent, and that for a long time, / that you do not fear me?
57:12 I myself will make known your righteousness / and your deeds, / and they will not help you.
57:13 When you scream for help, / let your collection save you! / A wind will lift them all away; / a puff of air will carry them off. / But the one who shelters in me will receive the land as an inheritance / and take possession of my holy mountain.
## Comfort for the humble
57:14 And someone will say, / "Heap it up, heap it up, clear the way! / Lift every stumbling block / out of my people's road!"
57:15 For the One who is high and lifted up says this, / the One who lives for all time, and Holy is his name: / "In the height and in holiness I dwell, / and with the crushed and the lowly in spirit, / to put life back into the spirit of the lowly / and life back into the heart of the crushed.
57:16 I will not go on suing forever, / nor stay angry for all time, / or else the spirit would sink away before me, / the living breaths that I have made.
57:17 Because of the sin of his greed I was angry, / and I struck him. / I hid my face and was angry, / but he went on turning away, / down the road of his own heart.
57:18 His ways I have seen, yet I will heal him. / I will guide him / and pay him back with comfort, / him and those among him who mourn.
57:19 I am the one who creates the fruit of lips: / 'Peace, peace, to the one who is far and the one who is near,' / says the LORD, 'and I will heal him.'"
57:20 But the wicked are like the churning sea / that is never able to lie still; / its waters churn up mud and slime.
57:21 "The wicked have no peace," says my God.
## The fast the LORD chooses
58:1 Call out from the throat, do not let up! / Lift your voice like a ram's horn! / Tell my people of their rebellion, / the house of Jacob of their sins.
58:2 They come looking for me every single day / and take pleasure in knowing my ways, / as if they were a nation that practiced righteousness / and had not forsaken the justice of its God. / They ask me for righteous rulings / and take pleasure in drawing near to God.
58:3 "Why did we fast, and you did not see? / Why did we humble ourselves, and you did not notice?" / Look, on the day of your fast you go after your own business / and drive all your workers hard.
58:4 Look, you fast only to quarrel and fight, / and to hit with a wicked fist. / Fasting the way you do today / will not make your voice heard on high.
58:5 Is a fast like this the one I would choose, / a day when someone humbles himself, / hangs his head like a bulrush / and spreads out sackcloth and ashes to lie on? / Do you call this a fast, / a day that pleases the LORD?
58:6 Is this not the fasting I choose: / to unlock the fetters of wickedness, / to loosen the ropes of the yoke, / to send the crushed away free, / and to snap every yoke in two?
58:7 Is it not to break your bread for the hungry, / and to take the afflicted and the uprooted under a roof? / If you see someone naked, cover him, / and do not hide yourself from your own flesh and blood.
58:8 Then like the dawn your light will burst through, / and your healing will sprout up fast. / Your righteousness will walk in front of you, / and the LORD's glory will gather you in from behind.
58:9 Then you will call out, and the LORD will answer. / You will shout for help, and he will say, "I am here." / If you remove the yoke from among you, / the jabbing finger and the wicked word,
58:10 if you offer your own self to the hungry / and fill the craving of the afflicted, / then light will shine out for you in the dark, / and your gloom will turn as bright as noon.
58:11 The LORD will lead you at all times / and fill your hunger in the scorched lands. / He will put strength into your bones. / You will be like a garden soaked with water, / like a spring of water / whose waters do not lie.
58:12 People from among you will build the ruins of long ago; / you will set up foundations laid generation after generation. / You will be called the Mender of the Gap in the Wall, / the Restorer of Roads to Live Along.
58:13 If you turn back your foot from the Sabbath, / from doing your own business on my holy day, / if you call the Sabbath a delight, / the LORD's holy day honored, / and you honor it by not going your own ways, / not pursuing your own business or talking on and on,
58:14 then the LORD will be your delight, / and I will set you riding over the land's high places. / I will feed you with the inheritance of your father Jacob. / The mouth of the LORD has spoken.
## Sin that separates
59:1 Look, the LORD's hand has not grown too short to save, / and his ear has not grown too heavy to hear.
59:2 No, it is your wrongdoings that have split you off from your God. / Your sins have hidden his face from you, / so that he will not listen.
59:3 For your hands are stained with blood, / and your fingers with wrongdoing. / Your lips have spoken lies; / your tongue mutters what is crooked.
59:4 No one sues for what is right; / no one goes to court honestly. / They trust in nothing and speak what is empty. / They conceive trouble and give birth to wickedness.
59:5 They hatch the eggs of vipers / and weave spiders' webs. / Whoever eats their eggs will die, / and if one is crushed, a snake hatches out.
59:6 Their webs will not make clothing; / they cannot cover themselves with what they make. / What they make is wickedness, / and acts of violence are in their hands.
59:7 Their feet run to evil, / and they hurry to shed innocent blood. / Their thoughts are thoughts of wickedness; / ruin and wreckage lie along their highways.
59:8 They do not know the way of peace, / and there is no justice on their tracks. / They have made their paths crooked for themselves; / whoever sets foot on them never knows peace.
## The people's confession
59:9 That is why justice is far from us, / and righteousness does not catch up with us. / We hope for light, and look, darkness! / For brightness, but we walk in deep gloom.
59:10 We grope at the wall like blind men; / like those who have no eyes we grope. / At noon we trip as if dusk had come. / Among the hale and hearty we are like the dead.
59:11 All of us growl like bears; / we moan and moan like doves. / We hope for justice, but there is none; / for salvation, but it is far from us.
59:12 For our rebellions before you are many, / and our sins speak up against us. / Our rebellions are with us, / and we know our wrongdoings:
59:13 rebelling and denying the LORD, / turning back from following our God, / speaking oppression and revolt, / conceiving lies and muttering them from the heart.
59:14 Justice has been shoved backward, / and righteousness keeps its distance. / For truth has tripped in the public square, / and uprightness cannot get in.
59:15 Truth is missing, / and whoever turns from evil is plundered. / The LORD saw it, / and it was evil in his eyes / that there was no justice.
## The LORD arms himself
59:16 He looked, and no one was there; / he was shocked that no one stepped in. / So his own arm brought him victory, / and his own righteousness held him up.
59:17 Righteousness he wore as his armor, / with salvation as a helmet on his head. / He dressed in vengeance for his clothing / and threw on zeal like a cloak.
59:18 As their deeds deserve, so he will repay: / fury to his foes, / payback to his enemies. / To the islands he will pay back what they are owed.
59:19 From the west they will fear the name of the LORD, / and from the rising of the sun, his glory. / For he will come like a river in a narrow channel, / driven on by the wind of the LORD.
59:20 "A redeemer will come to Zion, / to those in Jacob who turn from rebellion," / the LORD declares.
59:21 "As for me, this is my covenant with them," says the LORD. "My spirit, which is on you, and my words that I have put in your mouth, will not leave your mouth, or the mouth of your children, or the mouth of your children's children," says the LORD, "from now on and forever."
## Zion's light
60:1 Rise up and shine, for your light has come; / the glory of the LORD has dawned over you.
60:2 For look, darkness will blanket the earth, / and deep gloom the peoples, / but over you the LORD will dawn, / and his glory will be seen above you.
60:3 Nations will walk toward your light, / and kings toward the brightness of your sunrise.
60:4 Raise your eyes and look around you: / they have all gathered; they are coming to you. / Your sons arrive from far away, / and your daughters are carried at the hip.
60:5 Then you will see and glow with joy; / your heart will tremble and open wide, / for the riches of the sea will be turned toward you, / and the wealth of nations will come to you.
60:6 A flood of camels will cover you, / young camels from Midian and Ephah. / All of them will come from Sheba, / carrying gold and frankincense / and announcing the praises of the LORD.
60:7 Every flock of Kedar will be herded to you; / the rams of Nebaioth will be at your service. / They will go up on my altar and be accepted, / and I will make my splendid house more splendid still.
60:8 Who are these, flying along like a cloud, / like doves to their nesting holes?
60:9 For the islands wait for me, / the Tarshish ships first of all, / to bring your sons from far away, / and their silver and their gold with them, / for the name of the LORD your God, / for the Holy One of Israel, / because he has made you glorious.
60:10 Foreigners will build your walls back up, / and their kings will wait on you. / For in my fury I struck you, / but in my goodwill I have had pity on you.
60:11 Your gates will stand open all the time; / day and night they will not be shut, / so that the wealth of nations can be brought to you, / with their kings led in.
60:12 For the nation and the kingdom that will not serve you will perish; / those nations will be laid waste completely.
60:13 The splendor of Lebanon will come to you, / the cypress, the plane tree and the pine together, / to make the place of my sanctuary beautiful, / and I will honor the place where my feet rest.
60:14 The sons of those who crushed you will come to you bent low, / and all who looked down on you will bow at the soles of your feet. / They will call you the City of the LORD, / the Zion of the Holy One of Israel.
60:15 Where once you were abandoned and hated, / with no one passing through, / I will make you a pride that lasts forever, / a joy for generation after generation.
60:16 You will suck the milk of nations; / you will nurse at the breasts of kings. / And you will know that I am the LORD, your savior, / your redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.
60:17 In place of bronze I will bring gold, / in place of iron I will bring silver, / in place of wood, bronze, / and in place of stones, iron. / I will make peace your governor / and righteousness your taskmaster.
60:18 Violence will be heard in your land no more, / nor ruin and wreckage within your borders. / You will name your walls Salvation / and your gates Praise.
60:19 No longer will the sun be your light by day, / nor will the moon give you brightness with its glow. / The LORD will be your light forever, / and your God will be your beauty.
60:20 Your sun will not go down again, / and your moon will not withdraw, / for the LORD will be your light forever, / and the days of your mourning will be over.
60:21 Your people will all be righteous; / they will own the land forever. / They are the sprout of my planting, / what my hands have made, for me to be glorified.
60:22 The smallest will grow into a thousand, / and the least into a mighty nation. / I am the LORD; / in its own time I will hurry it on.
## Good news for the humble
61:1 The Spirit of the Lord GOD is on me, / because the LORD has anointed me / to bring good news to the humble. / He has sent me to bandage the broken in heart, / to proclaim release to the captives / and an opening wide for those who are bound,
61:2 to proclaim a year of the LORD's goodwill / and a day of our God's vengeance, / to comfort all who mourn,
61:3 to appoint for Zion's mourners, / to give them a garland instead of ashes, / oil of rejoicing in place of mourning, / a cloak of praise in place of a faint spirit. / People will call them Oaks of Righteousness, / the LORD's planting, to show his splendor.
61:4 They will build the ruins of long ago / and raise up places laid waste in former times. / They will make the ruined cities new, / places lying waste generation after generation.
61:5 Outsiders will stand and tend your flocks, / and foreigners will be your farmers and vineyard workers.
61:6 But you will be called the priests of the LORD; / people will speak of you as the ministers of our God. / You will eat the wealth of nations, / and you will take pride in their riches.
61:7 Instead of your shame, a double share; / instead of disgrace, they will shout for joy over their portion. / So in their land they will possess a double share, / and joy that lasts forever will be theirs.
61:8 For I, the LORD, love justice; / I hate robbery done with crookedness. / I will pay them their wages faithfully / and make a covenant with them that lasts forever.
61:9 Their children will be famous among the nations, / and their descendants among the peoples. / Everyone who sees them will recognize / that they are children the LORD has blessed.
61:10 I will be full of joy in the LORD; / my whole being will shout for joy in my God. / For he has dressed me in clothes of salvation; / he has wrapped me in a robe of righteousness, / like a bridegroom who wears his garland like a priest, / like a bride putting on her jewelry.
61:11 For as the earth sends up its shoots, / and as a garden makes its seeds sprout, / so the Lord GOD will make righteousness and praise / sprout up in front of all the nations.
## Zion's new name
62:1 Because of Zion I will not keep silent, / because of Jerusalem I will not rest, / until her righteousness goes out like a bright light / and her salvation like a torch on fire.
62:2 Nations will see your righteousness, / and all kings your glory. / A new name will be given you, / one the LORD's own mouth will pick out.
62:3 You will be a beautiful crown in the LORD's hand, / a king's turban in the palm of your God.
62:4 People will no longer say of you, "Abandoned," / or say of your land any more, "Desolate." / Instead you will be called My Delight Is in Her, / and your land, Married. / For the LORD delights in you, / and your land will be married.
62:5 For like a young man taking a virgin as his wife, / your sons will take you as wife. / With a bridegroom's joy over his bride / your God will rejoice over you.
62:6 On your walls, Jerusalem, I have posted guards; / all day and all night they will never keep silent. / You who keep the LORD reminded, / do not rest,
62:7 and give him no rest / until he makes Jerusalem firm / and makes her a thing of praise on the earth.
62:8 The LORD has taken an oath by his right hand / and by the arm of his strength: / "I will not again hand over your grain / as food for your enemies, / nor will foreigners drink the new wine / you sweated for.
62:9 No, those who bring it in will eat it / and praise the LORD, / and those who gather it will drink it / in my holy courts."
## Prepare the way
62:10 Go through, go through the gates! / Clear the way for the people! / Heap it up, heap up the highway! / Clear away the stones! / Lift a flag over the peoples!
62:11 Look, the LORD has made it heard / to the end of the earth: / "Tell Daughter Zion, / 'Look, your salvation is coming! / Look, he brings his reward with him, / and his wages go ahead of him.'"
62:12 They will be called the Holy People, / the Redeemed of the LORD. / And you will be called Wanted, / A City Not Abandoned.
## The one who trod the winepress
63:1 Who is this coming in from Edom, / from Bozrah in clothes dyed red? / Who is this, grand in his clothing, / leaning forward in the fullness of his strength? / "It is I, who speak with righteousness, / great at saving."
63:2 Why is there red on your clothing, / and why are your garments like those of someone stamping in a winepress?
63:3 "I have trodden the wine trough by myself; / from the peoples there was no one with me. / I stamped on them in my anger / and crushed them underfoot in my fury. / Their juice spattered on my clothes, / and I stained everything I wore.
63:4 For a day of vengeance was on my mind, / and the year to redeem my own had come.
63:5 I looked, and there was no one to help; / I was shocked that no one held me up. / So my own arm brought me victory, / and my fury, it held me up.
63:6 I stamped down peoples in my anger; / I made them drunk in my fury / and brought their juice down to the ground."
## Remembering the LORD's loyal love
63:7 I will call to mind the LORD's acts of loyal love, / the LORD's praiseworthy deeds, / for all that the LORD has done for us, / the great goodness to the house of Israel / that he has shown them in his mercy / and in his great loyal love.
63:8 For he said, "They are my people after all, / sons who will not betray me." / And he became their savior.
63:9 In every trouble of theirs, trouble came to him, / and the angel of his presence saved them. / Because he loved them and pitied them, he redeemed them. / He picked them up and carried them / all through the days of long ago.
63:10 But they rebelled / and grieved his holy spirit. / So he turned around and became their enemy; / he himself made war on them.
63:11 Then he remembered the days of long ago, / Moses and his people: / Where is the one who brought them up out of the sea / with the shepherds of his flock? / Where is the one who put his holy spirit / among them,
63:12 who made his glorious arm march at Moses' right hand, / who split the waters in front of them / to win himself a name forever,
63:13 who led them through the deep waters? / Like a horse in the wilderness, / they did not stumble.
63:14 Like cattle going down into the valley, / the Spirit of the LORD gave them rest. / So you led your people / to win yourself a splendid name.
## A prayer from the ruins
63:15 Look down from heaven and see / from your high home, holy and splendid. / Where are your zeal and your mighty deeds? / The deep stirring of your heart and your tender love / are held back from me.
63:16 For you are our Father. / Abraham does not know us, / and Israel does not recognize us. / You, LORD, are our Father; / "Our Redeemer from Long Ago" is your name.
63:17 Why, LORD, do you make us stray from your ways / and make our hearts hard so that we do not fear you? / Come back for the sake of your servants, / the tribes that belong to you as your inheritance.
63:18 Your holy people held possession for only a little while; / our foes have trampled your sanctuary.
63:19 For a long time we have been like people you never ruled, / like those who were not called by your name.
64:1 If only you would rip open the heavens and come down, / so the mountains would shake in front of you,
64:2 as fire sets brushwood alight, / as fire makes water boil, / to make your name known to your foes, / so the nations would tremble before you!
64:3 When you did fearsome things we did not hope for, / you came down, and the mountains shook in front of you.
64:4 No one has ever heard, from ancient days, / no one has caught the sound, / no eye has seen a God besides you, / one who acts for those who wait for him.
64:5 You meet the one who gladly does what is right, / those who remember you in your ways. / Look, you were angry, and we went on sinning. / We have stayed in them a long time. Will we be saved?
64:6 We have become like someone unclean, all of us, / and like a cloth from a woman's monthly period are all our right deeds. / We wither like a leaf, all of us, / and our wrongdoings carry us off like the wind.
64:7 No one calls on your name / or stirs himself to take hold of you. / For you have hidden your face from us / and let us melt away in the grip of our wrongdoing.
64:8 But now, LORD, you are our Father. / We are the clay, and you are the one who shaped us; / we are all what your hand has made.
64:9 Do not be so very angry, LORD, / and do not remember wrongdoing forever. / Please, look: we are all your people.
64:10 The cities of your holiness lie as open wilderness; / Zion is wilderness, / Jerusalem a desolate waste.
64:11 Our holy and splendid house, / where our ancestors praised you, / has been burned up in fire, / and everything we loved lies in ruins.
64:12 In the face of these things, LORD, will you hold back? / Will you keep silent and crush us beyond all bounds?
## Found by those who did not look
65:1 I let myself be asked by those who did not ask; / I let myself be found by those who did not look for me. / I said, "I am here, I am here," / to a nation that was not called by my name.
65:2 I have spread out my hands all day long / toward a rebellious people, / who walk the road that is not good, / following their own plans,
65:3 a people who keep provoking me / to my face, / sacrificing in the gardens / and making smoke on the bricks,
65:4 who sit among the tombs / and spend the night in hidden places, / who eat the meat of pigs, / with broth of foul meat in their pots,
65:5 who say, "Keep to yourself! Do not come near me, / for I would make you holy." / These are smoke in my nose, / a fire burning all day long.
65:6 Look, it is written down in front of me: / I will not keep silent until I have paid back, / paid it back into their lap,
65:7 your wrongdoings and your ancestors' wrongdoings together, says the LORD, / because they made smoke on the mountains / and insulted me on the hills. / I will measure out their pay for what they did before, / right into their lap.
65:8 The LORD says this: / "When new wine is found in a cluster of grapes, / and someone says, 'Do not destroy it, for there is a blessing in it,' / I will do the same for the sake of my servants, / so as not to destroy them all.
65:9 I will bring out offspring from Jacob, / and from Judah an heir for my mountains. / My chosen ones will take possession of it, / and my servants will live there.
65:10 The Sharon will become a pasture for sheep, / and the Valley of Achor a place where cattle lie down, / for my people who have looked for me.
## Luck, Fate and the LORD's servants
65:11 But you who abandon the LORD, / who forget my holy mountain, / who lay out a table for Luck / and fill cups of mixed wine for Fate,
65:12 I will make the sword your fate, / and all of you will kneel down to be slaughtered. / For I called, and you did not answer; / I spoke, and you did not listen. / You did what is evil in my eyes / and chose what I do not want."
65:13 So the Lord GOD says this: / "Look, my servants will eat, / but you will go hungry. / Look, my servants will drink, / but you will go thirsty. / Look, my servants will be glad, / but you will be put to shame.
65:14 Look, my servants will shout for joy / because their hearts are glad, / but you will cry out because your hearts hurt, / and you will howl because your spirits are broken.
65:15 You will leave your name behind / as a curse word for my chosen ones: / 'So may the Lord GOD kill you!' / But his servants he will call by another name,
65:16 so that whoever asks for a blessing in the land / will ask it by the God of Amen, / and whoever swears an oath in the land / will swear by the God of Amen. / For the troubles of the past will be forgotten / and hidden from my eyes.
## New heavens and a new earth
65:17 For look, I am creating new heavens and a new earth. / What came before will not be remembered, / nor rise up in anyone's heart.
65:18 No, rejoice and celebrate for all time / over what I am creating. / For look, I am creating Jerusalem to be a joy / and her people to be a gladness.
65:19 I will celebrate over Jerusalem / and be glad in my people. / The sound of weeping and the sound of crying out / will no longer be heard there.
65:20 No more will there be in it / a baby who lives only a few days, / or an old man who does not fill out his days. / For whoever dies at a hundred will die young, / and whoever falls short of a hundred / will be counted as cursed.
65:21 They will build houses and live in them; / they will plant vineyards and eat what they bear.
65:22 They will not build for someone else to live in, / or plant for someone else to eat. / For like the lifetime of a tree / will be the lifetime of my people, / and my chosen ones will wear out / what their own hands have made.
65:23 They will not toil for nothing / or bear children for sudden terror, / for they are the offspring of those the LORD has blessed, / and their descendants with them.
65:24 Even before they call, I will answer; / while they are still talking, I will have heard.
65:25 The wolf and the lamb will graze together, / and the lion, like the ox, will eat straw, / and dust will be the snake's food. / No one will hurt or ruin anything / anywhere on my holy mountain," says the LORD.
## Heaven is my throne
66:1 The LORD says this: / "The heavens are my throne, / and the earth is the stool for my feet. / Where then is the house that you would build for me? / Where is the place where I would rest?
66:2 My hand made all these things, / and so all these things came to be," / the LORD declares. / "But this is the one I will look to: / the poor one, stricken in spirit, / who trembles at my word.
66:3 One who slaughters an ox, one who kills a man; / one who sacrifices a lamb, one who breaks a dog's neck; / one who offers grain, pig's blood; / one who burns frankincense as a reminder, one who blesses an idol. / They have chosen their own ways, / and their souls delight in their disgusting things.
66:4 So I in turn will choose rough treatment for them / and bring on them what they fear. / For I called, and no one answered; / I spoke, and they did not listen. / They did what is evil in my eyes / and chose what I do not want."
66:5 Hear the word of the LORD, / you who tremble at his word: / "Your brothers who hate you, / who push you out because of my name, have said, / 'Let the LORD show his glory, / so we can see your joy!' / But they are the ones who will be put to shame.
66:6 A sound of uproar from the city! / A sound from the temple! / It is the sound of the LORD / paying back his enemies what they deserve.
## Zion gives birth
66:7 Before she was in labor, she gave birth; / before her pains came on her, / she delivered a boy.
66:8 Who has heard of anything like this? / Who has seen such things? / Can a land be brought to birth in one day? / Can a nation be born all at once? / Yet as soon as Zion went into labor, / she gave birth to her sons.
66:9 Would I bring a child to the point of birth / and not let it be born?" says the LORD. / "Would I, who bring to birth, shut the womb?" / says your God.
66:10 Celebrate with Jerusalem and be glad over her, / all you who love her! / Be full of joy with her, / all you who have been mourning over her,
66:11 so that you may nurse and be satisfied / at her comforting breast, / so that you may drink deeply and delight / in the rich flow of her glory.
66:12 For the LORD says this: / "Look, I will make peace flow out to her like a river, / and the wealth of nations like a stream in flood. / You will nurse and be carried at the hip / and bounced on the knees.
66:13 Like someone whose mother comforts him, / so I myself will comfort you; / in Jerusalem you will be comforted."
66:14 You will see, and your heart will be glad; / your bones will sprout like new grass. / The LORD's hand will be made known among his servants, / and his anger against his enemies.
## Fire, and all nations
66:15 For look, the LORD will come in fire, / and his chariots like the storm wind, / to pay back his anger in fury / and his rebuke in flames of fire.
66:16 For by fire the LORD will judge, / and by his sword, all flesh, / and many will be the ones the LORD has killed.
66:17 "Those who make themselves holy and pure / to go into the gardens, / following one in the middle, / those who eat the flesh of pigs, / vile things and mice, / will all come to an end together," the LORD declares.
66:18 "As for me, I know their deeds and their thoughts. The time is coming to gather all nations and languages, and they will come and see my glory.
66:19 I will set a sign among them, and I will send survivors from them to the nations: to Tarshish, Pul and Lud, who draw the bow, to Tubal and Javan, to the distant islands that have not heard of my fame or seen my glory. And they will tell of my glory among the nations.
66:20 They will bring all your brothers from all the nations as an offering to the LORD, on horses, in chariots and wagons, and on mules and camels, to my holy mountain, Jerusalem," says the LORD, "just as the Israelites bring the grain offering in a clean vessel to the house of the LORD.
66:21 And I will take some of them too to be Levite priests," says the LORD.
66:22 "For just as the new heavens and the new earth / that I am making will stand before me," the LORD declares, / "so your descendants and your name will stand.
66:23 From new moon to new moon, / and from Sabbath to Sabbath, / all flesh will come to bow down before me," says the LORD.
66:24 "Then they will go out and look at the corpses / of the people who rebelled against me. / For their worm will not die, / and their fire will not be put out, / and they will be a horror to all flesh."
