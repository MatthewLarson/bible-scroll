## The earth laid waste
24:1 Look, the LORD is about to empty the earth and strip it bare. / He will twist its face out of shape / and scatter the people who live on it.
24:2 People and priest will fare alike, / servant and master, / maid and mistress, / buyer and seller, / lender and borrower, / the one who collects a debt and the one who owes it.
24:3 The earth will be emptied, emptied out, / and looted, looted clean, / for the LORD has spoken this word.
24:4 The earth mourns and wilts; / the world fades and wilts; / the lofty among the earth's people fade away.
24:5 The earth is polluted beneath the people who live on it, / for they have stepped across the laws, / swapped out the rule / and annulled the everlasting covenant.
24:6 That is why a curse is eating the earth, / and those who live there are held guilty. / That is why the earth's people burn away, / and only a handful of humans are left.
24:7 The new wine mourns, the vine fades; / everyone whose heart was glad now groans.
24:8 The glad beat of tambourines has stopped; / the uproar of the partygoers is over; / the glad sound of the lyre has stopped.
24:9 They no longer sing as they drink their wine; / beer turns bitter in the drinker's mouth.
24:10 The city of chaos lies broken. / Every house is shut, and no one can get in.
24:11 In the streets people cry out over the wine. / Every joy has sunk into dusk; / the gladness of the earth has gone into exile.
24:12 What remains in the city is a wasteland, / and its gate lies smashed in a heap.
24:13 This is how it will be in the middle of the earth, among the peoples: / like the beating of an olive tree, / like the leftover grapes when the picking is done.
## Songs from the ends of the earth
24:14 They, for their part, raise their voices, / ringing out their joy at the LORD's majesty; / from the sea they cry aloud.
24:15 So honor the LORD in the lands of the dawn, / and in the far shores of the sea / honor the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.
24:16 From the edge of the earth we hear songs: / "Splendor to the Righteous One!" / But I said, "I grow thin, I grow thin! / Woe to me! / Double-crossers double-cross! / With double-crossing the double-crossers double-cross!"
## No escape
24:17 Terror, trench and trap / are over you, you who live on the earth!
24:18 Whoever runs from the sound of terror / will fall into the trench, / and whoever climbs up out of the trench / will be caught in the trap. / For the windows high above have been opened, / and the foundations of the earth quake.
24:19 The earth breaks, it breaks to pieces; / the earth cracks, it cracks wide open; / the earth totters, it totters and slides.
24:20 The earth reels and reels like a drunkard; / it rocks back and forth like a shack in a field. / Its rebellion weighs it down. / It falls, and it will not get up again.
## The LORD reigns on Zion
24:21 On that day the LORD will call to account / the army on high, up on high, / and the kings of the earth below, down on the earth.
24:22 They will be rounded up, a crowd of prisoners in a pit. / They will be locked in a dungeon, / and after many days they will be called to account.
24:23 The pale moon will blush, / and the hot sun will be ashamed, / because the LORD of Armies will be king on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, / and before his elders there will be glory.
## A song for the fall of the city
25:1 LORD, you are my God. / I will lift you high; I will thank your name, / for you have worked a wonder, / plans laid from long ago, / truth that holds firm.
25:2 You have turned a city into a pile of stones, / a walled town into a ruin. / The palace of outsiders is no longer a city, / and it will never be built again.
25:3 That is why a strong people will honor you, / and the city of brutal nations will fear you.
25:4 You have been a fortress for the poor, / a fortress for the needy in their trouble, / a shelter from the downpour, / a shade from the heat. / For the blast of the brutal / is like a downpour beating on a wall,
25:5 like heat in a parched land. / You hush the roar of outsiders. / As heat dies down under the shadow of a cloud, / the victory song of the brutal is silenced.
## The feast on the mountain
25:6 On this mountain the LORD of Armies will spread a feast for all the peoples: / a feast of fat foods, a feast of wine aged on its dregs, / fat foods rich with marrow, / aged wine strained clear.
25:7 Here on this mountain he will swallow up / the face-cloth that lies over all the peoples, / the woven sheet stretched over all the nations.
25:8 He will swallow death up for all time. / The Lord GOD will wipe the tears off every face, / and he will take the scorn his people bear away from all the earth, / for the LORD has spoken.
25:9 On that day it will be said: / "Here he is, our God! / We hoped in him, and he rescued us. / This is the LORD; we hoped in him. / Let us shout and celebrate, because he has rescued us."
25:10 For the LORD's hand will come to rest on this mountain, / but Moab will be trodden down where it stands, / the way straw is trodden down in the water of a manure pit.
25:11 He will spread out his hands in it, / as a swimmer spreads out his hands to swim, / and he will bring its pride low / for all the tricks of its hands.
25:12 The high fortress of your walls / he will bring down, he will lay low, / he will knock to the ground, down to the dust.
## The song of the strong city
26:1 On that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: / "Ours is a city of strength! / He makes rescue its walls and its rampart.
26:2 Open the gates, / and a righteous nation will come in, / one that stays true.
26:3 The mind that rests firm on you / you guard in peace, peace, / for it has put its trust in you.
26:4 Trust in the LORD for all time to come, / for Yah, the LORD, is a rock that lasts for all ages.
26:5 For he has brought down those who lived up high, / the town set high and safe. / He lays it low, he lays it low to the ground; / he knocks it down to the dust.
26:6 It is crushed under feet, / the feet of the afflicted, / the tread of the poor."
## Waiting for the LORD at night
26:7 The road for the righteous is straight and even; / you level the track of the righteous, making it straight.
26:8 Even on the road where your judgments lead, LORD, we wait for you. / Your name, the remembering of you, / is what our whole being craves.
26:9 With my whole being I crave you through the night; / my spirit inside me looks for you at daybreak. / For when your judgments reach the earth, / the people of the world learn what is right.
26:10 Show favor to the wicked, and still he will not learn what is right. / In a land where people deal straight, he will twist things, / and he will not see the majesty of the LORD.
26:11 LORD, your hand is lifted, but they do not look. / Let them look, and be put to shame by your passion for your people. / Yes, let the fire kept for your foes eat them up.
26:12 LORD, you will set out peace for us, / for even all our work, you have worked it for us.
26:13 LORD our God, masters other than you have owned us, / but you alone, your name alone, we keep in mind.
26:14 Dead men do not live again; / shades do not rise. / So you called them to account and wiped them out, / and you made every memory of them perish.
26:15 You have added to the nation, LORD, / you have added to the nation and shown your glory; / you have pushed out all the borders of the land.
## Labor pains and the dew of light
26:16 LORD, in distress they sought you out; / they poured out a murmur / when your discipline came on them.
26:17 As a pregnant woman near her time / twists and screams in her labor pains, / so we have been before you, LORD.
26:18 We were pregnant, we writhed in labor; / it was as if we gave birth to wind. / We have won no victories for the earth, / and no people have been born to fill the world.
26:19 Your dead will live! / My corpses will stand up! / Wake up and sing out, you who lie in the dust! / For your dew is dew that sparkles with light, / and the earth will bring her shades to birth.
26:20 Come, my people, go into your inner rooms / and close your door after you. / Hide for just a moment / until the fury goes past.
26:21 For look, the LORD is setting out from his place / to call the people of the earth to account for their guilt. / The earth will lay bare its blood, / and it will no longer cover up those killed on it.
## Leviathan
27:1 On that day the LORD will call to account, / with his sword, hard and great and strong, / Leviathan the fleeing serpent, / Leviathan the crooked serpent, / and he will kill the dragon that lives in the sea.
## The vineyard sung again
27:2 On that day: / "A delightful vineyard, sing of it!
27:3 I, the LORD, keep it; / I water it moment by moment. / So that no one lays a hand on it, / I keep it night and day.
27:4 There is no fury in me. / If only someone would give me thornbush and bramble to fight! / I would step out to battle against them / and burn them up, every one.
27:5 Or else let him cling to my fortress; / let him make peace with me; / peace with me let him make."
27:6 In the time to come, Jacob will put down roots; / Israel will flower and sprout, / and they will cover the surface of the world with fruit.
## Jacob's guilt removed
27:7 Did the LORD strike him down the way he struck down the one who struck him? / Was he killed the way his killers were killed?
27:8 With measured blows, by sending her away, you fought your case against her; / he blew her away with his harsh wind on the day the east wind blew.
27:9 So this is how Jacob's guilt will be atoned for, / and taking his sin away will bear this fruit: / he will grind every altar stone / to powder like lumps of chalk, / and no Asherah poles or incense stands will be left upright.
27:10 For the walled city sits alone, / a homestead cast off and abandoned like the desert. / A calf grazes there; / there it lies down and finishes off its branches.
27:11 When its shoots dry up, they snap off, / and women come and light their fires with them. / This is a people with no understanding, / so its Maker will show it no mercy, / and the one who shaped it will grant it no favor.
## The great ram's horn
27:12 On that day the LORD will beat out his harvest / from the flowing stream of the River to the Wadi of Egypt, / and you will be gleaned one by one, people of Israel.
27:13 On that day a great ram's horn will be blown, / and the strays perishing in the land of Assyria / and the outcasts in the land of Egypt will come / and bow down to the LORD on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.
## The drunkards of Ephraim
28:1 Woe to the crown that Ephraim's drunkards are so proud of, / to the wilting flower, its splendid beauty, / set on the head of a valley thick with fat, / the crown of men knocked flat by wine!
28:2 Look! The Lord has someone strong and powerful, / like a pounding hailstorm, a wind that wrecks, / like a flood of mighty waters sweeping over everything. / He will fling it to the ground with his hand.
28:3 It will be trampled underfoot, / the crown that Ephraim's drunkards are so proud of.
28:4 And the wilting flower, its splendid beauty, / set on the head of a valley thick with fat, / will be like the first fig of the year, ripe before summer: / the one who spots it / gulps it down while it is still in his hand.
28:5 On that day the LORD of Armies himself will be a crown of beauty / and a wreath of splendor for the remnant of his people,
28:6 a spirit of justice for the one seated for judgment, / and strength for those who push the fighting back at the gate.
## Priest and prophet reel
28:7 But these too sway from wine / and stumble around from beer. / Priest and prophet sway from beer; / they are swallowed up by wine; / they stumble from beer. / They sway as they see visions; / they totter as they give rulings.
28:8 Every table is smeared with vomit and filth; / no place is left clean.
28:9 "Who does he think he is teaching? / Who is he trying to make understand his message? / Babies just weaned from milk, / just pulled away from the breast?
28:10 It is tsav la-tsav, tsav la-tsav, / qav la-qav, qav la-qav, / a bit here, a bit there!"
28:11 Very well: with jabbering lips and a strange tongue / he will speak to this people,
28:12 he who had told them, / "This is where rest is; give the tired ones rest. / This is the place to settle down." / But they refused to hear.
28:13 So for them the word of the LORD will be / tsav la-tsav, tsav la-tsav, / qav la-qav, qav la-qav, / a bit here, a bit there, / so that they will go and trip backward, / and break, and be trapped and taken captive.
## A covenant with death
28:14 So listen to the word of the LORD, you men of mockery, / you rulers of this people here in Jerusalem.
28:15 For you have said, / "We have cut a covenant with death; / with Sheol we have signed a pact. / When the flooding whip goes through, / it will not come near us, / for we have made a lie our shelter / and hidden ourselves in deceit."
28:16 So the Lord GOD says this: / "Look, I have laid a stone in Zion for a foundation, / a stone that has been tested, / a cornerstone of great worth, a foundation firmly laid. / Whoever believes will not hurry.
28:17 I will make justice the measuring cord / and righteousness the level. / Hail will scour off the shelter made of lies, / and waters will wash over the hiding place.
28:18 Your covenant with death will be wiped out; / your pact with Sheol will not stand. / When the flooding whip goes through, / you will be the ground it tramples.
28:19 Every time it passes, it will carry you off. / Morning after morning it will pass, / by day and by night. / Just to understand the message will be pure terror."
28:20 The bed is short; you cannot stretch out. / The blanket is narrow; you cannot wrap up in it.
28:21 For the LORD will rise to his feet, as on Mount Perazim; / he will storm, as in the valley at Gibeon, / to do his deed, and his deed is strange, / to carry out his work, and his work is foreign.
28:22 So now, no more mocking, / or your bonds will grow tighter. / For I have heard of an end, a settled end, / from the Lord GOD of Armies, against the whole land.
## The farmer's wisdom
28:23 Lend your ears and hear my voice; / pay close heed and hear my words.
28:24 Does a farmer plow day after day to plant? / Does he keep on opening the soil and breaking its clods?
28:25 Once he has smoothed its surface, / does he not scatter black cumin and toss out cumin seed? / Does he not set wheat in rows, / barley in its marked place, / and spelt along the edge?
28:26 His God trains him in the right way to do it; / his God teaches him.
28:27 No one threshes black cumin with a sledge, / or rolls a cartwheel over cumin. / Black cumin is beaten out with a stick, / and cumin with a rod.
28:28 Grain for bread must be ground, / but no one keeps on threshing it forever. / He may drive his cartwheel and his horses over it, / but he does not grind it to powder.
28:29 This too has come out from the LORD of Armies. / He makes his counsel a wonder; / he makes his sound wisdom great.
## Woe to Ariel
29:1 Woe to Ariel, Ariel, / the town where David set up camp! / Pile year on year; / let the feasts keep circling.
29:2 Then I will squeeze Ariel hard, / and there will be moaning and groaning, / and to me she will be like an ariel, a fire-altar.
29:3 I will set my camp all around you; / I will close you in with a ring of posts / and put up siege works against you.
29:4 You will sink low and speak up from the ground; / your words will come up muffled from the dust. / Your voice will rise from the earth like the voice of a ghost, / and your words will twitter out of the dust.
29:5 But the horde of outsiders against you will be like powdery dust, / the horde of the brutal like chaff blowing past. / And it will happen suddenly, in a moment.
29:6 You will be visited by the LORD of Armies / with thunder and earthquake and a great roar, / with gale and storm and the flame of a fire that devours.
29:7 The horde of all the nations that wage war on Ariel, / everyone who attacks her and her fortress and squeezes her, / will be like a dream, like a vision in the night.
29:8 As a hungry man dreams, and look, he is eating, / then he wakes, and his belly is empty; / as a thirsty man dreams, and look, he is drinking, / then he wakes, and look, he is faint, his throat craving water: / so it will be for the horde of all the nations / that wage war on Mount Zion.
## A sealed scroll
29:9 Dawdle and be dumbfounded! / Blind your eyes and stay blind! / They are drunk, but not on wine; / they sway, but not from beer.
29:10 For the LORD has poured a spirit of deep sleep over you; / he has closed your eyes, the prophets, / and covered your heads, the visionaries.
29:11 The whole vision has become for you like the words of a scroll that is sealed. People hand it to someone who knows how to read and say, "Please read this." He says, "I am not able to, because it is sealed."
29:12 Or they hand the scroll to someone who cannot read and say, "Please read this." He says, "I never learned to read."
29:13 The Lord said: / "Because this people draws near with its mouth / and honors me with its lips / but has moved its heart far from me, / and their fear of me / is a rule made by humans and learned by heart,
29:14 therefore, look, I will once more do amazing things with this people, / amazing, astounding things. / The wisdom of its wise men will be lost, / and the understanding of its clever men will go into hiding."
## The potter and the clay
29:15 Woe to those who burrow deep / to keep their schemes hidden from the LORD! / Their work is done in a dark place, / and they say, "Who sees us? Who is aware of us?"
29:16 Your thinking is upside down! / Is the potter to be counted the same as the clay, / so that the thing made says of the one who made it, "He did not make me," / and the shaped thing says of its shaper, "He understands nothing"?
## The deaf will hear
29:17 Is it not just a very little while / until Lebanon turns into garden land / and the garden land will be thought of as a forest?
29:18 On that day the deaf will hear what a scroll says, / and the eyes of the blind will see / out of the gloom and the dark.
29:19 The lowly will find more and more joy in the LORD, / and the poorest of humankind will celebrate in the Holy One of Israel.
29:20 For the brutal will vanish, / the mocker will come to an end, / and all who stay awake to do harm will be cut down:
29:21 those who brand a man guilty with a word, / who lay snares for the one who calls out wrong in the gate, / and with empty nothings push the one in the right aside.
29:22 So the LORD, who ransomed Abraham, says this about the house of Jacob: / "Jacob will not be put to shame now; / his face will not go white now.
29:23 For when he sees his children, the work of my hands, in his midst, / they will treat my name as holy. / They will treat the Holy One of Jacob as holy / and will tremble before the God of Israel.
29:24 Those whose spirits have wandered will come to understanding, / and the grumblers will take in teaching."
## Down to Egypt
30:1 Woe to the rebel children, the LORD declares, / who make plans that do not come from me, / who pour out a drink offering my Spirit has no part in, / and so heap sin on top of sin.
30:2 They set off on the road down to Egypt / but have not asked for my word, / to take cover in Pharaoh's fortress / and find refuge in Egypt's shade.
30:3 Pharaoh's fortress will bring you shame, / and hiding in Egypt's shade will end in your disgrace.
30:4 Though his officials are already at Zoan / and his messengers have arrived at Hanes,
30:5 every one of them will be shamed by a people that cannot do them any good, / no help, no gain, / only shame and scorn.
30:6 The oracle about the animals of the Negev: / Through a land of trouble and hardship, / of the lioness and the lion, / of the viper and the flying burning serpent, / they carry their riches on the shoulders of donkeys / and their treasure loaded on camels' humps / to a people that cannot do them any good.
30:7 Egypt's help is a breath, an empty nothing. / So I have named her / "Rahab, who just sits there."
## Write it down
30:8 Now go, write it down on a tablet in their presence / and carve it into a scroll, / so that it will last for a day yet to come, / for all time, forever.
30:9 For this is a people that defies, / children who lie, / children who will not listen to the teaching of the LORD.
30:10 They say to the seers, "Stop seeing!" / and to the visionaries, "Do not see visions for us of what is right. / Talk to us of smooth things; / see visions of fantasies.
30:11 Get off the road! Step off the path! / Get the Holy One of Israel out of our sight!"
30:12 So the Holy One of Israel says this: / "Because you have thrown out this word, / and put your trust in oppression and deviousness, / and leaned on it,
30:13 therefore this guilt will be for you / like a breach in a high wall, ready to fall, / bulging out, / whose crash comes suddenly, in a moment.
30:14 It will be smashed like a potter's jar, / shattered without pity, / so that in the wreckage not one shard turns up / to scoop up embers from the hearth / or skim water from a pool."
30:15 For the Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel, says this: / "By turning back and resting you will be saved; / in calm and in trust your strength will lie." / But you refused.
30:16 "No," you said, "we will flee on horseback!" / So flee you will! / "We will ride the fast ones!" / So the ones chasing you will be faster.
30:17 One thousand of you will flee at one man's war cry; / at the war cry of five you will all flee, / until what is left of you is like a pole on top of a mountain, / like a signal flag on a hill.
## The LORD waits to be gracious
30:18 And so the LORD is waiting to show you favor; / and so he is lifted high, ready to show you compassion. / For the LORD is a God of justice. / Happy are all who wait for him!
30:19 Yes, a people will make its home in Zion, in Jerusalem. / You will not weep, no, never weep again. / He will surely show you grace at the sound of your crying out; / the moment he hears, he will answer you.
30:20 The Lord may give you the bread of distress and the water of hardship, / but your teacher will not hide in a corner anymore; / you will see your teacher with your own eyes.
30:21 Your ears will hear a word spoken behind you: / "This is the road. Walk on it," / whenever you swerve to the right or to the left.
30:22 Then you will treat as unclean / the silver plating on your carved idols / and the gold covering on your cast images. / You will scatter them like a stained menstrual cloth. / "Out!" you will say to it.
30:23 He will give rain for your seed when you sow the soil, / and the bread the soil yields will be rich and fat. / On that day your livestock will graze in a roomy meadow.
30:24 The oxen and the donkeys that till the soil / will eat salted feed, / winnowed with shovel and fork.
30:25 On every high mountain and on every hill raised up / there will be channels and streams of water / on the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall.
30:26 The moon's light will be like the sun's, / and the sun's light will be seven times as strong, / like the light of seven days, / on the day the LORD bandages the broken places of his people / and heals the wounds left by his blows.
## Fire for Assyria
30:27 Look! The name of the LORD is coming from far away, / his anger burning, his smoke rising thick. / His lips are full of rage, / and his tongue is like a fire that devours.
30:28 His breath is like a torrent in flood / that reaches up to the neck, / to shake the nations in a sieve of nothingness / and put a bridle that leads them astray / on the jaws of the peoples.
30:29 But for you there will be singing / like the night when a festival is made holy, / and your hearts will be glad, / like someone walking to the sound of the flute / to come to the mountain of the LORD, / to the Rock of Israel.
30:30 The LORD will make the splendor of his voice heard, / and he will show his arm as it comes down, / with furious anger and the flame of a fire that devours, / with a cloudburst, a downpour and hailstones.
30:31 At the voice of the LORD Assyria will be shattered; / with the rod he will strike.
30:32 Every stroke of the destined staff / that the LORD brings down on him / will come with tambourines and lyres; / with battles of the swinging arm he will fight against them.
30:33 For Topheth was laid out long ago; / it too has been made ready for the king. / Its fire pit has been dug deep and wide, / with fire and plenty of wood. / The breath of the LORD, like a stream of sulfur, / sets it on fire.
