## Ahaz at the water channel
7:1 In the days of Ahaz son of Jotham, son of Uzziah, king of Judah, Rezin king of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah, king of Israel, came up to Jerusalem for battle against it. But he was not able to win the fight against it.
7:2 The house of David was told, "Aram has settled in Ephraim." Then his heart shook, and the hearts of his people, as forest trees shake in a wind.
7:3 Then the LORD said to Isaiah, "Go out to meet Ahaz, you and your son Shear-jashub. Go to the end of the water channel of the Upper Pool, on the main road to the Launderers' Field.
7:4 Say to him, 'Watch yourself, and stay quiet. Do not be afraid. Do not let your heart go soft over these two tail ends of smoking logs, over the hot anger of Rezin and Aram and of Remaliah's son.
7:5 For Aram has made evil plans against you, and Ephraim and Remaliah's son with it. They say,
7:6 "Let's march up against Judah and throw it into terror. Let's break through its walls and take it for ourselves, and set the son of Tabeal on its throne as king."
7:7 The Lord GOD says this: It will not stand. It will not happen.
7:8 For the head of Aram is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin. In sixty-five more years Ephraim will be broken to pieces, no longer a people.
7:9 The head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remaliah's son. If you will not hold firm, you will not be held firm.'"
## The sign of Immanuel
7:10 Once more the LORD spoke to Ahaz. He said,
7:11 "Ask for a sign of your own from the LORD your God. Make it deep, down in Sheol, or make it high, up above."
7:12 Ahaz answered, "I won't ask. I won't test the LORD."
7:13 Then Isaiah said, "Listen, house of David! Wearing out people is not enough for you? Must you wear out my God as well?
7:14 Because of this, the Lord will give you a sign himself: Look, the young woman is pregnant and about to have a son, and she will name him Immanuel.
7:15 Curds and honey will be his food by the time he is able to say no to the bad and choose the good.
7:16 For before the boy is able to say no to the bad and choose the good, the land whose two kings fill you with dread will lie abandoned.
7:17 The LORD will bring days on you, on your people and on your father's family, days like none that have come since the day Ephraim split off from Judah. It will be the king of Assyria."
## On that day
7:18 On that day the LORD will whistle and call the fly from the farthest arms of Egypt's Nile, and the bee from the land of Assyria.
7:19 Every one of them will come and land in the gullies between the cliffs and in the cracks in the rocks, on every thorn bush and in every watering place.
7:20 On that day the Lord will shave with a hired razor, from across the River, with the king of Assyria. He will shave the head and the hair of the feet, and the razor will sweep the beard away as well.
7:21 On that day a person will keep a heifer and two sheep alive.
7:22 There will be so much milk that he will eat curds; curds and honey are what everyone left in the land will eat.
7:23 On that day, every spot that once held a thousand vines, worth a thousand pieces of silver, will be given up to thornbush and bramble.
7:24 Men will go there carrying arrows and a bow, for the whole land will be thornbush and bramble.
7:25 As for all the hills that were once worked with the hoe, you will not set foot on them, afraid of thornbush and bramble. They will be left for oxen to roam and sheep to trample.
## Quick to the plunder
8:1 The LORD said to me, "Get yourself a big tablet and write on it with a common pen, 'For Maher-shalal-hash-baz.'
8:2 I will have reliable witnesses confirm it for me: Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah."
8:3 Then I slept with the prophetess. She became pregnant and had a son, and the LORD told me, "Call him Maher-shalal-hash-baz.
8:4 Before the boy has learned to call out 'My father!' and 'My mother!', Damascus's riches and Samaria's plunder will be hauled off and laid before the king of Assyria."
## The waters of Shiloah and the River
8:5 Once more the LORD spoke to me. He said,
8:6 "Since this people has spurned the waters of Shiloah / that run softly along, / and takes joy in Rezin and Remaliah's son,
8:7 so look: the Lord is about to bring up on them / the River's water, strong and abundant, / the king of Assyria with all his glory. / It will climb over every channel / and spill across every bank.
8:8 It will surge on into Judah, / flooding as it goes, / until it is up to the neck. / Its wings will stretch out / over the whole width of your land, Immanuel."
8:9 Do your worst, you peoples, and be broken to pieces! / Give ear, all you far corners of the earth: / buckle on your weapons and be broken to pieces; / buckle on your weapons and be broken to pieces!
8:10 Plan your plans, and they will come apart. / Give your orders, and they will not stand, / for God is with us.
## Fear the LORD alone
8:11 The LORD spoke to me like this, his hand gripping me hard, and he warned me against going the way this people goes:
8:12 "When this people cries 'Conspiracy!' at everything, / you must not cry 'Conspiracy!' / What they fear, do not fear; / do not tremble before it.
8:13 It is the LORD of Armies you must count holy. / Let him be the one you fear; / let him be the one who makes you tremble.
8:14 He will become a holy place, / and a stone that people strike their foot on, / and a rock they trip over, / for both houses of Israel; / he will be a trap and a snare / for the people living in Jerusalem.
8:15 Many will trip on them; / they will fall and break, / be trapped and taken captive."
## Seal the teaching
8:16 Wrap up the witness. / Put a seal on the teaching / in the keeping of my disciples.
8:17 So I will wait on the LORD, / though he is hiding his face from Jacob's house; / I will keep looking to him.
8:18 Look at me and the children the LORD has given me: / we are signs and omens in Israel, / from the LORD of Armies, / who makes his home on Mount Zion.
8:19 Suppose they say to you, "Consult the ghosts of the dead and the spirits that know things, who chirp and mutter." Is a people not to consult its own God? Will they go to the dead about the living?
8:20 Back to the teaching and the witness! Those who will not speak in line with this word will see no dawn.
8:21 Worn down and starving, they will wander through the land. Hunger will drive them into a rage, and they will curse their king and their God. They will look up toward the sky,
8:22 and they will stare down at the earth. All they will see is distress and darkness, the gloom of hard times, and they will be driven out into the dark.
## A great light
9:1 Yet the land that was in distress will not be left in gloom. At first he made light of the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, but in the end he will give weight to the way of the sea, the far side of the Jordan, Galilee of the nations.
9:2 The people walking in the dark / have seen a great light; / for those who live in the land of deep shadow, / a light shines on them.
9:3 You have made the nation grow; / you have given it great joy. / They are glad in your presence, glad as at harvest time, / happy as men who share out the loot.
9:4 For the yoke that loaded them down, / the pole that lay on their shoulders, / the stick of the slave driver, / you have broken to pieces, as on Midian's day.
9:5 For every boot that stomped in the din of battle / and every cloak rolled in blood / will be burned up, / food for the fire.
9:6 For a child has been born to us, / a son has been given to us, / and the rule is on his shoulder. / He has named him / Wonder-Counselor, Mighty God, / Father Forever, Prince of Peace.
9:7 There will be no end to the growth of his rule or to its peace, / on David's throne and over his kingdom. / He will make it firm and hold it up / with justice and righteousness, / from now on and for all time. / The zeal of the LORD of Armies will make it happen.
## His hand is still stretched out
9:8 The Lord has sent out a word into Jacob; / it has landed on Israel.
9:9 The whole nation will find it out, / Ephraim and all who live in Samaria, / who boast with proud and swollen hearts:
9:10 "Bricks have come down? / We will rebuild in carved stone. / Sycamores are cut down? / We will use cedars instead."
9:11 So the LORD lifted high Rezin's foes against him / and spurred on his enemies,
9:12 Aram from the east and the Philistines from the west, / and they have eaten Israel up with mouths wide open. / In spite of all this, his anger has not turned back, / and his hand is still stretched out.
9:13 Yet the people have not turned back to the one who struck them; / they have not sought out the LORD of Armies.
9:14 So in one day the LORD has cut off head and tail from Israel, / the palm frond and the rush.
9:15 The head means the elder and the man of rank; / the tail means the prophet who teaches what is false.
9:16 The ones who lead this people have led them off the road, / and those they lead are engulfed.
9:17 So the Lord will not be glad over their young men, / nor show mercy to their fatherless and their widows. / Every one of them is a godless evildoer, / and every mouth talks outrage. / In spite of all this, his anger has not turned back, / and his hand is still stretched out.
9:18 Wickedness blazes up like fire, / eating up thornbush and bramble. / It catches in the forest thickets, / and they billow up in towering smoke.
9:19 The LORD of Armies is furious, / and the land is burned black; / the people have become food for the fire. / Not one of them will spare his brother.
9:20 They tear off food to the right and stay hungry; / they eat to the left and are never full. / Every man eats the flesh of his own arm:
9:21 Manasseh feeds on Ephraim, Ephraim on Manasseh, / and the two of them together are against Judah. / In spite of all this, his anger has not turned back, / and his hand is still stretched out.
10:1 Woe to those who lay down wicked rules, / and to the scribes who keep writing up misery,
10:2 pushing the weak out of court, / stealing what is right from the poor among my people, / so that widows become their loot / and the fatherless their plunder.
10:3 What are you going to do when the day of accounting comes, / and the storm of ruin rolls in from far off? / Who will you run to for help? / Where will you stash your riches?
10:4 You can only crouch beneath the prisoners / or fall beneath the bodies of the slain. / In spite of all this, his anger has not turned back, / and his hand is still stretched out.
## Assyria, the rod of my anger
10:5 Woe to Assyria, the rod of my anger! / It is a stick, and my rage is in their hand.
10:6 I am sending him at a godless nation; / I am giving him orders against the people I am angry with: / to grab plunder and carry off loot, / to tread them flat like mud in the street.
10:7 But he does not see it that way; / that is not how his heart thinks. / His heart is set on wiping out, / on cutting down nations, and not just a few.
10:8 He says, / "Aren't my officers kings, every one of them?
10:9 Calno went the way of Carchemish, didn't it? / Hamath the way of Arpad? / Samaria the way of Damascus?
10:10 My hand has caught the kingdoms of the worthless idols, / and they had more carved images than Jerusalem and Samaria.
10:11 What I did to Samaria and its worthless idols, / will I not do the same to Jerusalem and its images?"
10:12 Once the Lord has completed everything he is doing on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem, I will bring to account the fruit of the king of Assyria's swollen heart and the proud glory of his lofty eyes.
10:13 For he says, / "I did this with my own strong hand, / with my wisdom, because I am clever. / I wiped out the borders between peoples / and looted their stockpiles; / like a strong man I toppled the ones who sat enthroned.
10:14 My hand found the riches of the nations / as if it found a nest; / I scooped up the whole earth / the way someone gathers eggs that have been left. / Not one wing fluttered, / not one beak opened, not one peep."
10:15 Can an ax take credit over the man who chops with it? / Can a saw puff itself up against the man who works it? / That would be like a stick waving the hand that picks it up, / like a club lifting the one who is not made of wood!
10:16 That is why the Lord, the LORD of Armies, / will send leanness into his well-fed men; / under his glory a burning will burn / like fire burning.
10:17 Israel's Light will turn into fire, / its Holy One into flame; / in one day it will burn up / and eat his thornbush and his bramble.
10:18 The glory of his forest and his garden land / it will bring to an end, life and flesh alike; / it will be like a sick man wasting away.
10:19 The remnant of trees in his forest will be a handful, / few enough for a child to write them down.
## A remnant will return
10:20 On that day the remnant of Israel, the survivors of Jacob's house, will stop leaning on the one who beat them. They will truly lean on the LORD, the Holy One of Israel.
10:21 A remnant will return, Jacob's remnant, to the Mighty God.
10:22 Even if your people, Israel, were like the sand of the sea, it is a remnant of them that will return. A full end has been decided, flooding in with righteousness.
10:23 For an end, a settled end, is what the Lord GOD of Armies is about to bring about in the middle of the whole land.
10:24 So the Lord GOD of Armies says this: "You, my people living in Zion, do not fear Assyria. He strikes you with a rod and raises his stick over you, the way it was done in Egypt.
10:25 In just a little while longer, the rage will be spent, and my anger will turn to their ruin."
10:26 The LORD of Armies will crack a whip over him, like the beating he gave Midian at Oreb's Rock. He will hold his staff over the sea and raise it up, the way it was done in Egypt.
10:27 On that day the load he put on your shoulder will slide off, and his yoke will come off your neck. The yoke will snap because of fatness.
## The enemy marches on Jerusalem
10:28 He has reached Aiath, / gone on through Migron; / at Michmash he drops off his gear.
10:29 They have gone over the pass: / "We'll spend the night at Geba!" / Ramah shakes with fear; / Saul's Gibeah has run away.
10:30 Scream out, Daughter Gallim! / Pay attention, Laishah! / Poor Anathoth!
10:31 Madmenah has run off; / the people of Gebim have taken shelter.
10:32 Before the day is over he will stand at Nob / and shake his hand / against the mountain of Daughter Zion, / the hill of Jerusalem.
10:33 But look! The Lord, the LORD of Armies, / is hacking off the boughs with a frightful crash. / The trees that stand tallest will be chopped down, / and the high ones will come down low.
10:34 He will chop the thick forest down with iron, / and Lebanon will fall to a mighty one.
## The shoot from Jesse's stump
11:1 From Jesse's stump a shoot will come out, / and a sprout from his roots will bear fruit.
11:2 The LORD's Spirit will come to rest on him, / a spirit of wisdom and understanding, / a spirit of counsel and strength, / a spirit to know and to fear the LORD.
11:3 The fear of the LORD will be a sweet smell to him. / He will not give judgment by how things look to his eyes, / or settle cases by what reaches his ears.
11:4 Instead he will judge the weak rightly, / and give fair rulings for the lowly of the earth. / His mouth will be a rod to strike the earth, / and the breath of his lips will put the wicked to death.
11:5 He will wear righteousness as the band around his waist, / and faithfulness as the band around his hips.
11:6 The wolf will stay as a guest with the lamb; / the leopard will bed down beside the young goat; / calf and young lion and fat steer will be together, / and a small boy will herd them.
11:7 Cow and bear will graze; / their little ones will rest together; / the lion, like the ox, will eat straw.
11:8 A baby still at the breast will play over a cobra's hole; / a child just off the breast will reach his hand into a viper's lair.
11:9 No one will hurt or ruin anything / anywhere on my holy mountain, / for knowing the LORD will fill the earth / like waters covering the sea.
## The root of Jesse and the second exodus
11:10 On that day the root of Jesse will be standing as a flag for the peoples; / nations will come seeking him, / and the place where he rests will be glory.
11:11 On that day the Lord will put out his hand once more, a second time, to claim as his own the remnant of his people who are left, from Assyria and Egypt, from Pathros and Cush, from Elam and Shinar, from Hamath and from the islands of the sea.
11:12 He will lift a flag for the nations to see / and gather in Israel's outcasts; / Judah's scattered ones he will collect / from the earth's four corners.
11:13 The envy of Ephraim will be gone, / and Judah's harassers will be cut off. / Ephraim will no longer envy Judah, / and Judah will no longer harass Ephraim.
11:14 They will dive onto the shoulder of the Philistines to the west, / and together they will loot the people of the east. / Edom and Moab will be theirs to lay hands on, / and the Ammonites will take their orders.
11:15 The LORD will lay waste the tongue of Egypt's sea. / He will shake his hand over the River with his burning wind / and beat it down into seven gullies, / so that people walk across in their sandals.
11:16 There will be a road raised up for the remnant of his people, / those left over from Assyria, / like the road Israel had / on the day it came up out of the land of Egypt.
## The song of the rescued
12:1 On that day you will say, / "I give you thanks, LORD. / You were angry with me; / now let your anger turn back, / and comfort me.
12:2 Look, God is my rescue; / I will trust and not be afraid. / For my strength and my song is Yah, the LORD, / and he has become my rescue."
12:3 You will draw water with joy / out of the springs of rescue.
12:4 And on that day you will say, / "Give thanks to the LORD; call out his name. / Let the peoples know his deeds. / Remind them that his name is lifted high.
12:5 Make music for the LORD, for he has acted in triumph; / this is being made known through all the earth.
12:6 Cry out, ring out with joy, you who dwell in Zion, / for the Holy One of Israel among you is great!"
