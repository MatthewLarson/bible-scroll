## The heading of the book
1:1 The vision of Isaiah son of Amoz, which he saw about Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.
## Children who rebelled
1:2 Hear, you heavens! Listen, earth! / For the LORD has spoken: / "Children I have raised and lifted high, / and they have rebelled against me.
1:3 An ox knows the one who owns it, / and a donkey knows its master's feeding trough. / Israel does not know; / my people do not stop to think."
1:4 Woe to a nation that sins, / a people heavy with guilt, / a family of wrongdoers, / children who act corruptly! / They have left the LORD, / they have treated the Holy One of Israel with contempt, / they have turned into outsiders and gone backward.
1:5 Where can you be struck next, / as you keep on in your revolt? / The whole head is given over to sickness, / the whole heart is weak.
1:6 From the sole of the foot to the head / there is no healthy spot in it, / only bruises and welts / and raw wounds. / They have not been squeezed clean or bandaged / or eased with oil.
1:7 Your land is a waste, / your cities are burned with fire. / Your farmland, right before your eyes, / outsiders are eating it up; / it is a waste, like a land that outsiders overthrew.
1:8 And Daughter Zion is left over / like a booth among the vines, / like a night watchman's shack in a cucumber patch, / like a city hemmed in.
1:9 Had the LORD of Armies / not left a handful of us alive, / we would have become like Sodom, / we would look just like Gomorrah.
## Sacrifice without justice
1:10 Hear the LORD's word, / you rulers of Sodom! / Listen to the teaching of our God, / you people of Gomorrah!
1:11 "What use to me are all your many sacrifices?" / says the LORD. / "I am stuffed with rams for burnt offerings / and with the fat of fattened animals. / The blood of bulls, of lambs and of goats: / I take no delight in it.
1:12 When you come to be seen before me, / who asked this from your hands, / that you tramp through my courts?
1:13 Bring me no more empty grain offerings; / the smoke of incense is abhorrent to me. / New Moon and Sabbath, the summons to a gathering: / I cannot stand wrongdoing and a sacred assembly together.
1:14 My very self hates / your New Moons and your set feasts. / They weigh on me like a load; / I am worn out from lifting it.
1:15 When you hold out your palms to me, / I will cover my eyes from you. / Pile up prayer after prayer: / I will not hear. / Blood fills your hands.
1:16 Wash yourselves, become clean. / Get the evil you do / out from before my eyes. / Quit doing evil.
1:17 Learn how to do good. / Go after justice. / Put the ruthless in their place. / Give the orphan his rights; / take up the widow's case.
## Let us argue it out
1:18 "Come, then, let us argue it out," / says the LORD. / "If your sins are scarlet, / like snow they will turn white; / if they are red as crimson dye, / like wool they will be.
1:19 If you are willing and you listen, / you will eat the best the land has.
1:20 But if you say no and defy me, / the sword will eat you." / The LORD's own mouth has said it.
## The faithful town
1:21 How has she turned into a prostitute, / the town that was faithful! / She was filled with justice, / righteousness used to stay the night in her, / and now: killers.
1:22 Your silver has turned into dross; / your wine is watered down.
1:23 Your officials are rebels, / thieves' best friends. / All of them love a bribe / and run after a payoff. / They give the orphan no justice, / and the widow's lawsuit never gets in to them.
1:24 That is why the Lord declares, / the LORD of Armies, the Mighty One of Israel: / "Ha! I will ease my heart against my foes / and take my revenge on my enemies.
1:25 I will turn my hand against you. / I will refine your dross away, as lye does, / and take out all the tin mixed in you.
1:26 I will bring back your judges as they were long ago, / your counselors as they were at the start. / After that, your name will be / City of Righteousness, Faithful Town."
1:27 Through justice Zion will be ransomed; / through righteousness, those in her who turn back.
1:28 But a crash will come on rebels and sinners alike, / and those who leave the LORD will come to an end.
1:29 The oaks you craved will bring them shame, / and the gardens you picked will make you blush.
1:30 You will be like an oak / whose leaves are shriveling, / like a garden that gets no water.
1:31 The strong man will turn into flax fiber, / and what he made, into a spark. / The two will burn as one, / with no one to put it out.
## The LORD's mountain
2:1 The word that Isaiah son of Amoz saw about Judah and Jerusalem.
2:2 In the days to come / the mountain where the LORD's house stands / will be set firm at the head of the mountains, / lifted higher than the hills, / and like a river, all the nations will run to it.
2:3 Peoples in great numbers will set out, saying, / "Come on, let us climb the LORD's mountain, / up to the house of Jacob's God. / He will teach us from his ways, / and we will walk on his roads." / For from Zion comes teaching, / and from Jerusalem, the LORD's word.
2:4 He will be judge between the nations / and set things right for many peoples. / They will pound their swords into plowshares / and their spears into pruning knives. / No nation will raise a sword against another nation, / and they will never again learn how to make war.
2:5 House of Jacob, come, / let us walk in the LORD's light.
## The day of the LORD against all that is proud
2:6 You have cast off your people, / the house of Jacob. / They are crammed with what comes from the East, / with soothsayers like the Philistines have, / and they make deals with the children of foreigners.
2:7 Silver and gold fill their land, / and their treasures have no limit. / Horses fill their land, / and their chariots have no limit.
2:8 And worthless idols fill their land. / They bow low to what their hands have made, / to things their own fingers shaped.
2:9 So human beings bend low, / each one is brought down, / and you must not pardon them.
2:10 Get into the rock, / bury yourself in the dust, / away from the dread of the LORD / and his majestic splendor.
2:11 The proud eyes of humans will be lowered, / and the height of men will be bent low, / and the LORD alone will stand high / on that day.
2:12 For a day belongs to the LORD of Armies, / a day set against all that is proud and towering, / against all that rises up, so that it sinks down,
2:13 against all the cedars of Lebanon, / towering and rising up, / and against all the oaks of Bashan,
2:14 against all the towering mountains, / and against all the hills that rise up,
2:15 against every tall tower / and every wall built strong,
2:16 against all the ships of Tarshish / and against all the ships that people treasure.
2:17 The pride of humans will be bent low, / and the height of men will be lowered, / and the LORD alone will stand high / on that day.
2:18 As for the worthless idols, they will be gone, every one.
2:19 People will crawl into caves in the rocks / and into holes in the dirt, / away from the dread of the LORD / and his majestic splendor, / when he rises up to make the earth tremble in terror.
2:20 On that day people will toss / the worthless idols of silver and of gold / that they made to bow down to / out to the moles and the bats,
2:21 and they will crawl into cracks in the rocks / and into gaps in the cliffs, / away from the dread of the LORD / and his majestic splendor, / when he rises up to make the earth tremble in terror.
2:22 Stop relying on human beings: / all they have is a breath in their nose. / What do they count for?
## No one left to lead
3:1 For see, the Lord, the LORD of Armies, / is about to take away from Jerusalem and Judah / everything and everyone they lean on: / all their bread to lean on, / all their water to lean on;
3:2 the warrior and the fighting man, / the judge and the prophet, / the one who reads omens and the elder,
3:3 the commander of fifty and the man people look up to, / the adviser, the one wise in magic arts, / and the one clever at whispered spells.
3:4 "I will give them boys for their officials, / and spoiled children will rule over them."
3:5 Among the people, man will push around man, / neighbor will push around neighbor. / Boys will sneer at the old, / and the worthless will defy the honored.
3:6 One man will take hold of his brother / in their father's house: / "You own a coat! Be our chief! / This pile of rubble can be yours to rule."
3:7 On that day he will protest, / "I am no one to bandage wounds. / My house has no bread and no coat. / You will not make me chief of the people!"
3:8 For Jerusalem has tripped, / Judah has fallen down. / What they say and what they do is against the LORD; / they rebel right before his glorious eyes.
3:9 Their faces give them away: / the expression they wear speaks against them. / They announce their sin as Sodom did / and make no effort to cover it. / Woe to their souls! / They have paid themselves back with harm.
3:10 Say of the righteous: "Good!" / They will eat what their own deeds have grown.
3:11 Woe to the wicked one: "Bad!" / What his hands handed out will be handed to him.
3:12 My people! A child is their slave driver, / and women rule over them. / My people! Those who guide you send you wandering; / they have gobbled up the paths you travel.
## The LORD in court
3:13 The LORD has risen to argue his case; / he is on his feet to judge the peoples.
3:14 The LORD will come into court / with the elders of his people and its officials: / "You! You grazed the vineyard bare! / Your houses hold what you stole from the poor.
3:15 What gives you the right to beat my people to pieces / and grind the faces of the poor?" / the Lord GOD of Armies declares.
## The daughters of Zion
3:16 The LORD said: / Because Zion's daughters hold themselves high, / walk with chins up and necks stretched, / and make eyes at men, / prancing as they go / with a jingle at their ankles,
3:17 the Lord will make the crowns of their heads scabby, / and the LORD will strip their foreheads bare.
3:18 On that day the Lord will strip away the beauty of their ankle bangles, their little suns and their crescents,
3:19 the drops, the bracelets and the veils,
3:20 the headdresses, the ankle chains and the sashes, the perfume boxes and the charms,
3:21 the signet rings and the nose rings,
3:22 the party dresses, the overcoats, the wraps and the purses,
3:23 the mirrors, the linen undergarments, the turbans and the long veils.
3:24 Where there was spice, there will be rot; / where there was a belt, a rope; / where hair was carefully braided, a shaved head; / where a rich gown was, sackcloth tied around the waist; / a burn scar where beauty was.
3:25 The sword will cut down your men, / and battle, your strength.
3:26 Her city gates will moan and grieve; / she will be wiped clean and sit in the dirt.
4:1 On that day seven women will grab hold of one man and say, "We will eat food of our own and wear clothes of our own. Only let us carry your name. Rid us of our shame!"
## Holy survivors under a canopy
4:2 On that day the LORD's branch will become a thing of beauty and glory, and the fruit of the land will be the pride and the splendor of Israel's survivors.
4:3 Then the one left over in Zion, the one still remaining in Jerusalem, will be called holy: everyone in Jerusalem whose name is written down for life.
4:4 That will be once the Lord has washed off the filth of Zion's daughters and rinsed Jerusalem's bloodshed out of her, with a spirit of judgment and a spirit of burning.
4:5 Then over every place on Mount Zion, and over the crowds that gather there, the LORD will create a cloud in the daytime and smoke, and at night the bright light of a blazing fire, for a canopy will cover all the glory.
4:6 It will be a booth giving shade from the daytime heat, a place of safety and cover when the downpour and the rain come.
## The song of the vineyard
5:1 Let me sing for my dear one / my love's song about his vineyard. / My dear one had a vineyard / on a hilltop rich with oil.
5:2 He broke up its soil, picked out its stones / and planted it with red vines of the best kind. / In the middle of it he built a tower; / he even carved a wine vat out of the rock in it. / Then he hoped it would produce grapes, / and it produced stinking grapes.
5:3 "Now then, people living in Jerusalem, / men of Judah, / be the judges, please, / between me and my vineyard.
5:4 Is there anything else I could do for my vineyard / that I have not already done? / I hoped it would produce grapes. / Why did it produce stinking grapes?
5:5 So now, let me inform you / what I am about to do to my vineyard. / Its hedge: I will tear it out, / and it will become a place to graze. / Its wall: I will break through it, / and it will become a place for trampling.
5:6 I will leave it a ruin: / no one will prune it, no one will dig it, / and thornbush and bramble will shoot up. / And the clouds, I will give them orders / to drop no rain on it."
5:7 The vineyard of the LORD of Armies / is the house of Israel, / and the people of Judah / are the planting that was his joy. / He waited for justice: / look, bloodshed! / For righteousness: / look, an outcry!
## Six woes
5:8 Woe to those who string house onto house / and attach field to field / until no space remains, / and you are left to live by yourselves / in the middle of the land.
5:9 In my ears the LORD of Armies has spoken: / "I swear, many houses will be left in ruins, / big, beautiful houses with no one in them.
5:10 For ten acres of vines will make only a single bath of wine, / and a homer of seed will bring in just an ephah."
5:11 Woe to those who are up at dawn / chasing after beer, / who linger on as the evening grows dark / while wine heats them up.
5:12 Lyre and harp, drum and pipe / and wine make up their parties, / but the LORD's deeds they never notice, / and what his hands are making they do not see.
5:13 That is why my people go off into exile: / they have no knowledge. / Their nobles starve, / and their masses dry up from thirst.
5:14 So Sheol has made its throat wider / and opened its mouth with no limit, / and into it go her splendor and her masses, / her uproar and everyone who celebrates in her.
5:15 So human beings bend low, / each one is brought down, / and the eyes of the haughty are lowered.
5:16 But through justice the LORD of Armies stands tall, / and through righteousness the holy God proves that he is holy.
5:17 Lambs will feed there as if it were their own pasture, / and foreigners will eat among the ruins of the fat.
5:18 Woe to those who haul their guilt behind them / on ropes of nothingness, / who pull sin along / like a cart on its towlines,
5:19 and say, "Let him get moving! / Let him rush his work along so we can watch it! / Let the Holy One of Israel bring his plan close; / let it arrive, so we can find out what it is!"
5:20 Woe to those who say of evil, "It is good," / and of good, "It is evil," / who make dark into light / and light into dark, / who make bitter into sweet / and sweet into bitter!
5:21 Woe to those who are wise in their own eyes / and who seem to themselves to understand!
5:22 Woe to the strongmen at drinking wine, / the warriors at mixing beer,
5:23 who for a bribe say the wicked are in the right, / and take away the right that belongs to the righteous!
5:24 That is why, like straw eaten by a tongue of fire, / like hay that shrinks down in a flame, / their root will turn to rot / and their flower will rise like dust. / For they refused the teaching of the LORD of Armies, / and they treated the saying of the Holy One of Israel with contempt.
5:25 So the LORD's anger has flared up against his people. / He stretched out his hand against them and struck them; / the mountains trembled, / and their dead bodies were like trash in the middle of the streets. / In spite of all this, his anger has not turned back, / and his hand is still stretched out.
5:26 He will raise a flag for faraway nations; / he will whistle for it from the end of the earth, / and look, here it comes, fast and light!
5:27 No one in its ranks is weary, no one trips, / no one nods off, no one sleeps, / no one's belt is untied at the hips, / no one's sandal strap has snapped.
5:28 Its arrows have been sharpened, / and every bow is strung. / The hooves of its horses are counted as hard as flint, / its wheels as a windstorm.
5:29 It has a roar like a lioness; / it roars like the young lions. / It snarls, grabs the prey / and drags it away to safety, / and no one can snatch it back.
5:30 On that day it will growl over the prey / the way the sea growls. / Someone will look out over the land: / darkness, trouble! / Even the light has gone dark in the clouds.
## The vision in the temple
6:1 In the year King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord. He was sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, and the hems of his robe filled the temple.
6:2 Seraphim were standing above him. Six wings, six wings each one had: with two it covered its face, with two it covered its feet, and with two it would fly.
6:3 One kept calling to another, saying, "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Armies. Everything that fills the earth is his glory."
6:4 At the voice of the one who called, the bases of the doorways trembled, and smoke kept filling the house.
6:5 I said, "Woe to me! I am finished, because my lips are unclean, and the people I live among have unclean lips, and with my own eyes I have seen the King, the LORD of Armies!"
6:6 One of the seraphim flew over to me. In its hand was a hot stone it had lifted off the altar with tongs.
6:7 It laid it on my mouth and said, "Look: now that this has touched your lips, your guilt goes away and atonement is made for your sin."
6:8 And I heard the Lord's voice: "Whom am I to send? Who will go for us?" I said, "Here I am! Send me."
## Go and tell this people
6:9 He said, "Go, and say to this people: / 'Listen and listen, but do not understand; / look and look, but do not know.'
6:10 Make this people's heart fat, / make their ears heavy, / and paste their eyes shut, / or else they may see with their eyes / and hear with their ears / and understand with their heart / and turn back, and be healed."
6:11 Then I said, "Lord, how long?" He said: / "Until the cities crash into ruins with no one living in them, / the houses with no one in them, / and the farmland is crushed into a waste,
6:12 and the LORD has moved the people far off, / and abandoned places spread across the land.
6:13 And though a tenth is still left in it, / that too will be burned again, / like a terebinth or an oak / cut down, with only a stump standing. / Its stump is a holy seed."
