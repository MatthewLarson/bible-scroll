## The oracle concerning Babylon
13:1 The oracle concerning Babylon, which Isaiah son of Amoz saw.
## The LORD musters his army
13:2 On a mountain swept bare, lift up a flag! / Raise your voice to them! / Wave your hand, so they come through the gates of the nobles.
13:3 I myself have given my consecrated ones their orders; / for my anger I have also summoned my warriors, / those who exult in my greatness.
13:4 Hear the din on the mountains, / like the noise of a great crowd! / Hear the uproar of kingdoms, / of nations gathering! / The LORD of Armies is counting off / an army for war.
13:5 They are coming from a land far away, / from the edge of the sky: / the LORD and the weapons of his fury, / to ruin all the earth.
## The day of the LORD
13:6 Howl, for the day of the LORD is close! / It comes like havoc from the Almighty.
13:7 So all hands will drop limp, / and every person's heart will melt.
13:8 They will be terrified. / Cramps and pains will grip them; / they will writhe like a woman giving birth. / Each man will gape at his neighbor; / their faces will be faces of flame.
13:9 Look! The day of the LORD is coming, / cruel, with rage and the heat of anger, / to turn the earth into a wasteland / and wipe out the sinners in it.
13:10 For the sky's stars and constellations / will not flash with light. / The sun will be dark as it comes up, / and the moon will not make its light shine.
13:11 I will call the world to account for its evil, / the wicked for their wrongdoing. / I will stop the proud boasting of the arrogant / and bring low the haughtiness of tyrants.
13:12 I will make a man harder to find than pure gold, / a human being harder to find than gold from Ophir.
13:13 That is why I will make the heavens quake, / and the earth will lurch from its place / in the rage of the LORD of Armies, / on the day his anger blazes.
13:14 Like a hunted gazelle, / like a flock with no one to round it up, / they will each head back to their own people / and each run off to their own country.
13:15 Everyone who is found will be pierced; / everyone swept up will fall by the sword.
13:16 Their little ones will be battered to pieces in front of them; / men will plunder their homes / and rape their wives.
## The Medes against Babylon
13:17 Look, I am rousing the Medes against them, / who count silver as nothing / and have no taste for gold.
13:18 Their bows will strike young men down. / They will feel no pity for infants; / their eye will not spare a child.
13:19 And Babylon, the beauty of the kingdoms, / the Chaldeans' proud splendor, / will end like Sodom and Gomorrah, / which God overturned.
13:20 It will sit empty forever; / no one will live there, generation after generation. / No Arab will set up a tent there, / and shepherds will not bed down their flocks there.
13:21 Wild beasts of the desert will lie down there; / their houses will be crammed with owls. / Ostriches will make their home there, / and goat-demons will leap about there.
13:22 Hyenas will answer each other in its fortresses, / jackals in its pleasure palaces. / Its time is near, it is coming; / its days will not be drawn out.
## Israel brought home
14:1 For the LORD will show mercy to Jacob. / He will choose Israel again / and give them rest on their own ground. / Foreigners will join them / and cling to the house of Jacob.
14:2 Nations will take them and bring them to their place. / In the LORD's land the house of Israel will hold them / as male and female servants. / They will make captives of their captors / and rule over their slave drivers.
## A taunt over the king of Babylon
14:3 On the day the LORD gives you rest from your hurt and your unrest, and from the heavy work they made you do,
14:4 you will take up this taunt against the king of Babylon and say: / "How the slave driver has gone quiet, / the onslaught gone quiet!
14:5 The LORD has snapped the staff of the wicked, / the rod of those who ruled,
14:6 that beat the peoples in rage, / blow after blow without a break, / that trod nations down in anger, / hounding them with no one to hold it back.
14:7 All the earth rests, all is still; / people burst into shouts of joy.
14:8 The firs too are glad about you, / and the cedars of Lebanon: / 'Since the day you lay down, / no woodcutter has climbed up to us.'
14:9 Sheol down below is roused over you, / waiting to receive you as you arrive. / For you it wakes the shades, / all the he-goats of the earth; / it makes every king of the nations / get up from his throne.
14:10 They all take up the word and tell you, / 'So you are as feeble as we are! / You are just like us now!'
14:11 Down to Sheol goes your grandeur, / the hum of your harps. / Under you, maggots make your mattress; / worms are your bedcovers.
14:12 How you have fallen from the heavens, / Shining One, son of Dawn! / You are hacked down to the ground, / you who brought nations to their knees!
14:13 You said to yourself, / 'I will go up into the heavens; / I will lift my throne / higher than the stars of God. / I will sit on the Mount of Meeting / in the remote heights of Zaphon.
14:14 I will go up above the cloud tops; / I will make myself the Most High's equal.'
14:15 No! Down to Sheol you are thrust, / to the remote depths of the Pit.
14:16 People who see you will gaze at you / and look you over: / 'Is this the man who set the earth shaking / and rattled kingdoms,
14:17 who turned the world into a desert / and pulled its towns down, / who would not unlock the doors / to send his prisoners home?'
14:18 Every king of the nations, all of them, / lies down in glory, / each in his own house.
14:19 But you are thrown out, away from your tomb, / like a branch nobody wants, / wearing the dead as a garment, / men run through by swords, / who sink down to the stones of the Pit. / You are like a carcass stamped into the ground.
14:20 You will have no share with them in a grave, / because you wrecked your own land / and murdered your own people. / Let the brood of evildoers / never be spoken of again!
14:21 Set up a butcher's block for his sons / because of their fathers' wrongdoing, / so they never rise to take the earth for themselves / and cover the face of the world with cities."
14:22 "I will rise against them," the LORD of Armies declares. "I will wipe out from Babylon its name and remnant, children and grandchildren," the LORD declares.
14:23 "I will hand it over to the hedgehog and turn it into reedy pools of water. I will sweep it away with the broom of annihilation," the LORD of Armies declares.
## The LORD's plan for Assyria
14:24 The LORD of Armies has taken an oath: / "Just as I pictured it, it has come to be; / just as I planned it, that is how it will stand.
14:25 I will shatter Assyria in my own land / and tread him down on my mountains. / Then his yoke will come off them, / and his load will slide off their shoulder."
14:26 This is the plan drawn up for all the earth; / this is the hand stretched out over every nation.
14:27 For the LORD of Armies has made his plan; / who can break it? / It is his hand that is stretched out; / who can turn it back?
## Do not celebrate, Philistia
14:28 In the year King Ahaz died, this oracle came:
14:29 Philistia, all of you, do not celebrate / that the stick that beat you is snapped. / Out of the snake's root will come an adder, / and its offspring will be a flying burning serpent.
14:30 There will be grazing for the firstborn of the poor, / and the needy will lie down without fear. / But I will put your root to death by hunger, / and he will slay your remnant.
14:31 Howl, you gate! Scream, you city! / Philistia, all of you, go weak with fear! / Smoke is rolling in from the north, / and no one in its army lags behind.
14:32 And what reply will go back / to the envoys of the nation? / "The LORD laid Zion's foundations, / and the poor among his people will take shelter in her."
## The oracle concerning Moab
15:1 The oracle concerning Moab. / Yes, in a single night Ar of Moab is ravaged and silenced; / yes, in a single night Kir of Moab is ravaged and silenced.
15:2 Dibon has climbed to the temple, / to the high places, to weep. / Moab howls on Nebo and on Medeba. / Every head is shaved bare, / every beard clipped short.
15:3 They have tied on sackcloth in its streets. / On its rooftops and in its plazas / all of it howls, / running down with tears.
15:4 Heshbon screams, and Elealeh; / their cries carry all the way to Jahaz. / That is why Moab's fighting men shout in alarm; / Moab's spirit shakes within it.
15:5 My heart cries out for Moab. / Its runaways reach Zoar / and Eglath-shelishiyah. / Up the climb to Luhith / they go up crying; / along the Horonaim road / they wake the scream of disaster.
15:6 The waters of Nimrim have become wasteland. / The grass has dried up, the plants have died off, / and not a green thing remains.
15:7 So what they had saved, the riches they had made, / they haul across the Wadi of the Poplars.
15:8 For screaming circles the whole border of Moab: / its howling reaches Eglaim, / its howling reaches Beer-elim.
15:9 Dimon's waters are filled with blood, / yet I will add more trouble to Dimon: / a lion for the survivors of Moab, / and for the land's remnant.
## Moab's plea to Zion
16:1 Send the land's ruler a lamb / from Sela, by the desert road, / to the hill of Daughter Zion.
16:2 Like birds flitting about, / a nest thrown out, / so Moab's daughters will be / at the crossings of the Arnon.
16:3 "Bring advice; give a ruling. / At midday make your shadow / as dark as night. / Shelter the people driven out; / do not give away the one who runs.
16:4 Let Moab's outcasts live as guests with you; / be a cover for them from the one who ravages." / The oppressor will be finished; / the ravaging will stop; / the one who tramples will be gone from the land.
16:5 Then by loyal love a throne will be made firm, / and someone will sit on it faithfully / in David's tent, / a judge who searches out justice / and hurries to do righteousness.
## Moab's pride and Moab's grief
16:6 We have heard of Moab's pride, / proud beyond measure: / its proudness, its pride, its insolence, / and its boasting that rings false.
16:7 That is why Moab howls; / all of it howls over Moab. / You will moan for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth, / nothing but broken.
16:8 The terraces of Heshbon have dried up, / the vine of Sibmah too, / whose red grapes once laid low the lords of nations. / Its runners reached to Jazer / and wandered into the desert; / its shoots spread out / and crossed the sea.
16:9 So I cry with Jazer's crying / over the vine of Sibmah. / I soak you with my tears, / Heshbon and Elealeh, / for the harvest shout has fallen / on your summer fruit and your grain.
16:10 Happiness and delight are gathered up from the garden land. / No one sings in the vineyards, no one cheers. / The treader does not stamp out wine in the vats. / I have silenced the shout.
16:11 That is why my stomach throbs over Moab / like the strings of a harp, / and my inner self over Kir-heres.
16:12 When Moab shows up and wears himself out on the high place, and enters his sanctuary to pray, he will get nowhere.
16:13 That was the word the LORD spoke long ago about Moab.
16:14 But now the LORD has spoken: "In three years, counted the way a hired man counts his years, Moab's glory will be made worthless, with all its huge crowd, and its remnant will be small, tiny, without strength."
## The oracle concerning Damascus
17:1 The oracle concerning Damascus. / Look! Damascus is taken away and is a city no more; / it will be a ruin, a pile of rubble.
17:2 The towns of Aroer are abandoned. / They will belong to the flocks, / which will lie down with no one to frighten them.
17:3 Ephraim will lose its fortress, / and Damascus its kingdom. / What is left of Aram / will be like the glory of the Israelites, / the LORD of Armies declares.
## Jacob's glory fades
17:4 On that day Jacob's glory will shrink, / and his well-fed body will grow thin.
17:5 It will be as when a harvester gathers up the standing grain / and his arm cuts off the ears, / as when someone picks up the leftover ears / in the Valley of Rephaim.
17:6 Only scraps will be left in it, / as when an olive tree is knocked with sticks: / two or three olives high on the top branch, / four or five on the limbs of the fruit tree, / the LORD, the God of Israel, declares.
17:7 On that day a person will gaze toward the one who made him; / his eyes will see the Holy One of Israel.
17:8 He will no longer turn toward the altars / that his hands built, / or pay attention to what his fingers made, / the Asherah poles and the incense altars.
17:9 On that day their fortified towns / will be like the abandoned spots in the woods and on the summits / that people left when the Israelites came, / and it will all be a wasteland.
17:10 For you have forgotten the God who rescues you, / and you did not keep in mind the Rock where you shelter. / So you plant your delightful plants / and sow them with a stranger's vine shoots.
17:11 The same day you plant, you coax them up, / and by morning your seed is in flower, / yet the harvest is gone / in a day of sickness and pain that no one can heal.
## The roaring nations
17:12 Woe to the throng of many peoples, / booming like the boom of the seas, / and the clamor of nations, / crashing like the crash of mighty waters!
17:13 Nations crash like the crash of many waters, / but he scolds them and they run far off, / driven like husks on the hills before the wind, / like a whirl of thistle before a storm.
17:14 When evening comes, look, there is panic; / before dawn, there is nothing left of them. / That is the share of those who strip us, / the lot that falls to those who rob us.
## The land of buzzing wings
18:1 Woe to the land of buzzing wings / that lies beyond the rivers of Cush,
18:2 that sends its envoys down the sea / in reed boats on the face of the water! / Go, you quick messengers, / to a nation stretched tall and polished smooth, / to a people dreaded from here to far beyond, / a nation of power upon power that treads others down, / whose land is cut through by rivers.
18:3 Everyone who lives in the world, / all who make their home on earth, / you will see it when a flag goes up on the mountains; / you will hear it when the ram's horn is blown.
18:4 For the LORD said this to me: / "I will keep still and look on from my place, / like the glare of heat in the light, / like a dew cloud in the harvest heat."
18:5 Before the harvest, once the budding is done / and the blossom turns into a grape that is ripening, / he will slice off the shoots with knives / and hack away the tendrils and remove them.
18:6 They will be thrown out together / for the mountain vultures and the beasts of the land. / The vultures will feed on them through the summer, / and every beast of the land through the winter.
18:7 At that time a gift will be brought to the LORD of Armies: a nation stretched tall and polished smooth, from a people dreaded from here to far beyond, a nation of power upon power that treads others down, whose land is cut through by rivers. It will be brought to the place of the name of the LORD of Armies, Mount Zion.
## The oracle concerning Egypt
19:1 The oracle concerning Egypt. / Look! The LORD rides a fast cloud / as he comes into Egypt. / The worthless idols of Egypt shake at his presence, / and the heart of Egypt melts inside her.
19:2 I will goad Egypt against Egypt; / they will fight, a man with his brother, / a man with his neighbor, / town with town, kingdom with kingdom.
19:3 Egypt's spirit inside it will be emptied out, / and I will swallow up its plans. / They will seek out the worthless idols and the muttering spirits, / the ghosts of the dead and the spirits that know things.
19:4 I will hand Egypt over / into the grip of a harsh lord, / and a strong king will rule them, / the Lord, the LORD of Armies, declares.
## The Nile dries up
19:5 The water will drain from the sea, / and the river will shrink and go dry.
19:6 Its channels will reek; / the branches of Egypt's Nile will run low and dry; / reed and rush will wilt,
19:7 the bare banks beside the Nile, at the Nile's edge. / Every seeded field by the Nile will wither, / be blown off and vanish.
19:8 Those who fish will moan; / everyone who drops a line in the Nile will grieve, / and those who cast nets over the water / will droop.
19:9 The people who comb the flax / will hang their heads, / and so will the weavers of white cloth.
19:10 Its weavers will be crushed, / and everyone working for hire will be heartsick.
## The fools of Zoan
19:11 The officials of Zoan are pure fools; / the wise men among Pharaoh's advisers / give witless advice. / How dare you tell Pharaoh, / "I come from a line of sages, / from a line of kings of old"?
19:12 So where are they, your sages? / Let them please tell you, / and let them find out / what the LORD of Armies has in mind for Egypt.
19:13 Zoan's officials have played the fool; / the officials of Memphis are taken in. / The corner pillars of its tribes / have sent Egypt the wrong way.
19:14 The LORD has mixed into Egypt / a spirit of twisted thinking; / they have sent Egypt the wrong way in all it does, / the way a drunk wanders in his vomit.
19:15 No work Egypt does will come to anything, / whether by head or tail, / palm frond or rush.
## Egypt will know the LORD
19:16 On that day Egypt will be like women. It will shake and be in dread as the LORD of Armies swings his hand, as he swings it over them.
19:17 Judah's soil will make Egypt reel. Anyone who hears it named will panic at what the LORD of Armies is planning against them.
19:18 On that day five towns in the land of Egypt will speak the language of Canaan and take their oaths to the LORD of Armies. One of them will bear the name City of the Sun.
19:19 On that day the LORD will have an altar in the heart of the land of Egypt, and a standing stone for the LORD beside its border.
19:20 These will stand as a sign and a witness for the LORD of Armies in the land of Egypt. When they cry to the LORD under the hand of oppressors, he will send them someone to save them and fight for them, and he will set them free.
19:21 So the LORD will reveal himself to Egypt, and on that day Egypt will know the LORD. They will serve him with sacrifice and grain offering, and they will vow vows to the LORD and pay them.
19:22 The LORD will hit Egypt hard, hitting and then healing. They will come back to the LORD; he will let himself be moved by their prayers, and he will heal them.
19:23 On that day a highway will run from Egypt to Assyria. Assyria will enter Egypt, and Egypt Assyria, and Egypt will worship alongside Assyria.
19:24 On that day Israel will make a third with Egypt and Assyria, a blessing at the center of the world,
19:25 for the LORD of Armies will bless them and say, "Blessed is my people Egypt, and the work of my hands, Assyria, and my inheritance, Israel!"
## Isaiah walks naked and barefoot
20:1 It was the year Sargon king of Assyria sent the Tartan, who came to Ashdod, attacked it and took it.
20:2 At that time the LORD gave a message by means of Isaiah son of Amoz. He said, "Go and loosen the sackcloth from around your hips, and pull your sandals off your feet." He did it, and went about naked and barefoot.
20:3 The LORD said, "My servant Isaiah has walked about naked and barefoot for three years, a sign and an omen about Egypt and Cush.
20:4 That is how the king of Assyria will march off Egypt's prisoners and Cush's exiles, boys and old men, naked and barefoot, their backsides bare, to Egypt's disgrace.
20:5 People will be shattered and humiliated over Cush, which they looked to, and over Egypt, which they bragged about.
20:6 On that day whoever lives on this coastland will say, 'So this is what became of the people we looked to, where we ran for help to save us from the king of Assyria! And us? How will we get away?'"
## The oracle concerning the wilderness of the sea
21:1 The oracle concerning the wilderness of the sea. / Like storm winds that tear across the Negev, / it is coming out of the desert, / from a land of dread.
21:2 A hard vision has been told to me: / the double-crosser double-crosses, / the plunderer plunders. / Up, Elam! Close in, Media! / I am putting a stop to all its sighing.
21:3 So my insides are full of cramps; / pains have taken hold of me / like a woman's pains in childbirth. / What I hear doubles me over; / what I see fills me with dread.
21:4 My heart wanders; / shuddering has struck me with terror. / The evening I used to long for / he has turned into trembling for me.
21:5 Lay the table! Spread the carpets! / Eat! Drink! / On your feet, commanders! / Rub oil on the shield!
21:6 For the Lord said this to me: / "Go, set up a watchman. / Whatever he sees, let him announce it.
21:7 If he sees a column of riders, / horsemen two by two, / men mounted on donkeys, / men mounted on camels, / he must listen hard, / with all his attention."
21:8 Then he cried out like a lion: / "My Lord, I stand on the lookout tower / without a break all day, / and I keep my post / through every night.
21:9 And look, here it comes: a column of riders, / a man with a pair of horses!" / Then he spoke up and said: / "Fallen! Fallen is Babylon! / All the idols of its gods / are smashed into the ground."
21:10 You whom I have threshed, / child of my threshing floor, / what I heard from the LORD of Armies, / the God of Israel, / I have reported to you.
## The oracle concerning Dumah
21:11 The oracle concerning Dumah. / Someone is calling me from Seir: / "Watchman, how far gone is the night? / Watchman, how far gone is the night?"
21:12 The watchman answers: / "Morning arrives, and the night too. / If you would seek an answer, seek it. / Turn around and come again."
## The oracle concerning Arabia
21:13 The oracle concerning Arabia. / You will camp for the night among the scrub of Arabia, / you trading caravans of Dedan.
21:14 People of the land of Tema, / carry water out to the thirsty; / come with bread to meet the one who is running.
21:15 They are running from swords, / from the sword pulled from its sheath, / from the bow stretched taut, / from the weight of war.
21:16 For the Lord said this to me: "In one more year, counted the way a hired man counts his years, all of Kedar's glory will be finished.
21:17 Out of the number of archers, the fighting men of Kedar, those left over will be few." The LORD, the God of Israel, has said so.
## The oracle concerning the Valley of Vision
22:1 The oracle concerning the Valley of Vision. / What has gotten into you, then, / that every one of you has climbed onto the rooftops,
22:2 you city full of shouting, / noisy town, partying town? / The ones you lost were not cut down by swords; / they were not killed fighting.
22:3 Your officers all ran off at once; / they were tied up by the archers. / All who were found among you were captured together, / though they had run a long way.
22:4 That is why I said, "Turn your eyes away from me. / Let me cry my bitter tears. / Do not press me with comfort / over the ruin of my dear people."
22:5 For it is a day of uproar, of stamping feet, of panic, / from the Lord GOD of Armies, / in the Valley of Vision: / a wall rammed down, / a scream toward the mountain.
22:6 Elam lifted the quiver, / with men in chariots and horsemen, / and Kir took the cover off the shield.
22:7 The best of your valleys filled with chariots, / and the riders planted themselves facing the gate.
## You did not look to the Maker
22:8 He tore away Judah's covering. On that day you turned your eyes to the weapons in the House of the Forest.
22:9 You saw how many cracks there were in the City of David, and you gathered the water of the Lower Pool.
22:10 You made a list of Jerusalem's houses and pulled some down to shore up the wall.
22:11 Between the two walls you built a basin to hold the water of the Old Pool. But you never looked to the one who did all this; you paid no attention to the one who shaped it long before.
22:12 The Lord GOD of Armies called out on that day / for crying and for lament, / for shaved heads and the belt of sackcloth.
22:13 Instead, look: fun and festivity, / cattle killed, sheep butchered, / meat eaten and wine drunk! / "Eat and drink, / for tomorrow we die!"
22:14 The LORD of Armies opened my ears to this: / "I swear, there will be no atonement for this guilt of yours / until you die," / says the Lord GOD of Armies.
## Shebna and Eliakim
22:15 The Lord GOD of Armies says this: Come, go in to this steward, to Shebna, who is over the palace, and tell him:
22:16 What claim do you have here? What family do you have here, that you have dug yourself a tomb here, like a man who hews his grave on a height and chisels a home for himself in the rock?
22:17 Watch out, big man! The LORD is going to throw you, throw you far. He will grab hold of you,
22:18 wind you up, wind you round like a ball, and send you off into a land wide on every side. That is where you will die, and that is where your fine chariots will end up, you who bring shame on your master's household!
22:19 I will shove you out of your job, and he will tear you down from your position.
22:20 On that day I will send for my servant Eliakim son of Hilkiah.
22:21 I will put your tunic on him and tie your belt tight around him. I will place your power in his hand, and he will become a father to the people of Jerusalem and to the house of Judah.
22:22 I will lay the key of David's house on his shoulder. When he opens, no one will shut; when he shuts, no one will open.
22:23 I will hammer him in like a tent peg in solid ground, and he will be a throne of glory for his father's family.
22:24 On him they will hang the whole weight of his father's family: sprouts and shoots, every small vessel, from the basins to all the jugs.
22:25 On that day, the LORD of Armies declares, the peg hammered into solid ground will work loose. It will be chopped down and drop, and the weight it carried will be cut away, for the LORD has said it.
## The oracle concerning Tyre
23:1 The oracle concerning Tyre. / Howl, ships of Tarshish! / It is ruined: no house left, no way in. / As they came in from Cyprus, / the news was broken to them.
23:2 Hush, people of the coastland, / whom the traders of Sidon, / sailing across the sea, kept supplied.
23:3 Over the wide waters the grain of Shihor, / the crop of the Nile, was her income, / and she became the trading post of the nations.
23:4 Blush with shame, Sidon, / for the sea has spoken, / the fortress of the sea: / "I have felt no birth pains, I have borne no child; / I have raised no young men, / reared no young women."
23:5 As soon as Egypt hears the news, / they will writhe at what is told of Tyre.
23:6 Sail across to Tarshish! / Howl, you people of the coastland!
23:7 Is this the party city you had, / founded in the far-off past, / whose own feet carried her / far away to live as a foreigner?
23:8 Who made this plan against Tyre, / the city that handed out crowns, / whose dealers were princes, / whose traders were the great names of the world?
23:9 The LORD of Armies made the plan, / to drag through the dirt the pride of all splendor, / to make light of all the world's great names.
23:10 Overflow your land like the Nile, / Daughter Tarshish; / nothing holds you in any longer.
23:11 His hand reached out over the sea; / he set kingdoms trembling. / The LORD issued his command about Canaan: / tear down its fortresses.
23:12 He said, "You will not go on rejoicing, / crushed virgin, Daughter Sidon. / Go on to Cyprus, cross over; / even there no rest will be yours."
23:13 See the land of the Chaldeans: / this was the people that did not exist! / Assyria founded it for the desert creatures. / They put up their siege towers; / they stripped her strongholds to the ground; / he made her a heap of rubble.
23:14 Howl, ships of Tarshish, / for your fortress has been ruined.
## Tyre after seventy years
23:15 On that day Tyre will drop out of memory for seventy years, as long as one king lives. When the seventy years are over, Tyre will be like the prostitute in the song:
23:16 "Take your lyre and stroll through the town, / you prostitute no one remembers. / Pluck the strings well; sing song after song, / so people will think of you again."
23:17 After seventy years have ended, the LORD will turn his attention to Tyre, and she will go back to earning her fee and sell herself to every kingdom of the world on the face of the earth.
23:18 But her trade and her fee will be set apart as holy to the LORD. None of it will be put in storehouses or kept in a treasury. Her trade will belong to those who live in the LORD's presence, to give them food until they are full and elegant clothes.
