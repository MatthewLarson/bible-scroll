## Moses is dead: cross the Jordan
1:1 After the death of Moses the servant of the LORD, the LORD spoke to Joshua son of Nun, Moses' assistant. He said,
1:2 "Moses my servant is dead. So now get up and cross this Jordan, you and all this people, into the land I am about to give them, to the Israelites.
1:3 Every place where the sole of your foot treads, I have given to you, as I told Moses.
1:4 From the wilderness and this Lebanon to the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, and on to the Great Sea where the sun sets: that will be your territory.
1:5 As long as you live, no one will hold his ground against you. Just as I was with Moses, I will be with you. I will not let you down, and I will not abandon you.
1:6 Be strong and bold, because you are the one who will hand over to this people, as their inheritance, the land I swore to their ancestors to give them.
1:7 Only be strong and very bold. Be careful to do everything in the law that Moses my servant commanded you. Do not turn from it to the right or to the left, so that you will act wisely wherever you go.
1:8 This scroll of the law must not leave your mouth. Murmur it to yourself day and night, so that you will be careful to do everything written in it. Then you will make your way go well, and then you will succeed.
1:9 Did I not command you? Be strong and bold. Do not be terrified, and do not be shaken, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go."
## Ready to cross
1:10 Then Joshua gave orders to the officials of the people. He said,
1:11 "Go through the camp and order the people, 'Get food ready for yourselves, because in three days you will cross this Jordan. You will go in to take possession of the land the LORD your God is giving you to possess.'"
1:12 To the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joshua said,
1:13 "Remember the word that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you. He said, 'The LORD your God is giving you rest, and he will give you this land.'
1:14 Your wives, your little ones and your livestock may stay in the land Moses gave you beyond the Jordan. But all your strong warriors must cross over ahead of your brothers, armed for battle, and help them,
1:15 until the LORD gives your brothers rest as he has given you, and they too take possession of the land the LORD your God is giving them. Then you may go back to the land that is yours and take possession of it, the land Moses the servant of the LORD gave you on the other side of the Jordan, toward the sunrise."
1:16 They answered Joshua, "We will do all you have commanded us, and we will go anywhere you send us.
1:17 Just as we listened to Moses in everything, so we will listen to you. Only let the LORD your God be with you as he was with Moses!
1:18 Anyone who rebels against your orders and does not obey your words, in all that you command him, will be put to death. Only be strong and bold!"
## Rahab hides the spies
2:1 Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men out of Shittim as spies. He said, "Go and look over the land, and Jericho." So they went and came to the house of a prostitute named Rahab, and they lay down there.
2:2 The king of Jericho was told, "Look, some men from the Israelites have come here tonight to spy out the land."
2:3 So the king of Jericho sent word to Rahab: "Bring out the men who came to you, who came into your house, because they have come to spy out the whole land."
2:4 But the woman took the two men and hid them. Then she said, "It is true, the men did come to me, but I did not know where they came from.
2:5 When the gate was about to be shut at dark, the men went out. I do not know where the men went. Chase after them quickly, and you will catch them."
2:6 (She had brought them up onto the roof and hidden them among the stalks of flax that were spread out on her roof.)
2:7 So the men chased after them on the road to the Jordan, as far as the fords. The gate was shut right after the pursuers went out after them.
2:8 The men had not yet lain down when she came up to them on the roof.
2:9 She said to the men, "I know that the LORD has given you the land. Dread of you has fallen on us, and all who live in the land have melted away before you.
2:10 We have heard how the LORD dried up the water of the Sea of Reeds ahead of you when you came out of Egypt. We heard what you did to the two kings of the Amorites on the other side of the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you put under the ban.
2:11 When we heard it, our hearts melted. Because of you no one has any spirit left, for the LORD your God, he is God in the sky above and on the earth below.
2:12 Now please swear to me by the LORD that, since I have shown you loyal love, you too will show loyal love to my father's household. Give me a true sign
2:13 that you will let my father and my mother live, with my brothers and my sisters and all who belong to them, and that you will save our lives from death."
2:14 The men said to her, "Our lives stand in place of yours, to the death, as long as you say nothing about what we are doing. When the LORD gives us the land, we will deal with you in loyal love and faithfulness."
2:15 Then she let them down by a rope through the window. Her house was part of the town wall, and she lived right in the wall.
2:16 She said to them, "Head for the hill country, or the pursuers will come across you. Hide there for three days until they have gone back. Then go your way."
2:17 The men said to her, "We will be free from this oath you have made us swear.
2:18 Look, when we come into the land, you must tie this cord of scarlet thread in the window you let us down through. Gather your father and your mother, your brothers and all your father's household into your house with you.
2:19 If anyone goes out the doors of your house into the street, his blood will be on his own head, and we will be free of blame. But if anyone with you in the house is touched, his blood will be on our heads.
2:20 And if you tell about this business of ours, we will be free from the oath you have made us swear."
2:21 She said, "As you have said, so be it." Then she sent them off, and they went. And she tied the scarlet cord in the window.
2:22 They went into the hill country and stayed there three days, until the pursuers went back. The pursuers searched all along the road but did not find them.
2:23 Then the two men came back down from the hill country. They crossed over, came to Joshua son of Nun, and told him everything that had happened to them.
2:24 They said to Joshua, "The LORD has given the whole land into our hands. All who live in the land have melted away before us."
## The ark goes first
3:1 Joshua got up early in the morning. He and all the Israelites set out from Shittim and came to the Jordan, and they spent the night there before crossing.
3:2 At the end of three days the officials went through the camp
3:3 and commanded the people, "When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, with the Levite priests carrying it, set out from your place and follow it.
3:4 But keep a distance between you and it, about two thousand cubits by measure. Do not come near it. That way you will know which road to take, since you have not traveled it before."
3:5 Joshua said to the people, "Make yourselves holy, because tomorrow the LORD will do amazing things among you."
3:6 Joshua said to the priests, "Lift up the ark of the covenant and cross over ahead of the people." So they lifted up the ark of the covenant and went ahead of the people.
3:7 The LORD said to Joshua, "Today I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel, so they will know that I will be with you as I was with Moses.
3:8 Command the priests who carry the ark of the covenant, 'When you reach the edge of the water of the Jordan, stand still in the Jordan.'"
3:9 Joshua said to the Israelites, "Come over here and hear the words of the LORD your God."
3:10 Joshua said, "This is how you will know that the living God is among you, and that he will surely drive out ahead of you the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Hivites, the Perizzites, the Girgashites, the Amorites and the Jebusites.
3:11 Look, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is about to cross ahead of you into the Jordan.
3:12 Now pick twelve men from the tribes of Israel, one man for each tribe.
3:13 When the soles of the feet of the priests who carry the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth, come to rest in the water of the Jordan, the water of the Jordan will be cut off. The water flowing down from upstream will stand up in one heap."
## The Jordan stands still
3:14 So the people set out from their tents to cross the Jordan, with the priests carrying the ark of the covenant ahead of the people.
3:15 The Jordan runs full over all its banks the whole harvest season. When those carrying the ark reached the Jordan, and the feet of the priests carrying the ark dipped into the edge of the water,
3:16 the water flowing down from upstream stood still. It rose up in one heap a great distance away, at Adam, the town beside Zarethan. The water flowing down to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, was cut off completely. So the people crossed over opposite Jericho.
3:17 The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firm on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan, while all Israel crossed over on dry ground, until the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan.
## Twelve stones from the riverbed
4:1 When the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan, the LORD said to Joshua,
4:2 "Take twelve men from the people, one man from each tribe.
4:3 Command them, 'Pick up twelve stones from here, from the middle of the Jordan, from the spot where the priests' feet stood firm. Carry them over with you and set them down at the place where you stay tonight.'"
4:4 So Joshua called the twelve men he had appointed from the Israelites, one man from each tribe.
4:5 Joshua said to them, "Cross ahead of the ark of the LORD your God into the middle of the Jordan. Each of you lift one stone onto his shoulder, to match the number of the tribes of the Israelites.
4:6 This will be a sign among you. In time to come, when your children ask, 'What do these stones mean to you?'
4:7 tell them that the water of the Jordan was cut off in front of the ark of the covenant of the LORD. When it crossed the Jordan, the water of the Jordan was cut off. These stones will be a reminder to the Israelites forever."
4:8 The Israelites did just as Joshua commanded. They picked up twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, to match the number of the tribes of the Israelites, as the LORD had told Joshua. They carried them over with them to the place where they stayed and set them down there.
4:9 Joshua set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, at the spot where the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant had stood. They are there to this day.
4:10 The priests carrying the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan until everything was done that the LORD had commanded Joshua to tell the people, just as Moses had commanded Joshua. The people hurried and crossed over.
4:11 When all the people had finished crossing, the ark of the LORD and the priests crossed over in front of the people.
4:12 The Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over ahead of the Israelites, armed for battle, as Moses had told them.
4:13 About forty thousand men armed for war crossed over before the LORD to the battle, to the plains of Jericho.
4:14 On that day the LORD made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel. They revered him all the days of his life, as they had revered Moses.
4:15 Then the LORD said to Joshua,
4:16 "Command the priests carrying the ark of the testimony to come up out of the Jordan."
4:17 So Joshua commanded the priests, "Come up out of the Jordan."
4:18 The priests carrying the ark of the covenant of the LORD came up from the middle of the Jordan. As soon as the soles of the priests' feet were pulled out onto dry ground, the water of the Jordan went back to its place. It ran over all its banks as it had before.
## A memorial at Gilgal
4:19 The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month. They camped at Gilgal, on the east edge of Jericho.
4:20 Joshua set up at Gilgal the twelve stones they had taken from the Jordan.
4:21 He said to the Israelites, "In time to come, when your children ask their fathers, 'What are these stones?'
4:22 let your children know: 'Israel crossed this Jordan on dry ground.'
4:23 For the LORD your God dried up the water of the Jordan ahead of you until you had crossed, just as the LORD your God did to the Sea of Reeds, which he dried up ahead of us until we had crossed.
4:24 This was so that all the peoples of the earth would know how strong the LORD's hand is, and so that you would fear the LORD your God for all time."
5:1 All the kings of the Amorites on the west side of the Jordan, and all the kings of the Canaanites along the sea, heard that the LORD had dried up the water of the Jordan ahead of the Israelites until they had crossed. Their hearts melted. No spirit was left in them because of the Israelites.
## A second circumcision
5:2 At that time the LORD said to Joshua, "Make flint knives, and circumcise the Israelites again, a second time."
5:3 So Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the Israelites at the Hill of Foreskins.
5:4 This is why Joshua circumcised them. All the people who came out of Egypt, the males, all the men of fighting age, had died in the wilderness on the way after they came out of Egypt.
5:5 All the people who came out had been circumcised. But none of the people born in the wilderness on the way after they came out of Egypt had been circumcised.
5:6 For the Israelites walked forty years in the wilderness until the whole nation died off, the men of fighting age who came out of Egypt, because they had not listened to the voice of the LORD. The LORD had sworn to them that he would not let them see the land the LORD swore to their ancestors to give us, a land flowing with milk and honey.
5:7 He raised up their sons in their place, and these Joshua circumcised. They were uncircumcised, because no one had circumcised them on the way.
5:8 When the whole nation had finished being circumcised, they stayed put in the camp until they were well again.
5:9 The LORD said to Joshua, "Today I have rolled away from you the disgrace of Egypt." So that place is called Gilgal to this day.
## Passover in the land
5:10 The Israelites camped at Gilgal and kept the Passover on the evening of the fourteenth day of the month, on the plains of Jericho.
5:11 The day after the Passover they ate some of the produce of the land: bread without yeast and roasted grain, on that very day.
5:12 The manna stopped the next day, once they ate from the produce of the land. The Israelites had no more manna. That year they ate from the crops of the land of Canaan.
## The commander of the LORD's army
5:13 Once, when Joshua was by Jericho, he raised his eyes and looked, and there in front of him stood a man holding a drawn sword. Joshua went up to him and asked, "Are you on our side, or on our enemies' side?"
5:14 He said, "No. I am the commander of the LORD's army. Now I have come." Joshua fell face down to the ground and bowed down. He said to him, "What does my lord say to his servant?"
5:15 The commander of the LORD's army said to Joshua, "Pull your sandal off your foot, because the place you are standing on is holy." And Joshua did so.
## Seven days around Jericho
6:1 Now Jericho was shut up tight because of the Israelites. No one was going out, and no one was coming in.
6:2 The LORD said to Joshua, "See, I have given Jericho into your hand, with its king and its strong warriors.
6:3 March around the town, all you fighting men. Circle the town once. Do this for six days.
6:4 Seven priests are to carry seven ram's-horn trumpets ahead of the ark. On the seventh day you are to march around the town seven times, and the priests are to blow the trumpets.
6:5 When they blow a long blast on the ram's horn, as soon as you hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people are to give a great shout. Then the wall of the town will collapse where it stands, and the people will go up, every man straight ahead."
6:6 So Joshua son of Nun called the priests and said to them, "Lift up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry seven ram's-horn trumpets ahead of the ark of the LORD."
6:7 And he said to the people, "Go forward and march around the town, and let the armed men go ahead of the ark of the LORD."
6:8 When Joshua had spoken to the people, the seven priests carrying the seven ram's-horn trumpets before the LORD went forward and blew the trumpets, and the ark of the covenant of the LORD followed them.
6:9 The armed men walked ahead of the priests who blew the trumpets, and the rear guard walked behind the ark, while the trumpets kept sounding.
6:10 But Joshua commanded the people, "Do not shout. Do not let your voices be heard. Do not let a word come out of your mouths until the day I tell you, 'Shout!' Then you will shout."
6:11 So he had the ark of the LORD go around the town, circling it once. Then they went back to the camp and spent the night in the camp.
6:12 Joshua got up early in the morning, and the priests lifted up the ark of the LORD.
6:13 The seven priests carrying the seven ram's-horn trumpets ahead of the ark of the LORD kept walking and blowing the trumpets. The armed men walked ahead of them, and the rear guard walked behind the ark of the LORD, while the trumpets kept sounding.
6:14 On the second day they marched around the town once and went back to the camp. They did this for six days.
6:15 On the seventh day they got up at dawn and marched around the town in the same way seven times. Only on that day did they march around the town seven times.
6:16 The seventh time, when the priests blew the trumpets, Joshua said to the people, "Shout! For the LORD has given you the town.
## Under the ban
6:17 The town and everything in it are to be under the ban for the LORD. Only Rahab the prostitute is to live, she and all who are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers we sent.
6:18 But you, keep away from what is under the ban, or you will put things under the ban and then take some of them. Then you would make the camp of Israel something under the ban and bring trouble on it.
6:19 All the silver and gold, and the things made of bronze and iron, are holy to the LORD. They must go into the treasury of the LORD."
6:20 So the people shouted, and the trumpets were blown. When the people heard the sound of the trumpet, they gave a great shout, and the wall collapsed where it stood. The people went up into the town, every man straight ahead, and they captured the town.
6:21 They put everything in the town under the ban with the edge of the sword: men and women, young and old, cattle, sheep and donkeys.
## Rahab is spared
6:22 Joshua said to the two men who had spied out the land, "Go into the prostitute's house. Bring the woman out from there with all who belong to her, as you swore to her."
6:23 So the young men, the spies, went in and brought out Rahab with her father, her mother, her brothers and all who were hers. They brought out all her relatives and gave them a place outside the camp of Israel.
6:24 Then they burned the town with fire, and everything in it. Only the silver and gold and the things made of bronze and iron they put into the treasury of the house of the LORD.
6:25 But Joshua let Rahab the prostitute live, with her father's household and all who belonged to her. She has lived among Israel to this day, because she hid the messengers Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.
6:26 At that time Joshua made them swear an oath. He said, "Cursed before the LORD be the man who rises up and builds this town again, this Jericho. With his firstborn he will lay its foundation, and with his youngest he will hang its gates."
6:27 So the LORD was with Joshua, and word of him went out through all the land.
## Achan takes what was banned
7:1 But the Israelites acted unfaithfully in regard to what was under the ban. Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of what was under the ban. So the LORD's anger burned against the Israelites.
## Defeat at Ai
7:2 Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is near Beth-aven, east of Bethel. He said to them, "Go up and spy out the land." So the men went up and spied out Ai.
7:3 They came back to Joshua and said to him, "Do not send all the people up. Let about two or three thousand men go up and attack Ai. Do not make all the people toil up there, because the people of Ai are few."
7:4 So about three thousand men from the people went up there, but they ran from the men of Ai.
7:5 The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them. They chased them from the town gate as far as Shebarim and struck them down on the slope. The hearts of the people melted and turned to water.
7:6 Joshua tore his clothes and fell face down to the ground before the ark of the LORD until evening, he and the elders of Israel. They threw dust on their heads.
7:7 Joshua said, "Oh no, Lord GOD! Why did you bring this people all the way across the Jordan, just to give us into the hand of the Amorites to wipe us out? If only we had agreed to stay on the far side of the Jordan!
7:8 Please, Lord, what can I say, now that Israel has turned its back and run from its enemies?
7:9 The Canaanites and all who live in the land will hear of it. They will surround us and cut off our name from the earth. Then what will you do for your great name?"
7:10 The LORD said to Joshua, "Get up! Why are you lying on your face like this?
7:11 Israel has sinned. They have also broken my covenant that I commanded them. They have also taken some of what was under the ban. They have also stolen, also lied, and also put it among their own things.
7:12 That is why the Israelites cannot stand against their enemies. They turn their backs and run from their enemies, because they themselves have become something under the ban. I will not be with you anymore unless you destroy what is under the ban from among you.
7:13 Get up, make the people holy. Say, 'Make yourselves holy for tomorrow, for this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: There are things under the ban among you, Israel. You will not be able to stand against your enemies until you remove what is under the ban from among you.
7:14 In the morning you will come forward tribe by tribe. The tribe the LORD takes will come forward clan by clan. The clan the LORD takes will come forward household by household. And the household the LORD takes will come forward man by man.
7:15 The one who is taken with what is under the ban will be burned with fire, he and all that belongs to him, because he has broken the covenant of the LORD and has done a disgraceful thing in Israel.'"
## Achan is found out
7:16 Joshua got up early in the morning and had Israel come forward tribe by tribe, and the tribe of Judah was taken.
7:17 He had the clans of Judah come forward, and he took the clan of the Zerahites. He had the clan of the Zerahites come forward man by man, and Zabdi was taken.
7:18 He had Zabdi's household come forward man by man, and Achan son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken.
7:19 Joshua said to Achan, "My son, please give glory to the LORD, the God of Israel, and make your confession to him. Please tell me what you have done. Do not hide it from me."
7:20 Achan answered Joshua, "Truly, I am the one who sinned against the LORD, the God of Israel. Here is what I did.
7:21 Among the plunder I saw one fine cloak from Shinar, two hundred shekels of silver, and one tongue-shaped bar of gold, fifty shekels in weight. I craved them and took them. There they are, buried in the ground in the middle of my tent, and the silver is under it."
7:22 Joshua sent messengers, who ran to the tent. And look, it was hidden in his tent, and the silver was under it.
7:23 They took the things from inside the tent, brought them to Joshua and all the Israelites, and laid them out before the LORD.
7:24 Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the cloak and the bar of gold, his sons and his daughters, his cattle, his donkeys and his sheep, his tent and everything he had, and brought them up to the Valley of Achor.
7:25 Joshua said, "Why have you brought trouble on us? The LORD will bring trouble on you this day." Then all Israel stoned him with stones. They burned them with fire and stoned them with stones.
7:26 They raised over him a great heap of stones that is there to this day. Then the LORD turned from his burning anger. That is why the name of that place is the Valley of Achor to this day.
## Ai is taken
8:1 The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid, and do not be shaken. Take all the fighting men with you, and get up and go up to Ai. See, I have given the king of Ai into your hand, with his people, his town and his land.
8:2 Do to Ai and its king as you did to Jericho and its king. But you may take its plunder and its livestock as spoil for yourselves. Set an ambush against the town, behind it."
8:3 So Joshua and all the fighting men got ready to go up to Ai. Joshua chose thirty thousand strong warriors and sent them out by night.
8:4 He commanded them, "Look, you are to lie in ambush against the town, behind it. Do not go very far from the town. All of you be ready.
8:5 I and all the people with me will come near the town. When they come out to meet us as they did the first time, we will run away from them.
8:6 They will come out after us until we have drawn them away from the town, for they will say, 'They are running away from us as they did the first time.' While we run from them,
8:7 you will rise up from the ambush and take possession of the town. The LORD your God will give it into your hand.
8:8 When you have captured the town, set it on fire. Do as the LORD has said. See, I have given you your orders."
8:9 Joshua sent them off. They went to their hiding place and sat waiting between Bethel and Ai, on the west side of Ai, while Joshua spent the night among the people.
8:10 Early in the morning Joshua called the people together. He and the elders of Israel went up ahead of the people to Ai.
8:11 All the fighting men who were with him went up and came near, in front of the town. They camped north of Ai, with the valley between them and Ai.
8:12 He took about five thousand men and set them in ambush between Bethel and Ai, west of the town.
8:13 So they placed the people, the whole camp north of the town and its rear guard west of the town. That night Joshua went into the middle of the valley.
8:14 When the king of Ai saw this, the men of the town hurried and got up early and went out to meet Israel in battle, he and all his people, at the meeting place facing the Arabah. He did not know there was an ambush against him behind the town.
8:15 Joshua and all Israel gave way before them as if beaten, and fled along the road to the wilderness.
8:16 All the people in Ai were called out to chase them. They chased Joshua and were drawn away from the town.
8:17 No man in Ai or Bethel stayed behind; all went out after Israel. They left the town wide open as they chased Israel.
8:18 Then the LORD said to Joshua, "Hold out the javelin in your hand toward Ai, for I will give it into your hand." So Joshua held out the javelin in his hand toward the town.
8:19 As soon as he stretched out his hand, the men in ambush rose quickly from their place and ran. They entered the town, captured it and quickly set it on fire.
8:20 The men of Ai turned and looked behind them, and there was the smoke of the town rising to the sky. They had no strength to run this way or that, and the people who had been fleeing to the wilderness turned back against the pursuers.
8:21 When Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had captured the town and that the smoke of the town was rising, they turned back and struck down the men of Ai.
8:22 The others came out of the town to meet them, so the men of Ai were caught in the middle of Israel, some on this side and some on that. Israel struck them down until no one was left, not a survivor or a runaway.
8:23 But the king of Ai they caught alive and brought to Joshua.
8:24 Israel finished killing all who lived in Ai in the open country, in the wilderness where they had chased them, and all of them had fallen by the edge of the sword until they were finished off. Then all Israel went back to Ai and struck it with the edge of the sword.
8:25 All who fell that day, men and women, were twelve thousand, all the people of Ai.
8:26 Joshua did not lower the hand he had stretched out holding the javelin until he had put all who lived in Ai under the ban.
8:27 Only the livestock and the plunder of that town did Israel take as spoil for themselves, as the LORD had commanded Joshua.
8:28 So Joshua burned Ai and made it a ruin mound forever, a wasteland to this day.
8:29 He hung the king of Ai on a tree until evening. At sunset Joshua gave the order, and they took his body down from the tree and flung it at the entrance of the town gate. Over it they piled a great heap of stones, which is still there today.
## The altar on Mount Ebal
8:30 Then Joshua built an altar to the LORD, the God of Israel, on Mount Ebal,
8:31 as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded the Israelites, as it is written in the scroll of the law of Moses: an altar of whole stones, on which no one had swung an iron tool. On it they offered burnt offerings to the LORD and sacrificed peace offerings.
8:32 There, on the stones, Joshua wrote a copy of the law of Moses, which he wrote in the presence of the Israelites.
8:33 All Israel, with its elders, officials and judges, stood on either side of the ark, facing the Levite priests who carried the ark of the covenant of the LORD. The foreigner stood there as well as the native-born. Half of them stood in front of Mount Gerizim and half of them in front of Mount Ebal, as Moses the servant of the LORD had first commanded, to bless the people of Israel.
8:34 Then after this, Joshua read out every word of the law, the blessing and the curse, all just as it is written in the scroll of the law.
8:35 Of all that Moses had commanded, not one word was left that Joshua did not read aloud before the whole assembly of Israel, with the women, the little ones and the foreigners who went along among them.
## The kings join forces
9:1 Now all the kings on the west side of the Jordan heard of this: the kings in the hill country, in the Shephelah and all along the coast of the Great Sea toward Lebanon, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites.
9:2 They gathered together as one to fight against Joshua and Israel.
## The Gibeonites' trick
9:3 But when the people of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and Ai,
9:4 they too acted with cunning. They went and disguised themselves as envoys. They took worn-out sacks for their donkeys, and wineskins that were worn out, split and tied up.
9:5 They had worn-out, patched sandals on their feet and worn-out clothes on their bodies. All the bread they had packed for the journey was dried out and had turned to crumbs.
9:6 They went to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal and said to him and to the men of Israel, "We have come from a far country. So now make a covenant with us."
9:7 The men of Israel said to the Hivites, "Maybe you live right here among us. If so, how could we make a covenant with you?"
9:8 They said to Joshua, "We are your servants." Joshua said to them, "Who are you, and where do you come from?"
9:9 They said to him, "Your servants have come from a land very far away, drawn by the name of the LORD your God. We have heard the news about him and all that he did in Egypt,
9:10 and all that he did to the two kings of the Amorites on the other side of the Jordan, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth.
9:11 Our elders and all who live in our country said to us, 'Take food for the journey in your hand and go to meet them. Say to them, "We are your servants. So now make a covenant with us."'
9:12 Here is our bread. It was still warm when we took it from our houses as food for the road, on the day we set out to come to you. Now look, it has dried out and turned to crumbs.
9:13 These wineskins were new when we filled them, and look, they have split. And these clothes and sandals of ours are worn through from the very long journey."
9:14 The men took some of their food, but they did not ask the LORD what to do.
9:15 Joshua made peace with them. He made a covenant with them that they would live, and the leaders of the community gave them their oath.
## The trick found out
9:16 But three days after they made the covenant with them, the Israelites learned that these people were close by and lived right among them.
9:17 So the Israelites set out and came to their towns on the third day. Their towns were Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth and Kiriath-jearim.
9:18 But the Israelites did not attack them, because the leaders of the community had sworn an oath to them by the LORD, the God of Israel. The whole community grumbled against the leaders.
9:19 But all the leaders said to the whole community, "We have sworn an oath to them by the LORD, the God of Israel, so now we cannot touch them.
9:20 This is what we will do to them: we will let them live, so that anger will not come on us because of the oath we swore to them."
9:21 The leaders said to them, "Let them live." So they became the ones who cut wood and carried water for the whole community, just as the leaders had said about them.
9:22 Joshua called for them and said to them, "Why did you fool us by saying, 'We live very far from you,' when you live among us?
9:23 Now you are cursed. There will always be slaves among you, cutting wood and carrying water for the house of my God."
9:24 They answered Joshua, "Your servants were told plainly that the LORD your God commanded his servant Moses to give you the whole land and to wipe out all who live in the land ahead of you. So we were very afraid for our lives because of you, and we did this thing.
9:25 So here we are, in your hands. Do with us what you think is good and right."
9:26 So he did that for them. He rescued them from the hand of the Israelites, and they did not kill them.
9:27 That day Joshua made them the ones who cut wood and carried water for the community and for the altar of the LORD, at the place he would choose. They still do it to this day.
## Five kings attack Gibeon
10:1 Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem heard that Joshua had captured Ai and put it under the ban, doing to Ai and its king as he had done to Jericho and its king. He also heard that the people of Gibeon had made peace with Israel and were living among them.
10:2 So they were very afraid, because Gibeon was a great town, like one of the royal towns. It was greater than Ai, and all its men were warriors.
10:3 So Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem sent word to Hoham king of Hebron, Piram king of Jarmuth, Japhia king of Lachish and Debir king of Eglon:
10:4 "Come up to me and help me, and let us attack Gibeon, because it has made peace with Joshua and the Israelites."
10:5 So the five kings of the Amorites, the kings of Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish and Eglon, gathered and went up with all their armies. They camped against Gibeon and made war on it.
10:6 The men of Gibeon sent word to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal: "Do not let your servants down! Come up to us quickly and save us. Help us, because all the kings of the Amorites who live in the hill country have gathered against us."
10:7 So Joshua went up from Gilgal, he and all the fighting men with him, all the strong warriors.
10:8 The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid of them, for I have given them into your hand. None of them will hold his ground against you."
10:9 Joshua came on them suddenly, after marching all night from Gilgal.
10:10 The LORD sent them into confusion in front of Israel, and he struck them down in great numbers at Gibeon. He chased them up the Beth-horon pass and cut them down all the way to Azekah and Makkedah.
10:11 As they fled from Israel down the slope of Beth-horon, the LORD threw large stones down on them from the sky all the way to Azekah, and they died. More died from the hailstones than the Israelites killed with the sword.
## The sun stands still
10:12 Then Joshua spoke to the LORD, on the day the LORD handed the Amorites over to the Israelites. He said in the sight of Israel, "Sun, be still at Gibeon, / moon, in the Valley of Aijalon."
10:13 So the sun was still, / and the moon stood, / until the nation took revenge on its enemies. Is this not written in the Book of Jashar? The sun stood in the middle of the sky and did not hurry to go down for about a whole day.
10:14 There has never been a day like that one, before it or after it, when the LORD listened to a man's voice, for the LORD fought for Israel.
10:15 Then Joshua returned, and all Israel with him, to the camp at Gilgal.
## The five kings in the cave
10:16 Now these five kings had run away and hidden in the cave at Makkedah.
10:17 Joshua was told, "The five kings have been found hiding in the cave at Makkedah."
10:18 Joshua said, "Roll large stones over the mouth of the cave, and post men beside it to guard them.
10:19 But do not stop there yourselves. Chase your enemies and cut off their stragglers. Do not let them get into their towns, for the LORD your God has given them into your hand."
10:20 Joshua and the Israelites struck them with a very great defeat until they were finished off, though some survivors escaped and got into the walled towns.
10:21 Then all the people came back safely to Joshua at the camp at Makkedah. No one so much as sharpened his tongue against any of the Israelites.
10:22 Joshua said, "Open the mouth of the cave and bring those five kings out of the cave to me."
10:23 They did so. They brought those five kings out of the cave to him: the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish and the king of Eglon.
10:24 When they had brought these kings out to Joshua, Joshua called all the men of Israel. He said to the commanders of the fighting men who had gone with him, "Come here. Put your feet on the necks of these kings." So they came forward and put their feet on their necks.
10:25 Joshua said to them, "Do not be afraid, and do not be shaken. Be strong and bold. This is what the LORD will do to all the enemies you fight."
10:26 After that Joshua struck them down and killed them, and he hung them on five trees. They stayed hanging on the trees until evening.
10:27 When the sun went down, Joshua gave orders, and they took them down from the trees and threw them into the cave where they had hidden. They put large stones over the mouth of the cave, which are there to this very day.
## The southern towns
10:28 That day Joshua captured Makkedah and struck it and its king with the edge of the sword. He put them under the ban, every person in it. He left no survivor. He did to the king of Makkedah as he had done to the king of Jericho.
10:29 Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, passed on from Makkedah to Libnah and fought against Libnah.
10:30 The LORD gave it and its king into the hand of Israel too. He struck it with the edge of the sword, every person in it. He left no survivor in it. He did to its king as he had done to the king of Jericho.
10:31 Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, passed on from Libnah to Lachish. He camped against it and fought against it.
10:32 The LORD gave Lachish into the hand of Israel, and he captured it on the second day. He struck it with the edge of the sword, every person in it, just as he had done to Libnah.
10:33 Then Horam king of Gezer came up to help Lachish, but Joshua struck him and his people until he left him no survivor.
10:34 Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, passed on from Lachish to Eglon. They camped against it and fought against it.
10:35 They captured it that same day and struck it with the edge of the sword. He put every person in it under the ban that day, just as he had done to Lachish.
10:36 Then Joshua and all Israel with him went up from Eglon to Hebron and fought against it.
10:37 They captured it and struck it with the edge of the sword, its king, all its towns and every person in it. He left no survivor, just as he had done to Eglon. He put it under the ban, and every person in it.
10:38 Then Joshua and all Israel with him turned back to Debir and fought against it.
10:39 He captured it with its king and all its towns. They struck them with the edge of the sword and put every person in it under the ban. He left no survivor. He did to Debir and its king as he had done to Hebron, and as he had done to Libnah and its king.
## The whole south
10:40 So Joshua struck the whole land: the hill country, the Negev, the Shephelah and the slopes, with all their kings. He left no survivor. He put everything that breathed under the ban, as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded.
10:41 Joshua struck them from Kadesh-barnea to Gaza, and the whole land of Goshen as far as Gibeon.
10:42 Joshua captured all these kings and their land at one time, because the LORD, the God of Israel, fought for Israel.
10:43 Then Joshua returned, and all Israel with him, to the camp at Gilgal.
## The northern kings at Merom
11:1 When Jabin king of Hazor heard of this, he sent word to Jobab king of Madon, to the king of Shimron, to the king of Achshaph,
11:2 and to the kings in the north, in the hill country, in the Arabah south of Chinnereth, in the Shephelah and in the heights of Dor on the west,
11:3 to the Canaanites in the east and the west, the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites and the Jebusites in the hill country, and the Hivites below Hermon in the land of Mizpah.
11:4 They came out with all their armies, a people as many as the sand on the seashore, with a great many horses and chariots.
11:5 All these kings joined forces. They came and camped together at the waters of Merom to fight against Israel.
11:6 The LORD said to Joshua, "Do not be afraid of them, because tomorrow at this time I will hand all of them over, dead, to Israel. You must hamstring their horses and set fire to their chariots."
11:7 So Joshua and all the fighting men with him came against them suddenly at the waters of Merom and fell on them.
11:8 The LORD gave them into the hand of Israel. They struck them and chased them as far as Great Sidon and Misrephoth-maim, and east as far as the Valley of Mizpeh. They struck them down until no survivor was left.
11:9 Joshua did to them as the LORD had told him. He hamstrung their horses and set fire to their chariots.
## Hazor burned
11:10 At that time Joshua turned back and took Hazor, and he cut down its king with the sword. Before this, Hazor had been the head of all these kingdoms.
11:11 They struck every person in it with the edge of the sword, putting them under the ban. Nothing that breathed was left. And he burned Hazor with fire.
11:12 Joshua captured all the towns of these kings, and all their kings. He struck them with the edge of the sword and put them under the ban, as Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded.
11:13 But Israel did not burn any of the towns that stood on their mounds, except Hazor. Joshua burned that one alone.
11:14 The Israelites took all the plunder of these towns and the livestock as spoil for themselves. But they struck every human being with the edge of the sword until they had wiped them out. They left no one who breathed.
11:15 As the LORD had commanded his servant Moses, so Moses commanded Joshua, and so Joshua did. He did not leave out a thing from all that the LORD had commanded Moses.
## The land taken
11:16 So Joshua took this whole land: the hill country, all the Negev, the whole land of Goshen, the Shephelah, the Arabah, and the hill country of Israel with its Shephelah,
11:17 from Mount Halak, the bare mountain that goes up toward Seir, to Baal-gad in the Lebanon Valley at the foot of Mount Hermon. He captured all their kings, struck them down and killed them.
11:18 Joshua fought with all these kings for many days.
11:19 No town made peace with the Israelites except the Hivites who lived in Gibeon. Everything else they took in war.
11:20 For it was the LORD's doing to make their hearts stubborn, so that they would meet Israel in battle. That way they would be put under the ban and find no mercy, but be wiped out, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
11:21 At that time Joshua came and cut off the Anakim from the hill country, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, from all the hill country of Judah and from all the hill country of Israel. Joshua put them and their towns under the ban.
11:22 No Anakim were left in the land of the Israelites. Only in Gaza, in Gath and in Ashdod did some remain.
11:23 So Joshua took the whole land, just as the LORD had told Moses, and Joshua gave it as an inheritance to Israel, by their divisions and their tribes. Then the land was quiet, with no more war.
## Kings defeated east of the Jordan
12:1 These are the kings of the land whom the Israelites struck down, and whose land they took possession of, on the other side of the Jordan toward the sunrise, from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon, with all the Arabah on the east:
12:2 Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon. He ruled from Aroer, on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and from the middle of the valley, over half of Gilead as far as the Jabbok River, the border of the Ammonites.
12:3 He also ruled the Arabah as far as the Sea of Chinnereth on the east, and as far as the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, on the east, toward Beth-jeshimoth, and on the south below the slopes of Pisgah.
12:4 Then there was the territory of Og king of Bashan, one of the last of the Rephaim, who lived in Ashtaroth and in Edrei.
12:5 He ruled over Mount Hermon, Salecah and all Bashan as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, and over half of Gilead to the border of Sihon king of Heshbon.
12:6 Moses the servant of the LORD and the Israelites struck them down. And Moses the servant of the LORD gave their land as a possession to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh.
