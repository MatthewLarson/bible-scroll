## Land still to be taken
13:1 Joshua was old, far along in years. The LORD said to him, "You are old and far along in years, and a great deal of land is still left to take possession of.
13:2 This is the land that is left: all the regions of the Philistines and all the Geshurites,
13:3 from the Shihor, which faces Egypt, north to the border of Ekron. It is counted as Canaanite land. This is the land of the five rulers of the Philistines: the Gazites, the Ashdodites, the Ashkelonites, the Gittites and the Ekronites. There are the Avvites too
13:4 in the south. There is all the land of the Canaanites, from Mearah, which belongs to the Sidonians, to Aphek, as far as the border of the Amorites.
13:5 There is the land of the Gebalites, and all Lebanon toward the sunrise, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath.
13:6 I myself will drive out before the Israelites all who live in the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, all the Sidonians. Only, give out the land to Israel by lot as an inheritance, as I commanded you.
13:7 So now divide this land as an inheritance among the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh."
## The tribes east of the Jordan
13:8 Along with the other half of Manasseh, the Reubenites and the Gadites had already received their inheritance. Moses gave it to them on the far side of the Jordan, to the east, as Moses the servant of the LORD had granted them.
13:9 It ran from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and the town in the middle of the valley, and took in the whole high plain of Medeba as far as Dibon.
13:10 It took in all the towns of Sihon king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon, as far as the border of the Ammonites.
13:11 It took in Gilead, the territory of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah:
13:12 the whole kingdom of Og in Bashan, who ruled in Ashtaroth and in Edrei. He was one of the last of the Rephaim. Moses had struck them down and driven them out.
13:13 But the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites and the Maacathites. Geshur and Maacah live among Israel to this day.
13:14 Only to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance. The offerings by fire to the LORD, the God of Israel, are its inheritance, as he promised it.
## Reuben
13:15 Moses gave land to the tribe of the Reubenites, clan by clan.
13:16 Their territory ran from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and the town in the middle of the valley, and took in the whole high plain by Medeba,
13:17 Heshbon and all its towns on the high plain: Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon,
13:18 Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,
13:19 Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley,
13:20 Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth.
13:21 It took in all the towns of the high plain and the whole kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon. Moses had struck him down along with the leaders of Midian, Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur and Reba, the princes of Sihon who lived in the land.
13:22 Among those they killed, the Israelites also put Balaam son of Beor, the diviner, to the sword.
13:23 The border of the Reubenites was the Jordan and its bank. This was the inheritance of the Reubenites, clan by clan: the towns and their villages.
## Gad
13:24 Moses gave land to the tribe of Gad, to the Gadites, clan by clan.
13:25 Their territory was Jazer, all the towns of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites as far as Aroer, which faces Rabbah.
13:26 It ran from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the territory of Lo-debar.
13:27 In the valley it took in Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon. The Jordan was its border, up to the tip of the Sea of Chinnereth, on the east side of the Jordan.
13:28 This was the inheritance of the Gadites, clan by clan: the towns and their villages.
## Half of Manasseh
13:29 Moses gave land to the half-tribe of Manasseh. It belonged to half the tribe of the Manassites, clan by clan.
13:30 Their territory ran from Mahanaim through all Bashan, the whole kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all Havvoth-jair, the tent villages of Jair in Bashan: sixty towns.
13:31 Half of Gilead, with Ashtaroth and Edrei, the royal cities of Og in Bashan, went to the descendants of Machir son of Manasseh, to half the descendants of Machir, clan by clan.
13:32 These are the lands Moses gave out as inheritances on the plains of Moab, on the other side of the Jordan, east of Jericho.
13:33 But to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, as he promised them.
## The land west of the Jordan
14:1 These are the lands the Israelites received as their inheritance in the land of Canaan. They were given out to them by Eleazar the priest, by Joshua son of Nun, and by the family heads of the Israelite tribes.
14:2 Their inheritance was given by lot, as the LORD had commanded through Moses, for the nine and a half tribes.
14:3 For Moses had given the two and a half tribes their inheritance on the other side of the Jordan. But to the Levites he had given no inheritance among them.
14:4 The descendants of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim. The Levites were given no share in the land, only towns to live in, with pasturelands for their livestock and their goods.
14:5 The Israelites did as the LORD had commanded Moses, and they divided the land.
## Caleb asks for Hebron
14:6 The people of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite spoke to him: "You know the word the LORD spoke to Moses the man of God about me and about you at Kadesh-barnea.
14:7 I was forty when Moses the LORD's servant sent me out from Kadesh-barnea to scout the land. I brought him back word as I truly saw it.
14:8 The brothers who went up with me made the people's hearts melt. But I followed the LORD my God fully.
14:9 On that day Moses made a solemn promise: 'The land your foot has walked on will surely become an inheritance for you and for your children forever, because you followed the LORD my God fully.'
14:10 So now, look: the LORD has let me live, just as he promised. Forty-five years have passed since the LORD said this to Moses, back when Israel was making its way through the wilderness. And look at me now. I am eighty-five years old today.
14:11 Even today I have the strength I had on the day Moses sent me. I am as strong now as I was then, strong enough for war and for going out and coming back.
14:12 So now give me this hill country the LORD spoke of that day. You heard yourself that day that the Anakim were there, with large fortified towns. Perhaps the LORD will be with me, and I will drive them out, as the LORD said."
14:13 Joshua blessed Caleb son of Jephunneh and gave him Hebron as his inheritance.
14:14 So Hebron has belonged to Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite as his inheritance to this day, because he followed the LORD, the God of Israel, fully.
14:15 Hebron used to be called Kiriath-arba. Arba was the greatest man among the Anakim. Then the land had rest from war.
## Judah's borders
15:1 The lot for the tribe of the people of Judah, clan by clan, reached south to the border of Edom, to the Wilderness of Zin at the far south.
15:2 Their south border started from the end of the Salt Sea, from the tongue of water that points south.
15:3 It went out south of the Scorpion Pass, passed along to Zin, went up south of Kadesh-barnea, passed along to Hezron, went up to Addar and turned toward Karka.
15:4 It passed along to Azmon, went out to the Wadi of Egypt, and ended at the sea. This will be your south border.
15:5 The east border was the Salt Sea, up to the mouth of the Jordan. The north border started from the tongue of the sea at the mouth of the Jordan.
15:6 The border went up to Beth-hoglah and passed along north of Beth-arabah. Then the border went up to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben.
15:7 The border went up to Debir from the Valley of Achor and turned north to Gilgal, which faces the Ascent of Adummim, south of the ravine. The border passed along to the waters of En-shemesh and ended at En-rogel.
15:8 Then the border went up the Valley of Ben-hinnom along the south slope of the Jebusite city, that is, Jerusalem. The border went up to the top of the hill that faces the Valley of Hinnom on the west, at the north end of the Valley of Rephaim.
15:9 From the top of the hill the border turned to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah and went out to the towns of Mount Ephron. Then the border turned to Baalah, that is, Kiriath-jearim.
15:10 From Baalah the border turned west to Mount Seir, passed along the north slope of Mount Jearim, that is, Chesalon, went down to Beth-shemesh, and passed along to Timnah.
15:11 The border went out to the slope north of Ekron, turned to Shikkeron, passed along to Mount Baalah, and went out to Jabneel. The border ended at the sea.
15:12 The west border was the Great Sea and its shore. This was the border all around the people of Judah, clan by clan.
## Caleb and Achsah
15:13 As the LORD had ordered him, Joshua gave Caleb son of Jephunneh a share among the people of Judah: Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron. Arba was the father of Anak.
15:14 From there Caleb drove out the three sons of Anak, Sheshai, Ahiman and Talmai, descendants of Anak.
15:15 From there he went up against the people living in Debir. Debir used to be called Kiriath-sepher.
15:16 Caleb said, "Whoever attacks Kiriath-sepher and captures it, I will give him my daughter Achsah as his wife."
15:17 Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's brother, captured it. So Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as his wife.
15:18 When she came to Othniel, she pressed him to ask her father for a field. She got down from her donkey, and Caleb said to her, "What is it?"
15:19 She answered, "Give me a blessing. You have given me Negev land, so give me pools of water too." So he gave her the upper pools and the lower pools.

## Joseph's lot
16:1 The lot for the descendants of Joseph went out from the Jordan at Jericho, east of the waters of Jericho, into the wilderness that goes up from Jericho into the hill country to Bethel.
16:2 From Bethel it went out to Luz and passed along to the border of the Archites at Ataroth.
16:3 Westward it went down to the territory of the Japhletites, to the edge of Lower Beth-horon, then on to Gezer, and it ended at the sea.
16:4 So the descendants of Joseph, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their inheritance.
## Ephraim
16:5 This was the territory of the Ephraimites, clan by clan. The border of their inheritance on the east ran from Ataroth-addar to Upper Beth-horon.
16:6 The border went out to the sea, with Michmethath on the north. Then the border turned east to Taanath-shiloh and passed by it on the east to Janoah.
16:7 From Janoah it went down to Ataroth and Naarah, touched Jericho, and went out to the Jordan.
16:8 From Tappuah the border went west along the Kanah Valley and ended at the sea. This was the inheritance of the tribe of the Ephraimites, clan by clan,
16:9 together with the towns set apart for the Ephraimites inside the inheritance of the Manassites, all those towns with their villages.
16:10 But they did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer. The Canaanites live among Ephraim to this day, but they have been put to forced labor.
## Manasseh
17:1 This was the lot for the tribe of Manasseh, for he was Joseph's firstborn. Machir, Manasseh's firstborn and the father of Gilead, had received Gilead and Bashan, because he was a fighting man.
17:2 So the lot went to the rest of the Manassites, clan by clan: the descendants of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher and Shemida. These were the male descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph, clan by clan.
17:3 Now Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, had no sons. He had only daughters, and their names were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah.
17:4 They went up to Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun and the leaders. "The LORD commanded Moses to give us an inheritance among our relatives," they said. So, as the LORD had ordered, Joshua gave them an inheritance among their father's relatives.
17:5 So ten shares fell to Manasseh, besides the land of Gilead and Bashan on the other side of the Jordan,
17:6 because the daughters of Manasseh received an inheritance among his sons. The land of Gilead belonged to the rest of the Manassites.
17:7 The border of Manasseh ran from Asher to Michmethath, which faces Shechem. Then the border went south toward the people of En-tappuah.
17:8 The land of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, but the town of Tappuah on Manasseh's border belonged to the Ephraimites.
17:9 The border went down to the Kanah Valley. The towns south of the valley belonged to Ephraim, though they lay among the towns of Manasseh. Manasseh's border ran along the north side of the valley and ended at the sea.
17:10 The land to the south was Ephraim's, and the land to the north was Manasseh's, with the sea for its border. They reached Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.
17:11 Inside Issachar and Asher, Manasseh also had Beth-shean and its towns and Ibleam and its towns. It had the people of Dor and its towns and the people of En-dor and its towns. It also had the people of Taanach and its towns and the people of Megiddo and its towns. These were the three of Napheth.
17:12 But the Manassites could not take possession of these towns, because the Canaanites were set on living in that land.
17:13 As the Israelites gained strength, they made the Canaanites do forced labor. But as for driving them out, they did not do it.
## Joseph asks for more
17:14 The descendants of Joseph said to Joshua, "Why have you given me only one lot and one share as an inheritance? I am a large people, because the LORD has blessed me so far."
17:15 Joshua answered them, "If there are so many of you, go up into the forest. Clear land for yourselves there, in the land of the Perizzites and the Rephaim, if the hill country of Ephraim is too tight for you."
17:16 The descendants of Joseph replied, "The hill country will not hold us. And the Canaanites who live down in the valley all have iron chariots, whether at Beth-shean and its towns or in the Jezreel Valley."
17:17 Joshua said to the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and Manasseh, "You are a large people, and you have great strength. You will not have only one lot.
17:18 The hill country will be yours. It is forest, but you will clear it, and its farthest reaches will be yours. You will drive out the Canaanites, even though they have iron chariots and are strong."
## The tent at Shiloh
18:1 The whole community of the Israelites came together at Shiloh, and there they set up the tent of meeting. The land had been brought under their control.
18:2 Seven tribes were left among the Israelites who had not yet been given their inheritance.
18:3 So Joshua said to the Israelites, "How long will you hang back from going in to take possession of the land the LORD, the God of your ancestors, has given you?
18:4 Pick three men from each tribe. I will send them out to walk through the land and write a description of it, by the inheritance each will receive. Then they are to come back to me.
18:5 They are to divide it into seven parts. Judah will stay in its territory on the south, and the house of Joseph will stay in its territory on the north.
18:6 Write a description of the land in seven parts and bring it here to me. Then I will cast lots for you here before the LORD our God.
18:7 The Levites have no share among you, because the priesthood of the LORD is their inheritance. And Gad, Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance on the other side of the Jordan, to the east. Moses the servant of the LORD gave it to them."
18:8 So the men got ready to go. Joshua ordered those who were going to write a description of the land: "Go and walk through the land and write a description of it. Then come back to me, and I will cast lots for you here before the LORD in Shiloh."
18:9 The men went and passed through the land. They wrote a description of it in a scroll, town by town, in seven parts. Then they came back to Joshua at the camp in Shiloh.
18:10 Joshua cast lots for them in Shiloh before the LORD. There Joshua divided the land among the Israelites, by their divisions.

## Cities of refuge
20:1 Then the LORD said to Joshua,
20:2 "Tell the Israelites, 'Choose for yourselves the cities of refuge I told you about through Moses,
20:3 so that a killer who strikes someone down by mistake, without meaning to, can run there. They will be a refuge for you from the avenger of blood.
20:4 When he has fled to one of these cities, he must stand at the entrance to the city gate and tell the elders of that city what happened. The elders will then bring him inside to join them, and they will give him a place to live with them.
20:5 Should the avenger of blood come after him, they are not to give the killer up into his hand. He struck down his neighbor without meaning to, and he had not hated him in the past.
20:6 He is to stay in that city until he has stood trial before the community, and until the high priest of that time has died. Then the killer may go back to the town he fled from and to his own house there.'"
20:7 So they set apart Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath-arba (that is, Hebron) in the hill country of Judah.
20:8 East of Jericho, across the Jordan, they appointed Bezer in the wilderness on the high plain, from the tribe of Reuben; Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh.
20:9 These were the cities set aside for all the Israelites and for the foreigner living among them. Anyone who struck someone down by mistake could run there and not die at the hand of the avenger of blood before he stood trial before the community.
## Towns for the Levites
21:1 The heads of the Levite families came to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun, and to the heads of the families of the Israelite tribes.
21:2 They spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan: "The LORD commanded through Moses that we be given towns to live in, with their pasturelands for our livestock."
21:3 So at the LORD's command the Israelites gave the Levites these towns and their pasturelands out of their own inheritance.
21:4 The lot came out for the clans of the Kohathites. The descendants of Aaron the priest, who were Levites, received thirteen towns by lot from the tribes of Judah, Simeon and Benjamin.
21:5 The rest of the Kohathites received ten towns by lot from the clans of the tribe of Ephraim, the tribe of Dan and the half-tribe of Manasseh.
21:6 The Gershonites received thirteen towns by lot from the clans of the tribes of Issachar, Asher and Naphtali, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.
21:7 The Merarites, clan by clan, received twelve towns from the tribes of Reuben, Gad and Zebulun.
21:8 So the Israelites gave the Levites these towns and their pasturelands by lot, as the LORD had commanded through Moses.
## The priests' towns
21:9 From the tribe of the people of Judah and the tribe of the people of Simeon they gave these towns, which are named here one by one.
21:10 They went to the descendants of Aaron, one of the Kohathite clans of the Levites, because the first lot fell to them.
21:11 They gave them Kiriath-arba (Arba was the father of Anak), that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah, with the pastureland around it.
21:12 But the fields of the town and its villages they had given to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his property.
21:13 To the descendants of Aaron the priest they gave Hebron, the city of refuge for killers, with its pastureland, Libnah with its pastureland,
21:14 Jattir with its pastureland, Eshtemoa with its pastureland,
21:15 Holon with its pastureland, Debir with its pastureland,

21:18 Anathoth with its pastureland and Almon with its pastureland: four towns.
21:19 All the towns of the descendants of Aaron, the priests, were thirteen towns with their pasturelands.
## The other Kohathites
21:20 The rest of the Kohathite clans of the Levites received towns by lot from the tribe of Ephraim.
21:21 They were given Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, the city of refuge for killers, with its pastureland, Gezer with its pastureland,
21:22 Kibzaim with its pastureland and Beth-horon with its pastureland: four towns.

21:24 Aijalon with its pastureland and Gath-rimmon with its pastureland: four towns.

21:26 All the towns for the rest of the Kohathite clans were ten, with their pasturelands.
## The Gershonites
21:27 The Gershonite clans of the Levites were given, from the half-tribe of Manasseh, Golan in Bashan, the city of refuge for killers, with its pastureland, and Beeshterah with its pastureland: two towns.
21:28 From the tribe of Issachar they received Kishion with its pastureland, Daberath with its pastureland,

## Every promise kept
21:43 So the LORD gave Israel the whole land he had sworn to give their ancestors, and they took possession of it and made their homes there.
21:44 The LORD gave them rest all around, as he had sworn to their ancestors. Not one of all their enemies could stand up to them. The LORD handed every enemy they had over to them.
21:45 Not one of all the good words the LORD had spoken to the house of Israel fell short. Every one came true.
## The eastern tribes go home
22:1 Joshua now called together the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh.
22:2 He told them, "You have kept every command Moses the servant of the LORD gave you, and when I gave you orders, you listened to me every time.
22:3 All these many days, right up to today, you have not left your brothers. You have kept to the duty the LORD your God commanded you.
22:4 The LORD your God has now given your brothers rest, just as he told them he would. So turn around and go home to your tents, to the land that is yours. Moses the servant of the LORD gave it to you on the far side of the Jordan.
22:5 But take very great care to carry out the command and the law that Moses the servant of the LORD gave you. Love the LORD your God and walk in all his ways. Keep his commands and hold on to him. Serve him with all your heart and with all your being."
22:6 So Joshua blessed them and sent them off, and they went home to their tents.
22:7 (To one half of the tribe of Manasseh Moses had given land in Bashan. To the other half Joshua had given land with their brothers on the west side of the Jordan.) When Joshua sent them away to their tents, he blessed them
22:8 and said to them, "Go back to your tents with great wealth: with very large herds, with silver, gold, bronze and iron, and with a great many clothes. Share the plunder from your enemies with your brothers."
## The altar by the Jordan
22:9 So the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh turned back. They parted from the Israelites at Shiloh in the land of Canaan to go to the land of Gilead, their own land, which they had taken possession of at the LORD's command through Moses.
22:10 They came to Geliloth by the Jordan in the land of Canaan. There by the Jordan the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh built an altar, a big altar for all to see.
22:11 Word reached the Israelites: "Look! The Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built the altar at the edge of the land of Canaan, at Geliloth by the Jordan, on the Israelites' side."
22:12 At this news the whole community of the Israelites gathered at Shiloh, ready to go up and fight them.
22:13 The Israelites sent Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead.
22:14 With him went ten leaders, one leader for each family of all the tribes of Israel. Each was the head of his family among the clans of Israel.
22:15 They came to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead and said to them,
22:16 "The whole community of the LORD says this: 'What is this? You have acted unfaithfully against the God of Israel! Today you turned away from following the LORD. You built yourselves an altar, and today you are rebelling against the LORD.
22:17 Is the guilt of Peor too small a thing for us? To this day we have not made ourselves clean of it, and the plague came on the community of the LORD.
22:18 And you, today you are turning back from following the LORD! You rebel against the LORD today, and tomorrow his anger will fall on the whole community of Israel.
22:19 Is the land you hold unclean? Then come across to the land the LORD holds, where the LORD's tabernacle has its home, and take land for yourselves among us. Just do not rebel against the LORD. Do not rebel against us either, by building yourselves an altar apart from the altar of the LORD our God.
22:20 Think of Achan son of Zerah. When he acted unfaithfully about the things under the ban, was it not the whole community of Israel that anger fell on? He was one man, but he did not perish alone in his guilt.'"
## The altar of witness
22:21 Then the Reubenites, the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh gave their answer to the chiefs of Israel's clans:
22:22 "God of gods, the LORD! God of gods, the LORD! He knows, and Israel must know too! If this was rebellion, if it was unfaithfulness against the LORD, then do not rescue us today.
22:23 Suppose we built ourselves an altar to turn back from following the LORD. Suppose we meant to offer burnt offerings and grain offerings on it, or to make peace offerings on it. Then let the LORD himself make us answer for it.
22:24 No! We did this because we were anxious about something. We thought, 'In days to come your children may say to ours, "What is the LORD, the God of Israel, to you?
22:25 The Jordan is a border the LORD has put between us and you, you Reubenites and Gadites. There is no share in the LORD for you." In that way your children could keep our children from fearing the LORD.'
22:26 So we said, 'Let's do something for ourselves: let's build the altar. It won't be for burnt offerings or for sacrifices.
22:27 It will be a witness between us and you, and between the generations after us. It will show that we do the LORD's service in his presence with our burnt offerings, our sacrifices and our peace offerings. Then in days to come your children will not say to our children, "There is no share in the LORD for you."'
22:28 We also said, 'Should they ever talk that way to us or to our descendants in the future, this will be our answer: "Look at this copy of the LORD's altar. Our ancestors made it. It was not made for burnt offerings or for sacrifices. It is a witness between us and you."'
22:29 Rebel against the LORD? Never! Turn back from following the LORD today by building an altar for burnt offerings, grain offerings or sacrifices? We would never do it. The altar of the LORD our God stands in front of his tabernacle, and we would build no other."
22:30 Phinehas the priest heard what the Reubenites, the Gadites and the Manassites said. So did the community's leaders who were with him, the chiefs of Israel's clans. They were satisfied with it.
22:31 Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest said to the Reubenites, the Gadites and the Manassites, "This day has shown us that the LORD is among us, since you have not acted unfaithfully against him in this matter. That means you have kept the Israelites safe from the LORD's hand."
22:32 Then Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest and the leaders came back from the Reubenites and the Gadites in the land of Gilead to the land of Canaan, to the Israelites, and brought back word to them.
22:33 The Israelites liked what they heard, and they blessed God. Nobody spoke any longer of going up to fight them and lay waste the land where the Reubenites and Gadites lived.
22:34 The Reubenites and the Gadites gave the altar its name, saying, "It is a witness between us that the LORD is God."
## Joshua's farewell
23:1 A long time later, after the LORD had given Israel rest from all their enemies around them, Joshua was old, far along in years.
23:2 Joshua called together all Israel, its elders, its leaders, its judges and its officials, and said to them, "I am old, far along in years.
23:3 You have seen with your own eyes all that the LORD your God did to all these nations because of you. The LORD your God is the one who has fought for you.
23:4 See, I have given you these remaining nations by lot. They are an inheritance for your tribes, along with all the nations I have already cut off. They stretch from the Jordan to the Great Sea where the sun goes down.
23:5 The LORD your God himself will thrust them away ahead of you and drive them out of your path, and you will take possession of their land, as the LORD your God promised you.
23:6 Be very strong, then. Keep everything written in the book of the law of Moses and carry it out. Do not veer from it, right or left.
23:7 Do not go in among these nations that are left with you. Do not even speak the names of their gods, and do not make anyone swear by them. Do not serve those gods or bow down to them.
23:8 Instead, hold on to the LORD your God, as you have done to this day.
23:9 The LORD drove out nations from your path that were large and strong, and to this day no one has been able to stand up to you.
23:10 One of you makes a thousand run, because the LORD your God is the one who fights for you, as he promised you.
23:11 So take great care, for your lives depend on it, to love the LORD your God.
23:12 But suppose you do turn back and hold on to the rest of these nations, the ones still left among you. Suppose you marry them and go in among them, and they among you.
23:13 If so, you can be sure that the LORD your God will no longer drive these nations out before you. They will become a trap and a snare for you, a lash on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you vanish from this good land the LORD your God has given you.
23:14 Look, today I am going the way of all the earth. You know with all your heart and all your being that not one of all the good words the LORD your God spoke about you has fallen short. All of them have come true for you. Not one word of them has fallen short.
23:15 Every good word the LORD your God spoke to you has come true for you. In just the same way the LORD will bring every bad word on you, until he has wiped you out from this good land the LORD your God has given you.
23:16 Suppose you break the covenant the LORD your God laid on you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them. Then the LORD's anger will blaze up against you, and before long you will be gone from the good land he gave you."
## The covenant at Shechem
24:1 Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel at Shechem. He summoned Israel's elders, its leaders, its judges and its officials, and they came and stood before God.
24:2 Joshua said to all the people, "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'From long ago your ancestors lived on the far side of the River. Terah, the father of Abraham and Nahor, lived there, and they served other gods.
24:3 But I took your forefather Abraham from across the River and had him travel all over the land of Canaan. I made his family grow, and I gave him Isaac.
24:4 I gave Isaac his sons Jacob and Esau. The hill country of Seir I gave to Esau to take possession of. Jacob and his sons, though, went down to Egypt.
24:5 I sent Moses and Aaron, and I struck Egypt through the things I did in that land. Then I led you out.
24:6 I brought your ancestors out of Egypt, and you arrived at the sea. Egypt chased your ancestors to the Sea of Reeds with chariots and horsemen.
24:7 Your ancestors cried out to the LORD, and he set darkness between you and the Egyptians. He made the sea come over the Egyptians, and it closed over them. Your own eyes saw what I did in Egypt. After that you spent many years in the wilderness.
24:8 Next I led you into Amorite country on the far side of the Jordan. They made war on you, but I put them in your power. You took possession of their land, and I wiped them out ahead of you.
24:9 Then Balak son of Zippor, the king of Moab, got up and fought against Israel. He had Balaam son of Beor called in to curse you.
24:10 But I refused to listen to Balaam. Instead he blessed you over and over, and I saved you from his power.
24:11 You crossed the Jordan and reached Jericho. The men who held Jericho fought you, and so did the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Hivites and the Jebusites. I put them in your power.
24:12 I sent the hornet out in front of you, and it drove them from your path, the two kings of the Amorites. Your sword and your bow had no part in it.
24:13 I gave you a land you had not worked on, and towns you had not built, and you have settled in them. Vineyards and olive trees you never planted are now feeding you.'
## Choose today
24:14 "So now, fear the LORD. Serve him wholeheartedly and faithfully. Get rid of the gods your ancestors served on the far side of the River and in Egypt, and serve the LORD.
24:15 If in your eyes it is a bad thing to serve the LORD, then decide for yourselves today who it is you will serve. Will it be the gods your ancestors served on the far side of the River? Or will it be the gods of the Amorites, now that you live in their land? But I and my family will serve the LORD."
24:16 The people answered, "Leave the LORD and serve other gods? We would never do that!
24:17 The LORD our God is the one who led us and our ancestors up out of the land of Egypt, that house of slaves. He did these great signs where we could see them. He guarded us the whole way we went, as we passed through the middle of all those peoples.
24:18 The LORD drove all those peoples out of our way, even the Amorites living in this land. So we, for our part, will serve the LORD. He is our God."
24:19 But Joshua told the people, "You will not be able to serve the LORD. He is a holy God, a jealous God. He will not forgive your rebellious acts or your sins.
24:20 Leave the LORD and serve foreign gods, and he will turn against you. After all the good he has done you, he will bring harm on you and finish you off."
24:21 But the people told Joshua, "Not so! It is the LORD we will serve."
24:22 Then Joshua told the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves. You have chosen the LORD as the one you will serve." They said, "We are witnesses to it."
24:23 "Then get rid of the foreign gods you have among you, and bend your hearts toward the LORD, the God of Israel."
24:24 The people answered Joshua, "We will serve the LORD our God, and we will listen to what he says."
24:25 Joshua made a covenant for the people that day. He set out a rule and a ruling for them at Shechem.
24:26 Joshua wrote these words in the book of God's law. He also took a great stone and stood it upright there, beneath the oak at the LORD's sanctuary.
24:27 Joshua said to all the people, "Look at this stone. It will stand as a witness against us, because it heard everything the LORD said when he spoke with us. It will stand as a witness against you, so that you do not deny your God."
24:28 Joshua then let the people go, and each of them went home to his own inheritance.
## Three graves
24:29 When all this was over, Joshua son of Nun, the servant of the LORD, died. He had lived a hundred and ten years.
24:30 They buried him within the borders of his own inheritance, at Timnath-serah in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.
24:31 As long as Joshua was alive, Israel served the LORD. They went on serving him as long as the elders who lived on after Joshua were alive, men who knew all that the LORD had done for Israel.
24:32 The Israelites had carried Joseph's bones up from Egypt. They buried them at Shechem, in the plot of ground Jacob had bought from the sons of Hamor, Shechem's father, for a hundred qesitahs. That plot came to belong to Joseph's descendants as their inheritance.
24:33 Then Eleazar son of Aaron died. They buried him at Gibeah, the hill of his son Phinehas, which had been given to him in the hill country of Ephraim.
