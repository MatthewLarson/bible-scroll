## David grows old
1:1 King David was now old, and his days were many. They piled clothes on him, but he could not get warm.
1:2 So his servants said to him, "Let a young woman be found for my lord the king, a virgin. She will attend the king and look after him. She will lie in your embrace, and my lord the king will get warm."
1:3 They looked for a beautiful young woman in every part of Israel's land, and they found Abishag the Shunammite. They brought her to the king.
1:4 The young woman was beautiful, very beautiful indeed. She looked after the king and waited on him, but the king did not sleep with her.
## Adonijah reaches for the crown
1:5 Now Adonijah son of Haggith was raising himself up. "I am the one who will be king," he said. He got himself a chariot and horsemen, and fifty men ran in front of him.
1:6 His father had never in his life corrected him by saying, "Why did you do that?" He was also very good-looking, and his mother had borne him after Absalom.
1:7 He made his plans with Joab son of Zeruiah and with Abiathar the priest, and they took Adonijah's side and helped him.
1:8 But Zadok the priest, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, Nathan the prophet, Shimei, Rei and David's warriors did not side with Adonijah.
1:9 Adonijah offered sacrifices of sheep, cattle and fat calves by the Stone of Zoheleth, next to En-rogel. He invited all his brothers, the sons of the king, and all the men of Judah who were the king's servants.
1:10 But Nathan the prophet, Benaiah, the warriors and his brother Solomon he did not invite.
## Nathan and Bathsheba go to the king
1:11 Then Nathan spoke to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother. "Haven't you heard?" he said. "Adonijah son of Haggith is king now, and David our lord does not even know.
1:12 Come, please let me give you advice. Save your life and your son Solomon's life.
1:13 Go to King David. Say to him, 'My lord the king, you swore an oath to your servant, didn't you? You said, "Your son Solomon will reign after me. He is the one who will sit on my throne." Then why is Adonijah king?'
1:14 Look: while you are speaking there with the king, I will come in behind you and make your words complete."
1:15 So Bathsheba went in to the king, to his bedroom. The king was very old, and Abishag the Shunammite was waiting on him.
1:16 Bathsheba bent low and bowed down to the king. "What brings you here?" the king said.
1:17 "My lord," she said to him, "you took an oath by the LORD your God to your servant. You said, 'Your son Solomon will reign after me. He is the one who will sit on my throne.'
1:18 Now look: Adonijah is king! And now, my lord the king, you do not know it.
1:19 He has offered bulls, fat calves and sheep, a great many. All the king's sons are invited, and Abiathar the priest, and Joab the army commander. But your servant Solomon he did not invite.
1:20 As for you, my lord the king, all Israel is watching you, waiting for you to tell them who will sit on my lord the king's throne after him.
1:21 If not, then once my lord the king lies down with his ancestors, my son Solomon and I will be the ones held guilty."
1:22 Just then, while she was still talking with the king, in came Nathan the prophet.
1:23 Word was given to the king: "Look, Nathan the prophet!" Nathan came into the king's presence and bowed down to the king, face to the ground.
1:24 "My lord the king," Nathan said, "did you yourself say, 'Adonijah will reign after me. He is the one who will sit on my throne'?
1:25 Today he went down and offered bulls, fat calves and sheep, a great many. He invited all the king's sons, the army's commanders and Abiathar the priest. There they are, eating and drinking in front of him, and they have shouted, 'Long live King Adonijah!'
1:26 He did not invite me, your servant. He did not invite Zadok the priest, or Benaiah son of Jehoiada, or your servant Solomon.
1:27 Did this thing really come from my lord the king? And you did not tell your servant who would sit on my lord the king's throne after him?"
## David keeps his oath
1:28 King David replied, "Bring Bathsheba back to me." She came in and stood in front of the king.
1:29 Then the king swore an oath. "As the LORD lives, who has set my life free from every trouble," he said,
1:30 "I swore to you by the LORD, the God of Israel, 'Your son Solomon will reign after me. He is the one who will sit on my throne in my place.' And today that is what I will do."
1:31 Bathsheba bent low, face to the ground, and bowed down to the king. "May my lord King David live forever!" she said.
## Solomon is anointed at Gihon
1:32 Then King David said, "Bring me Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet and Benaiah son of Jehoiada." They came in before the king.
1:33 The king told them, "Take the servants of your lord along. Put my son Solomon on the mule that is mine, and bring him down to Gihon.
1:34 Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet will anoint him there as king over Israel. Then give a blast on the ram's horn and cry, 'Long live King Solomon!'
1:35 Follow him back up here. He will come in and sit on my throne, and he will reign in my place. He is the one I have named ruler over Israel and over Judah."
1:36 "Amen!" Benaiah son of Jehoiada answered the king. "So may the LORD say, the God of my lord the king.
1:37 The LORD has been with my lord the king. In the same way may he be with Solomon, and make Solomon's throne greater than the throne of my lord King David!"
1:38 So Zadok the priest went down, with Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Kerethites and the Pelethites. They put Solomon on King David's mule and led him to Gihon.
1:39 From the tent Zadok the priest took the horn of oil, and he anointed Solomon. The ram's horn sounded, and all the people cried, "Long live King Solomon!"
1:40 Everyone came up behind him, piping on flutes. Their joy was enormous, and the noise they made split the earth.
## The feast breaks up
1:41 The noise reached Adonijah and all the guests he had invited. They had just finished eating. Joab heard the blast of the ram's horn and said, "What is that sound? Why is the town in an uproar?"
1:42 The words were still in his mouth when Jonathan son of Abiathar the priest came in. "Come," said Adonijah. "You are a man of worth, so your news is good."
1:43 "No," Jonathan answered Adonijah. "Our lord King David has made Solomon king.
1:44 The king sent Zadok the priest with him, and Nathan the prophet, Benaiah son of Jehoiada, and the Kerethites and the Pelethites. They set him riding on the king's mule.
1:45 At Gihon, Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him king. They came up from there full of joy, and the town is buzzing. The noise you heard is that.
1:46 What is more, Solomon is sitting on the throne of the kingdom.
1:47 The king's servants have also come in to bless our lord King David. 'May God make the name of Solomon better than your name,' they said, 'and make his throne greater than yours!' And the king bowed down there on his bed.
1:48 The king also said this: 'Blessed is the LORD, the God of Israel. Today he has given me someone to sit on my throne, and my own eyes are seeing it.'"
1:49 All the guests Adonijah had invited trembled. They stood up and left, each man going his own way.
1:50 Adonijah was afraid of Solomon. He got up and went and took hold of the horns of the altar.
1:51 Someone told Solomon, "Look, Adonijah fears King Solomon. He is holding onto the horns of the altar and saying, 'First let King Solomon promise me on oath that he will not kill his servant with the sword.'"
1:52 Solomon answered, "If he acts as a man of worth, not a single hair of his will fall to the ground. But if any wrong turns up in him, he will die."
1:53 King Solomon sent for him, and men brought him down from the altar. Adonijah came and bowed down before King Solomon. "Go home," Solomon told him.
## David's last charge
2:1 The days of David's life were nearly over, and he gave his son Solomon these commands:
2:2 "I am going the way of all the earth. So be strong. Be a man.
2:3 Guard what the LORD your God has put in your care. Walk in his ways, and keep his rules, his commands, his rulings and his decrees, just as they are written in the law of Moses. Then you will act wisely and do well in everything you do and everywhere you turn.
2:4 And the LORD will make his word stand, the word he spoke about me: 'If your sons guard their way, walking before me in truth with all their heart and with all their being, then a man of yours will never be cut off from Israel's throne.'
2:5 And you know yourself what Joab son of Zeruiah did to me, what he did to the two commanders of Israel's armies, Abner son of Ner and Amasa son of Jether. He killed them. In time of peace he spilled blood as if at war, and he put that war blood on the belt at his waist and on the sandals on his feet.
2:6 Use your wisdom. Do not let his gray hair go down in peace to Sheol.
2:7 To the sons of Barzillai of Gilead, though, you must show loyal love. Count them among the ones who eat from your table. That is how they came to my side when I was running from your brother Absalom.
2:8 There is also Shimei son of Gera with you, the Benjaminite from Bahurim. He cursed me, a stinging curse, on the day I was going to Mahanaim. Then he came down to the Jordan to meet me, and I gave him my oath by the LORD: 'I will not kill you with the sword.'
2:9 But do not let him go unpunished. You are wise, and you will know how to deal with him. Bring his gray hair down to Sheol with blood on it."
2:10 David lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him in the City of David.
2:11 The time David was king over Israel came to forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.
## Adonijah asks for Abishag
2:12 Solomon sat on the throne of David his father, and his kingdom grew very secure.
2:13 Adonijah son of Haggith came to Bathsheba, Solomon's mother. "Is it peace that brings you?" she asked. "Peace," he said.
2:14 "I have a word for you," he said. "Say it," she replied.
2:15 "You know the kingship belonged to me," he said. "All Israel had set their faces toward me to make me king. Then the kingship turned around and became my brother's, for it came to him from the LORD.
2:16 Now I have one request to ask of you. Don't turn me down." "Go on," she told him.
2:17 "Please talk to King Solomon," he said, "since he will never turn you down. Let him give me Abishag the Shunammite to be my wife."
2:18 "All right," said Bathsheba. "I myself will speak to the king about you."
2:19 Bathsheba went in to King Solomon to speak with him about Adonijah. The king stood to meet her and bowed low before her. Then he took his throne again, and a throne was set out for the king's mother. She sat at his right.
2:20 "There is one small thing I want to ask you," she said. "Don't turn me down." "Ask, Mother," the king said. "I will not turn you down."
2:21 "Give Abishag the Shunammite to your brother Adonijah," she said, "to be his wife."
2:22 "Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? Why ask that?" King Solomon answered his mother. "Ask the kingship for him too! He is my older brother, after all. Ask it for him, and for Abiathar the priest, and for Joab son of Zeruiah!"
2:23 King Solomon took an oath by the LORD. "God do this to me, and more besides," he said, "if Adonijah has not spoken this word at the cost of his life!
2:24 Now, as the LORD lives, who made me firm and seated me on the throne of my father David, and who has made me a house just as he said he would: today Adonijah will be put to death."
2:25 King Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada to do it. Benaiah fell on Adonijah, and Adonijah died.
## Abiathar sent home
2:26 The king told Abiathar the priest, "Off to Anathoth, to your own fields! You are a man marked for death. But today I will not kill you, because you carried the ark of the Lord GOD in front of David my father, and you suffered everything my father suffered."
2:27 Solomon threw Abiathar out of the LORD's priesthood. In this way the LORD's word came true, the word he had spoken in Shiloh against Eli's family.
## Joab dies at the altar
2:28 The report came to Joab. Joab had turned to follow Adonijah, though he had not turned to follow Absalom. So Joab ran to the LORD's tent and grabbed the horns of the altar.
2:29 Word reached King Solomon: "Joab has run to the LORD's tent. Look, he is beside the altar." Solomon sent Benaiah son of Jehoiada. "Go," he said. "Fall on him."
2:30 Benaiah came to the LORD's tent. "This is the king's word," he told Joab. "Come out!" "No," said Joab. "Here is where I will die." Benaiah took the answer back to the king: "This is what Joab said. This is how he answered me."
2:31 "Do what he said," the king told him. "Fall on him, and bury him. Remove from me and from my father's house the blood Joab poured out for no reason.
2:32 The LORD will turn his blood back onto his own head. He fell on two men who were more in the right than he was, and better men, and he cut them down with the sword. My father David did not know about it. They were Abner son of Ner, commander of Israel's army, and Amasa son of Jether, commander of Judah's army.
2:33 Let their blood return on Joab's head and his children's heads for all time. But David, his children, his house and his throne will have peace from the LORD for all time to come."
2:34 Benaiah son of Jehoiada went up and fell on Joab and put him to death. Joab was buried at his own house in the wilderness.
2:35 In Joab's place the king made Benaiah son of Jehoiada head of the army. In Abiathar's place he put Zadok the priest.
## Shimei crosses the line
2:36 The king sent and called Shimei. "Build a house for yourself in Jerusalem," he told him, "and stay there. Do not leave it to go here or there.
2:37 If ever you go out and cross the Kidron Valley, know this for certain: you will surely die. Your blood will be on your head."
2:38 "That is fair," Shimei answered the king. "My lord the king has spoken, and your servant will do it." Shimei lived in Jerusalem for many days.
2:39 At the end of three years, two slaves belonging to Shimei escaped to Achish son of Maacah, the king of Gath. Someone told Shimei, "Look, your slaves are in Gath."
2:40 So Shimei got up and put a saddle on his donkey. He went to Gath, to Achish, to hunt for his slaves, and he brought them back from Gath.
2:41 Solomon heard that Shimei had left Jerusalem for Gath and come back.
2:42 The king sent and called Shimei. "I made you swear by the LORD, didn't I?" he said. "I warned you: 'The day you go out and travel here or there, know for certain that you will surely die.' And you told me, 'That is fair. I hear you.'
2:43 So why did you not keep the LORD's oath, and the order I laid on you?"
2:44 The king went on, "You know all the harm you did to David my father. Your own heart knows it. Now the LORD is turning your harm back onto your head.
2:45 King Solomon, though, will have the blessing, and the throne of David will stand firm in the LORD's presence for all time."
2:46 The king gave Benaiah son of Jehoiada his orders. Benaiah went out and fell on Shimei, and Shimei died. Now the kingdom was firm in Solomon's hand.
## Pharaoh's daughter and the high places
3:1 Solomon became Pharaoh's son-in-law. He married the daughter of Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and brought her into the City of David until he had finished building his own house, the LORD's house, and the wall that ran around Jerusalem.
3:2 Only, the people kept offering sacrifices at the high places. Up to that time no house had been built for the LORD's name.
3:3 Solomon loved the LORD. He lived by the rules his father David had kept. Only, he too sacrificed at the high places and sent offerings up in smoke there.
## The dream at Gibeon
3:4 The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice, since the greatest high place was there. On that altar Solomon offered up a thousand burnt offerings.
3:5 It was at Gibeon, in a dream in the night, that the LORD showed himself to Solomon. "Ask," God said. "What should I give you?"
3:6 Solomon answered, "You showed great loyal love to my father David, your servant, as he walked before you in truth and in what was right, with an honest heart toward you. And you have kept up this great loyal love for him. You gave him a son who sits on his throne, as he does today.
3:7 And now, LORD my God, it is you who made your servant king after David my father, and I am only a youngster. I don't know how to come and go.
3:8 Here is your servant in the middle of the people you chose, a great people, so many that no one can number them or count them.
3:9 Give your servant a heart that listens, so he can judge your people and see the difference between good and bad. Who is able to judge your people, as many as they are?"
3:10 The Lord liked what Solomon had asked for.
3:11 So God told him, "This is what you asked for. You did not ask to live many days. You did not ask for riches. You did not ask for the lives of your enemies. You asked for insight, to hear cases and judge them.
3:12 Look, I am doing just what you said. Look, I am giving you a heart that is wise and understanding. No one before you was your equal, and no one after you will rise up to equal you.
3:13 And what you did not ask, I am giving you as well: riches and glory. As long as you live, no one among the kings will be like you.
3:14 If you walk in my ways, keeping my rules and my commands the way your father David walked, I will also lengthen your days."
3:15 Solomon woke up. It had been a dream! He came to Jerusalem and stood in front of the ark of the Lord's covenant. There he offered up burnt offerings and made peace offerings, and he held a feast for all his servants.
## Two mothers and one child
3:16 Later two women came to the king. They were prostitutes, and they stood in front of him.
3:17 "Please, my lord," said one of the women. "I and this woman share one house. I had a baby there, with her in the house.
3:18 On the third day after I gave birth, this woman gave birth as well. We were by ourselves. No outsider was with us in the house; only the two of us were there.
3:19 Then this woman's son died in the night. She had lain on top of him.
3:20 In the middle of the night she got up. While your servant was sleeping, she took my son from beside me and laid him in her arms. Her dead son she laid in my arms.
3:21 I got up in the morning to feed my son, and there he was, dead. But when it was light I studied him carefully, and he was not my son, not the one I bore."
3:22 "No!" said the other woman. "My son is the live one, and your son is the dead one." And the first one kept saying, "No! Your son is the dead one, and my son is the live one." So they talked on in front of the king.
3:23 Then the king said, "This one says, 'This is my son, the live one, and your son is the dead one.' And that one says, 'No! Your son is the dead one, and my son is the live one.'"
3:24 "Get me a sword," the king said. They carried the sword in to the king.
3:25 "Cut the live child in two," said the king. "Give one half to the first woman and the other half to the second."
3:26 The mother of the live boy felt her love for her son burning inside her, and she spoke to the king. "Please, my lord, give her the live child," she said. "Whatever you do, don't kill him!" But the other woman said, "He won't be mine or yours. Cut!"
3:27 Then the king gave his answer. "Give the live child to her. Do not kill him. She is his mother."
3:28 All Israel heard about the ruling the king had made, and they feared the king. They could see that God's wisdom was in him for doing justice.
## Solomon's officials
4:1 So it was that King Solomon reigned over all Israel.
4:2 His officials were these: the priest, Azariah son of Zadok;
4:3 the scribes, Elihoreph and Ahijah, sons of Shisha; the recorder, Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud;
4:4 over the army, Benaiah son of Jehoiada; priests, Zadok and Abiathar;
4:5 over the officers, Azariah son of Nathan; priest and the king's friend, Zabud son of Nathan;
4:6 over the palace, Ahishar; over the forced labor, Adoniram son of Abda.
## Twelve officers to feed the king
4:7 Solomon had twelve officers posted over all Israel, and they kept the king and his household fed. Each one was responsible for one month of the year.
4:8 Their names were: Ben-hur, in the hill country of Ephraim;
4:9 Ben-deker, in Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh and Elon-beth-hanan;
4:10 Ben-hesed, in Arubboth; Socoh and all the land of Hepher were his;
4:11 Ben-abinadab, all of Naphath-dor (Taphath, Solomon's daughter, became his wife);
4:12 Baana son of Ahilud, in Taanach and Megiddo, and all of Beth-shean beside Zarethan below Jezreel, from Beth-shean to Abel-meholah, as far as the far side of Jokmeam;
4:13 Ben-geber, in Ramoth-gilead; the villages of Jair son of Manasseh in Gilead were his, and so was the region of Argob in Bashan, sixty great towns with walls and bronze bars;
4:14 Ahinadab son of Iddo, in Mahanaim;
4:15 Ahimaaz, in Naphtali (he too took a daughter of Solomon as his wife, Basemath);
4:16 Baana son of Hushai, in Asher and Bealoth;
4:17 Jehoshaphat son of Paruah, in Issachar;
4:18 Shimei son of Ela, in Benjamin;
4:19 Geber son of Uri, in the land of Gilead, the land of Sihon king of the Amorites and of Og king of Bashan. And there was one officer who was in the land.
## Plenty and peace
4:20 Judah and Israel were many, like the sand beside the sea in number. They were eating and drinking and glad.
4:21 Solomon was ruler over all the kingdoms from the River to the Philistines' land and to Egypt's border. They brought him tribute and served Solomon as long as he lived.
4:22 Every day Solomon's food took thirty cors of fine flour and sixty cors of ordinary flour,
4:23 ten fat oxen, twenty oxen from the pasture and a hundred sheep, not counting deer, gazelles, roe deer and fattened fowl.
4:24 He held power over the whole region Beyond the River, from Tiphsah to Gaza, over all the kings Beyond the River, and he had peace with everyone around him.
4:25 As long as Solomon lived, Judah and Israel stayed safe, everyone sitting under his own vine and his own fig tree, from Dan all the way to Beersheba.
4:26 For his chariots Solomon kept horses in forty thousand stalls, and he had twelve thousand horsemen.
4:27 Month by month, each in his turn, those officers kept King Solomon supplied, and everyone who came near King Solomon's table. Nothing was missing.
4:28 They also brought barley and straw for the horses and the swift steeds to wherever it was due, each man as he was assigned.
## Wiser than all the east
4:29 God gave Solomon wisdom, and understanding in great measure, and a heart as broad as the sand along the edge of the sea.
4:30 Solomon's wisdom went beyond the wisdom of all the peoples of the east and all Egypt's wisdom.
4:31 He was wiser than any human being: than Ethan the Ezrahite, than Heman and Calcol and Darda, the sons of Mahol. His name was known in all the nations around him.
4:32 Three thousand proverbs came from his lips, and there were one thousand and five of his songs.
4:33 He talked about trees, all the way from the cedar that stands in Lebanon to the hyssop that springs out of a wall. He talked about beasts and birds, about creatures that creep, and about fish.
4:34 From every people they came to listen to the wisdom of Solomon. They came from all the earth's kings, who had heard about his wisdom.
## Hiram and the cedars of Lebanon
5:1 Hiram, king of Tyre, sent his servants to Solomon. He had heard that they had anointed Solomon king to take his father's place, and Hiram had been a friend of David's for all those years.
5:2 Solomon sent word to Hiram. He said,
5:3 "You know about my father David. He could not build a house for the name of the LORD his God, because of the war that closed in around him, until the LORD put his enemies under the soles of his feet.
5:4 Now, though, the LORD my God has given me rest all around. There is no adversary, and nothing bad is happening.
5:5 So I have it in mind to build a house for the name of the LORD my God. That is what the LORD said to David my father: 'Your son, whom I will set on your throne after you, is the one who will build the house for my name.'
5:6 Now give the command. Let them cut down cedars in Lebanon for me. My servants will be there with your servants, and I will pay your servants' wages to you, whatever amount you name. You know we have nobody among us who can cut timber the way the Sidonians can."
5:7 Hiram heard what Solomon said and was overjoyed. "Blessed is the LORD today," he said, "for giving David a wise son to be over this great people."
5:8 Hiram sent back to Solomon, "I have heard what you sent to tell me. Everything you wish for in cedar wood and cypress wood, I will do.
5:9 My servants will haul it from Lebanon down to the sea. I will tie it into rafts on the sea and send it to whatever place you name to me. There I will take the rafts apart, and you can carry the logs off. And you will do what I wish: give my household food."
5:10 So Hiram kept on giving Solomon cedar wood and cypress wood, as much as he wanted.
5:11 Solomon, for his part, gave Hiram twenty thousand cors of wheat to feed his household, and twenty cors of oil from beaten olives. That is what Solomon gave Hiram every year.
5:12 And the LORD gave wisdom to Solomon, as he had told him he would. Hiram and Solomon were at peace, and they made a covenant between the two of them.
## The labor force
5:13 King Solomon drafted a labor force out of all Israel. The labor force came to thirty thousand men.
5:14 He sent them to Lebanon ten thousand at a time, a new group each month. They would be a month in Lebanon and two months at home. Adoniram was over the labor force.
5:15 Solomon also had seventy thousand men to carry loads, and eighty thousand to cut stone in the hill country.
5:16 This was apart from the chief officers Solomon had placed over the work, three thousand three hundred of them. They gave orders to the people who were doing the work.
5:17 The king gave orders, and they dug out great stones, stones of high value, to lay the house's foundation with blocks of cut stone.
5:18 Solomon's builders and Hiram's builders, with the men of Gebal, cut and shaped them. So they made the wood and the stones ready for building the house.
## Building the LORD's house
6:1 Four hundred and eighty years had passed since the Israelites came out of the land of Egypt. It was the fourth year that Solomon had reigned over Israel, in the month of Ziv, which is the second month. Then he built the house for the LORD.
6:2 The house King Solomon built for the LORD measured sixty cubits in length, twenty in width and thirty cubits in height.
6:3 The porch in front of the great hall of the house was twenty cubits long, matching the width of the house, and it stretched ten cubits out from the front of the house.
6:4 For the house he made windows, framed and fixed shut.
6:5 Against the wall of the house he built a structure all around, against the walls of the house around both the great hall and the inner room, and he made side rooms all around.
6:6 The bottom level of the side structure was five cubits wide, the middle six cubits and the third seven. On the outside of the house he set the wall back in steps all around, so that nothing had to be fastened into the walls of the house.
6:7 The house was built of stone that was made ready at the quarry. While they were building it, no sound of hammer or ax or any iron tool was heard in the house.
6:8 The door to the bottom level of side rooms was on the south side of the house. You went up by winding stairs to the middle level, and from the middle to the third.
6:9 He built the house and brought it to completion. He covered the house with a ceiling of cedar beams and boards.
6:10 Along the whole house he built the side structure, five cubits high, and it held onto the house by cedar timbers.
## The LORD's word to Solomon
6:11 Then the word of the LORD came to Solomon. It said:
6:12 "This house that you are building: if you live by my rules, and do what my rulings say, and keep all my commands and walk in them, then I will make good my word with you, the word I gave David your father.
6:13 I will dwell in the middle of the Israelites. I will not leave my people Israel."
## Cedar and gold inside
6:14 Solomon built the house, and he finished it.
6:15 On the inside he built the walls of the house with cedar boards, from the house's floor to the beams of the ceiling. He overlaid the inside with wood, and he laid the floor of the house with cypress boards.
6:16 At the far end of the house he walled off twenty cubits with cedar boards, from the floor up to the beams. He built this inside space as the inner room, the most holy place.
6:17 The house, meaning the great hall in front of the inner room, was forty cubits long.
6:18 Inside, the house had cedar carved with gourd shapes and opening blossoms. It was all cedar. Not a stone showed.
6:19 Deep inside the house, in its middle, he made the inner room ready as the place to put the ark of the LORD's covenant.
6:20 The front of the inner room was twenty cubits in length, twenty in width and twenty in height. He covered it with refined gold, and he covered the cedar altar too.
6:21 Solomon covered the inside of the house with refined gold. Across the front of the inner room he ran chains of gold, and he covered it with gold.
6:22 The whole house he covered with gold, until all of the house was done. And the whole altar that belonged to the inner room he covered with gold.
## The cherubim
6:23 Inside the inner room he made two cherubim from the wood of olive trees. They stood ten cubits tall.
6:24 Each wing of a cherub measured five cubits, the one wing and the other, so that from the end of one wing to the end of the other was ten cubits.
6:25 The other cherub was ten cubits as well. Both cherubim had one measure and one form.
6:26 One cherub stood ten cubits high, and so did the other.
6:27 He set the cherubim in the middle of the inner house. Their wings were spread wide. A wing of the first reached one wall, a wing of the second reached the opposite wall, and in the middle of the house their wings met, wing against wing.
6:28 He covered the cherubim in gold.
6:29 All around the walls of the house, in the inner room and the outer one, he carved designs: cherubim, palm trees and opening blossoms.
6:30 Even the floor of the house he covered with gold, in the inner room and the outer one.
## Doors and courtyard
6:31 At the doorway to the inner room he made doors of olive wood. Its doorframe and posts had five sides.
6:32 On the two olive-wood doors he carved cherubim, palm trees and opening blossoms. He covered them with gold, and he beat the gold down over the cherubim and the palm trees.
6:33 For the doorway of the great hall he did the same, making doorposts of olive wood. These had four sides.
6:34 There were two doors of cypress wood. Each door was made of two panels that folded on pivots.
6:35 He carved cherubim and palm trees and opening blossoms, and he covered the carvings with gold laid smoothly over them.
6:36 The inner courtyard he built with three layers of cut stone and a layer of cedar beams.
6:37 In the fourth year, in the month of Ziv, the foundation of the LORD's house was laid.
6:38 And in the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, which is the eighth month, the house was complete, every part of it, all according to its design. Seven years he had worked to build it.
## Solomon's palace
7:1 His own house Solomon was thirteen years in building, until he had finished all of it.
7:2 He also built the House of the Forest of Lebanon. It measured a hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide and thirty cubits high. It rested on four rows of cedar pillars, with cedar beams laid across the pillars.
7:3 Above the side rooms that sat on the pillars it had a ceiling of cedar. There were forty-five of them, fifteen in every row.
7:4 There were three rows of window frames, with window opposite window, three tiers of them.
7:5 All the doorways and their posts had square frames. At the front, too, window faced window, three tiers high.
7:6 He made the Porch of Pillars, fifty cubits long and thirty cubits wide. There was a porch at its front, with pillars, and a roof over them.
7:7 He also made the throne porch, where he would give judgments, the Porch of Judgment. It was lined with cedar from one floor to the other.
7:8 The house where he himself would live was in the other courtyard, further in from the porch, and it was built in the same way. And for Pharaoh's daughter, whom Solomon had taken as wife, he made a house like this porch.
7:9 All of these were built of valuable stones, cut to size and trimmed with saws on the inside and the outside, from the foundation up to the top layer, and on the outside as far as the great courtyard.
7:10 Its foundation was of valuable stones, huge stones, stones of ten cubits and stones of eight cubits.
7:11 Higher up were more valuable stones cut to size, and cedar.
7:12 All around the great courtyard there were three layers of cut stone and a layer of cedar beams, the same as for the inner courtyard of the LORD's house and for the porch of the house.
## Hiram the bronze worker
7:13 King Solomon sent for Hiram and had him brought from Tyre.
7:14 His mother was a widow from the tribe of Naphtali, and his father was a man from Tyre who worked in bronze. Hiram was filled with skill, understanding and know-how for doing every kind of work in bronze. He came to King Solomon and did all his work.
## The two bronze pillars
7:15 He formed the two bronze pillars. The first pillar stood eighteen cubits high, and a cord twelve cubits long would reach around the second.
7:16 He made two capitals of cast bronze to place on the pillars' tops. The first capital was five cubits tall, and the second capital was five cubits tall.
7:17 For the capitals on top of the pillars there were nets of checker work and twisted cords made like chains, seven for the first capital and seven for the second.
7:18 He made the pomegranates: two rows all around on the one network, to cover the capitals on top of the pillars. He did the same for the second capital.
7:19 The capitals on the tops of the pillars at the porch were lily work, four cubits of it.
7:20 The capitals on the two pillars also had, higher up, next to the bulge beside the network, two hundred pomegranates in rows all around. So it was on the second capital too.
7:21 He raised the pillars at the porch of the great hall. He stood up the south pillar and called its name Jakin, and he stood up the north pillar and called its name Boaz.
7:22 Lily work crowned the tops of the pillars, and the work on the pillars came to its end.
## The Sea and the stands
7:23 He made the Sea out of cast metal. From one lip to the other it was ten cubits, and it was round all the way around. It stood five cubits high, and a cord thirty cubits long would go around it.
7:24 Under its lip, all around, there were gourd shapes ringing it, ten to every cubit, going right around the Sea. There were two rows of gourds, cast as part of it when it was cast.
7:25 It stood on twelve oxen. Three looked toward the north, three toward the west, three toward the south and three toward the east. The Sea sat up on top of them, and their back parts all pointed inward.
7:26 Its metal was as thick as a hand is wide. Its lip was shaped like the lip of a cup, like a lily flower opening. It could hold two thousand baths.
7:27 Next he made the stands, ten of them, out of bronze. A single stand was four cubits long and four cubits wide, and three cubits high.
7:28 The stands were built like this. They had panels, and the panels fitted between crossbars.
7:29 On the panels between the crossbars were lions, oxen and cherubim. On the crossbars there was a base above, and below the lions and oxen there were wreaths of hanging work.
7:30 Every stand had four wheels of bronze, with bronze axles. Its four feet had brackets under the basin. The brackets were cast, with wreaths beside each one.
7:31 Its mouth, inside the crown at the top, came up one cubit. The mouth was round, made like a base, a cubit and a half across. On its mouth there were carvings too. Its panels were square, not round.
7:32 The four wheels were beneath the panels, and the wheel axles were joined to the stand. A wheel was a cubit and a half tall.
7:33 The wheels were built the way a chariot wheel is built. Everything about them was cast: axles, rims, spokes and hubs.
7:34 At the four corners of each stand there were four brackets, made in one piece with the stand.
7:35 On top of the stand was a ring, half a cubit high, round all the way. Its handles and its panels came out of the top of the stand itself.
7:36 On the flat parts of its handles and on its panels he cut cherubim, lions and palm trees, as much as each space would hold, and wreaths around them.
7:37 He made the ten stands this way. All of them had one casting, one measure and one form.
7:38 He made ten basins of bronze. A single basin could hold forty baths, and each basin was four cubits. One basin sat on each stand, for all ten stands.
7:39 He placed five of the stands at the south side of the house and five at its north side. The Sea he placed at the south side of the house, toward the southeast.
## Hiram's work listed
7:40 Hiram made the basins too, and the shovels and the bowls. So Hiram completed all the work that he did for King Solomon for the LORD's house:
7:41 the two pillars; the two bowl-shaped capitals on top of the pillars; the two networks to cover the two bowls of the capitals on top of the pillars;
7:42 the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, two rows of pomegranates for each network, to cover the two bowls of the capitals on the pillars;
7:43 the ten stands, with the ten basins on the stands;
7:44 the one Sea, with the twelve oxen under the Sea;
7:45 and the pots, the shovels and the bowls. All these objects Hiram made for King Solomon, for the LORD's house, of shining bronze.
7:46 In the Jordan valley the king cast them, in molds of clay earth, between Succoth and Zarethan.
7:47 Solomon did not weigh any of the objects, because there were far too many. No one ever knew how much the bronze weighed.
## The gold furnishings
7:48 Solomon made all the objects for the LORD's house as well: the altar of gold; the table of gold where the Presence Bread was laid;
7:49 the lampstands, five at the right and five at the left before the inner room, of refined gold; and the flower work, the lamps and the tongs, of gold;
7:50 the cups, wick trimmers, bowls, ladles and fire pans, of refined gold; and the gold door sockets, for the doors of the inner house, the most holy place, and for the doors of the house, that is, the great hall.
7:51 Now all the work King Solomon did for the LORD's house was complete. Solomon brought in the holy gifts of David his father, the silver and the gold and the objects, and stored them in the treasure rooms of the LORD's house.
## The ark comes to the house
8:1 Then Solomon called together the elders of Israel, all the tribal heads and the leaders of the Israelite families. They came to King Solomon in Jerusalem to bring the ark of the LORD's covenant up out of the City of David, that is, Zion.
8:2 Every man in Israel assembled before King Solomon in the month of Ethanim, at the feast. That is the seventh month.
8:3 All Israel's elders came, and the priests picked up the ark.
8:4 They carried up the LORD's ark, with the tent of meeting and all the holy objects that were in the tent. It was the priests and the Levites who carried them up.
8:5 In front of the ark stood King Solomon, and with him all the community of Israel who had gathered to him. They were sacrificing sheep and cattle, so many that no one could count them or number them.
8:6 The priests carried the ark of the LORD's covenant in to where it belonged: the inner room of the house, the most holy place, beneath the cherubim's wings.
8:7 For the cherubim's wings were spread out over the spot where the ark was, and from above, the cherubim made a covering over the ark and its poles.
8:8 The poles were long, and the tips of the poles showed from the holy place in front of the inner room, but they did not show outside. They have stayed there until today.
8:9 Nothing was inside the ark but the two tablets of stone. Moses had put them there at Horeb, when the LORD made his covenant with the Israelites as they came out of the land of Egypt.
## The cloud fills the house
8:10 As the priests came out from the holy place, the cloud filled the LORD's house.
8:11 The priests were not able to stand and serve, because of the cloud. The LORD's glory filled the LORD's house.
8:12 Then Solomon said, "The LORD has said / he will live in the thick dark cloud.
8:13 I have built you a princely house, / a settled place for you to live forever."
## Solomon blesses the people
8:14 The king turned to face them and blessed all the assembly of Israel. All Israel's assembly stood there.
8:15 "Blessed is the LORD, the God of Israel," he said. "His own mouth spoke to David my father, and his own hand has made it happen. He said,
8:16 'From the day I brought my people Israel out of Egypt, I have chosen no city out of all Israel's tribes as the place to build a house where my name would be. But I chose David to be over my people Israel.'
8:17 Building a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, was something David my father had set his heart on.
8:18 But the LORD told David my father, 'You set your heart on building a house for my name. It was good that you set your heart on it.
8:19 But you are not going to build the house. Instead your son, who will come out of your own body, will build the house for my name.'
8:20 Now the LORD has made his word come true, the word he spoke. I have stood up in place of David my father. I sit on Israel's throne, just as the LORD said, and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.
8:21 And I have set up a place there for the ark. In it lies the LORD's covenant, the one he made with our ancestors when he brought them out from the land of Egypt."
## Solomon's prayer
8:22 Solomon took his place before the LORD's altar, facing all the assembly of Israel. He lifted his open palms to the heavens,
8:23 and he said, "LORD, God of Israel, not in the heavens above, and not on the earth below, is there any God like you. You keep covenant and loyal love with your servants who walk in your presence with their whole heart.
8:24 For your servant David my father you have kept what you told him. Your mouth said it, and your hand has made it happen, as it stands this day.
8:25 So now, LORD, God of Israel, keep for your servant David my father what you told him: 'A man of yours will never be cut off from before me, from sitting on Israel's throne, if only your sons guard their way and walk before me as you walked before me.'
8:26 And now, God of Israel, please make your word stand firm, the word you spoke to David my father, your servant.
8:27 But can God truly make his home on earth? Look: the heavens, the heavens above the heavens, are not big enough for you. Then how could this house I built be enough!
8:28 All the same, LORD my God, turn toward your servant's prayer and his plea for grace. Hear the cry, the prayer your servant prays in front of you today.
8:29 Night and day keep your eyes open toward this house, the place you spoke of when you said, 'My name will be there.' Listen to the prayer your servant prays toward this place.
8:30 Listen to the pleas of your servant and of Israel your people when they pray toward this place. Yes, listen from the place where you live, from the heavens. Hear, and forgive.
8:31 "Say a man sins against his neighbor, and a curse oath is laid on him to make him swear. If he comes and swears before your altar in this house,
8:32 then you, in the heavens, hear and act. Be judge for your servants. Declare the guilty man guilty and bring his own ways down on his head. Declare the one in the right to be in the right, and give him what his rightness deserves.
8:33 "Say your people Israel go down in defeat before an enemy because they sinned against you. If they turn back to you, and acknowledge your name, and pray and plead for grace before you in this house,
8:34 then you, in the heavens, hear. Forgive your people Israel their sin, and bring them home to the land you gave their ancestors.
8:35 "Say the sky is locked shut and no rain falls, because they sinned against you. If they pray toward this place, and acknowledge your name, and turn back from their sin because you are pressing them down,
8:36 then you, in the heavens, hear. Forgive the sin of your servants, your people Israel. Yes, teach them the good road to walk on, and give rain on your land, the land you gave your people as their inheritance.
8:37 "Say there is hunger in the land, or deadly disease, or crops scorched or moldy, or swarms of locusts of every kind. Say their enemy lays siege to them in the land where their towns are. Whatever the blow, whatever the sickness,
8:38 every prayer, every plea for grace that comes from any person, from any of your people Israel, when each one knows the wound in his own heart and spreads his palms toward this house,
8:39 then you, in the heavens, the place where you live, hear. Forgive, and act. Give each person what fits all his ways, for you know his heart. You and no one else know the heart of every human being.
8:40 So they will fear you every day they are alive on the face of the ground you gave our ancestors.
8:41 "And the foreigner too, the one who is not from your people Israel: say he comes from a land far away for your name's sake,
8:42 since people will hear about your great name and your strong hand and your outstretched arm. If he comes and prays toward this house,
8:43 then you, in the heavens, the place where you live, hear. Do all that the foreigner calls to you for. So all the earth's peoples will know your name and fear you, just like your people Israel, and they will know that your name has been called over this house I built.
8:44 "Say your people march out to fight their enemy, on whatever road you send them. If they pray to the LORD facing the city you chose and the house I built for your name,
8:45 then from the heavens hear their prayer and their plea for grace, and do justice for them.
8:46 "Say they sin against you, since there is no human being who never sins, and you grow angry with them and hand them over to an enemy. Say their captors carry them off as prisoners to the enemy's land, whether far or near.
8:47 If, in the land where they were taken as prisoners, they take it to heart and turn back and plead for grace with you there in their captors' land, saying, 'We have sinned. We have gone wrong. We have done evil,'
8:48 and they come back to you with all their heart and all their being in the land of the enemies who carried them away, and they pray to you facing their land, the land you gave their ancestors, and the city you chose, and the house I built for your name,
8:49 then from the heavens, the place where you live, hear their prayer and their plea for grace, and do justice for them.
8:50 Forgive your people who sinned against you, and all the rebellions they committed against you. Let their captors look on them with pity and treat them with pity.
8:51 They are your people, after all, your own inheritance. You brought them out of Egypt, out from the middle of the iron furnace.
8:52 Keep your eyes open to the pleas of your servant and the pleas of your people Israel, and listen to them every time they call to you.
8:53 You separated them out from all the peoples of the earth to be your inheritance, just as you said by the hand of Moses your servant when you brought our ancestors out of Egypt, Lord GOD."
## Solomon blesses the assembly
8:54 Solomon came to the end of praying to the LORD all this prayer and plea for grace. Then he got up from in front of the LORD's altar. He had been down on his knees, with his palms spread toward the heavens.
8:55 Standing, he called out a blessing over all the assembly of Israel with a loud voice:
8:56 "Blessed is the LORD! He has given his people Israel a resting place, just as he said he would. Of all the good words he spoke by the hand of Moses his servant, not one word has fallen short.
8:57 The LORD our God be with us the way he was with our ancestors! May he not leave us, and may he not let us go.
8:58 May he bend our hearts to himself, so that we walk in all his ways and keep his commands, his rules and his rulings, which he laid on our ancestors.
8:59 These words of mine, the plea I made before the LORD: let them stay close to the LORD our God by day and by night. Then he will do justice for his servant and justice for his people Israel, each day what that day needs,
8:60 so that every people on earth will know that the LORD is God. There is no other.
8:61 So let your heart be whole with the LORD our God, walking in his rules and keeping his commands, as you are today."
## The dedication feast
8:62 The king, with all Israel at his side, was offering sacrifices in the LORD's presence.
8:63 As the peace offerings that he sacrificed to the LORD, Solomon gave twenty-two thousand cattle and a hundred and twenty thousand sheep. This is how the king and all the Israelites dedicated the LORD's house.
8:64 That day the king set apart as holy the center of the courtyard in front of the LORD's house. He had to offer up the burnt offering, the grain offering and the fat of the peace offerings there, because the bronze altar that stood before the LORD was too small to take them all: the burnt offering, the grain offering and the fat of the peace offerings.
8:65 At that time Solomon kept the feast, and all Israel with him. It was a huge gathering, from Lebo-hamath down to the Brook of Egypt, before the LORD our God, for seven days and then seven days more, fourteen days.
8:66 On the eighth day he let the people go. They blessed the king and went off to their tents. They were happy, their hearts were full, because of all the good the LORD had done for David his servant and for Israel his people.
