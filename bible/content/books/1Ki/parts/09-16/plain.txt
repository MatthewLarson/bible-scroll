## The LORD appears to Solomon again
9:1 Solomon finished building the LORD's house and the king's house, and everything else Solomon had wanted and was pleased to do.
9:2 Then the LORD appeared to Solomon a second time, just as he had appeared to him at Gibeon.
9:3 The LORD said to him, "I have heard your prayer and your plea for mercy that you made before me. I have made this house you built holy, and set my name there forever. My eyes and my heart will be there all the days to come.
9:4 As for you, if you walk before me as your father David walked, honest in heart and upright, doing all I have commanded you and keeping my rules and my rulings,
9:5 then I will make the throne of your kingdom over Israel stand forever. That is what I promised your father David when I said, 'A man of yours will never be cut off from Israel's throne.'
9:6 But if you or your sons ever turn back from following me, and do not keep my commands and my rules that I set before you, and you go and serve other gods and bow down to them,
9:7 then I will cut Israel off from the face of the land I gave them. This house that I made holy for my name I will throw away out of my sight. Israel will become an example in people's sayings and a target of their jokes among all the peoples.
9:8 As for this house, high as it is, everyone who passes by it will be stunned and will whistle. They will ask, 'Why has the LORD done this to this land and to this house?'
9:9 And the answer will be, 'Because they left the LORD their God, who brought their ancestors out of the land of Egypt. They took hold of other gods, bowed down to them and served them. That is why the LORD brought this whole disaster upon them.'"
## Twenty towns for Hiram
9:10 Twenty years went by while Solomon built the two houses, the LORD's house and the king's house.
9:11 Hiram king of Tyre had supplied Solomon with cedar logs, cypress logs and gold, as much as he wanted. At the end of that time King Solomon gave Hiram twenty towns in the land of Galilee.
9:12 Hiram came out from Tyre to see the towns Solomon had given him, but they did not look right to him.
9:13 "What sort of towns are these you have given me, my brother?" he said. That is why they are known as the land of Cabul even today.
9:14 Hiram had sent the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold.
## Solomon's forced labor and building
9:15 Here is the account of the forced labor King Solomon raised to build the LORD's house, his own house, the Millo, the wall of Jerusalem, and Hazor, Megiddo and Gezer.
9:16 Pharaoh king of Egypt had come up and captured Gezer. He burned it down and killed the Canaanites living in the city. Then he gave it as a parting gift to his daughter, Solomon's wife.
9:17 So Solomon rebuilt Gezer and Lower Beth-horon,
9:18 Baalath, and Tamar in the wilderness, in the land,
9:19 and all of Solomon's storage towns, the towns for his chariots and the towns for his horsemen, and whatever Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon and in all the land he ruled.
9:20 There were still people left from the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, who were not Israelites.
9:21 Their descendants were left after them in the land, because the Israelites had not been able to put them under the ban. Solomon drafted them as slave labor, and so it is to this day.
9:22 But Solomon made no one from the Israelites a slave. They were his fighting men, his servants, his officials, his officers, and the commanders of his chariots and his horsemen.
9:23 These were the chief overseers in charge of Solomon's work: five hundred and fifty of them, giving orders to the people who did the work.
9:24 Pharaoh's daughter moved up from the City of David to the house Solomon had built for her. It was after this that he built the Millo.
9:25 Three times a year Solomon offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar he had built for the LORD. He burned incense along with them on the altar that stood before the LORD. So he brought the house to completion.
## Ships to Ophir
9:26 King Solomon also built a fleet of ships at Ezion-geber, which is beside Eloth on the shore of the Sea of Reeds, in the land of Edom.
9:27 Hiram sent his own servants with the fleet, sailors who knew the sea, to work alongside Solomon's servants.
9:28 They reached Ophir, took gold from there, four hundred and twenty talents, and carried it to King Solomon.
## The queen of Sheba
10:1 The queen of Sheba heard the news about Solomon, news tied to the name of the LORD. So she came to put him to the test with riddles.
10:2 She arrived in Jerusalem with a very large company, and camels loaded with spices, a great deal of gold and precious stones. She came to Solomon and put to him everything she had in her heart.
10:3 Solomon answered every question she raised. Nothing she brought up was a secret to the king, and he left nothing unexplained.
10:4 The queen of Sheba saw all of Solomon's wisdom and the house he had built.
10:5 She saw the food on his table, the seating of his officials, the way his attendants stood and how they were dressed, his cupbearers, and the burnt offerings he offered up at the LORD's house. It left her breathless.
10:6 She said to the king, "What I heard back home about your sayings and your wisdom was the truth.
10:7 But I did not trust the words until I came here and my eyes saw for themselves. Why, they did not tell me the half! You have added wisdom and good fortune to all I had heard.
10:8 Your men are fortunate! Fortunate are these servants of yours, who stand in your presence day after day and listen to your wisdom!
10:9 May the LORD your God be blessed! He took delight in you and seated you on Israel's throne. The LORD's love for Israel lasts forever. That is why he made you king, to do what is just and right."
10:10 She gave the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, a huge amount of spices, and precious stones. Spices never again came in such amounts as the queen of Sheba gave King Solomon.
10:11 Besides this, Hiram's fleet, which carried gold from Ophir, brought great loads of almug wood from Ophir, and precious stones.
10:12 From the almug wood the king made supports for the LORD's house and for the king's house, and harps and lyres for the singers. No almug wood like it has come in or been seen to this day.
10:13 Whatever the queen of Sheba wanted and asked for, King Solomon gave her, on top of the gifts he gave her with a king's open hand. Then she and her servants turned and went home to her own land.
## Solomon's gold
10:14 Each year Solomon took in gold weighing six hundred and sixty-six talents.
10:15 That was apart from what the traders and the dealers' business brought in, and from all the Arabian kings and the governors of the land.
10:16 King Solomon had two hundred body-length shields made of hammered gold. He used six hundred shekels of gold on each one.
10:17 He made three hundred round shields of hammered gold too, with three minas of gold to each shield. The king placed them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon.
10:18 The king made a great throne of ivory as well, and covered it with refined gold.
10:19 Six steps led up to the throne. Behind it, the throne had a round top. The seat had an arm on each side, and two lions stood beside the arms.
10:20 Twelve lions stood there on the six steps, on this side and that. No kingdom ever had one made like it.
10:21 Every cup King Solomon drank from was gold, and every utensil in the House of the Forest of Lebanon was fine gold. None was silver. In Solomon's time silver was not thought worth anything.
10:22 For the king had Tarshish ships on the sea with Hiram's ships. One time in three years the Tarshish ships would arrive, bringing gold and silver, ivory, monkeys and apes.
10:23 So King Solomon became greater than any king on earth, in riches and in wisdom.
10:24 People from all the earth came looking to see Solomon's face and hear his wisdom, the wisdom God had placed in his heart.
10:25 Each one brought a gift: things of silver and things of gold, robes, weapons, spices, horses and mules, a set amount every year.
## Chariots and horses
10:26 Solomon built up a force of chariots and horsemen, one thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen. He based them in the chariot towns and with the king in Jerusalem.
10:27 In Jerusalem the king made silver like the stones, and he made cedars like the wild fig trees of the Shephelah, there were so many.
10:28 The horses Solomon had came out of Egypt and out of Que. The king's dealers would buy them from Que for a price.
10:29 A chariot came up out of Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for one hundred and fifty. In the same way the dealers brought them out to all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.
## Solomon's wives turn his heart
11:1 King Solomon gave his love to many women from foreign nations, and to Pharaoh's daughter too. There were women of Moab, Ammon and Edom, of Sidon, and of the Hittites.
11:2 They came from the nations the LORD had spoken of to the Israelites: "Do not go in among them, and they are not to come in among you. They will surely bend your hearts toward their gods." It was to them that Solomon held on, out of love.
11:3 Seven hundred wives belonged to him, women of noble rank, and three hundred concubines. His wives bent his heart away.
11:4 By the time Solomon was old, his wives had bent his heart to follow other gods. His heart was not whole with the LORD his God the way his father David's heart had been.
11:5 Solomon went after Ashtoreth, goddess of the Sidonians, and after Milcom, the vile idol of the Ammonites.
11:6 Solomon did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He did not follow the LORD all the way, as his father David had.
11:7 Then Solomon built a high place on the mountain facing Jerusalem, for Chemosh, the vile idol of Moab, and for Molech, the vile idol of the Ammonites.
11:8 That is what he did for every one of his foreign wives, so they could burn incense and sacrifice to their own gods.
## The LORD's sentence
11:9 The LORD grew angry with Solomon. Solomon's heart had swung away from the LORD, Israel's God, who had shown himself to him two times.
11:10 He had given him orders on this very matter, that he must not go after other gods. But Solomon did not keep to what the LORD had ordered.
11:11 So the LORD said to Solomon, "Because this is what you have done, and you have not kept my covenant and my rules that I laid on you, I am going to rip the kingdom from you. Rip it I will, and hand it to your servant.
11:12 Only, I will not do it while you live, for your father David's sake. From your son's hand I will rip it away.
11:13 And yet I will not rip away all the kingdom. One tribe I will give your son, because of David my servant and because of Jerusalem, the city I chose."
## Opponents: Hadad and Rezon
11:14 The LORD stirred up an adversary for Solomon, Hadad the Edomite. He came from the king's family line in Edom.
11:15 Back when David was dealing with Edom, Joab the army commander went up to bury the men who had been killed, and he struck down every male in Edom.
11:16 For six months Joab stayed there with all Israel, until he had cut off every male in Edom.
11:17 But Hadad got away to Egypt, along with some Edomite men who had served his father. Hadad was just a small boy.
11:18 They set out from Midian and came to Paran. They took men with them from Paran and came to Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt. Pharaoh gave Hadad a house and land, and ordered food for him.
11:19 Hadad won great favor with Pharaoh. Pharaoh gave him a wife, the sister of his own wife, Queen Tahpenes.
11:20 Tahpenes' sister bore Hadad his son Genubath, and Tahpenes weaned the boy inside Pharaoh's household. So Genubath was part of Pharaoh's household, among Pharaoh's sons.
11:21 In Egypt, Hadad heard that David had lain down with his ancestors and that Joab the army commander was dead. So Hadad said to Pharaoh, "Send me off, and I will go to my own land."
11:22 Pharaoh asked him, "What do you not have here with me, that you are trying to go off to your own land?" He answered, "Not a thing. Just let me go, please let me go."
11:23 God stirred up another adversary for Solomon, Rezon son of Eliada. Rezon had run away from his master, Hadadezer king of Zobah.
11:24 He collected men around him and became captain of a raiding band at the time David was killing the men of Zobah. They went to Damascus, settled there and took power in Damascus.
11:25 He was an adversary to Israel all Solomon's days, on top of the harm Hadad did. He hated Israel, and he was king over Aram.
## Ahijah and Jeroboam
11:26 Then there was Jeroboam son of Nebat, an Ephraimite from Zeredah, who served Solomon. His mother's name was Zeruah, a widow. He too lifted his hand against the king.
11:27 This is how it came about that he raised his hand against the king. Solomon had built the Millo, and he was closing the breach of the City of David his father.
11:28 The man Jeroboam was strong and able. Solomon saw that the young man got work done, and he set him over all the forced labor of the house of Joseph.
11:29 It was at that time that Jeroboam left Jerusalem. On the road, the prophet Ahijah the Shilonite found him. Ahijah had wrapped himself in a new cloak, and there was no one but the two of them out in the fields.
11:30 Ahijah grabbed the new cloak he had on and ripped it into twelve pieces.
11:31 Then he told Jeroboam, "Take ten pieces for yourself. This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: 'Look, I am tearing the kingdom out of Solomon's hand, and I will give the ten tribes to you.
11:32 One tribe will stay his, because of my servant David and because of Jerusalem, the city I chose from all the tribes of Israel.
11:33 This is because they left me. They bowed down to Ashtoreth, goddess of the Sidonians, to Chemosh, god of Moab, and to Milcom, god of the Ammonites. They did not walk in my ways, doing what is right in my eyes and keeping my rules and my rulings, like his father David.
11:34 Still, I will not take all the kingdom out of his hand. I will make him leader for as long as he lives, because of David my servant, my chosen one, who kept my commands and my rules.
11:35 It is from his son's hand that I will take the kingship, and I will give it to you: the ten tribes.
11:36 His son will get one tribe from me, so that a lamp will burn for David my servant before me always in Jerusalem, the city I chose for myself as the place for my name.
11:37 But you I will take, and you will reign over everything you long for. You will be king over Israel.
11:38 If you listen to every order I give you, if you walk in my ways and do right in my eyes, keeping my rules and my commands the way David my servant did, then I will be with you. I will build you a house that lasts, the way I built one for David, and Israel I will give to you.
11:39 For this I will bring David's line low, though not for all time.'"
11:40 So Solomon set out to kill Jeroboam. But Jeroboam got up and ran away to Egypt, to Shishak king of Egypt, and he remained in Egypt until Solomon's death.
## The end of Solomon's reign
11:41 The rest of Solomon's story, all he did and his wisdom, is written in the Scroll of Solomon's Deeds, is it not?
11:42 The time Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel was forty years.
11:43 Solomon lay down with his ancestors and was buried in the city of his father David. Rehoboam his son became king in his place.
## Rehoboam at Shechem
12:1 Rehoboam went to Shechem. All Israel had come to Shechem to make him king.
12:2 When Jeroboam son of Nebat heard of it, he was still in Egypt. He had run away there from King Solomon, and Jeroboam was living in Egypt.
12:3 So they sent for him and called him, and Jeroboam came with the whole assembly of Israel. They said to Rehoboam,
12:4 "Your father made our yoke a hard one. So now, make lighter the hard service your father demanded and the heavy yoke he set on us, and we will serve you."
12:5 He told them, "Go, and wait three days. Then return to me." So the people went.
12:6 King Rehoboam talked it over with the elders who used to stand before his father Solomon in his lifetime. "What answer do you counsel me to give these people?" he said.
12:7 They told him, "If you become a servant to this people today and serve them, if you answer them and speak good words to them, they will be your servants for all time."
12:8 But he set aside the counsel the elders had given him. Instead he talked it over with the boys he had grown up with, who stood in attendance on him.
12:9 He asked them, "What is your counsel? What word should we send back to these people? They have told me, 'Make lighter the yoke your father set on us.'"
12:10 The boys he had grown up with told him, "These people said to you, 'Your father made our yoke heavy, but you lighten it for us.' Here is what you say back to them: 'My smallest part is thicker than my father's hips.
12:11 My father loaded you with a heavy yoke, and I will make your yoke heavier still. My father punished you with whips, but I will punish you with scorpions.'"
12:12 On the third day Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam, just as the king had told them: "Return to me on the third day."
12:13 The king gave the people a hard answer. He set aside the counsel the elders had given him,
12:14 and spoke to them the way the boys had counseled: "My father made your yoke heavy, and I will make your yoke heavier still. My father punished you with whips, but I will punish you with scorpions."
12:15 The king would not listen to the people. The LORD had brought about this turn, so that he could make good the word he had spoken to Jeroboam son of Nebat by means of Ahijah the Shilonite.
## Israel breaks away
12:16 All Israel saw that the king would not listen to them. So the people sent the king this answer: "What is our share in David? / We have no inheritance in Jesse's son. / To your tents, Israel! / Now see to your own house, David!" So Israel went off to their tents.
12:17 The Israelites who lived in Judah's towns, though, had Rehoboam as their king.
12:18 Then King Rehoboam sent out Adoram, the man over the forced labor, and all Israel stoned him with stones until he died. King Rehoboam hurried to climb into his chariot and fled to Jerusalem.
12:19 Israel rose up in revolt against the house of David, and so it stands to this day.
12:20 Word reached all Israel that Jeroboam had returned. They sent for him, called him before the community and made him king over all Israel. No one followed the house of David except the tribe of Judah alone.
12:21 Rehoboam came to Jerusalem and called up all the house of Judah and the tribe of Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand picked warriors. They were to fight the house of Israel and bring the kingship back to Rehoboam son of Solomon.
12:22 But the word of God came to Shemaiah, the man of God:
12:23 "Speak to Rehoboam son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all the house of Judah and Benjamin, and to the rest of the people. Tell them,
12:24 'The LORD says this: You must not march up and fight with your kinsmen, the Israelites. Each of you, return to his own house, because this thing has come from me.'" So they listened to the LORD's word, and they turned back and went home, as the LORD had said.
## Jeroboam's calves
12:25 Jeroboam fortified Shechem in Ephraim's hill country and made his home there. Then he went out from there and fortified Penuel.
12:26 But Jeroboam said in his heart, "As things stand, the kingdom will swing back to David's house.
12:27 Suppose these people keep going up to make sacrifices in the LORD's house in Jerusalem. Then the hearts of this people will turn back to their master, Rehoboam king of Judah. They will kill me and go back to Rehoboam king of Judah."
12:28 So the king took counsel and made two calves of gold. "Enough of your going up to Jerusalem!" he told the people. "Here are your gods, Israel, that brought you up from the land of Egypt!"
12:29 He placed one calf in Bethel, and the other he put in Dan.
12:30 This matter turned into sin. The people walked before the one calf all the way to Dan.
12:31 He also made a house of high places, and he made priests out of the general run of the people, men who were not from the sons of Levi.
12:32 Jeroboam fixed a festival in the eighth month, on the fifteenth day of the month, like the festival held in Judah, and he went up on the altar. That is what he did in Bethel: he sacrificed to the calves he had made, and he stationed at Bethel the priests of the high places he had made.
12:33 On the fifteenth day of the eighth month, the month he had thought up out of his own heart, he went up on the altar he had made in Bethel. He set a festival for the Israelites, and he went up on the altar to burn incense.
## A man of God from Judah
13:1 Look: a man of God came out of Judah to Bethel by the LORD's word, while Jeroboam was standing beside the altar to burn incense.
13:2 He shouted out against the altar by the LORD's word: "Altar! Altar! The LORD says this: 'Look, a son is going to be born to David's house. His name will be Josiah. On you he will sacrifice the priests of the high places, who burn their incense on you. They will burn people's bones on you.'"
13:3 He gave a sign that same day too: "Here is the sign the LORD has spoken. Look, this altar is going to burst apart, and the fat-soaked ashes on it will spill out."
13:4 The king heard the word the man of God shouted against the altar at Bethel. Jeroboam reached out his hand from beside the altar and said, "Grab him!" But the hand he had reached out against him went stiff and dry, and he could not draw it back to himself.
13:5 And the altar burst apart, and the fat-soaked ashes spilled from the altar, as the man of God had given the sign by the LORD's word.
13:6 The king spoke up and said to the man of God, "Please beg the LORD your God to soften, and pray for me, so my hand comes back to me." The man of God begged the LORD to soften, and the king's hand came back to him and was as it had been before.
13:7 Then the king told the man of God, "Come to the house with me and refresh yourself. Let me give you a present."
13:8 The man of God said to the king, "You could give me half of your house, and I still would not go in with you. I will not eat any bread or drink any water in this place.
13:9 This is the order I was given by the LORD's word: 'Do not eat bread, do not drink water, and do not go back by the road you took.'"
13:10 So he left by a different road. He did not go back the way he had come to Bethel.
## The old prophet of Bethel
13:11 An old prophet lived in Bethel. One of his sons came and told him everything the man of God had done in Bethel that day. The words he had spoken to the king, they told those to their father too.
13:12 Their father asked them, "Which road did he take?" His sons had seen the road the man of God from Judah had gone by.
13:13 He told his sons, "Saddle me the donkey." They saddled the donkey for him, and he rode off on it.
13:14 He went after the man of God and found him sitting under the oak. "Are you the man of God who came from Judah?" he asked. "I am," he said.
13:15 "Come back to the house with me," he told him, "and eat some bread."
13:16 He answered, "I am not able to turn back with you or go in with you. I will not eat bread or drink water with you in this place.
13:17 I have an order by the LORD's word: 'Do not eat bread there or drink water, and do not go back by the road you took.'"
13:18 He said to him, "I am a prophet too, just like you. An angel spoke to me by the LORD's word: 'Take him back home with you to eat bread and drink water.'" He was lying to him.
13:19 So he went back with him, and ate bread in his house and drank water.
13:20 As they sat at the table, the LORD's word came to the prophet who had brought him back.
13:21 He shouted to the man of God who had come from Judah, "The LORD says this: 'You rebelled against what the LORD said. You did not keep the order the LORD your God gave you.
13:22 You went back, and you ate bread and drank water in the very place he told you, "Do not eat bread or drink water." Your corpse will not come to your ancestors' grave.'"
13:23 When he had eaten bread and had drunk, the old prophet saddled the donkey for him, for the prophet he had brought back.
13:24 He set off, and on the road a lion met him and killed him. His corpse lay flung down in the road. The donkey stood beside it, and the lion stood beside the corpse too.
13:25 Some men came by and saw the corpse flung in the road and the lion standing by the corpse. They went and told about it in the town where the old prophet lived.
13:26 The prophet who had brought him back from the road heard it and said, "That is the man of God who rebelled against what the LORD said. So the LORD has handed him to the lion, and it tore him and killed him, as the LORD's word had told him."
13:27 He told his sons, "Saddle me the donkey," and they saddled it.
13:28 He went and found the corpse flung in the road, and the donkey and the lion standing beside the corpse. The lion had not eaten the corpse, and it had not torn the donkey.
13:29 The prophet lifted the corpse of the man of God, laid it on the donkey and brought it back. The old prophet came to his own town to mourn and bury him.
13:30 He placed the corpse in his own grave, and they lamented over him, "Oh, my brother!"
13:31 After he had buried him, he said to his sons, "When I die, bury me in the grave where the man of God lies buried. Lay my bones next to his bones.
13:32 The word he shouted by the LORD's word against the altar in Bethel and against all the houses at the high places in the towns of Samaria is going to happen. It surely will."
13:33 Even after this happened, Jeroboam did not turn back from his evil road. Again he made priests for the high places out of the general run of the people. Anyone who wished, he installed as one of the priests of the high places.
13:34 This matter became the sin of Jeroboam's house, and it is what wiped it out and destroyed it from the surface of the ground.
## Jeroboam's wife in disguise
14:1 About that time Jeroboam's son Abijah fell ill.
14:2 Jeroboam said to his wife, "Please get up and change your looks, so that people will not know you are Jeroboam's wife. Go to Shiloh. Ahijah the prophet is there, the one who spoke about me becoming king over this people.
14:3 Take along ten loaves, some biscuits and a jar of honey, and go to him. He will let you know what is going to become of the boy."
14:4 Jeroboam's wife did so. She got up, went to Shiloh and came to Ahijah's house. Ahijah could not see, because his eyes had gone fixed with old age.
14:5 But the LORD had said to Ahijah, "Look, Jeroboam's wife is coming to seek a word from you about her son, because he is sick. Say this and that to her. When she comes in, she will be acting like a stranger."
14:6 As she was coming through the doorway, Ahijah heard the sound of her feet and said, "Come in, Jeroboam's wife! Why are you acting like a stranger? I have been sent to you with a hard message.
14:7 Go and say to Jeroboam, 'The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: I lifted you out from among the people, and I made you ruler over my people Israel.
14:8 I ripped the kingdom from David's house and handed it to you. But you were not like David my servant. He kept my commands and walked after me with his whole heart, doing nothing but what was right in my eyes.
14:9 You have done worse than everyone who came before you. You went and made yourself other gods, images of cast metal, to make me angry, and me you threw behind your back.
14:10 So look, I am about to bring trouble on Jeroboam's house. I will cut off every male of Jeroboam's in Israel, the one shut in and the one set free. I will sweep out what is left of Jeroboam's house the way someone sweeps out dung, until it is finished.
14:11 Whoever of Jeroboam's family dies in the town, the dogs will eat. Whoever dies in the open country, the birds of the sky will eat. For the LORD has spoken.'
14:12 And you, get up and go to your house. The moment your feet come into the town, the child will die.
14:13 All Israel will mourn for him and bury him. Of Jeroboam's family he alone will come to a grave, because in him alone, of all Jeroboam's house, something good toward the LORD, the God of Israel, was found.
14:14 The LORD will set up a king of his own over Israel, who will cut off Jeroboam's house. That is the day. And what am I saying? It is already now.
14:15 The LORD will strike Israel until it shakes like a reed in the water. He will pull Israel up from this good ground he gave their ancestors and scatter them beyond the River, because they made their Asherah poles and angered the LORD.
14:16 He will give Israel up because of the sins Jeroboam committed, the sins he made Israel commit."
14:17 Jeroboam's wife got up and went, and she came to Tirzah. Just as she reached the doorstep of the house, the boy died.
14:18 They buried him and all Israel mourned for him, just as the LORD's word had said through his servant Ahijah the prophet.
14:19 The rest of Jeroboam's story, how he made war and how he reigned, is there, written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel.
14:20 Jeroboam reigned twenty-two years. Then he lay down with his ancestors, and his son Nadab became king in his place.
## Rehoboam king of Judah
14:21 Meanwhile Rehoboam son of Solomon reigned in Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old at the start of his reign, and for seventeen years he reigned in Jerusalem, the city the LORD chose from all the tribes of Israel as the place to set his name. His mother was Naamah the Ammonite.
14:22 Judah did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. With the sins they sinned, they roused his jealousy more than their ancestors had done with all of theirs.
14:23 They too built themselves high places, standing stones and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every leafy tree.
14:24 There were also shrine men in the land. They did all the abhorrent things of the nations the LORD had driven out ahead of the Israelites.
14:25 In King Rehoboam's fifth year, Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem.
14:26 He took the treasures of the LORD's house and the treasures of the king's house. He took the lot. He even took all the gold shields Solomon had made.
14:27 In their place King Rehoboam made shields of bronze, and he put them in the care of the officers of the runners, who guarded the doorway of the king's house.
14:28 Each time the king went to the LORD's house, the runners would carry them, and then bring them back to the runners' guardroom.
14:29 The rest of Rehoboam's story, and all he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Judah, is it not?
14:30 War went on between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all their days.
14:31 Rehoboam lay down with his ancestors and was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. His mother was Naamah the Ammonite. His son Abijam became king in his place.
## Abijam king of Judah
15:1 Abijam took the throne over Judah in the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam son of Nebat.
15:2 He reigned in Jerusalem for three years. His mother's name was Maacah, Abishalom's daughter.
15:3 He walked in all the sins his father had committed before him. His heart was not whole with the LORD his God, the way the heart of his forefather David had been.
15:4 But because of David, the LORD his God gave him a lamp in Jerusalem. He raised up his son after him and kept Jerusalem standing.
15:5 For David did what was right in the LORD's eyes. He did not turn aside from anything the LORD commanded him his whole life long. The one exception was the affair of Uriah the Hittite.
15:6 War went on between Rehoboam and Jeroboam as long as he lived.
15:7 The rest of Abijam's story, and all he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Judah, is it not? There was war between Abijam and Jeroboam.
15:8 Abijam lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him in the City of David. His son Asa became king in his place.
## Asa king of Judah
15:9 Asa became king of Judah in the twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel.
15:10 He reigned forty-one years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Maacah, Abishalom's daughter.
15:11 Asa did what was right in the LORD's eyes, like his forefather David.
15:12 He drove the shrine men out of the land and took away all the filthy idols his ancestors had made.
15:13 He even took away the rank of queen mother from Maacah his mother, because she had made a horrid thing for Asherah. Asa cut down her horrid thing and burned it in the Kidron Valley.
15:14 The high places were not taken away, but Asa's heart was whole with the LORD all his days.
15:15 He brought into the LORD's house the things his father had set apart as holy, and his own holy gifts: silver, gold and utensils.
## Asa buys help from Aram
15:16 War went on between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days.
15:17 Baasha king of Israel came up against Judah and built up Ramah, so that no one could go out or come in for Asa king of Judah.
15:18 Then Asa took all the silver and gold that was left in the treasuries of the LORD's house and the treasuries of the king's house. He handed it to his servants, and King Asa sent them to Ben-hadad son of Tabrimmon, son of Hezion, the king of Aram who lived in Damascus, with this message:
15:19 "Let a covenant stand between me and you, between my father and your father. Look, I am sending you a payment of silver and gold. Go and break your covenant with Baasha king of Israel, so that he will pull back from me."
15:20 Ben-hadad listened to King Asa. He sent the commanders of his armies against Israel's towns, and he struck Ijon, Dan, Abel-beth-maacah, and all Kinnereth with all the land of Naphtali.
15:21 As soon as Baasha heard of it, he quit building Ramah and stayed in Tirzah.
15:22 Then King Asa called out all Judah, with no one excused. They hauled away the stones and timber of Ramah that Baasha had built with, and King Asa used them to fortify Geba of Benjamin and Mizpah.
15:23 The rest of all Asa's story, all his might, all he did and the towns he built, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Judah, is it not? But when he grew old he got a sickness in his feet.
15:24 Asa lay down with his ancestors and was buried with his ancestors in the city of his forefather David. His son Jehoshaphat became king in his place.
## Nadab king of Israel
15:25 Nadab son of Jeroboam took the throne over Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah, and he was king over Israel for two years.
15:26 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He walked in his father's way and in his father's sin, the sin he made Israel commit.
15:27 Baasha son of Ahijah, of the house of Issachar, plotted against him. Baasha struck him down at Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines, while Nadab and all Israel were laying siege to Gibbethon.
15:28 Baasha killed him in the third year of Asa king of Judah, and reigned in his place.
15:29 As soon as he was king, he struck down the whole house of Jeroboam. He did not leave Jeroboam a single living soul until he had wiped them out, as the LORD's word had said through his servant Ahijah the Shilonite.
15:30 It was because of the sins Jeroboam sinned and made Israel sin, and because of the anger he stirred in the LORD, the God of Israel, by them.
15:31 The rest of Nadab's story, and all he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
## Baasha king of Israel
15:32 War went on between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days.
15:33 In the third year of Asa king of Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah took the throne over all Israel at Tirzah, for twenty-four years.
15:34 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He walked in Jeroboam's way and in his sin, the sin he made Israel commit.
## Jehu's word against Baasha
16:1 The LORD's word came to Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha:
16:2 "I raised you up out of the dust and made you ruler over my people Israel. But you walked in Jeroboam's way and made my people Israel sin, angering me with their sins.
16:3 So look, I am about to sweep away Baasha and what is left of his house. I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat.
16:4 Whoever of Baasha's family dies in the town, the dogs will eat. Whoever of his dies in the open country, the birds of the sky will eat."
16:5 The rest of Baasha's story, what he did and his might, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
16:6 Baasha lay down with his ancestors and was buried in Tirzah. His son Elah became king in his place.
16:7 The LORD's word had also come through the prophet Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha and his house. It came because of all the evil he did in the LORD's eyes, angering him by the work of his hands and becoming like Jeroboam's house, and also because he struck that house down.
## Elah and Zimri
16:8 In the twenty-sixth year of Asa king of Judah, Elah son of Baasha took the throne over Israel at Tirzah, for two years.
16:9 His servant Zimri, commander of half the chariots, plotted against him. Elah was in Tirzah, drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza, who was in charge of the palace at Tirzah.
16:10 Zimri came in, struck him and killed him, in the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, and he reigned in his place.
16:11 As soon as he became king and sat on his throne, he struck down the whole house of Baasha. He did not leave him a single male, not a relative who could avenge him and not a friend.
16:12 So Zimri wiped out all Baasha's house, as the LORD's word against Baasha had said through Jehu the prophet.
16:13 It was because of all the sins of Baasha and the sins of his son Elah, the sins they sinned and made Israel sin, angering the LORD, the God of Israel, with their puffs of air.
16:14 The rest of Elah's story, and all he did, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
16:15 In the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, Zimri was king at Tirzah for seven days. The army was camped against Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines.
16:16 The army in camp heard the news: "Zimri has plotted, and he has struck down the king too!" So that day in the camp all Israel made Omri, the army commander, king over Israel.
16:17 Omri marched up from Gibbethon with all Israel, and they put Tirzah under siege.
16:18 When Zimri saw that the town had been taken, he went into the stronghold of the king's house. He burned the king's house down over himself, and he died.
16:19 It was because of the sins he sinned by doing what was evil in the LORD's eyes, by walking in Jeroboam's way and in the sin Jeroboam committed to make Israel sin.
16:20 The rest of Zimri's story, and the plot he carried out, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
## Omri builds Samaria
16:21 Then the people of Israel split in two. Half the people followed Tibni son of Ginath, to make him king, and the other half followed Omri.
16:22 The people who followed Omri overpowered the people who followed Tibni son of Ginath. Tibni died, and Omri reigned.
16:23 In the thirty-first year of Asa king of Judah, Omri took the throne over Israel, for twelve years. He reigned six years in Tirzah.
16:24 He bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer for two talents of silver. He built on the hill, and he named the town he built Samaria, after Shemer, the hill's owner.
16:25 Omri did what was evil in the LORD's eyes. He did worse than all who were before him.
16:26 He walked in all the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat and in his sin, the sin he made Israel commit, angering the LORD, the God of Israel, with their puffs of air.
16:27 The rest of Omri's story, what he did and the might he showed, is written in the scroll of the records of the kings of Israel, is it not?
16:28 Omri lay down with his ancestors and was buried in Samaria. His son Ahab became king in his place.
## Ahab king of Israel
16:29 Ahab son of Omri took the throne over Israel in the thirty-eighth year of Asa king of Judah. Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty-two years.
16:30 Ahab son of Omri did what was evil in the LORD's eyes, more than all who were before him.
16:31 Walking in the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat was a trifle to him. He married Jezebel daughter of Ethbaal, king of the Sidonians, and he took up the service of Baal and bowed down to him.
16:32 He put up an altar for Baal in the house of Baal he had built in Samaria.
16:33 Ahab also made the Asherah pole. Ahab went further than all the kings of Israel before him in what he did to anger the LORD, the God of Israel.
16:34 In his days Hiel the Bethelite built Jericho. With Abiram his firstborn he laid its foundation, and with Segub his youngest he set up its gates, as the LORD's word had said through Joshua son of Nun.
