## Elijah announces a drought
17:1 Elijah the Tishbite, one of the settlers of Gilead, said to Ahab, "By the life of the LORD, the God of Israel, whom I stand before: these coming years will have no dew and no rain unless I give the word."
17:2 Then the LORD's word came to him:
17:3 "Go away from here. Head east, and hide at the Kerith Brook, which faces the Jordan.
17:4 The brook will be where you drink, and the ravens have my orders to keep you fed there."
17:5 He set off and did as the LORD said. He went and lived beside the Kerith Brook, which faces the Jordan.
17:6 The ravens kept bringing him bread and meat each morning, and bread and meat each evening. For water he drank from the brook.
17:7 But as the days went by, the brook ran dry. No rain had fallen in the land.
## The widow of Zarephath
17:8 Then the LORD's word came to him:
17:9 "Up! Go to Zarephath, a town of Sidon, and make your home there. Look, I have given a widow there orders to feed you."
17:10 He got up and set out for Zarephath. As he reached the entrance to the town, he saw a widow there, picking up sticks. He called out to her, "Please get me some water in a cup, just a little, so I can drink."
17:11 She went off to fetch it, and he called out after her, "Please bring a bit of bread with you, too."
17:12 She answered, "By the life of the LORD your God, I don't have a single loaf. All I have is enough flour to fill my palm, left in the jar, and the jug has a little oil. Look, I'm picking up two sticks. Then I'll go in and cook it for me and my son. We'll eat it, and then we'll die."
17:13 "Do not be afraid," Elijah told her. "Go and do what you have in mind. Only, from what is there, make me one small round loaf first, and bring it out here to me. Afterward you can make some for you and your son.
17:14 For the LORD, the God of Israel, says this: 'The flour in the jar will not give out, and the oil in the jug will not fail, until the day the LORD gives rain on the face of the ground.'"
17:15 So she went off and did as Elijah told her. She ate, and he did, and so did her household, day after day.
17:16 The flour in the jar did not give out, and the oil in the jug did not fail, just as the LORD had said through Elijah.
## The widow's son lives again
17:17 After these things, the boy got sick, the son of the woman whose house it was. The sickness grew very strong, until not a breath remained in him.
17:18 "Man of God," she said to Elijah, "what is there between you and me? You came to me to bring my guilt to mind, and to put my son to death!"
17:19 He said to her, "Give me your son." He lifted the boy from her lap and took him up to the roof room where he was living. There he laid him down on his own bed.
17:20 He called out to the LORD, "LORD my God! Even the widow who gives me a place to stay? Have you done her harm too, putting her son to death?"
17:21 Three times he lay stretched out full length on top of the boy. He called to the LORD, "LORD my God, please, let the life of this boy come back inside him!"
17:22 The LORD granted Elijah's prayer. The boy's life came back inside him, and he lived.
17:23 Elijah took the boy and brought him down from the roof room into the house, and gave him to his mother. "See," said Elijah, "your son is living."
17:24 Then the woman told Elijah, "This proves it to me now: you are a man of God, and the LORD's word in your mouth is true."
## Elijah meets Obadiah
18:1 Many days went by. Then, in the third year, the LORD's word came to Elijah: "Go and let Ahab see you. I am about to give rain on the face of the ground."
18:2 Elijah went to let Ahab see him. The famine had hit Samaria hard.
18:3 Ahab sent for Obadiah, the man in charge of the royal house. Obadiah had a deep fear of the LORD.
18:4 Back when Jezebel was wiping out the prophets of the LORD, he took a hundred of them and hid them in groups of fifty in the cave. He kept them supplied with bread and water.
18:5 Ahab said to Obadiah, "Travel the land. Check every spring of water and every stream bed. We may find grass, and then we can save the lives of horse and mule, and not lose part of the herds."
18:6 They shared out the land to search it. Ahab took one road alone, and Obadiah took another road alone.
18:7 Obadiah was on the road when, suddenly, there was Elijah coming to meet him. Obadiah knew him and fell face down. "My lord Elijah, is that you?" he said.
18:8 "I am," he said to him. "Go and let your master know: Elijah is here."
18:9 But Obadiah said, "How have I sinned? Why put your servant into Ahab's hands, so he can kill me?
18:10 By the life of the LORD your God, my master has sent men to hunt for you in every nation and kingdom there is. When they said, 'He isn't here,' he made the kingdom or nation take an oath that they had not found you.
18:11 And now you say, 'Go and let your master know: Elijah is here!'
18:12 What will happen is this. I will walk away from you, and the LORD's Spirit will carry you off to who knows where. I will come with my news to Ahab, he will not find you, and he will kill me. And your servant has feared the LORD ever since he was a boy!
18:13 My lord, has no one told you what I did when Jezebel killed the prophets of the LORD? I hid a hundred men from among the LORD's prophets, in groups of fifty in the cave, and I kept them supplied with bread and water.
18:14 And now you say, 'Go and let your master know: Elijah is here!' He will kill me!"
18:15 Elijah said, "By the life of the LORD of Armies, whom I stand before: this very day I will let him see me."
18:16 Obadiah went off to meet Ahab and gave him the news, and Ahab came out to meet Elijah.
## Who brings trouble on Israel?
18:17 As soon as Ahab saw Elijah, he said, "So it's you, the one who brings trouble on Israel!"
18:18 Elijah answered, "It is not I who brought trouble on Israel. It is you and your father's house. You turned your backs on the LORD's commands, and you went after the Baals.
18:19 Now send out orders. Bring all Israel together to me on Mount Carmel, and the prophets of Baal, all four hundred fifty of them, and the four hundred prophets of Asherah who are fed at Jezebel's table."
18:20 Ahab sent messages to all the Israelites, and he brought the prophets together on Mount Carmel.
## The contest on Mount Carmel
18:21 Elijah stepped up to all the people and said, "How long will you limp along on two crutches? If the LORD is the God, go after him. If Baal is, go after him." But the people said not a word in answer.
18:22 Elijah went on, "I alone am left as a prophet of the LORD. The prophets of Baal number four hundred fifty men.
18:23 Have two young bulls brought to us. They can pick one bull for themselves, carve it up, and set the pieces on the wood, without lighting any fire. I will get the second bull ready and set it on the wood, and I will light no fire either.
18:24 You will call out to your god by name, and I will call out to the LORD by name. Whichever god answers with fire, he is the God." All the people answered, "That is fair."
18:25 Elijah told the prophets of Baal, "Pick out your bull and go first, because you are the many. Get it ready and call out to your god by name. But light no fire."
18:26 They took the bull that was handed to them and got it ready. Then from morning until midday they called out to Baal by name: "Baal, answer us!" Not a sound came, and nobody answered. They went limping around the altar that had been made.
18:27 When midday came, Elijah made fun of them. "Call out at the top of your voice," he said. "He's a god, after all! He may be lost in thought, or he has stepped away, or he is off on a trip. Or maybe he's asleep, and he'll wake up."
18:28 They called out at the top of their voices, and they cut themselves, as was their custom, with swords and spears, until blood poured down over them.
18:29 The noon hour went by, and they kept on prophesying until the time when the grain offering goes up. Still not a sound. Not an answer. Nobody was listening.
## The LORD answers by fire
18:30 Elijah told all the people, "Come close to me," and the whole crowd came close. Then he mended the LORD's altar, which had been knocked down.
18:31 He picked up twelve stones, to match the number of the tribes of Jacob's sons. This was the Jacob the LORD had spoken to, saying, "Israel will be your name."
18:32 Out of the stones he built an altar in the LORD's name. He made a ditch all around the altar, the size of a plot sown with two seahs of seed.
18:33 He stacked the wood. He carved up the bull and set it on top of the wood. "Get four jars filled with water," he said, "and pour them out over the burnt offering and the wood."
18:34 "Again," he said, and they did it a second time. "A third time," he said, and they did it a third time.
18:35 The water flowed all around the altar, and he filled the ditch with water as well.
18:36 When the hour came for the grain offering to go up, Elijah the prophet stepped up and said, "LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac and Israel, today make it known that you are God in Israel, and that I am your servant, and that I did all these things because you told me to.
18:37 Answer me, LORD! Answer me! Then this people will know that you, LORD, are the God, and that you yourself have turned their hearts backward."
18:38 Down came the LORD's fire. It ate up the burnt offering, the wood, the stones and the dirt, and it licked up the water in the ditch.
18:39 All the people saw it. They threw themselves face down and said, "The LORD, he is the God! The LORD, he is the God!"
18:40 Elijah told them, "Grab the prophets of Baal! Not one of them is to escape!" They grabbed them, and Elijah took them down to the Kishon River and slaughtered them there.
## The rain comes
18:41 Then Elijah told Ahab, "Go on up. Eat and drink. I hear the roar of rain."
18:42 Ahab went up to eat and drink. Elijah went up too, to the top of Carmel. There he crouched down on the ground, with his face between his knees.
18:43 "Please go up and look out toward the sea," he told his servant. The servant climbed up and looked. "Nothing," he said. "Go back," Elijah said, seven times over.
18:44 On the seventh time he said, "Look! There's a little cloud, like a person's hand, coming up out of the sea." Elijah said, "Go up and say to Ahab, 'Harness your team and go down, or the rain will hold you back.'"
18:45 Little by little the sky darkened with clouds and wind, and then a great rain came. Ahab got in his chariot and set off for Jezreel.
18:46 The LORD's hand was on Elijah. He bound up his clothes at the waist and ran ahead of Ahab, as far as the road into Jezreel.
## Elijah runs for his life
19:1 Ahab gave Jezebel a full report of what Elijah had done, including how he had put all the prophets to the sword.
19:2 Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah with these words: "The gods do this to me, and more besides, if by about this time tomorrow I have not made your life like the life of one of them."
19:3 Elijah was afraid. He got up and went to save his life. He came to Beersheba, the one in Judah, and left his servant there.
19:4 But he went on, one day's walk into the wilderness. There he sat down under a lone broom bush and begged to die. "Enough!" he said. "Now, LORD, take my life. I am not any better than my fathers."
19:5 He lay down under the lone broom bush and slept. Then, out of nowhere, an angel was touching him and saying, "Get up and eat!"
19:6 He looked up, and right by his head was a round loaf baked on coals, and a jug of water. He ate, he drank, and he lay back down.
19:7 The LORD's angel came again, a second time, and touched him. "Get up, eat," he said. "You have too far to go."
19:8 He got up, ate and drank, and on the strength of that meal he walked for forty days and forty nights, all the way to God's mountain, Horeb.
## The LORD at Horeb
19:9 He went into the cave there and spent the night. Then the LORD's word came to him. "Why are you here, Elijah?" he asked.
19:10 He said, "I have burned with zeal for the LORD, the God of Armies. The Israelites have left your covenant behind. They have broken down your altars. They have killed your prophets with the sword. I alone am left, just me, and they are hunting for my life, to take it away."
19:11 He said, "Go out. Stand on the mountain in front of the LORD." And look: the LORD was passing by. A wind came, huge and fierce, ripping at the mountains and smashing rocks to pieces ahead of the LORD. The LORD was not in the wind. After the wind came a quake. The LORD was not in the quake.
19:12 After the quake, a fire. The LORD was not in the fire. And after the fire, a sound of thin silence.
19:13 As soon as Elijah heard it, he covered his face with his cloak. He went out and stood in the opening of the cave. And look, a voice came to him: "Why are you here, Elijah?"
19:14 He said, "I have burned with zeal for the LORD, the God of Armies. The Israelites have left your covenant behind. They have broken down your altars. They have killed your prophets with the sword. I alone am left, just me, and they are hunting for my life, to take it away."
## Three to anoint
19:15 The LORD told him, "Go, return by your road to the wilderness of Damascus. Go there and anoint Hazael as king over Aram.
19:16 Jehu son of Nimshi you are to anoint as king over Israel. And Elisha son of Shaphat, from Abel-meholah, you are to anoint as prophet to take your place.
19:17 Whoever gets away from Hazael's sword, Jehu will kill. Whoever gets away from Jehu's sword, Elisha will kill.
19:18 But I will keep seven thousand left in Israel: every knee that has not bent to Baal, and every mouth that has not kissed him."
## Elisha follows Elijah
19:19 Elijah left that place and found Elisha son of Shaphat at his plowing. Twelve pairs of oxen were ahead of him, and he was with the twelfth. Elijah crossed over to him and tossed his cloak onto him.
19:20 He left the oxen and ran after Elijah. "Please, let me kiss my father and my mother," he said, "and I'll go after you." "Go, go back," Elijah said to him. "What have I done to you?"
19:21 He turned back from following him. He took the pair of oxen and sacrificed them. With the oxen's gear for fuel, he boiled their meat and handed it out to the people, who ate. Then he got up, went after Elijah, and waited on him.
## Ben-hadad besieges Samaria
20:1 Ben-hadad, the king of Aram, called up all his forces. He had thirty-two kings with him, along with horses and chariots. He marched up, surrounded Samaria, and made war on it.
20:2 He sent messengers into the city to Ahab king of Israel. They told him, "Ben-hadad says this:
20:3 'Your silver and gold belong to me. Your wives and your children, the finest ones, belong to me.'"
20:4 The king of Israel sent back this answer: "It is as you say, my lord the king. I belong to you, with everything I own."
20:5 The messengers came again. "Ben-hadad says this," they said. "'I sent you word: Hand over to me your silver and your gold, your wives and your children.
20:6 Now this is what will happen. At about this time tomorrow I am sending my servants to you. They will go through your house and your servants' houses. Whatever your eyes take delight in, they will pick up in their hands and carry off.'"
20:7 The king of Israel called together all the elders of the land. "Please, take note," he said. "You can see this man wants nothing but harm. He sent to me for my wives and my children, my silver and my gold, and I did not hold them back from him."
20:8 All the elders and all the people told him, "Don't listen. Don't give in."
20:9 So he told Ben-hadad's messengers, "Say to my lord the king: 'Everything you sent to your servant about the first time, I will do. But this thing I cannot do.'" The messengers went off and brought him back the reply.
20:10 Ben-hadad sent back to him: "The gods do this to me, and more besides, if the dust of Samaria is enough to give a handful to each of the troops who follow at my feet."
20:11 The king of Israel answered, "Tell him this: 'The man buckling his belt on should not brag like the man taking it off.'"
20:12 The word reached Ben-hadad as he sat drinking in the shelters, he and the kings. "Get in position!" he told his servants, and they moved into place against the city.
## Ahab wins at Samaria
20:13 Just then a certain prophet stepped up to Ahab king of Israel. He said, "The LORD says this: 'Have you seen this whole huge crowd? Look, today I am handing it over to you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'"
20:14 Ahab said, "Through whom?" He answered, "The LORD says this: 'Through the young men of the district officials.'" Ahab asked, "Who will start the fighting?" "You," he answered.
20:15 Ahab called up the young men of the district officials, two hundred thirty-two of them. After them he called up all the troops, the whole of the Israelites: seven thousand.
20:16 They marched out at midday. Ben-hadad was in the shelters drinking himself drunk, he and the kings, the thirty-two kings who were helping him.
20:17 The young men of the district officials went out first. Ben-hadad sent out scouts, and they reported to him, "Men have come out of Samaria."
20:18 "If they have come out to talk peace, take them alive," he said. "If they have come out for battle, take them alive all the same."
20:19 So these marched out of the city, the young men of the district officials, with the army following behind them.
20:20 Each of them cut down his man. Aram ran, and Israel chased them. Ben-hadad king of Aram got away on a horse, with horsemen.
20:21 The king of Israel came out and struck the horses and chariots. He beat Aram badly, with heavy slaughter.
20:22 The prophet came to the king of Israel again. "Go, build up your strength," he told him. "Think hard about what you are going to do. When the year comes around, the king of Aram will march against you."
## The god of the mountains
20:23 The servants of the king of Aram said to him, "Their god is a god of the mountains. That is how they beat us. But let's do battle with them on flat ground. Then we will be the stronger ones, for sure.
20:24 And here is what to do. Take the kings out of their posts, each one, and put governors in their place.
20:25 Then count out an army for yourself as big as the army that fell away from you, a horse in place of every horse and a chariot for every chariot. Let's fight them on flat ground. We will be the stronger ones, for sure." He did as they said.
20:26 When the year came around, Ben-hadad called up Aram and marched up to Aphek to make war on Israel.
20:27 The Israelites were called up too, and supplied with food, and they went out against them. The Israelites made camp facing them, like two tiny herds of goats, while Aram filled the land.
20:28 The man of God stepped up and spoke to the king of Israel. He said, "The LORD says this: 'Since Aram has said the LORD is a god of mountains and not a god of valleys, I will hand this whole huge crowd over to you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'"
20:29 The two armies camped facing each other for seven days. On the seventh day the battle began, and the Israelites cut down a hundred thousand of Aram's foot soldiers in a single day.
20:30 The rest ran into the city of Aphek, and the wall fell on twenty-seven thousand men who were left. Ben-hadad ran too. He came into the city and hid in a room inside a room.
## Ahab spares Ben-hadad
20:31 His servants told him, "Look, we have heard that the kings of the house of Israel are kings who show loyal love. Let us tie sackcloth on our waists and put ropes on our heads, and go out to the king of Israel. He may let you live."
20:32 So they tied sackcloth on their waists, with ropes on their heads, and came to the king of Israel. "Your servant Ben-hadad says, 'Please, let me live,'" they said. "Is he still alive?" he said. "He is my brother."
20:33 The men were watching for an omen. They quickly caught at it and said, "Your brother Ben-hadad!" "Go, bring him," he said. Ben-hadad came out to him, and he had him climb up into the chariot.
20:34 Ben-hadad said to him, "I will give back the towns my father took away from your father. And you can lay out trading streets for yourself in Damascus, the way my father did in Samaria." "And I," came the reply, "will let you go under a covenant." He made a covenant with him and set him free.
## A prophet's trap for the king
20:35 One man from the sons of the prophets spoke to his fellow prophet at the LORD's command. "Please, hit me," he said. But the man would not hit him.
20:36 He told him, "You did not listen to the LORD's voice. So look: when you walk away from me, the lion will kill you." He walked away from him, and the lion met him and killed him.
20:37 He found another man and said, "Please, hit me." The man hit him hard and wounded him.
20:38 Then the prophet went and waited for the king beside the road. He hid who he was with a bandage over his eyes.
20:39 As the king went by, he cried out to the king, "Your servant went out into the middle of the battle. And look, a man turned aside and brought me a man. He said, 'Watch this man. If he ever goes missing, your life will pay for his life, or else you will weigh out a talent of silver.'
20:40 But your servant was doing this and that, and the man was gone." The king of Israel said to him, "So that is your verdict. You have settled it yourself."
20:41 Quickly he took the bandage away from his eyes, and the king of Israel saw that he was one of the prophets.
20:42 He said to him, "The LORD says this: 'You let the man I put under the ban slip out of your hand. So your life will go for his life, and your people for his people.'"
20:43 The king of Israel headed for home, sulking and fuming, and came to Samaria.
## Naboth's vineyard
21:1 After these things, the following took place. In Jezreel there was a vineyard that belonged to Naboth the Jezreelite. It lay next to the palace of Samaria's king, Ahab.
21:2 Ahab spoke to Naboth. "Give me your vineyard," he said. "I want it for a garden of greens, because it sits right next to my house. I will give you a better vineyard in its place. Or if you would like that better, I will pay you silver for what it is worth."
21:3 But Naboth said to Ahab, "The LORD keep me from giving you my fathers' inheritance!"
21:4 Ahab came home sulking and fuming over what Naboth the Jezreelite had told him. He had said, "I will not give you my fathers' inheritance." Ahab lay down on his bed, turned his face away, and would not eat a bite.
21:5 Jezebel his wife came in to him. "Why is your spirit so low," she said, "and you won't eat anything?"
21:6 He told her, "I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite. I said to him, 'Give me your vineyard for silver. Or if you would rather, I will give you a vineyard in its place.' And he said, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'"
21:7 His wife Jezebel said to him, "Is that any way for you to rule as king over Israel? Up! Have something to eat, and feel glad at heart. I myself will give you Naboth the Jezreelite's vineyard."
## Jezebel's letters
21:8 She wrote letters, signing them with Ahab's name and stamping them with his seal. She sent the letters to the elders and the leading men of Naboth's town, his neighbors there.
21:9 This is what she wrote in the letters: "Call for a fast. Have Naboth sit at the head of the people.
21:10 Put two worthless men across from him, and have them testify against him: 'You have cursed God and the king!' Then take him outside and stone him, so that he dies."
21:11 The men of his town, the elders and the leading men who lived there, did just as Jezebel had sent them word. They did exactly what was written in the letters she had sent them.
21:12 They called for a fast and had Naboth sit at the head of the people.
21:13 The two worthless men came in and sat down facing him. Before all the people the worthless men testified against Naboth: "Naboth cursed God and the king!" They took him out of the town and stoned him with stones, and he died.
21:14 Then they sent word to Jezebel: "Naboth was stoned. He is dead."
21:15 When Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, she said to Ahab, "Up! Take possession of Naboth the Jezreelite's vineyard, the one he would not give you for silver. Naboth isn't alive anymore. He's dead."
21:16 Ahab heard the news that Naboth had died. He got up to go down to the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite and take possession of it.
## Elijah meets Ahab in the vineyard
21:17 Then the LORD's word came to Elijah the Tishbite:
21:18 "Get up and go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria. Look, he is in Naboth's vineyard. He has gone down there to take possession of it.
21:19 Tell him, 'The LORD says this: Have you murdered, and taken possession as well?' And tell him, 'The LORD says this: Right where the dogs licked up Naboth's blood, the dogs will lick up your own blood too.'"
21:20 Ahab said to Elijah, "You've found me, have you, my enemy?" "I have," he said. "I have found you, because you sold yourself to do what the LORD sees as evil.
21:21 'Look, I am bringing disaster on you. I will sweep you away. I will cut off every male of Ahab's, the bound and the free, in Israel.
21:22 I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah, because of how you have provoked me to anger and led Israel into sin.'
21:23 And about Jezebel, too, the LORD has spoken: 'The dogs will eat Jezebel by the rampart of Jezreel.'
21:24 Anyone of Ahab's who dies in the town, the dogs will eat. Anyone who dies out in the open, the birds of the sky will eat."
21:25 Truly, there was no one like Ahab, who sold himself to do what the LORD sees as evil, with Jezebel his wife egging him on.
21:26 He acted in abhorrent ways, very abhorrent, going after the idols, just like everything the Amorites had done, whom the LORD drove out ahead of the Israelites.
## Ahab humbles himself
21:27 As soon as Ahab heard these words, he ripped his clothes and put sackcloth on his bare skin. He fasted. He slept in the sackcloth, and he walked about slowly and quietly.
21:28 Then the LORD's word came to Elijah the Tishbite:
21:29 "Do you see that Ahab has humbled himself before me? Since he has humbled himself before me, I will not bring the disaster while he lives. In his son's lifetime I will bring the disaster on his house."
## Jehoshaphat joins the king of Israel
22:1 Three years passed quietly, with no war between Aram and Israel.
22:2 Then, during the third year, Jehoshaphat king of Judah came down to the king of Israel.
22:3 The king of Israel said to his servants, "Ramoth-gilead is ours, as you know. Yet we sit still and do not take it back from the king of Aram's hand."
22:4 He said to Jehoshaphat, "Will you come with me to battle at Ramoth-gilead?" Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "I am one with you, my people one with your people, my horses one with your horses."
22:5 But Jehoshaphat also told the king of Israel, "Please, today, ask what the LORD has to say."
## Four hundred prophets say yes
22:6 The king of Israel gathered the prophets, around four hundred men. "Shall I march on Ramoth-gilead to fight," he asked them, "or shall I let it be?" "Go up," they said. "The Lord will hand it over to the king."
22:7 Jehoshaphat said, "Isn't there still a prophet of the LORD around here? Let us ask him too."
22:8 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is still one man through whom we could seek the LORD, Micaiah son of Imlah. But I hate him. He does not prophesy good things about me, never anything but evil." Jehoshaphat said, "The king shouldn't talk like that."
22:9 The king of Israel called a court official over. "Hurry, get Micaiah son of Imlah," he said.
22:10 The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were each sitting on his throne, wearing robes, on the threshing floor at the opening of Samaria's gate. All the prophets were prophesying in front of them.
22:11 Zedekiah son of Kenaanah had made himself horns of iron. He said, "The LORD says this: 'With these you will butt Aram until you finish them off.'"
22:12 All the prophets were prophesying the same. "Go up to Ramoth-gilead and win," they said. "The LORD will hand it over to the king."
## Micaiah son of Imlah
22:13 The messenger who went to call Micaiah told him, "Look, the prophets speak with one mouth, all good for the king. Please let your word be like the word of one of them. Speak good."
22:14 But Micaiah said, "By the life of the LORD, what the LORD tells me is what I will speak."
22:15 He came to the king, and the king asked him, "Micaiah, shall we march on Ramoth-gilead to fight, or shall we let it be?" "Go up and win," he told him. "The LORD will hand it over to the king."
22:16 The king said to him, "How often do I have to put you under oath to speak only the truth to me in the LORD's name?"
22:17 Micaiah said, "I saw all Israel scattered on the mountains like a flock with no shepherd. And the LORD said, 'These have no masters. Let each of them go home in peace.'"
22:18 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Didn't I tell you? He does not prophesy good things about me, never anything but evil."
## The LORD's council
22:19 Micaiah said, "So listen to the LORD's word. I saw the LORD sitting on his throne, and all the army of heaven standing beside him, on his right and on his left.
22:20 The LORD said, 'Who will lure Ahab into going up, so that he falls at Ramoth-gilead?' One said one thing, and another said another.
22:21 Then the spirit came out and stood in front of the LORD. 'I will lure him,' it said.
22:22 'How?' the LORD asked it. 'I will go out,' it said, 'and I will be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' 'You will lure him,' he said, 'and you will win, too. Go out and do it.'
22:23 So now, look: the LORD has put a lying spirit in the mouth of all your prophets here. The LORD has spoken disaster against you."
22:24 Zedekiah son of Kenaanah came up and struck Micaiah on the cheek. "Just which way did the LORD's Spirit cross over from me to talk to you?" he said.
22:25 Micaiah said, "Look, you will see on that day, when you go into a room inside a room to hide."
22:26 The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and bring him back to Amon, the commander of the city, and to Joash, the king's son.
22:27 Tell them, 'The king says this: Put this man in the prison house. Feed him the bread of hardship and the water of hardship until I come back in peace.'"
22:28 Micaiah said, "If you really do come back in peace, the LORD has not spoken through me." And he said, "Listen, peoples, all of you!"
## Ahab dies at Ramoth-gilead
22:29 The king of Israel went up to Ramoth-gilead, and Jehoshaphat king of Judah with him.
22:30 The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and go into the battle. But you, put on your own robes." So the king of Israel went into the battle in disguise.
22:31 The king of Aram had given orders to his chariot commanders, thirty-two of them: "Don't fight anyone, small or great. Fight only the king of Israel, him alone."
22:32 When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they said, "That has to be the king of Israel!" They swung around to attack him, and Jehoshaphat cried out.
22:33 When the chariot commanders saw that he was not the king of Israel, they turned back from chasing him.
22:34 But one man pulled back his bow with no special aim, and he hit the king of Israel between the joints of his armor and the breastplate. The king called to the man at the reins, "Swing the chariot around and drive me out of the battle lines. I've been hit."
22:35 The fighting rose higher that day. The king was kept standing up in the chariot, facing Aram, and he died in the evening. Blood from the wound pooled in the bottom of the chariot.
22:36 At sundown a shout went through the camp: "Each man to his city! Each man to his land!"
22:37 So the king died. He was brought to Samaria, and they buried the king in Samaria.
22:38 They washed out the chariot at the pool of Samaria. The dogs licked up his blood, and the prostitutes bathed, according to the word the LORD had spoken.
22:39 The rest of Ahab's story, all he did, the ivory house he built and all the towns he built, is written down, isn't it, in the scroll of the day-by-day records of the kings of Israel?
22:40 Ahab lay down with his fathers, and his son Ahaziah became king in his place.
## Jehoshaphat king of Judah
22:41 In the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel, Jehoshaphat son of Asa became king over Judah.
22:42 He was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he ruled for twenty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother was named Azubah daughter of Shilhi.
22:43 He followed in every way the road his father Asa walked, and did not turn aside from it. He did what was right in the LORD's eyes. Only, the high places were not removed. The people still offered sacrifices and burned offerings at the high places.
22:44 Jehoshaphat also made peace with the king of Israel.
22:45 The rest of Jehoshaphat's story, the mighty deeds he did and the wars he fought, is written down, isn't it, in the scroll of the day-by-day records of the kings of Judah?
22:46 He swept out of the land the rest of the shrine men who had been left over in the days of his father Asa.
22:47 At that time Edom had no king. A deputy acted as king.
22:48 Jehoshaphat made Tarshish ships to sail to Ophir for gold. But they never went, because the ships were wrecked at Ezion-geber.
22:49 Then Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, "Let my servants go with your servants in the ships." But Jehoshaphat would not agree.
22:50 Jehoshaphat lay down with his ancestors and was buried with his ancestors in the city of his forefather David. His son Jehoram became king in his place.
## Ahaziah king of Israel
22:51 In the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, Ahaziah son of Ahab became king over Israel in Samaria. He ruled over Israel for two years.
22:52 He did what the LORD sees as evil. He walked in his father's road, in his mother's road, and in the road of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who led Israel into sin.
22:53 He served Baal and bowed down to him, and he provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger, just as his father had done in every way.
