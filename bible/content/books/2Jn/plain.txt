## Greetings to the chosen lady
1:1 The Elder, to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth. And I am not the only one: so do all who have come to know the truth,
1:2 for the sake of the truth that remains in us and will be with us for all time.
1:3 With us there will be grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.
## Walk in love
1:4 I was very glad to have found some of your children walking in truth, just as we received a command from the Father.
1:5 And now I ask you, lady. What I write you is not a new command. It is the one we have had from the beginning: that we love each other.
1:6 And love means this: that we walk by his commands. The command, just as you heard it from the beginning, is this: walk in it.
## Deceivers have gone out
1:7 For many deceivers went out into the world, people who do not confess Jesus Christ as coming in the flesh. This one is the deceiver and the antichrist.
1:8 Be on your guard for yourselves. Then you will not lose what we worked for, but will receive a full reward.
1:9 Anyone who goes ahead and does not remain in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever remains in the teaching, that one has both the Father and the Son.
1:10 If someone comes to you who does not bring this teaching, do not take him into the house, and do not say "Greetings!" to him.
1:11 For the one who says "Greetings!" to him shares in his evil works.
## Paper and ink
1:12 I have a lot to write to you, but I chose not to do it with paper and ink. Rather, I hope to be with you and to speak face to face, so that our joy will be filled up.
1:13 The children of your chosen sister send you greetings.
