## To Gaius, walking in the truth
1:1 The Elder, to Gaius the beloved, whom I love in truth.
1:2 Beloved, I pray that in every way you are doing well and are in good health, just as your soul is doing well.
1:3 For I was very glad when brothers kept coming and testifying to your truth, that you do walk in truth.
1:4 No joy of mine is greater than this: to hear of my own children walking in truth.
## Help for the traveling brothers
1:5 Beloved, whatever you do for the brothers, and strangers at that, you do faithfully.
1:6 They testified to your love in front of the church. Send them on their journey in a way worthy of God, and you will do well.
1:7 It was for the sake of the Name that they set out, and they take nothing from the Gentiles.
1:8 So we ourselves are bound to take such people under our care, and in that way we become fellow workers for the truth.
## Diotrephes, who loves to be first
1:9 I wrote something to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves to be first among them, does not welcome us.
1:10 For this reason, if I come, I will point out the works he is doing, babbling against us with evil words. And not satisfied with that, he himself does not welcome the brothers. He blocks those who want to, and drives them out of the church.
## Imitate the good: Demetrius
1:11 Beloved, do not imitate the bad, but the good. Anyone who does good is from God. Anyone who does bad has not seen God.
1:12 Demetrius has had testimony given for him by everyone, and by the truth itself. We testify for him as well, and you know our testimony is true.
## Ink and pen
1:13 I had a lot to write to you, but I do not want to write it to you with ink and pen.
1:14 But I hope to see you right away, and we will speak face to face. Peace to you. The friends send you greetings. Greet each of the friends by name.
