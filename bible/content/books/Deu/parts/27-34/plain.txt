## Stones on Mount Ebal
27:1 Moses and the elders of Israel gave the people this command: "Keep every command I am giving you today.
27:2 On the day you cross the Jordan into the land the LORD your God is giving you, set up some large stones and coat them with plaster.
27:3 Write all the words of this law on them once you have crossed. Do this so that you may go into the land the LORD your God is giving you. It is a land flowing with milk and honey, just as the LORD, your ancestors' God, told you.
27:4 So when you have crossed the Jordan, set up these stones on Mount Ebal, as I command you today, and coat them with plaster.
27:5 Build an altar there to the LORD your God, an altar of stones. Do not swing an iron tool over them.
27:6 Build the altar of the LORD your God out of whole, uncut stones, and offer burnt offerings on it to the LORD your God.
27:7 Sacrifice peace offerings there and eat them, and celebrate in the presence of the LORD your God.
27:8 And on the stones write all the words of this law, plainly and carefully."
## Today you have become his people
27:9 Then Moses and the Levite priests spoke to all Israel: "Be still and listen, Israel! Today you have become the people of the LORD your God.
27:10 So listen to the voice of the LORD your God, and do his commands and his rules that I am giving you today."
## Blessing and curse from two mountains
27:11 That same day Moses gave the people this command:
27:12 "When you have crossed the Jordan, these tribes are to stand on Mount Gerizim to bless the people: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph and Benjamin.
27:13 And these tribes are to stand on Mount Ebal for the curse: Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan and Naphtali.
27:14 Then the Levites will call out in a loud voice to every Israelite:
27:15 'A curse on anyone who makes an idol, carved or cast in metal, the work of a craftsman's hands, a thing the LORD finds abhorrent, and sets it up in secret!' And all the people will answer, 'Amen!'
27:16 'A curse on anyone who treats his father or his mother with contempt!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:17 'A curse on anyone who shifts his neighbor's boundary marker!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:18 'A curse on anyone who sends a blind person the wrong way on the road!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:19 'A curse on anyone who twists the justice owed to a foreigner, an orphan or a widow!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:20 'A curse on anyone who has sex with his father's wife, for he has lifted the wing of his father's cloak!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:21 'A curse on anyone who has sex with any animal!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:22 'A curse on anyone who has sex with his sister, whether she is his father's daughter or his mother's daughter!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:23 'A curse on anyone who has sex with his mother-in-law!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:24 'A curse on anyone who attacks his neighbor where no one sees!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:25 'A curse on anyone who takes a bribe to strike down someone whose blood is innocent!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'
27:26 'A curse on anyone who does not make the words of this law stand by doing them!' And all the people will say, 'Amen!'"
## If you listen
28:1 "If you truly listen to the LORD your God and take care to do all his commands that I am giving you today, the LORD your God will raise you higher than every nation on earth.
28:2 All these blessings will come on you and catch up with you, if you listen to the LORD your God:
28:3 You will be blessed in the city, and blessed out in the fields.
28:4 The fruit of your womb will be blessed, and the fruit of your soil and the fruit of your animals: the calves of your herds and the lambs of your flocks.
28:5 Your basket and your kneading bowl will be blessed.
28:6 You will be blessed coming in and blessed going out.
28:7 When enemies attack you, the LORD will see that they are beaten in front of you. They will march out at you by one road and scatter from you by seven roads.
28:8 The LORD will order his blessing to be with you, in your barns and in everything you set your hand to. He will bless you in the land the LORD your God is giving you.
28:9 If you keep the commands of the LORD your God and walk in his ways, the LORD will set you up as his own holy people, as he promised you with an oath.
28:10 Every people on earth will see that the LORD's name has been called over you, and they will stand in fear of you.
28:11 The LORD will give you more than enough of good things: in the fruit of your womb, the fruit of your animals and the fruit of your soil, in the land the LORD swore to your ancestors to give you.
28:12 The LORD will open the sky for you, his storehouse of good things, and send rain on your land in its season. He will bless all the work of your hands. Many nations will borrow from you, and you will borrow from none.
28:13 The LORD will make you the head and not the tail. You will only rise and never sink, if you listen to the commands of the LORD your God that I am giving you today, and take care to do them.
28:14 Do not leave the path of any word I command you today, either to the right or to the left, by going after other gods to serve them.
## If you will not listen
28:15 "But if you will not listen to the LORD your God and take care to do all his commands and his rules that I am giving you today, then all these curses will come on you and catch up with you:
28:16 You will be cursed in the city, and cursed out in the fields.
28:17 Your basket and your kneading bowl will be cursed.
28:18 The fruit of your womb will be cursed, and the fruit of your soil, the calves of your herds and the lambs of your flocks.
28:19 You will be cursed coming in and cursed going out.
28:20 The LORD will send the curse on you, with panic and angry rebuke, in everything you set your hand to do. It will go on until you are wiped out and quickly perish, because of the evil things you did when you abandoned me.
28:21 The LORD will make plague cling to you until he has wiped you off the land you are going in to take possession of.
28:22 The LORD will strike you down with wasting sickness, with fever and swelling, with scorching heat and the sword, and with blight and mildew. They will chase you until you perish.
28:23 The sky above you will turn to bronze, and the ground below you to iron.
28:24 Instead of rain the LORD will send powder and dust on your land. It will come down on you from the sky until you are destroyed.
28:25 The LORD will let you be beaten before your enemies. You will march out at them by one road and scatter from them by seven roads. Every kingdom on earth will shudder at you.
28:26 Your corpses will feed every bird that flies and every wild animal, and no one will be there to chase them off.
28:27 The LORD will strike you with Egypt's boils, with tumors, running sores and an itch, and nothing will cure them.
28:28 The LORD will strike you with madness and blindness and a mind in a daze.
28:29 You will feel your way at noon the way a blind person feels his way in the dark. You will not make your paths succeed. All your days you will only be wronged and robbed, and no one will come to save you.
28:30 A woman will be promised to you in marriage, and another man will force her. You will build a house and never live there. You will plant a vineyard and never get to use its grapes.
28:31 They will butcher your ox in front of you, and you will not taste it. They will drag your donkey away as you watch, and it will never come back. Your flocks will go to your enemies, and no one will save you.
28:32 Your sons and daughters will be handed over to another people while you look on. Your eyes will ache for them all day long, and there will be nothing you can do.
28:33 A people you have never known will eat what your soil grows and everything you have worked for. You will be nothing but crushed and beaten down all your days.
28:34 What you see with your own eyes will drive you out of your mind.
28:35 The LORD will strike you on the knees and legs with bad boils you cannot be healed of, from the soles of your feet to your scalp.
28:36 The LORD will march you off, with the king you put over yourselves, to a nation unknown to you and your ancestors. There you will serve other gods made of wood and stone.
28:37 You will become a sight that shocks people, an example in their sayings and a target of their jokes, among all the peoples the LORD drives you to.
28:38 You will sow plenty of seed in the field and harvest little, because the locusts will strip it.
28:39 You will plant and tend vineyards, but you will not drink their wine or gather their grapes, because the grubs will eat them.
28:40 Olive trees will grow all through your land, but you will have no oil to rub on your skin, because your olives will fall off.
28:41 You will father sons and daughters, but they will not stay yours, for they will go away as captives.
28:42 The whirring locust will take over all your trees and the crops of your soil.
28:43 The foreigner living among you will climb up and up above you, and you will go down and down.
28:44 He will be the one lending to you, not you to him. He will be the head, and you the tail.
28:45 All these curses will come on you. They will chase you and catch up with you until you are destroyed, because you did not listen to the LORD your God and keep the commands and rules he gave you.
28:46 They will be a sign and a wonder on you and on your descendants for all time.
28:47 You had more than enough of everything, yet you did not serve the LORD your God with joy and a glad heart.
28:48 So you will serve the enemies the LORD sends against you, hungry, thirsty, naked and lacking everything. He will lay an iron yoke on your neck until he has wiped you out.
## A nation from far away
28:49 "From far off, from the edge of the earth, the LORD will raise up a nation against you. It will dive on you the way an eagle swoops. You will not understand its language.
28:50 Its people will be grim-faced. They will not honor the old or spare the young.
28:51 They will eat the fruit of your animals and the fruit of your soil until you are destroyed. They will leave you no grain, no new wine, no olive oil, no calves from your herds or lambs from your flocks, until they have ruined you.
28:52 They will hem you in inside every town, until the tall, strong walls you rely on fall everywhere in your land. They will surround every town in the whole land the LORD your God gave you.
28:53 Under the siege, in the desperate straits your enemy squeezes you into, you will eat your own children, the flesh of the sons and daughters the LORD your God gave you.
28:54 The softest, most pampered man among you will turn mean toward his own brother, toward the wife he loves, and toward whatever children he has left.
28:55 He will not give any of them a share of his children's flesh that he is eating. He has nothing else left in the siege, in the straits your enemy squeezes you into in all your towns.
28:56 And the softest, most pampered woman among you will do the same. She is so dainty she would not risk setting the sole of her foot on the ground, yet she will turn mean toward the husband she loves, toward her son and toward her daughter.
28:57 She will hold back from them even the afterbirth that comes out from between her legs, and the children she bears. She will eat them in secret, because she has nothing at all, in the siege and the straits your enemy squeezes you into in your towns.
28:58 "If you are not careful to carry out every word of this law written in this scroll, by fearing this name full of glory and terror, the LORD your God,
28:59 then the LORD will make your plagues and your descendants' plagues beyond belief: huge plagues that never let go, and harsh sicknesses that never let go.
28:60 He will bring back on you every disease of Egypt that you dreaded, and they will stick to you.
28:61 The LORD will also bring on you every sickness and every plague that is not written in this scroll of the law, until you are destroyed.
28:62 You were once as many as the stars in the sky, but only a handful of you will be left, because you would not listen to the LORD your God.
28:63 Just as the LORD rejoiced over you, doing you good and making you many, so the LORD will rejoice over you as he ruins you and destroys you. You will be torn off the land you are going in to take possession of.
28:64 The LORD will scatter you among all the peoples, from one end of the earth to the other. There you will serve other gods that neither you nor your ancestors have known, gods of wood and stone.
28:65 Among those nations you will have no rest, and there will be no place for the sole of your foot to stop. There the LORD will give you a heart that shakes, eyes that fail and a spirit sinking into despair.
28:66 Your life will hang in front of you by a thread. You will live in dread night and day, with no confidence that you will survive.
28:67 In the morning you will say, 'I wish it were evening!' and in the evening, 'I wish it were morning!' because of the terror gripping your heart and the sights before your eyes.
28:68 The LORD will ship you back to Egypt, on the very road I told you that you would never see again. There you will try to sell yourselves to your enemies as slaves, men and women, and no one will want to buy you."
## The covenant in Moab
29:1 In the land of Moab the LORD commanded Moses to make a covenant with the Israelites, besides the covenant he had made with them at Horeb. These are its words.
29:2 Moses called all Israel together and said to them: "You have seen with your own eyes all that the LORD did in the land of Egypt. You saw what he did to Pharaoh, his servants and all his land:
29:3 the great testings you watched, the signs and those great wonders.
29:4 Yet to this day the LORD has not given you a heart to know with, eyes to see with or ears to hear with.
29:5 For forty years I led you through the wilderness. Your clothes did not fall apart on you, and your sandals did not wear through on your feet.
29:6 You had no bread to eat and no wine or beer to drink. That was so you would learn that I am the LORD your God.
29:7 When you came to this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to fight us, but we struck them down.
29:8 We captured their land and handed it over as an inheritance to the tribes of Reuben and Gad and to half the tribe of Manasseh.
29:9 So keep the words of this covenant and do them, so that you will succeed in all you do.
## All of you, standing here today
29:10 "All of you stand here today before the LORD your God: your chiefs and your tribes, your elders and your officers, every Israelite man,
29:11 with your little ones and your wives, and the foreigner living in your camp, from the one who cuts your firewood to the one who carries your water.
29:12 You are standing here to enter the covenant of the LORD your God and his curse-oath, which the LORD your God is making with you today.
29:13 Today he will set you up as his people, and he will be your God, as he promised you and as he swore to your ancestors, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob.
29:14 And I am making this covenant and this curse-oath not only with you.
29:15 It is for the one who is here, standing with us today before the LORD our God, and for the one who is not here with us today.
29:16 You know how we lived in the land of Egypt, and how we made our way through the middle of the nations you passed through.
29:17 You saw their loathsome things and their idols, the wood and stone, the silver and gold they kept with them.
29:18 Take care that no man or woman, no family or tribe, is here today whose heart is turning from the LORD our God to go and serve the gods of those nations. Take care that no root is here among you that bears poison and wormwood.
29:19 When such a person hears the words of this curse-oath, he congratulates himself in his heart. He thinks, 'All will be well with me, even if I follow my own stubborn heart.' That would sweep away the watered ground along with the dry.
29:20 The LORD will refuse to pardon him. Instead the LORD's anger and jealousy will flare up like smoke against that man. Every curse written in this scroll will crouch on him, and the LORD will wipe out his name from under the sky.
29:21 The LORD will set him apart from all the tribes of Israel for harm, under all the covenant's curses written in this scroll of the law.
## What the nations will ask
29:22 "Your children who grow up after you, the generation to come, will speak of it, and so will the foreigner who comes from a distant land. They will see the plagues of that land and the sicknesses the LORD has sent on it.
29:23 The whole land will be burned out with sulfur and salt, unsown, growing nothing, with not a blade of grass coming up. It will be like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the LORD overthrew in his anger and rage.
29:24 Every nation will ask, 'Why did the LORD treat this land this way? What made his anger burn so hot?'
29:25 And the answer will be: 'They walked away from the covenant of the LORD, their ancestors' God, which he made with them when he led them out of the land of Egypt.
29:26 They went off to serve other gods and bow down to them, gods they had never known, gods he had not assigned to them.
29:27 So the LORD's anger burned against that land, and he brought on it every curse written in this scroll.
29:28 Furious, raging, in great wrath, the LORD pulled them up by the roots from their own soil and flung them into another land, where they are today.'
29:29 What is hidden is for the LORD our God. What has been made known is for us and our children for all time, so that we will carry out every word of this law.
## Return and restoration
30:1 "A time will come when all these things have happened to you, the blessing and the curse I have laid before you. Then, among all the nations where the LORD your God has driven you, you will take them to heart.
30:2 You and your children will turn back to the LORD your God and listen to his voice with all your heart and all your being, doing everything I am commanding you today.
30:3 Then the LORD your God will turn back your captivity and show you compassion. He will gather you once more out of all the peoples where the LORD your God scattered you.
30:4 Even if you have been driven to the farthest edge of the sky, the LORD your God will gather you from there, and from there he will fetch you.
30:5 The LORD your God will bring you into the land your ancestors took possession of, and you will take possession of it. He will do you good and make you more numerous than your ancestors.
30:6 The LORD your God will circumcise your heart, and your descendants' hearts too, so that you will love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your being, and so have life.
30:7 The LORD your God will turn all these curses onto your enemies, onto the people who hated you and hounded you.
30:8 And you will turn back and listen to the voice of the LORD, and do all his commands that I am giving you today.
30:9 The LORD your God will make you overflow with good things in all the work of your hands: in the fruit of your womb, the fruit of your animals and the fruit of your soil. For the LORD will turn again to rejoice over you for your good, as he rejoiced over your ancestors,
30:10 when you listen to the voice of the LORD your God and keep his commands and his rules written in this scroll of the law, when you turn back to the LORD your God with all your heart and all your being.
## The word is very close
30:11 "This command I am giving you today is not too strange or hard for you, and it is not far away.
30:12 It is not in the sky, so that you would ask, 'Who will climb up into the sky for us and get it, and tell it to us so we can do it?'
30:13 It is not on the far side of the sea, so that you would ask, 'Who will cross over to the far side of the sea for us and get it, and tell it to us so we can do it?'
30:14 The word is close by you, very close: in your mouth and in your heart, for you to do.
## Choose life
30:15 "See, today I am placing before you life and what is good, death and what is bad.
30:16 What I command you today is to love the LORD your God, to walk in his ways and to keep his commands, his rules and his rulings. Then you will live and grow in number, and the LORD your God will bless you in the land you are going in to take possession of.
30:17 But if your heart turns away and you will not listen, and you are drawn off to bow down to other gods and serve them,
30:18 I warn you today: you will be destroyed, without a doubt. Your days will not be long on the soil you are crossing the Jordan to go in and take possession of.
30:19 Today I call the heavens and the earth to testify against you. Life and death are what I have put before you, blessing and curse. Choose life, then, so that you and your children will live,
30:20 by loving the LORD your God, listening to his voice and holding on to him. He is your life, and he gives you long days to live on the land the LORD swore to give to your ancestors, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob."
## Joshua will lead
31:1 Moses went and spoke these words to all Israel.
31:2 He said to them, "Today I am a hundred and twenty years old. I am no longer able to go out and come in. And the LORD has told me, 'You will not cross this Jordan.'
31:3 It is the LORD your God who will cross ahead of you. He is the one who will wipe out these nations before you, and you will drive them out. Joshua is the one who will cross at your head, as the LORD said.
31:4 The LORD will do to them what he did to Sihon and Og, the kings of the Amorites, and to their land, when he destroyed them.
31:5 The LORD will give them up to you, and you must treat them exactly as I commanded you.
31:6 Be strong and bold. Do not be afraid of them or tremble before them, because the LORD your God is the one marching with you. He will not let you down, and he will not abandon you."
31:7 Then Moses called Joshua and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be strong and bold. You are the one who will go in with this people to the land the LORD swore to their ancestors to give them. You are the one who will hand it over to them as their inheritance.
31:8 The LORD is the one going ahead of you, and he will be with you. He will not let you down, and he will not abandon you. Do not be afraid and do not be shaken."
## The law read every seven years
31:9 Moses put this law in writing and handed it to the priests, the sons of Levi, who carried the ark of the LORD's covenant, and to all of Israel's elders.
31:10 Then Moses commanded them: "At the end of every seven years, in the Year of Release, when the Feast of Booths comes round,
31:11 when all Israel comes to appear before the LORD your God at the place he will choose, read this law aloud in front of all Israel, in their hearing.
31:12 Call the people together, the men, the women and the children, and the foreigner who lives in your towns. Let them hear it and learn, so that they will fear the LORD your God and take care to do every word of this law.
31:13 Their children, who have not known it, must hear it too. They must learn to fear the LORD your God as long as you live on the soil you are crossing the Jordan to take possession of."
## The LORD warns Moses
31:14 The LORD said to Moses, "Your days are drawing close to death. Call Joshua, and both of you present yourselves at the tent of meeting, so that I can put him in charge." So Moses and Joshua went and presented themselves at the tent of meeting.
31:15 The LORD appeared at the tent in a pillar of cloud, which stood over the tent's entrance.
31:16 The LORD said to Moses, "You are about to lie down with your ancestors, and this people will rise up. They will chase like a prostitute after the gods of the foreigners in the land they are going into. They will abandon me and break my covenant that I made with them.
31:17 On that day my anger will burn against them. I will abandon them and hide my face from them, and they will be eaten up. Many disasters and troubles will find them, and on that day they will say, 'Haven't these disasters found us because our God is not among us?'
31:18 On that day I will keep my face hidden, because of all the evil they have done in turning to other gods.
31:19 "So now write down this song for yourselves and teach it to the Israelites. Put it in their mouths, so that this song will be my witness against the Israelites.
31:20 When I have brought them into the land I swore to their ancestors, a land flowing with milk and honey, they will eat their fill and grow fat. Then they will turn to other gods and serve them. They will treat me with contempt and break my covenant.
31:21 And when many disasters and troubles find them, this song will testify against them as a witness, because it will not be forgotten from the mouths of their descendants. I know the plans they are shaping even today, before I have brought them into the land I swore to give them."
31:22 Moses wrote this song down that very day and taught it to the Israelites.
31:23 Then he put Joshua son of Nun in charge and said, "Be strong and bold, because you will bring the Israelites into the land I swore to them, and I myself will be with you."
## The scroll beside the ark
31:24 Moses finished writing the words of this law on a scroll, all of it, to the very end.
31:25 Then he commanded the Levites who carried the ark of the LORD's covenant:
31:26 "Take this scroll of the law and put it beside the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God. Let it stay there as a witness against you.
31:27 I know your defiance and your stiff neck. Even now, while I am still alive among you, you have kept rebelling against the LORD. How much worse will it be after I die!
31:28 Gather to me all the elders of your tribes and your officers. I will speak these words where they can hear them, and call the heavens and the earth to testify against them.
31:29 For I know that after my death you will become thoroughly corrupt and turn aside from the way I have commanded you. In the last days trouble will come to meet you, because you will do what is evil in the LORD's eyes and anger him by what your hands have made."
31:30 Then Moses spoke the words of this song, all the way to the end, in the hearing of the whole assembly of Israel:
## The Rock and his people
32:1 Listen closely, you heavens, and let me speak; / let the earth hear what my mouth says.
32:2 Let my teaching come down like rain, / my words drip down like dew, / like fine drizzle on young grass, / like downpours on the green plants.
32:3 For I will call out the name of the LORD; / tell how great our God is!
32:4 He is the Rock; what he does is flawless, / and every path he takes is justice. / He is a God you can trust, with no wrong in him; / he is fair and he is straight.
32:5 They have spoiled themselves toward him; / they are not his children, they are a blot, / a warped and twisted generation.
32:6 Is this how you pay back the LORD, / you foolish people with no sense? / Isn't he your father, who brought you into being, / who made you and set you on your feet?
32:7 Remember the days of old times; / think over the years, one generation after another. / Ask your father, and he will tell you, / your old ones, and they will explain.
32:8 When the Most High handed out the nations' inheritance, / when he split up the children of Adam, / he marked out the peoples' borders / to match the number of the sons of God.
32:9 For the LORD's own share is his people; / Jacob is the inheritance measured out for him.
32:10 He came upon him in a wilderness land, / in a waste where the wild winds howl. / He circled around him and watched over him; / he guarded him like the pupil of his eye.
32:11 As an eagle rouses its nest / and hovers over its young, / spreads its wings and catches one / and carries it on its feathers,
32:12 The LORD by himself guided him, / and there was no strange god beside him.
32:13 He set him riding over the land's high places, / and he fed on what the fields grew. / He let him suck honey from the rock / and oil from hard flint,
32:14 butter from the cattle and milk from the sheep, / with fat from the lambs, / rams bred in Bashan, and goats, / with the plumpest kernels of wheat. / You drank the blood of the grape, red wine.
## Jeshurun grew fat
32:15 Jeshurun grew fat, and he kicked; / you got fat, you got thick, you got stuffed. / He threw off the God who made him / and treated the Rock who saved him as worthless.
32:16 With foreign gods they made him jealous; / with abhorrent things they made him angry.
32:17 They offered sacrifices to demons, not to God, / to gods they had not known, / to newcomers lately arrived, / which your ancestors never shuddered at.
32:18 You ignored the Rock who fathered you, / and forgot the God who went into labor with you.
32:19 The LORD saw, and he rejected them, / stung by what his sons and daughters did.
32:20 He said, "I will hide my face from them / and watch what becomes of them. / For they are a generation that turns everything upside down, / children with no faithfulness in them.
32:21 They stirred my jealousy with a not-god / and angered me with their puffs of air. / So I will stir their jealousy with a not-people, / and anger them with a nation of fools.
32:22 For my anger has set a fire blazing / that burns to the bottom of Sheol. / It eats up the earth and all it grows / and sets the mountains' foundations on fire.
32:23 I will heap troubles on them / and shoot every arrow I have at them.
32:24 They will be drained by famine, / eaten up by burning plague and bitter sickness. / I will set the teeth of wild animals on them, / with the venom of creatures sliding in the dust.
32:25 Outside, the sword will rob them of children, / and inside the rooms, terror, / for young man and young woman alike, / the nursing baby with the gray-haired man.
32:26 I thought, 'I will blow them away / and make all human memory of them stop,'
32:27 except that I dreaded the enemy's taunts, / that their foes would get it wrong, / that they would say, 'Our hand won the day; / it was not the LORD who did all this.'"
## Their rock is not our Rock
32:28 They are a nation with no good sense left, / without any understanding.
32:29 If they were wise, they would grasp this / and think about where they are heading.
32:30 How could one chase a thousand / and two make ten thousand run, / unless their Rock had sold them off / and the LORD had given them away?
32:31 Their rock is nothing like our Rock, / as even our enemies must judge.
32:32 Their vine is a cutting from Sodom's vine, / grown in Gomorrah's terraced fields. / Their grapes are poison grapes; / their bunches are bitter.
32:33 Their wine is the venom of snakes, / the cruel poison of cobras.
32:34 Have I not kept this in store, / locked and sealed in my treasure rooms?
32:35 Vengeance belongs to me, and payback, / for the time their foot slips. / Their day of ruin is close, / and what is coming for them hurries on.
## I, only I, am he
32:36 The LORD will judge his people / and show his servants pity, / when he sees their strength has run out / and no one is left, whether held fast or set loose.
32:37 He will say, "Where are their gods now, / the rock they ran to for shelter,
32:38 who ate the fat of their sacrifices / and drank the wine of their drink offerings? / Let them get up and help you! / Let them be your cover!
32:39 Look now: I, only I, am he; / there is no god alongside me. / I kill, and I make alive; / I strike down, and I make well; / and no one can pull anyone out of my hand.
32:40 I lift my hand to the sky / and swear, As I live forever,
32:41 when I sharpen my flashing sword / and my hand takes hold of judgment, / I will turn vengeance back on my foes / and pay back the ones who hate me.
32:42 I will make my arrows drunk with blood, / and my sword will eat flesh, / with the blood of the slain and the captives, / from the heads of the enemy's princes."
32:43 Shout for joy with him, you heavens, / and all you gods, bow down to him! / He will avenge his sons' blood / and turn vengeance back on his foes. / He will pay back the ones who hate him / and make atonement for his people's land.
## Your very life
32:44 Moses came and spoke all the words of this song in the hearing of the people, he and Hoshea son of Nun.
32:45 When Moses had finished speaking all these words to all Israel,
32:46 he said to them, "Set your hearts on every word I am solemnly giving you today, so that you will command your children to take care to do every word of this law.
32:47 For this is not an empty word for you. It is your life. Through this word your days will be long on the soil you are crossing the Jordan to take possession of."
## Go up and die
32:48 That very same day the LORD said to Moses,
32:49 "Go up into these Abarim mountains, to Mount Nebo in the land of Moab, facing Jericho, and see the land of Canaan, which I am giving the Israelites to hold.
32:50 On the mountain you climb, you will die and be gathered to your people, the way your brother Aaron died on Mount Hor and was gathered to his.
32:51 For both of you acted unfaithfully toward me among the Israelites at the waters of Meribah-kadesh in the wilderness of Zin, because you did not show my holiness among the Israelites.
32:52 You will see the land from a distance, but you will not go there, into the land I am giving the Israelites."
## The blessing of Moses
33:1 Before his death, Moses the man of God blessed the Israelites. This is the blessing.
33:2 He said, "The LORD came from Sinai / and rose like the dawn over them from Seir. / He beamed out from Mount Paran / and came from among ten thousands of holy ones; / at his right hand was a fiery law for them.
33:3 Yes, he loves the peoples; / all his holy ones are in your hand. / They bow down at your feet / and take up your words.
33:4 Moses commanded us a law, / the heritage of the assembly of Jacob.
33:5 He became king in Jeshurun / when the heads of the people gathered, / the tribes of Israel together."
33:6 "Let Reuben live and not die, / though his men be few."
33:7 And this is for Judah. He said, "Hear, LORD, the voice of Judah, / and bring him to his people. / With his own hands he fights for himself; / be a help to him against his foes."
## Levi, Benjamin and Joseph
33:8 About Levi he said, "Give Levi your Thummim, and your Urim to your loyal one, / whom you tested at Massah, / with whom you quarreled at the waters of Meribah.
33:9 He said of his father and mother, 'I have not seen them.' / He did not recognize his brothers / or acknowledge his own children. / For they kept your word / and guarded your covenant.
33:10 They will teach your rulings to Jacob / and your law to Israel. / They will bring incense for you to smell / and whole burnt offerings to your altar.
33:11 Bless his strength, LORD, / and accept the work of his hands. / Smash the backs of those who rise against him, / of those who hate him, so they rise no more."
33:12 About Benjamin he said, "The LORD's beloved will live in safety by him. / He covers him all day long, / and he rests between his shoulders."
33:13 About Joseph he said, "Blessed by the LORD be his land / with the richest gifts of the sky, the dew, / and of the deep that lies stretched out below,
33:14 with the richest crops the sun ripens / and the richest yield of each month,
33:15 with the best from the age-old mountains / and the richest of the hills that last forever,
33:16 with the richest of the earth and all that fills it, / and the favor of the one who lived in the bush. / Let these come on the head of Joseph, / on the crown of the one set apart from his brothers.
33:17 His firstborn bull is majestic; / his horns are a wild ox's horns. / With them he will butt the peoples, / every one of them, to the ends of the earth. / Such are Ephraim's tens of thousands, / such are Manasseh's thousands."
## The other tribes
33:18 About Zebulun he said, "Rejoice, Zebulun, when you go out, / and Issachar, in your tents.
33:19 They will call peoples to the mountain; / there they will offer right sacrifices. / For they will draw from the wealth of the seas / and the treasures hidden in the sand."
33:20 About Gad he said, "Blessed is the one who makes room for Gad! / Gad lies down like a lion / and tears an arm, yes, and the crown of the head.
33:21 He chose the best land for himself, / for a ruler's share was kept for him there. / He came with the heads of the people; / he carried out the justice of the LORD / and his rulings with Israel."
33:22 About Dan he said, "Dan is a lion's cub / who leaps out from Bashan."
33:23 About Naphtali he said, "Naphtali, filled with favor / and full of the LORD's blessing, / take possession of the west and the south."
33:24 About Asher he said, "Most blessed of sons is Asher. / May he be his brothers' favorite, / and may he dip his foot in oil.
33:25 The bolts of your gates will be iron and bronze, / and your strength will last as long as your days."
## None like God, Jeshurun
33:26 "No one is like God, Jeshurun! / He rides across the sky to help you, / across the clouds in his splendor.
33:27 The God of ancient times is a shelter, / and beneath are arms that last forever. / He drove the enemy out ahead of you / and said, 'Destroy them!'
33:28 So Israel has settled in safety; / Jacob's spring stands apart / in a land of grain and new wine, / where the skies drip with dew.
33:29 How happy you are, Israel! / Who is like you, a people saved by the LORD? / He is the shield that helps you / and the sword that makes you great. / Your enemies will come crawling to you, / and you will tread on their high places."
## The death of Moses
34:1 Moses climbed from the plains of Moab up Mount Nebo, to the peak of Pisgah, facing Jericho. There the LORD let him see the whole land: Gilead all the way to Dan,
34:2 all of Naphtali, the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, all the land of Judah out to the sea in the west,
34:3 the Negev, and the round plain of the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar.
34:4 Then the LORD said to him, "This is the land I swore to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, when I said, 'I will give it to your offspring.' I have let you see it for yourself, but you will not go over there."
34:5 There in the land of Moab, Moses the servant of the LORD died, at the LORD's word.
34:6 And he buried him in the valley in the land of Moab, facing Beth-peor. No one knows the place of his grave to this day.
34:7 Moses was a hundred and twenty years old when he died. His eyesight had not grown weak, and his vigor had not drained away.
34:8 The Israelites wept for Moses on the plains of Moab for thirty days. Then the days of weeping and mourning for Moses were over.
## No prophet like Moses
34:9 Moses had laid his hands on Joshua son of Nun, and so Joshua was filled with a spirit of wisdom. The Israelites listened to him and did what the LORD had commanded Moses.
34:10 No prophet has risen again in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face.
34:11 No one matched him in all the signs and wonders the LORD sent him to do in the land of Egypt, against Pharaoh, all his servants and all his land,
34:12 or in all the might of his strong hand, or in all the great and fearful deeds that Moses did in the sight of all Israel.
