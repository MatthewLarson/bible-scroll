## Words spoken beyond the Jordan
1:1 These are the words Moses spoke to all Israel on the far side of the Jordan. They were in the wilderness, in the Arabah across from Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth and Di-zahab.
1:2 It is eleven days from Horeb to Kadesh-barnea by the Mount Seir road.
1:3 In the fortieth year, on the first day of the eleventh month, Moses spoke to the Israelites. He told them all that the LORD had commanded him to tell them.
1:4 This was after he had struck down Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who lived in Ashtaroth, at Edrei.
1:5 On the far side of the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to make this law clear. He said,
## Enough of this mountain
1:6 "The LORD our God spoke to us at Horeb. He said, 'Enough of staying at this mountain!
1:7 Turn and break camp. Go to the hill country of the Amorites and to all the neighbors around them: in the Arabah, in the hills, in the Shephelah, in the Negev and along the seacoast. Go to the land of the Canaanites and to Lebanon, as far as the great river, the river Euphrates.
1:8 Look, I have set the land in front of you. Go in and take possession of the land the LORD swore to give to your ancestors, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, and to their descendants after them.'
## Leaders for the tribes
1:9 "At that time I said to you, 'I cannot carry you all by myself.
1:10 The LORD your God has made you many. Today you are like the stars of the sky in number.
1:11 May the LORD, the God of your ancestors, make you a thousand times more than you are, and bless you, just as he promised you!
1:12 But how can I carry by myself the strain of you, the weight of you and your quarrels?
1:13 From each of your tribes pick men who are wise, who have understanding and who are known, and I will make them your heads.'
1:14 You answered me, 'What you have told us to do is good.'
1:15 So I took the heads of your tribes, wise men who were known, and I made them heads over you. Some were commanders of thousands, some of hundreds, some of fifties and some of tens. I also gave your tribes officials.
1:16 At that time I gave orders to your judges. I said, 'Give your brothers a full hearing, and judge rightly between a man and his brother, or the foreigner who lives with him.
1:17 Do not play favorites when you judge. Listen to the small one just as to the great one. Do not be afraid of anyone, because the judgment is God's. Bring me any case that is too hard for you, and I will hear it.'
1:18 At that time I commanded you all the things you should do.
## The refusal at Kadesh
1:19 "Then we set out from Horeb. We walked through all that huge and fearsome wilderness you saw, along the road to the hill country of the Amorites, as the LORD our God had commanded us. And we came to Kadesh-barnea.
1:20 I said to you, 'You have reached the hill country of the Amorites, which the LORD our God is giving us.
1:21 Look, the LORD your God has set the land in front of you. Go up and take possession of it, as the LORD, the God of your ancestors, told you. Do not be afraid, and do not lose heart.'
1:22 Then all of you came to me and said, 'Let us send men ahead of us to search out the land for us. They can bring us word about the road we should take up and the towns we will come to.'
1:23 The plan seemed good to me. So I took twelve men from among you, one man for each tribe.
1:24 They turned and went up into the hill country. They came to the Valley of Eshcol and scouted it out.
1:25 They took some of the land's fruit in their hands and brought it down to us. They brought back word to us: 'The land the LORD our God is giving us is good.'
1:26 "But you were not willing to go up. You rebelled against the command of the LORD your God.
1:27 You grumbled in your tents. You said, 'The LORD hates us. That is why he brought us out of the land of Egypt: to hand us over to the Amorites to wipe us out.
1:28 Where are we going up to? Our brothers have made our hearts melt. They told us, "The people are bigger and taller than we are. The towns are large, walled up to the sky. And we saw the Anakim there too!"'
1:29 Then I said to you, 'Do not tremble, and do not be afraid of them.
1:30 The LORD your God is the one going ahead of you. He himself will fight for you, just like all he did for you in Egypt in front of your eyes,
1:31 and in the wilderness, where you saw that the LORD your God carried you, the way a man carries his son, all along the road you traveled until you came to this place.'
1:32 Even so, in this matter you did not trust the LORD your God.
1:33 He went ahead of you on the way to look for a place for you to camp. He went in fire at night, to show you the road to take, and in the cloud by day.
1:34 "The LORD heard the sound of your words. He grew angry and swore an oath:
1:35 'No man of these men, this evil generation, will see the good land I swore to give your ancestors.
1:36 Only Caleb son of Jephunneh will see it. I will give him and his sons the land his feet have walked on, because he followed the LORD fully.'
1:37 Because of you the LORD was angry with me as well. He said, 'You will not go in there either.
1:38 Joshua son of Nun, who serves you, will go in there. Make him strong, because he will lead Israel to take it as their inheritance.
1:39 And your little ones, who you said would become plunder, your children who do not yet know good from bad today, they will go in there. I will give it to them, and they will take possession of it.
1:40 But as for you, turn around and set out into the wilderness, by the road to the Sea of Reeds.'
1:41 "Then you answered me, 'We have sinned against the LORD. We will go up and fight, just as the LORD our God commanded us.' Each of you strapped on his weapons of war. You thought it would be easy to go up into the hill country.
1:42 But the LORD said to me, 'Tell them, "Do not go up, and do not fight, because I am not among you. Then you will not be struck down in front of your enemies."'
1:43 So I told you, but you would not listen. You rebelled against the LORD's command, and in your arrogance you marched up into the hill country.
1:44 The Amorites who lived in that hill country came out to meet you. They chased you the way bees do and crushed you in Seir, all the way to Hormah.
1:45 You came back and wept before the LORD. But the LORD would not listen to your voice or turn his ear to you.
1:46 So you stayed in Kadesh many days, all the days that you stayed there.
## Passing by Esau's land
2:1 "Then we turned and set out into the wilderness, by the road to the Sea of Reeds, as the LORD had told me. For many days we went around the hill country of Seir.
2:2 Then the LORD said to me,
2:3 'You have gone around this hill country long enough. Turn north.
2:4 Give the people this order: "You are about to pass through the territory of your relatives, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. They will be afraid of you, so be very careful.
2:5 Do not start a fight with them. I will not give you any of their land, not even room for the sole of a foot, because I have given the hill country of Seir to Esau as his own.
2:6 Buy food from them with silver to eat, and buy water from them with silver to drink.
2:7 For the LORD your God has blessed you in all the work of your hands. He knows every step of your journey through this huge wilderness. For forty years now the LORD your God has been with you. You have not lacked a thing."'
2:8 So we passed by our relatives, the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir. We left the Arabah road, which comes up from Elath and Ezion-geber. Then we turned and went along the road through the wilderness of Moab.
## Moab and the giants of old
2:9 "The LORD said to me, 'Do not harass Moab, and do not stir them up to war. I will not give you any of their land as your own, because I have given Ar to the descendants of Lot as their own.'
2:10 (The Emim used to live there. They were a great people, many in number, and tall like the Anakim.
2:11 People also counted them as Rephaim, like the Anakim, but the Moabites call them Emim.
2:12 The Horites used to live in Seir, but the descendants of Esau drove them out. They wiped them out in front of them and settled in their place, just as Israel did in the land the LORD gave them to possess.)
2:13 'Now get up and cross the Zered Valley.' So we crossed the Zered Valley.
2:14 From the time we left Kadesh-barnea until we crossed the Zered Valley was thirty-eight years. By then the whole generation of fighting men had died out from the camp, as the LORD had sworn to them.
2:15 The LORD's own hand was against them, to throw them into confusion and remove them from the camp, until they were all gone.
## Ammon and the Zamzummim
2:16 "When the last of the fighting men had died and was gone from among the people,
2:17 the LORD spoke to me. He said,
2:18 'Today you are about to cross the border of Moab at Ar.
2:19 When you come near the Ammonites, do not harass them, and do not start a fight with them. I will not give you any of the Ammonites' land as your own, because I have given it to the descendants of Lot as their own.'
2:20 (It too was counted as a land of the Rephaim. The Rephaim used to live there, but the Ammonites call them Zamzummim.
2:21 They were a great people, many in number, and tall like the Anakim. But the LORD wiped them out in front of the Ammonites, who drove them out and settled in their place.
2:22 He had done the same for the descendants of Esau, who live in Seir, when he wiped out the Horites in front of them. They drove them out and have lived in their place to this day.
2:23 As for the Avvim, who lived in villages as far as Gaza, the Caphtorim, who came from Caphtor, wiped them out and settled in their place.)
## Sihon king of Heshbon
2:24 "'Get up, set out and cross the Arnon Valley. Look, I am handing Sihon the Amorite, king of Heshbon, and his land over to you. Begin to take possession. Go to war against him.
2:25 This very day I will begin to put the dread and fear of you on the peoples everywhere under the sky. When they hear the news about you, they will shake and writhe in fear because of you.'
2:26 "So I sent messengers from the wilderness of Kedemoth to Sihon king of Heshbon with an offer of peace. I said,
2:27 'Let me pass through your land. I will keep to the road. I will not turn off to the right or to the left.
2:28 Sell me food for silver for me to eat, and give me water for silver for me to drink. Just let me pass through on foot,
2:29 as the descendants of Esau who live in Seir did for me, and the Moabites who live in Ar, until I cross the Jordan into the land the LORD our God is giving us.'
2:30 But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass through his land. For the LORD your God made his spirit hard and his heart stubborn, in order to hand him over to you, as he has done today.
2:31 The LORD said to me, 'Look, I have begun to give Sihon and his land over to you. Begin to take possession, so that you may own his land.'
2:32 Sihon came out to meet us in battle at Jahaz, he and all his people.
2:33 The LORD our God handed him over to us, and we struck him down, with his sons and all his people.
2:34 At that time we captured all his towns. We put every town under the ban, men, women and little ones. We did not leave a single survivor.
2:35 We took only the livestock as plunder for ourselves, and the loot from the towns we had captured.
2:36 From Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley, and the town in the valley, all the way to Gilead, there was not a town too high for us. The LORD our God handed them all over to us.
2:37 But you did not go near the land of the Ammonites, the whole bank of the Jabbok River, or the towns of the hill country, or any place the LORD our God had forbidden.
## Og king of Bashan
3:1 "Then we turned and went up by the road to Bashan, and Og king of Bashan came out to meet us, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei.
3:2 The LORD said to me, 'Do not be afraid of him, for I have handed him over to you, with all his people and his land. Do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon.'
3:3 So the LORD our God handed Og king of Bashan and all his people over to us as well. We struck him down until no survivor was left to him.
3:4 At that time we captured all his towns. There was not one town we did not take from them: sixty towns, the whole region of Argob, the kingdom of Og in Bashan.
3:5 All these were fortified towns with high walls, gates and bars, besides a great many open villages.
3:6 We put them under the ban, as we had done to Sihon king of Heshbon. We put every town under the ban, men, women and little ones.
3:7 But all the livestock and the loot from the towns we took as plunder for ourselves.
3:8 So at that time we took the land out of the hands of the two kings of the Amorites on the far side of the Jordan, from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon.
3:9 (The Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorites call it Senir.)
3:10 We took all the towns of the high plain, all Gilead and all Bashan as far as Salecah and Edrei, towns of Og's kingdom in Bashan.
3:11 (Only Og king of Bashan was left of the last of the Rephaim. Look, his bed was a bed of iron. Is it not in Rabbah of the Ammonites? It is nine cubits long and four cubits wide, by a man's cubit.)
## Land for two and a half tribes
3:12 "At that time we took possession of this land. I gave the Reubenites and the Gadites the land from Aroer by the Arnon Valley, and half the hill country of Gilead with its towns.
3:13 The rest of Gilead and all Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half-tribe of Manasseh. (The whole region of Argob, all that part of Bashan, used to be called a land of the Rephaim.
3:14 Jair, a descendant of Manasseh, took the whole region of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites. He named those villages of Bashan after himself, Havvoth-jair, as they are still called today.)
3:15 To Machir I gave Gilead.
3:16 To the Reubenites and the Gadites I gave the land from Gilead down to the Arnon Valley, with the middle of the valley as its border, and as far as the Jabbok River, the border of the Ammonites.
3:17 Their land also took in the Arabah, with the Jordan as its border, from Chinnereth down to the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, below the slopes of Pisgah on the east.
3:18 "At that time I gave you this command: 'The LORD your God has given you this land to take possession of. But all your strong men must cross over armed, ahead of your brothers the Israelites.
3:19 Only your wives, your little ones and your livestock may stay in the towns I have given you. (I know you have a lot of livestock.)
3:20 Stay with them until the LORD gives your brothers rest as he has given you, and they also take possession of the land the LORD your God is giving them on the other side of the Jordan. Then each of you may go back to the property I have given you.'
3:21 At that time I gave Joshua this command: 'Your own eyes have seen all that the LORD your God has done to these two kings. The LORD will do the same to all the kingdoms you are crossing over into.
3:22 Do not be afraid of them, because the LORD your God is the one who fights for you.'
## Moses asks to cross
3:23 "At that time I begged the LORD for mercy. I said,
3:24 'Lord GOD, you have begun to let your servant see your greatness and your strong hand. Who is a god in the heavens or on the earth who can do works like yours, mighty deeds like yours?
3:25 Please let me cross over and see the good land on the other side of the Jordan, that good hill country, and Lebanon.'
3:26 But the LORD was furious with me because of you, and he would not listen to me. The LORD said to me, 'That is enough from you! Do not speak to me about this matter again.
3:27 Go up to the top of Pisgah. Lift up your eyes to the west and the north, to the south and the east, and look with your own eyes. For you will not cross this Jordan.
3:28 Give Joshua his orders. Make him brave and strong, because he will cross over at the head of this people. He will lead them to take as their inheritance the land you will see.'
3:29 So we stayed in the valley across from Beth-peor.
## Listen and live
4:1 "Now, Israel, listen to the rules and the rulings I am teaching you to do. Do them so that you may live, and go in and take possession of the land the LORD, the God of your ancestors, is giving you.
4:2 Do not add anything to the word I am commanding you, and do not take anything away from it. Keep the commands of the LORD your God that I am giving you.
4:3 Your own eyes saw what the LORD did at Baal-peor. The LORD your God wiped out from among you every man who followed the Baal of Peor.
4:4 But you who held on to the LORD your God are all alive today.
4:5 Look, I have taught you rules and rulings, as the LORD my God commanded me, for you to do in the land you are entering to take possession of.
4:6 Keep them and do them. This will show your wisdom and your understanding to the peoples. When they hear about all these rules, they will say, 'This great nation is surely a wise and understanding people!'
4:7 For what great nation has a god as close to it as the LORD our God is to us, every time we call to him?
4:8 And what great nation has rules and rulings as just as this whole law that I am setting before you today?
## Remember the day at Horeb
4:9 "Only be careful, and watch yourself closely, so that you do not forget the things your eyes have seen. Do not let them slip from your heart as long as you live. Make them known to your children and to your grandchildren:
4:10 the day you stood before the LORD your God at Horeb, when the LORD said to me, 'Gather the people to me, and I will let them hear my words. Then they will learn to fear me all the days they live on the earth, and they will teach their children.'
4:11 You came near and stood at the foot of the mountain. The mountain was burning with fire up into the heart of the sky, with darkness, cloud and deep gloom.
4:12 Then the LORD spoke to you out of the fire. You were hearing the sound of words, but you were seeing no form. There was nothing but a voice.
4:13 He announced to you his covenant, which he commanded you to do: the Ten Words. And he wrote them on two tablets of stone.
4:14 At that time the LORD commanded me to teach you rules and rulings, for you to do in the land you are crossing over to take possession of.
## No form to copy
4:15 "So watch yourselves very carefully. You saw no form of any kind on the day the LORD spoke to you at Horeb out of the fire.
4:16 Do not ruin yourselves by making an idol for yourselves in the form of any figure, whether the likeness of a man or a woman,
4:17 the likeness of any animal on the earth, the likeness of any winged bird that flies in the sky,
4:18 the likeness of anything that creeps on the ground, or the likeness of any fish in the waters below the earth.
4:19 And when you raise your eyes to the sky and see the sun, the moon and the stars, the whole army of the sky, do not be pulled away to bow down to them and serve them. The LORD your God has given them as a share to all the peoples under the whole sky.
4:20 But the LORD took you and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be his own people, his inheritance, as you are today.
4:21 The LORD was angry with me because of your words. He swore that I would not cross the Jordan or enter the good land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.
4:22 I am going to die in this land. I will not cross the Jordan. But you are about to cross and take possession of that good land.
4:23 Be careful not to forget the covenant the LORD your God made with you. Do not make yourselves an idol in the form of anything the LORD your God has forbidden you.
4:24 For the LORD your God is a devouring fire, a jealous God.
## Scattered, and found again
4:25 "When you have had children and grandchildren and have been in the land a long time, you may ruin yourselves. You may make an idol in the form of anything, and do what is evil in the eyes of the LORD your God, and provoke him to anger.
4:26 If you do, I call the heavens and the earth as witnesses against you today: you will quickly die out from the land you are crossing the Jordan to take possession of. You will not live long on it. You will be wiped out completely.
4:27 The LORD will scatter you among the peoples. Only a few of you will be left among the nations where the LORD drives you.
4:28 There you will serve gods made by human hands, wood and stone, which cannot see or hear or eat or smell.
4:29 But from there you will search for the LORD your God, and you will find him, when you seek him with all your heart and with all your being.
4:30 When you are in trouble and all these things have happened to you, in days to come, you will turn back to the LORD your God and listen to his voice.
4:31 For the LORD your God is a God of compassion. He will not let you go or destroy you, and he will not forget the covenant with your ancestors that he swore to them.
## Has anything this great happened?
4:32 "Ask about the days long past, the days before you, from the day God created human beings on the earth. Ask from one end of the sky to the other. Has anything as great as this ever happened? Has anyone ever heard of anything like it?
4:33 Has any people heard the voice of a god speaking out of fire, as you have heard it, and lived?
4:34 Or has any god ever tried to come and take a nation for himself out of another nation, by trials, by signs and wonders, by war, by a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and by great terrors? Yet the LORD your God did all this for you in Egypt, before your eyes.
4:35 It was you who were shown this, so that you would know that the LORD is God. Apart from him there is no other.
4:36 From the sky he let you hear his voice, to train you. On earth he let you see his great fire, and you heard his words out of the fire.
4:37 Because he loved your ancestors and chose his descendants after him, he brought you out of Egypt with his own presence, by his great power,
4:38 to drive out nations greater and stronger than you ahead of you, to bring you in and give you their land as an inheritance, as it is today.
4:39 So know today, and take it to heart: the LORD is God in the sky above and on the earth below. There is no other.
4:40 Keep his rules and his commands that I am giving you today. Then it will go well with you and your children after you, and you will live long on the land the LORD your God is giving you for all time."
## Three cities of refuge
4:41 Then Moses set apart three cities on the far side of the Jordan, toward the sunrise.
4:42 Anyone who killed his neighbor by accident, without having hated him before, could run to one of these cities and save his life.
4:43 The cities were Bezer in the wilderness, on the high plain, for the Reubenites; Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadites; and Golan in Bashan for the Manassites.
## This is the law
4:44 This is the law Moses set before the Israelites.
4:45 These are the decrees, the rules and the rulings Moses spoke to the Israelites after they came out of Egypt,
4:46 on the far side of the Jordan, in the valley across from Beth-peor. This was in the land of Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, whom Moses and the Israelites struck down after they came out of Egypt.
4:47 They took possession of his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two kings of the Amorites on the far side of the Jordan, toward the sunrise.
4:48 The land ran from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley as far as Mount Sion, that is, Hermon,
4:49 with all the Arabah east of the Jordan, as far as the Sea of the Arabah, below the slopes of Pisgah.
## A covenant with us, the living
5:1 Moses called all Israel together and said to them, "Listen, Israel, to the rules and the rulings I am speaking in your hearing today. Learn them, and be careful to do them.
5:2 The LORD our God made a covenant with us at Horeb.
5:3 The LORD did not make this covenant with our ancestors. He made it with us, we who are here today, all of us alive.
5:4 Face to face the LORD spoke with you at the mountain, out of the fire.
5:5 At that time I was standing between the LORD and you, to tell you the LORD's word, because you were afraid of the fire and did not go up the mountain. He said:
## The Ten Words
5:6 'I am the LORD your God. I brought you out of the land of Egypt, that house of slaves.
5:7 You must not have any other gods in my presence.
5:8 You must not make yourself an idol in the form of anything up in the sky, or down on the earth, or in the waters below the land.
5:9 You must not bow down to them or serve them. For I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God. I bring the fathers' guilt home to the children, to the third and the fourth generation of those who hate me,
5:10 but I show loyal love to thousands, for those who love me and keep my commands.
5:11 You must not take up the name of the LORD your God for something empty. The LORD will not count anyone innocent who takes up his name for something empty.
5:12 Keep the day of the Sabbath, and set it apart as holy, as the LORD your God commanded you.
5:13 You have six days to work and get all your jobs done,
5:14 but the seventh day is a Sabbath for the LORD your God. Do no work on it: not you, not your son or your daughter, not your slave, man or woman, not your ox or your donkey or any of your animals, not the foreigner who lives in your towns. Then your slave, man or woman, can rest just as you do.
5:15 Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and the LORD your God brought you out from there with a strong hand and an outstretched arm. That is why the LORD your God has commanded you to keep the day of the Sabbath.
5:16 Honor your father and your mother, as the LORD your God commanded you, so that your days will stretch out long, and so that it will go well with you on the land the LORD your God is giving you.
5:17 You must not murder.
5:18 And you must not commit adultery.
5:19 And you must not steal.
5:20 And you must not testify against your neighbor as an empty witness.
5:21 And you must not crave your neighbor's wife. And you must not long for your neighbor's house, his field, his slave, man or woman, his ox or his donkey, or anything at all that belongs to your neighbor.'
## You go near for us
5:22 "These are the words the LORD spoke to your whole assembly at the mountain, out of the fire, the cloud and the deep darkness, with a loud voice. He added nothing more. He wrote them on two tablets of stone and gave them to me.
5:23 When you heard the voice out of the darkness, while the mountain was burning with fire, all the heads of your tribes and your elders came to me.
5:24 You said, 'Look, the LORD our God has shown us his glory and his greatness, and we have heard his voice out of the fire. Today we have seen that God can speak with a human being and the person still lives.
5:25 But now, why should we die? This great fire will burn us up. If we hear the voice of the LORD our God any more, we will die.
5:26 For who among all flesh has ever heard the voice of the living God speaking out of fire, as we have, and lived?
5:27 You go near and hear all that the LORD our God says. Then you tell us all that the LORD our God tells you, and we will listen and do it.'
5:28 "The LORD heard what you said when you spoke to me. The LORD said to me, 'I have heard what this people said to you. Everything they have said is right.
5:29 If only they always had a heart like this, to fear me and keep all my commands, so that it would go well with them and their children forever!
5:30 Go and tell them to go back to their tents.
5:31 But you, stay here with me, and I will tell you the whole command, the rules and the rulings. Teach them to the people, so that they may do them in the land I am giving them to take possession of.'
5:32 So be careful to do what the LORD your God has commanded you. Do not turn off to the right or to the left.
5:33 Walk in all the way the LORD your God has commanded you. Then you will live, and it will be good for you, and you will live long in the land you will take possession of.
## The command for the land
6:1 "This is the command, the rules and the rulings, that the LORD your God told me to teach you, for you to do in the land you are crossing over to take possession of.
6:2 Do them so that you will fear the LORD your God all the days of your life, you, your son and your grandson, by keeping all his rules and his commands that I am giving you, and so that your days will be long.
6:3 Listen, Israel, and be careful to do them. Then it will go well with you, and you will grow very many, in a land flowing with milk and honey, as the LORD, the God of your ancestors, promised you.
## Listen, Israel
6:4 "Listen, Israel: The LORD is our God. The LORD is one.
6:5 Love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your being and with all your strength.
6:6 Keep these words I am commanding you today on your heart.
6:7 Repeat them to your children again and again. Talk about them when you are sitting in your house and when you are out on the road, when you go to bed and when you get up.
6:8 Tie them as a sign on your hand, and let them be a band between your eyes.
6:9 Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your city gates.
## When you are full, do not forget
6:10 "The LORD your God will bring you into the land he swore to your ancestors, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob, to give you. It has large, fine cities that you did not build,
6:11 houses full of all kinds of good things that you did not fill, wells cut in the rock that you did not dig, and vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant. When you eat and are full,
6:12 watch yourself. Do not forget the LORD, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that house of slaves.
6:13 It is the LORD your God you must fear. He is the one you must serve, and by his name you must swear.
6:14 Do not follow other gods, any of the gods of the peoples around you.
6:15 For the LORD your God, who is among you, is a jealous God. Otherwise the anger of the LORD your God will blaze against you, and he will wipe you off the face of the ground.
6:16 Do not put the LORD your God to the test, the way you tested him at Massah.
6:17 Be sure to keep the commands of the LORD your God, and the decrees and rules he has given you.
6:18 Do what is right and good in the LORD's eyes. Then it will go well with you, and you will go in and take possession of the good land the LORD swore to give your ancestors,
6:19 by driving out all your enemies ahead of you, as the LORD has said.
## When your son asks
6:20 "In days to come your son will ask you, 'What do they mean, the decrees, the rules and the rulings the LORD our God commanded you?'
6:21 Then tell your son, 'We were Pharaoh's slaves in Egypt, and the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a strong hand.
6:22 Before our eyes the LORD sent signs and wonders, great and terrible, against Egypt, against Pharaoh and against his whole household.
6:23 But he brought us out from there, to bring us in and give us the land he swore to our ancestors.
6:24 The LORD commanded us to do all these rules and to fear the LORD our God, for our good always, so that he would keep us alive, as we are today.
6:25 And it will be our righteousness if we are careful to do this whole command before the LORD our God, as he commanded us.'
## Seven nations
7:1 "The LORD your God will bring you into the land you are entering to take possession of. He will clear away many nations ahead of you: the Hittites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, seven nations bigger and stronger than you.
7:2 When the LORD your God hands them over to you and you strike them down, you must put them under the ban completely. Do not make a covenant with them, and do not show them mercy.
7:3 Do not marry into their families. Do not give your daughters to their sons, and do not take their daughters for your sons.
7:4 For they will turn your sons away from following me, to serve other gods. Then the LORD's anger will blaze against you, and he will quickly wipe you out.
7:5 Instead, this is what you are to do to them: pull down their altars, break their standing stones to pieces, chop down their Asherah poles and burn their carved images.
## A people holy to the LORD
7:6 "For you are a people holy to the LORD your God. The LORD your God has chosen you out of all the peoples on the face of the ground to be his people, his own private treasure.
7:7 The LORD did not set his heart on you and choose you because you were more in number than any other people. You were the fewest of all the peoples.
7:8 It was because the LORD loved you, and because he kept the oath he swore to your ancestors, that the LORD brought you out with a strong hand and ransomed you from the house of slaves, from the power of Pharaoh king of Egypt.
7:9 So know that the LORD your God is God, the God who can be trusted. He keeps his covenant and his loyal love for a thousand generations with those who love him and keep his commands.
7:10 But he pays back to their face those who hate him, by destroying them. He will not be slow with anyone who hates him. He will pay him back to his face.
7:11 So keep the command, the rules and the rulings I am giving you today, and do them.
## The blessing of listening
7:12 "If you listen to these rulings and are careful to do them, then the LORD your God will keep his covenant and his loyal love with you, as he swore to your ancestors.
7:13 He will love you, bless you and make you many. He will bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your ground: your grain, your new wine and your olive oil, the calves of your herds and the lambs of your flocks. He will do this in the land he swore to your ancestors to give you.
7:14 You will be blessed more than all the peoples. No man or woman among you will be childless, and none of your animals will be either.
7:15 The LORD will turn every sickness away from you. He will not put on you any of the evil diseases of Egypt that you know about. He will lay them on all who hate you instead.
7:16 You must devour all the peoples the LORD your God hands over to you. Do not look on them with pity, and do not serve their gods, because that would be a trap for you.
## Do not fear them
7:17 "You may say to yourself, 'These nations are bigger than I am. How can I drive them out?'
7:18 Do not be afraid of them. Remember well what the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt:
7:19 the great trials your eyes saw, the signs and wonders, the strong hand and the outstretched arm by which the LORD your God brought you out. The LORD your God will do the same to all the peoples you are afraid of.
7:20 The LORD your God will even send the hornet among them, until the ones who are left and the ones hiding from you are gone.
7:21 Do not tremble before them, because the LORD your God is among you, a great and fearsome God.
7:22 The LORD your God will clear away these nations ahead of you bit by bit. You will not be able to finish them off all at once, or the wild animals would grow too many for you.
7:23 But the LORD your God will hand them over to you and throw them into great panic until they are destroyed.
7:24 He will hand their kings over to you, and you will wipe out their names from under the sky. No one will be able to stand against you, until you have destroyed them.
7:25 Burn the idols of their gods in the fire. Do not crave the silver or the gold on them, and do not take it for yourself, or it will be a trap for you. For it is abhorrent to the LORD your God.
7:26 Do not bring anything abhorrent into your house, or you will be put under the ban as it is. Hate it and loathe it completely, because it is under the ban.
## Not by bread alone
8:1 "Be careful to do every command I am giving you today, so that you may live and grow many, and go in and take possession of the land the LORD swore to your ancestors.
8:2 Remember the whole road the LORD your God has led you on these forty years in the wilderness. He did it to humble you and to test you, to know what was in your heart, whether you would keep his commands or not.
8:3 He humbled you and let you go hungry. Then he fed you with the manna, which you had never known, and your ancestors had never known either. He did it to teach you that people do not live by bread alone. People live by everything that comes out of the LORD's mouth.
8:4 Your clothes did not wear out on you, and your feet did not swell, these forty years.
8:5 So know in your heart that the LORD your God trains you the way a man trains his son.
8:6 Keep the commands of the LORD your God. Walk in his ways and fear him.
## A good land
8:7 "For the LORD your God is bringing you into a good land. It is a land of streams of water, of springs and deep waters that flow out in the valleys and in the hills,
8:8 a land of wheat and barley, of vines, figs and pomegranates, a land of olive oil and honey.
8:9 It is a land where you will eat bread without running short, where you will lack nothing. Its stones are iron, and you can dig copper out of its hills.
8:10 When you have eaten and are full, bless the LORD your God for the good land he has given you.
## When your heart grows proud
8:11 "Be careful not to forget the LORD your God by failing to keep his commands, his rulings and his rules that I am giving you today.
8:12 You will eat and be full. You will build fine houses and live in them.
8:13 Your herds and flocks will grow large, your silver and gold will pile up, and all you have will grow.
8:14 Then do not let your heart grow proud, so that you forget the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that house of slaves.
8:15 He led you through the huge and fearsome wilderness, with its burning snakes and scorpions, a thirsty land with no water. He brought water out of the flint rock for you.
8:16 He fed you in the wilderness with manna, which your ancestors had not known. He did it to humble you and to test you, so as to do you good in the end.
8:17 You may say to yourself, 'My own strength and the power of my hand have won me this wealth.'
8:18 But remember the LORD your God. He is the one who gives you the strength to win wealth, in order to keep his covenant that he swore to your ancestors, as he is doing today.
8:19 If you do forget the LORD your God and follow other gods, and serve them and bow down to them, I testify against you today that you will surely die out.
8:20 Like the nations the LORD is destroying ahead of you, so you will die out, because you would not listen to the voice of the LORD your God.
## Not because you are good
9:1 "Listen, Israel. Today you are about to cross the Jordan, to go in and drive out nations bigger and stronger than you, with large cities walled up to the sky.
9:2 They are a big and tall people, the Anakim. You know about them. You have heard it said, 'Who can stand up to the sons of Anak?'
9:3 So know today that the LORD your God is the one crossing over ahead of you, a devouring fire. He will destroy them. He will bring them down in front of you. Then you will drive them out and wipe them out quickly, as the LORD has told you.
9:4 When the LORD your God has pushed them out ahead of you, do not say to yourself, 'The LORD brought me in to take possession of this land because I am righteous.' No, it is because these nations are wicked that the LORD is driving them out ahead of you.
9:5 It is not because you are righteous or your heart is upright that you are going in to take possession of their land. It is because these nations are wicked that the LORD your God is driving them out ahead of you. He is also doing it to keep the word the LORD swore to your ancestors, to Abraham, to Isaac and to Jacob.
9:6 So know this: it is not because you are righteous that the LORD your God is giving you this good land to take possession of. You are a stiff-necked people.
## The calf at Horeb
9:7 "Remember, and do not forget, how you made the LORD your God angry in the wilderness. From the day you came out of the land of Egypt until you came to this place, you have kept on rebelling against the LORD.
9:8 Even at Horeb you made the LORD angry. The LORD was so angry with you that he was ready to destroy you.
9:9 I had gone up the mountain to receive the stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant the LORD had made with you. I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights. Bread I did not eat, and water I did not drink.
9:10 The LORD gave me the two stone tablets, written by the finger of God. On them were all the words the LORD had spoken with you on the mountain, out of the fire, on the day of the assembly.
9:11 At the end of the forty days and forty nights, the LORD gave me the two stone tablets, the tablets of the covenant.
9:12 Then the LORD said to me, 'Get up and go down from here quickly. Your people whom you brought out of Egypt have ruined themselves. They have been quick to turn from the way I commanded them. They have made themselves a cast idol.'
9:13 The LORD also said to me, 'I have seen this people. Look, they are a stiff-necked people.
9:14 Let go of me, so I can destroy them and wipe out their name from under the sky. And I will make you into a nation stronger and bigger than they are.'
9:15 "So I turned and went down from the mountain, while the mountain was burning with fire. The two tablets of the covenant were in my two hands.
9:16 I looked, and there it was: you had sinned against the LORD your God. You had made yourselves a calf cast from metal. You had been quick to turn from the way the LORD had commanded you.
9:17 So I took hold of the two tablets and threw them down from my two hands. I smashed them in front of your eyes.
9:18 Then I threw myself down before the LORD, as I had the first time, for forty days and forty nights. Bread I did not eat, and water I did not drink, because of all the sin you had committed by doing what was evil in the LORD's eyes and provoking him to anger.
9:19 For I was afraid of the anger and the rage the LORD had against you, enough to destroy you. But the LORD listened to me that time also.
9:20 The LORD was very angry with Aaron too, ready to destroy him. So at that time I prayed for Aaron as well.
9:21 Then I took the sinful thing you had made, the calf, and burned it in the fire. I crushed it, grinding it well until it was as fine as dust. And I threw its dust into the stream that ran down from the mountain.
## Rebels from the first day
9:22 "At Taberah, at Massah and at Kibroth-hattaavah you also made the LORD angry.
9:23 And when the LORD sent you from Kadesh-barnea and said, 'Go up and take possession of the land I have given you,' you rebelled against the command of the LORD your God. You did not trust him, and you did not listen to his voice.
9:24 You have been rebels against the LORD from the day I first knew you.
## Moses pleads for Israel
9:25 "So I lay face down before the LORD those forty days and forty nights that I lay there, because the LORD had said he would destroy you.
9:26 I prayed to the LORD and said, 'Lord GOD, do not destroy your people, your inheritance, whom you ransomed by your greatness and brought out of Egypt with a strong hand.
9:27 Remember your servants, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Do not look at the stubbornness of this people, or at their wickedness or their sin.
9:28 Otherwise the people of the land you brought us out of will say, "The LORD was not able to bring them into the land he promised them. And because he hated them, he brought them out to kill them in the wilderness."
9:29 But they are your people, your inheritance, whom you brought out by your great power and your outstretched arm.'
## New tablets and an ark
10:1 "At that time the LORD said to me, 'Cut two stone tablets like the first ones, and come up to me on the mountain. Also make yourself a wooden ark.
10:2 I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you smashed. Then put them in the ark.'
10:3 So I made an ark of acacia wood and cut two stone tablets like the first ones. Then I went up the mountain with the two tablets in my hand.
10:4 He wrote on the tablets, in the same writing as before, the Ten Words the LORD had spoken to you on the mountain, out of the fire, on the day of the assembly. And the LORD gave them to me.
10:5 Then I turned and came down from the mountain. I put the tablets in the ark I had made, and they are still there, as the LORD commanded me.
## Aaron's death and Levi's share
10:6 (The Israelites set out from Beeroth Bene-jaakan to Moserah. There Aaron died, and there he was buried. His son Eleazar served as priest in his place.
10:7 From there they set out to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a land of streams of water.
10:8 At that time the LORD set apart the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the LORD's covenant, to stand before the LORD to serve him, and to bless in his name, as they still do today.
10:9 That is why Levi has no portion or inheritance with his brothers. The LORD is his inheritance, as the LORD your God told him.)
10:10 "I stayed on the mountain forty days and forty nights, as I had the first time. And the LORD listened to me that time also. The LORD was not willing to destroy you.
10:11 The LORD said to me, 'Get up and go on your journey at the head of the people. Let them go in and take possession of the land I swore to their ancestors to give them.'
## What does the LORD ask?
10:12 "And now, Israel, what is the LORD your God asking from you? Only this: to fear the LORD your God, to walk in all his ways, to love him, and to serve the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your being,
10:13 and to keep the LORD's commands and his rules that I am giving you today, for your own good.
10:14 Look, the sky belongs to the LORD your God, even the highest heavens, the earth and everything in it.
10:15 Yet the LORD set his heart on your ancestors and loved them. He chose their descendants after them, you, out of all the peoples, as you see today.
10:16 So circumcise the foreskin of your heart, and do not harden your neck any longer.
10:17 For the LORD your God is the God of gods and the Lord of lords, the great, mighty and fearsome God. He does not play favorites, and he does not take a bribe.
10:18 He sees that the orphan and the widow get justice. He loves the foreigner and gives him food and clothing.
10:19 So you are to love the foreigner, for you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.
10:20 It is the LORD your God you must fear and serve. Hold on to him, and swear your oaths by his name.
10:21 He is your praise, and he is your God, who did for you these great and fearsome things that your own eyes have seen.
10:22 Your ancestors went down to Egypt seventy persons in all. And now the LORD your God has made you as many as the stars of the sky.
## What your own eyes saw
11:1 "So love the LORD your God, and keep what he requires, his rules, his rulings and his commands, every day.
11:2 Know today that I am not talking to your children, who have not known or seen the training of the LORD your God. I am talking to you. You saw his greatness, his strong hand and his outstretched arm,
11:3 his signs and the deeds he did in the middle of Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt and to his whole land.
11:4 You saw what he did to Egypt's army, its horses and its chariots: how he made the water of the Sea of Reeds flow over them as they chased you, and how the LORD destroyed them, so that they are gone to this day.
11:5 You saw what he did for you in the wilderness until you came to this place,
11:6 and what he did to Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, son of Reuben. The earth opened its mouth in the middle of all Israel and swallowed them up, with their households, their tents and every living thing that followed them.
11:7 Your own eyes have seen every great thing the LORD has done.
## A land that drinks the rain
11:8 "So keep the whole command I am giving you today. Then you will be strong enough to go in and take possession of the land you are crossing over to take possession of,
11:9 and you will live long on the land the LORD swore to give to your ancestors and to their descendants, a land flowing with milk and honey.
11:10 For the land you are entering to take possession of is not like the land of Egypt that you came out of. There you planted your seed and watered it with your foot, like a vegetable garden.
11:11 But the land you are crossing over to take possession of is a land of hills and valleys. It drinks water from the rain of the sky.
11:12 It is a land the LORD your God looks after. The eyes of the LORD your God are always on it, from the start of the year to the end of the year.
## If you listen
11:13 "If you truly listen to my commands that I am giving you today, to love the LORD your God and to serve him with all your heart and with all your being,
11:14 then I will give your land its rain at the right time, the early rain and the late rain. And you will gather in your grain, your new wine and your olive oil.
11:15 I will give grass in your fields for your animals, and you will eat and be full.
11:16 Watch yourselves, or your heart will be fooled. You will turn away and serve other gods and bow down to them.
11:17 Then the LORD's anger will blaze against you. He will shut up the sky, and there will be no rain. The ground will not give its crops, and you will quickly die out from the good land the LORD is giving you.
11:18 So put these words of mine on your heart and in your being. Tie them as a sign on your hand, and let them be a band between your eyes.
11:19 Teach them to your children, talking about them when you are sitting in your house and when you are out on the road, when you go to bed and when you get up.
11:20 Write them on the doorposts of your house and on your city gates.
11:21 Then your days and your children's days will be many on the land the LORD swore to your ancestors to give them, as many as the days the sky is above the earth.
## Every place your foot treads
11:22 "If you are careful to keep this whole command I am giving you to do, to love the LORD your God, to walk in all his ways and to hold on to him,
11:23 then the LORD will drive out all these nations ahead of you. You will drive out nations bigger and stronger than you.
11:24 Every place the sole of your foot steps on will be yours. Your border will run from the wilderness to Lebanon, and from the River, the Euphrates River, to the western sea.
11:25 No one will be able to stand against you. The LORD your God will put the dread and fear of you on the whole land you step on, as he promised you.
## A blessing and a curse
11:26 "Look: I am putting a blessing and a curse in front of you today,
11:27 the blessing if you listen to the commands of the LORD your God that I am giving you today,
11:28 and the curse if you do not listen to the commands of the LORD your God, but turn from the way I am commanding you today to follow other gods you have not known.
11:29 When the LORD your God brings you into the land you are entering to take possession of, you are to set the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.
11:30 Are they not on the other side of the Jordan, beyond the road to the west, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the Arabah, across from Gilgal, beside the great trees of Moreh?
11:31 For you are about to cross the Jordan to go in and take possession of the land the LORD your God is giving you. When you have taken possession of it and are living in it,
11:32 be careful to do all the rules and the rulings I am setting before you today.
