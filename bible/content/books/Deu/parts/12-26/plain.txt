## One place of worship
12:1 These are the rules and the rulings you must be careful to carry out in the land. The LORD, the God of your ancestors, has given it to you to take possession of. Keep them for as long as you live on its soil.
12:2 Wipe out completely all the places where the nations you are driving out served their gods: on the high mountains, on the hills, and under every leafy tree.
12:3 Break down their altars. Shatter their standing stones. Burn their Asherah poles with fire. Cut down the carved images of their gods, and wipe out their name from that place.
12:4 Do not do the same for the LORD your God.
12:5 Instead, seek out the place the LORD your God will choose out of all your tribes to put his name there as its dwelling. That is where you are to go.
12:6 Bring there your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the gifts you set aside, your vow offerings and your freewill offerings, and the firstborn of your herds and your flocks.
12:7 There you and your households will eat before the LORD your God. You will rejoice in everything you have put your hand to, because the LORD your God has blessed you.
12:8 Do not do all that we are doing here today, each of us doing whatever is right in his own eyes.
12:9 For up to now you have not come to the resting place and the inheritance the LORD your God is giving you.
12:10 But you will cross the Jordan and live in the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance. He will give you rest from all your enemies around you, and you will live in safety.
12:11 Then the LORD your God will choose a place to make his name live there. That is where you are to bring everything I am commanding you. Bring your burnt offerings and your sacrifices, your tithes and the gifts you set aside, and all the finest of the vow offerings you vow to the LORD.
12:12 And you will rejoice before the LORD your God, you and your sons and your daughters, your male and female slaves, and the Levite who lives in your towns, for he has no share or inheritance with you.
12:13 Be careful not to offer up your burnt offerings in any place you happen to see.
12:14 Offer them only in the place the LORD will choose in one of your tribes. There you will offer up your burnt offerings, and there you will do everything I am commanding you.
## Meat in your own towns
12:15 But you may slaughter and eat meat in any of your towns, as much as you like, as the LORD your God has blessed you. The unclean and the clean may both eat it, as they would a gazelle or a deer.
12:16 Only do not eat the blood. Pour it out on the ground like water.
12:17 In your towns you may not eat the tithe of your grain, your new wine or your olive oil. Do not eat the firstborn of your herds or your flocks there, or any of the vow offerings you vow, your freewill offerings, or the gifts you set aside.
12:18 Instead, eat them before the LORD your God, in the place the LORD your God will choose: you, your son and your daughter, your male and female slaves, and the Levite who lives in your towns. Rejoice before the LORD your God in everything you have put your hand to.
12:19 Be careful not to abandon the Levite as long as you live on your land.
12:20 The LORD your God will enlarge your territory, as he promised you. Then you may say, "I want to eat meat," because you long to eat meat. You may eat as much meat as you like.
12:21 The place the LORD your God will choose to put his name may be too far from you. If so, you may slaughter animals from the herd and the flock the LORD has given you, as I have commanded you. You may eat them in your towns, as much as you like.
12:22 Eat it just as gazelle and deer are eaten. The unclean and the clean alike may eat it.
12:23 Only hold firm and do not eat the blood, for the blood is the life. Do not eat the life with the meat.
12:24 Do not eat it. Pour it out on the ground like water.
12:25 Do not eat it, so that it will go well with you and with your children after you, because you will be doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD.
12:26 But the holy things that are yours, and your vow offerings, you are to pick up and take to the place the LORD will choose.
12:27 Offer your burnt offerings there, the meat and the blood, on the altar of the LORD your God. The blood of your other sacrifices is to be poured out on the altar of the LORD your God, but the meat you may eat.
12:28 Be careful to listen to all these words I am commanding you. Then it will go well with you and with your children after you for all time, because you will be doing what is good and right in the eyes of the LORD your God.
## Do not copy the nations
12:29 The LORD your God will cut off ahead of you the nations you are going in to drive out. When you have driven them out and are living in their land,
12:30 be careful that you are not trapped into following them after they have been destroyed ahead of you. Do not ask about their gods, saying, "How did these nations serve their gods? I will do the same."
12:31 Do not do the same for the LORD your God. For they have done for their gods every abhorrent thing the LORD hates. They even burn their sons and their daughters in the fire for their gods.
12:32 Be careful to do everything I am commanding you. Do not add to it, and do not take away from it.
## A prophet who says "other gods"
13:1 Suppose a prophet or someone who has dreams rises up among you and gives you a sign or a wonder,
13:2 and the sign or wonder he told you about comes true, and he says, "Let us follow other gods," gods you have not known, "and let us serve them."
13:3 Do not listen to the words of that prophet or that dreamer of dreams. For the LORD your God is testing you, to find out whether you love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your being.
13:4 It is the LORD your God you are to follow. Fear him. Keep his commands, listen to his voice, serve him and hold on to him.
13:5 That prophet or that dreamer of dreams must be put to death. He has urged rebellion against the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and ransomed you from the house of slavery. He tried to push you off the road the LORD your God commanded you to walk in. So you will sweep the evil out from among you.
## A relative who says "other gods"
13:6 Your brother, your own mother's son, may try to lure you in secret. Or it may be your son or your daughter, or the wife you hold close, or the friend who is like your own self. He may say, "Let us go and serve other gods," gods that neither you nor your ancestors have known,
13:7 some of the gods of the peoples around you, near you or far from you, from one end of the earth to the other.
13:8 Do not give in to him, and do not listen to him. Do not look on him with pity, do not spare him, and do not cover for him.
13:9 Instead, you must kill him. Your hand is to be the first against him to put him to death, and after that the hands of all the people.
13:10 Stone him with stones until he dies, for he tried to push you away from the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.
13:11 Then all Israel will hear and be afraid, and no one among you will ever again do an evil thing like this.
## A town that says "other gods"
13:12 Suppose you hear it said about one of the towns the LORD your God is giving you to live in,
13:13 "Some worthless men have gone out from among you and have led the people of their town astray, saying, 'Let us go and serve other gods,'" gods you have not known.
13:14 Then you must look into it, search it out, and ask questions with care. If it is true and the matter is certain, if this abhorrent thing has really been done among you,
13:15 you must strike down the people of that town with the sword. Put it under the ban, the town and everything in it, and its livestock too, with the sword.
13:16 Gather all its plunder into the middle of its town square, and burn the town and all its plunder with fire, as a whole offering to the LORD your God. It will be a ruin-mound for all time. It must never be built again.
13:17 None of what is under the ban is to stick to your hand, so that the LORD will turn from his burning anger. He will show you compassion, have compassion on you and make you many, as he swore to your ancestors,
13:18 because you will be listening to the voice of the LORD your God, keeping all his commands that I am commanding you today, and doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD your God.
## Children of the LORD
14:1 You are children of the LORD your God. Do not cut yourselves, and do not shave a bald spot between your eyes for someone who has died.
14:2 For you are a people holy to the LORD your God. The LORD has chosen you to be a people that is his own private treasure, out of all the peoples on the face of the earth.
## Clean and unclean food
14:3 Do not eat anything abhorrent.
14:4 These are the animals you may eat: the ox, the sheep and the goat,
14:5 the deer, the gazelle, the roe deer, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope and the wild sheep.
14:6 You may eat any animal that has a split hoof, divided all the way through into two, and that chews the cud.
14:7 But of those that chew the cud or that have a split hoof divided through, do not eat these: the camel, the hare and the rock hyrax. They chew the cud, but they do not have a split hoof. They are unclean for you.
14:8 And the pig has a split hoof but does not chew the cud. It is unclean for you. Do not eat their meat, and do not touch their dead bodies.
14:9 Of all the creatures in the water, you may eat these: anything that has fins and scales you may eat.
14:10 But anything that does not have fins and scales you must not eat. It is unclean for you.
14:11 You may eat any clean bird.
14:12 But these are the ones you must not eat: the eagle, the bearded vulture, the black vulture,
14:13 the buzzard, the falcon, any kind of kite,
14:14 any kind of raven,
14:15 the ostrich, the nighthawk, the gull, any kind of hawk,
14:16 the little owl, the great owl, the barn owl,
14:17 the desert owl, the carrion vulture, the cormorant,
14:18 the stork, any kind of heron, the hoopoe and the bat.
14:19 All winged swarming things are unclean for you. They must not be eaten.
14:20 Any clean winged creature you may eat.
14:21 Do not eat anything that has died on its own. You may give it to the foreigner who lives in your towns, and he may eat it, or you may sell it to a foreigner. For you are a people holy to the LORD your God. Do not boil a young goat in the milk of its mother.
## The tithe
14:22 Year after year you must set aside a tenth of all the crops your seed produces, all that comes up from the field.
14:23 Eat it before the LORD your God, in the place he will choose to make his name live there. Eat the tithe of your grain, your new wine and your olive oil, and the firstborn of your herds and your flocks. Do this so that you will learn to fear the LORD your God always.
14:24 But the LORD your God may bless you so much that the journey is too long for you. You may not be able to carry it all, because the place the LORD your God will choose to put his name is too far from you.
14:25 Then turn it into silver. Tie up the silver and take it in your hand, and go to the place the LORD your God will choose.
14:26 Spend the silver on anything you want: cattle, sheep, wine, beer, or anything else you would like. Eat there before the LORD your God and rejoice, you and your household.
14:27 And do not abandon the Levite who lives in your towns, for he has no share or inheritance with you.
14:28 When each three years come to an end, bring out the full tithe of your crops from that year and lay it up in your towns.
14:29 Then the Levite, since he has no share or inheritance with you, and the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow who live in your towns can come and eat and be satisfied. And the LORD your God will bless you in all the work you do with your hands.
## The year of release
15:1 At the end of every seven years, you are to make a release.
15:2 This is how the release works. Every lender is to let go of the loan he made to his neighbor. He must not press his neighbor, his brother, for payment, because a release for the LORD has been proclaimed.
15:3 You may press a foreigner for payment. But whatever your brother owes you, your hand must let go.
15:4 Yet there will be no one in need among you. For the LORD will richly bless you in the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance to take possession of,
15:5 if only you truly listen to the voice of the LORD your God and are careful to carry out this whole command that I am commanding you today.
15:6 For the LORD your God will bless you, as he promised you. You will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow. You will rule over many nations, but they will not rule over you.
15:7 Someone among you may be in need, one of your brothers, in any of your towns in the land the LORD your God is giving you. If so, do not harden your heart or close your hand against your brother in need.
15:8 Instead, open your hand wide to him, and lend him enough for whatever he lacks.
15:9 Be careful that no worthless thought rises in your heart, saying, "The seventh year, the year of release, is near," so that you turn cold toward your needy brother and give him nothing. He will cry out to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin.
15:10 Be sure to give to him, and do not let your heart be sour when you give to him. Because of this the LORD your God will bless you in all your work and in everything you put your hand to.
15:11 For there will never stop being people in need in the land. That is why I am commanding you: Open your hand wide to your brother, to the poor and the needy among you in your land.
## Setting slaves free
15:12 Suppose your brother, a Hebrew man or a Hebrew woman, sells himself to you. He is to serve you six years, and in the seventh year you are to release him as a free person.
15:13 When you release him as a free person, do not send him away with empty hands.
15:14 Load him up generously from your flock, your threshing floor and your winepress. Give to him as the LORD your God has blessed you.
15:15 Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and the LORD your God ransomed you. That is why I am commanding you this today.
15:16 But suppose he says to you, "I will not leave you," because he loves you and your household, since life with you is good for him.
15:17 Then take an awl and put it through his ear into the door, and he will be your slave for life. Do the same for your slave woman too.
15:18 Do not take it hard when you release him as a free person. For six years he has served you, and that service was worth twice the pay of a hired worker. And the LORD your God will bless you in everything you do.
## Firstborn animals
15:19 Set apart for the LORD your God every firstborn male born in your herd and your flock. Do not put the firstborn of your oxen to work, and do not shear the firstborn of your sheep.
15:20 Year after year you and your household are to eat it before the LORD your God, in the place the LORD will choose.
15:21 But if it has a defect, if it is lame or blind or has any serious defect, do not sacrifice it to the LORD your God.
15:22 Eat it in your towns. The unclean and the clean alike may eat it, as they would a gazelle or a deer.
15:23 Only do not eat its blood. Pour it out on the ground like water.
## Passover
16:1 Keep the month of Abib, and hold the Passover for the LORD your God, for in the month of Abib the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt by night.
16:2 Sacrifice the Passover to the LORD your God from the flock and the herd, in the place the LORD will choose to make his name live there.
16:3 Do not eat anything leavened with it. For seven days eat unleavened bread with it, the bread of hardship, because you came out of the land of Egypt in a rush. Do this so that all the days of your life you will remember the day you came out of the land of Egypt.
16:4 No leaven is to be seen with you anywhere in your territory for seven days. And none of the meat you sacrifice in the evening of the first day may be left overnight until morning.
16:5 You may not sacrifice the Passover in just any of the towns the LORD your God is giving you.
16:6 Sacrifice the Passover only at the place the LORD your God will choose to make his name live there. Do it in the evening, as the sun goes down, at the time of day you came out of Egypt.
16:7 Cook it and eat it in the place the LORD your God will choose. Then in the morning turn and go back to your tents.
16:8 For six days eat unleavened bread. On the seventh day hold a closing assembly for the LORD your God. Do no work.
## Weeks and Booths
16:9 Count seven weeks for yourself. Begin to count the seven weeks from the time the sickle is first put to the standing grain.
16:10 Then hold the Feast of Weeks for the LORD your God, with a freewill offering from your hand, as much as you can give, as the LORD your God blesses you.
16:11 Rejoice before the LORD your God, you, your son and your daughter, your male and female slaves, the Levite who lives in your towns, and the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow who are among you. Rejoice in the place the LORD your God will choose to make his name live there.
16:12 Remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and be careful to carry out these rules.
16:13 Hold the Feast of Booths for seven days, when you gather in the harvest from your threshing floor and your winepress.
16:14 Rejoice at your feast, you, your son and your daughter, your male and female slaves, the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow who live in your towns.
16:15 For seven days hold the feast for the LORD your God in the place the LORD will choose. For the LORD your God will bless you in all your crops and in all the work of your hands, and you will be nothing but glad.
16:16 Three times a year all your males are to appear before the LORD your God in the place he will choose: at the Feast of Unleavened Bread, at the Feast of Weeks and at the Feast of Booths. No one is to appear before the LORD with empty hands.
16:17 Each of you is to bring what his hand can give, as the LORD your God has blessed you.
## Judges and justice
16:18 Appoint judges and officials for yourself in all the towns the LORD your God is giving you, tribe by tribe. They are to judge the people with right judgment.
16:19 Do not bend justice. Do not play favorites. Do not take a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and turns the words of the righteous upside down.
16:20 Justice, justice is what you must pursue, so that you may live and take possession of the land the LORD your God is giving you.
## Forbidden worship
16:21 Do not plant any kind of tree as an Asherah pole beside the altar of the LORD your God that you will make.
16:22 And do not set up a standing stone, which the LORD your God hates.
17:1 Do not sacrifice to the LORD your God an ox or a sheep that has a defect, anything wrong with it at all, for that is abhorrent to the LORD your God.
17:2 Suppose there is found among you a man or a woman who does what is evil in the eyes of the LORD your God by breaking his covenant. It may be in one of the towns the LORD your God is giving you.
17:3 He has gone and served other gods and bowed down to them, or to the sun or the moon or any of the army of the heavens, which I did not command.
17:4 You are told about it and hear of it. Then look into it with care. If it is true and the matter is certain, if this abhorrent thing has been done in Israel,
17:5 bring out to your town gates that man or that woman who did this evil thing. Stone that man or that woman with stones until they die.
17:6 The one who is to die is to be put to death on the word of two witnesses or three witnesses. He must not be put to death on the word of one witness.
17:7 The hands of the witnesses are to be first against him to put him to death, and after that the hands of all the people. So you will sweep the evil out from among you.
## The highest court
17:8 A case may come up in your towns that is too hard for you to judge: one kind of bloodshed or another, one claim or another, one injury or another, matters of dispute. Then get up and go to the place the LORD your God will choose.
17:9 Go to the Levite priests, and to the judge who is in office in those days. Ask them, and they will tell you the verdict.
17:10 Act on the word they tell you from that place the LORD will choose. Be careful to do everything they teach you.
17:11 Act on the instruction they teach you and the ruling they give you. Do not turn aside from the word they tell you, to the right or to the left.
17:12 Anyone who acts with arrogance and will not listen to the priest who stands there to serve the LORD your God, or to the judge, that man must die. So you will sweep the evil out of Israel.
17:13 Then all the people will hear and be afraid, and they will not act with arrogance again.
## The king
17:14 You will come into the land the LORD your God is giving you, take possession of it and live in it. Then you may say, "I will set a king over me, like all the nations around me."
17:15 Then you may indeed set a king over you, the one the LORD your God will choose. Set a king over you from among your brothers. You may not put over you a foreigner, someone who is not your brother.
17:16 Only, he must not get himself many horses, or send the people back to Egypt to get more horses, for the LORD has told you, "You must never go back that way again."
17:17 He must not take many wives for himself, or his heart will turn aside. And he must not pile up great amounts of silver and gold for himself.
17:18 When he takes his seat on his royal throne, he is to write for himself a copy of this law on a scroll, taken from the one in the care of the Levite priests.
17:19 It is to stay with him, and he is to read it all the days of his life. So he will learn to fear the LORD his God, keeping all the words of this law and these rules and doing them.
17:20 Then his heart will not be lifted up above his brothers, and he will not turn aside from the command, to the right or to the left. So he and his sons will reign long over his kingdom in Israel.
## The share of priests and Levites
18:1 The Levite priests, the whole tribe of Levi, are to have no share or inheritance with Israel. They will eat the LORD's offerings by fire, which are his inheritance.
18:2 They will have no inheritance among their brothers. The LORD is their inheritance, as he promised them.
18:3 This is what the priests have a right to from the people, from those who offer a sacrifice, whether an ox or a sheep: they are to give the priest the foreleg, the two cheeks and the stomach.
18:4 Give him the first of your grain, your new wine and your olive oil, and the first of the wool sheared from your sheep.
18:5 For the LORD your God has chosen him out of all your tribes to stand and serve in the name of the LORD, him and his sons for all time.
18:6 Suppose a Levite comes from one of your towns anywhere in Israel where he has been living. If he comes with all his heart's desire to the place the LORD will choose,
18:7 he may serve in the name of the LORD his God, like all his fellow Levites who stand there before the LORD.
18:8 They are to eat equal shares, besides what he gets from selling what came to him from his fathers.
## The ways of the nations
18:9 When you come into the land the LORD your God is giving you, do not learn to do the abhorrent things those nations do.
18:10 There must not be found among you anyone who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, anyone who practices divination, reads signs, looks for omens or works sorcery,
18:11 or anyone who casts spells, or asks ghosts of the dead or spirits that know things, or seeks out the dead.
18:12 For everyone who does these things is abhorrent to the LORD. Because of these abhorrent things the LORD your God is driving them out ahead of you.
18:13 Be whole with the LORD your God.
18:14 These nations you are about to drive out listen to those who read signs and to those who practice divination. But as for you, the LORD your God has not let you do this.
## A prophet like Moses
18:15 The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among you, from your brothers. You must listen to him.
18:16 This is just what you asked of the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly. You said, "Do not let me hear the voice of the LORD my God again, and do not let me see this great fire anymore, or I will die."
18:17 And the LORD said to me, "What they have said is good.
18:18 I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brothers. I will put my words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command him.
18:19 Anyone who does not listen to my words, which he will speak in my name, I myself will call to account.
18:20 But the prophet who dares to speak a word in my name that I did not command him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet must die."
18:21 You may say in your heart, "How will we know the word the LORD has not spoken?"
18:22 When the prophet speaks in the name of the LORD and the thing does not happen or come true, that is a word the LORD has not spoken. The prophet has spoken it with arrogance. Do not be afraid of him.
## Cities of refuge
19:1 When the LORD your God cuts off the nations whose land the LORD your God is giving you, and you drive them out and live in their towns and their houses,
19:2 set apart for yourself three cities in the middle of the land the LORD your God is giving you to take possession of.
19:3 Prepare the road, and divide into three parts the territory of the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, so that any killer can run there.
19:4 This is the case of the killer who may run there and stay alive: someone who strikes his neighbor without meaning to, when he had not hated him in the past.
19:5 Say a man goes into the forest with his neighbor to cut wood. His hand swings the axe to cut down a tree, the iron head flies off the handle and hits his neighbor, and he dies. That man may run to one of these cities and stay alive.
19:6 Otherwise the avenger of blood may chase the killer while his anger is hot and catch him, because the road is long, and strike him dead. Yet the man did not deserve to die, since he had not hated his neighbor in the past.
19:7 That is why I am commanding you: Set apart for yourself three cities.
19:8 And the LORD your God may enlarge your territory, as he swore to your ancestors, and give you all the land he promised to give your ancestors,
19:9 because you are careful to keep this whole command I am commanding you today, to love the LORD your God and to walk in his ways always. Then add three more cities to these three.
19:10 So innocent blood will not be shed in the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, and blood guilt will not be on you.
19:11 But suppose someone hates his neighbor, lies in wait for him, attacks him and strikes him dead, and then runs to one of these cities.
19:12 The elders of his town are to send and take him from there and hand him over to the avenger of blood, and he will die.
19:13 Do not look on him with pity. Sweep the innocent blood out of Israel, and it will go well with you.
## Boundary markers
19:14 Do not move your neighbor's boundary marker, which the people before you set up in the inheritance you will receive in the land the LORD your God is giving you to take possession of.
## Witnesses
19:15 One witness alone is not to stand up against anyone for any wrong or any sin he may commit. A matter is to stand on the word of two witnesses or on the word of three witnesses.
19:16 If a witness bent on harm stands up against someone to accuse him of wrongdoing,
19:17 the two men who have the dispute are to stand before the LORD, before the priests and the judges who are in office in those days.
19:18 The judges are to look into it with care. If the witness is a false witness who has lied about his brother,
19:19 do to him what he plotted to do to his brother. So you will sweep the evil out from among you.
19:20 The rest will hear and be afraid, and no one will ever again do an evil thing like this among you.
19:21 Do not look on him with pity: life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.
## Going to war
20:1 When you go out to war against your enemies and see horses and chariots, an army bigger than yours, do not be afraid of them. For the LORD your God, who brought you up from the land of Egypt, is with you.
20:2 When you are about to go into battle, the priest is to come forward and speak to the army.
20:3 He is to say to them, "Hear, Israel. Today you are going into battle against your enemies. Do not let your hearts grow faint. Do not be afraid, do not panic, and do not tremble before them.
20:4 For the LORD your God is the one who goes with you, to fight for you against your enemies and to save you."
20:5 Then the officials are to speak to the army and say, "Has anyone built a new house and not yet dedicated it? Let him go back home, or he may die in the battle and another man dedicate it.
20:6 Has anyone planted a vineyard and not yet enjoyed its fruit? Let him go back home, or he may die in the battle and another man enjoy it.
20:7 Has anyone been promised a woman in marriage and not yet taken her as his wife? Let him go back home, or he may die in the battle and another man take her."
20:8 The officials are to go on speaking to the army and say, "Is anyone afraid and fainthearted? Let him go back home, so that his brothers' hearts do not melt like his."
20:9 When the officials have finished speaking to the army, they are to appoint commanders of troops to lead the army.
## Besieging a city
20:10 When you come near a city to fight against it, offer it peace.
20:11 If it answers with peace and opens its gates to you, all the people found in it will become forced laborers for you and serve you.
20:12 But if it will not make peace with you and makes war with you instead, lay siege to it.
20:13 When the LORD your God gives it into your hand, strike down every male in it with the sword.
20:14 But the women, the little children, the livestock and everything else in the city, all its plunder, you may take as spoil for yourself. You may make use of the spoil of your enemies that the LORD your God has given you.
20:15 That is what you are to do to all the cities that are very far from you, which are not cities of these nations here.
20:16 But in the cities of these peoples that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, do not let anything that breathes stay alive.
20:17 Put them under the ban completely: the Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites and the Jebusites, as the LORD your God has commanded you.
20:18 Then they will not teach you to do all the abhorrent things they have done for their gods, and you will not sin against the LORD your God.
## Trees in a siege
20:19 When you besiege a city for a long time, fighting against it to capture it, do not destroy its trees by swinging an axe against them. You may eat from them, but do not cut them down. Is the tree of the field a human being, to come under siege from you?
20:20 Only the trees you know are not fruit trees may you destroy and cut down. Use them to build siege works against the city that is making war with you, until it falls.
## An unsolved killing
21:1 Suppose someone is found killed, lying in the open country, in the land the LORD your God is giving you to take possession of, and no one knows who struck him down.
21:2 Your elders and your judges are to go out and measure the distance to the towns around the dead body.
21:3 The elders of the town nearest the body are to take a young cow from the herd that has never been worked and has never pulled in a yoke.
21:4 The elders of that town are to bring the cow down to a valley with a stream that never runs dry, land that is not plowed or sown. There in the valley they are to break the cow's neck.
21:5 Then the priests, the sons of Levi, are to come forward, for the LORD your God has chosen them to serve him and to bless in the name of the LORD. Every dispute and every case of injury is to be settled by their word.
21:6 All the elders of the town nearest the body are to wash their hands over the cow whose neck was broken in the valley.
21:7 They are to declare, "Our hands did not shed this blood, and our eyes did not see it done.
21:8 LORD, make atonement for your people Israel, whom you ransomed. Do not lay innocent blood on your people Israel." Then the blood will be atoned for on their behalf.
21:9 So you will sweep the innocent blood out from among you, because you will be doing what is right in the eyes of the LORD.
## A woman taken in war
21:10 When you go out to war against your enemies, and the LORD your God gives them into your hand, and you take captives,
21:11 you may see among the captives a beautiful woman, and desire her, and want to take her as your wife.
21:12 Bring her into your house. She is to shave her head and do her nails.
21:13 She is to take off the clothes she wore when captured, and live in your house, and mourn her father and her mother for a full month. After that you may go in to her and be her husband, and she will be your wife.
21:14 If you are not pleased with her later, let her go wherever she wishes. You must not sell her for silver. Do not treat her as a slave, since you have dishonored her.
## The firstborn's share
21:15 Suppose a man has two wives, one loved and the other unloved, and both the loved one and the unloved one bear him sons, and the firstborn son belongs to the unloved wife.
21:16 When he gives his sons what he owns as their inheritance, he may not treat the son of the loved wife as firstborn ahead of the son of the unloved wife, who is the real firstborn.
21:17 He must recognize the son of the unloved wife as firstborn by giving him a double share of everything he has. For he is the first fruit of his manhood. The right of the firstborn belongs to him.
## A rebellious son
21:18 Suppose someone has a stubborn and rebellious son who will not listen to his father or his mother. They discipline him, but he will not listen to them.
21:19 Then his father and his mother are to take hold of him and bring him out to the elders of his town, at the gate of his hometown.
21:20 They are to say to the elders of his town, "This son of ours is stubborn and rebellious. He will not listen to us. He is a glutton and a drunkard."
21:21 Then all the men of his town are to stone him with stones until he dies. So you will sweep the evil out from among you, and all Israel will hear and be afraid.
## A body on a tree
21:22 If someone has committed a sin that deserves death and is put to death, and you hang him on a tree,
21:23 his body must not stay on the tree overnight. Be sure to bury him that same day, for a man who is hanged is cursed by God. Do not make unclean the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.
## Your brother's loss
22:1 If you see your brother's ox or his sheep wandering loose, do not hide yourself from them. Be sure to bring them back to your brother.
22:2 If your brother does not live near you, or you do not know who he is, bring the animal home to your house. It is to stay with you until your brother comes looking for it. Then give it back to him.
22:3 Do the same with his donkey, do the same with his coat, and do the same with anything your brother loses that you find. You may not hide yourself.
22:4 If you see your brother's donkey or his ox fallen down on the road, do not hide yourself from them. Be sure to help him get it back on its feet.
## Keeping things in their kinds
22:5 A woman must not wear a man's gear, and a man must not put on a woman's clothing. For everyone who does these things is abhorrent to the LORD your God.
22:6 Suppose you come across a bird's nest along the road, in any tree or on the ground, with young birds or eggs, and the mother is sitting on the young or on the eggs. Do not take the mother along with the young.
22:7 Be sure to let the mother go, though you may take the young for yourself. Then it will go well with you, and you will live a long time.
22:8 When you build a new house, make a railing around your roof, so that you do not bring blood guilt on your house if someone falls from it.
22:9 Do not sow your vineyard with two kinds of seed, or the whole crop will become holy, both the seed you have sown and the fruit of the vineyard.
22:10 Do not plow with an ox and a donkey together.
22:11 Do not wear cloth woven of mixed threads, wool and linen together.
22:12 Make twisted tassels for yourself on the four corners of the cloak you wrap around yourself.
## A bride accused
22:13 Suppose a man takes a wife and goes in to her, and then hates her.
22:14 He makes up charges against her and gives her a bad name, saying, "I took this woman, but when I came near her, I did not find the signs of her virginity."
22:15 Then the young woman's father and her mother are to take the signs of the young woman's virginity and bring them out to the elders of the town at the gate.
22:16 The young woman's father is to say to the elders, "I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, and he hates her.
22:17 Now look, he has made up charges against her. He says, 'I did not find the signs of virginity in your daughter.' But here are the signs of my daughter's virginity." Then they are to spread out the cloth before the elders of the town.
22:18 The elders of that town are to take the man and punish him.
22:19 They are to fine him a hundred shekels of silver and give it to the young woman's father, because he has given a virgin of Israel a bad name. She is to stay his wife. He may never send her away as long as he lives.
22:20 But if the charge is true, and the signs of virginity were not found in the young woman,
22:21 they are to bring the young woman out to the door of her father's house, and the men of her town are to stone her with stones until she dies. For she has done a disgraceful thing in Israel by acting like a prostitute in her father's house. So you will sweep the evil out from among you.
## Adultery and rape
22:22 If a man is found lying with a woman who is married to a husband, both of them must die, the man who lay with the woman and the woman too. So you will sweep the evil out of Israel.
22:23 Suppose there is a young woman, a virgin promised in marriage to a man, and another man finds her in the town and lies with her.
22:24 Bring both of them out to the gate of that town and stone them with stones until they die. The young woman dies because she did not cry for help in the town, and the man because he dishonored his neighbor's wife. So you will sweep the evil out from among you.
22:25 But the man may find the young woman who is promised in marriage out in the open country, and overpower her and lie with her. Then only the man who lay with her must die.
22:26 Do nothing to the young woman. The young woman has no sin that deserves death. This case is like a man who attacks his neighbor and murders him.
22:27 For he found her in the open country. The young woman who was promised in marriage cried for help, but there was no one to rescue her.
22:28 Suppose a man finds a young woman who is a virgin and not promised in marriage, and he seizes her and lies with her, and they are found out.
22:29 The man who lay with her is to give the young woman's father fifty shekels of silver, and she will become his wife, because he dishonored her. He may never send her away as long as he lives.
22:30 A man must not take his father's wife. He must not lift the wing of his father's cloak.
## Who may enter the assembly
23:1 No man whose testicles are crushed or whose penis is cut off may enter the assembly of the LORD.
23:2 No one born of a forbidden union may enter the assembly of the LORD. Even to the tenth generation, none of his descendants may enter the assembly of the LORD.
23:3 No Ammonite or Moabite may enter the assembly of the LORD. Even to the tenth generation, none of their descendants may ever enter the assembly of the LORD.
23:4 For they did not come to meet you with bread and water on the road when you came out of Egypt. And Moab hired Balaam son of Beor, from Pethor in Aram-naharaim, to curse you.
23:5 But the LORD your God would not listen to Balaam. The LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because the LORD your God loves you.
23:6 Never seek their peace or their good, all your days.
23:7 Do not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother. Do not abhor an Egyptian, for you were a foreigner in his land.
23:8 Children born to them in the third generation may enter the assembly of the LORD.
## A clean camp
23:9 When you go out as an army camp against your enemies, keep yourself from anything bad.
23:10 If there is a man among you who is unclean because of something that happened in the night, he is to go outside the camp. He must not come into the camp.
23:11 As evening comes on, he is to wash with water. When the sun goes down, he may come back into the camp.
23:12 Have a place outside the camp, and go out there to relieve yourself.
23:13 Have a digging stick among your gear. When you squat outside, dig a hole with it, then turn and cover up your waste.
23:14 For the LORD your God walks about in the middle of your camp to rescue you and to hand your enemies over to you. So your camp must be holy. He must not see something indecent among you and turn away from you.
## Runaway slaves
23:15 Do not hand a slave over to his master when he has escaped from his master to you.
23:16 Let him live with you, among you, in the place he chooses in one of your towns, wherever he likes. Do not mistreat him.
## Money that is not holy
23:17 No daughter of Israel is to become a shrine woman, and no son of Israel a shrine man.
23:18 Do not bring a prostitute's fee or the price of a dog into the house of the LORD your God to pay any vow, for both of them are abhorrent to the LORD your God.
## Interest, vows and a neighbor's crops
23:19 Do not charge your brother interest: interest on silver, interest on food, interest on anything that can be lent at interest.
23:20 You may charge a foreigner interest, but you must not charge your brother interest. Then the LORD your God will bless you in everything you put your hand to in the land you are going into to take possession of.
23:21 When you make a vow to the LORD your God, do not be slow to pay it, for the LORD your God will surely ask it of you, and you will be guilty of sin.
23:22 But if you hold back from making a vow, you will not be guilty of sin.
23:23 Be careful to do what your lips have said, as you freely vowed to the LORD your God, what you promised with your own mouth.
23:24 When you go into your neighbor's vineyard, you may eat as many grapes as you want, until you are full. But do not put any in your basket.
23:25 When you go into your neighbor's standing grain, you may pick the heads of grain with your hand. But do not swing a sickle at your neighbor's standing grain.
## A divorced wife
24:1 Suppose a man takes a woman and marries her, and she does not find favor in his eyes, because he has found something indecent in her. He writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand, and sends her away from his house.
24:2 She leaves his house and goes and becomes another man's wife.
24:3 Then the second man hates her, writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand, and sends her away from his house. Or else the second man, who took her as his wife, dies.
24:4 In that case her first husband, who sent her away, may not take her back to be his wife after she has been made unclean. For that is abhorrent before the LORD. Do not bring sin on the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.
## Protecting the weak
24:5 When a man has newly married a wife, he is not to go out with the army, and no duty is to be laid on him. He is to be free for his household for one year, to bring joy to the wife he has married.
24:6 No one may take a pair of millstones, or even the upper millstone, as a pledge for a loan, for that would be taking a life as a pledge.
24:7 If someone is found stealing a person, one of his brothers, one of the Israelites, and treats him as a slave or sells him, that thief must die. So you will sweep the evil out from among you.
24:8 In a case of skin disease, be very careful to do everything the Levite priests teach you. Be careful to do what I commanded them.
24:9 Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam on the road when you came out of Egypt.
24:10 When you make your neighbor a loan of any kind, do not go into his house to take his pledge.
24:11 Stand outside, and the man you are lending to will bring the pledge out to you.
24:12 If he is a poor man, do not go to sleep with his pledge.
24:13 Be sure to give the pledge back to him at sundown, so that he can sleep in his coat and bless you. It will be righteousness for you before the LORD your God.
24:14 Do not take advantage of a hired worker who is poor and needy, whether he is one of your brothers or one of the foreigners who live in your land, in your towns.
24:15 Pay him his wages each day, before the sun goes down, because he is poor and his heart is set on it. Otherwise he will cry out to the LORD against you, and you will be guilty of sin.
24:16 Fathers must not be put to death because of their children, and children must not be put to death because of their fathers. Each one is to be put to death for his own sin.
24:17 Do not bend the justice due a foreigner or a fatherless child, and do not take a widow's garment as a pledge.
24:18 Remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and the LORD your God ransomed you from there. That is why I am commanding you to do this.
## Leave some for the poor
24:19 When you harvest your crop in your field and forget a sheaf in the field, do not go back to get it. It is to be for the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow, so that the LORD your God will bless you in all the work of your hands.
24:20 When you beat your olive trees, do not go back over the branches after yourself. What is left is to be for the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow.
24:21 When you pick the grapes of your vineyard, do not go back over it after yourself. What is left is to be for the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow.
24:22 Remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt. That is why I am commanding you to do this.
## Limits on punishment
25:1 Suppose there is a dispute between people, and they come to court, and the judges decide between them. They are to declare the one in the right to be in the right, and the guilty one to be guilty.
25:2 If the guilty one deserves a beating, the judge is to make him lie down and have him beaten in his presence, with the number of blows his wrong deserves.
25:3 He may give him forty blows, but no more. If he goes on to give him many more blows than these, your brother will be shamed before your eyes.
25:4 Do not muzzle an ox while it is threshing.
## A brother's widow
25:5 When brothers live together and one of them dies without a son, the dead man's wife must not marry outside the family, to an outsider. Her husband's brother is to go in to her and take her as his wife, and do a brother-in-law's duty to her.
25:6 The firstborn son she bears will carry on the name of his dead brother, so that his name will not be wiped out from Israel.
25:7 But the man may not want to take his brother's wife. Then his brother's wife is to go up to the elders at the gate and say, "My husband's brother refuses to raise up a name for his brother in Israel. He is not willing to do a brother-in-law's duty to me."
25:8 Then the elders of his town are to call him and speak to him. If he stands firm and says, "I do not want to take her,"
25:9 his brother's wife is to come up to him in front of the elders, pull his sandal off his foot, and spit in his face. She is to declare, "This is what is done to the man who will not build up his brother's house."
25:10 And his family will be called in Israel "the house of the man whose sandal was pulled off."
## A woman in a fight
25:11 Suppose two men are fighting each other, and the wife of one of them comes near to rescue her husband from the man striking him. She reaches out her hand and seizes him by his private parts.
25:12 Cut off her hand. Do not look on her with pity.
## Honest weights
25:13 Do not keep two kinds of weights in your bag, a heavy one and a light one.
25:14 Do not keep two kinds of measures in your house, a large one and a small one.
25:15 Keep a whole and right weight, and a whole and right measure, so that you will live long in the land the LORD your God is giving you.
25:16 For everyone who does these things, everyone who does wrong, is abhorrent to the LORD your God.
## Remember Amalek
25:17 Remember what Amalek did to you on the road when you came out of Egypt.
25:18 He met you on the road when you were faint and weary, and he cut down from behind all the stragglers at your rear. He did not fear God.
25:19 The LORD your God will give you rest from all your enemies around you, in the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance to take possession of. When he does, wipe out the memory of Amalek from beneath the sky. Do not forget!
## First fruits
26:1 When you come into the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, and you take possession of it and live in it,
26:2 take some of the first of all the fruit of the ground that you harvest from your land, the land the LORD your God is giving you. Put it in a basket, and go to the place the LORD your God will choose to make his name live there.
26:3 Go to the priest who is in office in those days and say to him, "I declare today to the LORD your God that I have come into the land. It is the land the LORD swore to our ancestors to give us."
26:4 The priest will take the basket from your hand and set it down before the altar of the LORD your God.
26:5 Then you are to speak up and say before the LORD your God, "My father was a wandering Aramean. He went down to Egypt and lived there as a foreigner, few in number. But there he became a nation, great, strong and many.
26:6 The Egyptians treated us badly and pressed us down, and they put hard labor on us.
26:7 We cried out to the LORD, the God of our ancestors. The LORD heard our voice and saw our misery, our hard work and our oppression.
26:8 The LORD brought us out of Egypt with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, with great terror, with signs and with wonders.
26:9 He brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.
26:10 And now, look, I have brought the first of the fruit of the ground that you, LORD, have given me." Then set it down before the LORD your God, and bow down before the LORD your God.
26:11 Rejoice in all the good things the LORD your God has given you and your household, you and the Levite and the foreigner who lives among you.
## The third-year tithe
26:12 In the third year, the year of the tithe, you will finish setting aside the whole tithe of your crops. When you have finished, give it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow, so that they may eat in your towns and be satisfied.
26:13 Then say before the LORD your God, "I have cleared the holy portion out of my house and have given it to the Levite and the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow, just as you commanded me. I have not gone against any of your commands, and I have not forgotten them.
26:14 I have not eaten any of it while I was in mourning. I have not removed any of it while I was unclean. I have not given any of it for the dead. I have listened to the voice of the LORD my God. I have done everything you commanded me.
26:15 Look down from your holy dwelling, from the heavens, and bless your people Israel and the land you have given us, as you swore to our ancestors, a land flowing with milk and honey."
## The LORD's own people
26:16 Today the LORD your God is commanding you to do these rules and rulings. Be careful to do them with all your heart and with all your being.
26:17 Today you have affirmed that the LORD is your God, and that you will walk in his ways, keep his rules, his commands and his rulings, and listen to his voice.
26:18 And today the LORD has affirmed that you are a people that is his own private treasure, as he promised you, and that you are to keep all his commands.
26:19 He will set you high above all the nations he has made, for praise and fame and honor, and you will be a people holy to the LORD your God, as he promised.
