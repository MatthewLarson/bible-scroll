## Greeting
1:1 From Paul, Silvanus and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. Grace to you and peace.
## A Church That Turned to God
1:2 We thank God at all times for all of you, and we mention you when we pray.
1:3 Before our God and Father we remember you without a break. We remember the work your faith does, the toil your love takes on, and the endurance that comes from your hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:4 Brothers whom God loves, we know that you are chosen.
1:5 For the good news we bring did not come to you in words only. It came with power too, and with the Holy Spirit, and with a great certainty. You know what kind of men we were among you, for your good.
1:6 And you became imitators of us, and of the Lord. Though it brought you great trouble, you welcomed the word with the joy the Holy Spirit gives.
1:7 So you became a pattern for every believer in Macedonia and in Achaia.
1:8 From you the word of the Lord has rung out, and not only in Macedonia and Achaia. Your faith toward God has gone out to every place, so we have no need to say a thing.
1:9 People themselves tell the story about us: the kind of welcome we had from you, and how you turned away from idols to God, to serve a God who lives and is true,
1:10 and to wait for his Son to come from the heavens. God raised him from the dead. He is Jesus, who rescues us from the anger that is coming.
## How Paul Lived Among Them
2:1 You know for yourselves, brothers, that our coming among you was not empty.
2:2 Before that, as you know, we had suffered and been treated with insults at Philippi. Even so, relying on our God, we found the courage to speak freely and tell you God's good news, with a great struggle going on.
2:3 When we appeal to you, it does not come from error or from impurity, and there is no trickery in it.
2:4 Instead, God put us to the test, approved us and trusted us with the good news, and that is how we speak. We do not aim to please people. We aim to please God, who puts our hearts to the test.
2:5 You know that we never used flattering words, and never put on a show to hide greed. God is witness to that.
2:6 We did not go looking for praise from people, whether from you or from others, though as Christ's apostles we could have made our weight felt.
2:7 Instead, we became infants among you. Like a nurse who keeps her own children warm and close,
2:8 that is how much we longed for you. We were glad to share with you not only God's good news but our very lives, since you had grown so dear to us.
2:9 Think back, brothers, to our hard work and our hardship. We worked night and day so we would not be a weight on any of you, while we announced God's good news to you.
2:10 You are our witnesses, and God is too: we behaved toward you believers in a holy, righteous and blameless way.
2:11 You know that, like a father with his own children, we dealt with each one of you.
2:12 We urged you, we cheered you on, and we solemnly told you to walk in a way worthy of God, who is calling you into his own kingdom and glory.
## The Word They Received
2:13 That is also why we thank God without a break. When you received God's word, the word you heard from us, you took it to heart not as the word of human beings but, as it truly is, the word of God. And it is at work in you who believe.
2:14 Brothers, you became imitators of God's churches that are in Judea in Christ Jesus. You suffered the same things at the hands of your own countrymen that they suffered at the hands of the Jews,
2:15 who killed the Lord Jesus as well as the prophets, and chased us out. They do not please God, and they set themselves against all people,
2:16 trying to stop us from speaking to the Gentiles so that they may be saved. The result is that they always fill up the measure of their sins. But the anger has reached them in the end.
## Longing to See Them
2:17 But we, brothers, were left like orphans when we were cut off from you for a short while, out of sight but not out of heart. So we tried all the harder, with a strong longing, to see your faces again.
2:18 For we wanted to come to you. I, Paul, wanted it more than once. But Satan got in our way.
2:19 For who is our hope or our joy, or the victor's crown we will be proud of, in front of our Lord Jesus at his coming? Isn't it you, too?
2:20 You, yes you, are our glory and our joy.
## Timothy Sent from Athens
3:1 So, unable to hold out any longer, we judged it good to stay behind in Athens on our own,
3:2 and we sent Timothy, our brother, who works together with God in the good news about Christ. We sent him to make you firm and to encourage you for the sake of your faith,
3:3 so that none of you would be shaken by these troubles. You yourselves know that this is the lot we were given.
3:4 In fact, when we were with you, we kept telling you ahead of time that we were going to be pressed hard. And as you know, that is what happened.
3:5 That is why I too, unable to hold out any longer, sent to learn how your faith was holding. I feared that the tester might have tested you in some way, and our toil would come to nothing.
## Timothy's Good News
3:6 But Timothy has just come back to us from you, with the good news of your faith and your love. He tells us that you keep warm memories of us all the time, and that you long to see us, as we long to see you.
3:7 That is why, brothers, in the middle of all our hardship and trouble, we have been comforted about you, through your faith.
3:8 For now we live, as long as you hold your ground in the Lord.
3:9 What thanks can we give back to God for you, to repay all the joy we have over you in the presence of our God?
3:10 We beg God night and day, as hard as we can, to let us see your faces, and to make up what is still missing in your faith.
## A Prayer for the Church
3:11 Now may our God and Father himself, and our Lord Jesus, open a straight road for us to come to you.
3:12 As for you, may the Lord make your love for one another and for all people grow and spill over, just as ours does for you.
3:13 That way he will make your hearts firm, without blame and holy, in the presence of our God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his holy ones.
## A Life That Pleases God
4:1 Finally, then, brothers, we ask you and appeal to you in the Lord Jesus. You took over from us the teaching about how you must walk and please God, and that is how you are walking already. Keep growing in it even more.
4:2 You know the orders we gave you, through the Lord Jesus.
4:3 God's will is this: your being made holy. That means you keep away from sexual sin.
4:4 Each one of you should know how to gain control of his own vessel in a holy and honorable way,
4:5 not driven by lustful passion, as the Gentiles are, who do not know God.
4:6 In this matter no one should cross the line and cheat his brother, because the Lord takes vengeance for all these things. We told you this before and gave you a strong warning.
4:7 For God did not call us to impurity. He called us to live in holiness.
4:8 So anyone who sets this aside is not setting aside a human being. He is setting aside God, the one who puts his Holy Spirit into you.
## Love and Quiet Work
4:9 Now, about love among brothers: you have no need for us to write to you. God himself has been your teacher in loving one another.
4:10 And you really are doing this for all the brothers in the whole of Macedonia. Still, we appeal to you, brothers, to keep on growing in it.
4:11 Set your ambition on living quietly, on looking after your own affairs, and on working with your hands, just as we instructed you.
4:12 Then you will walk in a decent way before those outside, and you will need no one.
## Those Who Have Fallen Asleep
4:13 We do not want you to be left without knowing, brothers, about those who are falling asleep. Then you will not grieve the way the others do, who have no hope.
4:14 For if we believe that Jesus died and rose, then we believe that God, in the same way, will bring with him those who fell asleep through Jesus.
4:15 Here is what we tell you, on the Lord's word: we who are alive, who remain until the coming of the Lord, will in no way get there before those who have fallen asleep.
4:16 For the Lord himself will come down from heaven with a shouted order, with an archangel's voice and with God's trumpet. Those who are dead in Christ will rise first.
4:17 Next, we who are alive and remain will be caught up along with them in clouds, to meet the Lord in the air. And so we will be with the Lord always.
4:18 So comfort one another with these words.
## The Day of the Lord
5:1 As for the times and the seasons, brothers, there is no need for us to write to you.
5:2 You yourselves know quite well that the day of the Lord is coming the way a thief comes at night.
5:3 Just when people say, "Peace and safety," ruin will fall on them all of a sudden, like birth pains on a woman who is pregnant. And there will be no escape for them.
5:4 But you, brothers, are not living in darkness, where the day could seize you the way a thief does.
5:5 You are all sons of light and sons of day. We are not of night, or of darkness.
5:6 So let us not sleep like the rest. Let us stay awake and keep our heads clear.
5:7 People who sleep, sleep at night, and people who get drunk, get drunk at night.
5:8 But we are people of the day, so let us keep a clear head. Let us wear the breastplate of faith and love, and let the hope of salvation be our helmet.
5:9 God did not appoint us to face anger. He appointed us to take hold of salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ.
5:10 He is the one who died for us, so that, whether we stay awake or sleep, we may live in company with him.
5:11 That is why you should comfort one another, and each of you should build up the other, as you are already doing.
## Life Together in the Church
5:12 We ask you, brothers, to take note of those who toil among you, who stand at your head in the Lord and warn you.
5:13 Hold them in the very highest regard, in love, for the sake of the work they do. Keep the peace among yourselves.
5:14 And we appeal to you, brothers: set straight those who are out of line, cheer up the timid, stand by the weak, and be patient toward everyone.
5:15 Make sure no one repays anyone evil for evil. Instead, always run after what is good, both for each other and for all.
5:16 Always be full of joy.
5:17 Pray without a break.
5:18 In everything give thanks. That is God's will for you in Christ Jesus.
5:19 Do not snuff out the Spirit.
5:20 Do not treat prophecies as nothing.
5:21 Instead, put everything to the test, and keep hold of what is good.
5:22 Keep away from evil in every form.
## Closing Prayer and Greetings
5:23 May the God of peace himself make you holy in every part. May your spirit and soul and body be kept whole and without blame at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.
5:24 The one who calls you can be trusted, and he will do it.
5:25 Brothers, pray for us.
5:26 Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss.
5:27 By the Lord, I bind you with an oath: this letter must be read aloud to all the brothers.
5:28 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.
