## Greeting
1:1 From Simeon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who were given a faith just as precious as ours, through the righteousness of our God and Savior, Jesus Christ.
1:2 Grace to you, and peace: may they be multiplied in the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.
## Everything for life and godliness
1:3 His divine power has made us a gift of all that belongs to life and godliness, through the knowledge of the one who called us with his own glory and goodness.
1:4 Through them he has made us a gift of promises, precious and very great, so that by these you may become sharers in the divine nature, once you have gotten away from the world's decay, which comes through desire.
1:5 This is exactly why you must bring all your eagerness to it: in your faith supply goodness, in goodness supply knowledge,
1:6 in knowledge, self-control, in self-control, endurance, in endurance, godliness,
1:7 in godliness, brotherly love, and in brotherly love, love.
1:8 For when these are present in you and overflowing, they do not leave you idle or without fruit in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.
1:9 But the one who lacks them is blind. He squints and sees only what is near, and he has forgotten how his old sins were cleansed.
1:10 So, brothers, be all the more eager to make your calling and election firm. As long as you practice these things, you will never trip.
1:11 For this is how a way in to the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, will be supplied to you, richly.
## Peter's last reminder
1:12 That is why I will be sure to keep reminding you about these things. You know them already, and you stand firm in the truth that is present with you.
1:13 While I am still in this tent, I consider it only right to rouse you by reminding you,
1:14 for I know that the laying aside of my tent is coming soon, just as our Lord Jesus Christ showed me.
1:15 I will also be eager to make sure that, after my departure, you can recall these things at every turn.
## We saw his majesty
1:16 We were not following clever myths that people had invented when we told you about the power and the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. No, we had become eyewitnesses of his greatness.
1:17 From God the Father he received honor and glory, when a voice of this kind was carried to him by the Majestic Glory: "My Son, my dearly loved one, is this. In him I have found delight."
1:18 And this voice, carried out of heaven, we heard with our own ears while we were with him on the holy mountain.
1:19 And the prophetic word is all the more firm for us. You do well to keep your eyes on it, like a lamp that shines in a murky place, until day shines through and the morning star comes up in your hearts.
1:20 Know this first: no prophecy of Scripture comes into being by a person's own interpretation.
1:21 For no prophecy was ever borne in by human will. Instead, people borne along by the Holy Spirit spoke from God.
## False teachers among you
2:1 Among the people there were false prophets too, as among you there will be false teachers. They will sneak in factions that bring ruin, and they will even disown the Master who bought them. So they bring swift ruin on themselves.
2:2 Many will follow their shameless self-indulgence, and because of them people will slander the way of truth.
2:3 Out of greed they will use you to make money, with words they have made up. Their judgment has been at work since long ago; it is not idle. Their ruin is not dozing.
## God knows how to rescue and to judge
2:4 God did not spare the angels who sinned. He threw them into Tartarus and handed them over to chains of gloom, to be kept for judgment.
2:5 He did not spare the ancient world either. He guarded Noah, the eighth one, a herald of righteousness, when he brought a flood on a world of ungodly people.
2:6 He reduced the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to ashes and condemned them to destruction, setting them up as an example for those who would be ungodly later.
2:7 Lot, though, was righteous, and God rescued him. He was worn down by the way lawless people lived in shameless self-indulgence.
2:8 Living there among them, the righteous man looked and listened, and day after day he tortured his own righteous soul over their lawless deeds.
2:9 So the Lord knows how to pull godly people out of testing, and how to keep the unrighteous for the day of judgment, to be punished.
## Bold and self-willed
2:10 This is true above all of those who go after the flesh in a craving that stains, and who look down on lordship. Daring and self-willed, they slander the glorious ones without a tremor,
2:11 while angels, who are greater in strength and power, do not bring a slanderous judgment against them before the Lord.
2:12 But these people are like animals that cannot think, mere creatures of nature, born to be trapped and destroyed. They slander things they are ignorant of, and in their destruction they too will be destroyed,
2:13 harmed in return as the pay for the harm they do. To them, luxury in the daytime is pleasure. They are stains and flaws, delighting in their deceptions as they feast with you.
2:14 Their eyes are full of an adulteress, eyes that never rest from sin. They bait unsteady souls. Their hearts are well exercised in greed. They are children under a curse!
2:15 Leaving the straight road behind, they have strayed. They have followed in the road of Balaam son of Bosor, who loved the pay for wrongdoing.
2:16 But he got a rebuke for his own lawbreaking: a voiceless beast of burden, speaking out in a human voice, stopped the prophet's madness.
## Promises of freedom
2:17 These people are waterless springs and mists driven by a squall, for whom the gloom of darkness has been kept.
2:18 For they utter puffed-up words of emptiness. By the cravings of the flesh, by shameless self-indulgence, they bait people who are truly getting away from those who spend their lives in error.
2:19 They promise them freedom, though they are slaves of decay themselves. For a person is made a slave by whatever defeats him.
2:20 For if they have escaped the filth of the world by knowing the Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, but are tangled up in it again and beaten, then in the end they are worse off than at the beginning.
2:21 Never knowing the way of righteousness would have been better for them than knowing it and then turning back from the holy command that was handed down to them.
2:22 For them the true proverb has come true: "A dog turns back to its own vomit," and, "A sow that was washed heads for a roll in the mud."
## Remember what was spoken beforehand
3:1 Beloved, I am already writing you this second letter. In both letters I am rousing your sincere minds by reminding you
3:2 to remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets, and the command your apostles passed on from the Lord and Savior.
3:3 Know this first: in the last days mockers will come with their mocking, people who live by their own desires
3:4 and say, "Where is the promise of his coming? Since the day the fathers fell asleep, all things have stayed just the way they were from the start of creation."
3:5 This escapes them, because they want it to: long ago there were heavens, and an earth that took shape out of water and by means of water, by the word of God.
3:6 Through these, the world of that day was flooded with water and came to ruin.
3:7 But the heavens and the earth of now, by that same word, have been put in storage for fire. They are being kept for the day of judgment and the ruin of ungodly people.
## A thousand years like one day
3:8 But beloved, do not let this one thing escape you: in the Lord's sight one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.
3:9 The Lord is not delaying his promise, the way some people think of delay. Rather, he bears patiently with you. He does not want any to be lost, but wants all to arrive at repentance.
3:10 But the day of the Lord will come like a thief. On it the heavens will go by with a rushing roar, the elements will burn and be dissolved, and the earth and the works in it will lie exposed.
3:11 All these things are to be dissolved like this. So how holy your ways of life must be, and how full of godliness,
3:12 while you wait for the coming of God's day and hurry it along. Because of it, the heavens will catch fire and be dissolved, and the elements will burn and melt.
3:13 But we, as he promised, are waiting for new heavens and a new earth, where righteousness makes its home.
## Grow in grace
3:14 That is why, beloved, as you wait for these things, you must be eager to be found by him without stain and without flaw, in peace.
3:15 Think of our Lord's patience as salvation. Our beloved brother Paul also wrote to you this way, with the wisdom that was given to him.
3:16 He does so in all his letters too, when he talks in them about these things. In them are some things hard to grasp. Ignorant and unsteady people will twist these, as they also twist the other Scriptures, to their own ruin.
3:17 You, then, beloved, have been told in advance. Watch yourselves, so that the error of lawless people does not sweep you away with them, and you fall from your own firm footing.
3:18 Instead, keep growing in grace and in the knowledge of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ. Glory to him, both now and to the day of eternity.
