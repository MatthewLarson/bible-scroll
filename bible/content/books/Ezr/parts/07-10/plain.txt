## Ezra Comes Up from Babylon
7:1 After these events, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, there was Ezra son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah,
7:2 son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub,
7:3 son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth,
7:4 son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki,
7:5 son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the head priest.
7:6 This Ezra went up from Babylon. He was a scribe, an expert in Moses' law, given by the LORD, the God of Israel. Because the LORD his God's hand was on him, the king gave him whatever he requested.
7:7 Some of the Israelites went up to Jerusalem too, along with some of the priests, the Levites, the singers, the gatekeepers and the temple servants. This was in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.
7:8 He came to Jerusalem in the fifth month. It was the king's seventh year.
7:9 On the first day of the first month the journey up from Babylon began, and on the first day of the fifth month he came to Jerusalem. His God's good hand was on him.
7:10 For Ezra had given his heart to searching out the LORD's law, to doing it, and to teaching its rules and rulings in Israel.
## The King's Letter
7:11 Here is a copy of the document King Artaxerxes gave Ezra, the priest and scribe, a scribe in the words of the LORD's commands and of his rules for Israel:
7:12 Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of the heavens: full greeting. And now:
7:13 A decree goes out from me. In my kingdom, whoever of the people of Israel, of its priests or of the Levites, is willing to go to Jerusalem with you, let him go.
7:14 You go as the envoy of the king and his seven counselors, to inspect Judah and Jerusalem by the law of your God that is in your hand.
7:15 You will carry with you the silver and gold that the king and his counselors have offered of their own free will to the God of Israel, who makes his home in Jerusalem.
7:16 Take also all the silver and gold you come upon anywhere in the province of Babylon, along with what the people and the priests freely give for the house of their God in Jerusalem.
7:17 Therefore, with this silver you will be sure to buy bulls, rams and lambs, and the grain offerings and drink offerings that go with them. Present them on the altar of your God's house in Jerusalem.
7:18 With the rest of the silver and gold, do whatever you and your brothers think best, as your God wills.
7:19 The vessels handed to you for the worship in your God's house, hand over in full before the God of Jerusalem.
7:20 If your God's house needs anything more and the task of supplying it falls to you, you may draw it from the king's treasure house.
7:21 From me, King Artaxerxes himself, goes this decree to every treasurer in Beyond the River: Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of the heavens, may ask anything of you, and it must be done to the letter,
7:22 as much as 100 talents of silver, 100 kors of wheat, 100 baths of wine and 100 baths of olive oil, and salt with no amount written down.
7:23 Everything the God of the heavens orders must be carried out with zeal for the house of the God of the heavens. Why let anger come on the kingdom of the king and his sons?
7:24 And we make known to you that no one has the right to lay tribute, tax or duty on the priests, the Levites, the singers, the gatekeepers, the temple servants, or anyone who serves this house of God.
7:25 As for you, Ezra, use the wisdom of your God that you hold in your hand. Appoint magistrates and judges who will try cases for all the people of Beyond the River, all who know your God's laws. Anyone who does not know them, you are to teach.
7:26 Whoever does not do your God's law and the king's law is to have sentence carried out on him strictly, whether death, or being rooted out, or a fine on his property, or prison.
## Ezra Blesses God
7:27 Blessed is the LORD, God of our ancestors! He has put this into the king's heart: to make the LORD's house in Jerusalem beautiful.
7:28 To me he has stretched out loyal love in front of the king and his counselors and all the king's mighty officials. I grew strong, for the LORD my God's hand was on me, and I gathered heads of families out of Israel to go up with me.
## The Families Who Went Up with Ezra
8:1 Here are the heads of the ancestral families, and the family records, of those who went up with me from Babylon in King Artaxerxes' reign:
8:2 Of the descendants of Phinehas, Gershom. Of the descendants of Ithamar, Daniel. Of the descendants of David, Hattush,
8:3 of the descendants of Shecaniah. Of the descendants of Parosh, Zechariah, and with him 150 males were enrolled by family.
8:4 Of the descendants of Pahath-moab, Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, and with him 200 males.
8:5 Of the descendants of Zattu, Shecaniah son of Jahaziel, and with him 300 males.
8:6 Of the descendants of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan, and with him 50 males.
8:7 Of the descendants of Elam, Jeshaiah son of Athaliah, and with him 70 males.
8:8 Of the descendants of Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael, and with him 80 males.
8:9 Of the descendants of Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel, and with him 218 males.
8:10 Of the descendants of Bani, Shelomith son of Josiphiah, and with him 160 males.
8:11 Of the descendants of Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai, and with him 28 males.
8:12 Of the descendants of Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan, and with him 110 males.
8:13 Of the descendants of Adonikam, the last of them, and these are their names: Eliphelet, Jeiel and Shemaiah, and with them 60 males.
8:14 Of the descendants of Bigvai, Uthai and Zaccur, and with him 70 males.
## Levites for the House of God
8:15 I brought them together by the river that runs to Ahava, where we stayed in camp for three days. I studied the people and the priests closely, and not one descendant of Levi could I find there.
8:16 Then I sent word to Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah and Meshullam, who were heads, and to Joiarib and Elnathan, who were teachers.
8:17 I gave them orders to go to Iddo, the head man at the place called Casiphia. I gave them a message to deliver to Iddo and his kinsmen, the temple servants at the place Casiphia, so that they would bring us men to serve in the house of our God.
8:18 Our God's good hand was on us, and so they brought us a man of good sense from the line of Mahli son of Levi, son of Israel. This was Sherebiah, with his sons and his brothers, 18 in all.
8:19 Also Hashabiah, and Jeshaiah with him from the line of Merari, with their brothers and their sons, 20 in all.
8:20 And of the temple servants, whom David and the officials had given to help the Levites in their work, 220 came. Every one of them was listed by name.
## A Fast by the Ahava River
8:21 There by the Ahava River I called for a fast. We were to humble ourselves before our God and ask him for a straight road for us, our little ones and everything we owned.
8:22 I was ashamed to ask the king for a troop and horsemen to help us against an enemy on the way. We had told the king, "Our God's hand is on everyone who seeks him, for their good. But his strength and his anger are against everyone who abandons him."
8:23 So we fasted and pleaded with our God over this, and he was moved by our pleading.
## The Gifts Weighed Out
8:24 Next I chose out twelve of the chief priests, and with them Sherebiah, Hashabiah and ten of their brothers with them.
8:25 I weighed out to them the silver, the gold and the vessels. These were the gift set aside for our God's house, offered by the king, his counselors, his officials and all the Israelites who were there.
8:26 Into their hands I weighed 650 talents of silver, 100 talents in silver vessels, and 100 talents of gold,
8:27 with 20 gold bowls valued at 1,000 darics, and two vessels of good gleaming bronze, as costly as gold.
8:28 Then I told them, "You belong to the LORD as holy men, and the vessels are holy things too. The silver and gold are a gift given freely to the LORD, the God of your ancestors.
8:29 Stay awake over them and keep them until you weigh them out before the chief priests, the Levites and the family heads of Israel in Jerusalem, in the storerooms of the LORD's house."
8:30 The priests and Levites took charge of the silver, gold and vessels by weight, to carry them to Jerusalem, to the house of our God.
## Arrival in Jerusalem
8:31 We broke camp at the Ahava River on the twelfth of the first month, heading for Jerusalem. Our God's hand was on us. He saved us from the grip of enemies and from those lying in wait on the road.
8:32 We arrived at Jerusalem and spent three days there.
8:33 On the fourth day the silver, the gold and the vessels were weighed out in our God's house and handed to Meremoth son of Uriah the priest, along with Eleazar son of Phinehas. The Levites Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui were with them.
8:34 It was all done by count and by weight, and the total weight was recorded then and there.
8:35 The people who had come back from captivity, the people of the exile, presented burnt offerings to the God of Israel. For all Israel there were 12 bulls, 96 rams, 77 lambs, and 12 male goats as a sin offering. All of it was a burnt offering to the LORD.
8:36 They also handed the king's decrees to the royal satraps and the governors of Beyond the River, who then backed the people and the house of God.
## The Report of Mixed Marriages
9:1 Once these things were done, the officials came up to me and said, "The people Israel, and the priests and Levites too, have failed to keep apart from the peoples of the lands and their abhorrent practices, those of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, the Ammonites, the Moabites, the Egyptians and the Amorites.
9:2 They have taken some of those peoples' daughters as wives, for themselves and for their sons. So the holy seed has mingled with the peoples of the lands, and the officials and deputies had their hands in this unfaithfulness first."
9:3 Hearing this, I ripped my garment and my robe, tore out some hair from my head and my beard, and sat down in shock.
9:4 All who trembled at what the God of Israel had said came and gathered to me over the unfaithfulness of the exile. I stayed sitting in shock until the time of the evening grain offering.
## Ezra's Prayer
9:5 When the evening grain offering came, I rose from my self-humbling, my garment and robe still torn. I sank to my knees and spread my palms out to the LORD my God,
9:6 and I said, "My God, I am ashamed and humiliated to lift my face toward you, my God. Our wrongdoing has piled up over our heads, and our guilt is so great it reaches the heavens.
9:7 Ever since our ancestors' time we have been in great guilt, right up to today. For our wrongs we were given into the hand of the kings of the lands, we and our kings and our priests: to the sword, to captivity, to looting, and to shame on our faces, just as we are now.
9:8 Now, for just a short while, favor has come to us from the LORD our God. He has let a band of survivors remain and has given us a tent peg in his holy place. In this way our God has brightened our eyes and given us a little life again in our slavery.
9:9 Slaves is what we are. But our God has not left us alone in our slavery. Before the kings of Persia he has stretched out loyal love to us. He has given us new life to set up our God's house again and to make its ruins stand, and he has given us a fence in Judah and Jerusalem.
9:10 So now, our God, after all this, what are we to say? We have abandoned your commands,
9:11 the commands you laid down through your servants the prophets: 'The land you go in to take possession of is a land of defilement, defiled by the peoples of the lands with their abhorrent practices. From edge to edge they have filled it with their uncleanness.
9:12 So now, your daughters you must not give to their sons, and their daughters you must not take for your sons. Never seek their peace or their good, for all time. Then you will grow strong, you will eat the good of the land, and you will pass it on to your children to own forever.'
9:13 All this has come on us for our evil deeds and our great guilt. Even so, you, our God, have held back and not given our wrongs their full weight, and you have given us survivors like these.
9:14 Are we to go back to breaking your commands, and become in-laws with the peoples of these abhorrent practices? Would your anger not burn against us until you made an end of us, until no remnant and no survivor was left?
9:15 LORD, God of Israel, you are in the right. We have been left as a band of survivors, as we are now. Look at us here in front of you, guilty as we are! Because of this, no one can stand in your presence."
## A Covenant to Send the Wives Away
10:1 Ezra was praying and making confession, weeping and lying face down in front of God's house. As he did, a huge assembly gathered to him from Israel: men and women and children. The people were weeping, a great weeping.
10:2 Shecaniah son of Jehiel, of the descendants of Elam, answered Ezra. "We are the ones who have acted unfaithfully toward our God," he said. "We have taken foreign wives into our homes, women from the peoples of the land. But now, there is hope for Israel over this.
10:3 Now then, let us make a covenant with our God to send out all the wives and the children born of them, as my lord advises, and as those who tremble at our God's command advise. Let it be done by the law.
10:4 Up! The matter rests on you. We stand with you. Be strong and get it done."
10:5 Ezra rose. He made the chief priests, the Levites and all Israel swear to act on this word, and they swore it.
10:6 Ezra then left the front of God's house and went into the room of Jehohanan son of Eliashib. He went there, but he did not eat food or drink water, because he was grieving over the unfaithfulness of the exile.
10:7 A call went out through Judah and Jerusalem to all the people of the exile: they were to assemble at Jerusalem.
10:8 Whoever failed to come within three days, as the officials and elders had decided, would have all his goods put under the ban, and he himself would be shut out from the assembly of the exile.
## The Assembly in the Rain
10:9 All the men of Judah and Benjamin did gather at Jerusalem in the three days. It was the twentieth day of the ninth month. The whole people sat in the open space by God's house, shaking over the matter and from the downpours.
10:10 Then Ezra the priest rose and spoke to them. "You have acted unfaithfully," he said. "You have taken foreign wives into your homes, piling more on Israel's guilt.
10:11 So now, make your confession to the LORD, the God of your ancestors, and do what pleases him. Set yourselves apart from the peoples of the land and from the foreign wives."
10:12 The whole assembly shouted back, "Right! What you have said, we are bound to do.
10:13 Only, there are a great many people, and this is the season of rains. We have no strength to stay standing out in the open. Besides, the work will take more than one day, or two, since we have done a great deal of wrong in this matter.
10:14 Let our officials act for the whole assembly. Everyone in our towns who has taken a foreign wife into his home should come at scheduled times, along with each town's elders and its judges. Let it go on until our God's blazing anger over this matter turns back from us."
10:15 Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah took a stand against this, and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite backed them.
10:16 So the people of the exile did it that way. Ezra the priest and certain men, heads of families from each ancestral house, all of them named, were set apart. They sat down on the first day of the tenth month to examine the matter.
10:17 They completed the work on all the men who had taken foreign wives by the first day of the first month.
