## Elihu steps forward
32:1 So the three men gave up answering Job, since in his own eyes he was in the right.
32:2 Then the anger of Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, from the clan of Ram, blazed up. It blazed against Job, because he held himself in the right rather than God.
32:3 It blazed against his three friends too, because they had come up with no answer, but had still declared Job guilty.
32:4 Elihu had held his words back from Job, because the others were older than he was.
32:5 When Elihu saw that the three men had no answer left in their mouths, his anger blazed.
## Elihu speaks to the elders
32:6 So Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite answered. He said: / "In years I am young, / and you are gray-haired. / So I shrank back, afraid / to show you what I think.
32:7 I said to myself, 'Let the days speak, / and let a crowd of years make wisdom known.'
32:8 But in fact the spirit within a mortal, / the Almighty's breath, is what grants understanding.
32:9 Those with many years are not the wise ones, / and the aged are not the ones who grasp what is just.
32:10 That is why I say, Hear me out. / I will lay out my knowledge, I too.
32:11 See, I held on while you spoke; / I gave ear to your reasoning / as you hunted for words.
32:12 I paid close attention to you, / and see, none of you set Job straight, / none of you had a reply to his words.
32:13 Do not tell yourselves, 'We have hit on wisdom. / God will rout him, not a human being.'
32:14 He has not lined up his words against me, / and I will not use your speeches to reply to him.
## Full of words
32:15 They are struck dumb; they answer no more. / The words have moved off and left them.
32:16 Am I to go on waiting since they say nothing, / since they stand there and reply no more?
32:17 I will give my share of the answer, I too; / I will lay out my knowledge, I too.
32:18 For I am crammed with words, / and the spirit in my belly squeezes me.
32:19 Look, my belly is wine with no outlet; / it is about to burst like new wineskins.
32:20 I will speak, and there will be room to breathe; / I will part my lips and reply.
32:21 Let me not play favorites with anyone, / or butter up any human being.
32:22 Flattering is not a skill I have; / otherwise my Maker would quickly carry me off.
## Elihu speaks to Job
33:1 Now then, Job, please hear my speeches, / and lend your ear to every word of mine.
33:2 Look, I am opening my mouth; / my tongue is forming words on my palate.
33:3 My words come out of an honest heart; / my lips speak pure knowledge.
33:4 God's spirit made me, / and the Almighty's breath keeps me alive.
33:5 Answer me back if you are able. / Line up your arguments in front of me, and stand your ground.
33:6 Look, I am the same as you before God; / I too was nipped off from clay.
33:7 See, you need not be shaken by dread of me, / and my weight will not bear down on you.
33:8 But you said it in my hearing, / and the sound of your words reached me:
33:9 'I am pure and free of rebellion; / I am spotless, and no guilt is mine.
33:10 Look, he invents grounds against me; / he treats me as an enemy of his.
33:11 He clamps my feet in the stocks; / he keeps an eye on every path I take.'
33:12 Look, on this point you are wrong; I will answer you. / God is far above a human being.
33:13 Why have you taken him to court / because he does not reply to all of a person's words?
## God speaks in the night
33:14 For God speaks one time, / and a second time, yet no one takes notice:
33:15 in a dream, a vision by night, / at the hour deep sleep falls on mortals / and they doze upon their beds.
33:16 Then he uncovers people's ears / and puts his seal on their correction,
33:17 to turn a person back from a deed, / and to shield a man from pride.
33:18 He holds his soul back from the Pit, / his life from crossing the River.
## God speaks through pain
33:19 Or he is disciplined on his sickbed with pain, / and a constant war goes on in his bones.
33:20 His life turns sick at bread, / his appetite at the dishes he loved.
33:21 His flesh melts out of sight, / and bones once hidden jut out.
33:22 His soul comes near the Pit, / his life near the death-dealers.
33:23 But if there is an angel standing by him, / a go-between, one in a thousand, / to tell a person where his uprightness lies,
33:24 then he shows him grace and says, / 'Spare him the descent into the Pit; / I have come upon a ransom.'
33:25 His flesh grows fresher than when he was a boy; / he returns to his young days.
33:26 He prays to God, who welcomes him; / he looks on God's face with shouts of joy, / and God gives that man back his righteousness.
33:27 He sings out before others and says, / 'I sinned, I bent what was straight, / yet I was not paid what I deserved.
33:28 He has rescued my soul from passing into the Pit, / and I will live to see the light.'
33:29 See, all this is God's doing, / two times, three times over, with a person,
33:30 to bring him back from the Pit, / to light him up with the light of the living.
## Listen, Job
33:31 Mark this, Job, and hear me out. / Keep still, and I will talk.
33:32 If you have words, reply to me; / talk, because I would like to clear your name.
33:33 If not, then you listen to me. / Be quiet, and I will school you in wisdom."
## Elihu speaks to the wise
34:1 Then Elihu answered. He said:
34:2 "You wise men, listen to what I say; / you who know, lend me your ear.
34:3 For the ear puts words to the test, / just as the mouth tastes food to eat.
34:4 Let us pick out justice for ourselves, / and come to know, between us, what is good.
34:5 For Job has said, 'I am righteous, / and God has pushed my case aside.
34:6 Should I lie about my right? / The arrow in me cannot be healed, though I have not rebelled.'
34:7 Is there anyone like Job? / He gulps down mockery like water.
34:8 He keeps company with those who do evil / and goes about with the wicked.
34:9 For he has said, 'It gets a man nothing / to be in God's good graces.'
## God does no wrong
34:10 Therefore, you thoughtful men, hear me: / God would never do wickedness; / the Almighty would never do wrong.
34:11 He repays a human being for his work, / and he brings on each person what his way of life has earned.
34:12 Surely God does nothing wicked, / and the Almighty does not twist what is right.
34:13 Who put the earth in his care? / Who set the whole world in place?
34:14 If he were to set his mind on it / and draw his spirit and breath back into himself,
34:15 every living body would die at once, / and human beings would turn back into dust.
34:16 If you have sense, hear this; / bend your ear to what I say.
34:17 Can someone who hates justice hold the reins? / Will you condemn the righteous and mighty One?
34:18 Would anyone say to a king, 'Worthless!' / or to noblemen, 'Wicked!'?
34:19 Yet God does not even take the side of princes / or prefer the rich over the poor, / since every one of them is something his hands made.
34:20 In an instant they are dead, in the middle of the night. / A people is shaken, and they pass away; / the mighty are removed, though no hand touches them.
34:21 His eyes watch the paths each person walks; / he sees every step.
34:22 There is no darkness, no deep shadow, / where evildoers can hide.
34:23 He does not set a man a further date / to go before God for judgment.
34:24 He breaks the powerful in pieces without an inquiry / and puts others in their place.
34:25 Because he knows what they do, / he turns them over by night, and they are ground down.
34:26 He slaps them down like the wicked they are, / in a place where people watch,
34:27 since they stopped following him / and gave no thought to any of his ways.
34:28 So they made the poor cry out to him, / and the shouts of the afflicted reached his ears.
34:29 When he stays silent, who can declare him guilty? / If he hides his face, who will spot him? / He is over a nation and a person all the same,
34:30 so that a godless man may not rule, / so that there may be no snares for the people.
## A verdict on Job
34:31 Suppose someone says to God, / 'I have suffered for it; I will not act corruptly.
34:32 Show me what is beyond my sight. / If I have acted wickedly, I will not repeat it.'
34:33 Should he pay back as you see fit, / because you refuse his way? / You are the one who must choose, not I. / So speak up: tell us what you know.
34:34 Men of sense will say to me, / and so will any wise man who hears me:
34:35 'Job talks without knowing, / and there is no insight in his words.'
34:36 I wish Job were tested to the very end / for answering like wicked men.
34:37 On top of his sin he heaps rebellion; / among us he claps in scorn / and makes his words against God more and more."
## Elihu speaks a third time
35:1 Then Elihu answered. He said:
35:2 "Do you count this as fair, / when you say, 'My rightness is greater than God's'?
35:3 For you ask what good it does you: / 'What do I gain more than if I had sinned?'
35:4 I will answer you with words, / you and your friends along with you.
35:5 Look up at the sky and see; / gaze at the clouds, high above you.
35:6 If you have sinned, how does that touch him? / If you pile up offenses, what difference is that to him?
35:7 If you are in the right, what gift is that to him? / What does he take from your hand?
35:8 Your wickedness reaches only someone like you; / your righteousness, only a fellow human.
## Why the cries go unanswered
35:9 Under crushing oppression people cry out; / they shout for help against the arm of the great.
35:10 Yet nobody asks, 'Where is God, who made me, / who puts songs in the night,
35:11 who gives us more teaching than the animals of the land, / and more wisdom than the birds of the sky?'
35:12 There they shout, and he gives no answer, / on account of the arrogance of the evil.
35:13 God will not hear what is hollow; / the Almighty does not look at it.
35:14 And all the more when you say you cannot see him, / that your lawsuit lies before him and you are waiting on him.
35:15 And now, since his anger has punished no one, / and he does not pay much heed to folly,
35:16 Job opens his mouth for nothing; / without knowledge he piles up words."
## Elihu speaks a fourth time
36:1 Elihu went on. He said:
36:2 "Wait for me a little, so I can make it plain to you / that God still has words to be spoken for him.
36:3 I will fetch my knowledge from far away, / and declare that my Maker is in the right.
36:4 For my words are no lie, I assure you; / one whose knowledge is complete is here with you.
## Suffering that teaches
36:5 See, God is strong, and rejects no one; / strong is he in the power of his mind.
36:6 He does not let the wicked live on, / and he gives justice to the poor.
36:7 He never looks away from the righteous; / he gives them seats on thrones beside kings, / seats them forever, and they rise high.
36:8 But if they are put in fetters, / held fast by the cords of misery,
36:9 then he tells them what they have done, / their rebellions, how they have acted proudly.
36:10 He uncovers their ears for discipline / and tells them to turn back from evil.
36:11 If they hear and serve him, / they live out their days in good things / and their years in delight.
36:12 If they refuse to hear, they cross the River / and perish without knowledge.
36:13 Those godless at heart nurse their anger; / even when he ties them up, they do not call for help.
36:14 Their soul dies while they are young, / and their life goes the way of the shrine men.
36:15 He frees the afflicted through their affliction, / and in their distress he opens their ears.
## A warning to Job
36:16 He was luring you, too, out of the jaws of trouble / into a wide place with no cramping under it, / and your table would be set full of rich food.
36:17 But you are filled with the verdict on the wicked; / verdict and justice have seized you.
36:18 Watch out, or rage will lure you into mockery; / do not let a large ransom turn you aside.
36:19 Can your cry for help, or all your straining strength, / set things right so you are free of trouble?
36:20 Do not yearn for the night, / when nations are lifted away from their places.
36:21 Watch yourself; do not turn to wrongdoing, / for you have picked that over suffering.
## God the great teacher
36:22 Look, God is lifted high in his power. / What teacher is like him?
36:23 Who has mapped out his way for him? / Who has ever told him, 'You did wrong'?
36:24 Remember to lift up his work in praise, / the work that people have sung about.
36:25 All humankind has looked at it; / mortals see it from far away.
36:26 See how great God is, beyond our knowledge! / His years cannot be counted or searched out.
## The coming storm
36:27 He pulls up the water drop by drop; / they filter into rain from his mist,
36:28 the clouds let it stream down / and drip on crowds of people.
36:29 Who can grasp how the clouds spread out, / the thunder from his pavilion?
36:30 See, he spreads his light out around him, / and the roots of the sea he covers over.
36:31 This is how he rules over nations / and hands out food in plenty.
36:32 He wraps his hands in light / and orders it to hit the mark.
36:33 His roar gives notice of him; / the herds, too, of what is coming up.
37:1 At this my heart pounds too / and springs up from where it belongs.
37:2 Hear it, all of you, hear the rage of his voice, / the low roar coming out of his mouth.
37:3 He lets it loose under the whole sky, / and his light to the edges of the earth.
37:4 Behind it a voice roars out; / he thunders with his voice of majesty, / and when his voice is heard he does not rein them in.
37:5 With his voice God thunders out marvels; / great are the things he does, and we cannot grasp them.
## Snow, ice and cloud
37:6 To the snow he says, 'Fall to the ground,' / and so to the rain shower, / the shower of his mighty rains.
37:7 He stamps a seal on every human hand, / so that everyone he made may know his work.
37:8 The wild animals creep into hiding / and settle down in their dens.
37:9 The storm wind comes out of its chamber, / and cold from the winds that scatter.
37:10 God breathes, and ice appears; / the wide waters are clamped down.
37:11 He weighs down the clouds with moisture; / his cloud scatters its light.
37:12 They swirl around, turning at his direction, / to carry out whatever he orders them / over the face of the whole inhabited earth.
37:13 Whether for a rod of punishment, or for his land, / or for loyal love, he makes it arrive.
## Questions for Job
37:14 Job, give your ear to this. / Stand still and take a hard look at the wonders of God.
37:15 Do you know when God gives them their orders / and makes the light of his cloud blaze out?
37:16 Do you understand how the clouds hang balanced, / the marvels of the One whose knowledge is complete?
37:17 You whose clothes feel hot / when the land lies hushed under the south wind,
37:18 can you join him in hammering out the skies, / hard as a mirror cast from metal?
37:19 Tell us what to say to him! / Because of the dark, we cannot lay out our case.
37:20 Should someone tell him I want to speak? / Would anyone ask to be swallowed whole?
## Golden splendor from the north
37:21 No one can look at the light just now, / so brilliant it is in the clouds, / since a wind has passed and cleared them.
37:22 From the north comes gold; / fearsome splendor rests on God.
37:23 The Almighty we cannot reach. / He is lofty in power, / and with justice and great righteousness he crushes no one.
37:24 That is why people fear him; / he has no regard for any of the wise of heart."
