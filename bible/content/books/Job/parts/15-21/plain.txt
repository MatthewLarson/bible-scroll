## Eliphaz speaks
15:1 Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered:
15:2 Would a wise man answer with knowledge that is only wind, / and fill his insides with the east wind?
15:3 Would he argue with a word that is no use, / with talk that does nobody any good?
15:4 Worse, you are undoing reverence, / and cutting short prayer in God's presence.
15:5 Your guilt is your mouth's teacher; / you have picked the speech of the cunning.
15:6 It is your own mouth that finds you guilty, not I. / Your own lips bear witness against you.
15:7 Was it you, born the first of humankind? / Were you birthed before the hills?
15:8 Were you a listener at God's council? / Do you hoard wisdom for yourself?
15:9 What do you know that we don't? / What do you grasp that isn't ours as well?
15:10 We have gray heads and old men among us, / men older than your father.
15:11 Is the comfort God gives too little for you, / a word dealt to you with gentleness?
15:12 Why has your heart swept you off? / What makes your eyes flash,
15:13 so that you aim your spirit at God / and push such words out of your mouth?
15:14 What is a human, that he should be clean? / How can one born of woman be in the right?
15:15 Look, even his holy ones he does not trust; / even the skies are not clean to his eyes.
15:16 How much less someone disgusting and spoiled, / a man who gulps down wrong like water!
## What the wise have handed down
15:17 I will show you; hear me out. / What I have seen, I will report:
15:18 things the wise have passed on, / not hiding what came from their fathers.
15:19 The land was handed to them alone, / and no outsider walked through their midst.
15:20 The wicked man twists in pain all his days, / all the counted years stored away for the brutal man.
15:21 Sounds of dread ring in his ears; / when all is quiet, the destroyer comes at him.
15:22 He has no faith that he will come back from the dark; / the sword lies in wait for him.
15:23 He wanders around looking for bread: "Where is it?" / He knows a dark day is ready, close at hand.
15:24 Trouble and hardship frighten him; / they overwhelm him like a king set for the attack.
15:25 For he lifted his hand against God / and played the warrior against the Almighty.
15:26 He rushes at him with his neck stiff, / behind the thick metal knobs of his shields.
15:27 His face is wrapped in his own fat; / he has packed fat around his hips.
15:28 He settled in towns that were wiped out, / in houses no one would live in, / marked to become piles of stone.
15:29 He will not keep his riches; what he has will not stand, / and his holdings will not reach across the land.
15:30 Darkness will not let him go. / Fire will dry up his young branches, / and he will be blown off by the breath of his mouth.
15:31 Let him not lean on what is hollow, fooling himself, / for hollowness will be his pay.
15:32 Before his day comes, it will be settled in full, / and his palm frond will not turn green.
15:33 He will lose his green grapes the way a vine does, / and drop his blossom the way an olive tree does.
15:34 The whole gang of the godless will have no children, / and fire will eat the tents built on bribes.
15:35 Pregnant with mischief, they give birth to wickedness; / their belly is busy shaping lies.
## Job speaks
16:1 Then Job answered:
16:2 I have heard plenty of talk like this. / You are all comforters who bring trouble!
16:3 Will words of wind ever stop? / What is bothering you, that you keep answering?
16:4 I could speak the way you do / if you stood where I stand. / I could stitch words together against you, / and wag my head at you.
16:5 I would make you strong with my mouth, / and my lips' comfort would hold back your grief.
16:6 If I talk, my pain is not held back. / If I stop, how much of it goes away?
## God has torn me
16:7 But now he has drained me. / You have wrecked my whole circle.
16:8 You have gripped me, and that has become a witness. / My wasted body stands up against me / and gives evidence to my face.
16:9 His anger rips me apart; he holds a grudge against me. / He grinds his teeth at me. / My foe stares me down with sharpened eyes.
16:10 Men gape at me with their mouths. / They hit me on the cheek to shame me. / They crowd in together around me.
16:11 God delivers me to the unjust; / he tosses me into the grip of the wicked.
16:12 I was living in peace, and he broke me to bits. / He took me by the neck and dashed me down. / He stood me up as a target for himself.
16:13 His bowmen ring me in. / He slices my kidneys open without mercy, / and spills my bile on the ground.
16:14 He breaks me open, gap on top of gap; / he charges me like a fighting man.
16:15 I stitched sackcloth onto my skin. / I have thrust my horn into the dust.
16:16 My face is flushed and raw from crying; / deep shadow lies on my eyelids.
16:17 This, though my hands have done no violence, / and the prayer I pray is clean.
## My witness is in heaven
16:18 Earth, do not hide my blood! / Let my outcry never find a place to stop.
16:19 Even now, look: my witness is in heaven; / the one who vouches for me is on high.
16:20 The ones who mock me are my friends! / My eye drips tears to God.
16:21 May he plead for a man before God, / as a mortal does for his friend.
16:22 For my years are few and numbered, / and I will walk a road I do not come back on.
17:1 My spirit is wrecked; my days are snuffed out. / Graves are what is left for me.
17:2 Truly, mockery is all I have around me, / and my eye spends the night on their taunts.
17:3 Please set down a deposit; stand bail for me with yourself. / Who else will shake my hand on it?
17:4 You have hidden understanding from their hearts. / That is why you will not lift them up.
17:5 Whoever betrays his friends for a cut of the profit, / his children's eyes will grow dim.
17:6 He has turned me into a proverb among the peoples; / I am a face for spitting in.
17:7 Sorrow has dimmed my eye; / my arms and legs are all like a shadow.
17:8 Honest men are stunned by this; / the innocent man is roused against the godless.
17:9 The righteous man keeps on his road, / and the man whose hands are clean keeps adding strength.
17:10 But go ahead, all of you, come back again; / I will not find among you one who is wise.
## Hope goes down to the dust
17:11 My days have gone by. My plans are ripped up, / the things my heart held dear.
17:12 They call night day. / "Light is close," they say, when darkness is here.
17:13 If all I look forward to is Sheol as my house, / if I lay out my bedding in the dark,
17:14 if I call the Pit "my father" / and the maggot "my mother" and "my sister,"
17:15 then where is my hope? / Who will ever catch sight of my hope?
17:16 Will they sink to the bars of Sheol? / Will we go down to the dust side by side?
## Bildad speaks
18:1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered:
18:2 How long before you put a stop to words? / Get some sense, then we will speak.
18:3 Why do you treat us like dumb animals? / Why are we fools in your sight?
18:4 You, ripping yourself apart in your rage: / must the world be left empty because of you? / Must a rock be shifted out of its place?
## The trap for the wicked
18:5 Still, the wicked man's light is snuffed, / and his fire's flame does not shine.
18:6 In his tent, the light turns black; / his lamp hanging over him dies out.
18:7 His firm stride grows cramped; / his own scheming brings him down.
18:8 His own feet send him into a net; / he strolls along on top of the netting.
18:9 A spring-trap catches his heel; / a noose clamps down on him.
18:10 Under the dirt a cord lies waiting for him, / and a snare along the trail.
18:11 On every side, sudden horrors scare him / and send him scattering at every step.
18:12 His strength starves, / and ruin stands by, waiting for him to slip.
18:13 Pieces of his skin are eaten away; / Death's firstborn feeds on his limbs.
18:14 He is ripped out of the tent he relied on / and paraded before the king of terrors.
18:15 Something not his settles in his tent; / sulfur is strewn over his pasture.
18:16 Underneath, his roots go dry; / overhead, his branch shrivels.
18:17 No one in the land remembers him anymore; / nobody in the street speaks his name.
18:18 They shove him out of the light into the dark; / they hunt him out of the world.
18:19 He leaves no child or grandchild among his people; / no one is left in the places he stayed.
18:20 Those who come after are stunned by his day, / and those of earlier times are gripped with a shudder.
18:21 Yes, that is what becomes of the homes of the wrongdoer; / that is the place of one who does not know God.
## Job speaks
19:1 Then Job answered:
19:2 How long will you grieve my soul / and grind me down with talk?
19:3 This is the tenth time you have shamed me. / You feel no shame as you abuse me.
19:4 Suppose I truly have erred. / My mistake is my own to keep.
19:5 If you truly mean to tower over me / and prove your case from my shame,
## Walled in by God
19:6 understand this: it is God who has bent my case, / and he has thrown his net around me.
19:7 If I scream "Violence!" no one answers me. / I yell for help, and justice is nowhere.
19:8 He has built a wall across my road; I can't get through. / He has laid darkness over my trails.
19:9 He pulled off the glory I wore / and lifted the crown from my head.
19:10 He demolishes me all around until I am finished; / he rips my hope up by the roots like a tree.
19:11 His anger is set ablaze toward me; / he treats me as one of his foes.
19:12 His raiding bands arrive all at once. / They pile up a roadway to reach me / and pitch camp all around my tent.
## Abandoned by everyone
19:13 My brothers he has moved far away from me; / the people who knew me have turned strangers.
19:14 My kin are gone; / those I knew best no longer remember me.
19:15 The foreigners who live in my house, and my slave girls, treat me as an outsider; / to them I am a foreigner now.
19:16 When I call my servant, he won't reply, / though I plead with him out loud.
19:17 My breath is foul to my wife; / I am sickening to the sons of my own womb.
19:18 Even small children reject me; / I get up, and they make fun of me.
19:19 Every man in my inner circle loathes me; / those I loved have turned on me.
19:20 My bones cling to my skin and my flesh. / I got away with only the skin of my teeth.
19:21 Be kind to me, be kind to me, you who are my friends! / God's hand has touched me.
19:22 Why do you chase me like God, / never full of eating my flesh?
## My redeemer lives
19:23 If only someone would put my words in writing, / cut them into an inscription,
19:24 with a stylus of iron, and lead, / cut into the rock for good!
19:25 As for me, I know that my redeemer lives, / and at last he will stand up on the dust.
19:26 Even after my skin has been stripped off like this, / from my flesh I will see God.
19:27 I will see him for myself; / my own eyes will see, and not a stranger. / My inmost being is spent with longing!
19:28 If you say, "Let's go after him! / The cause of it all is in him,"
19:29 then be afraid of the sword yourselves, / for fury earns the sword's penalties, / so that you may know there is judgment.
## Zophar speaks
20:1 Then Zophar the Naamathite answered:
20:2 So my restless thoughts push me to reply, / for I am stirred up inside.
20:3 I have listened to scolding that shames me, / and a spirit from my own insight answers for me.
## The joy of the wicked is short
20:4 Do you know this, known from ancient times, / from when humans were first put on the earth?
20:5 The cheering of the wicked is close to its end; / the glee of the godless is over in an instant.
20:6 Even if his greatness climbs to the sky / and his head brushes the clouds,
20:7 he will vanish for good, like his own manure. / People who used to see him will ask, "Where did he go?"
20:8 Like a dream he takes flight, and no one can find him; / like something seen at night, he is driven off.
20:9 An eye that once caught sight of him will not again; / his place will see him no more.
20:10 His sons will have to make it up to the poor; / he will hand his riches back with his own hands.
20:11 His bones were brimming with young strength; / it will lie down beside him in the dust.
20:12 Evil may be sweet to his taste, / and he may tuck it under his tongue.
20:13 He may save it and not let it slip away, / holding it back on the roof of his mouth.
20:14 Yet in his gut his food changes; / inside him it is cobra poison.
20:15 He gulped riches down, and he throws them up; / God forces them out of his belly.
20:16 He sucks on snake poison; / an adder's tongue slays him.
20:17 He will not get to look at the streams, / the rivers in flood with honey and butter.
20:18 He must return what he worked for, not gulp it down. / His business brings him riches he will never enjoy.
20:19 He broke the poor and left them to themselves. / He grabbed a house, but he never built it.
20:20 His belly never knew when to stop, / so nothing he craved will he keep safe.
20:21 He left nothing uneaten; / so his good fortune will not last.
20:22 At the height of his plenty, trouble will squeeze him; / every hard-pressed hand will strike at him.
20:23 Just as he goes to stuff his stomach, / God will let loose his burning anger on him / and rain it on him for his meal.
20:24 He runs from a weapon of iron; / a bow of bronze shoots him through.
20:25 He draws it, and it comes out through his back, / the flashing tip, out of his gallbladder. / Horrors fall on him.
20:26 Every kind of darkness is stored away for his treasures. / A fire no one blew on will swallow him; / it will go badly for anyone left in his tent.
20:27 The sky will lay his sin bare; / the land will take its stand against him.
20:28 What his house has grown will be hauled away, / swept off like streams on the day of his anger.
20:29 This is the share God gives a wicked person, / the inheritance God has spoken for him.
## Job speaks
21:1 Then Job answered:
21:2 Hear me out, hear every word, / and let this be all the comfort you offer.
21:3 Put up with me, and I will speak; / once I am done, go on and sneer.
21:4 As for me, is my complaint aimed at a man? / Then why should my temper not run short?
21:5 Turn to me and be shocked, / and put a hand over your mouth.
21:6 When I think back on it, I am shaken, / and a shudder takes hold of my flesh.
## Why do the wicked live on?
21:7 Why do the wicked stay alive, / reach old age, and even gain strength?
21:8 Their children stand firm in front of them, / their grandchildren right where they can see them.
21:9 Their homes are at peace, with nothing to fear; / no rod from God comes down on them.
21:10 Every time, their bull gets the cow pregnant; / their cow gives birth and never loses a calf.
21:11 They let their little boys out like lambs; / their children skip and dance.
21:12 They raise their voices to drum and lyre / and have a good time to the flute's tune.
21:13 They finish out their days in good times, / and they go down to Sheol in an instant.
21:14 They tell God, "Go away from us. / Knowing your ways is not what we want.
21:15 The Almighty, what is he, that we should serve him? / What do we get by begging him for anything?"
21:16 Yet see, their good fortune is not theirs to hold. / The plans of the wicked are far from me!
21:17 How many times does the wicked man's lamp go dark? / How many times does ruin fall on them, / or does he deal out pains in his anger?
21:18 Are they ever like stubble in front of the wind, / like husks that a whirlwind steals?
21:19 "God stores up a man's guilt for his children"? / Let God pay him back himself, so he feels it!
21:20 Let him watch his own downfall with his eyes; / let him drink down the Almighty's fury.
21:21 What pleasure does he take in his household once he is gone, / when his count of months has been cut short?
## Death takes both alike
21:22 Will someone instruct God about knowledge, / when he is the judge even of the lofty ones?
21:23 This man dies with his strength whole, / all of him at peace and untroubled.
21:24 His pails overflow with milk, / and the marrow in his bones is still moist.
21:25 That man dies with bitterness in his soul, / and he never once ate of the good things.
21:26 Both lie down in the same dust, / and the maggot spreads over them.
## Your answers are empty
21:27 See, I know the thoughts in your heads, / and the schemes you plot to do me wrong.
21:28 For you ask, "Whatever became of the nobleman's house, / the tent where the wicked made their home?"
21:29 Haven't you questioned people passing along the road? / Don't you recognize the evidence they bring:
21:30 that the evil man is spared on the day of disaster, / that such men are led away on the day of fury?
21:31 Who tells him what his conduct is, face to face? / Who gives him back what he has done?
21:32 He is borne in procession to the graves, / and people stand guard over his burial mound.
21:33 The dirt clods of the wadi lie sweet on him. / Every person comes along behind him, / and those in front of him cannot be counted.
21:34 How then can you comfort me with puffs of air? / What is left of your answers is faithlessness.
