## The high priest of a better covenant
8:1 Now here is the main point in all that is being said: the high priest we have is of that kind. He took his seat at the right hand of the Majesty's throne in the heavens.
8:2 He is a minister of the sanctuary and of the true tent, which the Lord pitched, not a human being.
8:3 Every high priest is put in office to offer gifts and sacrifices, so this one too had to have something to offer.
8:4 On earth he would not even be a priest, because priests are already there offering the gifts the law prescribes.
8:5 What they serve is a copy and a shadow of the things in heaven. That is why Moses, when he was about to finish the tent, was warned by God: "See to it," he says, "that you make everything by the pattern you were shown on the mountain."
8:6 But now the ministry he has been given is far greater, just as the covenant he is mediator of is better. That covenant has been made law on the basis of better promises.
8:7 If nothing had been wrong with that first covenant, no one would have looked for room for a second.
8:8 But God finds fault with them when he says: "Look, days are coming, says the Lord, when I will bring a new covenant to completion with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah.
8:9 It will not be like the covenant I made with their ancestors on the day I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt. Because they did not stay in my covenant, I for my part neglected them, says the Lord.
8:10 This is the covenant I will make with the house of Israel after those days, says the Lord: I will put my laws into their minds, and on their hearts I will write them. I will be their God, and they will be my people.
8:11 They will not teach one another, each his fellow citizen and each his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord.' For they will all know me, from the smallest of them to the greatest.
8:12 For I will deal mercifully with their wrongs, and I will never again remember their sins."
8:13 When he says "new," he has made the first one old. And whatever is made old and is aging is close to disappearing.
## The tent on earth and its worship
9:1 Now the first covenant also had rules for worship, and a sanctuary that belonged to this world.
9:2 A tent was set up, the first one. In it were the lampstand, the table and the loaves that were set out. This is called the holy place.
9:3 Behind the second veil stood the tent called the most holy place.
9:4 It had a golden altar of incense and the ark of the covenant, covered with gold all around. In the ark were a golden jar holding the manna, Aaron's staff that had budded, and the tablets of the covenant.
9:5 Over the ark stood the cherubim of glory, spreading their shadow over the atonement lid. Now is not the time to speak about these things one by one.
9:6 With things arranged this way, the priests keep going into the first tent, carrying out the acts of worship.
9:7 The second tent, though, is entered by the high priest alone, once a year, and never without blood. He offers it for himself and for the sins the people committed in ignorance.
9:8 The Holy Spirit was making this plain: as long as the first tent still stood, the way into the sanctuary had not yet been brought into view.
9:9 It is a parable for the time now present. Under it gifts and sacrifices are offered that cannot make the worshiper perfect in his conscience.
9:10 They are only about food and drink and different kinds of washing: rules for the flesh, laid on people until the time came to set things straight.
## Christ goes in with his own blood
9:11 But when Christ arrived as high priest of the good things that have now come, he went through the greater and more perfect tent, not made by human hands, that is, not of this creation.
9:12 He entered the sanctuary once for all, not through the blood of goats and calves but through his own blood, and so won eternal redemption.
9:13 For if the blood of goats and bulls, and a heifer's ashes sprinkled on people who have become unclean, make them holy so that their flesh is made clean,
9:14 how much more will the blood of Christ, who offered himself flawless to God through the eternal Spirit, clean our conscience from dead works, so that we may worship the living God!
## A covenant and a will
9:15 This is why he stands as mediator of a new covenant: a death has taken place that sets people free from the transgressions committed under the first covenant, so that those who have been called may receive the promise of the eternal inheritance.
9:16 For in the case of a will, someone must bring proof that the one who made it has died.
9:17 A will is firm only in the case of the dead, since it is never in force while the one who made it is alive.
9:18 So not even the first covenant was put into force apart from blood.
9:19 For Moses spoke every command of the law to all the people. Then he took the blood of the calves and the goats, along with water, scarlet wool and hyssop, and sprinkled both the scroll itself and all the people.
9:20 He said, "This is the blood of the covenant that God has given you as a command."
9:21 In the same way he sprinkled the tent and all the containers used in its service with the blood.
9:22 By the law's rules, almost everything is made clean with blood. And no forgiveness comes about without blood being poured out.
## The heavenly things and the one sacrifice
9:23 So then: the copies of what is in the heavens had to be made clean by these means, but the heavenly things themselves needed sacrifices better than these.
9:24 For Christ did not go into a sanctuary made by hands, a copy of the true one. He went into heaven itself, to appear now before the face of God on our behalf.
9:25 Nor did he go in to offer himself again and again, the way the high priest enters the sanctuary every year with someone else's blood.
9:26 If that were so, he would have needed to suffer many times, from the time the world was founded. But now, at the completion of the ages, he has been revealed once, to cancel sin through his sacrifice.
9:27 And just as it is in store for human beings to die once, and after this, judgment,
9:28 so Christ too was offered up once to bear the sins of many, and he will be seen a second time, apart from sin, by those who eagerly wait for him, for their salvation.
## A body you made ready for me
10:1 For the law holds a shadow of the good things to come, not the actual image of the things themselves. Year after year, for all time, the same sacrifices are offered, and by them the law can never make perfect those who come near.
10:2 If they could, the offerings would have come to an end, wouldn't they? For the worshipers, washed clean once, would no longer have sins on their conscience.
10:3 Instead, in these sacrifices sins are called to mind year after year.
10:4 There is no way the blood of bulls and goats can take sins away.
10:5 That is why, when he comes into the world, he says: "Sacrifice and offering you did not want, but you made a body ready for me.
10:6 Burnt offerings and sin offerings did not please you.
10:7 Then I said, 'Look, I have come (the scroll of the book has it written about me) to do your will, O God.'"
10:8 First he says, "Sacrifices and offerings, burnt offerings and sin offerings, you did not want, and they did not please you." These are the ones offered as the law requires.
10:9 Then he says, "Look, I have come to do your will." He removes the first so that he can set up the second.
10:10 It is by this will that we have been made holy, through the offering of Jesus Christ's body, once for all.
10:11 Every priest stands at his duties day after day and offers the same sacrifices over and over, though they can never strip sins away.
10:12 But this one offered one sacrifice for sins, good for all time, and took his seat at God's right hand.
10:13 From then on he is waiting until his enemies are set down as a footstool for his feet.
10:14 With one offering he has made perfect, for all time, the people who are being made holy.
10:15 The Holy Spirit is our witness too. For after he has said,
10:16 "This is the covenant I will make with them after those days, says the Lord: I will put my laws on their hearts, and on their minds I will write them,"
10:17 he goes on: "I will never again remember their sins and their lawless deeds."
10:18 Where these have been forgiven, there is no more offering for sin.
## Come near and hold on
10:19 So then, brothers: by the blood of Jesus we have the boldness to go into the sanctuary,
10:20 along a fresh and living way, which he has opened for us through the veil, that is, his flesh.
10:21 And we have a great priest over God's house.
10:22 So let us come near with a genuine heart, in faith that is fully sure, with hearts sprinkled free of a bad conscience and bodies bathed in clean water.
10:23 Let us keep a firm grip on the confession of our hope, never leaning aside, for the one who made the promise is trustworthy.
10:24 And let us pay attention to one another, so as to provoke love and good works.
10:25 We must not walk out on our gatherings, as some have made a habit of doing. Instead, let us encourage each other, and the closer you see the Day coming, the more you should do it.
## The second warning
10:26 For if we keep on sinning by choice after we have come to know the truth fully, there is no longer any sacrifice for sins left,
10:27 but a frightening wait for judgment and the fury of a fire about to eat up those who stand against him.
10:28 Whoever brushed aside the law of Moses died without pity on the word of two or three witnesses.
10:29 What do you think: how much worse a punishment will that person earn who has stamped on the Son of God, who has counted the blood of the covenant, the blood that made him holy, as something common, and who has treated the Spirit of grace with contempt?
10:30 For we know the one who said, "Vengeance is mine. I will pay back," and again, "The Lord will judge his people."
10:31 Falling into the hands of the living God is a terrifying thing.
## Do not throw away your boldness
10:32 But call to mind the days before, after you had received the light and you stood your ground through a great contest of suffering.
10:33 Sometimes you were put on display like a show in the theater, with insults and troubles. Sometimes you became partners of those who were treated like that.
10:34 You felt for the prisoners, and when your property was seized you took it with joy, knowing that you had a better possession of your own, one that lasts.
10:35 So do not toss aside your boldness: a great reward comes with it.
10:36 What you need is endurance, so that once you have done God's will you will receive the promise.
10:37 For "in just a little while, a very little, the one who is coming will come, and he will not be late.
10:38 But my righteous one will live by faith. And if he shrinks back, my soul is not pleased with him."
10:39 But we are not people of shrinking back, which leads to ruin. We are people of faith, which keeps the soul safe.
## The roll call of faith
11:1 Now faith means assurance about what is hoped for, and proof of realities no one can see.
11:2 It was by faith that the people of long ago were given a good witness.
11:3 By faith we understand that the ages were set in order by a word from God, so that what we see has not come into being out of what appears.
11:4 By faith Abel offered God a fuller sacrifice than Cain's. Through it he had witness borne to him that he was righteous, since God himself gave witness over his gifts. And through it he still speaks, though he died.
11:5 By faith Enoch was carried off, so that he did not see death, and "he was not found, because God had carried him off." For before he was carried off, it was witnessed of him that he had pleased God.
11:6 Without faith no one can please him at all. Whoever comes near to God has to believe that he is, and that he pays back those who search hard for him.
11:7 By faith Noah, given warning about things no one could see yet, acted out of reverence and built an ark so that his household would be saved. Through it he passed sentence on the world, and he came to inherit the righteousness that matches faith.
11:8 By faith Abraham, when God called him, did as he was told and set out for a place he was going to receive as his inheritance. He left, and he did not know where he was heading.
11:9 By faith he settled as a resident alien in the promised land, as though it belonged to others, and made his home in tents with Isaac and Jacob, who shared the same promise as heirs.
11:10 For he was waiting for the city with foundations, the city whose architect and maker is God.
11:11 By faith, together with Sarah herself, he received power to father children, even past the age for it, since he regarded the one who had promised as trustworthy.
11:12 That is why from one man, a man whose body was already as good as dead, there came children as many as the stars of the sky and as the sand along the sea's edge, too many to count.
11:13 All these people died still holding to faith. They had not received what was promised; they had only seen it from a long way off and greeted it. And they admitted that they were foreigners and temporary residents on the earth.
11:14 When people talk like that, they show that they are searching for a country of their own.
11:15 If the country in their minds had been the one they came out of, they would have had time to turn back.
11:16 Instead they are reaching for a better one, a country in heaven. So God is not ashamed of them, not ashamed to be named as their God, since he has a city ready for them.
11:17 By faith Abraham offered Isaac when God put him to the test. He had welcomed the promises, and yet he was offering his only son,
11:18 the son he had been told about: "It is through Isaac that offspring will be named for you."
11:19 He reckoned that God could raise people even from the dead, and from there, in a kind of parable, he did get him back.
11:20 By faith Isaac gave Jacob and Esau a blessing that reached even to things still to come.
11:21 By faith Jacob, at the point of death, gave a blessing to each of Joseph's sons, and he bowed down over the top of his staff.
11:22 By faith Joseph, when he was dying, spoke of the going out of the sons of Israel and gave instructions about his bones.
11:23 By faith, when Moses was born, his parents kept him hidden three months. They saw he was a fine child, and the king's decree did not frighten them.
11:24 By faith Moses, once he was grown, said no to being known as the son of Pharaoh's daughter.
11:25 He would rather suffer harm with the people of God than have the short-lived enjoyment of sin.
11:26 He judged that the disgrace of the Messiah was greater wealth than Egypt's treasures, because he kept his eyes on the reward.
11:27 By faith he left Egypt behind, unafraid of the king's rage. He kept going strong, as if he could see the one who cannot be seen.
11:28 By faith he kept the Passover and the splashing of the blood, so that the one who destroyed the firstborn would not touch them.
11:29 By faith they went through the Red Sea as if it were dry ground, but the Egyptians who tried it were swallowed up.
11:30 By faith Jericho's walls fell, after they had been circled for seven days.
11:31 By faith Rahab the prostitute was not destroyed along with the disobedient, since she had taken in the spies in peace.
11:32 What else can I say? Time will run out on me if I tell the story of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel and the prophets.
11:33 Through faith they defeated kingdoms, did what was righteous, gained promises, shut lions' mouths,
11:34 put out the power of fire, got away from the mouths of the sword, found strength when they were weak, grew mighty in battle, and routed the armies of foreigners.
11:35 Women got their dead back by resurrection. Others were tortured on the rack and would not take the release they were offered, so that they could win a better resurrection.
11:36 Others went through jeering and lashes, and besides that, chains and prison.
11:37 They were stoned. They were sawed in two. They died by murder with the sword. They wandered about dressed in sheepskins and goat hides, going without, hard pressed, ill treated.
11:38 The world was not worth them. They drifted through lonely places and mountains, caves and holes in the ground.
11:39 And all of them, though given a good witness through faith, did not receive the promise.
11:40 God had something better in view for us, so that they would not be made perfect without us.
## Run the race
12:1 So then, with so huge a cloud of witnesses surrounding us, we too must strip off every burden and the sin that wraps itself around us so easily. Let us keep running the race that lies ahead of us, and keep at it with endurance.
12:2 We fix our eyes on Jesus, the Founder and perfecter of faith. For the joy that lay ahead of him he endured a cross, scorning its shame, and he has taken his seat at the right of God's throne.
12:3 Weigh it up: think of the one who put up with so much hostility against himself from sinners, so that your souls do not tire and go slack.
12:4 Not yet have you held out to the point of blood in your fight against sin.
## The discipline of sons
12:5 And you have forgotten the exhortation that speaks to you as sons: "My son, do not think little of the Lord's discipline, and do not give up when he rebukes you.
12:6 For whoever the Lord loves, he disciplines, and he whips every son he takes in."
12:7 What you are enduring is for your discipline. God is dealing with you as his sons. Is there a son whose father does not discipline him?
12:8 But if you are without discipline, the discipline all sons have been partners in, then you are not sons at all but children born outside marriage.
12:9 Then again, we had the fathers of our flesh to discipline us, and we showed them respect. Will we not much more put ourselves under the Father of spirits, and live?
12:10 Those fathers trained us for a few days, by what looked right to them. But he does it for what really helps us, so that we may have a share in his holiness.
12:11 While it is happening, no discipline feels like a joy. It feels like grief. Afterward, though, it hands back a peaceful fruit, righteousness, to those who have been put through their training by it.
12:12 So straighten up the hands that hang slack and the knees that have gone weak.
12:13 Make level tracks for your feet, so the limping leg is not twisted out of joint but is healed instead.
## Esau's warning
12:14 Go after peace with everyone, and after holiness: without it no one will see the Lord.
12:15 Keep watch so that no one drops back from God's grace, so that no root of bitterness shoots up and makes trouble, staining many people,
12:16 and so that no one is sexually immoral, or treats holy things as nothing, as Esau did. In exchange for one meal he sold his own birthright.
12:17 You know that afterward he wanted to receive the blessing as his inheritance, and he was turned away. He found no place for repentance, though he searched for it with tears.
## Sinai and Zion
12:18 For you have not come near to something you can touch, a fire burning, black darkness, deep gloom and a whirlwind,
12:19 the blare of a trumpet and a voice of spoken words, a voice that made its hearers plead that not another word be added for them.
12:20 They could not stand what was being commanded: "Even an animal that touches the mountain must be stoned."
12:21 So fearful was what appeared that Moses said, "I am full of terror, and I am shaking."
12:22 But you have come near to Mount Zion, to the city of the living God, heavenly Jerusalem, to angels by the tens of thousands,
12:23 to a festive gathering, the assembly of the firstborn whose names are listed in the heavens, to God the judge of all, to the spirits of righteous people who have been made perfect,
12:24 to Jesus, who stands between as mediator of a new covenant, and to the blood of sprinkling, which speaks better than Abel.
## A kingdom that cannot be shaken
12:25 Be careful not to turn down the one who is speaking. Those people did not get away when they turned down the one who gave them warning on earth. Far less will we get away if we turn our backs on the one who speaks from the heavens.
12:26 Back then his voice made the earth shake. But now he has made a promise: "Once more I will shake not only the earth but heaven too."
12:27 That "once more" makes clear that the things that are shaken will be moved away, since they are things that were made, so that the things that are not shaken may remain.
12:28 So, since we are receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, let us give thanks, and so worship God in a way that pleases him, with reverence and dread.
12:29 For our God really is a devouring fire.
## Life together
13:1 Let love for the brothers remain.
13:2 Do not forget to welcome strangers, for by this some people have had angels as guests without knowing it.
13:3 Keep the prisoners in mind as though you were chained with them, and those who are badly treated, since you too live in a body.
13:4 Marriage is to be valued by all, and the marriage bed is to stay unstained. God will bring the sexually immoral and adulterers to judgment.
13:5 Do not let your life be ruled by love of money. Be satisfied with what you have. For he himself has said, "I will never let you down, and I will never abandon you."
13:6 So we take courage and say, "The Lord is the one who helps me, and I will not be afraid. What can a human being do to me?"
13:7 Call to mind your leaders, the ones who told you the word of God. Look closely at how their way of living ended, and imitate their faith.
13:8 Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today, and for all the ages.
## Outside the camp
13:9 Do not let teachings of many colors and foreign origin carry you off. It is good for the heart to be made firm by grace and not by foods; those who have walked by them have gained nothing.
13:10 We have an altar, and the people who worship in the tent have no right to eat from it.
13:11 The high priest carries the blood of animals into the sanctuary as an offering for sin, but their bodies are burned up outside the camp.
13:12 That is why Jesus, too, suffered outside the gate, so that by his own blood he might make the people holy.
13:13 So let us go out to him, outside the camp, carrying his disgrace.
13:14 We do not have a city here that remains. We are searching for the one that is coming.
13:15 So through him let us keep bringing up to God a sacrifice of praise, the fruit of lips that confess his name.
13:16 And do not forget kindness and sharing. Those are the kinds of sacrifices God takes pleasure in.
13:17 Listen to your leaders and yield to them. They lose sleep watching over your souls, as people who must give an account. Make it so they can do this with joy and not with sighs, since that would do you no good.
## Prayer, blessing and farewell
13:18 Keep praying for us. We are convinced our conscience is good, since we want to behave well in everything.
13:19 I am appealing to you even more to do this, so that I may be returned to you soon.
13:20 Now may the God of peace, who led up out of the dead the great shepherd of the sheep in the blood of an eternal covenant, our Lord Jesus,
13:21 fit you out with every good thing for doing his will, and do in us what is pleasing in his sight, through Jesus Christ. To him be glory forever and ever. Amen.
13:22 I ask you, brothers: put up with this word of exhortation. After all, I have sent you only a short letter.
13:23 Know that our brother Timothy has been released. If he comes soon enough, I will bring him along when I see you.
13:24 Greet all your leaders and all the saints. The people from Italy greet you.
13:25 Grace be with all of you.
