## A prophet in Josiah's days
1:1 The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.
## The whole earth swept away
1:2 I will bring everything to an end, a complete end, / off the surface of the ground, the LORD declares.
1:3 I will end people and animals. / I will end the birds of the sky and the fish of the sea, / and the stumbling blocks together with the wicked. / I will cut humankind off / from the surface of the ground, the LORD declares.
## Judah's idols
1:4 I will reach out my hand against Judah / and against everyone who lives in Jerusalem. / I will cut off from this place what is left of Baal, / the name of the idol priests along with the priests,
1:5 those who bow down to the army of the heavens / on their roofs, / those who bow down and swear oaths to the LORD / and also swear oaths by Milcom,
1:6 those who have backed away from following the LORD, / who have never sought the LORD / and never inquired of him.
## Silence before the Lord GOD
1:7 Hush before the Lord GOD, / for the day of the LORD is close. / The LORD has prepared a sacrifice. / He has made holy the ones he invited.
1:8 It will happen on the day of the LORD's sacrifice: / I will bring punishment on the officials, / on the king's sons, / and on everyone dressed in foreign clothing.
1:9 On that day I will bring punishment on everyone / who jumps over the doorsill, / and on those who pack their masters' house full / of violence and deceit.
1:10 On that day, the LORD declares, / a cry of alarm will sound from the Fish Gate, / a howl from the Second District, / and a great shattering from the hills.
1:11 Wail, you who live in the Mortar, / for all the people of Canaan have been wiped out, / and everyone loaded with silver is cut off.
1:12 At that time, with lamps in hand, I will search Jerusalem. / I will bring punishment on the men who have thickened on their dregs, / who say in their hearts, / "The LORD will do no good, and he will do no harm."
1:13 Their wealth will become plunder, / and their houses a ruin. / They will build houses and not live in them. / They will plant vineyards and not drink their wine.
## The great day of the LORD
1:14 Close is the great day of the LORD, / close and rushing on fast. / The noise of the day of the LORD is bitter. / There the warrior shouts out.
1:15 That day is a day of fury, / a day of trouble and distress, / a day of devastation and waste, / a day of darkness and gloom, / a day of cloud and deep darkness,
1:16 a day of the ram's horn and the battle cry / against the fortified cities / and the high corner towers.
1:17 I will squeeze humankind with distress, / and they will grope along like the blind, / because they have sinned against the LORD. / Their blood will be spilled like dust, / and their bodies like dung.
1:18 Their silver, their gold: / neither will be able to rescue them / on the day of the LORD's fury. / The whole earth will be eaten up / by the fire of his jealousy, / for he will make a complete end, / a terrifying one, / of everyone who lives on the earth.
## Seek the LORD
2:1 Come together and gather yourselves, / nation without shame,
2:2 before the decree takes birth, / before the day passes like chaff, / before the LORD's burning anger comes on you, / before the day of the LORD's anger comes on you.
2:3 All you humble people of the land, / you who have carried out his justice, / seek the LORD. / Seek righteousness. Seek humility. / Perhaps you will be hidden / on the day of the LORD's anger.
## The Philistine coast
2:4 For Gaza will be abandoned, / and Ashkelon will become a ruin. / At noon they will drive Ashdod out, / and Ekron will be uprooted.
2:5 Woe to those who live along the sea, / the nation of the Kerethites! / Canaan, land of the Philistines, / the word of the LORD is against you: / I will destroy you until no one is left living here.
2:6 The coast will become grazing land, / with dug wells for shepherds / and pens for sheep.
2:7 The coast will belong to what is left of the house of Judah. / There they will graze their flocks. / At evening they will lie down / in the houses of Ashkelon. / For the LORD their God will attend to them / and turn their fortunes around.
## Moab and Ammon
2:8 I have heard Moab's insults / and the Ammonites' taunts, / how they insulted my people / and puffed themselves up against their territory.
2:9 So as I live, the LORD of Armies declares, / the God of Israel, / Moab will become like Sodom, / and the Ammonites like Gomorrah: / a place of nettles, a pit of salt, / a ruin forever. / What is left of my people will plunder them, / and the survivors of my nation will take them over.
2:10 This will be theirs in place of their pride, / because they insulted the people of the LORD of Armies / and puffed themselves up against them.
2:11 The LORD will be a terror to them, / for he will make all the gods of the earth waste away. / Then everyone will bow down to him from their own place, / all the coastlands of the nations.
## Cush, Assyria and Nineveh
2:12 Cushites, you too / will lie slain by my sword.
2:13 He will reach out his hand against the north / and wipe out Assyria. / He will turn Nineveh into a ruin, / dry land like the desert.
2:14 Herds will lie down in the middle of it, / animals of every sort, a whole nation of them. / The desert owl and the hedgehog / will spend the night on the tops of its pillars. / From the window comes a singing voice, / and wreckage lies on the doorsill, / for he has torn the cedar paneling bare.
2:15 This is the city that exulted, / that sat secure, / that said in her heart, / "I am, and besides me there is nothing." / How she has become a ruin, / a place for animals to lie down! / Everyone who passes by her / hisses and waves a hand.
## The defiled city
3:1 Woe to the rebellious and defiled city, / the oppressing city!
3:2 She has not listened to any voice. / She has not accepted correction. / She has not trusted in the LORD. / She has not come near to her God.
3:3 Her officials in her midst are roaring lions. / Her judges are wolves of the evening / that leave nothing to gnaw on by morning.
3:4 Her prophets are reckless, / treacherous people. / Her priests treat what is holy as common / and treat the law with violence.
3:5 The LORD is righteous in her midst. / He does no wrong. / Morning after morning he brings his justice to the light. / It does not fail. / But the wrongdoer knows no shame.
3:6 I have cut off nations. / Their corner towers lie in ruins. / I have emptied their streets, / and no one passes through. / Their cities are laid waste: / no one is left, no one lives there.
3:7 I thought, "Surely you will fear me and take correction. / Then her home will not be cut off / by any of the punishments I set for her." / But they got up early / and corrupted everything they did.
## Pure lips and a humble people
3:8 So wait for me, the LORD declares, / for the day I rise up as a witness. / For this is my decision: / I will gather nations and assemble kingdoms, / and pour my indignation out on them, / all of my burning anger. / The whole earth will be eaten up / by the fire of my jealousy.
3:9 For then I will change the lips of the peoples into pure lips, / so that all of them call on the name of the LORD / and serve him side by side.
3:10 My worshipers, the daughter of my scattered ones, / will bring my offering / from beyond the rivers of Cush.
3:11 On that day you will not be ashamed / of any of your deeds, / by which you rebelled against me. / For then I will remove from your midst / those who revel in your pride, / and you will not lift yourself up again / on my holy mountain.
3:12 I will leave in your midst / a people afflicted and poor, / and they will take refuge / in the name of the LORD.
3:13 The remnant of Israel will do no injustice / and tell no lie. / A tongue of deceit / will not be found in their mouths. / They will graze and lie down, / with no one to frighten them.
## Rejoice, Daughter Zion
3:14 Daughter Zion, give a ringing cry. / Israel, raise a shout. / Daughter Jerusalem, rejoice / and celebrate with your whole heart.
3:15 The LORD has removed your judgments. / He has swept your enemy away. / The LORD, Israel's King, is in your midst. / You will not fear harm again.
3:16 On that day it will be said to Jerusalem, / "Do not be afraid, Zion. / Do not let your hands go slack.
3:17 The LORD your God is in your midst, / a warrior who rescues. / He will be glad over you with joy. / He will be quiet in his love. / He will cheer over you with a ringing shout.
3:18 I will gather those who grieve over the appointed feast, / who were yours. / The reproach on her was a burden.
3:19 Look, at that time I will deal / with everyone who afflicted you. / I will rescue those who limp / and gather those who were driven away. / I will make them a praise and a name / in every land where they were shamed.
3:20 At that time I will bring you in, / and when the time comes to gather you, / I will make you a name and a praise / among all the peoples of the earth, / when I turn your fortunes around / before your eyes," the LORD says.
