## The superscription
1:1 The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah. He saw it about Samaria and Jerusalem.
## The LORD comes down
1:2 Hear this, peoples, every one of you. / Pay attention, earth, and everything that fills it. / Let the Lord GOD stand as a witness against you, / the Lord from his holy temple.
1:3 See, the LORD is about to go out from where he is. / He will come down and stride across the high places of the earth.
1:4 Under him the mountains melt, / and the valleys split open, / like wax near a fire, / like water spilled down a hillside.
1:5 All of this comes from Jacob's rebellion, / from the sins of Israel's house. / Who is behind Jacob's rebellion? / Is it not Samaria? / Who is behind Judah's high places? / Is it not Jerusalem?
1:6 So I will turn Samaria into a ruin-heap in the open country, / into ground for planting vines. / I will roll her stones down into the valley / and strip her foundations bare.
1:7 All her carved images will be beaten to pieces. / All her wages will go up in fire. / I will turn all her idols into a wasteland. / She gathered them from a prostitute's pay, / and to a prostitute's pay they will go back.
## Mourning for Judah's towns
1:8 That is why I will mourn and howl. / I will walk around barefoot and stripped bare. / I will wail like the jackals / and moan like the ostriches.
1:9 Samaria's wounds cannot be healed. / The blow has come as far as Judah. / It has touched the gate of my people, / all the way to Jerusalem.
1:10 Do not tell it in Gath. / Do not weep at all. / In Beth-leaphrah / roll around in the dust.
1:11 Go on your way in nakedness and shame, you who live in Shaphir. / The people of Zaanan do not come out. / Beth-ezel is wailing: / it takes from you the ground it stood on.
1:12 The people of Maroth ache and wait for good, / but disaster has come down from the LORD / to the gate of Jerusalem.
1:13 People of Lachish, hitch the swift horses to the chariot. / You were the start of sin for Daughter Zion, / since Israel's rebellions were found in you.
1:14 Because of this you will hand over farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath. / The houses of Achzib will turn out to be a lie / to the kings of Israel.
1:15 Once more I will bring the one who takes possession to you, / people of Mareshah. / To Adullam will come the glory of Israel.
1:16 Make yourself bald and cut off your hair / for the children you love best. / Widen your bald patch like the vulture's, / for they will be taken from you into exile.
## Woe to the land-grabbers
2:1 Woe to those who plan trouble / and do evil from their beds! / When morning comes, they carry it out, / since their hands are strong enough.
2:2 They crave fields and grab them. / They crave houses and take them. / They crush a man and his household, / a person and the land he inherited.
2:3 So the LORD says this: / Look, I am planning a disaster against this clan. / You cannot slip your necks out from under it, / and you will not walk with your heads held high, / for it will be an evil time.
2:4 On that day someone will lift a taunt against you / and sing a mournful dirge: / "We are completely ruined! / He trades away my people's share. / How he takes it from me! / He hands out our fields to a traitor."
2:5 So you will have no one / to stretch a measuring line for you by lot / in the LORD's assembly.
## Do not rant
2:6 "Do not rant," they rant. / "No one should rant about these things. / Shame will not be turned back."
2:7 Is this what is said, house of Jacob? / "Is the LORD's Spirit cut short? / Are these his deeds?" / Do my words not do good / for the one who walks straight?
2:8 Only yesterday my people rose up to be an enemy. / You pull the fine cloak off the robe / of those who pass by in safety, / of men coming home from war.
2:9 You throw the women of my people / out of the houses they love. / From their children you take away / my honor for good.
2:10 Get up and go! / This is no resting place, / because it is unclean. / It will ruin you, and the ruin will be painful.
2:11 If a man walked around full of wind and told lies, / "I will rant to you of wine and beer," / he would be this people's ranter.
## The breaker and the flock
2:12 I will gather you, all of you, Jacob, without fail. / I will bring together what is left of Israel. / I will set them side by side like sheep in an enclosure, / like a herd in the middle of its pasture. / The place will be loud with people.
2:13 The one who breaks through goes up ahead of them. / They break out, go through the gate and leave by it. / Their king goes across in front of them, / and the LORD walks at their head.
## Hear, you rulers
3:1 I said: / Hear this, you who head Jacob, / you who rule the house of Israel. / Should you not be the ones who know justice?
3:2 You hate good and love evil. / You rip the skin off them / and the flesh off their bones.
3:3 You eat the flesh of my people. / You strip off their skin. / You smash their bones to pieces / and chop them up as for the pot, / like flesh in a cauldron.
3:4 Then they will cry out to the LORD, / but he will not answer them. / At that time he will hide his face from them, / because of the evil they did in their deeds.
## Prophets who sell their words
3:5 The LORD says this about the prophets / who lead my people astray: / They bite with their teeth and shout, "Peace!" / But whoever puts nothing in their mouths, / against him they declare a holy war.
3:6 So you will have night in place of visions, / and darkness in place of divining. / The sun will sink on the prophets, / and over them the day will turn black.
3:7 The visionaries will be ashamed, / and the diviners will blush. / All of them will cover their upper lips, / because there is no answer from God.
3:8 As for me, I am full of strength: / the LORD's Spirit is with me, along with justice and might, / so I can tell Jacob his rebellion / and Israel his sin.
## Zion will be plowed
3:9 Hear this, you heads of Jacob's house, / you rulers of Israel's house, / you who abhor justice / and bend crooked everything that is straight.
3:10 You build Zion with blood / and Jerusalem with wrongdoing.
3:11 Her heads judge for a bribe. / Her priests give rulings for a fee. / Her prophets read the future for silver. / And on the LORD they lean, saying, / "Is the LORD not in the middle of us? / No harm can come on us."
3:12 So because of you / Zion will be plowed like a field. / Jerusalem will be heaps of rubble, / and the mountain of the house will be high ground in a forest.
## The mountain of the LORD
4:1 In the days to come / the mountain where the LORD's house stands / will be set firm at the head of the mountains. / It will be lifted higher than the hills, / and like a river, peoples will run to it.
4:2 Many nations will set out, saying, / "Come on, let us climb the LORD's mountain, / up to the house of Jacob's God. / He will teach us from his ways, / and we will walk on his roads." / For from Zion comes teaching, / and from Jerusalem, the LORD's word.
4:3 He will be judge between many peoples / and set things right for mighty nations, however far away. / They will pound their swords into plowshares / and their spears into pruning knives. / No nation will raise a sword against another nation, / and they will never again learn how to make war.
4:4 Each person will sit beneath a vine of their own / and beneath a fig tree of their own, / and no one will make them afraid, / for the mouth of the LORD of Armies has spoken.
4:5 For all the peoples walk, / each in the name of its own god, / but we will walk in the name of the LORD our God / for ever and ever.
## The lame become a remnant
4:6 On that day, the LORD declares, / I will gather the lame / and bring together the outcasts, / the ones I have brought harm to.
4:7 I will make the lame a remnant, / and those driven far off a mighty nation. / The LORD will be king over them on Mount Zion / from now on and forever.
4:8 And you, Tower of the Flock, / hill of Daughter Zion: / it will come to you, / the first dominion will come, / the kingdom of Daughter Jerusalem.
## Labor pains, Babylon and the threshing floor
4:9 Now why do you cry out so loudly? / Is there no king in you? / Has your counselor died, / that pangs grip you like a woman giving birth?
4:10 Twist in pain and push, Daughter Zion, / like a woman in labor, / for now you will leave the city / and live in open country. / You will go as far as Babylon. / There you will be rescued, / and there the LORD will redeem you / from your enemies' grasp.
4:11 Now many nations have gathered against you. / They say, "Let her be made unclean, / and let our eyes gloat over Zion."
4:12 But they do not know the LORD's thoughts. / They have not grasped his purpose: / he has gathered them like cut grain onto the threshing floor.
4:13 Get up and thresh, Daughter Zion. / I will make your horn iron / and your hooves bronze, / and you will beat many peoples to pieces. / I will set apart for destruction their stolen gain for the LORD, / their wealth for the Lord of all the earth.
