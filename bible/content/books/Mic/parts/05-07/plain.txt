## The Siege, and the Ruler from Bethlehem
5:1 Now you will cut yourself, Daughter of Troops. / A siege has been set against us. / They are hitting the cheek of Israel's judge with a rod.
5:2 But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, / are too small to count among the clans of Judah. / From you one will come out for me / to be ruler in Israel. / His origins reach back to the distant past, / to days of long ago.
5:3 So he will hand them over / until the time a woman in labor gives birth. / Then the rest of his brothers will come back / to the Israelites.
5:4 He will stand and shepherd them / by the LORD's strength, / by the majesty of the name of the LORD his God. / They will stay, / for now his greatness will reach / to the ends of the earth.
5:5 This one will be peace. / If Assyria enters our land / and treads on our fortresses, / we will set up seven shepherds against him, / and eight leaders of men.
5:6 With the sword they will shepherd the land of Assyria, / and at its gates the land of Nimrod. / He will rescue us from Assyria / when it enters our land / and treads across our borders.
## The Remnant among the Nations
5:7 The remnant of Jacob will be, / in the middle of many peoples, / like dew from the LORD, / like heavy showers on the grass, / which put no hope in anyone / and do not wait for human beings.
5:8 The remnant of Jacob will be among the nations, / in the middle of many peoples, / like a lion among the animals of the forest, / like a young lion among the sheep flocks, / which tramples and tears whenever it passes through, / with no one to save.
5:9 Let your hand rise above those who oppose you, / and let all your enemies be cut off.
## The LORD Clears Out the Land
5:10 On that day, the LORD declares, / I will cut off your horses from your midst / and wipe out your chariots.
5:11 I will cut off the cities of your land / and pull down every one of your fortresses.
5:12 I will cut the sorceries out of your hands, / and you will have no sign-readers.
5:13 I will cut your carved images and standing stones / out from among you, / and never again will you bow down / to what your hands made.
5:14 I will uproot the Asherah poles from your midst / and demolish your cities.
5:15 I will carry out vengeance in anger and rage / against the nations that have not listened.
## The LORD's Case against His People
6:1 Listen, please, to what the LORD is saying. / Get up, argue your case with the mountains, / and let the hills hear your voice.
6:2 Listen to the LORD's case, you mountains, / you enduring foundations of the earth. / For the LORD has a case against his people, / and with Israel he will argue it out.
6:3 My people, what have I done to you? / How have I worn you out? / Testify against me.
6:4 I brought you up from the land of Egypt, / and I ransomed you from the house of slaves. / I sent Moses, Aaron and Miriam ahead of you.
6:5 My people, call to mind, please, / the plan of Balak king of Moab, / and the answer Balaam son of Beor gave him, / from Shittim to Gilgal, / so that you may know the right things the LORD has done.
## What the LORD Asks
6:6 What can I bring when I come to meet the LORD / and bow down before God on high? / Should I meet him with burnt offerings, / with year-old calves?
6:7 Will the LORD take pleasure in thousands of rams, / in tens of thousands of streams of oil? / Shall I give my firstborn for my rebellion, / the child of my own body for the sin of my life?
6:8 Human being, he has told you what is good. / What is the LORD asking from you? / Only to do justice, to love loyal love, / and to walk with your God in humility.
## The City of False Scales
6:9 The voice of the LORD calls out to the city, / and sound wisdom sees your name. / Listen, tribe, and whoever appointed it.
6:10 Is there still a wicked house, with treasures of wickedness, / and a skimpy ephah, which is cursed?
6:11 Can I be clean with wicked scales / and a bag of false weights?
6:12 Its rich people are full of violence, / its residents speak falsehood, / and the tongue in their mouth is deceit.
6:13 So I in turn will make you sick by striking you. / I will leave you desolate because of your sins.
6:14 You will eat, yet you will never be full, / and your emptiness will stay inside you. / You will carry things away but not get them to safety, / and what you do get to safety / I will give to the sword.
6:15 You will sow, yet reap nothing. / You will tread olives but never rub yourself with oil. / You will make new wine but never drink the wine.
6:16 The rules of Omri are kept, / and all the works of the house of Ahab, / and you walk in their schemes. / So I will make you a horror, / and its residents a thing to hiss at, / and you will bear the disgrace of my people.
## A Lament in a Land with No Loyal Person
7:1 Woe to me! / I have become like the gathering of summer fruit, / like the gleanings after the grape harvest. / Not one cluster is left to eat, / not one early fig, which my appetite craves.
7:2 The loyal have vanished from the land, / and there is no upright person among people. / They all set ambushes for blood, / and each man traps his brother in a net.
7:3 Both hands are set on evil, to do it well. / The official asks, and the judge wants a bribe. / The powerful man speaks the craving of his own self, / and they braid it together.
7:4 Even their finest is like a thorn bush, / and their most upright is worse than a hedge of thorns. / The day your watchmen spoke of, your punishment, has come. / Now their confusion will begin.
7:5 Do not believe a neighbor. / Do not trust a close friend. / Guard the openings of your mouth / from the woman who lies against your chest.
7:6 For a son dishonors his father. / A daughter stands up against her mother, / and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law. / The people of a man's own household are his enemies.
## I Will Wait for the LORD
7:7 I, though, will keep watch for the LORD. / I will wait for the God who saves me. / My God will listen to me.
7:8 Enemy of mine, do not gloat over me. / I have fallen, but I will get up. / I sit in the dark, but the LORD is my light.
7:9 I carry the LORD's anger, since I have sinned against him, / until he takes up my case and does me justice. / He will lead me out into the daylight, / and I will gaze at his righteousness.
7:10 My enemy will see it, / and shame will cover her. / She is the one who said to me, "Where is he, the LORD your God?" / My eyes will gaze at her. / Soon she will be trampled like street mud.
## A Day for Building Walls
7:11 It will be a day to build your walls. / On that day the boundary will lie far away.
7:12 They will come to you on that day, / out of Assyria and from Egypt's cities, / and from Egypt up to the River, / from one sea to another and from one mountain to another.
7:13 But the earth will turn into a wasteland / because of the people who live in it, / and the fruit of their deeds.
## Shepherd Your People
7:14 Be a shepherd to your people with your rod, / to the flock that is your own inheritance, / who live by themselves in a forest, / in the middle of orchards. / May they feed in Bashan and Gilead / like the days long ago.
7:15 I will show them wonders, / as in the days when you left the land of Egypt.
7:16 Nations will see and feel shame / over all their strength. / They will put a hand over their mouths. / Their ears will go deaf.
7:17 They will lick up dust like a snake, / like creatures that crawl on the ground. / They will tremble as they leave their strongholds. / They will come in dread to the LORD our God, / and they will fear you.
## Who Is a God Like You?
7:18 Who is a God like you, / who forgives guilt and passes over the rebellion / of the remnant of his inheritance? / He will not hold on to his anger forever, / since loyal love is what he delights in.
7:19 He will turn back and have compassion on us. / He will subdue our guilt. / Into the depths of the sea / you will throw all their sins.
7:20 You will give faithfulness to Jacob, / loyal love to Abraham, / as you swore to our ancestors / from ancient days.
