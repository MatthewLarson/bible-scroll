## The shepherd who was chosen
1:0 This psalm, written in David's own hand, stands outside the count. It is from when he fought Goliath man to man.
1:1 Among my brothers I was small, / the youngest of my father's household. / I kept my father's sheep.
1:2 My hands made an instrument; / my fingers tuned a harp.
1:3 And who will tell my Lord? / The Lord himself, he himself listens.
1:4 He himself sent out his angel / and lifted me from my father's sheep, / and he anointed me with the oil of his anointing.
1:5 My brothers were good-looking and big, / but the Lord took no delight in them.
## The Philistine
1:6 I went out to face the Philistine, / and he called down curses on me by his idols.
1:7 But I drew his own sword / and cut off his head, / and I lifted shame off the Israelites.
