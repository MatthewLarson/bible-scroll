## The book read in Babylon
1:1 These are the words of the book that Baruch wrote in Babylon. Baruch was the son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, son of Zedekiah, son of Hasadiah, son of Hilkiah.
1:2 It was the fifth year, the seventh day of the month, the time when the Chaldeans captured Jerusalem and set it on fire.
1:3 Baruch read the words of this book aloud for Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, to hear, and for all the people who came to the reading:
1:4 for the powerful men and the king's sons to hear, for the elders, and for all the people, from the least to the greatest, all who lived in Babylon by the river Sud.
1:5 They wept and fasted, and they prayed in the Lord's presence.
1:6 They gathered silver, each person as much as he could afford.
1:7 This they sent to Jerusalem: to the priest Joakim son of Hilkiah, son of Shallum, to the other priests, and to all the people found with him in Jerusalem.
1:8 That was when Baruch took the vessels of the Lord's house that had been carried out of the temple, to bring them back to the land of Judah. It was the tenth day of Sivan. They were silver vessels, made by Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah,
1:9 once Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had deported Jeconiah from Jerusalem, with the officials, the prisoners, the powerful men and the common people, and had brought him to Babylon.
1:10 Their message was: "Look, we have sent you silver. Use it to buy whole burnt offerings, sin offerings and frankincense. Make grain offerings, and bring them up to the altar of the Lord our God.
1:11 And pray that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon may live, and his son Belshazzar too, so that they last on earth as long as the sky does.
1:12 Then the Lord will give us strength and give light to our eyes. We will live in the shade of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and in the shade of his son Belshazzar. We will serve them for a long time and win their favor.
1:13 Pray to the Lord our God for us as well, because we sinned against the Lord our God, and even now the Lord's fury and his anger have not turned back from us.
1:14 You are to read out this book we are sending you and make confession with it in the Lord's house, on the day of the feast and on the days of its season.

## Righteousness belongs to the Lord
1:15 "This is what you are to say: Righteousness belongs to the Lord our God, but to us belongs the shame of our faces, as it is today: to the people of Judah, to the people living in Jerusalem,
1:16 to our kings and our officials, to our priests and our prophets, and to our ancestors.
1:17 We sinned before the Lord.
1:18 We disobeyed him. We did not listen to the voice of the Lord our God, to walk by the Lord's commands, which he set in front of us.
1:19 From the day the Lord brought our ancestors out of the land of Egypt until today, we have kept disobeying the Lord our God. We have carelessly refused to listen to his voice.
1:20 So the disasters have clung to us, and the curse that the Lord laid on Moses his servant on the day he brought our ancestors out of Egypt to give us a land flowing with milk and honey. It is as we see today.
1:21 We did not listen to the voice of the Lord our God in all the words of the prophets he sent to us.
1:22 Each of us went off after the plans of his own wicked heart, to serve other gods and to do what is evil in the eyes of the Lord our God.
2:1 "So the Lord carried out the word he spoke against us and against our judges who judged Israel, against our kings and our officials, and against the people of Israel and Judah.
2:2 Nothing like it has ever been done anywhere under the whole sky as he did in Jerusalem, just as it is written in the law of Moses:
2:3 that men among us would eat the flesh of their own sons and the flesh of their own daughters.
2:4 He made them subjects of all the kingdoms around us. He made them a disgrace and a waste among all the peoples around us, where the Lord scattered them.
2:5 They ended up underneath and not on top, because we sinned against the Lord our God and did not listen to his voice.
2:6 Righteousness belongs to the Lord our God, but to us and to our ancestors belongs the shame of our faces, as it is today.
2:7 All these disasters that the Lord spoke of against us have come upon us.
2:8 Yet we did not try to win the Lord over by turning back, each of us, from the plans of our wicked hearts.
2:9 The Lord kept watch over the disasters, and the Lord brought them on us, for the Lord is in the right in all the works he has commanded us.
2:10 But we did not listen to his voice, to walk by the Lord's commands, which he set in front of us.

## Lord, hear us
2:11 "And now, Lord God of Israel, you brought your people out of the land of Egypt with a strong hand, with signs and wonders, with great power and a raised arm, and made a name for yourself that lasts to this day.
2:12 We have sinned. We have acted wickedly. We have done wrong, Lord our God, against all your rulings.
2:13 Let your fury turn back from us, for only a few of us are left among the nations where you scattered us.
2:14 Lord, hear our prayer and our plea. Rescue us for your own sake, and make the people who deported us look on us with favor,
2:15 so that the whole earth may know that you are the Lord our God, for your name is called over Israel and over his people.
2:16 Lord, look down from your holy house and think of us. Lord, bend your ear and listen.
2:17 Open your eyes and see. It is not the dead in Hades, whose spirit was taken from within them, who will give the Lord glory and justice.
2:18 It is the soul crushed by grief, who walks bent over and weak, the eyes that grow dim, and the hungry soul, who will give you glory and righteousness, Lord.
2:19 We are not laying our plea for mercy in front of you, Lord our God, on the strength of what our ancestors and our kings did right.
2:20 For you have sent your fury and your anger on us, as you said through your servants the prophets:
2:21 'The Lord says this: Bow your shoulder and work for the king of Babylon, and you will go on living in the land I have given your ancestors.
2:22 But if you will not listen to the voice of the Lord and work for the king of Babylon,
2:23 I will put a stop to the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the sound of the groom and the sound of the bride, in the towns of Judah and outside Jerusalem. The whole land will become a waste, with no one living there.'
2:24 But we did not listen to your voice and work for the king of Babylon. So you carried out the words you spoke through your servants the prophets: that the bones of our kings and the bones of our ancestors would be dragged out of their resting places.
2:25 And look: they have been thrown out to the heat of the day and the frost of the night. They died in terrible pain, by famine and sword and banishment.
2:26 Because of the wickedness of the house of Israel and the house of Judah, you have made the house over which your name is called what it is today.

## The promise to Moses
2:27 "But you, Lord our God, have treated us with all your gentleness and all your great compassion,
2:28 as you spoke through your servant Moses on the day you commanded him to write your law in front of the Israelites:
2:29 'If you will not listen to my voice, this great swarm of people will surely shrink to a few among the nations where I will scatter them.
2:30 For I know they will not listen to me, because they are a stiff-necked people. But in the land of their exile they will come to their senses.
2:31 They will know that I am the Lord their God. I will give them a heart and ears that listen.
2:32 They will praise me in the land of their exile and remember my name.
2:33 They will turn back from their stubbornness and from their wicked deeds, for they will remember the way of their ancestors, who sinned before the Lord.
2:34 I will bring them back to the land I promised with an oath to their ancestors, to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and they will rule over it. I will make them many, and they will not dwindle.
2:35 I will set up an everlasting covenant with them, that I will be God to them and they will be a people to me. And I will never again move my people Israel out of the land I gave them.'
3:1 "Lord Almighty, God of Israel, it is a soul hemmed in and a spirit worn out that has cried out to you.
3:2 Listen, Lord, and have mercy, because we have sinned in your sight.
3:3 You are seated on your throne for all time, while we are perishing for all time.
3:4 Lord Almighty, God of Israel, listen now to the prayer of the dead of Israel and of the children of people who sinned in your sight. They did not listen to your voice, the voice of their God, and the disasters clung to us.
3:5 Do not call to mind our ancestors' wrongs; call to mind your hand and your name at this time.
3:6 For you are the Lord our God, and we will praise you, Lord.
3:7 That is why you set the fear of you on our hearts, so that we would call on your name. In our exile we will praise you, because we have turned out of our hearts all the wrongdoing of our ancestors who sinned before you.
3:8 Look, here we are today in our exile, where you scattered us, a disgrace and a curse and a debt to be paid for all the wrongs of our ancestors, who walked away from the Lord our God."

## Where is wisdom?
3:9 Listen, Israel, to the commandments of life; / open your ears and learn insight.
3:10 What has happened, Israel? / How is it that you are in the land of your enemies, / worn out with age in a strange land, / made unclean with the dead,
3:11 counted among those who go down to Hades?
3:12 You abandoned the spring of wisdom.
3:13 Had you walked on God's way, / your home would be in peace forever.
3:14 Learn where insight is found, and strength, and discernment, / so that you also come to know where long life and life are, / where light in the eyes is, and peace.
3:15 Who has found where she lives? / Who has gone into her storerooms?
3:16 Where are the rulers of the nations now, / who were masters of the wild animals on the earth,
3:17 who played with the birds of the sky, / who piled up silver and gold, the things people trust in, / and never stopped acquiring?
3:18 Those who worked silver with such care, / whose works are past finding out,
3:19 They disappeared and went down to Hades, / and others stood up to take their place.
3:20 Younger ones saw the light and lived on the earth, / but they did not know the way of understanding.
3:21 They did not grasp her paths or take hold of her. / Their children strayed far from their way.
3:22 No one heard of her in Canaan; / no one saw her in Teman.
3:23 The descendants of Hagar, who search for discernment on earth, / the merchants of Merran and Teman, / the storytellers and the searchers for discernment: / they did not know the way of wisdom / or keep her paths in mind.
3:24 Israel, how great God's house is, / how far his estate stretches!
3:25 Great it is, without end; / high it is, beyond measuring.
3:26 There the giants were born, the famous ones of the earliest days, / tall and huge, trained for war.
3:27 Not these were the ones God chose, / nor did he give them the way of understanding.
3:28 They died out because they had no insight; / they died out through their own bad counsel.
3:29 Who has climbed up into the sky and taken her, / and brought her down from the clouds?
3:30 Who has crossed to the far side of the sea and found her, / and will bring her back for choice gold?
3:31 No one knows her way. / No one can work out her path.
3:32 But the one who knows everything knows her; / he discovered her with his own discernment. / The one who made the earth ready to last for ages / filled it with animals that walk on four feet.
3:33 He sends the light out, and off it goes; / he summoned it, and it obeyed him, trembling.
3:34 The stars shone out, each at its post, and were full of joy.
3:35 He called them, and they answered, "Here we are!" / Joyfully they shone for the one who made them.
3:36 This is our God; / no other will be reckoned next to him.
3:37 He searched out every way of understanding / and gave it to his servant Jacob, / to Israel, loved by him.
3:38 After this she was seen on earth / and went about among human beings.
4:1 She is the book of the commands of God, / the law that lasts forever. / Everyone who holds on to her is headed for life; / those who let go of her will die.
4:2 Turn back, Jacob, and take hold of her; / make your way to the brightness, facing her light.
4:3 Do not hand your glory to someone else, / or what benefits you to a foreign nation.
4:4 How happy we are, Israel! / What pleases God has been made known to us.

## Jerusalem speaks to her children
4:5 Take heart, my people, / you who keep Israel's name alive!
4:6 You were sold to the nations, / not to be wiped out. / Because you made God angry, / you were given into your foes' power.
4:7 You roused the anger of the one who made you / by offering sacrifices to demons and not to God.
4:8 You forgot the one who fed you, the everlasting God, / and you brought sorrow to Jerusalem, who nursed you.
4:9 She saw God's anger falling on you, and she said: / "Listen, you who live alongside Zion! / God has laid a heavy grief on me.
4:10 I have seen my sons and daughters taken captive, / the captivity the Everlasting brought on them.
4:11 With gladness I brought them up; / with weeping and grief I sent them off.
4:12 No one should gloat over me, / a widow abandoned by many. / I am made desolate by my children's sins, / for they turned aside from God's law.
4:13 His rulings they never learned. / The ways of God's commandments they did not walk, / and the paths of training in his righteousness they did not set foot on.
4:14 Come, you who live alongside Zion, / and remember how my sons and daughters were taken captive, / the captivity the Everlasting brought on them.
4:15 From far away he brought a nation against them, / a nation without shame, speaking a foreign language. / They felt no shame before the old / and spared no child.
4:16 They carried off the widow's darlings / and stripped the lone woman of her daughters.
4:17 As for me, what can I do to help you?
4:18 The one who brought the disasters / is the one who will rescue you from your enemies' grip.
4:19 Walk on, children, walk on; / I am left behind, alone.
4:20 I have stripped off my robe of peace / and dressed in the sackcloth of my pleading. / As long as I live I will cry out to the Everlasting.
4:21 Take heart, children, cry out to God, / and he will pull you out from under their rule, out of your enemies' hands.
4:22 My hope for your salvation rests on the Everlasting, / and the Holy One has given me joy / over the mercy soon coming to you / from the Everlasting, your Savior.
4:23 With grief and weeping I let you go, / but God will return you to me / with joy and gladness for all time.
4:24 Just as those who live alongside Zion have watched you taken captive, / soon they will watch your salvation come from your God. / It will come over you in great glory / and in the brightness of the Everlasting.
4:25 Children, bear patiently the anger that has come on you from God. / Your enemy has hunted you down, / but you will soon see his ruin / and put your foot on their necks.
4:26 My pampered ones have gone down hard roads; / like sheep seized by enemies they were carried away.
4:27 Take heart, children, and cry out to God, / for the one who brought this on you will remember you.
4:28 Just as you once set your minds on wandering from God, / so now turn back and seek him ten times more.
4:29 The one who brought the disasters on you / will bring you, along with your salvation, / gladness that is everlasting."

## Take heart, Jerusalem
4:30 Take heart, Jerusalem: / the one who gave you your name will comfort you.
4:31 Miserable are those who hurt you / and gloated when you fell.
4:32 Miserable are the cities your children served as slaves; / miserable is the city that took your sons.
4:33 She was happy when you fell / and enjoyed your collapse; / now she will grieve when she lies empty herself.
4:34 I will take away the joy of her crowds, / and her proud boasting will turn into grief.
4:35 Fire from the Everlasting will fall on her for many long days, / and demons will make her their home for most of the time to come.
4:36 Jerusalem, look around you, toward the sunrise, / and see the gladness God is sending your way.
4:37 Here come your sons, whom you sent off! / They come gathered from sunrise to sunset / by the Holy One's word, / full of joy at God's glory.
5:1 Jerusalem, strip off the robe of your grief and your misery, / and dress forever in the beauty of the glory that comes from God.
5:2 Throw around yourself the cloak of the righteousness that comes from God; / bind on your head the turban of the glory of the Everlasting.
5:3 God will display your brightness / to the whole world under heaven.
5:4 For God will give you a name forever: / "Peace of Righteousness" and "Glory of Godliness."
5:5 Get up, Jerusalem! Stand on the heights. / Look around, toward the sunrise, / and see your children gathered from where the sun goes down to where it comes up, / by the Holy One's word, / glad because God has kept them in mind.
5:6 They left you on foot, / with enemies driving them; / but God is bringing them home to you, / lifted high in glory, like a king's throne.
5:7 God has given the order: every high mountain is to be brought low, / the age-old dunes too, / and the ravines are to be filled until the ground is level, / so that Israel may walk safely in the glory of God.
5:8 At God's command the forests and every sweet-smelling tree / have given Israel shade.
5:9 God will lead Israel in gladness / by the light of his own glory, / with the mercy and righteousness he gives.
