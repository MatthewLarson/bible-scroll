## The ram and the he-goat
8:1 I, Daniel, was shown a vision in the third year of King Belshazzar's rule. It came after the one I had been shown at the start.
8:2 I saw it in the vision. While I was watching, I was at Susa, the fortress in the province of Elam, and in the vision I stood beside the Ulai canal.
8:3 I raised my eyes and saw one ram standing in front of the canal. It had two horns. The two horns were tall, but one was taller than the other, and the taller one was the last to come up.
8:4 I watched the ram butting toward the west, the north and the south. Wild animals could not hold their ground in front of it, and nobody could free anyone from its grip. It did whatever it wanted, and it made itself great.
8:5 I was trying to understand it when, look, a he-goat came from the west over the face of the whole earth, and it did not touch the ground. The goat had a horn between its eyes that stood out.
8:6 It came up to the ram with the two horns, the one I had seen standing in front of the canal, and it ran at it in the heat of its strength.
8:7 I saw it close in on the ram, full of bitter anger. It hit the ram and snapped off both its horns, and the ram had no strength left to face it. The goat flung it to the ground and stamped on it. Nobody could pull the ram out of the goat's grip.
8:8 The he-goat grew enormous. Just as it became mighty, the great horn was broken off, and four striking horns came up where it had been, pointing to the four winds of the heavens.
## The small horn
8:9 From one of these a single horn came out. It began small, but it grew huge toward the south, the east and the Beautiful Land.
8:10 It grew until it reached the army of the sky. Some of that army, and some of the stars, it knocked down to the ground, and it stamped on them.
8:11 It puffed itself up against the prince of the army, even to him. From him the regular offering was lifted away, and the base of his sanctuary was flung down.
8:12 An army was handed over, along with the regular offering, in rebellion. It threw truth to the ground. It acted, and it succeeded.
8:13 Then I heard a holy one speaking. Another holy one said to the one who was speaking, whoever he was, "How long will the vision last: the regular offering, the rebellion that desolates, and the handing over of the holy place and the army to be trampled?"
8:14 He said to me, "Until 2,300 evenings and mornings have passed. Then the holy place will be set right."
## Gabriel explains
8:15 When I, Daniel, saw the vision, I tried to understand it. Just then someone who looked like a man was standing in front of me.
8:16 I heard a human voice from the middle of the Ulai. It called out, "Gabriel, help this man understand what he saw."
8:17 He walked up next to the place where I stood. As he came, terror seized me and I dropped on my face. "Understand, son of man," he said to me, "that this vision belongs to the time of the end."
8:18 As he spoke with me, I fell into a stupor, face down on the ground. He touched me and set me on my feet again, in the place where I had been standing.
8:19 He said, "Look, I am letting you know what will take place in the last part of the time of fury, for the vision belongs to the appointed time of the end.
8:20 The two-horned ram that you saw stands for the kings of Media and Persia.
8:21 The hairy he-goat stands for the king of Greece, and the great horn between its eyes stands for the first king.
8:22 The horn that broke, and the four that came up in its place, mean four kingdoms will rise out of one nation, but not with his strength.
8:23 At the end of their rule, when the rebels have run their course, a king will stand up, fierce in face and clever at riddles.
8:24 His strength will grow great, though not by his own strength. He will ruin things in amazing ways, and everything he does will work. He will wipe out powerful men and the people of the holy ones.
8:25 Because of his cleverness, trickery will do well in his hands. He will think himself great, and when people feel safe he will destroy many. He will take his stand against the prince of princes, yet he will be broken without a hand.
8:26 What was said about the evenings and mornings in the vision is true. But as for you, close up the vision, for it belongs to many days ahead."
8:27 I, Daniel, was worn out and lay sick for days. Then I got up and went back to the king's work. I was stunned by the vision, and no one could explain it.
## Daniel reads Jeremiah
9:1 In the first year of Darius son of Ahasuerus, a descendant of the Medes, who was made king over the Chaldeans' kingdom,
9:2 in the first year of his reign, I, Daniel, understood from the scrolls the number of years that the LORD's word to Jeremiah the prophet gave for the ruins of Jerusalem to run their course: seventy years.
9:3 I set my face toward the Lord God to look for him with prayer and pleading, with fasting, sackcloth and ashes.
## Daniel's prayer
9:4 I prayed to the LORD my God, and I confessed. This is what I said: "Please, Lord, great and fearsome God, who keeps the covenant and loyal love for those who love him and for those who keep his commands:
9:5 We have sinned and gone wrong; we acted wickedly and rebelled; we veered away from your commands and your rulings.
9:6 We did not listen to your servants the prophets. They spoke in your name to our kings and officials, to our ancestors, and to all the people of the land.
9:7 Righteousness belongs to you, Lord, but to us belongs the shame of our faces, as it is today: to the people of Judah, to the people living in Jerusalem, and to all Israel, near and far, in all the lands where you have driven them because of the unfaithfulness they showed against you.
9:8 LORD, the shame of our faces belongs to us, to our kings, our officials and our ancestors, because we have sinned against you.
9:9 Compassion and forgiveness belong to the Lord our God, though we rebelled against him.
9:10 We did not listen to the voice of the LORD our God, to walk by his laws, which he set in front of us through his servants the prophets.
9:11 All Israel overstepped your law and turned aside, refusing to listen to your voice. We sinned against him, and so the curse and the oath written in the law of Moses, the servant of God, were poured out on us.
9:12 He has carried out the word he spoke against us and against our judges who judged us, bringing a great disaster on us. No disaster like it has ever struck anywhere under the heavens, as it struck Jerusalem.
9:13 As it is written in the law of Moses, all this disaster came on us. Even so, we did not try to win the LORD our God over, by turning back from our guilt and taking your truth to heart.
9:14 The LORD kept watch over the disaster and brought it on us, for the LORD our God is in the right in everything he has done. But we did not listen to his voice.
9:15 Now, Lord our God, you brought your people out of the land of Egypt with a strong hand and made a name for yourself that lasts to this day. We have sinned. We have acted wickedly.
9:16 Lord, because of all your righteous deeds, please let your anger and your fury turn back from your city Jerusalem, your holy mountain. For because of our sins and the guilt of our ancestors, Jerusalem and your people have become a disgrace to all who live around us.
9:17 So now, our God, listen to the prayer of your servant and to his pleas for grace. For the Lord's sake, make your face shine on your sanctuary, which lies desolate.
9:18 My God, bend your ear and listen. Open your eyes and look at our ruined places and at the city over which your name is called. We are not laying our pleas in front of you because of our righteous deeds, but because of your great compassion.
9:19 Lord, hear! Lord, forgive! Lord, pay attention and act! Do not delay, for your own sake, my God, because your name is called over your city and your people."
## Gabriel and the seventy sevens
9:20 While I was still speaking and praying, confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel, and laying my plea for grace in front of the LORD my God for the holy mountain of my God,
9:21 while I was still speaking in prayer, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the start, came flying swiftly and reached me at the time of the evening grain offering.
9:22 He helped me understand and spoke with me. He said, "Daniel, I have just now come out to give you insight and understanding.
9:23 A word came out as soon as you began your pleas, and I have come to announce it, because you are highly treasured. Think over the word, and make sense of the vision.
9:24 Seventy sevens have been marked off for your people and your holy city: to end the rebellion, to finish sin, to make atonement for guilt, to bring in righteousness for the ages, to seal up vision and prophet, and to anoint a holy of holies.
9:25 Know this and understand it: from the time a word goes out to restore and rebuild Jerusalem, until an anointed one, a ruler: seven sevens, and sixty-two sevens it will be restored and built again, a plaza and a moat, and in the distress of the times.
9:26 After the sixty-two sevens an anointed one will be cut off, and there will be none for him. The people of a ruler who is coming will destroy the city and the holy place. Its end will come in the flood. Until the end there will be war, and desolations are fixed.
9:27 He will make a covenant strong for the many, for one seven. In the middle of the seven he will bring sacrifice and offering to a stop. On a wing of abominations comes one who makes desolate, until full destruction, the end that was decided, is poured out over the one who makes desolate."
## Three weeks of mourning
10:1 In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia, a word was uncovered to Daniel, who was named Belteshazzar. The word was true: it was about a great war. He grasped the word, and understanding about the vision was given to him.
10:2 In those days I, Daniel, was in mourning for three weeks of days.
10:3 I ate no fine food. No meat or wine came into my mouth, and I did not use any oil on myself until the three weeks of days were over.
10:4 On the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was beside the great river, the Tigris.
## The man in linen
10:5 I lifted my eyes and saw a man in linen clothes, his waist wrapped in gold from Uphaz.
10:6 His body shone like chrysolite. His face looked like lightning, and his eyes were burning torches. His arms and feet had the glow of polished bronze, and when his words came, they sounded like the roar of a crowd.
10:7 I, Daniel, alone saw the vision. The men with me did not see it, but a great terror fell on them, and they ran off to hide.
10:8 So I was left by myself to see this great vision. No strength stayed in me. My vigor was turned to ruin, and I could not keep any strength.
10:9 Then I heard the sound of his words, and as I heard it, I fell into a deep sleep with my face to the ground.
10:10 A hand touched me and stirred me up onto my knees and the palms of my hands.
10:11 He said to me, "Daniel, you are a highly treasured man. Understand the words I am about to say to you, and stand up straight where you are, for I have just now been sent to you." When he had spoken this word to me, I stood up, trembling.
## The message and the princes
10:12 He said to me, "Do not be afraid, Daniel. From the first day you set your mind to understand and to humble yourself in front of your God, your words were heard, and it is because of them that I have come.
10:13 But the prince of the kingdom of Persia stood against me for twenty-one days. Then Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me, and I was left there beside the kings of Persia.
10:14 I have come so that you will understand what is going to happen to your people in the last days; the vision is still for days ahead."
10:15 While he was speaking to me in these words, I turned my face to the ground and could not speak.
10:16 Then one who looked like a human being touched my lips. I opened my mouth and spoke to the one standing in front of me: "My lord, because of the vision, pangs have turned on me, and I have no strength left.
10:17 How can this servant of my lord speak with my lord? From now on no strength stays in me, and no breath is left in me."
10:18 Again the one who looked like a man touched me and gave me strength.
10:19 He said, "Do not be afraid, you highly treasured man. Peace to you. Be strong, be strong." As he spoke with me, I grew strong, and I said, "Let my lord speak, for you have given me strength."
10:20 He said, "Do you know why I came to you? I am about to return and do battle with the prince of Persia. As I leave, the prince of Greece is coming.
10:21 But I will tell you what is written in the writing of truth. No one stands firm with me against these except Michael, your prince."
