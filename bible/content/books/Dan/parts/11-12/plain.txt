## More kings of Persia
11:1 As for me, in the first year of Darius the Mede, I was standing at his side, a strength and a stronghold to him.
11:2 Now I will tell you the truth. Three more kings are about to rise for Persia. The fourth will gain wealth, far more than anyone else. When his wealth has made him strong, he will call everyone to arms against the kingdom of Greece.
## A mighty king and his divided kingdom
11:3 Then a powerful king will rise. He will rule a vast realm, and whatever he wants, he will do.
11:4 Once he is on his feet, his kingdom will crack apart and be shared out among the four winds of the sky. His children will not inherit it, and none of it will have the power he held. His kingdom will be torn up by the roots and handed to others, not to them.
## The king of the south and the king of the north
11:5 The king of the south will grow strong. One of his commanders, though, will grow stronger than the king himself and take power. His realm will be vast.
11:6 After some years the two will ally themselves. The daughter of the king of the south will go to the king of the north to seal a fair agreement. But she will not keep the strength of her arm, and he will not last, nor will his strength. In those times she will be handed over. So will the men who brought her, the one who fathered her, and the one who held her up.
11:7 In his place a shoot from her own roots will stand up. He will march on the army and break into the stronghold of the king of the north. He will take action against them and prove the stronger.
11:8 He will take their gods to Egypt as prisoners, along with the images they cast and their treasured objects of silver and gold. Then, for some years, he will stay clear of the king of the north.
11:9 Then the king of the north will enter the kingdom of the king of the south, but he will go back to his own land.
11:10 His sons will go to war and gather a great mass of armies. One of them will come on, flood the land and pass through. Then he will come back and fight on, up to the stronghold.
11:11 The king of the south will boil with rage. He will go out and do battle with the king of the north, who will muster a huge crowd, but that crowd will be handed over to the southern king.
11:12 When the crowd is carried off, his heart will swell with pride. He will strike down tens of thousands, yet he will not stay strong.
11:13 The king of the north will turn back and set up a crowd bigger than the first. At the end of those times, some years on, he will come in full force, with a vast army and much equipment.
11:14 In those days many will stand against the king of the south. Men of violence from your own people will grow bold, to make the vision stand, but they will stumble.
11:15 The king of the north will come, heap up an earthwork against a city and take that walled city. The armies of the south will not hold their ground, nor will his chosen soldiers, for they will have no strength left to stand.
11:16 The one who comes against him will do whatever he likes, and no one will stand before him. He will take his stand in the Beautiful Land, and destruction will be in his hand.
11:17 He will make up his mind to come with the full power of his kingdom, and he will strike a fair deal with the king of the south. He will offer him a young woman to be his wife, to wreck that kingdom. But the plan will not stand, and it will not work out for him.
11:18 Next he will aim for the coastlands and seize many of them. But a commander will put a stop to the scorn he has shown, and will hand that scorn back to him.
11:19 Then he will head back for the strongholds of his own land, but he will stumble and fall, and he will vanish.
## A despicable king
11:20 In his place will rise one who sends a taskmaster through the glory of the kingdom. A few days later he will be shattered, though not by anger and not in battle.
11:21 In his place will rise a despicable man. Nobody had handed him the glory of kingship. He will come in when things are quiet and take hold of the kingdom through slippery schemes.
11:22 Armies that sweep like a flood will be swept away ahead of him and smashed, and so will a ruler of the covenant.
11:23 Once they have joined with him, he will act with deceit. He will come up and grow strong with only a small nation behind him.
11:24 When things are quiet, he will come into the fattest lands of the province. He will do what his fathers and his fathers' fathers never did: he will hand out loot, plunder and property to his men. He will plot against the fortresses, but only for a time.
11:25 He will rouse his might and his nerve to march on the king of the south with a large army. The king of the south will prepare for battle with a huge, strong army, but he will not hold, because plots will be hatched against him.
11:26 The men at his own table will crush him. His army will be washed away, and many will fall, killed.
11:27 As for the two kings, their hearts lean toward harm. Across one table they will speak falsehood, and it will not work, for the end still belongs to the time set for it.
11:28 He will turn back to his land loaded with riches, and his heart will rise against the holy covenant. He will act, and then go back to his land.
## The holy covenant under attack
11:29 At the time set, he will come again into the south, but this time will not be like the first or the last.
11:30 Ships from Kittim will sail against him, and his courage will break. He will turn back and vent his fury on the holy covenant. He will act, and then he will return and take notice of those who abandoned the holy covenant.
11:31 Forces sent by him will rise up and defile the sanctuary, the fortress. They will do away with the regular offering and set up the abomination that desolates.
11:32 Those who act wickedly against the covenant he will corrupt with smooth words. But the people who know their God will be strong and take action.
11:33 Those among the people who have insight will give understanding to many. Yet for some days they will stumble, struck by sword and flame, taken captive and stripped of their goods.
11:34 As they stumble, a small help will come to them, and many will latch on to them through smooth talk.
11:35 Some of those who have insight will stumble. This is to refine them, to purge them and to whiten them, up to the time of the end, because it is yet to come, at the time set.
## The king who raises himself up
11:36 This king will follow his own will. Above every god he will raise himself, and he will make himself great, and against the God of gods he will speak things beyond belief. All will go well for him only until the fury has run its course, because what has been decided will be carried out.
11:37 He will not give heed to the gods of his fathers, nor to the desire of women, nor to any god, because he will lift himself above everything.
11:38 He will honor a god of fortresses in its place. A god his fathers never knew he will honor with gold and silver, costly stones and treasures.
11:39 With an alien god at his side he will deal with the strongest forts. The people he singles out he will honor more and more. He will put them in charge of the many and hand out the land for a price.
11:40 When the time of the end comes, the king of the south will lock horns with him. The king of the north will storm at him with chariots, horsemen and a great fleet, sweep into the lands, flood across them and pass through.
11:41 He will enter the Beautiful Land, and many will stumble. But these will escape his hand: Edom, Moab and the best of the Ammonites.
11:42 He will lay his hand on one country after another, and the land of Egypt will be no place of escape.
11:43 He will rule the hidden stores of gold and silver and all the treasures of Egypt, with Libyans and Cushites following at his heels.
11:44 Then rumors out of the east and the north will shake him, and he will march out raging, bent on wrecking and wiping out many.
11:45 He will set up the tents of his palace between the seas and the holy mountain of beauty. His end will come, and he will have no helper.
## Michael and the rising of the dead
12:1 At that time Michael will stand up, the great prince who stands guard over the sons of your people. A time of distress will come, with no equal from the day there was a nation until that moment. But in that same time your people will escape, everyone found written in the scroll.
12:2 Many of those asleep in the dusty ground will wake up. Some will wake to life without end, and some to shame, to a horror without end.
12:3 Those with insight will gleam / like the gleam of the dome of the sky. / Those who put the many in the right / will blaze like the stars, for ever and ever.
12:4 And you, Daniel, shut up the words and seal the scroll until the time of the end. Many will rove around, and knowledge will multiply.
## How long?
12:5 I, Daniel, saw it. Two others were standing there, one at the edge of the river on this side, and one at the edge on that side.
12:6 One of them asked the man in linen clothes, who was above the water of the river, "How long until the wonders come to their end?"
12:7 I heard the man in linen clothes, who was above the water of the river. He raised his right hand and his left hand to the sky and swore by the one who lives forever: "It will be a time, times and half a time. And when the smashing of the holy people's strength is done, all of this will be complete."
12:8 I myself heard it but could not grasp it. So I said, "My lord, how will these things end up?"
12:9 He said, "Go on, Daniel. The words are shut up and sealed, and so they stay until the time of the end.
12:10 Many will purge themselves, whiten themselves and be refined. The wicked will go on being wicked, and no wicked person will grasp any of it. But those with insight will understand.
12:11 There will be 1,290 days from the time the regular offering was taken away and the abomination that desolates was set up.
12:12 Blessed is the one who waits and reaches 1,335 days.
12:13 And you, go on to the end. You will rest, and you will rise to your share at the end of the days."
