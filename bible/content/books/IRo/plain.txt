## Greeting to the church in Rome
1:0 Ignatius, also called Theophorus, to the church that has received mercy in the majesty of the Most High Father and of Jesus Christ, his only Son. It is loved and given light by the will of the one who willed all things that exist, in keeping with the love of Jesus Christ our God. It also presides in the region of the Romans. It is worthy of God, worthy of honor, worthy to be called blessed, worthy of praise, worthy to reach its goal, worthy in its purity, and presiding over the love. It is named after Christ, named after the Father. I also greet it in the name of Jesus Christ, the Father's Son. To those who are united in flesh and in spirit to every one of his commandments, who are filled with God's grace without wavering and filtered clean of every foreign stain: abundant greetings, without blame, in Jesus Christ our God.
## Do not let your love harm me
1:1 I prayed to God, and he has granted that I will see your faces, which are worthy of God. I have been given even more than I asked for. For I am in chains in Christ Jesus, and I hope to greet you, if it is God's will that I be counted worthy to the end.
1:2 The beginning has been well arranged, if I obtain the grace to receive my lot without anything in the way. Your love frightens me: it may do me wrong. It is easy for you to do what you want. But for me it is hard to reach God, if you do not spare me.
2:1 I do not want you to please people. I want you to please God, just as you do please him. I will never have such a chance to reach God. And if you keep silent, you could never have a better deed written to your name. If you keep silent about me, I am a word of God. But if you fall in love with my flesh, I will once more be only a sound.
2:2 Do not give me anything more than to be poured out to God as a drink offering, while an altar still stands ready. Then you can form a choir in love and sing to the Father in Christ Jesus, because God has counted the overseer of Syria worthy to be found at the sunset, after sending for him from the sunrise. It is good to set, leaving the world for God, so that I may rise to him.
## To be a Christian, not only be called one
3:1 You have never envied anyone; you taught others. Now I want the things you command as you make disciples to hold firm as well.
3:2 Only ask for strength for me, inside and out, so that I may not only talk but also want it, and not only be called a Christian but also be found one. If I am found one, then I can also be called one, and be faithful at the time when the world no longer sees me.
3:3 Nothing that is seen is good. For our God, Jesus Christ, now that he is in the Father, is seen all the more. Christianity is not a work of persuading but of greatness, when the world hates it.
## God's wheat
4:1 I am writing to all the churches, and I charge them all that I am dying for God willingly, if you do not stop me. I beg you, do not become an untimely kindness to me. Let me be food for wild beasts. Through them I can reach God. I am God's wheat, and I am ground by the teeth of wild beasts so that I may be found pure bread of Christ.
4:2 Coax the wild beasts instead, so that they become my tomb and not a scrap of my body is left. Then when I have fallen asleep, I will not be a burden to anyone. Then I will truly be a disciple of Jesus Christ, when the world does not even see my body. Plead with Christ for me, so that through these tools I may be found a sacrifice.
4:3 I am not commanding you as Peter and Paul did. They were apostles; I am a condemned man. They were free; up to now I am a slave. But if I suffer, I will become Jesus Christ's freed person, and I will rise free in him. Now, in chains, I am learning to want nothing.
## From Syria to Rome
5:1 From Syria to Rome I am fighting wild beasts, by land and by sea, by night and by day. I am chained to ten leopards, that is, a squad of soldiers, and the better they are treated, the worse they become. By their wrongs I am being made more of a disciple, but I am not justified by that.
5:2 May I have the benefit of the beasts made ready for me. I pray that they will be quick with me. I will even coax them to eat me up quickly, since with some people the beasts lost their nerve and never touched them. And if they are unwilling and refuse, I will force them.
5:3 Bear with me. I know what is good for me. Now I am beginning to be a disciple. May nothing seen or unseen envy me, so that I may reach Jesus Christ. Fire and cross, battles with wild beasts, cuttings and tearings apart, scattering of bones, chopping of limbs, grinding of my whole body, the cruel tortures of the devil: let them come on me, only let me reach Jesus Christ.
## Let me imitate my God's suffering
6:1 The ends of the world and the kingdoms of this age will do me no good. It is better for me to die into Christ Jesus than to be king over the ends of the earth. I am looking for him who died for us. I want him who rose for our sake. My birth is upon me.
6:2 Forgive me, brothers. Do not keep me from living. Do not want me to die. Do not hand over to the world one who wants to be God's, and do not trick him with material things. Let me receive pure light. Once I am there, I will become a human being.
6:3 Allow me to be an imitator of the suffering of my God. If anyone has him within himself, let him understand what I want, and let him feel for me, since he knows what holds me in its grip.
## Living water and God's bread
7:1 The ruler of this age wants to snatch me away and ruin my mind that is set on God. So let none of you who are there help him. Belong to me instead, that is, to God. Do not speak of Jesus Christ and at the same time crave the world.
7:2 Do not let envy live among you. Even if I myself beg you when I am with you, do not be won over by me. Be won over instead by what I am writing to you. For I am alive as I write to you, and I long to die. My love has been crucified, and there is no fire in me that loves material things. But there is living water in me that speaks, saying inside me, "Come to the Father."
7:3 Food that decays gives me no delight, and neither do the pleasures of this life. I want God's bread, which is the flesh of Jesus Christ, who was from David's seed. And for drink I want his blood, which is love that does not decay.
## Believe me
8:1 I no longer want to live the way people do. And this will happen if you want it. Want it, so that you too may be wanted.
8:2 With these few lines I am asking you: believe me. Jesus Christ will make it plain to you that I am telling the truth. He is the mouth that does not lie, through which the Father has spoken truly.
8:3 Pray for me, that I may reach God. I have not written to you by the flesh, but by the mind of God. If I suffer, it will be because you wished it. If I am turned away, it will be because you hated me.
## The church in Syria
9:1 In your prayer, remember the church in Syria. It has God as its shepherd now, in my place. Jesus Christ alone will oversee it, along with your love.
9:2 But I am ashamed to be called one of them. I am not worthy, since I am the last of them and one born at the wrong time. But I have been shown mercy to be someone, if I reach God.
9:3 My spirit greets you, and so does the love of the churches that welcomed me in the name of Jesus Christ, and not as a passerby. Even the churches that did not lie on my earthly road went ahead of me from city to city.
## From Smyrna
10:1 From Smyrna I write you these things, by the Ephesians, who are worthy to be called blessed. Crocus, a name dear to me, is also here with me, along with many others.
10:2 As for those who went ahead of me from Syria to Rome for the glory of God, I believe you have come to know them. Tell them that I am near. They are all worthy of God and of you, and you should see that they are refreshed in everything.
10:3 I wrote these things to you on the ninth day before the Kalends of September. Farewell to the end, in the endurance of Jesus Christ.
