## The Twelve Mountains of Arcadia
78:1 After I had written down the commandments and parables of the Shepherd, the angel of repentance, he came to me and said, "I want to show you everything the Holy Spirit showed you, the Spirit that spoke with you in the form of the Church. For that Spirit is the Son of God.
78:2 Since you were too weak in the flesh, it was not made plain to you through an angel. But when you were made strong through the Spirit and grew strong in your strength, so that you could even see an angel, then the building of the tower was shown to you through the Church. You saw everything in a fine and reverent way, as if a virgin were showing you. But now you see it shown by an angel, though through the same Spirit.
78:3 Still, you must learn everything from me more exactly. That is the reason the glorious angel gave me to you, to live in your house: so that you may see everything with power, as before, and not be afraid of anything."
78:4 Then he took me away to Arcadia, to a breast-shaped mountain. He sat me down on the top of the mountain and showed me a great plain. Around the plain stood twelve mountains, and each mountain looked different.
78:5 The first was as black as soot. The second was bare, with no plants. The third was full of thorns and thistles.
78:6 The fourth had plants that were half dried up. The tops of the plants were green, but the parts near the roots were dry. And some plants dried up whenever the sun had scorched them.
78:7 The fifth mountain had green plants and was rough. The sixth mountain was full of cracks all over, some small and some large. The cracks had plants in them, but the plants were not very healthy. They looked more as if they had withered.
78:8 The seventh mountain had cheerful plants, and the whole mountain was thriving. Every kind of cattle and bird was grazing on that mountain, and the more the cattle and birds fed, the more the plants of that mountain flourished. The eighth mountain was full of springs, and every kind of creature the Lord had made drank from the springs of that mountain.
78:9 The ninth mountain had no water at all and was entirely a desert. It had wild animals in it, and deadly snakes that kill people. The tenth mountain had very large trees and was all in shade. Under the cover of the trees, sheep were lying down, resting and chewing their cud.
78:10 The eleventh mountain was thick with trees, and those trees were loaded with fruit, each adorned with a different kind of fruit, so that anyone who saw them would want to eat their fruit. The twelfth mountain was all white, and it looked cheerful. The mountain was most beautiful in itself.
## The Rock, the Gate and the Twelve Virgins
79:1 In the middle of the plain he showed me a great white rock that had risen up out of the plain. The rock was higher than the mountains. It was square, big enough to hold the whole world.
79:2 That rock was old, and a gate had been cut into it. But the cutting of the gate looked recent to me. The gate gleamed brighter than the sun, so that I was amazed at how bright the gate was.
79:3 Twelve virgins were standing around the gate. The four who stood at the corners seemed to me to be more glorious, though the others were glorious too. They stood at the gate's four sides, with two virgins between the corner ones on each side.
79:4 They were dressed in linen tunics and neatly belted, with their right shoulders bare, as if they were about to carry some load. That is how ready they were, because they were very cheerful and eager.
79:5 When I had seen these things, I was amazed inside myself, because I was seeing great and glorious things. And again I was puzzled about the virgins: delicate as they were, they stood there bravely, as if they were about to carry the whole sky.
79:6 Then the Shepherd said to me, "Why are you arguing inside yourself, puzzling, and bringing grief on yourself? Do not take on things you cannot understand, if you have any sense. Ask the Lord instead, so that you may receive discernment and grasp them.
79:7 You cannot see what is behind you, but you see what is in front of you. So let go of what you cannot see, and do not twist yourself in knots. Master the things you do see, and do not poke into the rest. I will make clear to you everything I show you. So look at what remains."
## The Building of the Tower
80:1 I saw six men arrive. They were tall and glorious and looked alike. They called a crowd of men, and the men who came were also tall, handsome and strong. The six men ordered them to build a tower above the rock. There was a great noise from those men who had come to build the tower, as they ran here and there around the gate.
80:2 The virgins standing around the gate kept telling the men to hurry with the building of the tower. The virgins had spread out their hands, as if they were about to receive something from the men.
80:3 The six men ordered stones to come up out of a deep place and go into the building of the tower. Up came ten square, bright stones, not cut from a quarry.
80:4 The six men called the virgins and ordered them to carry all the stones that were to go into the building of the tower. They were to go through the gate with them and hand them to the men who were going to build the tower.
80:5 The virgins loaded onto one another the first ten stones that had come up from the deep, and they carried them together, one stone at a time.
81:1 They carried in the same formation in which they had stood around the gate. Those who seemed strong had gone in under the corners of the stone, and the others had gone in under its sides. That is how they carried all the stones. They brought them through the gate, as they had been ordered, and handed them to the men for the tower, and the men took the stones and built.
81:2 The tower was built on the great rock, above the gate. So those ten stones were fitted together, and they filled the whole rock. They became the foundation of the building of the tower. The rock and the gate were holding up the whole tower.
81:3 After the ten stones, another twenty stones came up from the deep. These too were fitted into the building of the tower, carried by the virgins like the ones before. After these, thirty-five came up, and these too were fitted into the tower in the same way. After these, another forty stones came up, and all of them were set into the building of the tower. So there were four rows in the foundations of the tower.
81:4 Then the stones stopped coming up from the deep, and the builders also stopped for a little while. Then the six men again ordered the great crowd of people to bring stones from the mountains for the building of the tower.
81:5 So stones of many different colors were brought from all the mountains. The men cut them and handed them to the virgins, and the virgins carried them through the gate and handed them in for the building of the tower. When the many-colored stones were set into the building, they all turned white alike and lost their different colors.
81:6 But some stones were handed in for the building by the men, and they did not turn bright. They stayed just as they were when they were set in, because the virgins had not handed them in, and they had not been carried in through the gate. So these stones looked out of place in the building of the tower.
81:7 When the six men saw the stones that looked out of place in the building, they ordered them to be lifted out and taken back down to their own place, where they had been brought from.
81:8 And they said to the men who were bringing in the stones, "Do not hand any stones into the building yourselves at all. Put them down beside the tower, so that the virgins may carry them in through the gate and hand them in for the building. For if they are not carried in through the gate by the hands of these virgins," they said, "they cannot change their colors. So do not wear yourselves out for nothing," they said.
## A Pause in the Building
82:1 That day the building work was finished, but the tower was not completed, because more was going to be built onto it. So there was a pause in the building. The six men ordered all the builders to withdraw a little way and rest, but they ordered the virgins not to leave the tower. It seemed to me that the virgins had been left behind to guard the tower.
82:2 After everyone had withdrawn and was resting, I said to the Shepherd, "Sir, why was the building of the tower not completed?" "The tower cannot be completed yet," he said, "until its lord comes and tests this building. Then, if any stones are found rotten, he can replace them. For the tower is being built to suit his will."
82:3 "Sir," I said, "I would like to know what this building of the tower means, and about the rock and the gate, the mountains and the virgins, and the stones that came up from the deep, not cut, but went into the building just as they were.
82:4 And why were ten stones laid in the foundations first, then twenty, then thirty-five, then forty? And what about the stones that went into the building and were lifted out again and put away in their own place? Give my soul rest about all these things, sir, and let me know them."
82:5 "If you are not found to be idly curious," he said, "you will know everything. In a few days we will come here, and you will see the rest of what happens to this tower, and you will know all the parables exactly."
82:6 A few days later we came to the place where we had sat, and he said to me, "Let us go to the tower, for the owner of the tower is coming to inspect it." So we came to the tower, and there was no one at all near it except the virgins.
82:7 The Shepherd asked the virgins whether the master of the tower had arrived yet. They said that he was about to come to inspect the building.
## The Lord of the Tower Tests the Stones
83:1 And look, after a little while I saw a line of many men coming. In the middle was a man so tall that he stood higher than the tower.
83:2 The six men who had been in charge of the building walked with him on his right and on his left. All who had worked on the building were with him, and many other glorious beings were around him. The virgins who were guarding the tower ran up and kissed him, and they began to walk near him around the tower.
83:3 That man inspected the building so carefully that he felt each stone one by one. He was holding a rod in his hand, and he struck every stone that had been built in, one at a time.
83:4 When he struck them, some of them turned as black as soot, some scabby, some cracked, some chipped, some neither white nor black, some rough and not matching the other stones, and some with many stains. These were the kinds of rotten stones found in the building.
83:5 So he ordered all of these to be moved out of the tower and set beside it, and other stones to be brought and put in their place.
83:6 The builders asked him from which mountain he wanted stones brought to put in their place. He did not order them to be brought from the mountains. He ordered them to be brought from a plain that was nearby.
83:7 So the plain was dug up, and bright square stones were found there, and some round ones too. All the stones there were in that plain were brought, and the virgins carried them through the gate.
83:8 The square stones were cut and set in the place of the ones that had been lifted out. But the round ones were not set into the building, because they were hard to cut and the work on them went slowly. They were laid beside the tower, as if they were going to be cut and set into the building later, because they were very bright.
84:1 When the glorious man, the lord of the whole tower, had finished these things, he called the Shepherd to him. He handed over to him all the stones lying beside the tower, the ones thrown out of the building, and said to him,
84:2 "Clean these stones carefully, and put into the building of the tower the ones that can fit with the rest. Throw the ones that do not fit far away from the tower."
84:3 After giving the Shepherd these orders, he went away from the tower with everyone he had come with. But the virgins stood around the tower, guarding it.
84:4 I said to the Shepherd, "How can these stones go into the building of the tower when they have been rejected?" He answered me, "Do you see these stones?" "I see them, sir," I said. "I will cut most of these stones," he said, "and put them into the building, and they will fit with the rest of the stones."
84:5 "Sir," I said, "once they are trimmed all around, how can they fill the same space?" He answered me, "The ones that turn out small will be put in the middle of the building, and the larger ones will be set on the outside, and they will hold the others together."
84:6 After he had said this to me, he said, "Let us go, and in two days let us come back, clean these stones and put them into the building. Everything around the tower has to be cleaned. Otherwise the master may come suddenly, find the area around the tower dirty, and be angry. Then these stones will not go into the building of the tower, and I will look careless in the master's eyes."
84:7 Two days later we came to the tower, and he said to me, "Let us inspect all the stones and see which ones can go into the building." I said to him, "Sir, let us inspect them."
## The Shepherd Sorts the Rejected Stones
85:1 We started with the black stones and inspected them first. They were found to be just the same as when they had been put out of the building. The Shepherd ordered them to be moved away from the tower and set apart.
85:2 Then he inspected the scabby ones. He took many of them and cut them, and he ordered the virgins to lift them and put them into the building. The virgins lifted them and set them in the middle of the building of the tower. He ordered the rest to be laid with the black ones, because these too were found to be black.
85:3 Then he inspected the ones with cracks. He cut many of these and ordered the virgins to carry them into the building. They were set on the outside, because they were found to be sounder. But the rest could not be cut, because they had so many cracks. For that reason they were thrown out of the building of the tower.
85:4 Then he inspected the chipped ones. Many among them were found to be black, and some had developed large cracks. He ordered these too to be laid with the ones thrown out. But the ones left over he cleaned and cut, and ordered them to be set into the building. The virgins lifted them and fitted them into the middle of the building of the tower, because they were weaker.
85:5 Then he inspected the ones that were half white and half black. Many of them were found to be black, and he ordered these too to be taken away with the ones thrown out. All the rest were lifted by the virgins. Because they were white, the virgins themselves fitted them into the building. They were set on the outside, because they were found to be sound, strong enough to hold the ones set in the middle. Not one of them was chipped at all.
85:6 Then he inspected the rough and hard ones. A few of them were thrown out, because they could not be cut: they were found to be very hard. The rest of them were cut, lifted by the virgins and fitted into the middle of the building of the tower, because they were weaker.
85:7 Then he inspected the ones with stains. Of these, very few had turned black, and they were thrown out with the others. The ones left over were found bright and sound. The virgins fitted these into the building, and they were set on the outside because of their strength.
86:1 Then he came to inspect the white, round stones, and he said to me, "What are we to do about these stones?" "Sir," I said, "how should I know?" "Then you have no idea about them?"
86:2 "Sir," I said, "I do not have this skill. I am not a stonecutter, and I cannot understand it." "Do you not see that they are very round?" he said. "If I want to make them square, a great deal will have to be cut off them. And yet some of them must, of necessity, be set into the building."
86:3 "Then, sir," I said, "if it has to be done, why torment yourself? Why not choose the ones you want for the building and fit them in?" He chose the larger and brighter ones among them and cut them, and the virgins lifted them and fitted them into the outer parts of the building.
86:4 The rest that were left over were lifted and put back in the plain they had been brought from. But they were not thrown out. "Because," he said, "a little more of the tower still has to be built. And the master of the tower wants all these stones fitted into the building, because they are very bright."
86:5 Then twelve women were called. They were very beautiful in form and dressed in black, belted, with their shoulders bare and their hair hanging loose. These women seemed savage to me. The Shepherd ordered them to lift the stones that had been thrown out of the building and carry them away to the mountains they had been brought from.
86:6 They were cheerful about it. They lifted all the stones and carried them away and set them down where they had been taken from. When all the stones had been lifted and not one stone was lying around the tower anymore, the Shepherd said to me, "Let us walk around the tower and see whether there is any flaw in it." So I walked around it with him.
86:7 When the Shepherd saw how beautiful the building of the tower was, he was very cheerful. For the tower was built in such a way that when I saw it I longed for its building. It was built as if it were made from a single stone, without one joint in it. The stone looked as if it had been carved out of the rock. It seemed to me to be one solid stone.
## Hermas Left with the Virgins
87:1 As I walked around with him, I was cheerful too, seeing such good things. Then the Shepherd said to me, "Go and bring lime and fine bits of pottery, so that I can fill in the marks left by the stones that were lifted out and put into the building. Everything around the tower has to be made level."
87:2 I did as he ordered and brought them to him. "Help me," he said, "and the work will soon be finished." So he filled in the marks left by the stones that had gone into the building. Then he ordered the area around the tower to be swept and made clean.
87:3 The virgins took brooms and swept. They carried all the dung away from the tower and sprinkled water, and the place around the tower became cheerful and most beautiful.
87:4 The Shepherd said to me, "Everything has been cleaned. If the lord comes to inspect the tower, he has nothing to blame us for." When he had said this, he wanted to leave.
87:5 But I grabbed his bag and began to beg him by the Lord to explain to me what he had shown me. He said to me, "I have to be busy for a little while, and then I will explain everything to you. Wait for me here until I come."
87:6 I said to him, "Sir, what will I do here all alone?" "You are not alone," he said, "for these virgins are with you." "Then put me in their care," I said. The Shepherd called them to him and said to them, "I am putting this man in your care until I come." And he left.
87:7 So I was alone with the virgins. They were even more cheerful and were kind to me, especially the four more glorious ones.
88:1 The virgins said to me, "The Shepherd is not coming here today." "Then what shall I do?" I said. "Wait for him until evening," they said. "If he comes, he will talk with you. If he does not come, you will stay here with us until he comes."
88:2 I said to them, "I will wait for him until evening. If he does not come, I will go home and come back early in the morning." But they answered me, "You were put in our care. You cannot leave us."
88:3 "Then where shall I stay?" I said. "You will sleep with us," they said, "as a brother and not as a husband. For you are our brother, and from now on we are going to live with you, because we love you very much." But I was ashamed to stay with them.
88:4 Then the one who seemed to be the first of them began to kiss me and put her arms around me. When the others saw her holding me, they too began to kiss me and lead me around the tower and play with me.
88:5 It was as if I had become young again, and I began to play with them myself. Some of them were dancing in a ring, some were dancing, and some were singing. But I kept silent as I walked around the tower with them, and I was cheerful with them.
88:6 When evening came I wanted to go home, but they would not let me go. They held me back. So I stayed the night with them and slept beside the tower.
88:7 The virgins spread their linen tunics on the ground and had me lie down in the middle of them. They did nothing at all except pray, and I prayed with them without stopping, no less than they did. The virgins rejoiced that I was praying like that. I stayed there with the virgins until the next day, until the second hour.
88:8 Then the Shepherd arrived, and he said to the virgins, "You have not insulted him in any way, have you?" "Ask him," they said. I said to him, "Sir, I was glad to stay with them." "What did you have for supper?" he said. "Sir," I said, "I supped on words of the Lord all night." "Did they treat you well?" he said. "Yes, sir," I said.
88:9 "Now," he said, "what do you want to hear first?" "Sir," I said, "in the order you showed it, from the beginning. I ask you, sir, to make each thing clear to me just as I ask you about it." "Just as you wish," he said, "that is how I will explain it to you, and I will hide nothing at all from you."
## The Rock and the Gate Are the Son of God
89:1 "First of all, sir," I said, "make this clear to me: what is the rock, and what is the gate?" "This rock and the gate," he said, "are the Son of God." "Sir," I said, "how is it that the rock is old, but the gate is new?" "Listen and understand, you senseless man," he said.
89:2 "The Son of God is older than all his creation. So he became the Father's counselor for his creation. That is why the rock is old." "But why is the gate new, sir?" I said.
89:3 "Because he appeared in the last days, at the completion of the age," he said. "That is why the gate became new: so that those who are going to be saved may enter the kingdom of God through it.
89:4 Did you see," he said, "that the stones that came in through the gate were set into the building of the tower, but the ones that had not come in were thrown back out to their own place?" "I saw it, sir," I said. "In the same way," he said, "no one will enter the kingdom of God unless he receives his holy name.
89:5 Suppose you want to go into a city, and that city has a wall all around it and only one gate. Can you go into that city except through the gate it has?" "Sir," I said, "how could it be done any other way?" "Well then," he said, "you cannot go into the city except through the gate it has. In the same way, a person cannot enter the kingdom of God any other way except through the name of his Son, the one he loves.
89:6 Did you see the crowd building the tower?" he said. "I saw it, sir," I said. "All of those," he said, "are glorious angels. These are the wall around the Lord. But the gate is the Son of God. This is the one way in to the Lord. So no one will come in to him any other way except through his Son.
89:7 Did you see the six men," he said, "and the glorious, great man in the middle of them, the one who walked around the tower and rejected stones from the building?" "I saw him, sir," I said.
89:8 "The glorious man," he said, "is the Son of God, and those six are the glorious angels who support him on his right and on his left. Not one of these glorious angels," he said, "will come in to God without him. Whoever does not receive his name will not enter the kingdom of God."
## The Tower, the Virgins and the Women in Black
90:1 "And the tower," I said, "what is it?" "This tower," he said, "is the church."
90:2 "And these virgins, who are they?" "They are holy spirits," he said. "A person cannot be found in the kingdom of God any other way unless these clothe him in their garment. For if you receive only the name, and do not receive the garment from them, it will do you no good at all. For these virgins are powers of the Son of God. If you bear the name but do not bear his power, you will be bearing his name for nothing.
90:3 The stones you saw thrown out," he said, "are those who bore the name but did not put on the virgins' clothing." "Sir," I said, "what kind of clothing do they have?" "Their names themselves are their clothing," he said. "Whoever bears the name of the Son of God ought to bear their names too. For the Son himself bears the names of these virgins.
90:4 All the stones you saw go into the building of the tower," he said, "handed in by their hands, and staying in the building, have put on the power of these virgins.
90:5 That is why you see that the tower has become one single stone with the rock. In the same way, those who have believed in the Lord through his Son, and who put on these spirits, will become one spirit, one body, and their clothes will all be one color. Those who bear the names of the virgins have their home in the tower."
90:6 "Then, sir," I said, "why were the rejected stones thrown out? They did go through the gate, and they were set into the building of the tower by the hands of the virgins." "Since you care about everything," he said, "and you look into things exactly, listen about the rejected stones.
90:7 All of these," he said, "received the name of the Son of God, and they also received the power of these virgins. When they had received these spirits, they were made strong, and they were with the slaves of God. They had one spirit and one body and one garment, because they thought the same things and did what was right.
90:8 But after some time they were talked into it by the women you saw dressed in black clothes, with their shoulders bare and their hair loose, women beautiful in form. When they saw these women, they desired them, and they put on their power and took off the virgins' garment and power.
90:9 So these were thrown out of the house of God and handed over to those women. But those who were not fooled by the beauty of these women stayed in the house of God. There you have the explanation of the ones thrown out."
## The Pause Is for Repentance
91:1 "Then what about this, sir?" I said. "If these people, being what they are, repent and throw off their desire for these women, and come back to the virgins, and walk in their power and in their works, will they not enter the house of God?"
91:2 "They will enter," he said, "if they throw off the works of these women, take up the power of the virgins again, and walk in their works. That is the very reason the building had a pause: so that if these people repent, they may go into the building of the tower. But if they do not repent, then others will enter, and these will be thrown out for good."
91:3 For all these things I gave thanks to the Lord, because he had compassion on all who are called by his name. He sent the angel of repentance out to us who had sinned against him, and he renewed our spirit. We were already ruined and had no hope of living, and he made our life new.
91:4 "Now, sir," I said, "make clear to me why the tower was not built on the ground, but on the rock and on the gate." "Are you still foolish and senseless?" he said. "Sir," I said, "I have to ask you about everything, because I cannot understand anything at all. All these things are great and glorious and hard for people to understand."
91:5 "Listen," he said. "The name of the Son of God is great. It cannot be contained, and it holds up the whole world. If, then, all creation is held up by the Son of God, what do you think about those he has called, who bear the name of the Son of God and walk in his commandments?
91:6 So do you see whom he holds up? Those who bear his name with their whole heart. He himself became their foundation, and he gladly holds them up, because they are not ashamed to bear his name."
## The Names of the Virgins and the Women
92:1 "Sir," I said, "make clear to me the names of the virgins, and of the women dressed in black clothes." "Hear the names of the stronger virgins," he said, "the ones stationed at the corners.
92:2 The first is Faith, the second Self-control, the third Power, the fourth Patience. The others, stationed between them, have these names: Simplicity, Innocence, Purity, Cheerfulness, Truth, Discernment, Harmony, Love. The one who bears these names and the name of the Son of God will be able to enter the kingdom of God.
92:3 Hear also," he said, "the names of the women who have the black clothes. Four of these too are stronger. The first is Unbelief, the second Lack of Self-control, the third Disobedience, the fourth Deceit. Their followers are called Grief, Wickedness, Shameless Living, Bad Temper, Lying, Foolishness, Slander, Hatred. A slave of God who bears these names will see the kingdom of God, but he will not enter it."
92:4 "But sir," I said, "the stones fitted into the building from the deep, who are they?" "The first ten," he said, "the ones laid in the foundations, are the first generation. The twenty-five are the second generation of righteous men. The thirty-five are God's prophets and his servants. The forty are apostles and teachers of the preaching of the Son of God."
92:5 "Then, sir," I said, "why did the virgins hand these stones in for the building of the tower too, carrying them through the gate?"
92:6 "Because these were the first to bear these spirits," he said, "and they never parted from one another at all, neither the spirits from the people nor the people from the spirits. The spirits stayed with them until they fell asleep. If they had not had these spirits with them, they would not have been useful for the building of this tower."
