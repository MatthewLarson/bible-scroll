# Review: Hrm 025-049 (Vision 5 and the Mandates)

## Verdict

Sound work. I spot-checked 25:1, 29:1-8, 31:1-2, 31:6, 32:1-4, 35:2, 37:1, 43:1 and 46:4 against Lake's Greek. The translation is faithful, with one ambiguity at 29:1. The 25:0, 31:2 and 31:6 variants match Lake's footnotes in `texts/src/Hrm.app.json`. The Catechism quotations check out. There is no Augsburg or Dositheus claim anywhere in this part. The main problems are the "Revelation 5" heading, a naming split between "Commandment" and "Mandate", and a thin `penance` topic. `--check Hrm/025-049` gives 0 errors and 0 warnings. `--check Hrm` has 4 `cited_in` errors in book.json, which is outside this part (cross-part).

## Findings

1. **Major.** `plain.txt` 25:0 and `variants.json` 25:0 (`follow: 0`). The 0-verse prints "Revelation 5." On this site that reads as the book of Revelation, chapter 5. The chapter title, the `sum` and the section heading all say "Vision 5". Lake's footnote confirms the Sinaiticus word is ἀποκάλυψις, but Athos and the Ethiopic have ὅρασις (Vision), and Vision 1-4 are headed that way. Fix: print "Vision 5" in the translation, make the variant follow ὅρασις, and explain Sinaiticus's ἀποκάλυψις in the variant's `b`. If "Revelation" must stay, write "Apocalypse (Revelation) 5, in Sinaiticus".
2. **Minor.** Heading inconsistency. Mandate headings are `##` lines in plain.txt, while Vision 5's heading is a verse (25:0). Also, plain.txt says "Commandment 4" for chapters 29-32 while the chapter titles say "Mandate 4.1" to "4.4". Fix: pick one term (`terms.json` / the brief). If the 25:0 verse stays, say why it is a verse. Check how Visions 1-4 handle their headings (cross-part, other reviewers).
3. **Minor.** 29:1 "If you do this, you are committing a great sin." The Greek τοῦτο γὰρ ποιῶν points back to letting evil thoughts come into the heart. "This" in the translation can wrongly read as "keeping yourself pure". Fix: "For doing that [letting such thoughts rise] is a great sin."
4. **Minor.** 31:6 variant `why`. The claim that A's reading "no one doubts" is argument, not evidence. State plainly that Lake chose E(L) over A on his own judgment. The remaining facts are correct: Sinaiticus ends at the "φη" of φησί in 31:6, and the note on E(L) versus A is Lake's. Also, the 31:2 `why` says Sinaiticus is missing "from 30:3 to 31:4". The Lake note only says "to Mand. IV. 3, 4", so check that 31:4 is the right verse.
5. **Minor.** `penance.json` is not a duplicate. `repentance` is a stub, and `church-discipline`, `forgiveness`, `perseverance` and `baptism` exist and are linked. But the lead promises "disagreements among the churches" and the topic has only a New Testament section. Fix: add a short, sourced section on the churches' practice, or trim the lead. Use `ccc` (1446-1447) for the Catholic side and nothing unopened for the others. Also add `ccc` to its `sources` if cited. Add `church-discipline` to the topic's `related` list.
6. **Minor.** Catechism claims at chapters.json 31 (deep) were opened and verified at vatican.va. CCC 1446 reads "the second plank [of salvation] after the shipwreck which is the loss of grace". CCC 1447 has "only once in a lifetime" (order of penitents) and "envisioned the possibility of repetition". Fix: add the bracket "[of salvation]" and a `ccc` source entry, since the part's sources.json lacks one. Also chapters.json 31 `views.D` quotes the plank phrase without the bracket.
7. **Note.** The chapter-31 view on the Reformed and Lutheran side is "no church keeps Hermas's limit", and the ellipsis in the `D` view quote is unmarked. Do not add Augsburg or Dositheus unless opened.

## Fixes applied
1. Accepted (lead decision 2). 25:0 now prints "Vision 5."; the variant follows reading 1 (ὅρασις, A and E); `why` gives the reason (Lake's heads ὅρασις α-δ over Visions 1-4 in texts/src/Hrm.extra.json, Lake's "always quoted as the Fifth Vision", and the clash with the book of Revelation); `b` explains Sinaiticus's ἀποκάλυψις.
2. Accepted (lead decision 2). The twelve plain.txt headings "Commandment N" are now "Mandate N", matching the chapter titles. Mandate headings stay `##`; 25:0 stays a verse because Lake prints Ἀποκάλυψις ε as a line of his Greek text (Hrm.extra.json `heads`); Visions 1-4 in 001-024 use `##` only.
3. Accepted. 29:1 now reads "For if you let such thoughts rise, you are committing a great sin." (τοῦτο points back to ἀναβαινέτω; checked against Lake's Greek). No other note quotes the old wording.
4. Accepted. 31:6 `why` now says Lake chose E (L) over A by his own judgment, without the "no one doubts" argument. 31:2: checked Lake's note "to Mand. IV. 3, 4 (καρδιογνώστης)"; 31:4 is right, and the `why` now names the word.
5. Accepted. penance.json: new sourced section "Later practice" from CCC 1446-1447 (opened at vatican.va this session), labeled as the Catholic account, pointing to the Psalm 32 and Hebrews 6 cards for the other churches; lead trimmed to match; `ccc` added to sources. `church-discipline` was already in `related`.
6. Accepted. views.D now quotes "the second plank [of salvation] ..." with (1446); the deep section already had the bracket. `ccc` is a global key, so it is cited on the 31:6 note rather than duplicated in the part's sources.json.
7. Accepted (lead decisions 5 and 7). The flat "no church keeps" and "the churches today agree" sentences (31 deep, 31:6 note) are narrowed to the sourced Catholic statement (CCC 1447, "envisioned the possibility of repetition") plus the card pointers. No Augsburg or Dositheus added. Also cut unsourced superlatives: 26:1 "the strongest early sign", 26:1 "among the clearest early Christian statements", 31:6 "famous rule".
