## Vision 1: Rhoda and the woman in heaven
1:1 The man who raised me sold me to a woman named Rhoda in Rome. Many years later I came across her again, and I started to love her like a sister.
1:2 Some time after that I saw her bathing in the Tiber River. I gave her my hand and helped her out of the river. When I saw how beautiful she was, I thought to myself, "I would be a happy man if I had a wife like her, in looks and in character." That was all I had in mind, and nothing else at all.
1:3 Some time after that I was on my way to Cumae, praising the things God has made for how great and splendid and powerful they are. As I walked, I fell asleep. A spirit took hold of me and carried me off through a place with no road, where no one could travel. The ground was all cliffs, broken up by the streams of water. I crossed that river and came to level ground. There I knelt down and began to pray to the Lord and to confess my sins.
1:4 While I was praying, the sky opened, and I saw the woman I had desired. She greeted me from heaven and said, "Hello, Hermas."
1:5 I looked at her and said, "Lady, what are you doing here?" She answered, "I was taken up to heaven to bring your sins to light before the Lord."
1:6 I said to her, "So are you now the one who accuses me?" "No," she said, "but listen to the words I am going to say to you. God, who lives in heaven, who created from nothing the things that are, and multiplied them and made them grow for the sake of his holy church, is angry with you, because you sinned against me."
1:7 I answered her, "I sinned against you? Where, or when, did I ever say a shameful word to you? Didn't I always think of you as a goddess? Didn't I always treat you with respect, as a sister? Why do you tell these wicked and filthy lies about me, woman?"
1:8 She laughed at me and said, "The desire for wickedness rose up in your heart. Or don't you think it is an evil thing for a righteous man if an evil desire rises up in his heart? It is a sin," she said, "and a great one. For a righteous man makes righteous plans. As long as his plans are righteous, his good name stands firm in heaven, and he finds the Lord ready to make peace with him in everything he does. But those who make evil plans in their hearts bring death and captivity down on themselves. This is true above all of those who make this world their own and swagger in their wealth, and do not hold on to the good things that are coming.
1:9 Their souls will repent, but they have no hope. They have given up on themselves and on their own life. But you, pray to God, and he will heal your sins, and those of your whole household and of all the saints."
## The old woman with the book
2:1 After she had said these words, the heavens were shut. I was shaking all over and full of grief. I said to myself, "If even this sin is written down against me, how can I be saved? How will I win God's mercy for my sins, all of them? With what words will I ask the Lord to be kind to me?"
2:2 While I was thinking this over and turning it about in my heart, I saw in front of me a large white chair, made of wool as white as snow. An old woman came, dressed in shining clothes and holding a book in her hands. She sat down by herself and greeted me: "Hello, Hermas." Full of grief and in tears, I said, "Hello, lady."
2:3 She said to me, "Why the gloomy face, Hermas? You are patient and slow to take offense, and you are always laughing. Why do you look so downcast and not cheerful?" I said to her, "Because of a very good woman. She says I sinned against her."
2:4 She said, "Such a thing should never be found in a slave of God! But it did rise up in your heart about her. For God's slaves, a plan like that brings sin with it. It is an evil and shocking plan for a spirit so revered, one already tested, to desire an evil deed, and above all for Hermas, the self-controlled, who keeps away from every evil desire and is full of simplicity and great innocence.
3:1 "But that is not why God is angry with you. He wants you to turn your household around, for they have broken the law against the Lord and against you, their parents. You love your children too much, so you did not correct your household. You let it go to ruin. That is why the Lord is angry with you. But he will heal all the evil that has happened in your household before now. Because of their sins and lawless deeds, you have been ruined by the business of everyday life.
3:2 But the Lord's great compassion has had mercy on you and your household. He will make you strong and set you firm in his glory. Only do not be lazy. Take heart and make your household strong. A blacksmith hammers at his work until he makes it what he wants. In the same way, a righteous word spoken every day wins out over all wickedness. So do not stop correcting your children. For I know that if they repent with all their heart, they will be written in the books of life with the saints."
## The old woman reads God's glories
3:3 When she had finished these words, she said to me, "Do you want to hear me read?" I said, "Yes, lady, I do." She said to me, "Listen, then, and hear the glories of God." I heard great and wonderful things that I could not remember. All the words were terrifying, more than a person can bear. But I did remember the last words, for they were helpful to us and gentle:
3:4 "Look! The God of the powers, whom I love, created the world by his mighty power and his great discernment. By his glorious plan he clothed his creation with beauty. By his strong word he fixed the sky in place and set the earth firm on the waters. By his own wisdom and care he created his holy church, and he blessed her. Look! He is moving the heavens and the mountains, the hills and the seas, and everything is becoming level ground for his chosen. So he will give them what he promised, with great glory and joy, if they keep God's rules, which they received with great faith."
4:1 When she had finished reading, she got up from the chair. Four young men came, picked up the chair, and went off with it toward the east.
4:2 She called me over, touched my chest, and said to me, "Did you like my reading?" I said to her, "Lady, I like these last words, but the first ones were hard and harsh." She told me, "These last words are for the righteous. The first ones are for the nations and for those who have turned away from the faith."
4:3 While she was talking with me, two men appeared. They took her by the arms and went off toward the east, where the chair had gone. She went away cheerful, and as she went she said to me, "Be brave, Hermas!"
## Vision 2: The little book copied
5:1 I was on my way to Cumae at the same season as the year before. As I walked, I remembered last year's vision. Again a spirit took hold of me and carried me off to the same place as the year before.
5:2 When I came to the place, I knelt down and began to pray to the Lord and to praise his name, because he had thought me worthy and had made my earlier sins known to me.
5:3 When I got up from my prayer, I saw in front of me the old woman I had seen the year before. She was walking and reading a little book. She said to me, "Can you announce these things to God's chosen?" I said to her, "Lady, I cannot remember so much. But give me the little book, so that I can copy it." "Take it," she said, "and give it back to me."
5:4 I took it, went off to a spot in the countryside, and copied it all, letter by letter, because I could not make out where the syllables broke. As soon as I had finished the letters of the little book, it was suddenly snatched from my hand. I did not see who took it.
## The one chance to repent
6:1 Fifteen days later, after I had fasted and begged the Lord again and again, the meaning of the writing was revealed to me. This is what was written:
6:2 "Hermas, your children have rejected God. They have spoken evil against the Lord. They have betrayed their parents with great wickedness, and they have the name of people who betray their parents. And their betrayal did them no good. Instead, they added to their sins wild living and a jumble of wickedness, and so their lawless deeds have reached their full measure.
6:3 But make these words known to all your children, and to your wife, who is going to be your sister. For she does not hold back her tongue, and she uses it to do harm. But once she hears these words, she will hold it back, and she will find mercy.
6:4 After you have made these words known to them, as the Master commanded me to reveal them to you, then all the sins they committed before will be forgiven them. So will the sins of all the saints who have sinned up to this day, if they repent with all their heart and drive double-mindedness out of their hearts.
6:5 For the Master has sworn by his own glory about his chosen: if anyone sins any more now that this day has been set, he will not be saved. For repentance for the righteous has an end. The days of repentance are over for all the saints. But for the nations there is repentance until the last day.
6:6 So you will tell the leaders of the church to set their ways straight in righteousness, so that they may receive the promises in full, with great glory.
6:7 So stand firm, you who do what is right, and do not be double-minded, so that your passing may be with the holy angels. Blessed are you, all who endure the great suffering that is coming, and all who will not deny their life.
6:8 For the Lord has sworn by his Son that those who deny their Christ have been rejected from their life. That means those who are now about to deny him in the days to come. But to those who denied him before, he has been merciful, because of his great compassion.
7:1 "But you, Hermas, stop holding a grudge against your children, and do not neglect your sister, so that they may be cleansed of their past sins. They will be trained with a training that is right, if you do not hold a grudge against them. Holding a grudge brings death. And you, Hermas, have had great troubles of your own because of the wrongdoing of your household, because you did not care about them. Instead, you neglected them and got tangled up in your evil business dealings.
7:2 But you are saved because you did not turn away from the living God, and by your simplicity and great self-control. These have saved you, if you stay with them. They save everyone who does such things and lives in innocence and simplicity. Such people will overcome all wickedness and will last on into eternal life.
7:3 Blessed are all who do what is right. They will never be destroyed.
## A word for Maximus
7:4 And you will say to Maximus, 'Look, suffering is coming. If you think it best, deny again.' 'The Lord is near to those who turn back,' as it is written in Eldad and Modat, who prophesied to the people in the wilderness."
## Who the old woman is
8:1 Then, brothers, while I was asleep, a revelation came to me from a very handsome young man. He said to me, "The old woman you got the little book from: who do you think she is?" I said, "The Sibyl." "You are wrong," he said. "She is not." "Then who is she?" I asked. "The Church," he said. I said to him, "Then why is she old?" "Because," he said, "she was created first of all. That is why she is old. And the world was put in order for her sake."
8:2 After this I saw a vision in my house. The old woman came and asked me if I had already given the book to the elders. I said I had not. "You did well," she said, "for I have words to add. When I have finished all the words, they will be made known to all the chosen through you.
8:3 So you will write two little books, and you will send one to Clement and one to Grapte. Clement will send it to the cities abroad, for that is the task given to him. Grapte will instruct the widows and the orphans. But you will read it in this city with the elders who lead the church."
## Vision 3: The couch in the field
9:1 Brothers, this is what I saw.
9:2 I had fasted many times and begged the Lord to make plain to me the revelation he had promised to show me through that old woman. That same night the old woman appeared to me and said, "Since you are so needy and so eager to know everything, go to the field where you farm. At about the fifth hour I will appear to you and show you what you must see."
9:3 I asked her, "Lady, to what part of the field?" "Wherever you like," she said. I picked a lovely, out-of-the-way spot. But before I could speak to her and name the spot, she said to me, "I will come there, to the place you want."
9:4 So, brothers, I went to the field. I counted the hours and came to the place where I had arranged for her to come. There I saw an ivory couch set out. On the couch lay a linen cushion, and over it was spread a cloth of fine linen.
9:5 When I saw these things set out and no one in the place, I was stunned. Something like trembling seized me, and my hair stood on end. A kind of shudder came over me, because I was alone. Then I came to myself. I remembered the glory of God, took courage, knelt down, and confessed my sins to the Lord again, as I had before.
9:6 She came with six young men, whom I had seen before. She stood beside me and listened as I prayed and confessed my sins to the Lord. She touched me and said, "Hermas, stop asking everything about your sins. Ask about righteousness too, so that you may take some share of it home to your household."
9:7 She lifted me up by the hand, led me to the couch, and said to the young men, "Go and build."
9:8 When the young men had gone and we were alone, she said to me, "Sit here." I said to her, "Lady, let the elders sit first." "I am telling you," she said, "sit down."
9:9 I wanted to sit on the right side, but she would not let me. She motioned with her hand for me to sit on the left. As I sat thinking about it, upset that she would not let me sit on the right, she said to me, "Are you upset, Hermas? The place on the right belongs to others, those who have already pleased God and have suffered for the sake of the Name. You still lack a great deal before you can sit with them. But keep on in your simplicity, as you are doing, and you will sit with them. So will all who do the works they did and bear what they bore."
10:1 "What did they bear?" I asked. "Listen," she said. "Whips, prisons, great sufferings, crosses, and wild beasts, for the sake of the Name. That is why the right side of the holy place belongs to them, and to anyone who suffers for the Name. The left side belongs to the rest. But both, those who sit on the right and those on the left, have the same gifts and the same promises. The only difference is that those on the right sit there and have a certain glory.
10:2 You long to sit on the right with them, but you fall short in many things. Yet you will be cleansed of your failings. And all who are not double-minded will be cleansed of all their sins up to this day."
10:3 After saying this, she wanted to leave. But I fell at her feet and begged her, by the Lord, to show me the vision she had promised.
## The tower on the water
10:4 She took my hand again, raised me up, and sat me on the couch, on the left. She herself sat on the right. Lifting a shining staff, she said to me, "Do you see something great?" I said to her, "Lady, I see nothing." She said to me, "Look! Don't you see in front of you a great tower being built on the water, out of shining square stones?"
10:5 The tower was being built in a square by the six young men who had come with her. Tens of thousands of other men were bringing stones, some from the deep and some from the land, and handing them to the six young men. They took them and built.
10:6 All the stones dragged up from the deep they set into the building just as they were. They had been shaped already, and their joints fit those of the other stones. They were joined so closely to one another that the seams did not show. The building of the tower looked as if it were made from a single stone.
10:7 Of the other stones, the ones brought from the dry land, they threw some away and set some into the building. Others they smashed and threw far from the tower.
10:8 Many other stones lay around the tower, and they did not use them for the building. Some of them were scabby, some had cracks, some were cut too short, and some were white and round and did not fit into the building.
10:9 I saw other stones being thrown far from the tower. They landed on the road, but they did not stay on the road. They rolled off it into rough country where there was no road. Others fell into fire and were burning. Others fell near the water but could not roll into it, though they wanted to roll and get into the water.
## The tower explained
11:1 When she had shown me these things, she wanted to hurry away. I said to her, "Lady, what good does it do me to have seen these things if I do not know what they mean?" She answered me, "You are a sly man, wanting to know about the tower." "Yes, lady," I said, "so that I may tell the brothers. Then they will be more cheerful, and when they hear these things they will know the Lord in his great glory."
11:2 She said, "Many will hear. When they hear, some of them will rejoice and some will weep. But even these, if they hear and repent, will rejoice too. So listen to the parables of the tower, for I will reveal everything to you. And stop bothering me about revelation. These revelations have come to an end, for they are complete. But you will not stop asking for revelations, because you have no shame.
11:3 The tower that you see being built is I myself, the Church, who appeared to you both now and before. So ask whatever you want about the tower, and I will reveal it to you, so that you may rejoice with the saints."
11:4 I said to her, "Lady, since you have once counted me worthy to have everything revealed to me, reveal it." She said to me, "Whatever can be revealed to you will be revealed. Only keep your heart turned toward God, and do not be double-minded about anything you see."
11:5 I asked her, "Lady, why has the tower been built on water?" "I told you before," she said, "and you keep searching carefully. Since you keep searching, you are finding the truth. So listen to why the tower has been built on water. It is because your life was saved through water, and will be saved. The tower has been founded by the word of the almighty and glorious Name, and it is held together by the unseen power of the Master."
12:1 I answered her, "Lady, this is a great and wonderful thing. But the six young men who are building, who are they, lady?" "They are the holy angels of God who were created first. The Lord handed over his whole creation to them, to make it grow, to build it up, and to rule over all of it. So the building of the tower will be finished through them."
12:2 "And the others who are bringing the stones, who are they?" "They too are holy angels of God, but these six are above them. So the building of the tower will be completed. Then they will all rejoice together around the tower and give glory to God, because the building of the tower has been finished."
12:3 I asked her, "Lady, I would like to know what becomes of the stones, and what their power means." She answered me, "It is not that you are more worthy than everyone else to have this revealed to you. There are others ahead of you and better than you, to whom these visions ought to have been revealed. But it has been revealed to you, and will be revealed, so that the name of God may be glorified. It is for the sake of the double-minded, who argue in their hearts over whether these things are so or not. Tell them that all these things are true. Nothing in them is outside the truth. All of it is strong and sure and firmly founded.
## The stones that are built in
13:1 "Now listen about the stones that are going into the building. The square white stones whose joints fit together are the apostles and overseers and teachers and deacons. They lived by the reverence God asks, and they watched over, taught and served God's chosen in purity and reverence. Some of them have fallen asleep, and some are still living. They always agreed with one another, kept peace among themselves, and listened to one another. That is why their joints fit together in the building of the tower."
13:2 "And the ones dragged up from the deep and set into the building, whose joints fit with the other stones already built in, who are they?" "These are the ones who have suffered for the name of the Lord."
13:3 "Lady, I want to know who the other stones are, the ones brought from the dry land." She said, "The ones that go into the building without being cut are those whom the Lord has tested and approved, because they walked in the Lord's straight way and carried out his commandments rightly."
13:4 "And the ones being brought and set into the building, who are they?" "They are young in the faith, and faithful. The angels warn them to do good, because wickedness has been found in them."
13:5 "And the ones they were rejecting and throwing aside, who are they?" "These are people who have sinned and want to repent. That is why they were not thrown far from the tower: they will be useful for the building if they repent. So those who are going to repent will be strong in the faith if they repent now, while the tower is being built. But once the building is finished, they no longer have a place. They will be castaways. All they will have is this: to lie beside the tower.
## The stones that are rejected
14:1 "Do you want to know about the stones that were smashed and thrown far from the tower? These are the children of lawlessness. They believed, but in pretense, and every kind of wickedness stayed with them. That is why they have no salvation: they are no use for building, because of their wickedness. So they were broken up and thrown far away because of the Lord's anger, because they made him angry.
14:2 As for the many others you saw lying there, not going into the building: the scabby ones are those who have known the truth but did not stay in it."
14:3 "And the ones with cracks, who are they?" "These are the ones who hold things against one another in their hearts and are not at peace among themselves. They put on a face of peace, but when they leave one another, their wickedness stays in their hearts. These are the cracks that the stones have.
14:4 The stones that are cut short are those who have believed and have the greater part of their lives in righteousness, but some parts in lawlessness. That is why they are cut short and not whole."
14:5 "And the white round ones that do not fit into the building, who are they, lady?" She answered me, "How long will you be foolish and senseless, asking about everything and understanding nothing? These are people who have faith, but who also have the wealth of this world. When suffering comes, they deny their Lord because of their wealth and their business dealings."
14:6 I answered her, "Lady, then when will they be useful for the building?" "When the wealth that leads them astray is cut away from them," she said, "then they will be useful to God. A round stone cannot become square unless it is trimmed and loses part of itself. In the same way, those who are rich in this world cannot become useful to the Lord unless their wealth is cut away from them.
14:7 Learn it first from yourself. When you were rich, you were no use, but now you are useful and helpful for life. Be useful to God, all of you. For you yourself are being used as one of these very stones.
15:1 "The other stones, which you saw thrown far from the tower, falling onto the road and rolling off it into rough country with no road: these are people who have believed, but because of their double-mindedness they leave their true way. They think they can find a better way, so they wander and suffer as they walk through country with no road.
15:2 The ones falling into the fire and burning are those who have turned away from the living God for good. It no longer even enters their hearts to repent, because of their desires for wild living and the wicked things they have done.
15:3 Do you want to know who the others are, the ones that fall near the water and cannot roll into it? These are the ones who heard the message and wanted to be baptized in the name of the Lord. But then, when they call to mind the purity the truth demands, they repent of it and go back again after their evil desires."
15:4 So she finished explaining the tower.
15:5 Still shameless, I asked her more. All these stones that were thrown away and do not fit into the building of the tower: is there repentance for them, and do they have a place in this tower? "They have repentance," she said, "but they cannot fit into this tower.
15:6 They will fit into another place, a much lower one, and only when they have been tormented and have served out the days of their sins. They will be moved because they had a share in the righteous word. And then it will happen that they are moved out of their torments, because of the evil works they did. But if it does not enter their hearts, they are not saved, because their hearts are hard."
## The seven women
16:1 When I had stopped asking her about all these things, she said to me, "Do you want to see something else?" I was eager to see it, and I was overjoyed at the thought.
16:2 She looked at me, smiled, and said to me, "Do you see seven women around the tower?" "I see them, lady," I said. "This tower is held up by them, by the Lord's command.
16:3 Now hear what each of them does. The first of them, the one with her hands clasped, is called Faith. Through her God's chosen are saved.
16:4 The second, the one dressed for action and standing strong like a man, is called Self-control. She is the daughter of Faith. Whoever follows her becomes happy in his life, because he will keep away from every evil deed, believing that if he keeps away from every evil desire he will inherit eternal life."
16:5 "And the others, lady, who are they?" "They are daughters of one another. Their names are Simplicity, Understanding, Innocence, Reverence and Love. So when you do all the works of their mother, you can live."
16:6 "Lady," I said, "I would like to know what power each of them has." "Listen," she said, "to the powers they have.
16:7 Their powers hold on to one another, and they follow one another in the order in which they were born. From Faith is born Self-control, from Self-control Simplicity, from Simplicity Innocence, from Innocence Reverence, from Reverence Understanding, and from Understanding Love. Their works are pure and reverent and godly.
16:8 So whoever serves them like a slave and has the strength to hold on to their works will have his home in the tower with God's saints."
16:9 Then I asked her about the times: was it already the end? She cried out in a loud voice, "You senseless man! Don't you see that the tower is still being built? When the tower is finished being built, then the end comes. But it will be built up quickly. Do not ask me anything more. This reminder is enough for you and for the saints, and so is the renewing of your spirits.
16:10 But these things were not revealed to you alone. They were revealed so that you might make them plain to everyone,
16:11 after three days, for you must understand them first. And I command you first, Hermas, to speak all these words I am about to tell you into the ears of the saints, so that when they have heard them and done them, they may be cleansed of their wickedness, and you along with them.
## The Church's charge to her children
17:1 "Listen to me, children. I raised you in great simplicity, innocence and reverence, through the mercy of the Lord, who let righteousness fall on you drop by drop, so that you might be made righteous and holy, free of all wickedness and all crookedness. But you do not want to stop your wickedness.
17:2 So now listen to me. Keep the peace among yourselves. Look after one another and help one another. Do not take your share of the things God has made in heaps for yourselves alone, but share them with those in need.
17:3 For some bring sickness on their bodies by eating too much, and they ruin their flesh. But those who have no food have their flesh ruined because they do not have enough to eat, and their bodies waste away.
17:4 So this lack of sharing among you does harm to you who have and do not share with those in need.
17:5 Look at the judgment that is coming. You who have more than enough, seek out the hungry while the tower is not yet finished. For after the tower is finished, you will want to do good, and you will have no chance.
17:6 So watch out, you who swagger in your wealth. The needy may groan, and their groaning may go up to the Lord, and you will be shut out with all your goods, outside the door of the tower.
17:7 Now I speak to you who lead the church and to you who take the front seats. Do not be like the poisoners. The poisoners carry their drugs in boxes, but you carry your drug and your poison in your hearts.
17:8 You are hardened. You do not want to clean your hearts and blend your insight into one, with a pure heart, so that you may find mercy from the great King.
17:9 So watch out, children, that these splits do not rob you of your life.
17:10 How can you want to train the Lord's chosen when you have no training yourselves? So train one another, and keep the peace among yourselves, so that I too may stand cheerful before the Father and give an account for all of you to the Lord."
## Why the woman looked old, then young
18:1 When she had finished speaking with me, the six young men who were building came and carried her off to the tower. Four others picked up the couch and carried it off to the tower too. I did not see their faces, because they were turned away.
18:2 As she was going, I asked her to reveal to me the meaning of the three forms in which she had appeared to me. She answered me, "About these you must ask someone else, so that it may be revealed to you."
18:3 Brothers, in the first vision, last year, she appeared to me as very old and sitting in a chair.
18:4 In the second vision her face was younger, but her body and her hair were older. She stood while she spoke to me, and she was more cheerful than before.
18:5 In the third vision she was young all over and most beautiful. Only her hair was old. She was completely cheerful, and she sat on a couch.
18:6 I was deeply troubled, wanting to know what this revelation meant. Then I saw the old woman in a vision of the night, and she said to me, "Every request needs humility. So fast, and you will receive what you ask from the Lord."
18:7 So I fasted one day, and that same night a young man appeared to me. He said to me, "Why do you keep asking for revelations in your prayers, again and again? Be careful that by asking for so much you do not harm your body.
18:8 These revelations are enough for you. Can you see revelations stronger than the ones you have seen?"
18:9 I answered him, "Sir, I ask only this: that the revelation about the three forms of the old woman be made complete." He answered me, "How long will you all be senseless? It is your double-mindedness that makes you senseless, and your hearts not being turned toward the Lord."
18:10 I answered him again, "But from you, sir, we will learn them more exactly."
19:1 "Listen," he said, "about the forms you are asking about.
19:2 In the first vision, why did she appear to you old and sitting in a chair? Because your spirit is old and already withered. It has no strength, because of your weakness and your double-mindedness.
19:3 For old people who no longer hope to grow young again expect nothing but their last sleep. In the same way, you have been weakened by the business of everyday life. You have given yourselves over to listlessness, and you have not thrown your worries on the Lord. Instead your minds have been broken, and you have grown old with your griefs."
19:4 "Sir, I would like to know why she was sitting in a chair." "Because everyone who is weak sits in a chair because of his weakness, so that his weak body may be held up. Now you have the figure of the first vision.
20:1 "In the second vision you saw her standing, with a face younger and more cheerful than before, but with an older body and older hair. Listen," he said, "to this parable too.
20:2 When an old man has already given up hope for himself because of his weakness and his poverty, he waits for nothing but the last day of his life. Then suddenly an inheritance is left to him. When he hears of it, he gets up, overjoyed, and puts on strength. He no longer lies around, but stands up. His spirit, already ruined by what he did before, is made new. He no longer sits, but acts like a man. So it is with you, now that you have heard the revelation the Lord revealed to you,
20:3 for he had compassion on you. You renewed your spirits and laid aside your weakness. Strength came to you, and you were made strong in the faith. When the Lord saw how you had been made strong, he was glad. That is why he showed you the building of the tower, and he will show you other things too, if you keep the peace among yourselves with all your heart.
21:1 "In the third vision you saw her younger, lovely and cheerful, and lovely in her appearance.
21:2 When good news comes to someone who is grieving, he forgets his old griefs at once. He waits for nothing but the news he has heard. From then on he is strengthened for what is good, and his spirit is made new by the joy he has received. In the same way, you have received a renewing of your spirits by seeing these good things.
21:3 And as for your seeing her sitting on a couch: that is a firm position, because a couch has four legs and stands firm. For the world too is held together by four elements.
21:4 So those who have repented completely will be young and firmly founded, those who have repented with all their heart. You now have the revelation complete. Do not ask anything more about revelation. But if anything is needed, it will be revealed to you."
## Vision 4: The beast on the road
22:1 Brothers, this is what I saw twenty days after the vision before it. It was a figure of the suffering that is coming.
22:2 I was going out to the country along the Campanian Way. The place is about ten stades from the main road, and it is easy to get to.
22:3 As I walked alone, I asked the Lord to complete the revelations and visions he had shown me through his holy Church. I asked him to make me strong and to give repentance to his slaves who had stumbled, so that his great and glorious name might be glorified, because he had thought me worthy to be shown his wonders.
22:4 As I was praising him and giving him thanks, something like the echo of a voice answered me: "Do not be double-minded, Hermas." I began to reason to myself and say, "Why should I be double-minded, when the Lord has set me on so firm a foundation and I have seen such glorious things?"
22:5 I went on a little further, brothers, and suddenly I saw a cloud of dust that seemed to reach the sky. I began to say to myself, "Are cattle coming and kicking up dust?" It was about a stade away from me.
22:6 As the dust grew bigger and bigger, I suspected it was something divine. The sun shone out a little, and suddenly I saw a huge beast like some sea monster. Fiery locusts were coming out of its mouth. The beast was about a hundred feet long, and its head looked like a piece of pottery.
22:7 I began to weep and to ask the Lord to rescue me from it. Then I remembered the word I had heard: "Do not be double-minded, Hermas."
22:8 So, brothers, I put on the faith of the Lord. I remembered the great things he had taught me, and I took courage and gave myself up to face the beast. The beast came on with such a roar that it could have wrecked a city.
22:9 I came near it, and that huge monster stretched itself out on the ground. It put out nothing but its tongue, and it did not move at all until I had passed it.
22:10 On its head the beast had four colors: black, then the color of fire and blood, then gold, then white.
## The young woman in white
23:1 After I had passed the beast and gone on about thirty feet, suddenly a young woman met me. She was dressed as if she were coming out of a bridal room, all in white, with white sandals. She was veiled down to her forehead, and her head covering was a turban. Her hair was white.
23:2 I knew from the earlier visions that she was the Church, and I grew more cheerful. She greeted me: "Hello to you, man." And I greeted her back: "Hello, lady."
23:3 She answered me, "Did nothing meet you?" I said to her, "Lady, such a beast! It could destroy whole peoples. But by the power of the Lord and his great compassion I escaped it."
23:4 "You did well to escape it," she said, "because you threw your worry on God and opened your heart to the Lord, believing that you can be saved through nothing but the great and glorious Name. That is why the Lord sent his angel who is over the beasts, whose name is Thegri, and he shut its mouth so that it would not harm you. You have escaped great suffering because of your faith, and because you were not double-minded when you saw so huge a beast.
23:5 So go and tell the Lord's chosen all about his great deeds. Tell them that this beast is a figure of the great suffering that is coming. If you get ready ahead of time and repent toward the Lord with all your heart, you will be able to escape it, if your heart becomes pure and spotless and you serve the Lord without blame for the rest of the days of your life. Throw your worries on the Lord, and he will set them right.
23:6 You double-minded people, trust the Lord. He can do anything. He turns his anger away from you, and he sends whips on you who are double-minded. Woe to those who hear these words and refuse to listen! It would have been better for them not to be born."
## The four colors
24:1 I asked her about the four colors the beast had on its head. She answered me, "Again you are curious about such things!" "Yes, lady," I said. "Tell me what they are."
24:2 "Listen," she said. "The black is this world you live in.
24:3 The color of fire and blood means that this world must be destroyed by blood and fire.
24:4 The gold part is you who have escaped this world. For just as gold is tested by fire and becomes useful, so you too are being tested, you who live in the midst of them, the blood and the fire. So those who hold out and are put through the fire by them will be made clean. Just as gold throws off its dross, so you will throw off all grief and distress, and you will be made clean and useful for the building of the tower.
24:5 The white part is the age that is coming, in which God's chosen will live, for those whom God has chosen for eternal life will be spotless and pure.
24:6 So do not stop speaking into the ears of the saints. You have the figure of the great suffering that is coming, too. But if you are willing, it will be nothing. Remember what was written before."
24:7 When she had said this, she went away, and I did not see where she went, for a cloud came. I turned around, afraid, thinking that the beast was coming.
