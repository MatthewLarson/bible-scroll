## The stones from the deep and the seal
93:1 "Sir, make something more clear to me," I said. "What else are you looking for?" he said. "Sir," I said, "why did the stones come up out of the deep and get set into the building of the tower, when they had already borne these spirits?"
93:2 "They had to come up through water," he said, "so that they could be made alive. For they could not enter the kingdom of God any other way, unless they put off the deadness of their former life.
93:3 So these too, who had fallen asleep, received the seal of the Son of God and entered the kingdom of God. For before a person bears the name of the Son of God," he said, "he is dead. But when he receives the seal, he lays aside his deadness and takes up life.
93:4 So the seal is the water. They go down into the water dead, and they come up alive. So this seal was preached to them too, and they made use of it, so that they could enter the kingdom of God."
93:5 "Sir," I said, "why did the forty stones come up from the deep with them too, when they already had the seal?" "Because these apostles and teachers, who preached the name of the Son of God, fell asleep in the power and faith of the Son of God," he said, "and then preached to those who had fallen asleep before them as well. They themselves gave them the seal of the preaching.
93:6 So they went down into the water with them and came up again. But these went down alive and came up alive, while those who had fallen asleep before them went down dead and came up alive.
93:7 So it was through them that they were made alive and came to know the name of the Son of God. That is why they came up with them, were fitted with them into the building of the tower, and were built in with them without being cut. For they had fallen asleep in righteousness and in great purity. Only they did not have this seal. So now you have the explanation of these too." "I have it, sir," I said.
## Twelve mountains, twelve nations
94:1 "Now, sir, make the mountains clear to me. Why does each one look different from the others, and why are they so varied?" "Listen," he said. "These twelve mountains are the tribes that live in the whole world. The Son of God was preached to them through the apostles."
94:2 "But make clear to me, sir, why the mountains are so varied, each with a look of its own." "Listen," he said. "These twelve tribes that live in the whole world are twelve nations. They vary in insight and in mind. Just as you saw that the mountains vary, so the minds and the insight of the nations vary too. And I will make clear to you how each one acts."
94:3 "First, sir," I said, "make this clear. The mountains are so varied. So why, when their stones were set into the building, did they become bright with one color, like the stones that had come up out of the deep?"
94:4 "Because all the nations that live under heaven heard and believed," he said, "and they were called by the name of the Son of God. So when they received the seal, they had one insight and one mind. Their faith became one and their love one, and along with the name they bore the spirits of the virgins. That is why the building of the tower became bright with one color, like the sun.
94:5 But after they had come together and become one body, some of them made themselves unclean. They were thrown out of the family of the righteous and became again what they had been before, or rather even worse."
## Worse after knowing God
95:1 "Sir," I said, "how did they become worse, when they had come to know God?" "The one who does not know God and does evil," he said, "receives some punishment for his evil. But the one who has come to know God ought not to do evil any longer. He ought to do good.
95:2 So if the one who ought to do good does evil, does he not seem to do more evil than the one who does not know God? That is why those who have not known God and do evil are sentenced to death. But those who have known God and seen his mighty works, and still do evil, will be punished twice over and will die forever. This is how the church of God will be made clean.
95:3 You saw the stones taken out of the tower, handed over to the evil spirits, and thrown out from there. (And there will be one body of those made clean, just as the tower too became as if it were made of one stone after it was cleaned.) In the same way the church of God will be, after it has been made clean and has thrown away the evil, the hypocrites, the blasphemers, the double-minded, and those who do every kind of evil.
95:4 After these are thrown away, the church of God will be one body, one insight, one mind, one faith, one love. And then the Son of God will rejoice and be glad among them, because he has received his people back clean." "Sir," I said, "all this is great and glorious."
95:5 "Sir," I said, "make clear to me also the power and the deeds of each of the mountains, so that every soul that trusts in the Lord may hear and give glory to his great, wonderful and glorious name." "Listen," he said, "to how the mountains and the twelve nations vary.
## The black mountain and the bare mountain
96:1 "Those who believed from the first mountain, the black one, are like this. They are apostates, blasphemers against the Lord, and betrayers of God's slaves. For these there is no repentance, only death, and that is why they are black too. For their kind is lawless.
96:2 Those who believed from the second mountain, the bare one, are like this. They are hypocrites and teachers of evil. So these are like the first group: they have no fruit of righteousness. For just as their mountain has no fruit, so people like this have a name, but they are empty of faith, and there is no fruit of truth in them. So repentance is there for them, if they repent quickly. But if they are slow, their death will be with the first group."
96:3 "Sir," I said, "why is there repentance for these, but not for the first? What they do is almost the same." "There is repentance for these," he said, "because they did not blaspheme their Lord or become betrayers of God's slaves. But out of desire for gain they played the hypocrite, and each one taught what sinful people wanted. Still, they will pay some penalty. But repentance is there for them, because they did not become blasphemers or betrayers.
## The mountain of thorns and thistles
97:1 "Those who believed from the third mountain, the one with thorns and thistles, are like this. Some of them are rich, and others are tangled up in a great deal of business. The thistles are the rich, and the thorns are those tangled up in all kinds of business.
97:2 So these, who are tangled up in many kinds of business, do not stay close to God's slaves. They wander away, choked by their affairs. And the rich stay close to God's slaves only with difficulty, because they are afraid they will be asked for something. So people like this will enter the kingdom of God only with difficulty.
97:3 For just as it is hard to walk among thistles with bare feet, so it is hard for people like this to enter the kingdom of God.
97:4 But there is repentance for all of these, a quick one, so that in these days they may now make up for what they failed to do in earlier times, and do some good. If they repent and do some good, they will live to God. But if they stay in their ways, they will be handed over to those women, and the women will put them to death.
## The mountain of half-dry plants
98:1 "Those who believed from the fourth mountain are like this. That mountain had many plants, green at the top but dry at the roots, and some even dried up by the sun. Some of these believers are double-minded, and others have the Lord on their lips but not in their heart.
98:2 That is why their foundations are dry and have no strength. Only their words are alive; their works are dead. People like this are neither alive nor dead. So they are like the double-minded. For the double-minded are neither green nor dry: they are neither alive nor dead.
98:3 For just as these plants dried up when they saw the sun, so the double-minded, when they hear of suffering, worship idols out of cowardice and are ashamed of their Lord's name.
98:4 So people like this are neither alive nor dead. But these too will be able to live if they repent quickly. If they do not repent, they have already been handed over to the women who take away their life.
## The rough mountain: the know-it-alls
99:1 "Those who believed from the fifth mountain, the rough one with green plants, are like this. They are faithful, but slow to learn, stubborn and pleased with themselves. They want to know everything, and they know nothing at all.
99:2 Because of this stubbornness, discernment left them, and senseless folly came into them. They praise themselves as people with discernment, and they want to be self-appointed teachers, though they have no sense.
99:3 So because of this pride, many have been emptied out while they lifted themselves up. For stubbornness and empty confidence are a great demon. So many of these were thrown away. But some repented and believed, and they put themselves under those who have discernment, because they recognized their own folly.
99:4 And for the rest of people like this, repentance is there. For they were not evil, but rather foolish and senseless. If they repent, they will live to God. But if they do not repent, they will live with the women who do evil to them.
## The mountain of cracks: holding grudges
100:1 "Those who believed from the sixth mountain, the one with large and small cracks and withered plants in the cracks, are like this.
100:2 Those with the small cracks are the ones who hold things against one another. Their slander has made them wither in the faith. But many of them have repented. And the rest will repent too, when they hear my commandments, for their slanders are small, and they will repent quickly.
100:3 But those with the large cracks keep on in their slander, and they become people who hold grudges, raging at one another. So these were thrown away from the tower and rejected from its building. People like this will live only with difficulty.
100:4 If our God and Lord, who rules over all things and has authority over all his creation, holds no grudge against those who confess their sins, but shows mercy, does a human being, who is mortal and full of sins, hold a grudge against another human being, as if he could destroy him or save him?
100:5 And I, the angel of repentance, say to you: all of you who hold this way of thinking, put it away and repent, and the Lord will heal your former sins, if you make yourselves clean of this demon. If not, you will be handed over to it, to death.
## The green mountain: the single-hearted
101:1 "Those who believed from the seventh mountain are like this. On that mountain the plants were green and cheerful, and the whole mountain was thriving. Every kind of livestock and the birds of the sky grazed on the plants on this mountain, and the plants they grazed on grew all the more.
101:2 These people were always simple, innocent and blessed. They held nothing against one another, but always rejoiced over God's slaves. They had put on the holy spirit of these virgins, and they always had compassion for every person. From their own labor they supplied every person's needs, without reproaching anyone and without hesitating.
101:3 So the Lord saw their simplicity and their complete childlikeness, and he made them grow in the work of their hands and gave them favor in everything they did.
101:4 And I, the angel of repentance, say to you who are like this: stay as you are, and your descendants will never be wiped out. For the Lord has tested you and written you down among our number, and all your descendants will live with the Son of God. For you have received from his spirit.
## The mountain of springs: apostles and teachers
102:1 "Those who believed from the eighth mountain, where the many springs were and all the Lord's creation drank from the springs, are like this.
102:2 They are apostles and teachers who preached to the whole world and taught the Lord's word with reverence and purity. They kept back nothing at all for evil desire, but always lived in righteousness and truth, just as they had received the Holy Spirit. So people like this pass in with the angels.
## The desert mountain: deacons who stole, and those who denied
103:1 "Those who believed from the ninth mountain are like this. It was a desert mountain, with creeping things and wild beasts in it that destroy people.
103:2 Those with the stains are deacons who served badly. They robbed widows and orphans of their living, and they made gain for themselves from the ministry they had been given to carry out. If they stay in the same desire, they are dead, and there is no hope of life for them. But if they turn back and complete their ministry in purity, they will be able to live.
103:3 The scabby ones are those who have denied their Lord and have not turned back to him. They have become barren, like a desert. They do not stay close to God's slaves, but keep to themselves, and so they are destroying their own souls.
103:4 For a vine left inside some fence and neglected is ruined. The weeds make it a wasteland, and in time it grows wild and is no longer useful to its owner. In the same way, people like this have given up on themselves, and they have grown wild and become useless to their Lord.
103:5 So repentance is possible for these, if they are not found to have denied from the heart. But if someone is found to have denied from the heart, I do not know if he can live.
103:6 And I do not say this about these present days, as if someone who denies could then receive repentance. For it is impossible for anyone who is going to deny his Lord now to be saved. But for those who denied long ago, repentance seems to be there. So if anyone is going to repent, let him be quick about it, before the tower is finished. If not, he will be destroyed by the women, to death.
103:7 And the chipped stones are the deceitful and the slanderers. They are also the beasts you saw on the mountain. For just as the beasts destroy a person with their poison and kill him, so the words of people like this destroy a person and kill him.
103:8 So these are chipped in their faith because of the way they behave in themselves. But some repented and were saved. And the rest who are like this can be saved, if they repent. But if they do not repent, they will die at the hands of those women whose power they have.
## The mountain of sheltering trees: overseers who welcomed guests
104:1 "Those who believed from the tenth mountain, where there were trees sheltering some sheep, are like this.
104:2 They are overseers and people who welcome guests, who were always glad to take God's slaves into their homes, without pretending. And the overseers, by their ministry, always sheltered those in need and the widows without fail, and they always lived in purity.
104:3 So all these will be sheltered by the Lord forever. Those who have done these things are glorious in God's sight, and their place is already with the angels, if they go on serving the Lord to the end.
## The mountain of fruit trees: those who suffered for the name
105:1 "Those who believed from the eleventh mountain, where the trees were full of fruit, each adorned with a different kind of fruit, are like this.
105:2 They are the ones who suffered for the name of the Son of God. They suffered eagerly, with their whole heart, and they handed over their lives."
105:3 "Sir," I said, "why do all the trees have fruit, but some of them have finer fruit than others?" "Listen," he said. "All who have ever suffered for the name are glorious in God's sight, and the sins of all of them have been taken away, because they suffered for the name of the Son of God. Now listen to why their fruits vary, and why some are better.
105:4 All who were brought before the authorities and questioned," he said, "and did not deny, but suffered eagerly, are even more glorious in the Lord's sight. Theirs is the better fruit. But all who were cowardly and wavered, and debated in their hearts whether they would deny or confess, and then suffered, have smaller fruit, because this plan came up into their hearts. For this plan is evil, that a slave should deny his own master.
105:5 So watch out, you who are planning this, or this plan may stay in your hearts, and you will die to God. But you who suffer for the name ought to give glory to God, because God has counted you worthy to bear this name and to have all your sins healed.
105:6 So count yourselves blessed. Yes, consider it a great work you have done, if any of you suffers for God's sake. The Lord is giving you life, and you do not understand. For your sins had weighed you down, and if you had not suffered for the name of the Lord, you would have died to God because of your sins.
105:7 I am saying this to you who waver between denying and confessing: confess that you have a Lord, so that you are not handed over to prison for denying him.
105:8 If the Gentiles punish their slaves when one of them denies his master, what do you think the Lord, who has authority over all, will do to you? Take these plans out of your hearts, so that you may live to God forever.
## The white mountain: like babies
106:1 "Those who believed from the twelfth mountain, the white one, are like this. They are like little babies. No evil comes into their hearts. They have not known what wickedness is, but they have always stayed childlike.
106:2 So people like this will live in the kingdom of God without any doubt, because they did not make God's commandments unclean in any matter, but stayed childlike all the days of their life, keeping the same insight.
106:3 So all of you who stay this way," he said, "and become like babies, with no evil in you, will be more glorious than all those I have spoken of. For all babies are glorious in God's sight, and first in his presence. So blessed are you, all who put away wickedness from yourselves and put on innocence. You will live to God first of all."
106:4 When he had finished the parables of the mountains, I said to him, "Sir, now make clear to me about the stones that were taken from the plain and set into the building in place of the stones taken out of the tower, and about the round stones that were set into the building, and those that are still round."
## Stones from the roots of the white mountain
107:1 "Listen to all of this too," he said. "The stones taken from the plain and set into the building of the tower in place of the ones thrown away are the roots of the white mountain.
107:2 So, since those who believed from the white mountain were all found innocent, the lord of the tower ordered these from the roots of this mountain to be put into the building of the tower. For he knew that if these stones went into the building of the tower, they would stay bright, and not one of them would turn black.
107:3 But if he had added stones from the other mountains, he would have had to visit that tower again and clean it. But all these have been found white, those who have believed and those who are going to believe, for they are of the same kind. Blessed is this kind, because it is innocent.
107:4 Now listen about the round, bright stones too. They are all from the white mountain as well. And listen to why they were found round. Their riches darkened them a little and dimmed them from the truth. But they never turned away from God, and no evil word came out of their mouth, only all justice and the strength of truth.
107:5 So when the Lord saw their mind, that they could favor the truth and also stay good, he ordered their wealth to be cut back, but not taken from them completely, so that they could do some good with what was left to them. And they will live to God, because they are of a good kind. That is why they were cut down a little and set into the building of this tower.
## The round stones still waiting, and the shepherds
108:1 "But the others, which have stayed round until now and were not fitted into that building because they have not yet received the seal, have been put back in their place. For they were found very round.
108:2 This world and the empty show of their riches must be cut away from them, and then they will fit into God's kingdom. For they must enter God's kingdom, because the Lord has blessed this innocent kind. So not one of this kind will be lost. For even if one of them is tempted by the most wicked devil and does some wrong, he will quickly run back to his Lord.
108:3 I, the angel of repentance, count all of you happy who are innocent like babies, because your share is good and honored in God's sight.
108:4 And I tell all of you who have received this seal: keep your simplicity. Do not keep the memory of wrongs done to you. Do not stay in your wickedness, or in the bitter memory of wrongs. Become one in spirit, each of you. Heal these evil splits and remove them from among you, so that the Lord of the flocks may be glad about them.
108:5 He will be glad if they are all found healthy and none of them has fallen away. But if any of them is found to have fallen away, it will be woe to the shepherds.
108:6 And if the shepherds themselves are found to have fallen away, what will they say to the Master of the flock? That they fell away because of the sheep? They will not be believed. For it is unbelievable that a shepherd should suffer harm from sheep. Instead they will be punished for their lie. And I am a shepherd too, and I am bound to give a very strict account of you.
## The torn garment
109:1 "So heal yourselves while the tower is still being built.
109:2 The Lord lives among people who love peace, for peace truly is dear to him. But he stays far away from the quarrelsome and from those ruined by wickedness. So give the spirit back to him whole, just as you received it.
109:3 Suppose you give a new garment, whole, to a fuller, and you want to get it back whole again, but the fuller gives it back to you torn. Will you take it back? Won't you flare up at once and go after him with insults, saying, 'I gave you a whole garment. Why have you torn it and made it useless? Because of the tear you made in it, it cannot be used.' Won't you say all these words to the fuller about the tear he made in your garment?
109:4 So if you grieve like this over your garment and complain that you did not get it back whole, what do you think the Lord will do to you? He gave you the spirit whole, and you have made it completely useless, so that it can be of no use to its Lord. For it began to be useless once you had spoiled it. So will not the Lord of that spirit punish you with death for what you have done?"
109:5 "Certainly," I said, "he will punish all those he finds holding on to the memory of wrongs." "Do not trample on his kindness," he said, "but rather honor him, because he is so patient with your sins and is not like you. So repent, with a repentance that does you good.
## The marks smoothed over
110:1 "All these things written above I, the Shepherd, the angel of repentance, have shown and spoken to God's slaves. So if you believe, and listen to my words, and walk in them, and set your paths straight, you will be able to live. But if you stay in wickedness and in the memory of wrongs, no one of that kind will live to God. All that I had to say has now been said to you."
110:2 The Shepherd himself said to me, "Have you asked me about everything?" And I said, "Yes, sir." "Then why did you not ask me about the marks on the stones set into the building, and why we filled in the marks?" And I said, "I forgot, sir."
110:3 "Listen now about them," he said. "These are the people who have now heard my commandments and repented with all their hearts. When the Lord saw that their repentance was good and pure, and that they could stay in it, he ordered their earlier sins to be wiped out. For these marks were their sins, and they were smoothed level so that they would not show."
## The angel hands Hermas over
111:1 After I had finished writing this book, the angel who had handed me over to this Shepherd came into the house where I was and sat down on the couch, and this Shepherd stood at his right. Then he called me and said this to me:
111:2 "I have handed you and your house over to this Shepherd," he said, "so that he can protect you." "Yes, sir," I said. "So if you want to be protected from all trouble and all cruelty," he said, "and to succeed in every good work and word, and to have every virtue of justice, walk in his commandments, which I have given you, and you will be able to master all wickedness.
111:3 For if you keep his commandments, every desire and every sweet thing of this world will be under you, and success will follow you in every good undertaking. Take on yourself his maturity and self-restraint, and tell everyone that he is held in great honor and rank with the Lord, that he presides with great power and is mighty in his office. To him alone in the whole world the power of repentance has been given. Does he seem mighty to you? But all of you look down on his maturity and on the modesty he shows toward you."
## Hermas's ministry
112:1 I said to him, "Sir, ask him yourself whether, since he has been in my house, I have done anything out of order, anything by which I have offended him."
112:2 "I also know," he said, "that you have done nothing out of order, and that you will not. That is why I am saying these things to you, so that you will keep going. For he has given me a good report of you. But you will tell these words to the rest, so that those who have repented, or are going to repent, may think the same as you, and so that he may give me a good report of them, and I may give one to the Lord."
112:3 "Sir," I said, "I am telling everyone the great things of the Lord. And I hope that all who have sinned before, if they hear these things, will gladly repent and get their life back."
112:4 "So stay in this ministry," he said, "and carry it through to the end. All who carry out his commandments will have life, and great honor with the Lord. But all who do not keep his commandments are running away from their own life, and turning against him. They do not follow his commandments, but hand themselves over to death, and each of them becomes guilty of his own blood. But I tell you to serve these commandments, and you will have a cure for your sins.
## The virgins stay with Hermas
113:1 "And I have sent you these virgins to live with you, for I saw that they were friendly to you. So you have them as helpers, so that you can keep his commandments all the better. For these commandments cannot be kept without these virgins. I see that they are glad to be with you. But I will order them never to leave your house at all.
113:2 Only keep your house clean, for they will gladly live in a clean house. For they are clean and chaste and hard-working, and all of them have favor with the Lord. So if they find your house pure, they will stay with you. But if even a little uncleanness comes into it, they will leave your house at once. For these virgins love no uncleanness of any kind."
113:3 I said to him, "Sir, I hope I will please them, so that they will always be glad to live in my house. And just as this one, to whom you handed me over, finds no fault with me, these will find no fault with me either."
113:4 He said to the Shepherd, "I know that God's slave wants to live, and that he will keep these commandments and settle the virgins in purity."
113:5 When he had said this, he handed me over to the Shepherd again, and calling the virgins he said to them, "Since I see that you are glad to live in his house, I entrust him and his house to you, so that you never leave his house at all." And they were glad to hear these words.
## Do good before the tower is finished
114:1 Then he said to me, "Carry yourself bravely in this ministry. Tell everyone the great things of the Lord, and you will have favor in this ministry. So whoever walks in these commandments will live and be happy in his life. But whoever neglects them will not live, and will be unhappy in his life.
114:2 Tell everyone who is able to do right not to stop. Doing good works is good for them. And I say that every person must be rescued from hardship. For someone who is in need and suffers hardship in his daily life is in great torment and distress.
114:3 So whoever rescues a soul like this from distress wins great joy for himself. For someone troubled by hardship like this suffers the same torment as a person in chains. For many people, because of disasters like these, bring death on themselves when they cannot bear them. So whoever knows of a person's disaster like this and does not rescue him commits a great sin and becomes guilty of his blood.
114:4 So all of you who have received from the Lord, do good works, so that the building of the tower is not finished while you put off doing them. For the work of building it has been paused for your sake. So unless you hurry to do right, the tower will be finished, and you will be shut out."
114:5 After he had spoken with me, he got up from the couch, took the Shepherd and the virgins, and went away. But he told me that he would send the Shepherd and the virgins back to my house.
