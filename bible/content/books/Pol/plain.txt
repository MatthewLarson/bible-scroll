## Greeting
1:0 Polycarp and the elders with him, to the church of God that lives as a resident alien in Philippi. Mercy to you, and peace, from God Almighty and from Jesus Christ our Savior: may they be multiplied.
## Joy over the prisoners and over your faith
1:1 I shared your joy greatly in our Lord Jesus Christ. You welcomed the copies of true love, and you sent them on their way, as it was your part to do. They were wrapped in chains fit for saints, and those chains are the crowns of people truly chosen by God and our Lord.
1:2 I rejoiced too that the firm root of your faith, spoken of since early times, still lives and bears fruit for our Lord Jesus Christ. He endured for our sins until he reached death itself. God raised him, untying the birth pains of Hades.
1:3 You have not seen him, yet you believe in him, with a joy that words cannot tell, a joy made glorious. Many long to enter into that joy. For you know that it is by grace that you have been saved, not from works, but by the will of God through Jesus Christ.
## Serve God and remember the Lord's words
2:1 So tuck up your robes and serve God in fear and in truth. Leave behind empty, useless talk and the straying of the many. You have believed in the one who raised our Lord Jesus Christ from the dead and gave him glory and a throne at his right hand. All things in heaven and on earth were put under him, and every breathing thing worships him. He is coming as judge of the living and the dead, and God will demand his blood from those who refuse to obey him.
2:2 The one who raised him from the dead will raise us too, if we do his will, walk in his commands, and love what he loved. We must keep away from all unrighteousness, greed, love of money, slander and false testimony. We must not give back evil for evil, or abuse for abuse, or punch for punch, or curse for curse.
2:3 Instead, remember what the Lord said when he taught: "Do not judge, so that you are not judged. Forgive, and you will be forgiven. Show mercy, so that you will be shown mercy. The measure you use is the one that will be used to measure out to you." And: "Blessed are the poor, and those who are persecuted on account of righteousness, because the kingdom of God is theirs."
## Paul's wisdom and his letters
3:1 Brothers, I am writing to you about righteousness. I did not give myself leave to do it; you asked me first.
3:2 For neither I nor anyone like me is able to follow the wisdom of blessed and glorious Paul step by step. When he was with you, he taught the people of that time face to face, and he taught the word of truth exactly and firmly. And when he was away, he wrote you letters. Bend over them and read them closely, and you will be able to be built up toward the faith that was given you.
3:3 That faith is the mother of us all, with hope following behind it and love going ahead of it: love for God and Christ and for our neighbor. If anyone lives within these, he has fulfilled the command of righteousness. Whoever has love stays far away from every sin.
## Against the love of money
4:1 Love of money is where every trouble begins. We know that we carried nothing into the world, and we cannot carry anything out of it either. So let us arm ourselves with the weapons of righteousness, and let us first teach ourselves to walk in the Lord's command.
## Wives and widows
4:2 Then let us teach our wives to live in the faith given to them, in love and in purity. They are to cherish their own husbands with all truthfulness, love everyone alike with all self-control, and bring up their children in the training of the fear of God.
4:3 Let us teach the widows to be sensible about the faith of the Lord and to pray for everyone without stopping. They are to stay far from every false accusation, slander, false testimony, love of money and every evil. They should know that they are God's altar. Everything is checked for blemishes, and nothing gets past him: not our reasonings, not our thoughts, not one of the secrets of the heart.
## Deacons, young men and virgins
5:1 We know that "God is not sneered at." So we ought to live in a way worthy of his command and his glory.
5:2 In the same way, deacons must be blameless before his righteousness. They are deacons of God and Christ, not of human beings. They must not make false accusations, or say one thing to one person and another to the next. They must not love money. They must be self-controlled in everything, tenderhearted and hard-working, and live by the truth of the Lord, who became the servant of all. Please him in this present age, and we will also receive the age to come. For he promised us that he would raise us from the dead, and that if we live as citizens worthy of him, "we will also reign with him," if indeed we believe.
5:3 In the same way, the younger men must be blameless in everything. Above all they must take thought for purity and put a bridle on themselves to hold back from every evil. For it is good to be cut off from the desires in the world, because every desire wages war against the Spirit, and "neither those who commit sexual sin, nor soft men, nor men who lie with males will inherit the kingdom of God," nor will those who do what is out of place. So they must keep away from all these things and submit to the elders and deacons as to God and Christ. The virgins must live with a blameless and pure conscience.
## Elders
6:1 The elders too must be tenderhearted and merciful to everyone. They must turn back those who have wandered off and visit all who are sick. They must not neglect a widow or an orphan or a poor man, but always plan ahead for what is good before God and people. They must keep away from all anger, favoritism and unjust judging, and stay far from all love of money. They must not be quick to believe a charge against anyone, or be harsh when they judge, knowing that we all owe a debt of sin.
6:2 So if we ask the Lord to forgive us, we must forgive too. For we stand in front of the eyes of the Lord and God, and every one of us must stand at Christ's judgment seat and give an account for himself.
6:3 So let us serve him with fear and all reverence, as he himself commanded, and as did the apostles who brought us the good news, and the prophets who announced ahead of time the coming of our Lord. Let us be eager for what is good. Let us keep away from things that make people stumble, from false brothers, and from those who carry the name of the Lord as hypocrites and lead empty-headed people astray.
## Against those who deny the flesh
7:1 For "everyone who does not confess Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh is an antichrist." Whoever does not confess the testimony of the cross is from the devil. And whoever bends the Lord's sayings to fit his own desires, and says there is no resurrection and no judgment, that person is the firstborn of Satan.
7:2 So let us leave behind the emptiness of the many and their false teachings, and turn back to the word that was passed on to us at the start. Let us keep a clear head for prayer and keep on with fasting. In our requests let us ask the all-seeing God "not to bring us into testing," just as the Lord said, "The spirit is eager, the flesh weak."
## Imitate Christ's endurance
8:1 So let us hold on without stopping to our hope and to the down payment of our righteousness, Christ Jesus. He "carried our sins up in his own body onto the tree." He "did no sin, nor was trickery found in his mouth." But for our sake, so that we might live in him, he endured everything.
8:2 So let us imitate his endurance, and if we suffer for his name, let us give him glory. This is the model to trace that he laid down for us in himself, and we believed it.
## Endurance seen in the martyrs
9:1 So I urge all of you to obey the word of righteousness and to practice all endurance. You saw it right in front of you, not only in the blessed Ignatius, Zosimus and Rufus, but also in others from among you, and in Paul himself and the other apostles.
9:2 Be sure that none of them "ran for nothing." They ran in faith and righteousness, and they are in the place owed to them, with the Lord, with whom they also suffered. For they did not love "the present age," but the one who died for us and was raised by God for our sake.
## Firm in faith, kind to one another
10:1 Stand in these things, then, and follow the pattern the Lord set. Be firm in the faith and unchanging, lovers of the family of believers, loving one another, joined together in the truth. Wait on one another with the gentleness of the Lord, and look down on no one.
10:2 When you can do good, do not delay, because "alms rescue from death." All of you, submit to one another. Keep your way of life beyond blame among the Gentiles, so that you receive praise for your good works and the Lord is not insulted because of you.
10:3 But woe to the one through whom the name of the Lord is insulted. So teach everyone to live with a clear head, as you yourselves do.
## The case of Valens
11:1 I have been deeply grieved over Valens, who was once made an elder among you, that he understands so little of the place he was given. So I warn you: keep away from greed, and be pure and truthful. Keep away from every evil.
11:2 If someone cannot govern himself in these things, how can he preach it to anyone else? If anyone does not keep away from greed, he will be stained by idolatry and judged as if he were among the Gentiles, who do not know the Lord's judgment. Or do we not know that "the saints will judge the world," as Paul teaches?
11:3 But I have not noticed or heard of anything like this among you, among whom the blessed Paul worked, you who are at the beginning of his letter. For he boasts about you in all the churches, which were the only ones that knew the Lord at that time; we had not yet come to know him.
11:4 So, brothers, I am deeply grieved for him and for his wife. May the Lord grant them true repentance. You too must keep a level head in this matter. Do not treat such people as enemies. Call them back as members who can be hurt and who have wandered, so that you may save the body that all of you make up. For by doing this you build yourselves up.
## Be angry, but do not sin
12:1 For I trust that you have had good training in the sacred writings and that nothing gets past you. That has not been given to me. Only this: as it is said in these Scriptures, "Be angry, but do not sin," and "Do not let the sun set on your anger." Blessed is the one who remembers this, and I believe it is so with you.
## A prayer for the Philippians
12:2 Now may the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the eternal high priest himself, God's Son Jesus Christ, build you up in faith and truth, in all gentleness, free of anger, with patience, long-suffering, endurance and purity. May he give you a share and a portion among his saints, and to us with you, and to everyone under heaven who is going to believe in our Lord and God Jesus Christ and in his Father, who raised him from the dead.
12:3 Pray for all the saints. Pray also for kings, for authorities and rulers, for those who persecute you and hate you, and for the enemies of the cross, so that your fruit can be seen by everyone and you may be complete in him.
## Letters for Syria and the letters of Ignatius
13:1 You wrote to me, both you and Ignatius, asking that if anyone goes to Syria, he should carry the letter from you as well. I will do this if I get a good opportunity, either myself or someone I send as an envoy for you as well.
13:2 As you asked, we have sent you the letters of Ignatius, the ones he sent us, and the others we had here with us. They are attached to this letter, and you will be able to gain a great deal from them. For they contain faith and endurance and every kind of building up that has to do with our Lord. And let us know anything more certain you have learned about Ignatius himself and about those who are with him.
## Crescens and farewell
14:1 I have written this to you by the hand of Crescens. I commended him to you a little while ago, and I commend him again now. For he has lived among us without blame, and I believe he will do the same among you. And you are to consider his sister commended to you as well, when she comes to you. Farewell in the Lord Jesus Christ, in grace, with all who are yours. Amen.
