## Greeting to the church in Magnesia
1:0 Ignatius, also called Theophorus, to her who has been blessed by the grace of God the Father in Christ Jesus our Savior. In him I greet the church that is in Magnesia on the Maeander, and I send it abundant greetings in God the Father and in Jesus Christ.
## Why I write
1:1 I learned how well ordered your love for God is, and I was glad. So I decided to speak to you in the faith of Jesus Christ.
1:2 I have been counted worthy of a name most fit for God. So in these chains I carry around, I sing the praises of the churches. I pray that in them there will be a union of the flesh and spirit of Jesus Christ, who is our life always; a union of faith and love, and nothing ranks above love; and, most of all, a union of Jesus and the Father. If we endure in him all the abuse of the ruler of this age and escape it, we will reach God.
## Damas, Bassus, Apollonius and Zotion
2:1 So I was counted worthy to see you in Damas, your overseer, who is worthy of God, in your worthy elders Bassus and Apollonius, and in my fellow slave the deacon Zotion. May I have the benefit of him, because he is subject to the overseer as to the grace of God, and to the council of elders as to the law of Jesus Christ.
## Your young overseer
3:1 You too should not take advantage of your overseer's youth. Give him all respect, in keeping with the power of God the Father. I know that the holy elders have not taken advantage of how young he looks. As people wise in God, they defer to him, yet not to him, but to the Father of Jesus Christ, the overseer of all.
3:2 So, to honor the one who wanted us, it is right to obey without any pretending. For a person does not just fool this overseer who can be seen. He is cheating the one who cannot be seen. In such a case his reckoning is not with flesh, but with God, who knows the hidden things.
## Christians in name only
4:1 So it is right not only to be called Christians, but to be Christians. Some people do call him overseer, yet they do everything without him. To me such people do not seem to have a good conscience, because their meetings are not valid or held as commanded.
## Two coins
5:1 Everything comes to an end. Two things lie before us together, death and life, and each person is going to go to his own place.
5:2 It is like two coins, one of God and one of the world. Each has its own stamp set on it. The unbelievers bear the stamp of this world, and the believers, in love, bear the stamp of God the Father through Jesus Christ. Unless we freely choose to die through him into his suffering, his life is not in us.
## In the place of God
6:1 In the people I named above I have seen your whole congregation in faith, and I have loved it. So I urge you: be eager to do everything in God's harmony, with the overseer presiding in the place of God, and the elders in the place of the council of the apostles, and the deacons, who are dearest to me, entrusted with the ministry of Jesus Christ. He was with the Father before the ages and appeared at the end.
6:2 So all of you, take on God's own ways and respect one another. Let no one look at his neighbor by the flesh, but always love one another in Jesus Christ. Let there be nothing among you that could divide you. Be united with the overseer and with those who preside, as a type and a lesson of incorruption.
## One temple, one altar
7:1 The Lord was united with the Father and did nothing without him, either by himself or through the apostles. In the same way, you must do nothing without the overseer and the elders. Do not try to make anything seem right for you on your own. When you meet in one place, let there be one prayer, one request, one mind, one hope, in love, in the blameless joy, which is Jesus Christ. Nothing is better than he is.
7:2 All of you, run together as into one temple of God, as to one altar, to one Jesus Christ, who came forth from the one Father, was with the one, and went back to the one.
## Not by Judaism
8:1 Do not be led astray by other opinions, or by old myths that are of no use. For if we are still living by Judaism to this day, we admit that we have not received grace.
8:2 For the most godly prophets lived by Christ Jesus. That is why they were persecuted. They were inspired by his grace, so that those who refused to obey might be fully convinced that there is one God. He made himself known through Jesus Christ his Son, who is his Word, who came forth from silence. In every way the Son pleased the one who sent him.
## Living by the Lord's day
9:1 If, then, those who lived in the old ways came to a new hope, no longer keeping the Sabbath but living by the Lord's [day], on which our life also rose through him and through his death, which some deny, and through this mystery we received faith, and because of it we endure, so that we may be found disciples of Jesus Christ, our only teacher,
9:2 how will we be able to live without him? Even the prophets were his disciples in the Spirit and looked for him as their teacher. And so the one they rightly waited for came and raised them from the dead.
## The new yeast
10:1 So let us not be blind to his kindness. If he were to imitate us in the way we act, we would be finished. So now that we have become his disciples, let us learn to live by Christianity. Whoever is called by any name other than this does not belong to God.
10:2 So put away the bad yeast, which has grown old and sour, and change over to the new yeast, which is Jesus Christ. Be salted in him, so that none of you goes bad, since you will be judged by your smell.
10:3 It is out of place to talk of Jesus Christ and live in the Jewish way. For Christianity did not put its faith in Judaism. Judaism put its faith in Christianity, in which every tongue that believed in God was gathered together.
## Truly and surely
11:1 I say these things, my loved ones, not because I know that any of you are like this. But as one less than you, I want to guard you in advance, so that you do not get caught on the hooks of empty conceit. Instead, be fully convinced of the birth and the suffering and the resurrection, which took place in the time when Pontius Pilate was governor. These things were done truly and surely by Jesus Christ, our hope. May none of you ever be turned away from it.
## You are not puffed up
12:1 May I have the benefit of you in every way, if I am worthy. For even though I am in chains, I am not equal to one of you who are free. I know that you are not puffed up, for you have Jesus Christ within you. And even more, when I praise you, I know that you feel ashamed, as it is written, "A righteous person is his own accuser."
## Stand firm together
13:1 So be eager to stand firm in the decrees of the Lord and of the apostles, so that everything you do may turn out well for you, in flesh and spirit, in faith and love, in the Son and the Father and in the Spirit, in the beginning and at the end. Do it with your overseer, who is most worthy, with your council of elders, that well-woven spiritual crown, and with your godly deacons.
13:2 Be subject to the overseer and to one another, as Jesus Christ was to the Father, and the apostles to Christ and to the Father, so that there may be a union both of the flesh and of the spirit.
## Pray for the church in Syria
14:1 I know that you are full of God, so I have urged you only briefly. Remember me in your prayers, so that I may reach God. Remember the church in Syria too. I am not worthy to be called one of its people. For I need your united prayer and love in God, so that the church in Syria may be counted worthy to be refreshed with dew through your church.
## Greetings from Smyrna
15:1 The Ephesians greet you from Smyrna, the place I am writing to you from. They are here for the glory of God, as you are too, and they have refreshed me in every way, together with Polycarp, overseer of the Smyrnaeans. The other churches also greet you in honor of Jesus Christ. Farewell in God's harmony. Hold on to an unwavering spirit, which is Jesus Christ.
