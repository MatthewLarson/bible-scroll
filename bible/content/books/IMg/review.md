# Hostile review: Ignatius to the Magnesians (IMg)

## Verdict

The translation is faithful to Lake's Greek almost everywhere, and `node tools/build.js --check IMg` gives 0 errors and 0 warnings. Quotations from Lightfoot, Lake, Guy and the Catechism checked out against the opened copies (Lightfoot's *Apostolic Fathers* part 2 vol. 2 and *Christian Ministry*, Lake's OCR, Guy's OCR, vatican.va saved pages). The problems are these. The Plain English Translation of 9:1 drops the "If" that governs the whole sentence. One variant note makes a false claim about all witnesses. Guy's Adventist affiliation, and his journal's, appear in content with no opened source behind them. Several Lightfoot apparatus entries that bear on the Judaism and Sabbath readings are missing. Several date and "readers" claims are not sourced. The Sabbath and Lord's-day treatment is fair in tone, but its church voices are thin and partly not from the churches' own documents.

## Findings

### Major

1. **plain.txt 9:1, the "If" is gone.** The Greek is one conditional sentence: "Εἰ οὖν οἱ ἐν παλαιοῖς πράγμασιν ἀναστραφέντες εἰς καινότητα ἐλπίδος ἦλθον, μηκέτι σαββατίζοντες, ἀλλὰ κατὰ κυριακὴν ζῶντες, ... πῶς ἡμεῖς δυνησόμεθα ζῆσαι χωρὶς αὐτοῦ;" (the apodosis is the question in 9:2). The Plain English Translation makes 9:1 two flat assertions ("Those who lived in the old ways came to a new hope. They no longer keep the Sabbath...") and moves an "If that is so" into 9:2, where the Greek has none. This is the most quoted verse in the letter, and the change makes it a stronger proof text than Ignatius's argument. The writer's own 9:1 deep note says "The sentence is the 'if' half of the question". It also supplies "day" with κυριακήν with nothing marking the supplied word, so the text settles the day/life question that the variant leaves open. Fix: "If, then, those who lived in the old ways came to a new hope, no longer keeping the Sabbath but living by the Lord's (day) ..." and drop the added "If that is so" from 9:2. A bracket or a gloss on "day" would help.

2. **variants.json 6:1, false claim.** The text says "Lake: 'Cf. Trall. iii', where all the witnesses read 'the overseer is a type of the Father'". Lightfoot's apparatus at Trallians 3:1 (p2v2.txt around line 16100 and 16245) shows G and L reading υἱόν ("son"), which he calls corrupt and emends to τύπον. S and g have the "type" reading, A and Antioch paraphrase it, and L has "existentem filium patris". Fix: "where Lightfoot emends the corrupt υἱόν of G and L to τύπον, supported by S and g". This claim carries weight for the bishops dispute, so correct it exactly.

3. **Rule-2 violation, Guy's affiliation (notes.json 9:1 "Those who lived in the old ways" `p` and the views note at 9:1; book.json sections and `reading`).** Content says "the Adventist Fritz Guy", "the Adventist scholar Fritz Guy", "in the journal of the Seventh-day Adventist seminary (1964)" and "from a Seventh-day Adventist journal". Guy's OCR (.scratch/IMg/web/guy.txt) says only "La Sierra College". The PDF is a scan with no "Adventist" or "Andrews" text. The only "Adventist" in it is Guy's footnotes on other writers (Straw; "Seventh-day Adventist expositors"). The writer's sources-checked.md lists no source for either label. I verified the facts by web search today: the journal is published by the Seventh-day Adventist Theological Seminary at Andrews University, and Guy was an Adventist theologian (La Sierra University, Andrews Seminary). A search result did not itself link Guy to that journal, but his 1964 article is in the AUSS volume. The labels are true but were not in anything the writer opened. Fix: either cite an opened source in `guy-lords-day-1964` (the AUSS masthead or an obituary) or cut the labels. Guy's own footnote shows he is not simply the Adventist voice: it names Straw's "Seventh-day Adventist dissent" and describes SDA expositors in the third person. Calling him "the Adventist" positions him as that church's view.

4. **Missing variants that bear on the Judaism and Sabbath readings (variants.json).** Lightfoot's apparatus (opened) shows these, none entered:
   - 8:1 κατὰ Ἰουδαϊσμόν G, L "judaismum", but A "judicam legem" and g νόμον ἰουδαϊκόν ("Jewish law"). Roberts-Donaldson's shorter column, the site's own check text, reads "Jewish law", so a reader will see the difference. This is central to what "Judaism" means in 8:1.
   - 9:1 πράγμασιν GLA ("practices") against γράμμασιν g ("writings"). The long recension reads "old writings", which changes who the "old ways" people are.
   - 7:1 "ὃ ἐστιν Ἰησοῦς Χριστός" (L "quod", joy) against G εἷς (Roberts-Donaldson: "There is one Jesus Christ"), and "ἡνωμένος ὤν" omitted by S, A and g.
   - 6:1 the Syriac "council of angels" and Armenian "like a king of angels" for συνέδριον τῶν ἀποστόλων.
   Fix: add entries for 8:1 and 9:1 (γράμμασιν), and for 7:1 and 6:1 (council).

### Minor

5. **15:1 οἲ.** texts/orig reads οἲ (ι with psili and varia), an impossible breathing. Both Lake scans read "of" (OCR), Lightfoot prints οἱ. It needs the rough breathing, so correct it in tools/lake-fixes.json with the scan evidence. Separately, the Plain English Translation makes the Ephesians the subject of "refreshed me... together with Polycarp". That follows Lake's English. But Lightfoot's note on ὥσπερ καὶ ὑμεῖς says the Magnesians were present through their delegates, he prefers "ἅμα Πολυκάρπῳ" with ἀσπάζονται, and g reads ἀνεπαύσατε ("you refreshed me"). Add a note or variant showing the choice. Also add G's διάκριτον against ἀδιάκριτον (Lightfoot's apparatus; Lake prints ἀδιάκριτον with no note).

6. **Date asserted without a source.** The sabbath topic says "About AD 110" and the judaizers topic says "Fifty years after Galatians". The ch 9 settings say "Christians in Asia about 110". book.json itself says the letters are undated, and Lake gives only Eusebius's 108 and "Trajan (98-117)". Fix: use "in the reign of Trajan (Lake)" or "about 108 on Eusebius's Chronicle", and drop the "fifty years" arithmetic.

7. **Speculation stated as fact in the audience and readers.** ch 9 settings: "some of them Jewish by birth, others Gentiles drawn to Jewish practice". book.json audience: "Some in the region called him overseer but met without him (4:1)", and ch 4 H says "in Asia". Verse 4:1 says only "some". ch 14 readers: "a church it had never seen" is not in the text. ch 5 H: "a province where Christians could be put to death" is unsourced. Fix: remove, or source and mark.

8. **Views credited to the unnamed "many".** ch 8 `readings`: "This is how many later readers took him, and how the lines were used against Jews". The topic anti-judaism says "Read later... such lines were taken as a verdict on the Jewish people". Name an opened source or say "the site's topic page sets out...". ch 6 "Read as a lasting order... belong to the church as such" is the writer's wording, attributed to no one. ch 6 "under attack" in "Read as a plea for unity" is not in Lightfoot.

9. **Church voices at 6:1 and 9:1 (lens C of the brief).** The Orthodox row at 6:1 is *The A to Z of the Orthodox Church*, a reference work whose entry on Ignatius does not discuss this verse. It quotes accurately ("Ignatius stresses the importance of the bishop"), but it is not an Orthodox church document. The brief and the Apostolic Fathers lessons require a church's own document. The Sabbath views note has Catholic (CCC 2175, which I verified, with note 108 citing Ad Magn. 9,1), Lightfoot (an individual Anglican) and Guy (cautious, not a church statement). It has no Sunday-keeping Protestant confession, no Adventist church statement, no Seventh Day Baptist and no Messianic Jewish voice, though lens C names them. The card pointers cover this partly, but the note, as written, gives the Adventist reading only through one scholar who ends in agnosticism. No Jewish reader's view of "no longer keeping the Sabbath" appears. Fix: add one sourced line each from opened church documents, or say in the note that the cards carry the rest.

10. **Smaller factual and wording points.**
    - Lightfoot's "fictitious personages" (2:1 note; book.json reliability) is from *The Christian Ministry* and concerns a hypothetical forger ("Under any circumstances..."). It is quoted with that context cut.
    - variants 3:2 says ἡμᾶς and ὑμᾶς "were often confused" with no source.
    - variants 9:1 says Lake leaves out ζωήν "with the Latin and Armenian". Lake gives no note there, so the pairing is the writer's inference from Lightfoot.
    - variants 9:1 οἵτινες is glossed "which certain people deny", which does not parse (οἵτινες is "who").
    - The spec in book.json says "neither Lake nor Lightfoot links the two problems", and the ch 6 spec says "neither he nor Lake draws this picture". Both are negative claims not shown by a full read.
    - The ch 15 setting says "a few hours' walk (Lightfoot)". Lightfoot gives "four hours' walk" and 15 Roman miles, so the paraphrase is fine, but the attribution should match.
    - 1:2 "nothing ranks above them": ἧς is singular and refers to love.
    - 6:1 "Let the overseer preside" turns a genitive absolute (προκαθημένου) into a command.
    - 3:1 "as the power of God the Father deserves" adds a verb the Greek lacks (κατὰ δύναμιν).

## Checked and sound

- Lake, Lightfoot and Guy quotations at 8:2, 9:1, 9:2, 10:1-3 and 12:1, and the Guy "should probably be retained" and "ambiguous" conclusions.
- Lightfoot's "extravagant exaltation" and "needs and distractions of the age" (*Christian Ministry* pp. 87-88).
- CCC 1549 and 2175 with notes 27 and 108.
- Eusebius *Church History* 3.25 (First1KGreek copy) has no Ignatius, so book.json is right.
- Tradition and term consistency with IEp, IRo and ISm ("other opinions", "valid", "council of elders").
- Plain English Translation quotations of Romans 11:28-29 and Pol 5:3 match the site.
- No em-dashes in plain.txt.

## Fixes applied
1. Accepted (lead decision a). 9:1 now keeps the Greek's condition: "If, then, those who lived in the old ways came to a new hope, no longer keeping the Sabbath but living by the Lord's [day], ..." and runs on into 9:2 "how will we be able to live without him?" ("If that is so" removed). "day" is in square brackets, the site's mark for a supplied word (as Diognetus 7:7); the 9:1 word note, the Sabbath views note and the variant explain the bracket and the Greek manuscript's "life". Quotes in book.json (subtitle, lead, sections), chapters.json ch 9 and topics.json (sabbath, ignatius) updated. Anchors and gloss moved to the new wording.
2. Accepted (lead decision d). Variant 6:1 now says Lake prints "a type of the Father" at Trallians 3:1 and that in Lightfoot's apparatus (p2v2 lines 16096-16108, opened) G and L read υἱόν, which he calls corrupt and replaces with τύπον from S and g, loosely paraphrased in A; this agrees with the υἱόν variant ITr's fixer is adding.
3. Accepted (lead decision b). The labels "Adventist", "the Adventist scholar" and "from a Seventh-day Adventist journal" are removed everywhere; Guy is named only by his article and journal, and in the reading list as "a teacher at La Sierra College", which is what his article's heading says (guy.txt, opened).
4. Accepted (lead decision d). Seven variants added from Lightfoot's apparatus, opened this session: 6:1 "council of the apostles" (S angelorum consilii, A tanquam angeli regis, with Lightfoot's explanation); 7:1 ἡνωμένος ὤν (GL; om. S A [g] Dam-Rup); 7:1 ὅ / εἷς / Lightfoot's conjecture ὅς; 8:1 Ἰουδαϊσμόν L / νόμον Ἰουδαϊσμόν G / judaicam legem A, νόμον Ἰουδαϊκόν [g] (the reviewer's "G, L" corrected: G adds νόμον, which Guy's "according to Judaizing law" confirms); 9:1 πράγμασιν GLA / γράμμασιν g with Lightfoot's reasons; and, from finding 5, 15:1 ἀνέπαυσαν / ἀνεπαύσατε and ἀδιάκριτον / διάκριτον G.
5. Partly accepted (lead decision e). The οἲ breathing in texts/ is left to the lead. 15:1 is translated by sense (οἱ, "who", as Lightfoot prints it); the 15:1 Smyrna note, the new ἀνεπαύσατε variant, the ch 15 summary and the polycarp topic give Lightfoot's reading of "as you are too" (the Magnesians present through their delegates) and of "together with Polycarp" with "greet you". The διάκριτον variant was added (see 4).
6. Accepted (lead decision f). "About AD 110" in the sabbath topic is now "in the reign of Trajan (98-117, as Lake dates it)", the sabbath "common" line and lens H say "in Ignatius's day", ch 9 readers no longer give a year, and "Fifty years after Galatians" is cut.
7. Accepted. ch 9 readers rewritten (no claim about Jews by birth or Gentiles); audience "in the region" and ch 4 H "in Asia" replaced with what 4:1 says; reliability section 1 likewise; ch 14 "a church it had never seen" replaced; ch 5 H "a province where Christians could be put to death" cut (now "a prisoner in chains (1:2)").
8. Accepted (lead decision c). ch 8 reading now names Lightfoot ("a larger and better dispensation than Judaism", p2v2 line 14113, opened) and points to the anti-judaism topic for the later history; the same sentence in the anti-judaism contribution and book.json section 3 reworded to what that topic page says. ch 6 "Read as a lasting order... belong to the church as such" is now "Read as the church's order", resting on the Catechism quote; "under attack" cut.
9. Partly accepted (lead decision c). The 6:1 views note now says the A to Z is a dictionary by Prokurat, Golitzin and Peterson, not a church statement, quotes its sentence in full (atoz.htm, opened) and says it does not discuss the verse. The 9:1 Sabbath views note labels each voice (the Catholic Church in its Catechism; Lightfoot and Guy as scholars) and says the churches' own teaching (Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Reformed, Baptist, Adventist) is on the Revelation 1:10 card, with Colossians 2 and Exodus 20. Declined: no Seventh Day Baptist, Messianic Jewish or Jewish voice added, because no document of theirs on this verse was opened this session.
10. Accepted in each part. "fictitious personages" now quoted with its context and page (The Christian Ministry p. 50, lm1901.txt, opened) in the 2:1 note and reliability; variant 3:2 "often confused" cut; variant 9:1 summary now attributes the Latin and Armenian to Lightfoot's apparatus (dominicam L; dominicam diem sanctam et primam [A]); οἵτινες reading glossed "his death, who deny (it)"; both negative "neither Lake nor Lightfoot" claims cut; ch 15 now "within four hours' walk (Lightfoot)" (p2v2 line 10443); 1:2 now "nothing ranks above love"; 6:1 now "with the overseer presiding in the place of God" (genitive absolute), with the views note anchor and elders topic quote updated; 3:1 now "in keeping with the power of God the Father".
