## Discipline your son
30:1 About children. / A father who loves his son will keep the whip on him, / so that at the end of his days he can be glad.
30:2 The one who disciplines his son will gain from him / and will brag about him to the people he knows.
30:3 The one who teaches his son will make his enemy jealous, / and in front of his friends he will be glad of him.
30:4 His father dies, and it is as if he had not died, / since he has left one like himself to follow him.
30:5 While he lived, he watched his son and was glad, / and when he died, he did not grieve.
30:6 Against his enemies he has left an avenger, / and for his friends, someone who pays back their kindness.
30:7 Over his sons' lives he will be binding up wounds, / and at every cry his insides will churn.
30:8 A horse that is never tamed ends up hard to handle, / and a son left loose ends up reckless.
30:9 Coddle a child and he will make you gasp; / be his playmate and he will hurt you.
30:10 Do not laugh along with him, or you will share his pain, / and in the end your own teeth will be set on edge.
30:11 Do not give him free rein in his youth.
30:12 Bruise his ribs while he is still small, / or he may grow hard and refuse to obey you.
30:13 Discipline your son and work hard at him, / so that he does not stumble and disgrace you.
## Health is better than wealth
30:14 Better a poor man, healthy and strong of build, / than a rich man scourged in his own body.
30:15 Good health and a body in good shape are worth more than all gold, / a sturdy body more than endless wealth.
30:16 About food. / No riches beat a body in good health, / and no delight tops the joy of the heart.
30:17 Death is better than a bitter life, / or than a sickness that never leaves.
30:18 Good food heaped before a mouth that is shut / is like a spread of food laid out on a grave.
30:19 What good is an offering to an idol? / It cannot eat, and it cannot smell. / So it is with the one whom the Lord is hounding.
30:20 He looks with his eyes and groans, / like a eunuch who holds a young woman in his arms and groans.
## Do not give yourself to grief
30:21 Do not hand yourself over to grief, / or wear yourself down with your own thoughts.
30:22 A glad heart is a person's life, / and a man's joy is a long life.
30:23 Love yourself and comfort your heart, / and send grief far away from you, / for grief has killed many, / and there is no good in it.
30:24 Jealousy and anger shorten a person's days, / and worry brings on old age before its time. / A bright and good heart / will give care to its food and its meals.
## The last of the gleaners
30:25 Like a gleaner behind the grape pickers, / I came out ahead by the blessing of the Lord, / and filled the winepress like one who picks the grapes.
30:26 See that my toil was not for me only, / but for everyone who is looking for instruction.
30:27 Hear me, you great men of the people; / you leaders of the assembly, give ear.
## Keep your property while you live
30:28 Do not give your son or wife, your brother or friend, / power over you while you live. / Do not give your property to someone else, / or you may regret it and have to plead for it.
30:29 As long as you live and have breath in you, / do not hand your place to anyone.
30:30 It is better for your children to ask things of you / than for you to look to your sons' hands.
30:31 In everything you do, keep the upper hand. / Do not let a blot fall on your glory.
30:32 On the day your life's days come to an end, / when it is time to die, divide your estate.
## Slaves in the household
30:33 Fodder, a stick and loads are for a donkey; / bread, discipline and work are for a slave.
30:34 Make your slave work and you will have rest; / let his hands go slack and he will be after his freedom.
30:35 A yoke and a strap will bend the neck, / and for a slave who does wrong, the rack and torture.
30:36 Keep him busy so he does not loaf,
30:37 for idleness has been the teacher of much wrong.
30:38 Assign him the work that fits him, / and if he will not obey, weigh down his shackles. / But do not go too far with anyone, / and do nothing without fair judgment.
30:39 If you have a slave, let him be like yourself, / because you got him at the cost of blood. / If you have a slave, treat him like yourself, / because you need him as you need your own life.
30:40 If you mistreat him and he gets up and runs away, / which road will you take to look for him?
## Dreams are empty
31:1 A man without sense has empty and false hopes, / and dreams give fools wings.
31:2 Like someone grabbing at a shadow or chasing the wind / is the one who pays attention to dreams.
31:3 What you see in dreams is one thing set against another: / the likeness of a face before a face.
31:4 What can be made clean by something unclean? / What can be true that comes from a lie?
31:5 Divination, omens and dreams are all empty, / and the heart imagines things, like a woman in labor.
31:6 Unless they are sent from the Most High as a visit from him, / do not set your heart on them.
31:7 Dreams have led many astray, / and people who pinned their hopes on them fell.
31:8 Without any lie the law will be fulfilled, / and wisdom reaches its goal in a trustworthy mouth.
## What travel teaches
31:9 A well-taught man knows many things, / and a man of wide experience will speak with understanding.
31:10 One who has never been tested knows little,
31:11 but one who has wandered far will grow rich in shrewdness.
31:12 I have seen much in my wanderings, / and my understanding goes beyond my words.
31:13 Often I came near to death, / and I came through because of these things.
## The hope of those who fear the Lord
31:14 The spirit of those who fear the Lord will live,
31:15 for they hope in the one who saves them.
31:16 Whoever fears the Lord will not be afraid / or lose heart, for the Lord himself is his hope.
31:17 Blessed is the soul of the one who fears the Lord.
31:18 On whom does he rely? And who holds him up?
31:19 The Lord keeps his eyes on those who love him. / He is their powerful protection and their firm prop, / cover from the burning heat, cover at midday, / a watchman to keep them from tripping, / help to keep them from a fall.
31:20 He raises the soul high and gives light to the eyes; / he grants healing, life and blessing.
## Gifts God will not accept
31:21 A sacrifice from ill-gotten goods is an offering made in mockery,
31:22 and the mockeries of lawless men do not win his favor.
31:23 The Most High takes no pleasure in the offerings of the ungodly, / and a great number of sacrifices does not make atonement for sins.
31:24 Killing a son in front of his father: / that is what it is to bring a sacrifice / out of what belongs to the poor.
31:25 Bread for those in need is the poor man's life; / whoever robs them of it is a man of blood.
31:26 Whoever takes away his neighbor's means of living murders him,
31:27 and whoever holds back a hired man's pay sheds blood.
31:28 One man building, another pulling down: / what has either gained beyond weariness?
31:29 One man praying, another cursing: / which voice will the Master hear?
31:30 A man bathes after contact with a dead body / and then handles it again: what good was his bath?
31:31 So it is with someone who fasts for his sins / and then goes off and does the same things again. / Who will hear his prayer? / And what use was it to humble himself?
## The offering of keeping the law
32:1 Whoever keeps the law brings offerings in plenty;
32:2 whoever pays attention to the commandments offers a peace offering.
32:3 Whoever repays a kindness offers fine flour,
32:4 and whoever gives alms offers a sacrifice of praise.
32:5 The Lord is pleased when someone turns away from evil, / and turning away from wrongdoing makes atonement.
32:6 Do not appear before the Lord with empty hands,
32:7 for all these things are done because of the commandment.
32:8 The offering of a righteous person makes the altar rich with fat, / and its fragrance is before the Most High.
32:9 The sacrifice of a righteous man is accepted, / and its token portion will not be forgotten.
32:10 Give glory to the Lord with a good eye, / and do not cut short the firstfruits your hands have gathered.
32:11 With every gift, let your face be glad, / and set apart your tithe with joy.
32:12 Give to the Most High in keeping with what he gives, / with a good eye, as far as your hand can reach.
32:13 For the Lord pays back; / seven times over he will pay you back.
## The Lord hears the widow
32:14 Do not try to bribe him; he will not take it.
32:15 Do not lean on an unjust sacrifice, / for the Lord is judge, / and with him no one's face wins special honor.
32:16 He will not favor anyone over a poor man, / and he hears the plea of the wronged.
32:17 He will never ignore the cry of an orphan, / nor a widow as she pours out her words.
32:18 Do the widow's tears not roll down her cheek,
32:19 and is her outcry not against the man who made them fall?
32:20 Whoever serves with good will is welcomed, / and his plea will reach as high as the clouds.
32:21 The prayer of the humble has gone through the clouds, / and it will not be comforted until it comes near. / It will not leave until the Most High looks into it,
32:22 and judges rightly and carries out justice. / And the Lord will not delay, / nor will he be patient with them, / until he has broken the backs of the merciless,
32:23 and pays the nations back with vengeance, / until he sweeps away the mass of the arrogant / and shatters the scepters of the unjust,
32:24 until he pays each person back for what he has done, / and people's deeds by what they intended,
32:25 until he decides his people's case / and gladdens them with his mercy.
32:26 Mercy is beautiful in the time of his trouble, / like rain clouds in a season of drought.
## A prayer for Israel
33:1 Have mercy on us, Master, God of all,
33:2 and look upon us, and cast the fear of you on all the nations.
33:3 Raise your hand over the foreign nations, / and let them see your power.
33:4 As you showed yourself holy through us in front of them, / so show yourself great through them in front of us.
33:5 Let them know you, just as we have known, / that there is no God but you, Lord.
33:6 Renew your signs, and work wonders of a new kind.
33:7 Give glory to your hand and your right arm.
33:8 Rouse your fury and pour out your anger.
33:9 Take away the opponent and crush the enemy.
33:10 Hurry the time and remember your oath, / and let people tell of your mighty deeds.
33:11 Let the one who escapes be eaten up by fiery anger, / and let those who wrong your people find ruin.
33:12 Smash the heads of the enemy's rulers, / who say, "There is no one but us."
33:13 Gather all the tribes of Jacob,
## Sleepless over wealth
34:1 Staying up nights over riches melts a man's flesh, / and the worry of it chases sleep away.
34:2 Anxious waking takes slumber away, / and a serious illness shakes sleep off.
34:3 A rich man wears himself out piling up money, / and at rest he stuffs himself with his delicacies.
34:4 A poor man wears himself out as his living shrinks, / and at rest he goes in want.
34:5 Whoever loves gold will not be found in the right, / and whoever chases after ruin will have his fill of it.
34:6 Many have been handed over to a fall because of gold, / and their ruin stood right in front of them.
34:7 It is a log that trips up those who sacrifice to it, / and every fool is snared by it.
34:8 Blessed the rich man found with no blemish, / who did not run after gold.
34:9 Who is he? Let us call him blessed! / He has done marvels among his people.
34:10 Who has been put to the test by gold and proved complete? / Then let it be his boast. / Who could have stepped over the line and did not, / could have done wrong and did not?
34:11 His good fortune will stand firm, / and the assembly will tell of his alms.
## Manners at a great table
34:12 Are you seated at a great table? / Do not open your throat wide over it, / and do not say, "What a lot there is on it!"
34:13 Remember that an evil eye is a wicked thing. / What has been created more evil than the eye? / That is why it sheds tears at every turn.
34:14 Do not reach for whatever he is looking at, / and do not jostle him in the bowl.
34:15 Know what your neighbor wants by what you want yourself, / and give thought to every matter.
34:16 Eat the food put in front of you as a human being should, / and do not chomp on it, or you will be disliked.
34:17 Stop eating before the others, out of good training, / and do not gorge, or you may cause offense.
34:18 If you are sitting among many people, / do not reach out your hand ahead of them.
34:19 How little is enough for a well-trained person! / He does not lie gasping on his bed.
34:20 Healthy sleep comes with a moderate belly. / He gets up early, feeling like himself. / The misery of sleeplessness, of nausea and of cramps / is with the man who cannot get enough.
34:21 If you have been pressed into eating too much, / get up while the meal is going on and step away, / and you will find relief.
34:22 Listen to me, my child, and do not look down on me, / and in the end you will find my words hold good. / In all your work be quick and skillful, / and no sickness will come your way.
34:23 Lips will bless the one who is generous with his bread, / and the witness to his goodness can be trusted.
34:24 The town will grumble about one who is mean with his bread, / and the witness to his meanness is accurate.
## Wine
34:25 Do not play the hero over wine, / for wine has brought down many.
34:26 As the furnace tests a steel edge in the tempering, / so wine puts hearts to the test in the brawls of the proud.
34:27 Wine is as good as life to people / if you drink it in its proper measure. / What life is there for someone who goes without wine? / It was created for human gladness.
34:28 Wine drunk at the right time and in the right amount / brings joy to the heart and cheer to the soul.
34:29 Too much wine drunk brings a bitter soul, / with provoking and clashing.
34:30 Drunkenness swells a fool's rage until he stumbles; / it drains strength and brings on wounds.
34:31 At a wine party do not scold your neighbor / or look down on him while he is enjoying himself. / Do not say an insulting word to him, / and do not press him by demanding what he owes you.
## If you lead the banquet
35:1 Have they made you the leader of the feast? Do not puff yourself up. / Among them, be like any one of them. / Look after them, and only then take your seat.
35:2 When you have done all your duties, recline at the table, / so that you may enjoy yourself because of them / and win a wreath for keeping good order.
35:3 Speak up, elder, since it suits you, / but with exact knowledge, / and do not get in the way of the music.
35:4 When there is a performance, do not pour out chatter; / do not play the wise man out of season.
35:5 Like a seal of red gemstone on a gold ornament / is a band of musicians at a wine party.
35:6 Like an emerald seal in a golden setting / is the song of musicians over sweet wine.
35:7 Speak, young man, if you have to, / and barely then, if you are asked twice.
35:8 Sum up what you say: much in a few words. / Act like one who knows and yet stays silent.
35:9 Among great men do not put yourself on their level, / and while someone else is talking, do not chatter on.
35:10 Lightning rushes on before thunder, / and favor runs out ahead of a modest man.
35:11 Get up when it is time, and do not be the last to leave. / Hurry home, and do not dawdle.
35:12 Have your fun there and do what you have in mind, / but do not sin by a proud word.
35:13 And for all this, bless the one who made you, / who fills you to the brim with his good things.
## Those who seek the law
35:14 Whoever fears the Lord will accept discipline, / and those up at dawn will win his favor.
35:15 The seeker of the law will have his fill of it, / but the hypocrite finds it a snare.
35:16 Those who fear the Lord will find what is just / and set righteous deeds burning like a lamp.
35:17 A sinful man dodges correction / and will find a ruling that suits what he wants.
35:18 A man of good counsel will not ignore a thought, / but the stranger and the proud man do not shrink back in fear, / even after he has acted, by himself, without counsel.
35:19 Do nothing without counsel, / and after you act, have no regrets.
35:20 Do not walk on a road full of obstacles, / and do not stub your foot on stony ground.
35:21 Do not trust a road just because it has no obstacles,
35:22 and watch out for your own children.
35:23 In everything you do, trust yourself, / for this too is keeping the commandments.
35:24 Whoever trusts the law pays attention to the commandments, / and whoever is sure of the Lord will not end up the poorer.
## The law will not fail you
36:1 No evil will meet the one who fears the Lord; / when he is tested, he will be pulled out of it again.
36:2 A wise man will not hate the law, / but the man who only plays at it / is tossed like a ship in a squall.
36:3 A man of understanding will put his trust in the law, / and the law is as reliable to him as an inquiry made by the righteous.
36:4 Get your words ready, and then you will be heard; / bind your instruction together, and then answer.
36:5 The heart of a fool is a cart wheel, / and his reasoning goes around like an axle spinning.
36:6 A stallion kept for breeding is like a friend who mocks: / he neighs under everyone who sits on him.
## Why one day is above another
36:7 Why is one day above another, / when every day's light, all year long, comes from the sun?
36:8 By the Lord's knowledge they were set apart, / and he made the seasons and feasts different.
36:9 Some of them he raised high and made holy, / and some he made just part of the count of days.
36:10 So too, every person is from the soil, / and Adam was made out of earth.
36:11 With his vast knowledge the Lord marked them off from one another / and gave them different ways.
36:12 Some he blessed and lifted up; / some he set apart as holy and drew near to himself. / Some he cursed and humbled / and threw down from where they stood.
36:13 As clay is in the potter's hand, / and all its ways go as he pleases, / so are human beings in the hand of the one who made them, / to repay them as he judges.
36:14 Good stands across from evil, / and life across from death; / so the sinner stands across from the godly.
36:15 Look in the same way at all the works of the Most High: / they come two by two, one across from the other.
## The prayer for Israel resumes
36:16 I too kept watch, the last of all, / and you will give them their inheritance as at the beginning.
36:17 Lord, have mercy on a people that bears your name, / Israel, whom you likened to a firstborn.
36:18 Show compassion to the city of your holy place, / Jerusalem, the city of your rest.
36:19 Fill Zion with the praise of your mighty deeds, / and fill your people with your glory.
36:20 Give testimony for the ones you made at the beginning, / and raise up the prophecies that bear your name.
36:21 Pay wages to those who wait for you, / and let your prophets be proved trustworthy.
36:22 Hear, Lord, the prayer of those who plead with you, / as Aaron blessed your people. / Then everyone on earth will know / that you are the Lord, God of all the ages.
## Tasting and choosing
36:23 The belly eats every kind of food, / but some food is finer than other food.
36:24 The throat tastes the meat of game; / so a discerning heart tastes lies.
36:25 A crooked heart brings sorrow, / but a man who has seen much will repay him.
## Choosing a wife
36:26 A woman will accept any male, / but one daughter is better than another.
36:27 A woman's beauty makes a face light up, / and it rises above everything a man desires.
36:28 If there is mercy and gentleness on her tongue, / her husband is not like other men.
36:29 The man who gets a wife has made the first of his gains, / a helper who is his match and a pillar to lean on.
36:30 Where there is no fence, property gets plundered, / and where there is no wife, a man wanders about groaning.
36:31 Who will trust a nimble bandit / who darts from town to town? / So too with a man who has no nest of his own / and sleeps wherever evening catches him.
