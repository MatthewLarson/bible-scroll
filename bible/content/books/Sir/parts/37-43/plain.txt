## False friends and true
37:1 Every friend says, "I am his friend too." / But a friend can be a friend in name and nothing more.
37:2 Is it not a grief that comes close to death / to see a companion and friend become an enemy?
37:3 O evil scheming, where did you roll in from, / to cover the dry land with treachery?
37:4 A companion enjoys himself when his friend is glad, / but in a time of trouble he stands against him.
37:5 A companion shares his friend's labor for the sake of his stomach, / and when war comes he picks up a shield.
37:6 Do not let a friend slip from your soul, / and do not forget him when you have money.
## Choosing an adviser
37:7 Every adviser praises his own advice, / but there are those whose advice serves only themselves.
37:8 Watch yourself around an adviser. / Find out first what he needs, / for he will make his plans with himself in mind. / Perhaps he is casting lots over you
37:9 and tells you, "You are on a good road," / then keeps his distance to watch how things turn out for you.
37:10 Do not ask advice of someone who eyes you with suspicion, / and keep your plans hidden from those who envy you.
37:11 Do not ask a woman what to do about her rival, / or a coward about going to war, / a trader about a swap, / a buyer about a sale, / a grudging man about showing thanks, / a heartless man about kindness, / a lazy man about any job, / a drifting hired hand about finishing the work, / or an idle slave about a heavy workload. / Do not count on any of these for advice.
37:12 Instead, stay close to a godly man / whom you know keeps the commandments, / whose soul is like your own soul, / and who, if you stumble, will share your pain.
37:13 And hold firm to what your own heart advises, / for nothing is more trustworthy to you than that.
37:14 A man's soul sometimes tells him more / than seven watchmen posted on a high lookout.
37:15 And beyond all this, ask the Most High / to make your path straight in truth.
## Words and true wisdom
37:16 Every work begins with a word, / and every deed with a plan.
37:17 The heart leaves a track when it changes:
37:18 four kinds of things spring up, / good and evil, life and death, / and the tongue is their master all the time.
37:19 One man is shrewd and teaches many, / yet he is no use to himself.
37:20 Another is a clever talker and is hated; / he will end up without anything to eat,
37:21 for the Lord has not given him favor, / since he has been stripped of all wisdom.
37:22 One man is wise for his own sake, / and the fruits of his understanding can be trusted in his mouth.
37:23 A wise man instructs his own people, / and the fruits of his understanding can be trusted.
37:24 A wise man will be full of blessing, / and all who see him will say how happy he is.
37:25 A man's life lasts a counted number of days, / but the days of Israel cannot be counted.
37:26 The wise man will inherit trust among his people, / and his name will live forever.
## Do not overeat
37:27 My child, test your soul while you live. / See what is bad for it, and do not give it that.
37:28 For not all things suit all people, / and not every soul likes everything.
37:29 Do not crave every luxury without limit, / and do not throw yourself at the food.
37:30 Too much food brings pain, / and greed for food ends in a sick stomach.
37:31 Many have died from overeating, / but the one who is careful will add to his life.
## Honor the doctor
38:1 Honor the doctor with the honors due him, since you will need him, / for it was the Lord who created him.
38:2 Healing is from the Most High, / and he will receive a gift from the king.
38:3 A doctor's knowledge lets him hold his head high, / and the great admire him.
38:4 Out of the earth the Lord created remedies, / and a sensible man will not turn up his nose at them.
38:5 Did not a piece of wood once turn water sweet, / to make its power known?
38:6 He gave human beings knowledge, / so that his wonders would bring him glory.
38:7 With these he cures and removes a person's pain;
38:8 the ointment-maker blends his mixtures from them. / So his works never come to an end, / and from him comes peace over the face of the earth.
38:9 My child, when you are sick, do not neglect it, / but pray to the Lord: he is the one who will heal you.
38:10 Give up your wrongdoing and keep your hands straight, / and wash your heart clean of every sin.
38:11 Offer a soothing smell and a token portion of fine flour, / and make your offering rich, as though you were no more.
38:12 Then give the doctor his place, for it was the Lord who created him. / Keep him near you, because he is needed.
38:13 There are times when success lies in their hands,
38:14 for they also call on the Lord / to bless their work of bringing relief / and healing that keeps a patient alive.
38:15 Whoever sins before the one who made him, / may he fall into a doctor's hands!
## Mourning the dead
38:16 My child, shed tears over one who has died, / and begin the lament like someone who is suffering terribly. / Wrap his body in the way that is his due, / and do not fail to see him buried.
38:17 Weep bitterly and beat your breast with passion. / Mourn as he deserves, for a day or two, / so that no one can speak against you. / Then be comforted, because of what grief does:
38:18 from grief comes death, / and grief of heart bends a person's strength.
38:19 When the dead is carried away, grief also should pass by, / but a poor man's life weighs on the heart.
38:20 Do not hand your heart over to grief. / Turn it away, and remember the end.
38:21 Keep it in mind: there is no way back. / You will not help him, and you will hurt yourself.
38:22 Remember that his sentence will be yours too: / "Mine yesterday, and yours today."
38:23 Now that the dead man rests, let thoughts of him rest as well, / and take comfort over him once his spirit has gone out.
## Farmers and craftsmen
38:24 A scribe's wisdom comes with the chance of leisure; / whoever has less to do will become wise.
38:25 What wisdom can a plowman gain, / one whose pride is the shaft of his goad, / who drives the oxen and is taken up with their work, / and who talks of nothing but young bulls?
38:26 He gives his heart to cutting furrows, / and he lies awake over feed for the heifers.
38:27 The same is true of every craftsman and master builder, / who keeps at it night and day: / those who cut the engravings on seals, / patiently changing the design. / He gives his heart to making the copy true to life, / and he lies awake to finish the work.
38:28 The same is true of the smith who sits beside the anvil / and studies the unworked iron. / The fire's hot blast hardens his skin, / and he battles the furnace's heat. / The ring of the hammer fills his ear again and again, / and he keeps his eyes on the model of the piece he is making. / He gives his heart to finishing his works, / and he lies awake to decorate them at the end.
38:29 The same is true of the potter who sits at his work, / spinning the wheel with his feet. / He worries about his work all the time, / and every pot he makes is counted.
38:30 He presses the clay into shape with his arm / and bends its toughness under his feet. / He gives his heart to finishing the fine piece, / and he lies awake to clean out the kiln.
38:31 All these put their trust in their hands, / and each one is wise in his own work.
38:32 No city could be settled without them; / no one could live there as a stranger or walk about.
38:33 Yet they do not stand out in the assembly, / and they do not think through the covenant of judgment. / The judge's chair is not for them, / and they do not make righteousness and justice plain; / they are not found among the makers of proverbs.
38:34 But they keep the made world standing, / and their prayer is in the work of their craft.
## The scribe
39:1 It is not so with the one who gives his soul / to pondering the law of the Most High. / He digs out the wisdom of all the ancients / and busies himself with prophecies.
39:2 He keeps in mind the stories of famous men / and goes into the twists and turns of parables.
39:3 He hunts for the secrets hidden in proverbs / and spends his days with the riddles of parables.
39:4 He is in service among the powerful / and is seen in the presence of a ruler. / He passes through the lands of foreign nations, / for he has tried out the good and the bad in people.
39:5 He gives his heart to rising at dawn / to turn to the Lord who made him, / and he pleads before the Most High. / He opens his mouth to pray / and makes his plea about his sins.
39:6 If the Lord who is great so wills, / a spirit of understanding will fill him. / He himself will rain down words of wisdom, / and in prayer he will praise the Lord.
39:7 He will keep his counsel and knowledge straight / and think deeply on his secrets.
39:8 He will make known the instruction he teaches, / and his boast will be the Lord's covenant law.
39:9 His understanding will be praised by many, / and it will never be wiped out. / The memory of him will not fade, / and his name will live from generation to generation.
39:10 The nations will tell about his wisdom, / and the assembly will make his praise known.
39:11 If he stays alive, his name will outshine a thousand, / and if he is laid to rest, he has what he needs.
## All the Lord's works are good
39:12 I have thought of more, and I will tell it, / for I am as full as the moon at mid-month.
39:13 Listen to me, you holy sons, / and bud like a rose that grows beside a brook in the field.
39:14 Give off a sweet smell like incense, / and flower like the lily. / Spread your fragrance and sing a song of praise. / Bless the Lord over all the works.
39:15 Give his name its greatness / and praise him with thanks, / singing with your lips and playing the lyre. / This is how you will speak your thanks:
39:16 The Lord's works, every one of them, are very good, / and every command of his will be done in its time.
39:17 No one may say, "What is this? What is this for?" / for each thing will be sought out in its own time. / By his word the water stood like a mound, / and a word from his mouth made storehouses for the waters.
39:18 At his command, all that pleases him is done, / and no one can lessen his saving power.
39:19 The works of all flesh are in front of him, / and there is no hiding from his eyes.
39:20 He looks from age to age, / and nothing is surprising to him.
39:21 No one may say, "What is this? What is this for?" / for each thing was created for its use.
39:22 His blessing overflows like a river / and drenches the dry ground like a flood.
39:23 In the same way the nations will inherit his anger, / as when he turned waters into salt.
39:24 His ways are straight for those who are holy, / but full of stumbling blocks for the lawless.
39:25 Good things were made for good people from the start, / and bad things likewise for sinners.
39:26 The chief things a human life needs / are water, fire, iron and salt, / fine flour, wheat, honey and milk, / the blood of the grape, oil and clothing.
39:27 For the godly all these work for good, / but for sinners they are turned to harm.
39:28 Some winds were made to punish; / in their fury they lay on their whips harder. / At the end they will pour out their strength / and so satisfy the anger of the one who made them.
39:29 Fire and hail, famine and death, / all these were created to punish;
39:30 the teeth of wild beasts, scorpions and vipers, / and the sword that punishes the ungodly with destruction.
39:31 They rejoice at his command / and stand ready on the earth for when they are needed, / and at their hour they do not overstep his word.
39:32 That is why I was sure of this from the beginning. / I thought it through and set it down in writing.
39:33 The Lord's works, every one of them, are good, / and when the hour comes he provides whatever is needed.
39:34 No one may say, "This is worse than that," / for in its time each thing will be found worthy.
39:35 And now we have sung with all our heart and mouth: / bless the name of the Lord!
## The misery of human life
40:1 Much hard work was made for every person, / and a heavy yoke rests on the sons of Adam / from the day they leave their mother's womb / to the day they are buried in the mother of all.
40:2 Their troubled thoughts, the fear in their hearts, / the dread of what they expect: the day of death.
40:3 It is so for the king seated on a glorious throne / and for the man humbled to earth and ashes,
40:4 for the wearer of violet and a crown / and for the one wrapped in rough, unbleached linen:
40:5 anger and jealousy, dismay and turmoil, / the fear of death, bitterness and strife. / When it is time to rest on his bed, / his night's sleep scrambles his mind.
40:6 His rest is little, as good as nothing, / and after that, in his sleep, it is as if he were on watch by day. / He is shaken by what his heart sees, / like a man who has run from the front line of battle.
40:7 At the very moment of rescue he wakes, / amazed to find there was nothing to fear.
40:8 This comes to all flesh, both human and animal, / and seven times more to sinners:
40:9 death and bloodshed, strife and the sword.
40:10 All these were created against the lawless, / and the flood came because of them.
40:11 Everything that comes from the earth goes back to the earth, / and what comes from the waters returns to the sea.
## What lasts
40:12 Every bribe and every wrong will be wiped out, / but faithfulness will stand for all time.
40:13 The riches of unjust men will run dry like a river / and die away like a loud thunderclap in the rain.
40:14 While he opens his hands, he is glad, / but lawbreakers will fail in the end.
40:15 The offspring of the ungodly will not spread many branches; / their roots are unclean, on a cliff of bare rock.
40:16 They are like sedge at the water's edge or the bank of a river, / torn up first, ahead of all the grass.
40:17 Kindness is like a paradise rich in blessings, / and almsgiving remains forever.
## Better than both
40:18 Life is sweet for the one who has enough and for the worker, / but better than both is the one who finds a treasure.
40:19 Children and the founding of a city keep a name alive, / but better than both is a blameless wife.
40:20 Wine and music make the heart glad, / but better than both is the love of wisdom.
40:21 Pipe and harp sweeten a tune, / but better than both is a pleasant tongue.
40:22 Your eye longs for charm and beauty, / but better than both is the green of a sown field.
40:23 A friend and a companion turn up when needed, / but better than both is a wife with her husband.
40:24 Brothers and help are there in a time of trouble, / but better than both is almsgiving, which rescues.
40:25 Gold and silver set a man's feet on firm ground, / but better than both is advice held in high regard.
40:26 Money and strength make the heart proud, / but better than both is the fear of the Lord. / In the fear of the Lord nothing runs short, / and it needs no help from anywhere else.
40:27 The fear of the Lord is like a paradise of blessing, / and they have covered him with more than any glory.
## Do not beg
40:28 My child, do not live a beggar's life. / It is better to die than to beg.
40:29 A man who looks to someone else's table / lives a life that cannot be counted as living. / He defiles his soul with another man's food, / but a man of sense who is well instructed will guard against it.
40:30 A shameless man finds begging sweet in his mouth, / but a fire burns in his stomach.
## O death
41:1 O death, how bitter it is to think of you / for one who lives untroubled among the things he owns, / for a man with nothing to distract him, who prospers in everything / and still has the strength to enjoy a meal!
41:2 O death, your sentence is good / for someone in need whose strength is failing, / who is very old and worried about everything, / who resists everyone and can bear no more.
41:3 Do not be afraid of death's sentence. / Think of those who lived before you and those yet to come.
41:4 This is the Lord's sentence on all flesh, / so why refuse what pleases the Most High? / Whether it is ten years or a hundred or a thousand, / in Hades no one argues over the length of a life.
## The children of the ungodly
41:5 The children of sinners become abhorrent children, / and they spend their time around the homes of the ungodly.
41:6 The inheritance of sinners' children will be lost, / and disgrace will stay with their descendants.
41:7 An ungodly father will be blamed by his children, / for it is his fault that they are scorned.
41:8 Woe to you, ungodly men, / who have abandoned the law of God Most High!
41:9 If you are born, you are born for a curse, / and if you die, a curse is your share.
41:10 Everything that comes from the earth goes back to the earth, / and in the same way the ungodly pass from a curse into ruin.
## A good name
41:11 Human mourning is for the body, / but the name of sinners, which is not good, will be wiped out.
41:12 Care for your good name; / it will stay with you longer than a thousand great stores of gold.
41:13 A good life lasts a counted number of days, / but a good name remains for all time.
## True and false shame
41:14 Children, guard instruction in peace. / Wisdom kept hidden and treasure out of sight: / what good is either?
41:15 Better is a man who hides his folly / than a man who hides his wisdom.
41:16 So feel shame as my words direct, / for it is not good to keep every kind of shame, / and not everything is truly approved by everyone.
41:17 Be ashamed before father and mother of sexual sin, / and before a leader or a ruler of a lie;
41:18 before judge and magistrate, of wrongdoing; / before the congregation and the people, of lawlessness; / before partner and friend, of dishonesty;
41:19 before the place where you lodge, of theft; / before the truth of God and the covenant; / of leaning your elbow on the bread at a meal; / of rudeness in taking and giving;
41:20 before those who greet you, of silence; / of eyeing a courtesan;
41:21 of turning your face away from a relative; / of seizing another's share or gift; / of staring at a married woman;
41:22 of meddling with a man's slave girl, / and do not go near her bed; / before friends, of insulting words, / and after you give a gift, do not taunt;
42:1 of repeating what you have heard / and of giving away secrets. / Then your modesty will be real, / and everyone will look on you with favor. / But do not be ashamed of these things, / and do not show favoritism and so sin:
42:2 of the law of the Most High and the covenant, / and of a judgment that declares the ungodly in the right;
42:3 of reckoning up with a partner and with fellow travelers; / of handing out an inheritance among companions;
42:4 of keeping scales and weights accurate; / of gaining much or little;
42:5 of fair dealing in sales to merchants; / of disciplining your children often; / and of beating a bad slave until his side bleeds.
42:6 Put a seal on things where the wife is bad, / and lock up wherever many hands could reach.
42:7 Whatever you hand over, count it and weigh it, / and put all giving and taking in writing.
42:8 of disciplining the senseless and the fool, / and of the very old man who quarrels with young men. / Then you will be truly well instructed / and approved by everyone alive.
## A father's worry for his daughter
42:9 A daughter keeps her father awake in secret, / and his worry over her drives away sleep: / when she is young, that she may pass her bloom, / and when she is married, that she may be hated;
42:10 while she is a virgin, that she may be defiled / and find herself with child under her father's roof; / when she has a husband, that she may stray, / and once she is married, that she may be barren.
42:11 Set a firm guard on a headstrong daughter, / or she will make your enemies laugh at you, / give the town something to talk about, / have you called out before the people, / and shame you in front of the crowds.
42:12 Do not look at anyone for their beauty, / and do not sit around among women.
42:13 For the moth comes out of clothes, / and a woman's wickedness comes from a woman.
42:14 Better a man's wickedness than a woman who does good; / a woman who brings shame brings disgrace.
## The works of the Lord
42:15 I will remember the Lord's works now / and tell in full what I have seen. / His works exist by the Lord's words.
42:16 The sun, as it gives light, gazes over all things, / and his work is filled with his glory.
42:17 Not even to his holy ones has the Lord granted / to recount all his marvels, / which the Lord Almighty made firm / for the whole of things to rest secure in his glory.
42:18 He has searched out the deep and the heart, / and I have pondered their cunning ways. / For the Lord knows everything that can be known, / and he looks into the signs of the ages.
42:19 He declares what has passed and what will be, / and he uncovers the tracks of hidden things.
42:20 No thought gets past him; / not a single word is hidden from him.
42:21 He set in order the mighty works of his wisdom, / for he is from before the ages and for all ages. / Nothing has been added and nothing taken away, / and he needed no one to advise him.
42:22 How lovely all his works are, / even down to a spark one can see!
42:23 Every one of these lives and endures forever, / ready for every need, and all of them obey.
42:24 Everything is double, one thing facing another, / and nothing he made falls short.
42:25 Each one makes the good of the other stronger. / Who could ever see enough of his glory?
## Sun, moon, stars and rainbow
43:1 The pride of the heights is the clear, firm dome; / the look of the sky is a glorious sight.
43:2 The sun comes into sight and makes its announcement as it rises: / an amazing instrument, the work of the Most High.
43:3 When it stands at midday it dries the country out, / and who can hold out against its burning?
43:4 The stoker of a furnace works amid the heat, / but the sun scorches the hills three times over. / It blows out blasts of flame, / and its flashing beams dim the sight.
43:5 Great is the Lord who made it, / and his words drive it swiftly along its path.
43:6 The moon, too, keeps to its time in everything, / marking off the seasons, a sign that lasts forever.
43:7 The moon gives the sign for the feasts, / a light that grows smaller when its course is done.
43:8 The month is named after the moon, / and it grows wonderfully as it changes. / It is an instrument for the camps on high, / shining in the dome of the sky.
43:9 The sky's beauty, the stars' glory, / an ornament giving light in the Lord's high places.
43:10 They stand in place by holy words, as he decreed, / and they do not tire on their watch.
43:11 Look at the rainbow and bless the one who made it. / How lovely its bright glow is!
43:12 It rings the sky with a circle of glory; / the Most High's hands drew it taut.
## Snow, storm and sea
43:13 By his command he makes the snow settle, / and he sends his lightning bolts of judgment racing.
43:14 So his treasure-houses swing open, / and clouds take wing like birds.
43:15 In his greatness he makes the clouds strong, / and stones of hail break loose.
43:16 The mountains are shaken at the sight of him; / the south wind blows because he wills it.
43:17 His thunder's roar reproaches the earth, / as do the north wind's gale and the whirling storm. / Like birds swooping down, he sprinkles the snow, / and it falls the way locusts settle.
43:18 The eye is struck with wonder at how white and lovely it is, / and the heart is astonished as it comes down.
43:19 Like salt he spills the frost across the earth, / and frozen, it becomes sharp points like thorns.
43:20 When the chill wind blows from the north, / ice forms over the water. / It rests on every gathering of water, / and the water dresses in it like a breastplate.
43:21 It eats up the mountains and scorches the wilderness, / and burns the green growth out like fire.
43:22 A mist brings quick healing to everything, / and dew that comes after the heat brings refreshment.
43:23 His thinking quieted the deep, / and in it he planted islands.
43:24 Sailors on the sea tell how dangerous it is, / and we are amazed at what reaches our ears.
43:25 There too are strange and wonderful works, / living things of every kind, and the race of sea monsters.
43:26 Because of him, each thing comes safely to its end, / and by his word all things are put together.
## He is the all
43:27 We will say much and still not get to the end, / and the last word is: "He is the all."
43:28 How can we be strong enough to glorify him? / He is the Great One, beyond all his works.
43:29 The Lord is fearsome, great beyond measure, / and his power is a wonder.
43:30 When you glorify the Lord, lift him up as high as you can, / for he will still be higher. / As you raise him high, gather all your strength; / do not tire, for you will never get there.
43:31 Who has seen him, to describe him? / Who can praise his greatness as it really is?
43:32 Many hidden things are greater than these, / for we have seen only a few of his works.
43:33 The Lord made everything, / and he gave wisdom to the godly.
