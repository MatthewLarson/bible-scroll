## False friends and true
37:1 Every friend says, "I am his friend too." / But some friends are friends in name only.
37:2 Is it not a grief that comes close to death / when a companion and friend turns into an enemy?
37:3 O evil scheming, where did you roll in from, / to cover the dry land with treachery?
37:4 A companion enjoys himself when his friend is glad, / but in a time of trouble he stands against him.
37:5 A companion shares his friend's labor for the sake of his stomach, / and when war comes he picks up a shield.
37:6 Do not let a friend slip from your soul, / and do not forget him when you have money.
## Choosing an adviser
37:7 Every adviser praises his own advice, / but some give advice that serves themselves.
37:8 Watch yourself around an adviser. / Find out first what he needs, / for he will make his plans with himself in mind. / He may cast the lot against you
37:9 and tell you, "You are on a good road," / then stand off to one side to watch what happens to you.
37:10 Do not ask advice of someone who eyes you with suspicion, / and keep your plans hidden from those who envy you.
37:11 Do not consult a woman about her rival, / or a coward about war, / a merchant about a trade, / or a buyer about selling, / a jealous man about gratitude, / or a heartless man about kindness, / a lazy man about any job, / or a drifting hired hand about finishing the work, / or an idle slave about a lot of labor. / Do not count on any of these for advice.
37:12 Instead, stay close to a godly man / whom you know keeps the commandments, / whose soul is like your own soul, / and who will grieve with you if you stumble.
37:13 And hold firm to what your own heart advises, / for nothing is more trustworthy to you than that.
37:14 A man's soul sometimes tells him more / than seven lookouts sitting up high on a watchtower.
37:15 And beyond all this, ask the Most High / to make your path straight in truth.
## Words and true wisdom
37:16 Every work begins with a word, / and every deed with a plan.
37:17 The heart leaves a track when it changes:
37:18 four kinds of things spring up, / good and evil, life and death, / and what rules them all the time is the tongue.
37:19 One man is clever and teaches many, / yet he is no use to himself.
37:20 Another is a clever talker and is hated; / he will end up without anything to eat,
37:21 for the Lord has not given him favor, / since he has been stripped of all wisdom.
37:22 One man is wise for his own sake, / and the fruits of his understanding can be trusted in his mouth.
37:23 A wise man teaches his own people, / and the fruits of his understanding can be trusted.
37:24 A wise man will be full of blessing, / and everyone who sees him will call him happy.
37:25 A man's life lasts a counted number of days, / but the days of Israel cannot be counted.
37:26 The wise man will win trust among his people, / and his name will live forever.
## Do not overeat
37:27 My child, test your soul while you live. / See what is bad for it, and do not give it that.
37:28 For not everything is good for everyone, / and not every soul enjoys everything.
37:29 Do not be greedy for every delicacy, / and do not throw yourself at the food.
37:30 Too much food brings pain, / and greed for food ends in a sick stomach.
37:31 Many have died from overeating, / but the one who is careful will add to his life.
## Honor the doctor
38:1 Honor the doctor with the honors due him, since you will need him, / for it was the Lord who created him.
38:2 Healing comes from the Most High, / and he will receive a gift from the king.
38:3 A doctor's knowledge lets him hold his head high, / and the great admire him.
38:4 Out of the earth the Lord created remedies, / and a sensible man will not turn up his nose at them.
38:5 Did not a piece of wood once turn water sweet, / to make its power known?
38:6 He gave human beings knowledge, / so that he would be glorified in his wonders.
38:7 With these he heals and takes away a person's pain;
38:8 the ointment-maker makes a mixture out of them. / So his works never come to an end, / and from him comes peace over the face of the earth.
38:9 My child, when you are sick, do not neglect it, / but pray to the Lord: he is the one who will heal you.
38:10 Give up your wrongdoing and keep your hands straight, / and wash your heart clean of every sin.
38:11 Offer a fragrant sacrifice and a memorial portion of fine flour, / and make your offering rich, as though you were no more.
38:12 Then give the doctor his place, for it was the Lord who created him. / Do not let him leave you, for you need him.
38:13 There are times when success lies in their hands,
38:14 for they too will pray to the Lord / to grant them success in giving relief / and healing that keeps life going.
38:15 Whoever sins before the one who made him, / may he fall into a doctor's hands!
## Mourning the dead
38:16 My child, let your tears fall for the dead, / and begin the lament like someone who is suffering terribly. / Lay out his body as is right for him, / and do not neglect his burial.
38:17 Weep bitterly and beat your breast with passion. / Mourn as he deserves, for a day or two, / so that no one can speak against you. / Then be comforted, because of what grief does:
38:18 from grief comes death, / and grief of heart bends a person's strength.
38:19 When the dead is carried away, grief also should pass by, / but a poor man's life weighs on the heart.
38:20 Do not hand your heart over to grief. / Turn it away, and remember the end.
38:21 Do not forget: there is no coming back. / You will not help him, and you will hurt yourself.
38:22 Remember that his sentence will be yours too: / "Yesterday it was me, and today it is you."
38:23 When the dead is at rest, let his memory rest too, / and take comfort over him once his spirit has gone out.
## Farmers and craftsmen
38:24 A scribe's wisdom comes with the chance of leisure; / whoever has less to do will become wise.
38:25 How can someone become wise who holds a plow / and takes pride in the shaft of his goad, / who drives oxen and spends his life on their work, / and whose talk is all about young bulls?
38:26 He sets his heart on plowing furrows, / and he loses sleep over feed for the heifers.
38:27 So it is with every craftsman and master builder / who works by night as by day: / those who cut the engravings on seals, / patiently changing the design. / He sets his heart on making the copy true to life, / and he loses sleep to finish the work.
38:28 So it is with the smith sitting by the anvil, / intent on the raw iron. / The breath of the fire hardens his flesh, / and he struggles in the heat of the furnace. / The ring of the hammer fills his ear again and again, / and his eyes are fixed on the pattern of the tool. / He sets his heart on finishing his works, / and he loses sleep to decorate them at the end.
38:29 So it is with the potter sitting at his work / and turning the wheel with his feet. / He is always anxious over his work, / and every piece he makes is counted.
38:30 With his arm he molds the clay, / and with his feet he works its stiffness out. / He sets his heart on finishing the fine piece, / and he loses sleep to clean out the kiln.
38:31 All these rely on their hands, / and each one is wise in his own work.
38:32 Without them no city could be lived in; / no one could settle there or walk its streets.
38:33 Yet they do not stand out in the assembly, / and they do not think through the covenant of judgment. / They do not sit on the judge's seat, / and they do not make righteousness and justice plain; / they are not found among the makers of proverbs.
38:34 But they keep the made world standing, / and their prayer is in the work of their craft.
## The scribe
39:1 How different is the one who devotes his soul / to thinking on the law of the Most High! / He searches out the wisdom of all the ancients / and keeps himself busy with prophecies.
39:2 He keeps in mind the stories of famous men / and goes into the twists and turns of parables.
39:3 He searches out the hidden meanings of proverbs / and lives among the riddles of parables.
39:4 He serves among the great / and appears before rulers. / He travels through the lands of foreign nations, / for he has tested the good and the evil in people.
39:5 He sets his heart on rising early / to seek the Lord who made him, / and he makes his request before the Most High. / He opens his mouth in prayer / and asks pardon for his sins.
39:6 If the great Lord is willing, / he will be filled with a spirit of understanding. / He will pour out words of wisdom like rain / and give thanks to the Lord in prayer.
39:7 He will keep his counsel and knowledge straight / and think deeply on his secrets.
39:8 He will make known the instruction he teaches, / and he will take pride in the law of the Lord's covenant.
39:9 Many will praise his understanding, / and it will never be wiped out. / His memory will not fade, / and his name will live from generation to generation.
39:10 Nations will tell of his wisdom, / and the assembly will announce his praise.
39:11 If he lives long, he will leave a greater name than a thousand others, / and if he goes to his rest, that is enough for him.
## All the Lord's works are good
39:12 I have thought of more, and I will tell it, / for I am as full as the moon at mid-month.
39:13 Listen to me, you holy sons, / and bud like a rose growing by a stream in the field.
39:14 Give off a sweet smell like incense, / and flower like the lily. / Spread your fragrance and sing a song of praise. / Bless the Lord for all the works.
39:15 Declare the greatness of his name / and give him thanks with praise, / with songs on your lips and with lyres. / And this is what you will say as you give thanks:
39:16 The Lord's works, every one of them, are very good, / and every command of his will be done in its time.
39:17 No one may say, "What is this? What is this for?" / for each thing will be sought out in its own time. / At his word the water stood up like a heap, / and by a word from his mouth there were storehouses of water.
39:18 At his command, all that pleases him is done, / and no one can lessen his saving power.
39:19 The works of all flesh are in front of him, / and nothing can hide from his eyes.
39:20 He looks from age to age, / and nothing is surprising to him.
39:21 No one may say, "What is this? What is this for?" / for everything was created for its purpose.
39:22 His blessing overflows like a river / and drenches the dry ground like a flood.
39:23 In the same way the nations will receive his anger as their share, / as when he turned waters into salt.
39:24 His ways are straight for those who are holy, / but full of stumbling blocks for the lawless.
39:25 From the beginning, good things were created for good people, / and in the same way bad things for sinners.
39:26 The chief things a human life needs / are water, fire, iron and salt, / fine flour, wheat, honey and milk, / the blood of the grape, oil and clothing.
39:27 All these are good for the godly, / but for sinners they turn bad.
39:28 There are winds that were created for vengeance; / in their fury they make their lashes harsher. / At the time of the end they will pour out their strength / and so satisfy the anger of the one who made them.
39:29 Fire and hail, famine and death, / all these were created for vengeance;
39:30 the teeth of wild beasts, scorpions and vipers, / and the sword that takes vengeance on the ungodly and destroys them.
39:31 They rejoice at his command / and stand ready on the earth for when they are needed, / and when their time comes, they do not disobey his word.
39:32 That is why I was sure of this from the beginning. / I thought it through and set it down in writing.
39:33 The Lord's works, every one of them, are good, / and he will supply every need in its hour.
39:34 No one may say, "This is worse than that," / for everything will prove its worth in its time.
39:35 And now we have sung with all our heart and mouth: / bless the name of the Lord!
## The misery of human life
40:1 Much hard work was made for every person, / and a heavy yoke rests on the sons of Adam / from the day they leave their mother's womb / to the day they are buried in the mother of all.
40:2 Their troubled thoughts, the fear in their hearts, / the dread of what they expect: the day of death.
40:3 From the one who sits on a throne in glory / to the one brought low in dust and ashes,
40:4 from one who wears violet robes and a crown / to one dressed in rough unbleached linen,
40:5 there is anger and jealousy, alarm and unrest, / fear of death, resentment and quarrels. / Even when he rests on his bed, / sleep at night twists his thoughts.
40:6 His rest is little, as good as nothing, / and after that, in his sleep, it is as if he were on watch by day. / He is shaken by what his heart sees, / like a man who has run from the front line of battle.
40:7 Just when he reaches safety, he wakes up / and is amazed that there was nothing to fear.
40:8 This comes to all flesh, both human and animal, / and seven times more to sinners:
40:9 death and bloodshed, strife and the sword.
40:10 All these were created against the lawless, / and the flood came because of them.
40:11 Everything that comes from the earth goes back to the earth, / and what comes from the waters returns to the sea.
## What lasts
40:12 Every bribe and every wrong will be wiped out, / but good faith will stand forever.
40:13 The wealth of the unjust will dry up like a stream / and roar away like a great clap of thunder in a rainstorm.
40:14 While he opens his hands, he is glad, / but lawbreakers will fail in the end.
40:15 The children of the ungodly do not put out many branches; / they are unclean roots on a sheer rock,
40:16 like reed-grass by any water or on a riverbank, / pulled up before any other grass.
40:17 Kindness is like a garden full of blessings, / and almsgiving lasts forever.
## Better than both
40:18 Life is sweet for the one who has enough and for the worker, / but better than both is the one who finds a treasure.
40:19 Children and the founding of a city keep a name alive, / but better than both is a blameless wife.
40:20 Wine and music gladden the heart, / but better than both is the love of wisdom.
40:21 The flute and the harp make a song sweet, / but better than both is a pleasant tongue.
40:22 Your eye longs for charm and beauty, / but better than both is the green of a sown field.
40:23 A friend and a companion turn up when needed, / but better than both is a wife with her husband.
40:24 Brothers and help are there in a time of trouble, / but better than both is almsgiving, which rescues.
40:25 Gold and silver make a person stand firm, / but better than both is a wife who is valued.
40:26 Money and strength make the heart proud, / but better than both is the fear of the Lord. / With the fear of the Lord nothing is lacking, / and with it there is no need to look for help.
40:27 The fear of the Lord is like a garden of blessing, / and they have covered him with more than any glory.
## Do not beg
40:28 My child, do not live a beggar's life. / It is better to die than to beg.
40:29 A man who looks to someone else's table / lives a life that cannot be counted as living. / He defiles his soul with another man's food, / but a man of sense and good training will guard against it.
40:30 In the mouth of the shameless, begging tastes sweet, / but a fire burns in his stomach.
## O death
41:1 O death, how bitter it is to think of you / for one who lives untroubled among the things he owns, / for a man with nothing to distract him, who prospers in everything / and still has the strength to enjoy a meal!
41:2 O death, your sentence is good / for someone in need whose strength is failing, / who is very old and worried about everything, / who is contrary and has lost patience.
41:3 Do not be afraid of death's sentence. / Remember those who came before you and those who will come after.
41:4 This is the Lord's sentence on all flesh, / so why refuse what pleases the Most High? / Whether it is ten years or a hundred or a thousand, / in Hades no one argues over the length of a life.
## The children of the ungodly
41:5 The children of sinners become disgusting children, / and they spend their time around the homes of the ungodly.
41:6 The inheritance of sinners' children will be lost, / and disgrace will stay with their descendants.
41:7 Children will blame an ungodly father, / for they are disgraced because of him.
41:8 Woe to you, ungodly men, / who have abandoned the law of God Most High!
41:9 If you are born, you are born for a curse, / and if you die, a curse is your share.
41:10 Everything that comes from the earth goes back to the earth, / and so the ungodly go from curse to ruin.
## A good name
41:11 Human mourning is for the body, / but the name of sinners, which is not good, will be wiped out.
41:12 Take care of your name, / for it will last longer for you than a thousand great hoards of gold.
41:13 A good life lasts a counted number of days, / but a good name lasts forever.
## True and false shame
41:14 Children, keep instruction and be at peace. / Hidden wisdom and a treasure no one sees: / what use is either one?
41:15 Better is a man who hides his folly / than a man who hides his wisdom.
41:16 So feel shame as my words direct, / for it is not good to keep every kind of shame, / and not everything is truly approved by everyone.
41:17 Be ashamed before father and mother of sexual sin, / and before a leader or a ruler of a lie;
41:18 before a judge or a magistrate, of wrongdoing; / before the congregation and the people, of lawlessness; / before a partner or a friend, of dishonesty;
41:19 before the place where you live, of theft; / before the truth of God and the covenant; / of leaning your elbow on the bread at a meal; / of rudeness in taking and giving;
41:20 before those who greet you, of silence; / of looking at a prostitute;
41:21 of turning your face away from a relative; / of taking away someone's share or gift; / of staring at a married woman;
41:22 of meddling with a man's slave girl, / and do not go near her bed; / before friends, of insulting words, / and after you give a gift, do not taunt;
42:1 of repeating what you have heard / and of giving away secrets. / Then you will have true modesty / and find favor with everyone. / But do not be ashamed of these things, / and do not show favoritism and so sin:
42:2 of the law of the Most High and the covenant, / and of a judgment that gives the ungodly his due;
42:3 of settling accounts with a partner or with fellow travelers; / of dividing an inheritance among friends;
42:4 of keeping scales and weights accurate; / of gaining much or little;
42:5 of fair dealing in sales to merchants; / of disciplining your children often; / and of beating a bad slave until his side bleeds.
42:6 Where there is a bad wife, a seal is a good thing, / and where there are many hands, lock things up.
42:7 Whatever you hand over, count it and weigh it, / and put all giving and taking in writing.
42:8 of correcting the senseless and the fool, / and of the very old man who quarrels with young men. / Then you will be truly well taught / and approved by everyone alive.
## A father's worry for his daughter
42:9 A daughter keeps her father awake in secret, / and his worry over her drives away sleep: / when she is young, that she may pass her bloom, / and when she is married, that she may be hated;
42:10 while she is a virgin, that she may be defiled / and become pregnant in her father's house; / when she has a husband, that she may stray, / and once she is married, that she may be barren.
42:11 Keep a strict watch over a headstrong daughter, / or she may make you a joke to your enemies, / the talk of the town, / called out before the people, / and she will shame you before a great crowd.
42:12 Do not look at anyone for their beauty, / and do not sit around among women.
42:13 For the moth comes out of clothes, / and a woman's wickedness comes from a woman.
42:14 Better a man's wickedness than a woman who does good; / a woman who brings shame brings disgrace.
## The works of the Lord
42:15 I will remember the Lord's works now / and tell in full what I have seen. / His works exist by the Lord's words.
42:16 The shining sun looks down on everything, / and his work is full of his glory.
42:17 The Lord has not given even his holy ones the power / to tell all his wonders, / which the Lord Almighty has set firm / so that the universe might stand secure in his glory.
42:18 He has searched out the deep and the heart, / and I have pondered their cunning ways. / For the Lord knows everything that can be known, / and he looks into the signs of the ages.
42:19 He declares what has passed and what will be, / and he uncovers the tracks of hidden things.
42:20 No thought gets past him; / not a single word is hidden from him.
42:21 He set in order the mighty works of his wisdom, / for he is from before the ages and for all ages. / Nothing has been added and nothing taken away, / and he needed no one to advise him.
42:22 How lovely all his works are, / even down to a spark one can see!
42:23 All these things live and last forever, / ready for every need, and everything obeys.
42:24 All things come in pairs, one set against the other, / and he has made nothing incomplete.
42:25 Each one makes the good of the other stronger. / Who could ever see enough of his glory?
## Sun, moon, stars and rainbow
43:1 The pride of the heights is the clear, firm dome; / the look of the sky is a glorious sight.
43:2 The sun comes into sight and makes its announcement as it rises: / an amazing instrument, the work of the Most High.
43:3 At noon it parches the land, / and who can stand up to its blazing heat?
43:4 A man tends a furnace to work in the heat, / but the sun burns the mountains three times hotter. / It breathes out fiery blasts, / and its shining rays blind the eyes.
43:5 Great is the Lord who made it, / and his words drive it swiftly along its path.
43:6 The moon, too, keeps to its time in everything, / marking off the seasons, a sign that lasts forever.
43:7 The moon gives the sign for the feasts, / a light that grows smaller when its course is done.
43:8 The month is named after the moon, / and it grows wonderfully as it changes. / It is a beacon for the armies on high, / shining in the dome of the sky.
43:9 The glory of the stars is the beauty of the sky, / a shining array in the Lord's high places.
43:10 They stand in place by holy words, as he decreed, / and they never slacken at their watches.
43:11 Look at the rainbow and bless the one who made it. / How lovely its bright glow is!
43:12 It rings the sky with a circle of glory; / the Most High's hands drew it taut.
## Snow, storm and sea
43:13 By his command he makes the snow settle, / and he sends his lightning bolts of judgment racing.
43:14 That is why the storehouses are opened / and the clouds fly out like birds.
43:15 In his greatness he makes the clouds strong, / and hailstones are broken off.
43:16 When he appears, the mountains shake; / at his will the south wind blows.
43:17 The sound of his thunder rebukes the earth, / and so do the north wind's storm and the whirlwind. / He scatters the snow like birds flying down, / and it comes down like locusts landing.
43:18 The eye marvels at the beauty of its whiteness, / and the heart is amazed as it falls.
43:19 He pours frost on the earth like salt, / and when it freezes it turns into sharp points like thorns.
43:20 The cold north wind blows, / and ice freezes on the water. / It settles over every pool of water, / and the water puts it on like a breastplate.
43:21 It eats up the mountains and scorches the wilderness, / and it withers the green plants as fire does.
43:22 A mist brings quick healing to everything, / and dew that comes after the heat brings refreshment.
43:23 By his thought he stilled the deep / and planted islands in it.
43:24 Those who sail the sea tell of its dangers, / and we are amazed at what our ears hear.
43:25 In it are strange and wonderful works, / every kind of living thing, and the race of sea monsters.
43:26 Because of him, each thing comes safely to its end, / and by his word all things are put together.
## He is the all
43:27 We will say much and still not get to the end, / and the last word is: "He is the all."
43:28 Where will we find the strength to praise him? / For he is the Great One, above all his works.
43:29 The Lord is fearsome, great beyond measure, / and his power is a wonder.
43:30 When you glorify the Lord, lift him up as high as you can, / for he will still be higher. / When you lift him up, put out all your strength, / and do not grow tired, for you will never reach the end.
43:31 Who has seen him, to describe him? / Who can praise his greatness as it really is?
43:32 Many hidden things are greater than these, / for we have seen only a few of his works.
43:33 The Lord made everything, / and he gave wisdom to the godly.
