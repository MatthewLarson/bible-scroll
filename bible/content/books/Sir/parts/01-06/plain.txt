## Prologue
1:0 Many great gifts have come to us through the law and the prophets and the others who came after them. Because of them, Israel is rightly praised for instruction and wisdom. Those who read these books ought to gain knowledge for themselves, and more than that, people who love learning should be able to help those outside, both by what they say and by what they write. So my grandfather Jesus gave himself more and more to reading the law, the prophets and the other books of our ancestors. When he had gained real skill in them, he was moved to write something of his own on instruction and wisdom. He wanted those who love learning to take this in too, and so to grow much further in a life lived by the law. You are asked, then, to read with goodwill and close attention, and to pardon us wherever we seem to fall short in rendering some of the phrases, though we worked hard at the translation. Words spoken in Hebrew do not carry the same force when they are carried over into another language. And not only these words: the law itself, the prophecies and the rest of the books have no small difference when read in their own tongue. I arrived in Egypt in the thirty-eighth year under King Euergetes and lived there a while, and I found a copy that held no small instruction. So I judged it most necessary to put some effort and hard work of my own into translating this book. Through that stretch of time I spent many sleepless nights and much skill to finish the book and send it out, also for those living as foreigners in other lands who want to love learning and are shaping their ways to live by the law.
## Wisdom comes from the Lord
1:1 All wisdom comes from the Lord, / and it is with him for all ages.
1:2 Who will count the sand of the seas, / the drops of rain, the days of the ages?
1:3 Who will trace out the height of the sky, / the width of the earth, the deep, and wisdom?
1:4 Wisdom was created first of all things, / and understanding with insight from long ago.
1:5 [The spring of wisdom is the word of God in the heights, / and her paths are commandments that last forever.]
1:6 Who has had the root of Wisdom shown to him? / Who has come to know her clever plans?
1:7 [To whom has the instruction of wisdom been revealed and made plain? / Who has understood how many her paths are?]
1:8 Only one is wise, and he is fearsome indeed: / he sits on his throne, the Lord.
1:9 He himself created her, / looked at her and numbered her, / and poured her out over all his works.
1:10 With all flesh she stays, in the measure he gives, / and he has supplied her freely to those who love him.
## The fear of the Lord
1:11 The fear of the Lord is glory and pride, / gladness and a garland of joy.
1:12 The fear of the Lord will please the heart; / it gives gladness and joy and a long life.
1:13 It will go well at the last for the one who fears the Lord; / on the day his life ends, he will find favor.
1:14 The start of wisdom is to fear God. / She was formed along with the faithful while they were in the womb.
1:15 Among human beings she built her nest, a base that lasts forever, / and she will be entrusted to their descendants.
1:16 To fear the Lord is to have your fill of wisdom; / she gives people her fruit until they are drunk with it.
1:17 Her whole house she will stock with things people long for, / and the barns with what she grows.
1:18 Wisdom's crown is the fear of the Lord; / it makes peace spring up, and health that heals.
1:19 He looked at her and numbered her; / he poured down knowledge and understanding like rain, / and he lifted high the glory of those who hold on to her.
1:20 Wisdom's root is to fear the Lord, / and her branches are a long span of days.
1:21 [The fear of the Lord pushes sins away, / and where it stays, it turns anger aside.]
## Patience and an honest heart
1:22 Anger that is in the wrong can never be declared right, / for when his anger tips the scale, it brings his downfall.
1:23 A patient man holds out for a while, / and later gladness springs up for him again.
1:24 He keeps his words to himself for a while, / and faithful lips will tell how much he understood.
1:25 In wisdom's storerooms lies a proverb full of knowledge, / but to a sinner, reverence for God is something abhorrent.
1:26 If you have set your heart on wisdom, guard the commandments, / and the Lord will provide her for you.
1:27 Wisdom and discipline: that is what the fear of the Lord is, / and his delight is faith and gentleness.
1:28 Never refuse to obey the fear of the Lord, / and never come near him with a double heart.
1:29 Do not put on an act for people to talk about, / and keep a guard on your lips.
1:30 Do not raise yourself high, or you will fall / and pile disgrace on your own soul. / Then the Lord will lay bare what you hid / and throw you down in the middle of the assembly, / because you did not draw near to the fear of the Lord, / and deceit filled your heart.
## Ready to be tested
2:1 My child, if you step forward to be a servant of the Lord God, / make your soul ready for testing.
2:2 Keep your heart straight and stand firm, / and when hard times arrive, do not rush.
2:3 Hold tight to him and do not turn away, / so that you will grow at your life's end.
2:4 Take whatever is laid on you, / and in the turns that bring you low, be patient.
2:5 Fire is where gold is proved, / and the furnace of being brought low is where people who are acceptable are proved.
2:6 Believe him, and he will take your part. / Keep your paths straight, and hope in him.
2:7 Wait for his mercy, you who fear the Lord, / and do not swerve, so that you do not fall.
2:8 Believe in him, you who fear the Lord, / and your reward will never stumble.
2:9 Set your hope on good things, you who fear the Lord, / on gladness that lasts forever, and on mercy.
2:10 Look back at the ancient generations and see. / Who put his trust in the Lord and was shamed? / Who held fast to fearing him and was left alone? / Who called on him, and he looked past him?
2:11 The Lord is full of pity and mercy; / he forgives sins and rescues in the hour of distress.
## Woe to the faint-hearted
2:12 Woe to fearful hearts and to drooping hands, / and to the sinner who steps onto two paths!
2:13 Woe to the limp heart, for it will not believe! / So it will find no cover.
2:14 Woe to you who have given up holding on! / What will you do when the Lord pays his visit?
## Those who fear the Lord
2:15 Those who fear the Lord will not refuse what he says, / and those who love him will keep to his ways.
2:16 Those who fear the Lord will seek what he takes pleasure in, / and those who love him will be filled full of the law.
2:17 Those who fear the Lord will get their hearts ready, / and in his presence they will bring their souls low.
2:18 We will fall into the Lord's hands, / not into the hands of humans; / for as his greatness is, / so too is his mercy.
## Honor your father and mother
3:1 Hear me, children, me, your father, / and act on it, so that you will be kept safe.
3:2 For the Lord has given the father honor above the children / and has set the mother's authority firmly over her sons.
3:3 The one who honors a father makes atonement for sins,
3:4 and the one who gives glory to his mother is like someone storing up treasure.
3:5 The one who honors a father will be made glad by his own children, / and like one who stores up treasure is the one who gives glory to his mother, / and on the day he prays, he will be heard.
3:6 The one who gives glory to his father will have a long life, / and the one who listens to the Lord will give his mother rest.
3:7 He will serve those who brought him into the world / as if they were his masters.
3:8 In what you do and in what you say, honor your father, / so that his blessing will reach you.
3:9 A father's blessing props up his children's homes, / but a mother's curse pulls their foundations out by the roots.
3:10 Do not boast of your father's shame, / for there is no glory for you in his shame.
3:11 A man's glory comes from the honor of his father, / and a mother in disgrace is a reproach to her children.
3:12 My child, support your father in his old age; / all his life long, do not bring him sorrow.
3:13 And if his understanding fades, make allowances for him; / do not shame him while you have all your strength.
3:14 Mercy shown to a father will never be forgotten; / in place of your sins, it will be built up for you.
3:15 When your day of distress comes, it will call you to mind. / As warm, clear weather does to frost, so your sins will be dissolved.
3:16 Like someone who speaks against God is the one who walks out on his father, / and one who makes his mother angry is under the Lord's curse.
## Humble yourself
3:17 My child, get your work done with gentleness, / and you will be loved by a person who is acceptable.
3:18 As great as you are, make yourself that much lower, / and you will find favor in the Lord's presence.
3:19 [There are many who stand high and are held in glory, / yet it is to the gentle that he makes his secrets known.]
3:20 The Lord's power is great, / and it is the lowly who give him glory.
## Things too hard for you
3:21 Do not go looking for things harder than you can handle, / and do not dig into things stronger than you are.
3:22 Turn your mind to what you have been told to do; / the hidden things are nothing you need.
3:23 With things over and above your own work, do not busy yourself, / for more than human understanding has already been shown to you.
3:24 Many have been led off course by what they imagined, / and bad assumptions have sent their thoughts sliding.
3:25 [Without pupils in your eyes you will go without light; / without a share of knowledge, do not claim to have it.]
## The hard heart
3:26 Whoever loves danger will fall into it. / A hard heart will come to harm at the end; / a hard heart will be weighed down at the end.
3:27 A hard heart will be loaded with pains, / and the sinner will pile one sin on top of others.
3:28 The proud man's disaster has no cure, / because wickedness has grown in him like a rooted plant.
3:29 A thoughtful heart turns a proverb over and over, / and the wise man longs for an ear that listens.
## Alms put out sin
3:30 A blazing fire is put out by water, / and sins are atoned for by alms.
3:31 The one who returns kindness keeps the days ahead in mind, / and in his time of falling he will find something to lean on.
## Do not turn from the poor
4:1 My child, do not rob the poor man of what he lives on, / and do not drag out the wait for eyes that look to you in need.
4:2 Do not sadden a hungry soul, / and do not stir up a man who is at his wits' end.
4:3 Do not shake up a heart that is already angry, / and do not drag out a gift to the one who asks.
4:4 When a hard-pressed man begs, do not wave him off, / and do not keep your face turned from someone poor.
4:5 From a man who begs, do not turn your eyes, / and do not give a person room to curse you.
4:6 If he calls down curses on you because his soul is bitter, / the one who made him will listen to his plea.
## Justice for the weak
4:7 Make the assembly fond of you, / and lower your head before a man of rank.
4:8 Turn your ear to the poor, / and answer him with words of peace, gently.
4:9 Pull the one who is wronged out of the hand of the one doing wrong, / and do not be faint-hearted when you give judgment.
4:10 To orphans be as a father, / and to their mother be in place of a husband. / So you will be as a son of the Most High, / and he will love you with more love than your own mother has.
## Wisdom raises her sons
4:11 Wisdom lifts up her sons on high, / and she grips those who look for her.
4:12 Whoever loves her loves life, / and those who get up at dawn to go to her will be filled with gladness.
4:13 The one who keeps hold of her will receive glory as his inheritance, / and where she goes in, the Lord will give his blessing.
4:14 Those who serve her will do sacred service for the Holy One, / and those who love her, the Lord loves.
4:15 The one who listens to her will judge nations, / and the one who draws near her will pitch his tent without fear.
4:16 If you put your trust in her, you will receive her as your inheritance, / and his descendants will hold her as their own.
4:17 At the start she will go with him by crooked ways. / She will lay fear and terror on him, / she will put him through torment by her discipline / until she can rely on his soul, / and she will try him by her rulings.
4:18 Then she will return to him on the straight road and cheer him, / and lay bare her hidden things to him.
4:19 But if he strays, she will leave him behind / and give him up into the hands of his downfall.
## Good shame and bad shame
4:20 Keep an eye on the time, and keep clear of what is evil, / and where your own soul is concerned, feel no shame.
4:21 There is one kind of shame that leads a person into sin, / and another kind of shame that is honor and grace.
4:22 Do not favor anyone's face to the harm of your own soul, / and do not be so respectful that it makes you fall.
4:23 Do not hold back a word at a time when it could bring rescue.
4:24 Wisdom gets recognized through what a person says, / and instruction by a word from his tongue.
4:25 Do not talk back to the truth, / and feel ashamed of how little you have been taught.
4:26 Never be ashamed of confessing your sins, / and do not strain against the flow of a river.
4:27 Do not lay yourself down for a fool to walk on, / and do not favor the face of a powerful man.
4:28 Struggle for what is true right up to death, / and the Lord God will go to war on your side.
## No lion at home
4:29 Do not be rough when you speak / and sluggish and limp in what you do.
4:30 In your own house, do not act like a lion, / and do not let your imagination run wild about your servants.
4:31 Do not keep your hand stretched out for taking / and pulled in tight when it comes to paying back.
## Do not lean on riches
5:1 Do not rest your weight on your possessions, / and do not say, "It is enough for me."
5:2 Do not let your soul and your strength lead you / to go after what your heart desires.
5:3 And do not say, "Who can lord it over me?" / The Lord is an avenger, and he will make you pay.
## Do not presume on mercy
5:4 Do not say, "I sinned, and what did it do to me?" / For the Lord is long-suffering.
5:5 Do not grow fearless about atonement, / heaping one sin after another onto your sins.
5:6 And do not say, "Great is his pity: / the heap of my sins it will atone for." / For mercy and anger are both with him, / and his fury settles down on sinners.
5:7 Do not hold off from turning to the Lord, / and do not push it from one day to the next. / Without warning the Lord's anger will come out, / and when the time of vengeance comes, you will be wiped out.
5:8 Do not lean on money gotten by wrong, / for on the day disaster strikes it will do you no good at all.
## A steady tongue
5:9 Do not winnow your grain in every wind / or take every trail that comes along. / That is how it is with the sinner who has two tongues.
5:10 Be fixed firmly in your understanding, / and let what you say be one thing.
5:11 Be swift when it comes to listening, / and give your reply with patience.
5:12 If you have understanding, give your neighbor an answer; / if you do not, keep your hand on your mouth.
5:13 Talk can bring glory, and it can bring disgrace, / and a man's own tongue is what makes him fall.
5:14 Do not get known as a gossip, / and do not get trapped by your own tongue. / The thief has shame waiting for him, / and the man with two tongues, a bitter verdict.
5:15 In matters great and small, do not act in ignorance,
6:1 and do not trade being a friend for being an enemy. / A bad name means shame; it inherits disgrace. / That is how it is with the sinner who has two tongues.
## The appetite that devours
6:2 Do not puff yourself up in your soul's schemes, / or your soul will be torn to pieces like a bull.
6:3 Your own leaves you will devour, your fruit you will destroy, / and you will leave yourself standing like dry wood.
6:4 A wicked soul ruins the one who owns it / and turns him into something his enemies gloat over.
## Friends true and false
6:5 A sweet voice will multiply a man's friends, / and a tongue that speaks kindly, the warm greetings he receives.
6:6 Have many who live at peace with you, / but for advice, one out of a thousand.
6:7 When you get a friend, get him by putting him to the test, / and do not rush to rely on him.
6:8 There is a friend who is one only in his own season, / and he will not stay at your side when your day of distress comes.
6:9 There is a friend who switches over to hatred / and will expose the quarrel that shames you.
6:10 There is a friend who shares your table, / and he will not stay at your side when your day of distress comes.
6:11 In your good times he will be just like you, / and he will make free with your servants.
6:12 But if you are humbled, he will be against you / and keep himself out of your sight.
6:13 Stand apart from those who hate you, / and watch out for those who are your friends.
6:14 A faithful friend is a solid roof over you, / and the man who has found him has found treasure.
6:15 Nothing can be given in exchange for a faithful friend, / and no scale can weigh how fine he is.
6:16 A faithful friend is a medicine that gives life, / and those who fear the Lord will find him.
6:17 Whoever fears the Lord will set his friendships on a straight course, / for his neighbor will be just what he is.
## Seek wisdom while you are young
6:18 My child, from the time you are young, pick out instruction for yourself, / and right up to your gray hairs you will find wisdom.
6:19 Go to her as a farmer goes to plow and sow, / and look forward to her good fruit. / The work she takes will tire you only a little, / and before long you will be eating what she yields.
6:20 How rough she is for the undisciplined! / A man without sense will not stay with her.
6:21 To him she will be a heavy stone for testing strength, / and he will soon heave her away.
6:22 Wisdom lives up to her name, / and not many can see her clearly.
## Wisdom's yoke
6:23 Hear me, my child, take my view to heart, / and do not wave away my counsel.
6:24 Step into her shackles with your feet, / and let her collar go around your neck.
6:25 Get your shoulder under her and bear her weight, / and do not resent her ropes.
6:26 With your whole soul draw near to her, / and with all your power hold to her ways.
6:27 Follow her tracks and hunt for her, and she will be known to you. / When you have a firm hold, do not let her go.
6:28 When it is all over, you will come upon her rest, / and she will turn about and become gladness for you.
6:29 Then her shackles will become a mighty shelter for you, / and her collar a robe of glory.
6:30 A gold ornament is on her, / and her ropes are threads of blue.
6:31 You will dress in her as in a robe of glory / and set her on yourself as a garland of joy.
## Stand among the elders
6:32 My child, if you want it, you will be instructed, / and if you give your soul to it, you will grow shrewd.
6:33 If listening is what you love, you will take it in, / and if you bend your ear, you will be wise.
6:34 Take your place where the elders are many. / Is someone wise? Hold tight to him.
6:35 Be eager to hear every account of God's things, / and let no proverb of understanding get away from you.
6:36 If you spot someone with understanding, be at his house at dawn, / and let your foot rub the steps of his doorway smooth.
6:37 Let your mind dwell on the Lord's commands, / and murmur over his commandments at all times. / He will steady your heart, / and the wisdom you long for will be given you.
