## Steer Clear of Wrong and of High Office
7:1 Do not do what is evil, / and harm will not lay hold of you.
7:2 Keep your distance from wrongdoing, / and it will steer clear of you.
7:3 My son, do not plant seed in the furrows of injustice, / and you will not have to harvest them seven times over.
7:4 Do not ask the Lord for the power to lead, / or ask the king for a seat of honor.
7:5 Do not claim to be in the right before the Lord, / and do not show off your wisdom in front of the king.
7:6 Do not try to become a judge, / or you may not have the strength to root out wrongdoing. / You might be afraid of someone powerful / and put a stumbling block in front of your own uprightness.
7:7 Do not wrong the people of the city, / and do not bring yourself down in front of the crowd.
7:8 Do not tie one sin onto another, / for you will not go unpunished even for one.
7:9 Do not say, "He will notice how many gifts I bring, / and when I make an offering to God Most High, he will accept it."
7:10 Do not lose heart when you pray, / and do not overlook giving alms.
7:11 Do not laugh at someone whose soul is bitter, / for there is One who brings low and lifts high.
7:12 Do not plow up a lie against your brother, / and do not do the same to a friend.
7:13 Do not be willing to tell any lie at all, / for the habit of it leads to nothing good.
7:14 Do not chatter on among the elders, / and do not say the same words over and over when you pray.
7:15 Do not hate hard work, / or farm work, which the Most High created.
7:16 Do not count yourself in with the crowd of sinners; / remember that wrath will not be slow to come.
7:17 Bring your soul very low, / for the punishment of the ungodly is fire and the worm.
## Friends, Household and Parents
7:18 Do not trade a friend for something that does not matter, / or a true brother for the gold of Ophir.
7:19 Do not let a wise and good wife slip away from you, / for her graciousness is worth more than gold.
7:20 Do not mistreat a servant who works honestly, / or a hired man who gives you all he has.
7:21 Let your soul love a good servant, / and do not hold back his freedom.
7:22 Do you have livestock? Look after them, / and if they are useful to you, keep them.
7:23 Do you have children? Instruct them, / and bend their necks while they are young.
7:24 Do you have daughters? Watch over their bodies, / and do not turn a beaming face toward them.
7:25 Give your daughter in marriage, and you will have finished a great task. / But give her to a man with good sense.
7:26 Do you have a wife who pleases your soul? Do not drive her out.
7:27 Honor your father with all your heart, / and do not forget your mother's pains in childbirth.
7:28 Remember that it was through them you were born. / How will you ever repay them as they have done for you?
## Priests, the Poor and the Grieving
7:29 Revere the Lord with all your soul, / and hold his priests in high regard.
7:30 With all your strength love the One who made you, / and do not desert the ones who serve him in worship.
7:31 Fear the Lord and honor the priest. / Give him his share, as you were commanded from the beginning: / the guilt offering, the gift of the shoulders, / the sacrifice of consecration and the first fruits.
7:32 Hold out your hand to the poor too, / so your blessing will be made whole.
7:33 A gift wins favor with every living person, / and do not keep your kindness from the dead.
7:34 Do not be missing when others weep, / and grieve along with those who grieve.
7:35 Do not be slow to visit the sick, / for deeds like these will make you loved.
7:36 In all your affairs, remember your last end, / and you will never sin.
## People Not to Quarrel With
8:1 Do not get into a fight with a powerful man, / or you may fall into his hands.
8:2 Do not quarrel with a rich man, / or he may throw his weight against you. / For gold has ruined many people / and bent the hearts of kings.
8:3 Do not argue with a man who talks too much, / and do not pile wood on his fire.
8:4 Do not joke around with an untaught person, / so that your ancestors are not insulted.
8:5 Do not throw it in the face of someone who turns from sin. / Remember that every one of us is liable to be punished.
8:6 Do not slight a man when he is old, / for there are those among us who are aging too.
8:7 Do not gloat over anyone who has died; / remember that we all come to our end.
## Learn from the Wise and the Old
8:8 Do not brush aside what the wise have to say; / spend your time with their proverbs. / From them you will learn instruction / and how to serve important men.
8:9 Do not miss what the old men tell, / for they in turn were taught by their fathers. / From them you will learn understanding / and how to give an answer when it is needed.
## Dangerous Company
8:10 Do not fan the coals of a sinner into flame, / or you may be burned in his blazing fire.
8:11 Do not get up and give ground to an insolent man, / so that he does not lie in wait like an ambush for your words.
8:12 Do not lend to someone stronger than you. / If you do, think of the money as already gone.
8:13 Do not stand good for more than you can manage. / If you do stand good for someone, plan on paying it yourself.
8:14 Do not take a judge to court, / for they will decide in his favor because of his standing.
8:15 Do not set out on the road with a reckless man, / or he will weigh you down. / He will do whatever he pleases, / and you will die along with him through his folly.
8:16 Do not pick a fight with a hot-tempered man, / and do not cross the desert with him. / Blood means nothing to him, / and where no help is near, he will strike you down.
8:17 Do not make plans with a fool, / for he will not be able to keep a word quiet.
8:18 Do nothing secret in front of a stranger, / for you cannot know what it will give birth to.
8:19 Do not open your heart to everyone, / and do not let anyone walk off with your good fortune.
## Women and Desire
9:1 Do not be jealous over the wife you hold close, / and do not teach her an evil lesson that will be used against you.
9:2 Do not give your soul over to a woman / so that she tramples on your strength.
9:3 Do not go to meet a woman who sells her company, / or you may fall into her traps.
9:4 Do not keep company with a woman who plays the harp, / or you may be caught by her tricks.
9:5 Do not fix your eyes on a virgin, / or you will stumble and pay the penalty for her.
9:6 Do not give your soul to prostitutes, / or you will lose what you inherited.
9:7 Do not let your eyes roam in the city's streets, / and do not wander in its empty places.
9:8 Keep your eye off a woman with a lovely figure, / and do not stare at beauty that belongs to someone else. / Many have gone astray over a woman's beauty, / and from it love blazes up like a fire.
9:9 Never sit down with another man's wife, / and do not join her in drinking parties over wine, / or your soul may lean toward her / and in your spirit you may slide down to ruin.
## Old Friends and Dangerous Men
9:10 Never leave an old friend behind, / for a new one is not his equal. / New friend, new wine: / give it time to age, and you will enjoy drinking it.
9:11 Do not envy a sinner's glory, / for you do not know how he will be overthrown.
9:12 Do not delight in what the ungodly delight in; / remember: right up to Hades they will not be acquitted.
9:13 Keep far away from a man who has power to kill, / and you will not have to live in fear of death. / If you do go near him, make no mistake, / or he will take your life away. / Know that you are stepping through the middle of traps / and walking along the top of city walls.
## Good Company and Good Speech
9:14 As far as you are able, size up the people around you, / and take counsel with the wise.
9:15 Let your discussions be with people of understanding, / and let all your talk be about the law of the Most High.
9:16 Let righteous men share your table, / and let the fear of the Lord be what you boast in.
9:17 A piece of work wins praise by the skill of the craftsman's hands, / and a people's leader is shown to be wise by his words.
9:18 A loudmouth is feared in his city, / and someone reckless with his words will be hated.
## Rulers in the Hand of God
10:1 A wise judge will instruct his people, / and the rule of someone with understanding will be in good order.
10:2 Like the people's judge, so are his officials. / Like the city's ruler, so are all who live there.
10:3 An untaught king will ruin his people, / and a city will be peopled through the good sense of those in power.
10:4 Authority over the earth is in the Lord's hand, / and at the right time he will raise up over it the one who is needed.
10:5 A man's success is in the Lord's hand, / and he will put his own glory on the face of the scribe.
## Pride Was Not Made for Humans
10:6 Do not hold a grudge against your neighbor for every wrong he does, / and do nothing at all through acts of insolence.
10:7 Arrogance is hateful to the Lord and to people, / and to both of them doing wrong is an offense.
10:8 Kingdoms are carried from one nation to another / because of wrongdoing, arrogance and money.
10:9 Why are dust and ashes proud? / Even while he is alive, I have thrown his insides away.
10:10 A long illness, and the doctor jokes; / a king today, and tomorrow he will be dead.
10:11 For when a person dies, / his inheritance is creeping things, wild animals and worms.
10:12 Human pride begins when a man turns away from the Lord / and his heart pulls away from the One who made him.
10:13 For sin is where pride begins, / and the one who clings to it will rain down disgusting deeds. / That is why the Lord brought on them disasters no one had heard of / and overturned them completely.
10:14 The Lord has pulled down the thrones of rulers / and seated the gentle in their place.
10:15 The Lord has plucked up the roots of nations / and planted the humble in their place.
10:16 The Lord has overturned the lands of nations / and destroyed them down to the foundations of the earth.
10:17 He has withered some of them and destroyed them, / and he has put an end to their memory on the earth.
10:18 Arrogance was never made for mortals, / nor fierce anger for children born of women.
## Who Deserves Honor
10:19 What race is honored? The human race. / What race is honored? Those who fear the Lord. / What race is despised? The human race. / What race is despised? Those who break the commandments.
10:20 Among brothers, their leader is honored, / and those who fear the Lord are honored in his eyes.
10:21 [The fear of the Lord is the first step to being accepted, / but stubbornness and pride are the first step to being thrown out.]
10:22 The rich, the honored and the poor: / the fear of the Lord is what they boast in.
10:23 It is not right to look down on a poor man who has understanding, / and it is not fitting to honor a man who is a sinner.
10:24 The nobleman, the judge and the ruler are given honor, / but none of them is greater than the one who fears the Lord.
10:25 Free men will wait on a wise servant, / and a man who has knowledge will not complain about it.
## Honor and Self-Respect
10:26 Do not try to be too clever when you do your work, / and do not put on airs when you are in need.
10:27 Better a man who works at everything than one who wanders around, / or one who acts important and has no bread.
10:28 My child, bring honor to your soul by gentleness, / and give it the respect it is due.
10:29 Who will declare a man innocent when he sins against his own soul? / And who will honor someone who dishonors his own life?
10:30 A poor man is honored for what he knows, / and a rich man is honored for his wealth.
10:31 If a man is honored when he is poor, / how much more when he is rich? / And if a man is without honor when he is rich, / how much more when he is poor?
## Do Not Judge by Looks
11:1 The wisdom of a lowly man lifts his head high / and seats him among the great.
11:2 Do not praise a man for his good looks, / and do not loathe anyone for how he looks.
11:3 The bee is small among the creatures that fly, / but its fruit is the best of all sweet things.
11:4 Do not brag about the clothes you wear, / and do not lift yourself up on the day you are honored. / For the Lord's works are full of wonder, / and what he does among people is hidden.
11:5 Many absolute rulers have sat down on the ground, / and one nobody thought of has worn the crown.
11:6 Many powerful men have been deeply disgraced, / and famous men have been handed over to others.
## Listen Before You Speak
11:7 Do not find fault before you have looked into the matter; / understand first, and then correct.
11:8 Give no answer until you have heard, / and do not cut in in the middle of what is being said.
11:9 Do not argue about a matter that is none of your business, / and do not sit with sinners when they judge a case.
## Everything Comes from the Lord
11:10 My child, do not take on too many affairs. / If you multiply them, you will not stay free of blame. / If you chase after things, you will not catch them, / and if you run away, you will not escape.
11:11 One person works and strains and hurries, / and falls behind all the more.
11:12 Another is slow and needs someone to help him; / he has little strength and plenty of poverty. / Yet the Lord's eyes have looked on him for his good. / He has raised him up from his low condition
11:13 and lifted his head high, / and many people have been amazed at him.
11:14 Good and bad, life and death, / poverty and wealth: they come from the Lord.
11:15 [Wisdom, knowledge and knowing the law come from the Lord; / love and the ways of good deeds come from him.]
11:16 [Error and darkness were created along with sinners, / and evil grows old together with those who take pride in evil.]
11:17 The Lord's gift stays with the godly, / and what he approves will prosper for all time.
## The Man Who Says "I Have Found Rest"
11:18 There is one who grows rich by care and penny-pinching, / and here is the share he gets for it:
11:19 when he says, "I have found rest, / and now I will feast on my good things," / he does not know how much time will go by / before he leaves them to others and dies.
11:20 Stand firm in your covenant and give yourself to it, / and grow old at your work.
11:21 Do not be amazed at what sinners achieve. / Trust the Lord and keep at your work, / for in the Lord's eyes it is an easy thing / to make a poor man rich, quickly and all at once.
11:22 The Lord's blessing is the wage of the godly, / and in a moment he makes his blessing bloom.
11:23 Do not say, "What do I need? / And what good things will I have from now on?"
11:24 Do not say, "I have all I need. / What harm can come to me from now on?"
11:25 On a good day the bad is forgotten, / and on a bad day the good is not remembered.
11:26 For it is easy for the Lord, on the day a person dies, / to pay him back for the way he has lived.
11:27 One hour of misery makes a person forget all his comforts, / and at the end of a man's life what he has done comes to light.
11:28 Do not call anyone blessed until he has died, / since a man is known by his children.
## Whom You Bring Home
11:29 Do not bring just anyone into your home, / for a cheat has many ways to set a trap.
11:30 Like a decoy partridge in its cage / is the heart of a proud man; / like a spy, he watches for someone to fall.
11:31 He lies in wait, turning good into bad, / and he puts a stain on what is best.
11:32 One spark of fire makes a whole bed of coals, / and a sinner lies in wait for blood.
11:33 Watch out for a man who does harm, for he plans evil things; / he may leave a stain on you forever.
11:34 Take a stranger into your home, / and he will upset your life with trouble / and make you a stranger to your own household.
## Know Whom You Help
12:1 If you do a kindness, know who you are doing it for, / and you will be thanked for the good you do.
12:2 Do good to a godly person and you will be repaid, / if not by him, then by the Most High.
12:3 No good comes to someone who keeps on doing evil, / or to someone who does not give alms.
12:4 Give to the godly, / and do not come to the aid of the sinner.
12:5 Do good to someone humble, but do not give to the ungodly. / Keep your bread from him; do not give it to him, / or he will use it to get the better of you. / For you will get back twice as much evil / for all the good you do him.
12:6 For the Most High himself hates sinners, / and he will pay back the ungodly with punishment.
12:7 Give to the good, / and do not come to the aid of the sinner.
## Never Trust an Enemy
12:8 A friend is not recognized when times are good, / and an enemy is not hidden when times are bad.
12:9 When a man prospers, his enemies are sad, / and when he suffers, even his friend goes off.
12:10 Never trust your enemy, / for like bronze his wickedness gathers rust.
12:11 Even if he humbles himself and goes around bent low, / take care in your soul and be on guard against him. / Be to him like someone who polishes a mirror, / and you will know that he has not rusted through for good.
12:12 Do not stand him next to you, / or he may knock you over and stand in your place. / Do not seat him at your right hand, / or he may try to take your seat. / Then at last you will see what I meant, / and my words will sting you.
12:13 Who feels sorry for a snake charmer when he is bitten, / or for anyone who goes near wild animals?
12:14 So it is with someone who keeps company with a sinner / and gets tangled up in his sins.
12:15 He will stay with you for a while, / but if you start to fall, he will not stand by you.
12:16 With his lips the enemy makes his words sweet, / while in his heart he plots to tip you into a pit. / With his eyes the enemy sheds tears, / but give him an opening and no amount of blood will satisfy him.
12:17 If trouble comes your way, you will find him there ahead of you, / and while pretending to help, he will trip you by the heel.
12:18 He will shake his head and clap his hands, / whisper a great deal and change his expression.
