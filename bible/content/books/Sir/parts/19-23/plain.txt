## Drink, women and loose talk
19:1 A worker who gets drunk will never grow rich; / whoever looks down on small things will fall little by little.
19:2 Wine and women make men of understanding fall away, / and the man who joins himself to prostitutes grows more reckless.
19:3 Rot and worms will inherit him, / and a reckless soul will be taken away.
19:4 Whoever trusts too quickly is shallow, / and someone who sins does wrong to his own soul.
19:5 Whoever delights in wickedness will be condemned,
19:6 and the one who hates idle talk will meet less evil.
19:7 Never pass on what you hear, / and you will lose nothing by it.
19:8 Do not tell it to friend or enemy, / and unless keeping quiet would be a sin for you, do not uncover it.
19:9 Someone has heard you and is on guard against you, / and when the time comes he will hate you.
19:10 Have you heard something? Let it die with you. / Take heart: it will not make you burst.
19:11 A fool who hears a word goes into labor with it, / like a woman in labor with a baby.
19:12 Like an arrow stuck in the flesh of the thigh / is a word in a fool's belly.
## Question your friend
19:13 Question your friend: perhaps he did not do it, / and if he did do something, then he will not do it again.
19:14 Question your friend: perhaps he did not say it, / and if he did say it, then he will not say it twice.
19:15 Question a friend, for often it is slander, / and do not believe every word.
19:16 A person may slip without meaning it. / And who has never sinned with his tongue?
19:17 Question your neighbor before you threaten him, / and make room for the law of the Most High.
## True wisdom and false cleverness
19:18 [The fear of the Lord is the beginning of acceptance, / and wisdom wins love from him.]
19:19 [Knowing the Lord's commandments is discipline for life, / and those who do what pleases him pick fruit from the tree of immortality.]
19:20 All wisdom is the fear of the Lord, / and all wisdom includes doing what the law says.
19:21 [A slave who says to his master, 'I will not do what you want,' / angers the one who feeds him, even if he does it afterward.]
19:22 Knowing how to do evil is not wisdom, / and there is no good sense anywhere in the advice of sinners.
19:23 There is wickedness, and it is abhorrent, / and there is a fool who is only short of wisdom.
19:24 Better to fall short in understanding and fear God / than to be rich in good sense and break the law.
19:25 There is a shrewdness that is exact, yet unjust, / and there is one who twists things to bring out a verdict.
19:26 There is a scoundrel who goes about bowed low in black, / but inside he is full of deceit.
19:27 He hangs his head and acts as if he were deaf, / but where no one knows him, he will get the better of you first.
19:28 And if weakness keeps him from sinning, / he will do harm when he finds his chance.
19:29 A man is known by how he looks, / and a thoughtful person is recognized when you meet him face to face.
19:30 The clothes a man wears, a grin full of teeth / and the way a person walks report what he is like.
## When to speak and when to keep quiet
20:1 There is a rebuke that comes at the wrong time, / and there is someone who keeps quiet, and he is the sensible one.
20:2 How much better it is to rebuke than to stay angry! / The one who admits his fault will be kept from loss.
20:3 [How good it is, when you are rebuked, to show that you repent, / for in this way you will escape deliberate sin.]
20:4 Like a eunuch's craving to take a young woman's virginity / is a man who tries to bring about justice by force.
20:5 One person keeps quiet and turns out to be wise; / another is hated for all his chatter.
20:6 One keeps quiet because he has no answer; / another keeps quiet because he knows the right time.
20:7 A wise person will be silent until the right time, / but a braggart and a fool will run past the moment.
20:8 Whoever talks too much will be abhorred, / and whoever lords it over others will be hated.
## Gains that are losses
20:9 A man may find success in hard times, / and a lucky find may turn into a loss.
20:10 There is a gift that will do you no good, / and there is a gift that is repaid twice over.
20:11 There is a loss that comes because of glory, / and there is one who has lifted his head up from a lowly place.
20:12 There is one who buys much for little, / then pays for it seven times over.
20:13 The wise make themselves loved by what they say, / but the charms of fools will be poured out.
20:14 A fool's gift will do you no good, / for his eyes are many instead of one.
20:15 He gives little and taunts you a lot; / he opens his mouth like a town crier. / Today he lends, and tomorrow he wants it back: / such a person is hateful.
20:16 A fool will say, 'I have no friend, / and no one thanks me for my good deeds. / Those who eat my bread have nasty tongues.'
20:17 How often, and how many people, will laugh at him?
## Slips of the tongue
20:18 A slip on the floor is better than a slip of the tongue; / in the same way the downfall of evil people will come swiftly.
20:19 A man without charm is a tale told at the wrong time; / it will always be in the mouths of the undisciplined.
20:20 A proverb is rejected when it comes from a fool's mouth, / for he never says it at the right time.
20:21 One person is kept from sinning by being poor, / and when he lies down to rest, no guilt will sting him.
20:22 One person throws his life away out of shame, / and loses it in front of a foolish face.
20:23 One person, out of shame, makes promises to a friend, / and so has gained him as an enemy for nothing.
20:24 A lie is an ugly stain on a person; / it will always be in the mouths of the undisciplined.
20:25 A thief is preferable to a habitual liar, / but ruin is what both of them will inherit.
20:26 The way of life of a liar brings dishonor, / and his shame stays with him always.
## Sayings in proverbs
20:27 The wise will move ahead by what they say, / and a sensible person will please the great.
20:28 Whoever works the land will pile his heap of grain high, / and whoever pleases the great will atone for a wrong.
20:29 Guest gifts and presents blind the eyes of the wise; / like a muzzle on the mouth, they turn rebukes aside.
20:30 Wisdom that is hidden and treasure that no one can see: / what good is either of them?
20:31 Better a person who hides his folly / than a person who hides his wisdom.
## Run from sin
21:1 My child, have you sinned? Do not do it any more, / and pray about your past sins.
21:2 Run from sin as you would from a snake: / if you go near it, it will bite you. / Its teeth are a lion's teeth, / and they take human lives.
21:3 All lawlessness is like a two-edged sword; / the wound it makes has no cure.
21:4 Terror and violence will lay wealth waste; / so a proud man's house will be laid waste.
21:5 A poor man's prayer goes from his mouth to God's ears, / and the verdict for him comes swiftly.
21:6 Whoever hates correction walks in the sinner's tracks, / but whoever fears the Lord will turn back in his heart.
21:7 A powerful speaker is known far and wide, / but the thoughtful person notices when he slips.
21:8 Whoever builds his house with other people's money / is like a man gathering his stones for winter.
21:9 A gathering of the lawless is a heap of tow, / and their end is a blazing fire.
21:10 The road of sinners is made smooth and clear of stones, / but at its end is the pit of Hades.
## The wise and the fool
21:11 Whoever keeps the law masters his own thoughts, / and wisdom is the fear of the Lord brought to completion.
21:12 Someone who has no shrewdness cannot be disciplined, / but there is a shrewdness that piles up bitterness.
21:13 A wise man's knowledge will swell like a flood, / and his advice is like a spring of life.
21:14 A fool's insides are like a broken jar: / he will hold no knowledge at all.
21:15 When a man of knowledge hears a wise saying, / he praises it and adds to it. / When the pleasure-lover hears it, he dislikes it / and throws it behind his back.
21:16 A fool's storytelling is like a load to carry on a journey, / but charm is found on the lips of a person of understanding.
21:17 In the assembly people look for the sensible man to speak, / and they think over his words in their hearts.
21:18 To a fool, wisdom is like a house in ruins, / and the knowledge of a person without understanding is talk that has never been tested.
21:19 To the thoughtless, discipline is shackles on their feet, / like handcuffs on the right hand.
21:20 A fool raises his voice when he laughs, / but a shrewd man will barely smile, and quietly.
21:21 To a sensible person, discipline is a gold ornament, / like a bracelet on the right arm.
21:22 A fool's foot is quick to go into a house, / but a man of wide experience is shy in front of people.
21:23 A fool peeks into a house from the door, / but a disciplined man will stand outside.
21:24 Listening at the door shows a person's lack of discipline; / the sensible would find the disgrace too heavy to bear.
21:25 Strangers' lips grow heavy with talk like this, / but the words of the sensible are weighed on a scale.
21:26 Fools have their heart in their mouth, / but the heart of the wise is their mouth.
21:27 When an ungodly person curses Satan, / he curses his own soul.
21:28 A whisperer stains his own soul, / and he will be hated in his neighborhood.
## The lazy one and the undisciplined child
22:1 The lazy one is like a stone smeared with filth, / and everyone hisses at his disgrace.
22:2 The lazy one is like a lump of dung from the manure pile: / anyone who picks it up shakes it off his hand.
22:3 It is a father's shame to have fathered an undisciplined son, / and a daughter is born to his loss.
22:4 A sensible daughter will gain a husband of her own, / but one who brings shame is a grief to her father.
22:5 A brazen daughter shames both father and husband, / and both of them will treat her with contempt.
22:6 Talk at the wrong time is like music in a time of mourning, / but whippings and discipline are wise at every time.
22:7 Teaching a fool is like gluing broken pottery together, / like waking a sleeper out of deep sleep.
22:8 Whoever explains something to a fool is talking to someone half asleep; / when it is over, he will say, 'What was that?'
22:9 [Children who live good lives and have enough to live on / will cover up their parents' humble birth.]
22:10 [Children who swagger in scorn and lack of discipline / soil the noble birth of their own family.]
## Weep for the fool
22:11 Weep for the dead, for he has lost the light; / weep for a fool, for he has lost understanding. / Weep more gently for the dead, for he has found rest, / but a fool's life is worse than death.
22:12 Mourning for the dead lasts seven days, / but for a fool and an ungodly man, all the days of his life.
22:13 Do not talk at length with a fool, / and do not go off to visit a person without understanding. / Guard yourself against him, so that you will have no trouble / and will not be spattered when he shakes himself. / Turn away from him and you will find rest, / and his madness will not wear you out.
22:14 What is heavier than lead? / And what is its name but 'fool'?
22:15 Sand and salt and a lump of iron / are easier to carry than a person without understanding.
22:16 A wooden tie-beam built into a house / will not come loose in an earthquake; / so a heart made firm by well-thought-out counsel / will not lose courage when the moment comes.
22:17 A heart set firm on thoughtful understanding / is like the sanded plaster finish on a smooth wall.
22:18 Stakes set up on a high place / will not hold out against the wind; / so a timid heart that rests on a fool's way of thinking / will not hold out against any fear.
## Keeping a friend
22:19 Whoever pokes an eye will make tears fall, / and whoever pricks a heart makes its feelings show.
22:20 Throw a stone at birds and you scare them away, / and whoever taunts a friend will break up the friendship.
22:21 If you draw a sword against a friend, / do not give up hope, for there can be a way back.
22:22 If you open your mouth against a friend, / do not be afraid, for you can make peace again; / except for taunting and arrogance, / telling a secret and a treacherous blow: / at these, any friend will run away.
22:23 Earn your neighbor's trust while he is poor, / so that when good things come to him, you may have your fill with him. / Stay by him in his time of trouble, / so that you may share in his inheritance with him.
22:24 Before the fire come the steam and smoke of the furnace; / so insults come before bloodshed.
22:25 I will not be ashamed to shelter a friend, / and I will not hide from him.
22:26 And if harm comes to me because of him, / everyone who hears of it will be on guard against him.
## A prayer for guarded lips
22:27 Who will put a guard over my mouth / and a shrewd seal on my lips, / so that I will not fall by it, / and my tongue will not destroy me?
23:1 Lord, Father and Master of my life, / do not abandon me to what they plan; / do not let me fall because of them.
23:2 Who will set whips over my thoughts / and the discipline of wisdom over my heart, / so that they will not spare me for the wrongs I do without knowing / or let the sins of my mind slip by?
23:3 Then my wrongs done in ignorance will not multiply / and my sins will not pile up, / and I will not fall before my opponents / while my enemy gloats over me.
23:4 Lord, Father and God of my life, / do not give me haughty eyes,
23:5 and turn desire away from me.
23:6 Do not let the craving of the belly or lust for sex take hold of me, / and do not hand me over to a shameless soul.
## Discipline for the mouth
23:7 Listen, children, to discipline for the mouth: / whoever keeps it will never be caught. / By his own lips
23:8 the sinner will be left stranded; / the abusive and the arrogant will trip over them.
23:9 Do not get your mouth used to swearing oaths, / and do not make a habit of naming the Holy One.
23:10 For a slave who is questioned under the whip again and again / will never be without bruises, / and so whoever swears and names the Name all the time / will never be clean of sin.
23:11 A man who swears many oaths will be filled with lawlessness, / and the whip will not leave his house. / If he fails, his sin is on him, / and if he takes it lightly, he has sinned twice. / If he swore for nothing, he will not be found in the right, / for his house will be filled with troubles.
23:12 There is a way of speaking that is wrapped in death: / may it never be found among Jacob's heritage! / All these things will be far from the godly, / and they will not wallow in sins.
23:13 Do not get your mouth used to coarse, undisciplined talk, / for there is sinful speech in it.
23:14 Remember your father and your mother / when you sit in council among the great. / Otherwise you may forget yourself in front of them / and make a fool of yourself by your habits; / then you will wish you had never been born, / and you will curse the day of your birth.
23:15 A person who gets used to taunting words / will never become disciplined as long as he lives.
## Sexual sin and adultery
23:16 Two kinds of people pile up sins, / and the third brings on anger. / A hot soul is like a blazing fire: / it will not go out until it has burned itself up. / A man who commits sexual sin in the body of his own flesh / will not stop until he has set a fire blazing.
23:17 To a man given to sexual sin, all bread tastes sweet; / he will not let up until he dies.
23:18 A man strays from his own bed / and says to himself, 'Who sees me? / Darkness is all around me, and the walls hide me, / and no one sees me. What do I have to fear? / The Most High will not remember my sins.'
23:19 Human eyes are all he fears. / He does not know that the eyes of the Lord / are ten thousand times brighter than the sun. / They look upon all the ways of people / and see into hidden places.
23:20 Before all things were created, they were known to him, / and so they are after they have been completed.
23:21 This man will be punished in the city squares, / and he will be caught where he least expected it.
23:22 So it is also with a wife who leaves her husband / and gives him an heir by another man.
23:23 First, she has disobeyed the law of the Most High; / second, she has wronged her own husband; / and third, she has committed adultery through sexual sin / and borne children by another man.
23:24 She will be brought out before the assembly, / and there will be a reckoning for her children.
23:25 Her children will not spread out roots, / and her branches will bear no fruit.
23:26 She will leave behind a memory that is cursed, / and her disgrace will not be rubbed out.
23:27 Those who are left behind will learn / that nothing is better than the fear of the Lord / and nothing is sweeter than paying attention to the Lord's commandments.
