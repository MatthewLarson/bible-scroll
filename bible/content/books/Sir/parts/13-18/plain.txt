## Keep Your Distance from the Rich
13:1 Whoever touches pitch will get it on him, / and whoever keeps company with a proud man will end up like him.
13:2 Do not lift a load that is beyond you, / and do not go into partnership with someone stronger and richer than you. / What do a cooking pot and a metal cauldron have in common? / One will bang against the other, and the other will break.
13:3 A rich man does wrong, and then snorts with anger besides; / a poor man is wronged, and then has to plead for pardon besides.
13:4 If you are useful to him, he will put you to work; / if you come up short, he will leave you.
13:5 If you have something, he will make himself at home with you; / he will empty you out and feel no pain over it.
13:6 He has need of you, so he will lead you on. / He will smile at you and give you hope; / he will say nice things to you / and ask, "Is there anything you need?"
13:7 He will shame you with his fine food, / until he has emptied you twice or three times over, / and at the last he will make fun of you. / After that he will see you and leave you, / and wag his head at you.
13:8 Watch out that you are not fooled / and brought low in your merrymaking.
13:9 When a powerful man invites you, hold back, / and he will invite you all the more.
13:10 Do not crowd in, or you will be pushed away; / but do not stay far off, so that you are not forgotten.
13:11 Do not presume to talk with him as an equal, / and do not believe his many words. / With much talk he will test you, / and smiling all the while, he will size you up.
13:12 The man who does not keep your words to himself has no mercy; / he will not stop short of harming you or putting you in chains.
13:13 Guard yourself and take great care, / for your fall walks right beside you.
13:14 [Love the Lord all your life, / and call on him to save you.]
## Like Keeps to Like
13:15 Every living thing loves what is like it, / and every human the one near him.
13:16 All flesh gathers by kind, / and a man will cling to one like himself.
13:17 What can a wolf share with a lamb? / That is how a sinner stands with a godly man.
13:18 What peace can a hyena have with a dog? / And what peace can a rich man have with a needy one?
13:19 Wild donkeys in the desert are what lions hunt; / so the poor are pasture for the rich.
13:20 Humility is abhorrent to the proud; / so a poor man is abhorrent to the rich.
## How the World Treats Rich and Poor
13:21 A rich man who wobbles is propped up by friends, / but a lowly man who falls is shoved further away by friends.
13:22 When a rich man slips, many come to his aid; / he says things no one should say, and they declare him in the right. / A lowly man slips, and they scold him on top of it; / he speaks good sense, and no one gives him room.
13:23 The rich man speaks, and everyone goes quiet; / they lift his words as high as the clouds. / The poor man speaks, and they say, "Who is this?" / And if he trips, they help knock him over.
13:24 Wealth is good if there is no sin in it, / and poverty is evil in the mouth of the ungodly.
13:25 A person's heart changes the look of his face, / whether for good or for bad.
13:26 A cheerful face is the track a heart leaves when things are good, / but working out proverbs takes hard thinking.
## The Miser and the Good Day
14:1 Happy is the man who has not slipped with his mouth / and has not been stung with grief over sin.
14:2 Happy is the one whose own soul does not condemn him, / who has not fallen away from his hope.
14:3 Wealth does not suit a stingy man, / and what use is money to a grudging man?
14:4 Whoever hoards by denying himself hoards for others, / and others will feast on his goods.
14:5 A man who is hard on himself, who will he be kind to? / He takes no joy in what he owns.
14:6 There is no one more wicked than the man who grudges himself, / and this is what his wickedness earns him.
14:7 If he ever does something good, it happens when he forgets himself, / and at last he lets his wickedness show.
14:8 The man with the grudging eye is evil; / he turns his face away and looks past people.
14:9 A greedy man's eye is never filled by his share, / and evil wrongdoing dries up the soul.
14:10 An evil eye begrudges bread, / and he goes short at his own table.
14:11 My child, as much as you have, do yourself good, / and bring the Lord the offerings he deserves.
14:12 Remember that death will not be slow in coming, / and no one has shown you the covenant of Hades.
14:13 Before you die, be good to a friend; / reach out as far as your strength goes, and give to him.
14:14 Do not miss out on a good day, / and do not let a good thing you long for get past you.
14:15 Are you not going to leave your labors to someone else, / and your hard work to be split up by lot?
14:16 Give and take, and treat yourself, / for no one goes looking for luxury in Hades.
14:17 All flesh wears out like a garment, / for the covenant of ages past says, "You will die, yes die."
14:18 Like leaves thriving on a thick tree, / where some drop off and others grow, / that is how it is with generations of flesh and blood: / one comes to its end, and another is born.
14:19 Every work rots and fails, / and the one who made it goes along with it.
## The Man Who Courts Wisdom
14:20 Happy is the man whose thoughts dwell on Wisdom, / who reasons things out with his understanding,
14:21 who turns her ways over in his heart / and comes to understand her secrets.
14:22 Go out after her like a tracker, / and lie in wait at her entrances.
14:23 The one who stoops to look through her windows / will listen at her doorways.
14:24 The one who stops for the night near her house / will drive a peg into her walls.
14:25 He will set up his tent at her side / and stay in a lodging full of good things.
14:26 He will put his children in her shade / and camp under her branches.
14:27 Under her cover he will be safe from the heat, / and he will rest in her glory.
15:1 Whoever fears the Lord will do this, / and whoever has a firm hold on the law will win her.
15:2 She will come out to meet him like a mother, / and like the woman he married when he was young, she will take him in.
15:3 She will feed him bread of understanding, / and with wisdom's water she will quench his thirst.
15:4 He will be braced against her and will not bend; / he will cling to her and never be disgraced.
15:5 She will lift him higher than his neighbors, / and when the assembly gathers she will open his mouth.
15:6 Joy, and a crown of gladness, / and a name for all time will be his to inherit.
15:7 People without understanding will never lay hold of her, / and sinful men will never see her.
15:8 She keeps far off from pride, / and men who lie will never remember her.
## No One Can Blame God
15:9 Praise does not look right in a sinner's mouth, / because the Lord did not send it to him.
15:10 Praise is to be spoken with wisdom, / and the Lord will guide it on its way.
15:11 Do not say, "The Lord is the reason I turned away," / for what he hates you are not to do.
15:12 Do not say, "He himself sent me off course," / for he has no need of a man who sins.
15:13 The Lord hates everything abhorrent, / and those who fear him do not hold it dear.
15:14 He himself made the human being at the start / and left him in the hand of his own choice.
15:15 If you are willing, you will keep the commandments, / and keeping faith is for you to choose.
15:16 He has set fire and water before you; / put out your hand to whichever one you wish.
15:17 Life and death lie in front of people, / and what each one is pleased to choose will be given to him.
15:18 The Lord's wisdom is vast; / he is mighty in his rule, and his eyes take in everything.
15:19 His eyes are on those who fear him, / and he takes note of every work of man.
15:20 He gave no one an order to be ungodly, / and no one has his leave to sin.
## Better No Children Than Ungodly Ones
16:1 Do not set your heart on many children who are no good, / and do not be glad about sons who are ungodly.
16:2 Even if they grow in number, take no joy in them, / if the fear of the Lord is not with them.
16:3 Do not put your trust in their life, / and do not count on their place. / For one is worth more than a thousand, / and it is better to die with no children than to have ungodly ones.
16:4 One man with understanding can fill a city with people, / but a tribe of lawbreakers will become a wasteland.
16:5 My eyes have seen many things like this, / and my ear has heard of mightier ones.
## God Spared No One in the Past
16:6 Fire will be lit among a crowd of sinners, / and anger has burned against a nation that would not obey.
16:7 He did not forgive the giants of long ago, / who broke away from him in their strength.
16:8 He did not spare the place where Lot lived as a stranger, / the people he abhorred because of their pride.
16:9 He showed no pity to the nation marked for destruction, / who were rooted out in their sins,
16:10 and so it went with the six hundred thousand men on foot / who banded together with hard hearts.
16:11 Even if just one person is stiff-necked, / it would be amazing if he went free. / For mercy and anger both come from him: / he is a mighty lord of forgiveness, and he pours out anger.
16:12 His correction is as large as his mercy; / he will judge a man according to his works.
16:13 The sinner will not get away with what he has seized, / and he will not keep the patient godly man waiting in vain.
16:14 He will make room for every act of almsgiving; / each one will find according to his works.
16:15 [The Lord hardened Pharaoh so that he would not know him, / so that his works would become known to the world under heaven.]
16:16 [His mercy is plain to all creation, / and he divided his light and the darkness with adamant.]
## Do Not Say, "God Will Not Notice Me"
16:17 Do not say, "I can hide from the Lord. / Up there on high, who will remember me? / In such a crowd of people I will not be remembered, / for what is my one soul in a creation too great to measure?"
16:18 Look: heaven, and the heavens above the heavens, which are God's, / the deep and the earth will shake when he visits them.
16:19 The mountains and the earth's foundations together / shudder with trembling when he turns his gaze on them.
16:20 Yet no one's heart gives a thought to these things, / and who stops to ponder his ways?
16:21 There is a storm no human eye will see, / and most of his works are out of sight.
16:22 Who will report the righteous things he does? / Who will hold out for them? The covenant is a long way off.
16:23 A person short on sense thinks like this; / a fool who has lost his way thinks foolish thoughts.
## The Order of Creation
16:24 Hear me, my child, and learn true knowledge, / and set your heart on my words.
16:25 I will set out instruction by careful weight / and report knowledge exactly.
16:26 By the Lord's judgment his works have stood from the beginning, / and from the time he made them he set apart their portions.
16:27 He put his works in order for all time, / and gave them their rule for all their generations. / They never went hungry or wore out, / and they never fell away from their work.
16:28 Not one of them pressed hard on its neighbor, / and to the end of time they will not refuse his word.
16:29 After that, the Lord turned his eyes to the earth / and made it full of his good things.
16:30 He covered its face with every creature that has the breath of life, / and to the earth they go back.
## Humans Made from the Earth
17:1 The Lord created a human being out of earth / and turned him back into it again.
17:2 He gave them days that are counted and a fixed time, / and he gave them power over what is on the earth.
17:3 He clothed them with strength fit for them / and made them in his image.
17:4 He put the fear of them on all flesh, / so that they would be masters over beasts and birds.
17:5 [He gave them a mind as a sixth gift, sharing it out to them, / and as the seventh, speech, the interpreter of his workings.]
17:6 He gave them choice, a tongue and eyes, / ears, and a heart to think with.
17:7 He filled them with knowledge that understands / and pointed out to them what is good and what is evil.
17:8 He fixed his eye on their hearts / to show them how great his works are,
17:9 so that they would tell the wonders of his works.
17:10 And they will praise his holy name.
## The Covenant
17:11 He added knowledge to them / and gave them the law of life to inherit.
17:12 He set up a covenant with them that lasts forever / and pointed out his judgments to them.
17:13 Their eyes saw his majestic glory, / and their ears heard the glory of his voice.
17:14 He told them, "Watch out for anything unjust," / and he gave each one orders about his neighbor.
17:15 Their ways lie in front of him at all times; / nothing of them is hidden from his eyes.
17:16 [Every person from youth up is bent on evil, / and they were not able to make their hearts flesh in place of stone.]
17:17 For each nation he appointed a ruler, / but Israel is the Lord's share.
17:18 [He brings him up as his firstborn with discipline, / and he gives him a share of the light of love and does not let him go.]
17:19 Everything they do is like the sun in front of him, / and his eyes stay fixed on their ways without a break.
17:20 Not one of their wrongdoings was hidden from him; / every sin of theirs lies open before the Lord.
17:21 [But the Lord, being kind and knowing what he had shaped, / neither let them go nor abandoned them, but spared them.]
17:22 A man's almsgiving is with him like a seal ring, / and he will guard a person's kindness like the pupil of the eye.
17:23 After that he will rise and repay them; / he will bring down on their own heads what they have earned.
17:24 But for those who repent he has opened a road back, / and he has cheered on those whose endurance was running out.
## Turn Back to the Lord
17:25 Turn back to the Lord and leave your sins behind; / make your prayer before his face and cut down your stumbling.
17:26 Go back to the Most High and turn from what is unjust, / and hate the abhorrent thing fiercely.
17:27 In Hades, who will praise the Most High / instead of the living, who give him thanks?
17:28 Thanks dies away from a dead man as from someone who is not there; / it is the living and healthy who will praise the Lord.
17:29 The Lord's mercy is so great, / and so is his forgiveness for those who turn back to him!
17:30 For not everything can be in human beings, / since a son of man is not immortal.
17:31 What is brighter than the sun? Yet even it fails, / and an evil man sets his mind on flesh and blood.
17:32 He keeps watch over the army of heaven's heights, / but human beings, all of them, are earth and ashes.
## The Lord Who Lives Forever
18:1 The one who lives forever created all things together.
18:2 The Lord alone will be proven right.
18:3 [He steers the world with the span of his hand, / and all things obey his will, / for by his might he is King of all, / setting apart among them what is holy from what is common.]
18:4 He has allowed no one to tell his works in full, / and who will track down all his great deeds?
18:5 Who will number the might of his greatness? / Who could go on to tell of all his mercies?
18:6 There is no taking away and no adding, / and no tracking down the wonders of the Lord.
18:7 A person comes to the end and finds he is only starting, / and when he stops, he will be left at a loss.
18:8 What is a human being, and what is he worth? / What is the good he has, and the harm he does?
18:9 A person's days add up to a hundred years, if they are many.
18:10 Like a drop of water taken from the sea, like one grain of sand, / that is how few the years are within the day of eternity.
18:11 So the Lord bears with them a long time / and pours his mercy out on them.
18:12 He saw and understood that their end is evil, / and for that reason he gave his forgiveness in full measure.
18:13 Human mercy reaches one's neighbor, / but the Lord's mercy reaches all flesh. / He reproves, disciplines and teaches, / and turns them around like a shepherd with his flock.
18:14 He shows mercy to those who welcome discipline / and to those who hurry toward his judgments.
## How to Give
18:15 My child, do not put a stain on your good deeds, / and do not let any gift come with words that hurt.
18:16 Won't the dew bring relief from the burning heat? / So a word is worth more than a gift.
18:17 See, isn't a word worth more than a fine gift? / And a gracious man has both to give.
18:18 A fool throws insults and shows no grace, / and a present from a grudging man wears out the eyes.
## Before Trouble Comes
18:19 Study before you talk, / and see to your health before sickness comes.
18:20 Look into yourself ahead of the judgment, / and when the hour of visitation comes you will find forgiveness.
18:21 Make yourself low before illness comes, / and in the time of your sins let your turning back be seen.
18:22 Do not let anything keep you from paying a vow when it is due, / and do not wait until death to be justified.
18:23 Get yourself ready before you make a vow, / and do not act like a man who puts the Lord to the test.
18:24 Keep in mind the anger at the end of your days, / and the time of punishment when he turns his face away.
18:25 Remember the season of famine while you have your fill, / and poverty and want while you are rich.
18:26 Between dawn and dusk the season turns, / and all things are swift in the Lord's sight.
18:27 A wise man treats everything with care, / and in days of sinning he keeps clear of guilty acts.
18:28 Everyone with understanding recognizes wisdom, / and he will honor the man who has found her.
18:29 People who understand sayings grew wise themselves / and showered down exact proverbs.
## Self-Control
18:30 Do not walk behind your desires, / and keep yourself back from your appetites.
18:31 If you hand your soul whatever desire it delights in, / it will make you something your enemies gloat over.
18:32 Do not be glad over plenty of luxury, / and do not tie yourself to paying for it.
18:33 Do not end up poor by throwing in for banquets with loans, / while there is nothing in your money bag.
