## The Praise of the Fathers
44:1 A Hymn of the Fathers. / Let us praise men of glory now, / the fathers we were born from.
44:2 The Lord created great glory, / his majesty from ages past.
44:3 Some ruled in their kingdoms / and were men famous for their strength. / They gave advice by their understanding / and spoke out in prophecies.
44:4 They guided the people with their plans / and with their grasp of the people's writings; / their teaching was full of wise words.
44:5 Some searched out musical tunes / and set down poems in writing.
44:6 Some were rich men, well supplied with strength, / living at peace in the places where they settled.
44:7 Every one of these won honor in his own generation, / and in his days he was something to boast of.
44:8 Some of them left a name, / and their praises are still told.
44:9 And there are some who are not remembered at all. / They are gone as if they had never been. / They have become like the unborn, / and their children after them the same.
44:10 But these were men of mercy, / and their right deeds are not forgotten.
44:11 A good inheritance will remain with their offspring. / Their descendants live within the covenants.
44:12 Their offspring stood firm, / and so did their children, for their sake.
44:13 Their offspring will last for all time, / and nothing will wipe out their glory.
44:14 In peace they were buried, / and their name lives for generations.
44:15 Peoples will tell of their wisdom, / and the assembly will proclaim their praise.
## Enoch, Noah and Abraham
44:16 Enoch pleased the Lord and was carried off, / a pattern of repentance for the generations.
44:17 Noah was found blameless and righteous. / In the time of anger he became the exchange. / So a remnant was left for the earth, / and so the flood came.
44:18 With him covenants were made that last forever, / that never again would a flood wipe out all flesh.
44:19 Great was Abraham, father of a multitude of nations; / in glory no one was ever found his equal.
44:20 He guarded the law of the Most High, / and God took him into a covenant. / In his own flesh he set up the covenant, / and under testing he was found faithful.
44:21 That is why he bound himself to him with an oath: / in his offspring the nations would find blessing; / he would multiply him like the dust of the ground / and lift his offspring up like the stars, / and they would inherit from sea to sea, / from the River to the ends of the earth.
44:22 With Isaac too he did the same, for the sake of Abraham his father: / he made sure the blessing of all people and the covenant,
44:23 and he made them rest on the head of Jacob. / He recognized him with his blessings / and gave him the land as an inheritance. / He marked out his shares / and shared them out to twelve tribes. / From him he brought out a man of mercy, / who found favor in the eyes of all flesh,
## Moses and Aaron
45:1 Moses, loved by God and by people, / whose memory is spoken with blessings.
45:2 He let him share the glory of the holy ones / and raised him high, so that enemies feared him.
45:3 By his words he made the signs stop. / He honored him in the presence of kings. / He sent him with orders to his people / and let him see some of his glory.
45:4 He made him holy because he was faithful and humble; / out of all flesh he chose him.
45:5 He made him listen to his voice / and brought him into the deep darkness. / Face to face he handed him commandments, / the law of life and knowledge, / to teach Jacob the covenant / and Israel his judgments.
45:6 He lifted Aaron high, holy just as Moses was, / his brother from the tribe of Levi.
45:7 He made him a covenant that lasts forever / and put the people's priesthood into his hands. / He made him blessed in splendid dress / and belted a glorious robe around him.
45:8 He dressed him in the fullness of splendor / and made him strong with garments of might: / the undershorts, the long robe and the ephod.
45:9 He put pomegranates all around him, / and very many gold bells on every side, / to ring out as he walked, / so the temple would hear their chime, / a reminder for the children of his people.
45:10 He gave him a holy garment of gold, violet and purple, / an embroiderer's work, / with the breastpiece for decisions and the tokens of truth,
45:11 of twisted scarlet, a craftsman's work, / with precious stones cut like seals / and set in gold by a stonecutter, / to be a reminder, with writing engraved, / one for every tribe of Israel by number.
45:12 A gold crown was on his turban, / engraved like a holy seal, / a proud honor, a mighty work, / delights for the eyes, adorned and lovely.
45:13 Before him there had never been anything like them. / Never in all time has an outsider worn them, / only his own sons, / and his descendants always.
45:14 His sacrifices are burned whole / every day, twice a day without fail.
45:15 Moses installed him as priest / and poured the holy oil on him. / This was a covenant for all time to him, / and to his offspring for as long as there is a sky: / to serve God and act as priest together, / and to bless his people in the name.
45:16 From all who live he picked him out / to bring the offering to the Lord, / incense and a soothing smell as a token portion, / to make atonement for your people.
45:17 He gave him power through his commandments, / authority in the covenants of judgment, / to teach Jacob the decrees / and proclaim his law to Israel.
45:18 Outsiders banded together against him / and were jealous of him in the wilderness: / the men of Dathan and Abiram, / and Korah's band, hot with fury and anger.
45:19 The Lord saw, and it displeased him, / and they were wiped out in his furious anger. / He did strange signs to them / and ate them up with the flame of his fire.
45:20 He added to Aaron's glory / and gave him an inheritance. / He shared out to them the firstfruits of the first crops / and gave them bread first of all, in plenty.
45:21 For the Lord's sacrifices are their food; / he gave them to him and to his offspring.
45:22 But he will inherit nothing in the people's land; / no share among the people is his, / for he himself is your share, your inheritance.
## Phinehas
45:23 Third in glory comes Phinehas son of Eleazar, / for his zeal in the fear of the Lord. / When the people turned aside, he took his stand / with the good courage of his heart, / and he made atonement for Israel.
45:24 That is why a covenant of peace was set up for him: / he would be the champion of the holy place and of his people, / and the greatness of the priesthood / would belong to him and his offspring forever.
45:25 And there was a covenant with David, / a son from the tribe of Judah: / the inheritance of a king goes from one son to one son, / and Aaron's inheritance goes to his offspring as well.
45:26 May he put wisdom in your hearts, / so you judge his people rightly, / and their good things never disappear, / nor their glory, generation after generation.
## Joshua and Caleb
46:1 Strong in wars was Joshua son of Nun, / Moses' successor in prophecy. / He lived up to his name, / great in rescuing his chosen ones, / paying back the enemies who rose up, / to put Israel in possession of its inheritance.
46:2 What glory he won when he lifted his hands / and turned his sword against the cities!
46:3 Had anyone before him ever stood so? / The Lord himself led the enemy to him.
46:4 Was it not by his hand that the sun held back, / and a single day stretched to two?
46:5 He cried to the Most High Sovereign / when enemies crowded him on all sides, / and the great Lord heard them / and sent hailstones of crushing force.
46:6 He brought war crashing down on that nation, / and on the way down he destroyed those who resisted, / so the nations would learn their full armor: / that his war was waged before the Lord. / For he kept close behind the Sovereign.
46:7 Back in Moses' time he showed mercy, / he and Caleb son of Jephunneh. / They took a stand against the enemy, / kept the people from sinning / and quieted the evil complaining.
46:8 Of six hundred thousand men on foot, / only these two came through safe, / to bring them into what they would inherit, / a land flowing with milk and honey.
46:9 The Lord gave Caleb strength, / strength that lasted into his old age, / so he could climb to the high places of the land, / and his offspring kept it as their inheritance.
46:10 This was so every Israelite might see / that walking behind the Lord is good.
## The Judges and Samuel
46:11 Then the judges, each by his own name, / all whose hearts did not play the prostitute / and who never turned their backs on the Lord: / may their memory be spoken with blessings!
46:12 May their bones sprout again from their place, / and may their name be handed on / to the sons of these honored men.
46:13 His Lord loved Samuel, / the Lord's prophet, who set up the kingdom / and anointed leaders to rule his people.
46:14 He judged the community by the law of the Lord, / and the Lord came to the help of Jacob.
46:15 His faithfulness showed him to be a true prophet; / his faithfulness made him known as a reliable seer.
46:16 When enemies crowded him on all sides, / he called on the Lord, the Sovereign, / offering up a lamb still nursing.
46:17 The Lord thundered out of heaven; / with a great roar he let his voice be heard,
46:18 and he crushed the leaders of the people of Tyre / and all the rulers of the Philistines.
46:19 Before he lay down in his everlasting sleep, / he swore before the Lord and his anointed: / "Money, even sandals, I have taken from no one." / And not a single person charged him.
46:20 After he had gone to sleep in death, he still prophesied. / He showed the king his end / and lifted his voice from the earth in prophecy / to wipe out the wrongdoing of the people.
## Nathan and David
47:1 Next came Nathan, who rose up / to be a prophet while David lived.
47:2 As the fat is lifted off the peace offering and kept apart, / David was picked out from among the Israelites.
47:3 Lions were his playthings, like young goats, / and bears, like lambs from the flock.
47:4 Was he not only a boy when he killed a giant / and lifted the shame off the people, / when his hand let fly the stone from his sling / and brought Goliath's proud boast down?
47:5 He had called on the Lord Most High, / and the Lord put power in his right hand / to bring down a man of war / and lift up the horn of his people.
47:6 So they honored him over his tens of thousands, / praising him with blessings from the Lord / as the glorious crown was brought to him.
47:7 He crushed the enemies all around him / and made nothing of the Philistines who fought him; / he broke their horn, and it stays broken to this day.
47:8 In everything he did he gave thanks / to the Holy One Most High with glorious words. / With his whole heart he sang hymns / and loved the one who made him.
47:9 He set psalm singers in front of the altar, / and from their voices came sweet tunes.
47:10 He made the feasts beautiful / and set their seasons in order until the cycle was complete, / as the singers praised his holy name / and the sanctuary rang out from dawn.
47:11 His sins the Lord removed, / and he raised his horn high forever. / He gave him a covenant of kings / and a throne of glory in Israel.
## Solomon and the Split Kingdom
47:12 A son full of knowledge followed him / and lived in wide comfort thanks to him.
47:13 Solomon's reign was a time of peace, / for God gave him quiet on every border, / so that he might raise a house for his name / and make ready a sanctuary for all time.
47:14 How wise you grew in your youth, / filled with understanding like a river in flood!
47:15 Your soul covered the earth, / and you filled it with riddling proverbs.
47:16 Your name went as far as the distant islands; / for your peace people loved you.
47:17 For your songs, proverbs, parables / and explanations the lands marveled at you.
47:18 In the name of the Lord God, / who is called the God of Israel, / you heaped up gold as though it were tin / and had as much silver as lead.
47:19 But you gave your loins to women / and let them have power over your body.
47:20 You marred your glory with a flaw / and made your offspring unclean, / bringing wrath on your children; / I was stung with grief at your folly,
47:21 for the power was split in two, / and from Ephraim a rebel kingdom began.
47:22 Yet the Lord will never leave his mercy behind / or let anything he has made be ruined. / He will not erase the children of the one he chose, / nor uproot the line of the man who loved him. / To Jacob he left a remnant, / and to David a root that came from him.
47:23 Solomon lay down to rest with his fathers, / and after him he left, from his own offspring, / the folly of the people, a man short of sense: / Rehoboam, whose advice drove the people away. / And Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin / and gave Ephraim a road of sin.
47:24 Their sins grew very many, / until they were driven out of their land.
47:25 They hunted for every evil / till punishment fell on them.
## Elijah and Elisha
48:1 Then Elijah the prophet arose like fire, / and his word was blazing like a torch.
48:2 He sent hunger on them, / and his zeal cut their numbers down.
48:3 At the Lord's word he closed the sky, / and in the same way called down fire three times.
48:4 Elijah, what glory your wonders won you! / Who else could boast of being your equal?
48:5 By the word of the Most High you woke a dead man / out of death, out of Hades.
48:6 You toppled kings into ruin / and brought the great down from their beds.
48:7 At Sinai you heard a rebuke, / and at Horeb judgments of punishment.
48:8 You anointed kings to repay wrongs / and prophets to come after him as his successors.
48:9 A whirlwind of fire carried you up, / a chariot drawn by fiery horses.
48:10 You were written down in warnings for the times to come, / to still anger before it turns to rage, / to turn the heart of the father back to the son, / and to set the tribes of Jacob on their feet.
48:11 Happy those who saw you / and those made beautiful by love! / For we too will live a true life.
48:12 Elijah was wrapped from sight in the whirlwind, / and his spirit filled Elisha. / As long as he lived, no ruler made him tremble, / and no one ever mastered him.
48:13 No task was beyond him, / and even asleep in death his body prophesied.
48:14 While he lived, he did wonders, / and when he died, his works were a marvel.
48:15 Even so, the people would not repent / or turn from their sins, / till they were dragged away as plunder from their country / and spread out across the whole earth. / Only a small people remained, / and a ruler for David's house.
48:16 Some of them did what was pleasing; / others heaped up sins.
## Hezekiah and Isaiah
48:17 Hezekiah fortified his city / and led water inside it. / With iron he cut through solid rock / and built pools to hold the water.
48:18 In his days Sennacherib came up / and sent the Rabshakeh, and then he set out. / He raised his hand against Zion / and boasted loudly in his pride.
48:19 Then hearts and hands shook, / and they writhed like women giving birth.
48:20 They cried out to the merciful Lord / with their hands stretched toward him, / and from heaven the Holy One answered them fast / and set them free through Isaiah's hand.
48:21 He struck down the Assyrian camp, / and his angel destroyed them.
48:22 For Hezekiah did what the Lord liked / and stayed strong in the ways of David his father, / the ways Isaiah the prophet had laid on him, / that great prophet whose vision could be trusted.
48:23 In his time the sun stepped back, / and he added to the king's life.
48:24 By a great spirit he saw the last things / and comforted those who mourned in Zion.
48:25 He showed what was going to be, all the way to eternity, / and secrets before they arrived.
## Josiah and the Last Kings
49:1 Josiah's memory is a mix of incense / made up by a perfumer's craft. / It tastes like honey in every mouth, / like songs at a feast with wine.
49:2 He went straight ahead in turning the people back / and took away the abhorrent things of lawlessness.
49:3 He set his heart straight toward the Lord; / in the days of lawless men he made godliness strong.
49:4 Apart from David, Hezekiah and Josiah, / every one of them went badly wrong. / They left the law of the Most High behind, / and Judah's kings died out.
49:5 They gave their horn to others / and handed their glory to a nation of strangers.
49:6 That nation burned the chosen city where the sanctuary stood / and emptied its streets, because of Jeremiah.
49:7 They had done him harm, / though in the womb he was made holy as a prophet / to pull up, to harm and to destroy, / and in the same way to build and to plant.
## The Prophets and the Return
49:8 Ezekiel was shown a vision of glory / on the chariot of the cherubim.
49:9 For he remembered the enemies with a downpour / and did good to those who walk straight paths.
49:10 And may the bones of the Twelve Prophets / sprout again from their place. / He comforted Jacob / and rescued them with a hope they could trust.
49:11 How can we praise Zerubbabel enough? / He was like a seal ring on the right hand.
49:12 Jeshua son of Jozadak was the same. / In their time the two of them built the house / and raised up a people holy to the Lord, / made ready for everlasting glory.
49:13 Long remembered too is Nehemiah, / who lifted our fallen walls back up, / put gates and bars in place / and built up our houses again.
## Back to the Beginning
49:14 No one like Enoch was ever made on earth, / for he was the one lifted up from the earth.
49:15 Joseph was born a man, / a leader of his brothers and the support of his people, / and his bones were watched over.
49:16 Among humans Shem and Seth won honor, / but above every living thing in creation is Adam.
## Simon the High Priest
50:1 Simon son of Onias, the high priest, / mended the house while he lived / and in his days made the temple strong.
50:2 Under him the base of the double wall was laid to its full height, / the tall supporting wall of the temple's enclosure.
50:3 In his time they cut a pool to hold water, / a bronze basin as round as the sea.
50:4 He watched over his people to keep them from falling / and strengthened the city to hold out under siege.
50:5 What glory he had as the people crowded around him / when he stepped out from the house of the curtain!
50:6 He was like the star of dawn between clouds, / like the moon when its days are full,
50:7 like the sun beaming down on the temple of the Most High, / like the bow that lights up the glorious clouds,
50:8 like rose blossoms in the spring days, / like lilies where the water flows out, / like the new growth of Lebanon in summertime,
50:9 like fire and frankincense in the fire pan, / like a cup of beaten gold / decorated with every costly stone,
50:10 like an olive tree bursting with fruit, / like a cypress rising high into the clouds.
50:11 When he took up his robe of glory / and dressed in the fullness of splendor, / as he climbed to the holy altar, / he filled the sanctuary court with glory.
50:12 As he took the pieces of the sacrifice from the priests' hands, / standing at the altar's hearth, / his brothers ringed him like a crown, / like young cedars on Lebanon, / and circled him like the trunks of palms,
50:13 every son of Aaron in his glory, / the Lord's offering held in their hands / in front of all Israel's assembly.
50:14 To complete his service at the altars / and set out in beauty the offering of the Most High, the Almighty,
50:15 he stretched his hand out over the cup / and poured out some of the grape's blood. / At the base of the altar he poured it, / a soothing smell for the Most High, the King of all.
50:16 Then Aaron's sons cried out / and sounded the beaten-metal trumpets; / they made a great noise heard / for a reminder before the Most High.
50:17 At once the whole people rushed together / and fell face down on the ground / to bow before their Lord, / the Almighty, God Most High.
50:18 The singers praised with their voices, / and in the crowded house the music was sweet.
50:19 The people begged the Lord Most High / in prayer before the Merciful One, / until the Lord's ordered worship was done / and they had carried out his service to the end.
50:20 Then he came down and lifted his hands / over all the gathered Israelites / to speak the Lord's blessing with his lips / and to take pride in his name.
50:21 And again the people bowed low in worship, / to take the blessing from the Most High.
## A Blessing and a Signature
50:22 And now bless God, all of you, / the one who does great things everywhere, / who lifts up our days from the womb / and treats us with mercy.
50:23 May he give you a glad heart, / and may peace be ours in Israel in our time, / as it was in ages past.
50:24 May his mercy stay faithfully with us, / and may he set us free in his time.
50:25 There are two nations I loathe with all my soul, / and the third is no nation at all:
50:26 those who live on the hill of Samaria, the Philistines, / and the foolish people who live in Shechem.
50:27 In this book I have engraved instruction / that brings understanding and knowledge, / I, Jesus son of Sirach Eleazar, of Jerusalem, / who rained down wisdom from his heart.
50:28 Happy is the one who lives among these words; / whoever takes them into his heart will grow wise.
50:29 For if he does them, he will be strong for anything, / because the light of the Lord is his path.
## A Prayer of Thanks
51:1 A prayer of Jesus son of Sirach. / I will give you thanks, my Lord and King; / I will praise you as God, my Savior; / to your name I give thanks,
51:2 for you became my shelter and my help. / You set my body free from ruin, / from the snare of a slandering tongue, / from lips busy making lies. / Before those who stood around me you came to help,
51:3 and you rescued me, because your mercy and your name are so great, / from teeth grinding and ready to devour, / from the grip of men hunting my life, / from all the troubles that came on me,
51:4 from fire that choked me on every side, / from flames in the middle, flames I had not lit,
51:5 from deep in Hades' belly, / from a filthy tongue and a false word.
51:6 A wicked tongue spread lies about me to the king. / My soul came close to dying; / my life was right beside Hades down below.
51:7 They hemmed me in from all sides, / and no helper was there. / I watched for help from people, / and none came.
51:8 Then your mercy came to my mind, Lord, / and the things you have done since ages past: / you pull out those who wait for you / and save them from the grasp of the nations.
51:9 I lifted up my plea on the earth / and prayed to be rescued from death.
51:10 I called on the Lord, the Father of my lord, / not to abandon me in days of trouble, / in the time of the proud, when I had no help.
51:11 Your name I will praise without ceasing / and sing it hymns of thanksgiving. / And my plea was heard,
51:12 for you saved me from ruin / and pulled me out of an evil time. / That is why I will thank you and praise you / and bless the Lord's name.
## Seeking Wisdom
51:13 When I was still a youth, before I went wandering, / I openly asked for wisdom in my prayer.
51:14 Facing the temple I pleaded for her, / and I will keep seeking her to the last.
51:15 From flower to ripening grape / she made my heart glad. / My foot stepped on level ground; / since my youth I have tracked her.
51:16 I turned my ear to her only a little and took her in, / and I found plenty of instruction for myself.
51:17 Through her I went forward; / glory goes from me to the one who gives me wisdom.
51:18 For I made up my mind to live by her. / I was eager for what is good, / and I will never be put to shame.
51:19 My soul fought hard for her, / and I kept close watch on how I acted. / I stretched my hands up high / and mourned my not knowing her.
51:20 I aimed my soul at her. / From the start I won a heart with her, / and by cleansing I found her; / that is why I will never be left alone.
51:21 Deep inside I was stirred to look for her, / and so I gained a good thing to own.
51:22 As my wages the Lord gave me a tongue, / and I will use it to praise him.
51:23 Come close to me, you who are untaught, / and stay in the house of instruction.
51:24 And since you say you lack these things, / and your souls are very thirsty,
51:25 I have opened my mouth and spoken: / "Get her for yourselves without paying silver."
51:26 Bend your neck under the yoke / and let your soul take in instruction. / She is near, ready to be found.
51:27 Look and see for yourselves: I toiled a little / and found great rest for myself.
51:28 Pay a large sum of silver for instruction, / and through it you will gain much gold.
51:29 May your soul be glad in his mercy, / and may praising him never bring you shame.
51:30 Do your work before the time comes, / and he will give you your reward in his own time.
