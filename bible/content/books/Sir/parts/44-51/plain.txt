## The Praise of the Fathers
44:1 A Hymn of the Fathers. / Let us praise men of glory now, / the fathers we were born from.
44:2 The Lord created great glory, / his majesty from ages past.
44:3 Some ruled in their kingdoms / and were men famous for their strength. / They gave advice by their understanding / and spoke out in prophecies.
44:4 They led the people by their plans / and by their grasp of the people's writings; / their teaching was full of wise words.
44:5 Some searched out musical tunes / and set down poems in writing.
44:6 Some were rich men, well supplied with strength, / living at peace in the places where they settled.
44:7 Every one of these won honor in his own generation, / and in his days he was something to boast of.
44:8 Some of them left a name, / and their praises are still told.
44:9 But of others there is no memory. / They perished as if they had never existed. / They became like people never born, / and so did their children after them.
44:10 But these were men of mercy, / whose right deeds have not been forgotten.
44:11 A good inheritance will remain with their offspring. / Their descendants live within the covenants.
44:12 Their offspring stood firm, / and so did their children, for their sake.
44:13 Their offspring will remain forever, / and their glory will never be wiped out.
44:14 In peace they were buried, / and their name lives for generations.
44:15 Peoples will tell of their wisdom, / and the assembly will proclaim their praise.
## Enoch, Noah and Abraham
44:16 Enoch pleased the Lord and was taken away, / a pattern of repentance for the generations.
44:17 Noah was found perfect and righteous. / In the time of anger he became the exchange. / So a remnant was left for the earth, / and so the flood came.
44:18 Everlasting covenants were made with him, / so that all flesh would never again be wiped out by a flood.
44:19 Abraham was the great father of a multitude of nations, / and no one has been found like him in glory.
44:20 He kept the law of the Most High / and entered into a covenant with him. / He set up the covenant in his own flesh, / and when he was tested he was found faithful.
44:21 So the Lord promised him with an oath / that nations would be blessed through his offspring, / that he would make him as many as the dust of the earth / and raise his offspring as high as the stars, / and give them an inheritance from sea to sea, / from the River to the ends of the earth.
44:22 With Isaac too he did the same, for the sake of Abraham his father: / he made sure the blessing of all people and the covenant,
44:23 and he made them rest on the head of Jacob. / He recognized him with his blessings / and gave him the land as an inheritance. / He marked out his shares / and divided them among twelve tribes. / From him he brought out a man of mercy, / who found favor in the eyes of all flesh,
## Moses and Aaron
45:1 Moses, loved by God and by people, / whose memory is spoken with blessings.
45:2 He made him equal in glory to the holy ones / and made him great, so that his enemies were terrified.
45:3 By his words he made the signs stop. / He honored him in the presence of kings. / He gave him commands for his people / and showed him part of his glory.
45:4 For his faithfulness and humility he made him holy; / he chose him out of all flesh.
45:5 He let him hear his voice / and led him into the deep darkness. / Face to face he gave him the commandments, / the law of life and knowledge, / to teach Jacob the covenant / and Israel his judgments.
45:6 He raised up Aaron, a holy man like Moses, / his brother, from the tribe of Levi.
45:7 He made an everlasting covenant with him / and gave him the priesthood of the people. / He made him blessed in splendid dress / and wrapped a glorious robe around him.
45:8 He dressed him in perfect splendor / and made him strong with the garments of power: / the undershorts, the long robe and the ephod.
45:9 He put pomegranates all around him, / and very many gold bells on every side, / to ring out as he walked, / to make their sound heard in the temple, / a reminder for the children of his people.
45:10 He gave him a holy garment of gold, violet and purple, / an embroiderer's work, / with the breastpiece for decisions and the tokens of truth,
45:11 of twisted scarlet, a craftsman's work, / with precious stones cut like seals / and set in gold, a jeweler's work, / to be a reminder, with writing engraved / for each of the tribes of Israel.
45:12 A gold crown was on his turban, / engraved like a holy seal, / a proud honor, a mighty work, / delights for the eyes, adorned and lovely.
45:13 Before him there had never been anything like them. / No outsider has ever put them on, / only his sons / and his descendants for all time.
45:14 His sacrifices are burned whole / every day, twice a day without fail.
45:15 Moses ordained him / and anointed him with holy oil. / It became an everlasting covenant for him / and for his offspring as long as the heavens last, / to serve the Lord and be his priest / and to bless his people in the name.
45:16 He chose him out of all the living / to bring the offering to the Lord, / incense and a soothing smell as a token portion, / to make atonement for your people.
45:17 In his commandments he gave him / authority over the covenants of judgment, / to teach Jacob the decrees / and proclaim his law to Israel.
45:18 Outsiders plotted against him / and were jealous of him in the wilderness: / the men of Dathan and Abiram, / and Korah's band, hot with fury and anger.
45:19 The Lord saw it and was not pleased, / and they were wiped out in his furious anger. / He worked wonders against them / and burned them up in his flaming fire.
45:20 He added to Aaron's glory / and gave him an inheritance. / He shared out to them the firstfruits of the first crops / and gave them bread first of all, in plenty.
45:21 For they eat the Lord's sacrifices, / which he gave to him and to his offspring.
45:22 But in the people's land he has no inheritance, / and he has no share among the people, / for he himself is your share and inheritance.
## Phinehas
45:23 Phinehas son of Eleazar is third in glory, / because he was zealous in the fear of the Lord / and stood firm when the people turned away, / with the goodness of his eager heart. / And he made atonement for Israel.
45:24 So a covenant of peace was made with him, / that he should lead the sanctuary and his people, / and that he and his offspring / should have the dignity of the priesthood forever.
45:25 There was also a covenant with David, / a son of the tribe of Judah: / a king's inheritance passes from son to son alone, / and Aaron's inheritance belongs to his offspring too.
45:26 May he give you wisdom in your heart / to judge his people with righteousness, / so that their good things will not vanish, / nor their glory, for all their generations.
## Joshua and Caleb
46:1 Joshua son of Nun was mighty in wars. / He took Moses' place as prophet. / True to his name, he became great / in saving his chosen ones, / taking revenge on the enemies who rose against them, / so that he could give Israel its inheritance.
46:2 How glorious he was when he raised his hands / and swung his sword against cities!
46:3 Who before him ever stood like that? / For the Lord himself brought the enemies to him.
46:4 Was it not by his hand that the sun held back, / and one day became as long as two?
46:5 He called on the Most High Sovereign / when enemies pressed him on every side, / and the great Lord answered them / with hailstones of tremendous power.
46:6 He hurled battle down on that nation / and destroyed the opponents on the slope, / so that the nations would know their full armor / and that he was fighting his war before the Lord. / For he followed fully after the Sovereign.
46:7 In the days of Moses he did a loyal deed, / he and Caleb son of Jephunneh: / they stood up against the enemy, / held the people back from sin / and silenced their wicked grumbling.
46:8 These two alone were kept safe / out of six hundred thousand men on foot, / to bring the people into their inheritance, / into a land flowing with milk and honey.
46:9 The Lord gave Caleb strength, / and it stayed with him into old age, / so that he went up into the heights of the land, / and his offspring held it as an inheritance.
46:10 So all the Israelites could see / that it is good to follow the Lord.
## The Judges and Samuel
46:11 Then the judges, each by his own name, / all whose hearts did not play the prostitute / and who did not turn away from the Lord: / may their memory be spoken with blessings!
46:12 May their bones sprout again from their place, / and may their name be handed on / to the sons of these honored men.
46:13 Samuel, loved by his Lord, / a prophet of the Lord, established the kingdom / and anointed rulers over his people.
46:14 He judged the community by the law of the Lord, / and the Lord watched over Jacob.
46:15 By his faithfulness he was proved a true prophet, / and by his faithfulness he was known as a seer who could be trusted.
46:16 He called on the Lord, the Sovereign, / when his enemies pressed him on every side, / as he offered a lamb still nursing.
46:17 Then the Lord thundered from heaven / and made his voice heard with a great roar.
46:18 He crushed the leaders of the people of Tyre / and all the rulers of the Philistines.
46:19 Before the time of his everlasting sleep, / he called the Lord and his anointed to witness: / "I have not taken anyone's goods, / not even a pair of sandals." / And no one accused him.
46:20 Even after he had fallen asleep he prophesied. / He told the king how his life would end, / and he raised his voice from the earth in prophecy / to wipe out the people's wickedness.
## Nathan and David
47:1 After him Nathan rose up / to prophesy in the days of David.
47:2 As the fat is set apart from the peace offering, / so David was set apart from the Israelites.
47:3 He played with lions as if they were young goats, / and with bears as if they were lambs.
47:4 When he was young, did he not kill a giant / and take away the people's disgrace, / when he raised his hand with a stone in his sling / and brought down the boast of Goliath?
47:5 For he called on the Lord Most High, / who gave strength to his right hand / to strike down a man of war / and to raise up the horn of his people.
47:6 So they gave him glory for his tens of thousands / and praised him with the Lord's blessings, / when the crown of glory was brought to him.
47:7 For he wiped out enemies on every side / and brought the Philistines, his foes, to nothing. / He broke their horn, and it is broken to this day.
47:8 In all his works he gave thanks / to the Holy One, the Most High, with words of glory. / With all his heart he sang hymns, / and he loved his Maker.
47:9 He placed singers before the altar / to make sweet music with their voices.
47:10 He gave beauty to the festivals / and set the holy seasons in perfect order, / while they praised God's holy name / and the sanctuary rang with it from early morning.
47:11 The Lord took away his sins / and raised his horn high forever. / He gave him a covenant of kings / and a glorious throne in Israel.
## Solomon and the Split Kingdom
47:12 After him a son with knowledge rose up, / who lived at ease because of his father.
47:13 Solomon was king in days of peace. / God gave him rest on every side, / so that he could build a house for his name / and prepare a sanctuary to last forever.
47:14 How wise you became when you were young! / Like a river you overflowed with understanding.
47:15 Your soul covered the earth, / and you filled it with riddling proverbs.
47:16 Your name reached distant islands, / and you were loved for your peace.
47:17 For your songs, proverbs, parables / and explanations the nations marveled at you.
47:18 In the name of the Lord God, / who is called the God of Israel, / you gathered gold as if it were tin / and piled up silver as if it were lead.
47:19 But you gave your loins to women / and let them have power over your body.
47:20 You put a stain on your glory / and defiled your offspring. / You brought anger on your children, / and I was stung with grief at your folly,
47:21 so that the rule was split in two, / and a rebel kingdom arose out of Ephraim.
47:22 But the Lord will never abandon his mercy, / and none of his works will be destroyed. / He will never wipe out the descendants of his chosen one / or take away the offspring of the one who loved him. / He gave Jacob a remnant, / and David a root from his own line.
47:23 Solomon went to rest with his fathers / and left behind him one of his offspring, / the people's folly, short on understanding: / Rehoboam, who turned the people away by his advice. / Then came Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel sin / and set Ephraim on the way of sin.
47:24 Their sins grew very many, / until they were driven out of their land.
47:25 They searched out every kind of evil, / until punishment came upon them.
## Elijah and Elisha
48:1 Then Elijah the prophet arose like fire, / and his word burned like a torch.
48:2 He brought a famine on them, / and in his zeal he made them few.
48:3 By the Lord's word he shut up the sky, / and three times he brought down fire.
48:4 How glorious you were, Elijah, in your wonders! / Who can boast of being like you?
48:5 You raised a dead man from death / and from Hades by the word of the Most High.
48:6 You brought kings down to destruction / and famous men down from their beds.
48:7 At Sinai you heard a rebuke, / and at Horeb judgments of punishment.
48:8 You anointed kings to repay wrongs / and prophets to come after him as his successors.
48:9 You were taken up in a whirlwind of fire, / in a chariot with horses of fire.
48:10 You were written down in warnings for the times to come, / to calm anger before it breaks out in fury, / to turn the heart of the father back to the son, / and to set the tribes of Jacob on their feet.
48:11 Happy are those who saw you, / and those who have been adorned with love, / for we too will surely live.
48:12 Elijah was hidden in the whirlwind, / and Elisha was filled with his spirit. / In all his days no ruler shook him, / and no one overpowered him.
48:13 Nothing was too hard for him, / and even in death his body prophesied.
48:14 In his life he did wonders, / and in death his deeds were marvelous.
48:15 For all this the people did not repent / or leave their sins behind, / until they were carried off as plunder from their land / and scattered over all the earth. / The people left were very few, / with a ruler from the house of David.
48:16 Some of them did what was pleasing, / but others sinned more and more.
## Hezekiah and Isaiah
48:17 Hezekiah made his city strong / and brought water into the middle of it. / He cut through the rock with iron / and built pools for the water.
48:18 In his days Sennacherib came up / and sent the Rabshakeh, and then he set out. / He raised his hand against Zion / and boasted loudly in his pride.
48:19 Then their hearts and hands shook, / and they were in pain like women in labor.
48:20 They called on the Lord, the merciful one, / and spread out their hands to him. / The Holy One heard them quickly from heaven / and rescued them by the hand of Isaiah.
48:21 He struck the camp of the Assyrians, / and his angel wiped them out.
48:22 For Hezekiah did what pleased the Lord / and held firmly to the ways of David his father, / as Isaiah the prophet commanded him, / the great prophet, trustworthy in his vision.
48:23 In his days the sun went backward, / and he added life to the king.
48:24 By a great spirit he saw the last things / and comforted those who mourned in Zion.
48:25 He revealed what would happen to the end of time, / and hidden things before they came.
## Josiah and the Last Kings
49:1 The memory of Josiah is like blended incense / prepared by the skill of a perfumer. / In every mouth it is as sweet as honey, / like music at a banquet with wine.
49:2 He went straight ahead in turning the people back / and took away the abhorrent things of lawlessness.
49:3 He set his heart straight toward the Lord; / in the days of lawless men he made godliness strong.
49:4 Except for David, Hezekiah and Josiah, / they all did great wrong. / They abandoned the law of the Most High, / and the kings of Judah came to an end.
49:5 They gave their horn to others / and their glory to a foreign nation.
49:6 It set fire to the chosen city of the sanctuary / and left its streets empty, because of Jeremiah.
49:7 For they had mistreated him, / though he had been made holy as a prophet in the womb, / to pull up, to do harm and to destroy, / and also to build and to plant.
## The Prophets and the Return
49:8 Ezekiel saw the vision of glory / that God showed him above the chariot of the cherubim.
49:9 For he remembered the enemies with a downpour / and did good to those who walk straight paths.
49:10 And may the bones of the Twelve Prophets / sprout again from their place. / He comforted Jacob / and rescued them with a hope they could trust.
49:11 How can we praise Zerubbabel enough? / He was like a seal ring on the right hand.
49:12 So was Jeshua son of Jozadak. / In their days they built the house / and raised up a people holy to the Lord, / made ready for everlasting glory.
49:13 The memory of Nehemiah is great too. / He raised up our fallen walls, / set up gates and bars / and rebuilt our homes.
## Back to the Beginning
49:14 No one was ever created on the earth like Enoch, / for he was taken up from the earth.
49:15 Joseph was born a man, / a leader of his brothers and the support of his people, / and his bones were watched over.
49:16 Shem and Seth were honored among people, / but above every living thing in creation is Adam.
## Simon the High Priest
50:1 Simon son of Onias was the high priest. / In his lifetime he repaired the house, / and in his days he made the temple strong.
50:2 He laid the foundations for the high double wall, / the high retaining wall around the temple.
50:3 In his days the reservoir for water was dug out, / a bronze basin as broad around as the sea.
50:4 He took care to keep his people from ruin / and made the city strong against siege.
50:5 How glorious he was as the people gathered around him, / when he came out of the house of the curtain!
50:6 He was like the morning star among the clouds, / like the moon when its days are full,
50:7 like the sun shining on the temple of the Most High, / like the rainbow gleaming in clouds of glory,
50:8 like roses in bloom in springtime, / like lilies beside running water, / like a green shoot on Lebanon on a summer day,
50:9 like fire and incense in the censer, / like a vessel of hammered gold / set with every kind of precious stone,
50:10 like an olive tree loaded with fruit, / like a cypress towering up into the clouds.
50:11 When he put on his glorious robe / and dressed himself in perfect splendor, / when he went up to the holy altar, / he made the court of the sanctuary glorious.
50:12 When he received the portions from the priests' hands, / as he stood by the hearth of the altar, / his brothers were a crown around him, / like young cedars on Lebanon. / They surrounded him like palm trunks,
50:13 all the sons of Aaron in their splendor, / with the Lord's offering in their hands / before the whole assembly of Israel.
50:14 Finishing the service at the altars / and arranging the offering of the Most High, the Almighty,
50:15 he reached out his hand to the cup / and poured out the blood of the grape. / He poured it at the foot of the altar, / a soothing smell to the Most High, the King of all.
50:16 Then the sons of Aaron shouted. / They blew their trumpets of hammered metal / and made a great sound heard, / as a reminder before the Most High.
50:17 Then all the people hurried together / and fell on their faces to the ground / to bow down to their Lord, / the Almighty, God Most High.
50:18 The singers praised with their voices, / and in the crowded house the music was sweet.
50:19 The people prayed to the Lord Most High, / praying before the Merciful One, / until the Lord's order of worship was finished / and they had completed his service.
50:20 Then he came down and raised his hands / over the whole assembly of the Israelites, / to give the Lord's blessing with his lips / and to glory in his name.
50:21 And the people bowed in worship a second time / to receive the blessing from the Most High.
## A Blessing and a Signature
50:22 And now bless God, all of you, / the one who does great things everywhere, / who lifts up our days from the womb / and deals with us by his mercy.
50:23 May he give you joy of heart, / and may there be peace in our days in Israel, / as in the days of long ago.
50:24 May his mercy stay faithfully with us, / and may he set us free in his time.
50:25 Two nations my soul loathes, / and the third is not a nation at all:
50:26 those who live on the hill of Samaria, the Philistines, / and the foolish people who live in Shechem.
50:27 Instruction in understanding and knowledge / I have written in this book, / I, Jesus son of Sirach Eleazar, of Jerusalem, / who poured out wisdom from his heart like rain.
50:28 Happy is the one who spends his time with these things. / Whoever lays them to heart will become wise.
50:29 For if he does them, he will be strong for anything, / because the light of the Lord is his path.
## A Prayer of Thanks
51:1 A prayer of Jesus son of Sirach. / I will thank you, Lord and King, / and praise you, God my Savior. / I give thanks to your name,
51:2 for you have been my protector and helper. / You rescued my body from destruction, / from the trap of a slandering tongue, / from lips that make up lies. / In front of those who stood by, you were my helper.
51:3 You rescued me, as great as your mercy and your name are, / from the gnashing teeth of those ready to eat me, / from the hand of those who were after my life, / from the many troubles I had,
51:4 from the choking fire all around me, / from the middle of a fire I had not lit,
51:5 from the depths of the belly of Hades, / from an unclean tongue and lying words.
51:6 An unjust tongue slandered me to the king. / My soul came near to death, / and my life was close to Hades below.
51:7 They surrounded me on every side, / and there was no one to help. / I looked for someone to come to my aid, / and there was no one.
51:8 Then I remembered your mercy, Lord, / and your deeds from long ago: / you rescue those who wait for you / and save them from the hand of the nations.
51:9 I lifted up my plea on the earth / and prayed to be rescued from death.
51:10 I called on the Lord, the Father of my lord, / not to abandon me in days of trouble, / in the time of the proud, when I had no help.
51:11 I will praise your name always / and sing hymns of thanks. / And my prayer was heard.
51:12 For you saved me from destruction / and rescued me from a time of evil. / So I will give thanks and praise you / and bless the name of the Lord.
## Seeking Wisdom
51:13 While I was still young, before I went wandering, / I sought wisdom openly in my prayer.
51:14 In front of the temple I asked for her, / and I will search for her to the very end.
51:15 From the blossom to the ripening grape / my heart delighted in her. / My foot walked on a straight path; / from my youth I followed her tracks.
51:16 I bent my ear a little and received her, / and I found much instruction for myself.
51:17 I made progress in her. / To the one who gives me wisdom I will give glory.
51:18 For I made up my mind to live by her. / I was eager for what is good, / and I will never be put to shame.
51:19 My soul has wrestled for her, / and I was strict in what I did. / I spread out my hands to heaven / and grieved that I knew her so little.
51:20 I set my soul straight toward her. / From the beginning I gained a heart along with her, / and in purity I found her; / so I will never be abandoned.
51:21 My inner self was stirred to search for her; / that is why I gained a good possession.
51:22 The Lord gave me a tongue as my reward, / and with it I will praise him.
51:23 Come close to me, you who are untaught, / and stay in the house of instruction.
51:24 And since you say you lack these things, / and your souls are very thirsty,
51:25 I opened my mouth and said: / "Get it for yourselves without money.
51:26 Put your neck under the yoke, / and let your souls take in instruction. / She is close at hand to find.
51:27 See with your own eyes that I worked only a little / and found much rest for myself.
51:28 Pay a large sum of silver for instruction, / and through it you will gain much gold.
51:29 May your soul rejoice in his mercy, / and may you never be ashamed to praise him.
51:30 Do your work before the time comes, / and he will give you your reward in his own time."
