## A Living Sacrifice
12:1 So I appeal to you, brothers, by the compassion of God: place your bodies before him as a sacrifice that is alive, holy and welcome to God. This is your sensible worship.
12:2 Stop shaping yourselves to fit this age. Be transformed instead, by the renewing of your mind, so that you can test and prove what God's will is: what is good, welcome and complete.
## Many Parts, One Body
12:3 Because of the grace given to me, I tell every one of you: do not think more highly of yourself than you should. Think with sound judgment instead, as God has handed each of you a measure of faith.
12:4 A single body has many parts, and the parts do not all have the same work to do.
12:5 In the same way, many as we are, we are one body in Christ, and each of us is a part belonging to the others.
12:6 We have gifts that differ, by the grace given to us. If the gift is prophecy, let it be used in line with the proportion of faith.
12:7 If it is service, let it be used in serving. The teacher, in teaching.
12:8 The one who encourages, in encouraging. The one who gives, with generosity. The one who leads, with eagerness. The one who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.
## Love in Action
12:9 Let love be real, with no acting. Hate what is evil. Hold tight to what is good.
12:10 Be devoted to one another with the love of a family. Each of you be first to honor the other.
12:11 Do not let your eagerness lag. Be boiling in spirit. Be slaves of the Lord.
12:12 Rejoice in hope. Be patient in hardship. Stay steady in prayer.
12:13 Share in the needs of the saints. Chase after hospitality to strangers.
12:14 Bless your persecutors. Bless them, and never curse.
12:15 Be glad along with the glad. Weep along with those who weep.
12:16 Have the same attitude toward one another. Do not set your minds on lofty things, but go along with the lowly. Do not become wise in your own opinion.
12:17 Pay no one back evil for evil. Take thought for what is honorable in the eyes of all people.
12:18 If it can be done, and so far as it rests with you, keep the peace with all people.
12:19 Beloved, never take revenge yourselves, but make room for the anger. For it is written: "Vengeance is mine. I will pay back, says the Lord."
12:20 Instead: "If your enemy goes hungry, feed him. If he goes thirsty, give him something to drink. In doing this you will heap coals of fire on his head."
12:21 Do not be defeated by evil. Defeat evil with good.
## The Authorities
13:1 Every person must submit to the authorities above them. There is no authority that does not come from God, and the ones that exist have been set in their places by God.
13:2 So whoever resists the authority has set himself against God's arrangement, and those who set themselves against it will bring judgment on themselves.
13:3 Rulers are not something to fear for those who do good, but for those who do evil. Do you want to be free of fear of the authority? Then do good, and you will earn its praise.
13:4 It is God's servant, there for your good. But if you do evil, be afraid. It does not carry the sword for no reason. It is God's servant, an avenger to carry out anger on the person who does evil.
13:5 So you have to submit, and not only to escape the anger, but also for the sake of conscience.
13:6 This is also why you pay taxes. The authorities are God's public servants, giving their full time to this very work.
13:7 Hand over to each person what you owe. If it is the tax, pay the tax. If it is the customs duty, pay the duty. If it is respect, give respect. If it is honor, give honor.
## Love Fulfills the Law
13:8 Keep no debt hanging over anyone, except the debt of loving one another. Whoever loves the other person has fulfilled the law.
13:9 The commands "Do not commit adultery," "Do not murder," "Do not steal," "Do not covet," and any other command, are all gathered up in this saying: "You are to love your neighbor as yourself."
13:10 Love works no evil against the neighbor, and so love is the law fulfilled.
## The Day Is Near
13:11 And do all this, knowing what the moment is. It is already time for you to wake up out of sleep, because salvation is closer to us now than when we first believed.
13:12 The night is almost over. The day is close. So let us lay aside the works of darkness and put on the weapons of light.
13:13 Let us walk in a decent way, as in daylight: no drunken parties, no sleeping around and shameless living, no fighting and jealousy.
13:14 Put on the Lord Jesus Christ instead, and do not make arrangements for the flesh to feed its desires.
## The Weak and the Strong
14:1 Welcome the one whose faith is weak, but not so as to sit in judgment on their opinions.
14:2 Someone has faith enough to eat anything, but the weak person eats only vegetables.
14:3 Whoever eats must not treat with contempt the person who does not. Whoever does not eat must not sit in judgment on the person who does. God has welcomed that person.
14:4 Who are you, to judge a household slave who belongs to someone else? Whether he stays on his feet or falls is a matter for his own master. And he will stay on his feet, because the Lord has the power to keep him standing.
14:5 One person judges one day to stand above another, and another judges every day alike. Each one should be fully sure in their own mind.
14:6 Whoever keeps the day keeps it for the Lord. Whoever eats, eats for the Lord, and gives thanks to God. Whoever does not eat, does not eat for the Lord, and also gives thanks to God.
14:7 Not one of us lives for ourselves, and not one dies for ourselves.
14:8 When we live, we live for the Lord. When we die, we die for the Lord. In life or in death, then, we are the Lord's.
14:9 This is why Christ died and came to life: to be Lord of both the dead and the living.
14:10 Why do you judge your brother? And you, why do you look down on your brother? We will all stand in front of God's judgment seat.
14:11 For it is written: "As I live, says the Lord, every knee will bow to me, and every tongue will confess to God."
14:12 So each of us will give an account to God about ourselves.
## Do Not Make Others Stumble
14:13 So let us no longer judge one another. Decide this instead: no one is to put something to trip over, or a trap, in the way of a brother.
14:14 I know, and I am convinced in the Lord Jesus, that nothing is common in itself. But if someone counts a thing as common, then to that person it is common.
14:15 If your brother is being hurt over food, you are not walking by love anymore. Do not let your food be the ruin of someone Christ died for.
14:16 So do not let something that is good for you get a bad name.
14:17 The kingdom of God is not food and drink. It is righteousness, peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.
14:18 Whoever serves Christ by living like this is welcome to God and has the approval of people.
14:19 So let us run after what brings peace and what builds up one another.
14:20 Do not wreck the work of God over food. All things are clean, but it is wrong for a person to eat in a way that makes another stumble.
14:21 It is good not to eat meat or drink wine, or do anything else that makes your brother stumble.
14:22 As for the faith you have, keep it as your own, in God's sight. Blessed is the person who does not condemn himself by what he approves.
14:23 But the person who eats while having doubts has already been condemned, since the eating does not come from faith. Anything not done out of faith is sin.
## Christ Welcomed You
15:1 We who are strong have a duty to carry the weaknesses of those who are not strong, and not simply to please ourselves.
15:2 Each of us should please our neighbor, aiming at what is good, to build that person up.
15:3 Even Christ did not please himself. As it is written: "The insults of those who insult you fell on me."
15:4 All that was put in writing in earlier days was put in writing to instruct us, so that the endurance and the encouragement that the Scriptures give would bring us hope.
15:5 May the God who gives endurance and encouragement give you the same mind with one another, following Christ Jesus,
15:6 so that in one accord and with one mouth you give glory to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
15:7 So welcome one another, just as Christ welcomed you, for God's glory.
15:8 For I say that Christ has become a servant of the circumcised, to show God's truth, by making firm the promises made to the fathers,
15:9 and for the Gentiles to give glory to God for his mercy. As it is written: "For this reason I will give you thanks among the Gentiles, and make music to your name."
15:10 And again it says: "Be glad, you Gentiles, together with his people."
15:11 And again: "Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles, and let all the peoples give him praise."
15:12 And again Isaiah says: "There will be the root of Jesse, the one who rises up to rule the Gentiles. The Gentiles will set their hope on him."
15:13 May the God who is the source of hope fill you with every kind of joy and peace as you believe, so that hope spills over in you through the power of the Holy Spirit.
## Paul's Priestly Work
15:14 I am convinced about you, my brothers, and I say it as someone who knows: you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able to counsel one another.
15:15 Still, I have written to you more boldly on some points, to remind you, because of the grace God gave me,
15:16 that I should be Christ Jesus' minister to the Gentiles, doing a priest's work for the good news of God, so that the Gentiles may become an offering God welcomes, made holy by the Holy Spirit.
15:17 So in Christ Jesus I have reason to boast about my work for God.
15:18 I will not presume to speak of anything except what Christ has done through me to win the Gentiles to obedience, in word and in deed,
15:19 with the power of signs and wonders, and with the power of the Spirit of God. As a result, from Jerusalem and around in a circle as far as Illyricum, I have completed the preaching of the good news of Christ.
15:20 In this way I make it my ambition to announce the good news where Christ has not yet been named, so that I do not build on another person's foundation,
15:21 but, as it is written: "Those who were not told about him will see, and those who have not heard will understand."
## Plans for Rome and Spain
15:22 This is also why I was held back, again and again, from coming to you.
15:23 But now I have no more ground to work in these regions, and for many years I have had a longing to come to you,
15:24 when I make my trip to Spain. I hope to see you on my way through, and to be sent on by you to that place, after I have first had my fill of your company for a little while.
15:25 But now I am traveling to Jerusalem, to serve the saints.
15:26 Macedonia and Achaia chose gladly to pool a gift for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.
15:27 Yes, they chose it gladly, and they owe it to them. The Gentiles have shared in the spiritual things of the Jerusalem saints, so they ought to serve them with material things.
15:28 So when I have finished this task and delivered this fruit to them, safe and sealed, I will leave for Spain by way of your city.
15:29 I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of Christ's blessing.
15:30 I appeal to you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love that comes from the Spirit: join me in the struggle by praying to God for me,
15:31 that I be rescued from those in Judea who refuse to believe, and that my service in Jerusalem will be acceptable to the saints,
15:32 so that I may come to you with joy, by God's will, and find rest in your company.
15:33 The God of peace be with all of you. Amen.
## Phoebe
16:1 Let me recommend to you Phoebe, our sister. She is also a servant of the church in Cenchreae.
16:2 Welcome her in the Lord in a way fitting for the saints, and give her help in whatever business she needs from you. She has been a patron to many people, and to me myself.
## Greetings to Friends in Rome
16:3 Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus.
16:4 They risked their own necks to save my life. I am not the only one who thanks them: so do all the churches of the Gentiles.
16:5 Greet also the church that meets in their house. Greet Epaenetus, who is dear to me, the firstfruits of Asia for Christ.
16:6 Greet Mary, who toiled a great deal for you.
16:7 Greet Andronicus and Junia, my relatives and fellow prisoners. They are outstanding among the apostles, and they were in Christ before I was.
16:8 Greet Ampliatus, who is dear to me in the Lord.
16:9 Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys, who is dear to me.
16:10 Greet Apelles, tested and proven in Christ. Greet those from the household of Aristobulus.
16:11 Greet Herodion, my relative. Greet those from the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord.
16:12 Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who toil in the Lord. Greet dear Persis, who has toiled a great deal in the Lord.
16:13 Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother, who is a mother to me also.
16:14 Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them.
16:15 Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints with them.
16:16 Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ send you greetings.
## A Warning
16:17 I ask you, brothers, to keep a sharp eye on the people who stir up divisions and set stumbling blocks in the way of the teaching you learned. Steer clear of them.
16:18 People like that are not slaves of our Lord Christ. They serve their own bellies. With smooth words and flattering talk they fool the hearts of the unsuspecting.
16:19 Your obedience has reached everyone, so I am glad about you. Still, I want you to be wise when it comes to the good, and pure when it comes to evil.
16:20 The God of peace will crush Satan under your feet, and soon. The grace of our Lord Jesus be with you.
## Greetings from Corinth
16:21 Timothy, my fellow worker, greets you, and so do Lucius, Jason and Sosipater, my relatives.
16:22 I, Tertius, the one who wrote this letter down, greet you in the Lord.
16:23 Gaius, who is host to me and to the whole church, greets you. Erastus, the city treasurer, greets you, and so does our brother Quartus.
16:24 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
## The Closing Doxology
16:25 Now to the one who has the power to make you strong, according to my good news and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the unveiling of the mystery kept in silence for long ages,
16:26 but now brought into the open, and, through the writings of the prophets and by the command of the eternal God, made known to all the Gentiles, to lead them to the obedience of faith,
16:27 to the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory for all the ages. Amen.
