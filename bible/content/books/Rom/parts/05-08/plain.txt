## Peace with God
5:1 We have been justified by faith. So we have peace with God, through our Lord Jesus Christ.
5:2 Through him we have also been led in, by faith, to this grace where we now stand. And we boast in the hope of God's glory.
5:3 Not only that, but we even boast in our troubles. We know that trouble builds endurance,
5:4 and endurance builds tested character, and tested character builds hope.
5:5 And hope does not make us ashamed, because the Holy Spirit, who was given to us, has poured God's love out into our hearts.
5:6 When we were still weak and helpless, Christ died at the right time for the ungodly.
5:7 Hardly anyone will die for a person who is righteous. For someone who is good, perhaps a person would even dare to die.
5:8 God proves his own love toward us by this: we were still sinners when Christ died for us.
5:9 We have now been justified by his blood. So how much more sure it is that through him we will be saved from God's anger!
5:10 We were enemies, and yet we were reconciled to God by the death of his Son. If that is true, then now that we are reconciled, it is far more certain that we will be saved in his life!
5:11 And more than that: we boast in God himself, through our Lord Jesus Christ. It is through him that we have now received this reconciliation.
## Adam and Christ
5:12 Because of this, just as sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin, and so death passed to all people, because all sinned ...
5:13 Up until the law, sin was in the world. But with no law there, sin is not counted.
5:14 Even so, death ruled from Adam until Moses. It ruled even over those who had not sinned the way Adam did, by breaking a command. Adam is a figure of the one who was coming.
5:15 But the gift is nothing like the trespass. One person's trespass brought death to the many. How much more has God's grace overflowed to the many, along with the gift that comes in the grace of one man, Jesus Christ!
5:16 Nor is the gift like what came through the one who sinned. The judgment that followed one trespass ended in condemnation. But the gift that follows many trespasses ends in justification.
5:17 One person's trespass let death rule through that one person. How much more will those who receive grace in overflowing measure, and the gift of righteousness, rule in life through the one person, Jesus Christ!
5:18 So then, one trespass brought condemnation to all people. In the same way, one act of righteousness brings to all people the justification that gives life.
5:19 One man disobeyed, and the many were made sinners. In the same way, the one man obeyed, and the many will be made righteous.
5:20 Now the law slipped in so that the trespass would multiply. But where sin multiplied, grace overflowed even more,
5:21 so that, as sin ruled in the realm of death, grace might rule too. It rules through righteousness and leads to eternal life, through Jesus Christ our Lord.
## Dead to sin, alive to God
6:1 So what will we say? Should we stay in sin so that grace may multiply?
6:2 By no means! We died to sin. How can we still live in it?
6:3 Or are you unaware that those of us who were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into his death?
6:4 By baptism into death, then, we were buried with him. The purpose: as Christ was raised from the dead by the Father's glory, we also might walk in a life that is new.
6:5 If we have grown together with him in a death like his, we will certainly be part of his resurrection too.
6:6 This we know: our old self was crucified with him. The purpose was to strip the body ruled by sin of its power, so that we would not go on serving sin as slaves.
6:7 The one who has died has been freed from sin.
6:8 If we died with Christ, we believe that we will live with him as well.
6:9 We know that Christ, having been raised from the dead, will never die again. Death no longer lords it over him.
6:10 He died once for all, and in dying he died to sin. The life he lives now, he lives to God.
6:11 Think of yourselves in this way too: dead to sin, and alive to God in Christ Jesus.
6:12 So do not let sin be your king in your mortal body, with you obeying its desires.
6:13 And do not hand over the parts of your body to sin, as weapons of unrighteousness. Hand yourselves over to God instead, as people who were dead and are now alive. Hand over the parts of your body to God as weapons of righteousness.
6:14 Sin will not lord it over you. You are not under law. You are under grace.
## Slaves of righteousness
6:15 What follows? Are we to sin, because we are under grace and not under law? By no means!
6:16 Do you not know this? Whoever you hand yourselves over to as slaves, to obey, you are the slaves of the one you obey. You are slaves either of sin, which ends in death, or of obedience, which ends in righteousness.
6:17 Thanks be to God! You were once slaves of sin, but you have come to obey, from the heart, the form of teaching that you were handed over to.
6:18 Freed from sin, you now serve righteousness as slaves.
6:19 I am speaking in human terms, because your flesh is weak. As you once handed the parts of your body over as slaves to impurity and to lawlessness, leading to more and more lawlessness, so now hand them over as slaves to righteousness, leading to holiness.
6:20 While you were slaves of sin, you were free where righteousness was concerned.
6:21 What fruit did you harvest in those days, from things that now make you ashamed? Where those things end is death.
6:22 But now you have been freed from sin and have become God's slaves. Your fruit grows into holiness, and the end of it is eternal life.
6:23 Sin pays wages, and the wages are death. God gives a gift, and the gift is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.
## Released from the law
7:1 Brothers, I am speaking to people who know law. Surely you know that the law rules a person only as long as that person is alive?
7:2 Take a married woman. The law ties her to her husband while he is alive. If the husband dies, she is cleared of the law about the husband.
7:3 So while her husband lives, she will be called an adulteress if she becomes another man's wife. But if the husband dies, she is free from the law, and she is not an adulteress when she becomes another man's wife.
7:4 It is the same with you, my brothers. Through the body of Christ you were put to death in relation to the law, so that you might belong to another: the one who was raised from the dead. This was so that we could bear fruit for God.
7:5 While we were in the flesh, the passions of sins, which came by way of the law, were at work in the parts of our bodies. The fruit they bore was death.
7:6 But now we have been released from the law. We have died to the thing that held us, and so we serve in a new way, the Spirit's way, not in the old way of the written letter.
## The law and sin
7:7 What are we to say, then? Is the law sin? By no means! But I came to know sin only through the law. I would not have known what craving was if the law had not said, "You must not crave."
7:8 Sin found its opening in the commandment, and it produced in me every kind of craving. For without law, sin is dead.
7:9 There was a time when I was alive without law. Then the commandment came, sin revived, and I died.
7:10 And the commandment that was meant for life was found to be, for me, death.
7:11 Sin found its opening through the commandment, tricked me, and used it to kill me.
7:12 The law, then, is holy. The commandment is also holy, and righteous, and good.
7:13 Did something good, then, become death for me? By no means! It was sin. Sin worked death in me through something good, so that it would be seen for what it is. This was so that, through the commandment, sin would become sinful to the extreme.
## The divided self
7:14 We know that the law is spiritual. But I am made of flesh, sold under sin.
7:15 I do not recognize what I do. What I want to do, I do not do. What I hate, that is what I do.
7:16 But if I do the thing I do not want, I side with the law and say that it is good.
7:17 In that case, I am not the one doing it any longer. It is the sin that lives in me.
7:18 I know that nothing good lives in me, that is, in my flesh. The wish to do what is good is with me, but the doing of it is not.
7:19 The good that I want, I do not do. The evil that I do not want, that is what I keep doing.
7:20 And if I do what I have no wish to do, I am not the one doing it any longer. It is the sin that lives in me.
7:21 So I find this law: when I want to do the good, evil is right there with me.
7:22 In my inner self I take delight in the law of God.
7:23 But in the parts of my body I see another law. It fights against the law of my mind, and it takes me prisoner to the law of sin that is in my body's parts.
7:24 I am a wretched man! Who will rescue me out of this body of death?
7:25 Thanks be to God, through Jesus Christ our Lord! So this is how it stands: with my mind I myself serve the law of God, but with my flesh I serve the law of sin.
## No condemnation
8:1 So now there is no condemnation for people who are in Christ Jesus.
8:2 For the law of the Spirit of life, in Christ Jesus, has set you free from the law of sin and of death.
8:3 The law was powerless, because the flesh made it weak. So God did what it could not. He sent his own Son in the likeness of flesh that sin rules, and as a sin offering, and he condemned sin in the flesh.
8:4 He did this so that what the law rightly requires would be fulfilled in us, who walk not by the flesh but by the Spirit.
8:5 People who live by the flesh have their minds on the things of the flesh. People who live by the Spirit have their minds on the things of the Spirit.
8:6 To have the mind of the flesh is death. To have the mind of the Spirit is life and peace.
8:7 That is because the mind of the flesh is hostile to God. It does not submit to God's law, and it cannot.
8:8 People who are in the flesh cannot please God.
8:9 But you are not in the flesh. You are in the Spirit, if indeed God's Spirit lives in you. But if someone does not have the Spirit of Christ, that person is not his.
8:10 If Christ is in you, then the body is dead because of sin, but the Spirit is life because of righteousness.
8:11 And if the Spirit of the One who raised Jesus from the dead lives in you, then the One who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also bring your mortal bodies to life. He will do it because his Spirit lives in you.
## Children and heirs
8:12 So then, brothers, we owe a debt. But we do not owe it to the flesh, to live by the flesh.
8:13 If you live by the flesh, you are about to die. But you will live if you put to death the deeds of the body by the Spirit.
8:14 As many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are God's children.
8:15 You did not receive a spirit that makes you slaves again, to live in fear. You received the Spirit of adoption as children. By that Spirit we call out, "Abba, Father!"
8:16 The Spirit himself joins our spirit in testifying that we are God's children.
8:17 If we are children, we are also heirs. We are heirs of God, sharing the inheritance with Christ, as long as we suffer with him, so that we will also be glorified with him.
## The groaning creation
8:18 I add it all up, and the sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing with the glory that is about to be revealed to us.
8:19 The creation stands on tiptoe, waiting for God's children to be revealed.
8:20 The creation was subjected to futility. It was not its own choice. It was the choice of the One who subjected it. But it was subjected in hope
8:21 that the creation itself will also be set free from slavery to decay, and enter the freedom of the glory of God's children.
8:22 We know that all of creation groans as one and is in labor as one, right up to now.
8:23 And it is not creation alone. We too groan inside ourselves. We have the Spirit as the firstfruits, yet we wait for adoption as children, the redemption of our bodies.
8:24 In hope we were saved. But a hope that can be seen is no hope. Who hopes for something he is looking at?
8:25 When we hope for something unseen, we wait for it with endurance.
8:26 In the same way the Spirit takes hold with us in our weakness. We do not know what we should pray for. But the Spirit himself pleads for us, with groanings that words cannot say.
8:27 The One who searches hearts knows the mind of the Spirit, because the Spirit pleads for the saints in line with God.
8:28 We know that all things work together toward good for those who love God. These are the ones called according to his purpose.
8:29 Those he knew beforehand, he also decided beforehand would be formed to match the image of his Son, so that his Son would be the firstborn among many brothers.
8:30 He called the ones he decided beforehand. He justified the ones he called. And he glorified the ones he justified.
## More than conquerors
8:31 What, then, shall we say to all this? If God is for us, who is against us?
8:32 He did not hold back his own Son. He handed him over for us all. How could he not, along with him, freely give us everything?
8:33 Who will accuse the people God has chosen? God is the one who justifies them.
8:34 Who is there to condemn? Christ died, and more than that, he was raised. He is at God's right hand, and he pleads for us.
8:35 Who will separate us from the love of Christ? Will trouble? Or distress? Or persecution, or hunger, or having nothing to wear, or danger, or the sword?
8:36 As it is written, "For your sake we are facing death all day long. We have been counted as sheep to be slaughtered."
8:37 But in all these things we win an overwhelming victory through the One who loved us.
8:38 I am persuaded that neither death nor life, neither angels nor rulers, neither things present nor things to come, neither powers,
8:39 neither height nor depth, nor any other created thing, will have power to separate us from God's love, the love that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
