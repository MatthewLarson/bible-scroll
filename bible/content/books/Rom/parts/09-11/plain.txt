## Paul's Sorrow for Israel
9:1 I tell the truth in Christ. I am not lying. My conscience bears witness along with me in the Holy Spirit.
9:2 I carry a great grief, a sorrow in my heart that never stops.
9:3 I would even wish to be cursed myself, cut off from Christ, on behalf of my brothers, my relatives according to the flesh.
9:4 They are Israelites. Theirs is the adoption as children, and the glory, and the covenants, and the lawgiving, and the sacred service, and the promises.
9:5 The patriarchs are theirs. And from them, in his human descent, comes the Christ, who is over everything, God blessed forever. Amen.
## Who Is Israel?
9:6 I am not saying that God's word has failed. For not everyone who is from Israel is Israel.
9:7 And being Abraham's offspring does not make them all his children. Instead, "Your offspring will be named through Isaac."
9:8 In other words, the children born by the flesh are not God's children. The children born from the promise are the ones counted as his offspring.
9:9 For the word of promise is this: "At this time I will come, and Sarah will have a son."
9:10 And that is not all. There is also Rebecca, who conceived by one man, our father Isaac.
9:11 The children had not yet been born, and had not done anything good or bad, so that God's purpose, based on his choosing, would stand, not by works but by the one who calls.
9:12 She was told, "The elder will serve the younger."
9:13 Just as it is written: "I loved Jacob, but I hated Esau."
## Is God Unfair?
9:14 What, then, will we say? Is God unjust? By no means!
9:15 For he says to Moses, "I will show mercy to whoever I show mercy, and I will have compassion on whoever I have compassion."
9:16 So it is not up to the one who wills, or the one who runs, but to God, who shows mercy.
9:17 For the Scripture says to Pharaoh, "For this very reason I raised you up: that in you I might show my power, and that my name might be told through all the earth."
9:18 So then, he shows mercy to whom he wants, and he hardens whom he wants.
## The Potter and the Clay
9:19 Someone will say to me, "Then why does he still blame anyone? Who has ever stood against his plan?"
9:20 No, human being, who are you to argue against God? Will a shaped thing say to the one who shaped it, "Why did you make me like this?"
9:21 From one lump of clay, does the potter not have the authority to make one vessel for honor and another for dishonor?
9:22 What if God, wanting to show his anger and make his power known, bore with much patience the vessels of anger, fitted for destruction?
9:23 And what if it was so that the riches of his glory would be made known toward the vessels of mercy, which he prepared ahead of time for glory?
## Called From Jews and Gentiles
9:24 That means us, the ones he called, not only from among the Jews but also from among the Gentiles.
9:25 As he also says in Hosea: "I will call 'my people' those who are not my people, and 'loved' her who was not loved."
9:26 "And in the place where they were told, 'You are not my people,' there they will be named sons of the living God."
9:27 Isaiah shouts out about Israel: "Though the number of the Israelites is like the sand by the sea, only the remnant will be saved.
9:28 For the Lord will carry out his word on the earth, completing it and cutting it short."
9:29 And as Isaiah said earlier: "Unless the Lord of Armies had left us descendants, we would have become like Sodom and been made like Gomorrah."
## The Stone to Trip Over
9:30 What, then, will we say? That Gentiles, who were not running after righteousness, laid hold of righteousness, a righteousness that comes from faith.
9:31 But Israel, chasing after a law of righteousness, did not arrive at that law.
9:32 Why was that? Because they went after it not by faith but as if it came by works. They tripped over the stone of stumbling,
9:33 as it is written: "Look, I am setting in Zion a stone that makes people stumble and a rock that makes them fall, and the one who believes in him will not be put to shame."
## Zeal Without Knowledge
10:1 Brothers, what my heart longs for, and what I ask of God on their behalf, is their salvation.
10:2 I bear witness about them: they have zeal for God, but it does not go along with knowledge.
10:3 They did not know God's righteousness, and they were trying to set up a righteousness of their own. So they did not submit to God's righteousness.
10:4 For the end of the law is Christ, leading to righteousness for everyone who believes.
## The Word Near You
10:5 Moses writes that the person who does the righteousness that comes from the law will live by it.
10:6 But the righteousness that comes from faith speaks like this: "Do not say in your heart, 'Who will go up into heaven?'" (That is, to bring Christ down.)
10:7 "Or, 'Who will go down into the abyss?'" (That is, to bring Christ up from the dead.)
10:8 But what does it say? "Near you is the word, in your mouth and in your heart." That is the word of faith which we proclaim.
10:9 You will be saved if you confess Jesus as Lord with your mouth and in your heart believe that God raised him from the dead.
10:10 For belief in the heart leads to righteousness, and confession with the mouth leads to salvation.
10:11 For the Scripture says, "No one who believes in him will be put to shame."
10:12 For there is no difference between Jew and Greek. The same Lord is Lord of all, and he is rich toward everyone who calls on him.
10:13 For anyone at all who calls on the Lord's name will be saved.
## They Heard, but Did Not All Obey
10:14 But how can people call on someone they have not believed in? How can they believe in someone they have never heard? And how can they hear if no one proclaims him?
10:15 And how can people proclaim him unless they are sent? As it is written: "How lovely are the feet of those who tell the good news of good things!"
10:16 But they did not all obey the good news. For Isaiah says, "Lord, who has believed our report?"
10:17 So faith comes from listening, and listening comes by way of the word of Christ.
10:18 But I ask: is it that they did not hear? They did hear! "Their voice has gone out into the whole earth, and their words to the ends of the world."
10:19 But I ask: did Israel not know? First, Moses says, "I myself will stir you to jealousy by those who are no nation. By a nation without understanding I will make you angry."
10:20 And Isaiah goes so far as to say, "Those who were not looking for me found me. I became visible to those who were not asking about me."
10:21 But about Israel he says, "All day long I have stretched out my hands to a people who disobey and talk back."
## A Remnant, Chosen by Grace
11:1 So I ask: did God push his people away? By no means! I am an Israelite myself, from the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.
11:2 God did not push his people away, whom he knew beforehand. Or are you unaware of what the Scripture says in the story of Elijah, how he pleads with God against Israel?
11:3 "Lord, they have killed your prophets, they have torn down your altars, and I was left alone, and they are seeking my life."
11:4 But what did God's answer say to him? "I have left for myself seven thousand men who have not bent their knees to Baal."
11:5 In the same way, a remnant has come to be in this present time, chosen by grace.
11:6 Now if it comes by grace, it no longer comes from works. If it did, grace would no longer be grace.
11:7 What follows? Israel did not obtain what it is looking for. The chosen obtained it, and the rest were hardened,
11:8 as it is written: "God gave them a spirit of numbness: eyes that do not see and ears that do not hear, down to this very day."
11:9 And David says: "Let their table become a trap and a net, a stumbling block and a repayment to them.
11:10 Let their eyes go dark so that they cannot see, and keep their backs always bent over."
## Their Stumble, the World's Gain
11:11 So I ask: did they stumble so that they would fall? By no means! But because of their trespass, salvation has reached the Gentiles, to make them jealous.
11:12 If their trespass has enriched the world, and their defeat has enriched the Gentiles, how much more will their fullness bring!
11:13 Now I am speaking to you Gentiles. Since I am an apostle of the Gentiles, I honor my service,
11:14 hoping somehow to make my own flesh and blood jealous and to save some of them.
11:15 For if their casting off is the reconciling of the world, what will their welcome be but life from the dead?
## The Olive Tree
11:16 If the firstfruit is holy, the whole lump of dough is holy too. And if the root is holy, the branches are too.
11:17 If some of the branches were broken off, and you, a wild olive, were grafted in among them and became a sharer in the rich root of the olive tree,
11:18 do not boast over the branches. And if you do boast, remember: you are not holding up the root. The root is holding you up.
11:19 You will say, "Branches were snapped off, so that I might be grafted in."
11:20 Fair enough. It was by unbelief that they were broken off, and it is by faith that you stand. Do not think highly of yourself. Be afraid instead.
11:21 For if God did not spare the branches that belong to the tree by nature, he will not spare you either.
11:22 So consider God's kindness and God's severity. Severity falls on those who fell, but kindness is for you, if you stay in that kindness. If you do not, you will be cut off as well.
11:23 And they too, if they do not remain in unbelief, will be grafted in. God is able to graft them back in.
11:24 You were cut out of a tree that is wild by nature, and you were grafted, against nature, into a garden olive tree. Then how much more will these branches, which belong to it by nature, be grafted into their own tree!
## The Mystery About Israel
11:25 Brothers, I want you to know this mystery, so that you will not think you are wise on your own: a hardening has come upon part of Israel, until the full number of the Gentiles comes in,
11:26 and so all Israel will be saved, just as it is written: "The Deliverer will come out of Zion. He will turn ungodliness away from Jacob.
11:27 And this is the covenant from me for them: when I take away their sins."
11:28 From the point of view of the good news, they are enemies, for your sake. From the point of view of election, they are beloved, for the sake of the patriarchs.
11:29 For God does not take back his gifts and his call.
11:30 You yourselves once disobeyed God. Now you have been shown mercy because they disobeyed.
11:31 So they also have now been disobedient, so that, by the mercy shown to you, they also may now be shown mercy.
11:32 For God has shut everyone up together in disobedience, so that he can show mercy to everyone.
## The Depth of God's Riches
11:33 Oh, how deep are God's riches, his wisdom and his knowledge! No one can search out his judgments or follow the tracks of his ways!
11:34 "For who has come to know the Lord's mind? Or who has been his adviser?"
11:35 "Or who has given to him first, so that it will be paid back to him?"
11:36 For everything is from him, and through him, and for him. To him be the glory for all the ages. Amen.
