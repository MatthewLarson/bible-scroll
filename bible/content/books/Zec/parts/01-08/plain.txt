## A call to turn back
1:1 In the eighth month of the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet:
1:2 The LORD was angry with your ancestors, truly angry.
1:3 So tell them: The LORD of Armies says this: Return to me, the LORD of Armies declares, and I will return to you, says the LORD of Armies.
1:4 Do not be like your ancestors. The earlier prophets called out to them, "The LORD of Armies says this: Turn back, please, from your evil ways and your evil deeds." But they did not listen, and they did not pay attention to me, the LORD declares.
1:5 Your ancestors, where are they now? And the prophets, do they live forever?
1:6 But my words and my rules, the orders I gave my servants the prophets, did they not overtake your ancestors? So they turned back and said, "The LORD of Armies planned to deal with us as our ways and our deeds deserved, and that is what he has done to us."
## The riders among the myrtle trees
1:7 On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, the month of Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet:
1:8 In the night I saw a man riding a red horse. He had stopped among the myrtle trees in the hollow, and behind him were red horses, reddish-brown horses and white horses.
1:9 I asked, "What are these, my lord?" The angel who spoke with me said to me, "I will show you what these are."
1:10 Then the man standing among the myrtle trees answered, "These are the ones the LORD has sent to walk about the earth."
1:11 They answered the angel of the LORD, the one standing among the myrtle trees, "We have walked about the earth, and see, the whole earth is settled and quiet."
1:12 Then the angel of the LORD said, "LORD of Armies, how long will you refuse to have compassion on Jerusalem and the cities of Judah? You have been indignant with them for seventy years now."
1:13 The LORD answered the angel who spoke with me with good words, with comforting words.
1:14 Then the angel who spoke with me said to me, "Call out: The LORD of Armies says this: I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion, with a great jealousy.
1:15 I am very angry with the nations that are at ease. I was only a little angry, but they helped make the harm worse.
1:16 So the LORD says this: I have come back to Jerusalem with compassion. My house will be rebuilt in it, the LORD of Armies declares, and a measuring line will be stretched across Jerusalem.
1:17 Call out again: The LORD of Armies says this: My cities will overflow again with good things. Once more the LORD will comfort Zion, and once more he will choose Jerusalem."
## Four horns and four craftsmen
1:18 I looked up and saw four horns.
1:19 I asked the angel who spoke with me, "What are these?" He said to me, "These horns are what scattered Judah, Israel and Jerusalem."
1:20 Then the LORD let me see four craftsmen.
1:21 I asked, "What are they coming to do?" He said, "Those are the horns that scattered Judah, and no one lifted up his head. These craftsmen have come to terrify those horns and to throw down the horns of the nations that lifted a horn against the land of Judah to scatter it."
## The man with the measuring cord
2:1 I looked up and saw a man holding a measuring cord.
2:2 I asked, "Where are you going?" He said to me, "To measure Jerusalem, to see how wide it is and how long it is."
2:3 Then the angel who spoke with me went out, and another angel went out to meet him.
2:4 He said to him, "Run, speak to that young man: Jerusalem will be lived in like open country, without walls, because of the many people and animals in it.
2:5 I myself will be a wall of fire around her, the LORD declares, and inside her I will be her glory."
## Come away from Babylon
2:6 Come! Come! Run away from the northern land, the LORD declares. / I have spread you out like the four winds of the heavens, the LORD declares.
2:7 Come, Zion! Escape, you who live with Daughter Babylon.
2:8 For the LORD of Armies says this: After glory he sent me to the nations that looted you. / Whoever touches you touches the pupil of his eye.
2:9 See, I am about to wave my hand over them, / and they will become loot for their own slaves. / Then you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me.
2:10 Shout for joy and be glad, Daughter Zion. / I am about to come, and I will make my home among you, the LORD declares.
2:11 On that day many nations will attach themselves to the LORD, / and they will become my people. / I will make my home among you, / and you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you.
2:12 The LORD will take Judah as his own share in the holy land, / and again he will choose Jerusalem.
2:13 Hush, all flesh, in the LORD's presence! / He has stirred himself from his holy dwelling place.
## Joshua and the Accuser
3:1 He showed me Joshua the high priest. Joshua was standing in front of the angel of the LORD, and the Accuser was standing on his right to accuse him.
3:2 The LORD said to the Accuser, "May the LORD rebuke you, Accuser! Yes, may the LORD rebuke you, the one who has chosen Jerusalem. Is this man not a piece of firewood snatched from the fire?"
3:3 Joshua stood in front of the angel in clothes that were filthy.
3:4 The angel spoke to those standing before him: "Take the filthy clothes off him." Then he said to Joshua, "See, I have taken your guilt away from you, and I will dress you in fine robes."
3:5 I said, "Have them set a clean turban on his head." So they wrapped the clean turban around his head and put clothes on him, while the angel of the LORD stayed standing there.
3:6 The angel of the LORD gave Joshua this solemn charge:
3:7 The LORD of Armies says this: If you live by my ways and do the duty I assigned you, then you in turn will rule over my house and watch over my courtyards, and I will let you walk among these who stand here.
3:8 Listen, Joshua the high priest, you and the companions sitting in front of you. These men are a sign: I am about to bring my servant, the Branch.
3:9 See the stone I have set before Joshua. On this one stone are seven eyes. I am about to carve its inscription, the LORD of Armies declares, and I will remove the guilt of this land in one day.
3:10 On that day, the LORD of Armies declares, neighbors will call to neighbors to come and sit under a vine and under a fig tree.
## The golden lampstand
4:1 The angel who spoke with me returned and shook me awake, the way someone is stirred from sleep.
4:2 He asked me, "What are you looking at?" I said, "I see a lampstand made entirely of gold, with a bowl at its top. Seven lamps sit on it, with seven and seven pipes leading to the lamps at the top.
4:3 Beside it stand two olive trees, one to the right of the bowl and one to its left."
4:4 I answered the angel who spoke with me, "What are these, my lord?"
4:5 The angel who spoke with me answered, "Do you not know what these are?" "No, my lord," I said.
4:6 Then he answered me, "This is the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: / Not by strength and not by force, but by my Spirit, says the LORD of Armies.
4:7 Who are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will become level ground. / He will bring out the top stone to shouts of 'Favor, favor to it!'"
4:8 The word of the LORD came to me:
4:9 Zerubbabel's own hands founded this house, and his hands will bring it to completion. Then you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you.
4:10 Who has looked down on a day when things were small? They will rejoice when they see the tin stone in Zerubbabel's hand. These seven are the LORD's eyes, and they roam over all the earth.
4:11 Next I asked him, "What are these two olive trees, the one right of the lampstand and the one left of it?"
4:12 I asked again, a second time, "What are the two olive branches next to the two gold spouts that empty out the gold?"
4:13 He said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" "No, my lord," I said.
4:14 He said, "These are the two sons of fresh oil who stand by the Lord of the whole earth."
## The flying scroll
5:1 I looked up again and saw a scroll flying.
5:2 He asked me, "What do you see?" I said, "I see a flying scroll. It is twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide."
5:3 He said to me, "This is the curse that goes out across the whole country. Everyone who steals will be swept away, according to one side of it. And everyone who swears will be swept away, according to the other side of it.
5:4 I will send it out, the LORD of Armies declares. It will come into the house of the thief and into the house of the one who swears falsely by my name. It will stay inside that house and destroy it, its timbers and its stones."
## The woman in the ephah
5:5 The angel who spoke with me went out ahead and said to me, "Look up, please, and see what this is, the thing that is coming out."
5:6 I asked, "What is it?" He said, "This is the ephah that is going out." And he said, "This is how they look in all the land."
5:7 A round lead lid was raised, and a woman was sitting in the middle of the ephah.
5:8 He said, "This is Wickedness!" Then he shoved her back down inside the ephah and flung the lead weight across its mouth.
5:9 I looked up and saw two women coming out, and the wind filled their wings. Their wings were like a stork's, and they carried the ephah up between earth and sky.
5:10 I asked the angel who spoke with me, "Where are they taking the ephah?"
5:11 He said to me, "To build a house for it in the land of Shinar. Once the house is ready, the ephah will be placed there on its own stand."
## The four chariots
6:1 I looked up again and saw four chariots rolling out from between two mountains, mountains made of bronze.
6:2 Red horses pulled the first chariot and black horses the second.
6:3 White horses pulled the third chariot and dappled horses the fourth, every one of them strong.
6:4 I asked the angel who spoke with me, "What are these, my lord?"
6:5 The angel answered me, "These are the four winds of the heavens. They are going out after standing before the Lord of all the earth.
6:6 The black horses are heading for the land of the north, the white horses have gone out after them, and the dappled horses have gone out to the land of the south."
6:7 The strong horses went out, and they wanted to go and walk about the earth. He said, "Go, walk about the earth." So they walked about the earth.
6:8 Then he called out to me, "See, those going out to the land of the north have quieted my spirit in the land of the north."
## Crowns for Joshua
6:9 The word of the LORD came to me:
6:10 Take from the exiles, from Heldai, from Tobiah and from Jedaiah, and go yourself that same day to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah. They have come from Babylon.
6:11 Take silver and gold, make crowns, and set them on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.
6:12 Tell him: The LORD of Armies says this: Look, there is a man whose name is Branch. From his own place he will branch out, and he will build the LORD's temple.
6:13 He himself will build the LORD's temple. He will bear majesty, and he will take his seat and rule from his throne. He will also be a priest on that throne, and a plan of peace will be between the two of them.
6:14 The crowns will belong to Helem, Tobiah, Jedaiah and Hen son of Zephaniah, as a memorial in the LORD's temple.
6:15 Those far away will come and build the LORD's temple, and you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you. And it will be so if you really listen to the voice of the LORD your God.
## The question about fasting
7:1 On the fourth day of the ninth month, Kislev, in King Darius's fourth year, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah.
7:2 Bethel sent Sharezer and Regem-melech, with his men, to ask the LORD's favor,
7:3 and to say to the priests of the house of the LORD of Armies and to the prophets, "Am I to go on weeping in the fifth month and keeping myself apart, as I have done all these years?"
7:4 Then the word of the LORD of Armies came to me:
7:5 Tell all the people of the land and the priests: For these seventy years you fasted and mourned in the fifth month and the seventh. Did you really fast for me, for me?
7:6 And when you eat and drink, are you not the ones who eat and the ones who drink?
7:7 Are these not the words the LORD proclaimed through the earlier prophets, back when Jerusalem was settled and at ease, with her towns around her, and the Negev and the Shephelah were settled too?
## What the earlier prophets said
7:8 The word of the LORD came to Zechariah:
7:9 The LORD of Armies says this: Judge with true justice. Show loyal love and compassion, each to his brother.
7:10 Do not oppress the widow or the fatherless, the foreigner who lives among you or the poor. And do not plan harm against one another in your hearts.
7:11 But they refused to pay attention. They gave a defiant shoulder and made their ears too heavy to hear.
7:12 They turned their hearts into flint, so they would not hear the law and the words that the LORD of Armies had sent by his Spirit through the earlier prophets. So a great wrath came from the LORD of Armies.
7:13 When he called, they would not listen. So when they called, I would not listen, says the LORD of Armies.
7:14 I scattered them like a storm among all the nations they had not known. The land was left desolate behind them, with no one passing through or returning, and so they turned a land of delight into a waste.
## The LORD returns to Zion
8:1 The word of the LORD of Armies came:
8:2 The LORD of Armies says this: I am fiercely jealous for Zion, with a great jealousy, and my fury on her behalf is great.
8:3 The LORD says this: I have returned to Zion, and I will make my home in the middle of Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be called the City of Truth, and the mountain of the LORD of Armies the Holy Mountain.
8:4 The LORD of Armies says this: Once more old men and old women will sit in Jerusalem's squares, each holding a staff, because they have lived so many days.
8:5 Boys and girls will fill the city's squares, playing there.
8:6 The LORD of Armies says this: If it seems too wonderful to the remnant of this people in those days, will it also seem too wonderful to me? the LORD of Armies declares.
8:7 The LORD of Armies says this: I am about to save my people from the land of the east and from the land of the setting sun.
8:8 I will bring them, and they will live in the middle of Jerusalem. They will be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.
## Strong hands and true words
8:9 The LORD of Armies says this: Let your hands be strong, all of you who are hearing these words now from the mouths of the prophets. They spoke on the day the LORD of Armies' house was founded, so that the temple could be built.
8:10 Before those days, people earned no pay and animals brought in nothing. Whoever left home or came back had no peace from the enemy, because I turned everyone against his neighbor.
8:11 But now I will not treat the remnant of this people as in the earlier days, the LORD of Armies declares.
8:12 Seed will be sown in peace. The vine will yield its fruit, the land will bring forth its harvest, and the heavens will let their dew fall. All of this I will hand down as an inheritance to the remnant of this people.
8:13 Just as you, house of Judah and house of Israel, were a curse among the nations, so I will save you, and you will be a blessing. Do not be afraid. Let your hands be strong.
8:14 For the LORD of Armies says this: As I planned to do you harm when your ancestors made me angry, says the LORD of Armies, and I did not change my mind,
8:15 so in these days I have turned again and planned to do good to Jerusalem and the house of Judah. Do not be afraid.
8:16 These are the things you are to do: Speak truth, each of you to your neighbor. In your gates, judge with truth and with justice that makes for peace.
8:17 Do not plan harm against one another in your hearts, and do not love a false oath. For I hate all these things, the LORD declares.
## Fasts become feasts
8:18 The word of the LORD of Armies came to me:
8:19 The LORD of Armies says this: The fast of the fourth month, the fast of the fifth, the fast of the seventh and the fast of the tenth will turn into joy and gladness and good festivals for the house of Judah. So love truth and peace.
8:20 The LORD of Armies says this: Once more, peoples will come, even the residents of many cities.
8:21 The residents of one city will go to another and say, "Come, let us hurry to ask the LORD's favor and to seek the LORD of Armies. I will go too."
8:22 Many peoples and mighty nations will come to seek the LORD of Armies in Jerusalem and to ask the LORD's favor.
8:23 The LORD of Armies says this: In those days ten men, from every tongue the nations speak, will grab hold of the corner of one Jew's robe. They will say, "We want to go with you, for we have heard that God is with you."
