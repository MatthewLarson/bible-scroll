## The oracle against the neighbors
9:1 An oracle: the word of the LORD falls on the land of Hadrach, and Damascus is where it settles. / Every human eye, and every tribe of Israel, looks to the LORD.
9:2 It falls on Hamath too, Damascus's neighbor, / and on Tyre and Sidon, because they think themselves so clever.
9:3 Tyre built herself a fortress. / She stacked up silver like dust and gold like street mud.
9:4 Look, the Lord will take her over. / He will batter her strength in the sea, and fire will eat her up.
9:5 Ashkelon will watch and be terrified. Gaza will twist in great pain, and Ekron too, / because the thing she trusted has been put to shame. / Gaza will lose its king, and no one will settle in Ashkelon.
9:6 A mixed people will settle in Ashdod, / and I will cut off the Philistines' pride.
9:7 I will remove the blood from their mouths and the detestable things from between their teeth. / Those of them who remain will be kept for our God, like a chief in Judah, and Ekron will be like the Jebusites.
9:8 I will pitch my tent by my house to stand guard, so that no one passes by in either direction. / An oppressor will never cross over them again, because now I have seen with my own eyes.
## The king on a donkey
9:9 Be very glad, Daughter Zion. / Shout for joy, Daughter Jerusalem. / Look, your king is coming to you. / He is righteous, and he has been rescued. / He is poor and rides on a donkey, on a young male donkey, the foal of a she-donkey.
9:10 I will take the chariot away from Ephraim and the horse from Jerusalem. / The bow used in war will be cut off. / His word to the nations will be peace. / His rule will run from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth.
## The prisoners of hope
9:11 And you, too: because of the blood of your covenant, / I will let your prisoners go out of a pit with no water in it.
9:12 Come back to the fortress, you prisoners of hope. / This very day I announce it: I will give back double to you.
9:13 For I have bent Judah like a bow for myself, and I have loaded it with Ephraim. / I will wake your sons, Zion, against your sons, Greece, / and I will turn you into a hero's sword.
9:14 The LORD will show himself above them, and his arrow will fly out like lightning. / The Lord GOD will blast the ram's horn and walk in the gales from the south.
9:15 The LORD of Armies will be their defense. / They will eat, and they will crush the sling stones. / They will drink and be loud as if with wine. / They will be full like the sprinkling bowl, like the corners of the altar.
9:16 The LORD their God will save them on that day, his people, like a flock. / For they are stones from a crown, flashing above his land.
9:17 How good it will be, and how beautiful! / Grain will make young men bloom, and new wine will make young women bloom.
## Rain and false shepherds
10:1 Ask the LORD for rain when the late rains are due. / He is the one who makes the thunderclouds, and he gives people their showers, and green plants in the field to each one.
10:2 For the teraphim speak harm, and the diviners have seen lies. / The dreamers tell worthless dreams and comfort with nothing. / So the people set out like sheep. They suffer, because they have no shepherd.
10:3 I am furious at the shepherds, and I will call the lead goats to account. / For the LORD of Armies will take care of his flock, the house of Judah, / and turn them into his own war horse, splendid in battle.
10:4 From him come the cornerstone and the tent peg. / From him come the bow of war and every ruler, all together.
10:5 They will be like warriors trampling the mud of the streets in battle. / Since the LORD is with them, they will fight, and the men on horses will be put to shame.
## The LORD brings his flock home
10:6 I will make the house of Judah strong, and I will save the house of Joseph. / I will bring them home, because I have compassion on them. / They will be as though I had never rejected them. / I am the LORD their God, and I will answer them.
10:7 Ephraim will be as strong as a hero. Their hearts will feel the joy of wine. / Their children will see it and cheer, and their hearts will burst with joy in the LORD.
10:8 I will whistle for them and bring them together. / I have redeemed them, and they will grow as numerous as they ever were.
10:9 I will sow them among the peoples, and in far places they will remember me. / They will live, they and their children, and they will come back.
10:10 I will return them from the land of Egypt and gather them from Assyria. / I will bring them to the land of Gilead and to Lebanon, and even then the land will be too small for them.
10:11 He will cross the sea of distress and beat down the waves in the sea. / The Nile's deepest places will dry up. / Assyria's pride will be torn down, and Egypt's scepter will be gone.
10:12 I will give them strength through the LORD, and they will go about in his name, the LORD declares.
## The fallen forests
11:1 Open your doors, Lebanon, / so that fire can eat your cedars.
11:2 Wail, cypress, because the cedar has fallen, because the mighty trees are wrecked. / Wail, oaks of Bashan, because the forest no one could enter has come down.
11:3 Hear the shepherds wail, for their glory has been ruined! / Hear the roaring of young lions, for the thickets along the Jordan are ruined.
## The two staffs
11:4 The LORD my God says this: Shepherd the flock that is meant for killing.
11:5 Their buyers kill them and are never held guilty. Their sellers say, "Blessed is the LORD, I am getting rich!" And their own shepherds have no pity on them.
11:6 For I will no longer have compassion on those who live in the land, the LORD declares. Look, I am about to hand everyone over to his neighbor and to his king. They will crush the land, and I will not rescue anyone from their hand.
11:7 So I shepherded the flock meant for killing, the poor of the flock. I took two staffs for myself. One I named Favor, and the other I named Union. And I shepherded the flock.
11:8 Within one month I removed the three shepherds. I ran out of patience with them, and they hated me too.
11:9 Then I said, "I will not be your shepherd. Let the one that is dying die, and the one that is being destroyed be destroyed. Let those who are left eat each other's flesh."
11:10 Then I picked up my staff Favor and hacked it in pieces, breaking the covenant I had cut with all the peoples.
11:11 It was broken that day, and the poor of the flock, who had their eyes on me, understood that this was the LORD's word.
11:12 I said to them, "If it seems right to you, give me my pay. If not, keep it." So they weighed out my pay: thirty shekels of silver.
11:13 The LORD told me, "Throw it to the potter, that splendid price they put on me!" So I took the thirty shekels of silver and threw them in the LORD's house, to the potter.
11:14 Then I cut my second staff, Union, in pieces, to break the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.
## The foolish shepherd
11:15 Again the LORD said to me, "Take the tools of a foolish shepherd.
11:16 For look, I am about to raise up a shepherd in the land. He will not care for the sheep that are being destroyed. He will not look for the young one. He will not heal the broken one. He will not feed the one that stands firm. He will eat the meat of the fat sheep and tear off their hooves.
11:17 Woe to my worthless shepherd, who leaves the flock! / A sword against his arm and his right eye! / His arm will wither and dry up, / and his right eye will go dark and blind."
## Jerusalem, a stone too heavy
12:1 An oracle: the word of the LORD about Israel. This is the declaration of the LORD, who stretches out the heavens, lays the base of the earth, and forms the spirit of a human being inside him.
12:2 Look, I am about to make Jerusalem a bowl that makes all the peoples around her stagger. It will be that way for Judah too, in the siege against Jerusalem.
12:3 I will make Jerusalem, on that day, a stone too heavy for any of the peoples. All who lift it will tear themselves badly. Every nation on earth will gather to attack it.
12:4 On that day, the LORD declares, I will hit every horse with terror and its rider with madness. I will keep my eyes open on the house of Judah, while every horse belonging to the peoples goes blind.
12:5 Then Judah's clan leaders will think: "The people of Jerusalem are my strength, because of the LORD of Armies, their God."
12:6 On that day I will turn Judah's clan leaders into a fire pot in a stack of wood, a blazing torch in cut grain. They will burn up all the peoples around them, to the right and to the left. And Jerusalem will live again in its own place, in Jerusalem.
12:7 First the LORD will rescue the tents of Judah, so that the splendor of David's house and the splendor of the people living in Jerusalem will not be greater than Judah's.
12:8 On that day the LORD will put a shield around the people living in Jerusalem. The one who stumbles among them on that day will be like David, and David's house will be like God, like the angel of the LORD at their head.
12:9 On that day I will go after every nation that comes against Jerusalem, to wipe it out.
## The one they pierced
12:10 On the house of David and on the people living in Jerusalem I will pour out a spirit of favor and pleading. They will look to me, the one they pierced through, and they will cry out in grief over him, as people grieve for an only son. They will sob bitterly for him, as for a firstborn.
12:11 On that day there will be great mourning in Jerusalem, like the mourning of Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.
12:12 The land will mourn, clan by clan, each by itself: the clan of David's house by itself, and their wives by themselves; the clan of Nathan's house by itself, and their wives by themselves;
12:13 the clan of Levi's house by itself, and their wives by themselves; the clan of the Shimeites by itself, and their wives by themselves;
12:14 and all the clans that are left, each clan by itself, and their wives by themselves.
## A spring for sin
13:1 On that day a spring will be opened for the house of David and the people living in Jerusalem, for sin and for impurity.
13:2 On that day, the LORD of Armies declares, I will erase the names of the idols from the land, and they will not be remembered again. I will also remove the prophets and the spirit of uncleanness from the land.
13:3 When anyone prophesies again, his own father and mother, the ones who gave birth to him, will tell him, "You cannot stay alive, for you have spoken a lie in the LORD's name." Then his own father and mother will pierce him through while he is prophesying.
13:4 On that day every prophet will feel shame about his visions as he speaks. None will put on a cloak of hair to fool people.
13:5 He will say, "I am no prophet. I am a man who works the ground, because a man has owned me since I was young."
13:6 If someone says to him, "What are these wounds between your hands?" he will answer, "I was wounded in the house of my friends."
## Strike the shepherd
13:7 Sword, wake up against my shepherd, / against the man who is my partner, the LORD of Armies declares. / Strike the shepherd, and let the sheep be scattered, / and I will turn my hand on the little ones.
13:8 In the whole land, the LORD declares, / two parts will be cut off and die, and the third part will be left in it.
13:9 I will bring the third part into the fire, / and refine them the way silver is refined, and test them the way gold is tested. / He will call on my name, and I will answer him. / I will say, "This is my people," and he will say, "The LORD is my God."
## The day of the LORD
14:1 Look, a day of the LORD is coming, and the loot taken from you will be divided up in your midst.
14:2 I will gather every nation to Jerusalem for war. They will take the city, loot the houses and rape the women. Half of the city's people will be marched into exile, but the rest will not be removed from the city.
14:3 Then the LORD will march out and make war on those nations, as on the day he fought in battle.
14:4 His feet will stand, on that day, on the Mount of Olives, which faces Jerusalem on the east. The Mount of Olives will be split open from its middle, east and west, with a very great valley between. Half the mountain will shift to the north, and half of it to the south.
14:5 You will flee by the valley of my mountains, for the valley between the mountains will reach to Azal. You will run away just as you ran from the earthquake in the time of King Uzziah of Judah. And the LORD my God will arrive, and all the holy ones will be with you.
## Light and living water
14:6 On that day there will be no light, but cold and frost.
14:7 It will be one single day, known to the LORD: not day and not night. Then, at the hour of evening, there will be light.
14:8 On that day living waters will go out of Jerusalem, half of them toward the eastern sea and half toward the western sea. It will flow in summer and in winter alike.
14:9 The LORD will become king over the whole earth. On that day the LORD will be one, and his name will be one.
14:10 All the land will become flat, like the Arabah, from Geba down to Rimmon, south of Jerusalem. But Jerusalem will rise high and stay in its place, from the Benjamin Gate to the place of the First Gate, to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the king's winepresses.
14:11 People will live in it. It will never again be put under the ban, and Jerusalem will live in safety.
## The plague and the Feast of Booths
14:12 This is the plague the LORD will use to strike every people that fought against Jerusalem: Their flesh will rot while they are still standing. Their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues in their mouths.
14:13 On that day the LORD will throw them into a huge panic. Each man will grab his neighbor's hand, and then raise his own hand against it.
14:14 Judah will also be fighting at Jerusalem. The wealth of all the nations around will be gathered up: gold, silver and clothing in huge amounts.
14:15 A plague like that one will strike the horse, the mule, the camel, the donkey and every animal in those camps.
14:16 Then everyone left of all the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up every year to bow down to the King, the LORD of Armies, and to keep the Feast of Booths.
14:17 If any of the clans of the earth do not go up to Jerusalem to bow down to the King, the LORD of Armies, no rain will fall on them.
14:18 If the clan of Egypt does not go up or come, no rain will fall on them. The plague will strike them, the plague with which the LORD strikes the nations that do not go up to keep the Feast of Booths.
14:19 That is the price Egypt will pay, and the price every nation will pay that does not go up to keep the Feast of Booths.
14:20 On that day the bells on the horses will say, "Holy to the LORD." The cooking pots inside the LORD's house will be just like the bowls in front of the altar.
14:21 Every pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be a holy thing for the LORD of Armies. All who come to sacrifice will take some of them and boil meat in them. And on that day the house of the LORD of Armies will have no Canaanite in it any more.
