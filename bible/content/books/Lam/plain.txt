## The City Sits Alone
1:1 How she sits alone, the city that was crowded with people! / She who was great among the nations has become like a widow. / A princess among the provinces, she has been made a forced laborer.
1:2 She weeps and weeps in the night, her tears on her cheek. / Of all who loved her, she has no one to comfort her. / Every friend of hers has turned traitor; they have become her enemies.
1:3 Gone into exile is Judah, driven by misery and by heavy slavery. / She lives among the nations and has found no place to rest. / All who chased her have caught her in the narrow places.
1:4 Zion's roads are in mourning, since nobody travels up to the festival. / Her gates are all deserted; her priests sigh. / Her young women are in grief, and she herself tastes bitterness.
1:5 Her adversaries are now on top; her enemies live without worry. / The LORD has given her sorrow for her piled-up rebellions. / Her small children walked off as prisoners, driven ahead of the foe.
1:6 Every bit of her splendor has left Daughter Zion. / Her princes are like deer that cannot find grass, / and they trudge on, weak, in front of the hunter.
1:7 Jerusalem remembers her days of misery and homelessness, all the treasures she had in ancient days. / When her people fell into the foe's hand, no one came to help her. / Her foes looked at her; they laughed at her ruin.
1:8 Jerusalem sinned and sinned, and so she became an unclean thing. / Everyone who prized her looks down on her, because they have seen her bare. / She herself moans and turns her back.
1:9 Her uncleanness stained her hem; she never thought of how it would end. / She came down in astonishing ways, with no one to comfort her. / See my affliction, LORD, for the enemy has grown great.
1:10 A foe has reached out his hand to take all her treasures. / She watched nations walk into her sanctuary, / the very ones you ordered never to enter your assembly.
1:11 Her whole people sigh as they hunt for bread. / They trade away their treasures for food, to keep life in them. / See, LORD, and look: I have become worthless.
## Zion Speaks
1:12 Does this not concern you, you travelers on the road? / Look, and see: could there be any pain like mine, the pain that was done to me, / that the LORD laid on me in the day of his burning anger?
1:13 He sent fire from above into my bones, and it conquered them. / He laid out a net for my feet and forced me back. / He has made me a desolation, sick the whole day long.
1:14 The yoke of my rebellions is bound on; by his hand they are twisted together. / They have climbed onto my neck; he has drained my strength. / The Lord has put me in the grip of people I cannot stand up against.
1:15 The Lord has thrown away all my mighty men from my midst. / He has called a festival against me, to smash my young warriors. / The Lord has trodden the winepress for the virgin, Daughter Judah.
1:16 Over these things I am weeping; my eye, my eye runs down with water, / for a comforter is far from me, one who would bring my life back. / My children are ruined, for the enemy has won out.
1:17 Zion spreads out her hands; there is no one to comfort her. / The LORD has ordered that Jacob's neighbors all become his foes. / Jerusalem has become an unclean thing among them.
1:18 The LORD is righteous, for I defied his command. / Listen, all you peoples, and see my pain: / my young women and young men have gone away as prisoners.
1:19 I cried out to those who loved me, and they cheated me. / My priests and my elders died in the city / as they looked for food to bring their lives back.
1:20 Look, LORD, I am in distress; my insides are in turmoil, / and my heart is turned upside down inside me, because I defied him so bitterly. / Outside, the sword takes away children; inside the house, it is like death.
1:21 They have heard that I groan; I have no one to comfort me. / Every enemy of mine has heard of my trouble and is delighted that you did it. / You have brought on the day you proclaimed; now let them be like me.
1:22 May all their wickedness stand before you. / Do to them what you did to me for all my rebellions. / My groans are many, and my heart is ill.
## The Lord as Enemy
2:1 How the Lord, in his anger, has wrapped Daughter Zion in cloud! / From heaven to earth he has hurled the beauty of Israel, / and on the day of his anger he gave no thought to his footstool.
2:2 The Lord has swallowed every home of Jacob and shown no pity. / In his fury he has smashed the fortresses of Daughter Judah, / thrown them to the ground, and treated the kingdom and its princes as unholy.
2:3 In burning anger he has cut off every horn of Israel. / He pulled back his right hand when the enemy came on. / He has blazed in Jacob like a fire of flame, eating everything around.
2:4 He has strung his bow like an enemy; his right hand is set like a foe's. / He has killed everything dear to the eye. / In the tent of Daughter Zion he has emptied out his rage like fire.
2:5 The Lord has turned into an enemy. He swallowed Israel, / swallowed all her palaces, and ruined her fortresses. / He has multiplied mourning and moaning in Daughter Judah.
2:6 He has wrecked his shelter like a garden hut and ruined the place where he meets his people. / The LORD has made festival and Sabbath forgotten in Zion. / In the indignation of his anger he has treated king and priest with contempt.
2:7 The Lord has thrown out his altar and cast off his holy place. / The walls of her palaces he put into an enemy's hand. / Voices rang out in the LORD's house like voices on a festival day.
2:8 The LORD made up his mind to bring down the wall of Daughter Zion. / He stretched a measuring line over it and did not hold back his hand from swallowing. / He made rampart and wall mourn; together they have wasted away.
2:9 Down into the earth her gates have sunk; he has smashed and snapped her bars. / Her king and her princes are living among the nations; instruction is gone. / Her prophets, too, get no vision from the LORD.
2:10 On the ground they sit, silent, the elders of Daughter Zion. / Dust is on their heads; sackcloth is tied around them. / The young women of Jerusalem hang their heads down to the earth.
## The Poet Weeps
2:11 My eyes are worn out with tears; my insides churn; my liver is poured out on the ground / because of the ruin of my dear people, / as child and nursing baby faint in the squares of the city.
2:12 They keep asking their mothers, "Where are the grain and wine?" / as they collapse like the wounded in the open squares of the city, / as their lives drain away against their mothers' chests.
2:13 What can I tell you? What can I compare with you, Daughter Jerusalem? / What can I match with you to comfort you, virgin Daughter Zion? / Your ruin is as big as the sea; who can heal you?
2:14 Your prophets saw visions for you, empty and false. / They never uncovered your guilt, so as to reverse your captivity. / They saw for you oracles that were empty and led you astray.
2:15 Everyone going by on the road claps hands over you; they whistle and wag their heads at Daughter Jerusalem. / "Is this the city people used to call perfect in beauty, / the joy of all the earth?"
2:16 Every enemy of yours opens a wide mouth at you. / They hiss and grind their teeth, saying, "We have swallowed her! / This is the very day we waited for. We found it. We saw it."
2:17 The LORD did what he had planned. He finished his word, / the order he gave in the days of old. He tore down and spared nothing; / he let the enemy rejoice over you and lifted high the horn of your foes.
2:18 Their hearts cry out to the Lord. / Wall of Daughter Zion, make your tears run down like a stream, by day and by night. / Do not let yourself rest; do not let your eye be still.
2:19 Stand up! Call out in the night, as the night watches begin. / Empty your heart like water before the Lord's face. Lift up your hands to him / for the sake of your little ones' lives, who are fainting with hunger at every street corner.
## Zion Prays
2:20 See, LORD, and look: whom have you ever treated like this? / Should women eat their own offspring, the children they have cradled? / Should a priest and a prophet be put to death inside the Lord's sanctuary?
2:21 Young and old lie on the ground in the streets. / By the sword fell my young men and my young women. / On the day of your anger you killed them; you slaughtered and showed no pity.
2:22 You called together my terrors from all around, as for a festival day. / On the day of the LORD's anger there was no one who got away, no one left alive. / The children I held and raised, my enemy has finished off.
## Alef: I Am the Man
3:1 I am the man; I have seen affliction from the rod of his fury.
3:2 He drove me on and made me walk in darkness, with no light.
3:3 Only against me does he turn his hand, over and over, all day.
## Bet: Bone and Skin Worn Away
3:4 My flesh and my skin he has wasted; he has snapped my bones.
3:5 He has raised a siege wall against me and ringed me with poison and toil.
3:6 He has made me live in dark places, like those long dead.
## Gimel: Walled In
3:7 He fenced me in behind a wall, so I cannot leave; he made my bronze chain heavy.
3:8 Even when I shout and cry for help, he keeps my prayer out.
3:9 My roads he has blocked with cut stone; he twisted my paths.
## Dalet: Bear, Lion, Arrow
3:10 To me he is a lurking bear, a lion in its hiding places.
3:11 He pulled me off my road and ripped me apart; he left me ruined.
3:12 He has drawn his bow and made me the mark for his arrow.
## He: The Arrows and the Wormwood
3:13 He has pierced my kidneys with the arrows from his quiver.
3:14 To all my people I have become a joke, and their mocking song lasts all day.
3:15 He has stuffed me with bitterness; he has made me drink wormwood.
## Waw: Gravel and Ashes
3:16 He crushed my teeth with gravel and flattened me in the ashes.
3:17 My life is cut off from peace; I have forgotten what good is.
3:18 I thought, "My strength to endure has perished, and my hope from the LORD."
## Zayin: This I Bring Back
3:19 Remember my affliction and my homelessness, the wormwood and the poison.
3:20 My soul remembers it again and again and sinks down within me.
3:21 This I bring back to my heart; therefore I will wait.
## Chet: The LORD's Loyal Love
3:22 It is the LORD's acts of loyal love that we have not come to our end, that his compassions have not run out.
3:23 They are new for the mornings; your faithfulness is great.
3:24 My soul says, "My portion is the LORD, so I will wait for him."
## Tet: It Is Good to Wait
3:25 Good is the LORD to people who wait for him, to anyone who looks for him.
3:26 Waiting in silence for the LORD's rescue is good.
3:27 Good for a man: to carry a yoke while he is young.
## Yod: The Dust and the Cheek
3:28 Let him sit alone and be silent, for he has laid it on him.
3:29 Let him lay his mouth in the dust; maybe there is hope.
3:30 Let him hand his cheek to whoever hits him; let him have his fill of shame.
## Kaf: He Does Not Reject Forever
3:31 For the Lord does not reject forever.
3:32 Though he causes grief, he will have compassion, as great as his acts of loyal love.
3:33 For it is not from his heart that he afflicts and grieves human beings.
## Lamed: Justice Turned Aside
3:34 To trample all the land's prisoners under his feet,
3:35 to bend a person's rights aside before the face of the Most High,
3:36 to subvert someone in his lawsuit: does the Lord not see it?
## Mem: Both from One Mouth
3:37 Who ever spoke, and it happened, if the Lord did not order it?
3:38 Do not troubles and good both go out from the mouth of the Most High?
3:39 Why should any living person grumble, any man, over his sins?
## Nun: Let Us Return
3:40 Let us search our ways and examine them, and let us return to the LORD.
3:41 Let us raise our hearts along with our palms to God in the heavens.
3:42 We, we have rebelled and defied; you, you have not forgiven.
## Samekh: Wrapped in Cloud
3:43 You covered yourself with anger and chased us; you killed and did not spare.
3:44 You covered yourself with cloud, and no prayer gets through.
3:45 Among the peoples you made us rubbish and trash.
## Pe: Open Mouths
3:46 All our enemies have opened their mouths wide against us.
3:47 Terror and the pit have come on us, devastation and ruin.
3:48 Streams of water run down my eye over the ruin of my dear people.
## Ayin: My Eye Pours Out
3:49 My eye pours itself out without stopping, with no relief,
3:50 until the LORD looks down and sees from heaven.
3:51 My eye brings me pain over all the daughters of my city.
## Tsade: Hunted Like a Bird
3:52 Those who were my enemies for no reason hunted me like a bird.
3:53 They cut off my life in a pit and threw a stone on me.
3:54 Water flowed over my head; I said, "I am cut off."
## Qof: You Came Near
3:55 From the lowest pit, LORD, I called your name.
3:56 You heard my voice: "Do not hide your ear from my cry for relief."
3:57 On the day I called to you, you drew near and said, "Do not be afraid."
## Resh: You Took Up My Case
3:58 You took up my case, Lord; you bought back my life.
3:59 LORD, you saw the injury done to me; give judgment in my case.
3:60 You saw all their revenge, all their schemes against me.
## Shin: Their Insults
3:61 You heard their insults, LORD, all their schemes against me.
3:62 What my attackers say with their lips, and their muttering, is against me all day.
3:63 Look at their sitting down and their rising up: I am their mocking song.
## Tav: Repay Them
3:64 You will repay them, LORD, for what their hands have done.
3:65 You will give them a covered heart; your curse will be on them.
3:66 You will chase them in anger and wipe them out from under the LORD's heavens.
## The Gold Grows Dim
4:1 How the gold has lost its shine! The finest gold has changed. / Holy stones are strewn at every street corner.
4:2 The sons of Zion, so precious, who were weighed against fine gold, / how they are now counted as pottery jars, something a potter makes by hand!
4:3 Even jackals pull out the breast and let their pups nurse, / but my dear people has turned cruel, like the ostriches of the wilderness.
4:4 A baby's tongue is stuck to his palate with thirst. / Children ask for bread, and no one breaks any for them.
4:5 Those who ate fine food lie desolate in the streets. / Those raised in crimson clutch at the ash heaps.
4:6 The guilt of my dear people is greater than the sin of Sodom, / which was overturned in a moment, with no hand turned against it.
4:7 Her nobles were cleaner than snow, whiter than milk. / Their bodies were redder than coral; their polish was like sapphire.
4:8 Now their faces are blacker than soot; no one knows them in the streets. / Their skin has pulled tight over their bones, dry as wood.
4:9 Those killed by the sword were better off than those killed by hunger, / who drain away, pierced through, for lack of the field's harvest.
4:10 Women known for compassion boiled their own children with their own hands; / the children became their food when my dear people was ruined.
4:11 The LORD has used up his rage and emptied out his burning anger. / He set a fire blazing in Zion, and it ate her foundations.
## Prophets and Priests
4:12 The kings of the earth and everyone living in the world / had never believed that a foe or enemy could walk through the gates of Jerusalem.
4:13 Because of the sins of her prophets and the guilt of her priests, / the ones who poured out righteous blood in her midst.
4:14 They staggered blind through the streets, so stained with blood / that no one could touch their clothes.
4:15 "Keep away, unclean!" people called out to them. "Keep away, keep away, do not touch!" / They fled and wandered, and among the nations people said, "They cannot stay here any longer."
4:16 The face of the LORD has scattered them; he will not turn his eyes to them again. / Priests were not given respect, and elders were not shown kindness.
4:17 Our eyes still failed as we looked in vain for help. / From our watchtower we kept watch for a nation that does not save.
4:18 They tracked every step we took, so we could not go out into our squares. / Our end drew near; our days ran out. Our end had come.
4:19 Those who chased us were quicker than eagles in the sky. / On the mountains they ran us down; in the wilderness they set an ambush.
4:20 The breath of our nostrils, the LORD's anointed, was trapped in their pits, / he about whom we used to say, "In his shade we will live among the nations."
## Edom's Cup
4:21 Celebrate and be happy, Daughter Edom, living in the land of Uz. / The cup is coming around to you too; you will get drunk and expose yourself.
4:22 Your guilt is paid in full, Daughter Zion; he will not exile you again. / Daughter Edom, he will call your guilt to account and lay your sins bare.
## Remember Us, LORD
5:1 LORD, remember what has come over us; look, and see the insults we bear.
5:2 Our inheritance has passed into the hands of outsiders, / our homes into the hands of foreigners.
5:3 We are orphans, no father among us; / our mothers are as good as widows.
5:4 We drink our own water for money; / our own wood comes to us at a price.
5:5 Pursuers are at our necks; / we are exhausted and get no rest.
5:6 We gave a hand to Egypt and to Assyria, to get enough bread.
5:7 Our ancestors sinned, and they are gone; / we are the ones who carry their guilt.
5:8 Slaves have become our rulers; no one frees us from their power.
5:9 We bring in our bread at the risk of our lives, / from the sword that lurks in the wilderness.
5:10 Our skin glows like an oven / from the scorching of hunger.
5:11 Women were raped in Zion, / young women in the towns of Judah.
5:12 Princes were strung up by their hands; / elders were given no honor.
5:13 Young men carry the millstone, / while boys stumble with the wood on their backs.
5:14 Old men no longer sit at the gate; / young men no longer make their music.
5:15 Our hearts have lost their joy; / our dancing has become mourning.
5:16 The crown of our head has fallen. / Oh, how terrible for us, because we sinned!
5:17 Over this our heart aches; / over these things our eyes have gone dark.
5:18 Because of Mount Zion, which is desolate: / foxes roam over it.
## You, LORD, Rule Forever
5:19 You, LORD, sit as king forever; / your throne lasts from generation to generation.
5:20 Why do you keep forgetting us, / leaving us for days without end?
5:21 Bring us back to yourself, LORD, and we will come back. / Make our days new as in ancient times,
5:22 unless you have rejected us altogether, / and your anger against us has gone too far.
