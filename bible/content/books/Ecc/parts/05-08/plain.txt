## In the house of God
5:1 Keep watch over your feet when you go to the house of God. Coming close to listen is better than giving a sacrifice the way fools do, for they do not know that they are doing evil.
5:2 Do not rush to open your mouth, and do not let your heart hurry to bring out a word before God. God is in the heavens, and you are on the earth, so let your words be few.
5:3 For dreams come with too many tasks, and a fool's voice comes with too many words.
5:4 When you vow something to God, do not be slow to pay it, for fools give him no pleasure. Whatever you vow, pay it.
5:5 Not vowing is better than vowing and not paying.
5:6 Do not let your mouth bring sin on your own flesh, and do not say before the messenger, "It was unintended." Why make God angry over what your voice said, so that he ruins what your hands have done?
5:7 For where dreams are many, there are vapors and too many words. But you, fear God.
## Officials over officials
5:8 If you see in the province the poor oppressed, and justice and what is right stolen away, do not be shocked by the business. For high above each high official another one is watching, and still higher ones are over them.
5:9 Yet in all this a land has gain: a king for the farmed field.
## The lover of silver
5:10 The lover of silver never gets his fill of silver, and for whoever loves plenty, no harvest is enough. This too is vapor.
5:11 The more good things there are, the more mouths eat them up. What do their owners get from them, except a sight for their eyes?
5:12 A worker's sleep is sweet, whether he has eaten little or a lot. But the rich man's fullness leaves him no rest to sleep.
5:13 There is a sickening evil I have seen under the sun: riches guarded by their owner, and they bring him harm.
5:14 Then that wealth is gone, lost in an evil task. He becomes father to a son, and there is nothing at all in his hand.
5:15 Just as he came out of his mother's womb, naked, so he will go back, going the way he came. Of all his toil he will carry off nothing to take away in his hand.
5:16 And this too is a sickening evil: in every way as he came, so he will go. What gain is there for him, that he toils for the wind?
5:17 Besides, he eats in darkness all his days, and he is greatly frustrated, with his sickness and his anger.
## God's gift
5:18 Look, here is what I have seen to be good and beautiful: for a person to eat and drink and see good in all his toil that he toils at under the sun, through the numbered days of his life that God has given him. For that is his share.
5:19 And further: when God gives any person riches and property and lets him be master of them, to eat from them, to carry off his share and to be glad in his toil, this is God's gift.
5:20 For he will not often call to mind the days of his life, since God keeps him busy with the joy of his heart.
## Riches without rest
6:1 There is an evil I have seen under the sun, and it presses hard on human beings.
6:2 Take a man whom God gives riches and property and honor. His appetite goes without nothing it craves. Yet God does not let him be master of it, to eat from it; a foreigner eats it instead. This is vapor, and a sickness, an evil one.
6:3 A man may father a hundred children and live many years; the days of his years may be many. But if his appetite does not get its fill of good, and he does not even have a burial, I say: better off than he is the baby born dead.
6:4 It came in vapor and it goes away in darkness, and darkness covers its name.
6:5 It has never even seen the sun, nor known it; yet this one has more rest than that one.
6:6 Even if he lived a thousand years, and then a thousand more, and saw no good, does not everything go to one place?
## The appetite never filled
6:7 All the toil of a human being goes to feed his mouth, and still his appetite does not fill up.
6:8 For what edge does the wise man have over the fool? What does a poor man have, though he knows how to walk his way in front of the living?
6:9 What the eyes see is better than an appetite on the move. This too is vapor and chasing the wind.
## Who knows what is good?
6:10 Whatever has been already had its name called long ago. It is known what a human being is: he cannot take to court one who is stronger than he is.
6:11 There are many words that only add vapor. What edge is there for a human being?
6:12 Who knows what is good for a human being while he is alive, through the numbered days of his vapor of a life, which he spends like a shadow? Who will tell a human being what is going to be after him under the sun?
## Better than
7:1 Better a good name than good oil, / and the day of death than the day he was born.
7:2 Going to a house where people mourn is better / than going to a house where they drink, / for that is the end of every human being, / and the one who is alive will take it to heart.
7:3 Grief is better than laughter, / for when the face is sad the heart grows better.
7:4 The wise have their heart in a house of mourning, / and fools have their heart in a house of joy.
7:5 Listening to a wise man's scolding is better / than being one who listens to the songs of fools.
7:6 For like the crackle of thorns beneath the pot, / so is the fool's laughter. / This too is vapor.
7:7 For oppression makes a fool of the wise, / and a gift ruins the heart.
7:8 How a thing ends is better than how it starts; / a long-suffering spirit is better than a lofty one.
7:9 Do not rush in your spirit to be angry, / for anger comes to rest in the bosom of fools.
7:10 Do not say, "How is it that the earlier days were better than these days?" / It is not wisdom that makes you ask about this.
7:11 Wisdom along with an inheritance is good, / and an edge for those who see the sun.
7:12 For to be in wisdom's shade is to be in silver's shade, / but knowledge has this gain: wisdom gives life to those who have it.
7:13 Look at the work of God. / Who is able to set straight what he has bent?
7:14 On a good day, enjoy the good, / and on a bad day, take a look: / God made this one to match that one, / so that a human being will not find out anything about what comes after him.
## Not too righteous
7:15 I have seen it all in my days of vapor. There is a righteous man who is lost in his righteousness, and there is a wicked man who lasts a long time in his evil.
7:16 Do not be righteous too much, and do not act the wise man to excess. Why bring ruin on yourself?
7:17 Do not be wicked too much, and do not be a fool. Why die when it is not your time?
7:18 It is good to grab hold of this one without letting your hand rest from that one. For the one who fears God will come through all of them.
7:19 Wisdom does more to make the wise strong than ten rulers who are in a city.
7:20 For there is no human being on the earth so righteous that he does good and does not sin.
7:21 Also, do not give your heart to every word that people speak, or you may hear your slave cursing you.
7:22 Your own heart knows how often you too have cursed other people.
## What I found and did not find
7:23 By wisdom I put all this to the test. I said, "Let me become wise." But wisdom stayed far away from me.
7:24 What has been is far away, and deep, deep down. Who can find it?
7:25 I went around, I and my mind, to know and to explore and to seek out wisdom and a sum, and to know that wickedness is stupidity and that folly is madness.
7:26 And what I find is more bitter than death: the woman who is a hunter's trap, whose heart is a net, whose hands are ropes that bind. The one who is good in God's eyes will slip away from her, but the sinner will be caught by her.
7:27 "See, this is what I have found," says Qohelet, "adding one to one to find the sum,
7:28 something I am still seeking for myself and have not found. One man out of a thousand I have found, but a woman among all these I have not found.
7:29 Only, look at what I have found: God made human beings straight, but they have gone looking for many schemes."
## The king's word
8:1 Who is like the wise man? Who knows how to interpret a matter? Wisdom lights up a person's face, and the hardness of his face is changed.
8:2 I say, do what the king's mouth tells you, and do it with God's oath in mind.
8:3 Do not rush to walk out of his presence. Do not stand firm in a bad cause, since whatever he wants, he does.
8:4 A king's word is in command, and who will say to him, "What are you doing?"
8:5 The one who keeps a command will not meet with anything evil, and a wise heart knows there is a time and a judgment.
8:6 For every matter has its time and its judgment, though the evil that humans face presses hard on them.
8:7 He has no idea what is coming, and who can tell him how it will come?
8:8 No human being is master of the wind, able to shut the wind in. No one is master over the day of death. There is no sending home in battle, and wickedness will not rescue its masters.
8:9 I saw all this when I put my mind to everything that is done under the sun, in a time when one human being rules over another, to his harm.
## When justice is slow
8:10 And then I saw wicked people buried. They had come and gone from the holy place, and they were forgotten in the very city where they had acted so. This too is vapor.
8:11 Since the ruling on an evil deed is not carried out quickly, the hearts of human beings are full and ready to do evil.
8:12 A sinner may do evil a hundred times and still live a long life. Yet I also know that it will be good for those who fear God, who are afraid in his presence.
8:13 But it will not be good for the wicked man. Like a shadow, he will not stretch out his days, since he is not afraid in God's presence.
8:14 There is a vapor that is done on the earth: there are righteous people who meet with what fits the deeds of the wicked, and there are wicked people who meet with what fits the deeds of the righteous. I said that this too is vapor.
8:15 So I spoke in praise of joy: a human being has nothing good under the sun except to eat and drink and be glad. That will keep him company in his toil, through the days of his life that God has given him under the sun.
## No one can find it out
8:16 When I put my mind to knowing wisdom and to looking at the task that is done on the earth (for even by day and by night a person's eyes see no sleep),
8:17 I looked at all the work of God: a human being is not able to find out the work that is done under the sun. For all a person toils to seek, he will not find it. And even if the wise man thinks he will know it, he is not able to find it.
