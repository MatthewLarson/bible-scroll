## Greeting to the church in Philadelphia
1:0 Ignatius, also called Theophorus, to the church of God the Father and of the Lord Jesus Christ that is in Philadelphia of Asia. It has been shown mercy and is firmly settled in God's harmony. It rejoices in the suffering of our Lord without wavering, and it is fully convinced of his resurrection, in all mercy. I greet it in the blood of Jesus Christ, which is eternal and lasting joy, above all if they are one with the overseer and with the elders and deacons who are with him, appointed in keeping with the mind of Jesus Christ. By his own will he has made them firm and secure by his Holy Spirit.
## The overseer's silence
1:1 I know that this overseer did not gain his ministry, which serves the whole community, from himself or through human beings, or to puff himself up, but in the love of God the Father and of the Lord Jesus Christ. I am amazed at his forbearance: by keeping silent he can do more than those who talk emptily.
1:2 For he is tuned to the commandments as a lyre is to its strings. That is why my soul calls blessed his mind that is set on God. I have come to know that it is full of virtue and complete: his steadiness and his freedom from anger, in all the forbearance of the living God.
## Where the shepherd is
2:1 So, as children of the light of truth, run from division and from evil teachings. Where the shepherd is, follow there like sheep.
2:2 For many wolves that seem trustworthy use evil pleasure to take God's runners captive. But in your unity they will have no place.
## Not the Father's planting
3:1 Keep away from the evil weeds, which Jesus Christ does not cultivate, because they are not the Father's planting. Not that I found division among you; I found filtering.
3:2 For all who belong to God and to Jesus Christ are with the overseer. And all who repent and come to the unity of the church will belong to God too, so that they may be living in the way of Jesus Christ.
3:3 Do not be deceived, my brothers. If anyone follows a maker of splits, he does not inherit the kingdom of God. If anyone walks by a foreign way of thinking, he does not agree with the suffering.
## One thanksgiving, one altar
4:1 So be eager to use one thanksgiving. For there is one flesh of our Lord Jesus Christ, and one cup for union with his blood. There is one altar, as there is one overseer along with the council of elders and the deacons, my fellow slaves, so that whatever you do, you may do it God's way.
## The gospel and the prophets
5:1 My brothers, I am pouring myself out in love for you, and with great joy I am putting you on your guard. Yet it is not I, but Jesus Christ. I am in chains in him, and I am all the more afraid, since I am not yet complete. But your prayer will make me complete for God, so that I may reach the lot in which I was shown mercy. I have taken refuge in the gospel as in the flesh of Jesus, and in the apostles as in the church's council of elders.
5:2 We love the prophets too, because they too pointed to the gospel in what they proclaimed, and hoped in him and waited for him. By believing in him they were saved, since they were in the unity of Jesus Christ. They are saints worthy of love and worthy of wonder, given a good witness by Jesus Christ, and counted together in the gospel of the hope we share.
## If anyone interprets Judaism
6:1 But if anyone interprets Judaism to you, do not listen to him. It is better to hear Christianity from a circumcised man than Judaism from an uncircumcised one. But if neither of them speaks about Jesus Christ, to me they are grave markers and tombs of the dead, with nothing written on them but human names.
6:2 So run from the evil arts and the traps of the ruler of this age, or you may be worn down by his way of thinking and grow weak in love. Instead, all of you come together in one place with an undivided heart.
6:3 I give thanks to my God that my conscience is clear toward you. No one can boast, either in secret or in the open, that I was a burden to anyone in anything small or great. And for all among whom I spoke, I pray that they may not come to have it as a testimony against them.
## I cried out with God's voice
7:1 For even if some people wanted to deceive me in the flesh, the Spirit is not deceived, since it is from God. For it knows where it comes from and where it is going, and it exposes what is hidden. I cried out while I was among you. I spoke in a loud voice, the voice of God: "Pay attention to the overseer, and to the council of elders and the deacons."
7:2 Some suspected that I said this because I knew ahead of time about the division some were causing. But the one in whom I am in chains is my witness that I did not learn it from human flesh. The Spirit was preaching, saying this: "Do nothing without the overseer. Keep your flesh as God's temple. Love union. Run from divisions. Be imitators of Jesus Christ, as he is of his Father."
## The archives
8:1 So I was doing my own part, as a man made ready for union. But where there is division and anger, God does not live. So the Lord forgives all who repent, if they turn in repentance to the unity of God and to the council of the overseer. I trust in the grace of Jesus Christ, who will loosen every bond from you.
8:2 I urge you to do nothing to push yourselves forward, but to act in the way of learning Christ. For I heard some people say: "Unless I find it in the archives, I do not believe it in the gospel." When I told them, "It is written," they replied, "That is the question." But to me Jesus Christ is the archives. His cross, his death, his resurrection, and the faith that comes through him are the archives no one can tamper with. In these I want to be justified, through your prayer.
## The door of the Father
9:1 The priests are good too, but better is the high priest, who has been entrusted with the most holy place. Only to him have God's hidden things been entrusted. He himself is the Father's door, and through it Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, the prophets and apostles and the church come in. All these things are for the unity of God.
9:2 But the gospel has something special: the coming of the Savior, our Lord Jesus Christ, his suffering and the resurrection. For the beloved prophets pointed to him in what they proclaimed, but the gospel is the completion of incorruption. All these things together are good, if you believe in love.
## An envoy to Antioch
10:1 News has reached me that, in answer to your prayer and to the deep affection you have in Christ Jesus, the church in Antioch of Syria is at peace. So it is fitting for you, as a church of God, to elect a deacon to be an envoy of God there, to rejoice with them when they have gathered in one place, and to give glory to the Name.
10:2 Blessed in Jesus Christ is the one who will be counted worthy of such a ministry, and you will be given glory. If you want to, it is not impossible for you, for the sake of God's name, just as the nearest churches have sent overseers, and others elders and deacons.
## Philo, Rheus and greetings from Troas
11:1 About Philo, the deacon from Cilicia, a man who has been given a good witness: he is assisting me even now in the word of God, together with Rheus Agathopus, a chosen man, who is following me from Syria and has said goodbye to this life. They also bear witness for you, and I too give thanks to God for you, because you welcomed them, as the Lord also welcomed you. But may those who dishonored them be redeemed by the grace of Jesus Christ.
11:2 The love of the brothers in Troas greets you. I am writing to you from there by Burrhus, whom the Ephesians and Smyrnaeans sent with me to show honor. The Lord Jesus Christ will honor them. They hope in him in flesh, soul, spirit, faith, love and harmony. Farewell in Christ Jesus, the hope we share.
