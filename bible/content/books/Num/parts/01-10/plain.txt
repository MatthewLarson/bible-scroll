## Count the fighting men
1:1 The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tent of meeting. It was the first day of the second month, in the second year after they came out of the land of Egypt. He said,
1:2 "Count the heads of the whole community of the Israelites, by their clans and by their fathers' houses. List the names of every male, head by head.
1:3 Count every man in Israel who is twenty years old or older and able to serve in the army. You and Aaron are to enroll them company by company.
1:4 A man from each tribe is to be with you, each one the head of his fathers' house.
1:5 These are the names of the men who are to stand with you: for Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;
1:6 for Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;
1:7 for Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;
1:8 for Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;
1:9 for Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon;
1:10 for the sons of Joseph: for Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; for Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur;
1:11 for Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;
1:12 for Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;
1:13 for Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran;
1:14 for Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;
1:15 for Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan."
1:16 These were the ones called from the community, the leaders of their fathers' tribes. They were the heads of Israel's thousands.
1:17 Moses and Aaron took these men who had been marked out by name.
1:18 On the first day of the second month they called the whole community together. The people registered their family lines by their clans and their fathers' houses. They listed the names of those twenty years old and up, head by head,
1:19 as the LORD had commanded Moses. So he enrolled them in the wilderness of Sinai.
## Tribe by tribe
1:20 From the descendants of Reuben, Israel's firstborn, every male twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name, head by head. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:21 Those enrolled from the tribe of Reuben were 46,500.
1:22 From the descendants of Simeon, every male twenty years old and up who could go out to war was enrolled and listed by name, head by head. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:23 Those enrolled from the tribe of Simeon were 59,300.
1:24 From the descendants of Gad, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:25 Those enrolled from the tribe of Gad were 45,650.
1:26 From the descendants of Judah, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:27 Those enrolled from the tribe of Judah were 74,600.
1:28 From the descendants of Issachar, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:29 Those enrolled from the tribe of Issachar were 54,400.
1:30 From the descendants of Zebulun, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:31 Those enrolled from the tribe of Zebulun were 57,400.
1:32 From the sons of Joseph: from the descendants of Ephraim, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:33 Those enrolled from the tribe of Ephraim were 40,500.
1:34 From the descendants of Manasseh, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:35 Those enrolled from the tribe of Manasseh were 32,200.
1:36 From the descendants of Benjamin, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:37 Those enrolled from the tribe of Benjamin were 35,400.
1:38 From the descendants of Dan, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:39 Those enrolled from the tribe of Dan were 62,700.
1:40 From the descendants of Asher, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:41 Those enrolled from the tribe of Asher were 41,500.
1:42 From the descendants of Naphtali, everyone twenty years old and up who could go out to war was listed by name. They were listed by family lines, clans and fathers' houses.
1:43 Those enrolled from the tribe of Naphtali were 53,400.
1:44 These are the men Moses and Aaron enrolled, with the help of Israel's twelve leaders, one from each fathers' house.
1:45 All the Israelites enrolled by their fathers' houses, twenty years old and up, everyone in Israel who could go out to war,
1:46 all those enrolled came to 603,550.
## The Levites set apart
1:47 But the Levites were not enrolled among them by their fathers' tribe.
1:48 For the LORD had spoken to Moses. He had said,
1:49 "But do not enroll the tribe of Levi, and do not include them when you count the Israelites.
1:50 Put the Levites in charge of the tabernacle of the testimony, over all its things and over everything that belongs to it. They are to carry the tabernacle and all its things. They are to serve it and camp around the tabernacle.
1:51 When it is time for the tabernacle to move, the Levites will take it down. When it stops to camp, the Levites will set it up. If any outsider comes near it, he must be put to death.
1:52 The Israelites are to camp each man in his own camp and each man by his own banner, company by company.
1:53 The Levites are to pitch their tents all around the tabernacle of the testimony. Then wrath will not break out against the community of the Israelites. The Levites will keep watch over the tabernacle of the testimony."
1:54 The Israelites did this. They did everything the LORD had commanded Moses.
## The camp around the tent

2:2 "The Israelites are to camp each man by his banner, with the signs of their fathers' houses. They are to camp around the tent of meeting, facing it from a distance.

2:4 His company numbers 74,600.
2:5 Those camping next to him will be the tribe of Issachar. The leader of the people of Issachar is Nethanel son of Zuar.
2:6 His company numbers 54,400.
2:7 Then the tribe of Zebulun. The leader of the people of Zebulun is Eliab son of Helon.
2:8 His company numbers 57,400.

## South: Reuben's camp

## West: Ephraim's camp

2:32 These were the Israelites enrolled by their fathers' houses. All those enrolled in the camps, company by company, were 603,550.
2:33 But the Levites were not enrolled among the Israelites, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
2:34 The Israelites did everything the LORD had commanded Moses. That is how they camped by their banners, and that is how they set out, each man with his clan, by his fathers' house.
## Aaron's sons
3:1 These are the family lines of Aaron and Moses, from the day the LORD spoke with Moses on Mount Sinai.
3:2 These are the names of Aaron's sons: Nadab the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.
3:3 These are the names of Aaron's sons, the anointed priests, whose hands he filled to serve as priests.
3:4 But Nadab and Abihu died before the LORD in the wilderness of Sinai, when they brought strange fire near before the LORD. They had no sons. So Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests in the presence of Aaron their father.
## The Levites given to Aaron
3:5 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
3:6 "Present the tribe of Levi, and have them stand before Aaron the priest to serve him.
3:7 They will keep his duty and the duty of the whole community in front of the tent of meeting, doing the work of the tabernacle.
3:8 They will look after all the things of the tent of meeting and keep the duty of the Israelites, doing the work of the tabernacle.
3:9 Give the Levites to Aaron and his sons. Out of all the Israelites, they are the ones given over entirely to him.
3:10 Put Aaron and his sons in charge of their duties as priests. If any outsider comes near, he must be put to death."
## In place of the firstborn
3:11 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
3:12 "I myself have chosen the Levites out of the Israelites. They take the place of every firstborn, every first child born to an Israelite mother. The Levites belong to me.
3:13 For every firstborn is mine. On the day I struck down all the firstborn of Egypt, I made every firstborn in Israel holy to me, people and animals alike. They are mine. I am the LORD."
## The count of Levi
3:14 The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai. He said,
3:15 "Enroll the sons of Levi by their fathers' houses and by their clans. Enroll every male a month old and up."
3:16 So Moses enrolled them at the LORD's word, as he had been commanded.
3:17 These were Levi's sons by name: Gershon, Kohath and Merari.
3:18 These are the names of Gershon's sons by their clans: Libni and Shimei.
3:19 Kohath's sons by their clans: Amram, Izhar, Hebron and Uzziel.
3:20 Merari's sons by their clans: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites by their fathers' houses.
## Gershon: west of the tent
3:21 From Gershon came the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites. These were the Gershonite clans.
3:22 Those enrolled, counting every male a month old and up, were 7,500.
3:23 The Gershonite clans were to camp behind the tabernacle, on the west.
3:24 The leader of the fathers' house of the Gershonites was Eliasaph son of Lael.
3:25 At the tent of meeting the Gershonites were in charge of the tabernacle and the tent. They had its covering and the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting,
3:26 the hangings of the courtyard, and the screen for the entrance of the courtyard that surrounds the tabernacle and the altar. They had its ropes too, and all the work connected with them.
## Kohath: south of the tent
3:27 From Kohath came the clan of the Amramites, the clan of the Izharites, the clan of the Hebronites and the clan of the Uzzielites. These were the Kohathite clans.
3:28 Counting every male a month old and up, there were 8,600. They kept the duty of the holy place.
3:29 The clans of Kohath's descendants were to camp on the south side of the tabernacle.
3:30 The leader of the fathers' house of the Kohathite clans was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.
3:31 They were in charge of the ark, the table, the lampstand and the altars. They had the things of the holy place used in serving, and the screen, and all the work connected with them.
3:32 Eleazar son of Aaron the priest was chief of all the Levite leaders. He was in charge of those who stood guard over the holy place.
## Merari: north of the tent
3:33 From Merari came the clan of the Mahlites and the clan of the Mushites. These were the Merarite clans.
3:34 Those enrolled, counting every male a month old and up, were 6,200.
3:35 The leader of the fathers' house of the Merarite clans was Zuriel son of Abihail. Their camp was to be at the tabernacle's north side.
3:36 The Merarites were put in charge of the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, its posts and its bases. They had all its fittings and all the work connected with them,
3:37 and the posts of the courtyard all around, with their bases, their tent pegs and their ropes.
## Moses and Aaron: east of the tent
3:38 Moses, Aaron and Aaron's sons camped on the east side of the tabernacle, in front of the tent of meeting, toward the sunrise. They guarded the holy place on behalf of the Israelites. If any outsider came near, he was to be put to death.
3:39 All the Levites enrolled, whom Moses and Aaron enrolled at the LORD's word by their clans, every male a month old and up, were 22,000.
## Ransom for the extra firstborn
3:40 The LORD said to Moses, "Enroll every firstborn male of the Israelites a month old and up, and take a count of their names.
3:41 Take the Levites for me instead of every firstborn among the Israelites. I am the LORD. Take the Levites' animals instead of every firstborn among the Israelites' animals."
3:42 So Moses enrolled every firstborn among the Israelites, as the LORD had commanded him.
3:43 All the firstborn males, by the count of names, a month old and up, came to 22,273.
3:44 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
3:45 "Take the Levites in place of every firstborn among the Israelites, and the Levites' animals in place of their animals. The Levites will be mine. I am the LORD.
3:46 As the ransom for the 273 firstborn of the Israelites who are more than the number of the Levites,
3:47 collect five shekels for each person. Use the sanctuary shekel, which is twenty gerahs.
3:48 Hand the money over to Aaron and his sons as the ransom for the extra ones."
3:49 So Moses took the ransom money from those who were left over beyond the ones ransomed by the Levites.
3:50 From the Israelites' firstborn he received 1,365 shekels in silver, by the sanctuary shekel.
3:51 Moses gave the ransom money to Aaron and his sons at the LORD's word, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
## Kohath carries the holy things
4:1 The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron. He said,
4:2 "Count the heads of the Kohathites from among the Levites, by their clans and by their fathers' houses,
4:3 from thirty years old and up to fifty years old, everyone who joins the work force to do the work in the tent of meeting.
4:4 This is the work of the Kohathites in the tent of meeting: the most holy things.
4:5 When the camp is setting out, Aaron and his sons will go in. They will take down the veil that screens it off and cover the ark of the testimony with it.
4:6 Over that they will put a covering of fine leather, and on top they will spread a cloth of solid blue-violet. Then they will put its poles in place.
4:7 Over the table of the Presence they will spread a blue-violet cloth. On it they will put the plates, the pans, the bowls and the jugs for the drink offering. The bread that is always there will stay on it.
4:8 Over these they will spread a scarlet cloth, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put in its poles.
4:9 They will take a blue-violet cloth and cover the lampstand for the light. They will cover its lamps, its tongs and its trays, and all the oil jars used to tend it.
4:10 They will put it and all its things into a covering of fine leather and put it on a carrying frame.
4:11 Over the gold altar they will spread a blue-violet cloth, cover it with a covering of fine leather, and put in its poles.
4:12 They will take all the things used for the service in the sanctuary and wrap them in a blue-violet cloth. They will cover them with fine leather and put them on a carrying frame.
4:13 They will clear the fatty ashes from the altar and spread a purple cloth over it.
4:14 On it they will put all the things used for serving at it, all the things of the altar: the fire pans, the forks, the shovels and the bowls. They will spread over it a covering of fine leather and put in its poles.
4:15 When the camp is ready to move, Aaron and his sons will finish covering the holy furniture and all the holy utensils. Only then will the Kohathites come to carry them. They must not touch the holy things. If they do, they will die. These are the loads the Kohathites carry from the tent of meeting.
4:16 Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is to oversee the oil for the light, the sweet incense, the daily grain offering and the anointing oil. He oversees the whole tabernacle and everything in it, the sanctuary and its furnishings."
4:17 The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron. He said,
4:18 "Do not let the tribe of the Kohathite clans be cut off from among the Levites.
4:19 Do this for them so that they stay alive, and do not die, when they come close to the most holy things. Then Aaron and his sons must go in and give each man his task and tell him what to carry.
4:20 But the Kohathites must not go in and look at the holy things, not even for an instant. If they do, they will die."

4:22 "Count the heads of the Gershonites too, by their fathers' houses and by their clans.
4:23 From thirty years old and up to fifty years old, enroll everyone who joins the work force to do work in the tent of meeting.
4:24 This is the work of the Gershonite clans, in serving and in carrying:
4:25 They will carry the curtains of the tabernacle and the tent of meeting. They will carry its covering, the covering of fine leather on top of it, and the screen for the entrance of the tent of meeting.

4:27 All the work of the Gershonites, all their carrying and all their work, will be at the command of Aaron and his sons. You are to assign them the duty of all they carry.
4:28 This is the work of the Gershonite clans in the tent of meeting. Their duty will be under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.
## Merari carries the frames
4:29 "The Merarites: enroll them by their clans and by their fathers' houses.
4:30 From thirty years old and up to fifty years old, enroll everyone who joins the work force to do the work of the tent of meeting.

4:32 and the posts of the courtyard all around, with their bases, their tent pegs and their ropes. This includes all their fittings and all the work connected with them. Assign by name the things each one is to carry.
4:33 This is the work of the Merarite clans, all their work in the tent of meeting, under the hand of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest."

## Unclean people outside the camp
5:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
5:2 "Command the Israelites to send certain people out of the camp: everyone with a skin disease, everyone who has a discharge, and everyone who is unclean because of a dead body.
5:3 Send out both men and women. Put them outside the camp, so that they do not make their camps unclean, the camps where I live among them."
5:4 The Israelites did so. They sent them outside the camp. As the LORD had told Moses, so the Israelites did.
## Wrongs set right
5:5 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
5:6 "Tell the Israelites: Suppose a man or a woman does any of the wrongs people do to each other. In doing so they act unfaithfully toward the LORD, and that person is guilty.
5:7 They must confess the wrong they have done. The guilty one must pay back the full amount, add a fifth to it, and give it to the person he wronged.
5:8 If that person has no close relative to receive the payment, it belongs to the LORD and goes to the priest. This is in addition to the ram the priest uses to make atonement for the guilty one.
5:9 Every contribution of all the holy gifts that the Israelites bring to the priest will be his.
5:10 Each man's holy gifts will be his own. Whatever anyone gives to the priest will be the priest's."
## When a husband suspects his wife
5:11 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
5:12 "Tell the Israelites: Suppose a man's wife turns aside and acts unfaithfully toward him.
5:13 Another man sleeps with her, but her husband does not know. It stays hidden, and she has made herself unclean. No one is a witness against her, and no one caught her.
5:14 Then a spirit of jealousy seizes her husband and he grows suspicious of his wife, and she has in fact made herself unclean. Or a spirit of jealousy seizes him and he grows suspicious, though she has not made herself unclean.
5:15 In either case the man is to bring his wife to the priest, with the offering required for her: a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He must not pour oil on it or put frankincense on it. It is a grain offering for jealousy, a reminder offering that calls a wrong to mind.
## The bitter water
5:16 "The priest is to present her and have her stand before the LORD.
5:17 He will take holy water in a clay jar and add some dust from the floor of the tabernacle.
5:18 When he has the woman stand before the LORD, he will loosen her hair. He will put the reminder offering in her hands, the grain offering for jealousy. The priest himself will hold the bitter water that carries the curse.
5:19 Then the priest will make her swear an oath. He will say to her, 'Suppose no man has slept with you, and you have not turned aside into impurity while married to your husband. Then this bitter water that carries the curse will not harm you.
5:20 But suppose you have turned aside while married to your husband and made yourself unclean, and a man other than your husband has slept with you...'
5:21 Here the priest will put the woman under the oath of the curse. He will say to her, 'May the LORD make people curse and swear by your name among your people. May the LORD cause your thigh to waste away and your belly to swell.
5:22 May this water that carries the curse enter your body and make your belly swell and your thigh waste away.' Then the woman is to answer, 'Amen, amen.'
5:23 "The priest will write these curses in a scroll and then rinse the ink off into the bitter water.
5:24 He will give the woman the bitter water that carries the curse to drink. That water will enter her and cause bitter pain.
5:25 The priest will take the grain offering for jealousy from her hand. He will wave it before the LORD and present it at the altar.
5:26 He will take a handful of it as its token portion and make it go up in smoke on the altar. Then he will have the woman drink the water.
5:27 Once she has drunk it, this is what will happen if she has made herself unclean and acted unfaithfully toward her husband. The water that carries the curse will enter her and cause bitter pain. Her belly will swell and her thigh will waste away. Her name will become a curse among her people.
5:28 But if she has not made herself unclean and is pure, she will not be harmed, and she will be able to have children.
5:29 "This is the procedure for cases of jealousy. It applies when a wife turns aside while married to her husband and makes herself unclean,
5:30 or when a spirit of jealousy seizes a husband and he grows suspicious of his wife. He must bring her to stand before the LORD, where the priest will apply this whole law to her.
5:31 The husband will be free of guilt, but the woman will bear her own guilt."
## The Nazirite vow
6:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
6:2 "Tell the Israelites: If a man or a woman makes an extraordinary vow, a Nazirite vow, to be set apart for the LORD,
6:3 that person must keep away from wine and other strong drink. He is not to drink vinegar made from either one, or any juice pressed from grapes. He is not to eat grapes or raisins.
6:4 As long as he is a Nazirite, he must eat nothing that the grapevine produces, from the seeds to the skins.
6:5 Through all the days of his vow, no razor is to touch his head. Until the time he gave to the LORD is over, he is holy, and he lets the hair on his head grow long.
6:6 For as long as he is set apart for the LORD, he must not come near anyone who has died.
6:7 Even if his father or mother, or his brother or sister dies, he must not make himself unclean for them. The sign that he is set apart for his God is on his head.
6:8 All the days he is set apart, he is holy to the LORD.
## If someone dies beside him
6:9 "Suppose someone dies suddenly right next to him, so that the hair he has dedicated becomes unclean. Then on the day he becomes clean, the seventh day, he must shave his head.
6:10 On the eighth day he is to bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting.
6:11 The priest will offer one as a sin offering and one as a burnt offering. He will make atonement for him, for the sin he committed by being near the dead. That same day he is to make his head holy again.
6:12 He must start his days as a Nazirite for the LORD over again, and bring a male lamb in its first year as a guilt offering. The days before do not count, because his dedicated hair was made unclean.
## When the days are complete
6:13 "This is the procedure for the Nazirite when his time of being set apart is over. He is to be brought to the entrance of the tent of meeting.
6:14 There he will present his offering to the LORD: a flawless year-old male lamb for a burnt offering, a flawless year-old ewe lamb for a sin offering, and a flawless ram for a peace offering,
6:15 and a basket of bread without yeast: round loaves of fine flour mixed with oil, and thin wafers without yeast smeared with oil. He will bring their grain offerings and their drink offerings too.
6:16 The priest will present them before the LORD and offer the Nazirite's sin offering and burnt offering.
6:17 He will offer the ram to the LORD as a peace offering, along with the basket of bread without yeast. The priest will also offer the grain offering and drink offering that go with it.
6:18 Then, at the entrance of the tent of meeting, the Nazirite will shave off the hair he dedicated. He will take that hair and throw it into the fire burning under the peace offering.
6:19 After he has shaved it, the priest will take the boiled shoulder of the ram, one round loaf without yeast from the basket, and one wafer without yeast. He will put them into the Nazirite's hands.
6:20 The priest will wave these before the LORD as a wave offering. They are holy and belong to the priest, along with the breast that was waved and the thigh that was given as a contribution. Then the Nazirite is free to drink wine.
6:21 "This is the procedure for the Nazirite who makes a vow, and for the offering he owes the LORD for his dedication. He may add more if he can afford it. But he must do what his vow said, as the procedure for his Nazirite vow requires."
## The priests' blessing

## The seven lamps
8:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
8:2 "Tell Aaron: When you set up the seven lamps, make them shine forward, toward the front of the lampstand."
8:3 Aaron did so. He set up its lamps toward the front of the lampstand, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
8:4 The lampstand was made of hammered gold. Everything, from the base up to the flower cups, was hammered work. Moses made it to match the vision the LORD had shown him.
## The Levites made clean
8:5 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
8:6 "From among the Israelites take the Levites, and make them clean.
8:7 This is what you are to do to make them clean. Spatter the water for sin on them. Then they are to shave their whole body with a razor and wash their clothes, and so make themselves clean.
8:8 They are to take a young bull and its grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil. Then you are to take another young bull, a second one, as a sin offering.
8:9 Present the Levites in front of the tent of meeting, and gather the whole community of the Israelites.
8:10 When you present the Levites before the LORD, the Israelites are to lean their hands on them.
8:11 Aaron is to wave the Levites before the LORD, a wave offering from the Israelites, so that they may do the LORD's work.
8:12 Then the Levites are to lean their hands on the heads of the bulls. Offer one bull for a sin offering and the second for a burnt offering to the LORD, to make atonement for the Levites.
8:13 Then have the Levites stand in front of Aaron and his sons, and wave them as a wave offering to the LORD.
8:14 So you will separate the Levites from among the Israelites, and the Levites will be mine.
## Given in place of the firstborn
8:15 "After that the Levites will go in to work at the tent of meeting. But first you are to make them clean and wave them as a wave offering.
8:16 Out of all the Israelites, they are the ones given over entirely to me. I have claimed them as my own instead of every firstborn who opens the womb among the Israelites.
8:17 For every firstborn among the Israelites is mine, human and animal. On the day I struck down every firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set them apart as holy for myself.
8:18 And I have taken the Levites in place of every firstborn among the Israelites.
8:19 From among the Israelites I have given the Levites to Aaron and his sons as given ones. They are to do the work of the Israelites in the tent of meeting and make atonement for the Israelites. Then no plague will strike the Israelites when the Israelites come near the holy place."
8:20 Moses, Aaron and the whole community of the Israelites did this to the Levites. Everything the LORD had commanded Moses about the Levites, the Israelites did to them.
8:21 The Levites cleansed themselves from sin and washed their clothes. Aaron waved them as a wave offering before the LORD, and Aaron made atonement for them to make them clean.
8:22 After that the Levites went in to serve at the tent of meeting under Aaron and his sons. They did with the Levites just what the LORD had commanded Moses about them.
## The Levites' years of service
8:23 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
8:24 "This is for the Levites: from twenty-five years old and up, a man will come to join the work force. He will do the work of the tent of meeting.
8:25 From fifty years old he is to step back from the work force and work no more.
8:26 He may assist his brothers at the tent of meeting by standing guard, but he must not do the heavy work. This is how you are to arrange the Levites' duties."
## Passover at Sinai
9:1 The LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai. It was the first month of the second year after they came out of the land of Egypt. He said,
9:2 "The Israelites are to keep the Passover at its set time.
9:3 On the fourteenth day of this month, between the two evenings, you are to keep it at its set time. Keep it by all its rules and all its regulations."
9:4 So Moses told the Israelites to keep the Passover.
9:5 In the wilderness of Sinai, on the fourteenth day of the first month, between the two evenings, they kept the Passover. The Israelites did everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
## A second Passover
9:6 But there were some men who were unclean because of a human corpse, so they could not keep the Passover on that day. They came before Moses and before Aaron on that day.
9:7 They said to Moses, "A dead body has made us unclean. But why should we be shut out from presenting the LORD's offering at its set time along with the other Israelites?"
9:8 Moses answered, "Wait while I hear what the LORD commands for you."
9:9 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
9:10 "Tell the Israelites: Even if one of you is unclean from a dead body, or is far away on a journey, he may still keep the LORD's Passover. This holds for your descendants too.
9:11 They are to keep it in the second month, on its fourteenth day, between the two evenings. They will eat the lamb with bread without yeast and bitter herbs.
9:12 They must leave none of it until morning and must not break any of its bones. They are to keep it by all the rules of the Passover.
9:13 But a man who is clean and not on a journey, and still does not keep the Passover, is to be cut off from his people. He failed to present the LORD's offering at its time, so he will carry the penalty of his sin.
9:14 "If a foreigner living among you wants to keep the LORD's Passover, he must follow the Passover rules and its regulations. The same rule applies to all of you, the foreigner and the one born in the land."
## The cloud over the tabernacle
9:15 On the day the tabernacle was put up, the cloud covered it, the tent of the testimony. From evening until morning the cloud above the tabernacle looked like fire.
9:16 That is how it always was. The cloud covered it, and at night it looked like fire.
9:17 Whenever the cloud rose from above the tent, the Israelites set out after it. Wherever the cloud came down and stayed, they made camp.
9:18 At the LORD's word the Israelites set out, and at the LORD's word they camped. All the days that the cloud settled over the tabernacle, they stayed in camp.
9:19 Even when the cloud stayed over the tabernacle for a long time, the Israelites obeyed the LORD's order and did not move on.
9:20 Sometimes the cloud was over the tabernacle only a few days. At the LORD's word they camped, and at the LORD's word they set out.
9:21 Sometimes the cloud stayed only from evening until morning. When it rose in the morning, they set out. Or it stayed a day and a night, and when it rose, they set out.
9:22 Whether it was two days, a month or a longer time, the Israelites stayed in camp as long as the cloud rested over the tabernacle. When it rose, they moved on.
9:23 At the LORD's word they camped, and at the LORD's word they set out. They kept their duty to the LORD, at the LORD's word through Moses.
## Two silver trumpets
10:1 The LORD spoke to Moses. He said,
10:2 "Make yourself two trumpets of silver. Make them of hammered work. They will be for you to call the community together and to start the camps on their way.
10:3 When both are blown, the whole community is to gather to you at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
10:4 If only one is blown, the leaders, the heads of Israel's thousands, are to gather to you.
10:5 When you blow a battle blast, the camps that camp on the east will set out.
10:6 When you blow a second battle blast, the camps that camp on the south will set out. A battle blast is to be blown for them to set out.
10:7 But when you gather the assembly, blow, but do not sound a battle blast.
10:8 The sons of Aaron, the priests, are to blow the trumpets. This will be a rule for you for all time, through your generations.
10:9 When you go out to battle in your own land against an enemy who is attacking you, sound the battle blast on the trumpets. Then the LORD your God will remember you, and you will be rescued from your enemies.
10:10 Blow the trumpets also on your day of celebration, at your set feasts and on the first day of each month. Sound them over your burnt offerings and your peace offerings. Then they will call you to mind before your God. I am the LORD your God."
## Leaving Sinai
10:11 In the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth of the month, the cloud lifted from over the tabernacle of the testimony.
10:12 The Israelites set out on their journeys from the wilderness of Sinai, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.
10:13 This was the first time they set out at the LORD's word through Moses.
10:14 The banner of Judah's camp set out first, company by company. Nahshon son of Amminadab led its company.
10:15 Nethanel son of Zuar led the company of the tribe of Issachar.
10:16 Eliab son of Helon led the company of the tribe of Zebulun.
10:17 Then the tabernacle came down, and the Gershonites and Merarites, who carried it, set out.
10:18 Next the banner of Reuben's camp set out, company by company. Elizur son of Shedeur led its company.
10:19 Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai led the company of the tribe of Simeon.
10:20 Eliasaph son of Deuel led the company of the tribe of Gad.
10:21 Then the Kohathites set out, carrying the holy things. The tabernacle was to be set up before they arrived.
10:22 Next the banner of Ephraim's camp set out, company by company. Elishama son of Ammihud led its company.
10:23 Gamaliel son of Pedahzur led the company of the tribe of Manasseh.
10:24 Abidan son of Gideoni led the company of the tribe of Benjamin.
10:25 Last, the banner of Dan's camp set out, company by company, as the rear guard for all the camps. Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai led its company.
10:26 Pagiel son of Ocran led the company of the tribe of Asher.
10:27 Ahira son of Enan led the company of the tribe of Naphtali.
10:28 This was the order in which the Israelites set out, company by company, when they set out.
## Moses asks Hobab to come
10:29 Moses spoke to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses' father-in-law. He said, "We are leaving for the place the LORD spoke of when he said, 'I will give it to you.' Come along with us. We will be good to you, because the LORD has promised Israel good things."
10:30 Hobab answered, "No, I won't come. I'm going back to my own country and my own family."
10:31 But Moses said, "Please don't leave us. You know this wilderness and where to pitch our tents. You can be our eyes.
10:32 If you come along, then whatever good the LORD does for us, we will pass on to you."
## The ark goes ahead
10:33 They left the LORD's mountain and traveled for three days. All that way the ark of the LORD's covenant went in front of them, to scout out a resting place for them.
10:34 By day the LORD's cloud stayed above them whenever they moved on from the camp.
10:35 Each time the ark set out, Moses said, / "Rise up, LORD! / Let your enemies scatter, / let those who hate you run from your face."
10:36 And each time it came to rest, he said, / "Return, LORD, / to the tens of thousands of Israel's clans."
