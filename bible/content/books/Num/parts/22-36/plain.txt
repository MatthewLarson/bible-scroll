## Balak sends for Balaam
22:1 The Israelites set out and camped in the plains of Moab, on the other side of the Jordan from Jericho.
22:2 Balak son of Zippor saw everything Israel had done to the Amorites.
22:3 Moab was terrified of the people, because there were so many of them. Moab was sick with dread because of the Israelites.
22:4 Moab said to the elders of Midian, "Now this crowd will lick clean everything around us, the way an ox licks the green plants of the field bare." Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.
22:5 He sent messengers to Balaam son of Beor at Pethor, which is by the River, in the land of his own people, to call him. He said, "Look, a people has come out of Egypt. Look, they cover the face of the land, and they are settled right across from me.
22:6 So now come, please, and curse this people for me, because they are too strong for me. Perhaps then I will be able to strike them and drive them out of the land. For I know that the one you bless is blessed, and the one you curse is cursed."
22:7 The elders of Moab and the elders of Midian went with the fees for divination in their hands. They came to Balaam and told him Balak's words.
22:8 He said to them, "Stay here tonight, and I will bring you back word as the LORD speaks to me." So the officials of Moab stayed with Balaam.
22:9 God came to Balaam and said, "Who are these men with you?"
22:10 Balaam said to God, "Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, has sent to me to say,
22:11 'Look, the people that came out of Egypt covers the face of the land. Now come, curse them for me. Perhaps I will be able to fight against them and drive them out.'"
22:12 But God said to Balaam, "Do not go with them. Do not curse the people, because they are blessed."
22:13 Balaam got up in the morning and said to Balak's officials, "Go back to your land, because the LORD has refused to let me go with you."
22:14 So the officials of Moab got up and went to Balak and said, "Balaam refused to come with us."
22:15 Balak sent officials again, more of them, and more honored than these.
22:16 They came to Balaam and said to him, "This is what Balak son of Zippor says: 'Please do not let anything hold you back from coming to me.
22:17 I will honor you greatly, and whatever you tell me, I will do. So come, please, curse this people for me.'"
22:18 But Balaam answered Balak's servants, "Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the word of the LORD my God, to do anything small or great.
22:19 Now you too, please, stay here tonight, so that I can find out what more the LORD will say to me."
22:20 God came to Balaam that night and said to him, "If the men have come to call you, get up and go with them. But only the word I speak to you, that is what you must do."
## The donkey and the angel
22:21 So Balaam got up in the morning, saddled his donkey, and went with the officials of Moab.
22:22 But God's anger burned because he was going, and the angel of the LORD stood in the road as an adversary against him. Balaam was riding on his donkey, and his two servants were with him.
22:23 The donkey saw the angel of the LORD standing in the road with his sword drawn in his hand. So the donkey turned off the road and went into the field. Balaam beat the donkey to turn her back onto the road.
22:24 Then the angel of the LORD took his stand on a path between the vineyards, where a wall ran on this side and a wall on that side.
22:25 The donkey saw the angel of the LORD and pressed herself against the wall, and she crushed Balaam's foot against the wall. So he beat her again.
22:26 The angel of the LORD went farther on and stood where the way was tight, with no space to swing right or left.
22:27 The donkey saw the angel of the LORD and lay down under Balaam. Balaam's anger burned, and he beat the donkey with his stick.
22:28 Then the LORD opened the donkey's mouth, and she said to Balaam, "What have I done to you, that you have beaten me these three times?"
22:29 Balaam said to the donkey, "Because you have been toying with me! If I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you right now."
22:30 The donkey said to Balaam, "Am I not your donkey? You have ridden me all your life, up to this very day. Was I ever used to treating you like this?" He said, "No."
22:31 Then the LORD uncovered Balaam's eyes, and he saw the angel of the LORD standing in the road with his sword drawn in his hand. He bowed down and lay with his face to the ground.
22:32 The angel of the LORD said to him, "Why have you beaten your donkey these three times? Look, it was I who came out to block you, because you are rushing down a wrong road in front of me.
22:33 Three times the donkey saw me and turned away from me. Suppose she had not turned away. By now I would have killed you, and her I would have kept alive."
22:34 Balaam said to the angel of the LORD, "I have sinned. I did not know that you were standing in the road to meet me. So now, if it is wrong in your eyes, I will go back."
22:35 The angel of the LORD said to Balaam, "Go with the men. But only the word I speak to you, that is what you must speak." So Balaam went on with Balak's officials.
## Balak meets Balaam
22:36 When Balak heard that Balaam had come, he went out to meet him at the city of Moab on the Arnon border, at the far edge of his territory.
22:37 Balak said to Balaam, "Did I not make a point of sending to call you? Why did you not come to me? Am I really not able to honor you?"
22:38 Balaam said to Balak, "Look, I have come to you now. But am I able to say anything at all? The word God puts in my mouth, that is what I will speak."
22:39 Then Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Kiriath-huzoth.
22:40 Balak sacrificed cattle and sheep and sent some to Balaam and to the officials who were with him.
22:41 In the morning Balak took Balaam and brought him up to Bamoth-baal. From there he could see the edge of the people.
## The first oracle
23:1 Balaam said to Balak, "Build me seven altars here, and get ready for me seven bulls and seven rams here."
23:2 Balak did as Balaam had said, and Balak and Balaam offered up a bull and a ram on each altar.
23:3 Then Balaam said to Balak, "Stand here beside your burnt offering, and let me go off. Perhaps the LORD will come to meet me. Whatever word he shows me, I will tell you." And he went off to a bare hilltop.
23:4 God met Balaam, and Balaam said to him, "I have set out the seven altars, and I have offered up a bull and a ram on each altar."
23:5 The LORD put a word in Balaam's mouth and said, "Go back to Balak, and this is what you will say."
23:6 So he went back to him, and there he was, standing beside his burnt offering, he and all the officials of Moab.
23:7 Then Balaam took up his poem and said: "From Aram Balak has led me, / the king of Moab from the mountains of the east: / 'Come, curse Jacob for me, / come, denounce Israel.'
23:8 What curse can I lay on one God has not cursed? / What doom can I speak on one the LORD has not doomed?
23:9 From the top of the rocks I see him, / from the hills I watch him. / Here is a people that settles by itself, / and does not count itself one of the nations.
23:10 Who can count the dust of Jacob, / or number a fourth part of Israel? / May I die the way the upright die, / and may my end be like his!"
23:11 Balak said to Balaam, "What have you done to me? I brought you to curse my enemies, and look, you have blessed them, and blessed them again!"
23:12 He answered, "Is it not what the LORD puts in my mouth that I must take care to speak?"
## The second oracle
23:13 Balak said to him, "Come with me, please, to another place where you can see them. You will see only the edge of them; you will not see all of them. Curse them for me from there."
23:14 So he took him to the Field of Zophim, to the top of Pisgah. He built seven altars and offered up a bull and a ram on each altar.
23:15 Balaam said to Balak, "Stand here beside your burnt offering, while I go to meet him over there."
23:16 The LORD met Balaam and put a word in his mouth and said, "Go back to Balak, and this is what you will say."
23:17 He came to him, and there he was, standing beside his burnt offering, with the officials of Moab. Balak asked him, "What did the LORD say?"
23:18 Then Balaam took up his poem and said: "Get up, Balak, and hear; / listen to me, son of Zippor.
23:19 God is not a man, that he should lie, / or a human being, that he should change his mind. / Would he say a thing and not do it? / Would he speak and not carry it out?
23:20 Look, I was told to bless; / he has blessed, and I cannot turn it back.
23:21 He sees no trouble in Jacob, / he finds no mischief in Israel. / The LORD his God is with him, / and a king's battle cry rings out among them.
23:22 God brings them out of Egypt; / he has the horns of a wild ox.
23:23 For no omen works against Jacob, / no divining against Israel. / In time to come, people will say of Jacob, of Israel, / 'Look what God has done!'
23:24 Look, a people rises like a lioness, / and lifts itself up like a lion. / It does not lie down until it eats its prey / and drinks the blood of the slain."
23:25 Balak said to Balaam, "Then do not curse them at all, and do not bless them at all!"
23:26 Balaam answered Balak, "Did I not tell you, 'All that the LORD speaks, that is what I must do'?"
## Balak tries a third place
23:27 Balak said to Balaam, "Come, please, let me take you to another place. Perhaps it will be right in God's eyes for you to curse them for me from there."
23:28 So Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor, which looks down over the wasteland.
23:29 Balaam said to Balak, "Build me seven altars here, and get ready for me seven bulls and seven rams here."
23:30 Balak did as Balaam had said, and offered up a bull and a ram on each altar.
## The third oracle
24:1 Balaam saw that it was good in the LORD's eyes to bless Israel, so this time he did not go off to meet with omens, as he had each time before. He set his face toward the wilderness.
24:2 Balaam lifted his eyes and saw Israel camped tribe by tribe, and the Spirit of God came on him.
24:3 Then he took up his poem and said: "Balaam son of Beor speaks, / the man whose eye sees true speaks,
24:4 he who hears what God says speaks, / he who sees what the Almighty shows, / who drops to the ground, yet his eyes are uncovered:
24:5 How good are your tents, Jacob, / your dwellings, Israel!
24:6 They stretch out like streams in the valleys, / like gardens along a river, / like aloes the LORD himself planted, / like cedars growing by water.
24:7 Water will flow from his buckets, / and his seed will be by many waters. / His king will be higher than Agag, / and his kingdom will be lifted high.
24:8 God brings him out of Egypt; / he has the horns of a wild ox. / He will eat up the nations that oppose him, / he will crush their bones, / and strike them through with his arrows.
24:9 He crouched, he lay down like a lion, / and like a lioness: who will dare to rouse him? / Blessed be everyone who blesses you, / and cursed be everyone who curses you!"
24:10 Balak's anger burned against Balaam. He struck his hands together and said to Balaam, "I called you to curse my enemies, and look, you have blessed them and blessed them again, these three times!
24:11 So now, run off home! I said I would honor you greatly, but look, the LORD has kept you from honor."
24:12 Balaam said to Balak, "Did I not also tell the messengers you sent to me,
24:13 'Even if Balak gave me his house full of silver and gold, I could not go beyond the word of the LORD, to do good or bad on my own. What the LORD speaks, that is what I will speak'?
24:14 And now, look, I am going back to my people. Come, I will advise you what this people will do to your people in the last days."
## The fourth oracle: a star from Jacob
24:15 Then he took up his poem and said: "Balaam son of Beor speaks, / the man whose eye sees true speaks,
24:16 he who hears what God says speaks, / who knows what the Most High knows, / who sees what the Almighty shows, / who drops to the ground, yet his eyes are uncovered:
24:17 I see him, but not yet; / I look at him, but not close by. / A star will march out of Jacob, / and a scepter will rise from Israel. / It will smash the brows of Moab, / and tear down all the sons of Sheth.
24:18 Edom will become his property, / Seir, his enemy, will be taken, / while Israel does mighty deeds.
24:19 One from Jacob will rule, / and destroy the survivors from the city."
## Last oracles
24:20 Then he saw Amalek, and took up his poem and said: "Amalek was first among the nations, / but his end will be destruction."
24:21 Then he saw the Kenites, and took up his poem and said: "Your home is firm, / and your nest is set in the rock.
24:22 Yet Kain will be burned up. / How long until Asshur takes you captive?"
24:23 Then he took up his poem and said: "Ah, who can stay alive once God has set this in place?
24:24 Ships will come from the coast of Kittim; / they will bring down Asshur and bring down Eber, / and he too is headed for ruin."
24:25 Then Balaam got up and went back to his place, and Balak also went on his way.
## Baal of Peor
25:1 While Israel was staying at Shittim, the people began to commit sexual sin with the daughters of Moab.
25:2 The women invited the people to the sacrifices for their gods, and the people ate and bowed down to their gods.
25:3 So Israel tied itself to the Baal of Peor, and the LORD burned with anger against Israel.
25:4 The LORD said to Moses, "Take all the heads of the people and impale them before the LORD in the open sun, so that the LORD's burning anger may turn away from Israel."
25:5 Moses told Israel's judges, "Every one of you must put to death his own men who tied themselves to the Baal of Peor."
## The zeal of Phinehas
25:6 Just then an Israelite man came, bringing a Midianite woman to his relatives. He did it in plain sight of Moses and of the whole community of Israelites, while they wept at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
25:7 Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw it. He rose up out of the community and took a spear in his hand.
25:8 He went after the Israelite man into the inner tent and drove the spear through both of them, the Israelite man and the woman, through her belly. Then the plague on the Israelites was held back.
25:9 Those who died in the plague were twenty-four thousand.
25:10 The LORD spoke to Moses:
25:11 "Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned my rage back from the Israelites. He was zealous with my zeal among them, so I did not finish off the Israelites in my zeal.
25:12 So say this: 'Look, I am giving him my covenant: peace.
25:13 It will be his and his descendants' after him, a covenant of priesthood for all time, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for the Israelites.'"
25:14 The Israelite man who was struck down, struck down with the Midianite woman, was named Zimri son of Salu. He was a leader of a family clan of the Simeonites.
25:15 The Midianite woman who was struck down was named Cozbi daughter of Zur. Zur was the chief of a family clan in Midian.
25:16 The LORD spoke to Moses:
25:17 "Treat the Midianites as enemies and strike them down,
25:18 They treated you as enemies with their tricks. They tricked you in the matter of Peor. They tricked you in the matter of Cozbi, their sister, the daughter of a leader of Midian, who was struck down on the day of the plague over Peor."
## The second census
26:1 After the plague, the LORD said to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest,
26:2 "Count the heads of the whole community of Israelites, from twenty years old and up, by their fathers' houses, everyone in Israel who can go out to war."
26:3 So Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them in the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho, saying,
26:4 "Count those from twenty years old and up," as the LORD had commanded Moses. These were the Israelites who came out of the land of Egypt:
26:5 Reuben, Israel's firstborn. The descendants of Reuben: from Hanoch, the clan of the Hanochites; from Pallu, the clan of the Palluites;
26:6 from Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; from Carmi, the clan of the Carmites.
26:7 These were the clans of the Reubenites. Those enrolled were 43,730.
26:8 The son of Pallu was Eliab.
26:9 The sons of Eliab were Nemuel, Dathan and Abiram. These are the same Dathan and Abiram, men called from the community, who fought against Moses and Aaron in Korah's company when they fought against the LORD.
26:10 The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them, and Korah too. That company died when fire burned up 250 men, and they became a sign to warn others.
26:11 But the sons of Korah did not die.
26:12 The descendants of Simeon by their clans: from Nemuel, the clan of the Nemuelites; from Jamin, the clan of the Jaminites; from Jachin, the clan of the Jachinites;
26:13 from Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites; from Shaul, the clan of the Shaulites.
26:14 These were the clans of the Simeonites: 22,200.
26:15 The descendants of Gad by their clans: from Zephon, the clan of the Zephonites; from Haggi, the clan of the Haggites; from Shuni, the clan of the Shunites;
26:16 from Ozni, the clan of the Oznites; from Eri, the clan of the Erites;
26:17 from Arod, the clan of the Arodites; from Areli, the clan of the Arelites.
26:18 These were the clans of the descendants of Gad. Those enrolled were 40,500.
26:19 The sons of Judah were Er and Onan, but Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan.
26:20 The descendants of Judah by their clans were: from Shelah, the clan of the Shelahites; from Perez, the clan of the Perezites; from Zerah, the clan of the Zerahites.
26:21 The descendants of Perez were: from Hezron, the clan of the Hezronites; from Hamul, the clan of the Hamulites.
26:22 These were the clans of Judah. Those enrolled were 76,500.
26:23 The descendants of Issachar by their clans: Tola, the clan of the Tolaites; from Puvah, the clan of the Punites;
26:24 from Jashub, the clan of the Jashubites; from Shimron, the clan of the Shimronites.
26:25 These were the clans of Issachar. Those enrolled were 64,300.
26:26 The descendants of Zebulun by their clans: from Sered, the clan of the Seredites; from Elon, the clan of the Elonites; from Jahleel, the clan of the Jahleelites.
26:27 These were the clans of the Zebulunites. Those enrolled were 60,500.
26:28 The sons of Joseph by their clans: Manasseh and Ephraim.
26:29 The descendants of Manasseh: from Machir, the clan of the Machirites. Machir fathered Gilead; from Gilead, the clan of the Gileadites.
26:30 These were the descendants of Gilead: Iezer, the clan of the Iezerites; from Helek, the clan of the Helekites;
26:31 and Asriel, the clan of the Asrielites; and Shechem, the clan of the Shechemites;
26:32 and Shemida, the clan of the Shemidaites; and Hepher, the clan of the Hepherites.
26:33 Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, only daughters. The names of Zelophehad's daughters were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah.
26:34 These were the clans of Manasseh. Those enrolled were 52,700.
26:35 These were the descendants of Ephraim by their clans: from Shuthelah, the clan of the Shuthelahites; from Becher, the clan of the Becherites; from Tahan, the clan of the Tahanites.
26:36 These were the descendants of Shuthelah: from Eran, the clan of the Eranites.
26:37 These were the clans of the descendants of Ephraim. Those enrolled were 32,500. These were the descendants of Joseph by their clans.
26:38 The descendants of Benjamin by their clans: from Bela, the clan of the Belaites; from Ashbel, the clan of the Ashbelites; from Ahiram, the clan of the Ahiramites;
26:39 from Shephupham, the clan of the Shuphamites; from Hupham, the clan of the Huphamites.
26:40 The sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman: from Ard, the clan of the Ardites; from Naaman, the clan of the Naamites.
26:41 These were the descendants of Benjamin by their clans. Those enrolled were 45,600.
26:42 These were the descendants of Dan by their clans: from Shuham, the clan of the Shuhamites. These were the clans of Dan by their clans.
26:43 All the clans of the Shuhamites, as enrolled, were 64,400.
26:44 The descendants of Asher by their clans: from Imnah, the clan of the Imnites; from Ishvi, the clan of the Ishvites; from Beriah, the clan of the Beriites.
26:45 From the descendants of Beriah: from Heber, the clan of the Heberites; from Malchiel, the clan of the Malchielites.
26:46 The name of Asher's daughter was Serah.
26:47 These were the clans of the descendants of Asher. Those enrolled were 53,400.
26:48 The descendants of Naphtali by their clans: from Jahzeel, the clan of the Jahzeelites; from Guni, the clan of the Gunites;
26:49 from Jezer, the clan of the Jezerites; from Shillem, the clan of the Shillemites.
26:50 These were the clans of Naphtali by their clans. Those enrolled were 45,400.
26:51 These were the Israelites who were enrolled: 601,730.
## Dividing the land
26:52 The LORD spoke to Moses:
26:53 "The land is to be divided among these as their inheritance, by the number of names.
26:54 A big group gets a big inheritance, and a small group a small one. Each group's inheritance is to match the number of people enrolled in it.
26:55 But the land is to be divided by lot. They will inherit by the names of their ancestors' tribes.
26:56 Each inheritance will be divided by the lot, between the larger and the smaller."
## The Levites counted
26:57 These are the Levites who were enrolled by their clans: from Gershon, the clan of the Gershonites; from Kohath, the clan of the Kohathites; from Merari, the clan of the Merarites.
26:58 These are the clans of Levi: the clan of the Libnites, the clan of the Hebronites, the clan of the Mahlites, the clan of the Mushites, the clan of the Korahites. Kohath fathered Amram.
26:59 The name of Amram's wife was Jochebed, daughter of Levi, who was born to Levi in Egypt. She bore to Amram Aaron and Moses and their sister Miriam.
26:60 To Aaron were born Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.
26:61 But Nadab and Abihu died when they offered strange fire before the LORD.
26:62 The Levites enrolled were 23,000, every male a month old and up. They were not enrolled among the Israelites, because no inheritance was given to them among the Israelites.
26:63 These were the ones enrolled by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who enrolled the Israelites in the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho.
26:64 Not one man among them had been among those Moses and Aaron the priest enrolled when they enrolled the Israelites in the Wilderness of Sinai.
26:65 For the LORD had told them, "They are going to die in the wilderness." And no one was left of them except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
## The daughters of Zelophehad
27:1 Then the daughters of Zelophehad came forward. Zelophehad was the son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, of the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph. These were the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah.
27:2 They took their stand at the entrance of the tent of meeting, in front of Moses and Eleazar the priest, the leaders and the whole community. They said,
27:3 "Our father died in the wilderness. He was not one of Korah's followers, the band that gathered against the LORD. It was for his own sin that he died, and he had no sons.
27:4 Why should our father's name disappear from his clan just because he left no son? Give us land of our own among our father's brothers."
27:5 So Moses brought their case before the LORD.
27:6 The LORD said to Moses,
27:7 "Zelophehad's daughters have spoken rightly. Yes, give them land to own as an inheritance among their father's brothers. Hand their father's inheritance over to them.
27:8 Then say to the Israelites: 'When a man dies without a son, hand his inheritance over to his daughter.
27:9 If he has no daughter, his brothers get his inheritance.
27:10 If he has no brothers, his father's brothers get it.
27:11 If his father had no brothers, give it to the closest relative in his clan, and that man will own it.' This will be a rule of law for the Israelites, as the LORD commanded Moses."
## Joshua appointed to follow Moses
27:12 The LORD said to Moses, "Go up this mountain of the Abarim range and see the land that I have given to the Israelites.
27:13 Once you have seen it, you will be gathered to your people, you as well, the way your brother Aaron was gathered.
27:14 For you two rebelled against my word in the Wilderness of Zin. When the community quarreled, you did not show me holy before their eyes at the water." That was the water of Meribah at Kadesh in the Wilderness of Zin.
27:15 Moses spoke to the LORD:
27:16 "May the LORD, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the community,
27:17 who will go out in front of them and come in in front of them, who will lead them out and bring them in, so that the LORD's community will not be like sheep with no one to shepherd them."
27:18 The LORD said to Moses, "Take Joshua son of Nun, a man who has the Spirit in him, and lay your hand on him.
27:19 Have him stand before Eleazar the priest and before the whole community, and give him his orders in their sight.
27:20 Put some of your honor on him, so that the whole community of Israelites will listen to him.
27:21 He is to stand before Eleazar the priest, who will ask for him by the decision of the Urim before the LORD. At his word they will go out, and at his word they will come in, he and all the Israelites with him, the whole community."
27:22 So Moses carried out the LORD's command. He brought Joshua forward and stood him in front of Eleazar the priest and the whole community.
27:23 He laid his hands on him and gave him his orders, as the LORD had spoken through Moses.
## The daily offering
28:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
28:2 "Command the Israelites and tell them: 'Be sure to present to me my offering at its set time. It is my food, given as offerings by fire, a soothing smell for me.'
28:3 Tell them: 'This is the offering by fire that you are to present to the LORD: two flawless male lambs in their first year, day by day, as a regular burnt offering.
28:4 Offer one lamb in the morning, and offer the second lamb between the two evenings,
28:5 with a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with a quarter of a hin of oil from beaten olives.
28:6 It is a regular burnt offering, the one set up at Mount Sinai as a soothing smell, an offering by fire to the LORD.
28:7 Its drink offering is to be a quarter of a hin for each lamb. Pour out a drink offering of strong drink to the LORD in the holy place.
28:8 Offer the second lamb between the two evenings. Offer it with the same grain offering and drink offering as in the morning, an offering by fire, a soothing smell to the LORD.
## The Sabbath offering
28:9 On the Sabbath day: two flawless male lambs in their first year, and two tenths of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with oil, with its drink offering.
28:10 This is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, on top of the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.
## The new moon offering
28:11 At the beginnings of your months, present a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls from the herd, one ram, and seven flawless male lambs in their first year,
28:12 with three tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each bull, and two tenths of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for the one ram,
28:13 and a tenth of fine flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each lamb. It is a burnt offering, a soothing smell, an offering by fire to the LORD.
28:14 Their drink offerings are to be half a hin of wine for a bull, a third of a hin for the ram, and a quarter of a hin for a lamb. This is the burnt offering for each new moon, month by month, through the months of the year.
28:15 Also one male goat is to be offered to the LORD as a sin offering, on top of the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.
## Passover and Unleavened Bread
28:16 In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is the LORD's Passover.
28:17 On the fifteenth day of this month is a feast. For seven days unleavened bread is to be eaten.
28:18 On the first day there is a holy gathering. Do no daily work.
28:19 Present an offering by fire, a burnt offering to the LORD: two young bulls from the herd, one ram, and seven male lambs in their first year. They must be flawless.

## Vows
30:1 Moses spoke to the heads of the tribes of the Israelites: "This is the word the LORD has commanded.
30:2 When a man makes a vow to the LORD, or swears an oath that binds him to a pledge, he may not go back on his word. Everything his mouth has spoken, he must do.
30:3 If a woman makes a vow to the LORD and binds herself with a pledge while she is young and in her father's house,
30:4 Suppose her father hears about the vow and the pledge, and he keeps quiet. Then every vow of hers holds, and every pledge she made holds too.
30:5 But suppose her father refuses her on the day he hears. Then none of her vows and pledges hold. Since her father refused her, the LORD will forgive her.
30:6 If she marries while her vows are on her, or a rash promise from her lips that she has bound herself with,
30:7 and her husband hears of it, and he keeps quiet on the day he hears, then her vows hold. The pledges she made hold too.
30:8 But her husband may forbid her on the day he hears of it. Then he cancels the vow that is on her and the rash promise from her lips that she bound herself with. And the LORD will forgive her.
30:9 A widow or a divorced woman is held to her vow. Every pledge she makes stays binding on her.
30:10 If a woman in her husband's house makes a vow, or binds herself with a pledge under oath,
30:11 and her husband hears of it but keeps quiet and does not refuse her, then every vow of hers holds. Every pledge she made holds too.
30:12 But if her husband clearly cancels them on the day he hears them, nothing that came from her lips, whether her vows or the pledge on herself, will stand. Her husband has canceled them, and the LORD will forgive her.
30:13 Her husband can let any vow stand, and any sworn pledge to humble herself. Or her husband can cancel it.
30:14 But say her husband keeps quiet toward her day after day. Then he lets every vow and every pledge of hers stand. By keeping quiet on the day he heard, he made them stand.
30:15 But if he cancels them some time after he has heard of them, then he will carry her guilt."
30:16 These are the rules the LORD gave Moses for a man and his wife, and for a father and his young daughter still living in his house.

35:6 Six of the cities you give the Levites will be cities of refuge, where someone who has killed a person can run for safety. On top of these, give them forty-two more cities.
35:7 In all, you will give the Levites forty-eight cities, along with the pastureland around them.
35:8 The cities will come out of the land the Israelites own. A tribe with much land gives more cities, and a tribe with little gives fewer. Each tribe gives the Levites cities to match the size of the inheritance it receives."
## Cities of refuge
35:9 The LORD spoke to Moses:
35:10 "Speak to the Israelites and tell them: 'When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,
35:11 pick out some cities to serve you as cities of refuge. A killer who has struck someone down by mistake can run to one of them.
35:12 The cities will give you a place to hide from the avenger. Then the killer will not be put to death before he has stood trial in front of the community.
35:13 The cities you give will be six cities of refuge for you.
35:14 Give three cities on the other side of the Jordan and three cities in the land of Canaan. They will be cities of refuge.
35:15 These six cities will be a refuge for the Israelites, and for the foreigner and the settler living among them, so that anyone who strikes someone down by mistake can flee there.
## Murder or accident
35:16 But if a man strikes someone with an iron tool and the person dies, the man is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.
35:17 If he strikes someone with a stone in his hand, big enough to kill, and the person dies, he is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.
35:18 Or if he strikes someone with a wooden tool in his hand, deadly enough to kill, and the person dies, he is a murderer. The murderer must be put to death.
35:19 It is the avenger of blood who will put the murderer to death. When he catches him, he will kill him.
35:20 Suppose someone pushes another person out of hate, or hides and waits and throws something at him, and the person dies.
35:21 Or suppose he hits him with his fist out of hostility, and the person dies. Then the one who hit him must be put to death, for he is a murderer. The avenger of blood will kill the murderer when he catches him.
35:22 But suppose a man pushes someone all of a sudden, with no hostility. Or he throws something at him without lying in wait.
35:23 Or, not seeing him, he drops a stone on him that is big enough to kill, and the person dies. Yet the man was not his enemy and did not mean him harm.
35:24 In such cases the community is to judge between the man who struck the blow and the avenger of blood, following these rules.
35:25 The community must save the killer from the avenger of blood and send him back to the city of refuge where he ran. He must stay there until the high priest dies, the one anointed with the holy oil.
35:26 But the killer might go out past the border of the city of refuge he ran to.
35:27 If the avenger of blood finds him outside that border and kills him, the avenger will not be guilty of blood.
35:28 For the killer was required to stay in his city of refuge until the high priest died. Only after the high priest's death may the killer go back to his own land.
35:29 These things will be a rule of law for you through your generations, wherever you live.
## Blood pollutes the land
35:30 Whoever strikes someone dead, the killer is to be put to death only on the word of witnesses. One witness alone may not testify against anyone so that he dies.
35:31 Do not take a ransom for the life of a murderer who is guilty and deserves to die. He must be put to death.
35:32 And do not take a ransom for someone who has fled to his city of refuge, to let him return to live in the land before the death of the priest.
35:33 Do not pollute the land you live in. Blood pollutes the land. No atonement can be made for the land for the blood shed on it, except by the blood of the one who shed it.
35:34 Do not make the land unclean where you live, where I live in your midst, for I, the LORD, live among the Israelites.'"
## Daughters who inherit marry within the tribe
36:1 Then the family heads of the clan of Gilead's descendants came forward. Gilead was the son of Machir, son of Manasseh, of the clans of Joseph's descendants. They spoke in front of Moses and the leaders, the family heads of the Israelites.
36:2 "Sir," they said, "the LORD ordered you to divide the land among the Israelites by lot, as their inheritance. The LORD also ordered you, sir, to hand our brother Zelophehad's inheritance to his daughters.
36:3 Now say they marry men from some other Israelite tribe. Then their inheritance will be cut away from what our fathers left us. It will be added to the tribe they marry into, and our share, the one that fell to us by lot, will shrink.
36:4 Then when the Israelites' Jubilee comes, their inheritance will be joined for good to their husbands' tribe. It will be cut away from the inheritance of our fathers' tribe."
36:5 Moses gave the Israelites a command at the LORD's word: "The tribe of Joseph's descendants has spoken rightly.
36:6 This is what the LORD commands about Zelophehad's daughters: 'Let them marry whoever is good in their eyes, only they must marry within a clan of their father's tribe.
36:7 An inheritance of the Israelites must not move from one tribe to another. Every Israelite must keep the inheritance of his fathers' tribe.
36:8 Any daughter who inherits land in an Israelite tribe must marry a man from a clan of her father's tribe. That way every Israelite will keep what his fathers left him.
36:9 No inheritance may move from one tribe to another. Each Israelite tribe must keep its own inheritance.'"
36:10 Zelophehad's daughters did as the LORD had commanded Moses.
36:11 Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, became the wives of their uncles' sons.
36:12 They married into clans of the descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph, so their inheritance stayed in their father's clan and tribe.
36:13 These are the commands and the laws the LORD gave the Israelites through Moses on the plains of Moab, beside the Jordan across from Jericho.
