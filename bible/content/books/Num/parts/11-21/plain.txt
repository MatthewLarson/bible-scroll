## Fire at Taberah
11:1 Now the people became like people moaning about hard times, and it sounded evil in the LORD's ears. The LORD heard it, and his anger burned. The LORD's fire burned among them and ate up the edge of the camp.
11:2 The people cried out to Moses. Moses prayed to the LORD, and the fire sank down.
11:3 So the place was named Taberah, because the LORD's fire had burned among them.
## Craving for meat
11:4 The hangers-on who were among them were seized by a craving, and the Israelites too began to weep again. They said, "Who will feed us meat?
11:5 We remember the fish we ate in Egypt for nothing, the cucumbers, the melons, the leeks, the onions and the garlic.
11:6 But now our appetite has dried up. There is nothing at all. We have nothing to look at but the manna!"
11:7 The manna was like coriander seed, and it looked like bdellium.
11:8 The people went around and gathered it. They ground it in hand mills or pounded it in a mortar, boiled it in a pot and made it into cakes. It tasted like a cake made with rich oil.
11:9 When the dew came down on the camp at night, the manna came down with it.
## Moses' burden
11:10 Moses heard the people weeping, clan by clan, each man at the entrance of his tent. The LORD's anger burned hot, and in Moses' eyes it was evil.
11:11 Moses said to the LORD, "Why have you done harm to your servant? Why have I not found favor in your eyes, that you lay the load of this whole people on me?
11:12 Did I conceive this whole people? Did I give birth to them? Yet you say to me, 'Carry them in your arms to the land you swore to their ancestors, the way a foster father carries a nursing baby.'
11:13 Where am I to get meat to give this whole people? They keep weeping to me and saying, 'Give us meat, so we can eat!'
11:14 I cannot carry this whole people alone. It is too heavy for me.
11:15 If you are going to deal with me like this, kill me, please, kill me outright, if I have found favor in your eyes. Do not let me look on my own misery."
## Seventy elders and the spirit
11:16 The LORD said to Moses, "Gather for me seventy men from the elders of Israel, men you know to be elders of the people and its officers. Take them to the tent of meeting, and have them take their stand there with you.
11:17 I will come down and speak with you there. I will draw off some of the spirit that is on you and put it on them. They will carry the load of the people with you, so that you do not carry it alone.
11:18 "And to the people say, 'Make yourselves holy for tomorrow, and you will eat meat. For you wept in the LORD's ears and said, "Who will feed us meat? We had it good in Egypt!" So the LORD will give you meat, and you will eat.
11:19 You will eat it not for one day, or two days, or five days, or ten days, or twenty days,
11:20 but for a whole month, until it comes out of your nostrils and turns your stomach. For you have rejected the LORD who is among you, and you have wept before him, saying, "Why did we ever come out of Egypt?"'"
11:21 But Moses said, "The people I am among number six hundred thousand men on foot. And you say, 'I will give them meat, and they will eat for a whole month'!
11:22 If flocks and herds were slaughtered for them, would there be enough for them? If all the fish of the sea were gathered for them, would there be enough for them?"
11:23 The LORD said to Moses, "Is the LORD's hand too short? Now you will see whether what I say comes true for you or not."
11:24 So Moses went out and told the people the LORD's words. He gathered seventy men from the elders of the people and had them stand around the tent.
11:25 Then the LORD came down in the cloud and spoke to him. He drew off some of the spirit that was on him and put it on the seventy men, the elders. When the spirit settled on them, they prophesied, but they did not do it again.
11:26 Two men had stayed behind in the camp. The name of one was Eldad, and the name of the second was Medad. The spirit settled on them too. They were among those written down, but they had not gone out to the tent, and they prophesied in the camp.
11:27 A young man ran and told Moses, "Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp!"
11:28 Joshua son of Nun, Moses' assistant from his youth, spoke up: "My lord Moses, hold them back!"
11:29 But Moses said to him, "Are you being zealous for my sake? If only all the LORD's people were prophets, if only the LORD would put his spirit on them!"
11:30 Then Moses went back to the camp, he and the elders of Israel.
## The quail
11:31 A wind set out from the LORD. It swept quail in from the sea and dropped them beside the camp. They lay all around the camp, about a day's walk on this side and a day's walk on that side, about two cubits above the face of the ground.
11:32 The people got up and gathered the quail all that day and all that night and all the next day. The one who gathered least gathered ten homers. They spread them out for themselves all around the camp.
11:33 The meat was still between their teeth, not yet chewed up, when the LORD's anger burned against the people, and the LORD struck the people with a very great plague.
11:34 So the place was named Kibroth Hattaavah, because there they buried the people who had craved.
11:35 From Kibroth Hattaavah the people set out for Hazeroth, and they stayed at Hazeroth.
## Miriam and Aaron against Moses
12:1 Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of the Cushite woman he had married, for he had married a Cushite woman.
12:2 They said, "Is it really only through Moses that the LORD has spoken? Has he not spoken through us too?" And the LORD heard.
12:3 Now the man Moses was very humble, more than any human being on the face of the ground.
12:4 Suddenly the LORD said to Moses, to Aaron and to Miriam, "Come out, you three, to the tent of meeting." And the three of them came out.
12:5 The LORD came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tent. He called Aaron and Miriam, and the two of them came out.
12:6 He said, "Hear my words, please. / If there is a prophet of the LORD among you, / I make myself known to him in a vision; / I speak with him in a dream.
12:7 Not so my servant Moses. / In all my house he is faithful.
12:8 Mouth to mouth I speak with him, / openly and not in riddles, / and he looks at the form of the LORD. / So why were you not afraid / to speak against my servant, against Moses?"
12:9 The LORD's anger burned against them, and he went away.
12:10 When the cloud moved off from over the tent, there was Miriam, struck with a skin disease, like snow. Aaron turned toward Miriam, and there she was, struck with the disease.
12:11 Aaron said to Moses, "Oh, my lord, please do not lay on us the sin we have so foolishly committed, the sin we have done.
12:12 Please do not let her be like the dead, like one who comes out of his mother's womb with half his flesh eaten away."
12:13 So Moses cried out to the LORD, "God, please, heal her, please!"
12:14 The LORD said to Moses, "If her father had only spit in her face, would she not be put to shame for seven days? Let her be shut outside the camp for seven days. After that she may be brought back in."
12:15 So Miriam was shut outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not set out until Miriam was brought back in.
12:16 After that the people set out from Hazeroth and camped in the Wilderness of Paran.
## Twelve men sent to scout
13:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
13:2 "Send men to scout out the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the Israelites. Send one man from each tribe of their fathers, every one a leader among them."
13:3 So Moses sent them from the Wilderness of Paran at the LORD's word. All of them were men who were heads of the Israelites.
13:4 These were their names: from the tribe of Reuben, Shammua son of Zaccur;
13:5 from the tribe of Simeon, Shaphat son of Hori;
13:6 from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;
13:7 from the tribe of Issachar, Igal son of Joseph;
13:8 from the tribe of Ephraim, Hoshea son of Nun;
13:9 from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti son of Raphu;
13:10 from the tribe of Zebulun, Gaddiel son of Sodi;
13:11 from the tribe of Joseph, that is, the tribe of Manasseh, Gaddi son of Susi;
13:12 from the tribe of Dan, Ammiel son of Gemalli;
13:13 from the tribe of Asher, Sethur son of Michael;
13:14 from the tribe of Naphtali, Nahbi son of Vophsi;
13:15 from the tribe of Gad, Geuel son of Machi.
13:16 These are the names of the men Moses sent to scout out the land. And Moses called Hoshea son of Nun "Joshua."
13:17 Moses sent them to scout out the land of Canaan and told them, "Go up there into the Negev, and go up into the hill country.
13:18 See what the land is like. See whether the people who live in it are strong or weak, few or many.
13:19 What is the land they live in like? Is it good or bad? What are the towns they live in like? Are they open camps or strongholds?
13:20 What is the soil like? Is it rich or thin? Are there trees in it or not? Be bold, and bring back some of the fruit of the land." It was the season of the first ripe grapes.
## The land and its fruit
13:21 So they went up and scouted out the land, from the Wilderness of Zin as far as Rehob, near Lebo Hamath.
13:22 They went up through the Negev and came to Hebron. Ahiman, Sheshai and Talmai, the descendants of Anak, were there. (Hebron had been built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.)
13:23 They came to the Valley of Eshcol, and from there they cut down a branch with a single cluster of grapes. They carried it on a pole between two of them, along with some of the pomegranates and some of the figs.
13:24 That place was called the Valley of Eshcol because of the cluster the Israelites cut down there.
13:25 At the end of forty days they came back from scouting out the land.
13:26 They went and came to Moses and Aaron and the whole community of Israelites in the Wilderness of Paran, at Kadesh. They brought back word to them and to the whole community, and they showed them the fruit of the land.
13:27 They gave Moses their account: "We went into the land where you sent us. And yes, it is flowing with milk and honey, and this is its fruit.
13:28 But the people who live in the land are fierce, and the towns are walled and very large. We saw the descendants of Anak there as well.
13:29 Amalek lives in the land of the Negev. The Hittites, the Jebusites and the Amorites live in the hill country. The Canaanites live by the sea and along the bank of the Jordan."
13:30 Then Caleb hushed the people before Moses and said, "We will go straight up and take possession of it, for we will win against it, without a doubt."
13:31 But the men who had gone up with him said, "We cannot go up against that people, because it is stronger than we are."
13:32 So they put out to the Israelites a bad report about the land they had scouted. They said, "The land we passed through to scout out is a land that eats up the people who live there. All the people we saw in it are men of great size.
13:33 There we saw the Nephilim, the descendants of Anak, who come from the Nephilim. In our own eyes we were like grasshoppers, and so we were in their eyes."
## The night of weeping
14:1 Then the whole community lifted up their voices and cried out, and the people wept that night.
14:2 All the Israelites complained against Moses and Aaron. The whole community said to them, "If only we had died in the land of Egypt! Or in this wilderness, if only we had died!
14:3 Why is the LORD bringing us into this land, only to fall by the sword? Our wives and our little ones will become plunder. Isn't going back to Egypt the better thing for us?"
14:4 And they said to one another, "Let's appoint a leader and go back to Egypt."
14:5 Then Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before the whole assembly of the community of Israelites.
14:6 Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh, two of those who had scouted out the land, tore their clothes.
14:7 They said to the whole community of Israelites, "The land we passed through to scout out is a good land, very, very good.
14:8 If the LORD is pleased with us, he will bring us into this land and give it to us, a land that flows with milk and honey.
14:9 Only do not rebel against the LORD. And do not be afraid of the people of the land, for they are our bread. Their shade has turned away from them, and the LORD is with us. Do not be afraid of them!"
14:10 But the whole community talked of stoning them. Then the glory of the LORD appeared at the tent of meeting to all the Israelites.
## Moses pleads for the people
14:11 The LORD said to Moses, "How long will this people spurn me? How long will they not believe in me, after all the signs I have done among them?
14:12 I will strike them with plague and strip them of their inheritance, and I will make you into a nation greater and mightier than they are."
14:13 But Moses said to the LORD, "Then Egypt will hear, for you brought this people up from among them by your strength.
14:14 They will tell it to the people who live in this land. These people have heard that you, LORD, are among this people. They know that you, LORD, are seen eye to eye, and that your cloud stands over them. They know you go in front of them in a pillar of cloud by day and in a pillar of fire by night.
14:15 If you put this people to death as one man, the nations who have heard the report about you will say,
14:16 'The LORD was not able to bring this people into the land he swore to them, so he slaughtered them in the wilderness.'
14:17 So now, please, let the Lord's power be great, just as you have said:
14:18 'The LORD is slow to anger and great in loyal love, forgiving guilt and rebellion. Yet he will by no means leave guilt unpunished. He brings the fathers' guilt home to the children, to the third and the fourth generation.'
14:19 Forgive, please, the guilt of this people as your great loyal love deserves, just as you have forgiven this people from Egypt until now."
14:20 The LORD said, "I have forgiven, as you asked.
14:21 But as surely as I live, and as surely as the LORD's glory will fill all the earth, I swear this:
14:22 These men saw my glory and my signs that I did in Egypt and in the wilderness. Yet they have tested me these ten times and have not listened to my voice.
14:23 Not one of them will see the land I swore to their ancestors. None of those who spurned me will see it.
14:24 But my servant Caleb had a different spirit with him, and he followed me fully. So I will bring him into the land where he went, and his offspring will possess it.
14:25 Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites are living in the valley. Tomorrow turn and set out into the wilderness by the road to the Sea of Reeds."
## Forty years for forty days
14:26 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron:
14:27 "How long must I bear with this evil community that keeps complaining against me? I have heard the complaints the Israelites keep making against me.
14:28 Say to them, 'As I live, declares the LORD, I will do to you just what you have spoken in my ears.
14:29 In this wilderness your dead bodies will fall, every one of you who was enrolled, from twenty years old and up, all of you who complained against me.
14:30 Not one of you will come into the land where I raised my hand to settle you, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.
14:31 But your little ones, who you said would become plunder, I will bring in, and they will know the land you rejected.
14:32 But as for you, your dead bodies will fall in this wilderness.
14:33 Your children will be shepherds in the wilderness for forty years. They will suffer for your prostitution until every one of your dead bodies has fallen in the wilderness.
14:34 By the number of the days you scouted out the land, forty days, a day for a year, a day for a year, you will bear your guilt forty years. Then you will learn what it means when I oppose you.'
14:35 I, the LORD, have spoken. I will surely do this to this whole evil community that has gathered against me. In this wilderness they will come to their end, and there they will die."
14:36 Moses had sent men to scout out the land. They came back and made the whole community complain against him by spreading a bad report about the land.
14:37 Those men who spread the evil report about the land died by the plague before the LORD.
14:38 But of the men who went to scout out the land, Joshua son of Nun and Caleb son of Jephunneh lived.
## Defeat at Hormah
14:39 Moses spoke these words to all the Israelites, and the people mourned bitterly.
14:40 They got up early in the morning and went up toward the top of the hill country, saying, "Here we are. We will go up to the place the LORD spoke of, for we have sinned."
14:41 But Moses said, "Why are you going against the LORD's command? It will not work.
14:42 Do not go up, for the LORD is not among you. Do not let yourselves be struck down before your enemies.
14:43 For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there in front of you, and you will fall by the sword. Because you have turned back from following the LORD, the LORD will not be with you."
14:44 But they dared to go up to the top of the hill country. Yet the ark of the LORD's covenant and Moses did not move from the middle of the camp.
14:45 Then the Amalekites and the Canaanites who lived in that hill country came down and struck them and beat them down as far as Hormah.
## Grain and wine with the offerings
15:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
15:2 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them, 'You are going to come into the land where you will live, which I am giving you.
15:3 There you will make an offering by fire to the LORD from the herd or from the flock, to make a soothing smell for the LORD. It may be a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to keep a vow, or as a freewill offering, or at your appointed feasts.
15:4 When you do, the one who presents his offering is to present a grain offering to the LORD as well. It is to be a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a quarter of a hin of oil.
15:5 Also prepare a quarter of a hin of wine for the drink offering with the burnt offering or the sacrifice, for each lamb.
15:6 Or for a ram, prepare a grain offering of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a third of a hin of oil,
15:7 and for the drink offering present a third of a hin of wine, a soothing smell for the LORD.
15:8 When you prepare a young bull as a burnt offering or a sacrifice, to keep a vow, or as peace offerings to the LORD,
15:9 present with the young bull a grain offering of three tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with half a hin of oil,
15:10 and present half a hin of wine for the drink offering, an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
15:11 This is how it is to be done for each bull or each ram, or for each lamb or young goat.
15:12 However many you prepare, do the same for each one, by their number.
15:13 Everyone born in the land is to do these things this way when he presents an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
15:14 A foreigner may be living with you, or anyone else among you through your generations. If he makes an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD, he is to do just as you do.
15:15 The assembly will have one rule for you and for the foreigner living with you, a rule for all time through your generations. You and the foreigner will be the same before the LORD.
15:16 There will be one law and one ruling for you and for the foreigner living with you.'"
## The first of the dough
15:17 The LORD spoke to Moses:
15:18 "Speak to the Israelites and say to them, 'When you come into the land where I am bringing you,
15:19 and you eat from the food of the land, set aside a gift for the LORD.
15:20 From the first of your dough, set aside a loaf as a gift. Set it aside just as you set aside the gift from the threshing floor.
15:21 Through your generations you are to give the LORD a gift from the first of your dough.
## Sins by mistake and sins with a high hand
15:22 "'Now if you go astray and do not keep all these commands the LORD has spoken to Moses,
15:23 all that the LORD has commanded you through Moses, from the day the LORD gave the command and onward through your generations,
15:24 if it was done by mistake, without the community seeing it, this is what to do. The whole community is to offer one young bull as a burnt offering, a soothing smell for the LORD. With it they are to bring its grain offering and its drink offering, as the rule requires, and one male goat as a sin offering.
15:25 The priest will make atonement for the whole community of Israelites, and they will be forgiven, because it was a mistake. They have brought their offering for their mistake: an offering by fire to the LORD, and their sin offering before the LORD.
15:26 The whole community of Israelites will be forgiven, and the foreigner living among them too, for the whole people were in the mistake.
15:27 "'If one person sins by mistake, he is to present a female goat a year old as a sin offering.
15:28 The priest will make atonement before the LORD for the person who went astray and sinned by mistake, to make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.
15:29 For the one born among the Israelites and for the foreigner living among them, you will have one law for anyone who acts by mistake.
15:30 "'But the person who acts with a high hand, whether born in the land or a foreigner, is insulting the LORD. That person will be cut off from among his people.
15:31 Because he has despised the LORD's word and broken his command, that person must be cut off completely. His guilt is on him.'"
## The man gathering wood
15:32 While the Israelites were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering wood on the Sabbath day.
15:33 Those who found him gathering wood brought him to Moses and Aaron and to the whole community.
15:34 They put him under guard, because it had not been made clear what should be done to him.
15:35 Then the LORD said to Moses, "The man must be put to death. The whole community is to stone him with stones outside the camp."
15:36 So the whole community brought him outside the camp and stoned him with stones, and he died, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
## Tassels to remember
15:37 The LORD said to Moses:
15:38 "Speak to the Israelites. Tell them to make tassels for themselves on the corners of their clothes, through their generations. They are to put a cord of blue-violet on the tassel of each corner.
15:39 It will be a tassel for you. You will see it and remember all the LORD's commands and do them. You will not go scouting after your own heart and your own eyes, which you go after like a prostitute.
15:40 So you will remember and do all my commands, and you will be holy to your God.
15:41 I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt to be your God. I am the LORD your God."
## Korah's revolt
16:1 Korah son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, took men, along with Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth, who were Reubenites.
16:2 They rose up before Moses, with two hundred fifty Israelite men, leaders of the community, chosen for the assembly, men with a name.
16:3 They gathered against Moses and Aaron and said to them, "Enough of you! The whole community is holy, every one of them, and the LORD is among them. So why do you raise yourselves above the LORD's assembly?"
16:4 When Moses heard it, he fell on his face.
16:5 Then he spoke to Korah and to all his band: "In the morning the LORD will make known who is his and who is holy. He will bring that one near to himself. The one he chooses he will bring near to himself.
16:6 Do this, Korah, you and all your band: take fire pans for yourselves.
16:7 Put fire in them and set incense on them before the LORD tomorrow. The man the LORD chooses will be the holy one. Enough of you, sons of Levi!"
16:8 Moses said to Korah, "Listen, please, you sons of Levi.
16:9 Does it seem small to you? The God of Israel has set you apart from the community of Israel to bring you near to himself. You do the work of the LORD's tabernacle, and you stand before the community to serve them.
16:10 He has brought you near, and all your brothers the sons of Levi with you, and now you seek the priesthood too!
16:11 So it is against the LORD that you and all your band have gathered. And Aaron, what is he, that you complain against him?"
16:12 Then Moses sent to summon Dathan and Abiram, sons of Eliab. But they said, "We will not come up!
16:13 Does it seem small that you brought us up out of a land flowing with milk and honey, to kill us in the wilderness? Must you also make yourself prince over us?
16:14 Besides, you have not brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey, or given us fields and vineyards to inherit. Will you gouge out the eyes of these men? We will not come up!"
16:15 Moses grew very angry and said to the LORD, "Do not turn toward their offering. I have not taken one donkey from them, and I have not done harm to one of them."
16:16 Moses said to Korah, "You and all your band, be before the LORD tomorrow, you and they and Aaron.
16:17 Each of you take his fire pan and put incense on it, and each of you present his fire pan before the LORD, two hundred fifty fire pans. You and Aaron too, each with his fire pan."
16:18 So each man took his fire pan, put fire in it and set incense on it. They stood at the entrance of the tent of meeting, with Moses and Aaron.
16:19 Korah gathered the whole community against them at the entrance of the tent of meeting. Then the glory of the LORD appeared to the whole community.
16:20 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron:
16:21 "Separate yourselves from this community, so I can finish them off in a moment."
16:22 But they fell on their faces and said, "God, God of the spirits of all flesh, when one man sins, will you be angry with the whole community?"
16:23 The LORD spoke to Moses:
16:24 "Speak to the community and say, 'Get away from around the dwelling of Korah, Dathan and Abiram.'"
16:25 Moses got up and went to Dathan and Abiram, and the elders of Israel went after him.
16:26 He spoke to the community: "Turn away, please, from the tents of these wicked men. Do not touch anything that is theirs, or you will be swept away in all their sins."
16:27 So they got away from around the dwelling of Korah, Dathan and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram had come out and were standing at the entrance of their tents, with their wives, their sons and their little ones.
## The earth opens
16:28 Then Moses said, "By this you will know that the LORD has sent me to do all these deeds, and that they did not come from my own heart:
16:29 If these men die the way every human being dies, and the fate of every human being comes on them, then the LORD has not sent me.
16:30 But the LORD may create something new. The ground may open its mouth and swallow them and all that is theirs, so that they go down alive to Sheol. Then you will know that these men have spurned the LORD."
16:31 Just as he finished speaking all these words, the ground under them was split in two.
16:32 The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them up: them, their households, every person who was Korah's, and everything they owned.
16:33 They and all that was theirs went down alive to Sheol. The earth closed over them, and they were gone from among the assembly.
16:34 All Israel around them fled at their cries, for they said, "The earth might swallow us too!"
16:35 And fire came out from the LORD and ate up the two hundred fifty men who were offering the incense.
## The fire pans and the altar
16:36 The LORD spoke to Moses:
16:37 "Tell Eleazar son of Aaron the priest to take the fire pans up out of the burning, and scatter the fire far off, for they are holy,
16:38 the fire pans of these men who sinned at the cost of their lives. Have them hammered into thin sheets as a covering for the altar, because they presented them before the LORD and they became holy. They will be a sign for the Israelites."
16:39 So Eleazar the priest took the bronze fire pans that the men who were burned had presented, and they were hammered out as a covering for the altar.
16:40 It was a reminder for the Israelites, just as the LORD had spoken to Eleazar through Moses. No outsider, no one who is not of Aaron's offspring, was to come near to burn incense before the LORD, or he would become like Korah and his band.
## Aaron stands between the dead and the living
16:41 The next day the whole community of Israelites complained against Moses and Aaron and said, "You have killed the LORD's people!"
16:42 When the community gathered against Moses and Aaron, they turned toward the tent of meeting, and there the cloud was covering it, and the glory of the LORD appeared.
16:43 Moses and Aaron came to the front of the tent of meeting.
16:44 The LORD spoke to Moses:
16:45 "Get away from this community, so I can finish them off in a moment." And they fell on their faces.
16:46 Moses said to Aaron, "Take the fire pan, put fire from the altar in it, and lay incense on it. Take it quickly to the community and make atonement for them, for wrath has gone out from the LORD's presence. The plague has begun."
16:47 Aaron took it as Moses had said and ran into the middle of the assembly. The plague had already begun among the people. He put on the incense and made atonement for the people.
16:48 He stood between the dead and the living, and the plague was held back.
16:49 Those who died in the plague were fourteen thousand seven hundred, besides those who died in the matter of Korah.
16:50 Then Aaron returned to Moses at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the plague had been held back.
## Aaron's staff buds
17:1 The LORD spoke to Moses:
17:2 "Speak to the Israelites, and take from them a staff for each father's house, from all their leaders by their fathers' houses, twelve staffs. Write each man's name on his staff.
17:3 Write Aaron's name on the staff of Levi, for there is one staff for the head of each father's house.
17:4 Lay them down in the tent of meeting in front of the testimony, where I meet with you.
17:5 The staff of the man I choose will sprout. So I will quiet the complaints of the Israelites that they keep making against you, and be rid of them."
17:6 So Moses spoke to the Israelites, and all their leaders gave him a staff, one staff for each leader, by their fathers' houses, twelve staffs. Aaron's staff was among their staffs.
17:7 Moses laid the staffs down before the LORD in the tent of the testimony.
17:8 The next day Moses went into the tent of the testimony, and there was the staff of Aaron, for the house of Levi, sprouted! It had brought out buds, burst into blossom and ripened almonds.
17:9 Then Moses brought out all the staffs from before the LORD to all the Israelites. They looked, and each man took his staff.
17:10 The LORD said to Moses, "Put Aaron's staff back in front of the testimony, to be kept as a sign for the rebels. So you will put an end to their complaints against me, and they will not die."
17:11 Moses did it. Just as the LORD commanded him, so he did.
17:12 The Israelites said to Moses, "Look, we are dying! We perish, we all perish!
17:13 Anyone who comes near, anyone who comes near the LORD's tabernacle, will die. Are we to die to the last one?"
## Duties of priests and Levites
18:1 The LORD said to Aaron, "You and your sons and your father's house with you will bear the guilt of the sanctuary. And you and your sons with you will bear the guilt of your priesthood.
18:2 Bring near with you your brothers too, the tribe of Levi, your father's tribe. They will be joined to you and serve you, while you and your sons with you are before the tent of the testimony.
18:3 They are to keep their duty to you and their duty to the whole tent. Only they must not come near the holy objects or the altar, so that neither they nor you will die.
18:4 They will be joined to you and keep the duty of the tent of meeting, all the work of the tent, and no outsider may come near you.
18:5 You are to keep the duty of the holy place and the duty of the altar, so that there will be no more wrath on the Israelites.
18:6 Look, I myself have taken your brothers the Levites from among the Israelites. They are a gift for you, given to the LORD, to do the work of the tent of meeting.
18:7 But you and your sons with you are to keep your priesthood. It covers every matter of the altar and what is inside the curtain, and you are to serve there. I give you your priesthood as a gift of service. The outsider who comes near is to be put to death."
## The priests' share
18:8 The LORD spoke to Aaron: "Look, I myself have given you charge of the gifts set aside for me, all the holy gifts of the Israelites. I give them to you and to your sons as your portion, a share for all time.
18:9 This will be yours from the most holy things, the parts kept back from the fire. Every offering of theirs that they pay back to me is yours: every grain offering, every sin offering and every guilt offering. It is most holy for you and for your sons.
18:10 Eat it as a most holy thing. Every male may eat it. It is holy to you.
18:11 This is also yours: the gift set aside from what they give, all the wave offerings of the Israelites. I give them to you, and to your sons and your daughters with you, as a share for all time. Everyone in your household who is clean may eat it.
18:12 I give you the first of what they give to the LORD: all the best of the fresh oil, and all the best of the new wine and grain.
18:13 The first ripe fruit of all that is in their land, which they bring to the LORD, will be yours. Everyone in your household who is clean may eat it.
18:14 Everything devoted in Israel will be yours.
18:15 Every firstborn of the womb, of all flesh, that they present to the LORD, whether human or animal, will be yours. But you must buy back the firstborn of humans, and you are to buy back the firstborn of unclean animals.
18:16 As for their redemption price, buy them back from a month old, at the value you set. It is five shekels of silver by the shekel of the holy place, which is twenty gerahs.
18:17 But the firstborn of a cow, or the firstborn of a sheep, or the firstborn of a goat, you must not buy back. They are holy. Splash their blood on the altar, and turn their fat to smoke as an offering by fire, a soothing smell for the LORD.
18:18 Their meat will be yours, like the breast of the wave offering and like the right thigh. It will be yours.
18:19 All the holy gifts that the Israelites set aside for the LORD I give to you, and to your sons and your daughters with you. They are a share for all time. It is a covenant of salt for all time before the LORD, for you and for your offspring with you."
18:20 The LORD said to Aaron, "You will have no inheritance in their land, and no portion among them will be yours. I am your portion and your inheritance among the Israelites.
## The Levites' tithe
18:21 "To the descendants of Levi, look, I give every tithe in Israel as an inheritance, in return for the work they do, the work of the tent of meeting.
18:22 The Israelites must not come near the tent of meeting again, or they will bear sin and die.
18:23 The Levites are the ones who will do the work of the tent of meeting, and they will bear their guilt. It is a rule for all time through your generations. Among the Israelites they will have no inheritance.
18:24 For the tithe of the Israelites, which they set aside as a gift for the LORD, I give to the Levites as an inheritance. That is why I have said of them, 'Among the Israelites they will have no inheritance.'"
18:25 The LORD spoke to Moses:
18:26 "Speak to the Levites and say to them, 'You will take from the Israelites the tithe that I give you from them as your inheritance. When you do, set aside from it a gift for the LORD, a tenth of the tithe.
18:27 Your gift will be counted to you as if it were grain from the threshing floor, or the full yield from the winepress.
18:28 So you too will set aside the LORD's gift from all the tithes you take from the Israelites. From them you will give the LORD's gift to Aaron the priest.
18:29 Out of all the gifts given to you, set aside every gift due to the LORD, from all the best of it, the part of it that is holy.'
18:30 "Say to them, 'When you set aside the best of it, it will be counted to the Levites like the yield of the threshing floor and the yield of the winepress.
18:31 You may eat it in any place, you and your household, for it is your wages in return for your work in the tent of meeting.
18:32 You will not bear sin because of it, once you have set aside the best of it. Do not make the holy gifts of the Israelites common, so that you will not die.'"
## The red heifer
19:1 The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron:
19:2 "This is the rule of the law that the LORD has commanded. Tell the Israelites to bring you a red heifer, flawless, with no defect in it. No yoke must ever have come on it.
19:3 Give it to Eleazar the priest. He is to take it outside the camp, and someone is to slaughter it in front of him.
19:4 Eleazar the priest is to take some of its blood with his finger and spatter some of its blood seven times toward the front of the tent of meeting.
19:5 Then the heifer is to be burned before his eyes. Its skin, its flesh and its blood are to be burned, along with its dung.
19:6 The priest is to take cedar wood, hyssop and scarlet yarn and throw them into the middle of the fire that burns the heifer.
19:7 Then the priest is to wash his clothes and bathe his body in water. After that he may come into the camp, but the priest will be unclean until evening.
19:8 The one who burns it is also to wash his clothes in water and bathe his body in water, and he will be unclean until evening.
19:9 A man who is clean is to gather up the ashes of the heifer and set them down outside the camp in a clean place. They will be kept for the community of Israelites for the water for impurity. It is a sin offering.
19:10 The one who gathers the ashes of the heifer is to wash his clothes, and he will be unclean until evening. This will be a rule for all time, for the Israelites and for the foreigner living among them.
## Uncleanness from the dead
19:11 "Whoever touches a dead body, any human corpse, will be unclean seven days.
19:12 He is to cleanse himself with it on the third day and on the seventh day, and then he will be clean. But if he does not cleanse himself on the third day and on the seventh day, he will not be clean.
19:13 Anyone who touches a dead body, the corpse of a person who has died, and does not cleanse himself, has made the LORD's tabernacle unclean. That person will be cut off from Israel. Because the water for impurity was not splashed on him, he will be unclean. His uncleanness is still on him.
19:14 "This is the procedure when a person dies in a tent. All who come into the tent and all who are in it will be unclean seven days.
19:15 And every open container that has no lid tied on it is unclean.
19:16 And anyone out in the open field who touches someone killed by the sword, or a dead body, or a human bone, or a grave, will be unclean seven days.
19:17 "For the unclean person, take some of the ash from the burning of the sin offering, and put running water on it in a container.
19:18 Then a clean person is to take hyssop and dip it in the water. He is to spatter it on the tent and on all the containers and on the people who were there. He is also to spatter it on the one who touched the bone, or the one killed, or the dead body, or the grave.
19:19 The clean person is to spatter it on the unclean person on the third day and on the seventh day. On the seventh day he will free him from sin. Then the one cleansed is to wash his clothes and bathe in water, and in the evening he will be clean.
19:20 But a person who is unclean and does not cleanse himself, that person will be cut off from among the assembly, for he has made the LORD's sanctuary unclean. The water for impurity has not been splashed on him. He is unclean.
19:21 This will be a rule for them for all time. The one who spatters the water for impurity is to wash his clothes, and the one who touches the water for impurity will be unclean until evening.
19:22 Anything the unclean person touches will be unclean, and the person who touches it will be unclean until evening."
## Miriam dies; water from the rock
20:1 The Israelites, the whole community, came into the Wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed at Kadesh. Miriam died there and was buried there.
20:2 There was no water for the community, and they gathered against Moses and Aaron.
20:3 The people quarreled with Moses and said, "We wish we had perished when our brothers perished before the LORD!
20:4 Why have you brought the LORD's assembly into this wilderness, for us and our livestock to die here?
20:5 Why did you bring us up out of Egypt, to bring us to this evil place? It is not a place of seed, or figs, or vines, or pomegranates, and there is no water to drink!"
20:6 Moses and Aaron went from the assembly to the entrance of the tent of meeting and fell on their faces, and the glory of the LORD appeared to them.
20:7 The LORD spoke to Moses:
20:8 "Take the staff, and gather the community, you and your brother Aaron. Speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will give out its water. So you will bring water out of the rock for them, and give the community and their livestock a drink."
20:9 So Moses took the staff from before the LORD, just as he had commanded him.
20:10 Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly in front of the rock, and he said to them, "Listen, please, you rebels! Are we to bring water out of this rock for you?"
20:11 Then Moses raised his hand and struck the rock with his staff two times. Water came out in plenty, and the community and their livestock drank.
20:12 But the LORD said to Moses and to Aaron, "You did not believe me, to treat me as holy in the Israelites' eyes. Because of that, you will not lead this assembly into the land I have given them."
20:13 These were the waters of Meribah, where the Israelites quarreled with the LORD, and he showed himself holy among them.
## Edom says no
20:14 Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom: "Your brother Israel says this: You know all the hardship that has found us.
20:15 Our fathers went down to Egypt, and we lived in Egypt many days. The Egyptians did harm to us and to our fathers.
20:16 We cried out to the LORD, and he heard our voice. He sent an angel and brought us out of Egypt. And here we are in Kadesh, a town at the edge of your territory.
20:17 Please let us pass through your land. We will not pass through field or vineyard, and we will not drink water from a well. We will walk on the King's Highway. We will not turn aside to the right or the left until we have passed through your territory."
20:18 But Edom said to him, "You will not pass through me, or I will come out with the sword to meet you."
20:19 The Israelites said to him, "We will go up by the highway. If we drink your water, I and my livestock, I will pay its price. It is nothing. Just let me pass through on foot."
20:20 But Edom said, "You will not pass through." And Edom came out to meet him with a heavy force of people and with a strong hand.
20:21 So Edom refused to let Israel pass through its territory, and Israel turned away from it.
## Aaron dies on Mount Hor
20:22 They set out from Kadesh, and the Israelites, the whole community, came to Mount Hor.
20:23 At Mount Hor, on the border of the land of Edom, the LORD said to Moses and Aaron,
20:24 "Aaron is to be gathered to his people. He will not come into the land I have given the Israelites, because you rebelled against my word at the waters of Meribah.
20:25 Take Aaron and his son Eleazar and bring them up Mount Hor.
20:26 Strip Aaron of his garments and put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron will be gathered, and he will die there."
20:27 Moses did just as the LORD had commanded. They went up Mount Hor before the eyes of the whole community.
20:28 Moses stripped Aaron of his garments and put them on his son Eleazar, and Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.
20:29 When the whole community saw that Aaron had died, all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.
## Victory at Hormah
21:1 The Canaanite king of Arad, who lived in the Negev, heard that Israel was coming by the road of Atharim. He fought against Israel and took some of them captive.
21:2 Then Israel made a vow to the LORD and said, "If you will indeed give this people into my hand, I will devote their towns to destruction."
21:3 The LORD listened to Israel's voice and handed over the Canaanites. They devoted them and their towns to destruction, and the place was named Hormah.
## The bronze snake
21:4 They set out from Mount Hor by the road to the Sea of Reeds, to go around the land of Edom. But the people's patience grew short on the way.
21:5 The people spoke against God and against Moses: "Why have you brought us up out of Egypt to die in the wilderness? There is no bread, and there is no water, and we are sick of this flimsy food!"
21:6 So the LORD sent the burning snakes among the people. They bit the people, and many people of Israel died.
21:7 The people came to Moses and said, "We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD and against you. Pray to the LORD, so that he will take the snakes away from us." So Moses prayed for the people.
21:8 The LORD said to Moses, "Make yourself a burning one and put it on a pole. Anyone who has been bitten and looks at it will live."
21:9 So Moses made a snake of bronze and put it on the pole. Whenever a snake bit someone, he would look at the bronze snake and live.
## The journey to Moab
21:10 The Israelites set out and camped at Oboth.
21:11 They set out from Oboth and camped at Iye Abarim, in the wilderness that faces Moab, toward the sunrise.
21:12 From there they set out and camped in the Valley of Zered.
21:13 From there they set out and camped on the other side of the Arnon, which is in the wilderness that comes out from the territory of the Amorites. For the Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.
21:14 That is why it is said in the Book of the Wars of the LORD: / "Waheb in Suphah, and the valleys, / the Arnon,
21:15 and the slope of the valleys / that stretches to the site of Ar / and leans on the border of Moab."
21:16 From there they went on to Beer, the well where the LORD said to Moses, "Gather the people, and I will give them water."
21:17 Then Israel sang this song: / "Spring up, well! / Sing to it,
21:18 the well that princes dug, / that the nobles of the people dug out / with a scepter, with their staffs." From the wilderness they went to Mattanah,
21:19 from Mattanah to Nahaliel, from Nahaliel to Bamoth,
21:20 and from Bamoth to the valley in the country of Moab, by the top of Pisgah, which looks down over the wasteland.
## Sihon and Og
21:21 Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, saying,
21:22 "Let me pass through your land. We will not turn aside into field or vineyard, and we will not drink water from a well. We will walk on the King's Highway until we have passed through your territory."
21:23 But Sihon did not let Israel pass through his territory. Sihon gathered all his people and went out into the wilderness to meet Israel. He came to Jahaz and fought against Israel.
21:24 Israel struck him with the edge of the sword. They took possession of his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok, as far as the Ammonites, for the border of the Ammonites was strong.
21:25 Israel took all these towns, and Israel settled in all the towns of the Amorites, in Heshbon and all its villages.
21:26 For Heshbon was the city of Sihon king of the Amorites. He had fought against the former king of Moab and taken all his land from his hand, as far as the Arnon.
21:27 That is why the singers of sayings say: / "Come to Heshbon! Let it be built! / Let the city of Sihon be set up!
21:28 For fire went out from Heshbon, / a flame from the town of Sihon. / It ate up Ar of Moab, / the lords of the heights of the Arnon.
21:29 Woe to you, Moab! / You are lost, people of Chemosh! / He has made his sons refugees / and his daughters captives / to Sihon, king of the Amorites.
21:30 But we shot them down. / Heshbon is lost, as far as Dibon. / We laid them waste as far as Nophah, / which reaches to Medeba."
21:31 So Israel settled in the land of the Amorites.
21:32 Moses sent men to scout out Jazer, and they captured its villages and drove out the Amorites who were there.
21:33 Then they turned and went up by the road to Bashan, and Og king of Bashan came out to meet them, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei.
21:34 The LORD said to Moses, "Do not be afraid of him, for I have handed him over to you, with all his people and his land. Do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon."
21:35 So they struck him down, with his sons and all his people, until no survivor was left to him, and they took possession of his land.
