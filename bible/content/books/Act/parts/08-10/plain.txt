## Saul attacks the church
8:1 Saul was in full agreement with putting him to death. That very day the church in Jerusalem came under severe persecution. Everyone but the apostles scattered through the country districts of Judea and Samaria.
8:2 Devout men gathered up Stephen's body for burial and raised a loud lament over him.
8:3 But Saul was mauling the church. He went into one house after another, hauled off men and women alike, and handed them over for prison.
## Philip in Samaria
8:4 Those who had been scattered went from place to place, telling the good news of the word.
8:5 As for Philip, he went down to the city of Samaria, where he announced the Messiah to the people.
8:6 With one mind the crowds listened closely to the things Philip said, as they heard him and watched the signs he was doing.
8:7 Many people had unclean spirits, and the spirits came out of them, shrieking at the top of their voices. Many paralyzed and lame people were cured too.
8:8 Great joy came over that city.
## Simon the magician
8:9 A man named Simon had been in the city before this, practicing magic and astounding the Samaritan people. He made himself out to be someone great.
8:10 Everyone, small and great alike, listened closely to him. "This man is the Power of God," they said, "the one called Great."
8:11 They listened closely to him because for a long time he had kept them astounded with his magic.
8:12 But Philip told them the good news about the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, and they believed him. Men and women alike were baptized.
8:13 Simon himself believed too. After he was baptized he stayed close to Philip, and he was astounded as he watched the signs and great acts of power that were taking place.
## Peter and John in Samaria
8:14 The apostles in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had welcomed the word of God, so they sent Peter and John to them.
8:15 The two went down and prayed for them, asking that they would receive the Holy Spirit.
8:16 Up to then the Spirit had fallen on none of them. All they had received was baptism into the name of the Lord Jesus.
8:17 So the two began to lay their hands on them, and the people received the Holy Spirit.
8:18 Simon saw that it was through the laying on of the apostles' hands that the Spirit was given, and he offered them money.
8:19 "Give me this authority as well," he said, "so that whoever I lay my hands on will receive the Holy Spirit."
8:20 But Peter said to him, "May your silver go to ruin, and you with it, for thinking you could get God's gift with money!
8:21 You have no portion or lot in this matter, because your heart is not straight with God.
8:22 So repent of this evil of yours and plead with the Lord. Perhaps he will forgive you for what you had in your heart.
8:23 I can see that you are steeped in bitter poison and tied up in the chains of wrongdoing."
8:24 Simon replied, "Pray for me yourselves, to the Lord. Ask that none of what you have said will come upon me."
8:25 After they had given their solemn testimony and spoken the word of the Lord, Peter and John started back to Jerusalem, telling the good news in many Samaritan villages on the way.
## Philip and the Ethiopian official
8:26 An angel of the Lord said to Philip, "Get up and go south, to the road running down from Jerusalem to Gaza." It is deserted.
8:27 So he got up and went. And look, there was an Ethiopian man, a eunuch, a court official of the Kandake, queen of the Ethiopians. He was over all her treasure. He had come to Jerusalem to worship,
8:28 and now he was going home. He was sitting in his chariot, reading the prophet Isaiah.
8:29 The Spirit said to Philip, "Go up and keep close to this chariot."
8:30 Philip ran up and heard him reading the prophet Isaiah. "Do you really know what you are reading?" he asked.
8:31 He answered, "How could I, without someone to guide me?" Then he urged Philip to come up and sit beside him.
8:32 This was the part of Scripture he was reading: "Like a sheep he was taken off to slaughter, and like a lamb that makes no sound in front of the one who shears it, he does not open his mouth.
8:33 When he was brought low, justice was snatched from him. Who will tell of his generation? For his life is removed from the earth."
8:34 In answer the eunuch said to Philip, "I beg you, tell me: whom does the prophet mean here? Himself, or some other man?"
8:35 Philip opened his mouth. He started from this same passage of Scripture and told him the good news about Jesus.
8:36 Traveling on along the road, they reached some water, and the eunuch said, "Look, water! What is stopping me from being baptized?"
8:37 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
8:38 He gave the order to halt the chariot. The two of them went down into the water, Philip and the eunuch together, and Philip baptized him.
8:39 As soon as they came up from the water, the Lord's Spirit snatched Philip away. The eunuch never saw him again, for he went on his way rejoicing.
8:40 Philip turned up at Azotus. He went through all the towns telling the good news, until he came to Caesarea.
## Saul on the Damascus road
9:1 Meanwhile Saul's every breath was still threat and murder against the Lord's disciples. He went to the high priest
9:2 and requested letters from him, addressed to the synagogues of Damascus. If he found any people there who were of the Way, men or women, he would tie them up and bring them to Jerusalem.
9:3 He was on the road and nearing Damascus when all at once light from heaven flashed all around him.
9:4 He dropped to the ground. Then he heard a voice speaking to him: "Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me?"
9:5 "Who are you, Lord?" Saul asked. He replied, "I am Jesus, whom you are persecuting.
9:6 But get up and go into the city. There you will be told what you have to do."
9:7 His traveling companions stood dumbstruck. They heard the voice, but they saw no one.
9:8 Saul rose from the ground. His eyes were open, yet he saw nothing, so they took his hand and guided him into Damascus.
9:9 For three days he was unable to see, and he took no food or drink.
## Ananias and Saul
9:10 There was a disciple in Damascus named Ananias. The Lord said to him in a vision, "Ananias!" And he answered, "Here I am, Lord."
9:11 The Lord told him, "Get up and go to the street they call Straight. At Judas' house, look for a Tarsus man named Saul. Even now he is praying,
9:12 and he has seen a man in a vision, one named Ananias, coming in and putting his hands on him so his sight will come back."
9:13 Ananias replied, "Lord, I have heard many people talk about this man, and all the evil things he did to your saints in Jerusalem.
9:14 Now he is here, with authority from the chief priests to tie up everyone who calls on your name."
9:15 But the Lord said to him, "Go, for I have chosen this man as a vessel to carry my name. He will bring it before Gentiles, before kings, and before the Israelites.
9:16 I myself will show him how many things he must suffer on behalf of my name."
9:17 Ananias left and went into the house. He put his hands on Saul and said, "Brother Saul, the Lord has sent me. It is Jesus, who appeared to you on your way here. He sent me so your sight will come back and you will be filled with the Holy Spirit."
9:18 Right away what looked like scales dropped from his eyes, and his sight came back. Then he stood up and was baptized,
9:19 and when he had taken food he grew strong again. For a number of days he was with the Damascus disciples.
## Saul preaches in Damascus
9:20 Without delay he was in the synagogues proclaiming Jesus. He declared that this man is the Son of God.
9:21 Everyone who heard him was astounded. "This is the man who ravaged the people in Jerusalem who call on this name, isn't it?" they said. "And didn't he come here just to put them in chains and take them to the chief priests?"
9:22 But Saul kept growing stronger. He left the Jews living in Damascus confused, as he put the proof together that this man is the Messiah.
9:23 Some considerable time later, the Jews agreed together to do away with him,
9:24 but word of their plot reached Saul. They were even keeping watch on the gates day and night, to do away with him.
9:25 His disciples, though, put him in a big basket one night and lowered him through the wall.
## Saul in Jerusalem
9:26 When he reached Jerusalem, Saul kept trying to attach himself to the disciples. They all feared him and did not believe he was a disciple.
9:27 Barnabas, however, took hold of Saul and led him to the apostles. He described to them how Saul had seen the Lord on the road, how the Lord had spoken to him, and how in Damascus he had spoken out boldly in Jesus' name.
9:28 He was with them, coming and going in Jerusalem.
9:29 He spoke out boldly in the Lord's name. He talked with the Hellenists and argued with them, and they set about trying to kill him.
9:30 The brothers found out about it. They brought him down to Caesarea and sent him on his way to Tarsus.
9:31 Across all Judea, Galilee and Samaria, then, the church was at peace. It was being built up and kept moving forward in the fear of the Lord, and with the Holy Spirit's encouragement it grew larger and larger.
## Aeneas healed at Lydda
9:32 Peter was traveling through the whole region, and he also went down to the saints living in Lydda.
9:33 He came across a man there called Aeneas, who had been lying on a mat for eight years, paralyzed.
9:34 "Aeneas," Peter told him, "Jesus Christ is healing you. Get up and make your own bed." He got up right away.
9:35 Everyone who lived in Lydda and the plain of Sharon saw him, and they turned to the Lord.
## Tabitha raised at Joppa
9:36 At Joppa there was a disciple, a woman named Tabitha (translated, the name is Dorcas). She was full of good works and of the alms she gave.
9:37 It happened during those days that she became ill and died. They washed her and laid her out in an upstairs room.
9:38 Joppa was not far from Lydda. When the disciples heard Peter was there, they sent two men to plead with him: "Do not hesitate; come on over to us."
9:39 So Peter got up and went along with them. When he got there, they brought him up to the room, and all the widows came and stood by him in tears. They showed him the tunics and cloaks Dorcas used to make while she was with them.
9:40 Peter sent them all outside. He knelt and prayed, then turned toward the body and said, "Tabitha, get up." Her eyes opened. She saw Peter and sat up.
9:41 Taking her by the hand, he lifted her up. Then he called in the saints and the widows and stood her before them alive.
9:42 The news spread through the whole of Joppa, and many came to believe in the Lord.
9:43 Peter remained in Joppa for quite a few days, staying with one Simon, who tanned hides.
## Cornelius sees an angel
10:1 A man named Cornelius lived in Caesarea. He was a centurion in the unit called the Italian Cohort.
10:2 He was devout; he and his whole household feared God. He gave much in alms to the people, and he prayed to God constantly.
10:3 About the ninth hour of the day, in a vision, he plainly saw an angel of God. The angel came in to him and said, "Cornelius!"
10:4 Cornelius stared at him, terrified, and said, "What is it, Lord?" The angel told him, "Your prayers have gone up before God, and your alms too, as a remembrance.
10:5 Send some men to Joppa now, and summon a man named Simon, the one called Peter.
10:6 He is staying as a guest with Simon, a tanner who has a house by the sea."
10:7 As soon as the angel speaking with him had left, Cornelius called two of his household servants and a devout soldier, one of his personal attendants.
10:8 He told them all of it and sent them on their way to Joppa.
## Peter's vision
10:9 The next day the men were traveling and drawing near the city. Around the sixth hour, Peter climbed up to the flat roof to pray.
10:10 He grew very hungry and wanted to eat. While they were getting the food ready, a trance came on him.
10:11 He saw heaven opened up and some kind of container coming down, like a great linen sheet let down by its four corners to the ground.
10:12 In it were all the four-footed animals and creeping things of the earth and birds of the sky.
10:13 Then a voice came to him: "Get up, Peter; kill and eat."
10:14 But Peter said, "Not at all, Lord! I have never eaten anything common and unclean."
10:15 The voice spoke to him once more, a second time: "What God has made clean, you must not call common."
10:16 This happened three times, and right away the container was taken up into heaven.
## Peter goes to Caesarea
10:17 Peter was at a loss, wondering to himself what the vision he had seen might mean. Just then the men Cornelius had sent, who had asked their way to Simon's house, came and stood at the gate.
10:18 They called out and asked if a Simon known as Peter was a guest there.
10:19 Peter was still turning the vision over in his mind when the Spirit said to him, "Look, three men are searching for you.
10:20 Get up, go down, and go along with them. Have no doubts at all, for I am the one who sent them."
10:21 So Peter went down to the men. "Here I am, the man you are searching for," he said. "What has brought you here?"
10:22 They answered, "Cornelius the centurion sent us. He is a righteous man who fears God, and the whole Jewish nation speaks well of him. A holy angel gave him instructions to summon you to his house and listen to the words you bring."
10:23 Peter asked them in and put them up as his guests. The next day he got up and left with them, and some of the brothers from Joppa came too.
10:24 On the next day he came into Caesarea. Cornelius was expecting them. He had gathered his relatives and his closest friends.
10:25 Just as Peter was going in, Cornelius came to meet him. He threw himself at Peter's feet and bowed down before him.
10:26 But Peter pulled him to his feet. "Get up," he said. "I am a human being myself."
10:27 Still chatting with him, Peter went in and found a crowd of people who had come together.
10:28 He said to them, "You know yourselves that for a Jewish man, keeping company with a foreigner or visiting one is not allowed. But to me God has shown that no human being is to be called common or unclean.
10:29 That is why I raised no argument and came when you summoned me. Now I am asking: what is the reason you summoned me?"
10:30 Cornelius said, "Four days ago, at just this hour, I was praying in my house at the ninth hour. Then, look, a man dressed in brilliant clothing stood before me.
10:31 He said, 'Cornelius, God has listened to your prayer, and your alms are remembered in his presence.
10:32 Send someone to Joppa, then, and call for Simon, the one known as Peter. He is a guest at the seaside home of Simon the tanner.'
10:33 I sent for you at once, and you did well to come. So now all of us are present here before God, ready to listen to all the Lord has given you orders to say."
## Peter speaks in Cornelius' house
10:34 Then Peter opened his mouth. "I truly grasp it now," he said: "God does not play favorites.
10:35 No, in every nation the one who fears him and practices righteousness is welcome to him.
10:36 God sent his word to the Israelites, bringing them the good news of peace by way of Jesus Christ, and he is Lord of all.
10:37 You know yourselves the things that took place across all Judea. They began in Galilee, after the baptism John preached.
10:38 It is about Jesus from Nazareth: how God anointed him with the Holy Spirit and with power. He went through the land doing good. He healed all those the devil was crushing under his power, because God was with him.
10:39 As for us, we are witnesses of all the things he did, both in the land of the Jews and in Jerusalem. They did away with him, hanging him on a tree,
10:40 but this man God raised on the third day, and he let him be plainly seen,
10:41 not by the whole people but by witnesses God had picked beforehand. We are those witnesses. We shared meals with him, eating and drinking, after he rose from among the dead.
10:42 He gave us orders to preach to the people and to bear solemn witness that God has marked him out to judge the living and the dead.
10:43 Every prophet bears witness to him. Through his name, all who believe in him will have their sins forgiven."
## The Spirit falls on the Gentiles
10:44 Peter was still saying these things when the Holy Spirit fell on everyone who was listening to the word.
10:45 Those of the circumcision who believed, everyone who had come along with Peter, were astounded. The Holy Spirit had been poured out as a gift even on the Gentiles.
10:46 They could hear them speaking in tongues and declaring God's greatness. Then Peter spoke up:
10:47 "Surely no one can hold back the water and stop these people from being baptized? They received the Holy Spirit the same as we ourselves."
10:48 So he gave orders for them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Afterward they asked him to stay on with them for some days.
