## To Theophilus: the second book
1:1 In my first book, Theophilus, I wrote about everything Jesus began to do and to teach,
1:2 until the day he was taken up. Before that, through the Holy Spirit, he had given his orders to the apostles he had chosen.
1:3 To these men he showed himself alive after his suffering, with many sure proofs. For forty days he kept appearing to them and telling them about the kingdom of God.
1:4 As he was sharing a meal with them, he gave them a firm order not to go away from Jerusalem, but to wait for the Father's promise. "You heard it from me," he said.
1:5 "For John baptized in water, but you will be baptized in the Holy Spirit before many days have gone by."
## Taken up into the sky
1:6 The ones who had gathered there kept asking him, "Lord, is this the time you are giving the kingdom back to Israel?"
1:7 He told them, "Knowing the times or the seasons does not belong to you. The Father has put them under his own authority.
1:8 Instead, the Holy Spirit will come upon you, and you will receive power. You will be witnesses for me in Jerusalem, and in all of Judea and Samaria, and as far as the farthest end of the earth."
1:9 With these words, while they looked on, he was lifted up. Then a cloud received him, and their eyes could no longer see him.
1:10 As he was going, they kept their eyes fixed on the sky. And look: two men in white clothing stood there beside them.
1:11 "Galileans," they said, "why are you standing here staring up at the sky? This same Jesus, taken up from you into the sky, will come this same way, just as you watched him go into the sky."
## Waiting and praying
1:12 After that they made their way back to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives, as it is called. It is close to Jerusalem, as far as a person may walk on the Sabbath.
1:13 After they came in, they went up to the room upstairs, where they had been staying: Peter and John, James and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas son of James.
1:14 With one mind, all of these men gave themselves steadily to prayer. Some women were with them too, and Mary, the mother of Jesus, and his brothers.
## Matthias takes Judas' place
1:15 During those days, with about a hundred and twenty people gathered in one place, Peter got up among the brothers and spoke.
1:16 "Brothers," he said, "what the Scripture said had to come true. Through the mouth of David, the Holy Spirit told ahead of time about Judas. He was the one who showed the way to the men who seized Jesus.
1:17 He was counted as one of us, and he got his allotted share in this service."
1:18 (With the pay he got for doing wrong, this man came to own a piece of land. Falling flat on his face, he split open across his middle, and all his insides spilled out.
1:19 This became known to everyone who lived in Jerusalem. So in their own speech that piece of land came to be called Akeldama, meaning Field of Blood.)
1:20 "For it is written in the book of Psalms, 'Let his farmstead be left empty, and let no one live in it,' and, 'Let another man take over his charge.'
1:21 So we need one of the men who have kept company with us the whole time the Lord Jesus lived and moved among us,
1:22 from John's baptizing until the day he was taken up from us. One of these men must join us as a witness to his resurrection."
1:23 They set two men before the group: Joseph, the one called Barsabbas, whose other name was Justus, and Matthias.
1:24 And they prayed, "Lord, you know every heart. Make it plain which one of these two you have picked
1:25 to fill this place of service and of being an apostle, the place Judas walked away from, to go to his own place."
1:26 They handed out lots for them. The lot fell on Matthias, and he was counted in with the eleven apostles.
## The Spirit comes at Pentecost
2:1 As the day of Pentecost came to its fullness, they were all gathered together in the same place.
2:2 All at once there was a noise out of heaven, like a fierce wind being driven along. It filled all the house where they sat.
2:3 Tongues that looked like fire appeared to them, parting so that one settled on each of them.
2:4 They were filled, every one of them, with the Holy Spirit, and they started speaking in other languages as the Spirit gave them words to declare.
2:5 Reverent Jews from every nation in the whole world were living in Jerusalem at that time.
2:6 At this sound a great crowd gathered. They were thrown into confusion, because each one of them heard these men speaking his own native language.
2:7 They were beside themselves with wonder. "Look," they said, "every one of these men talking is from Galilee, isn't he?
2:8 So how is each of us hearing them in the language we were born into?
2:9 We are Parthians, Medes and Elamites. We live in Mesopotamia, in Judea and Cappadocia, in Pontus and Asia,
2:10 in Phrygia and Pamphylia, in Egypt and the parts of Libya near Cyrene. Some of us are visitors from Rome. We are Jews and proselytes.
2:11 Some are Cretans and Arabs. And we hear them speaking in our own languages about the great things of God!"
2:12 Beside themselves and at a loss, they kept saying to each other, "What can this be?"
2:13 But others jeered, "They're full of sweet wine!"
## Peter explains: Joel's promise
2:14 Peter took his stand with the Eleven. Lifting his voice, he spoke out to them: "Jews, and all you who live in Jerusalem! I want you to know this. Open your ears to my words.
2:15 You assume these men are drunk. They are not, for it is only the third hour of the day.
2:16 Instead, this is the very thing spoken through Joel the prophet:
2:17 'This is what will happen in the last days,' God says: 'I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh. Your sons and your daughters will prophesy. Your young men will see visions, and your older men will have dreams.
2:18 Even on my slaves, both men and women, I will pour out my Spirit in those days, and they will prophesy.
2:19 I will give wonders up in the sky and signs down on the earth: blood and fire and a haze of smoke.
2:20 The sun will be changed into darkness and the moon into blood, before the day of the Lord comes, that great and shining day.
2:21 And then anyone at all who calls on the Lord's name will be saved.'
## Peter explains: Jesus raised and exalted
2:22 "Israelites, listen to what I say. Jesus the Nazarene was a man God put on display before you. God proved it by deeds of power, wonders and signs, which he did through Jesus right among you, as you well know.
2:23 This man was given up by the plan God had fixed and by what God knew ahead of time. And you used the hands of men outside the law to nail him up and kill him.
2:24 God raised him, untying the birth pains of death, since death could not possibly keep him in its grip.
2:25 For David says of him, 'I kept seeing the Lord in front of me at all times. He is at my right hand, so that nothing will shake me.
2:26 That is why my heart was happy and my tongue celebrated. Even my flesh will make its home in hope.
2:27 For you will not leave my life behind in Hades, and you will not allow your holy one to see decay.
2:28 You let me know the roads of life. You will fill me with gladness before your face.'
2:29 "Brothers, I can speak freely to you about the patriarch David. He died, he was buried, and his tomb is among us still today.
2:30 He was a prophet, then, and he knew God had made him a promise under oath, that one from the fruit of his body would sit on his throne.
2:31 He looked ahead and spoke about the Messiah rising from the dead. The Messiah was not left behind in Hades, and his flesh never saw decay.
2:32 This Jesus is the one God raised, and every one of us is a witness of it.
2:33 So now he is raised high at God's right hand. The Father promised the Holy Spirit, and Jesus has received that promise from him. He has poured out this, which you are seeing and hearing.
2:34 David did not go up into the heavens. Yet he says, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand,
2:35 until I set your enemies down as a footstool for your feet."'
2:36 "Then the whole house of Israel must know beyond doubt: God has made him both Lord and Messiah, this very Jesus you crucified."
## Three thousand baptized
2:37 These words pierced their hearts. They asked Peter and the rest of the apostles, "What are we to do, brothers?"
2:38 Peter said to them, "Repent, and let each one of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ, for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.
2:39 This promise belongs to you, and to your children, and to all those far away, all whom the Lord our God calls to come to him."
2:40 He testified with many more words and kept urging them, "Be saved from this twisted generation."
2:41 So the people who welcomed his word were baptized. On that day about three thousand persons were added.
## A shared life
2:42 They held steadily to the teaching of the apostles and to the fellowship, to the breaking of the bread and to the prayers.
2:43 Every person was gripped by awe, and many wonders and signs kept happening through the apostles.
2:44 All who believed kept together, and they held everything in common.
2:45 They would sell their land and their goods and hand out the money among them all, to each as he needed.
2:46 Day after day they kept going to the temple with one mind. In one house after another they broke bread, and they shared their meals with joy and with hearts free of pretense.
2:47 They praised God and were well liked by all the people. Each day the Lord kept adding to the gathering those who were being saved.
## A lame man walks
3:1 Peter and John were on their way up to the temple for the hour of prayer, the ninth hour of the day.
3:2 And a man lame from his mother's womb was being carried along. Every day they would set him down at the temple door called Beautiful, so he could beg for alms from the people going into the temple.
3:3 When Peter and John were about to enter, he saw them and asked to be given alms.
3:4 Peter, together with John, fixed his eyes on him. "Look at us," he said.
3:5 The man kept his eyes on them, hoping they would give him something.
3:6 Peter said, "I own no silver and no gold. But I give you what I have: in the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, walk!"
3:7 Gripping him by his right hand, Peter raised him up. In that moment the soles of his feet and his ankles grew firm.
3:8 With a leap he was on his feet and walking around. He went into the temple with them, walking about, leaping, and praising God.
3:9 The whole crowd of people saw him walk about and praise God.
3:10 They knew him: this was the man who used to sit for alms by the Beautiful Gate of the temple. Astonishment filled them, and they were beside themselves over what had happened to him.
## Peter speaks in Solomon's Porch
3:11 He would not let go of Peter and John. So all the people came running together to them, astounded, in what is called Solomon's Porch.
3:12 Peter saw it and answered the people: "Israelites, why does this surprise you? Why are you staring at us, as if we had made him walk through some power or holiness of our own?
3:13 It is the God of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, the God of our ancestors, who has given glory to his servant Jesus. Yet you handed him over. You disowned him in front of Pilate, when Pilate had judged that he should be released.
3:14 The Holy and Righteous One you disowned, and you asked to be granted a man who was a murderer.
3:15 You put to death the Founder of life, the one God raised from among the dead. We are witnesses of it.
3:16 It was on the basis of faith in his name that his name gave strength to this man, whom you see and know. The faith that comes through Jesus has given him this wholeness of body, before the eyes of all of you.
3:17 "Now, brothers, I know that you did not understand what you were doing, and neither did your rulers.
3:18 But in this way God carried out what he had announced long before through the mouth of all the prophets: his Messiah was to suffer.
3:19 So repent and turn around, to have your sins wiped away. Then times of relief may come from the Lord's presence,
3:20 and he may send Jesus, the Messiah he chose ahead of time for you.
3:21 Heaven must take him in until the times of the restoring of all things, which God spoke of through the mouth of his holy prophets from ages past.
3:22 Moses said, 'From among your brothers the Lord God will raise up a prophet for you, a prophet like me. You must listen to him in everything he tells you.
3:23 Every person who will not listen to that prophet will be wiped out from among the people.'
3:24 In fact every prophet who spoke, Samuel and those who followed him one after another, told about these days too.
3:25 You belong to the family of the prophets and of the covenant God made with your ancestors. He said to Abraham, 'In your offspring all the clans of the earth will be blessed.'
3:26 It was for you first that God raised up his servant and sent him out. He sent him to bring you blessing, as each of you turns away from your evil deeds."
## Arrested
4:1 Peter and John were still talking to the people when the priests came at them, with the commander of the temple and the Sadducees.
4:2 They were deeply upset that these two were teaching the people and declaring that in Jesus there is resurrection from the dead.
4:3 They laid hands on them and held them under guard until the morning, since evening had already come.
4:4 Yet many who had heard the word came to believe, and the count of the men reached about five thousand.
## Before the Sanhedrin
4:5 The next morning their rulers, the elders and the scribes, gathered
4:6 in Jerusalem. Annas the high priest was there, and Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and all who came from high-priestly families.
4:7 They stood the two in the middle of them and pressed them with questions: "By what kind of power, or in what kind of name, have people like you done this?"
4:8 Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, answered them: "Rulers of the people, and elders:
4:9 since today we are on trial over a kindness done to a sick man, and you ask by what means this man has been saved,
4:10 then all of you must know, and all the people of Israel too: this man stands in front of you whole through the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene. You crucified him, and God raised him from the dead. It is through him that this man is here.
4:11 He is the stone that you, the builders, treated as nothing, the stone that has become the head of the corner.
4:12 And there is no salvation in anyone else. Nowhere under heaven is there another name given among human beings, a name in which we must be saved."
4:13 They watched Peter and John speak so freely, and they grasped that these men had no schooling and held no office. They were amazed. They also realized these two had been Jesus' companions.
4:14 And with the healed man in plain view, standing there beside them, they could say nothing to oppose them.
4:15 They told the apostles to step outside the Sanhedrin, and then they talked it over among themselves.
4:16 "What are we to do with these people?" they said. "It is plain to everyone who lives in Jerusalem that a well-known sign has happened through them. We have no way to deny it.
4:17 But this must not spread among the people any further. So let's threaten them, so that they never again say a word to anyone on the basis of this name."
4:18 Then they called them back in and gave them strict orders: no speaking at all, and no teaching, in Jesus' name.
4:19 Peter and John answered them, "Is it right before God to heed you rather than God? Decide for yourselves.
4:20 We cannot keep from telling the things we saw and heard."
4:21 They added more threats and then released them. Because of the people, they found no way to punish them, since everyone was giving glory to God over what had taken place.
4:22 For the man this healing sign had happened to was past forty years of age.
## The believers pray
4:23 Once they were set free, they went back to their own group and told them all that the chief priests and the elders had said.
4:24 On hearing it, with one mind they lifted their voice to God: "Master, you are the one who made heaven and earth and the sea and all that is in them.
4:25 Through the Holy Spirit, by the mouth of your servant David, our father, you said, 'Why did the nations rage, and why did the peoples plot empty plans?
4:26 The kings of the earth stationed themselves, and the rulers were gathered into one place, against the Lord and against his Messiah.'
4:27 And it really happened. Here in this city they were gathered together against Jesus, your holy servant, whom you anointed: Herod and Pontius Pilate, along with Gentiles and the peoples of Israel.
4:28 They did all that your hand and your purpose had marked out ahead of time to happen.
4:29 So now, Lord, see how they threaten us. Let your slaves speak your word with complete freedom and courage,
4:30 as you reach out your hand to bring healing, and as signs and wonders happen through the name of Jesus, your holy servant."
4:31 As they finished praying, the place where they had gathered shook. Every one of them was filled with the Holy Spirit, and they went on speaking God's word freely and fearlessly.
## Everything in common
4:32 The great company of believers had one heart and one soul. Not one of them called anything he owned his own. Instead, all they had belonged to them in common.
4:33 The apostles went on bearing witness, with great power, that the Lord Jesus had risen, and rich grace rested on all of them.
4:34 Nobody among them was in need, for whoever owned fields or houses would sell them and bring what the sale brought in.
4:35 They placed it at the feet of the apostles, and it was handed out to each one according to what he needed.
4:36 There was Joseph, a Levite born in Cyprus, who had been given the name Barnabas by the apostles. Translated, the name means "Son of Encouragement."
4:37 He had a field, which he sold. He brought the money and placed it at the feet of the apostles.
