## Slaves, Heirs and Children
4:1 What I am saying is this. As long as the heir is a minor, he is no better off than a slave, though he is master of it all.
4:2 Instead, he is under guardians and household managers until the day his father chose ahead of time.
4:3 So it was with us. While we were minors, we were kept in slavery under the basic elements of the world.
4:4 But when the time had come to its fullness, God sent out his Son. He was born of a woman. He was born under the law.
4:5 His purpose was to buy the freedom of those under the law, so that we would receive adoption as sons.
4:6 And because you are sons, God sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, and the Spirit cries out, "Abba, Father!"
4:7 So you are no slave anymore. You are a son. And since you are a son, you are also an heir, through God.
## Turning Back
4:8 Back then, you did not know God. You were slaves to beings who are not gods by their nature.
4:9 But now you know God. Better said, God knows you. How is it that you are turning around, back to those weak and poor elements? Do you really want to be slaves to them all over again?
4:10 You are keeping close track of days, months, seasons and years.
4:11 I am afraid for you. I may have worn myself out over you for nothing.
## Paul's Plea
4:12 Brothers, I am pleading with you: be like me, for I have become like you. You did me no wrong in any way.
4:13 You know that it was because of a weakness of my body that I first told you the good news.
4:14 The test that my body brought you did not make you look down on me or spit in disgust. You took me in as if I were an angel of God, as if I were Christ Jesus.
4:15 So where is that joy of yours now? I am a witness that you would have dug out your own eyes and given them to me, if you could have.
4:16 Am I your enemy now, just because I told you the truth?
4:17 They are eager for you, but not for any good purpose. They want to lock you out, so that you will be eager for them.
4:18 It is good to be eagerly sought in a good cause at all times, and not only when I am with you.
4:19 My children, I am in labor pains for you all over again, until Christ has taken shape in you.
4:20 How I wish I were with you now, so that I could speak in a different voice! I do not know what to make of you.
## Hagar and Sarah
4:21 Answer me, you who want to be under the law: do you not hear what the law says?
4:22 Scripture tells us that Abraham had two sons. One was the slave woman's son, and the other was the free woman's.
4:23 The slave woman's son was born in the way of the flesh. But the free woman's son was born by way of the promise.
4:24 These things are being said as an allegory. The two women are two covenants. One covenant is from Mount Sinai, and it bears children into slavery. This one is Hagar.
4:25 Now Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia. She lines up with the Jerusalem of today, which is in slavery along with its children.
4:26 The Jerusalem above, though, is free. She is our mother.
4:27 For it is written: "Be glad, barren woman, you who bear no children! Break out and cry aloud, you who have no labor pains! For the children of the desolate woman are many, more than those of the woman who has a husband."
4:28 As for you, brothers, you are children born through the promise, as Isaac was.
4:29 But in those days the child born by the flesh persecuted the child born by the Spirit. It is just the same now.
4:30 But what does Scripture say? "Throw out the slave woman and her son. For the slave woman's son will certainly never share the inheritance with the son of the free woman."
4:31 So, brothers, we are not the slave woman's children, but the free woman's.
## Stand Firm in Freedom
5:1 Christ has set us free, to live in freedom. So hold your ground, and do not get tangled up again in a yoke of slavery.
5:2 Listen, I, Paul, am telling you: if you get circumcised, Christ will not help you at all.
5:3 Once more I give this warning to every man who gets circumcised: he becomes a debtor, bound to do the whole law.
5:4 Those of you who are being justified by the law have been cut off from Christ. You have fallen away from grace.
5:5 We ourselves, through the Spirit and out of faith, are waiting with longing for the righteousness we hope for.
5:6 In Christ Jesus, circumcision has no strength and neither does uncircumcision. Only faith has strength, faith working through love.
5:7 Your run was going well. Who cut in front of you, so that you no longer obey the truth?
5:8 That way of persuading does not come from the One who calls you.
5:9 A little yeast works through the whole batch of dough.
5:10 I have confidence in you, through the Lord, that you will not take a different view. As for the one who is disturbing you, he will carry the judgment, whoever he may be.
5:11 Brothers, as for me, if I am still announcing circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? If that were so, the cross would have stopped being a stumbling block.
5:12 I wish the people who are throwing you into turmoil would go all the way and castrate themselves!
## Serve One Another in Love
5:13 You were called to freedom, brothers. But do not let your freedom become a launching point for the flesh. Through love, be slaves to one another.
5:14 The whole law stands fulfilled in a single saying, this one: "You are to love your neighbor as yourself."
5:15 But if you keep biting and devouring one another, take care: you may be destroyed by each other.
## Walking by the Spirit
5:16 I say it this way: walk by the Spirit, and you will never carry out what the flesh desires.
5:17 The flesh desires what is against the Spirit, and the Spirit desires what is against the flesh. The two are opposed to each other, so that you do not do whatever you wish.
5:18 If the Spirit is leading you, you are not under law.
5:19 The works of the flesh are easy to see. They are sexual immorality, uncleanness, shameless living,
5:20 idol worship, sorcery, hatreds, fighting, jealousy, outbursts of anger, selfish ambition, splits, factions,
5:21 envy, drunkenness, wild parties, and things like these. I tell you these things in advance, just as I told you before: people who practice such things will not inherit God's kingdom.
5:22 But the Spirit's fruit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,
5:23 gentleness, self-control. No law stands against things like these.
5:24 Those who are Christ's have crucified the flesh, with its passions and its desires.
5:25 If we live by the Spirit, let us also fall in line with the Spirit.
5:26 Let us not become puffed up with empty pride, challenging one another and envying one another.
## Carrying One Another
6:1 Brothers, suppose someone is overtaken by some slip. You who live by the Spirit should put that person back on his feet, with a gentle spirit. Look to yourself, in case you are tempted as well.
6:2 Carry each other's heavy loads. If you do, you will fulfill the law of Christ.
6:3 A person who thinks he is somebody, when he is nobody, is deceiving his own mind.
6:4 Let each one test his own work. Then whatever he has to boast about will be his in himself alone, and not in comparison with anyone else.
6:5 For each person will carry his own pack.
6:6 Let the one who is being taught the word be a partner with the one who teaches, in every good thing.
6:7 Do not be led astray. God is not sneered at. Whatever a person sows, that is what he will also reap.
6:8 Whoever sows into his own flesh will harvest decay from the flesh. Whoever sows into the Spirit will harvest eternal life from the Spirit.
6:9 We must not lose heart while doing good. In the proper season we will bring in the harvest, as long as we do not quit.
6:10 So then, whenever we have the chance, let us work for the good of everyone, and above all for those in the household of faith.
## Paul's Own Hand
6:11 Look at the big letters I am using as I write to you in my own hand.
6:12 Everyone who wants to make a fine appearance in the flesh is pressing you to be circumcised. The only reason is to keep from being persecuted because of the cross of Christ.
6:13 The very ones who are having themselves circumcised do not keep the law, either. They just want you circumcised, so that your flesh becomes something for them to boast about.
6:14 As for me, may I never boast in anything but the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which I have been crucified to the world, and the world has been crucified to me.
6:15 Circumcision is nothing, and so is uncircumcision. A new creation is what matters.
6:16 May peace and mercy be on all who will walk by this rule, and on the Israel of God.
6:17 From now on, let nobody hand me more hardship. I am carrying the marks of Jesus in my own body.
6:18 Brothers, may the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen.
