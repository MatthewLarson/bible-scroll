## Greeting
1:1 From Paul, an apostle: not an apostle from people, and not through any human being, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead.
1:2 Also from all the brothers who are with me. To the churches of Galatia:
1:3 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ.
1:4 He gave himself for our sins, so that he could take us out of the evil age that is now with us. That was the will of our God and Father.
1:5 To him belongs the glory for ever and ever. Amen.
## No other good news
1:6 It amazes me that you are switching so soon from the one who called you by the grace of Christ to a different good news.
1:7 It is not a second good news at all. There are only some people who are upsetting you and want to twist the good news of Christ around.
1:8 But if we ourselves, or even an angel from heaven, should tell you good news that is at odds with what we told you, let him be accursed.
1:9 As we have said before, so I say again right now: if anyone tells you good news that is at odds with what you received, let him be accursed.
1:10 Right now, am I trying to win people over, or God? Or am I trying to please people? If I were still pleasing people, I would not be a slave of Christ.
## Where Paul's good news came from
1:11 I want you to know, brothers, that the good news I told you does not follow human standards.
1:12 I did not receive it from any human being, and no one taught it to me. It came through a revelation of Jesus Christ.
1:13 You have heard how I used to live when I was in Judaism: I persecuted the church of God beyond all measure, and I was trying to wreck it.
1:14 I was going further in Judaism than many my own age in my nation, and I was far more zealous for the traditions of my fathers.
1:15 But when it pleased God, who had set me apart from the womb of my mother and called me through his grace,
1:16 to reveal his Son in me, so that I would tell the good news of him among the Gentiles, I did not at once talk it over with flesh and blood.
1:17 I also did not go up to Jerusalem, to the men who had been apostles before me. Instead I left for Arabia, and later I came back to Damascus.
## Paul after his call
1:18 Then, three years later, I went up to Jerusalem to meet Cephas, and I stayed with him for fifteen days.
1:19 But I saw no other apostle, except James, the brother of the Lord.
1:20 As for what I am writing to you, look: before God, I am not lying.
1:21 After that I went to the districts of Syria and Cilicia.
1:22 The churches of Judea that are in Christ did not know me by sight.
1:23 All they had was a report: "The man who once persecuted us is now telling the good news of the faith that he once tried to wreck."
1:24 And they kept giving glory to God because of me.
## The meeting in Jerusalem
2:1 Fourteen years after that, I went up to Jerusalem again. I went with Barnabas, and I took Titus along too.
2:2 I went up in answer to a revelation. I put before them the good news that I proclaim among the Gentiles. I did it in private, before those who were held in high esteem, for fear that I might be running, or had run, for nothing.
2:3 But Titus, who was with me, was a Greek, and not even he was made to get circumcised.
2:4 This was because of the false brothers who were slipped in. They sneaked in to scout out our freedom, which we hold in Christ Jesus, so that they will make us slaves.
2:5 We did not give way to them in submission for even an hour, so that the truth of the good news would continue with you.
2:6 As for those held in esteem as being something, whatever they once were makes no difference to me, since God does not go by a person's face. These men of esteem added nothing to me.
2:7 Instead, they saw that I had been trusted with the good news for the uncircumcised, just as Peter was with the good news for the circumcised.
2:8 For the one who was at work in Peter, making him an apostle to the circumcised, was also at work in me, for the Gentiles.
2:9 When they recognized the grace given to me, James, Cephas and John, who are counted as pillars, gave Barnabas and me their right hands in partnership: we were to go to the Gentiles, and they to the circumcised.
2:10 They asked only that we remember the poor. That same thing I was eager to do.
## Opposing Cephas at Antioch
2:11 Now when Cephas came to Antioch, I resisted him to his face, because he was under condemnation.
2:12 Before some people came from James, he used to eat with the Gentiles. But when they came, he began to draw back and keep himself apart, because he was afraid of those from the circumcision.
2:13 The rest of the Jews joined him in acting a part, so that even Barnabas was carried away by the act.
2:14 But when I saw that they were not walking a straight path toward the truth of the good news, I said to Cephas in front of them all: "If you, who are a Jew, live in the Gentile way and not the Jewish way, how can you compel the Gentiles to live in the Jewish way?"
## Justified by faith
2:15 By nature we are Jews, and not sinners from the Gentiles.
2:16 A person, we know, is not justified by works of the law, except through faith in Jesus Christ. So we too believed in Christ Jesus, that we might be justified by faith in Christ rather than by works of the law. For by works of the law no flesh will be justified.
2:17 Suppose that, in seeking to be justified in Christ, we ourselves also turn out to be sinners. Does that make Christ a servant of sin? By no means!
2:18 If I build again those same things that I once pulled down, then I make myself out to be a lawbreaker.
2:19 For I myself, through the law, died to the law, so that I may live for God.
2:20 I was crucified together with Christ. I am alive, but it is no longer I; Christ is alive in me. As for my life now in the flesh, I live it by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself up for me.
2:21 I do not cancel the grace of God. For if righteousness comes through the law, then Christ died for no reason.
## Foolish Galatians
3:1 You senseless Galatians! Who has put a spell on you? In front of your eyes Jesus Christ was put on public display as the crucified one.
3:2 This is the one thing I want to learn from you: was it by works of the law that you received the Spirit, or by the hearing of faith?
3:3 Are you that senseless? You started out in the Spirit. Do you now finish in the flesh?
3:4 Did you suffer so much for nothing, if it truly was for nothing?
3:5 Then the one who gives you the Spirit and does powerful works among you: is it by works of the law that he does this, or by the hearing of faith?
## The sons of Abraham
3:6 In the same way, Abraham "believed God, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness."
3:7 So recognize that the ones who are from faith, these are the sons of Abraham.
3:8 The Scripture saw ahead that God would justify the Gentiles by faith. So it told the good news ahead of time to Abraham: "All the nations will be blessed in you."
3:9 So the ones who are from faith are blessed along with Abraham, who believed.
3:10 As many as are from works of the law are under a curse. For it is written: "Cursed is everyone who does not stay with all the things written in the book of the law, so as to do them."
3:11 It is clear that no one is justified by the law in God's sight, since "The righteous will live by faith."
3:12 But the law does not come from faith. Instead, "The one who does them will live by them."
3:13 Christ bought us free from the curse of the law, having become a curse for us. For it is written: "Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree."
3:14 He did this so that the blessing of Abraham would reach the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, and so that we would receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.
## The promise comes first
3:15 Brothers, I am speaking in human terms. Even with a human covenant that has been ratified, no one cancels it or adds anything to it.
3:16 Now, to Abraham the promises were spoken, and to his offspring. The text does not say, "and to offsprings," as in the case of many, but as in the case of one, "and to your offspring." That one is Christ.
3:17 This is what I am saying: a covenant was confirmed beforehand by God. The law that came four hundred and thirty years later does not make it invalid, with the result that the promise becomes useless.
3:18 If the inheritance is from the law, it is no longer from a promise. But God graciously gave it to Abraham through a promise.
## What the law was for
3:19 Why, then, the law? It was added on account of transgressions, until the offspring would come, the one to whom the promise was made. It was arranged through angels, by the hand of a mediator.
3:20 Now a mediator is not of one, but God is one.
3:21 Is the law, then, against the promises of God? By no means! For if a law had been given that could make alive, then righteousness would truly have come from the law.
3:22 But the Scripture shut up everything under sin, so that the promise that comes from faith in Jesus Christ would be given to those who believe.
3:23 Before this faith came, we were kept under guard under the law, locked up until the faith that was about to come was revealed.
3:24 So the law has become our attendant until Christ, so that we would be justified by faith.
3:25 Now that faith has come, we are no longer under the attendant.
## One in Christ
3:26 For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus.
3:27 Every one of you who was baptized into Christ has put Christ on.
3:28 There is no Jew and no Greek. There is no slave and no free person. There is no male and female. For all of you are one in Christ Jesus.
3:29 Now if you belong to Christ, you are Abraham's offspring, and heirs in line with the promise.
