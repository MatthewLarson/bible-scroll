## The Lord's teaching
1:0 The teaching of the Lord through the twelve apostles to the Gentiles.
## The way of life
1:1 There are two ways, one of life and one of death, and there is a great difference between the two ways.
1:2 Now the way of life is this. First, you are to love the God who made you. Second, you are to love your neighbor as yourself. And whatever you would not want to happen to you, do not do to another either.
## Love your enemies, give freely
1:3 The teaching of these words is this: Bless the ones who curse you, and pray for your enemies, and fast for the people who persecute you. For what thanks is there if you love the people who love you? Don't even the Gentiles do the same? But as for you, love the people who hate you, and you will have no enemy.
1:4 Keep away from the desires of flesh and body. If someone gives you a slap on your right cheek, turn the other one to him as well, and you will be perfect. If someone drafts you into service for one mile, go with him for two. If someone takes away your outer robe, give him your tunic too. If someone takes from you what is yours, do not ask for it back. You could not, anyway.
1:5 Keep giving to anyone who asks you, and do not ask for anything back. For the Father wants his own gracious gifts to be given to all. Blessed is the one who gives as the commandment says, for he is innocent. Woe to the one who receives! If someone who is in need receives, he will be innocent. But the one who is not in need will have to pay the penalty: why did he take it, and for what? He will be put in custody and questioned about what he has done, and he will not come out of there until he has paid off the last cent.
1:6 But about this it has also been said, "Let your alms sweat in your hands until you know who you are giving to."
## The second commandment
2:1 The teaching's second commandment is this:
2:2 You must not murder. You must not commit adultery. You must not sexually abuse boys. You must not commit sexual sin. You must not steal. You must not practice magic. You must not make magic potions. You must not murder a child by abortion, and you must not kill one that has been born. You must not crave what belongs to your neighbor.
2:3 You must not swear falsely. You must not give false testimony. You must not speak badly of others. You must not keep remembering wrongs done to you.
2:4 You must not be two-minded or two-tongued, for a double tongue is a death trap.
2:5 What you say must not be false or empty. It must be filled with action.
2:6 You must not be greedy, or a swindler, or a hypocrite, or spiteful, or proud. You must not plot evil against your neighbor.
2:7 You must not hate anyone. Some you are to correct, some you are to pray for, and some you are to love more than yourself.
## My child
3:1 My child, run from every evil thing, and from everything like it.
3:2 Do not be hot-tempered, for anger leads the way to murder. Do not be jealous, or fond of fighting, or given to rage, for from all these things murders are born.
3:3 My child, do not be full of desire, for desire leads the way to sexual sin. Do not be foul in speech, or have a roving eye, for from all these things adulteries are born.
3:4 My child, do not read omens from birds, since that leads the way to idol worship. Do not be a chanter of spells, or an astrologer, or one who does magic cleansings, and do not even want to look at these things, for from all these things idol worship is born.
3:5 My child, do not be a liar, since lying leads the way to stealing. Do not be fond of money, or hungry for empty glory, for from all these things thefts are born.
3:6 My child, do not be a complainer, because complaining leads the way to blasphemy. Do not be stubborn, or evil-minded, for from all these things blasphemies are born.
3:7 But be gentle, since the gentle will inherit the earth.
3:8 Be slow to anger, and merciful, and free of evil, and quiet, and good. Tremble at all times at the words you have heard.
3:9 You must not lift yourself up, or let your soul grow brash. Your soul must not stick close to the high and mighty. Instead, you are to live among the righteous and the lowly.
3:10 You are to accept whatever happens to you as good, knowing that without God nothing happens.
## Honor, peace and sharing
4:1 My child, night and day you are to remember the one who speaks God's word to you, and honor him as the Lord. For where the Lord's lordship is spoken of, the Lord is there.
4:2 Every day you are to seek out the faces of the saints, so that you may find rest in their words.
4:3 You must not long for a split. Instead, you are to make peace between people who are fighting. You are to judge fairly. When you correct people for their wrongs, you must not play favorites.
4:4 You must not be double-minded about whether it will happen or not.
4:5 Do not be someone who holds out his hands to take, but pulls them back when it is time to give.
4:6 If you have earned something with your hands, you are to give it as ransom for your sins.
4:7 You must not hesitate to give, and you must not grumble when you give. For you will find out who is the good one who pays back your wages.
4:8 You must not turn away from the person in need. Instead, you are to have everything in common with your brother, and you must not say that anything is your own. For if you are partners in what never dies, how much more in the things that die?
## Children and slaves
4:9 You must not take your hand off your son or your daughter. Instead, from their youth you are to teach them to fear God.
4:10 You must not give orders in bitterness to your slaves, men or women, who hope in the same God, or they may stop fearing the God who is over both of you. For he does not come to call people by their standing, but those whom the Spirit has made ready.
4:11 And you who are slaves are to submit to your masters as to a figure of God, with modesty and fear.
4:12 You are to hate every kind of hypocrisy and everything that does not please the Lord.
4:13 You must never abandon the Lord's commandments. Keep the things you received, without adding or taking away anything.
4:14 In the church you are to confess your wrongs, and you must not come to your prayer with a bad conscience. This is the way of life.
## The way of death
5:1 But the way of death is this. First of all, it is evil and full of cursing: murders, adulteries, lusts, acts of sexual sin, thefts, acts of idol worship, acts of magic, acts of sorcery, robberies, false testimonies, hypocrisies, a double heart, deceit, pride, malice, stubbornness, greed, foul talk, jealousy, brashness, haughtiness, boasting.
5:2 They persecute good people. They hate truth and love lies. They do not know the wages of righteousness. They do not hold tight to what is good, or to just judgment. They lie awake, not for good but for evil. Gentleness and endurance are far from them. They love worthless things and chase after rewards. They show no mercy to the poor, and they do not wear themselves out for the one who is worn down. They do not know the one who made them. They are murderers of children and destroyers of what God has formed. They turn away the person in need and wear down the one who is in trouble. They are advocates for the rich and lawless judges of the poor, sinners through and through. Children, may you be rescued from all these!
## The Lord's yoke
6:1 Watch out that no one leads you away from this way of the teaching, since he teaches you apart from God.
6:2 For if you can carry the whole yoke of the Lord, you will be perfect. But if you cannot, do what you can.
6:3 About food, carry what you can. But be very careful to keep away from food sacrificed to idols, for it is the worship of dead gods.
## Baptism
7:1 About baptism, baptize like this. First say all these things, then baptize into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, in living water.
7:2 If you do not have living water, baptize in other water. If you cannot do it in cold water, do it in warm.
7:3 If you have neither, pour water three times onto the head, into the name of Father and Son and Holy Spirit.
7:4 Before the baptism, the one who baptizes and the one being baptized should fast first, along with any others who can. And you are to order the one being baptized to fast one day or two before it.
## Fasting and prayer
8:1 Your fasts must not be with the hypocrites. For they fast on the second day of the week and the fifth. But you are to fast on the fourth day and on Preparation Day.
8:2 And do not pray like the hypocrites. Instead, pray like this, as the Lord commanded in his good news: "Our Father, who is in heaven, may your name be honored as holy. May your kingdom come. May your will be done, as in heaven, so also on earth. Give us today our bread for the coming day. And forgive us the debt we owe, just as we ourselves forgive the people who owe us. And do not bring us into testing, but rescue us from the evil one. For yours is the power and the glory forever."
8:3 Pray like this three times a day.
## The thanksgiving
9:1 About the thanksgiving, give thanks like this.
9:2 First, about the cup: "We thank you, our Father, for the holy vine of your servant David, which you made known to us through your servant Jesus. To you be the glory forever."
9:3 And about the broken bread: "We thank you, our Father, for the life and knowledge that you made known to us through your servant Jesus. To you be the glory forever.
9:4 As this broken bread had been scattered over the mountains, and was gathered and became one, so let your church be gathered from the ends of the earth into your kingdom. For yours is the glory and the power through Jesus Christ forever."
9:5 No one is to eat or drink from your thanksgiving, but only those baptized into the Lord's name. For the Lord has also said about this, "Do not give what is holy to the dogs."
## After the meal
10:1 After you have eaten your fill, give thanks like this:
10:2 "We thank you, holy Father, for your holy name, which you made to pitch its tent in our hearts, and for the knowledge and faith and immortality that you made known to us through your servant Jesus. To you be the glory forever.
10:3 You, Master, the Almighty, created everything for the sake of your name. You gave people food and drink to enjoy, so that they would thank you. But to us you have given the gift of spiritual food and drink, and eternal life, through your servant.
10:4 Above all, we thank you because you are mighty. To you be the glory forever.
10:5 Lord, remember your church. Rescue it from every evil and make it perfect in your love. Gather it from the four winds, the church you have made holy, into your kingdom that you prepared for it. For yours is the power and the glory forever.
10:6 Let grace come, and let this world pass away. Hosanna to the God of David! Whoever is holy, let him come. Whoever is not, let him repent. Marana tha! Amen."
10:7 But allow the prophets to give thanks as much as they want.
## Teachers and apostles
11:1 Whoever comes, then, and teaches you all the things said above, welcome him.
11:2 But if the teacher himself turns and teaches a different teaching, to do away with this one, do not listen to him. But if he teaches so as to add righteousness and knowledge of the Lord, welcome him as the Lord.
11:3 About the apostles and prophets: act like this, as the decree of the good news says.
11:4 Every apostle who comes to you is to be welcomed as the Lord.
11:5 He is to stay only one day, and the next as well if there is need. If he stays for three, he is a false prophet.
11:6 As the apostle sets out, he is to accept nothing but bread, until he stops for the night. If he asks for money, he is a false prophet.
## Testing the prophets
11:7 And any prophet who is speaking in the Spirit, you must not test or judge. For every sin will be forgiven, but this sin will not be forgiven.
11:8 But not everyone who speaks in the Spirit is a prophet, only the one who has the Lord's ways. So it is by their ways that the false prophet and the prophet will be known.
11:9 Every prophet who orders a table set in the Spirit must not eat from it himself. Otherwise he is a false prophet.
11:10 Any prophet who teaches the truth is a false prophet if he does not do what he teaches.
11:11 But any prophet who has been tested and is true, who acts out an earthly mystery of the church, yet does not teach others to do what he himself does, is not to be judged by you. His judgment is with God, for the prophets of old did the same.
11:12 Whoever says in the Spirit, "Give me silver," or something else, you are not to listen to him. But if he tells you to give for others who are going without, no one is to judge him.
## Travelers
12:1 Everyone who comes in the Lord's name is to be welcomed. Then, after you have tested him, you will know him, for you will have understanding of right and left.
12:2 If the newcomer is a traveler passing through, help him all you can. But he is not to stay with you more than two days, or three if he must.
12:3 If he wants to settle down with you and has a trade, he is to work and eat.
12:4 But if he has no trade, use your good sense to plan ahead, so that no Christian will live idle among you.
12:5 If he does not want to do this, he is a Christ-peddler. Be on your guard against people like that.
## Firstfruits for the prophets
13:1 Every true prophet who wants to settle down with you has earned his food.
13:2 In the same way, a true teacher, like the worker, has also earned his food.
13:3 So take every firstfruit, from what comes from the winepress and the threshing floor, and from the cattle and the sheep. Give the firstfruits to the prophets, for they are your high priests.
13:4 If you have no prophet, give them to the poor.
13:5 If you bake bread, take the firstfruits and give them as the commandment says.
13:6 So too with a jar of wine or oil: when you open it, take the firstfruits and give them to the prophets.
13:7 Of silver, too, and of clothing and of anything you own, take the firstfruits, as much as you think right, and give them as the commandment says.
## The Lord's day
14:1 On the Lord's day of the Lord, when you have gathered, break bread and give thanks. But first confess your wrongs, so that your sacrifice may be pure.
14:2 But anyone who has a quarrel with his companion must not meet with you until they have made peace, so that your sacrifice is not made unclean.
14:3 For this is the sacrifice the Lord spoke of: "In every place and time, present to me a pure sacrifice. For I am a great king, says the Lord, and my name is wonderful among the nations."
## Overseers and deacons
15:1 So choose for yourselves overseers and deacons who are worthy of the Lord: men who are gentle, free from the love of money, truthful and tested. For they too perform for you the service of the prophets and teachers.
15:2 So do not look down on them. For they are the honored ones among you, together with the prophets and teachers.
15:3 Correct each other, not in anger, but in peace, the way you have it in the good news. Anyone who has gone wrong against someone else: let no one talk with him, and let him hear nothing from you, until he repents.
15:4 Your prayers, your alms and all your deeds: do them in the way you have it in our Lord's good news.
## Stay awake
16:1 Stay awake over your life. Do not let your lamps go out, and do not let your belts come loose. Be ready, for you do not know the hour when our Lord is coming.
16:2 Come together often, seeking what your souls need. For all the time you have had faith will do you no good unless you are made perfect in the last season.
## The last days
16:3 For in the last days the false prophets and the corrupters will multiply. The sheep will turn into wolves, and love will turn into hate.
16:4 For as lawlessness grows, people will hate each other and persecute and hand each other over. And then the world-deceiver will appear as a son of God. He will do signs and wonders, and the earth will be handed over into his hands, and he will do wicked things that have never happened, ever.
16:5 Then the human creation will come into the fire of testing. Many will be tripped up and will be lost. But those who hold out in their faith will be saved by the curse itself.
16:6 And then the signs of the truth will appear. First, the sign of something spread out in the sky. Next, the sign of a trumpet's sound. And third, a resurrection of the dead.
16:7 But not of all the dead. Instead, as it has been said, "The Lord will come, and all the saints with him."
16:8 Then the world will see the Lord coming on the clouds of the sky.
