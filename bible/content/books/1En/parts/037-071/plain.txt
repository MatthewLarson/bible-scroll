## The second vision: the vision of wisdom
37:1 This is the second vision that Enoch saw, the vision of wisdom. Enoch was the son of Jared, son of Mahalalel, son of Kenan, son of Enosh, son of Seth, son of Adam.
37:2 This is the beginning of the words of wisdom that I raised my voice to speak to the people who live on the earth: Listen, you people of long ago! And look, you who come later! Hear the words of the Holy One, which I will speak in the presence of the Lord of Spirits.
37:3 It would be best to tell them only to those of old. But we will not hold back the beginning of wisdom from those who come later either.
37:4 Until now the Lord of Spirits has never given wisdom like the wisdom I have received. I received as much as my mind could hold, as the Lord of Spirits wished. He has given me a share in eternal life.
37:5 Three parables came to me, and I raised my voice and told them to the people who live on the earth.
## The first parable: where will sinners live?
38:1 The first parable. / When the community of the righteous appears, / and sinners are judged for their sins / and driven from the face of the earth,
38:2 when the Righteous One appears before the eyes of the righteous, / the chosen ones whose deeds hang on the Lord of Spirits, / and light shines on the righteous and chosen who live on the earth, / where will the sinners live then? / Where will those who denied the Lord of Spirits find rest? / It would have been better for them never to have been born.
38:3 When the secrets of the righteous are uncovered, / the sinners will be judged, / and the wicked will be driven away from the righteous and chosen.
38:4 From then on, those who hold the land will no longer be mighty or high. / They will not be able to look at the faces of the holy ones, / for the Lord of Spirits has made his light shine / on the faces of the holy, the righteous and the chosen.
38:5 At that time the kings and the powerful will be destroyed. / They will be handed over to the righteous and the holy.
38:6 From then on no one will ask the Lord of Spirits to have mercy on them, / for their life will be over.
## Enoch is carried to the end of the heavens
39:1 In those days, chosen and holy children will come down from the high heavens, and their offspring will become one with the children of humans.
39:2 In those days Enoch received books of jealous anger and fury, and books of terror and turmoil. "They will receive no mercy," says the Lord of Spirits.
39:3 At that time a cloud and a whirlwind snatched me up from the face of the earth and set me down at the far end of the heavens.
39:4 There I saw another vision: the homes of the righteous and the resting places of the holy ones.
39:5 My eyes saw their homes among the angels / and their resting places among the holy ones. / They were asking, pleading and praying for the children of humans. / Righteousness flows in front of them like water, / and mercy like dew on the ground. / So it is among them forever and ever.
39:6 In those days my eyes saw the place of the chosen ones of righteousness and faith. / Righteousness will fill their days, / and the righteous and chosen will be past counting in his presence forever and ever.
39:7 I saw their homes under the wings of the Lord of Spirits. / All the righteous and chosen in his presence will blaze like fiery lights. / Their mouths will be full of blessing, / and their lips will praise the name of the Lord of Spirits. / Righteousness will never run out in his presence there.
39:8 I wanted to live there, and my soul longed for that home. My share has been there all along, for this is what was settled for me in the presence of the Lord of Spirits.
39:9 In those days I praised the name of the Lord of Spirits and lifted it high with blessings and praises, because he has made me firm in blessing and glory, as the Lord of Spirits willed.
39:10 For a long time my eyes gazed at that place. I blessed him and said, "Blessed is he, and may he be blessed from the beginning and forever."
39:11 In his presence nothing ever comes to an end. Before the world was created, he knew what the world would be, and what would come in every generation.
39:12 Those who never sleep bless you. They stand in front of your glory, and they bless you, praise you and lift you high, saying, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of Spirits. He fills the earth with spirits."
39:13 There my eyes saw all those who never sleep. They stand in front of him and bless him, saying, "Blessed are you, and blessed is the name of the Lord forever and ever."
39:14 My face was changed, until I could no longer look.
## The four faces around the throne
40:1 After this I saw thousands of thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand. No one could number or count those who stand before the glory of the Lord of Spirits.
40:2 On the four sides of the Lord of Spirits I saw four faces, different from those who stand there. I learned their names, because the angel who went with me told me their names and showed me all the hidden things.
40:3 I heard the voices of those four faces as they praised the Lord of glory.
40:4 The first voice blesses the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.
40:5 I heard the second voice blessing the Chosen One and the chosen ones who hang on the Lord of Spirits.
40:6 I heard the third voice asking and praying for those who live on the earth, and pleading in the name of the Lord of Spirits.
40:7 I heard the fourth voice driving away the satans. It would not let them come into the presence of the Lord of Spirits to accuse the people who live on the earth.
40:8 After this I asked the angel of peace who went with me, who showed me everything that is hidden, "Who are these four faces that I saw, whose voices I heard and wrote down?"
40:9 He said to me, "The first is holy Michael, who is merciful and slow to anger. The second, who is in charge of every disease and every wound of the children of humans, is Raphael. The third, who is in charge of every power, is holy Gabriel. The fourth, who is in charge of repentance and hope for those who will inherit eternal life, is Phanuel."
40:10 These are the four angels of the Lord God Most High, and these are the four voices I heard in those days.
## The secrets of the sky
41:1 I saw all the secrets of the heavens. I saw how the kingdom is divided, and how people's deeds are weighed on the scales.
41:2 I saw the homes of the chosen and the homes of the holy ones. There my eyes saw all the sinners who deny the name of the Lord of Spirits being driven out and dragged away. They could not stand, because of the punishment that goes out from the Lord of Spirits.
41:3 There my eyes saw the secrets of the lightning and the thunder. I saw the secrets of the winds, how they are shared out to blow across the earth, and the secrets of the clouds and the dew. I saw where they come out in that place, and how from there they soak the dust of the earth.
41:4 There I saw locked storehouses, and the winds are divided out of them. I saw the storehouse of the hail, the storehouse of the mist and the clouds. Its cloud has hovered over the earth since the world began.
41:5 I saw the storehouses of the sun and the moon. I saw where they come out and where they go back, and their glorious return. I saw how one is given more honor than the other, and their splendid circuit. They do not leave their circuit. They add nothing to it and take nothing from it. They keep faith with each other, by the oath that binds them.
41:6 The sun goes out first and travels its path at the command of the Lord of Spirits, and his name is mighty forever and ever.
41:7 After this I saw the hidden and the visible path of the moon. It finishes the course of its path in that place by day and by night. They stand opposite each other in the presence of the Lord of Spirits. They give thanks and praise and never rest, for their thanksgiving is their rest.
41:8 For the shining sun makes many turnings, for blessing and for curse. The course of the moon's path is light for the righteous and darkness for sinners, in the name of the Lord. He separated light from darkness. He divided the spirits of human beings, and he made the spirits of the righteous strong, in the name of his righteousness.
41:9 No angel can hold it back, and no power is able to stop it. For he appoints a judge over them all, and he judges them all in his presence.
## Wisdom finds no home
42:1 Wisdom found no place to live, / so a home was made for her in the heavens.
42:2 Wisdom went out to live among the children of humans, / but she found no home. / Wisdom went back to her place / and took her seat among the angels.
42:3 Then wrongdoing came out of her storerooms. / She found people she was not looking for, / and she lived among them, / like rain in the desert / and dew on thirsty ground.
## Stars that stand for the righteous
43:1 I saw other lightnings and the stars of heaven. I saw how he calls each one of them by its name, and they obey him.
43:2 I saw how they are weighed on a fair scale by their light, by the width of their places and the day they appear. I saw their turning, how one lightning sets off another, and their turning that matches the number of the angels. They keep faith with one another.
43:3 I asked the angel who went with me, who showed me what is hidden, "What are these?"
43:4 He said to me, "The Lord of Spirits has shown you what they are a picture of. These are the names of the righteous who live on the earth and believe in the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever."
44:1 I saw other things about the lightning: how some of the stars rise and become lightning, and cannot leave that new form.
## The second parable: the Chosen One on the throne
45:1 This is the second parable, about those who deny the name of the home of the holy ones and of the Lord of Spirits.
45:2 They will not go up to heaven, / and they will not reach the earth. / This will be the lot of the sinners / who deny the name of the Lord of Spirits. / They are being kept like this / for the day of suffering and distress.
45:3 On that day my Chosen One will sit on the throne of glory, / and he will sort out their deeds. / Their resting places will be beyond counting. / Their spirits will grow strong inside them / when they see my Chosen One, / and those who have called on my holy and glorious name.
45:4 On that day I will make my Chosen One live among them. / I will change heaven / and make it a blessing and a light forever.
45:5 I will change the earth and make it a blessing, / and I will make my chosen ones live on it. / But those who sin and go astray will not set foot on it.
45:6 For I have watched over my righteous ones and filled them with peace, / and I have made them live in my presence. / But my judgment on the sinners is near: / I will wipe them off the face of the earth.
## The Head of Days and the Son of Man
46:1 There I saw one who had a head of days, / and his head was white like wool. / With him was another, whose face looked like a human face. / His face was full of grace, like one of the holy angels.
46:2 I asked one of the angels who went with me, who showed me all the hidden things, about that Son of Man: who he was, where he came from, and why he went with the Head of Days.
46:3 He answered me, / "This is the Son of Man who has righteousness. / Righteousness lives with him. / He will uncover all the storehouses of what is hidden, / because the Lord of Spirits has chosen him. / His lot has won out in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, / in uprightness forever.
46:4 This Son of Man whom you have seen / will rouse the kings and the powerful from their couches, / and the strong from their thrones. / He will loosen the reins of the strong / and crush the teeth of sinners.
46:5 He will throw kings off their thrones and out of their kingdoms, / because they do not lift him high or praise him. / They do not humbly admit / where their kingdom came from.
46:6 He will push down the faces of the strong / and fill them with shame. / Darkness will be their home, / and worms will be their bed. / They will have no hope of rising from their beds, / because they do not lift high the name of the Lord of Spirits.
46:7 These are the ones who judge the stars of heaven / and raise their hands against the Most High. / They trample the dry land and live on it. / All their deeds are wrong, and their deeds show it. / Their power rests on their wealth. / Their faith is in the gods they have made with their hands, / and they deny the name of the Lord of Spirits.
46:8 They hound the houses of his congregation, / and the faithful who hang on the name of the Lord of Spirits."
## The blood of the righteous
47:1 In those days the prayer of the righteous went up, / and the blood of the righteous rose from the earth, / into the presence of the Lord of Spirits.
47:2 In those days the holy ones who live in the heavens above / will join together with one voice. / They will plead and pray, praise and give thanks, / and bless the name of the Lord of Spirits, / because of the blood of the righteous that has been poured out. / They will pray that the prayer of the righteous may not be wasted / in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, / that justice may be done for them, / and that they may not have to wait forever.
47:3 In those days I saw the Head of Days as he sat down on the throne of his glory. / The books of the living were opened in front of him. / All his army in the heavens above and his council stood before him.
47:4 The hearts of the holy ones were filled with joy, / because the number of the righteous had been reached, / the prayer of the righteous had been heard, / and the blood of the righteous had been avenged / in the presence of the Lord of Spirits.
## The name named before the sun
48:1 In that place I saw a spring of righteousness that never runs dry. / Many springs of wisdom surrounded it. / All the thirsty drank from them / and were filled with wisdom. / They made their homes with the righteous, the holy and the chosen.
48:2 At that hour that Son of Man was named / in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, / and his name before the Head of Days.
48:3 Before the sun and the signs were created, / before the stars of heaven were made, / his name was named in the presence of the Lord of Spirits.
48:4 He will be a staff for the righteous and the holy, / to lean on so that they do not fall. / He will be the light of the nations, / and the hope of those whose hearts are sick.
48:5 All who live on the dry land will fall down and worship before him. / They will bless him and praise him, / and sing to him, to the name of the Lord of Spirits.
48:6 For this reason he was chosen and hidden in his presence, / before the world was created, and forever.
48:7 The wisdom of the Lord of Spirits has revealed him to the holy and the righteous, / for he has kept the lot of the righteous safe. / They have hated and rejected this world of wrongdoing. / They have hated all its works and its ways, in the name of the Lord of Spirits. / For they are saved in his name, / and it is his will that they live.
48:8 In those days the kings of the earth will hang their heads, / and so will the strong who hold the land, / because of what their hands have done. / For on the day of their distress and trouble / they will not be able to save themselves.
48:9 "I will hand them over to my chosen ones. / Like straw in fire and like lead in water, / so they will burn before the righteous / and sink before the holy, / and no trace of them will be found."
48:10 On the day of their trouble there will be rest on the earth. / They will fall before him and not get up again. / No one will take them by the hand and lift them up, / for they have denied the Lord of Spirits and his Anointed One. / Blessed be the name of the Lord of Spirits!
49:1 For wisdom has been poured out like water, / and glory will never fail in his presence, forever and ever.
49:2 For he is mighty in all the secrets of righteousness. / Wrongdoing will pass away like a shadow, / and it will have no ground to stand on. / For the Chosen One stands in the presence of the Lord of Spirits. / His glory lasts forever and ever, / and his might for all generations.
49:3 In him lives the spirit of wisdom, / the spirit that gives understanding, / the spirit of knowledge and might, / and the spirit of those who have fallen asleep in righteousness.
49:4 He will judge the hidden things. / No one will be able to speak an empty word in his presence. / For he is the Chosen One in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, / as the Lord willed.
## A change for the holy ones
50:1 In those days a change will come for the holy and the chosen. / The light of days will rest on them, / and glory and honor will turn to the holy ones.
50:2 On the day of distress, evil will be stored up against the sinners. / But the righteous will win in the name of the Lord of Spirits. / He will let the others see it, / so that they may repent / and give up the works of their hands.
50:3 They will have no honor in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, / but they will be saved through his name. / The Lord of Spirits will have mercy on them, / for his mercy is great.
50:4 He is righteous in his judgment, / and wrongdoing cannot stand before his glory. / At his judgment, whoever does not repent will be destroyed in his presence.
50:5 "From now on I will have no mercy on them," says the Lord of Spirits.
## The earth gives back its dead
51:1 In those days the earth will give back what was entrusted to it. / Sheol will give back what it has received, / and the place of destruction will repay what it owes.
51:2 He will choose the righteous and the holy from among them, / for the day has come near when they will be saved.
51:3 In those days the Chosen One will sit on his throne. / All the secrets of wisdom will pour out of the counsel of his mouth, / for the Lord of Spirits has given them to him and has glorified him.
51:4 In those days the mountains will leap like rams, / and the hills will skip like lambs that have drunk their fill of milk. / The faces of all the angels in heaven will shine with joy.
51:5 For in those days the Chosen One will rise up. / The earth will rejoice, / the righteous will live on it, / and the chosen will walk about on it.
## The mountains of metal
52:1 After those days I was in the place where I had seen all the visions of what is hidden. A whirlwind had snatched me up and carried me to the west.
52:2 There my eyes saw the secrets of heaven, everything that will happen on the earth: a mountain of iron, a mountain of copper, a mountain of silver, a mountain of gold, a mountain of soft metal and a mountain of lead.
52:3 I asked the angel who went with me, "What are these things I have seen in secret?"
52:4 He said to me, "All these things you have seen will serve the authority of his Anointed One, so that he may give orders and be mighty on the earth."
52:5 That angel of peace answered me, "Wait a little while, and you will see. Everything hidden that the Lord of Spirits has set in place will be revealed to you.
52:6 These mountains that your eyes have seen, / the mountain of iron, the mountain of copper, the mountain of silver, / the mountain of gold, the mountain of soft metal and the mountain of lead, / all of them will be like wax before the fire in front of the Chosen One, / and like water pouring down on those mountains from above. / They will be powerless at his feet.
52:7 In those days no one will be saved, / not by gold and not by silver. / No one will be able to get away.
52:8 There will be no iron for war, / and no one will put on a breastplate. / Bronze will be no use. / Tin will be no use and will count for nothing, / and no one will want lead.
52:9 All these things will be rejected and wiped off the face of the earth / when the Chosen One appears before the face of the Lord of Spirits."
## The valley that is never full
53:1 There my eyes saw a deep valley with its mouth wide open. All who live on the dry land, on the sea and on the islands will bring him gifts, presents and tribute, but that deep valley will never be filled.
53:2 Their hands do lawless deeds. / The sinners devour everyone they lawlessly crush. / But the sinners will be destroyed before the face of the Lord of Spirits. / They will be driven off the face of his earth, / without end, forever and ever.
53:3 For I saw the angels of punishment going about and getting ready all the tools of Satan.
53:4 I asked the angel of peace who went with me, "For whom are they getting these tools ready?"
53:5 He said to me, "They are getting them ready for the kings and the powerful of this earth, so that they may be destroyed by them.
53:6 After this the Righteous and Chosen One will make the house of his congregation appear. From then on they will no longer be held back, in the name of the Lord of Spirits.
53:7 These mountains will be like flat ground in front of him, / and the hills will be like a spring of water. / The righteous will have rest from the cruelty of sinners."
## Chains for the armies of Azazel
54:1 I turned and looked at another part of the earth, and there I saw a deep valley with fire burning in it.
54:2 They brought the kings and the powerful and threw them into that deep valley.
54:3 There my eyes saw them making their tools, chains of iron too heavy to weigh.
54:4 I asked the angel of peace who went with me, "These chains: for whom are they being made ready?"
54:5 He said to me, "These are being made ready for the armies of Azazel. They will take them and throw them into the lowest pit of condemnation. They will cover their jaws with rough stones, as the Lord of Spirits commanded.
54:6 Michael and Gabriel, Raphael and Phanuel will seize them on that great day. On that day they will throw them into the burning furnace, so that the Lord of Spirits may pay them back for their wrongdoing. For they became servants of Satan and led astray the people who live on the dry land.
## The waters of the Flood
54:7 In those days punishment will go out from the Lord of Spirits. All the storehouses of the waters above the heavens will be opened, and so will the springs below the heavens and below the earth.
54:8 All the waters will join with the waters above the heavens. The water above the heavens is male, and the water below the earth is female.
54:9 They will wipe out all who live on the dry land and all who live under the ends of heaven.
54:10 Because of this they will see the wrong they have done on the earth, and because of this they will be destroyed."
55:1 After this the Head of Days changed his mind and said, "I destroyed all who live on the dry land for nothing."
55:2 He swore by his great name, "From now on I will not do this to all who live on the dry land. I will put a sign in the heavens. It will be a pledge of faith between me and them forever, as long as heaven is above the earth.
55:3 After this, it will be by my command. When I choose to seize them by the hand of the angels, on the day of distress and pain, my anger and my punishment will rest on them," says God, the Lord of Spirits.
55:4 "You mighty kings who live on the dry land, you will have to watch my Chosen One sit on the throne of my glory. He will judge Azazel and all his company and all his armies in the name of the Lord of Spirits."
## The Parthians and the Medes
56:1 There I saw armies of the angels of punishment going out. They held nets and chains of iron and bronze.
56:2 I asked the angel of peace who went with me, "To whom are these angels with the nets going?"
56:3 He said to me, "Each to their own chosen and beloved ones, so that they may be thrown into the chasm at the bottom of the valley.
56:4 Then that valley will be filled with their chosen and beloved ones. The days of their life will come to an end, and from then on the days when they led people astray will not be counted.
56:5 In those days the angels will gather and hurl themselves toward the east, on the Parthians and the Medes. / They will stir up the kings, so that a restless spirit comes over them. / It will shake them off their thrones. / They will burst out like lions from their dens, / and like hungry wolves among their flocks.
56:6 They will go up and trample the land of his chosen ones. / The land of his chosen ones will be a threshing floor and a beaten path in front of them.
56:7 But the city of my righteous ones will trip up their horses. / They will start to kill one another, / and their own right hand will grow strong against them. / A man will not know his neighbor or his brother, / and a son will not know his father or his mother, / until the corpses of their slaughter are past counting. / Their punishment will not be for nothing.
56:8 In those days Sheol will open its mouth, / and they will sink down into it. / That will be their ruin: / Sheol will swallow up the sinners in front of the chosen ones."
## The wagons from the east and the west
57:1 After this I saw another army, of wagons with people riding on them. They came on the wind from the east and from the west, as far as the south.
57:2 The noise of their wagons was heard. When this uproar happened, the holy ones in heaven took notice, and the pillars of the earth were shaken from their place. The sound was heard from the ends of the earth to the ends of heaven, all in one day.
57:3 They will all fall down and worship the Lord of Spirits. This is the end of the second parable.
## The third parable: the light of eternal life
58:1 I began to speak the third parable, about the righteous and about the chosen.
58:2 Blessed are you, the righteous and the chosen, / for your lot will be glorious.
58:3 The righteous will live in the light of the sun, / and the chosen in the light of eternal life. / The days of their life will have no end, / and the days of the holy ones will be past counting.
58:4 They will seek the light / and find righteousness with the Lord of Spirits. / Peace to the righteous with the Lord of eternity!
58:5 After this the holy ones will be told / to seek out in heaven the secrets of righteousness, / the share that faith receives. / For it has risen like the sun on the dry land, / and the darkness has passed.
58:6 There will be light that never ends, / and no count of days will close on them. / For darkness will have been destroyed first, / and light will stand firm before the Lord of Spirits. / The light of uprightness will stand firm forever before the Lord of Spirits.
## The secrets of the lightning and the thunder
59:1 In those days my eyes saw the secrets of the lightnings and of the lights, and how they carry out judgment. They flash for blessing or for curse, as the Lord of Spirits wills.
59:2 There I saw the secrets of the thunder: how it crashes high in heaven and its sound is heard. They showed me the dwellings of the dry land, and the sound of the thunder, whether for peace and blessing or for a curse, by the word of the Lord of Spirits.
59:3 After this all the secrets of the lights and the lightnings were shown to me. They flash for blessing and for plenty.
## A vision from Noah's book: heaven shakes
60:1 In the five hundredth year, in the seventh month, on the fourteenth day of the month, in the life of Enoch: in that parable I saw how the heaven of heavens shook with a great shaking. The army of the Most High and the angels, thousands of thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand, were shaken with a great shaking.
60:2 Then I saw the Head of Days sitting on the throne of his glory, and the angels and the righteous were standing around him.
60:3 A great trembling seized me, and fear took hold of me. My hips gave way and went limp, my insides melted, and I fell on my face.
60:4 Holy Michael sent another holy angel, one of the holy angels, and he raised me up. When he raised me up, my spirit came back. For I had not been able to bear the sight of that army, and its uproar, and the shaking of heaven.
60:5 Holy Michael said to me, "Why does a vision like this shake you so? Until today the day of his mercy has lasted. He has been merciful and slow to anger toward the people who live on the dry land.
60:6 But the day is coming, with the power, the punishment and the judgment that the Lord of Spirits has prepared, for those who bow to the righteous judgment, for those who deny the righteous judgment, and for those who take his name in vain. That day has been prepared: for the chosen a covenant, and for sinners an inquiry."
## Leviathan and Behemoth
60:7 On that day two monsters will be separated. One is a female monster named Leviathan. She will live in the depths of the sea, above the springs of the waters.
60:8 The male is named Behemoth. With his chest he holds a desert that no one can see, named Dendayn. It lies east of the garden where the chosen and the righteous live, the place where my grandfather was taken up. He was the seventh from Adam, the first human being the Lord of Spirits made.
60:9 I asked that other angel to show me the power of those monsters: how they were separated on one day and thrown, one into the depths of the sea and one onto the dry land of the desert.
60:10 He said to me, "You, son of man, here you are trying to know what is hidden."
## The spirits of the weather
60:11 The other angel who went with me, who shows me hidden things, told me what is first and what is last: in heaven above, beneath the dry land in the depths, at the ends of heaven and at the foundations of heaven.
60:12 He told me about the storehouses of the winds: how the winds are divided and weighed, and how the springs and the winds are counted, each by the power of its spirit. He told me about the power of the moon's light, a power fit for its task, and the divisions of the stars by their names, and how every division is divided.
60:13 He told me about the thunders and the places where they fall, about every division made among the lightnings so that they flash, and about their army, so that they obey at once.
60:14 For the thunder has resting places, given to it while it waits for its sound. Thunder and lightning are never separated. They are not one, yet the two go together by the spirit and are never apart.
60:15 For when the lightning flashes, the thunder gives out its voice, and the spirit makes it pause at its time and divides between them equally. For the storehouse of their times is like sand. At its time each one is reined in as if by a bridle, and the power of the spirit turns it back and drives it across the many regions of the earth.
60:16 The spirit of the sea is male and strong. By the power of its strength it pulls the sea back with a bridle, and in the same way the sea is driven forward and scattered among all the mountains of the earth.
60:17 The spirit of the frost is its own angel, and the spirit of the hail is a good angel.
60:18 The spirit of the snow has left because of its strength. There is a special spirit in it, and what rises from it is like smoke. Its name is hoarfrost.
60:19 The spirit of the mist does not share their storehouses. It has a storehouse of its own, for its course is glorious, in light and in darkness, in winter and in summer. Its storehouse is bright, and it has its own angel.
60:20 The spirit of the dew lives at the ends of heaven and is joined to the storehouses of the rain. Its course is in winter and in summer. Its clouds and the clouds of the mist are joined, and one gives to the other.
60:21 When the spirit of the rain moves out of its storehouse, the angels come, open the storehouse and lead it out. When it is scattered over the whole dry land, it joins at every season with the water on the dry land.
60:22 For the waters are for the people who live on the dry land. They are food for the dry land from the Most High who is in heaven. That is why the rain has a measure, and the angels are in charge of it.
60:23 I saw all these things as far as the garden of the righteous.
60:24 The angel of peace who was with me said to me, "These two monsters, made ready by the greatness of God, will feed, so that the punishment of God may not be in vain. Children will be killed with their mothers, and sons with their fathers.
60:25 When the punishment of the Lord of Spirits rests on them, it will rest on them so that the punishment of the Lord of Spirits does not come on them for nothing. After that the judgment will take place, with his mercy and his patience."
## Angels with measuring cords
61:1 In those days I saw that long cords were given to those angels. They took wings for themselves and flew off toward the north.
61:2 I asked the angel, "Why have they taken these long cords and gone?" He said to me, "They have gone to measure."
61:3 The angel who went with me said to me, "These angels are bringing the measures of the righteous and the ropes of the righteous, so that they may lean on the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.
61:4 The chosen will begin to live with the chosen. These are the measures that will be given to faith, and they will make the word of righteousness strong.
61:5 These measures will uncover all the secrets of the depths of the earth: those who were destroyed by the desert, and those who were eaten by the fish of the sea and by the wild animals. They will come back and lean on the day of the Chosen One. For no one will be destroyed in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, and no one can be destroyed."
61:6 Those in the heavens above were all given a command and power, one voice and one light, like fire.
61:7 With their first words they blessed that one. They lifted him high and praised him with wisdom, and they were wise in speech and in the spirit of life.
## The Chosen One judges the angels
61:8 The Lord of Spirits seated the Chosen One on the throne of his glory. He will judge all the works of the holy ones in heaven above, and their deeds will be weighed on the scales.
61:9 When he lifts up his face to judge their hidden ways by the word of the name of the Lord of Spirits, and their path by the way of the righteous judgment of God Most High, they will all speak with one voice. They will bless and praise, lift high and declare holy the name of the Lord of Spirits.
61:10 He will call all the army of the heavens, and all the holy ones above, and the army of God: the cherubim, the seraphim and the ophanim, all the angels of power and all the angels of the dominions, and the Chosen One, and the other power that is on the dry land and over the water.
61:11 On that day they will raise one voice. They will bless, praise, honor as holy and lift high, in the spirit of faith, in the spirit of wisdom and patience, in the spirit of mercy, in the spirit of judgment and peace, and in the spirit of goodness. They will all say with one voice, "Blessed is he, and blessed be the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever."
61:12 All who never sleep in heaven above will bless him. All his holy ones in heaven will bless him, and all the chosen who live in the garden of life, and every spirit of light that is able to bless, praise, lift high and honor your holy name. All flesh will praise and bless your name beyond its strength, forever and ever.
61:13 For the mercy of the Lord of Spirits is great, and he is slow to anger. All his works and all his power, all that he has made, he has revealed to the righteous and the chosen, in the name of the Lord of Spirits.
## The kings before the throne
62:1 This is what the Lord commanded the kings, the mighty, the high ones and those who live on the earth. He said, "Open your eyes and lift up your horns, if you are able to recognize the Chosen One."
62:2 The Lord of Spirits sat on the throne of his glory. / The spirit of righteousness was poured out on him. / The word of his mouth kills all the sinners, / and all the wrongdoers are destroyed before his face.
62:3 On that day all the kings, the mighty, the high ones and those who hold the dry land will stand up. / They will see and recognize him / as he sits on the throne of his glory. / The righteous are judged in righteousness before him, / and no empty word is spoken in his presence.
62:4 Pain will come on them like the pain of a woman in labor, / who has a hard time giving birth / when her child enters the mouth of the womb / and she struggles to bring it out.
62:5 They will look at one another, one group at the other. / They will be terrified and hang their heads. / Pain will seize them / when they see that Son of a Woman / sitting on the throne of his glory.
62:6 The kings, the mighty and all who hold the earth / will praise, bless and lift high the one who rules over everything, / the one who was hidden.
62:7 For from the beginning the Son of Man was hidden. / The Most High kept him in the presence of his power / and revealed him to the chosen.
62:8 The community of the holy and the chosen will be sown, / and all the chosen will stand before him on that day.
62:9 All the kings, the mighty, the high ones and those who rule the dry land / will fall on their faces before him. / They will worship and put their hope in that Son of Man. / They will plead with him and beg him for mercy.
62:10 But that Lord of Spirits will press them / so that they hurry out from his presence. / Their faces will be filled with shame, / and darkness will grow deeper on their faces.
62:11 The angels of punishment will take them, / to pay them back for mistreating his children and his chosen ones.
62:12 They will be a sight for the righteous and for his chosen ones. / They will rejoice over them, / because the anger of the Lord of Spirits rests on them, / and the sword of the Lord of Spirits is drunk on them.
62:13 The righteous and the chosen will be saved on that day. / From then on they will never see the faces of the sinners and the wrongdoers.
62:14 The Lord of Spirits will live over them. / They will live with that Son of Man, / and eat, and lie down and get up, forever and ever.
62:15 The righteous and the chosen will have risen from the earth. / They will no longer hang their heads, / and they will have put on the clothing of life.
62:16 This will be the clothing of life from the Lord of Spirits. / Your clothes will not wear out, / and your glory will not fade in the presence of the Lord of Spirits.
## Too late to repent
63:1 In those days the mighty kings who hold the dry land will beg the angels of his punishment, to whom they have been handed over, to give them a little rest. They will want to fall down and worship before the Lord of Spirits and confess their sins in his presence.
63:2 They will bless and praise the Lord of Spirits and say, / "Blessed is the Lord of Spirits, the Lord of kings, / the Lord of the mighty and the Lord of the rich, / the Lord of glory and the Lord of wisdom. / Every hidden thing is bright before you.
63:3 Your power lasts for all generations, / and your glory forever and ever. / All your secrets are deep and past counting, / and your righteousness is beyond reckoning.
63:4 Now we have learned that we should praise and bless the Lord of kings, / the one who is king over all kings."
63:5 They will say, / "If only someone would give us rest, / so that we could praise him, thank him and bless him, / and confess our faith before his glory!
63:6 Now we long for a little rest, but we do not find it. / We are driven away, and we cannot take hold of it. / Light has passed away from in front of us, / and darkness is our home forever and ever.
63:7 For we did not believe in his presence. / We did not praise the name of the Lord of kings. / We did not praise the Lord for all his works. / Our hope was in the scepter of our kingdom and in our glory.
63:8 On the day of our suffering and distress he does not save us. / We find no rest to confess that our Lord is faithful in all his works, / in all his judgments and his justice. / His judgments show no favoritism.
63:9 We pass away from his presence because of our deeds, / and all our sins have been counted in righteousness."
63:10 Then they will say to themselves, / "Our souls are full of the wealth of wrongdoing, / but it does not keep us from going down out of it / into the crushing weight of Sheol."
63:11 After this their faces will be filled with darkness and shame before that Son of Man. They will be driven from his presence, and the sword will stay before his face among them.
63:12 This is what the Lord of Spirits says: "This is the law and the judgment for the mighty, the kings, the high ones and those who hold the dry land, in the presence of the Lord of Spirits."
## The angels who taught what was hidden
64:1 I saw other forms hidden in that place.
64:2 I heard the voice of the angel saying, "These are the angels who came down from heaven to the earth. They revealed what was hidden to the children of humans, and they led the children of humans astray to commit sin."
## Noah cries out to Enoch
65:1 In those days Noah saw that the earth had tilted and that its destruction was near.
65:2 He set out from there and went to the ends of the earth. He cried out to his grandfather Enoch. Noah said in a bitter voice, "Hear me, hear me, hear me," three times.
65:3 He said to him, "Tell me what is being done on the earth. Why is the earth so afflicted and shaken? Maybe I will be destroyed with it."
65:4 Right after that there was a great uproar on the earth. A voice was heard from heaven, and I fell on my face.
65:5 My grandfather Enoch came and stood beside me. He said to me, "Why have you cried out to me with such bitter crying and weeping?
65:6 A command has gone out from the presence of the Lord against those who live on the dry land: this will be their end. For they have learned all the secrets of the angels, all the violence of the satans and all their hidden power. They have learned all the power of those who practice sorcery, the power of spells, and the power of those who cast metal idols for the whole earth.
65:7 They have learned how silver is produced from the dust of the earth, and how soft metal comes to be on the earth.
65:8 For lead and tin are not produced from the earth like the first. A spring produces them, and an angel stands in it, and that angel is the highest."
65:9 After this my grandfather Enoch took me by the hand and raised me up. He said to me, "Go, for I have asked the Lord of Spirits about this uproar on the earth.
65:10 He said to me, 'Because of their wrongdoing their judgment is settled, and I will not hold it back. Because of the months they searched out and learned, the earth will be destroyed, and those who live on it.'
65:11 For these there is no way back, ever, because they showed people what was hidden, and they are condemned. But not you, my son. The Lord of Spirits knows that you are pure and good, free of this blame about the secrets.
65:12 He has made your name firm among the holy ones. He will keep you safe from those who live on the dry land. He has made your offspring firm in righteousness, for kings and for great glory. From your offspring will flow a spring of the righteous and the holy, past counting, forever."
## The angels over the waters
66:1 After this he showed me the angels of punishment. They are ready to come and set loose all the power of the water beneath the earth, to bring judgment and destruction on all who live and stay on the dry land.
66:2 The Lord of Spirits commanded the angels who were going out not to raise their hands, but to keep watch. For those angels were in charge of the power of the waters.
66:3 Then I left the presence of Enoch.
## The word of God to Noah
67:1 In those days the word of God came to me. He said to me, "Noah, your lot has come up before me, a lot without blame, a lot of love and uprightness.
67:2 Now the angels are making something of wood. When they come out from that task, I will put my hand on it and keep it safe. From it will come the seed of life, and a change will come, so that the dry land will not be left empty.
67:3 I will make your offspring firm in my presence forever and ever. I will scatter those who live with you over the face of the dry land. It will be blessed and will multiply on the dry land, in the name of the Lord."
## The valley of fire and the healing waters
67:4 They will lock up those angels who showed wrongdoing in that burning valley. My grandfather Enoch had shown it to me before, in the west, near the mountains of gold, silver, iron, soft metal and tin.
67:5 I saw that valley. There was a great upheaval in it, and the waters were churning.
67:6 When all this happened, a smell of sulfur came from that fiery molten metal and from the upheaval that churned them in that place. It mixed with those waters. That valley of the angels who led people astray burns beneath that land.
67:7 Rivers of fire come out through its valleys. There the angels who led astray the people who live on the dry land are punished.
67:8 In those days those waters will serve the kings, the mighty, the high ones and those who live on the dry land, for healing soul and body, but for punishing the spirit. Their spirit is full of lust, so that they will be punished in their bodies. For they denied the Lord of Spirits. They see their punishment every day, and yet they do not believe in his name.
67:9 As the burning of their bodies grows greater, so a change will come over their spirit, forever and ever. For no one may speak an empty word in the presence of the Lord of Spirits.
67:10 For judgment will come on them, because they trust in the lust of their body and deny the Spirit of the Lord.
67:11 Those same waters will change in those days. For when those angels are punished in those days, the heat of those springs of water will change. When the angels come up, the water of the springs will change and turn cold.
67:12 I heard holy Michael answering and saying, "This judgment by which the angels are judged is a witness against the kings, the mighty and those who hold the dry land.
67:13 For these waters of judgment are for the healing of the angels and for the death of their bodies. They will not see and will not believe that those waters will change and become a fire that burns forever."
## Michael and Raphael on the judgment of the angels
68:1 After this my grandfather Enoch gave me the teaching of all the secrets in the book, and the parables that had been given to him. He put them together for me in the words of the book of the parables.
68:2 On that day holy Michael answered Raphael and said, "The power of the spirit seizes me and makes me tremble, because the judgment of the secrets, the judgment of the angels, is so harsh. Who can bear the harsh judgment that has been carried out, which stays in force and makes them melt away?"
68:3 Holy Michael answered again and said to Raphael, "Whose heart would not soften over it? Whose insides would not be shaken by this word of judgment that has gone out against them, because of those who led them out like this?"
68:4 When he stood before the Lord of Spirits, holy Michael said this to Raphael: "I will not take their side under the eye of the Lord. For the Lord of Spirits is angry with them, because they act as if they were the Lord.
68:5 Because of this the hidden judgment will come on them forever and ever. For no angel and no human being will receive a share of it. They alone have received their judgment, forever and ever."
## The names of the fallen angels
69:1 After this, the judgment will terrify them and make them tremble, because they showed this to the people who live on the dry land.
69:2 Here are the names of those angels. These are their names: the first of them is Shemihazah, the second Artaqifa, the third Armen, the fourth Kokabel, the fifth Turael, the sixth Rumyal, the seventh Danel, the eighth Neqael, the ninth Baraqel, the tenth Azazel, the eleventh Armaros, the twelfth Batarel, the thirteenth Basasael, the fourteenth Ananel, the fifteenth Turyal, the sixteenth Simapesiel, the seventeenth Yetarel, the eighteenth Tumael, the nineteenth Tarel, the twentieth Rumael and the twenty-first Izezeel.
69:3 These are the chiefs of their angels, and the names of their leaders over hundreds, over fifties and over tens.
69:4 The name of the first is Yeqon. He is the one who led all the children of the holy angels astray. He brought them down to the dry land and led them astray through the daughters of humans.
69:5 The second is named Asbeel. He gave the children of the holy angels evil advice and led them astray, so that they ruined their bodies with the daughters of humans.
69:6 The third is named Gadreel. He is the one who showed the children of humans every deadly blow. He led Eve astray. He showed the children of humans the weapons of death: the shield, the breastplate and the sword for killing, and every weapon of death.
69:7 From his hand they have gone out against the people who live on the dry land, from that time and forever.
69:8 The fourth is named Penemue. He showed the children of humans the bitter and the sweet, and he showed them all the secrets of their wisdom.
69:9 He taught people to write with ink and papyrus. Because of this many have gone astray, from age to age, until this day.
69:10 For people were not born for this, to make their faith firm with pen and ink like this.
69:11 For people were created no differently from the angels, to stay righteous and pure. Death, which destroys everything, would not have touched them. But they are being destroyed through this knowledge of theirs, and through this power it is devouring me.
69:12 The fifth is named Kasdeya. He showed the children of humans all the evil blows of spirits and demons: the blows that strike the unborn child in the womb so that it dies, the blows to the soul, the bite of the snake, and the blows that strike in the heat of noon, from the offspring of the snake whose name is Tabaet.
## The hidden oath that holds the world
69:13 This is the number of Kasbeel, the chief of the oath. He showed it to the holy ones when he lived high above in glory, and its name is Beqa.
69:14 This angel asked holy Michael to show them the hidden name, so that they might see that hidden name and speak it in the oath. Then those who showed the children of humans everything that was hidden would tremble before that name and that oath.
69:15 This is the power of this oath, for it is powerful and strong. He placed this oath, Akae, in the hand of holy Michael.
69:16 These are the secrets of this oath, and they are strong through his oath. / Heaven was hung in place before the world was created, / and it stays forever.
69:17 Through it the earth was founded on the water. / Out of the hidden places of the mountains come beautiful waters for the living, / from the creation of the world and forever.
69:18 Through that oath the sea was created. / As its foundation, for the time of its rage, he set the sand for it, / and it does not cross over, from the creation of the world and forever.
69:19 Through that oath the depths were made firm. / They stand and do not move from their place, from age to age.
69:20 Through that oath the sun and the moon finish their course. / They do not stray from their command, from age to age.
69:21 Through that oath the stars finish their course. / He calls their names and they answer him, from age to age.
69:22 So it is for the waters and their spirits, / for the winds and all the spirits, / and for their paths from all the quarters of the winds.
69:23 There the storehouses of the voice of the thunder and the light of the lightning are kept. / There the storehouses of the hail and the frost are kept, / and the storehouses of the mist, the rain and the dew.
69:24 All of them believe and give thanks before the Lord of Spirits. / They praise him with all their power, / and their food is in all their thanksgiving. / They give thanks, praise and lift high the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.
69:25 This oath is strong over them. / They are kept by it, their paths are kept, / and their courses are not destroyed.
## The name of the Son of Man revealed
69:26 They had great joy. / They blessed, praised and lifted high, / because the name of that Son of Man had been revealed to them.
69:27 He sat on the throne of his glory, / and the whole of judgment was given to him, the Son of Man. / He makes the sinners pass away and destroys them from the face of the earth, / with those who led the world astray.
69:28 They will be bound with chains / and locked up in their gathering place of destruction. / All their works will pass away from the face of the earth.
69:29 From now on there will be nothing that corrupts, / for that Son of Man has appeared / and has sat down on the throne of his glory. / All evil will pass away and go from before his face, / and the word of that Son of Man will be strong before the Lord of Spirits. / This is the third parable of Enoch.
## Enoch is taken up
70:1 After this, while he was still alive, his name was lifted up into the presence of that Son of Man and into the presence of the Lord of Spirits, away from those who live on the dry land.
70:2 He was lifted up on the chariots of the spirit, and the name was gone from among them.
70:3 From that day I was no longer counted among them. He set me between the two winds, between the north and the west, where the angels took the cords to measure for me the place for the chosen and the righteous.
70:4 There I saw the first fathers and the righteous, who have lived in that place from the beginning.
## Enoch in the heaven of heavens
71:1 After this my spirit was carried away and went up into the heavens. I saw the sons of the angels walking on flames of fire. Their clothes and their robes were white, and the light of their faces was like snow.
71:2 I saw two rivers of fire. The light of that fire shone like jacinth, and I fell on my face before the Lord of Spirits.
71:3 The angel Michael, one of the chief angels, took me by my right hand and raised me up. He led me out to all the secrets of mercy and the secrets of righteousness.
71:4 He showed me all the secrets of the ends of heaven, all the storehouses of the stars, and all the lights, from where they go out before the face of the holy ones.
71:5 Then the spirit carried Enoch off into the heaven of heavens. There, in the middle of that light, I saw something like a house built of crystal stones, and between those stones were tongues of living fire.
71:6 My spirit saw the ring of fire that circled that house. On its four sides were rivers full of living fire, and they circled that house.
71:7 Around it were seraphim, cherubim and ophanim. These are the ones who never sleep, who guard the throne of his glory.
71:8 I saw angels who could not be counted, thousands of thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand, circling that house. Michael, Raphael, Gabriel and Phanuel, and the holy angels above the heavens, go in and out of that house.
71:9 Out of that house came Michael and Gabriel, Raphael and Phanuel, and many holy angels past counting.
71:10 With them was the Head of Days. His head was white and pure like wool, and his clothing was beyond describing.
71:11 I fell on my face. My whole body went limp, and my spirit was changed. I cried out with a loud voice, with the spirit of power, and I blessed, praised and lifted him high.
71:12 These blessings that came out of my mouth were pleasing before that Head of Days.
71:13 That Head of Days came with Michael and Gabriel, Raphael and Phanuel, and thousands and ten thousands of angels past counting.
## "You are the Son of Man"
71:14 That angel came to me and greeted me with his voice. He said to me, "You are the Son of Man who was born for righteousness. Righteousness has made its home on you, and the righteousness of the Head of Days will not leave you."
71:15 He said to me, "He proclaims peace to you in the name of the world to come. For peace has gone out from there since the creation of the world, and so it will be for you forever and ever.
71:16 All who will come and walk on your path, since righteousness will never leave you, will have their homes with you and their lot with you. They will not be separated from you, forever and ever.
71:17 So there will be long life with that Son of Man. The righteous will have peace, and the righteous will have an upright way, in the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever."
