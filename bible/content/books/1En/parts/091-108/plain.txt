## Enoch calls his family together
91:1 "Now, my son Methuselah, call all your brothers to me, / and gather all your mother's sons to me. / For a voice is calling me, / and a spirit has been poured out on me, / so that I can show you everything / that will happen to you, forever."
91:2 So Methuselah went and called all his brothers to him, and he gathered his relatives.
91:3 Enoch spoke to all his children about righteousness. He said, "Listen, my children, to every word of your father, / and pay close attention to the voice of my mouth. / I am warning you solemnly, my dear ones: / love uprightness and walk in it.
91:4 Do not come near uprightness with a double heart, / and do not keep company with people whose hearts are double. / Walk in righteousness, my children. / It will lead you on good paths, / and righteousness will go with you as your companion.
91:5 For I know that violence will grow strong on the earth, / and a great punishment will be carried out on the earth. / Then all injustice will come to an end. / It will be cut off at its roots, / and the whole building it has put up will pass away.
91:6 Then injustice will happen all over again. / Every deed of injustice, of violence and of wrongdoing / will reach its full measure on the earth a second time.
91:7 When injustice, sin, blasphemy and violence grow in every deed, / and when perversion, wrongdoing and uncleanness grow, / a great punishment will come from heaven on all of them. / The holy Lord will come out in anger and punishment / to carry out judgment on the earth.
91:8 In those days violence will be cut off at its roots, / and so will the roots of injustice, together with deceit. / They will be destroyed from under heaven.
91:9 Everything that belongs to the nations will be handed over, / and their towers will burn with fire. / They will be removed from the whole earth. / They will be thrown into the judgment of fire, / and they will perish in anger, in a mighty judgment that lasts forever.
91:10 The righteous will rise from their sleep, / and wisdom will rise and be given to them.
## The last weeks of the world
91:11 After that the roots of injustice will be cut off, / and sinners will be destroyed by the sword. / The blasphemers will be cut off in every place. / Those who plot violence and those who commit blasphemy / will perish by the knife.
91:12 After that there will be another week, the eighth, the week of righteousness. / A sword will be given to it, / so that justice and righteousness can be carried out on those who oppress, / and sinners will be handed over into the hands of the righteous.
91:13 At its end they will gain houses because they are righteous, / and a house will be built for the Great King, / in glory forever.
91:14 After that, in the ninth week, / a righteous judgment will be revealed to the whole world. / All the works of the wicked will vanish from the whole earth. / The world will be written down for destruction, / and all people will look to the path of uprightness.
91:15 After that, in the tenth week, in its seventh part, / the eternal judgment will come. / It will be carried out on the Watchers, / and the great eternal heaven will spring up from among the angels.
91:16 The first heaven will depart and pass away, / and a new heaven will appear. / All the powers of the heavens will shine / seven times as bright, forever.
91:17 After that there will be many weeks without number, forever. / They will be lived in goodness and righteousness, / and from then on sin will never be mentioned again, forever.
## Two paths
91:18 Now I am telling you, my children, / and I am showing you the paths of righteousness and the paths of violence. / I will show them to you again, / so that you will know what is coming.
91:19 So listen, my children. / Walk in the paths of righteousness, / and do not walk in the paths of violence. / For all who walk in the paths of injustice / will perish forever."
## Enoch's letter
92:1 Written by Enoch the scribe, praised by all people and judge of the whole earth: this complete teaching of wisdom. It is for all my children who live on the earth, and for the generations to come who will practice uprightness and peace.
92:2 Do not let your spirits grieve because of the times, / for the Holy Great One has set days for everything.
92:3 The righteous one will rise from sleep. / He will rise and walk in the path of righteousness, / and his whole path and all his comings and goings / will be in goodness and grace forever.
92:4 He will be gracious to the righteous one / and give him uprightness forever. / He will give him authority, / and the righteous one will live in goodness and righteousness / and walk in light that lasts forever.
92:5 Sin will perish in darkness forever, / and from that day on it will never be seen again.
## Ten weeks of history
93:1 After this Enoch began to speak from the books.
93:2 Enoch said, "I will tell you about the children of righteousness, / about the chosen ones of the world, / and about the plant of righteousness and uprightness. / I, Enoch, will make these things known to you, my children, / as they were shown to me in the vision of heaven. / I learned them from the word of the holy angels, / and I came to understand them from the heavenly tablets."
93:3 Then Enoch went on speaking from the books. He said, "I was born the seventh, in the first week, / while justice and righteousness still held firm.
93:4 After me, in the second week, great evil will arise, / and deceit will spring up. / In that week the first end will come, / and in it a man will be saved. / After it is over, injustice will grow, / and he will make a law for the sinners.
93:5 After that, in the third week, at its end, / a man will be chosen as the plant of righteous judgment. / After him the plant of righteousness will come, forever.
93:6 After that, in the fourth week, at its end, / visions of the holy and righteous will be seen. / A law for every generation will be made for them, / and an enclosure.
93:7 After that, in the fifth week, at its end, / the house of glory and of the kingdom / will be built to stand forever.
93:8 After that, in the sixth week, / all who live in it will be blind, / and the hearts of all of them will wickedly turn away from wisdom. / In that week a man will go up. / At its end the house of the kingdom will be burned with fire, / and the whole family of the chosen root will be scattered.
93:9 After that, in the seventh week, / a perverse generation will arise. / Its deeds will be many, / and all its deeds will be perverse.
93:10 At its end, chosen righteous ones will be chosen / from the eternal plant of righteousness. / They will be given teaching seven times over, / about all his creation."
## Who can know these things?
93:11 Is there any human being who can hear the voice of the Holy One and not be shaken? Who can think his thoughts? Who can look at all the works of heaven?
93:12 Who is able to understand the works of heaven, or see a soul or a spirit and tell about it? Who can go up and see all their ends, and think them through, or do what they do?
93:13 Is there anyone who can know how wide and how long the earth is? To whom have the measurements of all these things been shown?
93:14 Or is there anyone who can know the length of the sky and how high it is, what it rests on, how many stars there are, and where all the lights go to rest?
## Choose the path of righteousness
94:1 Now I say to you, my children: love righteousness and walk in it. / For the paths of righteousness deserve to be welcomed, / but the paths of injustice will suddenly be destroyed and vanish.
94:2 To certain people of a generation to come / the paths of violence and of death will be revealed. / They will keep far away from them / and will not follow them.
94:3 And now I say to you who are righteous: / do not walk in the path of evil and violence, / or in the paths of death. / Do not go near them, or you will be destroyed.
94:4 Instead, look for righteousness / and choose it for yourselves, and a life worth choosing. / Walk in the paths of peace, / so that you will live and do well.
94:5 Hold on to my words in the thoughts of your hearts, / and do not let my word be wiped out of your hearts. / For I know that sinners will tempt people / to do evil to wisdom. / No place will be found for her, / and no kind of temptation will grow less.
## Woe to those who build with sin
94:6 Woe to those who build injustice and violence, / and who lay deceit as a foundation! / They will be thrown down suddenly, / and they will have no peace.
94:7 Woe to those who build their houses with sin! / They will be torn down from every foundation, / and they will fall by the sword. / Those who pile up gold and silver / will suddenly perish in the judgment.
94:8 Woe to you rich! You have trusted in your wealth, / and you will leave your wealth behind, / because you did not remember the Most High / while you were wealthy.
94:9 You have committed blasphemy and injustice. / You have made yourselves ready for the day when blood is poured out, / for the day of darkness, and for the day of the great judgment.
94:10 This is what I say, and I am telling you plainly: / the one who created you will overthrow you. / No one will pity you when you fall, / and your Creator will rejoice when you are destroyed.
94:11 In those days your righteous ones / will be a reproach to the sinners and the wicked.
## Enoch weeps
95:1 If only my eyes were a cloud full of water! / Then I would weep over you / and pour out my tears like water from a cloud, / and find rest from the grief in my heart.
95:2 Who gave you permission to insult others and do evil? / Judgment will catch up with you, sinners.
95:3 Righteous ones, do not be afraid of the sinners! / The Lord will once again hand them over to you, / so that you can carry out judgment on them as you wish.
95:4 Woe to you who lay curses that cannot be lifted! / Healing will be far from you because of your sins.
95:5 Woe to you who pay back your neighbor with evil! / You will be paid back for what you have done.
95:6 Woe to you, lying witnesses, / and to those who weigh out injustice! / You will suddenly perish.
95:7 Woe to you, sinners, for you hunt down the righteous! / You yourselves will be handed over and hunted down, / you people of injustice, / and their yoke will lie heavy on you.
## Hope, you righteous
96:1 Have hope, you righteous! / The sinners will suddenly perish in front of you, / and you will rule over them as you wish.
96:2 On the day of the sinners' trouble, / your young will rise and soar like eagles. / Your nest will be higher than the vultures' nests. / You will climb up and go into the cracks of the earth / and the clefts of the rock, forever, / like rock badgers hiding from the unjust. / And the unjust will groan over you and wail like sirens.
96:3 But you who have suffered, do not be afraid. / You will be healed. / A bright light will shine on you, / and you will hear a voice of rest from heaven.
96:4 Woe to you, sinners! Your wealth makes you look like the righteous, / but your hearts expose you as sinners. / This fact will be a witness against you, / a record of the evil you have done.
96:5 Woe to you who gulp down the finest wheat, / and drink the strength from the very root of the spring, / and trample the lowly with your power!
96:6 Woe to you who drink water at every hour! / You will suddenly be paid back. / You will be used up and dry out, / because you have abandoned the spring of life.
96:7 Woe to you who do injustice, deceit and blasphemy! / It will be a record against you, for evil.
96:8 Woe to you mighty ones, / who use your might to crush the righteous! / The day of your destruction is coming. / In those days many good days will come for the righteous, / on the day of your judgment.
## The prayer of the righteous
97:1 Believe it, you righteous: the sinners will be put to shame / and will perish on the day of injustice.
97:2 Be sure of this: the Most High has your destruction in mind, / and the angels will rejoice over your destruction.
97:3 What will you do then, sinners? / Where will you run on that day of judgment, / when you hear the sound of the prayer of the righteous?
97:4 You will not be like them. / This word will be a witness against you: / "You kept company with sinners."
97:5 In those days the prayer of the righteous will reach the Lord, / and the days of your judgment will come for you.
97:6 Every word of your injustice will be read out / before the Holy Great One. / Your faces will be covered with shame, / and every deed built on injustice will be thrown out.
## Woe to those who get rich unjustly
97:7 Woe to you, sinners, out on the open sea or on dry land! / The memory they keep of you is evil.
97:8 Woe to you who gain silver and gold, but not by righteousness, / and who say, "We have become rich, truly rich. / We have property, / and we have gotten everything we wanted.
97:9 Now let us do what we have planned. / We have piled up silver and filled our storerooms, / and the workers on our estates are as many as water."
97:10 Like water your lie will run away. / Your wealth will not stay with you; / it will quickly fly up and leave you. / For you got all of it by injustice, / and you will be handed over to a great curse.
## Sin is not sent from heaven
98:1 Now I swear to you, both wise and foolish: / you will see many things on the earth.
98:2 You men will put on more jewelry than a woman / and more colored clothes than a young girl. / In royalty and grandeur and power, / in silver and gold and purple, / in honor and in fine food, / they will be poured out like water.
98:3 Because of this they lack teaching and wisdom. / Because of it they will perish, / along with their property, / and with all their glory and honor. / In shame, in slaughter and in deep poverty, / their spirits will be thrown into the furnace of fire.
98:4 I swear to you, sinners: / a mountain has never become a slave, and never will, / and a hill does not become a woman's servant girl. / In the same way, sin was not sent upon the earth. / People created it themselves, / and those who commit it will fall under a great curse.
98:5 Barrenness was not given to a woman. / Because of what her own hands have done, / she dies without children.
98:6 I swear to you, sinners, by the Holy Great One: / all your evil deeds are laid open in the heavens. / Not one of your deeds of violence is covered or hidden.
98:7 Do not think in your spirits or say in your hearts / that you do not know and do not see / that every sin is written down in heaven, / every day, before the Most High.
98:8 From now on you know / that all the violence you do to others / is written down every day, / until the day of your judgment.
## Woe to the fools
98:9 Woe to you fools! You will perish because of your foolishness. / You do not listen to the wise, / and good things will never come your way.
98:10 Now know that you are ready for the day of destruction. / Do not hope to live, sinners. / You will go away and die, / for you know of no ransom. / You have been made ready for the day of the great judgment, / for the day of distress and great shame for your spirits.
98:11 Woe to you whose hearts are stiff, / who do evil and eat blood! / Where do you get the good things you eat and drink until you are full? / From all the good things that the Lord, the Most High, / has poured out on the earth. / So you will have no peace.
98:12 Woe to you who love deeds of injustice! / Why do you hope that good will come to you? / Know that you will be handed over into the hands of the righteous. / They will cut your throats and kill you, / and they will have no pity on you.
98:13 Woe to you who are glad when the righteous are in distress! / No grave will be dug for you.
98:14 Woe to you who brush aside the words of the righteous! / You will have no hope of life.
98:15 Woe to you who write lying words and the words of the wicked! / They write their lies so that people will hear them / and never let go of foolishness.
98:16 They will have no peace, but will die a sudden death.
## Woe to those who twist the law
99:1 Woe to those who practice wickedness, / who praise and honor lying words! / You will perish, / and you will have no happy life.
99:2 Woe to you who twist the words of uprightness / and break the eternal law! / They make themselves into what they were not: sinners. / They will be trampled down on the earth.
99:3 In those days, you righteous, be ready / to lift up your prayers to be remembered. / Lay them before the angels as a witness, / so that the angels can lay the sin of the sinners / before the Most High, to be remembered.
99:4 In those days the nations will be shaken, / and the families of the nations will rise up / on the day of destruction.
99:5 In those days those in need will go out / and snatch up their children and throw them away. / Their children will slip from their hands. / They will throw away even the babies they are nursing / and never go back to them. / They will have no pity on the ones they love.
99:6 Again I swear to you, sinners: / sin is being made ready for a day of bloodshed that never stops.
99:7 They worship stones. / Some carve images of gold and silver, of wood and clay. / Some worship unclean spirits and demons / and every kind of idol in their shrines. / But no help of any kind will come from them.
99:8 They will become wicked because their hearts are foolish. / Their eyes will be blinded by the fear in their hearts / and by what they see in their dreams.
99:9 Through these things they will become wicked and afraid, / because they did all their work by a lie / and worshiped stone. / They will perish in an instant.
99:10 But in those days, blessed are all who accept the words of wisdom and learn them, / who keep to the paths of the Most High / and walk in the path of righteousness, / and who do not turn wicked along with the wicked. / They will be saved.
99:11 Woe to you who spread evil to your neighbors! / You will be killed in Sheol.
99:12 Woe to you who lay foundations of sin and deceit, / and who bring bitterness on the earth! / Because of it they will be used up.
99:13 Woe to you who build your houses with the hard labor of others, / whose building materials are all bricks and stones of sin! / I tell you, you will have no peace.
99:14 Woe to those who reject the measure / and the eternal inheritance of their fathers, / and whose souls go chasing after idols! / They will have no rest.
99:15 Woe to those who do injustice and help violence along, / who kill their neighbors, / until the day of the great judgment!
99:16 For he will throw down your glory / and bring trouble into your hearts. / He will stir up his angry spirit / and destroy every one of you with the sword. / All the righteous and holy will remember your sins.
## The day of slaughter
100:1 In those days, in one place, fathers and their sons will be struck down together, / and brothers will fall dead beside one another, / until their blood flows like a river.
100:2 A man will not hold back his hand in mercy / from killing his sons and his grandsons. / A sinner will not hold back his hand from his honored brother. / From dawn until sunset they will kill one another.
100:3 A horse will wade up to its chest in the blood of sinners, / and a chariot will sink in it up to its top.
100:4 In those days the angels will go down into the hiding places / and gather into one place all who helped sin along. / On that day the Most High will rise / to carry out the great judgment on all the sinners.
100:5 He will appoint guards from the holy angels / over all the righteous and holy, / to guard them like the pupil of an eye, / until all evil and all sin come to an end. / Even if the righteous sleep a long sleep, / they have nothing to fear.
100:6 Then the wise among people will see what is true, / and the children of the earth will understand all the words of this book. / They will know that their wealth cannot save them / when their sins bring them crashing down.
100:7 Woe to you, sinners, when you torment the righteous / on the day of hard anguish and burn them with fire! / You will be paid back for what you have done.
100:8 Woe to you whose hearts are crooked, / who lie awake to think up evil! / Fear will overtake you, / and no one will help you.
100:9 Woe to you, sinners! Because of the words of your mouths / and the deeds of your hands, the work of your wickedness, / you will burn in blazing flames of fire.
## Heaven and earth as witnesses
100:10 Now know that the angels will search out your deeds in heaven, / from the sun, the moon and the stars, / because of your sins. / For on the earth you pass judgment on the righteous.
100:11 He will call every cloud, mist, dew and rain to testify against you. / For they will all be held back from you / and will not come down on you, / and they will keep your sins in mind.
100:12 Go ahead, give gifts to the rain, / so that it will not refuse to come down on you, / and to the dew, if it will take your gold and silver!
100:13 When snow and frost fall on you with their cold, and all the snowy winds with all their blows, in those days you will not be able to stand up to them.
## Look at the sea
101:1 Look closely at the sky, all you children of heaven, / and at every work of the Most High. / Fear him, and do no evil in front of him.
101:2 If he shuts the windows of heaven / and holds back the rain and the dew from coming down on the earth because of you, / what will you do then?
101:3 If he sends his anger on you and on all you do, / will you not be the ones begging him? / For you speak proud and hard words against his righteousness. / You will have no peace.
101:4 Do you not see the ship captains? / Their ships are tossed by the waves and shaken by the winds, / and they are in distress.
101:5 That is why they are afraid. / All their fine goods go out to sea with them, / and their hearts expect nothing good. / They think the sea will swallow them / and they will perish in it.
101:6 Are not the whole sea, all its waters and all its motion / the work of the Most High? / Has he not sealed up all its doings / and bound all of it with sand?
101:7 When he rebukes it, it is afraid and dries up, / and all its fish die, and everything in it. / But you sinners on the earth do not fear him.
101:8 Did he not make heaven and earth and everything in them? / Who gave understanding and wisdom / to all that move on the earth and in the sea?
101:9 The ship captains are afraid of the sea, are they not? / Yet the sinners do not fear the Most High.
## When the fire comes
102:1 In those days, when he brings a terrible fire on you, / where will you run, and where will you find safety? / When he sends out his voice against you, / will you not be shaken and afraid?
102:2 All the lights of heaven will be shaken with great fear, / and the whole earth will be shaken, / and will tremble and quake.
102:3 All the angels will carry out their orders / and will try to hide from the presence of the Great Glory. / The children of the earth will tremble and shake. / But you sinners are cursed forever, / and you will have no peace.
## Do not fear death, you righteous
102:4 Do not be afraid, you souls of the righteous. / Have hope, you who died in righteousness.
102:5 Do not grieve that your soul went down in grief / into great distress, wailing and sorrow, into Sheol, / or that in your lifetime your body did not receive / what your goodness deserved. / Look instead to the day that belongs to the sinners, / the day of curse and punishment.
102:6 When you die, the sinners say about you, / "The righteous die just as we die. / What good did their deeds do them?
102:7 Look, they die like us, in grief and darkness. / What do they have that we do not? / From now on we are equal.
102:8 What will they receive? What will they see, forever? / Look, they too are dead, / and from now on, forever, they will never see light."
102:9 I tell you sinners: you are content to eat and drink, / to strip people naked and rob and sin, / to gain property and see good days.
102:10 Have you seen the righteous, how their end was peace? / No violence was found in them until the day they died.
102:11 Yet they perished and became as if they had never been, / and their souls went down into Sheol in distress.
103:1 Now I swear to you, the righteous, / by his great glory and honor, / by his honored kingdom and by his greatness, / I swear to you:
103:2 I know this mystery. / I have read it in the heavenly tablets / and seen the books of the holy ones, / and I found written and engraved in them about the righteous
103:3 that every good thing, joy and honor are prepared for them / and written down for the spirits of those who died in righteousness. / Much good will be given to you in return for your labors, / and your share will be far greater than the share of the living.
103:4 Your spirits will live, you who died in righteousness. / Their spirits will rejoice and be glad, / and their memory will stay before the face of the Great One / for all the generations of the world. / So do not fear the way the sinners insult you.
## The sinners' easy death
103:5 Woe to you, sinners, when you die in your sins, / and those like you say about you, / "How blessed are the sinners! / They saw all their days.
103:6 Look, they died in good times and in wealth. / They saw no distress or killing in their lives. / They died with honor, / and no judgment was carried out on them while they lived."
103:7 Do you not know that their souls will be brought down into Sheol? / They will be miserable, and their distress will be great.
103:8 Into darkness and chains and burning flame, / where the great judgment is, / your spirits will go. / The great judgment will last for every generation, forever. / Woe to you, for you will have no peace!
## The lament of the righteous
103:9 Do not say about the righteous and good who are still alive, / "In the days of our trouble we worked hard and wore ourselves out. / We saw every trouble and met much evil. / We were used up and became few, / and our spirits shrank.
103:10 We were destroyed, and no one helped us with a word or a deed. / We had no strength and found nothing. / We were tortured and destroyed, / and we did not expect to live from one day to the next.
103:11 We hoped to be the head, but we became the tail. / We worked hard as we labored, / but we had no say over the fruit of our labor. / We became food for the sinners, / and the unjust laid their heavy yoke on us.
103:12 Those who hate us and beat us have ruled over us. / We bowed our necks to those who hate us, / and they had no pity on us.
103:13 We wanted to get away from them, to escape and rest, / but we found no place to run where we would be safe from them.
103:14 In our distress we complained about them to the rulers, / and we cried out against those who devour us. / But the rulers paid no attention to our cry / and did not want to hear our voice.
103:15 Instead they helped those who rob us and devour us, / those who have made us few. / They hide their violence / and do not take their yoke off us. / They devour us, scatter us and kill us. / They hide our murder, / and they do not remember that they raised their hands against us."
## You will shine like the stars
104:1 I swear to you, righteous ones: in heaven the angels speak of you for good / before the glory of the Great One. / Your names are written before the glory of the Great One.
104:2 Have hope! For before, you were put to shame by evil and distress, / but now you will shine like the lights of heaven, / and you will be seen, / and the gates of heaven will be opened for you.
104:3 Keep crying out for judgment, / and it will appear for you. / For all your distress will be demanded from the rulers, / and from everyone who helped those who robbed you.
104:4 Have hope, and do not give up your hope, / for you will have great joy, like the angels of heaven.
104:5 What will you have to do? / You will not need to hide on the day of the great judgment. / You will not be found among the sinners, / and the eternal judgment will stay away from you, / for all the generations of the world.
104:6 Now do not be afraid, you righteous, / when you see the sinners grow strong and get what they desire. / Do not become their companions. / Keep far away from their violence, / for you will be companions to the army of heaven.
104:7 You sinners say, "None of our sins will be searched out or written down." / But they will write down all your sins every day.
104:8 Now I am showing you / that light and darkness, day and night, / see all your sins.
104:9 Do not be wicked in your hearts, and do not lie. / Do not change the words of uprightness. / Do not call the words of the Holy Great One a lie, / and do not praise your idols. / For all your lying and all your wickedness / lead not to righteousness but to great sin.
## The books of Enoch
104:10 Now I know this mystery: / many sinners will change and twist the words of uprightness. / They will speak evil words and lie, / they will make up great fabrications, / and they will write books of their own words.
104:11 But when they write down all my words truthfully, in their own languages, / and do not change or leave out anything from my words, / but write down all of it truthfully, / everything I first testified about them,
104:12 then I know another mystery: / books will be given to the righteous and the wise, / to bring joy and uprightness and much wisdom.
104:13 The books will be given to them. / They will believe in them and rejoice in them, / and all the righteous who learned from them / all the paths of uprightness will be rewarded.
105:1 "In those days," says the Lord, "they are to call the children of the earth and testify to them about their wisdom. Show it to them, for you are their guides, and rewards over all the earth.
105:2 For I and my son will be united with them forever, in the paths of uprightness, all their lives. You will have peace. Rejoice in truth, you children of uprightness!"
## The birth of Noah
106:1 Some time later my son Methuselah took a wife for his son Lamech. She became pregnant by him and gave birth to a son.
106:2 The baby's body was white as snow and red as a rose in bloom. The hair of his head and his curls were white like wool, and his eyes were beautiful. When he opened his eyes, he lit up the whole house like the sun, and the whole house was full of light.
106:3 When he rose up out of the midwife's hands, he opened his mouth and spoke with the Lord of righteousness.
106:4 His father Lamech was afraid of him. He ran away and went to his own father, Methuselah.
106:5 He said to him, "I have fathered a strange son. He is not like a human being. He looks like the children of the angels of heaven. His nature is different, and he is not like us. His eyes are like the rays of the sun, and his face is glorious.
106:6 It seems to me that he is not from me but from the angels. I am afraid that something astonishing will happen on the earth in his days.
106:7 So now, my father, I am here to beg you and ask you to go to our father Enoch and hear the truth from him, for he lives among the angels."
106:8 When Methuselah heard what his son said, he came to me at the ends of the earth, for he had heard that I was there. He shouted, and I heard his voice and came to him. I said to him, "Here I am, my son. Why have you come to me?"
106:9 He answered me, "I have come to you because of a serious matter, and I have come near because of a disturbing sight.
106:10 Now, my father, listen to me. A son has been born to my son Lamech, and there is no one like him. His nature is not like human nature. His color is whiter than snow and redder than a rose in bloom, and the hair of his head is whiter than white wool. His eyes are like the rays of the sun. He opened his eyes and lit up the whole house.
106:11 He rose up out of the midwife's hands, opened his mouth, and blessed the Lord of heaven.
106:12 His father Lamech was frightened and ran to me. He does not believe the child is his. He thinks the child looks like one of the angels of heaven. So here I am. I have come to you so that you can tell me the truth."
106:13 I, Enoch, answered him, "The Lord will do new things on the earth. I already knew this and saw it in a vision, and I told you. In the generation of my father Jared, some from the heights of heaven broke the word of the Lord.
106:14 Look, they are committing sin and breaking the law. They have joined themselves to women and sin with them. They have married some of them and fathered children by them.
106:15 There will be great destruction on the whole earth. There will be a flood, and there will be great destruction for one year.
106:16 This child who has been born to you will be left on the earth, and his three sons will be saved with him. When all the people on the earth die, he and his sons will be saved.
106:17 They will father beings on the earth who are not of spirit but of flesh. There will be a great punishment on the earth, and the earth will be washed clean of all corruption.
106:18 Now tell your son Lamech that the child who was born is truly his son. Name him Noah, for he will be left to you as a remnant. He and his sons will be saved from the destruction that will come on the earth because of all the sin and all the injustice that will reach their full measure on the earth in his days.
106:19 After that there will be even more injustice than what was done on the earth before. For I know the mysteries of the holy ones. The Lord himself showed them to me and made them known to me, and I read them in the heavenly tablets.
107:1 I saw written on them that generation after generation will do wrong, until a generation of righteousness arises. Then wrongdoing will be destroyed, sin will pass away from the earth, and every good thing will come upon it.
107:2 Now, my son, go and tell your son Lamech that this child who was born is truly his son. It is not a lie."
107:3 When Methuselah heard the words of his father Enoch, who had shown him everything that was hidden, he went back. He had seen it all. He named that child Noah, for he will bring joy to the earth after all the destruction.
## Another book of Enoch
108:1 Another book that Enoch wrote for his son Methuselah and for those who will come after him and keep the law in the last days.
108:2 You who have kept it, and who wait in these days until an end comes to those who do evil, and an end to the power of the wrongdoers:
108:3 wait until sin passes away. For their names will be wiped out of the holy books, their offspring will be destroyed forever, and their spirits will be killed. They will cry out and wail in a place that is a shapeless wilderness, and they will burn in the fire, for there is no earth there.
108:4 I saw there something like a cloud that could not be seen through, for it was so deep that I could not see over it. I saw the flame of its fire blazing brightly, and things like shining mountains circling and swaying back and forth.
108:5 I asked one of the holy angels who was with me, "What is this shining thing? It is not a sky, only the flame of a blazing fire, and the sound of crying and weeping, wailing and fierce pain."
108:6 He said to me, "This place you see is where they throw the spirits of the sinners and the blasphemers, of those who do evil, and of those who twist everything that God has spoken through the mouth of the prophets, the things that are to be done.
108:7 For there are records about them, written and engraved above in heaven, so that the angels may read them and know what is going to happen to the sinners. There are records about the spirits of the humble, of those who made their bodies suffer and were repaid by God, and of those who were put to shame by evil people.
108:8 They loved God. They did not love gold or silver or any good thing in the world, but they handed their bodies over to torment.
108:9 From the time they came into being, they did not long for the food of the earth. They thought of themselves as a breath that passes away, and they lived by that. The Lord tested them hard, and their spirits were found pure, so that they could bless his name.
108:10 I have told all the blessings meant for them in the books. He has given them their reward, for they were found to love heaven more than the breath of life in this world. Evil people trampled them down, and they heard insults and blasphemy from them and were put to shame, yet they kept blessing me.
108:11 Now I will call the spirits of the good who belong to the generation of light, and I will transform those who were born in darkness, who in their bodies were not repaid with the honor their faithfulness deserved.
108:12 I will bring out into shining light those who love my holy name, and I will seat each one on his throne of honor.
108:13 They will shine for times without number, for God's judgment is righteousness. To the faithful he will give faithfulness, in the dwelling place of upright paths.
108:14 They will see those who were born in darkness led into darkness, while the righteous shine.
108:15 The sinners will cry out when they see them shining, and they themselves will go where days and times have been written down for them."
