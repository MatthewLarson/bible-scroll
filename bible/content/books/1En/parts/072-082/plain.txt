## The book of the lights
72:1 This is the book about how the lights of the sky move. It tells about each one: its kind, its rule, its seasons, its name, where it comes from, and its months. Uriel, the holy angel who was with me and who guides them, showed me these things. He showed me their whole written record as it really is, and how it goes for every year of the world and forever, until the new creation is made that will last forever.
## Six gates in the east and west
72:2 This is the first law of the lights. The light called the sun comes out through the gates of the sky in the east and sets through the gates of the sky in the west.
72:3 I saw six gates where the sun comes out and six gates where it goes down. The moon also rises and sets through these gates, and so do the leaders of the stars and the stars they lead. There are six in the east and six in the west, all in exact order, one after the other. To the right and left of these gates are many windows.
72:4 First the great light comes out, whose name is the sun. Its circle is like the circle of the sky, and it is completely full of fire that gives light and heat.
72:5 The wind drives the chariot it rides up in. The sun goes down out of the sky and goes back around through the north to reach the east. It is guided so that it comes to the right gate and shines on the face of the sky.
## The sun through the year
72:6 In this way it comes out in the first month through the great gate. It goes out through the fourth of those six gates on the sunrise side.
72:7 In that fourth gate, where the sun comes out in the first month, there are twelve open windows. Flame comes out of them when they are opened at their time.
72:8 When the sun rises in the sky, it comes out through that fourth gate for thirty mornings, and it sets exactly in the fourth gate in the west of the sky.
72:9 During those days the daytime grows longer each day and the night grows shorter each night, until the thirtieth morning.
72:10 On that day the daytime is longer than the night by two parts. The day is exactly ten parts and the night eight parts.
72:11 Then the sun comes out from that fourth gate and sets in the fourth. It moves on to the fifth gate in the east for thirty mornings. It comes out through it and sets in the fifth gate.
72:12 Then the daytime grows longer by two parts and becomes eleven parts, and the night grows shorter and becomes seven parts.
72:13 The sun goes back to the east and enters the sixth gate. It rises and sets in the sixth gate for thirty-one mornings, because of its sign.
72:14 On that day the daytime is longer than the night: it becomes twice as long as the night. The day becomes twelve parts, and the night grows shorter and becomes six parts.
72:15 Then the sun sets out to make the daytime shorter and the night longer. The sun goes back to the east, enters the sixth gate, and rises and sets from it for thirty mornings.
72:16 When the thirty mornings are over, the daytime has grown shorter by exactly one part. The day is eleven parts and the night seven.
72:17 The sun leaves the sixth gate in the west and goes to the east. It rises in the fifth gate for thirty mornings and sets again in the fifth gate in the west.
72:18 On that day the daytime has grown shorter by two parts. The day is ten parts and the night eight.
72:19 The sun leaves that fifth gate, setting in the fifth gate of the west. It rises in the fourth gate for thirty-one mornings, because of its sign, and sets in the west.
72:20 On that day the daytime and the night are equal. They are the same length: the night is nine parts and the day nine parts.
72:21 The sun leaves that gate and sets in the west. It goes back to the east and rises in the third gate for thirty mornings, and sets in the third gate in the west.
72:22 On that day the night begins to grow longer than the daytime, until the thirtieth morning. The daytime grows shorter day by day until the thirtieth day. The night becomes exactly ten parts and the day eight.
72:23 The sun leaves that third gate and sets in the third gate in the west. It goes back to the east and comes out through the second gate of the east for thirty mornings. In the same way it sets in the second gate in the west of the sky.
72:24 On that day the night is eleven parts and the day seven.
72:25 On that day the sun leaves the second gate and sets in the second gate in the west. It goes back to the east, to the first gate, for thirty-one mornings, and sets in the first gate in the west.
72:26 On that day the night grows longer and becomes twice as long as the daytime. The night is exactly twelve parts and the day six.
72:27 The sun has now gone through all its stations. It turns back again over those same stations. It enters that gate for thirty mornings and sets in the west opposite it.
72:28 On that day the night is shorter by one part. It becomes eleven parts, and the day seven.
72:29 The sun has come back and entered the second gate in the east. For thirty mornings it rises and sets, going back over its stations.
72:30 On that day the night grows shorter. The night is ten parts and the day eight.
72:31 On that day the sun leaves the second gate and sets in the west. It goes back to the east and rises in the third gate for thirty-one mornings, and sets in the west of the sky.
72:32 On that day the night grows shorter and becomes nine parts, and the day nine parts. Night and day are equal. The year comes to exactly 364 days.
## The law of the sun
72:33 The length and shortness of the day and of the night come from the sun's course. That is what makes them different.
72:34 Because of the sun, its course grows longer day after day and shorter night after night.
72:35 This is the law of the sun and its course, and how it turns back each time it returns. Sixty times it returns and comes out. This is the great light, which lasts forever and is called the sun, for ever and ever.
72:36 What comes out is the great light. It is named for the way it looks, as the Lord commanded.
72:37 As it rises, so it sets. It does not grow smaller and does not rest. It runs day and night in its chariot. Its light is seven times brighter than the moon's, but in size the two are equal.
## The moon
73:1 After this law I saw another law, for the smaller light, whose name is the moon.
73:2 Its circle is like the circle of the sky. The wind drives the chariot it rides in, and light is given to it by measure.
73:3 Every month the places where it rises and sets change. Its days are like the days of the sun. When its light is full, it is one seventh of the sun's light.
73:4 This is how it rises. Its first appearance in the east comes on the thirtieth morning. On that day it becomes visible, and for you it is the beginning of the month. On the thirtieth morning it is with the sun, in the gate where the sun comes out.
73:5 Half of it stands out by one seventh part. The rest of its circle is empty and has no light, except for one seventh part of it, the fourteenth part of its light.
73:6 When it receives a seventh part and half of its light, its light amounts to one part of fourteen, and a half.
73:7 It sets with the sun. When the sun rises, the moon rises with it and receives half of one part of light. In that night, at the start of its morning, at the beginning of the moon's day, the moon sets with the sun. It is dark that night for thirteen parts and a half.
73:8 On that day it rises with exactly a seventh part. It comes out and moves away from where the sun rises, and in the rest of its days it shines through its fourteen parts.
## The moon's course through the months
74:1 I saw another course and another law for the moon: how, by that law, it makes its course through the months.
74:2 Uriel, the holy angel who guides them all, showed me all of it. I wrote down their positions as he showed me. I wrote down their months as they are, and how their light looks until fifteen days are complete.
74:3 By fourteenths it completes all its light in the east, and by fourteenths it completes all its darkness in the west.
74:4 In some months it changes where it sets, and in some months it follows its own course.
74:5 In two months the moon sets with the sun in the two middle gates, the third and the fourth.
74:6 It comes out for seven days, then turns around and comes back to the gate where the sun comes out. There it completes all its light. Then it moves away from the sun, and in eight days it enters the sixth gate, from which the sun comes out.
74:7 When the sun comes out from the fourth gate, the moon comes out for seven days, until it comes out from the fifth. Then in seven days it comes back to the fourth gate and completes all its light. Then it moves away and enters the first gate in eight days.
74:8 Then in seven days it comes back again to the fourth gate, from which the sun comes out.
74:9 This is how I saw their positions: how the sun rises and sets according to the order of their months.
## Sun years and moon years
74:10 When five years are added together, the sun has thirty days more. All the days that belong to one of those five years, when they are complete, are 364 days.
74:11 The sun and the stars run ahead by six days a year. Over five years, at six days each, they come out thirty days ahead, and the moon falls thirty days behind the sun and the stars.
74:12 The moon brings in the years for them exactly. All of them keep their positions forever. They do not get ahead or fall behind by a single day, but change the year with exact rightness, every 364 days.
74:13 In three years their days are 1,092. In five years they are 1,820 days, so that in eight years there are 2,912 days.
74:14 For the moon by itself, the days in three years come to 1,062, and in five years it falls 50 days behind. For in its course, it is added to the 62 days.
74:15 In five years it has 1,770 days, so that the moon's days in eight years are 2,832.
74:16 In eight years it falls behind by 80 days. All the days it falls behind in eight years are 80.
74:17 The year is completed exactly by their stations and the stations of the sun. They rise from the gates through which the sun rises and sets for thirty days.
## The four added days
75:1 The leaders of the heads of thousands are in charge of all creation and all the stars. They also go with the four days that are added. They do not leave their place in the whole count of the year. They serve on the four days that are not counted in the count of the year.
75:2 Because of them people make mistakes about these days. For those lights really do serve at the stations of the world: one in the first gate, one in the third, one in the fourth and one in the sixth. So the year is made exact by its 364 stations.
75:3 Uriel showed me the signs, the seasons, the years and the days. He is the angel whom the Lord of glory has set forever over all the lights of the sky, in the sky and in the world. They are to rule over the face of the sky, to be seen on the earth, and to lead the day and the night. They are the sun, the moon, the stars, and all the serving creatures that circle in all the chariots of the sky.
## Windows, gates and chariots
75:4 In the same way Uriel showed me twelve open gates in the circle of the sun's chariot in the sky. The sun's rays come out of them, and heat comes out of them onto the earth when they are opened at their set times.
75:5 They are also for the winds and the spirit of the dew, when they are opened, standing open in the sky at the edges.
75:6 I saw twelve gates in the sky at the ends of the earth. The sun, the moon, the stars and all the works of the sky come out of them in the east and the west.
75:7 There are many open windows to their left and right. One window at its time gives off heat. They match the gates through which the stars come out as he commanded them, and through which they set, by their number.
75:8 I saw chariots in the sky running through the world, above and below those gates where the stars that never set turn.
75:9 One is greater than all the others, and it goes around the whole world.
## The twelve gates of the winds
76:1 At the ends of the earth I saw twelve gates open for all the winds. The winds come out of them and blow over the earth.
76:2 Three of them open on the front of the sky, three in the west, three on the right of the sky and three on the left.
76:3 The first three are toward the east. Three are toward the north. Three come after them, on the left, toward the south. And three are in the west.
76:4 Through four of them come winds of blessing and well-being. From the other eight come winds of punishment. When they are sent, they destroy the whole earth, the water on it, all who live on it, and everything in the water and on dry land.
76:5 The first wind, called the east wind, comes out of the first gate toward the east, the one that leans toward the south. From it come ruin, drought, heat and destruction.
76:6 Through the second gate, the middle one, comes what is right: rain, fruit, well-being and dew come from it. Through the third gate, toward the north, come cold and drought.
76:7 After these come the south winds, through three gates. Through the first of them, the one that leans toward the east, comes a hot wind.
76:8 Through the middle gate next to it come sweet smells, dew, rain, well-being and life.
76:9 Through the third gate, toward the west, come dew, rain, locusts and ruin.
76:10 After these come the north winds. From the seventh gate, toward the east, which leans toward the south, come dew and rain, locusts and ruin.
76:11 From the middle gate, straight ahead, come rain, dew, life and well-being. Through the third gate, toward the west, which leans toward the north, come mist, ice, snow, rain, dew and locusts.
76:12 After these come the fourth group of winds, toward the west. Through the first gate, which leans toward the north, come dew, rain, ice, cold, snow and frost.
76:13 From the middle gate come dew and rain, well-being and blessing. Through the last gate, toward the south, come drought and ruin, burning and destruction.
76:14 So the twelve gates of the four quarters of the sky are complete. I have shown you all their laws, all their punishments and all the well-being they bring, my son Methuselah.
## The four quarters of the world
77:1 The first quarter is called the east, because it is the first. The second is called the south, because the Most High will come down there. Yes, the One who is blessed forever will come down there above all.
77:2 The west quarter is named "the Waning," because there all the lights of the sky wane and go down.
77:3 The fourth quarter, named the north, is divided into three parts. One of them is where people live. The second holds seas of water, deep places, forests, rivers, darkness and mist. The third part holds the garden of righteousness.
## Mountains, rivers and islands
77:4 I saw seven high mountains, higher than all the mountains on the earth. Frost comes out of them, and the days, the seasons and the years pass and go around.
77:5 I saw seven rivers on the earth, bigger than all other rivers. One of them comes from the west and pours its water into the Great Sea.
77:6 Two of them come from the north to the sea, and pour their water into the Erythraean Sea in the east.
77:7 The other four come out on the north side, two to their own sea, the Erythraean Sea, and two into the Great Sea. They empty there, though some say they empty into the desert.
77:8 I saw seven great islands in the sea and on the land: two on the land and five in the Great Sea.
## The names of the sun and moon
78:1 These are the names of the sun: the first is Oryares and the second Tomas.
78:2 The moon has four names. The first is Asonya, the second Ebla, the third Benase and the fourth Erae.
78:3 These are the two great lights. Their circles are like the circle of the sky, and the two are the same size.
78:4 In the sun's circle there are seven parts of light that it has beyond the moon. The light is passed over by measure until the seventh part of the sun has gone across.
78:5 They set and go into the gates of the west. They go around by the north, and come out through the gates of the east onto the face of the sky.
## How the moon grows and wanes
78:6 When the moon rises and appears in the sky, it has half of a seventh part of light. On the fourteenth day it completes all its light.
78:7 Light is passed into it, fifteen parts in all, until on the fifteenth day its light is complete, by the sign of the year. It comes to fifteen parts, and the moon grows by halves of sevenths.
78:8 As it wanes, on the first day it goes down to fourteen parts of its light. On the second day it goes down to thirteen parts, on the third to twelve, on the fourth to eleven, on the fifth to ten, on the sixth to nine, on the seventh to eight, on the eighth to seven, on the ninth to six, on the tenth to five, on the eleventh to four, on the twelfth to three, and on the thirteenth to two. On the fourteenth it goes down to half a seventh, and on the fifteenth day all the light that was left disappears.
78:9 In some months the moon has twenty-nine days each, and at times twenty-eight.
78:10 Uriel showed me another law: when light is passed to the moon, and from which side the sun passes it.
78:11 All the time the moon is growing in its light, it takes the light across while it faces the sun. In fourteen days its light is complete in the sky. When it is all lit up, its light is full in the sky.
78:12 The first day is called the new moon, because on that day the light rises on it.
78:13 It becomes full exactly on the day when the sun goes down in the west and the moon goes up from the east at night. The moon shines all night until the sun rises across from it, and the moon is seen across from the sun.
78:14 From the side where the moon's light first came out, there it wanes again, until all its light disappears and the days of the month are over. Then its circle is empty, without light.
78:15 It makes three months of thirty days at its time, and three months of twenty-nine days each. In these it completes its waning in the first period, and in the first gate, in 177 days.
78:16 In the time when it comes out, it appears for three months of thirty days each, and for three months of twenty-nine days each.
78:17 At night, for twenty days each time, it looks like a man. By day it looks like the sky, because there is nothing else in it except its light.
## Enoch finishes the lesson
79:1 And now, my son Methuselah, I have shown you everything. The whole law of the stars of the sky is complete.
79:2 He showed me all their laws for each day, for each season of each rule, for each year and its going out, and its order in each month and each week.
79:3 He showed me the waning of the moon that happens in the sixth gate. In this sixth gate its light becomes full, and from there begin the month and the waning.
79:4 And he showed me the waning that happens in the first gate in its time, until 177 days are complete. Counted in weeks, that is twenty-five weeks and two days.
79:5 It falls behind the sun and the order of the stars by exactly five days in one period, when this place that you see has been gone through.
79:6 This is the look and the pattern of each light, which Uriel the great angel, their leader, showed me.
## When the order breaks down
80:1 In those days Uriel answered me. He said, "Look, Enoch, I have shown you everything. I have revealed it all to you, so that you could see this sun and this moon, those who lead the stars of the sky, and all who turn them: their work, their times and their goings out.
80:2 In the days of the sinners the years will be shortened. / Their seed will be late in their land and in their fields. / Everything on the earth will change / and will not appear at its time. / The rain will be held back, / and the sky will stand still.
80:3 In those times the fruit of the earth will be late / and will not grow at its time, / and the fruit of the trees will be held back at its time.
80:4 The moon will change its order / and will not be seen at its time.
80:5 In those days the sky will be seen, / and drought will come at the edge of the great chariot in the west. / It will shine more brightly than the order of light allows.
80:6 Many of the leaders of the stars will stray from the command. / They will change their paths and their tasks, / and will not appear at the times set for them.
80:7 The whole order of the stars will be hidden from the sinners. / The thoughts of those who live on the earth will go wrong about them. / They will turn away from all their ways. / They will go wrong and treat the stars as gods.
80:8 Evil will grow over them, / and punishment will come on them / to destroy them all."
## The heavenly tablets
81:1 He said to me, "Enoch, look at the book of the heavenly tablets. / Read what is written on them, / and learn every single thing."
81:2 I looked at everything on the heavenly tablets. I read all that was written there and understood it all. I read the book and everything written in it: all the deeds of human beings, and of all the children of flesh on the earth, to the last generations of the world.
81:3 Then I blessed the Lord, the King of glory forever, because he has made all the works of the world. I praised the Lord for his patience, and I spoke a blessing over the children of the world.
81:4 At that time I said, "Blessed is the man who dies righteous and good, / with no book of wrongdoing written about him / and no guilt found against him."
## One year to teach
81:5 Then those three holy ones brought me and set me down on the earth in front of the door of my house. They said to me, "Make everything known to your son Methuselah. Show all your children that no flesh is righteous before the Lord, because he is the one who made them.
81:6 We will leave you with your children for one year, until you give your commands, so that you can teach your children, write it down for them, and tell it to all your children. In the second year they will take you away from among them.
81:7 Let your heart be strong, / for the good will make righteousness known to the good. / The righteous will rejoice with the righteous, / and they will greet one another.
81:8 But the sinner will die with the sinner, / and the one who turns away will go down with the one who turns away.
81:9 Those who do what is right will die because of what people do, / and they will be gathered in because of the deeds of the wicked."
81:10 In those days they finished speaking with me. I came to my people, blessing the Lord of the ages.
## Keep the books
82:1 And now, my son Methuselah, I am telling you all these things and writing them down for you. I have revealed everything to you and given you books about all of it. My son Methuselah, keep the books your father wrote with his own hand, and pass them on to the generations of the world.
82:2 I have given wisdom to you and to your children, / and to the children you will have, / so that they may give it to their children for all generations forever: / this wisdom that is beyond their thoughts.
82:3 Those who understand it will not sleep. / They will listen closely to learn this wisdom. / It will taste better to those who eat it than fine food.
## Blessed are those who count right
82:4 Blessed are all the righteous! Blessed are all who walk in the way of righteousness and do not sin as the sinners do in counting all their days. In those days the sun travels across the sky. It goes in and out through the gates for thirty days at a time, with the heads of thousands of the order of the stars. With them go the four that are added, which divide the four parts of the year. They lead them and go in with them on four days.
82:5 Because of them people go wrong. They do not count these days in the count of the whole year. People go wrong about them and do not know them exactly.
82:6 For they truly belong in the count of the year and are written down forever: one in the first gate, one in the third, one in the fourth and one in the sixth. The year is complete in 364 days.
82:7 Their account is true, and the count written down is exact. For Uriel showed me the lights, the months, the feasts, the years and the days, and breathed on me. The Lord of all the creation of the world put him in command of the host of heaven for my sake.
82:8 He has power over night and day in the sky, to make light shine on people: the sun, the moon, the stars and all the powers of the sky that turn in their circles.
## The leaders of the stars
82:9 This is the law of the stars, which set in their places, at their times, at their feasts and in their months.
82:10 These are the names of those who lead them. They watch that the stars go in at their times, in their order, in their seasons, in their months, in their terms of rule and in their positions.
82:11 Their four leaders, who divide the four parts of the year, go in first. After them come the twelve leaders of the orders, who divide the months. For the 364 days there are the heads of thousands, who separate the days. For the four days added to them there are the leaders who divide the four parts of the year.
82:12 These heads of thousands are added between leader and led, each one behind a station, and their leaders make the divisions.
82:13 These are the names of the leaders who divide the four parts of the year and are appointed over them: Milkiel, Helemmelek, Meleyal and Narel.
82:14 The names of those who lead them are Adnarel, Iyasusael and Elumiel. These three follow the leaders of the orders. There is one who follows the three leaders of the orders, and they follow the leaders of the stations, who divide the four parts of the year.
## Spring and summer
82:15 At the beginning of the year Milkiel rises first and rules. He is called Tamaini and Sun. All the days of his rule are ninety-one days.
82:16 These are the signs of the days that will be seen on the earth in the days of his rule: sweat, heat and heaviness. All the trees bear fruit, and leaves come out on all the trees. There is the wheat harvest, the rose in bloom, and all the flowers that bloom in the field. But the trees of the rainy season dry up.
82:17 These are the names of the leaders under them: Berkeel and Zelbesael. Another, who is added as a head of a thousand, is named Heloyasef. With that, the days of this one's rule are complete.
82:18 The next leader after them is Helemmelek, whom they call the Shining Sun. All the days of his light are ninety-one days.
82:19 These are the signs of his days on the earth: burning heat and dryness. The trees bring their fruit ripe and ready and give their fruit to dry. The sheep mate and become pregnant. They gather in all the fruit of the earth, all that is in the fields, and the winepress. These things happen in the days of his rule.
82:20 These are the names, the orders and the leaders of those under them, the heads of thousands: Gedael, Keel and Heel. The name of the head of a thousand added to them is Asfael. With that, the days of his rule are complete.
