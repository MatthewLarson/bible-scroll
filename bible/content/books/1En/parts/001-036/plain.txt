## Enoch's blessing
1:1 The word of blessing of Enoch. With it he blessed the chosen and the righteous who will be living on the day of trouble, when all the evil and the wicked are taken away.
1:2 Enoch took up his message and spoke. He was a righteous man whose eyes God had opened, and he saw the vision of the Holy One in the heavens. The angels showed it to me, and I heard everything from them. I understood what I saw. It was not for this generation, but for a distant generation still to come.
## The Holy Great One is coming
1:3 I spoke about the chosen, and I took up my message about them: / The Holy Great One will come out from his dwelling.
1:4 The eternal God will tread from there on Mount Sinai. / He will appear with his camp, and he will appear from heaven in the strength of his power.
1:5 Everyone will be afraid. / The Watchers will shake, and great fear and trembling will grip them, to the ends of the earth.
1:6 The high mountains will be shaken, and the high hills will sink down. / They will melt like wax in front of a flame.
1:7 The earth will be torn open, and everything on the earth will perish. / There will be judgment on everyone, on all the righteous too.
1:8 But he will make peace with the righteous and guard the chosen. / Mercy will be on them. / They will all belong to God. / They will prosper and be blessed, and God's light will shine on them.
1:9 Look! He has come with ten thousands of holy ones / to carry out judgment on all. / He will destroy the wicked / and convict all flesh / of all their wicked deeds that they have done, and of the harsh words they have spoken, and of everything that wicked sinners have said against him.
## Learn from the world God made
2:1 Watch everything that happens in the sky. See how the lights in the sky never change their paths. Each one rises and sets in its order, at its own time, and none of them breaks its command.
2:2 Look at the earth, and think about what happens on it from beginning to end. Not one of God's works changes; each one shows itself as it should.
2:3 Look at summer and winter. The whole earth fills with water, and clouds, dew and rain settle on it.
3:1 Watch and see how in winter all the trees seem dried out and stripped of their leaves, except fourteen trees that do not drop theirs. They hold on to the old leaves for two or three years, until the new ones come.
4:1 Watch the days of summer, too, when the sun stands over the earth, facing it. You look for shade and shelter from the sun's heat. The ground burns with scorching heat, and you cannot walk on the earth or on a rock because it is so hot.
5:1 Watch how the trees clothe themselves in green leaves and bear fruit. Think about all of it, and know that the One who lives forever made all these things for you.
5:2 Year after year his works go on in front of him. All his works serve him and do not change. Everything is done just as God has ordered.
5:3 See how the seas and the rivers together carry out their work.
## A word for the wicked and the chosen
5:4 But you have not stood firm. / You have not done what the Lord commands. / You have turned away, and with your unclean mouths you have spoken proud and harsh words against his greatness. / You hard-hearted people, you will have no peace!
5:5 So you will curse your days, and the years of your life will perish. / The everlasting curse on you will grow, and you will find no mercy.
5:6 In those days you will make your names an everlasting curse for all the righteous. / The sinners will curse you continually, and it will be the same for you as for the sinners.
5:7 But for the chosen there will be light, joy and peace, and they will inherit the earth. / For you, the wicked, there will be a curse.
5:8 Then wisdom will be given to the chosen. / They will all live, and they will not do wrong, either through wickedness or through pride. / Those who have wisdom will be humble.
5:9 They will not do wrong again, and they will not be condemned all the days of their life. / They will not die in wrath or in anger. / They will complete the full number of the days of their life. / They will grow old in peace, and the years of their joy will be many, in gladness and everlasting peace, all the days of their life.
## The angels' oath
6:1 In those days, when human beings had become many, beautiful and lovely daughters were born to them.
6:2 The angels, the children of heaven, saw them and wanted them. They said to one another, "Come on, let's choose wives for ourselves from among human beings, and let's father children."
6:3 Semyaza, their leader, said to them, "I am afraid you may not really be willing to go through with this. Then I alone will have to pay for this great sin."
6:4 They all answered him, "Let's all swear an oath and bind one another with curses, so that none of us backs out of this plan, but we carry it out."
6:5 Then they all swore together and bound one another with curses to do it.
6:6 There were two hundred of them in all. They came down in the days of Jared on the top of Mount Hermon. They called it Mount Hermon because there they had sworn and bound one another with curses.
6:7 These are the names of their leaders: Semyaza, who was their leader, Urakib, Rameel, Kokabel, Tamiel, Ramuel, Danel, Ezeqeel, Baraqel, Azazel, Armaros, Batrael, Ananel, Zaqebe, Samsaweel, Sartael, Turel, Yomyael and Arazyal.
6:8 These are the chiefs of the two hundred angels.
## Giants on the earth
7:1 They and all the others with them took wives for themselves. Each one chose a woman for himself, and they began to go to them and sleep with them. They taught them potions and spells and the cutting of roots, and they showed them plants.
7:2 The women became pregnant and gave birth to huge giants, each of them three thousand cubits tall.
7:3 The giants ate up everything people worked to produce, until people could no longer feed them.
7:4 Then the giants turned against the people and began to eat them.
7:5 They began to sin against birds, animals, creeping things and fish. They ate one another's flesh and drank the blood.
7:6 Then the earth brought a charge against the lawless ones.
## Forbidden knowledge
8:1 Azazel taught people to make swords, knives, shields and breastplates. He showed them the metals and how to work them, and how to make bracelets and jewelry. He taught them to use eye paint and to make their eyelids beautiful. He showed them every kind of costly and choice stone, all the colors of dye, and the changing of the world.
8:2 Great wickedness spread, and there was much sexual sin. People went astray, and all their ways became corrupt.
8:3 Semyaza taught all who cast spells and cut roots. Armaros taught how to break spells, Baraqel taught the star-readers, Kokabel taught the signs, Tamiel taught how to watch the stars, and Asradel taught the path of the moon.
8:4 As people were being destroyed, they cried out, and their voice reached heaven.
## The archangels plead
9:1 Then Michael, Uriel, Raphael and Gabriel looked down from heaven. They saw much blood being shed on the earth, and all the lawlessness being done on the earth.
9:2 They said to one another, "The sound of their cries! The earth, left empty, cries out all the way to the gates of heaven.
9:3 And now, holy ones of heaven, the souls of human beings are pleading with you. They say, 'Bring our case before the Most High.'"
9:4 They said to their Lord, the King, "You are Lord of lords, God of gods and King of kings. / The throne of your glory stands through all the generations of the world. / Your name is holy and praised through all the generations of the world. / It is blessed and praised!
9:5 You made everything, and you have power over everything. / Everything lies uncovered and open before you. / You see everything, and nothing can hide from you.
9:6 You see what Azazel has done: how he taught every kind of wrong on the earth, and revealed the eternal secrets that were kept in heaven.
9:7 Semyaza has taught spells, the one you gave authority to rule over those who are with him.
9:8 They have gone to the daughters of human beings, and slept with those women, and made themselves unclean. / They revealed all these sins to them.
9:9 The women gave birth to giants, and because of this the whole earth has been filled with blood and wrongdoing.
9:10 And now the souls of the dead are crying out and pleading at the gates of heaven. / Their groaning has gone up, and they cannot get away from the lawlessness being done on the earth.
9:11 You know everything before it happens. / You know these things and all that concerns them, yet you tell us nothing. / What should we do to them about this?"
## Orders from heaven
10:1 Then the Most High, the Great and Holy One, spoke. He sent Uriel to the son of Lamech
10:2 and said to him, "Say to him in my name, 'Hide yourself.' Tell him about the end that is coming. The whole earth will be destroyed. The waters of a flood are about to come over the whole earth, and everything on it will be destroyed.
10:3 Now teach him how to escape, so that his descendants may remain for the whole earth."
10:4 The Lord also said to Raphael, "Bind Azazel hand and foot and throw him into the darkness. Open up the desert that is in Dudael, and throw him in there.
10:5 Throw rough, sharp stones on him, and cover him with darkness. Let him stay there forever. Cover his face so that he cannot see light.
10:6 On the great day of judgment he will be thrown into the fire.
10:7 Heal the earth the angels have ruined. Announce the healing of the earth: I will heal the earth, so that not all human beings will perish because of all the secret things the Watchers told and taught their sons.
10:8 The whole earth has been ruined through the works that Azazel taught. Write down every sin against him."
10:9 The Lord said to Gabriel, "Go against the bastards and the outcasts, the children of sexual sin. Destroy the children of sexual sin and the children of the Watchers from among human beings. Drive them out and send them against one another, so that they destroy each other in battle. They will not have long lives.
10:10 They will all plead with you, but their fathers will be granted nothing for them. They hope to live forever, and each of them hopes to live five hundred years."
10:11 The Lord said to Michael, "Go and make this known to Semyaza and the others with him, who joined themselves to women and made themselves unclean with them in all their uncleanness.
10:12 When all their sons have killed one another and they have watched their loved ones destroyed, bind them for seventy generations under the hills of the earth. Keep them there until the day of their judgment and their end, until the judgment that lasts forever and ever is complete.
10:13 In those days they will be led away to the depths of fire, into torment, and into the prison where they will be locked up forever and ever.
10:14 Whoever is burned and destroyed from then on will be bound together with them until the end of all generations.
10:15 Destroy all the lustful spirits and the children of the Watchers, for they have done wrong to human beings.
## A world made new
10:16 Wipe out every wrong from the face of the earth. Every evil deed will come to an end, and the plant of righteousness and truth will appear. It will become a blessing: deeds of righteousness and truth will be planted with joy forever.
10:17 Then all the righteous will escape. / They will live until they have fathered a thousand children, and they will complete all the days of their youth and their sabbaths in peace.
10:18 In those days the whole earth will be farmed in righteousness. / All of it will be planted with trees and filled with blessing.
10:19 They will plant every kind of pleasant tree on it, and they will plant vines on it. / The vine planted there will give fruit in plenty. / Every seed sown there will yield a thousand measures for one, and one measure of olives will yield ten presses of oil.
10:20 Cleanse the earth from all oppression, all wrong, all sin, all wickedness and all uncleanness that is done on the earth. / Wipe them out from the earth.
10:21 All human beings will become righteous. / All nations will worship and bless me, and all of them will bow down to me.
10:22 The earth will be clean from all corruption, all sin, all punishment and all torment. / I will never again send a flood on it, for all generations and forever.
11:1 In those days I will open the storehouses of blessing in heaven, to send them down on the earth, on the work and the labor of human beings.
11:2 Peace and truth will be partners through all the days of the world and through all its generations."
## Enoch the scribe
12:1 Before all these things, Enoch had been hidden. No human being knew where he was hidden, where he lived, or what had happened to him.
12:2 All his work was with the holy ones and with the Watchers in his days.
12:3 I, Enoch, was blessing the great Lord, the King of the ages. Then the Watchers called me, Enoch the scribe, and said to me,
12:4 "Enoch, scribe of righteousness, go and announce this to the Watchers of heaven who left high heaven, the holy and eternal place. They made themselves unclean with women. They did what human beings do and took wives for themselves, and they have caused great ruin on the earth.
12:5 They will have no peace on the earth and no forgiveness of sin.
12:6 Because they delight in their children, they will see their loved ones murdered, and they will groan over the destruction of their children. They will plead forever, but they will have no mercy and no peace."
## The Watchers' petition
13:1 Enoch went and said to Azazel, "You will have no peace. A great judgment has gone out against you: you will be bound.
13:2 You will have no relief, no request granted, and no mercy, because of the wrong you taught, and because of all the deeds of blasphemy, wrong and sin that you showed to human beings."
13:3 So I went and spoke to all of them together. Every one of them was afraid, and fear and trembling took hold of them.
13:4 They asked me to write a petition for them, so that they might be forgiven, and to carry their petition up to God in heaven.
13:5 From then on they could not speak, or even lift their eyes toward heaven, because they were ashamed of the sins for which they had been condemned.
13:6 So I wrote down their petition and their prayers, about their spirits and about the deeds of each one, and about what they asked: that they might have forgiveness and long life.
13:7 Then I went and sat down by the waters of Dan, in Dan, southwest of Hermon. I read their petition until I fell asleep.
13:8 A dream came to me, and visions fell on me. I saw visions of punishment, and I was to tell them to the children of heaven and rebuke them.
13:9 When I woke up, I went to them. They were all sitting together, weeping, in Ubelseyael, which is between Lebanon and Senir, with their faces covered.
13:10 I told them all the visions I had seen in my sleep, and I began to speak words of righteousness and to rebuke the Watchers of heaven.
## Enoch is taken up to heaven
14:1 This is the book of the words of righteousness and of the rebuke of the eternal Watchers, as the Holy Great One commanded in that vision.
14:2 In my sleep I saw what I will now tell with a tongue of flesh and with my own breath. The Great One gave human beings a mouth, so that they could speak with it and understand with the heart.
14:3 Just as he created human beings and gave them the power to understand words of knowledge, so he created me and gave me the power to rebuke the Watchers, the children of heaven.
14:4 I wrote out your petition, but in my vision it was shown to me that your petition will not be granted, not in all the days of the world. Judgment has been passed on you, and nothing will be granted to you.
14:5 From now on you will never go up into heaven again, for all eternity. The order has gone out to bind you inside the earth for all the days of the world.
14:6 Before that, you will see the destruction of your beloved sons. You will not keep them: they will fall in front of you by the sword.
14:7 Your petition for them will not be granted, and neither will your petition for yourselves. Even as you weep and pray, you will not say a single word of the writing I have written.
14:8 This is how the vision was shown to me. In the vision, clouds called me and a mist called me. The paths of the stars and the lightning flashes rushed me along and drove me on. In the vision the winds made me fly, hurried me, and lifted me up into heaven.
14:9 I went in until I came near a wall built of hailstones, with tongues of fire all around it, and it began to frighten me.
14:10 I went into the tongues of fire and came near a great house built of hailstones. The walls of that house were like a floor paved with slabs of hailstone, and its floor was hail.
14:11 Its ceiling was like the path of the stars and the lightning, with fiery cherubim between them, and their sky was water.
14:12 Blazing fire surrounded its walls, and its doors burned with fire.
14:13 I went into that house. It was as hot as fire and as cold as hail, and there was no pleasure or life in it. Fear covered me, and trembling took hold of me.
14:14 As I shook and trembled, I fell on my face, and I saw in the vision
14:15 another house, greater than the first. Its whole doorway stood open in front of me, and it was built of tongues of fire.
14:16 In every way it went far beyond the first in glory, honor and size, so that I cannot describe its glory and its size to you.
14:17 Its floor was fire. Above it were lightning flashes and the paths of the stars, and its ceiling was blazing fire.
14:18 I looked and saw a high throne inside. It looked like frost, its wheels were like the shining sun, and there was the sound of cherubim.
14:19 Rivers of blazing fire came out from under the throne, and I could not look at it.
14:20 The Great Glory sat on it. His robe shone brighter than the sun and was whiter than any snow.
14:21 None of the angels could go in and look at his face, because of his majesty and glory, and no flesh could look at him.
14:22 Blazing fire was all around him, and a great fire stood in front of him. None of those around him could come near him. Myriads upon myriads were in front of him, but he had no need of holy counsel.
14:23 The holy ones who were near him did not go away from him or leave him by night or by day.
14:24 Until then I had been lying face down, trembling. Then the Lord called me with his own mouth and said to me, "Come here, Enoch, and hear my holy word."
14:25 He lifted me up and brought me near to the door, but I kept my face turned down.
## God's answer to the Watchers
15:1 He answered me with his own voice and said to me, "Listen! Do not be afraid, Enoch, righteous man and scribe of righteousness. Come here and hear my voice.
15:2 Go and say to the Watchers of heaven who sent you to plead for them: 'You ought to plead for human beings, not human beings for you.
15:3 Why did you leave high heaven, the holy and eternal place? You lay with women and made yourselves unclean with the daughters of human beings. You took wives for yourselves and acted like the children of the earth, and you fathered giants as your sons.
15:4 You were holy, spiritual beings, living a life that never ends. Yet you made yourselves unclean with women. With the blood of flesh you fathered children. You desired the blood of human beings, and you did what flesh and blood do, those who die and perish.
15:5 This is why I gave them wives: so that they could plant seed in them and have children by them, and so the work on earth would go on.
15:6 But from the beginning you were spiritual, living a life that never ends, never dying through all the generations of the world.
15:7 So I did not make wives for you, because the spiritual ones of heaven have their home in heaven.
## Where evil spirits come from
15:8 And now the giants, who were born of spirits and flesh, will be called evil spirits on the earth, and the earth will be their home.
15:9 Evil spirits have come out of their bodies. Because they were created from above, and their beginning and first foundation was from the holy Watchers, they will be evil spirits on the earth. They will be called evil spirits.
15:10 The spirits of heaven have their home in heaven, but the spirits of the earth, who were born on the earth, have their home on the earth.
15:11 The spirits of the giants trouble the clouds. They do wrong, destroy, attack, fight and crush on the earth, and they cause grief. They eat no food, they do not thirst, and they cannot be seen.
15:12 These spirits will rise up against human beings and against women, because they came out of them.
16:1 From the days of the slaughter, destruction and death of the giants, when the spirits went out from the souls of their flesh, they will go on destroying without facing judgment. They will destroy like this until the day of the end, the great judgment, when the great age will be brought to its end for the Watchers and the wicked.'
16:2 And now, as for the Watchers who sent you to plead for them, who were once in heaven,
16:3 say to them: 'You were in heaven, but the hidden things had not yet been revealed to you. You knew a worthless secret, and in the hardness of your hearts you told it to the women. Through this secret, women and men are doing more and more evil on the earth.'
16:4 So say to them, 'You will have no peace.'"
## Enoch's first journey
17:1 They took me to a place where the beings there were like blazing fire, and whenever they wished, they looked like men.
17:2 They brought me to a stormy place and to a mountain whose peak reached into heaven.
17:3 I saw the places of the lights and of the thunder at the ends of the world, in the depths. There were a bow of fire, arrows and their quivers, a sword of fire, and all the lightning flashes.
17:4 They took me to what is called the water of life, and to the fire of the west, which receives every setting of the sun.
17:5 I came to a river of fire whose fire flows like water and pours into the great sea in the west.
17:6 I saw all the great rivers. I reached the great darkness and went to the place where no flesh walks.
17:7 I saw the dark mountains of winter and the place where the waters of all the deep pour out.
17:8 I saw the mouths of all the rivers of the earth and the mouth of the deep.
18:1 I saw the storehouses of all the winds. I saw how with them he has furnished all creation and the foundations of the earth.
18:2 I saw the cornerstone of the earth. I saw the four winds that hold up the earth and the dome of the sky.
18:3 I saw how the winds stretch out the heights of heaven. They stand between heaven and earth: they are the pillars of heaven.
18:4 I saw the winds that turn the sky and bring the disk of the sun and all the stars to their setting.
18:5 I saw the winds on the earth that carry the clouds. I saw the paths of the angels. At the end of the earth I saw the dome of the sky above.
## Seven mountains and a prison
18:6 I went on toward the south. There a place burns day and night, where there are seven mountains of precious stone, three toward the east and three toward the south.
18:7 Of those toward the east, one was of colored stone, one of pearl, and one of healing stone. Those toward the south were of red stone.
18:8 The middle one reached up to heaven like the throne of God. It was of a stone called peka, and the top of the throne was of sapphire.
18:9 I saw a blazing fire, and what was in all the mountains.
18:10 I saw a place there, the end of the great earth. There the heavens come to an end.
18:11 I saw a deep chasm in the earth, with pillars of heavenly fire. Among them I saw pillars of fire coming down from heaven, beyond measure both in height and in depth.
18:12 Beyond that chasm I saw a place with no dome of the sky above it and no solid ground beneath it. There was no water on it and no birds. It was a desert place.
18:13 There I saw something terrible: seven stars like great burning mountains. When I asked about them,
18:14 the angel said, "This place is the end of heaven and earth. It has become a prison for the stars of heaven and for the army of heaven.
18:15 The stars rolling over the fire are the ones that broke God's command at the beginning of their rising, because they did not come out at their proper times.
18:16 He was angry with them and bound them until the time when their sin is completed, ten thousand years."
## The prison of the angels
19:1 Uriel said to me, "The angels who mingled with women will stand here. Their spirits take on many forms. They ruin human beings and lead them astray to offer sacrifices to demons as if to gods. They will stand here until the great day of judgment, when they will be judged until they are finished.
19:2 And their wives, who led the angels of heaven astray, will become sirens."
19:3 I, Enoch, alone saw these sights, the ends of everything. No human being will see as I have seen.
## The archangels
20:1 These are the names of the holy angels who keep watch.
20:2 Uriel, one of the holy angels, who is over the world and over Tartarus.
20:3 Raphael, one of the holy angels, who is over the spirits of human beings.
20:4 Raguel, one of the holy angels, who takes vengeance on the world and on the lights.
20:5 Michael, one of the holy angels, who is set over the best of humankind, in charge of the people.
20:6 Saraqael, one of the holy angels, who is over the spirits of human beings who sin in their spirits.
20:7 Gabriel, one of the holy angels, who is over the serpents, the garden and the cherubim.
20:8 Remiel, one of the holy angels, whom God has put in charge of those who rise.
## The prison of the stars
21:1 I traveled on to a place where nothing had been made.
21:2 There I saw something terrible. There was no sky above and no solid ground below, only an empty place, bare and terrible.
21:3 There I saw seven stars of heaven bound together in it, like great mountains, burning like fire.
21:4 Then I said, "For what sin have they been bound, and why have they been thrown here?"
21:5 Uriel, one of the holy angels, who was with me and was guiding me, said to me, "Enoch, what are you asking about? What are you so eager to find out?
21:6 These are some of the stars that broke the command of God Most High. They have been bound here until ten thousand ages are completed, the number of the days of their sins."
21:7 From there I went to another place, even more terrible than this one. I saw a terrible thing there: a great fire, burning and blazing. The place had a cleft in it that went down to the deep, full of great pillars of fire falling down. I could not see how far it reached or how wide it was, and I could not even guess.
21:8 Then I said, "How frightening this place is, and how terrible to look at!"
21:9 Then Uriel, one of the holy angels, who was with me, answered me. He said to me, "Enoch, why are you so afraid? Why are you so shaken by this terrible place, and by the sight of this pain?"
21:10 He said to me, "This place is the prison of the angels. They will be held here forever."
## The places of the dead
22:1 From there I went to another place. He showed me in the west a great, high mountain of hard rock.
22:2 In it were four hollow places, deep and wide and very smooth, as smooth as something rolled flat, deep and dark to look at.
22:3 Then Raphael, one of the holy angels, who was with me, answered me. He said to me, "These hollow places were made so that the spirits, the souls of the dead, may gather in them. They were created for this, so that all the souls of human beings would gather here.
22:4 These places were made to hold them until the day of their judgment and until their appointed time. That appointed time is long, lasting until the great judgment comes on them."
22:5 I saw the spirits of human beings who had died, and their voice reached up to heaven, pleading.
22:6 Then I asked Raphael, the angel who was with me. I said to him, "Whose spirit is this, whose voice reaches up like this and pleads?"
22:7 He answered me, "This is the spirit that came out of Abel, whom his brother Cain killed. Abel keeps pleading against him until Cain's descendants are wiped from the face of the earth, and his descendants perish from among the descendants of human beings."
22:8 Then I asked about him and about the judgment of all, and I said, "Why is one separated from another?"
22:9 He answered me, "These three were made so that the spirits of the dead might be kept apart. This is how the souls of the righteous have been set apart: here is the spring of water with light above it.
22:10 In the same way, a place has been made for sinners when they die and are buried in the earth, and judgment did not come on them during their lives.
22:11 Here their souls are kept apart in this great pain until the great day of judgment, punishment and torment for those who curse, forever, and of vengeance for their souls. There he will bind them forever, even those from the beginning of the world.
22:12 A place has been set apart in the same way for the souls of those who plead and tell about their destruction, when they were killed in the days of the sinners.
22:13 And a place like this has been made for the souls of people who were not righteous but sinners, complete in their wrongdoing. They will be with the wrongdoers, like them. Their souls will not be killed on the day of judgment, and they will not be raised from there."
22:14 Then I blessed the Lord of glory and said, / "Blessed is my Lord, the Lord of glory and righteousness, / who rules over everything forever!"
## The restless fire
23:1 From there I went to another place, toward the west, to the ends of the earth.
23:2 I saw a blazing fire that kept running without rest. It did not stop its course by day or by night, but ran on the same.
23:3 I asked, "What is this, that never rests?"
23:4 Then Raguel, one of the holy angels, who was with me, answered me. He said to me, "This running fire you have seen in the west is the fire of all the lights of heaven."
## The mountain throne and the fragrant tree
24:1 From there I went to another place on the earth, and he showed me a mountain of fire that blazed day and night.
24:2 I went toward it and saw seven glorious mountains, each different from the others. Their stones were precious and beautiful. All of them were glorious, splendid to see and lovely in form. Three were toward the east, set one on top of another, and three toward the south, set one on top of another. Between them were deep, rough valleys, none of them joining another.
24:3 The seventh mountain stood in the middle of these. In their height they were all like the seat of a throne, and fragrant trees surrounded it.
24:4 Among them was a tree whose scent I had never smelled before. None of the others was like it, and no other tree was like it. Its scent was sweeter than every other fragrance. Its leaves, its blossoms and its wood never wither. Its fruit is beautiful, like the clusters of a date palm.
24:5 Then I said, "Look at this beautiful tree! It is lovely to see, its leaves are fine, and its fruit is a great delight to look at."
24:6 Then Michael answered me. He was one of the holy and honored angels, who was with me and was in charge of them.
25:1 He said to me, "Enoch, why are you asking me about the scent of this tree? Why are you so eager to know?"
25:2 I, Enoch, answered him, "I want to know about everything, but most of all about this tree."
25:3 He answered me, "This high mountain you have seen, whose top is like the Lord's throne, is his throne. The Holy Great One, the Lord of glory, the eternal King, will sit there when he comes down to visit the earth with goodness.
25:4 As for this beautiful, fragrant tree, no flesh has the right to touch it until the great judgment, when he will take vengeance on all and everything is completed forever. Then it will be given to the righteous and the humble.
25:5 From its fruit, life will be given to the chosen. It will be planted toward the north, in the holy place, by the house of the Lord, the eternal King.
25:6 Then they will be full of joy and gladness, and they will enter the holy place. Its scent will be in their bones. They will live a long life on the earth, as your fathers lived. In their days no sorrow, plague, hardship or punishment will touch them."
25:7 Then I blessed the Lord of glory, the eternal King, because he has prepared such things for righteous people. / He created them and promised to give them these things.
## The center of the earth
26:1 From there I went to the middle of the earth. I saw a blessed and fertile place, with branches that lived on and sprouted from a tree that had been cut down.
26:2 There I saw a holy mountain. Under the mountain, on its east side, was water, and it flowed toward the south.
26:3 Toward the east I saw another mountain, higher than this one. Between them was a deep, narrow valley, and water flowed through it too, along the mountain.
26:4 To the west of it was another mountain, lower and not very high, with a valley below it between them. There were other deep, dry valleys at the ends of all three.
26:5 All the valleys were deep and narrow, cut in hard rock, and no trees were planted on them.
26:6 I was amazed at the rock and amazed at the valley. I was truly amazed.
## The cursed valley
27:1 Then I said, "Why is this land blessed and all of it full of trees, while this valley between them is cursed?"
27:2 Then Uriel, one of the holy angels, who was with me, answered me. He said to me, "This cursed valley is for those who are cursed forever. Everyone who speaks improper words against God with their mouths, and says harsh things about his glory, will be gathered here. Here they will be gathered, and here is the place of their judgment.
27:3 In the last days the sight of a righteous judgment on them will be shown before the righteous forever, for all days. Here those who have found mercy will bless the Lord of glory, the eternal King.
27:4 In the days of the others' judgment, they will bless him for the mercy he has given them as their share."
27:5 Then I blessed the Lord of glory. I spoke to him and praised his greatness as it deserves.
## Journey to the east
28:1 From there I went east, into the middle of the mountain range of the desert. I saw a wilderness, lonely and empty, but full of trees
28:2 and of plants grown from seed. Water gushed out from above onto it.
28:3 It looked like a great stream flowing toward the northwest, and from every side water and dew rose up.
29:1 I went to another place away from the desert, and I came near the east side of that mountain range.
29:2 There I saw trees of judgment, giving out above all the scent of frankincense and myrrh. The trees were not like one another.
30:1 Beyond these, past the eastern mountain and not far off, I saw another place: valleys of water that never ran dry.
30:2 I saw a beautiful tree there, with a scent like mastic.
30:3 Along the sides of those valleys I saw fragrant cinnamon. And I went on past these toward the east.
31:1 I saw another mountain with trees on it. Water flowed out, and something like nectar flowed from it, called sarira and galbanum.
31:2 Beyond that mountain I saw another mountain, with aloe trees on it. Those trees were full of something like almonds, and it was hard.
31:3 When people take that fruit, it smells sweeter than any perfume.
32:1 After these scents, as I looked north over the mountains, I saw seven mountains full of fine nard, fragrant trees, cinnamon and pepper.
32:2 From there I went over the tops of all those mountains, far toward the east. I passed over the Red Sea and went far beyond it, and I passed over the angel Zotiel.
## The Garden of Righteousness
32:3 I came to the Garden of Righteousness. Beyond those trees I saw many great trees growing there, with a good scent, large, very beautiful and glorious. There was the tree of wisdom: those who eat from it come to know great wisdom.
32:4 It looks like a carob tree. Its fruit is like the clusters of a vine, very beautiful, and the scent of that tree spreads far.
32:5 I said, "How beautiful this tree is, and how lovely and pleasing to look at!"
32:6 The holy angel Raphael, who was with me, answered me, "This is the tree of wisdom. Your father of long ago and your mother of long ago, who lived before you, ate from it. They learned wisdom, their eyes were opened, and they knew that they were naked. Then they were driven out of the garden."
## The gates of heaven
33:1 From there I went to the ends of the earth. There I saw great beasts, each one different from the others. There were birds too, different in looks, in beauty and in voice, each one different from the others.
33:2 East of those beasts I saw the ends of the earth, where the sky rests, and the gates of heaven were open.
33:3 I saw how the stars of heaven come out. I counted the gates they come out of, and I wrote down all their exits, for each star by itself. I wrote their number and their names, their order, their positions, their times and their months, as the angel Uriel, who was with me, showed me.
33:4 He showed me everything and wrote it down. He also wrote down for me their names, their laws and their duties.
34:1 From there I went north, to the ends of the earth, and there I saw a great and glorious wonder at the ends of the whole earth.
34:2 There I saw three gates of heaven open in the sky. North winds come out through each of them. When they blow, there is cold, hail, frost, snow, dew and rain.
34:3 From one gate the wind blows for good. But when they blow through the other two gates, they blow with force, and hardship comes on the earth.
35:1 From there I went west, to the ends of the earth. There I saw three open gates, as I had seen in the east: the same number of gates and the same number of exits.
36:1 From there I went south, to the ends of the earth. There I saw three open gates of heaven, and from them come the south wind, dew, rain and wind.
36:2 From there I went east, to the ends of the sky. There I saw three gates of heaven open toward the east, with small gates above them.
36:3 The stars of heaven pass through each of those small gates and travel west along the path that has been shown to them.
## Enoch blesses the Lord
36:4 Whenever I saw this, I blessed him, and at all times I bless the Lord of glory, who has done great and glorious wonders. / He did them to show the greatness of his work to his angels and to the souls of human beings, so that they would praise his work and all his creation. / They would see the work of his power, praise the great work of his hands, and bless him forever.
