## The first dream: the earth falls
83:1 And now, my son Methuselah, I will show you all the visions I saw, telling them to you.
83:2 I saw two visions before I married, and the two were not alike. I saw the first when I was learning to write. I saw the other, a terrible vision, before I married your mother. And I pleaded with the Lord about them.
83:3 I was lying down in the house of my grandfather Mahalalel. In a vision I saw the sky being thrown down. It was torn loose, and it fell onto the earth.
83:4 When it fell onto the earth, I saw the earth swallowed up in a great abyss. Mountains hung on top of mountains, and hills sank down onto hills. Tall trees were cut off at their trunks, thrown down, and sunk in the abyss.
83:5 Then a word fell into my mouth. I raised my voice and cried out, "The earth is destroyed!"
83:6 I was lying beside my grandfather Mahalalel, and he woke me. He said to me, "Why are you crying out like this, my son? Why are you wailing like this?"
83:7 I told him the whole vision I had seen. He said to me, "You have seen something terrible, my son. Your dream is a powerful one. It is about the hidden things of all the sin of the earth. The earth is going to sink into the abyss and be destroyed with a great destruction.
83:8 So now, my son, get up and plead with the Lord of glory, since you are a man of faith. Ask that a remnant may be left on the earth, and that he will not wipe out the whole earth.
83:9 My son, all this will come from heaven onto the earth, and there will be a great destruction on the earth."
83:10 Then I got up. I prayed and pleaded, and I wrote down my prayer for the generations of the world. I will show you all of it, my son Methuselah.
83:11 When I went outside and down, I saw the sky, the sun rising in the east and the moon setting in the west, a few stars, and the whole earth, everything as he had known it from the beginning. Then I blessed the Lord of judgment and praised his greatness. For he brings the sun out of the windows of the east. It climbs and rises across the face of the sky, and sets out, and goes along the path it was shown.
## Enoch's prayer
84:1 I lifted up my hands in righteousness and blessed the Holy Great One. I spoke with the breath of my mouth and with a tongue of flesh, which God made for human beings to speak with. He gave them breath, a tongue and a mouth so that they could speak:
84:2 "Blessed are you, Lord, King, great and mighty in your greatness, / Lord of all the creation of heaven, / King of kings and God of the whole world. / Your rule, your kingdom and your greatness last forever and ever, / and your power through every generation. / All the heavens are your throne forever, / and the whole earth is the footstool for your feet forever and ever.
84:3 For you made all things, and you rule over all things. / No deed is too hard for you, not even one. / Not one bit of wisdom leaves you; / it does not turn away from the place of your throne or from your presence. / You know and see and hear everything, / and nothing is hidden from you, for you see everything.
84:4 And now the angels of your heavens are doing wrong, / and your anger rests on human flesh until the great day of judgment.
84:5 And now, God and Lord and great King, I plead with you and ask you to grant my request: / leave me descendants on the earth. / Do not wipe out all human flesh / and leave the earth empty, / so that the destruction lasts forever.
84:6 And now, my Lord, wipe out from the earth the flesh that has made you angry. / But the flesh of righteousness and honesty, set it up as a plant that grows from seed forever. / Do not hide your face from the prayer of your servant, Lord."
## The second dream: Adam and his sons
85:1 After this I saw another dream, and I will show you all of it, my son.
85:2 Enoch raised his voice and spoke to his son Methuselah: "I am speaking to you, my son. Listen to my words, and turn your ear to the dream your father saw.
85:3 Before I married your mother Edna, I saw a vision on my bed. A bull came out of the earth, and that bull was white. After it a young cow came out, and with her came two more calves. One of them was black, and one was red.
85:4 The black calf gored the red one and chased him across the earth. From then on I could no longer see that red calf.
85:5 That black calf grew up, and that young cow came with him. I saw many cattle come from him. They looked like him and followed after him.
85:6 And that first cow went away from that first bull to search for the red calf. She did not find him, and she wailed over him bitterly and kept searching for him.
85:7 I watched until that first bull came to her and calmed her. From that time on she did not cry out.
85:8 After that she gave birth to another white bull. After him she gave birth to many cattle and black calves.
85:9 In my sleep I saw that white bull. He grew in the same way and became a great white bull. Many white cattle came from him, and they looked like him.
85:10 They began to father many white cattle that looked like them, one after another.
## The stars that fell
86:1 Again I looked with my eyes as I slept. I saw the sky above, and a single star fell from the sky. It got up and ate and grazed among those cattle.
86:2 After this I saw large and black cattle. All of them changed their stalls, their pastures and their calves, and they began to moan, one with another.
86:3 Again I saw in the vision. I looked up at the sky and saw many stars come down. They threw themselves out of the sky to that first star. They were among those calves and cattle, grazing with them, in among them.
86:4 I watched them, and I saw them all bring out their private parts like horses. They began to mount the cows of the cattle. All the cows became pregnant and gave birth to elephants, camels and donkeys.
86:5 All the cattle were afraid of them and terrified. They began to bite with their teeth, to swallow, and to gore with their horns.
86:6 Then they began to eat those cattle. And all the children of the earth began to tremble and shake in front of them and to run away from them.
87:1 Again I saw them, and they began to gore each other and swallow each other, and the earth began to cry out.
## The seven white ones
87:2 I raised my eyes again to the sky, and I saw in the vision beings who looked like white men coming out of the sky. Four came out of that place, and three with them.
87:3 The three who came out last took me by the hand and lifted me away from the generations of the earth. They raised me up onto a high place and showed me a tower high above the earth. All the hills were low beside it.
87:4 They said to me, "Stay here until you see everything that happens to those elephants, camels and donkeys, and to the stars, and to all the cattle."
88:1 I saw one of the four who had come out first. He seized that first star, the one that fell from the sky, tied its hands and feet, and threw it into an abyss. That abyss was narrow and deep, harsh and dark.
88:2 One of them drew his sword and gave it to those elephants, camels and donkeys. They began to strike each other, and the whole earth shook because of them.
88:3 As I watched in the vision, one of the four who had come out threw stones from the sky. He gathered and took all the great stars whose private parts were like those of horses. He tied all of them by their hands and feet and threw them into a crack in the earth.
## Noah and the Flood
89:1 One of those four went to that white bull and taught him a secret, while he trembled. That bull was born, and he became a man. He built himself a great boat and lived on it. Three cattle lived with him in that boat, and it was covered over them.
89:2 Again I raised my eyes toward the sky. I saw a high roof with seven torrents on it. Those torrents poured a great deal of water into an enclosure.
89:3 I looked again, and springs opened on the ground in that great enclosure. That water began to bubble up and rise over the ground. I kept watching that enclosure until all its ground was covered with water.
89:4 Water, darkness and mist kept growing over it. I watched the height of that water. It rose higher than that enclosure, poured over the top of it, and stood on the earth.
89:5 All the cattle of that enclosure crowded together, until I saw them sinking, swallowed up and dying in that water.
89:6 But that boat floated on the water. All the cattle, elephants, camels and donkeys sank to the bottom, with all the animals, and I could not see them anymore. They could not get away. They died and sank into the deep.
89:7 Again I saw in the vision, until those torrents were taken away from that high roof. The cracks in the earth were leveled off, and other deep places opened.
89:8 The water began to go down into them until the earth showed again. That boat settled on the ground. The darkness pulled back, and there was light.
89:9 That white bull who had become a man came out of that boat, and the three cattle with him. One of the three was white like that bull, one of them was red like blood, and one was black. Then that white bull left them.
## Abraham to the Exodus
89:10 They began to father wild animals and birds. From all of them came nations of many kinds: lions, leopards, dogs, wolves, hyenas, wild boars, foxes, rock badgers, pigs, falcons, vultures, kites, eagles and ravens. And among them a white bull was born.
89:11 They began to bite one another. And the white bull who was born among them fathered a wild donkey and a white bull with it. The wild donkeys grew many.
89:12 The bull who was born from him fathered a black wild boar and a white sheep. The boar fathered many boars, and that sheep fathered twelve sheep.
89:13 When those twelve sheep had grown, they handed one of them over to the donkeys. Those donkeys in turn handed that sheep over to the wolves, and that sheep grew up among the wolves.
89:14 The Lord brought the eleven sheep to live with it and graze with it among the wolves. They grew in number and became many flocks of sheep.
89:15 Then the wolves began to be afraid of them. They crushed them until they did away with their young ones, and threw their young into a river of deep water. Those sheep began to cry out over their young and to complain to their Lord.
89:16 A sheep who escaped from the wolves ran away and went over to the wild donkeys. And I saw the sheep wailing and crying out and begging their Lord with all their strength. At last the Lord of the sheep came down at the sound of the sheep, from a high dwelling. He came to them and looked after them.
89:17 He called the sheep who had escaped from the wolves. He spoke with it about the wolves, telling it to warn them not to touch the sheep.
89:18 So the sheep went to the wolves at the Lord's word. Another sheep met that sheep and went with it. The two of them went together into the meeting of those wolves. They spoke to them and warned them not to touch the sheep from then on.
89:19 After that I saw how the wolves treated the sheep even more harshly, with all their strength, and the sheep cried out.
89:20 Their Lord came to the sheep and began to strike those wolves. The wolves began to wail, but the sheep grew quiet and from then on did not cry out.
89:21 I watched the sheep until they went out from among the wolves. The wolves' eyes were blinded, and those wolves went out after the sheep with all their strength.
89:22 The Lord of the sheep went with them, leading them, and all his sheep followed him. His face was shining and glorious, and he was awesome to look at.
89:23 But the wolves began to chase those sheep, until they caught up with them at a sea of water.
89:24 That sea split apart, and the water stood up on this side and on that side in front of them. Their Lord, who was leading them, stood between them and the wolves.
89:25 Those wolves still did not see the sheep. The sheep went into the middle of that sea, and the wolves followed them. Those wolves ran after them into that sea.
89:26 When they saw the Lord of the sheep, they turned to run away from his face. But the sea came together and became as it had been created. The water filled up and rose until it covered those wolves.
89:27 I watched until all the wolves who had chased those sheep were dead and had sunk.
## The wilderness and the land
89:28 The sheep got away from that water and went out into a wilderness where there was no water and no grass. They began to open their eyes and to see. I saw the Lord of the sheep looking after them and giving them water and grass, and that sheep going ahead and leading them.
89:29 That sheep went up to the top of that high rock, and the Lord of the sheep sent it to them.
89:30 After that I saw the Lord of the sheep standing in front of them. He was awesome and mighty to look at. All those sheep saw him and were afraid of his face.
89:31 All of them were afraid and shook because of him. They cried out to the sheep with him, the other sheep who was among them, "We cannot stand in front of our Lord or look at him!"
89:32 The sheep who led them went back up to the top of that rock. Then the sheep began to grow blind and to wander from the path he had shown them, and that sheep did not know it.
89:33 The Lord of the sheep was very angry with them. That sheep found out, came down from the top of the rock, and came to the sheep. He found that most of them had been blinded and had wandered from his path.
89:34 When they saw him, they were afraid and shook in front of him, and they wanted to go back to their own folds.
89:35 That sheep took other sheep with him and came to the sheep who had wandered off. He began to kill them, and the sheep were afraid of him. So that sheep brought back the sheep who had wandered, and they went back into their folds.
89:36 In this vision I watched until that sheep became a man. He built a house for the Lord of the sheep and set all the sheep in that house.
89:37 I watched until the sheep who had met the sheep that led them fell asleep. I watched until all the large sheep were dead and small ones rose up in their place. They came into a pasture and drew near a river.
89:38 The sheep who led them, who had become a man, went away from them and fell asleep. All the sheep searched for him and cried over him with loud crying.
89:39 I watched until they stopped crying for that sheep and crossed that river. And there rose up sheep to lead them, all of them in place of those who had fallen asleep, and they led them.
89:40 I watched the sheep until they came into a good place, a pleasant and glorious land. I watched those sheep until they had eaten their fill. And that house was among them in the pleasant land.
## Judges and kings
89:41 Sometimes their eyes were open, and sometimes they were blind, until another sheep arose. It led them and brought them all back, and their eyes were opened.
89:42 Then dogs, foxes and wild boars began to eat those sheep, until the Lord of the sheep raised up another sheep from among them, a ram, who led them.
89:43 That ram began to butt those dogs, foxes and wild boars on this side and that, until he had destroyed them all.
89:44 And that sheep whose eyes were opened saw that ram among the sheep. The ram gave up his glory. He began to butt those sheep and trample them, and he behaved in a shameful way.
89:45 The Lord of the sheep sent the sheep to another sheep and raised that one up to be a ram and to lead the sheep in place of the one who had given up his glory.
89:46 It went to him and spoke to him alone. It raised that ram up and made him ruler and leader of the sheep. Through all this, those dogs kept crushing the sheep.
89:47 The first ram chased the second ram, and the second ram got up and ran away from him. And I watched until those dogs brought the first ram down.
89:48 That second ram rose up and led the little sheep. That ram fathered many sheep and fell asleep. Then a little sheep became ram in his place, and he became ruler and leader of those sheep.
89:49 Those sheep grew and multiplied. All the dogs, foxes and wild boars were afraid and ran from him. That ram butted and killed all the wild animals, and those animals had no power among the sheep anymore. They did not steal anything at all from them.
89:50 That house became large and wide, and it was built for those sheep. A high tower was built on that house for the Lord of the sheep. The house was low, but the tower rose up and was high. The Lord of the sheep stood on that tower, and they set a full table before him.
## Prophets, and the house left behind
89:51 Again I saw those sheep wander off once more. They went many different ways and left that house of theirs. The Lord of the sheep called some sheep from among them and sent them to the sheep, but the sheep began to kill them.
89:52 One of them was rescued and was not killed. It sprang away and cried out against the sheep, and they wanted to kill it. But the Lord of the sheep rescued it from the sheep's hands. He brought it up to me and had it stay here.
89:53 He sent many other sheep to those sheep, to warn them and to grieve over them.
89:54 After this I saw that when they left the house of the Lord of the sheep and his tower, they wandered off in everything, and their eyes were blinded. And I saw how the Lord of the sheep brought great slaughter on them in their flocks, until those sheep called that slaughter down on themselves and betrayed his place.
89:55 He left them in the hands of lions, leopards, wolves and hyenas, in the hands of foxes, and to all the wild animals. Those wild animals began to tear those sheep apart.
89:56 I saw that he left that house of theirs and their tower. He threw all of them into the hands of the lions, to tear them apart and eat them, into the hands of all the wild animals.
89:57 Then I began to cry out with all my strength. I called to the Lord of the sheep and pointed out to him that the sheep were being eaten by all the wild animals.
89:58 But he stayed silent, though he saw it. He was glad that they were eaten, swallowed and carried off, and he left them in the hands of all the animals to be eaten.
## The seventy shepherds
89:59 He called seventy shepherds and threw those sheep to them to look after them. He said to the shepherds and their companions, "From now on each one of you is to look after the sheep. Do everything I command you.
89:60 I am handing them over to you counted. I will tell you which of them are to be destroyed, and you destroy those." And he handed those sheep over to them.
89:61 He called another one and said to him, "Watch carefully and see everything the shepherds do to these sheep. For they will destroy more of them than I have commanded them.
89:62 Write down every excess and every destruction the shepherds carry out: how many they destroy at my command, and how many they destroy on their own. Write down against each shepherd all the destruction he does.
89:63 Read out to me by number how many they destroy on their own and how many they hand over to be destroyed. This will be my evidence against them. Then I will know everything the shepherds do, so that I may hand them over, and I will see what they do, whether they keep the command I gave them or not.
89:64 Do not let them know. Do not show it to them or warn them. Just write down all the destruction done by the shepherds, each one in his own time, and bring all of it up to me."
89:65 I watched as those shepherds looked after the sheep, each in his time. They began to kill and destroy more than they were ordered to, and they left those sheep in the hands of the lions.
89:66 The lions and leopards ate and swallowed most of those sheep, and the wild boars ate with them. They burned that tower and tore down that house.
89:67 I grieved very deeply over the tower, because that house of the sheep had been torn down. After that I could not see those sheep, whether they went into that house or not.
89:68 The shepherds and their companions handed those sheep over to all the wild animals to eat them. Each of them received a counted number in his time. And for each of them, the other one wrote in a book how many of them he destroyed.
89:69 Each one killed and destroyed far more than he was assigned. And I began to weep and grieve very much over those sheep.
89:70 So in the vision I saw the one who was writing. I saw how he wrote down each one that those shepherds destroyed, every day. He brought the book up, laid it down and showed all of it to the Lord of the sheep: all they had done, all that each of them had done away with, and all that they had handed over to be destroyed.
89:71 The book was read out in front of the Lord of the sheep. He took the book in his hand, read it, sealed it and laid it down.
89:72 After this I saw the shepherds looking after the sheep for twelve hours. Then three of those sheep turned back. They came and went in and began to build up everything that had fallen down from that house. The wild boars tried to stop them, but they could not.
89:73 They began to build again as before. They raised up that tower, and it was called the high tower. They began again to set a table in front of the tower, but all the bread on it was defiled and not pure.
89:74 On top of all this, the eyes of those sheep were blinded, and they did not see. Their shepherds were the same. They handed many of them over to their shepherds to be destroyed, and they trampled the sheep with their feet and ate them.
89:75 The Lord of the sheep stayed silent until all the sheep were scattered across the open country and mixed in with the animals. And the shepherds did not rescue them from the hands of the animals.
89:76 The one who wrote the book carried it up, showed it and read it out before the dwelling of the Lord of the sheep. He pleaded with him for them and begged him, as he showed him everything their shepherds had done. And he gave evidence before him against all the shepherds.
89:77 Then he took that book, laid it down beside him, and went out.
90:1 I watched until the time when thirty-seven shepherds had looked after the sheep in this way. All of them finished, each in his own time, like the first ones. Then others received the sheep into their hands to look after them, each shepherd in his own time.
## The birds of prey
90:2 After this I saw in the vision all the birds of the sky coming: eagles, vultures, kites and ravens. The eagles were leading all the birds. They began to eat those sheep, to peck out their eyes and to eat their flesh.
90:3 The sheep cried out because the birds were eating their flesh. And I cried out and grieved in my sleep over that shepherd who was looking after the sheep.
90:4 I watched as those sheep were eaten by the dogs, the eagles and the kites. They left them no flesh at all, no skin and no sinew, until only their bones were standing. Then their bones fell to the ground, and the sheep became few.
90:5 I watched until the time when twenty-three shepherds had looked after them. They finished, each in his own time, fifty-eight periods in all.
## The lambs and the great horn
90:6 Then little lambs were born to those white sheep. They began to open their eyes and to see, and to cry out to the sheep.
90:7 But the sheep did not cry back to them. They did not listen to what the lambs told them. They were very deaf, and their eyes were blinded, very badly.
90:8 In the vision I saw how the ravens flew down on those lambs. They seized one of those lambs, and they smashed the sheep to pieces and ate them.
90:9 I watched until horns grew on those lambs, and the ravens knocked their horns down. And I watched until one great horn sprouted on one of those sheep, and their eyes were opened.
90:10 It looked at them, and their eyes were opened. It cried out to the sheep, and the rams saw it, and they all ran to it.
90:11 In spite of all this, those eagles, vultures, ravens and kites kept tearing at the sheep, swooping down on them and eating them. The sheep kept silent, but the rams wailed and cried out.
90:12 Those ravens fought and struggled with it. They wanted to remove its horn, but they could not overpower it.
90:13 I watched them until the shepherds came, with the eagles, those vultures and the kites. They shouted to the ravens to smash the horn of that ram. They struggled and fought with it, and it struggled with them and cried out for its help to come.
90:14 I watched until that man came, the one who wrote down the names of the shepherds and took them up before the Lord of the sheep. He helped it and showed it everything. His help had come down to that ram.
90:15 And I watched until the Lord of the sheep came to them in anger. All who saw him ran away, and all of them fell into his shadow, away from his face.
90:16 The eagles, vultures, ravens and kites gathered together. They brought with them all the sheep of the open country. They all came together and helped each other to smash that ram's horn.
90:17 I saw the man who writes the book at the Lord's command. He opened the book of the destruction that the last twelve shepherds had caused. He showed before the Lord of the sheep that they had destroyed far more than those before them.
## The judgment
90:18 I watched until the Lord of the sheep came to them. He took in his hand the rod of his anger and struck the earth. The earth split open, and all the animals and the birds of the sky fell away from those sheep. They sank into the earth, and it closed over them.
90:19 I watched until a great sword was given to the sheep. The sheep went out against all the wild animals to kill them, and all the animals and the birds of the sky ran away from them.
90:20 I watched until a throne was set up in the pleasant land, and the Lord of the sheep sat on it. The other one took all the sealed books and opened those books before the Lord of the sheep.
90:21 The Lord called those seven first white ones. He commanded them to bring before him those stars whose private parts were like those of horses, starting with the first star, the one that led the way and fell first. And they brought them all before him.
90:22 He spoke to the man who had been writing in front of him, who was one of the seven white ones. He said to him, "Take those seventy shepherds to whom I handed over the sheep. Once they took them, they killed more than I commanded them."
90:23 And I saw them all tied up, and they all stood in front of him.
90:24 The judgment was carried out first on the stars. They were judged and found guilty, and they went to the place of judgment. They were thrown into a deep place full of blazing fire and full of pillars of fire.
90:25 Those seventy shepherds were judged and found guilty, and they too were thrown into that fiery abyss.
90:26 At that time I saw an abyss like that one open up in the middle of the earth, full of fire. They brought those blinded sheep, and all of them were judged and found guilty. They were thrown into that fiery deep, and they burned. That abyss was to the right of that house.
90:27 I saw those sheep burning, and their bones were burning.
## The new house
90:28 I stood and watched until they folded up that old house. They carried out all its pillars, and all the beams and ornaments of that house were folded up with it. They carried it out and put it in a place to the south of the land.
90:29 And I watched until the Lord of the sheep brought a new house, larger and higher than the first one. He set it up in the place of the first one, which had been folded up. All its pillars were new, and its ornaments were new and larger than those of the old first house that he had taken away. And all the sheep were inside it.
90:30 I saw all the sheep that were left, and all the animals on the earth, and all the birds of the sky. They were falling down and bowing to those sheep, pleading with them and obeying them in every word.
90:31 After this, those three who were dressed in white, who had lifted me up before, took me by the hand, with the hand of that ram holding me too. They lifted me up and set me down among those sheep, before the judgment took place.
90:32 Those sheep were all white, and their wool was thick and clean.
90:33 All that had been destroyed and scattered, and all the wild animals and all the birds of the sky, gathered in that house. The Lord of the sheep was full of joy, because they had all become good and had come back to his house.
90:34 I watched until they laid down the sword that had been given to the sheep. They brought it back into his house, and it was sealed up before the Lord's face. All the sheep were invited into that house, but it could not hold them.
90:35 The eyes of all of them were opened, and they saw what is good. There was not one among them who could not see.
90:36 And I saw that that house was large and wide and very full.
## The white bull
90:37 I saw that a white bull was born, with large horns. All the wild animals and all the birds of the sky were afraid of him and pleaded with him all the time.
90:38 And I watched until all their kinds were changed, and they all became white cattle. The first among them became a word, and that word became a great animal with large black horns on its head. The Lord of the sheep rejoiced over them and over all the cattle.
## Enoch wakes and weeps
90:39 I slept among them. Then I woke up, and I had seen everything.
90:40 This is the vision I saw while I slept. I woke up and blessed the Lord of righteousness and gave him glory.
90:41 Then I wept bitterly, and my tears would not stop until I could not bear it. When I looked, they ran down because of what I had seen. For everything will come and be fulfilled, and all the deeds of human beings, each in its turn, were shown to me.
90:42 That night I remembered my first dream, and I wept over it and was shaken, because I had seen that vision.
