## Jeremiah locked up during the siege
32:1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD came in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah. That was Nebuchadnezzar's eighteenth year.
32:2 At that time the army of the king of Babylon was laying siege to Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet was shut up in the courtyard of the guard, which was part of the house of the king of Judah.
32:3 Zedekiah king of Judah had shut him up there and said, "Why do you prophesy like this? You say, 'The LORD says this: Look, I am about to hand this city over to the king of Babylon, and he will capture it.
32:4 Zedekiah king of Judah will not get away from the hand of the Chaldeans. He will certainly be handed over to the king of Babylon, and he will speak with him mouth to mouth, and his eyes will see his eyes.
32:5 He will take Zedekiah to Babylon, and Zedekiah will stay there until I turn my attention to him, the LORD declares. If you fight the Chaldeans, you will not succeed.'"
## The field at Anathoth
32:6 Jeremiah said, "The word of the LORD came to me:
32:7 'Look, Hanamel son of Shallum, your uncle, is about to come to you and say, "Buy my field at Anathoth for yourself, because the right of redemption is yours: you can buy it."'
32:8 Then Hanamel, my uncle's son, came to me in the courtyard of the guard, just as the LORD had said. He said to me, 'Please buy my field at Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin. The right of possession is yours, and the right of redemption is yours. Buy it for yourself.' Then I knew that this was the word of the LORD.
32:9 So I bought the field at Anathoth from Hanamel, my uncle's son, and I weighed out the silver for him: seventeen shekels of silver.
32:10 I wrote it down in a document and sealed it. I called in witnesses, and I weighed the silver on a balance.
32:11 Then I took the document of purchase. One copy was sealed, with the instructions and the conditions. The other copy was open.
32:12 In the sight of Hanamel my uncle's son, of the witnesses who had signed the document of purchase, and of all the Judeans sitting in the courtyard of the guard, I handed the document to Baruch son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah.
32:13 In front of them all I gave Baruch this order:
32:14 'The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Take these documents, this sealed document of purchase and this open document, and put them in a clay jar so that they will last for many days.
32:15 For the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Houses, fields and vineyards will be bought again in this land.'
32:16 After I had given the document of purchase to Baruch son of Neriah, I prayed to the LORD:
32:17 "Ah, Lord GOD! Look, you made the heavens and the earth by your great power and by your outstretched arm. Nothing at all is too wonderful for you.
32:18 You show loyal love to thousands, and you repay the guilt of the fathers into the laps of their children after them. You are the great and mighty God. The LORD of Armies is your name.
32:19 You are great in counsel and mighty in deeds. Your eyes are open over all the ways of human beings, to give each person what comes from his ways and from the fruit of his deeds.
32:20 You set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, and until today, in Israel and among humankind. You made a name for yourself, as it is today.
32:21 You brought your people Israel out of the land of Egypt with signs and wonders, with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, and with great terror.
32:22 You gave them this land that you swore to their ancestors to give them, a land flowing with milk and honey.
32:23 They came and took possession of it. But they did not listen to your voice, and they did not walk in your law. Nothing you commanded them to do did they do. So you made all this disaster happen to them.
32:24 Look, the siege ramps have reached the city to capture it. The city has been handed over to the Chaldeans who are fighting against it, because of the sword, famine and disease. What you spoke has happened, and you are watching it.
32:25 And yet you, Lord GOD, have said to me, 'Buy the field with silver, and call in witnesses,' though the city has been handed over to the Chaldeans."
32:26 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
32:27 "Look, I am the LORD, the God of all flesh. Is anything too wonderful for me?
32:28 Therefore the LORD says this: Look, I am about to hand this city over to the Chaldeans and to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will capture it.
32:29 The Chaldeans who are fighting against this city will come in and set it on fire. They will burn it down, together with the houses on whose roofs people made smoke for Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods, to make me angry.
32:30 For the people of Israel and the people of Judah have done nothing but evil in my eyes since their youth. The people of Israel have done nothing but make me angry with the work of their hands, the LORD declares.
32:31 This city has stirred up my anger and my rage from the day they built it until today, so that I will remove it from my sight.
32:32 I will do this because of all the evil of the people of Israel and the people of Judah, which they did to make me angry: they, their kings, their officials, their priests and their prophets, the people of Judah and those living in Jerusalem.
32:33 They turned to me with their backs, not their faces. I taught them, getting up early to teach, but they would not listen and take correction.
32:34 They put their detestable things in the house that is called by my name, and made it unclean.
32:35 They built the high places of Baal in the Valley of Ben-hinnom, to make their sons and their daughters pass over to Molech. I never commanded this, and it never came into my heart that they would do this abhorrent thing and so make Judah sin.
32:36 And now, therefore, the LORD, the God of Israel, says this about this city, of which you say, 'It has been handed over to the king of Babylon by sword, famine and disease':
32:37 Look, I am gathering them from all the lands where I drove them, in my anger, my rage and my great wrath. I will bring them back to this place, and I will let them live in safety.
32:38 They will become my people, and I will become their God.
32:39 I will give them one heart and one way, so that they fear me all their days, for their good and for the good of their children after them.
32:40 I will cut an everlasting covenant with them: I will not turn back from following them to do them good. I will put the fear of me in their hearts, so that they do not turn aside from me.
32:41 I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in faithfulness, with all my heart and all my being.
32:42 For the LORD says this: Just as I brought all this great disaster on this people, so I will bring on them all the good that I am announcing to them.
32:43 Fields will be bought in this land, the land of which you say, 'It is a ruin, with no human being or animal in it. It has been handed over to the Chaldeans.'
32:44 People will buy fields with silver. They will write the documents, seal them and call in witnesses, in the land of Benjamin, in the area around Jerusalem, in the towns of Judah, in the towns of the hill country, in the towns of the Shephelah and in the towns of the Negev. For I will turn their fortunes around, the LORD declares."
## Healing and rebuilding
33:1 The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah a second time, while he was still shut up in the courtyard of the guard:
33:2 "The LORD says this, the LORD who made it, the LORD who formed it to set it firm, the LORD is his name:
33:3 Call to me, and I will answer you. I will tell you great things and hidden things that you do not know.
33:4 For the LORD, the God of Israel, says this about the houses of this city and the houses of the kings of Judah, which were pulled down because of the ramps and because of the sword:
33:5 People are coming to fight the Chaldeans and to fill the houses with the bodies of those I struck down in my anger and my rage, because I hid my face from this city on account of all their evil.
33:6 Look, I am bringing it healing and cure, and I will heal them. I will uncover for them an abundance of peace and faithfulness.
33:7 I will turn the fortunes of Judah and the fortunes of Israel around, and I will rebuild them as they were at first.
33:8 I will cleanse them from all their guilt, the guilt of their sinning against me. I will forgive all their guilt, all the ways they sinned against me and rebelled against me.
33:9 And it will become for me a name of joy, a praise and an honor before all the nations of the earth. They will hear of all the good I am doing for them, and they will be in awe and tremble because of all the good and all the peace I am making for it.
33:10 The LORD says this: Again there will be heard in this place, which you say is a ruin with no human being and no animal, in the towns of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem that lie desolate, with no human being, no one living there and no animal,
33:11 the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the sound of the groom and the sound of the bride, the sound of those who say, "Give thanks to the LORD of Armies, for the LORD is good, for his loyal love is forever," and of those who bring a thank offering to the LORD's house. For I will turn the fortunes of the land around as they were at first, the LORD says.
33:12 The LORD of Armies says this: In this place, which is a ruin with no human being and no animal, and in all its towns, there will again be a pasture for shepherds, where they let their flocks lie down.
33:13 In the towns of the hill country, the towns of the Shephelah, the towns of the Negev, the land of Benjamin, the area around Jerusalem and the towns of Judah, flocks will again pass by the hands of the one who counts them, the LORD says.
33:14 Look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when I will carry out the good word that I spoke to the house of Israel and to the house of Judah.
33:15 In those days and at that time I will make a Branch of righteousness sprout for David, and he will carry out justice and righteousness in the land.
33:16 In those days Judah will be saved, and Jerusalem will settle in security. This is what one will call her: "The LORD is our righteousness."
33:17 For the LORD says this: David will never be without a man sitting on the throne of the house of Israel.
33:18 And the Levite priests will never be without a man before me to offer up a burnt offering, to burn a grain offering and to make a sacrifice, every day."
33:19 The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
33:20 "The LORD says this: If you can break my covenant with the day and my covenant with the night, so that day and night do not come at their proper time,
33:21 then my covenant with David my servant can also be broken, so that he has no son reigning on his throne, and my covenant with the Levites, the priests who serve me.
33:22 Just as the army of the heavens cannot be counted and the sand of the sea cannot be measured, so I will make more and more the offspring of David my servant and the Levites who serve me."
33:23 The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
33:24 "Have you not noticed what this people is saying? 'The two clans that the LORD chose, he has now rejected.' They look down on my people, so that in their eyes it is no longer a nation.
33:25 The LORD says this: If I have not made my covenant with day and night, and fixed the rules of heaven and earth,
33:26 then I might also reject the offspring of Jacob and of David my servant, and not take from his offspring rulers over the offspring of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. But I will turn their fortunes around, and I will have compassion on them."
## A message to Zedekiah
34:1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, with all his army and all the kingdoms of the earth under the rule of his hand, and all the peoples, was fighting against Jerusalem and all its towns:
34:2 "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Go and say to Zedekiah king of Judah, 'The LORD says this: Look, I am about to hand this city over to the king of Babylon, and he will burn it with fire.
34:3 You yourself will not get away from his hand. You will certainly be captured and handed over to him. Your eyes will see the eyes of the king of Babylon, and he will speak with you mouth to mouth, and you will go to Babylon.
34:4 Even so, hear the word of the LORD, Zedekiah king of Judah. The LORD says this about you: You will not die by the sword.
34:5 You will die in peace. As burnings were made for your fathers, the earlier kings who came before you, so people will make a burning for you, and they will mourn for you, "Alas, lord!" For I myself have spoken this word, the LORD declares.'"
34:6 Jeremiah the prophet spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem,
34:7 while the army of the king of Babylon was fighting against Jerusalem and against all the towns of Judah that were left, against Lachish and against Azekah. These were the only fortified towns left among the towns of Judah.
## The slaves freed and taken back
34:8 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after King Zedekiah cut a covenant with all the people in Jerusalem to announce a release for them:
34:9 Each person was to let his male slave and his female slave, the Hebrew man and the Hebrew woman, go free. No one was to make a fellow Judean, his own brother, work as his slave.
34:10 All the officials and all the people who had entered the covenant obeyed: each one let his male slave and his female slave go free, so that no one would make them work as slaves any more. They obeyed and let them go.
34:11 But afterward they turned around again. They took back the male and female slaves they had let go free, and they forced them to be slaves again.
34:12 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah from the LORD:
34:13 "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: I myself cut a covenant with your ancestors on the day I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery. I said:
34:14 'At the end of seven years each of you must let go his brother, the Hebrew who sells himself to you. He is to serve you six years, and then you must send him away from you, free.' But your ancestors did not listen to me, and they did not turn their ear to me.
34:15 Today you turned around and did what is right in my eyes: each of you announced a release to his neighbor. You cut a covenant before me in the house that is called by my name.
34:16 But you turned around again and profaned my name. Each of you took back his male slave and his female slave whom you had let go free, to go where they wished, and you forced them to be your male and female slaves again.
34:17 Therefore the LORD says this: You have not listened to me and announced a release, each to his brother and each to his neighbor. Look, I am announcing a release for you, the LORD declares: to the sword, to the disease and to the famine. I will make you a terror to all the kingdoms of the earth.
34:18 I will make the men who broke my covenant, who did not carry out the words of the covenant they cut before me, into the calf they cut in two and passed between its pieces:
34:19 the officials of Judah and the officials of Jerusalem, the court officials, the priests and all the people of the land who passed between the pieces of the calf.
34:20 I will hand them over to their enemies and to those who seek their lives. Their dead bodies will become food for the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth.
34:21 Zedekiah king of Judah and his officials I will hand over to their enemies, to those who seek their lives, and to the army of the king of Babylon, which has gone away from you.
34:22 Look, I am about to give the command, the LORD declares, and I will bring them back to this city. They will fight against it, capture it and burn it with fire. I will make the towns of Judah a ruin with no one living in them."
## The Rechabites and the wine
35:1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:
35:2 "Go to the house of the Rechabites and speak with them. Bring them to the LORD's house, into one of the rooms, and give them wine to drink."
35:3 So I took Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah, son of Habazziniah, and his brothers and all his sons, and the whole house of the Rechabites.
35:4 I brought them to the LORD's house, to the room of the sons of Hanan son of Igdaliah, the man of God. It was next to the room of the officials, above the room of Maaseiah son of Shallum, the keeper of the threshold.
35:5 Then I set bowls full of wine and cups in front of the men of the house of the Rechabites, and I said to them, "Drink wine."
35:6 But they said, "We do not drink wine. Our ancestor Jonadab son of Rechab gave us this command: 'You must never drink wine, you or your sons.
35:7 You must not build a house, sow seed, plant a vineyard or own one. You must live in tents all your days, so that you will live many days on the land where you live as foreigners.'
35:8 We have listened to the voice of Jonadab son of Rechab, our ancestor, in everything he commanded us: never to drink wine all our days, we, our wives, our sons and our daughters,
35:9 and never to build houses to live in. We own no vineyard or field, and we have no seed.
35:10 We have lived in tents. We have obeyed, and we have done everything our ancestor Jonadab commanded us.
35:11 But when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up into the land, we said, 'Come, let us go to Jerusalem, away from the army of the Chaldeans and the army of Aram.' So we are living in Jerusalem."
35:12 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
35:13 "The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Go and say to the men of Judah and to those living in Jerusalem, 'Will you not accept correction and listen to my words?' the LORD declares.
35:14 The words of Jonadab son of Rechab have been carried out: he commanded his sons not to drink wine, and to this day they have not drunk any, because they obey the command of their ancestor. But I have spoken to you, getting up early to speak, and you have not listened to me.
35:15 I sent you all my servants the prophets, getting up early to send them, and said, 'Please turn back, each of you, from your evil way. Make your deeds good, and do not go after other gods to serve them. Then you will live in the land I gave to you and to your ancestors.' But you did not turn your ear, and you did not listen to me.
35:16 The sons of Jonadab son of Rechab have carried out the command of their ancestor that he gave them, but this people has not listened to me.
35:17 Therefore the LORD, the God of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Look, I am about to bring on Judah and on all those living in Jerusalem all the disaster I spoke against them, because I spoke to them and they did not listen, and I called to them and they did not answer."
35:18 But to the house of the Rechabites Jeremiah said, "The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Because you have listened to the command of your ancestor Jonadab, kept all his commands and done everything he ordered you,
35:19 therefore the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Jonadab son of Rechab will never be without a man standing before me, all the days."
## Jeremiah has Baruch write the scroll
36:1 In the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:
36:2 "Take a scroll for yourself and write on it all the words I have spoken to you about Israel, about Judah and about all the nations, from the day I first spoke to you in the days of Josiah until today.
36:3 Perhaps the house of Judah will hear of all the disaster I am planning to do to them, so that each will turn back from his evil way. Then I will forgive their guilt and their sin."
36:4 So Jeremiah called Baruch son of Neriah, and Baruch wrote on a scroll, from Jeremiah's mouth, all the words the LORD had spoken to him.
36:5 Then Jeremiah gave Baruch an order: "I am barred from going to the LORD's house.
36:6 So you go, and on a fast day read aloud the words of the LORD from the scroll you wrote from my mouth, in the hearing of the people in the LORD's house. Read them also in the hearing of all the people of Judah who come in from their towns.
36:7 Perhaps their plea will fall before the LORD, and each will turn back from his evil way. For great is the anger and the rage that the LORD has spoken against this people."
36:8 Baruch son of Neriah did all that Jeremiah the prophet had commanded him. He read the words of the LORD from the scroll in the LORD's house.
36:9 In the fifth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, in the ninth month, all the people in Jerusalem and all the people who had come to Jerusalem from the towns of Judah proclaimed a fast before the LORD.
36:10 Then Baruch read the words of Jeremiah from the scroll in the LORD's house, in the room of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper courtyard at the entrance of the New Gate of the LORD's house, in the hearing of all the people.
36:11 When Micaiah son of Gemariah, son of Shaphan, heard all the words of the LORD from the scroll,
36:12 he went down to the king's house, to the room of the scribe. All the officials were sitting there: Elishama the scribe, Delaiah son of Shemaiah, Elnathan son of Achbor, Gemariah son of Shaphan, Zedekiah son of Hananiah, and all the other officials.
36:13 Micaiah told them all the words he had heard when Baruch read from the scroll in the hearing of the people.
36:14 Then all the officials sent Jehudi son of Nethaniah, son of Shelemiah, son of Cushi, to say to Baruch, "Take in your hand the scroll you read aloud in the hearing of the people, and come." So Baruch son of Neriah took the scroll in his hand and went to them.
36:15 They said to him, "Sit down, please, and read it in our hearing." So Baruch read it in their hearing.
36:16 When they heard all the words, they turned to one another in fear. They said to Baruch, "We must certainly tell the king all these words."
36:17 They asked Baruch, "Please tell us, how did you write all these words, from his mouth?"
36:18 Baruch told them, "He called out all these words to me from his mouth, and I wrote them in ink on the scroll."
36:19 The officials said to Baruch, "Go, hide yourselves, you and Jeremiah, and let no one find out where you are."
36:20 They went in to the king in the court, after putting the scroll away in the room of Elishama the scribe, and they told the king all these words.
36:21 The king sent Jehudi to get the scroll. He got it from the room of Elishama the scribe and read it aloud in the hearing of the king and of all the officials standing beside the king.
36:22 It was the ninth month, and the king was sitting in the winter house, with a fire burning in the brazier in front of him.
36:23 Whenever Jehudi had read three or four columns, the king tore them off with the scribe's knife and threw them into the fire in the brazier, until the whole scroll was gone in the fire in the brazier.
36:24 The king and all his servants who heard all these words were not afraid, and they did not tear their clothes.
36:25 Elnathan, Delaiah and Gemariah also pleaded with the king not to burn the scroll, but he did not listen to them.
36:26 Instead the king ordered Jerahmeel the king's son, Seraiah son of Azriel and Shelemiah son of Abdeel to seize Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet. But the LORD hid them.
36:27 After the king burned the scroll with the words Baruch had written from Jeremiah's mouth, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
36:28 "Again, take another scroll for yourself, and write on it all the first words that were on the first scroll, which Jehoiakim king of Judah burned.
36:29 And about Jehoiakim king of Judah you are to say: 'The LORD says this: You burned this scroll and said, "Why did you write on it that the king of Babylon will certainly come and destroy this land and wipe out human beings and animals from it?"
36:30 Therefore the LORD says this about Jehoiakim king of Judah: No one belonging to him will sit on David's throne. His corpse will be flung out into the heat of the day and the frost of the night.
36:31 I will punish him, his offspring and his servants for their guilt. I will bring on them, on those living in Jerusalem and on the people of Judah, all the disaster I have spoken against them, because they would not listen.'"
36:32 Then Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah the scribe. Baruch wrote on it, from Jeremiah's mouth, all the words of the scroll that Jehoiakim king of Judah had burned in the fire. And still more words, many like them, were added to them.
## Zedekiah asks Jeremiah to pray
37:1 King Zedekiah son of Josiah took the throne in place of Coniah son of Jehoiakim, whom Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had made king in the land of Judah.
37:2 But neither he nor his servants nor the people of the land listened to the words the LORD spoke through Jeremiah the prophet.
37:3 King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest to Jeremiah the prophet with this message: "Please pray for us to the LORD our God."
37:4 Now Jeremiah was still coming and going among the people, for they had not yet put him in prison.
37:5 The army of Pharaoh had come out of Egypt. The Chaldeans who had Jerusalem under siege heard the news about them, and they pulled away from Jerusalem.
37:6 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet:
37:7 "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: This is what you are to say to the king of Judah, who sent you to me to consult me: Look, Pharaoh's army, which came out to help you, is about to go back to its own land, Egypt.
37:8 The Chaldeans will come back and fight against this city. They will capture it and burn it with fire.
37:9 The LORD says this: Do not fool yourselves by saying, 'The Chaldeans will surely go away from us.' They will not go away.
37:10 Even if you struck down the whole army of the Chaldeans who are fighting you, and only men run through with wounds were left, each in his tent, they would get up and burn this city with fire."
## Jeremiah arrested
37:11 When the Chaldean army had pulled away from Jerusalem on account of Pharaoh's army,
37:12 Jeremiah set out from Jerusalem to go to the land of Benjamin, to claim a share there among the people.
37:13 When he was at the Benjamin Gate, a captain of the guard named Irijah son of Shelemiah, son of Hananiah, was there. He arrested Jeremiah the prophet and said, "You are going over to the Chaldeans!"
37:14 "A lie!" Jeremiah said. "I am not going over to the Chaldeans." But Irijah did not listen to him. He took hold of Jeremiah and led him to the officials.
37:15 The officials were furious with Jeremiah. They struck him and put him in the lockup, the house of Jonathan the scribe, for they had made it into the prison.
37:16 So Jeremiah came into the house of the cistern, into the cells, and stayed there many days.
37:17 Then King Zedekiah sent for him and had him brought out, and the king questioned him privately in his house. "Is there a word from the LORD?" he asked. "There is," Jeremiah said. "You will be handed over to the king of Babylon."
37:18 Jeremiah also said to King Zedekiah, "What sin have I committed against you, or your servants, or this people, that you have put me in prison?
37:19 And where are your prophets who prophesied to you, 'The king of Babylon will not come against you or against this land'?
37:20 And now, please listen, my lord the king. Please let my plea fall before you: do not send me back to the house of Jonathan the scribe, or I will die there."
37:21 King Zedekiah gave an order, and they put Jeremiah in the courtyard of the guard. Every day they gave him a round loaf of bread from the street of the bakers, until all the bread in the city was gone. So Jeremiah stayed in the courtyard of the guard.
## Jeremiah thrown into the cistern
38:1 Shephatiah son of Mattan, Gedaliah son of Pashhur, Jucal son of Shelemiah and Pashhur son of Malkijah heard the words Jeremiah was saying to all the people:
38:2 "The LORD says this: Anyone who stays in this city will die by the sword, by famine and by disease. But anyone who goes out to the Chaldeans will live. His own life will be his loot, and he will live.
38:3 The LORD says this: This city will certainly be handed over to the army of the king of Babylon, and he will capture it."
38:4 The officials said to the king, "Please, let this man be put to death. For this is how he is weakening the hands of the fighting men who are left in this city, and the hands of all the people, by speaking words like these to them. This man is not seeking the welfare of this people, but their harm."
38:5 King Zedekiah said, "Look, he is in your hand. The king is not able to do anything against you."
38:6 So they took Jeremiah and threw him into the cistern of Malkijah the king's son, which was in the courtyard of the guard. They let Jeremiah down with ropes. There was no water in the cistern, only mud, and Jeremiah sank into the mud.
38:7 Ebed-melech the Cushite, a court official in the king's house, heard that they had put Jeremiah into the cistern. The king was sitting in the Benjamin Gate.
38:8 Ebed-melech went out from the king's house and spoke to the king:
38:9 "My lord the king, these men have done evil in everything they have done to Jeremiah the prophet. They threw him into the cistern, and he will die where he is, of hunger, for there is no more bread in the city."
38:10 The king ordered Ebed-melech the Cushite, "Take thirty men under your command from here, and bring Jeremiah the prophet up from the cistern before he dies."
38:11 So Ebed-melech took the men with him and went to the king's house, to a place under the storehouse. He took from there old rags and worn-out clothes and let them down by ropes to Jeremiah in the cistern.
38:12 Ebed-melech the Cushite said to Jeremiah, "Please put these old rags and clothes under your armpits, beneath the ropes." Jeremiah did so.
38:13 They drew Jeremiah out with the ropes and brought him up from the cistern, and he stayed on in the courtyard of the guard.
## Zedekiah questions Jeremiah in secret
38:14 King Zedekiah sent for Jeremiah the prophet and had him brought to the third entrance of the LORD's house. The king said to Jeremiah, "I am going to ask you something. Do not hide a thing from me."
38:15 Jeremiah said to Zedekiah, "If I tell you, will you not certainly put me to death? And if I give you advice, you will not listen to me."
38:16 Then King Zedekiah swore to Jeremiah in secret: "As the LORD lives, who made this life for us, I will not put you to death, and I will not hand you over to these men who seek your life."
38:17 Jeremiah said to Zedekiah, "The LORD, the God of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: If you will go out to the officers of the king of Babylon, you will live. This city will not be burned with fire, and you and your household will live.
38:18 But if you do not go out to the officers of the king of Babylon, this city will be handed over to the Chaldeans. They will burn it with fire, and you yourself will not get away from their hand."
38:19 King Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, "I am afraid of the Judeans who have gone over to the Chaldeans. They may hand me over to them, and they will abuse me."
38:20 "They will not hand you over," Jeremiah said. "Please listen to the voice of the LORD in what I am telling you. Then it will go well with you, and you will live.
38:21 But if you refuse to go out, this is what the LORD has shown me:
38:22 Look, all the women still left in the house of the king of Judah are about to be brought out to the officers of the king of Babylon. And those women will say: 'The men at peace with you have misled you and overpowered you. Your feet have sunk in the mud, and they have turned back.'
38:23 They will bring out all your wives and your children to the Chaldeans. You yourself will not get away from their hand. You will be seized by the hand of the king of Babylon, and this city will burn with fire."
38:24 Zedekiah said to Jeremiah, "Let no one know about these words, and you will not die.
38:25 If the officials hear that I have spoken with you, and they come to you and say, 'Please tell us what you said to the king. Do not hide it from us, and we will not put you to death. And what did the king say to you?'
38:26 then you are to say to them, 'I was letting my plea fall before the king, that he would not send me back to the house of Jonathan to die there.'"
38:27 All the officials came to Jeremiah and questioned him. He told them just as the king had commanded him. Then they said nothing more to him, for the matter had not been overheard.
