## The heading
1:1 The words of Jeremiah, the son of Hilkiah, from among the priests who lived at Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin.
1:2 It came to him in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his rule.
1:3 It also came in the days of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, and went on until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah son of Josiah, king of Judah, until Jerusalem went into exile in the fifth month.
## The call
1:4 The word of the LORD came to me:
1:5 "Before I shaped you in the belly, I knew you; / before you came out of the womb, I made you holy. / I gave you to the nations as a prophet."
1:6 I said, "Oh, Lord GOD! I do not know how to speak. I am just a boy."
1:7 The LORD said to me, "Do not say, 'I am a boy.' You will go to every person I send you to, and you will speak all that I command you.
1:8 Have no fear of them. I am with you to pull you free, the LORD declares."
1:9 Then the LORD stretched out his hand and touched my mouth. And the LORD said to me, "Look, I have set my words in your mouth.
1:10 Look, this day I am putting you in charge of the nations and the kingdoms: to pull up and pull down, to destroy and demolish, to build and to plant."
## The almond branch and the boiling pot
1:11 The word of the LORD came to me: "What do you see, Jeremiah?" I said, "I see an almond branch."
1:12 The LORD said to me, "You did well to see it, for I am watching over my word to do it."
1:13 The word of the LORD came to me a second time: "What do you see?" I said, "I see a pot with the fire fanned under it, and its face is turned away from the north."
1:14 The LORD said to me, "From the north the disaster will burst open on all the people living in this land.
1:15 For look, I am calling all the clans of the northern kingdoms, the LORD declares. They will come, and each one will set his throne at the opening of Jerusalem's gates, and against all its walls on every side, and against all the cities of Judah.
1:16 And I will speak my rulings against them for all their wickedness: they have left me, they have sent up smoke to other gods, and they have bowed down to the work of their own hands.
## A city with walls of bronze
1:17 But you, tie your belt tight, stand up, and tell them everything I command you. Do not break down before them, or I will break you down in front of them.
1:18 As for me, look, today I have made you a city with strong walls, an iron pillar, and walls of bronze against the whole land: against the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests and the people of the land.
1:19 They will make war on you, but they will not beat you, for I am with you, the LORD declares, to pull you free."
## Remembering the bride
2:1 The word of the LORD came to me:
2:2 Go and call out in the ears of Jerusalem. / The LORD says this: / I remember the loyal love of your youth, / the love of your engagement, / how you walked after me in the wilderness, / in a land where nothing was sown.
2:3 Israel was a holy thing for the LORD, / the first of his produce. / All who devour it are held guilty; / disaster comes on them, the LORD declares.
## What wrong did your ancestors find?
2:4 Hear the word of the LORD, house of Jacob, and all the clans of the house of Israel.
2:5 The LORD says this: / What fault did your ancestors find in me? / Why did they move far away from me, / walk after emptiness, / and become empty themselves?
2:6 They never asked, "Where is the LORD, / who brought us up from the land of Egypt, / who led us through the wilderness, / through a land of barren plain and pit, / a land of parched ground and deep darkness, / a land no one has traveled / and no person has settled?"
2:7 I brought you to a land of orchards, / to eat its fruit and its good things. / But you came and made my land unclean, / and my own inheritance you made an abhorrent thing.
2:8 The priests never asked, "Where is the LORD?" / Those who held the law in their hands did not know me. / The shepherds rebelled against me, / and the prophets prophesied by Baal / and went after what cannot help them.
2:9 So once more I will argue my case with you, the LORD declares, / and with your grandchildren I will argue it.
2:10 Cross over to the shores of Cyprus and look. / Send to Kedar and think hard. / See whether anything like this has ever happened.
2:11 Has any nation traded its gods, / though they are not gods at all? / But my people have traded their Glory / for something that cannot help.
2:12 Be stunned at this, you heavens! / Shake with horror and be left empty, the LORD declares.
## Cisterns that leak
2:13 My people have done two wrongs: / me they have left, the spring of living water, / to dig themselves cisterns, / cisterns full of cracks / that do not hold the water.
2:14 Is Israel a slave, or one born in the house? / Why has he become loot?
2:15 Over him young lions have roared, / they have raised their voices. / They have turned his land into a wasteland; / his towns are burned, with no one living in them.
2:16 Even the people of Memphis and Tahpanhes / will crush your skull.
2:17 Did you not do this to yourself, / by leaving the LORD your God / while he was leading you on the road?
2:18 And now, what good is the road to Egypt to you, / to drink the water of the Shihor? / And what good is the road to Assyria to you, / to drink the water of the River?
2:19 Your own evil will discipline you, / and your turnings away will accuse you. / Know, then, and see: / it is bad and bitter to leave the LORD your God, / to have no fear of me in you, / the Lord GOD of Armies declares.
## A vine gone bad
2:20 Long ago I broke your yoke, / I tore off your straps, / and you said, "I will not serve." / For on every lofty hill / and under every leafy tree / you were sprawled out, a prostitute.
2:21 But I planted you as a choice vine, / all of it seed that could be trusted. / How then did you change yourself for me / into the spoiled shoots of a foreign vine?
2:22 You may scrub with lye, / you may stock up on soap, / but your guilt is still a stain in front of me, / the Lord GOD declares.
2:23 How can you say, "I have not made myself unclean, / I have not walked after the Baals"? / Consider your path in the valley; / admit what you have done, / a swift young she-camel / tangling up her own paths,
2:24 a wild donkey at home in the wilderness, / gulping the wind in her craving. / In her heat, who can turn her back? / None who look for her need wear themselves out; / in her month they will find her.
2:25 Hold your foot back from going barefoot / and your throat from thirst. / But you said, "It is no use! / I love strangers, / and I will go after them."
2:26 A thief feels shame once he is found out; / that is how the house of Israel has been put to shame, / they, their kings, their officials, / their priests and their prophets.
2:27 They say to a piece of wood, "You are my father," / and to a stone, "You gave birth to us." / They have shown me the back of the neck, / not the face. / Yet in their time of disaster they say, / "Get up and save us!"
2:28 And where are your gods, / the ones you made for yourself? / Let them stand up, if they can save you / in your time of disaster. / For the number of your cities / is the number of your gods, Judah.
## The case against Judah
2:29 Why do you argue your case with me? / All of you have rebelled against me, the LORD declares.
2:30 I struck your children for nothing; / they did not learn the lesson. / Your own sword has eaten your prophets / like a lion that destroys.
2:31 You people of this generation, consider the word of the LORD. / Have I been a wilderness to Israel, / or a land of deep darkness? / Why do my people say, "We wander where we please; / we will not come to you anymore"?
2:32 Does a young woman forget her ornaments, / or a bride her sashes? / Yet my people have forgotten me / for days beyond counting.
2:33 How well you set up your road / in your search for love! / So even wicked women / have learned your ways from you.
2:34 Even on your skirts is found / the blood of poor, innocent people. / You did not catch them tunneling in. / But there is all of this,
2:35 and you say, "I am innocent. / His anger has surely turned away from me." / Look, I am taking you to court / for saying, "I have not sinned."
2:36 Why do you take your road so lightly, / changing it again and again? / Egypt will shame you / as Assyria shamed you.
2:37 You will leave this one too / with your hands on your head, / for the LORD has thrown away the ones you trust, / and you will get no good from them.
## A husband who takes his wife back?
3:1 Suppose a man sends his wife away, / and she walks out on him / and belongs to another man. / Can he go back to her again? / Would that land not be completely polluted? / But you have been a prostitute with many companions. / Yet return to me, the LORD declares.
3:2 Raise your eyes to the bare hilltops and see. / Where have you not been lain with? / By the roads you sat waiting for them / like a nomad in the wilderness. / You polluted the land / with your prostitution and your evil.
3:3 So the showers were held back, / and no spring rain came. / But you kept a prostitute's forehead / and refused to feel shame.
3:4 Did you not call to me just now, / "You are my father, the dearest friend of my young days.
3:5 Will he hold a grudge forever? / Will he keep it to the end?" / Look, that is what you say, / and you keep doing the evil things, and you get your way.
## Two sisters
3:6 The LORD said to me in the days of King Josiah: Have you seen what Israel, who turned away, has done? She went up on every high hill and under every leafy tree, and there she acted as a prostitute.
3:7 I thought, after she had done all these things, "She will return to me." She did not return. Her treacherous sister Judah watched it happen.
3:8 I saw that because of every act of adultery by Israel, who turned away, I had sent her away and had given her a certificate of divorce. Even so, her treacherous sister Judah was not afraid. She went and acted as a prostitute too.
3:9 She treated her prostitution as a small thing, and it polluted the land. She committed adultery with stone and with wood.
3:10 Yet even after all this, Judah, her treacherous sister, did not turn back to me with all her heart. She did it only with a lie, the LORD declares.
3:11 The LORD said to me: Israel, who turned away, has shown herself more in the right than treacherous Judah.
## Turn back, you who turned away
3:12 Go and call out these words toward the north. / Say: Turn back, Israel, who turned away, the LORD declares. / I will not let my face fall in anger on you, / for I am loyal, the LORD declares; / I will not hold a grudge forever.
3:13 Only admit your guilt: / you rebelled against the LORD your God, / you scattered your ways about to strangers / under every leafy tree, / and you did not listen to my voice, the LORD declares.
3:14 Turn back, children who turn away, the LORD declares, for I am your husband. I will take you, one from a town and two from a clan, and bring you to Zion.
3:15 I will give you shepherds who are like my own heart, and they will tend you with knowledge and insight.
3:16 In those days, when you have grown many and fruitful in the land, the LORD declares, people will not say any more, "The ark of the covenant of the LORD." It will not rise up in anyone's mind. No one will remember it or miss it, and no other one will be made.
3:17 At that time people will call Jerusalem the LORD's throne, and all the nations will gather to it, to the name of the LORD, to Jerusalem. They will never again walk after the stubbornness of their evil hearts.
3:18 In those days the house of Judah will walk beside the house of Israel. Together they will come out of a northern land to the land I gave your ancestors as an inheritance.
## Come home
3:19 I said to myself, / "How I will set you among the children / and give you a land of delight, / an inheritance finer than that of any nation!" / I said you would call me "My Father," / and never turn away from following me.
3:20 But as a woman betrays her companion, / so you have betrayed me, house of Israel, / the LORD declares.
3:21 A sound is heard on the bare hills, / the weeping and pleading of the Israelites, / because they have twisted their way, / they have forgotten the LORD their God.
3:22 Turn back, children who turn away, / and I will heal your turning away. / "Here we are, we have come to you, / for you are the LORD our God.
3:23 Truly the hills are a lie, / the roaring crowds on the mountains. / Truly the salvation of Israel / is in the LORD our God.
3:24 The Shameful Thing has eaten up / everything our ancestors worked for since our youth: / their flocks and herds, / their sons and daughters.
3:25 Let us lie down in our shame, / and let our humiliation cover us. / Against the LORD our God we have sinned, / we and our ancestors, / from our youth to this very day, / and we have not listened to the voice of the LORD our God."
## If you turn back
4:1 If you will turn back, Israel, the LORD declares, / back to me you will turn. / And if you remove your detestable things from my sight / and do not wander,
4:2 and you swear, "As the LORD lives," / in truth, in justice and in righteousness, / then the nations will bless themselves by him, / and in him they will boast.
4:3 For the LORD says this to the people of Judah and to Jerusalem: / Plow up new ground for yourselves, / and do not sow among thorns.
4:4 Be circumcised for the LORD, / and take away the foreskins of your hearts, / people of Judah and everyone living in Jerusalem, / or my fury will blaze out like fire / and burn with no one to put it out, / because of the evil of what you have done.
## The ram's horn in Judah
4:5 Declare it in Judah, and make it heard in Jerusalem. / Say: "Blow the ram's horn in the land!" / Call out at the top of your voice and say: / "Gather together! Let us go into the walled cities!"
4:6 Raise a flag toward Zion. / Run for cover! Do not stand around! / For I am bringing disaster from the north, / and a great crash.
4:7 A lion has come up out of his thicket, / a nation-destroyer has broken camp. / He has marched out from his place / to leave your land a wasteland. / Your cities will be ruined, / with nobody left to live in them.
4:8 For this reason tie on sackcloth, / mourn and wail, / because the burning anger of the LORD / has not turned back from us.
4:9 On that day, the LORD declares, / the heart of the king will fail, / and the heart of the officials. / The priests will be appalled, / and the prophets stunned.
4:10 Then I said, "Oh, Lord GOD! You have truly deceived this people and Jerusalem, telling them, 'You will have peace,' while the sword has come to the throat."
4:11 At that time it will be said to this people and to Jerusalem: / "A scorching wind from the bare hills in the wilderness / is on the road to my dear people. / It is not for winnowing or for cleaning grain.
4:12 A wind too strong for that will come at my command. / Now I myself will pronounce my rulings against them."
4:13 Look, he is rising like the clouds, / his chariots like the whirlwind, / his horses faster than eagles. / Woe to us, for we are devastated!
4:14 Wash your heart clean of evil, Jerusalem, / so that you can be saved. / How long will your wicked thoughts / spend the night inside you?
4:15 Listen! A voice is telling the news from Dan, / announcing trouble from the hill country of Ephraim.
4:16 Tell the nations, "They are here!" / Announce it to Jerusalem: / Siege troops are coming from a faraway country, / and they have raised their voices against the cities of Judah.
4:17 Like watchmen over a field they surround her, / because she rebelled against me, the LORD declares.
4:18 Your way of living and your deeds / have done these things to you. / This is your evil, and it is bitter, / because it has struck your heart.
## My insides, my insides
4:19 My innards, my innards! I am writhing. / The walls of my heart! / My heart is raging inside me; I cannot keep quiet. / For you have heard, my soul, the sound of the ram's horn, / the shout of war.
4:20 Crash after crash is proclaimed, / for the whole land is ruined. / Suddenly my tents are ruined, / my tent curtains in an instant.
4:21 How long will I keep seeing the flag / and hearing the ram's horn?
4:22 For my people are foolish. / Me they do not know. / They are silly children, and they have no sense. / They are clever at doing wrong, / but doing good they do not know.
## Back to shapeless and empty
4:23 I looked at the earth, and it was shapeless and empty. / I looked at the heavens, and they had no light.
4:24 I looked at the mountains, and they were shaking, / and all the hills rocked back and forth.
4:25 I looked, and there was no human being, / and every bird of the sky had flown away.
4:26 I looked, and the orchard land was a wilderness, / and all its cities had been pulled down / before the LORD, / before his burning anger.
4:27 For the LORD says this: / The whole land will be a desolation, / but I will not make a complete end.
4:28 Because of this the earth will be in mourning, / and the sky above will turn black. / For I have spoken, I have planned it, / and I have not changed my mind, / and I will not turn back from it.
4:29 Every city is fleeing / from the noise of horsemen and bow-shooters. / They go into the thickets; / they climb up on the rocks. / Every city is deserted, / and no one lives in them.
4:30 And you, ravaged one, what are you doing, / dressing in scarlet, / putting on golden jewelry, / stretching your eyes wide with paint? / You make yourself pretty for nothing. / Your lovers reject you; / they want your life.
4:31 I have heard a sound like a woman in labor, / anguish like a woman having her first child: / the sound of Daughter Zion, gasping, / holding out her hands. / "Oh, how terrible for me! I am fainting at the hands of killers."
## Is there one honest person?
5:1 Go back and forth through the streets of Jerusalem. / Look closely, take note, / and search her public squares: / can you find a person / who acts with justice, / who looks for faithfulness? / Then I will forgive her.
5:2 Even when they say, "As the LORD lives," / they swear to a lie.
5:3 LORD, are your eyes not looking for faithfulness? / You hit them, but they did not wince. / You ground them down, but they refused to learn the lesson. / They set their faces harder than rock. / They refused to turn back.
5:4 And I said to myself, "They are only the poor ones. / They are acting like fools, / because they do not know the way of the LORD, / the justice of their God.
5:5 I will go to the great ones and speak to them, / for they know the way of the LORD, / the justice of their God." / But they too, all of them together, / had broken the yoke / and torn off the straps.
5:6 That is why a lion out of the woods will cut them down, / a wolf from the desert plains will tear them apart, / and a leopard crouches, watching over their towns. / Whoever walks out of them will be ripped to pieces, / because their rebellions are many / and their turnings away are countless.
5:7 For which of these things should I forgive you? / Your children have left me / and swear by what are not gods. / I stuffed them full, and they committed adultery / and gathered in crowds at a prostitute's house.
5:8 They are stallions, fed up and full of lust, / each one neighing at his neighbor's wife.
5:9 For these things, will I not call them to account? the LORD declares. / On a nation like this, / will I not take revenge for myself?
5:10 Go up along her rows of vines and ruin them, / but do not make a complete end. / Take away her shoots, / because they are not the LORD's.
5:11 For the house of Israel and the house of Judah / have betrayed me completely, the LORD declares.
5:12 They denied the LORD and said, / "It is not he. / No harm will come to us. / We will not see sword or famine.
5:13 The prophets will be wind, / and the word is not in them. / So let it be done to them."
5:14 So the LORD, the God of Armies, says this: / Because you have spoken this word, / look, I am turning the words in your mouth into fire, / and these people into firewood, / and it will eat them up.
5:15 Look, I will bring a nation on you from far away, house of Israel, the LORD declares: / a nation that has lasted, a nation from ancient times, / a nation whose language you do not know, / so you cannot make out what they say.
5:16 Their quiver is an open grave; / every one of them is a warrior.
5:17 They will devour your harvest and your bread. / They will devour your sons and your daughters. / They will devour your flocks and your cattle. / They will devour your vines and your fig trees. / With the sword they will batter down / the walled cities you trust.
5:18 Yet even in those days, the LORD declares, / I will not make a complete end of you.
5:19 And when you ask, "For what reason did the LORD our God do all this to us?" you will say to them, "In the same way that you left me and served foreign gods in your own land, you will serve outsiders in a land that is not yours."
5:20 Tell this to the house of Jacob, / and make it heard in Judah:
5:21 Listen to this, you foolish people without a mind, / who have eyes, and they see nothing, / who have ears, and they hear nothing.
5:22 Is it me you do not fear? the LORD declares. / Is it before me that you do not tremble? / I made the sand a border for the sea, / a boundary set forever by decree, / and it cannot cross it. / The waves heave, but they cannot win; / they roar, but they cannot cross it.
5:23 But these people have a heart / that is stubborn and rebellious. / They have turned off the road and gone away.
5:24 They never say to themselves, / "Let us have fear for the LORD our God, / who gives rain, the autumn and the spring rain, / each at its time, / and guards for us / the weeks fixed for harvest."
5:25 Your wrongs have pushed these away, / and your sins have held good things back from you.
5:26 For wicked people are found among my people. / They lie in wait, crouching like bird-catchers. / They set a deadly trap / and catch human beings.
5:27 As a cage is full of birds, / so their houses are full of fraud. / That is how they became great and got rich.
5:28 They have grown fat and glossy. / They overflow in matters of evil. / They do not plead the case, the case of the orphan, / to see that they succeed, / and the rights of the poor / they do not judge.
5:29 For these things, will I not call them to account? the LORD declares. / On a nation like this, / will I not take revenge for myself?
5:30 A horror, an appalling thing, / has happened in the land:
5:31 The prophets prophesy by a lie, / the priests rule at their side, / and my people love it this way. / But what will you do at the end of it?
## Disaster looks down from the north
6:1 Flee for safety, people of Benjamin, / out of the middle of Jerusalem! / In Tekoa blow the ram's horn, / and over Beth-hakkerem raise a signal, / because disaster is peering down from the north, / and a great crash.
6:2 I have compared Daughter Zion / to a dainty pasture.
6:3 Shepherds with their flocks will come to her. / They will pitch their tents all around her, / and each will graze the place under his own hand.
6:4 Consecrate war against her! / Get up, let us go up at noon! / Woe to us, for the day has turned, / for the evening shadows are stretching out.
6:5 Get up, let us go up by night / and tear down her citadels.
6:6 For the LORD of Armies says this: / Chop down the trees / and pile up an assault ramp against Jerusalem. / This city is the one to be called to account, / because it is nothing but oppression all through.
6:7 Water stays fresh in a well, / and so her evil stays fresh in her. / Violence and ruin are heard in her; / before my face always / are sickness and wounds.
6:8 Learn the lesson, Jerusalem, / or my soul will be torn away from you, / and I will turn you into a desolation, / a land where no one lives.
6:9 The LORD of Armies says this: / They will glean the remnant of Israel as thoroughly as a vine. / Like a grape picker, / pass your hand over the branches again.
6:10 Who is there for me to speak to and warn, / so that they will listen? / Look, their ears are uncircumcised, / and they cannot pay attention. / Look, the word of the LORD has turned into a reproach for them; / they find no delight in it.
6:11 The LORD's fury fills me, / and I am worn out trying to hold it back. / Pour it out over the child in the street / and over the circle of young men together, / for husband and wife will both be caught, / the old together with the one full of days.
6:12 Their houses will pass to others, / their fields and their wives together, / for I will stretch out my hand / against those living in the land, the LORD declares.
6:13 From the smallest of them to the biggest, / every one grabs at gain he has no right to. / From prophet to priest, / every one deals in lies.
6:14 They have healed the broken place of my people / as though it were a trifle, / saying, "Peace, peace," / when there is no peace.
6:15 Are they ashamed of the abhorrent thing they have done? / No, they are not ashamed in the least, / and they do not even know what humiliation is. / So they will fall among those who fall. / At the time I call them to account, / they will stumble, the LORD says.
6:16 The LORD says this: / Take your stand on the roads and look. / Ask about the paths of long ago: / where is the road that is good? / Walk on it, and you will find rest for yourselves. / But they said, "We will not walk on it."
6:17 I set watchmen over you: / "Listen for the sound of the ram's horn!" / But they said, "We will not listen."
6:18 So listen, you nations, / and know, you congregation, / what is among them.
6:19 Listen, earth! / Look, I am bringing disaster on this people, / the fruit of their own schemes, / because they have not paid attention to my words, / and my teaching they have rejected.
6:20 Why do you bring me frankincense from Sheba, / and sweet cane from a faraway land? / Your burnt offerings cannot be accepted, / and your sacrifices do not please me.
6:21 So the LORD says this: / Look, I am about to lay stumbling blocks in front of these people, / and they will trip over them, / fathers and sons together; / neighbor and friend will die.
6:22 The LORD says this: / Look, a people is coming from a northern land, / a great nation is roused / from the far ends of the earth.
6:23 They take up bow and spear, / cruel and without pity. / Their noise is like the roar of the sea. / They ride on horses, / lined up like one man for battle, / against you, Daughter Zion.
6:24 We have heard the news of them, / and our hands hang limp. / Distress has seized us, / pain like a woman in labor.
6:25 Do not go out into the field, / and do not walk on the road, / for the enemy has a sword. / Terror all around.
6:26 My dear people, tie on sackcloth / and roll in ashes. / Mourn as for an only child, / a wailing of bitterness, / because all at once the destroyer / will come on us.
6:27 I have made you a tester among my people, a fortress, / so that you may observe and test their way.
6:28 All of them are the worst of rebels, / walking around spreading slander. / They are bronze and iron; / all of them deal in ruin.
6:29 The bellows pant, / the lead is finished off by the fire. / For nothing the smelter goes on smelting, / because the bad ones have not been separated out.
6:30 People call them rejected silver, / for the LORD has rejected them.
