## The oracle against Babylon
50:1 The word that the LORD spoke concerning Babylon, concerning the land of the Chaldeans, through Jeremiah the prophet.
## Babylon is taken
50:2 Tell it among the nations; make it heard! / Lift up a flag; make it heard! / Hide nothing. Say: / "Babylon is captured. / Bel is put to shame; / Marduk is shattered. / Her images are put to shame; / her filthy idols are shattered."
50:3 For a nation has come up against her from the north. / It will turn her land into a waste, / and no one will live there. / From human to beast, / they have fled; they are gone.
## Israel comes home
50:4 In those days and at that time, the LORD declares, / the Israelites will come, / they and the people of Judah together. / Weeping as they walk, they will go on / and seek the LORD their God.
50:5 They will ask for the road to Zion, / their faces set this way. / They will come and join themselves to the LORD / by a covenant for all time / that will not be forgotten.
50:6 Lost sheep, that is what my people have been. / Their shepherds made them stray / and turned them aside on the mountains. / From mountain to hill they wandered off; / they forgot where they lay down to rest.
50:7 Everyone who came upon them ate them up. / Their foes said, "We bear no guilt, / since they sinned against the LORD, / the pasture where right is at home, / the hope of their ancestors, the LORD."
## Flee from Babylon
50:8 Run from the heart of Babylon! / Get out of the land of the Chaldeans! / Be like the billy goats / that go out in front of the flock.
50:9 For look, I am rousing a gathering of great nations / and bringing them up against Babylon from the land of the north. / They will draw up their lines against her; / from there she will be captured. / Its arrows are like a skilled fighter / who does not return empty.
50:10 Chaldea will become loot; / everyone who loots her will eat their fill, / the LORD declares.
## The plunderers shamed
50:11 Though you are glad, though you gloat, / you looters of my inheritance, / though you prance like a heifer at threshing / and neigh like stallions,
50:12 your mother will be put to deep shame; / the one who gave you birth will hang her head. / See: the hindmost of the nations, / desert, parched ground and steppe!
50:13 The LORD's fury will leave her empty of people; / she will be all a waste. / Whoever goes by Babylon will be stunned / and whistle at all her wounds.
50:14 Form your ranks around Babylon, / every archer who bends a bow! / Fire at her; hold back no arrow, / because it is against the LORD she has sinned.
50:15 Shout the battle cry all around her! / She has held out her hand; / her towers have tumbled, / her walls are pulled down. / This is the LORD's vengeance: / take your vengeance on her! / As she has done, do to her.
50:16 Cut off from Babylon the one who sows, / and the one who swings the sickle at harvest time. / From the oppressor's sword / they will each head back to their own people / and each run off to their own country.
## A scattered sheep brought back
50:17 Israel is a scattered sheep; / lions have hunted him off. / First the king of Assyria ate him up; / now this last one, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, / has gnawed his bones.
50:18 That is why the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: / Look, I will call the king of Babylon and his land to account, / as I called the king of Assyria to account.
50:19 I will lead Israel back to his own meadow, / and he will graze on Carmel and in Bashan; / in the hill country of Ephraim and in Gilead / he will eat until he is full.
50:20 In those days and at that time, the LORD declares, / Israel's wrongdoing will be looked for, / but it will not be there, / and Judah's sins, / but they will not be found. / For I will pardon those I leave as a remnant.
## Attack Merathaim and Pekod
50:21 Go up against the land of Merathaim, / against it and against those who live in Pekod! / Kill them and put them under the ban, / going after them, the LORD declares. / Do all that I have ordered you.
50:22 Battle sounds in the land, / and a great shattering!
50:23 How the hammer of all the earth / has been hacked off and smashed! / How Babylon has turned into a horror / among the nations!
50:24 I laid a snare for you, / and yes, Babylon, you were caught, / and you never knew it. / You were found out, and yes, seized, / because you picked a fight with the LORD.
50:25 The LORD has thrown open his storehouse / and carried out the weapons of his fury. / This is a task for the Lord GOD of Armies / in the land of the Chaldeans.
50:26 Come at her from the far end! / Break into her storehouses of grain; / heap her up like piles of grain, / and put her under the ban; / let her have no remnant.
50:27 Cut down all her bullocks; / down they go to the slaughter! / Woe to them! Their day has come, / the time when they are called to account.
50:28 The sound of runaways and survivors / from the land of Babylon, / telling in Zion / of the vengeance of the LORD our God, / vengeance for his temple!
## Insolence brought down
50:29 Call up the bowmen against Babylon, / every one who bends a bow! / Pitch your tents around her; / let her have no survivor. / Repay her by her own works; / whatever she did, do it to her. / For she has been insolent to the LORD, / to the Holy One of Israel.
50:30 So her young men will fall in her open squares, / and all her men of war will be struck silent / on that day, the LORD declares.
50:31 Look, I am coming at you, Insolence, / the Lord GOD of Armies declares, / for your day has come, / the time when I call you to account.
50:32 Insolence will trip and fall, / and no one will raise him up. / I will set fire to his cities, / and it will eat up all that is around him.
## The strong redeemer
50:33 The LORD of Armies says this: / The Israelites are crushed down, / and the people of Judah together with them. / All who took them captive keep their grip on them; / they refuse to let them go.
50:34 Their redeemer is strong; / the LORD of Armies is his name. / He will plead their case in full, / so that the earth may rest / and the people of Babylon be shaken.
## A sword on Babylon
50:35 A sword on the Chaldeans, the LORD declares, / and on the people of Babylon, / on her officials and on her wise men!
50:36 A sword on the babblers, / and they will turn into fools! / A sword on her warriors, / and they will be shattered!
50:37 A sword on his horses and on his chariots, / and on all the mixed crowd in her, / and they will turn into women! / A sword on her treasures, / and they will be looted!
50:38 A drought on her waters, / and they will dry up! / For it is a land of carved idols, / and they act like madmen over their horrors.
50:39 That is why wild beasts of the desert will live there with hyenas, / and ostriches will make their home in her. / It will sit empty forever after; / no one will live there, generation after generation.
50:40 As when God overturned Sodom and Gomorrah / and their neighbor towns, the LORD declares, / no one will live there, / and no human being will stay there.
## The foe from the north
50:41 Look, a people is coming from the north, / a great nation, / and many kings are roused / from the far ends of the earth.
50:42 They take up bow and spear, / cruel and without pity. / Their noise is like the roar of the sea. / They ride on horses, / lined up like one man for battle, / against you, Daughter Babylon.
50:43 The king of Babylon has heard the news of them, / and his hands hang limp. / Distress has seized him, / pain like a woman in labor.
50:44 Look, like a lion climbing up from the Jordan's thick brush / to pasture that never runs dry, / so in an instant I will make them run from it, / and whoever is chosen, him I will set over it. / For who is like me? Who can call me to court? / What shepherd is there who can stand before me?
50:45 That is why you should hear the LORD's plan, / which he has planned against Babylon, / and his purposes that he has purposed / against the land of the Chaldeans: / they will surely be dragged off, the smallest of the flock; / surely their pasture will be made a ruin over them.
50:46 At the shout, "Babylon is seized!" / the earth quakes, / and a cry is heard among the nations.
## A destroying wind
51:1 The LORD says this: / Look, I am rousing against Babylon / and against the people of Leb-qamai / a destroying wind.
51:2 I will send outsiders to Babylon who will winnow her / and strip her land bare, / for they will come at her from all around / on the day of evil.
51:3 The archer must not draw his bow; / the soldier must not rise in his coat of mail. / Show her young men no pity; / put her whole army under the ban.
51:4 The slain will fall in the land of the Chaldeans, / the pierced in her streets.
51:5 For Israel and Judah are not widowed / of their God, the LORD of Armies, / but their land is full of guilt / before the Holy One of Israel.
51:6 Escape from the heart of Babylon, / and each of you save your life! / Do not be silenced in her punishment. / For it is the LORD's time of vengeance; / he is paying back to her what she has earned.
51:7 A cup of gold, that was Babylon, / in the hand of the LORD, / getting the whole earth drunk. / Nations drank from her wine; / so the nations act like madmen.
51:8 All at once Babylon has fallen and lies broken. / Howl for her! / Bring balm for her wound; / maybe she will get well.
51:9 We gave Babylon treatment, / but she was not healed. / Let her be! Let us go home, / each to his own land. / For her sentence has reached the sky; / it is lifted up to the clouds.
51:10 The LORD has brought out our righteous acts. / Come, let us recount in Zion / the work of the LORD our God.
## The Medes are coming
51:11 Put an edge on the arrows! Fill the quivers! / The LORD has roused the spirit of the kings of Media, / for his purpose is set against Babylon, to destroy her. / For this is the LORD's vengeance, / vengeance for his temple.
51:12 Lift up a flag against the walls of Babylon! / Tighten the watch; / set up guards; / make ready the men in ambush! / For the LORD has planned it, and he has also acted on it, / on what he spoke about those who live in Babylon.
51:13 You who dwell beside many waters, / great in treasures, / your end has come, / the measure where you are cut off.
51:14 The LORD of Armies has taken an oath by his own life: / I will surely fill you with men like locusts, / and they will raise the harvest shout over you.
## The Maker of all
51:15 He made the earth with his power. / He set the world firm with his wisdom. / With his understanding he stretched out the heavens.
51:16 When he gives his voice, a rumble of water fills the sky. / He lifts up the clouds from the far end of the earth. / He makes the lightning flash for the rain, / and he leads the wind out of its storerooms.
51:17 Every person has become dull, without knowledge. / Every metalsmith is shamed by his idol, / because the image he casts is a lie, / and there is no breath in them.
51:18 They are empty breath, work worth mocking. / When their time of punishment comes, they will perish.
51:19 Not like these is the one who is Jacob's share, / for he formed everything, / and the tribe that is his inheritance. / The LORD of Armies is his name.
## The LORD's war club
51:20 A club you are to me, / weapons of war. / With you I smash nations; / with you I wreck kingdoms.
51:21 With you I smash horse and rider; / with you I smash chariot and driver.
51:22 With you I smash man and woman; / with you I smash old man and boy; / with you I smash young man and young woman.
51:23 With you I smash shepherd and flock; / with you I smash farmer and team; / with you I smash governors and deputies.
51:24 I will repay Babylon and everyone living in Chaldea / for all the evil they did in Zion / while you watched, the LORD declares.
51:25 I am coming at you, mountain of ruin, / the LORD declares, / you ruiner of all the earth! / I will reach out my hand against you; / I will tumble you down from the crags / and turn you into a mountain of burning.
51:26 No one will take from you a stone for a corner, / or a stone for the foundations, / for you will be a waste for all time, / the LORD declares.
## The nations called to war
51:27 Raise a flag in the land; / sound the ram's horn among the nations! / Set nations apart as holy for war against her; / call kingdoms to come against her: / Ararat, Minni and Ashkenaz. / Set an officer over the attack; / bring up horses like bristling locusts.
51:28 Set nations apart as holy for war against her: / the kings of Media, / its governors and all its deputies, / and every land under its rule.
51:29 The land shook and writhed in pain, / for the LORD's plans hold firm against Babylon, / to turn the land of Babylon into a waste / where nobody lives.
51:30 The fighters of Babylon have given up the battle; / they sit tight in the forts. / Their might has run dry; / they have turned into women. / Fire has caught her homes; / her bolts are snapped.
51:31 Runner dashes to meet runner, / messenger to meet messenger, / to tell the king of Babylon / that his city is taken from end to end.
51:32 The fords have been seized, / the reed beds burned with fire, / and the men of war are in a panic.
51:33 For the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: / Daughter Babylon is a threshing floor / when it is being stamped flat. / Just a little longer, / and harvest time will come for her.
## Zion's complaint answered
51:34 Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has eaten me up; / he has thrown me into confusion. / He has set me down like an empty jar. / He has swallowed me like a sea monster; / he has filled his belly with my fine foods / and rinsed me out.
51:35 "May the wrong done to me and to my flesh be on Babylon!" / says she who lives in Zion. / "May my blood be on the people of Chaldea!" / says Jerusalem.
51:36 So the LORD says this: / Look, I am pleading your case, / and I will avenge the wrong done to you. / I will turn her sea to dry ground / and leave her fountain parched.
51:37 Babylon will turn into piles of stones, / a home for jackals, / a horror and something to whistle at, / without anyone living there.
51:38 All together, like lions they roar; / they snarl like lion cubs.
51:39 While they are in heat, I will lay out their feast / and get them drunk, / so that they make merry, / and then they will sleep an endless sleep / and not wake up, the LORD declares.
51:40 I will lead them down like lambs to be slaughtered, / like rams along with he-goats.
51:41 How Sheshach has been taken, / the pride of all the earth caught! / How Babylon has turned into a horror / among the nations!
51:42 The sea has come up over Babylon; / under the uproar of its waves she is buried.
51:43 Her cities are a waste, / parched ground and steppe, / a land where nobody lives / and no human being goes through.
51:44 I will call Bel to account in Babylon; / I will pull out of his mouth what he gulped down. / No more will nations stream to him. / Even the wall of Babylon has fallen.
## Come out of her, my people
51:45 Come out of her, my people! / Let each one save his life / from the LORD's blazing anger.
51:46 Do not lose heart; do not be afraid / of the news that goes around the land. / News comes in one year, / and after it, in the next year, more news: / violence in the land, ruler on ruler.
51:47 That is why, look, days are coming / when I will call the carved idols of Babylon to account. / All her land will be put to shame; / all her slain will drop in the middle of her.
51:48 Then the heavens and the earth / and all that is in them / will shout for joy over Babylon, / for from the north the destroyers will come to her, / the LORD declares.
51:49 Babylon too must fall, / for the slain of Israel, / as by Babylon's doing / the slain of all the earth have fallen.
51:50 You who got away from the sword, keep going; do not stop! / From far off, remember the LORD, / and let Jerusalem rise up in your heart.
51:51 "We are put to shame, because we have heard taunts; / humiliation covers our faces, / because outsiders have gone into / the holy places of the LORD's house."
51:52 That is why, look, days are coming, the LORD declares, / when I will call her carved idols to account, / and all through her land / the wounded will groan.
51:53 Though Babylon should go up to the sky / and make her high fortress strong, / from me the destroyers will come to her, / the LORD declares.
## The end of Babylon
51:54 The sound of a scream from Babylon, / of a great shattering from the land of the Chaldeans!
51:55 For the LORD is laying Babylon waste; / he is putting an end to the great noise in her. / Their waves roar like many waters; / their voices raise an uproar.
51:56 For a destroyer has come against her, against Babylon. / Her warriors are captured; / their bows are broken. / For the LORD is a God of repayments; / he will pay back without fail.
51:57 I will make her officials and her wise men drunk, / her governors, her deputies and her warriors; / they will sleep an endless sleep / and not wake up, / declares the King; / the LORD of Armies is his name.
51:58 The LORD of Armies says this: / The wide walls of Babylon / will be razed to their base, / and her high gates / will go up in flames. / So peoples labor for emptiness, / nations for the fire, / and they wear themselves out.
## Seraiah's scroll
51:59 This is the word that Jeremiah the prophet gave as an order to Seraiah son of Neriah, son of Mahseiah, when he went with Zedekiah king of Judah to Babylon in the fourth year of his reign. Seraiah was the officer in charge of rest stops.
51:60 Jeremiah had written in one scroll all the disaster that was coming on Babylon, all these words that are written concerning Babylon.
51:61 Jeremiah told Seraiah, "When you reach Babylon, take a look, and read all these words out loud.
51:62 And say, 'LORD, you yourself have spoken against this place, to cut it off, so that no one lives in it, from human to beast, for it will be a waste for all time.'
51:63 When you have finished reading out this scroll, tie a stone on it and throw it into the middle of the Euphrates.
51:64 And say, 'Like this Babylon will sink and will not come up, because of the disaster I am bringing on her, and they will wear themselves out.'" Up to this point are the words of Jeremiah.
## Zedekiah
52:1 Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah, from Libnah.
52:2 He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes, just as Jehoiakim had done.
52:3 All this happened in Jerusalem and Judah because the LORD was angry, until at last he flung them away from his face. Then Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.
## The siege of Jerusalem
52:4 Then, in the ninth year of his reign, on the tenth day of the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon arrived at Jerusalem, he and his whole army. They camped against the city, and they built a siege wall all the way around it.
52:5 The city stayed under siege until King Zedekiah's eleventh year.
52:6 On the ninth day of the fourth month, hunger gripped the city hard. The people of the land had no bread.
52:7 Then the city was breached. All the fighting men ran away and left the city by night through the gate between the double walls, by the king's garden, even with the Chaldeans surrounding the city. They went off toward the Arabah.
52:8 The Chaldean army went after the king and caught Zedekiah on the Jericho plains. All his troops had scattered and left him.
52:9 They took the king prisoner and hauled him up to the king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, and there he pronounced sentence on him.
52:10 The king of Babylon slaughtered Zedekiah's sons while he watched. He also slaughtered all the officials of Judah at Riblah.
52:11 Zedekiah's eyes he put out. He bound him in bronze fetters, and the king of Babylon brought him to Babylon. He put him in prison until the day he died.
## The temple burned
52:12 On the tenth day of the fifth month, which was the nineteenth year of King Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, Nebuzaradan came into Jerusalem. He was chief of the guard, and he stood in the service of Babylon's king.
52:13 He set the LORD's house on fire, and the king's house, and all the houses of Jerusalem. Every house of any importance, he burned with fire.
52:14 All Jerusalem's walls, all the way around, were torn down by the whole Chaldean army that was with the chief of the guard.
52:15 Some of the poor of the people, the rest of the people who were left in the city, those who had gone over to the king of Babylon, and whatever was left of the craftsmen: Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard marched them off into exile.
52:16 Some of the poor of the land Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard left behind, to tend vines and to plow.
52:17 The bronze pillars of the LORD's house, the stands, and the bronze Sea in the LORD's house, the Chaldeans smashed, and they hauled all the bronze off to Babylon.
52:18 They took the pots, the shovels, the wick trimmers, the bowls, the ladles, and every bronze object used for service there.
52:19 The basins, the fire pans, the bowls, the pots, the lampstands, the ladles and the libation jars, the gold ones for their gold and the silver ones for their silver: the chief of the guard took them.
52:20 The two pillars, the one Sea, the twelve bronze bulls that were under the stands, which King Solomon made for the LORD's house: no one could weigh the bronze in all those objects.
52:21 As for the pillars, one pillar stood eighteen cubits tall, and a cord of twelve cubits went around it. It was four fingers thick, and hollow.
52:22 It had a bronze capital on it. The capital of the one pillar rose five cubits, with network and pomegranates on it all around, all of bronze. The second pillar was like it, pomegranates and all.
52:23 There were ninety-six pomegranates on the sides. All the pomegranates on the network all around came to a hundred.
## The leaders put to death
52:24 The chief of the guard took Seraiah the head priest, Zephaniah the deputy priest, and the three guards of the doorway.
52:25 Out of the city he took one court official who had been in charge over the fighting men, and seven men of those who saw the king's face, who were still in the city. He took the secretary of the army commander, the one who drafted the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found inside the city.
52:26 Nebuzaradan, chief of the guard, took them and marched them to the king of Babylon at Riblah.
52:27 At Riblah in the land of Hamath the king of Babylon struck them down and put them to death. And so Judah went into exile, away from its own soil.
## The exiles counted
52:28 These are the people whom Nebuchadnezzar took into exile: in the seventh year, 3,023 Judeans;
52:29 in Nebuchadnezzar's eighteenth year, 832 people from Jerusalem;
52:30 in Nebuchadnezzar's twenty-third year, Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard took 745 Judeans into exile. All the people came to 4,600.
## Jehoiachin set free
52:31 It was the thirty-seventh year since Jehoiachin king of Judah went into exile, the twelfth month, the twenty-fifth day of the month. Evil-merodach king of Babylon, in the year his reign began, lifted up the head of Jehoiachin king of Judah and brought him out of the prison house.
52:32 He spoke good words to him and set his throne above the thrones of the kings who were with him in Babylon.
52:33 He changed out of his prison clothes, and he ate bread in the king's presence always, every day he lived.
52:34 His food allowance was a steady one, given him by the king of Babylon, each day's share on its day, until the day of his death, for as long as he lived.
