## Zedekiah asks for a wonder
21:1 This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD when King Zedekiah sent Pashhur son of Malkijah to him, together with the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah. They said:
21:2 "Please ask the LORD about us, because Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon is making war on us. Perhaps the LORD will deal with us in line with all his wonders, so that the king moves off and leaves us."
## The LORD fights against the city
21:3 Jeremiah said to them, "Tell Zedekiah this:
21:4 'The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Look, I am about to turn around the weapons of war that are in your hands, the ones you are using to fight the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans, who have you under siege outside the wall. I will gather them into the middle of this city.
21:5 I will fight you myself, with a hand stretched out and a strong arm, in anger, in rage and in great wrath.
21:6 I will strike down everyone who lives in this city, human and beast. A great disease will kill them.
21:7 After that, the LORD declares, I will give Zedekiah king of Judah, his servants, the people, and those left in this city after the disease, the sword and the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. I will give them into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who hunt for their lives. He will strike them down with the edge of the sword. He will not spare them. He will have no pity and no compassion.'
## The way of life and the way of death
21:8 To this people you are to say, 'The LORD says this: Look, I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death.
21:9 Anyone who stays in this city will die by the sword, by famine and by disease. But anyone who goes out and deserts to the Chaldeans who have you under siege will live, and his own life will be his loot.
21:10 For I have fixed my face on this city to harm it and not to help it, the LORD declares. Into the hand of the king of Babylon it will be handed over, and he will burn it down.'"
## A word for the house of David
21:11 To the royal house of Judah: Hear the word of the LORD.
21:12 House of David, the LORD says this: / "Each morning, give a true verdict. / Pull the robbed man out of the oppressor's hand, / or my fury will go out like fire / and burn, with no one to put it out, / because of the evil of your deeds."
21:13 Look, I am against you, / you who sit in the valley, / you rock on the plain, the LORD declares, / you who say, "Who can come down on us? / Who can get into our hiding places?"
21:14 I will punish you as the fruit of your deeds deserves, the LORD declares. / I will set a fire in her forest, / and it will eat up everything around her.
## Go down to the king's house
22:1 The LORD says this: "Go down to the house of the king of Judah and speak this word there.
22:2 Say, 'Hear the word of the LORD, king of Judah, you who sit on David's throne, you, your servants and your people who come in through these gates.
22:3 The LORD says this: Carry out justice and righteousness. Pull the robbed man out of the oppressor's hand. Do not mistreat or abuse the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow. Do not shed innocent blood in this place.
22:4 If you carry out this word, kings will keep coming through the gates of this house, kings who sit on David's throne. They will ride in chariots and on horses, with their servants and their people.
22:5 But if you refuse to listen to these words, I have sworn by myself, the LORD declares: this house will become a ruin.'"
22:6 For the LORD says this about the house of the king of Judah: / "You are Gilead to me, / the top of Lebanon. / Surely I will make you a wilderness, / cities where no one lives.
22:7 I will set destroyers apart against you, / each man with his weapons. / They will chop down your finest cedars / and throw them on the fire."
22:8 Many nations will pass by this city, and each will say to his neighbor, "Why did the LORD do this to such a great city?"
22:9 And the answer will be, "Because they abandoned the covenant of the LORD their God. They bowed down to other gods and served them."
## Shallum, Jehoiakim and Coniah
22:10 Do not weep for the dead man / or shake your head over him. / Weep, weep for the one who is leaving, / for he will never come back / or see the land where he was born.
22:11 For the LORD says this about Shallum son of Josiah, king of Judah, who became king in place of his father Josiah and who went out from this place: "He will never come back here.
22:12 In the place where they took him into exile, there he will die. He will never see this land again."
22:13 Woe to the man who builds his house without righteousness, / his upper rooms without justice, / who has his fellow citizen work for nothing / and never hands over his pay,
22:14 who says, "I will build myself a roomy house / with airy upper rooms." / He cuts windows into it, / covers it with cedar, / and paints it red.
22:15 Does competing in cedar make you king? / Your father also ate and drank, did he not? / And he carried out justice and righteousness. / Then it went well for him.
22:16 He took up the case of the poor and the needy; / then all was well. / Is this not knowing me? / the LORD declares.
22:17 But your eyes and your heart / are set on nothing / except your profit, / the blood of the innocent to spill, / and fraud and crushing others to carry out.
22:18 So the LORD says this about Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah: / "No one will mourn for him, saying, / 'Alas, my brother!' or 'Alas, my sister!' / No one will mourn for him, saying, / 'Alas, lord!' or 'Alas, his majesty!'
22:19 He will be buried the way a donkey is buried, / dragged off and thrown out / beyond the gates of Jerusalem."
22:20 Climb Lebanon and cry out; / in Bashan let your voice ring; / shout from Abarim, / for all your lovers are smashed.
22:21 I spoke to you when you felt safe, / but you said, "I will not listen." / This has been your way since you were young: / you have never listened to my voice.
22:22 Wind will graze all your shepherds, / and your lovers will march off as captives. / Then you will blush with shame and be humiliated / for all your wickedness.
22:23 You who live in Lebanon, / nested among the cedars, / how you will be pitied when labor pains come to you, / anguish like a woman giving birth!
22:24 "As I live, the LORD declares, if Coniah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah were the seal ring on my right hand, from that very spot I would pull you off.
22:25 I will give you into the hand of those who hunt for your life, into the hand of those you fear, into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and the hand of the Chaldeans.
22:26 I will fling you and the mother who gave birth to you into another land, a place where neither of you was born, and there you will die.
22:27 They will long to go back to that land, but they will never go back there."
22:28 Is this man Coniah a despised, shattered jar? / Is he a vessel no one delights in? / Why have he and his children been flung out / and tossed into a land they have never known?
22:29 Land, land, land, / hear the word of the LORD!
22:30 The LORD says this: "Put this man in the record as stripped of children, a man who will not prosper in his lifetime. For none of his offspring will prosper as one who sits on David's throne and rules again in Judah."
## Shepherds who scatter, a Branch who rules
23:1 Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter the flock of my pasture, the LORD declares.
23:2 So the LORD, the God of Israel, says this about the shepherds who shepherd my people: "You have scattered my flock and driven them away. You have not paid attention to them. Now I am about to pay attention to you, to the evil of your deeds, the LORD declares.
23:3 I myself will collect what is left of my flock from all the lands where I drove them, and I will turn them back toward their meadow. They will be fruitful and grow many.
23:4 I will raise up shepherds over them who will shepherd them. They will not be afraid again or terrified, and not one will be missing, the LORD declares."
23:5 Look, days are coming, the LORD declares, / when I will raise up for David a righteous Branch. / He will rule as king and show good sense, / and he will carry out justice and righteousness in the land.
23:6 In his days Judah will be saved, / and Israel will settle in security. / This is the name by which he will be called: / "The LORD is our righteousness."
23:7 So look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when people will no longer say, "As the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites up from the land of Egypt,"
23:8 but, "As the LORD lives, who brought up and brought back the descendants of the house of Israel out of the land of the north, and out of every land where I drove them." Then they will live on their own soil.
## Against the prophets
23:9 About the prophets: / my heart is shattered within me; / all my bones tremble. / I have become like a staggering drunk, / like a man wine has washed over, / because of the LORD, / because of his holy words.
23:10 The land is packed with adulterers. / Under the curse the land is parched, / and the wilderness pastures are dry. / They run a wicked course, / and they misuse their strength.
23:11 For prophet and priest alike are defiled. / Even in my own house I have found their evil, the LORD declares.
23:12 So their path will be to them like slippery ground in the dark. / They will be shoved and will fall on it. / For I will bring disaster on them / in the year they are punished, the LORD declares.
23:13 Among the prophets of Samaria I saw something sickening: / they prophesied by Baal / and made my people Israel wander.
23:14 But among the prophets of Jerusalem I have seen something horrifying. / They are adulterers, and their lives are lies. / They put strength into the hands of evildoers, / so that not one gives up his evil. / All of them are like Sodom to me, / and the city's people like Gomorrah.
23:15 So the LORD of Armies says this about the prophets: / Look, I am about to feed them wormwood / and give them poisoned water to drink. / For from the prophets of Jerusalem / pollution has gone out to the whole land.
23:16 The LORD of Armies says this: / Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you. / They are leading you into emptiness. / They speak a vision from their own hearts, / not from the mouth of the LORD.
23:17 They keep saying to those who despise me, / "The LORD has said: You will have peace." / And to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his own heart / they say, "No harm will come to you."
23:18 For who has taken a stand in the council of the LORD / so that he saw and heard his word? / Who has given heed to his word and listened?
23:19 Look! The storm of the LORD! / Fury has gone out, / a whirling storm; / it will burst on the heads of the wicked.
23:20 The LORD's anger will not turn back / until he has done and carried out / the plans of his heart. / In the last days you will understand it fully.
23:21 I never sent these prophets, / but they ran anyway. / I never spoke to them, / but they prophesied anyway.
23:22 If they had stood in my council, / they would have let my people hear my words / and turned them back from their evil way, / from the evil of their deeds.
23:23 Am I a God at close range, the LORD declares, / and not a God at a distance?
23:24 Can anyone hide in secret places / so that I do not see him? the LORD declares. / Is it not the heavens and the earth that I fill? the LORD declares.
23:25 I have heard what the prophets say, the ones who prophesy lies in my name: "I had a dream! I had a dream!"
23:26 How long? Is there anything in the heart of the prophets who prophesy lies, prophets whose message is the fraud of their own hearts?
23:27 They plan to make my people forget my name by their dreams, which they tell one another, just as their ancestors forgot my name for Baal.
23:28 A prophet who has a dream can tell his dream, but the one who has my word must speak my word truthfully. What has straw got to do with grain? the LORD declares.
23:29 Is my word not like fire, the LORD declares, / and like a hammer that smashes rock?
23:30 So look, I am against the prophets, the LORD declares, who steal my words, each from his neighbor.
23:31 Look, I am against the prophets, the LORD declares, who wag their tongues and announce, "He declares."
23:32 Look, I am against those who prophesy dreams that are lies, the LORD declares. They tell them and make my people wander with their falsehoods and their wild boasts. I never sent them, and I never commanded them. They bring this people no benefit at all, the LORD declares.
## What oracle?
23:33 When this people, or a prophet, or a priest asks you, "What is the oracle of the LORD?" you are to say to them, "What oracle? I will throw you off, the LORD declares."
23:34 As for the prophet, the priest or the people who says, "The oracle of the LORD," I will punish that man and his household.
23:35 This is what each of you is to say to his neighbor and to his brother: "What did the LORD answer?" and "What has the LORD said?"
23:36 But you must never again mention "the oracle of the LORD," because each man's own word will become his oracle, and you have twisted the words of the living God, the LORD of Armies, our God.
23:37 This is what you are to say to the prophet: "What did the LORD answer you?" and "What has the LORD said?"
23:38 But if you say, "The oracle of the LORD," then the LORD says this: Because you say these words, "The oracle of the LORD," although I sent word to you, "You must not say, 'The oracle of the LORD,'"
23:39 therefore, look, I will utterly forget you. I will throw you away from my face, you and the city that I gave to you and your ancestors.
23:40 I will put on you a disgrace that lasts forever, and a shame that lasts forever, that will never be forgotten.
## Two baskets of figs
24:1 The LORD showed me two baskets of figs set in front of the LORD's temple. This was after Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had taken Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, into exile from Jerusalem, along with the officials of Judah, the craftsmen and the smiths, and had brought them to Babylon.
24:2 In one basket were figs, very good ones, like the first figs of the season. In the other basket were figs, very bad ones, too spoiled to eat.
24:3 The LORD said to me, "What do you see, Jeremiah?" I said, "Figs. The good ones are very good, and the bad ones are very bad, too spoiled to eat."
24:4 Then the word of the LORD came to me:
24:5 "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Like these good figs, so I will acknowledge the exiles of Judah, whom I sent away from this place to the land of the Chaldeans, for their good.
24:6 I will set my eyes on them for good and bring them back to this land. I will build them up and not demolish them. I will plant them and not pull them up.
24:7 I will give them a heart to know me, that I am the LORD. They will become my people, and I will become their God, because they will come back to me with their whole heart.
24:8 But like the bad figs, too spoiled to eat, yes, the LORD says this, so I will treat Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials, the remnant of Jerusalem who are left in this land, and those who live in the land of Egypt.
24:9 I will make them a terror, an evil, to all the kingdoms of the earth, a disgrace, a byword, a taunt and a curse in all the places where I drive them.
24:10 I will send the sword, the famine and the disease against them until they are gone from the ground that I gave to them and to their ancestors."
## Twenty-three years of warning
25:1 The word that came to Jeremiah about all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah. That was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.
25:2 Jeremiah the prophet spoke it to all the people of Judah and to all who lived in Jerusalem:
25:3 "From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, until this very day, these twenty-three years, the word of the LORD has come to me. I have spoken to you, getting up early to speak, and you have not listened.
25:4 The LORD sent you all his servants the prophets, getting up early to send them, and you did not listen or even turn your ear to hear.
25:5 They said, 'Please, turn back, each of you, from your evil way and from the evil of your deeds. Live on the land that the LORD gave to you and your ancestors, from long ago to forever.
25:6 Do not trail after other gods, serving them and bowing down to them. Do not stir my anger with what your hands have made, and I will do you no harm.'
25:7 But you did not listen to me, the LORD declares. You made me angry with the work of your hands, to your own harm.
## Seventy years
25:8 So the LORD of Armies says this: Because you have not listened to my words,
25:9 look, I am about to send for all the clans of the north, the LORD declares, and for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, my servant. I will bring them against this land and the people who live in it, and against all these nations around it. I will put them under the ban and make them a horror, a hissing and ruins forever.
25:10 I will take away from them the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the sound of the groom and the sound of the bride, the sound of the hand mill and the light of the lamp.
25:11 All this land will become a ruin and a horror, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon seventy years.
25:12 When the seventy years are over, I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation, the LORD declares, for their guilt, and the land of the Chaldeans. I will make it a wasteland forever.
25:13 I will make that land face every word I spoke against it, all that is written in this book, what Jeremiah prophesied against all the nations.
25:14 For they too will be worked as slaves by many nations and great kings, and I will repay them as their actions deserve and as their hands have worked."
## The cup of the LORD's rage
25:15 For this is what the LORD, the God of Israel, said to me: "Take this cup, the wine of rage, from my hand, and make all the nations to whom I send you drink from it.
25:16 They will drink, reel back and forth, and go mad because of the sword I am about to send among them."
25:17 So I took the cup from the LORD's hand and made all the nations drink it, all those to whom the LORD sent me:
25:18 Jerusalem and the towns of Judah, its kings and its officials, to make them a ruin, a horror, a hissing and a curse, as they are today;
25:19 Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his officials and all his people;
25:20 all the mixed peoples; all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the land of the Philistines: Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron and what is left of Ashdod;
25:21 Edom, Moab and the Ammonites;
25:22 all the kings of Tyre, all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the coastland across the sea;
25:23 Dedan, Tema, Buz and all who have their hair cut at the corners;
25:24 all the kings of Arabia and all the kings of the mixed peoples who live in the wilderness;
25:25 all the kings of Zimri, all the kings of Elam and all the kings of Media;
25:26 all the kings of the north, near and far, one after another, and all the kingdoms of the earth that are on the face of the ground. And the king of Sheshach will drink after them.
25:27 "Say to them, 'The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Drink and get drunk and throw up. Fall, and do not get up again, because of the sword I am about to send among you.'
25:28 If they refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink, say to them, 'The LORD of Armies says this: You must drink!
25:29 Look, I am beginning to bring harm on the city that is called by my name. Do you think you can go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, because I am calling a sword down on everyone who lives on the earth, declares the LORD of Armies.'"
## The LORD roars
25:30 "As for you, you are to prophesy all these words to them and say to them: / 'The LORD will roar from on high; / from his holy dwelling he will give his voice. / He will roar and roar against his home, / a shout like the shout of grape treaders, / against everyone who lives on the earth.
25:31 The uproar has reached the end of the earth, / for the LORD has a case against the nations. / He is going into judgment with all flesh. / The wicked he has given over to the sword, the LORD declares.'
25:32 The LORD of Armies says this: / 'See how disaster spreads / from nation to nation. / A great whirlwind is roused / from the far ends of the earth.'
25:33 On that day those killed by the LORD will lie from one end of the earth to the other. No one will cry for them, collect them or bury them. They will lie like manure on the surface of the ground.
25:34 Howl, you shepherds, and scream! / Wallow in ashes, you mighty ones of the flock! / For the days of your slaughter are complete, / and so is your scattering, / and you will fall like a precious vessel.
25:35 Escape will be lost to the shepherds, / and rescue to the lords of the flock.
25:36 Hear the cry of the shepherds / and the wail of the lords of the flock, / for the LORD is laying waste their pasture.
25:37 The peaceful meadows are silenced / by the burning anger of the LORD.
25:38 He has left his hiding place like a young lion, / for their land has become a horror / because of the oppressor's fierce anger / and because of his burning anger."
