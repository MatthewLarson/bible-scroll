## The temple sermon put on trial
26:1 When Jehoiakim son of Josiah had just become king of Judah, this word came from the LORD:
26:2 "The LORD says this: Stand in the courtyard of the LORD's house. Speak to all the people from Judah's towns who come to bow down in the LORD's house. Tell them every word I order you to say. Do not cut out a single word.
26:3 Maybe they will listen and turn back, each from his bad way. Then I will change my mind about the harm I am planning to do to them because of their wicked deeds.
26:4 Tell them, 'The LORD says this: If you refuse to listen to me and live by my law, which I put in front of you,
26:5 and refuse to heed the words of my servants the prophets, whom I keep sending to you, getting up early to send them, though you have not listened to them,
26:6 then I will make this house like Shiloh, and I will make this city a word that every nation on earth uses to curse.'"
26:7 The priests, the prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speak these words in the LORD's house.
26:8 Jeremiah finished saying everything the LORD had ordered him to say to all the people. Then the priests, the prophets and all the people grabbed him. "You will certainly die!" they said.
26:9 "Why did you prophesy in the LORD's name, 'This house will end up like Shiloh, and this city will be wasted, with no one left to live in it'?" And all the people crowded around Jeremiah in the LORD's house.
26:10 The officials of Judah heard about these things. They went up from the king's house to the LORD's house and sat down at the entrance of the New Gate of the LORD's house.
26:11 Then the priests and the prophets spoke to the officials and to all the people. "This man has earned a death sentence," they said. "He prophesied against this city. You heard him with your own ears."
26:12 Then Jeremiah told all the officials and all the people, "The LORD sent me to prophesy against this house and this city every word you have heard.
26:13 So now, make your ways and your deeds good, and listen to the voice of the LORD your God. Then the LORD will change his mind about the harm he has spoken against you.
26:14 As for me, here I am in your hands. Do to me whatever seems good and right to you.
26:15 But be very sure of this: if you put me to death, you are bringing innocent blood on yourselves, on this city and on everyone who lives in it. The LORD truly did send me to you, to speak all these words in your hearing."
26:16 Then the officials and all the people said to the priests and the prophets, "This man does not deserve a death sentence. He spoke to us in the name of the LORD our God."
26:17 Then some of the elders of the land stood up and spoke to the whole gathering of the people.
26:18 "Micah of Moresheth was prophesying in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah," they said. "He told all the people of Judah, 'The LORD of Armies says this: Zion will be plowed like a field. Jerusalem will be heaps of rubble, and the mountain of the house will be high ground in a forest.'
26:19 Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah put him to death? Did Hezekiah not fear the LORD and beg the LORD for mercy? And the LORD changed his mind about the harm he had spoken against them. But we are about to do a great evil against our own lives."
26:20 There was also a man who prophesied in the LORD's name, Uriah son of Shemaiah, from Kiriath-jearim. He prophesied against this city and this land in words just like Jeremiah's.
26:21 King Jehoiakim, all his warriors and all the officials heard his words, and the king tried to have him killed. When Uriah heard of it, he was afraid. He ran away and went to Egypt.
26:22 King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt: Elnathan son of Achbor, and other men with him, went to Egypt.
26:23 They brought Uriah out of Egypt and took him to King Jehoiakim, who cut him down with the sword and had his corpse thrown into the burial ground of the common people.
26:24 But Ahikam son of Shaphan stood by Jeremiah, and so he was not handed over to the people to be killed.
## The yoke of Babylon
27:1 When Zedekiah son of Josiah had just become king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:
27:2 The LORD said this to me: "Make yourself straps and yoke-bars, and put them on your neck.
27:3 Then send them to the king of Edom, the king of Moab, the king of the Ammonites, the king of Tyre and the king of Sidon, by the messengers who have come to Jerusalem, to Zedekiah king of Judah.
27:4 Give them this order for their masters: 'The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this. Here is what you must say to your masters:
27:5 I made the earth, and the people and the animals on the face of the earth, by my great strength and my outstretched arm. I give it to anyone I think right.
27:6 And now I myself have handed all these lands over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, my servant. I have even given him the wild animals, to serve him.
27:7 All the nations will serve him, and his son, and his son's son, until the time comes for his own land too. Then many nations and great kings will make him their slave.
27:8 If any nation or kingdom will not serve him, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and will not put its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, I will deal with that nation by sword, famine and disease, the LORD declares, until I have put an end to it by his hand.
27:9 As for you, do not listen to your prophets, your diviners, your dreams, your fortune-tellers or your sorcerers, who keep telling you, "You will not serve the king of Babylon."
27:10 They are prophesying a lie to you, so as to take you far from your land. I will drive you out, and you will perish.
27:11 But the nation that brings its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him, I will let stay on its own land, the LORD declares. It will farm the land and live there.'"
27:12 I spoke to Zedekiah king of Judah in the same words. "Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon," I said. "Serve him and his people, and live.
27:13 Why should you die, you and your people, by the sword, by famine and by disease? The LORD has said that is what comes to any nation that will not serve the king of Babylon.
27:14 Do not listen to the words of the prophets who keep telling you, 'You will not serve the king of Babylon.' It is a lie they are prophesying to you.
27:15 'I did not send them,' the LORD declares. 'They are prophesying a lie in my name, so that I will drive you out and you will perish, you and the prophets who prophesy to you.'"
27:16 Then I spoke to the priests and to all this people. "The LORD says this," I said. "Do not listen to the words of your prophets who prophesy to you, 'Look, the objects of the LORD's house will be brought back from Babylon now, very soon.' It is a lie they are prophesying to you.
27:17 Do not listen to them. Serve the king of Babylon and stay alive. Why let this city become a ruin?
27:18 If they are prophets, and if the word of the LORD is with them, let them plead with the LORD of Armies that the objects left in the LORD's house, in the house of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem do not go to Babylon.
27:19 For the LORD of Armies says this about the pillars, the Sea, the stands and the rest of the objects left in this city,
27:20 the ones Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he took Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon, with all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem.
27:21 Yes, the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this about the objects left in the LORD's house, in the house of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem:
27:22 'They will be taken to Babylon, and there they will stay until the day I call for them, the LORD declares. Then I will bring them up and return them to this place.'"
## Hananiah breaks the yoke
28:1 It was in that same year, early in the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, in the fifth month: the prophet Hananiah son of Azzur, a man from Gibeon, said to me in the LORD's house, with the priests and all the people looking on,
28:2 "The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon.
28:3 Within two full years I am bringing back to this place all the objects of the LORD's house. Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took them from this place and carried them off to Babylon.
28:4 And I will bring back to this place Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, with all the exiles of Judah who went to Babylon, the LORD declares, for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon."
28:5 Then the prophet Jeremiah answered the prophet Hananiah in front of the priests and all the people standing in the LORD's house.
28:6 "Amen!" the prophet Jeremiah said. "May the LORD do exactly that. May the LORD carry out the words you have prophesied, and bring the objects of the LORD's house and all the exiles back from Babylon to this place.
28:7 Only hear this word that I am about to speak in your ears and in the ears of all the people.
28:8 The prophets who came before you and me, in the earliest times, prophesied against many lands and great kingdoms: war, disaster and disease.
28:9 As for the prophet who prophesies peace: when that prophet's word comes true, then he will be known as the prophet the LORD truly sent."
28:10 Then the prophet Hananiah took the yoke-bar from the neck of the prophet Jeremiah and smashed it.
28:11 In front of all the people Hananiah said, "The LORD says this: This is how I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, within two full years, from off the neck of all the nations." And the prophet Jeremiah went his way.
28:12 After the prophet Hananiah had smashed the yoke-bar from the neck of the prophet Jeremiah, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
28:13 "Go and tell Hananiah, 'The LORD says this: You have broken yoke-bars of wood, but you will make yoke-bars of iron in their place.'
28:14 For the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: I have put a yoke of iron on the neck of all these nations, to serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and they will serve him. I have even given him the wild animals."
28:15 Then the prophet Jeremiah said to the prophet Hananiah, "Listen, Hananiah! The LORD did not send you, and you have made this people trust in a lie.
28:16 So the LORD says this: I am about to send you off the face of the earth. This year you will die, because you have spoken rebellion against the LORD."
28:17 And the prophet Hananiah died that same year, in the seventh month.
## A letter to the exiles
29:1 Here are the words of the letter that the prophet Jeremiah sent from Jerusalem to the elders who were still left among the exiles, to the priests, to the prophets and to all the people Nebuchadnezzar had carried away into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon.
29:2 It was after King Jeconiah had gone out from Jerusalem, with the queen mother, the court officials, the officials of Judah and Jerusalem, the craftsmen and the smiths.
29:3 The letter went by the hand of Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent to Babylon, to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. It said:
29:4 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this to all the exiles I sent into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon:
29:5 Build houses and settle in them. Plant gardens and eat what they grow.
29:6 Take wives and have sons and daughters. Find wives for your sons and give your daughters to husbands, and let them bear sons and daughters. Grow in number there and do not dwindle.
29:7 Seek the peace of the city I sent you to as exiles, and pray for it to the LORD. When it has peace, you will have peace.
29:8 For the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Do not let your prophets who are among you, or your diviners, deceive you. Do not listen to the dreams you are getting them to dream.
29:9 It is a lie they are prophesying to you in my name. I did not send them, the LORD declares.
29:10 For the LORD says this: When seventy years for Babylon are over, I will turn my attention to you. I will keep my good word to you and bring you back to this place.
29:11 I know the plans I am making for you, the LORD declares. They are plans for peace, not for harm. I mean to give you a future, and hope with it.
29:12 You will call to me, come and pray to me, and I will hear you.
29:13 You will look for me and find me, when you search for me with all your heart.
29:14 I will let you find me, the LORD declares. I will turn your fortunes around. I will gather you from all the nations and all the places where I have driven you, the LORD declares, and I will bring you back to the place I sent you away from into exile.
29:15 You say, "The LORD has raised up prophets for us in Babylon."
29:16 But the LORD says this about the king who sits on David's throne, and about all the people living in this city, your relatives who did not go out with you into exile:
29:17 The LORD of Armies says this: Look, I am sending against them the sword, famine and disease. I will make them like disgusting figs, too bad to eat.
29:18 I will chase them with sword, famine and disease. I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, and a curse, a shock, a thing to hiss at and a disgrace among all the nations where I have driven them,
29:19 because they did not listen to my words, the LORD declares, the words I sent to them by my servants the prophets, getting up early to send them. But you would not listen, the LORD declares.
29:20 So you, all the exiles I sent away from Jerusalem to Babylon, hear the word of the LORD.
29:21 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this about Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, who are prophesying a lie to you in my name: I am about to hand them over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will kill them before your eyes.
29:22 From them a curse will be taken up by all the exiles of Judah in Babylon: "May the LORD make you like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire."
29:23 For they have done an outrage in Israel. They committed adultery with their neighbors' wives, and in my name they spoke lying words that I did not command them. I am the one who knows, and I am a witness, the LORD declares.
29:24 To Shemaiah the Nehelamite you are to say:
29:25 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: You are the one who sent letters in your own name to all the people in Jerusalem, to the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah and to all the priests. In them you said,
29:26 "The LORD has made you priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, to be overseers in the LORD's house over every man who raves and plays the prophet. You should put him in the stocks and the iron collar.
29:27 So now, why have you not scolded Jeremiah of Anathoth, who plays the prophet among you?
29:28 For he has sent us word in Babylon: 'It will be long. Build houses and settle in them. Plant gardens and eat what they grow.'"
29:29 Zephaniah the priest read this letter aloud in the hearing of the prophet Jeremiah.
29:30 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
29:31 Send word to all the exiles: The LORD says this about Shemaiah the Nehelamite: Because Shemaiah has prophesied to you when I did not send him, and has made you trust in a lie,
29:32 the LORD says this: I am going to punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his descendants. None of his line will live on among this people, and he will not see the good I am going to do for my people, the LORD declares, because he has spoken rebellion against the LORD.
## The book of comfort: Jacob's trouble and healing
30:1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:
30:2 The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Write down for yourself in a book all the words I have spoken to you.
30:3 For look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when I will turn the fortunes of my people Israel and Judah around, says the LORD. I will bring them back to the land I gave their ancestors, and they will take possession of it.
30:4 Here are the words the LORD spoke about Israel and about Judah.
30:5 For the LORD says this: / We have heard a sound of trembling, / dread, and no peace.
30:6 Ask, and look: / does a man ever give birth? / Then why do I see every strong man / with his hands on his hips like a woman in labor, / and every face turned a sickly yellow-green?
30:7 Alas, that day is so great! / Nothing is like it. / It is a season of anguish for Jacob, / and out of that anguish he will be saved.
30:8 On that day, the LORD of Armies declares, I will break his yoke off your neck and tear your straps apart, and strangers will no longer make him their slave.
30:9 Instead, they will serve the LORD their God and David their king, whom I will raise up for them.
30:10 As for you, Jacob my servant, do not be afraid, the LORD declares; / Israel, do not be shattered. / See, I am the one who will save you from far away, / and your children from the land of their captivity. / Jacob will come back and rest undisturbed, / at ease, with no one to scare him.
30:11 For I am with you, to save you, the LORD declares. / I will finish off all the nations / where I have scattered you, / but I will not finish you off. / I will discipline you fairly, / and I will not simply let you off.
30:12 For the LORD says this: / Your fracture is past healing; / your wound is severe.
30:13 No one takes up your case to bind your sore; / there is no remedy, no cure for you.
30:14 All your lovers have forgotten you; / they do not come looking for you. / For I have struck you as an enemy strikes, / with the punishment a cruel man gives, / because of how great your guilt is, / how many your sins.
30:15 Why cry out about your fracture? / Your pain cannot be healed. / Because of how great your guilt is, / how many your sins, / I have done these things to you.
30:16 So all who eat you up will be eaten up, / and all your enemies will go into captivity, every last one. / Those who loot you will be looted, / and all who rob you I will hand over to be robbed.
30:17 For I will make new skin grow for you, / and I will heal you of your wounds, the LORD declares, / because they have called you an outcast: / "That is Zion; no one cares about her."
30:18 The LORD says this: / I am about to turn the fortunes of Jacob's tents around, / and I will have pity on his homes. / The city will be rebuilt on its ruin heap, / and the citadel will stand in its old place.
30:19 Thanksgiving will come from them, / and the sound of people laughing. / I will make them many, and they will not be few; / I will give them honor, and they will not be small.
30:20 His children will be as they were long ago; / his community will be set firm before me, / and I will punish all who oppress him.
30:21 His leader will be one of his own; / his ruler will come out from among him. / I will let him come near, and he will approach me, / for who would put his own heart on the line / to come close to me? the LORD declares.
30:22 And you will be my people, / and I will be your God.
30:23 Look! The storm of the LORD! / Fury has gone out, / a whirling storm; / it will burst on the heads of the wicked.
30:24 The LORD's burning anger will not turn back / until he has done and carried out / the plans of his heart. / In the last days you will understand it.
31:1 At that time, the LORD declares, I will be the God of all the clans of Israel, and they will be my people.
## Love that lasts forever
31:2 The LORD says this: / A people who survived the sword / found favor in the wilderness, / when I went to bring Israel rest.
31:3 From far away the LORD appeared to me: / I have loved you with a love that lasts forever; / that is why I have kept drawing you with loyal love.
31:4 I will build you up again, and you will be rebuilt, / virgin Israel. / Once more you will take up your tambourines / and go out to the dance of the merrymakers.
31:5 Once more you will plant vineyards / on the hills of Samaria. / The planters will plant / and put the fruit to everyday use.
31:6 Yes, there is a day when watchmen will call out / in Ephraim's hill country, / "Get up, let us go up to Zion, / to the LORD our God."
31:7 For the LORD says this: / Sing for joy for Jacob; / shout aloud at the head of the nations. / Make it heard, give praise and say, / "LORD, save your people, / the remnant of Israel."
31:8 Look, I am bringing them from the land of the north; / I will gather them from the far edges of the earth. / Among them are the blind and the lame, / the pregnant woman and the one in labor together; / a great crowd will come back here.
31:9 With tears they will come, / and with prayers for mercy I will bring them. / I will lead them to streams of water, / by a straight road where they will not trip. / For I have become a father to Israel, / and Ephraim is my firstborn.
31:10 Hear the word of the LORD, you nations, / and announce it on the islands far away. / Say: "The one who scattered Israel is the one who will gather him, / and he will watch over him as a shepherd watches his flock."
31:11 For the LORD has ransomed Jacob / and redeemed him from a hand too strong for him.
31:12 They will come and sing out on Zion's height; / they will stream to the LORD's good gifts: / to the grain, the new wine and the olive oil, / to the young of the flock and the herd. / Their whole being will be like a watered garden, / and they will never again grow faint.
31:13 Then the virgin will rejoice in the dance, / and young men and old men together. / I will change their mourning into joy. / I will comfort them, and I will make them glad where there was grief.
31:14 I will soak the priests with rich food, / and my people will be filled with my good gifts, the LORD declares.
31:15 The LORD says this: / A voice is heard in Ramah, / mourning and bitter weeping: / Rachel crying over her children. / She refuses to be comforted over her children, / because they are gone.
31:16 The LORD says this: / Stop your voice from weeping / and your eyes from shedding tears, / for your work will be paid, the LORD declares; / they will come back from the enemy's land.
31:17 Hope is waiting for your future, the LORD declares: / the children will come home to their own borders.
31:18 I have heard, I have truly heard Ephraim grieving: / "You disciplined me, and I took the discipline, / like a calf that was never trained. / Turn me back, and I will turn back, / for you are the LORD my God.
31:19 For after my turning, I was sorry; / after I came to know, I slapped my thigh. / I felt shame, even disgrace, / because I was carrying the reproach of my youth."
31:20 Is Ephraim my treasured son, / the child I take delight in? / Every time I speak against him, / I find I still remember him. / So my insides churn for him; / I will have mercy on him, I truly will, the LORD declares.
31:21 Mark the road for yourself; / set up signposts. / Fix your mind on the highway, / the road you went by. / Turn back, virgin Israel; / turn back to these towns of yours.
31:22 How long will you waver this way and that, / daughter who keeps turning away? / For the LORD has created a new thing in the land: / a woman will surround a man.
## Judah blessed again
31:23 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Once again they will say these words in the land of Judah and in its towns, when I turn their fortunes around: "May the LORD bless you, home of righteousness, holy mountain!"
31:24 Judah and all its towns will live there together, the farmers and those who travel with the flocks.
31:25 For I will give the weary their fill to drink, and I will fill everyone who is faint.
31:26 At this I woke up and looked around. My sleep had been sweet to me.
31:27 Look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when I will sow seed in the house of Israel and the house of Judah: the seed of people and the seed of animals.
31:28 Just as I watched over them to pull up and pull down, to demolish and destroy and to bring harm, so I will watch over them to build and to plant, the LORD declares.
31:29 In those days people will no longer say, "The fathers ate sour grapes, and the children's teeth go numb."
31:30 No, each one will die for his own guilt. Whoever eats the sour grapes, it is his own teeth that will go numb.
## A new covenant
31:31 Look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when I will cut a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah.
31:32 It will not be like the covenant I cut with their ancestors on the day I took hold of their hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt. They broke that covenant of mine, though I had married them, the LORD declares.
31:33 This is the covenant I will cut with the house of Israel after those days, the LORD declares: I will put my law inside them, and on their hearts I will write it. I will be their God, and they will be my people.
31:34 They will not teach any more, one person his neighbor and another his brother, saying, "Know the LORD!" Because they will all know me, from the smallest of them to the greatest, the LORD declares. For I will forgive their guilt, and their sin I will remember no more.
31:35 The LORD says this: / He gives the sun to light the day. / He has fixed the rules for the moon and stars to light the night. / He stirs the sea, and its waves roar. / The LORD of Armies is his name.
31:36 If these rules ever leave my sight, the LORD declares, / then the offspring of Israel too will stop / being a nation before me for all time.
31:37 The LORD says this: / If anyone can measure the sky above / and search out the earth's foundations below, / then I too will reject all the offspring of Israel / for all that they have done, the LORD declares.
## The city rebuilt for the LORD
31:38 Days are coming, the LORD declares, when the city will be built again for the LORD, from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.
31:39 The measuring line will go on from there, opposite it, to the hill of Gareb, and then turn around to Goah.
31:40 The whole valley of the corpses and the fat ashes, and all the fields as far as the Kidron Valley, to the corner of the Horse Gate on the east, will be holy to the LORD. It will never again be pulled up or demolished, ever.
