## The temple sermon put on trial
26:1 When Jehoiakim son of Josiah had just become king of Judah, this word came from the LORD:
26:2 "The LORD says this: Stand in the courtyard of the LORD's house and speak to all Judah's towns as they come to bow down in the LORD's house. Tell them every word I have commanded you to speak to them. Do not hold back a single word.
26:3 Perhaps they will listen and turn back, each one from his evil way. Then I will change my mind about the evil I am planning to bring on them because their deeds are evil.
26:4 You are to say to them, 'The LORD says this: Suppose you will not listen to me, to walk in my law that I set before you,
26:5 and to listen to the words of my servants the prophets, whom I keep sending to you, getting up early to send them, though you have not listened.
26:6 Then I will make this house like Shiloh, and I will make this city a word that every nation on earth uses to curse.'"
26:7 Jeremiah spoke these words in the LORD's house, and the priests, the prophets and all the people heard him.
26:8 Jeremiah finished speaking everything the LORD had commanded him to speak to all the people. Then the priests, the prophets and all the people grabbed him. "You will die for this!" they said.
26:9 "Why have you prophesied in the LORD's name that this house will be like Shiloh, and this city will be a ruin with no one living in it?" And all the people pressed in on Jeremiah in the LORD's house.
26:10 The officials of Judah heard these words. They came up from the king's house to the LORD's house and sat down at the entrance of the LORD's New Gate.
26:11 Then the priests and the prophets spoke to the officials and to all the people. "The sentence for this man is death," they said. "He prophesied against this city. You heard him with your own ears."
26:12 Then Jeremiah spoke to all the officials and all the people. "It was the LORD who sent me," he said, "to prophesy every word you have heard against this house and this city.
26:13 So now, put your ways and your deeds right, and listen to the voice of the LORD your God. Then the LORD will change his mind about the evil he has spoken against you.
26:14 As for me, here I am in your power. Treat me however you think is good and fair.
26:15 But be very sure of this: if you kill me, you are putting innocent blood on yourselves, on this city and on everyone who lives in it. The LORD truly did send me to you, to speak all these words into your ears."
26:16 Then the officials and all the people said to the priests and the prophets, "There is no death sentence for this man. It was in the name of the LORD our God that he spoke to us."
26:17 Then certain men from among the land's elders stood up and spoke to the whole gathering of the people.
26:18 "Micah the Morashtite was prophesying in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah," they said. "He said to all the people of Judah, 'The LORD of Armies says this: Zion will be plowed like a field. Jerusalem will be heaps of rubble, and the mountain of the house will be high ground in a forest.'
26:19 Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah put him to death? Did Hezekiah not fear the LORD and beg the LORD for mercy? And the LORD changed his mind about the evil he had spoken against them. But we are about to do great evil against our own lives."
26:20 There was also a man who prophesied in the LORD's name, Uriah son of Shemaiah, from Kiriath-jearim. He prophesied against this city and this land in words just like Jeremiah's.
26:21 King Jehoiakim, all his warriors and all the officials heard his words, and the king tried to have him killed. When Uriah heard of it, he was afraid. He ran away and went to Egypt.
26:22 King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt: Elnathan son of Achbor, and other men with him, went to Egypt.
26:23 They fetched Uriah out of Egypt and brought him before King Jehoiakim. The king cut him down with the sword and had his corpse thrown among the graves of ordinary people.
26:24 But the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so they did not give him into the people's hands to be killed.
## The yoke of Babylon
27:1 When Zedekiah son of Josiah had just become king of Judah, this word came to Jeremiah from the LORD:
27:2 The LORD said this to me: "Make yourself the straps and bars of a yoke and wear them on your neck.
27:3 Then send them to the king of Edom, the king of Moab, the king of the Ammonites, the king of Tyre and the king of Sidon, by the hand of the messengers who are coming to Jerusalem, to Zedekiah king of Judah.
27:4 Give them this order for their masters: 'The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this. Here is what you must say to your masters:
27:5 By my great strength and my outstretched arm I made the earth, and the people and the animals on the face of the earth. I give it to whoever is right in my eyes.
27:6 And now I myself have given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, my servant. I have even given him the wild animals, to serve him.
27:7 All the nations will serve him, and his son, and his son's son, until the time comes for his own land too. Then many nations and great kings will make him their slave.
27:8 If any nation or kingdom will not serve him, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and will not put its neck into the yoke of the king of Babylon, I will deal with that nation by sword, famine and disease, the LORD declares, until I have used his hand to finish them.
27:9 As for you, do not listen to your prophets, your diviners, your dreams, your fortune-tellers or your sorcerers, who keep saying to you, "You will not serve the king of Babylon."
27:10 They are prophesying a lie to you, and the result will be that you are taken far from your land. I will drive you out, and you will perish.
27:11 But the nation that brings its neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serves him, I will leave on its own land, the LORD declares. It will farm the land and live there.'"
27:12 I spoke to Zedekiah king of Judah in the same words. "Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon," I said. "Serve him and his people, and live.
27:13 Why should you and your people die by sword, famine and disease, as the LORD has said about any nation that will not serve the king of Babylon?
27:14 Do not listen to the words of the prophets who keep telling you, 'You will not serve the king of Babylon.' It is a lie they are prophesying to you.
27:15 'I did not send them,' the LORD declares. 'They are prophesying a lie in my name, and the result will be that I drive you out and you perish, you and the prophets who prophesy to you.'"
27:16 Then I spoke to the priests and to all this people. "The LORD says this," I said. "Do not listen to the words of your prophets who prophesy to you, 'Look, the objects of the LORD's house will be brought back from Babylon now, very soon.' It is a lie they are prophesying to you.
27:17 Do not listen to them. Serve the king of Babylon and stay alive. Why let this city become a heap of ruins?
27:18 If they are prophets, and if the word of the LORD is with them, let them press the LORD of Armies with their prayers, so that the objects left in the LORD's house, in the house of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem do not go to Babylon.
27:19 For the LORD of Armies says this about the pillars, the Sea, the stands and the rest of the objects left in this city,
27:20 the ones Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he took Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah away from Jerusalem as a captive to Babylon, with all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem.
27:21 Yes, the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this about the objects left in the LORD's house, in the house of the king of Judah and in Jerusalem:
27:22 'They will be taken to Babylon, and there they will stay until the day I call for them, the LORD declares. Then I will bring them up and return them to this place.'"
## Hananiah breaks the yoke
28:1 That same year, at the start of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, in the fifth month, the prophet Hananiah son of Azzur, from Gibeon, spoke to me in the LORD's house, in front of the priests and all the people.
28:2 "The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this," he said. "I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon.
28:3 Before two full years are over, I will bring back to this place all the objects of the LORD's house that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took from this place and carried to Babylon.
28:4 And I will bring back to this place Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, with all the exiles of Judah who went to Babylon, the LORD declares, for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon."
28:5 Then the prophet Jeremiah answered the prophet Hananiah in front of the priests and all the people standing in the LORD's house.
28:6 "Amen!" the prophet Jeremiah said. "May the LORD do it! May the LORD make the words you have prophesied come true, and bring the objects of the LORD's house and all the exiles back from Babylon to this place.
28:7 Only hear this word that I am speaking into your ears and into the ears of all the people.
28:8 The prophets who came before me and before you, from long ago, prophesied war, disaster and disease against many lands and against great kingdoms.
28:9 As for the prophet who prophesies peace: when that prophet's word comes true, then he will be known as the prophet the LORD truly sent."
28:10 Then the prophet Hananiah lifted the bar from the prophet Jeremiah's neck and smashed it.
28:11 In front of all the people Hananiah said, "The LORD says this: This is how I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, before two full years are over, from off the neck of all the nations." And the prophet Jeremiah went his way.
28:12 After the prophet Hananiah had smashed the bar from the prophet Jeremiah's neck, the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
28:13 "Go and tell Hananiah, 'The LORD says this: You have broken bars of wood, but you will make bars of iron in their place.'
28:14 For the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: I have put a yoke of iron on the neck of all these nations, to serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and they will serve him. I have even given him the wild animals."
28:15 Then the prophet Jeremiah said to the prophet Hananiah, "Listen, Hananiah! The LORD did not send you. You have made this people trust in a lie.
28:16 So the LORD says this: I am about to send you off the face of the earth. This year you will die, because you have spoken rebellion against the LORD."
28:17 And the prophet Hananiah died that same year, in the seventh month.
## A letter to the exiles
29:1 Here are the words of the letter the prophet Jeremiah sent from Jerusalem. It went to the elders who were still left among the exiles, to the priests, to the prophets and to all the people Nebuchadnezzar had carried away into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon.
29:2 It was after King Jeconiah had gone out from Jerusalem, with the queen mother, the court officials, the officials of Judah and Jerusalem, the craftsmen and the smiths.
29:3 He sent it in the care of Elasah son of Shaphan and Gemariah son of Hilkiah, whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, in Babylon. The letter said:
29:4 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this to all the exiles I sent into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon:
29:5 Build houses to live in. Plant gardens and eat their fruit.
29:6 Marry wives and have sons and daughters. Get wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, so that they can bear sons and daughters. Grow in number there; do not shrink.
29:7 Seek the peace of the city I sent you to as exiles, and pray for it to the LORD. When it has peace, you will have peace.
29:8 For the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Do not let your prophets who are among you, or your diviners, deceive you. Do not listen to the dreams you are getting them to dream.
29:9 It is a lie they are prophesying to you in my name. I did not send them, the LORD declares.
29:10 For the LORD says this: When the seventy years for Babylon are over, I will turn my attention to you. I will make my good word to you stand, and bring you back to this place.
29:11 I know the plans I am making for you, the LORD declares. They are plans for peace, not for disaster, to give you a future and a hope.
29:12 You will call to me, come and pray to me, and I will hear you.
29:13 You will look for me and you will find me, because you will search for me with all your heart.
29:14 I will let you find me, the LORD declares. I will turn your fortunes around. I will gather you from all the nations and all the places where I have driven you, the LORD declares, and I will bring you back to the place I sent you away from into exile.
29:15 You say, "The LORD has raised up prophets for us in Babylon."
29:16 But the LORD says this about the king who sits on David's throne, and about all the people living in this city, your relatives who did not go out with you into exile:
29:17 The LORD of Armies says this: Look, I am sending against them the sword, famine and disease. I will make them like disgusting figs, too bad to eat.
29:18 I will chase them with sword, famine and disease. I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, and a curse, a shock, a thing to hiss at and a disgrace among all the nations where I have driven them,
29:19 because they did not listen to my words, the LORD declares, the words I sent to them by my servants the prophets, getting up early to send them. But you would not listen, the LORD declares.
29:20 So you, all the exiles I sent away from Jerusalem to Babylon, hear the word of the LORD.
29:21 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this about Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, who are prophesying a lie to you in my name: I am about to hand them over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and he will kill them before your eyes.
29:22 From them a curse will be taken up by all the exiles of Judah in Babylon: "May the LORD make you like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire."
29:23 For they have done an outrage in Israel. They committed adultery with their neighbors' wives, and in my name they spoke lying words that I did not command them. I am the one who knows, and I am a witness, the LORD declares.
29:24 To Shemaiah the Nehelamite you are to say:
29:25 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: You are the one who sent letters in your own name to all the people in Jerusalem, to the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah and to all the priests. In them you said,
29:26 "The LORD has made you priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, to be an overseer in the LORD's house over every man who raves and plays the prophet. You should put him in the stocks and the neck iron.
29:27 So now, why have you not scolded Jeremiah of Anathoth, who plays the prophet among you?
29:28 For he has sent us word in Babylon: 'It will be long. Build houses to live in. Plant gardens and eat their fruit.'"
29:29 Zephaniah the priest read this letter aloud in the hearing of the prophet Jeremiah.
29:30 Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
29:31 Send word to all the exiles: The LORD says this about Shemaiah the Nehelamite: Because Shemaiah has prophesied to you when I did not send him, and has made you trust in a lie,
29:32 the LORD says this: I am going to punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite and his descendants. None of his line will live on among this people, and he will not see the good I am going to do for my people, the LORD declares, because he has spoken rebellion against the LORD.
## The book of comfort: Jacob's trouble and healing
30:1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:
30:2 The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Write down on a scroll every word I have spoken to you.
30:3 For look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when I will turn the fortunes of my people Israel and Judah around, says the LORD. I will bring them back to the land I gave their ancestors, and they will take possession of it.
30:4 Here are the words the LORD spoke about Israel and about Judah.
30:5 For the LORD says this: / We have heard a cry of terror, / of dread, and there is no peace.
30:6 Ask, and see: / can a male give birth? / Why then do I see every strong man / clutching his sides like a woman giving birth, / and every face gone sickly pale?
30:7 Alas! That day is great; / there is none like it. / It is Jacob's time of trouble, / yet out of it he will be saved.
30:8 On that day, the LORD of Armies declares, I will break his yoke off your neck and tear your straps apart, and strangers will no longer make him their slave.
30:9 They will serve the LORD their God and David their king, whom I will raise up for them.
30:10 So you, Jacob my servant, do not be afraid, the LORD declares; / do not be terrified, Israel. / For I am about to save you from far away, / and your descendants from the land where they are captives. / Jacob will come back and be at peace and at ease, / with no one to frighten him.
30:11 For I am with you to save you, the LORD declares. / I will make a full end of all the nations / where I have scattered you, / but I will not make a full end of you. / I will discipline you with justice; / I will not let you off as innocent.
30:12 For the LORD says this: / Your fracture is past healing; / your wound is sore.
30:13 No one takes up your case to bind your sore; / there is no healing, no new skin for you.
30:14 All your lovers have forgotten you; / they do not come looking for you. / For I have struck you as an enemy strikes, / with the punishment a cruel man gives, / because of how great your guilt is, / how many your sins.
30:15 Why cry out about your fracture? / Your pain cannot be healed. / Because of how great your guilt is, / how many your sins, / I have done these things to you.
30:16 So all who eat you up will be eaten up, / and all your foes, every one of them, will go into captivity. / Those who loot you will be looted, / and all who rob you I will hand over to be robbed.
30:17 For I will make new skin grow for you, / and I will heal you of your wounds, the LORD declares, / because they have called you an outcast: / "That is Zion; no one cares about her."
30:18 The LORD says this: / I am about to turn the fortunes of Jacob's tents around, / and I will have pity on his homes. / The city will be rebuilt on its mound, / and the fortress will sit where it rightly belongs.
30:19 Thanksgiving will come from them, / and the sound of people laughing. / I will make them many, and they will not be few; / I will give them honor, and they will not be small.
30:20 His children will be as in days of old; / his community will be set firm before me, / and I will punish all who oppress him.
30:21 His prince will come from his own people; / his ruler will go out from his midst. / I will let him come near, and he will approach me, / for who is the one who would risk his life / to come close to me? the LORD declares.
30:22 And you will be my people, / and I will be your God.
30:23 Look! The storm of the LORD! / Fury has gone out, / a whirling storm; / it will burst on the heads of the wicked.
30:24 The LORD's burning anger will not turn back / until he has done and carried out / the plans of his heart. / In the last days you will understand it.
31:1 At that time, the LORD declares, I will be the God of all the clans of Israel, and they will be my people.
## Love that lasts forever
31:2 The LORD says this: / A people who survived the sword / found favor in the wilderness, / when I went to bring Israel rest.
31:3 From far away the LORD appeared to me: / I have loved you with a love that lasts forever; / that is why I have kept drawing you with loyal love.
31:4 Again I will build you, and you will be built, / virgin Israel. / Again you will put on your tambourines / and go out in the dances of the merrymakers.
31:5 Again you will plant vineyards / on the hills of Samaria. / The planters will plant / and use the fruit as their own.
31:6 For a day is coming when watchmen will call out / on the hills of Ephraim, / "Get up, let us go up to Zion, / to the LORD our God."
31:7 For the LORD says this: / Sing for joy for Jacob; / shout aloud for the chief of the nations. / Make it heard, give praise and say, / "LORD, save your people, / the remnant of Israel."
31:8 Look, I am bringing them from the land of the north; / I will gather them from the far edges of the earth. / Among them are the blind and the lame, / the pregnant woman and the one in labor together; / a great crowd will come back here.
31:9 They will come with weeping; / pleading for mercy, they will be led by me. / I will guide them beside streams of water, / on a straight road where they will not stumble. / For I am a father to Israel, / and Ephraim is my firstborn.
31:10 Hear the word of the LORD, you nations, / and tell it in the islands far away. / Say: "The one who scattered Israel will gather him, / and keep him as a shepherd keeps his flock."
31:11 For the LORD has ransomed Jacob / and redeemed him from the hand of one stronger than he.
31:12 They will come and sing out on Zion's height; / they will stream to the LORD's good gifts: / to the grain, the new wine and the olive oil, / to the young of the flock and the herd. / Their life will be like a watered garden, / and they will never again grow faint.
31:13 Then the virgin will rejoice in the dance, / and young men and old men together. / I will turn their mourning to happiness; / I will comfort them and make them glad after their sorrow.
31:14 I will soak the priests with rich food, / and my people will be filled with my good gifts, the LORD declares.
31:15 The LORD says this: / A voice is heard in Ramah, / lament and bitter crying: / Rachel weeping for her children; / she refuses to be comforted for her children, / because they are gone.
31:16 The LORD says this: / Hold back your voice from weeping / and your eyes from tears, / for your work will be paid, the LORD declares; / they will come back from the enemy's land.
31:17 There is hope for your future, the LORD declares; / the children will come home to their own borders.
31:18 I have heard, I have truly heard Ephraim grieving: / "You disciplined me, and I took the discipline, / like a calf not trained to the yoke. / Turn me back, and I will turn back, / for you are the LORD my God.
31:19 For after my turning, I was sorry; / after I came to know, I struck my thigh. / I was ashamed and humiliated, / because I bore the disgrace of my youth."
31:20 Is Ephraim my dear son? / Is he the child I delight in? / For whenever I speak against him, / I still remember him, I truly do. / That is why my insides churn for him; / I will surely have mercy on him, the LORD declares.
31:21 Set up road markers for yourself; / put up signposts. / Set your mind on the highway, / the road you went by. / Turn back, virgin Israel; / turn back to these towns of yours.
31:22 How long will you waver this way and that, / daughter who keeps turning away? / For the LORD has created a new thing in the land: / a woman will surround a man.
## Judah blessed again
31:23 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Once again they will say these words in the land of Judah and in its towns, when I turn their fortunes around: "May the LORD bless you, home of righteousness, holy mountain!"
31:24 Judah and all its towns will live there together, the farmers and those who travel with the flocks.
31:25 For I will give the weary their fill to drink, and I will fill everyone who is faint.
31:26 Then I woke and looked around; my sleep had been sweet.
31:27 Look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when I will sow seed in the house of Israel and the house of Judah: the seed of people and the seed of animals.
31:28 Just as I watched over them to uproot and to pull down, to tear down, to destroy and to bring harm, so I will watch over them to build and to plant, the LORD declares.
31:29 In those days people will no longer say, "The fathers ate sour grapes, and the children's teeth go numb."
31:30 No, each one will die for his own guilt. Whoever eats the sour grapes, it is his own teeth that will go numb.
## A new covenant
31:31 Look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when I will cut a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah.
31:32 It will not be like the covenant I cut with their ancestors when I took hold of their hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt. They broke that covenant of mine, though I had married them, the LORD declares.
31:33 For this is the covenant I will cut with the house of Israel after those days, the LORD declares: I will put my law inside them, and on their hearts I will write it. I will be their God, and they will be my people.
31:34 They will not teach any more, one person his neighbor and another his brother, saying, "Know the LORD!" Because they will all know me, from the smallest of them to the greatest, the LORD declares. For I will forgive their guilt, and their sin I will remember no more.
31:35 The LORD says this, / who gives the sun to light the day / and the fixed rules of the moon and stars to light the night, / who stirs up the sea so that its waves roar; / the LORD of Armies is his name:
31:36 If these rules ever vanish from before me, the LORD declares, / then the offspring of Israel too will cease / to be a nation before me for all time.
31:37 The LORD says this: / If the sky above can be measured / and the earth's foundations below explored, / then I too will reject all the offspring of Israel / for all that they have done, the LORD declares.
## The city rebuilt for the LORD
31:38 Look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when the city will be rebuilt for the LORD, from the Tower of Hananel to the Corner Gate.
31:39 The measuring line will go out again, straight ahead to the hill of Gareb, and then turn toward Goah.
31:40 The whole valley of the corpses and the fat ashes, and all the fields as far as the Kidron Valley, to the corner of the Horse Gate on the east, will be holy to the LORD. It will never again be uprooted or torn down, ever.
