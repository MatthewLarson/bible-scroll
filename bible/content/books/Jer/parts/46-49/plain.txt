## Egypt routed at Carchemish
46:1 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet about the nations.
46:2 For Egypt. About the army of Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt. It was at the Euphrates River, at Carchemish, and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck it down in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah.
46:3 Line up the shields, the small and the large, / and move up to battle!
46:4 Hitch up the horses, / and into the saddle, you riders! / Stand in line in your helmets! / Burnish the lances; / get into your coats of mail!
46:5 What is this I see? / They are shattered; they fall back. / Their warriors are battered down. / They break and run / and never turn around. / Terror all around, / the LORD declares.
46:6 Not even the swift can run, / not even the warrior escape. / Up north, beside the Euphrates River, / they trip and fall.
46:7 Who is this rising like the Nile, / his waters heaving like the rivers?
46:8 Egypt rises like the Nile, / its waters heaving like the rivers. / It said, "I will rise; I will cover the land; / I will destroy towns and the people living in them."
46:9 Up, you horses! / Race wildly, you chariots! / Out with the warriors: / men of Cush and Put who grip the shield, / Ludites who grip and bend the bow.
46:10 That day belongs to the Lord GOD of Armies, / a day of vengeance, to get vengeance on his foes. / The sword will eat until it is full / and drink its fill of their blood. / For the Lord GOD of Armies has a sacrifice / in the land of the north, by the Euphrates River.
46:11 Go up to Gilead and get balm, / you virgin, Daughter Egypt! / It is useless, all the cures you try; / no wound of yours will close.
46:12 Nations have heard how you are disgraced; / the whole earth is full of your screaming. / One warrior trips on another, / and the two of them go down together.
## Nebuchadnezzar against Egypt
46:13 The word the LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet, about how Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon would come and strike the land of Egypt.
46:14 Announce it in Egypt; / make it heard in Migdol; / make it heard in Memphis and in Tahpanhes. / Say: "Get in position! Get ready! / The sword is eating everything around you."
46:15 Why are your strong ones swept away? / Not one stood firm, for the LORD pushed him down.
46:16 He makes many stumble; / they fall, one man against the next. / They say, "Up! Let's go back to our own people, / to the land of our birth, / away from the cruel sword."
46:17 There they cried, "Pharaoh king of Egypt? / Just noise! He let the set time slip by."
46:18 As I live, the King declares, / whose name is the LORD of Armies: / one is coming like Tabor among the mountains, / like Carmel at the edge of the sea.
46:19 Pack what you need for exile, / Daughter Egypt, you who live there! / For Memphis will become a waste, / a burned ruin where no one lives.
46:20 Egypt is a pretty, pretty heifer; / a gadfly from the north is coming, it is coming!
46:21 Even her hired soldiers among her / are like stall-fed calves. / But they too wheel around and bolt, every one; / no one stands firm, / for their day of disaster has come over them, / the time of their punishment.
46:22 Her voice is like a snake that slides away, / for they march in with an army; / they come at her with axes, / like woodcutters.
46:23 They have cut down her forest, the LORD declares, / a forest no one could explore; / there are more of them than locusts, / far too many to count.
46:24 Daughter Egypt is shamed; / she is handed over to a northern people.
46:25 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, has said: "I am about to call to account Amon of Thebes, and Pharaoh, and Egypt with her gods and her kings, Pharaoh and everyone who trusts in him.
46:26 I will hand them over to those who seek their lives, to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his servants. After this, people will live there again as they did long ago, the LORD declares."
## Do not be afraid, Jacob
46:27 As for you, Jacob my servant, do not be afraid; / Israel, do not be shattered. / See, I am the one who will save you from far away, / and your children from the land of their captivity. / Jacob will come back and rest undisturbed, / at ease, with no one to scare him.
46:28 You, Jacob my servant, do not be afraid, the LORD declares, / for I am with you. / I will finish off all the nations / where I have driven you, / but I will not finish you off. / I will discipline you fairly, / and I will not simply let you off.
## The sword against Philistia
47:1 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet about the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza.
47:2 The LORD says this: / Look! Water is rising in the north / and turning into a stream in flood. / It will sweep over the land and everything in it, / over towns and the people who live there. / Human beings will scream, / and all who live in the land will howl.
47:3 At the sound of his stallions' pounding hooves, / the roar of his chariots, / the din of his wheels, / fathers do not turn around for their sons; / their hands have gone weak.
47:4 For the day has come / to ravage all the Philistines, / to cut off every last helper / left to Tyre and Sidon. / The LORD is ravaging the Philistines, / what is left of the people from the island of Caphtor.
47:5 Baldness has come on Gaza; / Ashkelon is silenced. / You who are left in their valley, / how long will you gash yourselves?
47:6 Alas, sword of the LORD! / How long before you are quiet? / Draw yourself back into your sheath; / settle down and be still.
47:7 How can you be quiet, / when the LORD has given it orders? / Against Ashkelon, against the shore of the sea, / that is where he has sent it.
## Moab's towns fall
48:1 About Moab. / The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: / Woe to Nebo: it is ravaged! / Kiriathaim is shamed; it is captured. / The high fort is shamed and shattered.
48:2 No one praises Moab anymore. / In Heshbon they plotted harm against her: / "Come on, let's wipe her out as a nation!" / You too, Madmen, will be silenced; / the sword will come after you.
48:3 Hear the screaming from Horonaim: / "Ravaging and great disaster!"
48:4 Moab is broken; / her little ones have raised a scream.
48:5 For up the climb to Luhith / they go up crying, crying as they go; / for on the way down to Horonaim / people hear the anguished scream of disaster.
48:6 Flee! Get out alive! / Be like a juniper out in the wilderness.
48:7 Since you put your trust in what you made and in your treasures, / you too will be captured, / and Chemosh will go out into exile, / his priests and his officials together.
48:8 The ravager will come to every town; / not one town will get away. / The valley will be lost / and the plateau wiped out, / just as the LORD has said.
48:9 Give Moab wings, / for she must fly away. / Her towns will become a waste, / with no one living in them.
48:10 Cursed is the one who does the LORD's work halfheartedly, / and cursed is the one who keeps his sword clean of blood.
## Wine left on its dregs
48:11 From his youth Moab has lived at ease, / lying undisturbed on his dregs. / No one has emptied him from one jar to the next; / he has never gone off into exile. / That is why he keeps the flavor he had, / and his smell has not changed.
48:12 That is why the days are coming, the LORD declares, / when I will send him men who tilt wine jars. / They will tilt him, / pour out his vessels / and break their jars to pieces.
48:13 Then Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, / just as the house of Israel was ashamed / of Bethel, the one they relied on.
48:14 How can you say, "We are fighters, / men with the strength for war"?
48:15 Moab is ravaged; someone has come up against its towns, / and the pick of its young men have gone down to the slaughter, / the King declares, whose name is the LORD of Armies.
48:16 Moab's disaster is about to arrive; / the harm coming to it hurries on, fast.
48:17 Shake your heads over it, all you neighbors around it, / all who know its name. / Say, "How it is broken, the staff of strength, / the rod of splendor!"
## Come down, Dibon
48:18 Get down from your place of honor; / sit in the thirsty dust, / Daughter Dibon, you who live there. / For the ravager of Moab has come up against you; / he has torn down your strongholds.
48:19 Go out to the road, Aroer, you who live there; / stand and look out. / Question the man on the run, the woman who got away: / "What has happened?"
48:20 "Moab is shamed; it is shattered!" / Howl and scream! / Spread the news along the Arnon: / Moab is ravaged!
48:21 Judgment has come to the plateau country: to Holon, to Jahzah and to Mephaath,
48:22 to Dibon, to Nebo and to Beth-diblathaim,
48:23 to Kiriathaim, to Beth-gamul and to Beth-meon,
48:24 to Kerioth and to Bozrah, and to all the towns of the land of Moab, far and near.
48:25 Moab's horn is cut off, / and its arm is broken, / the LORD declares.
48:26 Get him drunk, / for he made himself great against the LORD. / Moab will splash in his own vomit, / and he too will become a laughingstock.
48:27 Wasn't Israel the laughingstock for you? / Had he been caught with thieves, / that every time you talked about him / you would toss your head?
48:28 Abandon the towns and go live in the crags, / people of Moab. / Be like the dove that makes her nest / in the walls beyond the mouth of a chasm.
## Moab's pride and the prophet's grief
48:29 We have heard of Moab's pride, / proud beyond measure: / its loftiness, its pride, its proudness, / and the haughtiness of its heart.
48:30 I know its insolence myself, the LORD declares, / and it rings false; / its boasts ring false, / and what they do rings false.
48:31 That is why I howl for Moab; / for all of Moab I cry out, / and moaning goes up for the men of Kir-heres.
48:32 More than Jazer's crying / I cry for you, vine of Sibmah. / Your shoots crossed the sea; / they reached as far as the sea of Jazer. / On your summer fruit and your grape harvest / the ravager has fallen.
48:33 Happiness and delight are gathered up from the garden land, / from the land of Moab. / I have cut off the wine from the vats; / no one treads them with a shout. / The shout is no harvest shout.
48:34 From the scream of Heshbon to Elealeh, / all the way to Jahaz, they raise their voice, / from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah. / For even the waters of Nimrim / have become wasteland.
48:35 I will put an end in Moab, the LORD declares, / to the one who makes offerings on the high place / and burns incense to his gods.
48:36 That is why my heart throbs over Moab / like flutes; / my heart throbs like flutes / over the men of Kir-heres. / That is why the riches they had made are lost.
48:37 For every head is shaved bare / and every beard clipped short. / On every hand there are gashes, / and around the waist, sackcloth.
48:38 On all Moab's rooftops and in its plazas / all of it is mourning, / for I have broken Moab / like a jar no one wants, / the LORD declares.
48:39 How it is shattered! How they howl! / How Moab turns its back in shame! / Moab will be a laughingstock / and a terror to everyone around it.
## No escape for Moab
48:40 For the LORD says this: / See, like an eagle he swoops down / and spreads his wings out over Moab.
48:41 Kerioth is taken, / and the strongholds are seized. / On that day Moab's warriors will have hearts / like a woman's heart when her pains begin.
48:42 Moab will be wiped out as a people, / because he made himself great against the LORD.
48:43 Terror, trench and trap / are over you, you who live in Moab, / the LORD declares.
48:44 Whoever runs from the terror / will fall into the trench, / and whoever climbs up out of the trench / will be caught in the trap. / For I will bring on Moab / the year of their punishment, / the LORD declares.
48:45 In the shadow of Heshbon / those who fled stand without strength. / For fire went out from Heshbon, / a flame from the midst of Sihon. / It ate up the brow of Moab, / the skulls of the sons of noise.
48:46 Woe to you, Moab! / The people of Chemosh are lost, / for your sons are taken off into captivity, / and your daughters into captivity.
48:47 Yet I will turn Moab's fortunes around / in the last days, the LORD declares. / Thus far the judgment on Moab.
## Ammon and Milcom
49:1 About the Ammonites. / The LORD says this: / Has Israel no sons? / Has he no heir? / Then why has Milcom become Gad's heir, / and why do his people live in its towns?
49:2 So the days are coming, the LORD declares, / when I will make the battle cry heard / against Rabbah of the Ammonites. / It will become a heap of ruins, / and its villages will be set on fire. / Then Israel will drive out those who drove him out, / the LORD has said.
49:3 Howl, Heshbon, for Ai is ravaged! / Cry out, daughters of Rabbah! / Put on sackcloth and mourn; / run back and forth among the walls. / For Milcom will go into exile, / his priests and his officials together.
49:4 Why do you boast of valleys, / of your valley that flows, / you daughter who turns away, / who trusts in her treasures and says, / "Who will come against me?"
49:5 See, I will bring terror on you, / the Lord GOD of Armies declares, / from all around you. / You will be scattered, each running straight ahead, / with no one to gather those who flee.
49:6 But afterward I will turn the fortunes of the Ammonites around, / the LORD declares.
## Edom stripped bare
49:7 About Edom. / The LORD of Armies says this: / Is there no wisdom left in Teman? / Has advice vanished from the discerning? / Has their wisdom gone rotten?
49:8 Run! Turn around! Find deep places to live, / you people of Dedan! / For I will bring Esau's disaster on him, / at the time I call him to account.
49:9 If grape pickers came to you, / wouldn't they leave some grapes to glean? / If thieves came by night, / they would wreck only as much as they wanted.
49:10 But I am the one who has stripped Esau bare; / I have uncovered his hiding places, / and he cannot hide himself. / His children are ravaged, his brothers and his neighbors, / and he is gone.
49:11 Leave your orphans behind; I will keep them alive, / and your widows may put their trust in me.
49:12 For the LORD says this: Look, people whose sentence was not to drink the cup are going to drink it all the same. And you, will you get off free? You will not get off free. You will drink it, without fail.
49:13 For I have sworn by my own self, the LORD declares, that Bozrah will become a thing of horror, of scorn, of ruin and of cursing, and all her towns will lie in ruins forever.
## Brought down from the heights
49:14 Word has come to me from the LORD: / a messenger has gone out among the nations, / "Muster and march against her! / Up, to war!"
49:15 For see, I am making you small among the nations, / looked down on by everyone.
49:16 The dread you spread / and the arrogance of your heart have fooled you, / you who live in the cracks of the rock, / who keep your grip on the high hill. / You may build your nest high, like the eagle, / yet I will bring you down from there, / the LORD declares.
49:17 Edom will become a thing of horror. / All who pass by will be stunned / and will whistle at all its wounds.
49:18 It will be like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah / and the towns around them, the LORD says: / no one will live there, / and no human will stay there even for a while.
49:19 See, like a lion climbing up / out of the Jordan's thickets / toward pasture that never fails, / in a moment I will chase them off it. / Whoever is chosen, him I will set over it. / For who is like me? Who can call me to account? / Is there a shepherd who can face me?
49:20 So listen to the plan the LORD has drawn up against Edom, / the schemes he has worked out against the people of Teman: / the smallest of the flock will surely be dragged away; / their pasture will surely be left in horror over them.
49:21 The earth quakes at the noise of their fall; / there is screaming, and its sound is heard at the Sea of Reeds.
49:22 See, like an eagle he rises and swoops down / and spreads his wings out over Bozrah. / On that day Edom's warriors will have hearts / like a woman's heart when her pains begin.
## Damascus loses heart
49:23 About Damascus. / Hamath and Arpad are shamed, / for the news they have heard is bad. / They melt away; / there is worry on the sea; / it cannot rest.
49:24 Damascus has gone slack; / she turns to run; / terror lays hold of her. / Anguish and labor pains have gripped her / like a woman bearing a child.
49:25 Why was it not abandoned, / the city of praise, / the town of my joy?
49:26 That is why its young men will fall in its plazas, / and all its fighting men will be silenced on that day, / the LORD of Armies declares.
49:27 I will set fire to the wall of Damascus, / and it will eat up the fortresses of Ben-hadad.
## Kedar and Hazor
49:28 About Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck down. / The LORD says this: / Get up and go up against Kedar; / ravage the people of the East!
49:29 Their tents and flocks will be taken, / their tent cloths and all their gear; / their camels they will lead off for themselves. / People will shout at them, / "Terror all around!"
49:30 Run! Wander far away! / Find deep places to live, you who live in Hazor, / the LORD declares, / for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon has drawn up a plan against you; / he has worked out a scheme against you.
49:31 Get up and go up against a nation at ease, / living without a care, the LORD declares, / a nation with no city gates and no bars, / whose people live on their own.
49:32 Their camels will become plunder, / their huge herds loot. / I will scatter to every wind / those who cut the hair at their temples, / and from every side I will bring their disaster on them, / the LORD declares.
49:33 Hazor will be a den for jackals, / a waste for all time. / No one will live there, / and no human will stay there even for a while.
## The bow of Elam
49:34 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet about Elam, early in the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah:
49:35 The LORD of Armies says this: / Look, I am breaking the bow of Elam, / the heart of their strength.
49:36 I will bring four winds on Elam / from the four corners of the sky, / and scatter them to all those winds. / There will not be a nation / that Elam's exiles do not come to.
49:37 I will shatter Elam in front of their enemies, / in front of those who seek their lives. / I will bring harm on them, / my burning anger, the LORD declares; / I will send the sword after them / until I have finished them off.
49:38 I will set my throne in Elam / and destroy from there its king and officials, / the LORD declares.
49:39 But in the last days / I will turn Elam's fortunes around, / the LORD declares.
