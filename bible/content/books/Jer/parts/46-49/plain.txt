## Egypt routed at Carchemish
46:1 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet about the nations.
46:2 About Egypt, and the army of Pharaoh Neco king of Egypt that was at the Euphrates River, at Carchemish. Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck it down in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah.
46:3 Arrange your small shields and your body shields, / and step up for the fight!
46:4 Harness the horses. / Up on them, you horsemen! / Station yourselves in helmets. / Rub the spears bright. / Put on the scale armor!
46:5 Why do I see it? / They are terrified and falling back. / Their warriors are beaten to pieces. / They run and run and do not turn back. / Terror all around, / the LORD declares.
46:6 Let the fast not run away, / and the warrior not escape. / Northward, beside the Euphrates River, / they stumble and fall.
46:7 Who is this, coming up like the Nile, / its waters tossing like rivers?
46:8 It is Egypt that comes up like the Nile, / its waters tossing like rivers. / It said, "I will come up and cover the land. / I will wipe out the city and those who live in it."
46:9 Climb, you horses! Run wild, you chariots! / Let the fighting men march out: / Cush and Put, who hold the shield, / and the Ludites, who take hold of the bow and bend it.
46:10 That day belongs to the Lord GOD of Armies. / It is a day of vengeance, / when he settles accounts with his enemies. / The sword will eat until it is full / and soak itself in their blood. / For the Lord GOD of Armies has a sacrifice / in the northern land, by the Euphrates River.
46:11 Go up to Gilead and fetch balm, / virgin, Daughter Egypt. / You have piled up cures for nothing; / there is no healing for you.
46:12 Nations have heard of your disgrace, / and your shriek fills the earth. / For warrior stumbles over warrior, / and both of them fall together.
## Nebuchadnezzar against Egypt
46:13 The LORD spoke to Jeremiah the prophet about the time when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon would come and strike the land of Egypt.
46:14 Tell it in Egypt, and sound it out in Migdol; / sound it out in Memphis and in Tahpanhes. / Say, "Take your stand and make yourself ready, / for the sword is eating all around you."
46:15 Why was your mighty one thrown down? / He could not stand, for the LORD shoved him down.
46:16 He makes the stumblers many; / each falls against his neighbor. / They say, "Get up! Let's go back to our own people, / to the land of our kin, / away from the crushing sword."
46:17 There they call out, / "Pharaoh king of Egypt is all noise. / He has let the right moment slip by."
46:18 As I live, the King declares, / whose name is the LORD of Armies: / one is coming like Tabor among the mountains, / like Carmel beside the sea.
46:19 Get your gear ready for exile, / you who sit in Daughter Egypt. / For Memphis will become a waste, / burned out, with no one living there.
46:20 Egypt is a lovely, lovely heifer; / a stinging fly from the north is coming, is coming!
46:21 Even her hired soldiers inside her / are like stall-fed calves. / But they too will turn and run, all together; / they will not make a stand. / For the day of their ruin is coming on them, / the time when they are called to account.
46:22 Her sound is like a snake creeping away, / for they march in with an army; / they come at her with axes, / like men who chop wood.
46:23 They will cut down her forest, the LORD declares, / a forest no one can search out. / They are more than the locusts; / there is no counting them.
46:24 Daughter Egypt is put to shame; / she is given into the hand of a northern people.
46:25 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says: "I am about to call Amon of Thebes to account, and Pharaoh, and Egypt with her gods and her kings. I will call Pharaoh to account, and everyone who trusts in him.
46:26 I will hand them over to those who want them dead, to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and his servants. After that, people will settle there again, as in the days of long ago, the LORD declares."
## Do not be afraid, Jacob
46:27 As for you, Jacob my servant, do not be afraid; / Israel, do not be shattered. / See, I am the one who will save you from far away, / and your children from the land of their captivity. / Jacob will come back and rest undisturbed, / at ease, with no one to scare him.
46:28 You, Jacob my servant, do not be afraid, the LORD declares, / for I am with you. / I will finish off all the nations / where I have driven you, / but I will not finish you off. / I will discipline you fairly, / and I will not simply let you off.
## The sword against Philistia
47:1 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet about the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza.
47:2 The LORD says this: / Look, waters are coming up from the north / and will become a flooding stream. / They will wash over the land and all that fills it, / the city and those who live in it. / People will cry out, / and everyone living in the land will howl.
47:3 At the sound of the stamping hooves of his strong horses, / at the rattle of his chariots, / the din of his wheels, / fathers do not turn back for their children; / their hands have gone limp.
47:4 For the day is coming / to ravage all the Philistines, / to cut off from Tyre and Sidon / every survivor who could help. / For the LORD is about to ravage the Philistines, / the remnant of the island of Caphtor.
47:5 Baldness has come to Gaza. / Ashkelon is silent. / Remnant of their valley, / how long will you gash yourselves?
47:6 Alas, sword of the LORD! / How long until you are quiet? / Pull yourself back into your sheath; / rest, and be still.
47:7 How can you be quiet, / when the LORD has given the command? / Against Ashkelon and the coast of the sea, / there he has set it.
## Moab's towns fall
48:1 About Moab. / The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: / Woe to Nebo, for it is ravaged! / Kiriathaim is shamed and taken; / the high fort is shamed and shattered.
48:2 There is no more praise for Moab. / In Heshbon they have planned evil against her: / "Come, let's cut her down till she is a nation no more." / You too, Madmen, will be struck dumb; / the sword is coming after you.
48:3 Hear the scream from Horonaim: / "Ravaging and great disaster!"
48:4 Moab is broken; / her little ones have raised a scream.
48:5 For up the climb to Luhith / they go up crying, crying as they go; / for on the way down to Horonaim / people hear the anguished scream of disaster.
48:6 Run! Save your lives! / Be like a juniper bush in the wilderness.
48:7 Because you trusted in your handiwork and your treasures, / you too will be caught. / Chemosh will go out into exile, / his priests and his officials together.
48:8 A ravager will enter every town, / and no town will slip away. / The valley will perish, / and the tableland will be wiped out, / as the LORD has said.
48:9 Hand Moab wings, / for she will surely fly off. / Her towns will turn into ruins, / with no one living in them.
48:10 Cursed is the one who does the LORD's work in a lazy way, / and cursed is the one who holds his sword back from blood.
## Wine left on its dregs
48:11 Moab has been carefree since he was young, / sitting still on his dregs. / He was never poured from one jar into another, / and he never went off into exile. / So his taste is still in him, / and his scent has not changed.
48:12 So look, days are coming, the LORD declares, / when I will send him men to tip jars. / They will tip him out, / empty his vessels / and smash his jars to pieces.
48:13 Then Moab will be ashamed because of Chemosh, / as the house of Israel was ashamed / because of Bethel, their place of trust.
48:14 How can you say, "We are heroes, / men with strength for the fight"?
48:15 Moab is ravaged, and the invader has come up into its towns. / The best of its young men have gone down to the slaughter, / the King declares, / whose name is the LORD of Armies.
48:16 The disaster of Moab is near; / its calamity is racing in, fast.
48:17 Mourn with him, all of you around him, / all who know his name. / Say, "Look how it is broken, / the staff of strength, the rod of splendor!"
## Come down, Dibon
48:18 Come down from the place of honor / and sit on dry, thirsty ground, / you who live in Daughter Dibon. / For the ravager of Moab has come up against you; / he has torn down your strongholds.
48:19 Take your place beside the road, you who live in Aroer, / and keep watch. / Ask the man who runs and the woman who slips away, / say, "What has happened?"
48:20 "Moab is shamed, for it is shattered!" / Howl and cry out! / Tell it at the Arnon: / Moab is ravaged!
48:21 And judgment has come to the tableland: to Holon, to Jahaz and to Mephaath,
48:22 to Dibon, to Nebo and to Beth-diblathaim,
48:23 to Kiriathaim, to Beth-gamul and to Beth-meon,
48:24 to Kerioth and to Bozrah, and to all the towns of the land of Moab, far and near.
48:25 Moab's horn is cut off, / and its arm is broken, / the LORD declares.
48:26 Make him drunk, / for he has made himself great against the LORD. / Moab will flop around in his own vomit, / and he too will be a laughingstock.
48:27 Was Israel not a laughingstock to you? / Was he caught in the company of thieves, / that whenever you spoke about him / you bobbed your head?
48:28 Leave the towns and settle on the cliff, / you who live in Moab. / Be like the dove that makes her nest / on the sides of the mouth of a gorge.
## Moab's pride and the prophet's grief
48:29 We have heard of Moab's pride, / proud beyond measure: / its loftiness, its pride, its proudness, / and the haughtiness of its heart.
48:30 I know its insolence myself, the LORD declares, / and it rings false; / its boasts ring false, / and what they do rings false.
48:31 That is why I howl for Moab; / for all of Moab I cry out, / and a moan rises for the men of Kir-heres.
48:32 More than Jazer's crying / I cry for you, vine of Sibmah. / Your shoots crossed the sea; / they reached as far as the sea of Jazer. / On your summer fruit and your grape harvest / a ravager has fallen.
48:33 Happiness and delight are gathered up from the garden land, / from the land of Moab. / I have stopped the wine in the vats; / no one treads them with a shout. / The shout is no harvest shout.
48:34 From the scream of Heshbon to Elealeh, / all the way to Jahaz, they raise their voice, / from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah. / For even the waters of Nimrim / have become wasteland.
48:35 I will put an end in Moab, the LORD declares, / to the one who makes offerings on the high place / and burns incense to his gods.
48:36 That is why my heart throbs over Moab / like flutes; / my heart throbs like flutes / over the men of Kir-heres. / That is why the riches they had made are lost.
48:37 For every head is shaved bare / and every beard clipped short. / On every hand there are gashes, / and around the waist, sackcloth.
48:38 On all Moab's rooftops and in its plazas / all of it is mourning, / for I have broken Moab / like a jar no one wants, / the LORD declares.
48:39 How it is shattered! How they howl! / How Moab has turned its back in shame! / Moab will be a laughingstock / and a terror to everyone around it.
## No escape for Moab
48:40 For the LORD says this: / See, like an eagle he swoops down / and spreads his wings out over Moab.
48:41 Kerioth is taken, / and the strongholds are seized. / On that day Moab's warriors will have hearts / like a woman's heart when her pains begin.
48:42 Moab will be wiped out as a people, / because he made himself great against the LORD.
48:43 Terror, trench and trap / are over you, you who live in Moab, / the LORD declares.
48:44 Whoever runs from the terror / will fall into the trench, / and whoever climbs up out of the trench / will be caught in the trap. / For I will bring on Moab / the year of their punishment, / the LORD declares.
48:45 In the shadow of Heshbon / those who fled stand without strength. / For fire went out from Heshbon, / a flame from the midst of Sihon. / It ate up the brow of Moab, / the skulls of the sons of noise.
48:46 Woe to you, Moab! / The people of Chemosh are lost, / for your sons are taken off into captivity, / and your daughters into captivity.
48:47 Yet I will turn Moab's fortunes around / in the last days, the LORD declares. / Thus far the judgment on Moab.
## Ammon and Milcom
49:1 About the Ammonites. / The LORD says this: / Does Israel have no sons? / Does he have no heir? / Why then has Milcom taken over Gad, / and why do his people live in its towns?
49:2 So look, days are coming, the LORD declares, / when I will make a shout of battle heard / against Rabbah of the Ammonites. / It will become a mound of ruin, / and its daughter towns will go up in flames. / Then Israel will take over from those who took over from him, / the LORD says.
49:3 Howl, Heshbon, for Ai is ravaged! / Cry out, daughters of Rabbah! / Tie on sackcloth and mourn; / dash back and forth inside the enclosure walls. / For Milcom will go into exile, / his priests and his officials together.
49:4 Why do you brag about your valleys, / your valley that runs with water, / you daughter who turns away, / who trusts in her treasures and says, / "Who will come against me?"
49:5 See, I am bringing terror on you, / the Lord GOD of Armies declares, / from everyone around you. / Each of you will be driven straight ahead, / and no one will gather the runaways.
49:6 But afterward I will turn the fortunes of the Ammonites around, / the LORD declares.
## Edom stripped bare
49:7 About Edom. / The LORD of Armies says this: / Is wisdom finished in Teman? / Have the discerning run out of advice? / Has their wisdom turned sour?
49:8 Flee! Turn back! Go down deep to live, / you who live in Dedan! / For I will bring on him the ruin of Esau, / the hour when I call him to account.
49:9 If grape harvesters came to you, / would they leave no gleanings? / If thieves came in the night, / they would wreck only what they needed.
49:10 But I myself have stripped Esau bare. / I have laid open his secret places, / and he cannot hide. / His descendants are ravaged, with his kin and his neighbors, / and he is not there.
49:11 Leave your orphans behind; I will keep them alive, / and your widows may depend on me.
49:12 For the LORD says this: Look, people who were never sentenced to drink the cup are drinking it anyway. And you, will you go free? You will not go free. You will drink it.
49:13 For I have sworn by my own self, the LORD declares, that Bozrah will become a waste, a disgrace, a ruin and a curse, and all her towns will be ruins forever.
## Brought down from the heights
49:14 I have heard a report from the LORD, / and an envoy was sent among the nations: / "Gather together and come against her! / Get up for the battle!"
49:15 For look, I am making you small among the nations, / despised among people.
49:16 The terror you cause has fooled you, / and so has the pride of your heart, / you who live in the cracks of the cliff, / who hold the height of the hill. / Though you make your nest as high as the eagle's, / from there I will bring you down, / the LORD declares.
49:17 Edom will become a horror. / Everyone who passes by will gasp / and hiss at all its wounds.
49:18 As when Sodom and Gomorrah were overturned, / and their neighbor towns, the LORD says, / no one will live there, / and no human being will stay there.
49:19 Look, like a lion climbing up from the Jordan's thick brush / to pasture that never runs dry, / so in an instant I will make him run from it, / and whoever is chosen, him I will set over it. / For who is like me? Who can call me to court? / What shepherd is there who can stand before me?
49:20 That is why you should hear the LORD's plan, / which he has planned against Edom, / and his purposes that he has purposed / against those who live in Teman: / they will surely be dragged off, the smallest of the flock; / surely their pasture will be made a ruin over them.
49:21 At the sound of their fall the earth shakes. / A cry! Its sound is heard at the Sea of Reeds.
49:22 See, like an eagle he rises and swoops down / and spreads his wings out over Bozrah. / On that day Edom's warriors will have hearts / like a woman's heart when her pains begin.
## Damascus loses heart
49:23 About Damascus. / Hamath and Arpad are ashamed, / for they have heard bad news. / They melt away. / There is anxiety on the sea; / it cannot be still.
49:24 Damascus goes limp; she turns to run, / and panic takes hold of her. / Distress and pangs have seized her / like a woman giving birth.
49:25 How is the city of praise not deserted, / the town of my joy?
49:26 So her young men will fall in her plazas, / and all her fighting men will be struck silent on that day, / the LORD of Armies declares.
49:27 I will light a fire in the wall of Damascus, / and it will eat up the citadels of Ben-hadad.
## Kedar and Hazor
49:28 About Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon struck. / The LORD says this: / Get up, go up against Kedar, / and ravage the sons of the East!
49:29 People will take their tents and flocks, / their tent curtains and all their gear; / they will lead off their camels for themselves. / And they will shout over them, / "Terror all around!"
49:30 Run! Flee far away! / Go down deep to live, you who live in Hazor, / the LORD declares, / for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon / has laid a plan against you / and drawn up a scheme against you.
49:31 Get up, go up against a nation that is at ease, / living in safety, the LORD declares, / a nation with no double gate and no bolt, / whose people live by themselves.
49:32 Their camels will become spoil, / and their huge herds loot. / I will scatter to every wind / those who cut the hair at their temples, / and from every side I will bring their ruin on them, / the LORD declares.
49:33 Hazor will be a den for jackals, / a waste for all time. / No one will live there, / and no human being will stay there.
## The bow of Elam
49:34 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet about Elam, early in the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah:
49:35 The LORD of Armies says this: / I am about to break the bow of Elam, / the first and best of their strength.
49:36 I will bring four winds against Elam / from the four edges of the sky, / and scatter them to all these winds. / There will be no nation / where Elam's outcasts do not go.
49:37 I will break Elam to pieces in front of their enemies, / in front of those who want them dead. / I will bring calamity on them, / the heat of my anger, the LORD declares, / and send the sword after them / until I have finished them.
49:38 I will place my throne in Elam / and wipe out from there the king and the officials, / the LORD declares.
49:39 But in the last days / I will turn Elam's fortunes around, / the LORD declares.
