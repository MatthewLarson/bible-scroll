## The Temple Sermon
7:1 A word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. It said:
7:2 Take your stand in the gateway of the LORD's house. Call out this word there. Say, "Hear the LORD's word, all Judah, you who pass through these gates to bow down to the LORD."
7:3 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Make your ways and your deeds good. Then I will let you stay in this place.
7:4 Do not trust in lying words. Some people say, "The LORD's temple, the LORD's temple, the LORD's temple: that is what these buildings are!"
7:5 Instead, if you really do make your ways and your deeds good, and really do justice between a man and his neighbor,
7:6 and you do not oppress the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow, and you do not shed innocent blood in this place, and you do not walk after other gods to your own hurt,
7:7 then I will let you stay in this place, in the land I gave to your ancestors from age to age.
7:8 Look at you, trusting in lying words that cannot help you.
7:9 Do you steal, murder and commit adultery? Do you swear to a lie, send up smoke to Baal and walk after other gods that you have never known?
7:10 Then you come and stand before me in this house, the house called by my name, and you say, "We are rescued!" You say it so you can go on doing all these abhorrent things.
7:11 Has this house, the one called by my name, become a cave for bandits in your eyes? Look, I too have seen it, the LORD declares.
7:12 Go on, then, to my place that was in Shiloh, where I first made my name live. Look at what I did to it because of the evil of my people Israel.
7:13 Now, because you have done all these deeds, the LORD declares, and I spoke to you, rising early to speak, and you did not listen, and I called you, and you did not answer,
7:14 I will do to this house, the one called by my name, the one you trust in, and to the place I gave to you and to your ancestors, just what I did to Shiloh.
7:15 I will throw you out from before me, just as I threw out all your kin, the whole offspring of Ephraim.
## Do Not Pray for This People
7:16 You, then: do not pray on behalf of this people. Raise no cry of entreaty and no prayer for them. Do not press me, for I will not be listening to you.
7:17 Can you not see what they are doing in Judah's towns and in Jerusalem's streets?
7:18 The children are gathering the wood. The fathers are lighting the fire. The women are kneading dough to make cakes for the Queen of Heaven. And they pour out drink offerings to other gods, to make me angry.
7:19 Is it me they are making angry? the LORD declares. Is it not rather themselves, to the shame of their own faces?
7:20 For this reason the Lord GOD says this: Look, I am about to pour out my anger and my fury on this place. It will fall on people and on animals, on the field's trees and on the fruit of the ground. It will burn and will not go out.
## Obedience Before Sacrifice
7:21 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Pile your burnt offerings on top of your sacrifices. Eat the meat yourselves!
7:22 I did not speak to your ancestors, and on the day I brought them out of the land of Egypt I gave them no orders about burnt offering or sacrifice.
7:23 No: this is the command I gave them. Listen to my voice. Then I will be your God, and you will be my people. Walk the whole road I order you to take, so that it will go well with you.
7:24 But they did not listen. They never bent an ear. They walked by their own counsels, in the stubbornness of their evil hearts, and they went backward and not forward.
7:25 Since the day your ancestors came out of the land of Egypt, right up to today, I have sent to you every one of my servants the prophets. Each day I rose early and sent them.
7:26 But they did not listen to me, and they bent no ear. They made their necks stiff. They did more evil than their ancestors did.
7:27 You will tell them all these words, but they will not listen to you. You will call out to them, but they will not answer you.
7:28 Then say to them, This is the nation that would not listen to the voice of the LORD its God and would not take correction. Faithfulness is gone. It has been cut off from their mouths.
## The Valley of Slaughter
7:29 Shave off your long hair and fling it away. / On the bare hills, raise up a funeral song. / For the LORD has rejected and abandoned / the generation he is furious with.
7:30 The people of Judah have done what is evil in my eyes, the LORD declares. They have put their disgusting things in the house called by my name, to make it unclean.
7:31 They have built the high places of Topheth, in Ben-hinnom's Valley, so that they can burn their sons and their daughters in the fire. I never commanded it. It never came up into my heart.
7:32 For this reason, look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when people will no more say "Topheth" and "Ben-hinnom's Valley." They will say "the Valley of Slaughter." They will bury the dead in Topheth, for there will be nowhere else.
7:33 The corpses of this people will be food for the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth. No one will be there to frighten them away.
7:34 In the towns of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem I will put a stop to the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the sound of the groom and the sound of the bride. For the land will become a ruin.
## Bones Under the Sun
8:1 At that time, the LORD declares, they will bring out of their graves the bones of the kings of Judah, the bones of its officials, the bones of the priests, the bones of the prophets and the bones of those who lived in Jerusalem.
8:2 They will spread the bones out before the sun, the moon and all the army of the heavens: the ones they loved and served, the ones they followed, asked for guidance and bowed down to. The bones will not be gathered up or buried. They will be like dung on the surface of the ground.
8:3 Death will be chosen over life by all the remnant who are left from this evil family, in all the places where I have driven them, the LORD of Armies declares.
## A People That Will Not Turn Back
8:4 You are to say to them, The LORD says this: / Do people fall down and never stand up again? / Does anyone turn away and never turn back?
8:5 Why, then, has this people, Jerusalem, turned away with a turning that never ends? / They hold fast to deceit. / They refuse to turn back.
8:6 I paid attention and I heard it: / they do not speak what is right. / No one feels sorry for his wickedness, asking, "What have I done?" / Every one of them keeps turning back to his own course, / like a horse rushing into the fight.
8:7 The stork too, up in the sky, knows the times set for it. / The turtledove, the swift and the crane watch for the time of their coming. / But my people do not know the LORD's order.
## The Lying Pen
8:8 How can you say, "We are wise, and the LORD's law is with us"? / But look: the scribes' lying pen has turned it into a lie.
8:9 The wise will be ashamed. / They will be struck with terror and be caught. / They have thrown out the LORD's word, / so what wisdom do they have?
8:10 That is why I will hand their wives to other men / and their fields to men who will take them over. / From the smallest to the biggest, / every one grabs at gain he has no right to. / From prophet to priest, / every one deals in lies.
8:11 They have healed the broken place of my dear people / as though it were a trifle, / saying, "Peace, peace," / when there is no peace.
8:12 Are they ashamed of the abhorrent thing they have done? / No, they are not ashamed in the least, / and they do not even know what humiliation is. / So they will fall among those who fall. / At the time of their punishment, / they will stumble, the LORD says.
## No Grapes on the Vine
8:13 I will gather them up and sweep them away, the LORD declares. / There will be no grapes on the vine / and no figs on the fig tree, / and the leaf will wither. / What I gave them will pass away from them.
8:14 Why are we sitting still? / Gather together! Let us go into the fortified cities / and be silenced there, / for the LORD our God has silenced us / and has made us drink poisoned water, / because we have sinned against the LORD.
8:15 We waited for peace, and nothing good came. / We waited for a time of healing, and there was terror.
8:16 From Dan comes the sound of his horses snorting. / At the loud neighing of his powerful horses the whole land shakes. / They have come, and they have eaten up the land and all it holds, / the city and the people living in it.
8:17 I am about to send snakes among you, / vipers that no charm can tame, / and they will bite you, the LORD declares.
## Is There No Balm in Gilead?
8:18 My cheer is gone, and sorrow rests on me. / My heart is faint within me.
8:19 Listen! The cry for help of my dear people from a land far away: / "Is the LORD not in Zion? Is her king not in her?" / The LORD answers, "Why have they made me angry with their carved images, with their empty foreign gods?"
8:20 The harvest is over. The summer has ended. / And we have not been saved.
8:21 Over the breaking of my dear people I am broken. / I mourn. Horror has seized me.
8:22 Is there no balm in Gilead? Is no healer there? / Why, then, has the healing of my dear people not come up?
## If Only My Head Were Water
9:1 Who will give water to my head and a fountain of tears to my eyes, / so that I could weep day and night / for the slain of my dear people?
9:2 Who will give me a lodge for travelers out in the wilderness? / I would leave my people and go away from them, / because all of them are adulterers, / a band of traitors.
## A People of Lies
9:3 They bend their tongues, their bow of lies. / They have grown strong in the land, but not for faithfulness. / They go from one evil to the next, / and me they do not know, the LORD declares.
9:4 Everyone must guard against his neighbor. / Do not rely on any brother. / For every brother is a trickster, / and every neighbor goes around spreading slander.
9:5 Each one makes a fool of his neighbor, / and they do not speak truth. / They have trained their tongues to tell lies. / They wear themselves out committing wrong.
9:6 You sit in the middle of deceit, deceit on deceit. / Because of deceit they refuse to know me, the LORD declares.
9:7 For this reason the LORD of Armies says this: / Look, I am about to smelt them and test them. / What else can I do with my dear people?
9:8 Their tongue is a hammered arrow. It speaks deceit. / With his mouth each man speaks peace to his neighbor, / but inside him he lays his ambush.
9:9 Will I not punish them for these things, the LORD declares? / Will I not take revenge on a nation like this?
## Weeping for the Land
9:10 For the mountains I will weep and wail. / For the pastures of the wilderness I will raise a lament. / They are burned out, with no one to pass through. / No one hears the voice of the herds. / From the birds of the sky to the animals, / all have fled and gone.
9:11 I will turn Jerusalem into stone heaps, a home for jackals. / I will turn the towns of Judah into a waste where no one lives.
9:12 Who is wise enough to understand this? / Who has the LORD's mouth spoken to, so that he can tell it out? / Why was the land destroyed, / burned out like a desert with no one passing through?
9:13 The LORD said: It is because they left my law, the one I set out in front of them. They did not listen to my voice and did not walk by it.
9:14 No, they went after the stubbornness of their own hearts and after the Baals, which their ancestors taught them to follow.
9:15 This is why the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: / Look, I am about to feed this people wormwood / and give them poisoned water to drink.
9:16 I will scatter them among nations they have never known, they or their ancestors. / I will send the sword out behind them / until I have made an end of them.
## Call the Wailing Women
9:17 The LORD of Armies says this: / Think it over, and send for the women who wail, so they may come. / Send word to the skilled women, so they may come.
9:18 Let them hurry and lift up a wailing for us, / so our eyes run down with tears / and our eyelids pour with water.
9:19 For the sound of wailing is heard from Zion: / "How we have been ruined! We are put to great shame, / because we have left the land, / because they have thrown down our homes."
9:20 So hear the LORD's word, you women, / and let your ear receive the word of his mouth. / Teach your daughters a song of mourning, / and each woman teach her neighbor a funeral lament.
9:21 Death has crept up to our windows and come inside our strongholds, / to cut the children off from the street, / the young men from the squares.
9:22 Say: This is the LORD's declaration. / The corpses of people will lie like dung on the surface of the fields, / like cut grain behind the reaper. / No one will gather them.
## Boast of This
9:23 The LORD says this: / A wise person should not boast about his wisdom. / A strong person should not boast about his strength. / A rich person should not boast about his riches.
9:24 If anyone is to boast, let it be in this alone: / that he shows insight and knows me, / that I am the LORD, who acts in loyal love, justice and righteousness on the earth. / These are the things I delight in, the LORD declares.
## Circumcised and Uncircumcised
9:25 Look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when I will punish everyone circumcised in a foreskin:
9:26 Egypt, Judah, Edom, the Ammonites, Moab, and all those who live in the wilderness with their hair cut at the corners. For all the nations are uncircumcised, and all the house of Israel is uncircumcised in heart.
## The Idols and the Living God
10:1 Listen to the word the LORD has spoken to you, house of Israel.
10:2 The LORD says this: / Do not learn the way of the nations. / Do not panic at the signs in the sky, / though the nations panic at them.
10:3 The customs of the peoples are empty breath. / Someone cuts a tree from the forest. / The hands of a craftsman work it with a tool.
10:4 They make it handsome with silver and gold. / They hold it in place with nails and hammers, / so that it does not wobble.
10:5 Like a scarecrow in a cucumber field, they cannot speak. / Someone always has to pick them up, for they take no steps. / Do not be afraid of them. / They can do no harm, and they can do no good either.
10:6 There is no one like you, LORD. / You are great, and great is your name in strength.
10:7 Who would not be afraid of you, king of the nations? / It is right for you to be feared. / Among all the wise men of the nations and in all their kingdoms / there is none like you.
10:8 They are all stupid and foolish together. / What empty idols teach is wood!
10:9 Silver hammered thin is shipped in from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz: / the work of a craftsman and of a metalsmith's hands. / Violet and purple cloth is their clothing, / the work of skilled men, every bit.
10:10 But the LORD is God in truth. / He himself is the living God and a king who lasts forever. / When his anger comes, the earth shakes. / The nations cannot hold up under his fury.
10:11 This is what you are to say to them: "The gods who did not make the heavens and the earth will perish from the earth and from under these heavens."
10:12 He made the earth with his power. / He set the world firm with his wisdom. / With his understanding he stretched out the heavens.
10:13 When he gives his voice, a rumble of water fills the sky. / He lifts up the clouds from the far end of the earth. / He makes the lightning flash for the rain, / and he leads the wind out of its storerooms.
10:14 Every person has become dull, without knowledge. / Every metalsmith is shamed by his idol, / because the image he casts is a lie, / and there is no breath in them.
10:15 They are empty breath, work worth mocking. / When their time of punishment comes, they will perish.
10:16 Not like these is the one who is Jacob's share, / for he formed everything, / and Israel is the tribe of his own inheritance. / The LORD of Armies is his name.
## Pack Your Bundle
10:17 Pick your bundle up off the ground, / you who sit under siege.
10:18 For the LORD says this: / Look, I am about to fling out the people of the land at this time. / I will squeeze them hard, so that they find out.
10:19 Alas for me, for my fracture! My wound is grievous. / But I said, "This sickness is mine, and I will carry it."
10:20 My tent is a wreck. Every one of my tent cords is torn apart. / My children have walked out from me, and they are not. / No one is left to put up my tent again / or to hang my curtains.
10:21 For the shepherds have acted stupidly / and have not looked for the LORD. / For this reason they have had no success, / and all their flock has been scattered.
10:22 A voice! A report is on its way, a great din out of the northern land, / to turn the cities of Judah into a wasteland, / a home for jackals.
## Correct Me, LORD
10:23 I know, LORD, that the way of a human being does not belong to him. / It does not belong to a man who walks to direct his own steps.
10:24 Correct me, LORD, but by what is just. / Do not do it in your anger, / or you will make me less and less.
10:25 Pour out your fury on the nations that have not known you, / and on the families that have not called on your name. / They have eaten up Jacob. / They have eaten him up and finished him off, / and they have laid waste his home.
## The Broken Covenant
11:1 A word came to Jeremiah from the LORD. It said:
11:2 Hear the words of this covenant and speak them to everyone in Judah and to the people who live in Jerusalem.
11:3 Say to them, The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Cursed is anyone who does not listen to the words of this covenant,
11:4 the words I commanded your ancestors on the day I brought them out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace. I said, Listen to my voice and do the things I command, all of them. Then you will become my people, and I will become your God,
11:5 so that I can make my oath stand, the oath I swore to your ancestors, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, which is what it is today. And I answered and said, "Amen, LORD."
11:6 Then the LORD said to me, Call out all these words in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem. Say, Hear the words of this covenant, and do them.
11:7 I gave your ancestors a strong warning on the day I brought them up out of the land of Egypt, and I have kept on warning them to this day, rising early to do it. I said, Listen to my voice.
11:8 But they did not listen, and they never bent an ear. Each of them walked in the stubbornness of his own evil heart. So I brought on them all the words of this covenant that I commanded them to keep, and they did not keep them.
11:9 Then the LORD said to me, A plot has been found among the men of Judah and among those who live in Jerusalem.
11:10 They have turned back to the sins of their ancestors from long ago, who refused to hear my words. They have walked after other gods to serve them. The house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken my covenant, which I made with their ancestors.
11:11 That is why the LORD says this: Look, I am bringing a calamity on them that they cannot walk out of. They will cry out to me, and I will not listen to them.
11:12 The cities of Judah and the people of Jerusalem will go and cry out to the gods they send up smoke to. But those gods will not save them at all in their time of calamity.
11:13 Judah, your gods are as many as your cities. In Jerusalem you have made altars to the Shame, one for each street, altars for sending up smoke to Baal.
11:14 You, then: do not pray on behalf of this people. Raise no cry of entreaty and no prayer for them, for I will not be listening when they call to me in the time of their calamity.
11:15 What is my beloved doing in my house, / when she carries out her plot with the many? / Can holy meat make your evil pass away from you, / so that you can celebrate?
11:16 A green olive tree, lovely, with fine fruit: / that is the name the LORD gave you. / With the sound of a great roar / he has set fire to it, / and its branches are ruined.
11:17 The LORD of Armies, who planted you, has pronounced calamity on you because of the evil that the house of Israel and the house of Judah did to themselves. They made me angry by sending up smoke to Baal.
## The Plot Against Jeremiah
11:18 The LORD made it known to me, and I knew it. Then you showed me what they were doing.
11:19 As for me, I was like a tame lamb being led away to slaughter. / I did not know what plans they had worked out against me. / They said, "Let us ruin the tree along with its bread. / Let us cut him off from the land of the living, / so that no one will mention his name again."
11:20 LORD of Armies, you judge with righteousness. / You test the kidneys and the heart. / Let me watch you pay them back, / because I have opened my case to you.
11:21 That is why the LORD says this about the men of Anathoth, who are after your life. They say, "Do not prophesy in the LORD's name, or you will die by our hand."
11:22 So the LORD of Armies says this: Look, I am about to punish them. Their young men will die by the sword. Their sons and their daughters will die of hunger.
11:23 They will have no remnant. I will bring calamity on the men of Anathoth in the year of their punishment.
## Why Do the Wicked Prosper?
12:1 You are righteous, LORD, / whenever I bring a case to you. / Even so, I want to speak with you about what is just. / Why does the way of the wicked go well? / Why do all the treacherous live at ease?
12:2 You planted them, and they put down roots. / They keep growing, and they even bear fruit. / You are close to their mouths / and far from their kidneys.
12:3 But you, LORD, know me. You see me. / You test my heart, and it is with you. / Pull them away like sheep for the slaughter. / Set them aside for the day of killing.
12:4 How long must the land mourn, / and the plants of every field dry up? / Because of the evil of those who live in it, / the animals and birds are gone. / For people have said, "He will not see our end."
12:5 If you have run with men on foot, and they have worn you out, / how can you compete with horses? / And if you feel secure in a land of peace, / what will you do in the Jordan's thick brush?
12:6 Your own brothers and the house of your father, / even they have betrayed you. / Even they have shouted after you at the top of their voices. / Do not believe them, / even when they speak kindly to you.
## My House Abandoned
12:7 I have left my house. / I have given up my inheritance. / I have handed the one my whole being loves / into the hands of her enemies.
12:8 My own inheritance has become a lion in the thicket to me. / She has let out her roar at me. / That is why I have come to hate her.
12:9 Is my inheritance a speckled bird of prey to me? / Are birds of prey circling around her? / Go, gather every animal of the field. / Bring them to eat.
12:10 Many shepherds have wrecked my vineyard. / They have trampled my share of the field. / They have turned my favorite field / into a wilderness, a wasteland.
12:11 They have made it a wasteland. / It mourns to me, desolate. / All the land has been laid waste, / for no one takes it to heart.
12:12 Over all the bare hills in the wilderness destroyers have come. / The LORD has a sword, and it is eating / from one end of the land to the other. / No living thing has peace.
12:13 They have sown wheat, and they have reaped thorns. / They have worn themselves out, with no profit. / Be ashamed of your harvests, / because of the burning anger of the LORD.
## Neighbors and a Promise
12:14 The LORD says this about all my wicked neighbors, who reach out and touch the inheritance I gave my people Israel to own: Look, I am about to uproot them from their land. And I will uproot the house of Judah from among them.
12:15 After I have uprooted them, I will turn again and show them compassion. I will bring them back, each person to his own inheritance and each to his own land.
12:16 It will happen that if they carefully learn the ways of my people, and swear by my name, "By the life of the LORD," just as they taught my people to swear by Baal, then they will be built up in the middle of my people.
12:17 But if they will not listen, I will uproot that nation, uprooting it and destroying it, the LORD declares.
