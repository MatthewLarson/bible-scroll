## The city falls
39:1 It was the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month. Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon arrived at Jerusalem, he and his whole army, and they shut the city in under siege.
39:2 In the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, the city was breached.
39:3 All the officials of the king of Babylon came in and sat down in the Middle Gate: Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsekim the Rab-saris, Nergal-sharezer the Rab-mag, and all the rest of the officials of the king of Babylon.
39:4 When Zedekiah king of Judah saw them, he and all the fighting men ran away. By night they slipped out of the city on the road by the king's garden, through the gate between the double walls, and the king headed for the Arabah.
39:5 But the Chaldean army went after them and caught Zedekiah on the Jericho plains. They took him prisoner and hauled him up to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, and there he pronounced sentence on him.
39:6 At Riblah the king of Babylon slaughtered Zedekiah's sons while he watched. The king of Babylon also slaughtered all the nobles of Judah.
39:7 Zedekiah's eyes he put out. He bound him in bronze fetters, to take him to Babylon.
39:8 The king's house and the house of the people the Chaldeans burned with fire, and the walls of Jerusalem they tore down.
39:9 The rest of the people who were left in the city, those who had gone over to him, and everyone else who was left: Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard marched them off into exile in Babylon.
39:10 Some of the people, the poor ones who had nothing at all, Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard left in the land of Judah. That same day he gave them vineyards and fields.
## Jeremiah set free
39:11 Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had commanded Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard about Jeremiah. He said:
39:12 "Take him and watch over him. Do him no harm at all. Whatever he tells you, do exactly that for him."
39:13 So Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard sent word, with Nebushazban the Rab-saris, Nergal-sharezer the Rab-mag and all the chiefs of the king of Babylon.
39:14 They sent and took Jeremiah out of the courtyard of the guard and handed him over to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, to bring him home. So he stayed among the people.
## A promise for Ebed-melech
39:15 Now the word of the LORD had come to Jeremiah while he was shut up in the courtyard of the guard:
39:16 "Go and say to Ebed-melech the Cushite: The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: I am about to bring my words on this city for harm and not for good. On that day they will come true right in front of you.
39:17 But on that day I will rescue you, the LORD declares. You will not be handed over to the men you are afraid of.
39:18 I will surely save you. You will not fall by the sword. Your life will be yours as loot, because you trusted in me, the LORD declares."
## Jeremiah at Ramah
40:1 This is the word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD after Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard had sent him away from Ramah. When Nebuzaradan took him, he was bound in chains among all the exiles of Jerusalem and Judah who were being taken off to Babylon.
40:2 The chief of the guard took Jeremiah and told him: "The LORD your God announced this calamity for this place,
40:3 The LORD has brought it, and he has done exactly what he said. You all sinned against the LORD and did not listen to his voice, so this has happened to you.
40:4 Now today I have loosed you from the chains on your hands. If it looks good to you to come with me to Babylon, come, and I will watch over you. If it looks bad to you to come with me to Babylon, hold back. The whole land is open before you. Go wherever looks good and right to you."
40:5 While Jeremiah was still not turning around, Nebuzaradan said, "Go back to Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has put in charge of the towns of Judah. Live with him among the people. Or go anywhere else that seems right to you." Then the chief of the guard gave him a food allowance and a present, and let him go.
40:6 Jeremiah went to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah. He settled there with him, among the people still left in the land.
## Gedaliah at Mizpah
40:7 All the commanders of the forces who were out in the open country, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had put Gedaliah son of Ahikam in charge of the land. They heard that he had put men, women and children in his care, some of the poorest of the land, who had not been taken into exile to Babylon.
40:8 So they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan and Jonathan the sons of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth, the sons of Ephai the Netophathite, and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite, they and their men.
40:9 Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, gave an oath to them and to their men. "You have nothing to fear from serving the Chaldeans," he said. "Stay in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and things will be good for you.
40:10 As for me, I will stay at Mizpah to stand before the Chaldeans who come to us. But you, gather wine, summer fruit and oil. Put them in your jars, and settle in the towns you have seized."
40:11 All the Judeans who were in Moab, among the Ammonites, in Edom and in all the other lands also heard that the king of Babylon had given Judah a remnant, and that he had put Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, in charge of them.
40:12 So all the Judeans came back from every place where they had been driven. They came to the land of Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and they gathered wine and summer fruit in great abundance.
## The warning Gedaliah would not believe
40:13 Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces in the open country came to Gedaliah at Mizpah.
40:14 "Do you really know," they asked him, "that Baalis king of the Ammonites has sent Ishmael son of Nethaniah to kill you?" But Gedaliah son of Ahikam did not believe them.
40:15 Then Johanan son of Kareah spoke to Gedaliah in secret at Mizpah. "Please let me go and strike down Ishmael son of Nethaniah," he said. "No one will know. Why should he kill you? Are all the Judeans gathered around you to be scattered? Is the remnant of Judah to perish?"
40:16 But Gedaliah son of Ahikam said to Johanan son of Kareah, "Do not do this thing. You are speaking a lie about Ishmael."
## Gedaliah murdered
41:1 In the seventh month Ishmael son of Nethaniah, son of Elishama, one of the royal line and one of the king's chief men, came with ten men to Gedaliah son of Ahikam at Mizpah. There at Mizpah they ate a meal together.
41:2 Then Ishmael son of Nethaniah and the ten men with him got up and struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, with the sword. He killed the man the king of Babylon had put in charge of the land.
41:3 Ishmael also struck down all the Judeans who were with Gedaliah at Mizpah, and the Chaldeans who were found there, the fighting men.
41:4 On the second day after he had killed Gedaliah, when no one yet knew about it,
41:5 eighty men came from Shechem, from Shiloh and from Samaria. Their beards were shaved off, their clothes were torn, and they had cut themselves. They had grain offerings and frankincense in their hands to bring to the house of the LORD.
41:6 Ishmael son of Nethaniah came out of Mizpah to meet them. He kept walking and weeping. When he reached them, he said to them, "Come to Gedaliah son of Ahikam."
41:7 But once they were in the middle of the city, Ishmael son of Nethaniah, he and the men with him, slaughtered them and put them in the cistern.
41:8 But ten men among them said to Ishmael, "Do not kill us! We have hidden stores in the field: wheat, barley, oil and honey." So he held back and did not kill them with their brothers.
41:9 As for the cistern where Ishmael threw all the bodies of the men he had struck down, beside Gedaliah, it was the one King Asa had made because of Baasha king of Israel. Ishmael son of Nethaniah filled it with the dead.
41:10 Ishmael took captive all the rest of the people at Mizpah: the king's daughters and everyone left there, whom Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard had put in the charge of Gedaliah son of Ahikam. Then Ishmael son of Nethaniah led them off as his captives and started across toward the Ammonites.
## Johanan chases Ishmael
41:11 Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces with him heard of all the evil Ishmael son of Nethaniah had done.
41:12 So they gathered all their men and marched out to fight Ishmael son of Nethaniah. They found him by the great waters at Gibeon.
41:13 When all the people with Ishmael saw Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces with him, they were glad.
41:14 All the people whom Ishmael had carried off from Mizpah wheeled around and went back to Johanan son of Kareah.
41:15 But Ishmael son of Nethaniah escaped from Johanan with eight men and went to the Ammonites.
41:16 Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces with him then gathered up everyone who was left. These were the people he had won back from Ishmael son of Nethaniah, from Mizpah, after Ishmael struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam: fighting men, women, children and court officials, brought back from Gibeon.
41:17 They set out and camped at Geruth Kimham, near Bethlehem, planning to go on to Egypt,
41:18 away from the Chaldeans. They were afraid of them, because Ishmael son of Nethaniah had struck down Gedaliah son of Ahikam, the man the king of Babylon had put in charge of the land.
## The people ask for the LORD's word
42:1 Then all the commanders of the forces came near, with Johanan son of Kareah and Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah, and all the people, from the smallest to the greatest.
42:2 They said to Jeremiah the prophet, "Please listen to our plea, and pray for us to the LORD your God, for this whole remnant. We used to be many, but only a few of us are left, as your own eyes see.
42:3 Let the LORD your God tell us the way we should go and the thing we should do."
42:4 Jeremiah the prophet said to them, "I have heard you. I am going to pray to the LORD your God as you ask. Whatever answer the LORD gives you, I will tell you. I will not hold back a single word from you."
42:5 They said to Jeremiah, "May the LORD be a true and faithful witness against us if we do not do everything the LORD your God sends you to tell us.
42:6 Whether it is good or bad, we will listen to the voice of the LORD our God, to whom we are sending you, so that it may go well with us. For we will listen to the voice of the LORD our God."
## The answer: stay
42:7 After ten days the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah.
42:8 He called Johanan son of Kareah, all the commanders of the forces who were with him, and all the people, from the smallest to the greatest.
42:9 He said to them: "The LORD, the God of Israel, to whom you sent me to lay your plea before him, says this:
42:10 If you will truly stay in this land, I will build you up and not demolish you. I will plant you and not pull you up. For I have changed my mind about the harm I have done to you.
42:11 Do not fear the king of Babylon, whom you fear now. Do not fear him, the LORD declares, for I am with you to save you and to pull you out of his hand.
42:12 I will grant you mercy, so that he will have mercy on you and bring you back to your own soil.
42:13 But if you say, 'We will not live in this land,' refusing to listen to the voice of the LORD your God,
42:14 and if you say, 'No, we will go into the land of Egypt, where we will never see war, never hear the blast of the ram's horn, and never go hungry for bread. There we will settle,'
42:15 then hear the word of the LORD now, you remnant of Judah. The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: If you are truly set on going to Egypt, and you go to live there as foreigners,
42:16 then the sword you fear will find you there in Egypt, and the famine you worry about will cling to your heels, right into Egypt. There you will die.
42:17 All the men who set their faces to go to Egypt to live there as foreigners will die by the sword, by famine and by disease. Not one of them will be left alive or get away from the calamity I am bringing on them.
42:18 For the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Just as my anger and my fury poured out on those living in Jerusalem, so my fury will pour out on you the moment you enter Egypt. You will become a curse, a horror, a word used in cursing and a disgrace. You will never see this place again.
42:19 The LORD has spoken to you, remnant of Judah: Do not go to Egypt. Know this for certain: today I have warned you.
42:20 You have led your own lives astray. For you sent me to the LORD your God and said, 'Pray for us to the LORD our God. Tell us everything the LORD our God says, and we will do it.'
42:21 Today I have told you, and you have not listened to the voice of the LORD your God, or to anything he sent me to tell you.
42:22 So now, know this for certain: you will die by the sword, by famine and by disease in the place where you want to go and live as foreigners."
## "You are lying"
43:1 When Jeremiah finished telling all the people all the words of the LORD their God, everything the LORD their God had sent him to say to them, all these words,
43:2 Azariah son of Hoshaiah, Johanan son of Kareah and all the arrogant men said to Jeremiah, "You are speaking a lie! The LORD our God has not sent you to say, 'You must not go into Egypt to stay there as foreigners.'
43:3 Baruch son of Neriah is turning you against us. He wants to hand us over to the Chaldeans, to have them put us to death or march us off to Babylon."
43:4 So Johanan son of Kareah, all the commanders of the forces and all the people did not listen to the voice of the LORD, who told them to stay in the land of Judah.
43:5 Johanan son of Kareah and all the commanders of the forces took the whole remnant of Judah, the ones who had come back from all the nations where they had been driven, to live in the land of Judah:
43:6 the men, the women, the children, the king's daughters, and every person Nebuzaradan the chief of the guard had left with Gedaliah son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan. They also took Jeremiah the prophet and Baruch son of Neriah.
43:7 They went into the land of Egypt, for they did not listen to the voice of the LORD, and they came as far as Tahpanhes.
## Stones at Tahpanhes
43:8 In Tahpanhes the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah:
43:9 "Take large stones in your hand and hide them in the mortar of the brick terrace at the entrance of Pharaoh's house in Tahpanhes, while the Judeans watch.
43:10 Then say to them: The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: I am about to send for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, my servant, and bring him. I will set his throne above these stones that I have hidden, and he will spread his grand tent over them.
43:11 He will come and strike the land of Egypt. Those meant for death, to death; those meant for captivity, to captivity; those meant for the sword, to the sword.
43:12 I will start a fire in the temples of Egypt's gods. He will burn the temples and carry the gods away as captives. He will pick the land of Egypt clean, as a shepherd picks his cloak clean of lice, and he will leave there in peace.
43:13 He will smash the standing stones of Beth-shemesh in the land of Egypt, and the temples of Egypt's gods he will burn with fire."
## The Queen of Heaven
44:1 This is the word that came to Jeremiah about all the Judeans who were living in the land of Egypt, in Migdol, Tahpanhes, Memphis and the land of Pathros:
44:2 "The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: You yourselves saw all the calamity I brought on Jerusalem and on all the towns of Judah. Look at them today. They are ruins, and no one lives in them,
44:3 because of the evil they did to make me angry, going to burn incense and serve other gods, gods they had never known, not they, not you, and not your ancestors.
44:4 I sent you all my servants the prophets, rising early to send them, to say: Please do not do this abhorrent thing that I hate.
44:5 But they would not listen, or even bend an ear, to turn from their evil and stop burning incense to other gods.
44:6 So my fury and my anger poured out and burned in the towns of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem, and they became a ruin and a desolation, as they are today.
44:7 And now the LORD, the God of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Why are you doing such great harm to yourselves? You are cutting off from Judah man and woman, child and nursing baby, so that none of you will be left as a remnant.
44:8 You make me angry by what your hands have made, burning incense to other gods here in Egypt, the land you came to as foreigners. So you will be cut off, and you will become a curse and a disgrace among all the nations of the earth.
44:9 Have you forgotten the evil deeds of your ancestors, the evil deeds of the kings of Judah and of their wives, your own evil deeds and the evil deeds of your wives, which they did in the land of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?
44:10 To this day they have not been humbled, and they have not feared. They have not lived by my law or by my rules, the ones I put in front of you and your ancestors.
44:11 So the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: I am about to set my face against you for harm, and to cut off all Judah.
44:12 I will take the remnant of Judah, who set their faces to go to the land of Egypt and live there as foreigners. They will all meet their end in the land of Egypt. They will fall by the sword and by famine. From the smallest to the greatest they will meet their end, by the sword and by famine. They will die, and they will become a curse, a horror, a word used in cursing and a disgrace.
44:13 I will punish those who live in the land of Egypt, as I punished Jerusalem, with the sword, with famine and with disease.
44:14 No one from the remnant of Judah who came to Egypt to live there will get away or be left alive to go back to the land of Judah. They long to go back and live there, but they will not go back, except for a few who run away."
44:15 Then all the men who knew their wives had been burning incense to other gods, and all the women standing there in a great crowd, and all the people living in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah:
44:16 "We refuse to listen to you about the word you have spoken to us in the name of the LORD.
44:17 Everything that has come out of our mouths, we will surely do. We will burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and pour out drink offerings to her, just as we did, we and our ancestors, our kings and our officials, in the towns of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem. Then we had plenty of bread, we were doing well, and we never saw trouble.
44:18 But from the time we quit burning incense to the Queen of Heaven and pouring out drink offerings for her, we have been without everything, and the sword and famine have finished us off.
44:19 And when we burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and pour out drink offerings to her, is it without our husbands that we make cakes to shape her image and pour out drink offerings to her?"
44:20 Jeremiah said to all the people, to the men and to the women, to all the people who had answered him:
44:21 "The incense you burned in the towns of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem, you and your ancestors, your kings and your officials, and the people of the land: was it not that very incense the LORD remembered? Did it not come into his mind?
44:22 The LORD could not put up with it any longer: your evil deeds, the abhorrent things you did. Your land has become a waste and a desolation, a curse with no one living in it, as it is today.
44:23 This calamity has happened to you, as you see today, because you burned incense and sinned against the LORD. You did not listen to the voice of the LORD, and you did not live by his law, his rules or his decrees."
44:24 Jeremiah said to all the people and to all the women, "Hear the word of the LORD, all Judah in the land of Egypt.
44:25 The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: You and your wives have spoken with your mouths and carried it out with your hands. You said, 'We will surely keep the vows we made, to burn incense to the Queen of Heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her.' Very well! Make your vows stand. Carry out your vows!
44:26 So hear the word of the LORD, all Judah who live in the land of Egypt: Look, I swear by my great name, the LORD says, that my name will never again be spoken by the mouth of any person of Judah anywhere in the land of Egypt, saying, 'As the Lord GOD lives.'
44:27 Look, I am keeping watch over them, for harm and not for good. Every person of Judah in the land of Egypt will meet their end by the sword and by famine, until they are finished.
44:28 Those who escape the sword will go back from the land of Egypt to the land of Judah, a few in number. Then all of the remnant of Judah who came to Egypt to live as foreigners will find out whose word stands: mine or theirs.
44:29 And this will be your sign, the LORD declares: I am going to punish you right here in this place. Then you will know that my words against you, for harm, will surely stand.
44:30 The LORD says this: I am about to hand Pharaoh Hophra king of Egypt over to his enemies, to those who are after his life, just as I handed Zedekiah king of Judah over to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, his enemy, who was after his life."
## A word for Baruch
45:1 This is the word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in a scroll from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:
45:2 "The LORD, the God of Israel, says this to you, Baruch:
45:3 You said, 'How terrible for me! The LORD has piled sorrow on top of my pain. I am exhausted from my groaning, and I have found no rest.'
45:4 Say this to him: The LORD says this: What I have built I am about to demolish, and what I have planted I am about to pull up, even this whole land.
45:5 And you, do you go on seeking great things for yourself? Stop seeking them! For I am about to bring calamity on all flesh, the LORD declares. But I will give you your life as loot in every place you go."
