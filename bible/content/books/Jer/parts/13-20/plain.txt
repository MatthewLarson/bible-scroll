## The linen loincloth
13:1 The LORD said this to me: "Go and buy yourself a loincloth of linen. Wrap it around your hips, but never put it in water."
13:2 So I bought the loincloth, as the LORD had said, and wrapped it around my hips.
13:3 Then the LORD's word came to me a second time:
13:4 "Take the loincloth you bought, the one on your hips. Get up, go to Perath, and hide it there in a crack of the rock."
13:5 So I went and hid it at Perath, just as the LORD had ordered me.
13:6 Many days later the LORD said to me, "Get up, go to Perath, and take from there the loincloth I ordered you to hide."
13:7 So I went to Perath and dug, and I took the loincloth from the place where I had hidden it. And there it was, ruined. It was good for nothing.
13:8 Then the LORD's word came to me:
13:9 "The LORD says this: So I will ruin Judah's pride, and Jerusalem's great pride.
13:10 This wicked people refuse to listen to my words. They walk in the stubbornness of their own hearts and have gone after other gods, to serve them and bow down to them. They will become like this loincloth, good for nothing.
13:11 As a loincloth clings to a person's hips, so I made all the house of Israel and all the house of Judah cling to me, the LORD declares. They were to be my people, my name, my praise and my honor. But they would not listen."
## Jars full of wine
13:12 "Say this word to them: 'The LORD, the God of Israel, says this: Every jar will be filled with wine.' And they will say to you, 'Do we not know that every jar will be filled with wine?'
13:13 Then say to them: 'The LORD says this: I am about to fill with drunkenness everyone who lives in this land: the kings who sit on David's throne, the priests, the prophets, and everyone living in Jerusalem.
13:14 I will smash them against one another, parents and children together, the LORD declares. I will not pity them, spare them or have compassion. I will not hold back from destroying them.'"
## Give glory before the dark
13:15 Hear and listen. / Do not be proud, / for the LORD has spoken.
13:16 Give glory to the LORD your God / before he brings darkness, / before your feet stumble / on the mountains at twilight. / You wait for light, / but he turns it into deep shadow / and makes it thick darkness.
13:17 But if you will not listen to this, / my whole self will weep in secret places / over your pride. / It will weep and weep, / and my eye will run down with tears, / because the LORD's flock has been taken into exile.
13:18 Say to the king and to the queen mother: / "Sit down low, / for it has come down from your heads, / your crown of splendor."
13:19 The cities of the Negev are locked up. / There is no one to open them. / All of Judah is in exile; / the whole of it is carried away.
## Jerusalem's shame
13:20 Lift up your eyes and see / those who come from the north. / Where is the flock that was given to you, / your beautiful sheep?
13:21 What will you say when he sets over you, as your head, / the friends you yourself taught to be close to you? / Will pains not seize you, / like a woman giving birth?
13:22 You may think in your heart, / "Why has this come on me?" / Because your guilt is so great, / your skirts were pulled up / and your heels were treated with violence.
13:23 Can a Cushite change his skin, / or a leopard its spots? / Then you, too, might do good, / you who have been schooled in evil.
13:24 So I will scatter them like chaff / blown away by the wind from the wilderness.
13:25 This is your lot, / the share I have measured out to you, the LORD declares, / because you forgot me / and trusted in falsehood.
13:26 And I, in turn, will strip off your skirts to your face, / and your shame will show.
13:27 Your adulteries and your neighing, / your shameless prostitution on the hills in the field: / I have seen your detestable things. / Woe to you, Jerusalem! / You will not be clean. / How much longer?
## The drought
14:1 The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah about the droughts.
14:2 Judah mourns, / and her gates droop. / They sit in mourning on the ground, / and Jerusalem's cry goes up.
14:3 Their great ones send their young servants after water. / They reach the cisterns, / but there is no water to be found. / They come back with empty jars, / shamed and humiliated, / with their heads covered.
14:4 The soil is split, / for no rain has come to the land. / The farmers are ashamed; / they cover their heads.
14:5 In the open country the doe / drops her fawn and leaves it, / for there is no grass.
14:6 On the bald hilltops wild donkeys stand / and gasp for air like jackals. / Their eyes grow dim / because there is nothing green.
## The people pray
14:7 Our guilt gives evidence against us, / but you, LORD, act for the sake of your name. / Our turnings away are many, / and it is you we have sinned against.
14:8 Hope of Israel, / you who rescue it when times are hard, / why do you act like a foreigner in the land, / a traveler who pulls off the road for one night?
14:9 Why do you look like someone stunned, / a fighter with no power to rescue? / But you are right here with us, LORD, / and your name is spoken over us. / Do not abandon us!
## The LORD refuses
14:10 To this people the LORD says: / "They like to roam. / They never keep their feet still. / So the LORD takes no pleasure in them. / Now he will call their guilt to mind / and punish their sins."
14:11 Then the LORD said to me, "Do not pray for the good of this people.
14:12 Even if they fast, I will not listen to their cry for help. Even if they bring a burnt offering and a grain offering, I will not be pleased with them. By sword, by famine and by disease I will put an end to them."
## The prophets who say peace
14:13 I said, "Ah, Lord GOD! The prophets are telling them, 'You will never see a sword, and famine will not touch you. I will give you true peace right here.'"
14:14 The LORD said to me, "The prophets are prophesying falsehood in my name. I did not send them. I gave them no command. I did not speak to them. What they prophesy to you is a false vision, divination, a worthless idol, and the deceit of their own hearts.
14:15 So here is what the LORD says about the prophets who speak in my name without my sending them, who claim, 'Neither sword nor famine will touch this land.' Sword and famine will finish those very prophets.
14:16 As for the people they prophesy to, famine and sword will leave them thrown into Jerusalem's streets, with no one to bury them, their wives, their sons and their daughters. I will pour out on them the evil they have done."
## Tears and a second prayer
14:17 "You will say this word to them: / 'Let my eyes run down with tears / night and day, and never stop, / for the virgin, my dear people, / has been broken with a great breaking, / a wound that makes her very sick.
14:18 If I go out to the field, / look, those killed by the sword! / If I enter the city, / look, the sicknesses of famine! / Prophet and priest alike / roam in a land they do not know.'"
14:19 Have you finally thrown Judah away? / Does Zion disgust you with your whole being? / Why did you strike us, / and now there is no cure for us? / We waited for peace, and nothing good came. / We waited for a time of healing, and there was terror.
14:20 LORD, we know our wickedness, / the guilt of our ancestors, / for we have sinned against you.
14:21 Do not spurn us, for your name's sake. / Do not treat the throne of your glory with contempt. / Remember. Do not break / your covenant with us.
14:22 Can any of the nations' empty idols make it rain? / Can the heavens pour down showers on their own? / Is it not you, LORD our God? / We wait for you, / since you have done all this.
## Not even Moses and Samuel
15:1 The LORD said to me, "If Moses and Samuel were standing in my presence, my heart still would not be with this people. Send them out of my sight, and let them go.
15:2 And when they ask you, 'Where shall we go?' say to them, 'The LORD says this: Those meant for death, to death. Those meant for the sword, to the sword. Those meant for famine, to famine. Those meant for captivity, to captivity.'
15:3 I will appoint four kinds over them, the LORD declares: the sword to kill, dogs to drag off, and the birds of the sky and the beasts of the earth to devour and to destroy.
15:4 I will make them a terror to all the kingdoms of the earth because of Manasseh son of Hezekiah, king of Judah, and what he did in Jerusalem."
15:5 Who will take pity on you, Jerusalem? / Who will sorrow with you? / Who will turn aside to ask whether you are well?
15:6 You left me, the LORD declares; / you keep going backward. / So I have stretched out my hand against you and destroyed you. / I am tired of changing my mind.
15:7 I have winnowed them with a winnowing fork / in the gates of the land. / I have made them childless; I have destroyed my people. / They did not turn from their ways.
15:8 I have made their widows more numerous / than the sand of the seas. / At noon I have brought a destroyer / against the mother of the young men. / Suddenly I have made anguish / and terror fall on her.
15:9 She who bore seven grows faint; / she gasps for breath. / Her sun has set while it is still day. / She is ashamed and disgraced. / And the rest of them I will give to the sword / before their enemies, the LORD declares.
## Jeremiah's first complaint
15:10 Woe to me, my mother, that you gave birth to me, / a man of strife and a man of contention for the whole land! / I have not lent money, and no one has lent to me, / yet every one of them curses me.
15:11 The LORD said, "Surely I will free you for good. Surely I will make the enemy come to you with a plea in an evil time, in a time of distress.
15:12 Can iron break iron from the north, / or bronze?
15:13 I will hand over your wealth and your treasures to be plundered, free of charge, / because of all your sins, / throughout your territory.
15:14 I will make your enemies cross into a land you do not know, / for a fire has been lit in my anger; / it will burn against you."
15:15 You know, LORD. / Remember me and attend to me, / and take revenge for me on those who hound me. / Do not take me away by being slow to anger. / Know that for your sake I bear reproach.
15:16 Your words were found, and I ate them. / Your word became a joy to me, / and gladness to my heart, / for your name is called over me, / LORD God of Armies.
15:17 I never sat in the circle of those who make merry. / I took no joy in it. / Because of your hand I sat alone, / for you had filled me with indignation.
15:18 Why is my pain unending, / my wound beyond curing, / unwilling to heal? / You have become for me like something that deceives, / like waters that cannot be trusted.
## The LORD answers
15:19 Therefore the LORD says this: / "If you turn back, I will turn you back, / and you will stand before me. / If you bring out what is precious from what is worthless, / you will be like my mouth. / They will come back to you, / but you must not go back to them.
15:20 To this people I will make you / a bronze wall with strong defenses. / They will make war on you, / but they will not beat you, / for I am with you, / to save you and set you free, the LORD declares.
15:21 I will pull you out of the hands of the wicked / and buy you back from the grip of the ruthless."
## No wife, no funeral, no feast
16:1 The LORD's word came to me:
16:2 "Do not take a wife, and do not have sons or daughters in this place."
16:3 For the LORD says this about the sons and daughters born in this place, and about the mothers who bore them and the fathers who fathered them in this land:
16:4 "Terrible sicknesses will kill them. No one will mourn them, and no one will bury them. They will be like dung on the surface of the ground. Sword and famine will finish them, and their bodies will feed the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth."
16:5 For the LORD says this: "Do not enter a house of mourning. Do not go to wail, and do not show them sympathy. I have taken back my peace from this people, the LORD declares, my loyal love and my compassion.
16:6 Great and small will die in this land. They will not be buried. No one will mourn for them, cut himself, or shave his head for them.
16:7 No one will break bread for the mourners to comfort them over the dead. No one will give them a cup of comfort to drink for a father or a mother.
16:8 And do not go into a house of feasting to sit with them, to eat and drink."
16:9 For the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: "I am about to end in this place, before your eyes and in your days, the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the sound of the groom and the sound of the bride."
## Why this is happening
16:10 "When you tell this people all these words, they will ask you, 'Why has the LORD announced all this great disaster against us? What have we done wrong? What is our sin against the LORD our God?'
16:11 Answer them: 'Because your ancestors left me, the LORD declares. They went after other gods, served them and bowed down to them. Me they left, and my law they did not keep.
16:12 You, though, have done worse than your ancestors. Look, every one of you is walking after the stubbornness of his own evil heart and refuses to listen to me.
16:13 So I will fling you out of this land into a land that neither you nor your ancestors knew. There you will serve other gods day and night, because I will give you no favor.'"
## A new exodus
16:14 "So look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when it will no longer be said, 'As the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites up from the land of Egypt,'
16:15 but, 'As the LORD lives, who brought the Israelites up out of the land of the north, and out of every land where he had driven them.' I will return them to the soil I gave to their ancestors.
## Fishermen and hunters
16:16 Look, I am sending for many fishermen, the LORD declares, and they will fish for them. Afterward I will send for crowds of hunters, who will track them down on every mountain and every hill and in the cracks of the rocks.
16:17 For my eyes watch all their ways. None is hidden from my face, and their guilt cannot be covered from my sight.
16:18 First I will repay them twice over for their guilt and their sin, because they have defiled my land with the corpses of their detestable things and filled my inheritance with their abhorrent things."
## The nations will come
16:19 LORD, you are my strength and my fortress, / my place to run in the day of distress. / To you the nations will come / from the ends of the earth, and they will say: / "Falsehood is all our ancestors owned, / emptiness, nothing that could help."
16:20 Can anyone make gods for himself? / Those are not gods!
16:21 "So look, this time I will make them know. / I will make them know my hand and my might, / and they will know that my name is the LORD."
## Judah's sin, engraved
17:1 Judah's sin is written with an iron pen, with a flint point. It is cut into the tablet of their heart and into the horns of your altars,
17:2 as their children remember their altars and their Asherah poles by the green trees, on the high hills.
17:3 O my mountain in the open country! Your wealth and all your treasures I will hand over to be plundered, and your high places too, because of sin throughout your territory.
17:4 You yourself will let go of the inheritance I gave you. I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know, for you have kindled a fire in my anger that will burn forever.
## Two ways to trust
17:5 The LORD says this: / "Cursed is the one who trusts in humankind, / who takes flesh for his strength / while his heart turns aside from the LORD.
17:6 He will be like a bush in the desert plain / and will not see good when it comes. / He will dwell in the parched places of the wilderness, / in a land of salt where no one makes a home.
17:7 Blessed is the one who trusts in the LORD, / whose trust is the LORD.
17:8 He will be like a tree planted beside water, / sending its roots out toward the stream. / Heat may come, and it sees nothing of it. / Its foliage stays lush, / and in a year of drought it feels no anxiety. / It does not stop yielding fruit.
17:9 More crooked than anything is the heart, / and it is sick beyond healing. / Who can know it?
17:10 I, the LORD, search the heart / and test the kidneys, / to give each person the reward of his ways, / the fruit of his deeds."
17:11 Like a partridge that gathers a brood it did not lay / is the one who piles up riches, but not by what is right. / Halfway through his days they will leave him, / and at his end he will be a fool.
## A prayer for healing
17:12 Our sanctuary is the place of a glorious throne, / set on high from the beginning.
17:13 O hope of Israel, LORD, / all who leave you will be ashamed. / Those who turn away from me will be written in the earth, / for they have left the LORD, / the spring of living water.
17:14 Heal me, LORD, and I will be healed. / Save me, and I will be saved, / for you are my praise.
17:15 Look, they keep saying to me, / "Where is the word of the LORD? / Let it come!"
17:16 But I have not hurried away from following you as a shepherd, / and I have not longed for the deadly day. / What my lips said, you know; / it was right in front of you.
17:17 Do not become a terror to me. / You are my refuge in the day of calamity.
17:18 Let my pursuers be ashamed, but not me. / Let them be struck with terror, but not me. / Bring on them the day of calamity, / and break them with a double break.
## The sabbath gates
17:19 The LORD said this to me: "Go and take your stand at the gate of the people's sons, the one the kings of Judah use to come in and to go out, and then at every gate of Jerusalem.
17:20 Say to them: Hear the LORD's word, you kings of Judah, all Judah and all who live in Jerusalem, who come in by these gates.
17:21 The LORD says this: Be on guard with your lives! Do not haul a load on the sabbath day, and do not bring one in through Jerusalem's gates.
17:22 No load is to go out of your houses on the sabbath day, and you are to do no work of any kind. Set the sabbath day apart as holy, just as I commanded your ancestors.
17:23 They did not listen. They did not even bend an ear. They made their necks stiff, refusing to listen and refusing to take correction.
17:24 But if you truly listen to me, the LORD declares, bringing no load in through the gates of this city on the sabbath day, setting the sabbath day apart as holy, and doing no work on it,
17:25 then kings and princes who sit on David's throne will come in through the gates of this city, riding chariots and horses, they and their officials, the men of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem. And this city will remain for ever.
17:26 From the towns of Judah and the villages around Jerusalem, from Benjamin's land, from the lowlands, the hill country and the Negev, people will come bringing burnt offering and sacrifice, grain offering and frankincense, and thank offerings for the house of the LORD.
17:27 But if you will not listen to me, to set the sabbath day apart as holy, and not to carry a load or enter Jerusalem's gates on the sabbath day, then I will kindle a fire in its gates, and it will eat up Jerusalem's fortresses and not be put out."
## The potter's house
18:1 The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD:
18:2 "Get up and go down to the potter's house, and there I will let you hear my words."
18:3 So I went down to the potter's house. There he was, at work on the wheel.
18:4 The vessel he was making out of clay was ruined in the potter's hand, so he turned and made it into a different vessel, whatever looked right to the potter to do.
18:5 Then the LORD's word came to me:
18:6 "Am I unable to treat you, house of Israel, as this potter treats his clay? the LORD declares. Look, as clay is in the potter's hand, so are you in my hand, house of Israel.
18:7 At one moment I may speak about a nation or a kingdom, to pull it up, pull it down and destroy it.
18:8 But if that nation I spoke against turns back from its wickedness, I will change my mind about the harm I planned to do to it.
18:9 At another moment I may speak about a nation or a kingdom, to build it and plant it.
18:10 But if it does what is evil in my eyes and does not listen to my voice, I will change my mind about the good I said I would do for it.
18:11 So now speak to the men of Judah and the residents of Jerusalem: The LORD says this: Look, I am shaping harm against you and making a plan against you. Turn back, each of you, from your evil way, and make your ways and your deeds good."
18:12 But they say, "It is hopeless! We will follow our own schemes, and each of us will do as his stubborn, evil heart pleases."
## The people who forgot
18:13 Therefore the LORD says this: / "Ask, please, among the nations: / Who has heard the like of these things? / The virgin Israel has done / something truly horrifying.
18:14 Does the snow of Lebanon leave the rock of the open country? / Or are the cool waters that flow from far away ever uprooted?
18:15 For my people have forgotten me. / They burn incense to nothing. / It made them stumble in their ways, / on the paths of long ago, / so they walk on side paths, / on a road not built up,
18:16 making their land a ruin, / something to whistle at forever. / Everyone who passes by will be appalled / and shake his head.
18:17 As an east wind scatters, so I will scatter them / in front of the enemy. / They will see my back, not my face, / on the day of their disaster."
## Jeremiah's second complaint
18:18 Then they said, "Come, let us devise plots against Jeremiah, for teaching will not be lost from the priest, nor advice from the wise, nor the word from the prophet. Come, let us strike him with the tongue, and let us give no heed to any of his words."
18:19 Turn to me, LORD, / and listen to the voice of those who argue against me.
18:20 Is good paid back with evil? / They have dug a pit for my life! / Remember how I stood in front of you, / speaking good about them, / to turn your fury away from them.
18:21 So hand their sons over to hunger / and pour them out to the sword's power. / Let their wives lose their children and become widows; / let their husbands be put to death, / their young men struck down by the sword in battle.
18:22 May a cry be heard from their houses / when you bring a gang of raiders on them without warning. / For they have dug a pit to trap me / and hidden snares for my feet.
18:23 But you, LORD, know every plan they have made against me for my death. / Do not make atonement for their guilt; / do not wipe out their sin from before you. / Let them be made to stumble in front of you. / Act against them in the time of your anger.
## The potter's jar
19:1 The LORD says this: "Go and get a potter's jar, an earthenware flask. Bring some of the people's elders and some of the priests' elders,
19:2 and go out to the Valley of Ben-hinnom by the entrance of the Potsherd Gate. There announce the words I will speak to you.
19:3 Say: Hear the word of the LORD, kings of Judah and people living in Jerusalem. The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Look, I am bringing a disaster on this place, so terrible that the ears of everyone who hears of it will tingle.
19:4 They have left me. They have treated this place as foreign. They have burned incense in it to other gods, gods unknown to them, to their ancestors and to the kings of Judah. They have filled this place with the blood of the innocent.
19:5 They have built the high places of Baal to burn their children in the fire as burnt offerings to Baal. I never commanded it, I never spoke of it, and it never came up into my heart.
19:6 So look, days are coming, the LORD declares, when people will stop calling this place Topheth and Ben-hinnom's Valley. They will call it the Valley of Slaughter.
19:7 In this place I will empty out the plans of Judah and Jerusalem. They will fall by the sword in front of their enemies, at the hand of those who hunt their lives. Their corpses I will hand over as food for the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth.
19:8 I will make this city a ruin, something to whistle at. Everyone who passes by will be appalled and will whistle at all its wounds.
19:9 I will have them eat their own sons' flesh and their daughters' flesh, and each will eat his neighbor's flesh, in the siege and in the pressure that their enemies, who hunt their lives, will bring on them.
19:10 Then break the jar before the eyes of the men who go with you,
19:11 and say to them: The LORD of Armies says this: In this way I will break this people and this city, as one breaks a potter's vessel that cannot be made whole again. People will bury the dead in Topheth for lack of room anywhere else.
19:12 So I will treat this place and those who live in it, the LORD declares, making this city like Topheth.
19:13 Jerusalem's houses and the houses of the kings of Judah will be unclean like the place called Topheth, every house on whose roof incense was burned to all the host of heaven and drink offerings were poured out to other gods."
19:14 Then Jeremiah left Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy, and stood in the courtyard of the LORD's house. He said to all the people,
19:15 "The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, says this: Look, every disaster that I spoke against this city and all its towns I am about to bring on them, because they have made their necks stiff and will not listen to my words."
## Pashhur and the stocks
20:1 Pashhur son of Immer the priest, who was the officer in charge in the house of the LORD, heard Jeremiah prophesying these words.
20:2 Pashhur struck Jeremiah the prophet and put him in the stocks at the Upper Gate of Benjamin in the house of the LORD.
20:3 The next day, when Pashhur brought Jeremiah out of the stocks, Jeremiah said to him, "The LORD has not called your name Pashhur, but Terror all around.
20:4 For the LORD says this: I am about to turn you into a terror, to yourself and to all your friends. They will fall by their enemies' swords while your eyes look on. And all Judah I will give into the king of Babylon's hand; he will deport them to Babylon and cut them down with the sword.
20:5 I will hand over all the riches of this city, all it has earned, everything precious in it, and all the treasures of the kings of Judah to their enemies. They will plunder them, seize them and carry them off to Babylon.
20:6 As for you, Pashhur, and everyone living in your house, you will go into captivity. To Babylon you will go; there you will die and there be buried, you and all the friends to whom you prophesied falsehood."
## Jeremiah's last complaint
20:7 You enticed me, LORD, and I was enticed. / You were stronger than I, and you won. / I have become a joke the whole day through; / everyone laughs at me.
20:8 Every time I open my mouth I am shouting, / "Violence! Ruin!" / For the word of the LORD has brought me / nothing but shame and scorn all day long.
20:9 Then I said, "I will not mention him, / and I will not speak in his name again." / But it became in my heart like a burning fire, / shut up in my bones. / I wore myself out trying to hold it in, / and I could not.
20:10 For I have heard the whispering of the crowd: / "Terror all around! Tell on him! Let us tell on him!" / Everyone who was my friend is watching for my fall: / "Maybe he can be enticed. Then we will beat him / and have our revenge on him."
20:11 But the LORD stands with me, a dread champion. / That is why those who chase me will trip and fail to win. / They will be utterly ashamed, because they have not succeeded, / with a shame forever that will not be forgotten.
20:12 LORD of Armies, you test the righteous. / You see the kidneys and the heart. / Let me watch you pay them back, / because I have opened my case to you.
20:13 Sing to the LORD, praise the LORD! / He has saved a poor person's life / from the clutches of evildoers.
## Cursed be the day
20:14 Cursed is the day / on which I was born. / The day my mother bore me, / may it not be blessed.
20:15 Cursed is the person who brought my father word, / "A boy has been born to you, a son!" / and made him glad, so very glad.
20:16 May that person be like the cities / the LORD overturned without changing his mind. / May he hear a cry of distress in the morning / and a shout of alarm at noon.
20:17 Why did he not put me to death in the womb? / Then my mother would have been my grave, / her womb pregnant for ever.
20:18 Why was it that I came out of the womb, / to see trouble and sorrow, / so that my days come to an end in shame?
