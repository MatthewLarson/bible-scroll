## The seven brothers prove the case
13:1 If, then, the seven brothers looked down on suffering that went all the way to death, everyone, from every side, has to agree that godly reason is master in its own right over the passions.
13:2 Suppose they had been slaves to their passions and had eaten the defiling food. Then we would say that their passions had beaten them.
13:3 But that is not what happened. By reason, which God praises, they won out over their passions.
13:4 No one can overlook the mind's rule here. They overcame both passion and pain.
13:5 They did not turn back from pains by fire. So how can anyone refuse to grant that their good reasoning was master of passion?
13:6 Think of the towers that jut out at a harbor's mouth. They break the threats of the waves and give calm water to ships sailing in to anchor.
13:7 In the same way the young men's good reasoning, like seven towers, guarded the harbor of godliness and beat back the passions' lack of restraint.
## Brothers cheer each other on
13:8 They formed a holy chorus of godliness and kept giving each other courage. They said,
13:9 "Brothers, let us die like brothers for the law. Let us copy the three young men in Syria, who looked down on a furnace that was a match for this ordeal.
13:10 Let us not be cowards when it is time to prove our godliness."
13:11 One said, "Courage, brother!" Another said, "Hold out like a nobleman!"
13:12 Another said, "Remember where you come from. Remember by whose fatherly hand Isaac was willing to be slaughtered for godliness."
13:13 Each one and all of them together looked at one another, glowing and very bold. "We will give ourselves to God with our whole heart," they said. "He gave us our souls. Let us lend our bodies to the work of guarding the law.
13:14 Let us not fear the man who thinks he will kill us.
13:15 For a great contest and danger lie ahead for the soul: eternal torment is waiting for those who break God's commandment.
13:16 So let us put on, as armor, the rule over the passions that divine reason gives.
13:17 If we suffer like this, we will be received by Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and all our fathers will praise us."
13:18 As each brother was dragged away, the ones still left said to him, "Brother, do not shame us. Do not let down the ones who have died before us."
## The bond between brothers
13:19 You know the bonds of love that tie human beings together. God's providence, which is wise in all things, handed them out to children through their fathers. It planted them through the mother's womb.
13:20 There each brother lived for the same length of time. There each was shaped over the same months. Each grew from the same blood. Each was brought to birth through the same soul.
13:21 They were born after the same length of waiting. They drank milk from the same springs. From those embraces brotherly souls are raised together.
13:22 They grow closer still by being brought up together, by living side by side each day, and by the rest of their schooling, and by our training in God's law.
13:23 Brotherly love is by nature full of shared feeling. So the seven brothers felt even more deeply for one another, and they were of one mind.
13:24 They were taught by the same law and practiced the same virtues. They grew up together in a just way of life. So they drew all the closer to one another.
13:25 A shared zeal for what was good and noble made their oneness of mind stronger.
13:26 Together with godliness, it made their brotherly love even more precious to them.
13:27 Nature, daily life together and good character all made their brotherly bond grow. Even so, the ones left behind held out for godliness's sake. They watched their brothers being abused and tortured to death.
14:1 More than that, they even urged them on to the torture. So they not only looked down on pain. They also mastered the passions of their love for their brothers.
## Seven in harmony
14:2 O reasoning of theirs, more kingly than a king, freer than free men!
14:3 What a holy, well-tuned harmony the seven brothers had in godliness!
14:4 Not one of the seven boys was a coward. Not one held back from death.
14:5 Instead, as if they were running on the road to immortality, they all hurried toward death by torture.
14:6 Hands and feet move in step with the soul's lead. In the same way those holy boys agreed together to die for godliness, as if godliness were an immortal soul moving them.
14:7 What an all-holy seven, brothers in harmony! There were seven days in the making of the world, centered on godliness.
14:8 In the same way the boys danced in a ring around the number seven and broke up the fear of the tortures.
14:9 Now, we shudder just hearing about what those young men went through. But they did not only see it. They did not only hear the threat spoken on the spot. They went through it, and they held out, and that under pains by fire.
14:10 What could hurt more than that? Fire's power is sharp and quick, and it soon broke their bodies apart.
## Even a mother's love gave way to reason
14:11 Do not think it strange that reason ruled those men under torture. Even a woman's mind looked down on pains more varied than theirs.
14:12 The mother of the seven young men held out as each of her children was racked.
14:13 Think how tangled are the cords of a parent's love for children. It pulls everything toward the feeling that comes from deep inside.
14:14 Even animals without reason feel the same tender love for their young that human beings do.
14:15 Take the birds. The tame ones that live on the roofs of houses fight to shield their chicks.
14:16 Others make their nests on the tops of mountains, in the clefts of ravines, in holes in trees or at the tops of trees. There they hatch their young and drive off anyone who comes near.
14:17 Even if they cannot keep the intruder away, they fly around and around their young, aching with love. They call out in their own voice and help their children any way they can.
14:18 But why do we need animals without reason to show the love that parents have for their children?
14:19 Even bees, at the season for making honeycomb, fight off anyone who comes close. They strike those who come near their hive with their sting as if with a sword, and they defend it to the death.
14:20 But love for her children did not move the young men's mother, who had a soul like Abraham's.
## The mother's choice
15:1 O reason of the sons, tyrant over the passions! O godliness, dearer to the mother than her children!
15:2 Two things lay before the mother: godliness, or the safety of her seven sons for a short time, as the tyrant had promised.
15:3 She loved godliness more, the godliness that keeps people safe for eternal life with God.
15:4 How can I describe the passions of a parent's love for children? They stamp a wonderful likeness of soul and of looks onto a child's small features. Mothers above all feel more for their children than fathers do.
15:5 For the weaker in spirit mothers are, and the more children they have, the more they love their children.
15:6 Of all mothers, the mother of the seven loved her children most. In seven pregnancies her tender love for them had been planted deep within her.
15:7 Because of the many labor pains she had gone through for each of them, she could not help but feel for them.
15:8 But because she feared God, she set aside her children's safety for a short time.
15:9 What is more, her sons were good and noble and obeyed the law, and this made her love them even more.
15:10 They were just, self-restrained and courageous. They had great souls. They loved their brothers. And they loved their mother so much that they obeyed her even to death by keeping the law.
15:11 So many things in a mother's love were pulling her to share their pain. Even so, in not one of them could the tortures of every kind turn her reason aside.
15:12 Instead, the mother urged each child alone, and all of them together, to die for godliness.
15:13 O holy nature! O ties of parents, and tender love toward parents! O nursing care, and the unconquerable passions of mothers!
15:14 The mother watched them racked and burned one by one. Yet she did not change her mind, because of godliness.
## What she saw
15:15 She saw her children's flesh melting in the fire. She saw the fingers of their feet and hands jerking on the ground. She saw the flesh of their heads, down to their chins, hanging in front of them like masks.
15:16 O mother, the pains that tested you now were more bitter than your labor pains for them!
15:17 O you, the only woman who gave birth to complete godliness!
15:18 Your firstborn did not change your mind as he breathed out his life. Neither did the second, looking at you in pity under torture, nor the third, as he died.
15:19 You watched the eyes of each one under the tortures. They stared like a bull's at the same abuse. You saw their nostrils show that death was near. You did not cry.
15:20 You saw children's flesh cut off and piled on children's flesh. You saw hands cut off and piled on hands, and heads cut off and piled on heads, and dead bodies falling on dead bodies. You saw the place made a mass grave for your children by the tortures. You did not shed a tear.
15:21 No songs of the Sirens, no swan's voice, draws listeners to hear as strongly as the voices of children crying out to their mother under torture.
15:22 How great were the tortures, and how many, by which the mother herself was tortured, while her sons were tortured on wheels and with hot irons!
15:23 But godly reason gave her heart a man's courage in the very midst of her passions. It strengthened her to look past her love for her children for the moment.
15:24 She saw seven children destroyed. She saw the many tangled kinds of racking. Even so, the noble mother embraced them and let them go, because of her faith in God.
## Two votes in the council of her soul
15:25 It was as if she saw, in the council chamber of her own soul, fierce counselors: nature, birth, love of children and her children's racking.
15:26 The mother held two votes, one that would bring death to her children and one that would save them.
15:27 She did not choose the safety that would save seven sons for a little while.
15:28 Instead, as a daughter of Abraham, she remembered his God-fearing steadfastness.
15:29 O mother of the nation, defender of the law, shield of godliness, prize-winner in the contest of the heart!
15:30 O you who held out more nobly than males, and endured more bravely than men!
15:31 Noah's ark carried the world in the flood that filled the world, and it held out strong against the waves.
15:32 In the same way you, guardian of the law, were flooded on every side by the flood of the passions. Strong winds, your sons' tortures, held you in their grip. Yet you nobly endured the storms that came against godliness.
## A woman proves the case
16:1 If, then, even a woman, an old woman and the mother of seven children, endured watching her children's tortures, even to death, then we all must admit that godly reason is sovereign over the passions.
16:2 So I have shown that not only men have overcome the passions. A woman too looked down on the worst tortures.
16:3 The lions around Daniel were not so savage, and Mishael's furnace blazing with the fiercest fire did not burn so hot, as her nature as a loving mother burned inside her as she watched her seven sons tortured.
16:4 But the mother put out passions so many and so strong by reason that comes from godliness.
## The lament she did not make
16:5 Think about this too. If the woman had been weak in soul, even though she was their mother, she would have wailed over them. She might have said something like this:
16:6 "Oh, how wretched I am, wretched three times over and many times more! I bore seven sons, and I have become the mother of none.
16:7 Oh, seven pregnancies for nothing! Seven times ten months for no good! Nursing that bore no fruit, breast-feeding that brought only grief!
16:8 For nothing, my children, I went through so many labor pains and the harder worries of raising you.
16:9 Oh, my sons! Some of you never married, and those who married did so for nothing. I will not see your children. No one will call me grandmother and count me happy.
16:10 Oh, I had many children, and fine ones! Now I am a widow, alone, with much to mourn.
16:11 And when I die, I will have no son to bury me." But the holy, God-fearing mother did not mourn any of them with a lament like this.
16:12 She did not try to turn any of them away from death. She did not grieve over them as though they were dying.
16:13 Instead, it was as if she had a mind of steel. She was bearing the full number of her sons again, this time into immortality. So she begged them and urged them on to die for godliness.
## God's soldier
16:14 O mother, soldier of God for godliness, an old woman and a woman! Your steadfastness defeated even the tyrant. In what you did and what you said, you were found stronger than a man.
16:15 When you were arrested with your children, you stood and watched Eleazar being tortured. And you said to your children in the Hebrew language,
16:16 "My children, the contest is noble. You have been called to it to testify on behalf of the nation. Fight hard for the law of our fathers.
16:17 It would be shameful for this old man to endure pains for godliness while you, who are younger, shrink back in terror at the tortures.
16:18 Remember: you came into the world and have enjoyed life because of God.
16:19 That is why you owe it to God to endure every hardship for his sake.
16:20 For his sake our father Abraham hurried to slaughter his son Isaac, the father of our nation. And seeing the father's hand coming down on him with the sword, he did not cower.
16:21 Daniel the righteous was thrown to the lions. Hananiah, Azariah and Mishael were flung as if from a sling into a furnace of fire. They endured for God.
16:22 So you too, who have the same faith in God, must not be upset.
16:23 For it makes no sense for people who know godliness not to stand up to hardship."
16:24 These were the words the mother of seven used as she urged on each of her sons. She persuaded them to die rather than break God's commandment.
16:25 Besides, they saw this too: those who die for God's sake live to God, as Abraham, Isaac and Jacob do, and all the patriarchs.
## The mother's death
17:1 Some of the spearmen said that when she too was about to be seized and put to death, she flung herself onto the pyre, so that no one would touch any part of her body.
17:2 O mother, with your seven sons you broke the tyrant's force! You made his evil plans come to nothing, and you showed how noble faith is.
17:3 Like a roof nobly set on the pillar of your sons, you bore the earthquake of the tortures without leaning.
17:4 So take heart, mother with a holy soul! Hold on nobly to the hope that your endurance gives you in God.
17:5 The moon does not stand as grand among the stars in the sky as you do. You lit the way to godliness for your seven sons, who shine like stars. God holds you in honor now, and with them you have a fixed place in heaven.
17:6 For your bearing of children came from Abraham the servant.
## The epitaph
17:7 Suppose it were open to us, like some painter, to picture the godliness of your story. Would not those who saw it shudder? They would see a mother of seven children who endured all kinds of tortures, even to death, for godliness.
17:8 It would even be right to write these words on the tomb itself, so that the people of our nation would remember them:
17:9 "Here an old priest is buried, with an old woman and seven sons. They lie here because of the violence of a tyrant who wanted to tear down the Hebrews' way of life.
17:10 They won justice for their nation, fixing their eyes on God and enduring the tortures until death."
## A contest of God
17:11 It was truly a divine contest that they went through.
17:12 For at that time Virtue was the judge who gave out the prizes, and she tested them by endurance. The winner's prize was incorruption, in a life that lasts long.
17:13 Eleazar fought the first round. The mother of the seven sons entered the games. The brothers competed.
17:14 The tyrant was their opponent. The world and the whole human race looked on.
17:15 The fear of God won, and she crowned her own athletes.
17:16 Who did not marvel at the athletes of the law that is true? Who was not amazed?
17:17 Even the tyrant and his whole council stood in wonder at how they endured.
17:18 Because of it they now stand beside God's throne and live the blessed age.
17:19 For Moses says, "And all who have been made holy are under your hands."
## A life in exchange
17:20 So these, made holy for God's sake, have been honored. And not only with this honor. They are honored also because through them the enemy did not overcome our nation,
17:21 the tyrant was punished, and the homeland was made clean.
17:22 They became, as it were, a life in exchange for the nation's sin. Through the blood of those godly people and their atoning death, divine providence saved Israel, which had suffered so much before.
17:23 For the tyrant Antiochus looked at the courage of their virtue and at their endurance under the tortures. Then he held up their endurance to his soldiers as an example.
17:24 He found his soldiers noble and brave in battles on foot and in sieges. He plundered and defeated all his enemies.
## Obey this law
18:1 Israelites, children born of Abraham's line, obey this law. Be godly in every way.
18:2 Know that godly reason is master of the passions, and not only of the pains that come from within but also of those that come from outside.
18:3 That is why those who gave their bodies up to pain for godliness were not only admired by human beings. They were also counted worthy of a share from God.
18:4 Through them the nation had peace. It brought back the rule of good law in the homeland and drove the enemy out by siege.
18:5 The tyrant Antiochus has been punished on earth, and now that he is dead he is still being punished. For when he could not, by any means at all, force the people of Jerusalem to live like foreigners and to be cut off from their fathers' nation,
## The mother's words to her sons
18:6 then he set out from Jerusalem and took his army against the Persians. The righteous woman, the mother of the seven sons, also said these things to her children:
18:7 "I was a chaste young girl, and I never crossed the threshold of my father's house. I guarded the rib that was built.
18:8 No destroyer of the wilderness, no seducer in the open field, ruined me. The destroyer who deceives, the snake, did not spoil the purity of my virginity. I stayed with my husband through the years of my prime.
18:9 When these children were grown, their father died. He was blessed. He had sought a life blessed with good children, and he never had to suffer a season of losing them.
18:10 While he was still with us, he taught you the law and the prophets.
18:11 He read to us about Abel, killed by Cain, and Isaac, offered as a whole burnt offering, and Joseph in prison.
18:12 He told us about Phinehas the zealous one. He taught you about Hananiah, Azariah and Mishael in the fire.
18:13 He praised Daniel in the lions' pit too, and called him blessed.
18:14 He reminded you of the scripture of Isaiah, which says, 'Even if you walk through fire, the flame will not burn you.'
18:15 He sang to us the words of David, the writer of hymns, who says, 'Many are the troubles of the righteous.'
18:16 He quoted to us the proverb of Solomon, who says, 'It is a tree of life to all who do his will.'
18:17 He assured us of the words of Ezekiel, who says, 'Will these dry bones live?'
18:18 And he did not forget the song that Moses taught, which teaches, 'I will kill, and I will make alive.'
18:19 This is your life and the happiness of your days."
## The tyrant judged, the sons gathered home
18:20 O bitter day, and yet not bitter, when the bitter tyrant of the Greeks lit fire under his savage cauldrons! In boiling rage he brought the seven sons of Abraham's daughter to the catapult, and back to his tortures again.
18:21 He blinded the pupils of their eyes, cut out their tongues and killed them with all kinds of tortures.
18:22 For this divine Justice has gone after the accursed man and will go after him still.
18:23 But Abraham's children, with their prize-winning mother, are gathered together in the place of their fathers. From God they have received souls that are pure and immortal.
18:24 To him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.
