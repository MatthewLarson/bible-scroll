# Hostile review: 4 Maccabees book-level files (book.json, sources.json, proposal topic maccabean-martyrs)

Reviewed: `content/books/4Ma/book.json` (whole file, including the card "Is 4 Maccabees Scripture?" and the `canon` block), `content/books/4Ma/sources.json`, `content/proposals/works/topics/maccabean-martyrs.json`. Tier `orth`. Brief `tools/brief-4ma.md`; model `content/books/3Ma/review-book.md`.

## What I checked
- `VERBOSE=1 node tools/build.js --check 4Ma`: 0 errors, 0 warnings (484 verses, 261 notes, 60 variants, 162 glosses, 18 chapter pages, 50 topic contributions).
- By script, every quoted string in book.json against the current plain.txt of all books. Every quotation of 4 Maccabees matches its verse word for word (1:1, 1:10, 1:11, 1:17, 2:5, 5:22-24, 6:27-29, 7:19, 14:5, 14:9, 14:20, 16:13, 16:20, 16:25, 17:9-10, 17:22, 18:23). The quotations of 2 Maccabees (3:5, 4:8, 6:18, 7:9, 7:38, 7:41), Hebrews, Romans, Luke, Mark and Isaiah match the current Plain English Translation. Not matched, as expected: the quotations of Townshend, Swete, Bechtel, Reid and the church documents. I checked those against the writer's log and, where I could, against the page.
- Opened in this session: azbyka.ru/biblia (Russian Synodal list); Wikipedia "4 Maccabees"; De Young's post (the writer's saved copy of the page, `.scratch/4Ma-book/dl/af.htm`); the Coptic diocese answer qid 2012 (saved copy); Townshend's text (APOT 2 scan, lines 66664-66680, on Lloyd); Swete's Introduction (saved copy, "Four leaves taken from the binding of Cod. Porfirianus"); bible-researcher.com's page on the Claromontanus list; a search result for the Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft's 1534 heading; `texts/orig/4Ma.json` at 2:5, 4:10, 13:17, 17:10, 17:22; `content/works.json` (entry `4-maccabees`, name and tier correct); the global sources.
- Not opened: the Orthodox Study Bible's own table of contents, any printed Greek Orthodox Bible, Origen's list in Eusebius HE 6.25, Gallagher and Meade, Cowley.

## Verdict

Careful, honest and readable. Nothing is invented, the site's own voice never calls the book Scripture, and the reliability analysis is labeled as Claude's and ranks no church. The quotations are accurate, and the maccabean-martyrs topic duplicates nothing: `martyrs` (Ideas) and `maccabean-revolt` (Events) are general, and neither names the seven or Eleazar. The weak points are the church rows. The Claromontanus list names a fourth book of Maccabees and a first and a second but no third, and the page buries it in a Wikipedia aside while building its headline on "the Apostolic Canons count three". "Greek Bibles print it in an appendix" generalizes one ambiguous sentence. Three rows (Ethiopian, Lutheran, parts of the Orthodox row) rest on Wikipedia or ignore stronger sources already in the project. The sibling 3 Maccabees file went another way on the Russian Synodal Bible. There is also one false statement about the book itself ("quotes no Bible verse until 18:10-19").

Findings: **0 critical, 7 major, 12 minor.**

## Findings

### Major

**1. Major: the Claromontanus list contradicts the headline "No early list counts it" and the arithmetic "three, not four" (book.json lead; facts; traditions card "Orthodox"; canon.accepted_by "Eastern Orthodox"; rejected_why "No early list counts it, except by a disputed reckoning"; sources `wikipedia-4-maccabees`).**
- The page says only that Wikipedia "reports one Latin list, in Codex Claromontanus, that has it." No other fact about that list is given, and the heading calls the reckoning "disputed" without saying by whom.
- I opened bible-researcher.com/claromontanus.html. Under "Maccabeorum" it lists "lib. primus", "lib secundus" and "lib. quartus" (1000 lines). No third book. The page dates the list "about A.D. 400" and says the catalog is "thought to be from the third century." So the one list that has a fourth book has no third. Apostolic Canon 85 says "three" and does not say which three.
- Consequences: (a) "count three, not four" is the site's arithmetic. Canon 85 never names the books. (b) The lead's "The oldest list that counts books of Maccabees for the Eastern churches" has no support. If the Claromontanus date of the third century is right, it is older than canon 85, and I could not check which is older. Origen's list in Eusebius HE 6.25 also names Maccabees, and I did not open it. "Oldest" and "for the Eastern churches" are both unproved. (c) The Wikipedia citation is to Gallagher and Meade, which the writer did not open.
- *Fix:* take "oldest" out of the lead. Give the Claromontanus entry its own sentence in the Orthodox row and in rejected_why with the facts above (1, 2 and 4, no 3, a Latin list of uncertain date, from bible-researcher.com or from Gallagher and Meade once opened). Say that canon 85 does not name its three. Retitle the rejected_why section to "Almost no early list counts it".

**2. Major: "Greek Bibles print it in an appendix" generalizes one ambiguous sentence (lead; facts "Scripture for"; lens O; card; canon.summary; accepted_by; sections "Its place in Greek and other Bibles"; how_to_read).**
- The NRSV preface says: "4 Maccabees appears in an appendix to the Greek Bible." In context "the Greek Bible" can mean the Septuagint text (Rahlfs also prints it in an appendix) and not the Bibles of the Church of Greece. The site writes "Greek Bibles" (plural) and "modern Greek Bibles print it in an appendix," and ties the Orthodox row to it.
- De Young speaks of manuscripts ("In later Greek manuscripts, 4 Maccabees is included in an appendix"), not of printed church Bibles. Wikipedia says "More recently, the Greek Church moved it to an appendix", with no source I could see. No printed Greek Orthodox Bible (Apostoliki Diakonia, the Church of Greece's edition) was opened; the log admits this.
- Related gap: the brief and the 3 Maccabees review rest the Orthodox row on the Orthodox Study Bible's front matter. The log says its treatment of 4 Maccabees was not opened. A forum thread I found says the OSB once planned the book and no longer includes it. That is anecdotal and I could not settle it. If the OSB leaves it out, that is the strongest English-language Orthodox source and the row ignores it.
- *Fix:* write "The NRSV preface says 4 Maccabees appears in an appendix to 'the Greek Bible'; De Young says Greek manuscripts put it in an appendix." Drop "Greek Bibles print" and "modern Greek Bibles print" unless an edition is opened. Open the OSB table of contents and front matter and say what it does. Apply the change in all eight places above.

**3. Major: the Russian Synodal marking is asserted here and was removed from the sibling book, and the row sets two claims side by side without reconciling them (card "Orthodox"; canon.accepted_by; lead; facts "Scripture for"; source `russian-synodal-list-azbyka`).**
- I opened azbyka.ru/biblia. The Old Testament list has "Маккавейская 1-я", "2-я" and "3-я", each with the asterisk defined as "Неканоническая". There is no fourth book. The claim is therefore true as stated, so this is not an invented source.
- But the 3 Maccabees review (finding 1) and its fix removed the same claim because "azbyka.ru gave nothing" to that fixer. The two books now disagree on a fact about the same Bible. Cause: one of the two writers did not find the page.
- The 4Ma card then quotes the NRSV preface that the books "up to and including 3 Maccabees are recognized as Deuterocanonical Scripture by the Greek and the Russian Churches". That sits beside "marks the three as non-canonical", in the same row, with no word on why they differ (the NRSV speaks of the Russian Church's practice, the Synodal list is a printed Bible's apparatus).
- *Fix:* add one sentence saying the two statements are about different things and that the azbyka marking is of the Synodal Bible's own list. Report to the lead that 3Ma's Orthodox row can now restore the marking, with azbyka.ru/biblia as the primary.

**4. Major: the Ethiopian row rests on Wikipedia and sends the reader to a church list that names "II and III Maccabees" (card "Ethiopian Orthodox"; canon.accepted_by; facts; sources `wiki-meqabyan`).**
- The same defect as 3 Maccabees finding 5. The row quotes the church list ("I Maccabees", "II and III Maccabees") and then says these are not the Greek books, on the authority of Wikipedia "Meqabyan".
- The 3Ma fix replaced Wikipedia with `cowley-ethiopian` (R. W. Cowley, "The Biblical Canon of the Ethiopian Orthodox Church Today", *Ostkirchliche Studien* 23, 1974), which is now in the global sources (content/sources.json line 134). Both books should use it.
- *Fix:* use Cowley's statement (as quoted in the 3Ma fix) and delete `wiki-meqabyan` from 4Ma/sources.json. Mark "these are the Meqabyan" as an identification the site draws from Cowley.

**5. Major: the Lutheran row, and the Luther 1534 claim, rest on Wikipedia, and a printed Bible is not a church statement (card "Lutheran"; canon.accepted_by "Lutheran churches"; sources `wiki-luther-bible`).**
- Wikipedia cites Brecht for the heading. The brief asked the reviewer to find a primary or scholarly source. I found one in a search result: the Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft (die-bibel.de/luther-und-die-apokryphen). It gives the 1534 heading as "Apocrypha: Das sind Bücher: so der heiligen Schrift nicht gleich gehalten, und doch nützlich und gut zu lesen sind", and lists the Apocrypha with the first and second books of Maccabees only, adding that other books such as 3 and 4 Esdras were not taken in. I saw this only as the search result's text, so the fixer must open the page. It confirms both the heading and the contents of Luther's Apocrypha, in the editors' own words.
- Also: a Bible's table of contents is Luther's choice, not "the Lutheran churches". If no Lutheran confession addresses 4 Maccabees (the Book of Concord is not about this), say so. Put the German original beside the English.
- *Fix:* replace `wiki-luther-bible` with the DBG page once opened; head the row "Lutheran (Luther's Bible)"; say that the Lutheran confessions were not found to rule on the question.

**6. Major: the Orthodox row says "ratified" and "oldest" and leaves out what else canon 85 and Trullo 2 contain (traditions card "Orthodox"; canon.accepted_by).**
- The 3Ma fix added that canon 85 also lists "two Epistles of Clement" and Clement's Constitutions among the "venerable and sacred" books, and lacks Wisdom, Tobit and Baruch, and that Trullo 2 rejects the Constitutions in the same breath as ratifying the eighty-five canons. A reader of 4Ma sees only "ratified ... count three". The list is therefore not a church-wide Old Testament canon. The 4Ma writer opened the same pages (log: Trullo 2 "rejects the Constitutions").
- *Fix:* copy the 3Ma wording (Clement and the Constitutions; Trullo 2's rejection) into the Orthodox row and rejected_why "No early list counts it". Keep the sentence that no Orthodox source opened names 4 Maccabees as canonical.

**7. Major: the page says 4 Maccabees "quotes no Bible verse as a text to expound until the mother's last speech (18:10-19)", which its own text contradicts (section "A speech, not a history"; compare canon.provenance "What it builds on in the Hebrew Bible").**
- 2:5 reads: "For the law says, 'You must not crave your neighbor's wife, or anything at all that belongs to your neighbor.'" The Greek (texts/orig/4Ma.json 2:5) is "λέγει γοῦν ὁ νόμος Οὐκ ἐπιθυμήσεις τὴν γυναῖκα τοῦ πλησίον σου, οὐδὲ ὅσα τῷ πλησίον σού ἐστιν." The provenance section itself cites 2:5-6 and Exodus 20:17.
- The inference built on it (that the book is a lecture because it does not expound a text) cannot rest on the sentence as written. Townshend's point may be narrower (no passage is read out and explained); the page must say that, not "quotes no verse".
- *Fix:* "It quotes the tenth commandment (2:5) and its heroes' stories as examples, but it does not read out and expound one passage of Scripture until the mother's last speech (18:10-19)."

### Minor

**8. Minor: "Commentators agree" on Hebrews 11:35 (cited_in Hebrews 11:35-36).** The sources are two commentaries (Meyer; the Expositor's Greek Testament), and neither is quoted as saying the Maccabean martyrs are meant. Use "Meyer and the Expositor's Greek Testament take Hebrews 11:35 to mean the Maccabean martyrs" or name more commentators. The same hedge-and-consensus problem shows in the topic lead ("Hebrews 11:35 most likely remembers them"); the 2 Maccabees card's wording "Most commentators think" is the one to copy. The statement that the "better resurrection" matches 2 Maccabees 7:9, 14 is fair. Note that `at: 9:12` ties only to the wheel (τροχός); the Hebrews word is ἐτυμπανίσθησαν and 2 Maccabees 6:19 and 6:28 have τύμπανον. The text partly says this.

**9. Minor: Romans 3:25 gives one scholar's side of a debate (cited_in Romans 3:25; section "The martyrs' death and the New Testament").** Only Bailey is named, and he argues against the parallel. The scholars who make "the common practice of paralleling" are not named, so "disputed" shows only one voice. Name at least one proponent from an opened source, or say that none was opened. Also, "The Greek word is hilastērion, which 17:22 also uses" is true only of Sinaiticus's reading. Swete's text (A) has the adjective ἱλαστηρίου θανάτου ("atoning death"). The section "The martyrs' death" says so; the cited_in text should too.

**10. Minor: the Luke entry is labeled "disputed" with nobody disputing it (cited_in Luke 16:22-23; 20:38, `at: 13:17`).** The only source is Townshend, who discusses Luke 16:23 and a manuscript addition at 13:17. The log records that the Bible Hub page for Luke 20:38 does not mention 4 Maccabees. So the Luke 20:38 link ("live to God", 7:19; 16:25) is the writer's own comparison, with no commentator. Label it "shared tradition" or "Claude's observation", and point `at` to 7:19 for Luke 20:38.

**11. Minor: "saints of the whole church, kept on 1 August" (lead; canon.summary).** The sources opened are the OCA (1 August, Orthodox) and Townshend (calendars of Carthage, the Syrians and the Arabians, sermons of Augustine and Leo). Neither shows that any church today other than the Orthodox keeps 1 August. Write "saints of the Orthodox Church, kept on 1 August (OCA), honored in the West in the past (Townshend)".

**12. Minor: "by a Protestant scholar" (reading, deSilva).** The 3Ma fix removed the same unchecked label. Remove it here too. The annotations on Hadas ("argues for Antioch and a date about AD 40") rest on Wikipedia's summary, as the page says elsewhere; note that or open Hadas.

**13. Minor: patristic claims taken from Wikipedia.** "Ambrose made a partial Latin translation of it in On Jacob and the Happy Life (Wikipedia)". The wikitext in the writer's folder does say "an independent partial translation of 4 Maccabees" in *De Jacob et vita beata* (388), and cites a scholarly work. No primary text was opened. The brief asks for verifiable patristic claims. Either open De Jacob or say "Wikipedia reports". The Chrysostom claims rest on Townshend and are fine.

**14. Minor: structure of `accepted_by`.** The Georgian and Syriac rows are in `accepted_by` yet say "no statement from the Georgian Church / the Syriac churches". The Anglican row attaches the NRSV and RSV, which are ecumenical editions and not Anglican statements. Move the NRSV sentence to the lead or the Orthodox row, and either retitle the group or mark those two rows "Read, no church statement".

**15. Minor: "ten times the 2 Maccabees figure" (reliability §3, "What does not").** 4:17 has 3,660 talents a year; 2 Maccabees 4:8-9 has 360 plus 80 talents (and 150 more for the gymnasium). Against 360 it is about ten times; against 440 about eight. Write "about ten times the 360 talents".

**16. Minor: "the same four that Wisdom of Solomon 8:7 names" (section "The philosophy").** The site's Wisdom 8:7 reads "self-control and understanding, righteousness and courage", while 4Ma uses "self-restraint", "good sense" and "justice". The identity is right in Greek but a reader comparing the two pages sees different words. Add "(rendered differently in the Plain English Translation of Wisdom)".

**17. Minor: "as some of the guards said" (section "How it retells 2 Maccabees").** The Plain English Translation at 17:1 says "Some of the spearmen". Use the same word.

**18. Minor: Lloyd under "Reformed and other Protestant churches" (canon.accepted_by).** Townshend, in the scan (lines 66668-66676), says Lloyd, an Oxford scholar, "keeps the vials of his wrath for certain Roman theologians" who tried to make out that 4 Maccabees was canonical on Josephus's authority. The paraphrase is correct, but Lloyd was an Anglican, not a Reformed writer; move the sentence to the Anglican row. The card has no Baptist row; the Baptist Confession (1689, ch. 1) repeats Westminster 1.3 and could be added only after opening it.

**19. Minor: the lead calls scholars' dating "first century AD" while the facts and audience list a Hadrianic view (Dupont-Sommer).** Say "most scholars date it to the first century AD, some later".
