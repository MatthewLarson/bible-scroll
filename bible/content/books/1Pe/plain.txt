## Greeting
1:1 From Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the chosen temporary residents of the Dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia.
1:2 You were chosen in keeping with what God the Father knew beforehand, set apart as holy by the Spirit, for obedience and for sprinkling with the blood of Jesus Christ. Grace to you, and peace: may they be multiplied.
## Born again into a living hope
1:3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. His mercy is great, and in keeping with it he gave us new birth into a hope that is alive, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,
1:4 and into an inheritance that cannot decay, cannot be stained and cannot wither. It is held in the heavens for you,
1:5 and God's power, working through faith, keeps you under guard for a salvation that stands ready to be revealed when the last time comes.
1:6 Over this you shout for joy, even though now, for a short while, if it has to be so, you are made to grieve by trials of many kinds.
1:7 That is so that your faith, once proven, will be found to earn praise and glory and honor when Jesus Christ is unveiled. Its proving is worth far more than gold, which perishes even though fire tests it.
1:8 You love him without having seen him. You do not see him even now, yet you believe in him and shout for joy, with a joy that words cannot tell, a joy made glorious,
1:9 since you are receiving what your faith is aiming at: salvation for your souls.
## The prophets searched
1:10 This salvation is what the prophets searched for and dug into, the prophets who foretold the grace that was coming to you.
1:11 They kept asking which time, or what sort of time, the Spirit of Christ within them meant when that Spirit bore witness in advance to the sufferings that would come to Christ and the glories after them.
1:12 It was made known to them that the service they gave in these matters was not for themselves but for you. The same things have now been reported to you by the people who brought you the good news through the Holy Spirit, who was sent from heaven. Angels would love to bend down and peer into them.
## Be holy
1:13 So tuck up the robes of your mind and get ready to move. Stay sober. Put all your hope on the grace that is on its way to you when Jesus Christ is unveiled.
1:14 Be children who obey. Do not shape yourselves to fit the desires you had before, when you did not know.
1:15 Instead, just as the one who called you is holy, become holy yourselves in every part of the way you live,
1:16 because it is written, "You will be holy, because I am holy."
1:17 And if the one you call on as Father is the one who judges each person's work without favoritism, then live in fear during your time as resident aliens.
1:18 You know that you were ransomed out of the futile way of life that your fathers passed on to you, and not with things that perish, silver or gold,
1:19 but with blood of great worth, like that of a lamb with no flaw and no stain: the blood of Christ.
1:20 Known in advance, before the world was founded, he has been brought into the open in the last of the times, for your sake.
1:21 Through him you have come to trust God, who raised him from among the dead and gave him glory. And so your faith and your hope rest on God.
## Love from a pure heart
1:22 Your obedience to the truth has made your souls pure, ready for a love of the brothers that does not pretend. So love one another without slackening, from a pure heart.
1:23 You have been given new birth. The seed it came from was not one that decays but one that cannot decay: God's word, which is alive and remains.
1:24 For "all flesh is like grass, and all its glory like the flower of the grass. The grass dries up, and the flower falls off,
1:25 but the Lord's word remains forever." And this is the word that was brought to you as good news.
## Milk and the living stone
2:1 So take off all ill will and all trickery, the pretending, the jealousies and every kind of slander.
2:2 Like babies just born, be hungry for the milk of the word, with no trickery mixed in, so that by it you grow up into salvation,
2:3 since you have tasted it: the Lord is kind.
2:4 You keep coming to him, a stone that is alive. People threw him out as worthless, but in God's eyes he is chosen and of great worth.
2:5 And you are being built up, as stones that live, into a spiritual house, to be a holy priesthood that brings spiritual sacrifices, welcome to God through Jesus Christ.
2:6 For Scripture contains this: "Look, I am setting in Zion a stone, a cornerstone, chosen and of great worth, and the one who believes in him will not be put to shame."
2:7 The honor, then, is yours, you who believe. But for those who refuse to believe, "the stone that the builders threw out, this one has become the head of the corner,"
2:8 and "a stone that makes people stumble and a rock that makes them fall." Refusing to obey, they trip over the word, and that is what they were also set for.
2:9 But you are a chosen family, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people God has made his own, so that you may tell out the mighty acts of the one who called you out of darkness into his astonishing light.
2:10 Once you were no people, but now you are God's people. You were the ones not shown compassion, but now you have been shown compassion.
## Temporary residents among the Gentiles
2:11 Beloved, I urge you, as resident aliens and temporary residents, keep away from the flesh's desires. They are at war with the soul.
2:12 Keep your way of life fine and good among the Gentiles. Then, in the very thing they slander you for, calling you wrongdoers, they will see your good works for themselves and give glory to God on the day he visits.
2:13 Submit yourselves to every authority that humans create, because of the Lord. Submit to the king as the one ranked highest,
2:14 or to governors, since he sends them out to bring justice on wrongdoers and to commend people who do good.
2:15 God wants it this way: by doing good you are to muzzle the ignorance of senseless people.
2:16 You are free, yet do not hold your freedom up as a veil to hide wrongdoing. You are free as God's slaves.
2:17 Give honor to all people. Love the brothers. Fear God. Give honor to the king.
## Household slaves and the example of Christ
2:18 You who are household slaves, submit yourselves to your masters in all fear. Do this not only for the good and fair ones, but for the crooked ones too.
2:19 This is grace: when someone, out of conscience toward God, bears up under sorrows and suffers unjustly.
2:20 What glory is there in holding out when you sin and are hit with fists for it? But when you do good and suffer for it, and hold out, that is grace before God.
2:21 It was for this that you were called, because Christ too suffered on your behalf. He left behind for you a model to trace, so that you would follow in his tracks.
2:22 "He did no sin, nor was trickery found in his mouth."
2:23 People heaped abuse on him, and he gave no abuse back. He suffered and made no threats, but kept handing it all over to the one who judges rightly.
2:24 He himself carried our sins up in his body onto the tree, so that we would be done with sins and live for righteousness. By his welts you were healed.
2:25 You were wandering like sheep, but now you have turned around and come to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.
## Wives and husbands
3:1 Likewise, you wives, submit yourselves to your own husbands. If some of them refuse to obey the word, they can still be won over, without a word, by how their wives live,
3:2 as they watch you live a clean life in fear.
3:3 Do not let your adorning be the outside kind, the braiding of hair, the putting on of gold, or the wearing of clothes.
3:4 Let it be what is hidden in the heart, the inner person, clothed in what does not decay: a gentle and calm spirit. That is worth a great deal to God.
3:5 That was the adorning of the holy women long ago who hoped in God. They submitted themselves to their own husbands,
3:6 as Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him "lord." You have become her children when you do good and are not frightened by any terror.
3:7 Husbands, likewise: share your home with your wives with knowledge, giving honor to the female as to a weaker vessel, and as to co-heirs with you of the grace of life. Then nothing will cut off your prayers.
## Suffering for doing good
3:8 To sum up: all of you, think as one, feel with one another, love like brothers, have tender hearts and humble minds.
3:9 Do not give back evil for evil or abuse for abuse. Do the opposite: speak blessing. You were called for that very thing, so that blessing would be your inheritance.
3:10 For "the one who wants to love life and to see good days must stop his tongue from evil, and his lips from speaking trickery.
3:11 He must turn aside from evil and do what is good. He must look for peace and run after it.
3:12 For the Lord's eyes turn toward the righteous, and his ears toward their prayer, but the Lord's face is set against those who do evil."
3:13 And if you are devoted to what is good, who will do you harm?
3:14 But even if you do suffer on account of righteousness, you are blessed. "Do not fear what they fear, and do not be shaken."
3:15 Instead, in your hearts count Christ as Lord and holy. Be ready, always, to speak in your defense when anyone asks you to account for the hope that is in you. But do it gently and with fear.
3:16 Keep your conscience good, so that in the very thing for which you are spoken against, the people who abuse your good way of life in Christ will be shamed.
3:17 If it is what God wants, suffering for doing good is better than suffering for doing wrong.
## Christ, the spirits in prison and the flood
3:18 For Christ too suffered for sins, a single time, the one who was righteous for those who were not, so that he could lead you to God. In the flesh he was put to death; in the spirit he was made alive.
3:19 In the spirit, too, he went and made a proclamation to the spirits in prison,
3:20 who had refused to obey once, long ago, when God's patience was waiting in the days of Noah, while the ark was being made ready. In it a few, eight souls, were carried safe through water.
3:21 And baptism, the counterpart of that water, saves you now as well. This is not the removing of dirt from the flesh but the pledge of a good conscience made to God. It saves through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,
3:22 who is at God's right hand, having traveled into heaven, and angels, authorities and powers have been placed under him.
## Done with sin
4:1 Christ, then, suffered in the flesh. Arm yourselves too with the same intent: the one who has suffered in the flesh has broken with sin,
4:2 and so he spends the time he has left in the flesh living no longer by human desires but by God's will.
4:3 The time already gone by was plenty for carrying out what the Gentiles want: you lived in shameless living, desires, heavy drinking, wild parties, drinking bouts and forbidden idol worship.
4:4 Now they find it strange that you do not run with them into the same gush of reckless living, and they slander you.
4:5 They will answer for it to the one who is prepared to judge the living and the dead.
4:6 That is the reason the good news was announced even to the dead: so that they would be judged in the flesh by human measure, but live in the spirit by God's measure.
## The end is near
4:7 The end of all things has come close. So be sensible and keep a clear head for prayer.
4:8 More than anything, hold on to a love for one another that never slackens, because love lays a cover over a great number of sins.
4:9 Open your homes to one another as guests, without grumbling.
4:10 Each of you has been given a gift. Put it to work serving each other, like good household managers of God's many-sided grace.
4:11 The one who speaks should speak as one handing on oracles of God. The one who serves should serve with the strength God furnishes. In this way God will be glorified in all things through Jesus Christ. To him belong the glory and the might forever and ever. Amen.
## The fiery ordeal
4:12 Beloved, the fiery ordeal among you has come to test you. Do not find it strange, as though you were meeting something foreign to you.
4:13 Instead, as far as you share in Christ's sufferings, be glad. Then when his glory is unveiled you will be glad too, and shout for joy.
4:14 If you are insulted over the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory, the Spirit of God, comes to rest on you.
4:15 Let none of you suffer for being a murderer, a thief, a wrongdoer, or a busybody in other people's affairs.
4:16 But if it is for being a Christian, there is no shame in it. Let him give glory to God in this matter.
4:17 The time has come for judgment to start from God's house. If it starts with us first, what will the end be for those who refuse to obey God's good news?
4:18 And "if the righteous person is barely saved, where will the ungodly man and the sinner appear?"
4:19 So those who suffer because God wills it should go on doing good and entrust their souls to the Creator, who is faithful.
## To the elders and the young
5:1 To the elders among you, I urge this, as your fellow elder, a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and a partner in the glory that is about to be unveiled:
5:2 shepherd God's flock among you, overseeing it, not under pressure but freely, in God's way; not out of greed for dirty money but gladly.
5:3 Do not act as lords over those allotted to you; be patterns for the flock instead.
5:4 Then when the Chief Shepherd comes into view, you will win the wreath of glory that never withers.
5:5 Likewise, you younger ones, submit yourselves to the elders. And all of you, tie humility on toward one another, because "God takes his stand against the proud, but to the humble he gives grace."
## Stand against the devil
5:6 So let yourselves be brought low under God's strong hand, so that he can lift you high at the right time.
5:7 Throw every worry you have onto him, because you matter to him.
5:8 Keep a clear head; stay awake. Your opponent, the devil, is walking about like a lion that roars, looking for someone to gulp down.
5:9 Stand up against him, solid in your faith. You know that the same sufferings are being carried through to the end by your brothers in the world.
5:10 When you have suffered for a short time, the God of every grace, who called you in Christ into his glory that lasts forever, will himself put you right. He will set you firm, give you strength and lay a foundation under you.
5:11 To him belongs the might forever. Amen.
## Final greetings
5:12 Through Silvanus, a brother I count faithful, I have written you these few lines. I am urging you and bearing witness that this is God's true grace. Stand in it.
5:13 Greetings to you from her who is in Babylon, chosen along with you, and from Mark, my son.
5:14 Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to you, all who are in Christ.
