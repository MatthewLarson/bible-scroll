## A report and a call to battle
1:1 The vision of Obadiah. / The Lord GOD says this about Edom: / We have heard a report from the LORD, / and an envoy was sent among the nations: / "Rise up! Let us rise up against her for battle!"
## Pride in the rocks
1:2 Look, I am making you small among the nations. / You are greatly despised.
1:3 The pride of your heart has fooled you, / you who live in the cracks of the cliff, / whose home is the height. / You say in your heart, / "Who will lower me to the ground?"
1:4 If you rise high like the eagle, / if your nest is set among the stars, / from there I will bring you down, / the LORD declares.
## Plundered and betrayed
1:5 If thieves came to you, / if robbers in the night, / (how ruined you are!) / would they not steal only what they need? / If grape harvesters came to you, / would they not leave gleanings?
1:6 How Esau has been searched through, / his hidden stores pried out!
1:7 They sent you away to the border, / all your covenant partners. / Your friends in peace deceived you and overpowered you. / Those who eat your bread set a trap under you. / He has no understanding.
1:8 On that day, the LORD declares, / will I not destroy the wise from Edom, / and understanding from Mount Esau?
1:9 Your warriors will be shattered with fear, Teman, / so that every man on Mount Esau is cut off by slaughter.
## Violence against a brother
1:10 You did violence to your brother Jacob, / and for that shame will cover you. / You will be cut off forever.
1:11 You stood aside on that day, / the day strangers took his wealth away, / when foreigners came in through his gates / and cast lots for Jerusalem. / You were just like one of them.
1:12 Do not look on with delight at your brother's day, / the day of his misfortune. / Do not be glad about the people of Judah / on the day they are lost. / Do not talk big / on the day of trouble.
1:13 Do not enter my people's gate / on the day of their calamity. / Do not look with delight at their trouble, you too, / on the day of their calamity. / Do not reach out a hand for their wealth / on the day of their calamity.
1:14 Do not stand at the crossroads / to cut off their survivors. / Do not hand over those who are left / on the day of trouble.
## The day of the LORD
1:15 For the day of the LORD is near, against all the nations. / It will be done to you just as you did it. / Your payback will return on your own head.
1:16 You drank on my holy mountain. / So all the nations will drink on and on. / They will drink, they will gulp it down, / and they will become as if they had never been.
## Zion and Mount Esau
1:17 But on Mount Zion there will be survivors, / and it will be holy. / The house of Jacob will take over their own property.
1:18 The house of Jacob will be a fire, / the house of Joseph a flame, / and the house of Esau stubble. / They will blaze among them and devour them. / No one will be left of the house of Esau. / The LORD has said it.
1:19 The people of the Negev will take over Mount Esau, / and the people of the Shephelah, the land of the Philistines. / They will take over the fields of Ephraim and the fields of Samaria, / and Benjamin will take Gilead.
1:20 As for the exiles of this army of Israelites: / they will take Canaan's land as far as Zarephath. / As for the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad: / they will take the cities of the Negev.
1:21 Rescuers will go up on Mount Zion / to judge Mount Esau, / and royal power will belong to the LORD.
