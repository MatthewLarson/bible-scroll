## Order in the worship God commanded
40:1 Since these things are clear to us, and we have bent down to peer into the depths of the knowledge of God, we ought to do in good order everything the Master told us to carry out at the times he set.
40:2 He commanded that the offerings and the services be carried out, and not at random or without order, but at fixed times and hours.
40:3 He himself, by his highest will, settled where he wants them carried out and by whom, so that everything would be done in a holy way, as it pleases him, and be acceptable to his will.
40:4 So those who make their offerings at the times laid down are acceptable and blessed. They follow the Master's laws, and they do not go wrong.
40:5 For the high priest has been given his own services. The priests have been assigned their own place. The Levites have their own ministries laid on them. The layperson is bound by the rules for laypeople.
## Each in his own rank
41:1 Brothers, each of us must please God in his own rank, with a good conscience, not stepping over the fixed rule of his service, and with reverence.
41:2 Brothers, the daily sacrifices, the sacrifices for vows, and the sacrifices for sin and for guilt are not presented just anywhere. They are presented in Jerusalem alone. And even there they are not presented in every place, but in front of the sanctuary, at the altar, after the high priest and those already named who serve there have checked the offering for any flaw.
41:3 So those who do anything against what fits his will have death as their penalty.
41:4 You see, brothers: the more knowledge we have been counted worthy of, the more danger we stand in.
## The apostles and those they appointed
42:1 The apostles were given the good news for us by the Lord Jesus Christ. Jesus the Christ was sent out from God.
42:2 So Christ comes from God, and the apostles come from Christ. Both came about in good order, by God's will.
42:3 So they received their orders. They were fully convinced by the resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ and made firm in faith by the word of God. Then, with the full certainty the Holy Spirit gives, they went out preaching the good news that the kingdom of God was about to come.
42:4 So they went preaching from region to region and city to city. They tested their firstfruits by the Spirit and appointed them overseers and deacons for those who would later believe.
42:5 And this was nothing new. Overseers and deacons had been written about many ages before. For somewhere the Scripture says this: "I will appoint their overseers in righteousness and their deacons in faith."
## Moses and Aaron's rod
43:1 Why be surprised, then, if those who had been entrusted in Christ by God with such a work appointed the men just named? Even the blessed Moses, "a faithful attendant in the whole house," wrote down in the sacred books everything he was commanded, and the rest of the prophets followed him and added their witness to the laws he gave.
43:2 Jealousy once broke out over the priesthood, and the tribes were rebelling over which of them should be honored with that glorious title. So Moses ordered the twelve tribal leaders to bring him rods, each with the name of one tribe written on it. He took them, tied them together, and sealed them with the rings of the tribal leaders. Then he laid them away in the tent of the testimony, on God's table.
43:3 He shut the tent and sealed the keys, just as he had sealed the rods.
43:4 And he said to them, "Brothers, the tribe whose rod sprouts is the one God has chosen to be his priests and to serve him."
43:5 When morning came, he called together all Israel, the six hundred thousand men. He showed the tribal leaders the seals, opened the tent of the testimony, and brought out the rods. Aaron's rod was found not only sprouted but bearing fruit.
43:6 What do you think, beloved? Did Moses not know ahead of time that this would happen? He knew it very well. But he did it this way so that there would be no disorder in Israel, and so that the name of the true and only God would be glorified. To him be the glory for ever and ever. Amen.
## The wrong of removing the elders
44:1 Through our Lord Jesus Christ, our apostles also knew that the title of overseer would be fought over.
44:2 That is why, since they had been given full knowledge ahead of time, they appointed the men already named. Afterward they added a rule that if they fell asleep, other tested men should take over their service.
44:3 Some were appointed by the apostles, or later by other respected men with the whole church agreeing. They have served the flock of Christ without blame, with humility, quietly and with no vulgar self-seeking, and for many years everyone has spoken well of them. So we do not think it right to throw these men out of their service.
44:4 For it will be no small sin on our part if we throw out those who have presented the gifts of the office of overseer blamelessly and in a holy way.
44:5 Blessed are the elders who traveled the road ahead of us and whose departure was fruitful and complete. They have no fear that anyone will move them from the place set up for them.
44:6 For we see that you have moved some men out of the service they honored without blame, though they were living good lives.
## The righteous were never thrown out by the holy
45:1 You love a quarrel, brothers, and you are zealous over things that have to do with salvation.
45:2 You have bent down to look into the sacred Scriptures, the true ones, the ones given through the Holy Spirit.
45:3 You know that nothing unjust or falsified is written in them. You will not find righteous people thrown out by holy men.
45:4 Righteous people were hunted down, but by the lawless. They were put in prison, but by the unholy. They were stoned by lawbreakers. They were killed by men who had taken up a foul and unjust jealousy.
45:5 They suffered these things and bore them with honor.
45:6 For what are we to say, brothers? Was Daniel thrown into the lions' pit by people who feared God?
45:7 Or were Ananias, Azarias and Misael shut up in the fiery furnace by people who worshiped the Most High with magnificent and glorious worship? Never! Then who did these things? Hateful men, full of every evil. Their rage drove their quarrel so far that they put those who served God with a holy and blameless purpose through torture. They did not know that the Most High is the champion and shield of those who worship his all-excellent name with a pure conscience. To him be the glory for ever and ever. Amen.
45:8 But those who held out in confidence inherited glory and honor. They were lifted up, and God wrote their names in his memorial for ever and ever. Amen.
## Why is there splitting among you?
46:1 So we too must hold fast to examples like these, brothers.
46:2 For it is written, "Cling to the holy, because those who cling to them will be made holy."
46:3 And again in another place it says, "With an innocent man you will be innocent, and with a chosen man you will be chosen, and with a crooked man you will twist."
46:4 So let us cling to the innocent and the righteous. These are God's chosen.
46:5 Why are there fights and outbursts of anger and divisions and splits and war among you?
46:6 Is it not one God we have, and one Christ, and one Spirit of grace poured out on us? Is our calling in Christ not one?
46:7 Why do we pull apart and tear to pieces the members of Christ? Why do we rebel against our own body, and come to such madness that we forget that we are members of one another? Remember the words of the Lord Jesus.
46:8 For he said, "Woe to that man! It would have been good for him if he had not been born, rather than trip up one of my chosen. It would have been better for him to have a millstone hung on him and be drowned in the sea, than to turn aside one of my chosen."
46:9 Your split has turned many aside. It has thrown many into discouragement, many into doubt, and all of us into grief. And your rebellion goes on.
## Take up Paul's letter
47:1 Take up the letter of the blessed Paul the apostle.
47:2 What did he write to you first, at the start of the gospel?
47:3 He was truly led by the Spirit when he wrote to you about himself and Cephas and Apollos, because even then you had taken sides.
47:4 But that taking of sides brought less sin on you, because you sided with apostles who had a good name and with a man those apostles had tested.
47:5 But now think about who it is that has turned you aside and lessened the honor of your famous love for one another.
47:6 It is shameful, beloved, very shameful, and unworthy of a life lived in Christ, to hear that the solid and ancient church of the Corinthians is rebelling against its elders because of one or two people.
47:7 And this report has reached not only us, but also people who do not share our faith. So your foolishness brings insults on the name of the Lord and works up danger for yourselves as well.
## The gate of righteousness
48:1 So let us remove this quickly. Let us fall down before the Master and weep as we beg him to be merciful, to be reconciled to us, and to bring us back to the pure and honorable life of our love for one another.
48:2 For this is the gate of righteousness that opens into life, as it is written: "Open the gates of righteousness for me, so that I may go in through them and give thanks to the Lord.
48:3 This is the Lord's gate. The righteous will go in through it."
48:4 Many gates stand open, but the gate of righteousness is the one in Christ. Blessed are all who have gone in through it and keep their path straight in holiness and righteousness, carrying out everything without disturbance.
48:5 Let a man be faithful. Let him be able to speak out knowledge. Let him be wise in judging what is said. Let him be pure in what he does.
48:6 The bigger a man seems, the more he should humble himself and look for what benefits everyone, not just himself.
## What love does
49:1 Whoever has love in Christ must do what Christ commands.
49:2 The bond of God's love: who is able to explain it?
49:3 The splendor of its beauty: who is equal to telling it?
49:4 The height that love lifts us to is beyond words.
49:5 Love joins us to God. Love lays a cover over a great number of sins. Love puts up with everything and is patient in everything. There is nothing vulgar in love, nothing arrogant. Love has no split. Love does not rebel. Love does everything in harmony. In love all of God's chosen were made perfect. Without love nothing pleases God.
49:6 In love the Master took us to himself. Because of the love he had for us, Jesus Christ our Lord gave his blood for us, by God's will: his flesh for our flesh, and his soul for our souls.
## Pray to be found in love
50:1 You see, beloved, how great and wonderful love is. Its perfection is beyond explaining.
50:2 Who is able to be found in it, except those God counts worthy? So let us plead and ask for his mercy, that we may be found in love, free of human taking of sides, without blame.
50:3 All the generations from Adam to this day have passed away. But those who were made perfect in love by the grace of God have a place among the godly, and they will be shown openly when the kingdom of Christ comes to visit.
50:4 For it is written, "Go into your inner rooms for a very little while, until my anger and my rage pass by. Then I will remember a good day, and I will raise you up out of your tombs."
50:5 Blessed are we, beloved, if we have been doing God's commands in the harmony of love, so that through love our sins may be forgiven us.
50:6 For it is written, "Blessed are those whose lawless acts have been forgiven, and whose sins have been covered over. Blessed is the man whose sin the Lord will never reckon, and in whose mouth there is no deceit."
50:7 This blessing came to those whom God chose through Jesus Christ our Lord. To him be the glory for ever and ever. Amen.
## Confess, and do not harden your hearts
51:1 So whatever wrong we have slipped into and done because of the attacks of the enemy, let us ask to be forgiven. And those who were the leaders of the rebellion and the division ought to look to the hope we all share.
51:2 For those who live in fear and love would rather suffer harm themselves than see their neighbors suffer it. They would rather take blame on themselves than on the harmony that was handed down to us so well and so justly.
51:3 For a man does well to confess his wrongs rather than harden his heart, the way the hearts of those who rebelled against God's attendant Moses were hardened. Their judgment was plain for all to see.
51:4 For "they went down alive into Hades," and "death will be their shepherd."
51:5 Pharaoh and his army and all the leaders of Egypt, with their chariots and their riders, were sunk in the Red Sea and died. There was no other reason for it than this: their foolish hearts were hardened after the signs and wonders were done in the land of Egypt through God's attendant Moses.
## The sacrifice God wants
52:1 Brothers, the Master of all things needs nothing. He needs nothing from anyone except the confession made to him.
52:2 For David, the chosen one, says, "I will confess to the Lord, and it will please him more than a young calf that is growing horns and hoofs. Let the poor see it and be glad."
52:3 And again he says, "Sacrifice to God a sacrifice of praise, and pay your vows to the Most High. Call on me in the day of your trouble, and I will rescue you, and you will give me glory.
52:4 For a broken spirit is the sacrifice for God."
## Moses offers himself for the people
53:1 For you know the sacred Scriptures, beloved. You know them well, and you have bent down to look into the oracles of God. So we write these things to remind you.
53:2 When Moses went up the mountain and spent forty days and forty nights in fasting and humbling himself, God said to him, "Go down from here quickly, because your people, whom you led out of the land of Egypt, have broken the law. They have been quick to step off the road you commanded them to walk. They have made themselves cast idols."
53:3 And the Lord said to him, "I have spoken to you once and twice, saying, 'I have seen this people, and look, it is stiff-necked.' Let me wipe them out. I will blot out their name from under heaven, and I will make you into a great and wonderful nation, far bigger than this one."
53:4 And Moses said, "No, Lord! Forgive this people their sin, or blot me out too from the book of the living."
53:5 What great love! What perfection that cannot be outdone! The attendant speaks boldly to his Lord. He asks forgiveness for the crowd of people, or else he asks to be blotted out with them.
## Let the one who caused it leave
54:1 So who among you is noble? Who is tender-hearted? Who is filled with love?
54:2 Let him say: "If the rebellion and fighting and splits are because of me, I will step aside. I will go away wherever you want, and I will do what the people tell me. Only let Christ's flock live in peace with the elders who have been appointed."
54:3 Whoever does this will win great fame for himself in Christ, and every place will welcome him, because "the earth and all that fills it belong to the Lord."
54:4 This is what those who live as citizens of God's commonwealth, a citizenship no one regrets, have done and will do.
## Those who gave themselves for others
55:1 Let us bring examples from the nations as well. Many kings and rulers, when a time of plague came on, were told by an oracle to hand themselves over to death, and they did, so that by their own blood they might rescue their citizens. Many left their own cities so that the rebellion there would go no further.
55:2 We know of many among us who have handed themselves over into chains so that they could ransom others. Many handed themselves over into slavery, took the price paid for them, and fed others with it.
55:3 Many women, made strong by the grace of God, have done many brave deeds.
55:4 The blessed Judith, when her city was under siege, asked the elders to let her go out into the camp of the foreigners.
55:5 So she handed herself over to danger and went out for love of her homeland and of her people under siege. And the Lord handed Holofernes over by a woman's hand.
55:6 No less did Esther, perfect in faith, put herself in danger to rescue the nation of Israel when it was about to be destroyed. For by her fasting and her humbling herself she pleaded with the all-seeing Master of the ages. He saw how lowly her soul was and rescued the people for whose sake she had risked her life.
## Accept discipline
56:1 So let us also plead for those who are caught in some wrong, that forbearance and humility may be given them, so that they give way, not to us, but to God's will. In this way the mercy and remembrance they find with God and the saints will be fruitful and complete.
56:2 Let us accept discipline, beloved. No one ought to be angry at it. The warnings we give one another are good and help more than words can say, for they join us to God's will.
56:3 For this is what the holy word says: "The Lord disciplined me hard, but he did not hand me over to death."
56:4 "For whoever the Lord loves, he disciplines, and he whips every son he takes in."
56:5 "For a righteous man will discipline me in mercy and correct me," it says, "but do not let the oil of sinners anoint my head."
56:6 And again it says, "Blessed is the man the Lord has corrected. Do not refuse the warning of the Almighty. For he causes pain, and he puts things right again.
56:7 He struck, and his hands healed.
56:8 Six times he will rescue you from hard straits, and the seventh time no evil will touch you.
56:9 In famine he will rescue you from death, and in war he will set you free from the hand of iron.
56:10 He will hide you from the whip of the tongue, and you will not be afraid when evils come.
56:11 You will laugh at the unjust and the lawless, and you will not be afraid of wild animals.
56:12 For the wild animals will be at peace with you.
56:13 Then you will know that your house will be at peace, and the home of your tent will not fail.
56:14 And you will know that your offspring will be many, and your children like all the grass of the field.
56:15 And you will come to the grave like ripe grain harvested in its season, or like a heap on the threshing floor gathered in at the right hour."
56:16 You see, beloved, how great a protection there is for those disciplined by the Master. For he is a good Father, and he disciplines us so that we may be shown mercy through his holy discipline.
## Submit to the elders
57:1 So you who laid the foundation of the rebellion, submit to the elders. Let yourselves be disciplined into repentance, bending the knees of your heart.
57:2 Learn to submit. Put away the boasting and arrogant self-will of your tongue. For it is better for you to be found small and respected in the flock of Christ than to seem to stand out and be thrown out of his hope.
57:3 For this is what the all-excellent Wisdom says: "Look, I will pour out to you a saying from my breath, and I will teach you my word.
57:4 I called, and you did not listen. I stretched out my words, and you paid no attention. You made my advice count for nothing, and you would not obey my corrections. So I too will laugh at your ruin. I will gloat when destruction comes on you, when sudden panic arrives and overthrow is there like a storm, or when trouble and siege come on you.
57:5 For when you call on me, I will not listen to you. The wicked will look for me and will not find me. For they hated wisdom and did not choose the fear of the Lord. They would not pay attention to my advice, and they sneered at my corrections.
57:6 So they will eat the fruit of their own way and be filled with their own ungodliness.
57:7 Because they wronged the simple, they will be killed, and a reckoning will destroy the ungodly. But whoever listens to me will make his home in hope, trusting, and will rest without fear of any evil."
## Take our advice
58:1 So let us obey his all-holy and glorious name and escape the threats Wisdom spoke before against those who disobey, so that we may make our home in trust in the most holy name of his majesty.
58:2 Accept our advice, and you will have nothing to regret. For as God lives, and as the Lord Jesus Christ lives, and the Holy Spirit, the faith and the hope of the chosen: whoever does the decrees and commands God has given, in humility, with steady forbearance, and without regret, will be enrolled and counted in the number of those who are saved through Jesus Christ. Through him be glory to him for ever and ever. Amen.
## The great prayer
59:1 But if some disobey what God has said through us, let them know that they will tie themselves up in wrongdoing and in no small danger.
59:2 But we will be innocent of this sin. We will make our request and plea with all our hearts, asking that the Creator of all things keep unbroken the counted number of his chosen in the whole world, through his beloved servant Jesus Christ. Through him he called us from darkness into light, from not knowing into knowing the glory of his name.
59:3 Grant us, Lord, to hope in your name, the source of all creation, as you open the eyes of our heart to know you, who alone are Most High among the highest, who rest as the Holy One among the holy. You humble the insolence of the proud. You break up the plans of the nations. You lift the lowly up high and bring the lofty low. You make rich and make poor. You kill and make alive. You alone are the finder of spirits and the God of all flesh. You look into the depths. You watch over human deeds. You help those in danger and save those who have given up hope. You are the Maker and the Overseer of every spirit. You multiply the nations on earth, and out of them all you have chosen those who love you through Jesus Christ, your beloved servant. Through him you disciplined us, made us holy and gave us honor.
59:4 Master, we ask you to become our helper and our protector. Save those among us who are in trouble. Show mercy to the lowly. Lift the fallen to their feet. Appear to those who need you. Make the sick well. Bring back the wanderers among your people. Fill the hungry. Ransom our prisoners. Raise up the weak. Encourage the faint-hearted. May all the nations come to know you: that you alone are God, that Jesus Christ is your servant, and that we are your people, the sheep of your pasture.
## Forgive us
60:1 For through what you have done, you made the everlasting order of the world plain to see. You, Lord, made the whole inhabited world. You are faithful through all the generations, righteous in your judgments, wonderful in strength and magnificence, wise in creating and understanding in setting firmly what you made, good in what we see and kind to those who trust in you. Merciful and compassionate one, forgive us our lawless acts and our wrongs, our failings and our faults.
60:2 Do not count every sin of your slaves and your slave women. Wash us clean with the cleansing of your truth. Guide our steps, so that we walk in holiness of heart and do what is good and pleasing before you and before our rulers.
60:3 Yes, Master, let your face shine on us for good, in peace, so that your strong hand may shelter us and your high arm rescue us from every sin. And rescue us from those who hate us without cause.
60:4 Grant harmony and peace to us and to everyone living on the earth, as you gave it to our fathers when they called on you in a holy way, in faith and truth. Make us obedient to your almighty and glorious name, and to our rulers and governors on the earth.
## For our rulers
61:1 You, Master, gave them the authority of kingship through your magnificent might, which is beyond words, so that we, knowing the glory and honor you have given them, would submit to them and in no way go against your will. Give them, Lord, health, peace, harmony and steadiness, so that they may govern without stumbling in the rule you have given them.
61:2 For you, Master in heaven, King of the ages, give the sons of men glory and honor and authority over what is on the earth. Lord, guide their plans by what is good and pleasing before you, so that they govern in peace and gentleness, in a godly way, with the authority you have given them, and so find your mercy.
61:3 You alone are able to do these things for us, and greater good things still. We give you thanks through the high priest and protector of our souls, Jesus Christ. Through him be glory and majesty to you, now and for all generations and for ever and ever. Amen.
## What this letter has said
62:1 Brothers, we have written to you enough about what belongs to our religion and what helps most toward a good life, for those who want to steer their lives in a godly and just way.
62:2 For we have touched on every point of faith and repentance, real love, self-control, self-restraint and endurance. We have reminded you that you must please God Almighty in a holy way, in righteousness, truth and patience. You must live in harmony, holding no grudges, in love and peace with steady forbearance, just as our fathers we pointed to pleased him by being humble toward God, the Father and Maker, and toward all people.
62:3 And we were all the more glad to remind you of these things, because we knew well that we were writing to men who are faithful and highly respected and who have bent down to look into the oracles of God's instruction.
## Bow the neck and be at peace
63:1 So it is right for us to come to examples so many and so great, to bow our necks and take our place in obedience, so that we may stop this empty rebellion and reach without any blame the goal truly set before us.
63:2 For you will give us joy and gladness if you obey what we have written through the Holy Spirit, and cut out the lawless anger of your jealousy, as we have pleaded with you in this letter for peace and harmony.
63:3 We have sent faithful and self-restrained men who have lived among us without blame from youth to old age. They will be witnesses between you and us.
63:4 We did this so that you may know that all our care has been, and still is, that you be at peace soon.
## Blessing
64:1 Now may the all-seeing God, the Master of spirits and Lord of all flesh, who chose the Lord Jesus Christ, and us through him to be a people that is his own private treasure, give to every soul that has called on his magnificent and holy name faith, fear, peace, endurance and patience, self-control, purity and self-restraint, so that they may please his name, through our high priest and protector Jesus Christ. Through him be glory and majesty, might and honor to him, now and into all the ages of the ages. Amen.
## Send the messengers back
65:1 Send back to us quickly, in peace and with joy, the men we sent, Claudius Ephebus and Valerius Bito, with Fortunatus too, so that they may report all the sooner the peace and harmony we pray for and long for, and so that we too may soon be glad about your settled order.
65:2 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you, and with all people everywhere whom God has called through him. Through him be glory, honor, might and majesty, an everlasting throne, to him, from ages past into the ages of the ages. Amen. The letter of the Romans to the Corinthians.
