## Greeting
1:0 Ignatius, also called Theophorus, to the church that is worthy to be called blessed, the church in Ephesus of Asia. It has been blessed with greatness by the fullness of God the Father. Before the ages it was marked out ahead of time to be, always, for a glory that lasts and does not change. It is united and chosen in true suffering, by the will of the Father and of Jesus Christ our God. Warmest greetings in Jesus Christ and in a joy without blame.
## Thanks for Onesimus and the others
1:1 In God I welcomed your much-loved name, which you have gained by a righteous nature, by faith and love in Christ Jesus our Savior. You are imitators of God. By the blood of God you rekindled the work that comes naturally to you, and you carried it through to perfection.
1:2 For you heard that I was on my way from Syria in chains, for the sake of the name and the hope we share. You heard that I hope, through your prayer, to reach the goal of fighting wild beasts in Rome, so that by reaching it I may be able to be a disciple. And you were eager to see me.
1:3 In the name of God I have received your whole large community in Onesimus, a man of love beyond words, and your overseer. I pray that you love him in the way of Jesus Christ, and that all of you be like him. Blessed is the one who gave you, worthy as you are, the gift of having such an overseer.
2:1 As for my fellow slave Burrhus, your deacon in God's way, blessed in everything, I pray that he may stay on with me, for your honor and the overseer's. And Crocus too, worthy of God and of you, whom I received as a living sample of your love, has refreshed me in every way. May the Father of Jesus Christ refresh him in the same way, together with Onesimus, Burrhus, Euplus and Fronto. Through them I have seen all of you in love.
2:2 May I always have the benefit of you, if I am worthy. So it is right in every way to give glory to Jesus Christ, who has given glory to you. Then, made complete in one submission and submitting to the overseer and the council of elders, you may be made holy in everything.
## One mind and one song
3:1 I do not command you as though I were someone. Though I am bound in the Name, I am not yet complete in Jesus Christ. Right now I have only made a start at being a disciple, and I talk to you as people who learn alongside me. For I needed you to rub me down with oil: with faith, warning, endurance and patience.
3:2 But since love does not let me keep silent about you, I have taken the lead in urging you to run together with the mind of God. For Jesus Christ, our life that cannot be pulled apart from us, is the Father's mind, just as the overseers who are appointed throughout the ends of the earth are in the mind of Jesus Christ.
4:1 That is why it is fitting for you to run together with the overseer's mind, as you already do. For your council of elders, rightly famous and worthy of God, is fitted to the overseer as strings are to a lyre. So in your harmony and your love that sounds in tune, Jesus Christ is sung.
4:2 Each one of you, become a choir too. Then, sounding together in harmony and taking God's pitch in unity, you will sing with one voice through Jesus Christ to the Father. He will hear you, and through the good you do he will recognize that you are members of his Son. So it is useful for you to be in unity without blame, so that you may also share in God always.
## The overseer and the altar
5:1 In a short time I came to have such a close bond with your overseer, not a human one but a spiritual one. If so, how much more do I call you blessed, who are blended with him as the church is with Jesus Christ, and as Jesus Christ is with the Father, so that all things may be in tune in unity!
5:2 Let no one be misled. If anyone is not within the altar, he goes without God's bread. For if the prayer of one or two has such strength, how much more the prayer of the overseer and of the whole church!
5:3 So the one who does not gather in one place is already proud, and he has set himself apart. For it is written, "God takes his stand against the proud." So let us be eager not to take a stand against the overseer, so that we may be submitting to God.
6:1 And the more anyone sees the overseer keeping silent, the more he should fear him. For anyone the master of the house sends to manage his own household, we must receive just like the one who sent him. So plainly we ought to look at the overseer as at the Lord himself.
6:2 Onesimus himself praises highly your good order in God: you all live by the truth, and no heresy makes its home among you. In fact, you listen to no one more than to someone who speaks truthfully about Jesus Christ.
## The one doctor
7:1 For some people make a habit of carrying the Name around with wicked deceit, while they do other things unworthy of God. You must keep away from them as you would from wild beasts. They are rabid dogs that bite on the sly. You must guard against them, for they are hard to heal.
7:2 There is one doctor, / both of flesh and of spirit, / born and unborn, / God in man, / in death true life, / both from Mary and from God, / first able to suffer and then beyond suffering: / Jesus Christ our Lord.
8:1 So let no one trick you, just as you are not being tricked, since you are God's through and through. For when no quarrel that could torment you is lodged among you, then you are living God's way. I am the lowliest of your servants, and I give myself as an offering for you Ephesians, a church famous through the ages.
8:2 People of the flesh cannot do the things of the spirit, and people of the spirit cannot do the things of the flesh, just as faith cannot do the things of unbelief, and unbelief cannot do the things of faith. But even what you do in the flesh is spiritual, for you do everything in Jesus Christ.
9:1 But I have learned that some people from over there passed through with evil teaching. You did not let them sow it among you. You stopped your ears so as not to take in what they sow, since you are stones of the Father's temple, made ready for the building of God the Father. You are carried up to the heights by the crane of Jesus Christ, which is the cross, with the Holy Spirit for the rope. Your faith is your hoist, and love is the road that brings you up to God.
9:2 So you are all fellow travelers: God-bearers and temple-bearers, Christ-bearers, bearers of holy things, adorned in every way with the commandments of Jesus Christ. I too exult in these things. I have been counted worthy to talk with you through what I write and to rejoice with you, because by the standards of human life you love nothing but God alone.
## Pray for the others
10:1 And pray without a break for the rest of humanity too, for there is hope that they may repent and find God. So let them become your disciples, at least through your deeds.
10:2 Against their anger, be gentle. Against their boasting, be humble. Against their insults, offer your prayers. Against their error, stand firm in the faith. Against their savagery, be mild. Do not be eager to imitate them in return.
10:3 Let us be found their brothers by our forbearance. Let us be eager to be imitators of the Lord, to see who can be wronged, robbed or set aside the most. So no weed of the devil will be found among you. Instead, you may remain in Jesus Christ in all purity and self-restraint, in flesh and in spirit.
## The last times
11:1 The last times are here. From now on, let us feel shame. Let us fear God's patience, so that it does not turn into judgment against us. Either let us fear the anger to come, or let us love the grace that is here now: one of the two. Only let us be found in Christ Jesus, to live the true life.
11:2 Without him let nothing seem right to you. In him I carry my chains around, the pearls of the spirit. In them may I rise again through your prayer. May I always have a share in it, so that I may be found in the lot of the Christians of Ephesus, who have always agreed with the apostles in the power of Jesus Christ.
12:1 I know what I am, and to whom I write. I am condemned; you have received mercy. I am in danger; you are made firm.
12:2 You are the road that those being killed on their way to God pass along. You share the mysteries with Paul, who was made holy, who was given a good witness, who deserves to be called blessed. I pray to be found walking in his tracks when I reach God. In every letter he remembers you in Christ Jesus.
## Meet more often
13:1 So be eager to come together more often for the thanksgiving to God and for his glory. For when you gather in one place often, the powers of Satan are pulled down, and his ruin is undone by the harmony of your faith.
13:2 Nothing is better than peace. By it every war among those in heaven and those on earth is brought to an end.
14:1 None of this escapes you if you hold your faith and love toward Jesus Christ completely. They are where life starts and where it ends: faith is the start, and love is the end. When the two come together in unity, they are God, and everything else that makes up a noble life follows them.
14:2 No one who claims faith sins, and no one who has gained love hates. The tree shows itself by its fruit. In the same way, those who claim to be Christ's will be seen by what they do. For the work now is not a matter of claiming. It is whether someone is found in the power of faith to the end.
## Silence and speech
15:1 Better to be silent and be something than to talk and be nothing. Teaching is good, if the one who speaks does what he says. So there is one Teacher, who spoke, and it came to be. And even what he has done in silence is worthy of the Father.
15:2 The one who truly possesses Jesus' word can also hear his quiet, so that he may be complete: so that he may act through what he says and be known through what he keeps silent.
15:3 Nothing escapes the Lord. Even our secrets are near him. So let us do everything as people in whom he lives, so that we may be his temples, and he may be our God in us. And so he is, and he will appear before our faces, by the way we rightly love him.
## Those who ruin the faith
16:1 Do not be deceived, my brothers: those who ruin households will not inherit the kingdom of God.
16:2 So if those who did these things in the flesh died, how much more will someone who by evil teaching ruins the faith of God, for which Jesus Christ was crucified! Such a person, having made himself filthy, will go into the fire that cannot be put out, and so will anyone who listens to him.
17:1 This is why the Lord received perfumed oil on his head: so that he could breathe incorruption on the church. Do not be anointed with the foul smell of the teaching of the ruler of this age, or he may take you captive, away from the life set before you.
17:2 Why do we not all become sensible, since we have received the knowledge of God, which is Jesus Christ? Why are we perishing in our folly, not knowing the gift that the Lord has truly sent?
## The cross and the hidden mysteries
18:1 My spirit is the lowliest servant of the cross, which is a stumbling block to those who do not believe, but to us salvation and eternal life. Where is the wise person? Where is the debater? Where is the boasting of those who are called people of understanding?
18:2 For our God, Jesus the Christ, was carried in the womb by Mary, by God's plan, of the seed of David and of the Holy Spirit. He was born and was baptized, so that through his suffering he might purify the water.
19:1 And Mary's virginity and her giving birth escaped the notice of the ruler of this age, and so did the death of the Lord: three mysteries to be shouted aloud, which were done in God's quiet.
19:2 How, then, were they shown to the ages? A star blazed in the sky, outshining all the stars. Its light was beyond words, and its newness was astonishing. All the other stars, with the sun and moon, became a choir around the star, but it surpassed them all with its light. And there was confusion: where did this new thing come from, so unlike them?
19:3 From then on all magic was undone, and every chain of evil vanished. Ignorance was torn down. The old kingdom was destroyed, as God was made known in a human way, for the newness of everlasting life. What had been brought to completion with God now made its start. From then on everything was stirred up, because the undoing of death was being prepared.
## The second little book, and the one bread
20:1 If Jesus Christ counts me worthy through your prayer, and if it is his will, I will explain more to you in the second little book that I mean to write to you. It will be about the plan I have begun to speak of, the plan concerning the new man Jesus Christ: his faith and his love, his suffering and his resurrection.
20:2 I will do it above all if the Lord reveals to me that all of you, one by one and together, come together in grace, by name, in one faith and in Jesus Christ, who in the flesh was of David's family, the Son of Man and Son of God. Then you will obey the overseer and the council of elders with an undistracted mind, breaking one bread, which is the medicine of immortality, the antidote against dying, so that we live in Jesus Christ forever.
## Farewell
21:1 I am a life in exchange for you and for those you sent to Smyrna for the honor of God, the city I am writing to you from. I give thanks to the Lord, and I love Polycarp as I love you too. Remember me, as Jesus Christ also remembers you.
21:2 Pray for the church in Syria. From there I am being taken in chains to Rome, I who am the last of the faithful there, as I was counted worthy to be found for the honor of God. Farewell in God the Father and in Jesus Christ, the hope we share.
