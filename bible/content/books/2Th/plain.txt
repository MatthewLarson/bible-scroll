## Greeting
1:1 From Paul, Silvanus and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
1:2 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
## Thanks for a faith that grows under pressure
1:3 We owe God thanks for you at all times, brothers. It is only right, because your faith is growing far beyond what it was, and the love that each of you shows to all the rest keeps increasing.
1:4 That is why we ourselves speak proudly of you among the churches of God. We tell of your endurance and your faith through all the persecutions and troubles you are bearing.
## The Lord's coming and God's just judgment
1:5 All of it is a plain sign of God's just judgment. The purpose is that you be counted worthy of God's kingdom, and it is for that kingdom that you suffer.
1:6 After all, it is right in God's sight to give back trouble to the people who are troubling you,
1:7 and to give rest to you who are being troubled, and to us with you, at the revealing of the Lord Jesus from heaven with the angels of his power,
1:8 in a flame of fire. He will hand out punishment to people who do not know God, and to people who will not obey the good news of our Lord Jesus.
1:9 They will pay the penalty, eternal ruin from the face of the Lord and from the glory of his strength.
1:10 This will be when he comes on that day to have his glory shown in his saints and to draw wonder from all who have believed, and you are among them, because you believed the witness we brought you.
## A prayer for the readers
1:11 That is why we keep praying for you at all times: that our God will count you worthy of the call he gave you, and with power will bring to completion every good thing you long to do and every work that comes from faith.
1:12 Then the name of our Lord Jesus will have glory in you, and you will have glory in him, in keeping with the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.
## The day of the Lord is not here yet
2:1 We ask you, brothers, on the subject of our Lord Jesus Christ's coming and our gathering to him:
2:2 do not let yourselves be quickly shaken out of your senses or thrown into alarm, not by a spirit, not by a word, not by a letter that seems to be from us, claiming that the day of the Lord is already here.
## What must come first
2:3 Do not let anyone trick you by any means. That day will not come until the rebellion arrives first and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of ruin.
2:4 He is the opponent who lifts himself up over everything people call a god or worship. He goes so far as to sit down in the temple of God and put himself on display, claiming that he is God.
2:5 Don't you remember? I kept telling you this when I was still with you.
2:6 You know, too, what is holding him back now, so that he is revealed only when his own time comes.
2:7 The mystery of lawlessness is at work already. Only, the one who is holding him back now will do so until he is gone from the scene.
2:8 And then the lawless one will be revealed. The Lord Jesus will do away with him by the breath of his mouth and make him powerless by the appearing of his coming.
2:9 The lawless one's coming is driven by Satan's working, with every kind of power, and with signs and wonders that are a lie,
2:10 and with every deceit that unrighteousness uses on those who are on the road to ruin, because they did not accept the love of the truth so that they could be saved.
2:11 That is why God sends them a working that leads them astray, and so they come to believe the lie.
2:12 The outcome is that judgment falls on all who refused to believe the truth and took pleasure in unrighteousness.
## Chosen and called
2:13 As for us, we owe God thanks for you at all times, brothers whom the Lord loves. God chose you as firstfruits for salvation, by the Spirit who makes you holy and by faith in the truth.
2:14 It was for this that he called you, by means of the good news we preached, so that you might come to possess the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.
2:15 So, brothers, hold your ground. Keep a firm grip on the traditions you were taught, whether we taught them by speaking or in our letter.
2:16 Our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God our Father, loved us. By grace he gave us a comfort that never ends and a hope that is good.
2:17 May he comfort your hearts and make you strong in everything good that you do and say.
## Pray for us
3:1 One more thing, brothers: pray for us. Pray that the word of the Lord will race ahead and be given glory, as it was among you,
3:2 and that we will be rescued from people who are twisted and evil. Faith, after all, does not belong to everyone.
3:3 But the Lord is faithful, and he will give you a firm footing and keep you safe from the evil one.
3:4 In the Lord we feel sure of you: what we command, you are doing, and you will go on doing it.
3:5 May the Lord lead your hearts straight into the love of God and into the endurance of Christ.
## Those who live out of line
3:6 Brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ we give you this command: hold yourselves apart from any brother who lives out of line and not by the tradition they received from us.
3:7 You know for yourselves that you are to imitate us. When we were among you, we did not live out of line.
3:8 We did not take bread from anyone as a free gift. We toiled and labored hard, working night and day, so we would not be a weight on any of you.
3:9 Not that we lack the right. We did it to make ourselves a pattern for you, so that you would imitate us.
3:10 Back when we were with you, we kept giving you this command: "Whoever does not want to work should not eat either."
3:11 We hear that some of you are living out of line, doing no work at all but working hard at minding other people's business.
3:12 Our command to people like that, and our appeal to them in the Lord Jesus Christ, is that they work quietly and eat bread that is their own.
3:13 And you, brothers, must not give up on doing good.
3:14 If someone refuses to obey our word given in this letter, mark that person out, and do not mix with him, so that he will feel shame.
3:15 But do not treat him like an enemy. Correct him as a brother.
## Last words in Paul's own hand
3:16 The Lord of peace himself: may he give you peace continually, in every way there is. May the Lord be with all of you.
3:17 The greeting is in my own hand, Paul's. That is the sign in every letter: I write like this.
3:18 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.
