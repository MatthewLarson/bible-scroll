## The LORD weighs the heart
16:1 Setting the heart's thoughts in order is a person's part, / but the tongue's answer comes from the LORD.
16:2 A person thinks all his ways are spotless, / but the LORD is the one who weighs spirits.
16:3 Roll what you do onto the LORD, / and your plans will be made firm.
16:4 The LORD has made everything with its purpose in view, / the wicked too, for the evil day.
16:5 The LORD finds every haughty heart abhorrent; / you can shake hands on it: that person will not go free.
16:6 Guilt is atoned for by loyal love and faithfulness, / and through the fear of the LORD a person steers away from evil.
16:7 If the LORD likes the road a person walks, / he will have even his enemies at peace with him.
16:8 A little with what is right is better / than great harvests that come without justice.
16:9 A person's heart maps out his road, / but the LORD fixes where his steps fall.
## The king's lips
16:10 There is divination on a king's lips; / when he gives judgment, his mouth must not break faith.
16:11 The scale and the balance of justice are the LORD's; / he made every stone in the bag of weights.
16:12 Kings find it abhorrent to act wickedly, / for righteousness is what holds a throne steady.
16:13 Lips that speak rightly win a king's favor; / he loves the one whose words are straight.
16:14 A king's fury is death's messengers, / but a wise man will calm it.
16:15 When the king's face shines, there is life; / his goodwill is like a cloud heavy with the late rains.
## Better than gold
16:16 Gaining wisdom is far better than gold, / and gaining understanding is a better choice than silver.
16:17 The upright have a raised highway that steers away from evil; / the one who watches his way keeps his life safe.
16:18 Pride comes before a crash, / a lofty spirit before a stumble.
16:19 Being humble in spirit among the humble is better / than sharing out loot with the proud.
16:20 Pay attention to the word, and you will find what is good; / how happy is the one who trusts in the LORD!
16:21 Someone wise at heart gets a name for discernment, / and sweet speech makes teaching go further.
16:22 Good sense is a spring of life to the one who has it, / but the discipline of stubborn fools is folly.
16:23 A wise person's heart gives his mouth good sense / and gives his lips more to teach.
16:24 Kind words are a comb full of honey: / sweet to the soul, medicine for the bones.
16:25 There is a road that looks straight to a person, / but in the end it comes out on the roads of death.
16:26 A laborer's appetite labors on his behalf, / because his mouth presses him hard.
## Troublemakers
16:27 A worthless man digs up evil, / and what is on his lips burns like fire.
16:28 A twisted person lets loose quarrels, / and a gossip splits up the closest of friends.
16:29 A violent man coaxes his neighbor along / and walks him down a road that is no good.
16:30 He shuts his eyes to plot twisted things; / he presses his lips together, and the harm is done.
16:31 Gray hair is a crown of beauty, / found by those who walk in righteousness.
16:32 Being slow to anger is better than being a mighty soldier, / and ruling your own spirit beats capturing a town.
16:33 People throw the lot into the lap, / but every verdict it gives comes from the LORD.
## Quiet and quarrels
17:1 A dry bit of bread with quiet is better / than a house stuffed with sacrificial feasts and fighting.
17:2 A servant with good sense will take charge of a son who brings disgrace, / and will get a share of the inheritance along with the brothers.
17:3 Silver goes in the melting pot and gold in the furnace, / but it is the LORD who puts hearts to the test.
17:4 A wrongdoer pays heed to evil talk; / a liar listens closely to a tongue that ruins.
17:5 Mocking a poor man is taunting the one who made him; / a person who enjoys someone's disaster will not go free.
17:6 Children's children are the crown old people wear, / and children find their glory in their fathers.
17:7 Lofty words are out of place for a godless fool, / and lying lips far more so for a noble.
17:8 A bribe is a magic stone in the eyes of its owner; / wherever he turns, he comes out ahead.
17:9 Laying a cover over a wrong is how you go after love; / bringing the matter up again splits up the closest of friends.
17:10 One rebuke goes further into someone with understanding / than a hundred lashes into a fool.
17:11 A bad man is out for nothing but rebellion, / so a merciless messenger will be sent against him.
17:12 Meet a mother bear robbed of her cubs, / but do not meet a fool in his folly.
17:13 Whoever repays good with evil / will never be rid of evil in his own house.
17:14 The start of a quarrel is like letting water loose, / so before the fight breaks out, let it go.
17:15 Declaring the wicked innocent and declaring the righteous guilty: / the LORD finds both of them abhorrent.
17:16 What good is the price of wisdom in a fool's hand / when he has no heart for it?
## Friends and brothers
17:17 At every time a friend keeps loving, / and a brother is born for the hard days.
17:18 A senseless person strikes palms / and stands good for his neighbor in front of him.
17:19 Whoever loves wrongdoing loves a fight; / whoever raises his gate high is asking for a crash.
17:20 A person with a crooked heart does not find good, / and one whose tongue turns things around falls into trouble.
17:21 He who fathers a fool does so to his own grief; / the father of a godless fool knows no joy.
17:22 A glad heart does as much good as a cure, / but a beaten-down spirit leaves the bones dry.
17:23 The wicked man accepts a bribe slipped from a robe / to twist the roads that justice should take.
17:24 For someone with understanding, wisdom is right before his face; / a fool's eyes are off at the far ends of the earth.
17:25 A son who is a fool is heartache to his father / and bitterness to the woman who gave him birth.
17:26 Punishing an innocent person with a fine is not good, / nor beating nobles because they are honest.
17:27 Holding back your words shows real knowledge; / a person with a cool spirit has understanding.
17:28 Even a stubborn fool, if he stays silent, is counted as wise; / with his lips shut, he seems to have discernment.
## Words that run deep
18:1 The person who cuts himself off is chasing his own wants; / he flares up at every piece of sound wisdom.
18:2 A fool has no taste for understanding; / he only wants his own mind put on display.
18:3 Along with the wicked comes contempt, / and with shame comes scorn.
18:4 The words from a person's mouth are deep water, / a flowing brook, a spring of wisdom.
18:5 Showing favor to the wicked is wrong, / and so is pushing the righteous aside at a trial.
18:6 A fool's lips walk into a quarrel, / and his mouth calls out for a beating.
18:7 His own mouth wrecks the fool; / his lips set a trap for his life.
18:8 A gossip's words are like treats gulped down; / they slide into the deepest rooms of the belly.
18:9 A man who goes slow at his job / is brother to the man who wrecks things.
## A strong tower
18:10 The LORD's name is a tower of strength; / the righteous run inside and are lifted out of reach.
18:11 A rich man's wealth is a fortress town to him, / and in his own imagining it is a high wall.
18:12 A person's heart grows proud just before the crash, / and before honor goes humility.
18:13 Giving a reply before you have heard / is foolish of you and a disgrace.
18:14 A man's spirit will hold him up through sickness, / but a broken spirit, who can carry that?
18:15 A discerning mind gets hold of knowledge, / and wise ears hunt for it.
18:16 A gift clears a wide path for the one who gives it / and guides him into the company of the great.
18:17 In his dispute, the man who goes first seems in the right; / then his neighbor shows up and puts him to the test.
18:18 The lot brings quarrels to an end / and parts the powerful.
18:19 An offended brother is harder to take than a strong town, / and quarrels are like the bolts of a fortress.
18:20 A man fills his belly with what his mouth bears; / he eats his fill of the crop his lips bring in.
18:21 Death and life are in the tongue's hand, / and those who love it will eat its fruit.
18:22 The one who finds a wife has found something good, / and he draws favor from the LORD.
18:23 A poor man has to beg when he speaks, / and a rich man answers with hard words.
18:24 Someone with lots of friends can be broken by them, / but some who love you hold on tighter than a brother.
## The poor man with integrity
19:1 A poor man living with integrity is better / than someone with twisted lips who is a fool.
19:2 Eagerness without knowledge is no good; / rush along on your feet and you miss the mark.
19:3 A man's folly turns his road upside down, / and then he is furious at the LORD in his heart.
19:4 Wealth brings in friend after friend, / but a poor man gets cut off from the friend he has.
19:5 A witness who lies will not go free; / a man who breathes out lies will not get away.
19:6 Plenty of people court the favor of a generous lord, / and everyone is friends with the giver of gifts.
19:7 A poor man's own brothers all hate him; / how much further his friends stay from him! / He runs after them with words, and they are gone.
19:8 To get sense is to love your own life; / guard understanding and you will find what is good.
19:9 A witness who lies will not go free; / a man who breathes out lies will perish.
19:10 A life of comfort is not fitting for a fool, / far less a slave ruling princes.
19:11 A person's good sense slows down his anger, / and his honor lies in letting a wrong go by.
19:12 The king's fury growls like a young lion; / his goodwill falls like dew on the grass.
## Home and inheritance
19:13 A son who is a fool brings disaster on his father, / and a wife's quarrels are a drip that never stops.
19:14 Houses and riches come down from one's fathers, / but a wife with good sense comes from the LORD.
19:15 Laziness drops a person into a heavy sleep, / and an idle soul goes hungry.
19:16 Keep the command and you keep your life; / take no care for your ways and you will die.
19:17 Showing kindness to a poor man is a loan made to the LORD, / and he will pay him back for what he did.
19:18 Discipline your son, since hope remains; / but do not set yourself on bringing about his death.
19:19 A hothead must pay the fine; / bail him out, and you will be bailing him out again.
19:20 Hear advice and take discipline, / so that later on you will be wise.
19:21 A person's heart is full of plans, / but the LORD's purpose is the one that holds.
19:22 What makes a man desirable is his loyal love, / and a poor man is better than a liar.
19:23 The fear of the LORD means life: / one goes to bed satisfied, and no harm pays a visit.
19:24 The lazy one sinks his hand into the bowl / and cannot be bothered to lift it back to his mouth.
19:25 When a mocker is beaten, a naive person gets shrewd; / when a discerning man is corrected, knowledge sinks in.
19:26 A son who mistreats his father and chases away his mother / is a son who shames and humiliates.
19:27 My son, stop listening to discipline / if you want to wander from the words of knowledge.
19:28 A worthless witness makes a joke of justice, / and the wicked swallow wrongdoing whole.
19:29 Judgments stand ready for mockers, / and blows for the backs of fools.
## Wine and the king's anger
20:1 Wine is a mocker, and beer is a rowdy; / anyone who reels under their power is not wise.
20:2 The dread a king inspires growls like a young lion; / provoke him and you put your own life at risk.
20:3 Staying clear of a fight is to a man's honor, / but every stubborn fool lets loose.
20:4 The lazy one does not plow when winter comes; / at harvest he goes looking, and there is nothing.
20:5 A plan in someone's heart is deep water, / but a man with understanding can haul it up.
20:6 Many people call out their own loyal love, / but someone truly faithful, who can find?
20:7 A righteous man lives with integrity; / how happy his children are after him!
20:8 When a king takes his seat on the judgment throne, / his eyes sift out every evil.
20:9 Who can claim, "My heart I have made clean; / I am pure of my sin"?
20:10 One weight and another, one measure and another: / the LORD finds both of them abhorrent.
20:11 Even a boy shows who he is by what he does, / whether his ways are pure and upright.
20:12 An ear that hears and an eye that sees: / both of them are the LORD's making.
## Buyers and borrowers
20:13 Do not be fond of sleep, or you will end up poor; / keep your eyes open and you will eat your fill.
20:14 "It's junk, it's junk," says the buyer, / and off he goes, bragging.
20:15 Gold exists, and red coral in plenty, / but lips with knowledge are a precious vessel.
20:16 Take the garment of a man who stands good for an outsider; / take a pledge from him for a foreign woman.
20:17 To a man, bread from lying is sweet, / but afterward his mouth gets filled with grit.
20:18 Plans stand firm on good advice, / so make war with skilled steering.
20:19 One who goes around telling tales lays bare what was said in confidence, / so keep no company with someone whose lips are always open.
20:20 Curse your father or your mother, / and your lamp will go out when the dark is deepest.
20:21 Property grabbed in a hurry at the start / will have no blessing at its end.
20:22 Do not say, "I will get even for the evil." / Wait for the LORD, and he will rescue you.
20:23 The LORD finds one weight and another abhorrent, / and a cheating scale is no good.
20:24 A man's steps come from the LORD; / what human, then, can figure out his own path?
20:25 It is a snare to blurt out, "This is holy!" / and to start thinking only after the vows are made.
## The LORD's lamp
20:26 A wise king sifts the wicked like grain / and runs the wheel over them.
20:27 The human breath is the LORD's lamp, / searching through every inner room of the belly.
20:28 Loyal love and faithfulness stand guard over a king; / with loyal love he holds up his throne.
20:29 Young men take pride in their strength; / old men's beauty is their gray hair.
20:30 Bruising wounds scour evil away, / and blows clean out the inner rooms of the belly.
## The king's heart in the LORD's hand
21:1 Like water in irrigation ditches, the king's heart lies in the LORD's hand; / he steers it wherever he likes.
21:2 A person thinks every road he takes is straight, / but the LORD is the one who weighs hearts.
21:3 Doing righteousness and justice / is something the LORD would choose over sacrifice.
21:4 Lofty eyes and a swollen heart: / the lamp of the wicked is sin.
21:5 A hard worker's plans lead only to gain; / everyone in a rush heads only to need.
21:6 Treasure gained by a lying tongue is a puff of breath blown away; / those who seek it are seeking death.
21:7 The violence of the wicked sweeps them off, / since they will not do what is just.
21:8 A guilty man's road twists and turns, / but the pure one's work is straight.
21:9 Living in a corner up on the roof is better / than a shared house with a woman who picks fights.
21:10 A wicked person's whole being hungers for evil; / even his neighbor finds no pity in his eyes.
21:11 When a mocker pays the fine, the naive grow wise; / when the wise is given insight, he takes in knowledge.
21:12 The righteous one studies the house of the wicked; / he flings the wicked into disaster.
21:13 Plug your ears to a poor man's cry, / and when you yourself cry out, no one will answer.
21:14 A present slipped over quietly cools down anger, / and a bribe tucked in the robe, even fierce rage.
21:15 Doing justice is a delight to the righteous / and a terror to those who work evil.
21:16 A man who wanders off the road of good sense / will end up resting with the assembly of the shades.
## Wine, oil and treasure
21:17 Love a good time and you will end up poor; / love wine and oil and you will not grow rich.
21:18 The wicked man is the price paid to ransom the righteous, / and the traitor stands in for the upright.
21:19 Living out in the wilderness is better / than living with a woman who quarrels and seethes.
21:20 Treasure worth having, and oil, are stored in the wise man's house, / but a fool of a man swallows it up.
21:21 Chase after righteousness and loyal love, / and you will find life, righteousness and honor.
21:22 One wise man can climb into a city full of warriors / and pull down the strength it relied on.
21:23 Watch your mouth and your tongue, / and you keep your life out of troubles.
21:24 The proud, puffed-up man has a name: Mocker; / he acts in a flood of arrogance.
21:25 What a lazy person craves is what kills him, / for his hands won't take up a job.
21:26 All day long he does nothing but crave, / while the righteous person gives and holds nothing back.
21:27 When the wicked offer sacrifice, it is abhorrent, / still worse if they bring it with a scheme in mind.
21:28 A lying witness will be destroyed, / but someone who listens will have the last word.
21:29 A wicked man puts on a hard face, / but the upright man thinks through his way.
21:30 There is no wisdom, no understanding, / no counsel that holds up before the LORD.
21:31 You may get the horse ready for the day of war, / but the victory is the LORD's.
## A name worth more than riches
22:1 A name is worth choosing over great riches; / being well thought of is better than silver and gold.
22:2 The rich man and the poor man meet; / the LORD made all of them.
22:3 A shrewd person sees trouble coming and takes cover; / the naive go right on by and are punished.
22:4 Humility, the fear of the LORD, brings its payoff: / riches, honor and life.
22:5 Thorns and traps line the crooked man's path; / guard your life and you will stay far from them.
22:6 Train a young person according to his way; / even after he grows old, he will not leave it.
22:7 The rich lord it over the poor, / and the one who borrows becomes the lender's slave.
22:8 Plant wrongdoing and you will harvest disaster; / the rod he swung in his rage will come to an end.
22:9 The person with a kind eye will be blessed, / since he gives some of his bread to the poor.
22:10 Throw out the mocker and the fighting leaves with him; / the quarrels and the abuse come to a stop.
22:11 Whoever loves purity of heart, whose lips speak grace, / the king is his friend.
22:12 The eyes of the LORD protect knowledge, / but he twists up the words of the faithless.
22:13 The lazy one says, "A lion is out there! / I will be murdered in the middle of the town square!"
22:14 The mouth of strange women is a deep pit; / the one the LORD is angry with will fall in there.
22:15 A young person's heart has folly knotted into it; / a disciplining rod is what will put it far from him.
22:16 Squeeze the poor to make yourself richer, / give presents to the rich, and you will end up in want.
## The words of the wise
22:17 Lean in and listen to the words of the wise, / and set your mind on what I know.
22:18 It is a pleasant thing to store them in your belly / and to have them all set in place on your lips.
22:19 I have made them known to you today, yes, to you, / so that the LORD will be the one you trust.
22:20 Haven't I put thirty sayings in writing for you, / with advice and knowledge in them,
22:21 to make you know the truth, words that can be trusted, / so you can carry back a true answer to the ones who sent you?
22:22 Do not rob a poor man just because he is poor, / and do not trample the afflicted at the town gate.
22:23 For the LORD will argue their case in court, / and he will rob of life those who rob them.
22:24 Do not keep company with a man ruled by anger, / and do not go along with someone full of rage,
22:25 in case you pick up his ways / and set a trap for your own life.
22:26 Do not join those who clap hands on a pledge, / those who stand good for loans.
22:27 If you have nothing to pay with, / why let them take the bed right out from under you?
22:28 Do not shift the old boundary line / that your ancestors laid down.
22:29 You see a man who is quick at his work? / He will take his place before kings, / not before people of no account.
