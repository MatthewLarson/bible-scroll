## What the proverbs are for
1:1 The proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel.
1:2 They are for knowing wisdom and discipline, / for grasping sayings of understanding,
1:3 for taking in the discipline of good sense, / of rightness, justice and straight dealing,
1:4 to give the naive shrewdness, / and a young man knowledge and discretion.
1:5 The wise, by listening, will learn still more, / and a person of understanding will acquire the art of steering,
1:6 for understanding a proverb and a figure, / what the wise say and the riddles they pose.
1:7 The fear of the LORD is where knowledge begins. / Wisdom and discipline? Stubborn fools despise them.
## Do not go with the gang
1:8 My son, hear your father's discipline, / and do not walk away from your mother's teaching.
1:9 They will be a charming wreath on your head / and a string of beads around your neck.
1:10 My son, if sinners try to lure you, / do not go along.
1:11 If they say, "Come with us! / Let's set a trap for blood; / let's hide and wait for an innocent man, for nothing.
1:12 We'll gulp them down alive, the way Sheol does, / whole, like people going down into the Pit.
1:13 We'll find every kind of costly treasure; / we'll fill our houses with loot.
1:14 You'll cast your lot among us; / there will be one money bag for all of us."
1:15 My son, do not walk down the road with them; / keep your foot back from their tracks.
1:16 For their feet race toward evil, / and they rush to spill blood.
1:17 For nothing the net is spread out / before the eyes of every winged thing.
1:18 These men are setting a trap for their own blood; / they hide and wait for their own lives.
1:19 So go the paths of all who grab at crooked gain: / it robs the ones who hold it of their life.
## Wisdom calls in the street
1:20 Wisdom shouts out in the street; / in the open squares she makes her voice heard.
1:21 She calls out above the noisy crowds; / at the entrances of the gates, in the city, she speaks her words:
1:22 "How long, naive ones, will you be in love with naivety? / How long will mockers enjoy their mocking, / and will fools go on hating knowledge?
1:23 Turn back when I rebuke you. / Look: I will pour out my spirit to you; / I will teach you my words.
1:24 Since I called and you said no, / since I reached out my hand and no one took notice,
1:25 since you brushed aside all my advice / and wanted nothing to do with my rebuke,
1:26 so I in my turn will laugh at your disaster; / I will jeer when the thing you dread comes,
1:27 when the thing you dread comes like devastation / and your disaster arrives in a whirlwind, / when trouble and hardship come down on you.
1:28 Then they will call me, and I will give no answer; / they will hunt hard for me and never find me.
1:29 This is because they hated knowledge; / the fear of the LORD was not their choice.
1:30 They were unwilling to take my advice; / they sneered at all my rebukes.
1:31 So they will eat the fruit their own way has grown / and be stuffed with their own plans.
1:32 Turning away is what kills the naive; / being at ease is what destroys fools.
1:33 But the one who listens to me will settle in safety / and rest easy, free from the dread of harm."
## What wisdom will save you from
2:1 My son, if you take in my words / and hide my commands away like treasure,
2:2 so that your ear listens closely to wisdom / and you lean your heart toward understanding,
2:3 yes, if you cry out to understanding / and call aloud to discernment,
2:4 if you hunt for it the way people hunt for silver / and search it out as you would buried treasure,
2:5 then the fear of the LORD will be clear to you, / and the knowledge of God is what you will find.
2:6 It is the LORD who gives wisdom; / knowledge and understanding come out of his mouth.
2:7 He keeps sound wisdom in store for the upright, / a shield for those who live wholeheartedly.
2:8 He keeps guard over the paths of justice / and protects the way of his loyal ones.
2:9 Then you will understand rightness, justice and straight dealing, / every good track to travel.
2:10 Wisdom will enter your heart, / and your soul will find knowledge delightful.
2:11 Discretion will stand watch over you, / and understanding will keep you safe,
2:12 to rescue you from an evil road, / from the man whose talk is upside down,
2:13 from people who abandon straight paths / to go along roads of darkness,
2:14 who are happy when they do wrong / and thrill to evil's upside-down ways,
2:15 whose roads twist, / and who are devious on every track they take.
2:16 It will rescue you from the strange woman, / from the foreign woman whose words are smooth,
2:17 who walked out on the close friend she had when she was young; / the covenant of her God has slipped from her mind.
2:18 For her house is sinking down to death, / and her tracks lead down to the shades.
2:19 All who go in to her never return; / they never reach the roads of life again.
2:20 So you will travel the road of the good, / and the paths of the righteous are what you will keep.
2:21 The land will be home for upright people, / and the wholehearted will be left in it,
2:22 but from the land the wicked will be cut off, / and the treacherous pulled up out of it.
## Trust the LORD and honor him
3:1 My son, my teaching: do not forget it. / My commands: let your heart guard them.
3:2 For a long span of days, years of life / and peace is what they will add to you.
3:3 Never let loyal love and faithfulness leave you. / Tie them around your neck; / write them on your heart's tablet.
3:4 Then you will find favor and good insight / in the eyes of God and of people.
3:5 Trust the LORD with your whole heart, / and do not lean on what you yourself understand.
3:6 Know him on every road you take, / and he will level your paths.
3:7 Do not be wise in your own eyes. / Fear the LORD, and stay clear of evil.
3:8 It will bring healing to your navel / and a drink to your bones.
3:9 Give the LORD honor from what you own, / from the first share of everything you harvest.
3:10 Your storehouses will be full and more than full, / and new wine will burst out of your vats.
3:11 The LORD's discipline, my son, do not reject, / and do not recoil from his rebuke.
3:12 For whoever the LORD loves, he rebukes, / the way a father treats the son he takes pleasure in.
## Happy is the one who finds wisdom
3:13 Happy is the person who has found wisdom, / the person who draws out understanding.
3:14 Trading in her pays better than trading in silver, / and what she yields beats gold.
3:15 She is worth more than red coral; / of all the things you want, none is her equal.
3:16 She holds length of days in her right hand, / and wealth and honor in her left.
3:17 Her roads are roads of delight, / and every one of her paths is peace.
3:18 To those who grab hold of her she is a tree of life, / and each one who keeps a grip on her is called happy.
3:19 It was by wisdom that the LORD founded the earth, / by understanding that he made the heavens firm.
3:20 By his knowledge the deeps burst open, / and the skies drip with dew.
3:21 My son, do not let them stray from your eyes; / guard sound wisdom and discretion.
3:22 They will mean life for your soul / and a lovely charm on your neck.
3:23 You will go down your road securely, / and you will not stub your foot.
3:24 If you lie down, there will be nothing to fear; / you will lie there and sleep sweetly.
3:25 Do not be frightened by a sudden panic, / or by the devastation of the wicked when it comes.
3:26 The LORD will be the one you rely on; / he will keep your foot out of the trap.
## Do good to your neighbor
3:27 Do not keep good from its owners / when your hand has the power to do it.
3:28 Do not say to your neighbor, "Go on, come back later; / tomorrow I'll give it to you," when you have it right there.
3:29 Do not plow up trouble for your neighbor / while he is living securely next to you.
3:30 Do not pick a fight with anyone for nothing, / if he has done you no harm.
3:31 Do not be jealous of a man of violence; / do not pick a single one of his roads.
3:32 For the devious are something abhorrent to the LORD, / but with the upright he shares his secrets.
3:33 On the house of a wicked man lies the LORD's curse; / the homestead of the righteous he blesses.
3:34 Mockers he mocks, / but the humble he gives his favor.
3:35 Honor is what the wise inherit, / and fools carry away disgrace.
## What my father taught me
4:1 Sons, listen to the discipline a father gives; / pay close attention so that you come to know understanding.
4:2 What I hand you is good learning; / my teaching: do not abandon it.
4:3 For I too was once a boy at my father's side, / young and tender, the only one my mother had.
4:4 He used to teach me and tell me, / "Let your heart grip my words. / Keep my commands and live.
4:5 Acquire wisdom; acquire understanding. / Do not forget, and do not swerve from what my mouth says.
4:6 Do not abandon her, and she will keep you; / love her, and she will stand guard over you.
4:7 Where wisdom starts: acquire wisdom! / With all you have gained, acquire understanding.
4:8 Hold her high, and she will raise you up; / she will honor you when you hold her close.
4:9 She will put a charming wreath on your head; / a splendid crown she will hand to you."
## Two roads
4:10 Hear me, my son; take in what I say, / and you will have more and more years of life.
4:11 In wisdom's road I have trained you; / I have guided you down tracks that run straight.
4:12 As you walk, nothing will cramp your stride; / even running, you will not trip.
4:13 Grab discipline and never let go. / Keep her safe: she is your life.
4:14 The path of the wicked: do not go onto it. / The road of evil men: do not stride along it.
4:15 Steer clear of it; do not travel it. / Swerve away from it and keep going.
4:16 They do not sleep if they have done no harm; / sleep is snatched from them until they have tripped someone up.
4:17 Wickedness is the bread they eat, / and violence the wine they drink.
4:18 The road of the righteous is like the first gleam of morning, / which keeps getting brighter until the day stands at noon.
4:19 The wicked walk a road as black as night; / they cannot tell what they keep tripping over.
## Guard your heart
4:20 My son, listen closely to my words; / lean your ear toward my sayings.
4:21 Do not let them stray from your eyes; / keep them in the middle of your heart.
4:22 To those who find them, they are life, / and healing for all their flesh.
4:23 More than anything you keep watch over, watch over your heart, / for the sources of life go out from it.
4:24 Rid yourself of a crooked mouth; / put devious lips far away from you.
4:25 Your eyes: let them look forward. / Your eyelids: let them aim straight ahead of you.
4:26 Level the track your foot walks on, / and every road you take will be steady.
4:27 Do not veer off to the right or the left; / steer your foot clear of evil.
## Honey on her lips
5:1 My son, listen closely to my wisdom; / lean your ear toward my understanding,
5:2 so that you hold on to discretion / and knowledge is kept safe on your lips.
5:3 Honey drips from the strange woman's lips, / and her palate is smoother than oil.
5:4 What she leads to at last is bitter as wormwood, / as sharp as a sword that cuts both ways.
5:5 Her feet are going down to death; / Sheol is where her steps take hold.
5:6 She never takes the level road of life; / her tracks wander, and she does not even know.
5:7 So now, sons, listen to me; / do not swerve from what my mouth says.
5:8 Put distance between your road and her; / do not come close to her house's doorway,
5:9 or you will hand your splendor over to others / and your years to a cruel man,
5:10 or outsiders will eat their fill of your strength, / and what you toiled for will go to a foreigner's house.
5:11 You will groan when your end comes, / when your flesh and your body waste away,
5:12 and you will say, "How I hated discipline, / and rebuke, how my heart spurned it!
5:13 I paid no heed to what my teachers said; / to the people training me I would not lend an ear.
5:14 Very nearly I came to complete disaster / in the middle of the assembly and the community."
## Drink from your own well
5:15 Drink the water your own cistern holds, / the streams that run inside your own well.
5:16 Should your springs spill out into the street, / channels of water into the open squares?
5:17 They should belong to you and you only, / not to outsiders alongside you.
5:18 Let your spring be blessed. / Take your joy from the woman you married when you were young.
5:19 A doe to love, an ibex full of charm: / let her breasts give you your fill at all times; / be lost in love for her always.
5:20 Why, my son, would you be lost in a strange woman / and put your arms around a foreign woman's breast?
5:21 The LORD has a person's ways right before his eyes, / and he weighs every track he takes.
5:22 The wicked man is trapped by his own wrongdoing; / the ropes of his sin will hold him tight.
5:23 He dies because he had no discipline; / in the greatness of his folly he is lost.
## Get out of a rash pledge
6:1 My son, if you have stood good for your neighbor, / if you have struck palms for an outsider,
6:2 if the words of your own mouth have snared you, / the words of your own mouth have caught you,
6:3 do this, then, my son, and get yourself out, / for you have landed in your neighbor's palm: / go, throw yourself down, and badger your neighbor.
6:4 Do not let your eyes sleep / or your eyelids doze off.
6:5 Pull yourself loose like a gazelle out of a hand, / like a bird out of the bird-catcher's hand.
## Go to the ant
6:6 Go to the ant, you lazy one; / look at its ways and get wise.
6:7 It has no chief, / no official, no ruler,
6:8 but in summer it lays in its food; / at harvest time it gathers what it will eat.
6:9 Lazy one, how long will you lie in bed? / When are you going to get up from your sleep?
6:10 A little sleep, a little drowsing, / a little folding of the hands to lie down,
6:11 and your poverty will come at you like a drifter, / your lack like a man with a shield.
## The troublemaker
6:12 A worthless man, a troublemaker, / walks around with a crooked mouth.
6:13 He winks with his eyes, / shuffles his feet, / and points his fingers.
6:14 Upside-down things are in his heart; / he plows up evil all the time; / he sets quarrels loose.
6:15 That is why his disaster will arrive without warning; / in a flash he will be smashed, and nothing will heal him.
## Seven things the LORD hates
6:16 The LORD hates six things, / and seven are abhorrent to his very self:
6:17 eyes held high, a tongue of lies, / and hands that spill innocent blood,
6:18 a heart that plows up plans to cause trouble, / feet in a hurry to run to evil,
6:19 a lying witness who breathes out lies, / and one who sets quarrels loose among brothers.
## Fire in the lap
6:20 Guard your father's command, my son, / and do not walk away from your mother's teaching.
6:21 Keep them tied to your heart at all times; / knot them around your neck.
6:22 As you walk about, it will guide you; / as you lie down, it will keep watch over you; / and once you are awake, it will talk things over with you.
6:23 A command is a lamp, teaching is light, / and the rebukes that discipline gives are the road to life,
6:24 to guard you from a wicked woman, / from the foreign woman's slippery tongue.
6:25 Do not crave her beauty in your heart; / do not let her catch you with her eyelids.
6:26 A prostitute may cost a round of bread, / but a man's wife goes hunting for a life that is precious.
6:27 Can a man rake fire up against his chest / and his clothes not burn?
6:28 Or can someone walk across burning coals / and not blister his feet?
6:29 The same goes for one who goes in to his neighbor's wife: / anyone who touches her will not be held innocent.
6:30 A thief is not looked down on when he steals / to fill his belly because he is hungry,
6:31 but once he is found out, he must repay seven times over; / every last thing his household owns he must hand over.
6:32 A man who commits adultery with a woman is senseless; / only someone bent on destroying himself would do it.
6:33 Beatings and shame are what he will find, / and his disgrace will not be rubbed out.
6:34 Jealousy is the fury of a man, / and on the day of revenge he will not hold back.
6:35 He will not look at any payment to buy you off; / he will not give in, however big you make the bribe.
## Wisdom your sister
7:1 My son, keep my words, / and hide my commands away like treasure.
7:2 Keep my commands and live, / and guard my teaching as you guard the pupil of your eye.
7:3 Tie them on your fingers; / write them on your heart's tablet.
7:4 Tell wisdom, "You are my sister," / and call understanding your kinswoman,
7:5 to guard you from the strange woman, / from the foreign woman whose words are smooth.
## What I saw from my window
7:6 Once, from my house window, / I looked down through my lattice,
7:7 and among the naive I saw, / among the boys I spotted, a senseless young man.
7:8 He was crossing the street close to her corner, / and he took a stroll in the direction of her house,
7:9 at dusk, in the evening of the day, / in the black of night and the gloom.
7:10 And there, a woman steps out to meet him, / in a prostitute's clothes, her heart kept hidden.
7:11 Noisy she is, and headstrong; / her feet will not settle in her house.
7:12 One moment she is out in the street, the next in the squares; / beside every corner she sets her trap.
7:13 She takes hold of him and kisses him. / She puts on a hard face and tells him,
7:14 "Sacrifices of peace offerings were due from me; / today I have paid my vows.
7:15 That is why I came out to meet you, / eagerly seeking your face, and I found you.
7:16 I've laid spreads over my couch, / striped cloth of Egyptian linen.
7:17 My bed I've sprinkled / with myrrh, aloes and cinnamon.
7:18 Come on, let's soak ourselves in love until morning; / let's revel in lovemaking.
7:19 The man of the house is away; / he has set off on a trip far away.
7:20 The money pouch went along in his hand; / he will be home on the day of the full moon."
7:21 She sways him with her much persuading; / with her smooth lips she sweeps him along.
7:22 He goes after her right away, / as an ox walks to the slaughter, / like a stubborn fool in shackles going off to be punished,
7:23 until an arrow slices through his liver. / Like a bird hurrying into a trap, / he has no idea that his life is at stake.
7:24 So now, sons, listen to me; / listen closely to the sayings of my mouth.
7:25 Your heart must not veer off onto her roads; / do not wander down her paths.
7:26 She has made many fall wounded; / those she has killed are a vast crowd.
7:27 Roads to Sheol, that is her house, / going down to the bedrooms of death.
## Wisdom calls at the gates
8:1 Is that not Wisdom calling, / Understanding giving voice?
8:2 At the top of the high places, along the road, / at the crossroads, she stations herself.
8:3 Next to the gates, at the mouth of the town, / where the doors open in, she shouts:
8:4 "It is you men I am calling; / my voice is for every human being.
8:5 Naive ones, understand shrewdness; / fools, understand with your heart.
8:6 Listen: I will speak about princely things, / and when my lips open, straight dealing comes out.
8:7 My palate murmurs truth, / and wickedness is something abhorrent to my lips.
8:8 Every word from my mouth is spoken in rightness; / nothing in them is bent or crooked.
8:9 To one who understands, all of them are plain; / to those who find knowledge, they are level.
8:10 Take my discipline, and not silver; / take knowledge before the finest gold.
8:11 For wisdom is better than red coral; / of all the things people want, none is her equal.
## Through me kings reign
8:12 I, Wisdom, make my home in shrewdness; / knowledge and discretion I find.
8:13 The fear of the LORD means hating evil. / Pride and arrogance, the evil road / and the mouth of upside-down words: I hate them.
8:14 Mine are advice and sound wisdom; / I am Understanding, and might is mine.
8:15 It is through me that kings are kings / and that rulers carve out laws that are right.
8:16 Through me princes rule as princes, / and nobles, all who judge with rightness.
8:17 Those who love me, I love; / those who hunt hard for me will find me.
8:18 Wealth and honor stay with me, / and riches that last, and righteousness.
8:19 What I bear is better than gold, than pure gold, / and what I yield beats the finest silver.
8:20 My walk is on the road of righteousness, / down the middle of the paths of justice,
8:21 so that those who love me inherit real wealth, / and I fill their treasure rooms.
## Before the world began
8:22 The LORD acquired me, the first of his way, / before his works of long ago.
8:23 From long ago I was installed, / from the start, from the earth's earliest times.
8:24 Before there were any deeps, I was born, / before there were springs heavy with water.
8:25 Before the mountains were sunk into their bases, / ahead of the hills, I was born,
8:26 when he had not yet made earth and open country, / or the first clods of the world's soil.
8:27 I was there when he fixed the heavens in place, / when he carved a circle on the surface of the deep,
8:28 when he made the skies above firm, / when the fountains of the deep surged with strength,
8:29 when he laid down for the sea its fixed line, / so the waters would not pass beyond what his mouth ordered, / when he carved out the earth's foundations.
8:30 And I was at his side, a craftsman. / I was delight, day after day, / playing in front of him all the time,
8:31 playing all over the world, his earth, / and my delights were with human beings.
## Happy are those who listen
8:32 So now, sons, listen to me: / happy are those who guard my ways.
8:33 Hear discipline and get wise; / do not brush it aside.
8:34 Happy is the person who listens to me, / staying awake at my doors day after day, / keeping watch by the posts of my doorways.
8:35 The one who finds me has found life, / and he draws favor from the LORD.
8:36 The one who misses me does violence to his own soul; / everyone who hates me is in love with death."
## Wisdom's feast
9:1 Wisdom has built her house; / she has cut her pillars, seven of them.
9:2 She has butchered her animals and mixed her wine; / she has laid out her table too.
9:3 She has sent her serving girls out; / she calls from the tops of the town's high places:
9:4 "Who is naive? Let him come over here!" / To anyone senseless she says,
9:5 "Come, have some of my bread, / and drink the wine I mixed.
9:6 Give up being naive, and live; / stride along the road of understanding."
## Mockers and the wise
9:7 Discipline a mocker, and you pick up disgrace for yourself; / rebuke a wicked man, and you come away with a stain.
9:8 Do not rebuke a mocker, in case he hates you; / rebuke the wise, and he will love you for it.
9:9 Give something to a wise man, and he will grow wiser; / let a righteous man know something, and he will learn still more.
9:10 The start of wisdom is the fear of the LORD, / and knowing the Holy One is understanding.
9:11 It is by me that your days grow many / and that years of life are added to you.
9:12 If you have become wise, it is for yourself that you are wise; / if you mock, you will carry it by yourself.
## Woman Folly's feast
9:13 Woman Folly is noisy; / she is all naivety and knows nothing whatever.
9:14 She sits down at the doorway of her house, / on a chair on the town's high places,
9:15 calling out to people going by on the road, / who are keeping to a straight path:
9:16 "Who is naive? Let him come over here!" / And to anyone senseless she says,
9:17 "Water you steal tastes sweet, / and bread from hiding places is a delight."
9:18 He has no idea that the shades are there, / that the guests she invited are down in Sheol's depths.
