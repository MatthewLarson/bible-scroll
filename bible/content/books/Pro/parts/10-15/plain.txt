## The proverbs of Solomon
10:1 The proverbs of Solomon. / A wise son makes a father glad, / but a son who is a fool is his mother's grief.
10:2 Treasures won by wickedness are no help, / but righteousness rescues from death.
10:3 The LORD will not leave the righteous to starve, / but he shoves aside what the wicked crave.
10:4 A slack hand at work leaves a person poor, / but the hand of hard workers brings riches.
10:5 One who brings the crop in during summer is a son with good sense; / one who sleeps through harvest is a son who brings disgrace.
10:6 Blessings come down on the head of the righteous, / but the mouth of the wicked keeps violence covered.
10:7 The righteous are remembered with blessing, / but the name of the wicked rots away.
10:8 The one who is wise at heart takes in commands, / but a stubborn fool who keeps talking is brought down.
10:9 The one who walks with integrity walks safe, / but the one who twists his paths will be exposed.
10:10 One who winks an eye causes pain, / and a stubborn fool who keeps talking is brought down.
10:11 The mouth of the righteous is a spring of life, / but the mouth of the wicked keeps violence covered.
10:12 Hatred rouses fights, / but love lays a cover over all wrongdoing.
10:13 On the lips of the discerning you find wisdom; / for the back of the senseless there is a rod.
10:14 The wise keep knowledge stored away, / but a stubborn fool's mouth is ruin close at hand.
10:15 A rich man's wealth is a fortress town to him; / what wrecks the poor is their poverty.
10:16 The righteous man's wages go toward life; / the wicked man's harvest goes toward sin.
10:17 One who keeps discipline is a path toward life, / but one who turns his back on rebuke sends people the wrong way.
## Words, few and many
10:18 Hatred kept under cover makes for lying lips, / and the one who spreads gossip is a fool.
10:19 With many words, wrongdoing does not stop; / the one who keeps his lips in check has good sense.
10:20 Silver of the finest is the righteous man's tongue; / the wicked man's heart is worth next to nothing.
10:21 The righteous man's lips shepherd many people, / but stubborn fools die because they have no sense.
## The LORD's blessing
10:22 It is the LORD's blessing that makes a person rich, / and toil adds nothing to it.
10:23 To a fool, doing something vile is like play; / so is wisdom to a person of understanding.
10:24 The thing a wicked man dreads is the thing that reaches him, / but the righteous will be given what they long for.
10:25 When the storm passes, there is no wicked man left, / but the righteous man is a foundation that lasts for all time.
10:26 As vinegar is to teeth and smoke is to eyes, / so the lazy one is to those who send him on an errand.
10:27 The fear of the LORD adds days, / but the wicked find their years shortened.
10:28 What the righteous wait for is joy, / but what the wicked hope for is lost.
10:29 A stronghold for those with integrity is the way of the LORD, / and a ruin for those who do wrong.
10:30 Nothing will ever topple the righteous, / and the wicked will not stay settled in the land.
10:31 The righteous man's mouth bears wisdom as its fruit, / but a tongue that twists words will be cut out.
10:32 The righteous man's lips know what wins favor; / the mouth of the wicked knows what is twisted.
## Honest scales
11:1 Scales that cheat are abhorrent to the LORD, / but a weight that is full and true wins his favor.
11:2 In comes arrogance, and in comes shame; / but wisdom stays with the modest.
11:3 Upright people are led by their own integrity; / treacherous people are ruined by their own twisting.
11:4 Wealth is no help on the day of fury, / but righteousness rescues from death.
11:5 A wholehearted man's righteousness clears his road straight ahead; / a wicked man falls through his own wickedness.
11:6 Upright people are rescued by their righteousness; / treacherous people are trapped in their own craving.
11:7 When a wicked person dies, his hope comes to an end, / and the hope he placed in strength has perished.
11:8 The righteous man gets pulled free of trouble, / and the wicked man takes his place in it.
## A town and its people
11:9 A godless man can wreck his neighbor with his mouth, / but the righteous get free through knowledge.
11:10 A town celebrates when the righteous prosper, / and there is a cheer when the wicked are gone.
11:11 The blessing of the upright raises a town high, / but the mouth of the wicked tears it down.
11:12 One who looks down on his neighbor is senseless, / but a person of understanding stays silent.
11:13 One who goes around telling tales lays bare what was said in confidence, / but one with a faithful spirit keeps the matter under cover.
11:14 Where no one steers, a nation falls; / where advisers are many, there is rescue.
11:15 Stand good for an outsider's loan, and you will be badly hurt; / hate the handshake that seals a pledge, and you are safe.
11:16 A woman of grace takes hold of honor, / and the ruthless take hold of riches.
11:17 A person of loyal love does good to his own self; / a cruel person stirs up trouble for his own flesh.
11:18 The wicked man earns pay that turns out fake; / the one who sows righteousness earns pay that is real.
11:19 Firm righteousness leads to life, / but one who chases evil heads for his own death.
11:20 Those with twisted hearts are abhorrent to the LORD, / and he takes pleasure in those whose road is blameless.
11:21 You can shake hands on it: the evil man will not go free, / but the children of the righteous will escape.
11:22 A gold ring in a pig's snout: / a lovely woman who has turned her back on good sense.
11:23 The righteous long for nothing but good; / what the wicked hope for ends in fury.
## Generous and grasping
11:24 Someone scatters freely, and he ends up with still more; / someone else keeps back what is fair to give, and ends up only in want.
11:25 A generous soul will grow fat, / and the one who gives others water will get a drink himself.
11:26 The one who hoards grain, people curse, / but blessing comes down on the head of the one who sells it.
11:27 One who is eager for good is looking for favor, / but whoever hunts for evil, evil will find him.
11:28 The one who leans on his riches, he is the one who falls, / but the righteous will burst out like new leaves.
11:29 Stir up trouble in your own house, and wind is all you will inherit; / the stubborn fool ends up a slave to the one with a wise heart.
11:30 The fruit of a righteous man is a tree of life, / and one who wins people over is wise.
11:31 Look: if the righteous get their due here on earth, / how much more the wicked man and the sinner!
## Loving discipline
12:1 To love discipline is to love knowledge; / one who hates being rebuked is as dumb as an animal.
12:2 The good man draws favor from the LORD, / but the schemer he declares guilty.
12:3 Wickedness gives nobody a firm footing, / but nothing shakes the root of the righteous.
12:4 A woman of strength is her husband's crown, / but one who shames him is like rot in his bones.
12:5 What the righteous plan is fair; / the course the wicked steer is deceit.
12:6 What the wicked say is an ambush for blood, / but the mouth of the upright gets them out.
12:7 Turn the wicked over, and there is nothing left of them; / the house of the righteous stays standing.
12:8 People praise a man as far as his good sense goes, / but a crooked heart ends up held in contempt.
12:9 Better a man little thought of who has a servant / than one who acts important and has no bread.
12:10 A righteous man knows what his animal needs, / but the compassion of the wicked is cruel.
12:11 The man who tills his soil gets his fill of bread; / the man who chases empty things is senseless.
12:12 The wicked covet what evil men catch in their nets, / but the root of the righteous gives its yield.
## Lips that wound and heal
12:13 In the wrongdoing of the lips lies an evil trap, / but the righteous man gets out of trouble.
12:14 A man is filled with good from the fruit his mouth bears, / and what a person's hands have done is paid back to him.
12:15 A stubborn fool thinks his own way is straight, / but the one who listens to advice is wise.
12:16 A stubborn fool's vexation is known that very day, / but the shrewd man keeps an insult covered.
12:17 One who breathes out the truth makes known what is right, / but a witness of lies declares deceit.
12:18 Some people blurt words that stab like a sword, / but the tongue of the wise is a cure.
12:19 A lip that tells the truth stands firm for all time; / a tongue that lies, only for a wink.
12:20 Those who plow up evil have deceit in their hearts, / but those who counsel peace have joy.
12:21 No trouble at all will fall to the righteous man, / but the wicked are full of harm.
12:22 Lips that lie are abhorrent to the LORD, / but those who live faithfully win his favor.
12:23 A shrewd man hides what he knows, / but the heart of fools calls out its folly.
12:24 Hard-working hands will hold power, / but a slack hand will end up doing forced labor.
12:25 Worry in someone's heart bends it low, / but a good word makes it glad.
12:26 A righteous man scouts out the road for his friend, / but the road the wicked take sends them wandering.
12:27 Slackness never gets its catch to the roasting fire, / but a diligent man gains precious wealth.
12:28 On the road of righteousness there is life; / and where its path goes, death is not.
## A father's discipline
13:1 A father's discipline: a wise son takes it in; / a mocker will not listen to a scolding.
13:2 A person eats good things from the fruit of his mouth, / but what the treacherous crave is violence.
13:3 One who keeps watch over his mouth guards his life; / one who opens his lips wide has ruin coming.
13:4 The lazy one craves, but his appetite gets nothing; / the appetite of hard workers is fed until it is fat.
13:5 A righteous man hates a lying word, / but a wicked man makes a stink and brings disgrace.
13:6 Righteousness stands guard over the one whose way has integrity, / but wickedness brings down the sinner.
## Rich and poor
13:7 One person acts rich and has nothing at all; / another acts poor and has great wealth.
13:8 A man's wealth may have to buy back his life, / but the poor man never even hears the threat.
13:9 The light of the righteous burns cheerfully, / but the lamp of the wicked will go out.
13:10 Pride only ever breeds fights, / but those who let themselves be advised have wisdom.
13:11 Wealth that comes out of thin air shrinks away, / but one who gathers it handful by handful will have more.
13:12 Hope dragged out makes the heart sick, / but a longing that comes true is a tree of life.
13:13 One who scorns a word will be bound to pay for it, / but one who fears a command, he will be rewarded.
13:14 The teaching of the wise is a spring of life, / so that a person can steer clear of death's traps.
13:15 Good sense brings favor, / but the road of the treacherous is hard as rock.
13:16 A shrewd man does everything with knowledge, / but a fool lays his folly out for all to see.
13:17 A messenger who is wicked drops into trouble, / but an envoy you can trust is healing.
13:18 Need and shame wait for the one who brushes off discipline, / but the one who takes rebuke to heart will be honored.
13:19 A longing that has come true tastes sweet to a person, / and turning away from evil is what fools find abhorrent.
## Walking with the wise
13:20 The one who walks with the wise grows wise, / but one who keeps company with fools comes to grief.
13:21 Trouble hunts down sinners, / but the righteous are paid back with good.
13:22 A good man passes an inheritance down to his grandchildren, / and what a sinner has built up is stored away for the righteous.
13:23 The newly broken ground of the poor yields plenty of food, / but some are swept away where there is no justice.
13:24 Whoever keeps back his rod hates his son, / but one who loves him seeks him out early with discipline.
13:25 A righteous man eats until his appetite is satisfied, / but the belly of the wicked goes empty.
## The wisest of women
14:1 The wisest of women builds her house, / but folly tears it down with her own hands.
14:2 To walk straight is to fear the LORD; / to take crooked roads is to despise him.
14:3 A stubborn fool's mouth puts out a shoot of pride, / but the lips of the wise guard them.
14:4 No cattle, and the feed trough stays clean; / but by an ox's strength the harvest is large.
14:5 A witness you can trust will not tell lies, / but a false witness breathes out lies.
14:6 A mocker looks for wisdom, and there is none, / but knowledge is no trouble for the discerning.
14:7 Keep your distance from a fool; / lips of knowledge are something you will not meet in him.
14:8 A shrewd man's wisdom lies in understanding his own road, / but the folly of fools is deceit.
14:9 Stubborn fools sneer at the guilt offering, / but among the upright there is goodwill.
14:10 Only the heart knows how bitter it feels, / and no outsider mixes in its joy.
14:11 The wicked man's house will be torn out, / but the upright man's tent will burst into bloom.
14:12 There is a road that looks straight to a person, / but in the end it comes out on the roads of death.
14:13 A heart may hurt even while it laughs, / and joy can finish as sorrow.
14:14 A heart that has turned back will be stuffed with its own ways, / and a good man will be filled with what is his.
## The naive and the shrewd
14:15 A naive person trusts every word he hears, / but a shrewd person watches where each step goes.
14:16 A wise man is wary and keeps away from evil, / but a fool flares up and feels safe.
14:17 One quick to anger acts out folly, / and the schemer is hated.
14:18 Folly is what the naive inherit; / knowledge is the crown the shrewd put on.
14:19 Evil men will bow low in front of good men, / the wicked at a righteous man's gates.
14:20 A poor man is hated even by his neighbor, / but those who love a rich man are many.
14:21 One who looks down on his neighbor sins, / but happy is the one who is kind to the lowly.
14:22 Do not those who plow up evil wander off? / But loyal love and faithfulness are for those who plow up good.
14:23 Every kind of toil brings some gain, / but lip-talk ends up only in want.
14:24 Their riches are the crown of the wise; / the folly of fools is just folly.
14:25 A witness who tells the truth rescues lives, / but one who breathes out lies is all deceit.
## A strong place to trust
14:26 In the fear of the LORD lies a strong security, / and his children will have a shelter.
14:27 The fear of the LORD is a spring of life, / so that a person can steer clear of death's traps.
14:28 The more people there are, the more splendid the king; / with no nation left, a ruler is wrecked.
14:29 Slow to anger means great understanding; / a short temper lifts folly high.
14:30 A calm heart is life for the body, / but jealousy is rot in the bones.
14:31 Oppressing the poor is taunting the one who made them; / showing kindness to the needy honors him.
14:32 The wicked man is thrown down by his own evil, / but the righteous man takes refuge in his death.
14:33 Wisdom settles in the heart of the discerning; / among fools, too, she makes herself known.
14:34 Righteousness raises a nation up, / but sin brings peoples shame.
14:35 The king's favor goes to a servant with good sense, / but his fury will be for the one who brings disgrace.
## A soft-spoken answer
15:1 A soft-spoken answer turns fury back, / but a hurtful word makes anger climb.
15:2 The tongue of the wise puts knowledge to good use, / but the mouth of fools bubbles over with folly.
15:3 In every place the LORD has his eyes open, / on the lookout over evil people and good.
15:4 A tongue that heals is a tree of life, / but if it twists, it shatters the spirit.
15:5 A stubborn fool spurns his father's discipline, / but one who heeds rebuke grows shrewd.
15:6 A righteous man's house is a great store of wealth, / but a wicked man's income brings trouble with it.
15:7 The lips of the wise scatter knowledge abroad, / but the heart of fools is not firm.
15:8 When the wicked sacrifice, the LORD finds it abhorrent; / when the upright pray, he is pleased.
15:9 The LORD finds the road of the wicked abhorrent, / and he loves whoever runs after righteousness.
15:10 Harsh discipline is for the one who leaves the road; / the one who hates rebuke will die.
15:11 Sheol and Abaddon are in full view of the LORD; / how much more the hearts of human beings!
15:12 A mocker does not care to be set straight; / to the wise he will not go.
15:13 A happy heart brightens the face, / but a heart in pain leaves the spirit broken.
15:14 A discerning heart looks for knowledge, / but the mouth of fools grazes on folly.
15:15 For the afflicted every day is a hard one, / but a good heart is a feast that never stops.
## Better a little
15:16 A little, along with the fear of the LORD, is better / than a big store of treasure with turmoil inside it.
15:17 A plate of vegetables with love at the table is better / than a stall-fed ox served with hatred.
15:18 A man full of rage starts fights, / but a man slow to anger settles a quarrel down.
15:19 A lazy person's road is like a thornbush hedge, / but the upright have a path that is a raised highway.
15:20 A wise son makes a father glad, / but a man who is a fool looks down on his mother.
15:21 A senseless person enjoys his folly, / but a person of understanding keeps his steps straight.
15:22 Without someone to talk them over, plans break down; / with plenty of advisers, they hold.
15:23 A person is glad when his mouth has an answer; / a word at the right moment, how good it is!
15:24 The road of life runs upward for one with good sense, / to keep him away from Sheol down below.
## The LORD and the proud
15:25 The LORD pulls up the house of the proud, / but he makes the widow's boundary stone stand.
15:26 The LORD finds evil plans abhorrent, / but words of kindness are clean.
15:27 One greedy for crooked gain stirs up trouble in his own house, / but one who hates bribes will live.
15:28 A righteous heart thinks over how to answer, / but the mouth of the wicked bubbles over with evil.
15:29 The LORD keeps far away from the wicked, / but the prayers of the righteous he hears.
15:30 Shining eyes gladden the heart; / good news fattens the bones.
15:31 An ear that hears the rebuke that brings life / will lodge among the wise.
15:32 One who brushes off discipline throws his own life away, / but one who listens to rebuke gains sense.
15:33 The fear of the LORD is wisdom's discipline, / and before honor goes humility.
