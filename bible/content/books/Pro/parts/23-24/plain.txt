## At a ruler's table
23:1 When you sit at a ruler's table to eat, / pay close attention to what is in front of you.
23:2 Put a knife to your throat / if you are a man of large appetite.
23:3 Do not crave his fine foods, / for that bread tells lies.
## Wealth with wings
23:4 Do not toil to make yourself rich. / Let your understanding tell you to stop.
23:5 Will you let your eyes fly to it? Then it is gone. / It is sure to make itself wings / and take off like an eagle for the open sky.
## The grudging host
23:6 Do not take a meal from a man with a grudging eye, / and do not crave his fine foods.
23:7 For he is the kind who keeps score inside. / "Eat and drink!" he tells you, / but his heart is not with you.
23:8 The scrap you ate you will throw up, / and your polite words will be thrown away.
23:9 Do not talk where a fool can hear; / he will only sneer at your sensible words.
## The orphan's field
23:10 Do not shift the old boundary line, / and do not trespass on the fields of orphans.
23:11 For their redeemer is strong. / He will fight their court case against you.
## Discipline and the rod
23:12 Bring your heart to discipline / and your ears to the sayings of knowledge.
23:13 Do not hold back discipline from a youth. / When you hit him with the rod, he will not die.
23:14 It is you who hit him with the rod, / and you will rescue his life from Sheol.
## A father's joy
23:15 My son, if your heart grows wise, / my own heart will be glad as well.
23:16 My insides will exult / when your lips speak honest words.
23:17 Do not let your heart be jealous of sinners; / be jealous instead for the fear of the LORD, all day long.
23:18 Then there will be a future for you, / and what you hope for will not be cut off.
23:19 You, my son, listen and grow wise, / and steer your heart along the road.
23:20 Do not keep company with wine-swillers, / with people who stuff themselves with meat.
23:21 For the drunkard and the glutton lose everything they have, / and sleepiness leaves them wearing rags.
23:22 Pay attention to your father; he is the one who fathered you. / Do not look down on your mother because she has grown old.
23:23 Buy truth, and do not sell it: / wisdom, discipline and understanding.
23:24 A righteous man's father will shout for joy, / and a wise son makes the man who fathered him glad.
23:25 So let your father and mother be happy, / and let the woman who bore you celebrate.
## The deep pit
23:26 Give me your heart, my son, / and let your eyes keep watch on my ways.
23:27 For a prostitute is a deep pit, / and a foreign woman is a narrow well.
23:28 She too crouches in hiding like a robber, / and she makes more men unfaithful.
## The drinker's song
23:29 Who cries "Oh!"? Who cries "Alas!"? / Who has fights? Who has grumbling? / Who gets hurt for nothing? / Who has bleary eyes?
23:30 Those who sit late over wine, / who come to test out the mixed wine.
23:31 Do not stare at the wine as it turns red, / as it shows its gleam in the cup / and slides down so easily.
23:32 Later, like a snake, it bites; / like a viper, it strikes.
23:33 Your eyes will see what is strange, / and your heart will pour out twisted talk.
23:34 You will be like a man lying down out on the open sea, / like one stretched out on the top of a ship's mast.
23:35 You will say, "Someone hit me, but I am not sick. / Someone beat me, and I didn't know. / When will I wake up? / I'll go hunting for it again."
## Do not envy the wicked
24:1 Do not be jealous of people who do evil, / and do not wish you could be one of their crowd.
24:2 For their minds mull over ruin, / and their lips speak of trouble.
## A house built by wisdom
24:3 A house is built with wisdom, / and with understanding it stands firm.
24:4 Through knowledge the rooms fill up / with all sorts of riches, precious and delightful.
24:5 Strength is in a wise man, / and a man of knowledge makes his power grow.
24:6 For with skilled steering you make war, / and where advisers are many, there is rescue.
24:7 Wisdom is out of a stubborn fool's reach; / in the city gate he keeps his mouth shut.
24:8 Someone who plots harm / gets the name "master schemer."
24:9 To scheme folly is to sin, / and people find a mocker abhorrent.
24:10 If you go slack on the day of hard times, / your strength is too slim.
## Rescue the condemned
24:11 Rescue the people being hauled off to their deaths; / and those tottering toward the slaughter, if only you would hold them back!
24:12 You may say, "Look, we didn't know about this." / Surely the one who weighs hearts sees through that. / He who guards your life knows, / and he will give every human being what his deeds deserve.
## Honey and hope
24:13 My son, eat honey, because it is good, / and dripping honeycomb tastes sweet on your palate.
24:14 Know that wisdom is the same for your soul. / Find it, and you will have a future, / and what you hope for will not be cut off.
24:15 Wicked man, do not set an ambush at the home of a righteous person; / do not raid the place where he lies down to rest.
24:16 Seven times a righteous person falls, and up he gets; / but trouble trips up the wicked.
## When your enemy falls
24:17 When your enemy falls, do not be glad, / and when he trips, keep your heart from cheering,
24:18 or the LORD will see it and count it evil, / and turn his anger away from your enemy.
24:19 Do not burn up inside over people who do evil; / do not envy the wicked.
24:20 For the evil person will have no future; / the lamp of the wicked will go out.
24:21 Fear the LORD, my son, and fear the king. / Have nothing to do with people who want things changed.
24:22 For their disaster will rise up suddenly, / and who knows what ruin the two of them can bring?
## More sayings of the wise
24:23 These too are by the wise: / Favoring one face over another in judgment is not good.
24:24 Whoever tells the wicked, "You are righteous," / peoples will curse him, nations will rage at him.
24:25 But it will be sweet for those who correct him, / and on them comes a blessing full of good things.
24:26 Lips get a kiss / from the one who answers honestly.
24:27 Get your outdoor work settled; / have it ready in the field for yourself. / Afterward you can build your house.
24:28 Do not take the stand against your neighbor for nothing. / Will your lips lead people astray?
24:29 Do not say, "As he did to me, so I will do to him. / I will give the man what his deeds deserve."
## The lazy one's field
24:30 I went by a lazy person's field, / by a senseless person's vineyard.
24:31 And there it was: thistles had sprung up over all of it, / weeds had covered the ground, / and its stone fence had been pulled down.
24:32 I looked on and gave it thought; / I saw it, and I accepted its discipline.
24:33 A little sleep, a little drowsing, / a little folding of the hands to lie down,
24:34 and your poverty will come at you like a drifter, / your lack like a man with a shield.
