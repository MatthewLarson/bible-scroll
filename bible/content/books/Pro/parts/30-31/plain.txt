## The words of Agur
30:1 The words of Agur son of Jakeh, the oracle. / So says the man to Ithiel, to Ithiel and Ucal:
30:2 Surely I am more beast than man; / no human understanding is mine.
30:3 I have not learned wisdom, / and knowledge of the Holy One I do not have.
30:4 Who has climbed up to heaven and come back down? / Who has collected the wind in his two fists? / Who has bundled up the waters in a robe? / Who has put all the ends of the earth in place? / What is his name, and what is his son's name? / You must know!
30:5 Every saying of God is refined like metal. / He is a shield for those who take shelter in him.
30:6 Add nothing to his words, / or he will correct you, and you will be shown up as a liar.
## Agur's prayer
30:7 Two things I ask from you. / Do not hold them back from me before I die.
30:8 Put emptiness and lying words far away from me. / Do not give me poverty or riches. / Feed me with the bread that is my share,
30:9 or else I may be full and deny you, / and say, "Who is the LORD?" / Or I may be left poor and steal, / and lay my hands on the name of my God.
30:10 Do not run down a servant to his master, / or he will curse you, and the guilt will be yours.
## Four kinds of people
30:11 There is a generation that curses its father / and does not bless its mother.
30:12 There is a generation that is pure in its own eyes, / yet has not been washed of its own filth.
30:13 There is a generation whose eyes are so high, / whose eyelids are lifted so far up!
30:14 There is a generation whose teeth are swords / and whose back teeth are carving knives, / to eat up the poor from the land / and the needy from among humankind.
## Sayings by number
30:15 The leech has two daughters: / "Give!" and "Give!" / Here are three that are never full, / four that never say, "Plenty!":
30:16 Sheol, and the closed-up womb, / land that never has its fill of water, / and fire, which never says, "Plenty!"
30:17 The eye that makes fun of a father / and looks down on obeying a mother, / the ravens of the stream bed will peck it out, / and young eagles will eat it.
30:18 Three things are a wonder beyond me, / and four I cannot fathom:
30:19 the way of the eagle in the sky, / the way of a snake on a rock, / the way of a ship in the heart of the sea, / and the way of a man with a young woman.
30:20 So too is the way of a woman who commits adultery. / She eats and wipes her mouth clean, / and she says, "I did no wrong."
30:21 The earth quakes beneath three things, / and beneath four it cannot hold up:
30:22 beneath a slave when he takes the throne, / and a godless fool when he has his fill of bread,
30:23 beneath a woman no one loves, when she is married, / and a slave woman when she inherits her mistress's place.
30:24 Four are among the small things of the earth, / yet they are wise, schooled in wisdom:
30:25 The ants: a people with no strength, / but in summer they get their food ready.
30:26 The rock badgers: a people with no might, / yet they set their houses in the cliff.
30:27 The locusts have no king, / but out they go in rows, every one of them.
30:28 The lizard can be held in two hands, / but there it is, in a king's palaces.
30:29 Three things step out well, / four walk with a fine stride:
30:30 the lion, a hero among the animals, / which turns back from nothing,
30:31 the rooster with its trim waist, or the billy goat, / and a king with his troops beside him.
30:32 If you have played the godless fool by lifting yourself up, / or if you have been scheming, hand to mouth!
30:33 For squeezing milk brings out curds, / squeezing the nose brings out blood, / and squeezing anger brings out a quarrel.
## The words of Lemuel
31:1 The words of Lemuel, a king: the oracle with which his mother disciplined him.
31:2 What, my son? / What, son of my womb? / What, son of my vows?
31:3 Do not hand your strength over to women, / or your ways to what wipes out kings.
31:4 It is not for kings, Lemuel, / not for kings to drink wine, / or for rulers to ask, "Where is the beer?"
31:5 Otherwise he may drink and forget what the law says, / and twist the case of all the afflicted.
31:6 Give beer to someone who is perishing, / and wine to those who are bitter in spirit.
31:7 He will drink and forget how poor he is, / and he will not recall his trouble anymore.
31:8 Open your mouth for the one who cannot speak, / for the cause of all who are left to fade away.
31:9 Open your mouth and judge what is right. / Take up the case of the poor and the needy.
## The woman of strength
31:10 A woman of strength, who can find one? / Her price is far beyond red coral.
31:11 Her husband's heart trusts in her, / and he never runs short of plunder.
31:12 She pays him back with good, never evil, / for as long as she lives.
31:13 She looks for wool and flax, / and her hands do the work gladly.
31:14 She has become like a trader's ships: / from far away she brings in her bread.
31:15 While it is still night, she is up, / handing out food to her household / and their share to her serving girls.
31:16 She sets her plans on a field and gets it. / From the fruit of her palms she plants a vineyard.
31:17 She binds up her waist with might / and toughens her arms.
31:18 She tastes that her trading is good. / Her lamp does not go out through the night.
31:19 Her hands reach out to the distaff, / and her palms grip the spindle.
31:20 She spreads her palm open to the poor; / her hands reach out to the needy.
31:21 Snow brings her no fear for her household, / for her whole household wears scarlet.
31:22 She makes bedspreads for herself. / Fine linen and purple are what she wears.
31:23 People know her husband at the gates, / when he sits with the elders of the land.
31:24 She sews linen wraps and sells them, / and hands over belts to the traders.
31:25 Her clothing is might and splendor, / and she laughs at the days ahead.
31:26 When she opens her mouth, it is with wisdom, / and on her tongue is the teaching of loyal love.
31:27 She keeps watch on how her household is going, / and she never eats the bread of laziness.
31:28 Her sons stand up and call her happy. / Her husband praises her too:
31:29 "Many daughters have done strong things, / but you have climbed above them all."
31:30 Charm is a lie, and beauty is a breath. / A woman who fears the LORD, she is the one to be praised.
31:31 Give her a share of the fruit of her hands, / and at the gates let her deeds praise her.
