## War, idols and Mastema's spirits
11:1 In the thirty-fifth jubilee, in the third week, in its first year, Reu married a woman named Ara, the daughter of Ur son of Kesed. She bore him a son, and he named him Serug, in the seventh year of this week.
11:2 In this jubilee the sons of Noah began to fight one another. They took captives and killed each other, one man his brother. They shed human blood on the earth and ate blood. They built strong cities, with walls and towers. One man raised himself above the people, and they set up the first kingdoms. They went to war, people against people, nation against nation and city against city. All of them did evil. They gathered gear for battle and taught their sons to fight. They began to capture one city after another and to sell male and female slaves.
11:3 Ur son of Kesed built the city of Ara of the Chaldeans and named it after himself and after his father.
11:4 They made molten images for themselves, and each one bowed down to the idol, the molten image he had made for himself. They began to make carved images and unclean likenesses. Cruel spirits helped them and led them astray, so that they did wrong and lived in impurity and sin.
11:5 Prince Mastema worked hard to bring all this about. He sent out other spirits, those who had been put under his hand, to do every kind of wrong and sin and every kind of crime, to ruin and destroy and to shed blood on the earth.
11:6 That is why Serug was given the name Serug: everyone had turned aside to do every kind of sin.
11:7 He grew up and lived in Ur of the Chaldeans, near the father of his wife's mother, and he worshiped idols. In the thirty-sixth jubilee, in the fifth week, in its first year, he married a woman named Melka, the daughter of Kaber, a daughter of his father's brother.
11:8 She bore him Nahor in the first year of this week. Nahor grew up and lived in Ur of the Chaldeans. His father taught him the studies of the Chaldeans, how to tell the future and read omens by the signs in the sky.
11:9 In the thirty-seventh jubilee, in the sixth week, in its first year, Nahor married a woman named Iyaska, the daughter of Nestag, of the Chaldeans.
11:10 She bore him Terah in the seventh year of this week.
## The ravens
11:11 Prince Mastema sent ravens and other birds to eat the seed sown in the ground, to ruin the land and to rob people of their work. Before they could plow the seed in, the ravens picked it up off the ground.
11:12 That is why he named him Terah: the ravens and the birds were making them poor and eating up their seed.
11:13 The years began to be barren because of the birds. They ate every fruit of the trees right off the trees. Only with great effort could people save a little of all the land's crops in those days.
11:14 In this thirty-ninth jubilee, in the second week, in the first year, Terah married a woman named Edna, the daughter of Abram, the daughter of his father's sister.
11:15 In the seventh year of this week she bore him a son. He named him Abram, after his mother's father, because that man had died before his daughter conceived a son.
11:16 The child began to see how the whole earth had gone wrong, how everyone wandered after carved images and impurity. His father taught him to write. When he was two weeks of years old, he parted from his father so that he would not bow down to idols with him.
11:17 He began to pray to the Creator of all things to save him from the error of humankind, and to keep his share in life from falling into error, into impurity and shameful ways.
11:18 The time came to sow seed in the ground. Everyone went out together to guard the seed from the ravens. Abram went out with them, a boy of fourteen.
11:19 A cloud of ravens came to eat the seed. Abram ran out to meet them before they could settle on the ground. He shouted at them before they could land and eat the seed: "Do not come down! Go back to the place you came from!" And they turned back.
11:20 He did this to the clouds of ravens seventy times that day. Of all the ravens, not one settled in any field where Abram was, not even one.
11:21 Everyone with him in all the fields saw him shout and saw all the ravens turn back. His name became great in all the land of the Chaldeans.
11:22 That year all who meant to sow came to him, and he went with them until the time for sowing was over. They sowed their land, and that year they brought home enough grain. They ate and were full.
11:23 In the first year of the fifth week Abram taught the woodworkers who made gear for oxen. They built a box above the ground, facing the frame of the plow, to hold the seed. The seed would fall from it onto the blade of the plow and be hidden in the earth. Then they no longer feared the ravens.
11:24 They made the same thing above the ground on every plow frame. They sowed and worked all the land as Abram told them, and they no longer feared the birds.
## Abram and his father
12:1 In the seventh year of the sixth week Abram spoke to his father Terah. "Father!" he said. "Here I am, my son," Terah said.
12:2 Abram said, "What help or joy do we get from these idols that you worship and bow down to?
12:3 There is no spirit in them at all. They are mute things that lead the heart astray. Do not worship them.
12:4 Worship the God of heaven, who sends down rain and dew on the earth and does everything on the earth. He created everything by his word, and all life comes from his presence.
12:5 Why do you worship things that have no spirit in them? Human hands made them, and you carry them on your shoulders. They give you no help. They bring only great shame to those who make them and lead astray the hearts of those who worship them. Do not worship them."
12:6 His father said to him, "I know it too, my son. But what can I do about the people who have made me serve in front of them?
12:7 If I tell them the truth, they will kill me. Their hearts cling to the idols, to worship them and praise them. Keep quiet, my son, or they will kill you."
12:8 Abram said the same thing to his two brothers. They were angry with him, and he kept quiet.
12:9 In the fortieth jubilee, in the second week, in its seventh year, Abram married a woman named Sarai, his father's daughter, and she became his wife.
12:10 His brother Haran married in the third year of the third week. His wife bore him a son in the seventh year of this week, and he named him Lot.
12:11 His brother Nahor also married.
## Abram burns the house of idols
12:12 In the sixtieth year of Abram's life, that is, the fourth week, in its fourth year, Abram got up at night and burned the house of idols. He burned everything in the house, and no one knew.
12:13 They got up in the night and tried to save their gods from the fire.
12:14 Haran rushed in to save them, but the fire flared up over him. He was burned in the fire and died in Ur of the Chaldeans before his father Terah, and they buried him in Ur of the Chaldeans.
12:15 Terah left Ur of the Chaldeans with his sons to go to the land of Lebanon and the land of Canaan, and he settled in Haran. Abram lived with his father in Haran for two weeks of years.
## Abram watches the stars
12:16 In the sixth week, in its fifth year, Abram sat up through the night on the first day of the seventh month. He watched the stars from evening until morning to see what the year would bring in rain. He sat alone and watched.
12:17 A word came into his heart, and he said, "All the signs of the stars and the signs of the moon and the sun are in the Lord's hand. Why am I searching them out?
12:18 If he wants, he sends rain morning and evening. If he wants, he holds it back. Everything is in his hand."
12:19 That night he prayed: "My God, my God, God Most High, you alone are my God. You created everything, and all that has come to be and all that is, is the work of your hands. You and your kingdom I have chosen.
12:20 Save me from the power of the evil spirits who rule over the thoughts of the human heart. Do not let them lead me away from you, my God. Make me and my offspring stand firm forever, so that we do not go astray from now on and forever."
12:21 He said, "Should I go back to Ur of the Chaldeans? They want to see me come back to them. Or should I stay here in this place? Let the right road in your sight go well in your servant's hands, so that he may follow it. Do not let me walk in the error of my heart, my God."
## The call and the language of creation
12:22 When he finished speaking and praying, the word of the Lord was sent to him through me: "Go out from your land, from your kin and from your father's house, to the land that I will show you. I will make you a great and numerous nation.
12:23 I will bless you and make your name great, and you will be blessed on the earth. All the nations of the earth will find blessing in you. I will bless those who bless you, and I will curse those who curse you.
12:24 I will be God to you, to your son, to your son's son and to all your offspring. Do not be afraid. From now on and to all the generations of the earth, I am your God."
12:25 The Lord God said to me, "Open his mouth and his ears, so that he can hear and speak with his tongue in the language that has been revealed." For it had ceased from the mouths of all humankind since the day of the overthrow.
12:26 I opened his mouth, his ears and his lips, and I began to speak with him in Hebrew, in the language of creation.
12:27 He took the books of his fathers, which were written in Hebrew, and copied them. From then on he began to study them, and I explained to him everything that was too hard for him. He studied them through the six months of the rains.
12:28 In the seventh year of the sixth week he spoke with his father. He told him that he would leave Haran to go to the land of Canaan, to see it and then come back to him.
12:29 His father Terah said to him, "Go in peace. May the eternal God make your path straight. The Lord be with you and keep you from all evil. May he give you grace, mercy and favor in the eyes of those who see you, and may no human being rule over you to harm you. Go in peace.
12:30 If you see a land that pleases your eyes to live in, come and take me to you. Take Lot with you, the son of your brother Haran, as your own son. The Lord be with you.
12:31 Leave your brother Nahor with me until you come back in peace. Then all of us will go with you together."
## Abram in the land of Canaan
13:1 Abram left Haran. He took his wife Sarai and Lot, the son of his brother Haran, to the land of Canaan. He came into Asshur and traveled on as far as Shechem, and he stayed near a tall oak.
13:2 He looked, and the land was very pleasant, from the entrance to Hamath as far as the tall oak.
13:3 The Lord said to him, "To you and to your offspring I will give this land."
13:4 He built an altar there and offered on it a burnt offering to the Lord, who had appeared to him.
13:5 He moved on from there to the mountain of Bethel, with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east, and pitched his tent there.
13:6 He looked, and the land was very wide and good. Everything grew on it: vines, figs and pomegranates, oaks and holm oaks, terebinths and olive trees, cedars, cypresses and pines, and every tree of the field. And there was water on the mountains.
13:7 He blessed the Lord, who had brought him out of Ur of the Chaldeans and brought him to this mountain.
13:8 In the first year of the seventh week, on the first day of the first month, he built an altar on this mountain and called on the name of the Lord: "You are my God, the eternal God."
13:9 On the altar he offered a burnt offering to the Lord, so that the Lord would be with him and not leave him all the days of his life.
13:10 He moved on from there toward the south and came to Hebron. Hebron was built at that time. He stayed there two years. Then he went into the land of the south, as far as Bealoth. There was a famine in the land.
## Abram in Egypt
13:11 Abram went to Egypt in the third year of the week. He lived in Egypt five years before his wife was taken from him.
13:12 Tanis in Egypt was built at that time, seven years after Hebron.
13:13 When Pharaoh seized Sarai, Abram's wife, the Lord struck Pharaoh and his household with great plagues because of Sarai, Abram's wife.
13:14 Abram was very rich in property: sheep, cattle, donkeys, horses and camels, male and female slaves, and a great deal of silver and gold. Lot, his brother's son, was rich too.
13:15 Pharaoh gave back Sarai, Abram's wife, and sent him out of the land of Egypt. He went to the place where he had pitched his tent at first, to the place of the altar, with Ai on the east and Bethel on the west. He blessed the Lord his God, who had brought him back in peace.
13:16 In this forty-first jubilee, in the third year of the first week, he came back to this place. He offered a burnt offering on the altar and called on the name of the Lord: "God Most High, you are my God forever and ever."
## Lot leaves
13:17 In the fourth year of this week Lot parted from him. Lot settled in Sodom, and the men of Sodom were great sinners.
13:18 It grieved Abram to the heart that his brother's son had parted from him, because he had no children.
13:19 In that year, when Lot was taken captive, the Lord spoke to Abram after Lot had parted from him, in the fourth year of this week. He said, "Raise your eyes and look out from the place where you are living, north and south, west and east.
13:20 All the land that you see I will give to you and to your offspring forever. I will make your offspring like the sand of the sea. Even if someone could count the sand of the earth, your offspring could not be counted.
13:21 Get up and walk its length and its width, and see it all, for I will give it to your offspring." And Abram went to Hebron and lived there.
## The war of the kings and the tithe
13:22 That year Chedorlaomer king of Elam came, with Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Sellasar and Tergal king of the nations. They killed the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Sodom ran away. Many fell wounded in the Valley of Siddim, by the Salt Sea.
13:23 They took Sodom, Admah and Zeboiim captive. They also took Lot captive, the son of Abram's brother, and all his goods, and he went off as far as Dan.
13:24 Someone who had escaped came and told Abram that his brother's son had been taken captive.
13:25 He armed the servants born in his house. Abram went and killed Chedorlaomer and came back. He set aside a tenth of everything and gave it to Melchizedek. This tithe is laid on Abram and on his offspring: the first tithe, for the Lord. The Lord set it as an ordinance forever, that they should give it to the priests who serve before him, for them to keep forever.
13:26 There is no limit of days to this law. He has set it for the generations forever that they must give the Lord a tenth of everything: of grain, of wine and of oil, of cattle and of sheep.
13:27 He gave it to his priests to eat and drink with joy before him.
13:28 The king of Sodom came to Abram, bowed down before him and said, "Our lord Abram, give us the people you rescued, and let the plunder be yours."
13:29 Abram said to him, "I raise my hands to God Most High: not a thread, not a sandal strap, nothing that is yours will I take, so that you will never say, 'I made Abram rich.' Only what the young men have eaten, and the share of the men who went with me, Aner, Eshcol and Mamre. They will take their share."
## The covenant between the pieces
14:1 After these things, in the fourth year of this week, on the first day of the third month, the word of the Lord came to Abram in a dream: "Do not be afraid, Abram. I am your protector, and your reward will be very great."
14:2 Abram said, "Lord, Lord, what will you give me? I am going on without children. The son of Maseq, my slave woman, is Eliezer of Damascus, and he will be my heir. You have given me no offspring. Give me offspring!"
14:3 He said to him, "This man will not be your heir. One who comes out of your own body, he will be your heir."
14:4 He brought him outside and said to him, "Look toward the sky and number the stars, if you can number them."
14:5 He looked up at the sky and saw the stars. And he said to him, "So will your offspring be."
14:6 Abram put his trust in the Lord, and it was reckoned to him as righteousness.
14:7 He said to him, "I am the Lord, who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give you the land of the Canaanites to hold forever, and to be God to you and to your offspring after you."
14:8 Abram said, "Lord, Lord, how will I know that I will possess it?"
14:9 He said to him, "Bring me a three-year-old heifer, a three-year-old goat, a three-year-old sheep, a turtledove and a pigeon."
14:10 He took all these in the middle of the month. He was living by the oak of Mamre, which is near Hebron.
14:11 He built an altar there and sacrificed all these. He poured out their blood on the altar and cut them in two down the middle. He laid the halves facing each other, but he did not cut the birds in two.
14:12 Birds came down on the laid-out pieces, and Abram kept them away. He did not let the birds touch them.
14:13 As the sun was setting, a trance fell on Abram, and suddenly a great, dark terror fell on him. Abram was told, "Know for certain that your offspring will be foreigners in a strange land. People there will make them slaves and oppress them for four hundred years.
14:14 But I will judge the nation they serve. After that they will come out of there with great wealth.
14:15 You yourself will go in peace to your fathers; you will be buried at a good old age.
14:16 In the fourth generation they will come back here, for the sin of the Amorites is not yet complete."
14:17 He woke up from his sleep and got up. The sun had set, and there was a flame. There was an oven, smoking, and a flame of fire passed between the laid-out pieces.
14:18 On that day the Lord made a covenant with Abram: "To your offspring I will give this land, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates: the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites, the Perizzites, the Rephaim, the Phakorites, the Hivites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Girgashites and the Jebusites."
14:19 The day passed, and Abram offered up the laid-out pieces and the birds, with their offerings and drink offerings, and the fire consumed them.
14:20 On this night we made a covenant with Abram, like the covenant we had made with Noah in this month. Abram renewed the feast and its ordinance for himself forever.
## Hagar and Ishmael
14:21 Abram was glad, and he told all these things to his wife Sarai. He believed that he would have offspring, but she did not give birth.
14:22 Sarai gave her husband Abram advice. She said, "Go in to Hagar, my Egyptian slave woman. Maybe I can build up offspring for you through her."
14:23 Abram listened to his wife Sarai and said to her, "Do it." Sarai took Hagar, her Egyptian slave woman, and gave her to her husband Abram to be his wife.
14:24 He went in to her, and she conceived and bore a son. He named him Ishmael, in the fifth year of this week. That was the eighty-sixth year of Abram's life.
## Abram's feast of firstfruits
15:1 In the fifth year of the fourth week of this jubilee, in the third month, in the middle of the month, Abram held the feast of the firstfruits of the wheat harvest.
15:2 He offered a new offering on the altar, the first of the grain, to the Lord. He offered a young cow, a ram and a sheep on the altar as a burnt offering to the Lord. He offered their offerings and drink offerings, all of them on the altar, with frankincense.
## The covenant of circumcision
15:3 The Lord appeared to Abram and said to him, "I am God Almighty. Be pleasing in my sight, and be whole.
15:4 I will set my covenant between me and you, and I will make you grow very great."
15:5 Abram fell on his face, and the Lord spoke with him and said,
15:6 "Here is my ordinance with you: I will make you the father of many nations.
15:7 No longer will you be called Abram. From now on and forever your name will be Abraham, because I have appointed you father of many nations.
15:8 I will make you very great. I will turn you into nations, and kings will come from you.
15:9 I will set my covenant between me and you and your offspring after you, generation after generation, as an ordinance forever, to be God to you and to your offspring after you.
15:10 I will give you and your offspring after you the land where you have lived as a foreigner, the land of Canaan, to rule forever, and I will be their God."
15:11 The Lord said to Abraham, "As for you, keep my covenant, you and your offspring after you. Circumcise every male among you. Circumcise your foreskins. It will be the sign of my ordinance forever between me and you.
15:12 You are to circumcise the child on the eighth day, every male, generation after generation: the one born in the house, and any foreigner's son you have bought for money and acquired, though he is not from your offspring.
15:13 The one born in your house must be circumcised, and those you bought with money must be circumcised. My covenant will be in your flesh as an ordinance forever.
15:14 A male who is not circumcised, the flesh of whose foreskin was not cut on the eighth day, that person will be rooted out from his family, because he has broken my covenant."
15:15 The Lord said to Abraham, "Your wife Sarai will no longer be called Sarai, because Sarah is her name.
15:16 I will bless her and give you a son from her. I will bless him, and he will become a people, and kings of nations will come from him."
15:17 Abraham fell on his face and was glad. He said in his heart, "A man a hundred years old, will a son be born to him? And Sarah, ninety years old, will she give birth?"
15:18 And Abraham said to the Lord, "If only Ishmael could live in your presence!"
15:19 The Lord said, "Yes. And Sarah will bear you a son, and you will name him Isaac. I will set up my covenant with him, a covenant forever, and with his offspring after him.
15:20 As for Ishmael too, I have heard you. I will bless him, make him grow and give him very many descendants. He will father twelve chieftains, and I will set him up as a great people.
15:21 But my covenant I will set up with Isaac, whom Sarah will bear to you at this time next year."
15:22 When he finished speaking with him, the Lord went up from Abraham.
15:23 Abraham did as the Lord had told him. He took his son Ishmael and all who were born in his house and all he had bought with money, every male in his house, and he circumcised the flesh of their foreskins.
15:24 On that very day Abraham was circumcised. Those born in his house, the men of his household and all he had bought with money, even from foreigners, were circumcised with him.
## The law of the eighth day
15:25 This law is for all generations forever. The days may not be cut short, and not one day of the eight may be passed over, because it is an eternal ordinance, set and written on the heavenly tablets.
15:26 Anyone born whose flesh is not circumcised by the eighth day does not belong to the children of the ordinance that the Lord made with Abraham. He belongs to the children of destruction. There is no sign on him that he belongs to the Lord. He is marked for ruin and destruction, to be wiped off the earth and rooted out of the earth, because he has broken the covenant of the Lord our God.
15:27 For all the angels of the presence and all the angels of holiness were made this way from the day they were created. In front of the angels of the presence and the angels of holiness he made Israel holy, to be with him and with his holy angels.
15:28 As for you, command the children of Israel to keep the sign of this covenant in their generations as an ordinance forever, and they will not be rooted out of the land.
15:29 For the command was set for the covenant, to be kept forever by all the children of Israel.
15:30 For the Lord did not bring Ishmael, his sons, his brothers or Esau near to himself. He did not choose them for being children of Abraham, because he knew them. But he chose Israel to be his people.
15:31 He made it holy and gathered it from all humankind. For there are many nations and many peoples, and all of them are his. Over all of them he set spirits in power, to lead them astray from him.
15:32 But over Israel he set no one in power, no angel and no spirit, because he alone is their ruler. He guards them and will demand them back from the hand of his angels and his spirits, and from the hand of all who carry out his commands, so that he may guard them and bless them, and they may be his and he may be theirs from now on and forever.
15:33 And now I tell you: the children of Israel will be false to this ordinance. They will not circumcise their sons by all this law. When they circumcise their sons, they will leave some of the flesh of circumcision. And all of them, sons of Beliar, will leave their sons uncircumcised, just as they were born.
15:34 There will be great anger from the Lord against the children of Israel, because they have left his covenant and turned away from his word. They have provoked him and blasphemed, because they do not keep the ordinance of this sign. They have made themselves like the nations, to be removed and rooted out of the land. For them there will be no more pardon or forgiveness, no pardon or forgiveness for any sin of this everlasting error.
## The visitors at Mamre and Sodom
16:1 On the first day of the fourth month we appeared to Abraham at the oak of Mamre. We spoke with him and told him that a son would be given to him by his wife Sarah.
16:2 Sarah laughed, because she heard that we had spoken these words with Abraham. We warned her, and she was afraid and denied that she had laughed at the words.
16:3 We told her the name of her son, as it is set and written on the heavenly tablets: Isaac.
16:4 And when we came back to her at the set time, she had conceived a son.
16:5 In this month the Lord carried out his judgments on Sodom, Gomorrah and Zeboiim and all the region of the Jordan. He burned them with fire and sulfur and wiped them out to this day, just as I have now told you all their deeds: they were wicked and great sinners. They made themselves unclean, committed sexual sin in their flesh and did impure things on the earth.
16:6 In the same way the Lord will carry out judgment on the places where people act like Sodom in its impurity, just as he judged Sodom.
16:7 But we rescued Lot, because the Lord remembered Abraham. He brought Lot out from the middle of the overthrow.
16:8 Lot and his daughters committed a sin on the earth that had never been done on the earth from the days of Adam until his time: the man lay with his daughter.
16:9 It has been commanded and engraved on the heavenly tablets about all his offspring, that he will remove them and root them out, judge them as Sodom was judged, and leave that man no offspring on the earth on the day of judgment.
## Isaac is born
16:10 In this month Abraham moved from Hebron. He went and settled between Kadesh and Shur, in the mountains of Gerar.
16:11 In the middle of the fifth month he moved from there and settled at the Well of the Oath.
16:12 In the middle of the sixth month the Lord visited Sarah and did for her what he had said.
16:13 She conceived and bore a son in the third month, in the middle of the month, at the time the Lord had told Abraham. On the feast of the firstfruits of the harvest Isaac was born.
16:14 Abraham circumcised his son at eight days old. He was the first to be circumcised by the covenant that has been set forever.
16:15 In the sixth year of the fourth week we came to Abraham at the Well of the Oath. We appeared to him, as we had told Sarah that we would come back to her, and she too had conceived a son.
16:16 We came back in the seventh month and found Sarah pregnant before us. We blessed her and told her everything that had been decreed for him: he would not die until he had fathered six sons, and he would see them before he died. But through Isaac his name and offspring would be named.
16:17 All the offspring of his sons would become nations and be counted among the nations. But from the sons of Isaac one would become a holy offspring and would not be counted among the nations.
16:18 For he would become the share of the Most High. All his offspring had fallen to what God owns, to be a treasured people for the Lord above all peoples, and to be a kingdom, a priesthood and a holy people.
16:19 We went on our way and told Sarah everything we had told him, and both of them were very, very glad.
## The first Feast of Booths
16:20 He built an altar there to the Lord, who had rescued him and who was giving him joy in the land where he lived as a foreigner. He held a feast of joy in this month for seven days, near the altar he had built at the Well of the Oath.
16:21 He built booths for himself and for his servants at this feast. He was the first to keep the Feast of Booths on the earth.
16:22 During these seven days he offered on the altar, day after day, a burnt offering to the Lord: two bulls, two rams, seven sheep and one young goat for sin, to make atonement through it for himself and for his offspring.
16:23 For the peace offering he offered seven rams, seven young goats, seven sheep and seven male goats, with their offerings and drink offerings. He burned all their fat on the altar, a choice burnt offering with a pleasing smell.
16:24 Morning and evening he burned fragrant spices: frankincense, galbanum, stacte, nard, myrrh, sweet spice and costus. He offered all seven together, crushed, in equal parts, mixed and pure.
16:25 He held this feast for seven days, glad with all his heart and with all his soul, he and everyone in his house. No foreigner was with him, and no one uncircumcised.
16:26 He blessed his Creator, who had created him in his generation, for he had created him as he pleased. For he knew and saw that from him would come the plant of righteousness for the eternal generations, and from him a holy offspring, to be like the one who made everything.
16:27 He blessed and was glad, and he named this feast the feast of the Lord, a joy that pleases God Most High.
16:28 We blessed him forever, and all his offspring after him in all the generations of the earth, because he kept this feast at its time, as the testimony of the heavenly tablets says.
16:29 That is why it is set on the heavenly tablets concerning Israel that they are to keep the Feast of Booths seven days with joy in the seventh month, accepted before the Lord, a law forever in their generations, every year.
16:30 There is no limit of days for this, because it is set forever concerning Israel: they are to keep it, live in booths, put wreaths on their heads and take leafy branches and willows from the stream.
16:31 Abraham took palm fronds and the fruit of beautiful trees. Every day he went around the altar with the branches, seven times a day. In the morning he praised and thanked his God for everything with joy.
