## Levi Made Priest at Bethel
32:1 Jacob stayed that night at Bethel. Levi dreamed that they had appointed him and made him a priest of God Most High, him and his sons forever. He woke from his sleep and blessed the Lord.
32:2 Jacob got up early in the morning on the fourteenth of this month. He set aside a tenth of everything that had come with him, from people to animals, from gold to every dish and garment. He gave a tenth of it all.
32:3 In those days Rachel became pregnant with her son Benjamin. Jacob counted his sons, starting from him and going up, and the count went up and came down on Levi as the Lord's portion. His father dressed him in the clothes of the priesthood and filled his hands.
32:4 On the fifteenth of this month he brought to the altar fourteen bulls from the herd, twenty-eight rams, forty-nine sheep, seven lambs and twenty-one young goats. They were a burnt offering on the altar, a pleasing offering with a sweet smell before God.
32:5 This was his offering for the vow he had made to give a tenth, with the offerings and drink offerings that went with them.
32:6 When the fire had eaten it up, he burned incense on top of the fire. For the peace offering he gave two bulls, four rams, four sheep, four male goats, two year-old sheep and two young goats. He did this each morning for seven days.
32:7 He, all his sons and his men ate there with joy for seven days. They blessed and thanked the Lord, who had rescued him from all his trouble and had granted him what he asked in his vow.
32:8 He set aside a tenth of all the clean animals and made them a burnt offering. The unclean animals he gave to his son Levi, and all the people he gave to him too.
32:9 Levi served as priest at Bethel before his father Jacob, out of all his ten brothers, and he was a priest there. Jacob paid his vow. In this way he set aside a tenth again for the Lord and made it holy, and it became holy.
## The Tithe Eaten Before the Lord
32:10 That is why it is ordained on the heavenly tablets as a law to set aside a tenth again and eat it before the Lord, year after year, in the place chosen for his name to live there. This law has no limit of days. It is forever.
32:11 This ordinance is written so that it will be kept every year: the second tithe is eaten before the Lord in the place that has been chosen. None of it may be left over from this year to the year that follows.
32:12 For the grain is to be eaten in its own year, until the days when the year's grain is harvested; the wine until the days of the wine; and the oil until the days of its season.
32:13 Whatever is left over and grows old is to be counted as defiled. Let it be burned in the fire, for it has become unclean.
32:14 So they are to eat it together in the sanctuary and not let it grow old.
32:15 All the tithe of cattle and sheep is holy to the Lord. It belongs to his priests, who eat it before him year after year. For this is what is ordained and engraved about the tithe on the heavenly tablets.
## Jacob Told Not to Build at Bethel
32:16 The next night, on the twenty-second of this month, Jacob planned to build up that place, to put a wall around its courtyard, and to make it holy, a holy place forever for himself and his children after him.
32:17 The Lord appeared to him in the night, blessed him and said to him, "Your name will not be called only Jacob. Israel is what they will call you."
32:18 He said to him again, "I am the Lord who made heaven and earth. I will make you grow and will multiply you greatly. Kings will come from you, and they will judge everywhere that human feet have walked.
32:19 I will give your descendants all the earth that is under heaven. They will judge all the nations as they please. After that they will gather in the whole earth and inherit it forever."
32:20 When he finished speaking with him, he went up from him, and Jacob watched until he had gone up into heaven.
32:21 In a vision of the night he saw an angel coming down from heaven with seven tablets in his hands. He gave them to Jacob, and Jacob read them. He read everything written in them that would happen to him and his sons in all the ages to come.
32:22 The angel showed him everything written on the tablets and said to him, "Do not build up this place. Do not make it a sanctuary forever, and do not live here, for this is not the place. Go to the house of Abraham your father and live there with Isaac your father until the day your father dies.
32:23 For you will die in peace in Egypt, and you will be buried with honor in this land, in the tomb of your fathers, with Abraham and Isaac.
32:24 Do not be afraid. Everything will happen just the way you have seen and read it. Now write it all down, just as you have seen and read it."
32:25 Jacob said, "My lord, how can I remember everything I have read and seen?" He said to him, "I will bring it all back to your memory."
32:26 Then he went up from him. Jacob woke from his sleep and remembered everything he had read and seen, and he wrote down all the words he had read and seen.
## The Added Day
32:27 He kept one more day there and offered sacrifices on it just as he had on the earlier days. He named it "Addition," because this day was added, and the earlier days he called "the Feast."
32:28 This is how it was shown that it should be, and it is written on the heavenly tablets. That is why it was revealed to him to keep this day and add it to the seven days of the feast.
32:29 It was named "Addition" because it goes up into the testimony of the feast days, by the count of the days of the year.
## Deborah, Benjamin and Rachel
32:30 On the night of the twenty-third of this month, Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died. They buried her below the city, under the oak by the stream. He named that place "Deborah's Stream," and the oak "the Oak of Mourning for Deborah."
32:31 Rebekah went back home to Isaac, Jacob's father. Jacob sent rams, male goats and sheep with her, so that she could make his father a meal the way he liked it.
32:32 Jacob followed his mother until he came near the land of Kabratan, and he settled there.
32:33 Rachel gave birth to a son in the night. She named him "Son of My Pain," because she suffered when she gave birth to him. But his father named him Benjamin. This was on the eleventh of the eighth month, in the first year of the sixth week of this jubilee.
32:34 Rachel died there and was buried in the land of Ephrath, that is, Bethlehem. Jacob built a standing stone on Rachel's grave, by the road above her grave.
## Reuben and Bilhah
33:1 Jacob went and settled south of Magdaladraef. Then he went to his father Isaac, he and his wife Leah, on the first day of the tenth month.
33:2 Reuben saw Bilhah, Rachel's slave woman and his father's concubine, bathing in water in a hidden place, and he desired her.
33:3 He hid at night, and in the night he went into Bilhah's house. He found her lying alone on a bed, asleep in her house.
33:4 He lay with her. She woke up and looked, and there was Reuben, lying with her in the bed. She pulled back the edge of her covering, grabbed him and screamed, and she saw that it was Reuben.
33:5 She was ashamed because of him. She let go of him, and he ran away.
33:6 She grieved terribly over this, and she told no one at all.
33:7 When Jacob came and looked for her, she said to him, "I am not clean for you. I have been defiled and kept from you, for Reuben has defiled me. He lay with me in the night while I was asleep, and I did not know it until he uncovered my skirt and lay with me."
33:8 Jacob was very angry with Reuben for lying with Bilhah, for he had uncovered his father's covering.
33:9 Jacob never came near her again, because Reuben had defiled her. Any man who uncovers his father's covering does a very evil thing, for he is hateful before the Lord.
## The Law Against Lying with a Father's Wife
33:10 That is why it is written and ordained on the heavenly tablets that a man must not lie with his father's wife or uncover his father's covering, for it is unclean. They must surely die together, the man who lies with his father's wife and the woman as well, because they have done an unclean thing on the earth.
33:11 There must be nothing unclean before our God in the nation he has chosen as his own possession.
33:12 And again it is written a second time: "Cursed is anyone who lies with his father's wife, for he has uncovered his father's nakedness." And all the Lord's holy ones said, "So be it, so be it."
33:13 And you, Moses, command the Israelites to keep this word, for it carries the sentence of death. It is unclean, and there is never any atonement to atone for the man who has done this. He must be put to death: killed, stoned with stones, and rooted out from among the people of our God.
33:14 For no man in Israel who does this may stay alive a single day on the earth, because he is hateful and unclean.
33:15 Let no one say, "Reuben was granted life and forgiveness after he lay with the concubine who was his father's wife, and so was she, though she had a husband and her husband, his father Jacob, was still alive."
33:16 For until then the ordinance, the judgment and the law had not been revealed in full for everyone. In your days it is revealed, as a law for its own times and days, and an eternal law for the generations forever.
33:17 This law has no end of days, and there is no atonement for it. Both of them must be rooted out from among the people. On the day they do it, they are to be put to death.
33:18 And you, Moses, write it down for Israel, so they will keep it, and not do anything like this, and not sin a sin that brings death. For the Lord our God is the judge. He does not play favorites, and he does not take a bribe.
33:19 Tell them these words of the ordinance, so they will listen, keep them and be on guard, and not be destroyed or rooted out of the land. For all who do this on the earth before our God are uncleanness, an abomination, a stain and a pollution.
33:20 There is no greater sin than the sexual sin they commit on the earth. For Israel is a holy nation to the Lord its God, a nation that is his inheritance, a nation of priests, a kingdom of priests, and his own possession. No uncleanness like this may be seen among the holy nation.
## Jacob Comes Home to Isaac
33:21 In the third year of the sixth week, Jacob and all his sons moved and settled at the house of Abraham, near his father Isaac and his mother Rebekah.
33:22 These are the names of Jacob's sons: Reuben his firstborn, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar and Zebulun, the sons of Leah; the sons of Rachel, Joseph and Benjamin; the sons of Bilhah, Dan and Naphtali; the sons of Zilpah, Gad and Asher; and Dinah, Leah's daughter, Jacob's only daughter.
33:23 They came and bowed down to Isaac and Rebekah. When the two saw them, they blessed Jacob and all his sons. Isaac was overjoyed, because he saw the sons of Jacob, his younger son, and he blessed them.
## The War with the Amorite Kings
34:1 In the sixth year of this week of the forty-fourth jubilee, Jacob sent his sons, and his servants with them, to pasture their sheep in the pastures of Shechem.
34:2 Seven kings of the Amorites gathered against them, hiding under the trees, to kill them and carry off their animals.
34:3 Jacob, Levi, Judah and Joseph were at home with their father Isaac, because his spirit was low and they could not leave him. Benjamin was the youngest, so he stayed with him.
34:4 There came the king of Tafu, the king of Ares, the king of Seragan, the king of Selo, the king of Gaaz, the king of Betoron and the king of Maanisakir, and all who live in these hills and who live in the forests of the land of Canaan.
34:5 Word was brought to Jacob: "The kings of the Amorites have surrounded your sons, and their herds have been carried off."
34:6 He set out from his house, he and his three sons, all his father's servants and his own servants, and he went against them with six thousand men who carried swords.
34:7 He killed them in the pastures of Shechem and chased those who ran away, killing them with the edge of the sword. He killed Ares, Tafu, Seragan, Selo, Maanisakir and Gaaz.
34:8 He gathered back his herds. He overpowered them and laid a tribute on them: they were to pay him five kinds of produce from their land. He built Robel and Tamnatares.
34:9 He came back in peace and made peace with them, and they became his servants until the day he and his sons went down to Egypt.
## Joseph Sold into Egypt
34:10 In the seventh year of this week, he sent Joseph from his house to the land of Shechem to find out how his brothers were doing, and Joseph found them in the land of Dothan.
34:11 They dealt deceitfully with him and plotted to kill him. Then they changed their minds and sold him to Ishmaelite traders, who took him down to Egypt and sold him to Potiphar, Pharaoh's eunuch, the chief of the cooks, priest of the city of Elew.
34:12 Jacob's sons killed a young male goat, dipped Joseph's robe in its blood, and sent it to their father Jacob on the tenth of the seventh month.
34:13 He mourned all that night, because they had brought it to him in the evening. He grew feverish with grief over Joseph's death and said, "An evil beast has eaten Joseph." Everyone in his household mourned with him that day. They grieved and mourned with him all that day.
34:14 His sons and his daughter got up to comfort him, but he would not be comforted over his son.
34:15 That day Bilhah heard that Joseph was lost, and she died while mourning for him. She had been living in Qafratef. His daughter Dinah also died after Joseph was lost. So these three griefs came on Israel in one month.
34:16 They buried Bilhah across from Rachel's tomb, and they buried his daughter Dinah there too.
34:17 He kept mourning for Joseph for a year and did not stop, for he said, "Let me go down to the grave mourning for my son."
## Why Israel Keeps the Day of Atonement
34:18 That is why it is ordained for the Israelites to afflict themselves on the tenth of the seventh month, the day the news that made him weep for Joseph reached Jacob his father. On that day they are to make atonement for themselves with a young goat, on the tenth of the seventh month, once a year, for their sins. For they had grieved their father's tender love for his son Joseph.
34:19 This day has been ordained so that on it they will grieve for their sins, for all their wrongdoing and for all their errors, and cleanse themselves on this day once a year.
## The Wives of Jacob's Sons
34:20 After Joseph was lost, Jacob's sons took wives for themselves. The name of Reuben's wife was Oda. The name of Simeon's wife was Adebaa, a Canaanite. The name of Levi's wife was Melka, one of the daughters of Aram, of the descendants of the sons of Terah. The name of Judah's wife was Betasuel, a Canaanite. The name of Issachar's wife was Hezaqa. The name of Zebulun's wife was [Neeman]. The name of Dan's wife was Egla. The name of Naphtali's wife was Rasuu, of Mesopotamia. The name of Gad's wife was Maka. The name of Asher's wife was Iyona. The name of Joseph's wife was Asenath, the Egyptian. The name of Benjamin's wife was Iyaska.
34:21 Simeon changed his mind and took a second wife from Mesopotamia, as his brothers had done.
## Rebekah's Last Words to Jacob
35:1 In the first year of the first week of the forty-fifth jubilee, Rebekah called her son Jacob and gave him a command about his father and his brother: he was to honor them all the days of his life.
35:2 Jacob said, "I will do everything you have commanded me. For this will bring me honor and greatness, and righteousness before the Lord, that I honor them.
35:3 You yourself, mother, know all my deeds and everything in my heart from the day I was born until today: all my days I think good toward everyone.
35:4 How could I not do what you have commanded me, to honor my father and my brother?
35:5 Tell me, mother, what crookedness you have seen in me, and I will turn away from it, and mercy will be on me."
35:6 She said to him, "My son, in all my days I have seen nothing crooked in you, only upright deeds. But I will tell you the truth, my son: I will die this year. I will not live through this year, for I have seen the day of my death in a dream. I will not live beyond a hundred and fifty-five years, and now I have finished all the days of my life that I was to live."
35:7 Jacob laughed at his mother's words, because his mother had told him she was going to die. She was sitting across from him with her strength still in her, and her strength had not weakened. She went in and out and could see, her teeth were strong, and no sickness had touched her all the days of her life.
35:8 Jacob said to her, "I would be blessed, mother, if my days came near the days of your life and my strength stayed with me the way your strength has. You will not die. You are only joking with me about your death."
## Rebekah Asks Isaac to Make Esau Swear
35:9 She went in to Isaac and said to him, "I have one request to ask of you. Make Esau swear that he will not harm Jacob or hunt him down in hatred. For you know Esau's mind: it has been crooked since he was young, and there is no goodness in him. He wants to kill Jacob after you die.
35:10 You know everything he has done from the day his brother Jacob went to Haran until today. He has left us with his whole heart and done us harm. He has taken your flocks for himself and carried off all your goods right in front of you.
35:11 When we begged him and asked for what was ours, he acted like a man doing us a kindness.
35:12 He is bitter against you because you blessed Jacob, your son who is blameless and upright. For there is no evil in Jacob, only goodness. Since he came from Haran until today he has not held back anything from us. He brings us everything in its season, every day, and he is glad with his whole heart when we take from his hand, and he blesses us. He has not left us from the day he came from Haran until today. He lives with us at home all the time, honoring us."
35:13 Isaac said to her, "I too know and see the deeds of Jacob, who is with us, how he honors us with his whole heart. I used to love Esau more than Jacob, because he was born first. But now I love Jacob more than Esau, for Esau has done many evil deeds. There is no righteousness in him, for all his ways are injustice and violence, and there is no righteousness around him.
35:14 Now my heart is troubled because of his deeds. Neither he nor his descendants will be saved, for they are the ones who will be destroyed from the earth and rooted out from under heaven. For he has left the God of Abraham and gone after his wives, after their uncleanness and their error, he and his sons.
35:15 You tell me to make him swear that he will not kill his brother Jacob. Even if he swears, he will not keep it. He will not do good, only evil.
35:16 But if he wants to kill his brother Jacob, he will be handed over into Jacob's hands, and he will not escape from his hands, for he will fall into his hands.
35:17 Do not be afraid for Jacob, for Jacob's guardian is great, mighty and honored, and praised above Esau's guardian."
## Esau and Jacob Swear to Their Mother
35:18 Rebekah sent for Esau and called him, and he came to her. She said to him, "I have a request to ask of you, my son. Say that you will do it, my son."
35:19 He said, "I will do everything you tell me. I will not refuse your request."
35:20 She said to him, "I ask you that on the day I die you bring me and bury me near Sarah, your father's mother. I ask that you and Jacob love each other, and that neither of you want evil for the other, only love. Then you will prosper, my sons, and be honored in the land, and no enemy will gloat over you. You will be a blessing and a mercy in the eyes of all who love you."
35:21 He said, "I will do everything you tell me. On the day you die I will bury you near Sarah, my father's mother, as you wish, so that her bones are near your bones.
35:22 I will also love my brother Jacob more than all flesh. I have no brother in all the earth but him alone. It is no great thing for me to love him, for he is my brother. We were sown together in your body, and we came out together from your womb. If I do not love my brother, whom will I love?
35:23 I myself beg you to urge Jacob about me and about my sons, for I know that he will surely be king over me and my sons. On the day my father blessed him, he made him the higher one and me the lower.
35:24 I swear to you that I will love him and not want evil for him all the days of my life, only good." He swore to her about all this.
35:25 Then she called Jacob in front of Esau and gave him the same command she had spoken to Esau.
35:26 Jacob said, "I will do what pleases you. Believe me, no evil will come from me or from my sons against Esau. I will be first in nothing except love."
35:27 She and her sons ate and drank that night, and she died that night, three jubilees and one week of years and one year old. Her two sons, Esau and Jacob, buried her in the double cave near Sarah, their father's mother.
## Isaac's Last Words
36:1 In the sixth year of this week, Isaac called his two sons, Esau and Jacob, and they came to him. He said to them, "My children, I am going the way of my fathers, to the eternal house where my fathers are.
36:2 Bury me near my father Abraham, in the double cave in the field of Ephron the Hittite, which Abraham bought as a place to bury. In the tomb I dug for myself, bury me there.
36:3 This is what I command you, my sons: do what is right and upright on the earth, so that the Lord will bring on you everything the Lord said he would do for Abraham and his descendants.
36:4 Among yourselves, my sons, love your brothers as a man loves his own soul. Let each one look for what is good for his brother, and work together on the earth. Let them love one another as their own souls.
36:5 About idols, I command and warn you: reject them, hate them and do not love them, for they are full of deceit for those who worship them and those who bow down to them.
36:6 Remember, my sons, the Lord, the God of Abraham your father. After this I too worshiped him and served him in righteousness and in joy, so that he would multiply you and increase your descendants like the stars of heaven in number, and plant you on the earth as the plant of righteousness that will not be rooted out for all generations forever.
36:7 Now I will make you swear a great oath, for there is no oath greater than this one: by the name that is glorious and honored, great and splendid, wonderful and mighty, which made the heavens and the earth and all things together, that you will fear him and worship him,
36:8 and that each one will love his brother with tender love and righteousness, and that no one will want evil for his brother from now on and forever, all the days of your life, so that you will prosper in all your deeds and not be destroyed.
36:9 If one of you plans evil against his brother, know from now on that whoever plans evil against his brother will fall into his hand and be rooted out of the land of the living, and his descendants will be destroyed from under heaven.
36:10 On the day of turmoil and curse, of wrath and fury, he will burn up his land, his city and everything that is his with flaming, devouring fire, just as he burned Sodom. He will be blotted out of the book of the instruction of humankind. He will not be entered in the book of life, but in the book of those who perish, and he will go away into an eternal curse. So their sentence will be renewed forever, in hatred and curse, in wrath and pain, in fury, in plagues and in sickness for all time.
36:11 I am telling you and warning you, my sons, about the judgment that will come on the man who wants to harm his brother."
## Isaac Divides His Goods and Dies
36:12 That day he divided all he owned between the two of them. He gave the larger share to the one who was born first, with the tower and everything around it, and everything Abraham had gained at the Well of the Oath.
36:13 He said, "I am making this larger share greater for the one who was born first."
36:14 Esau said, "I sold it to Jacob. I gave my birthright to Jacob. Let it be given to him. I have nothing at all to say about it, for it is his."
36:15 Isaac said, "May a blessing rest on you, my sons, and on your descendants today, for you have given me rest. My heart is not grieved about the birthright, that you would do something wicked over it.
36:16 May the Lord Most High bless the man who does what is right, him and his descendants forever."
36:17 When he had finished commanding them and blessing them, they ate and drank together in front of him. He was glad, because they were of one mind. They went out from him, rested that day and slept.
36:18 That day Isaac slept on his bed, glad, and he slept the eternal sleep. He died a hundred and eighty years old. He had completed twenty-five weeks of years and five years. His two sons, Esau and Jacob, buried him.
36:19 Esau went to the land of Edom, to the mountains of Seir, and lived there.
36:20 Jacob lived in the hills of Hebron, in the tower in the land where his father Abraham had lived as a foreigner. He worshiped the Lord with all his heart, by the commands that had been made known, by the way the days of his generations are divided.
## Leah's Death
36:21 His wife Leah died in the fourth year of the second week of the forty-fifth jubilee. He buried her in the double cave near his mother Rebekah, to the left of the grave of Sarah, his father's mother.
36:22 All her sons and his sons came to weep for Leah his wife with him and to comfort him over her, for he was mourning her.
36:23 For he loved her very much after her sister Rachel died, because she was blameless and upright in all her ways and honored Jacob. In all the days she lived with him he never heard a harsh word from her mouth, for she was gentle and peaceable, upright and honorable.
36:24 He remembered all the deeds she had done in her life, and he mourned her deeply, for he loved her with all his heart and with all his soul.
## Esau's Sons Want War
37:1 On the day that Isaac, the father of Jacob and Esau, died, Esau's sons heard that Isaac had given the birthright to Jacob, his younger son, and they were very angry.
37:2 They quarreled with their father, saying, "Why, when you are the elder and Jacob the younger, did your father give Jacob the birthright and pass you over?"
37:3 He said to them, "Because I sold my birthright to Jacob for a little lentil stew. On the day my father sent me to hunt, catch something and bring it to him to eat so that he would bless me, Jacob came with cunning and brought my father food and drink. My father blessed him and put me under his hand.
37:4 Now our father has made us swear, him and me, that neither of us will plan evil against his brother, and that we will go on in love and peace, each with his brother, and not corrupt our ways."
37:5 They said to him, "We will not listen to you and make peace with him. Our strength is greater than his strength, and we are stronger than he is. We will go against him, kill him and destroy his sons. If you will not go with us, we will harm you too.
37:6 Now listen to us. Let us send to Aram, Philistia, Moab and Ammon, and choose for ourselves chosen men who are eager for battle. Let us go against him, fight him and wipe him off the earth before he grows strong."
37:7 Their father said to them, "Do not go, and do not make war with him, or you will fall before him."
37:8 They said to him, "This is just what you have done from your youth until today. You are putting your neck under his yoke. We will not listen to these words."
37:9 They sent to Aram and to Aduram, their father's friend, and with them they hired a thousand fighting men, chosen warriors.
37:10 To them came hired men from Moab and from the Ammonites, a thousand chosen men; from Philistia a thousand chosen warriors; from Edom and from the Horites a thousand chosen fighters; and from the Kittim, strong warriors.
37:11 They said to their father, "Go out with them and lead them, or we will kill you."
37:12 He was filled with wrath and fury when he saw that his sons were forcing him to go ahead and lead them against his brother Jacob.
37:13 But then he remembered all the evil that had lain hidden in his heart against his brother Jacob, and he did not remember the oath he had sworn to his father and his mother, that he would plan no evil against his brother Jacob all his days.
## Esau Comes Against Jacob's Tower
37:14 In all this, Jacob did not know that they were coming against him for battle. He was mourning his wife Leah until they came very near the tower with four thousand men, warriors and chosen fighters.
37:15 The men of Hebron sent word to him: "Your brother has come against you to fight you, with four thousand men who wear swords and carry shields and weapons." They told him because they loved Jacob more than Esau, for Jacob was a more generous and merciful man than Esau.
37:16 But Jacob did not believe it until they came very near the tower.
37:17 Then he shut the gates of the tower, stood on the battlements and spoke with his brother Esau. He said, "What a fine comfort you have come to comfort me with, for my wife who has died! Is this the oath you swore to your father, and again to your mother, before they died? You have broken the oath, and in the hour you swore it to your father you were condemned."
37:18 Then Esau answered him, "Neither human beings nor the animals of the earth have an oath of righteousness that, once they swear it, holds forever. Every day they plan evil against one another, and how each may kill his enemy and his foe.
37:19 You hate me and my sons forever. There is no keeping brotherhood with you.
37:20 Listen to these words that I am telling you: / If the boar changes its skin / and makes its bristles as soft as wool, / or if horns grow out on its head / like the horns of a stag or a sheep, / then I will keep brotherhood with you. / The breasts were parted from their mother, / for you have not been a brother to me.
37:21 And if the wolves make peace with the lambs, / so as not to eat them or attack them, / and if their hearts are set on doing them good, / then there will be peace in my heart toward you.
37:22 And if the lion becomes the ox's friend, / is bound with it under one yoke, / plows with it and makes peace with it, / then I will make peace with you.
37:23 And when the raven turns white like the raza, / then know that I love you / and will make peace with you. / You will be rooted out, / and your sons will be rooted out, / and there will be no peace for you."
37:24 Jacob saw that Esau meant him evil from his heart and with all his soul, to kill him, and that he came charging like the boar that runs onto the spear that pierces and kills it and does not turn back from it.
37:25 Then he told his own people and his servants to attack him and all his companions.
## Jacob Kills Esau
38:1 After that, Judah spoke to his father Jacob and said to him, "Draw your bow, father, shoot your arrows, bring down the enemy and kill the foe. May the strength be yours, for we will not kill your brother. He is close to you and like you, and with us he is held in honor."
38:2 Then Jacob drew his bow, shot an arrow, struck his brother Esau and brought him down.
38:3 Again he shot an arrow and struck Aduran the Aramean in his left breast, and killed him.
38:4 Then Jacob's sons went out, they and their servants, dividing themselves on the four sides of the tower.
38:5 Judah went out first, and Naphtali and Gad with him, and fifty servants with him, on the south side of the tower. They killed everyone they found in front of them, and not one of them escaped.
38:6 Levi, Dan and Asher went out on the east side of the tower, with fifty men, and they killed the fighting men of Moab and Ammon.
38:7 Reuben, Issachar and Zebulun went out on the north side of the tower, with fifty men, and they killed the fighters of the Philistines.
38:8 Simeon, Benjamin and Hanoch, Reuben's son, went out on the west side of the tower, with fifty men. They killed four hundred strong fighting men of Edom and of the Horites, and six hundred ran away. Four of Esau's sons ran away with them and left their father lying dead where he had fallen, on the hill in Aduram.
38:9 Jacob's sons chased them as far as the mountains of Seir. But Jacob buried his brother on the hill in Aduram and went back to his house.
## Edom Made to Serve
38:10 Jacob's sons pressed hard on Esau's sons in the mountains of Seir and bowed their necks, so that they became servants to Jacob's sons.
38:11 They sent to their father to ask whether they should make peace with them or kill them.
38:12 Jacob sent word to his sons to make peace. They made peace with them and put the yoke of servitude on them, so that they would pay tribute to Jacob and his sons always.
38:13 They kept paying tribute to Jacob until the day he went down to Egypt.
38:14 The sons of Edom have not escaped the yoke of servitude that the twelve sons of Jacob put on them, to this day.
## The Kings of Edom
38:15 These are the kings who reigned in Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites, to this day, in the land of Edom.
38:16 Balak son of Beor reigned in Edom, and the name of his city was Danaba.
38:17 Balak died, and Jobab son of Zara, from Bosir, reigned in his place.
38:18 Jobab died, and Asam from the hill country of Teman reigned in his place.
38:19 Asam died, and Adat son of Bard, who struck down Midian in the country of Moab, reigned in his place. The name of his city was Awut.
38:20 Adat died, and Saloman from Emaseqa reigned in his place.
38:21 Saloman died, and Saul from Raabot on the River reigned in his place.
38:22 Saul died, and Baelunan son of Achbor reigned in his place.
38:23 Baelunan son of Achbor died, and Adat reigned in his place. The name of his wife was Maytabit daughter of Matrit, daughter of Metabedzaab.
38:24 These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom.
