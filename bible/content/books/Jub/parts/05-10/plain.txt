## The Angels and Their Sons
5:1 When the children of humankind began to grow many on the face of the whole earth, daughters were born to them. In a certain year of this jubilee the angels of the Lord saw them, that they were good to look at. So they took wives for themselves from all of them, whomever they chose, and the women bore them children. These were giants.
5:2 Wrongdoing grew on the earth. All flesh ruined its way: people, cattle, wild animals, birds and everything that walks on the earth. All of them ruined their ways and their order, and they began to eat one another. Wrongdoing grew on the earth, and every thought that the minds of all humankind formed was evil like this all day long.
5:3 The Lord looked at the earth, and there it was, ruined. All flesh had ruined its order, and everyone on the earth had done evil before his eyes.
5:4 He said that he would wipe out humankind and all flesh on the face of the earth that he had created.
5:5 Only Noah found favor before his eyes.
5:6 He was very angry with his angels whom he had sent to the earth. He ordered that they be torn out of all their authority, and he told us to bind them in the depths of the earth. And there they are, bound in their midst and kept apart.
5:7 Against their sons an order went out from before his face, that they be struck down with the sword and removed from under the sky.
5:8 He said, "My spirit will not stay on humans forever, since they are flesh. Their days will be 120 years."
5:9 He sent his sword among them, so that each would kill the other. They began to kill one another until all of them fell by the sword and were wiped off the earth.
5:10 Their fathers were watching. After this they were bound in the depths of the earth until the day of the great judgment, when judgment will be carried out on all who ruined their ways and their deeds before the Lord.
## The Judgment Written in Heaven
5:11 He wiped out all of them from their places, and not one of them was left whom he did not judge for all their wickedness.
5:12 He made a new and righteous nature for all his works, so that they would never sin in their whole nature, and each would be righteous in its own kind at all times.
5:13 The judgment of all of them has been decreed and written on the heavenly tablets, and there is no injustice in it. As for all who step off their path, the path decreed for them to walk in: if they do not walk in it, judgment is written for every creature and for every kind.
5:14 Nothing is left out, in heaven or on earth, in light or in darkness, in Sheol or in the deep or in the place of darkness. All their judgments are decreed, written and engraved.
5:15 He will judge everyone: the great by his greatness and the small by his smallness, each one by his way.
5:16 He does not play favorites, and he does not take gifts, when he says he will carry out judgment on each one. If someone gave everything on the earth, he would not play favorites or accept it from him, for he is the righteous judge.
5:17 About the children of Israel it has been written and decreed: if they turn to him in righteousness, he will forgive all their wrongdoing and pardon all their sins.
5:18 It is written and decreed that he will show mercy to all who turn from all their guilt, once a year.
5:19 As for all who ruined their ways and their plans before the flood, he showed favor to no one but Noah alone. He showed him favor for the sake of his sons, whom he saved from the waters of the flood because of him. For his heart was righteous in all his ways, just as had been commanded about him, and he had not stepped away from anything decreed for him.
## The Flood
5:20 The Lord said that he would wipe out everything on dry land: people, cattle, wild animals, birds and whatever moves on the earth.
5:21 He commanded Noah to make himself an ark, to save himself from the waters of the flood.
5:22 Noah made the ark in every way as he had commanded him, in the second jubilee of years, in the fifth week of years, in its fifth year.
5:23 He went in during its sixth year, in the second month, from the first day of the second month until the sixteenth. He went in, with all that we brought to him, into the ark. And the Lord shut it from outside on the evening of the seventeenth.
5:24 The Lord opened seven windows of the sky, and the mouths of the springs of the great deep, seven mouths in number.
5:25 The windows began to pour water down from the sky for forty days and forty nights, and the springs of the deep sent water up too, until the whole world was full of water.
5:26 The waters grew on the earth. The waters rose fifteen cubits above every high mountain. The ark was lifted up off the earth, and it drifted on the face of the waters.
5:27 The water stayed on the face of the earth for five months, 150 days.
5:28 The ark moved on and settled on the top of Lubar, one of the mountains of Ararat.
5:29 In the fourth month the springs of the great deep were shut, and the windows of the sky were held back. On the first day of the seventh month all the mouths of the depths of the earth were opened, and the water began to go down into the deep below.
5:30 On the first day of the tenth month the tops of the mountains could be seen, and on the first day of the first month the earth could be seen.
5:31 The waters drained from the earth in the fifth week, in its seventh year, and on the seventeenth day of the second month the earth was dry.
5:32 On its twenty-seventh day he opened the ark and sent out of it the wild animals, the birds and whatever moves.
## Noah's Offering and the Covenant
6:1 On the first day of the third month he went out of the ark, and he built an altar on that mountain.
6:2 He appeared on the earth. He took a young goat and made atonement with its blood for all the guilt of the earth, because everything that had been on it had been wiped out, except those that were in the ark with Noah.
6:3 He put its fat on the altar. He took an ox, a ram, a sheep, goats, salt, a turtledove and a young dove, and he offered a whole burnt offering on the altar. He put on them an offering mixed with oil, sprinkled wine, and laid frankincense over everything. He sent up a pleasing smell, one that is welcome before the Lord.
6:4 The Lord smelled the pleasing smell, and he made a covenant with him that there would be no more waters of a flood to ruin the earth. All the days of the earth, seedtime and harvest will not stop. Cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night will not change their order, and they will never stop.
6:5 "As for you, grow and multiply on the earth, and become many on it. Be a blessing in the midst of the fear and the dread of you. I will put it on everything that is on the earth and in the sea.
6:6 Look, I have given you every wild animal, every winged thing, everything that moves on the earth and in the waters, the fish, and everything for food. Just as with the green plants, I have given you all of it to eat.
6:7 But flesh with its life, with the blood, you must not eat, for the life of all flesh is in the blood. This is so that your own blood will not be demanded for your lives. From the hand of every person, from the hand of every one, I will demand a human's blood.
6:8 Whoever spills a human's blood, by a human his blood will be spilled, for in the image of the Lord he made humankind.
6:9 As for you, grow and multiply on the earth."
6:10 Noah and his sons swore an oath that they would not eat any blood that is in any flesh. And he made a covenant before the Lord God forever, for all the generations of the earth, in this month.
## The Law of Blood
6:11 That is why he told you to make a covenant with the children of Israel too, in this month, on the mountain, with an oath. You were to sprinkle blood on them because of all the words of the covenant that the Lord was making with them for all time.
6:12 This testimony is written for you, so that you keep it always: you must not eat any blood, on any day, of wild animals or birds, all the days of the earth. A person who eats the blood of a wild animal, of cattle or of birds, all the days of the earth, he and his offspring will be rooted out of the land.
6:13 You, command the children of Israel not to eat any blood, so that their names and their offspring may stay before the Lord our God always.
6:14 This law has no limit of days, for it is forever. They must keep it for the generations, so that they keep pleading for themselves with blood before the altar. Every day, at the hour of morning and of evening, they are to seek forgiveness for themselves continually before the Lord, so that they keep it and are not rooted out.
6:15 To Noah and his sons he gave a sign: never again would there be a flood on the earth.
6:16 He put his bow in the cloud as the sign of the covenant that lasts forever, that there would never again be waters of a flood on the earth to ruin it, all the days of the earth.
## The Feast of Weeks
6:17 For this reason it has been decreed and written on the heavenly tablets that they should keep the Feast of Weeks in this month, once a year, to renew the covenant every year.
6:18 This whole feast was kept in heaven from the day of creation until the days of Noah, twenty-six jubilees and five weeks of years. Noah and his sons kept it for seven jubilees and one week of years, until Noah died. From the day Noah died, his sons ruined it until the days of Abraham, and they ate blood.
6:19 But Abraham alone kept it, and Isaac and Jacob and his children kept it up to your days. In your days the children of Israel forgot it, until I renewed it for them at this mountain.
6:20 You too, command the children of Israel to keep this feast in all their generations, as a commandment for them: one day in the year, in this month, they are to hold the feast.
6:21 For it is the Feast of Weeks, and it is the Feast of Firstfruits. This feast is double and of two kinds. Hold it just as it is written and engraved about it.
6:22 For I have written in the book of the first law, in what I wrote for you, that you should hold it at its time, one day a year. I have also told you about its offering, so that the children of Israel would remember it and hold it through their generations in this month, one day each year.
## The Year of 364 Days
6:23 The first day of the first month, the first day of the fourth month, the first day of the seventh month and the first day of the tenth month are days of remembrance and days of the seasons. In the four divisions of the year they are written and decreed as a testimony forever.
6:24 Noah decreed them for himself as feasts for the generations forever, so that through them they became a memorial for him.
6:25 On the first day of the first month he was told to make himself the ark. On that day the earth became dry, and he opened the ark and saw the earth.
6:26 On the first day of the fourth month the mouths of the depths of the deep below were shut. On the first day of the seventh month all the mouths of the depths of the earth were opened, and the water began to go down into them.
6:27 On the first day of the tenth month the tops of the mountains could be seen, and Noah was glad.
6:28 For this reason he decreed them for himself as feasts of remembrance forever, and this is how they are decreed.
6:29 They are set up on the heavenly tablets. Thirteen weeks lie between each of them and the next, from one remembrance to the other: from the first to the second, from the second to the third, and from the third to the fourth.
6:30 All the days of the commandment come to fifty-two weeks of days, and the whole year is complete.
6:31 This is how it is engraved and decreed on the heavenly tablets. No one may pass over a single year, from year to year.
6:32 Now you, command the children of Israel to keep the years by this count: 364 days. That makes a complete year. They must not upset its time, in its days or in its feasts, for everything will happen in them as their testimony says. They will not leave out a day or upset a feast.
6:33 But if they pass over them and do not keep them as he commanded, then all of them will upset their times. The years will be shifted out of this order. They will upset the times, the years will be shifted, and they will pass over their ordinances.
6:34 All the children of Israel will forget and will not find the path of the years. They will forget the start of the month, the season and the Sabbath, and they will go wrong about every ordinance of the years.
6:35 I know this, and from now on I will make it known to you. It is not from my own heart. For this is how the book is written before me, and the division of the days is decreed on the heavenly tablets. It is so that they will not forget the feasts of the covenant and walk in the feasts of the nations, after their error and their ignorance.
6:36 For there will be people who watch the moon closely, by the moon's own sightings. But the moon ruins the seasons and comes ten days early, year after year.
6:37 For this reason years will come on them when they ruin the order. They will make the day of testimony a despised day and an unclean day a feast day. They will mix up all the days, the holy with the unclean and the unclean day with the holy day, for they will go wrong about the months, the Sabbaths, the feasts and the jubilee.
6:38 For this reason I command you and testify to you, so that you testify to them. For after your death your children will ruin it, so that they will not make the year 364 days only. For this reason they will go wrong about the start of the month, the season, the Sabbath and the feasts, and they will eat every kind of blood with every kind of flesh.
## Noah's Vineyard
7:1 In the seventh week, in its first year, in this jubilee, Noah planted vines on the mountain where the ark had come to rest, named Lubar, one of the mountains of Ararat. They bore fruit in the fourth year. He guarded their fruit and picked it that year, in the seventh month.
7:2 He made wine from it and put it into a jar, and he kept it until the fifth year, until the first day, the first day of the first month.
7:3 He held the day of this feast with joy. He made a whole burnt offering to the Lord: one young bull from the herd, one ram, seven sheep a year old, and one young goat, to make atonement with it for himself and for his sons.
7:4 He prepared the young goat first and put some of its blood on the flesh that was on the altar he had made. All the fat he laid on the altar where he made the burnt offering, with the bull, the ram and the sheep, and he laid all their flesh on the altar.
7:5 He put all their offerings, mixed with oil, on it. Afterward he sprinkled wine on the fire he had made on the altar before. He put incense on the altar and sent up a pleasing smell, welcome before the Lord his God.
7:6 He was glad, and he and his children drank some of this wine with joy.
7:7 Evening came, and he went into his tent. He was drunk, so he lay down and fell asleep, and while he slept he lay uncovered in his tent.
7:8 Ham saw his father Noah naked. He went outside and told his two brothers.
7:9 Shem took a garment and got up, he and Japheth. They put the garment on their shoulders with their faces turned back, and they covered their father's nakedness. Their faces were turned back.
7:10 Noah woke from his sleep and knew all that his younger son had done to him. He cursed his son and said, "A curse on Canaan! An owned slave he will be to his brothers."
7:11 He blessed Shem and said, "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Shem! Let Canaan be his slave.
7:12 May the Lord make room for Japheth, and may the Lord live in the dwelling of Shem. Let Canaan be their slave."
## Three Sons, Three Cities
7:13 Ham knew that his father had cursed his younger son, and it displeased him that he had cursed his son. He separated from his father, he and his sons with him: Cush, Mizraim, Put and Canaan.
7:14 He built himself a city and named it after his wife, Neelatamauk.
7:15 Japheth saw it and was jealous of his brother. He too built himself a city and named it after his wife, Adataneses.
7:16 But Shem stayed with his father Noah. He built a city beside his father at the mountain, and he too named it after his wife, Sedeqatelbab.
7:17 These three cities are near Mount Lubar: Sedeqatelbab in front of the mountain on its east, Neelatamauk on its south side, and Adataneses toward the west.
7:18 These are the sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur and Arpachshad, who was born two years after the flood, Aram and Lud.
7:19 The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech and Tiras. These are the sons of Noah.
## Noah's Charge to His Grandsons
7:20 In the twenty-eighth jubilee Noah began to command his grandsons with the ordinances and commandments, all the justice he knew. He testified to his sons that they should do what is right, cover the nakedness of their flesh, bless the one who created them, honor father and mother, love one another, and guard themselves from sexual sin, from uncleanness and from all wrongdoing.
7:21 "It was because of these three things that the flood came on the earth. It came because of the sexual sin of the Watchers. Against the command of their judgments they went after the daughters of humans, and they took wives for themselves from all of them, whomever they chose. They made the beginning of uncleanness.
7:22 They fathered sons, the Naphidim, and none of them were alike. They ate one another. Yarbeh killed the Naphil, the Naphil killed the Elyo, the Elyo killed humankind, and people killed one another.
7:23 Every one sold himself to do wrong and to spill much blood, and the earth was filled with wrongdoing.
7:24 After these came all the others: the wild animals and the birds, whatever moves and whatever walks on the earth. Much blood was spilled on the earth, and every thought and wish of humans thought up emptiness and evil all the time.
7:25 The Lord wiped out everything from the face of the earth because of their deeds and because of the blood they spilled in the middle of the earth.
7:26 We were left, I and you, my children, and everything that came with us into the ark. And now I see how your deeds have begun: you are not walking in righteousness. You have started to walk in the path of ruin. You are splitting off from one another and are jealous of one another, and so, my sons, you are not living in harmony, each with his brother.
7:27 For I see, and look, the demons have begun to lead you and your children astray. Now I am afraid for you, that after my death you will spill human blood on the earth, and you too will be wiped off the face of the earth.
7:28 For everyone who spills human blood, and everyone who eats the blood of any flesh, will all be wiped out from the earth.
7:29 No one who eats blood, or who spills blood on the earth, will be left. No offspring or descendants will be left to him alive under the sky. For they will go into Sheol, they will go down into the place of judgment, and they will all be taken away into the darkness of the deep by a cruel death.
7:30 Let no blood at all be seen on you, from all the blood there is, on all the days when you kill any wild animal, cattle or anything that flies on the earth. Do a good work for yourselves by covering what is spilled on the face of the earth.
7:31 Do not be like someone who eats with the blood. Be firm, so that no one eats blood in front of you. Cover the blood, for this is what I was commanded to testify to you and to your children, with all flesh.
7:32 Do not eat the life with the flesh, so that your blood, the blood of your life, is not demanded from the hand of any flesh that spills it on the earth.
7:33 For the earth will not be clean from the blood that has been spilled on it, for by the blood of the one who spilled it the earth will be made clean in all its generations.
7:34 Now, my children, listen. Do justice and righteousness, so that you may be planted in righteousness over the face of the whole earth, and your honor may be lifted up before my God, who saved me from the waters of the flood.
7:35 Look, you will go and build cities for yourselves, and plant in them every plant that is on the earth, and every tree that bears fruit too.
7:36 For three years its fruit is not to be picked by anyone to eat. In the fourth year its fruit will be made holy, and they are to offer up the first fruit, welcome before the Lord Most High, who made heaven and earth. Let them offer up plenty of the first of the wine and the oil as first fruit on the altar of the Lord that receives it. What is left, the servants of the Lord's house are to eat before the altar that receives it.
7:37 In the fifth year make its release, so that you release it in righteousness and uprightness. Then you will be righteous, and everything you plant will do well.
7:38 For this is what Enoch, your father's father, commanded Methuselah his son, and Methuselah commanded Lamech his son, and Lamech commanded me all that his fathers had commanded him.
7:39 And I too command you, my sons, as Enoch commanded his son in the first jubilees. While he was alive, the seventh in his generation, he commanded and testified to his son and his grandsons until the day of his death."
## Kainam's Find
8:1 In the twenty-ninth jubilee, in the first week, at its beginning, Arpachshad took a wife. Her name was Rasueja, daughter of Susan, daughter of Elam. She bore him a son in the third year of this week, and he named him Kainam.
8:2 The boy grew up, and his father taught him to write. Then he set out to find a place where he could seize a city of his own.
8:3 He found a writing that people of earlier times had carved on a rock. He read what was in it, copied it, and went astray because of it. For in it was the teaching of the Watchers, by which they used to read the omens of the sun, the moon and the stars, and every sign of the sky.
8:4 He wrote it down and said nothing about it, because he was afraid to tell Noah about it, in case Noah would be angry with him over it.
8:5 In the thirtieth jubilee, in the second week, in its first year, he took a wife. Her name was Melka, daughter of Abaday son of Japheth. In its fourth year he fathered a son and named him Shelah, for he said, "I have truly been sent."
8:6 In the fourth year he was born. Shelah grew up and took a wife. Her name was Muak, daughter of Kesed, his father's brother, in the thirtieth jubilee, in the fifth week, in its first year.
8:7 She bore him a son in its fifth year, and he named him Eber. He took a wife, and her name was Azurad, daughter of Nebrod, in the thirty-second jubilee, in the seventh week, in its third year.
8:8 In the sixth year she bore him a son, and he named him Peleg. For in the days when he was born the children of Noah began to divide the earth among themselves. That is why he named him Peleg.
8:9 They divided it among themselves in a wicked way, and they told Noah.
## The Earth Divided by Lot
8:10 At the beginning of the thirty-third jubilee they divided the earth into three portions, for Shem, Ham and Japheth, each by his inheritance. This was in the first year of the first week, while one of us who had been sent was staying with them.
8:11 He called his sons, and they came to him, they and their children. He divided the earth into the lots that his three sons would take. They put out their hands and took the writing from the bosom of their father Noah.
8:12 On the writing Shem's lot came out: the middle of the earth, which he would take as an inheritance for himself and his sons for the generations forever. It runs from the middle of the Rafa mountains, from where the water flows out of the Tina River. His portion goes west through the middle of this river, and goes until it reaches the waters of the deeps, from which this river comes out. This river comes out and pours its waters into the sea of Me'at, and this river goes on to the Great Sea. All that is toward the north belongs to Japheth, and all that is toward the south belongs to Shem.
8:13 It goes until it reaches Karas. This is in the bosom of the tongue that faces south.
8:14 His portion goes toward the Great Sea, and it goes straight until it reaches the west of the tongue that faces south, for this sea is named the Tongue of the Egyptian Sea.
8:15 From there it turns south toward the mouth of the Great Sea, on the shore of the waters. It goes west to Afra, and it goes until it reaches the waters of the Gihon River, and south of the waters of the Gihon, along the banks of this river.
8:16 It runs east until it reaches the Garden of Eden, on the south side of it, to the south. From the east of the whole land of Eden and of all the east, it turns east and goes on until it reaches the east of the mountain called Rafa. Then it goes down to the bank where the Tina River flows out.
8:17 This portion came out by lot for Shem and his sons, to own forever, for his generations forever.
8:18 Noah was glad that this portion came out for Shem and his sons. He remembered everything he had spoken in prophecy with his own mouth. He had said, "Blessed be the Lord, the God of Shem, and may the Lord live in the dwelling of Shem."
8:19 He knew that the Garden of Eden is the Holy of Holies and the dwelling of the Lord, Mount Sinai the center of the desert, and Mount Zion the center of the navel of the earth. These three were created as holy places, facing one another.
8:20 He blessed the God of gods, who had placed the Lord's word in his mouth, and he blessed the Lord forever.
8:21 He knew that a portion of blessing, a blessed one, had come to Shem and his sons for their generations forever. It held the whole land of Eden, the whole land of the Red Sea, the whole land of the east, and India, on the Red Sea and its mountains. It held the whole land of Bashan, the whole land of Lebanon and the islands of Caphtor, all the mountains of Senir and Amana, and the mountains of Asshur in the north. It held the whole land of Elam, Asshur, Babel, Susa and Media, all the mountains of Ararat, and all the region beyond the sea, beyond the mountains of Asshur toward the north. It is a blessed and wide land, and everything in it is very good.
8:22 For Ham the second portion came out, beyond the Gihon toward the south, on the right of the garden. It goes toward the south and goes to all the mountains of fire. It goes west toward the sea of Atel, and goes west until it reaches the sea of Ma'uk, the sea into which everything goes down that is not destroyed.
8:23 It goes out north to the edge of Gadir, and goes out to the coast of the waters of the sea, to the waters of the Great Sea, until it reaches the Gihon River. Then it goes along the Gihon River until it reaches the right side of the Garden of Eden.
8:24 This is the land that came out for Ham as the portion he would own forever, for himself and his sons, for their generations forever.
8:25 For Japheth the third portion came out, beyond the Tina River, to the north of where its waters flow out. It goes toward the northeast, to the whole region of Gog and all that lies east of them.
8:26 It goes north, to the north, and goes toward the mountains of Qelt in the north and toward the sea of Ma'uk. It goes out to the east of Gadir, as far as the edge of the waters of the sea.
8:27 It goes until it reaches the west of Fara, and it turns back toward Aferag, and it goes east toward the waters of the sea of Me'at.
8:28 It goes toward the edge of the Tina River, to the northeast, until it reaches the boundary of its waters toward Mount Rafa, and it turns around toward the north.
8:29 This is the land that came out for Japheth and his sons as the portion of his inheritance, which he would own for himself and his sons for their generations forever: five great islands, and a great land in the north.
8:30 But it is cold, while the land of Ham is hot. The land of Shem is neither hot nor cold, but it is a mix of cold and heat.
## The Grandsons' Portions
9:1 Ham divided his land among his sons. The first portion came out for Cush, toward the east. West of him was the portion of Mizraim, west of him the portion of Put, and west of him the portion of Canaan, and west of him was the sea.
9:2 Shem too divided his land among his sons. The first portion came out for Elam and his sons, east of the Tigris River, until it reaches the east of the whole land of India. It runs along the Red Sea at its edge, and takes in the waters of Dedan, all the mountains of Mebri and Ela, all the land of Susa, and all that lies beside Pharnak, as far as the Red Sea and the Tina River.
9:3 For Asshur the second portion came out: the whole land of Asshur, Nineveh, Shinar and Sak, as far as near India, and it goes up to the Wadafa River.
9:4 For Arpachshad the third portion came out: the whole land of the region of the Chaldeans, east of the Euphrates, near the Red Sea; all the waters of the desert, as far as near the tongue of the sea that faces Egypt; and the whole land of Lebanon, Senir and Amana, as far as near the Euphrates.
9:5 For Aram the fourth portion came out: the whole land of Mesopotamia between the Tigris and the Euphrates, north of the Chaldeans, as far as near the mountains of Asshur and the land of Arara.
9:6 For Lud the fifth portion came out: the mountains of Asshur and all that belongs to them, until it reaches the Great Sea and reaches the east of Asshur his brother.
9:7 Japheth too divided the land of his inheritance among his sons.
9:8 The first portion came out for Gomer, to the east, from the north side as far as the Tina River. In the north, for Magog, came out all the inner parts of the north, until it reaches the sea of Me'at.
9:9 Madai's portion came out for him to hold: from west of his two brothers out to the islands and to the shores of the islands.
9:10 For Javan the fourth portion came out: every island, and the islands that lie toward the border of Lud.
9:11 For Tubal the fifth portion came out: the middle of the tongue that comes near the border of Lud's portion, as far as the second tongue, and the region beyond the second tongue, into the third tongue.
9:12 For Meshech the sixth portion came out: all the region beyond the third tongue, until it reaches the east of Gadir.
9:13 For Tiras the seventh portion came out: four great islands in the middle of the sea, which lie near the portion of Ham. The islands of Kamaturi fell by lot to the sons of Arpachshad, as his inheritance.
## The Oath on the Lots
9:14 So the sons of Noah divided it among their children in the presence of Noah their father. He made them all swear an oath with a curse, cursing anyone who would try to take a portion that had not come out by his lot.
9:15 They all said, "So be it! So be it!" for themselves and for their sons forever, in their generations, until the day of judgment, when the Lord God will judge them with sword and fire for all the unclean evil of their wrongdoing, with which they have filled the earth: guilt, uncleanness, sexual sin and sin.
## Noah's Prayer Against the Spirits
10:1 In the third week of this jubilee the unclean demons began to lead Noah's grandchildren astray, to make fools of them and to destroy them.
10:2 The sons of Noah came to Noah their father and told him about the demons who were leading astray, blinding and killing his grandsons.
10:3 He prayed before the Lord his God and said, "God of the spirits that are in all flesh, you have shown mercy to me and saved me and my sons from the waters of the flood. You did not let me perish as you did the children of ruin. Your kindness to me has been great, and your mercy to my soul has been great. Let your kindness be lifted up over the children of your children. Do not let evil spirits rule over them, so that they will not wipe them out from the earth.
10:4 But bless me and my sons, so that we may grow and multiply and fill the earth.
10:5 You know how your Watchers, the fathers of these spirits, acted in my days. As for these spirits who are alive, shut them up and hold them in the place of judgment. Do not let them bring ruin on the sons of your servant, my God, for they are savage and were created to destroy.
10:6 Do not let them rule over the spirits of the living, for you alone know their judgment. Do not let them have power over the children of the righteous, from now on and forever."
## Mastema and the Tenth of the Spirits
10:7 Our God told us to bind them all.
10:8 Then the chief of the spirits, Mastema, came and said, "Lord, Creator, leave some of them before me. Let them listen to my voice and do everything I tell them. For if some of them are not left to me, I will not be able to carry out the power of my will among humankind. For these are for ruining and leading astray before my judgment, because the evil of humankind is great."
10:9 He said, "Let a tenth of them remain before him, and let nine parts go down into the place of judgment."
10:10 He told one of us to teach Noah all their cures, for he knew that they would not walk in uprightness or strive in righteousness.
10:11 We did just as he had said. All the evil ones who were savage we bound in the place of judgment, and a tenth of them we left to serve before Satan on the earth.
10:12 We told Noah all the cures for their diseases, along with their ways of leading people astray, so that he could heal with the plants of the earth.
10:13 Noah wrote down in a book everything we taught him, every kind of cure, and so the evil spirits were kept from going after the sons of Noah.
10:14 He gave all the books he had written to Shem, his oldest son, for he loved him much more than all his sons.
## Noah's Death
10:15 Noah lay down with his fathers and was buried on Mount Lubar in the land of Ararat.
10:16 He completed 950 years in his life: nineteen jubilees, two weeks of years and five years.
10:17 In his life on earth he went beyond all humankind because of his righteousness, in which he was perfect, all except Enoch. For Enoch's work was created as a testimony to the generations of the world, so that he would report all the deeds of every generation on the day of judgment.
## The City and the Tower
10:18 In the thirty-third jubilee, in the first year of this second week, Peleg married a woman named Lomna, daughter of Sinaor. In the fourth year of this week she bore him a son, and he named him Reu. He said, "Look, humankind has turned evil with a wicked plan: to build themselves a city and a tower in the land of Shinar."
10:19 For they had moved from the land of Ararat eastward, to Shinar. In his days they built the city and the tower, saying, "Let's go up by it into heaven."
10:20 They began to build. In the fourth week they baked brick with fire, and the bricks served them as stone. The clay they used as mortar was asphalt that comes out of the sea and out of the springs of water in the land of Shinar.
10:21 They built it for forty-three years. They kept building it with whole bricks, thirteen in it, and a third of one for its height. Its height rose 5,433 cubits and two spans, and ten stades.
10:22 The Lord our God said to us, "Look, they are one people, and they have begun to act, and now nothing will be held back from them. Come, let us go down and mix up their languages, so that no one will understand the other's speech. They will be scattered into cities and into nations, and one plan will no longer stay among them, until the day of judgment."
10:23 The Lord came down, and we came down with him, to see the city and the tower that humankind had built.
10:24 He mixed up every sound of their languages, and no one understood the speech of another anymore. Then they stopped building the city and the tower.
10:25 For this reason the whole land of Shinar is called Babel, because there God mixed up all the languages of humankind. From there they were scattered into their cities, each by his language and his nation.
10:26 The Lord sent a wind against the tower and threw it down on the earth. It was between Asshur and Babylon in the land of Shinar, and they named it "Overthrow."
10:27 In the fourth week, in its first year, at its beginning, in the thirty-fourth jubilee, they were scattered from the land of Shinar.
## Canaan Takes Shem's Land
10:28 Ham and his sons went into the land he was to own, which he had received as his portion, in the land of the south.
10:29 Canaan saw that the land of Lebanon, as far as the Brook of Egypt, was very good. He did not go into the land of his inheritance, west toward the sea. He settled in the land of Lebanon, east and west, from the border of Lebanon and on the edge of the sea.
10:30 Ham his father, and Cush and Mizraim his brothers, said to him, "You have settled in a land that is not yours, that did not come out to us by lot. Do not do this. For if you do this, you and your children will fall in the land and be cursed through rebellion. For through rebellion you have settled, and through rebellion your children will fall, and you will be rooted out forever.
10:31 Do not settle in the dwelling of Shem, for it came out to Shem and his sons by their lot.
10:32 You are cursed, and you will be cursed more than all the sons of Noah, by the curse we swore to with an oath before the holy judge and before Noah our father."
10:33 But he did not listen to them. He settled in the land of Lebanon, from Hamath to the entrance of Egypt, he and his sons, to this day.
10:34 That is why that land is called the land of Canaan.
## Madai Settles in Media
10:35 Japheth and his sons went toward the sea and settled in the land of their portion. Madai, though, saw the land of the sea, and he did not like it. He begged a share from Elam, Asshur and Arpachshad, his wife's brothers, and he has lived in the land of Medqin, near his wife's brother, to this day.
10:36 He named his dwelling place, and the dwelling place of his sons, Medqin, after the name of Madai their father.
