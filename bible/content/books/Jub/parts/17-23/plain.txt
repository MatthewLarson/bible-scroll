## Isaac weaned, Ishmael sent away
17:1 In the first year of the fifth week of this jubilee, Isaac was weaned. Abraham held a great feast in the third month, on the day his son Isaac was weaned.
17:2 Ishmael, the son of Hagar the Egyptian, was there in his place, in front of his father Abraham. Abraham rejoiced and blessed the Lord, because he had seen children of his own and had not died childless.
17:3 He remembered the word the Lord had spoken to him on the day Lot parted from him. He rejoiced because the Lord had given him offspring on the earth to inherit the earth, and he blessed the Creator of all with all his mouth.
17:4 Sarah saw Ishmael playing and dancing, and Abraham rejoicing with great joy, and she became jealous of Ishmael. She said to Abraham, "Throw out this slave woman and her son. The son of this slave woman will not share the inheritance with my son Isaac."
17:5 The matter troubled Abraham deeply, because of his slave woman and because of his son, that he should drive them away from him.
17:6 The Lord said to Abraham, "Do not let it trouble you because of the boy and because of the slave woman. In everything Sarah has told you, listen to her words and do it, for it is through Isaac that a name and offspring will be named for you.
17:7 But as for the son of this slave woman, I will make him a great nation, because he is your offspring."
17:8 Abraham got up early in the morning. He took bread and a skin of water and put them on the shoulders of Hagar and the boy, and sent her away.
17:9 She went and wandered in the desert of Beersheba. The water in the skin ran out, and the boy grew thirsty. He could not go on, and he fell down.
17:10 His mother took him and threw him down under an olive tree. Then she went and sat down across from him, about a bowshot away, for she said, "I will not watch my child die." As she sat, she wept.
17:11 An angel of God, one of the holy ones, said to her, "Why are you weeping, Hagar? Get up, take the boy and hold him by your hand, for the Lord has heard your voice and has seen the boy."
17:12 She opened her eyes and saw a well of water. She went and filled her skin with water and gave her boy a drink. Then she got up and went toward the desert of Paran.
17:13 The boy grew up and became a bowman. The Lord was with him, and his mother took a wife for him from the daughters of Egypt.
17:14 She bore him a son, and he named him Nebaioth, for she said, "The Lord was near me when I called on him."
## Mastema proposes a test
17:15 In the seventh week, in its first year, in the first month of this jubilee, on the twelfth of that month, there were voices in heaven about Abraham: that he was faithful in everything the Lord told him, that he loved the Lord, and that in every hardship he was faithful.
17:16 Then Prince Mastema came and said before God, "Look, Abraham loves his son Isaac and delights in him more than in anything else. Tell him to offer him up as a burnt offering on the altar. Then you will see whether he will carry out this command, and you will know whether he is faithful in everything you test him with."
17:17 The Lord knew that Abraham was faithful in every hardship he had laid on him. He had tested him with his land and with famine. He had tested him with the wealth of kings. He had tested him again with his wife, when she was taken from him by force, and with circumcision. And he had tested him with Ishmael and with his slave woman Hagar, when he sent them away.
17:18 In everything he was tested with, he was found faithful. His soul did not lose patience, and he was not slow to act, for he was faithful and a lover of the Lord.
## The binding of Isaac
18:1 The Lord said to him, "Abraham, Abraham!" He said, "Here I am."
18:2 He said, "Take your beloved son, the one you love, Isaac. Go to the high land and offer him up on one of the mountains that I will point out to you."
18:3 So he got up early in the morning and saddled his donkey. He took his two young servants with him, and his son Isaac. He split the wood for the burnt offering and went to the place. On the third day he saw the place far off.
18:4 He came to a well of water and said to his young servants, "Stay here with the donkey. The boy and I will go. When we have worshiped, we will come back to you."
18:5 He took the wood for the burnt offering and laid it on his son Isaac, and in his own hand he took the fire and the knife. The two of them went on together to that place.
18:6 Isaac said to his father, "Father!" He said, "Here I am, my son." Isaac said to him, "Here are the fire, the knife and the wood. But where is the lamb for the burnt offering, Father?"
18:7 He said, "The Lord will see to a lamb for the burnt offering himself, my son." And he came near the place of the mountain of the Lord.
18:8 He built an altar and laid the wood on the altar. He bound his son Isaac and laid him on the wood that was on top of the altar. Then he stretched out his hand to take the knife to slaughter his son Isaac.
18:9 I was standing in front of him and in front of Prince Mastema. The Lord said, "Tell him not to lay his hand on the boy and not to do anything to him, for I know that he fears the Lord."
18:10 I called to him from heaven and said to him, "Abraham!" He was terrified and said, "Here I am."
18:11 I said to him, "Do not lay your hand on the boy, and do nothing to him. For now I know that you fear the Lord, and you have not held back your son, your firstborn, from me."
18:12 Prince Mastema was put to shame. Abraham raised his eyes and looked, and there was a single ram, caught by its horns. Abraham went and took the ram and offered it up as a burnt offering in place of his son.
18:13 Abraham named that place "The Lord Has Seen," so that people say, "The Lord has seen." It is Mount Zion.
18:14 The Lord called to Abraham by his name a second time from heaven, just as we appeared in order to speak to him in the name of the Lord.
18:15 He said, "By myself I have sworn, says the Lord: because you have done this thing and have not held back your son, your firstborn whom you love, from me, I will bless you richly and make your offspring very many, like the stars of the sky and like the sand on the seashore. Your offspring will inherit the cities of their enemies.
18:16 All the nations of the earth will be blessed by your offspring, because you have listened to my voice. I have made it known to all that you are faithful to me in everything I have told you. Go in peace."
18:17 Abraham went back to his young servants. They got up and went together to Beersheba, and Abraham lived by the Well of the Oath.
18:18 Every year he kept this festival for seven days with joy. He named it the festival of the Lord, after the seven days in which he went and came back in peace.
18:19 That is how it is ordained and written on the heavenly tablets for Israel and its offspring: they are to keep this festival seven days, with festival joy.
## Sarah's death, the tenth trial
19:1 In the first year of the first week of the forty-second jubilee, Abraham went back and lived across from Hebron, that is, Kiriath-arba, for two weeks of years.
19:2 In the first year of the third week of this jubilee, the days of Sarah's life came to an end, and she died in Hebron.
19:3 Abraham went to weep for her and to bury her. We tested him to see whether his spirit was patient and whether he would complain in the words of his mouth. He was found patient in this too, and he was not upset.
19:4 For with a patient spirit he talked with the sons of Heth, so that they would give him a place to bury his dead.
19:5 The Lord gave him favor with everyone who saw him. He asked the sons of Heth gently, and they gave him the land of the double cave across from Mamre, that is, Hebron, for forty pieces of silver.
19:6 They pleaded with him, saying, "We will give it to you for nothing." But he would not take it from their hands for nothing. He paid the price of the place, the silver in full. He bowed down to them twice, and after this he buried his dead in the double cave.
19:7 All the days of Sarah's life were a hundred and twenty-seven years, that is, two jubilees, four weeks of years and one year. These are the days of the years of Sarah's life.
19:8 This is the tenth trial with which Abraham was tested, and he was found faithful and patient in spirit.
19:9 He did not say a single word about the promise of the land, that the Lord had said he would give it to him and to his offspring after him. He begged for a place there to bury his dead, for he was found faithful, and he was written down on the heavenly tablets as the friend of the Lord.
## Isaac's wife, Abraham's third wife, Jacob and Esau
19:10 In the fourth year of it, he took a wife for his son Isaac. Her name was Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel son of Nahor, Abraham's brother. She was the sister of Laban. Bethuel, who fathered the Bethuelites, was the son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother.
19:11 Abraham took a third wife for himself. Her name was Keturah, one of the children of the servants born in his household, for Hagar had died before Sarah.
19:12 She bore him six sons, Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak and Shuah, in two weeks of years.
19:13 In the sixth week, in its second year, Rebekah bore Isaac two sons, Jacob and Esau. Jacob was blameless and upright, and Esau was a fierce man, a man of the field, and hairy. Jacob lived in tents.
19:14 The young men grew up. Jacob learned to write, but Esau did not learn, because he was a man of the field and a hunter. He learned war, and all his deeds were fierce.
19:15 Abraham loved Jacob, but Isaac loved Esau.
## Abraham commends Jacob to Rebekah
19:16 Abraham saw Esau's deeds, and he understood that it was through Jacob that a name and offspring would be named for him. He called Rebekah and gave her orders about Jacob, because he saw that she loved Jacob far more than Esau.
19:17 He said to her, "My daughter, watch over my son Jacob, / for he will take my place on the earth, / to be a blessing among humankind / and the pride of all the offspring of Shem.
19:18 For I know that the Lord will choose him to be a people of his own, out of all who are on the face of the earth.
19:19 Look, my son Isaac loves Esau more than Jacob, but I see that you truly love Jacob.
19:20 Do him still more good, / and let your eyes rest on him in love, / for he will be a blessing to us on the earth / from now on, through all the generations of the world.
19:21 Let your hands be strong, / and let your heart rejoice in your son Jacob, / for I have loved him far more than all my sons. / He will be blessed forever, / and his offspring will fill the whole earth.
19:22 If anyone can count the sand of the earth, / his offspring too will be counted.
19:23 All the blessings with which the Lord has blessed me and my offspring will belong to Jacob and to his offspring for all time.
19:24 Through his offspring my name will be blessed, and the name of my fathers, Shem, Noah, Enoch, Mahalalel, Enosh, Seth and Adam.
19:25 They will serve to lay the foundations of heaven, / to make the earth firm, / and to renew all the lights that are in the dome of the sky."
19:26 He called Jacob in front of his mother Rebekah. He kissed him and blessed him, and said,
19:27 "Jacob, my beloved son, whom my soul loves, may God bless you from above the dome of the sky. May he give you all the blessings with which he blessed Adam, Enoch, Noah and Shem. All that he spoke of to me, and all that he said he would give me, may he make it cling to you and to your offspring forever, for as long as the days of heaven last above the earth.
19:28 The spirits of Mastema will not rule over you or over your offspring, to turn you away from following the Lord, who is your God from now on and forever.
19:29 May the Lord God be a father to you, and you a firstborn son to him, and a people for all time. Go in peace, my son."
19:30 And the two of them went out together from Abraham.
19:31 Rebekah loved Jacob with all her heart and with all her soul, far more than Esau, but Isaac loved Esau far more than Jacob.
## Abraham's charge to all his sons
20:1 In the forty-second jubilee, in the first year of the seventh week, Abraham called Ishmael and his twelve sons, Isaac and his two sons, and the six sons of Keturah and their sons.
20:2 He commanded them to keep the way of the Lord: to do what is right, for each one to love his neighbor, and to act the same way in every conflict, so that each would deal with the others by doing justice and right on the earth.
20:3 He told them to circumcise their sons, by the covenant he had made with them, and not to turn aside to the right or to the left from any of the paths the Lord commanded us, and that we should keep ourselves from all sexual sin and impurity and put away all sexual sin and impurity from among us.
20:4 "If any woman or girl among you commits sexual sin, burn her with fire. Let no one commit sexual sin with her, following their eyes and their hearts. And let them not take wives for themselves from the daughters of Canaan, for the offspring of Canaan will be rooted out of the land."
20:5 He told them about the judgment of the giants and the judgment of Sodom, how they were judged for their wickedness. Because of sexual sin, impurity and the corruption among them, they died in their sexual sin.
20:6 "Guard yourselves from all sexual sin and impurity, / and from all the pollution of sin, / so that you do not make our name a curse, / and your whole life a thing to gloat over, / and all your children destroyed by the sword, / and you become cursed like Sodom, / and all who are left of you like the people of Gomorrah.
20:7 I beg you, my sons, love the God of heaven, / and hold fast to all his commands. / Do not walk after their idols / or after their impurities.
20:8 Do not make yourselves gods of cast metal or carved images, / for they are empty, / and there is no spirit in them at all. / They are made by hands, / and all who trust in them trust in nothing. / Do not serve them, and do not bow down to them.
20:9 Serve the Most High God, and bow down to him always. / Hope for his face at all times, / and do what is right and upright before him, / so that he may take pleasure in you and give you his mercy, / and send you rain morning and evening, / and bless all the work you have done on the earth. / May he bless your bread and your water, / and bless the fruit of your womb and the fruit of your land, / the herds of your cattle and the flocks of your sheep.
20:10 You will be a blessing on the earth, / and all the nations of the earth will long for you. / They will bless your sons in my name, / so that they may be blessed as I am."
20:11 He gave gifts to Ishmael and his sons and to the sons of Keturah, and he sent them away from his son Isaac. He gave everything to his son Isaac.
20:12 Ishmael and his sons, and the sons of Keturah and their sons, went together and settled from Paran to the entrance of Babylon, in all the land that faces the east, toward the desert.
20:13 They mixed with one another, and they were called Arabs and Ishmaelites.
## Abraham's charge to Isaac
21:1 In the sixth year of the seventh week of this jubilee, Abraham called his son Isaac and commanded him: "I have grown old. I do not know the day I will die, for I am full of my days.
21:2 Look, I am a hundred and seventy-five years old. All the days of my life I have kept the Lord in mind, and I have tried with all my heart to do his will and to walk uprightly in all his ways.
21:3 My soul has hated idols, so that I might keep myself to doing the will of the one who created me.
21:4 For he is the living God. He is holy and faithful, and he is righteous above all. With him there is no favoring of faces and no taking of gifts, for God is righteous and carries out judgment on all who break his commands and despise his covenant.
21:5 And you, my son, keep his commands, his ordinances and his judgments. Do not walk after unclean things, or after carved images, or after cast images.
21:6 Do not eat any blood of wild animal or of cattle, or of any bird that flies in the sky.
## The law of sacrifice
21:7 If you slaughter a sacrifice as a peace offering that will be accepted, slaughter it and pour out its blood on the altar. Offer all the fat of the offering on the altar, with fine flour and the grain offering mixed with oil, along with its drink offering. Offer it all together on the altar of burnt offering, a soothing smell before the Lord.
21:8 Place the fat of the peace offering on the fire that is on the altar, as well as the fat on the belly, all the fat on the inner parts and the two kidneys, all the fat that is on them and on the loins, and the liver, rolled up together with the kidneys.
21:9 Offer all this as a soothing smell that will be accepted before the Lord, with its grain offering and its drink offering, a soothing smell, the food of the offering to the Lord.
21:10 Eat its meat on that day and on the next day. Do not let the sun go down on it on the next day before it is eaten. Let none of it be left over for the third day, for it will not be accepted. It has not been chosen, and it will not be called an offering again. All who eat it bring sin on themselves. For this is what I found written in the book of my forefathers, in the words of Enoch and in the words of Noah.
21:11 Scatter salt on all your offerings. Do not let the covenant of salt be missing from any of your offerings before the Lord.
21:12 As for the wood of the sacrifices, take care which wood you bring onto the altar. It is these: cypress, defran, sagad, pine, fir, cedar, savin, palm, olive, myrtle, laurel, the qedar whose name is arbat, and balsam.
21:13 Of these kinds of wood, lay under the sacrifice on the altar only pieces that have been checked by their look. Do not lay on it any split or dark wood, but wood that is hard and firm, without any flaw, whole and new growth. Do not lay on it old wood, for its fragrance has gone out of it. There is no fragrance in it anymore as there was before.
21:14 Except for these kinds of wood, there is no other that you should place on the altar, for their fragrance is distinct, and the smell of their fragrance goes up to heaven.
21:15 Keep this command and do it, my son, so that you may be upright in all your deeds.
21:16 At all times be clean in your body. Wash yourself with water before you go to make an offering on the altar. Wash your hands and your feet before you come near the altar. And when you have finished making the offering, wash your hands and your feet again.
21:17 Let no blood be seen on you or on your clothes. Be on guard with blood, my son, be very much on guard. Cover it with dirt.
21:18 Do not eat blood, for blood is the life. Do not eat any blood at all.
## Blood for blood
21:19 Do not take gifts for any human blood, so that it is not shed freely because of you, without judgment. For it is blood that is shed that makes the earth sin, and the earth cannot be made clean of human blood except by the blood of the one who shed it.
21:20 Do not take a payment or a gift for human blood. Blood for blood: that is what will be accepted before the Most High God. Keeping this will be for good, so that you are kept from every evil, and he saves you from every kind of death.
21:21 I see, my son, / that all the deeds of humankind are sin and evil, / and all their works are impurity, abomination and pollution, / and there is no righteousness with them.
21:22 Be careful not to walk in their ways, / or to step in their paths, / and to sin a sin that leads to death before the Most High God. / Then he will hide his face from you, / and hand you back to your own wrongdoing, / and root you out of the land, / and your offspring from under heaven, / and your name and your offspring will perish from all the earth.
21:23 Turn away from all their deeds and from all their impurity. / Keep what the Most High God requires, / do his will, and be upright in everything.
21:24 Then he will bless you in all your deeds, / and raise up from you the plant of righteousness through all the earth, / through all the generations of the earth, / and my name and your name will not be forgotten under heaven for all days.
21:25 Go in peace, my son. / May the Most High God, my God and your God, make you strong to do his will. / May he bless all your offspring, and the rest of your offspring, for the generations forever, / with every righteous blessing, / so that you may be a blessing in all the earth."
21:26 And Isaac went out from him rejoicing.
## The last Feast of Weeks
22:1 In the first week of the forty-fourth jubilee, in the second year, the year in which Abraham died, Isaac and Ishmael came from the Well of the Oath to their father Abraham, to keep the Feast of Weeks, which is the feast of the first fruits of the harvest. Abraham rejoiced, because both his sons had come.
22:2 For Isaac had many possessions at Beersheba. Isaac would go and look over his possessions and then come back to his father.
22:3 In those days Ishmael came to see his father, and they all came together. Isaac slaughtered a sacrifice as a burnt offering and offered it on his father's altar that he had made in Hebron.
22:4 He sacrificed a peace offering and held a feast of joy in front of his brother Ishmael. Rebekah made new cakes from the new grain and gave them to her son Jacob to bring to his grandfather Abraham, from the first fruits of the land, so that he might eat and bless the Creator of all before he died.
22:5 Isaac too sent by Jacob's hand to Abraham a fine peace offering, so that he might eat and drink.
## Abraham's thanks and prayer
22:6 He ate and drank, and he blessed the Most High God, / who created heaven and earth, / who made all the rich things of the earth / and gave them to humankind / to eat and drink and bless their Creator.
22:7 "Now I give you thanks, my God, because you have let me see this day. Look, I am a hundred and seventy-five years old, an old man full of days, and all my days have been peace for me.
22:8 The sword of the enemy has not overcome me in anything you have given me or my children, all the days of my life until this day.
22:9 May your mercy and your peace be on your servant and on the offspring of his sons, so that they may be your chosen people and your inheritance out of all the nations of the earth, from now on, for all the days of the generations of the earth, for all the ages."
## Abraham blesses Jacob
22:10 He called Jacob and said, "My son Jacob, may the God of all bless you and make you strong to do what is right and his will before him. May he choose you and your offspring, so that you become a people for his inheritance, by his will, for all time. And you, my son Jacob, come near and kiss me."
22:11 He came near and kissed him, and he said, "Blessed be my son Jacob, / and all his sons, to the Lord Most High for all the ages. / May the Lord give you offspring of righteousness, / and may he make some of your sons holy in the middle of the whole earth. / May nations serve you, / and may all the nations bow down before your offspring.
22:12 Be strong in front of people, / and rule over all the offspring of Seth. / Then your ways and the ways of your sons will be right, / so that they become a holy people.
22:13 May the Most High God give you all the blessings / with which he blessed me, / and with which he blessed Noah and Adam. / May they rest on the holy head of your offspring / through all generations and forever.
22:14 May he cleanse you from all the filth of impurity, / so that you may be forgiven every wrong you did without knowing it. / May he make you strong and bless you, / and may you inherit the whole earth.
22:15 May he renew his covenant with you, / so that you may be a people for his inheritance for all the ages, / and may he be God to you and to your offspring in truth and in righteousness / for all the days of the earth.
## Keep apart from the nations
22:16 And you, my son Jacob, remember my words, / and keep the commands of your father Abraham. / Separate yourself from the nations, / and do not eat with them. / Do not do as they do, / and do not become their companion, / for their deeds are impure, / and all their ways are polluted, abominable and foul.
22:17 They offer their sacrifices to the dead, / and they bow down to demons. / They eat among the graves, / and all their deeds are empty and worthless.
22:18 They have no heart to understand, / and their eyes do not see / what their deeds are, / and how they go astray, saying to wood, 'You are my God,' / and to a stone, 'You are my Lord, and you are my rescuer.' / They have no heart.
22:19 And you, my son Jacob, / may the Most High God help you, / and the God of heaven bless you, / and take you far from their impurity and from all their error.
22:20 Be careful, my son Jacob, not to take a wife from any of the offspring of the daughters of Canaan, / for all his offspring is to be rooted out of the earth.
22:21 For because of Ham's wrong, Canaan went astray, / and all his offspring will be wiped out from the earth, and all that is left of him, / and no one born from him will be saved on the day of judgment.
22:22 As for all who serve idols, and the hateful ones, / they have no hope at all in the land of the living, / for they will go down to Sheol, / and into the place of judgment they will go. / They will have no memory left on the earth. / As the people of Sodom were taken from the earth, / so all who serve idols will be taken away.
22:23 Do not be afraid, my son Jacob, / and do not be shaken, son of Abraham. / May the Most High God keep you from destruction, / and from all the paths of error may he rescue you.
22:24 This house I have built for myself, to put my name on it on the earth. It is given to you and to your offspring forever, and it will be called the house of Abraham. It is given to you and to your offspring forever, for you will build my house and set up my name before God forever. Your offspring and your name will stand through all the generations of the earth."
22:25 He finished commanding him and blessing him.
## The last night
22:26 The two of them lay down together on one bed. Jacob slept in the arms of Abraham, his father's father. Abraham kissed him seven times, and his love and his heart rejoiced over him.
22:27 He blessed him with all his heart and said, "It is the Most High God, the God of all and Creator of all, who brought me out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give me this land to inherit forever, so that I might raise up a holy offspring, to be blessed forever."
22:28 He blessed Jacob: "My son, over whom I rejoice with all my heart and all my love: Lord, may your kindness and your mercy be lifted over him and over his offspring for all time.
22:29 Do not leave him or treat him as nothing, from now until the days of eternity. May your eyes be open over him and over his offspring, to keep him, to bless him and to make him holy as a people for your inheritance.
22:30 Bless him with all your blessings, from now until all the days of eternity. Renew your covenant and your kindness with him and with his offspring, by all your will, through all the generations of the earth."
## The death of Abraham
23:1 He laid two of Jacob's fingers on his eyes, and he blessed the God of gods. He covered his face, stretched out his feet and slept the sleep of eternity, and he was gathered to his fathers.
23:2 Through all this Jacob was lying in his arms, and he did not know that Abraham, his father's father, was dead.
23:3 Jacob woke from his sleep, and there was Abraham, cold as ice. He said, "Father, father!" But no one answered, and he knew that he was dead.
23:4 He got up from his arms and ran and told his mother Rebekah. Rebekah went to Isaac in the night and told him. They went together, and Jacob with them, with a lamp in his hand. When they went in, they found Abraham lying dead.
23:5 Isaac fell on his father's face. He wept and kissed him.
23:6 Voices were heard in Abraham's house. His son Ishmael got up and went to his father Abraham, and he wept for his father Abraham, he and all Abraham's household. They wept with great weeping.
23:7 His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the double cave, near his wife Sarah. All the men of his house wept for him forty days, Isaac and Ishmael and all their sons, and all the sons of Keturah in their own places. Then the days of weeping for Abraham came to an end.
23:8 He lived three jubilees and four weeks of years, a hundred and seventy-five years, and he completed the days of his life, old and full of days.
## Shrinking lives
23:9 For the days of the ancients, the days of their lives, were nineteen jubilees. After the Flood they began to fall short of nineteen jubilees, to grow fewer in jubilees, to grow old quickly and to come to the end of their days, because of much trouble and the evil of their ways, except for Abraham.
23:10 For Abraham was perfect in all his deeds with the Lord, and pleasing in righteousness all the days of his life. Yet even he did not complete four jubilees in his life before he grew old in the face of evil and came to the end of his days.
23:11 All the generations that arise from now until the day of the great judgment will grow old quickly, before they complete two jubilees. Their knowledge will leave them because of their old age, and all their knowledge will vanish.
23:12 In those days, if a man lives a jubilee and a half of years, they will say about him, "He has lived long, but most of his days have been pain, sorrow and hardship, and there is no peace.
23:13 For disaster comes on disaster, wound on wound, hardship on hardship, bad news on bad news, sickness on sickness, and every evil judgment like these, one after another: sickness and ruin, snow, hail and frost, fever, chills and numbness, famine, death, sword and captivity, and every disaster and pain."
23:14 All this will come on an evil generation that makes the earth sin with the impurity of sexual sin, with pollution, and with their abominable deeds.
23:15 Then they will say, "The days of the ancients were many, as many as a thousand years, and good. But look, the days of our life, if a man lives long, are seventy years, and if he is strong, eighty years, and all of them evil. There is no peace at all in the days of this evil generation."
## The young turn against the old
23:16 In that generation the sons will accuse their fathers and their elders of sin and injustice, of the words of their mouths, and of the great evils they do, and of abandoning the covenant the Lord made between them and himself, that they should keep and do all his commands, his ordinances and all his laws, without turning aside to the left or to the right.
23:17 For all of them have done evil, and every mouth speaks wrong. All their deeds are impurity and abomination, and all their ways are pollution, impurity and ruin.
23:18 Look, the earth will perish because of all their deeds. There will be no seed of the vine and no oil, for all their deeds are faithless. All will perish together, wild animals, cattle and birds, and all the fish of the sea, because of humankind.
23:19 They will quarrel with one another, the young with the old and the old with the young, the poor with the rich, the lowly with the great, and the beggar with the ruler, over the law and the covenant. For they have forgotten command and covenant, feast and month, Sabbath and jubilee, and every judgment.
23:20 They will rise up with swords and with fighting to bring them back to the way. But they will not turn back until much blood has been shed on the earth, one by another.
23:21 Those who escape will not turn back from their wickedness to the way of righteousness. They will all lift themselves up to cheat and to get rich, so that each may take all that belongs to his neighbor. They will name the great name, but not in truth and not in righteousness. They will defile the holiest place with their impurity and the corruption of their pollution.
## The nations punish, the children return
23:22 A great punishment will come from the Lord on the deeds of this generation. He will hand them over to the sword and to judgment, to captivity, to plunder and to being devoured.
23:23 He will stir up against them the sinners of the nations, who have no mercy or kindness for them, and who will show favor to no one, old or young or anyone, for they are evil and strong, doing more evil than all humankind. They will bring turmoil on Israel and wrong on Jacob. Much blood will be shed on the earth, and there will be no one to gather the dead and no one to bury them.
23:24 In those days they will cry out and call and pray to be saved from the hand of the sinners, the nations, but there will be no one to save them.
23:25 The heads of children will turn white with gray hair. A child of three weeks of years will look old, like someone whose years are a hundred. Their bodies will be stunted by hardship and oppression.
23:26 In those days the children will begin to study the laws, to seek the command and to return to the path of righteousness.
23:27 The days will begin to grow many and long, and so will humankind, generation after generation and day after day, until their days come near a thousand years, to more years than the days had been before.
23:28 There will be no old man, and no one who has had his fill of days, for all of them will be infants and children.
23:29 They will complete all their days and live in peace and in joy. There will be no satan and no evil one to destroy, for all their days will be days of blessing and healing.
23:30 Then the Lord will heal his servants. They will rise up and see great peace, and drive out their enemies. The righteous will see and give thanks, and rejoice with joy forever and ever. They will see all their judgments and all their curses fall on their enemies.
23:31 Their bones will rest in the earth, and their spirits will have much joy. They will know that it is the Lord who carries out judgment and shows mercy to hundreds and to thousands, and to all who love him.
23:32 And you, Moses, write down these words, for this is how they are written, and they are entered in the testimony of the heavenly tablets for all generations forever.
