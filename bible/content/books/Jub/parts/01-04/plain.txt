## Prologue
1:0 This is the account of the division of the days of the law and of the testimony, of the events of the years, by their weeks of years and their jubilees, through all the years of the world. It is what the Lord told Moses on Mount Sinai, when he went up to receive the stone tablets of the law and the command at the Lord's word, as he had told him to go up to the top of the mountain.
## Moses goes up Mount Sinai
1:1 In the first year after the Israelites went out of Egypt, in the third month, on the sixteenth day of the month, the Lord spoke to Moses. He said, "Come up to me on the mountain, and I will give you two stone tablets of the law and the command, which I have written for you to teach them."
1:2 Moses went up the mountain of the Lord. The glory of the Lord settled on Mount Sinai, and a cloud shaded it for six days.
1:3 On the seventh day he called to Moses out of the middle of the cloud, and the glory of the Lord looked like a blazing fire on the top of the mountain.
1:4 Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights. The Lord made known to him what came first and what is still to come, the account of the division of all the days, of the law and of the testimony.
## Israel will turn away
1:5 He said to him, "Pay close attention to every word I speak to you on this mountain, and write it in a book. Then their descendants will see that I have not abandoned them, for all the evil they have done in straying from the ordinance that I am setting up today between me and you for their generations on Mount Sinai.
1:6 So when all these things come upon them, they will know that I have been more in the right than they in all their judgments and in all their deeds. They will know that I have truly been with them.
1:7 "Now write down for yourself all these words that I am making known to you today. For I know how rebellious and stiff-necked they are, even before I bring them into the land I swore to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, saying, 'I will give your offspring a land flowing with milk and honey.' They will eat and be full.
1:8 Then they will turn to a foreign god, to gods that cannot save them from any of their trouble. And this testimony will be heard as a witness.
1:9 For they will forget all my commandments, everything I command them. They will follow the nations, their uncleanness and their shame, and they will serve their gods. Those gods will become a stumbling block for them, a trouble, a misery and a trap.
1:10 "Many will die. They will be taken captive and fall into the enemy's hands, because they have abandoned my ordinances and my commands, the feasts of my covenant, my Sabbaths, and the holy things I set apart for myself among them: my tent and my sanctuary, which I set apart for myself in the middle of the land, to put my name on it, so that it would live there.
1:11 They will make themselves high places, sacred groves and carved idols. Each will bow down to his own and go astray. They will sacrifice their children to demons and to all the works of their hearts' error.
1:12 I will send them witnesses to testify to them, but they will not listen. They will kill the witnesses and hunt down those who search out the law. They will tear down everything and set about doing evil in my sight.
1:13 I will hide my face from them and hand them over to the nations, to be taken captive, plundered and devoured. I will remove them from the land and scatter them among the nations.
1:14 They will forget all my law, all my commands and all my judgments. They will go wrong about the start of the month and about Sabbaths, feasts, jubilees and ordinances.
## Israel will come back
1:15 "After that they will turn back to me from among the nations with all their heart, all their soul and all their strength. I will gather them from among all the nations. They will seek me, and I will let them find me, when they seek me with all their heart and all their soul. I will show them abundant peace with righteousness.
1:16 I will transplant them as a plant of uprightness, with all my heart and all my soul. They will be a blessing and not a curse. They will be the head and not the tail.
1:17 I will build my sanctuary among them and live with them. I will be their God, and they will be my people in truth and righteousness.
1:18 I will not abandon them or turn away from them, for I am the Lord their God."
## Moses prays for his people
1:19 Moses fell on his face and prayed. He said, "Lord my God, do not abandon your people and your inheritance to wander in the error of their hearts. Do not hand them over to the nations, to rule over them and make them sin against you.
1:20 Lord, let your mercy rise over your people. Create an upright spirit in them. Do not let the spirit of Beliar rule them, to accuse them before you and to trap them away from all the paths of righteousness, so that they perish from your presence.
1:21 They are your people and your inheritance, whom you rescued by your great power from the hand of Egypt. Create a clean heart and a holy spirit in them. Do not let them be trapped in their sins from now on and forever."
## The Lord's answer
1:22 The Lord said to Moses, "I know how contrary they are. I know their thoughts and their stiff necks. They will not listen until they confess their own sin and the sin of their fathers.
1:23 After this they will turn back to me in all uprightness, with all their heart and all their soul. I will circumcise the foreskin of their hearts and the foreskin of their descendants' hearts. I will create a holy spirit in them and make them clean, so that they will not turn away from following me, from that day on and forever.
1:24 Their souls will cling to me and to all my commands, and they will keep my commands. I will be their father, and they will be my children.
1:25 All of them will be called children of the living God. Every angel and every spirit will know them. They will know that these are my children, that I am their father in uprightness and righteousness, and that I love them.
## The angel is told to write
1:26 "Write down for yourself all these words that I am making known to you on this mountain: what came first, what comes last, and what will happen in all the divisions of the days in the law and in the testimony, by their weeks and their jubilees, forever, until I come down and live with them for all ages to come."
1:27 Then he said to the angel of the presence, "Write for Moses from the first creation until my sanctuary is built among them for all ages to come.
1:28 The Lord will appear before the eyes of all. Everyone will know that I am the God of Israel, the father of all Jacob's children, and King on Mount Zion for all ages to come. Zion and Jerusalem will be holy."
1:29 Then the angel of the presence, who goes in front of the camps of Israel, took the tablets of the division of the years from the time of creation, of the law and of the testimony, by their weeks and their jubilees, year by year in their full number. They run from the day of the new creation, when the heavens and the earth and all their creatures will be renewed by the powers of heaven and by all the creation of the earth, until the sanctuary of the Lord is created in Jerusalem on Mount Zion. All the lights will be renewed for healing, for peace and for blessing for all the chosen of Israel, so that it may be this way from that day on, for all the days of the earth.
## The works of the first day
2:1 The angel of the presence spoke to Moses at the Lord's word, saying: Write the whole account of creation, how in six days the Lord God finished all his work and everything he created, and kept Sabbath on the seventh day. He made it holy for all ages and set it as a sign for all his works.
2:2 For on the first day he created the heavens above, the earth, the waters and all the spirits who serve before him. These are the angels of the presence, the angels of holiness, the angels of the spirit of fire, the angels of the spirit of the winds, and the angels of the spirit of the clouds, of darkness and of all things, of hail and of frost; the angels of the voices, of the thunders and of the lightnings; the angels of the spirits of cold and heat, of winter, spring, autumn and summer; and the angels of all the spirits of his works in the heavens, on the earth and in all the depths. He also made darkness and light, dawn and evening, which he prepared in the knowledge of his heart.
2:3 Then we saw his works, and we blessed him and praised him for all his works. For he made seven great works on the first day.
## The second, third and fourth days
2:4 On the second day he made the dome in the middle of the waters. On that day the waters were divided: half went up above it, and half went down below the dome that is in the middle, over the face of the whole earth. This was the only work he made on the second day.
2:5 On the third day he acted as he had spoken. He told the waters to pass from the face of the whole earth into one place, and the dry land to appear.
2:6 The waters did as he told them. They drew back from the face of the earth into one place outside this dome, and the dry land appeared.
2:7 On that day he created for it all the seas, each in its own gathering place, all the rivers, the gatherings of water in the mountains and over all the earth, all the lakes, and all the dew of the earth. He made the seed that is sown for its seed, everything that sprouts, the trees that bear fruit, the forests, and the garden of Eden, in Eden, for delight and for all. These four great kinds he made on the third day.
2:8 On the fourth day the Lord made the sun, the moon and the stars. He set them in the dome of the sky to shine over the whole earth, to rule the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness.
2:9 The Lord made the sun a great sign on the earth for days, for Sabbaths, for months, for feasts, for years, for Sabbaths of years, for jubilees and for all the seasons of the years.
2:10 It divides the light from the darkness, and it is for flourishing, so that everything that sprouts and grows on the earth may flourish. These three kinds he made on the fourth day.
## The fifth and sixth days
2:11 On the fifth day he created great sea monsters in the depths of the waters, for these were the first creatures of flesh made by his hands: the fish and everything that moves in the waters, and everything that flies, the birds and all their kinds.
2:12 The sun rose over them to make them thrive, and over everything on the earth: all that sprouts from the ground, all the trees that bear fruit, and all flesh. These three kinds he made on the fifth day.
2:13 On the sixth day he made all the wild animals of the earth, all the herd animals, and everything that moves on the earth.
2:14 After all this he made one human being: he made them a man and a woman. He gave him rule over everything on the earth and in the seas, over everything that flies, over the wild animals and the herd animals, over everything that moves on the earth, and over the whole earth. Over all this he gave him rule. These four kinds he made on the sixth day.
2:15 In all there were twenty-two kinds.
2:16 He finished all his work on the sixth day: everything in the heavens and on the earth, in the seas and in the depths, in the light and in the darkness, and in everything.
## The great sign of the Sabbath
2:17 He gave us a great sign, the Sabbath day, so that we would work six days and keep Sabbath on the seventh day from all work.
2:18 He told all of us, the angels of the presence and the angels of holiness, these two great kinds, to keep Sabbath with him in heaven and on earth.
2:19 He said to us, "Look, I will set apart a people for myself from among my nations, and they will keep Sabbath. I will make them holy as my own people, and I will bless them. Just as I have made the Sabbath day holy and keep it holy for myself, so I will bless them. They will be my people, and I will be their God.
2:20 I have chosen the descendants of Jacob from among all that I have seen. I have written him down as my firstborn son and made him holy to myself for all ages to come. I will teach them the Sabbath day, so that they keep Sabbath on it from all work."
2:21 So he created in it a sign by which they would keep Sabbath with us on the seventh day: to eat and drink and bless the one who created everything, just as he blessed and made holy for himself a people who stand out from all nations, and so that they would keep Sabbath together with us.
2:22 He made his desires rise as a pleasing fragrance that is accepted before him all the days.
2:23 There were twenty-two heads of humankind from Adam down to him, and twenty-two kinds of work were made before the seventh day. This one is blessed and holy, and that one is blessed and holy too. The one together with the other came to be for holiness and for blessing.
2:24 To this one it was given that they would always be the blessed and holy ones of the first testimony and law, just as the seventh day was made holy and blessed.
## The law of the Sabbath
2:25 He created heaven and earth and everything that was created in six days. The Lord gave a holy festival day for all his works. That is why he commanded about it that anyone who does any work on it is to die, and whoever defiles it must surely die.
2:26 Now you, command the Israelites to keep this day, to keep it holy, not to do any work on it, and not to defile it, for it is holier than all other days.
2:27 Anyone who profanes it must surely die, and anyone who does any work on it must surely die forever. So the Israelites are to keep this day through their generations and not be uprooted from the land, for it is a holy day and a blessed day.
2:28 Every person who keeps it and keeps Sabbath on it from all his work will be holy and blessed through all the days, just like us.
2:29 Make known and tell the Israelites the law of this day: that they are to keep Sabbath on it and not abandon it in the error of their hearts; that it is not right to do work on it, to do on it whatever they please; that they are not to prepare on it anything to eat or drink, or draw water, or carry in through their gates any load that they did not prepare for themselves on the sixth day in their homes.
2:30 They are not to carry anything out or in from house to house on this day, for it is holier and more blessed than any day of the jubilee of jubilees. We kept Sabbath on it in the heavens before it was made known to any flesh to keep Sabbath on it on the earth.
2:31 The Creator of all gave his blessing. But he did not make all peoples and nations holy to keep Sabbath on it, only Israel. To them alone he granted to eat and drink and keep Sabbath on it on the earth.
2:32 The Creator of all, who created this day, blessed it for blessing, for holiness and for glory above all days.
2:33 This law and testimony was given to the Israelites as a law forever, for their generations.
## Adam names the animals
3:1 During the six days of the second week, at the Lord's word, we brought to Adam all the wild animals, all the herd animals, all the birds, everything that moves on the earth and everything that moves in the water, each by its kind and each by its form: the wild animals on the first day, the herd animals on the second day, the birds on the third day, everything that moves on the earth on the fourth day, and what moves in the water on the fifth day.
3:2 Adam named them all, each by its name, and whatever he called them, that became its name.
3:3 During these five days Adam was seeing all of them, male and female of every kind on the earth. But he was alone, and he found no one to help him who was like him.
3:4 The Lord said to us, "It is not good for the man to be by himself. Let us make him a helper who is his match."
## The woman is made
3:5 The Lord our God made a deep sleep fall on him, and he slept. He took one bone from among his bones for the woman, and this rib was what the woman was created from, out of his bones. He built up flesh in its place, and he built the woman.
3:6 He woke Adam from his sleep, and when he woke, he got up on the sixth day. He brought him to her, and he knew her and said to her, "This one at last is bone from my bones and flesh from my flesh. This one will be called my wife, because she was taken from her husband."
3:7 That is why a man and his wife will be one, and why a man leaves his father and his mother and sticks close to his wife, and they become one flesh.
## The law for mothers after a birth
3:8 In the first week Adam was created, and the rib, his wife. In the second week he showed her to him. That is why the command was given to keep seven days for a male and two times seven days for a female in their impurity.
3:9 After Adam had completed forty days in the land where he was created, we brought him into the garden of Eden to work it and to guard it. His wife they brought in on the eightieth day. After this she entered the garden of Eden.
3:10 That is why the command is written on the heavenly tablets for a woman who gives birth: if she bears a male, she is to stay in her impurity seven days, like the first seven days. Then for thirty-three days she is to stay in the blood of her purifying. She must not touch anything holy or come into the sanctuary until she completes these days for a male.
3:11 For a female, she is to stay two weeks of days in her impurity, like the first two weeks, and sixty-six days in the blood of her purifying. In all they will be eighty days.
3:12 When she had completed these eighty days, we brought her into the garden of Eden, for it is holier than all the earth, and every tree planted in it is holy.
3:13 That is why the rule of those days was ordained for a woman who gives birth to a male or a female: she must not touch anything holy or come into the sanctuary until these days for a male or a female are completed.
3:14 This is the law and testimony that was written for Israel, to keep for all time.
## Seven years in the garden
3:15 In the first week of the first jubilee, Adam and his wife were in the garden of Eden seven years, working it and guarding it. We gave him work, and we taught him to do everything that farming needs.
3:16 He worked while he was naked, and he did not know it and was not ashamed. He guarded the garden from the birds, the wild animals and the herd animals. He gathered its fruit and ate, and he put away the rest for himself and his wife. He put away what was being kept.
## The snake and the fruit
3:17 When the seven years he spent there were over, exactly seven years, in the second month, on the seventeenth day, the snake came and approached the woman. The snake said to the woman, "Did the Lord command you, 'You must not eat from any fruit of the trees in the garden'?"
3:18 She said to it, "From all the fruit of the trees in the garden the Lord told us, 'Eat.' But about the fruit of the tree in the middle of the garden he told us, 'Do not eat from it, and do not touch it, so that you will not die.'"
3:19 The snake said to the woman, "You will not die, not you! For the Lord knows that on the day you eat, your eyes will be opened, and you will be like gods, and you will know good and evil."
3:20 The woman saw that the tree was pleasant and delightful to the eye, and that its fruit was good to eat. She took some and ate.
3:21 She first covered her nakedness with fig leaves. Then she gave some to Adam, and he ate. His eyes were opened, and he saw that he was naked.
3:22 He took fig leaves and sewed them together, made himself a loincloth, and covered his nakedness.
## The curses
3:23 The Lord cursed the snake and was angry with it forever. He was angry with the woman too, because she listened to the snake's voice and ate. He said to her,
3:24 "I will make your sorrow and your pain very great. In sorrow you will give birth to children. Your return will be to your husband, and he will rule over you."
3:25 And to Adam he said, "Because you listened to your wife and ate from the tree that I commanded you not to eat from, the ground is cursed on your account. It will sprout thorns and thistles for you, and you will eat your bread by the sweat of your face, until you go back to the ground you were taken from. For you are earth, and to earth you will go back."
3:26 He made clothes of skin for them, dressed them, and sent them out of the garden of Eden.
## Outside the garden
3:27 On that day, as Adam went out of the garden of Eden, he burned incense as a pleasing fragrance: frankincense, galbanum, stacte and spices, in the morning at sunrise, once he had covered his nakedness.
3:28 On that day the mouths of all the wild animals, the herd animals, the birds, everything that walks and everything that moves were shut, so that they could not speak. For all of them used to talk with one another in one speech and one language.
3:29 He sent out of the garden of Eden all flesh that was in the garden of Eden. All flesh was scattered, each by its kind and each by its nature, into the places created for them.
3:30 To Adam alone, out of all the wild animals and herd animals, he gave a way to cover his nakedness.
3:31 That is why it is commanded on the tablets, for all who know the judgment of the law, that they cover their nakedness and not uncover themselves as the nations uncover themselves.
3:32 On the first day of the fourth month Adam and his wife went out of the garden of Eden. They lived in the land of Elda, in the land where they were created.
3:33 Adam named his wife Eve.
3:34 They had no son until the first jubilee. After this he knew her.
3:35 He worked the land as he had been taught in the garden of Eden.
## Cain and Abel
4:1 In the third week of the second jubilee she gave birth to Cain, in the fourth she gave birth to Abel, and in the fifth she gave birth to his daughter Awan.
4:2 In the first year of the third jubilee, Cain killed Abel, because God accepted Abel's offering from his hand but did not accept Cain's sacrifice.
4:3 He killed him in the field, and his blood cried out from the ground to heaven, complaining that he had been murdered.
4:4 The Lord rebuked Cain because of Abel, because he had killed him. He made him a wanderer on the earth because of his brother's blood, and he cursed him on the earth.
4:5 That is why it is written on the heavenly tablets: "Cursed is anyone who strikes his neighbor by treachery." And all who saw it are to say, "So be it." And anyone who saw it and did not report it is to be cursed like him.
4:6 That is why we, when we come before the Lord our God, report all the sin that is done in heaven and on earth, in the light and in the darkness, and everywhere.
## Seth and the children of Adam
4:7 Adam and his wife mourned for Abel four weeks of years. In the fourth year of the fifth week they became happy again. Adam knew his wife again, and she bore him a son, and he named him Seth. For he said, "The Lord has raised up for us another offspring on the earth in place of Abel, because Cain killed him."
4:8 In the sixth week he fathered his daughter Azura.
4:9 Cain took his sister Awan as his wife, and she bore him Enoch at the end of the fourth jubilee. In the first year of the first week of the fifth jubilee, houses were built on the earth. Cain built a city and named it after his son Enoch.
4:10 Adam knew his wife Eve, and she bore nine more sons.
4:11 In the fifth week of the fifth jubilee Seth took his sister Azura as his wife, and in the fourth year she bore him Enosh.
4:12 He was the first to call on the name of the Lord on the earth.
4:13 In the third week of the seventh jubilee Enosh took his sister Noam as his wife. She bore him a son in the third year of the fifth week, and he named him Kenan.
4:14 At the end of the eighth jubilee Kenan took his sister Mualeleth as his wife. She bore him a son in the ninth jubilee, in the first week, in the third year of that week, and he named him Mahalalel.
## The Watchers come down
4:15 In the second week of the tenth jubilee Mahalalel took as his wife Dinah, the daughter of Barakiel, a daughter of his father's sister. She bore him a son in the third week, in its sixth year, and he named him Jared, because in his days the angels of the Lord came down to the earth. They are called the Watchers. They came to teach humankind and to do justice and uprightness on the earth.
4:16 In the eleventh jubilee Jared took a wife. Her name was Baraka, the daughter of Rasuyal, a daughter of his father's sister. This was in the fourth week of that jubilee. She bore him a son in the fifth week, in the fourth year of the jubilee, and he named him Enoch.
## Enoch, the first to write
4:17 He was the first of humankind, of those born on the earth, to learn writing, knowledge and wisdom. He wrote down the signs of the sky in a book, by the order of their months, so that people would know the seasons of the years by their order, month by month.
4:18 He was the first to write a testimony. He testified to humankind among the generations of the earth. He told the weeks of the jubilees, made known the days of the years, set the months in order, and told the Sabbaths of the years, as we had made them known to him.
4:19 In a vision while he slept, he saw what has been and what will be, how it will go for humankind through their generations until the day of judgment. He saw and understood everything. He wrote it as a testimony and placed it on the earth for all humankind and for their generations.
4:20 In the seventh week of the twelfth jubilee he took a wife. Her name was Edni, the daughter of Danel, a daughter of his father's sister. In the sixth year of that week she bore him a son, and he named him Methuselah.
4:21 He was also with the angels of God for six jubilees of years. They showed him everything on the earth and in the heavens, the rule of the sun, and he wrote it all down.
4:22 He testified to the Watchers who had sinned with the daughters of men, for they had begun to unite with the daughters of the earth and so become defiled. Enoch testified against all of them.
4:23 He was taken from among humankind, and we led him into the garden of Eden in greatness and honor. And there he is, writing down the judgment and condemnation of the world and all the wickedness of humankind.
4:24 Because of him the waters of the Flood did not come on any of the land of Eden. For he was set there as a sign, to testify against all humankind and to tell all the deeds of each generation until the day of judgment.
4:25 He burned the evening incense of the sanctuary, which is acceptable before the Lord, on the Mountain of the South.
4:26 For the Lord has four places on the earth: the garden of Eden, the Mountain of the East, this mountain where you are today, Mount Sinai, and Mount Zion. It will be made holy in the new creation, to make the earth holy. Through it the earth will be made holy from all its guilt and its uncleanness through the generations of the world.
## From Methuselah to Noah
4:27 In the fourteenth jubilee Methuselah took as his wife Edna, the daughter of Ezrael, a daughter of his father's sister. This was in the third week, in the first year of that week. He fathered a son and named him Lamech.
4:28 In the third week of the fifteenth jubilee Lamech took a wife. Her name was Betenos, the daughter of Barakiel, a daughter of his father's sister. In that week she bore him a son, and he named him Noah, saying, "This is the one who will comfort me for my sorrow and all my work, and for the ground that the Lord cursed."
## The deaths of Adam and Cain
4:29 At the end of the nineteenth jubilee, in the seventh week, in its sixth year, Adam died. All his sons buried him in the land where he was created. He was the first to be buried in the earth.
4:30 He fell seventy years short of a thousand years, for a thousand years are one day in the testimony of the heavens. That is why it was written about the tree of knowledge, "On the day you eat from it, you will die." That is why he did not complete the years of this day: he died within it.
4:31 At the end of that jubilee Cain was killed, after him in the same year. His house fell on him, and he died inside his house. He was killed by its stones. For with a stone he had killed Abel, and with a stone he was killed by a just judgment.
4:32 That is why it was ordained on the heavenly tablets: "With the tool a man uses to kill his neighbor, with it he will be killed. As he wounded him, so it will be done to him."
4:33 In the twenty-fifth jubilee Noah took a wife. Her name was Emzara, the daughter of Rakiel, a daughter of his sister. This was in the first year of the fifth week. In its third year she bore him Shem, in its fifth year she bore him Ham, and in the first year of the sixth week she bore him Japheth.
