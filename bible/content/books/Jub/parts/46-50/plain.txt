## Israel grows in Egypt
46:1 After Jacob died, the Israelites multiplied in the land of Egypt and became a great nation. All of them were of one heart, so that each brother loved his brother and each man helped his brother. They multiplied and increased greatly, for ten weeks of years, all the days of Joseph's life.
46:2 There was no satan and nothing evil all the days of Joseph's life that he lived after his father Jacob, because all the Egyptians honored the Israelites all the days of Joseph's life.
## The death of Joseph
46:3 Joseph died at one hundred and ten years old. He had lived seventeen years in the land of Canaan, and ten years as a slave. He spent three years in prison and eighty years under the king, ruling the whole land of Egypt.
46:4 He died, and all his brothers, and that whole generation.
46:5 Before he died, he ordered the Israelites to carry his bones with them at the time when they would go out of the land of Egypt.
46:6 He made them swear an oath about his bones. For he knew that when the day came, the Egyptians would not again bring him out and bury him in the land of Canaan. Makamaron, the king of Canaan, was living in the land of Assyria. He fought the king of Egypt in the valley and killed him there, and he chased the Egyptians as far as the gates of Ermon.
46:7 But he could not get into Egypt, because another, new king had become king of Egypt, and he was stronger than he was. So he went back to the land of Canaan. The gates of Egypt were shut, and no one went out of Egypt and no one came in.
46:8 Joseph died in the forty-sixth jubilee, in the sixth week, in its second year. They buried him in the land of Egypt, and all his brothers died after him.
## The bones of Jacob's sons
46:9 The king of Egypt went out to fight the king of Canaan in the forty-seventh jubilee, in the second week, in its second year. Then the Israelites brought out the bones of all of Jacob's sons except the bones of Joseph. They buried them in the field, in the double cave, in the hill country.
46:10 Most of them went back to Egypt, but a few of them stayed behind in the hill country of Hebron. Your father Amram stayed with them.
46:11 The king of Canaan defeated the king of Egypt, and he shut the gates of Egypt.
## The oppression begins
46:12 He made an evil plan against the Israelites, to make them suffer. He said to the people of Egypt,
46:13 "Look, the people of the Israelites have grown and become more than we are. Come, let us outsmart them before they become too many. Let us make them suffer under slave labor before war comes on us, and before they too fight against us. If not, they will join our enemy and leave our land, for their hearts and their faces are turned toward the land of Canaan."
46:14 He set slave-gang bosses over them to make them suffer under slave labor. They built strong cities for Pharaoh, Pithom and Rameses. They also built every wall and every rampart that had fallen down in the land of Egypt.
46:15 They made them serve under oppression. But however much they mistreated them, that much more they multiplied, and that much more they increased.
46:16 And the people of Egypt came to loathe the Israelites.
## Moses is born
47:1 In the seventh week, in the seventh year, in the forty-seventh jubilee, your father came from the land of Canaan. You were born in the fourth week, in its sixth year, in the forty-eighth jubilee. That was the time of distress for the Israelites.
47:2 Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, gave an order about them: they were to throw their children, every male that was born, into the river.
47:3 They went on throwing them in for seven months, until the day you were born. Your mother hid you for three months, and people reported her.
47:4 So she made an ark for you, coated it with pitch and asphalt, and set it in the grass at the bank of the river. She put you in it for seven days. Your mother came at night and nursed you, and by day your sister Miriam guarded you from the birds.
## Pharaoh's daughter
47:5 In those days Tarmut, Pharaoh's daughter, came out to bathe in the river. She heard your voice as you were crying. She told her Hebrew women to bring you, and you were brought to her.
47:6 She took you out of the ark and felt pity for you.
47:7 Your sister said to her, "Shall I go and call one of the Hebrew women for you, to take care of this baby and nurse him for you?" She said to her, "Go."
47:8 So she went and called your mother, Jochebed. Pharaoh's daughter gave her wages, and she raised you.
47:9 After that, when you had grown, they took you to Pharaoh's house, and you became her son. Your father Amram taught you writing. When you had completed three weeks of years, he brought you into the royal court.
## Moses kills an Egyptian and runs
47:10 You stayed at the court three weeks of years, until the time when you went out from the royal court. You saw an Egyptian beating your companion, one of the Israelites. You killed him and hid him in the sand.
47:11 On the next day you found two of the Israelites quarreling. You said to the one who was in the wrong, "Why are you hitting your brother?"
47:12 He grew angry and hot with rage, and he said, "Who put you as a chief and a judge over us? Do you want to kill me, as you killed the Egyptian?" You were afraid, and you ran away because of what he said.
## Mastema on the road to Egypt
48:1 In the sixth year of the third week of the forty-ninth jubilee you left and lived there for five weeks of years and one year. Then you came back to Egypt in the second week, in the second year, in the fiftieth jubilee.
48:2 You yourself know what was spoken to you on Mount Sinai, and what Prince Mastema wanted to do to you when you were going back to Egypt, on the road, at the shelter.
48:3 Did he not want with all his strength to kill you and to save the Egyptians from your hand? For he saw that you had been sent to carry out judgment and vengeance on the Egyptians.
48:4 But I rescued you from his hand. Then you did the signs and wonders that you were sent to do in Egypt, against Pharaoh and against his whole household, against his servants and against his people.
## The ten judgments
48:5 The Lord took great vengeance on them for Israel's sake. He struck them and killed them with blood and frogs, with lice and dog-flies, and with evil sores that boiled up in blisters. He struck their animals with death, and them with hailstones: with these he destroyed everything that grew for them. He struck them with locusts that ate up what the hail had left them, with darkness, and in their firstborn, both people and animals. And the Lord took vengeance on all their gods and burned them with fire.
48:6 Everything was sent by your hand, for you to do before it was done. And you spoke with the king of Egypt in front of all his servants and in front of his people.
48:7 Everything happened by your word. Ten great and terrible judgments came on the land of Egypt, so that you might carry out on it the vengeance owed for Israel.
48:8 The Lord did all of it for Israel's sake. He did it by his ordinance, which he made in a covenant with Abraham, that he would take vengeance on them just as they had forced Israel into slavery.
48:9 Prince Mastema stood against you, right in front of you. He wanted to make you fall into Pharaoh's hands. He helped the sorcerers of Egypt, and they stood against you and did their works in front of you.
48:10 We let them do the evil things, but we did not let healing be done by their hands.
48:11 The Lord struck them with evil sores, and they could not stand their ground, for we made them lose the power to do even one sign.
## Mastema bound at the sea
48:12 In spite of all the signs and wonders, Prince Mastema was not put to shame. He went so far as to gather his strength and shout to the Egyptians to chase after you with all the might of the Egyptians: with their chariots, with their horses, and with all the crowds of the peoples of Egypt.
48:13 I stood between you and the Egyptians, and between the Egyptians and Israel. We rescued Israel from his hand and from the hand of the people. And the Lord brought them through the middle of the sea as if through dry land.
48:14 All the people whom he brought out to chase after Israel, the Lord our God threw into the middle of the sea, into the depths of the abyss, beneath the Israelites. Just as the people of Egypt had thrown their children into the river, he took vengeance on a million of them. A thousand strong, bold men perished for one nursing baby of your people whom they had thrown into the river.
48:15 On the fourteenth day, the fifteenth, the sixteenth, the seventeenth and the eighteenth, Prince Mastema was bound and locked up behind the Israelites, so that he could not accuse them.
48:16 On the nineteenth day we let them loose, so that they could help the Egyptians and chase after the Israelites.
48:17 He hardened their hearts and made them bold. They were made bold by the Lord our God, so that he could strike the Egyptians and throw them into the middle of the sea.
48:18 On the fourteenth day we bound him, so that he could not accuse the Israelites on the day when they were asking the people of Egypt for things and clothing: things of silver, things of gold and things of bronze. This was so that they could strip the Egyptians in return for the slavery they had forced on them.
48:19 And we did not bring the Israelites out of Egypt empty-handed.
## The law of the Passover
49:1 Remember the commandment that the Lord gave you about the Passover: you are to keep it at its time, on the fourteenth of the first month. You are to slaughter it before evening comes, and they are to eat it at night, on the evening of the fifteenth, from the time the sun sets.
49:2 For this night is the beginning of the feast and the beginning of the joy. On it you were sitting and eating the Passover in Egypt, and all the powers of Mastema were sent out to kill every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from Pharaoh's firstborn to the firstborn of the captive slave woman at the mill, and the animals too.
49:3 This is the sign the Lord gave them. Into every house where they saw the blood of a year-old lamb on its doorway, they were not to go in to kill, but to pass over it. So all who were in the house would be saved, because the sign of the blood was on its doorway.
49:4 The powers of the Lord did everything just as the Lord commanded them. They passed over all the Israelites. The plague did not come on them to destroy a single life among them, from animal to human, down to the dog.
49:5 The plague in Egypt was very heavy. There was no house in Egypt without a dead body in it, and weeping and wailing.
49:6 All Israel sat eating the meat of the Passover and drinking wine. They praised, blessed and thanked the Lord, the God of their fathers, and they were ready to go out from under the yoke of Egypt and from the cruel slavery.
## Keep it on its day
49:7 You too, remember this day all the days of your life. Keep it from year to year, all the days of your life, once a year on its day, by all its law. Do not move it from one day to another, or from one month to another.
49:8 For it is an everlasting ordinance, engraved on the heavenly tablets for all the Israelites, that they are to keep it every year on its day, once a year, through all their generations. It has no time limit, for it is set forever.
49:9 A man who is clean but does not come to keep it at the time of its day, to bring an offering that pleases the Lord and to eat and drink before the Lord on the day of its feast, that man, clean and close by, is to be cut off. Because he did not bring the Lord's offering at its time, that man will carry his sin himself.
## Between the evenings
49:10 Let the Israelites come and keep the Passover on the day of its set time, on the fourteenth of the first month, between the evenings. That is from the third part of the day to the third part of the night, for two parts of the day are given to the light, and the third part to the evening.
49:11 This is what the Lord commanded you: to keep it between the evenings.
49:12 It may not be slaughtered at any hour of daylight, only at the time at the edge of evening. They are to eat it at evening time, until the third part of the night. Whatever is left of all its meat from the third part of the night on, they are to burn in the fire.
## No bone broken
49:13 They must not boil it in water or eat it raw. It must be roasted in the fire, cooked through in the fire with care, the head with the inner parts and with its feet. They are to roast it in the fire, and no bone of it may be broken. For no bone of the Israelites will be crushed.
49:14 That is why the Lord commanded the Israelites to keep the Passover on the day of its set time. Not one bone of it may be broken, for it is a feast day and a commanded day. It may not be moved from one day to another, or from month to month. It is to be kept on the day of its feast.
49:15 You too, command the Israelites to keep the Passover in their days, every year, once a year on the day of its set time. Then a pleasing remembrance will rise before the Lord, and no plague will come on them to kill or to strike in that year, when they have kept the Passover at its time in every way, as it was commanded.
## Only before the Lord's sanctuary
49:16 It may not be eaten outside the sanctuary of the Lord, but in front of the sanctuary of the Lord. The whole people of Israel's assembly is to keep it at its time.
49:17 Every person who has come on its day is to eat it in the sanctuary of your God, before the Lord, from twenty years old and up. For this is how it is written and set down: they are to eat it in the sanctuary of the Lord.
49:18 When the Israelites come into the land that they are to possess, into the land of Canaan, they will set up the Lord's tabernacle in the middle of the land, in one of their tribes, until the Lord's sanctuary is built in the land. Until then they are to come and keep the Passover in the middle of the Lord's tabernacle, and slaughter it before the Lord year after year.
49:19 And in the days when a house has been built in the Lord's name in the land of their inheritance, they are to go there and slaughter the Passover in the evening, as the sun goes down, at the third part of the day.
49:20 They are to bring its blood up onto the base of the altar and put its fat on the fire that is on the altar. They are to eat its meat roasted in the fire in the courtyard of the sanctuary, in the Lord's name.
49:21 They may not keep the Passover in their towns or in any place except before the Lord's tabernacle, or else before the house where his name has come to live. Then they will not wander away from the Lord.
## The Feast of Unleavened Bread
49:22 And you, Moses, command the Israelites to keep the ordinance of the Passover as it was commanded to you. Tell them its year, every year, and its day among the days, and the Feast of Unleavened Bread: they are to eat unleavened bread for seven days, to keep its feast, and to bring its offering day by day during those seven days of joy before the Lord, on the altar of your God.
49:23 For you kept this feast in haste when you went out of Egypt, until you crossed the sea into the Wilderness of Shur, for you finished it on the shore of the sea.
## Sabbaths of the land, and the jubilees
50:1 After this law I made known to you the days of the Sabbaths in the Wilderness of Sinai, which is between Elim and Sinai.
50:2 I also told you about the Sabbaths of the land on Mount Sinai, and about the years of jubilees within the Sabbaths of years. But its year we have not told you, until you go into the land that you are to possess.
50:3 The land too will keep its Sabbaths while they live on it, and they will know the year of the jubilee.
50:4 That is why I have set out for you the weeks of years and the jubilees: forty-nine jubilees from the days of Adam to this day, and one week of years and two years. Forty more years lie ahead for learning the Lord's commandments, until they cross over into the land of Canaan, crossing the Jordan to its western side.
50:5 The jubilees will go by until Israel is made clean of all the guilt of sexual sin and impurity, of defilement, of sin and of error. Then it will live in the whole land in safety. It will no longer have any satan or any evil one, and the land will be clean from that time on, for all time.
## The Sabbath law
50:6 Look, I have written down for you the commandment of the Sabbaths too, and all the rulings of its laws.
50:7 Six days you will do work, but the seventh day is the Sabbath of the Lord your God. Do no work at all on it: you and your children, your male and female slaves, all your animals, and the foreigner who is with you.
50:8 Anyone who does any work on it is to die. Every man who treats that day as common, who lies with his wife, or who talks about doing something on it, setting out on a journey on it, or any buying or selling; whoever draws water on it that he did not get ready for himself on the sixth day; and whoever lifts anything to carry it out of his tent or out of his house: he is to die.
50:9 On the Sabbath do no work that you have not prepared for yourselves on the sixth day. The day is for eating and drinking, for resting and keeping Sabbath from all work on this day, and for blessing the Lord your God, who has given you a day of feasting and a holy day. This day is a day of the holy kingdom for all Israel, among their days, for all time.
50:10 For it is a great honor that the Lord has given to Israel: to eat, to drink and to be full on this feast day, and to rest on it from all the work that belongs to human labor. The only exceptions are burning incense and bringing offerings and sacrifices before the Lord, for the days and for the Sabbaths.
50:11 Only this work is to be done on the Sabbath days, in the sanctuary of the Lord your God: to make atonement for Israel with gifts continually, day after day, as a pleasing reminder before the Lord, so that he may accept them forever, day after day, just as you were commanded.
50:12 Any person who does work, or goes on a journey, or works a farm, whether at his home or in any place; whoever lights a fire or rides on any animal or travels by ship on the sea; any person who strikes or kills anything; whoever slaughters an animal or a bird; whoever traps a wild animal, a bird or a fish; or whoever fasts or makes war on the Sabbath day:
50:13 the person who does any of these things on the Sabbath day is to die. So the Israelites are to keep Sabbath by the commandments of the Sabbaths of the land, as it is written in the tablets that he gave into my hands, for me to write for you the laws of each time, time by time, in the division of its days. Here ends the account of the division of the days.
