## The Birthright and the Famine
24:1 After Abraham died, the Lord blessed his son Isaac. Isaac set out from Hebron and went and lived by the Well of the Vision, in the first year of the third week of this jubilee, for seven years.
24:2 In the first year of the fourth week a famine took hold of the land. It was another famine, besides the first one that came in Abraham's days.
24:3 Jacob cooked a lentil stew, and Esau came in from the field hungry. He said to his brother Jacob, "Give me some of this red stew." Jacob said, "Hand over to me your birthright, your right as the firstborn, and I will give you bread, and some of this stew as well."
24:4 Esau said to himself, "I am going to die. What is this birthright for?" And he said to Jacob, "I have given it to you."
24:5 Jacob said, "Swear to me today." So Esau swore to him.
24:6 Jacob gave his brother Esau bread and stew, and he ate until he was full. So Esau despised the birthright. That is why Esau was named Edom: because of the red stew that Jacob gave him for his birthright.
24:7 So Jacob became the elder, and Esau was brought down from his rank.
## Isaac Among the Philistines
24:8 The famine lay over the land, and in the second year of this week Isaac set out to go down to Egypt. He went to the king of the Philistines, to Gerar, to Abimelech.
24:9 The Lord appeared to him and said, "Do not go down to Egypt. Live in the land I tell you of. Stay as a foreigner in this land, and I will be with you and bless you.
24:10 To you and to your offspring I will give all this land. I will make good my oath that I swore to Abraham your father. I will make your offspring as many as the stars of the sky, and I will give your offspring all this land.
24:11 In your offspring all the nations of the earth will be blessed, because your father listened to my voice and kept what I required of him: my commands, my laws, my ordinances and my covenant. Now listen to my voice and live in this land."
24:12 So he lived in Gerar for three weeks of years.
24:13 Abimelech gave orders about him and about everything that was his: "Any man who touches him or anything of his will surely die."
24:14 Isaac grew great among the Philistines. He came to own much: cattle and sheep, camels and donkeys, and much property.
24:15 He sowed in the land of the Philistines and brought in a hundred times as much. Isaac became very great, and the Philistines were jealous of him.
24:16 All the wells that Abraham's servants had dug while Abraham was alive, the Philistines had stopped up after Abraham's death and filled with dirt.
24:17 Abimelech said to Isaac, "Go away from us, because you are far greater than we are." In the first year of the seventh week Isaac left there and stayed as a foreigner in the valleys of Gerar.
24:18 They dug out again the wells of water that the servants of his father Abraham had dug, which the Philistines had stopped up after his father Abraham died. He gave them the names his father Abraham had given them.
24:19 Isaac's servants dug a well in the valley and found living water. The herdsmen of Gerar quarreled with Isaac's herdsmen. "This water is ours," they said. So Isaac named that well Hardship, "because they made things hard for us."
24:20 They dug a second well and quarreled over that one too, and he named it Narrow. He moved on, and they dug another well. Over this one they did not quarrel, and he named it Wide. Isaac said, "Now the Lord has made wide room for us, and we have grown in the land."
## The Well of the Oath
24:21 From there he went up to the Well of the Oath, in the first year of the first week of the forty-fourth jubilee.
24:22 The Lord appeared to him that night, on the first day of the first month, and said, "I am the God of Abraham your father. Do not be afraid, because I am with you. I will bless you, and I will surely make your offspring as many as the sand of the earth, for the sake of Abraham my servant."
24:23 He built an altar there, the one his father Abraham had built first. He called on the name of the Lord and offered a sacrifice to the God of Abraham his father.
24:24 They dug a well and found living water.
24:25 Isaac's servants dug another well and did not find water. They went and told Isaac they had not found water. Isaac said, "I swore an oath to the Philistines today, and this thing has happened to us."
24:26 He named that place the Well of the Oath, because there he had sworn an oath to Abimelech, to Ahuzzath his friend and to Phicol his commander.
24:27 That day Isaac realized that he had sworn to them under pressure, to make peace with them.
## Isaac Curses the Philistines
24:28 That day Isaac cursed the Philistines. He said, "Cursed are the Philistines, for the day of wrath and fury, out of all the nations! May the Lord make them a laughingstock and a curse, a target of anger and fury, in the hands of the sinners, the nations, and in the hand of the Kittim.
24:29 Whoever escapes the enemy's sword and the Kittim, may the righteous people root them out in judgment from under the sky. For they will be enemies and foes of my children all their days on the earth.
24:30 No remnant will be left to them, and no one who is saved on the day of the wrath of judgment. For all the offspring of the Philistines are marked for destruction, for uprooting and for removal from the earth. None of the Caphtorim will have any name or offspring left on the earth.
24:31 Even if he goes up to the sky, from there he will come down. Even if he makes himself strong on the earth, from there he will be dragged out. Even if he hides among the nations, from there he will be uprooted. Even if he goes down to Sheol, there his condemnation will be great, and there he will have no peace at all.
24:32 If he goes into captivity, those who hunt for his life will kill him on the road. No name or offspring will be left to him in all the earth, for he will go off into a curse that lasts forever."
24:33 This is how it is written and engraved about him on the heavenly tablets: this is to be done to him on the day of judgment, so that he is uprooted from the earth.
## Rebekah Warns Jacob
25:1 In the second year of this week, in this jubilee, Rebekah called her son Jacob and spoke to him. "My son," she said, "do not take a wife for yourself from the daughters of Canaan, as Esau your brother did. He took two wives from the offspring of Canaan, and they have made my soul bitter with all their unclean deeds. All their deeds are sexual immorality and lust. There is no righteousness in them at all, for it is evil.
25:2 I love you very much, my son. My heart and my tender love bless you every hour of the day and every watch of the night.
25:3 So now, my son, listen to my voice and do what your mother wants. Do not take a wife from the daughters of this land, only from my father's house and my father's family. Take a wife for yourself from my father's house. Then God Most High will bless you, your children will be a righteous generation, and your offspring will be holy."
## Jacob's Promise
25:4 Then Jacob spoke with his mother Rebekah. "Look, mother," he said, "I am nine weeks of years old. I have not known any woman or touched one. I have not been engaged to one, and I do not even think of taking a wife from any of the offspring of Canaan's daughters.
25:5 For I remember, mother, the words of Abraham our father, what he commanded me: not to take a wife from any of the offspring of Canaan's house. I will take a wife from the offspring of my father's house, from my own family.
25:6 I have heard before this, mother, that daughters have been born to Laban your brother, and I have set my heart on them, to take a wife from among them.
25:7 That is why I have kept watch over my spirit, so as not to sin or go wrong in any of my ways all the days of my life. About lust and sexual immorality, father Abraham gave me many commands.
25:8 In spite of all he commanded me, for these twenty-two years my brother has argued with me and kept telling me, 'My brother, marry a sister of my two wives.' But I refuse to do what he did.
25:9 I swear to you, mother, that all the days of my life I will not take a wife from any of the offspring of Canaan's daughters. I will not do wrong the way my brother did.
25:10 Do not be afraid, mother. Be sure of it: I will do what you want. I will walk in an upright way, and I will never make my ways corrupt."
## Rebekah Blesses Jacob
25:11 Then she lifted her face toward heaven, spread out the fingers of her hands and opened her mouth. She blessed God Most High, who created heaven and earth, and she gave him thanks and praise.
25:12 She said, "Blessed be the Lord God, and may his name be blessed forever and ever! He has given me Jacob, a pure son and a holy offspring. For he is yours, and may his offspring be yours for all time, in every generation forever.
25:13 Bless him, Lord, and put a righteous blessing in my mouth, so that I may bless him."
25:14 At that moment a spirit of righteousness came down into her mouth. She placed both her hands on Jacob's head and said,
25:15 "Blessed are you, righteous Lord, God of the ages. / May he bless you more than all the generations of humankind. / May he give you, my son, the path of righteousness, / and show righteousness to your offspring.
25:16 May he make your sons many in your lifetime, / and may they arise as many as the months of the year. / May their sons be many and great, more than the stars of the sky, / and may their number be more than the sand of the sea.
25:17 May he give them this good land, / as he said he would give it to Abraham and to his offspring after him for all time, / and may they hold it as their own forever.
25:18 May I see blessed children born to you, my son, while I live, / and may all your offspring be blessed and holy.
25:19 As you have given rest to your mother's spirit in her lifetime, / so the womb of the woman who bore you blesses you. / My tender love and my breasts bless you, / and my mouth and my tongue praise you greatly.
25:20 Grow and spread out over the earth, / and may your offspring be complete in all the world, in the joy of heaven and earth. / May your offspring rejoice, / and on the great day of peace may it have peace.
25:21 May your name and your offspring last through all the ages. / May God Most High be their God, / may the God of righteousness live among them, / and may his sanctuary be built by them for all the ages.
25:22 Whoever blesses you, blessed! / And all flesh that curses you falsely, may it be cursed!"
25:23 She kissed him and said to him, "May the Lord of the world love you / as your mother's heart and her tender love rejoice in you and bless you." And then she stopped blessing.
## Isaac Sends Esau to Hunt
26:1 In the seventh year of this week Isaac called Esau, his older son, and said to him, "My son, I have grown old. Look, my eyes have grown too dim to see, and I do not know the day I will die.
26:2 So now, take your hunting gear, your quiver and your bow, and go out to the field. Hunt for me and catch something for me, my son. Make me a savory meal, the kind my soul loves, and bring it to me, so that I may eat and my soul may bless you before I die."
26:3 But Rebekah heard Isaac speaking to Esau.
26:4 Esau went out early to the field to hunt, catch game and bring it to his father.
## Rebekah's Plan
26:5 Rebekah called her son Jacob and said to him, "Listen, I heard your father Isaac talking with Esau your brother. He said, 'Hunt for me, make me a savory meal and bring it to me. I will eat, and I will bless you in the presence of the Lord before I die.'
26:6 Now, my son, listen to my voice in what I command you. Go to your flock and bring me two good young goats, and I will make them into a savory meal for your father, the kind he loves. You will bring it to your father, and he will eat and bless you in the presence of the Lord before he dies, and you will be blessed."
26:7 Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Mother, I will not hold back anything my father eats that pleases him. But I am afraid, my mother, that he will recognize my voice and want to feel me.
26:8 You know that I am smooth and Esau my brother is hairy. In his eyes I will look like someone doing wrong, doing a thing he did not tell me to do. He will be angry with me, and I will bring a curse on myself, not a blessing."
26:9 Rebekah his mother said to him, "Let your curse fall on me, my son. Only listen to my voice."
26:10 Jacob listened to the voice of Rebekah his mother. He went and brought two good, fat young goats and took them to his mother, and his mother made them the way he loved.
26:11 Rebekah took the fine clothes of Esau her older son, which were with her in the house, and dressed Jacob her younger son in them. She put the skins of the young goats on his hands and on the bare part of his neck.
26:12 She gave him the meal and the bread she had made, into the hand of her son Jacob.
## Jacob Takes the Blessing
26:13 He went in to his father and said, "I am your son. I have done as you told me. Get up, sit and eat of what I have hunted, father, so that your soul may bless me."
26:14 Isaac said to his son, "How did you find it so fast, my son?"
26:15 Jacob said, "Your God put it in my way."
26:16 Isaac said to him, "Come close, so I can feel you, my son, whether you are my son Esau or not."
26:17 Jacob came close to Isaac his father, and he felt him and said,
26:18 "The voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are Esau's hands." He did not recognize him, because heaven had turned events so as to take away his power to perceive. Isaac did not know him, because his hands were hairy like Esau's hands, so that he would bless him.
26:19 He said, "Are you my son Esau?" He said, "I am your son." Isaac said, "Bring it close to me, so I can eat of what you have hunted, my son, and my soul may bless you."
26:20 He brought it close to him, and he ate. He brought him wine, and he drank.
26:21 Isaac his father said to him, "Come close and kiss me, my son." He came close and kissed him.
26:22 He smelled the smell of his clothes, blessed him and said, "Look, the smell of my son / is like the smell of a field the Lord has blessed.
26:23 May the Lord give to you and make you many / from the dew of the sky and from the dew of the earth. / May he give you plenty of grain and oil. / May nations serve you, / and peoples bow down to you.
26:24 Be lord over your brothers, / and may your mother's sons bow down to you. / May all the blessings with which the Lord blessed me / and blessed Abraham my father / be yours and your offspring's forever. / Whoever curses you, cursed! / Whoever blesses you, blessed!"
## Esau's Bitter Cry
26:25 As soon as Isaac had finished blessing his son Jacob, and Jacob had gone out from Isaac his father, he hid. And Esau his brother came in from his hunting.
26:26 He too made a savory meal and brought it to his father. He said to his father, "Let my father get up and eat of my game, so that your soul may bless me."
26:27 Isaac his father said to him, "Who are you?" He said to him, "I am your firstborn, your son Esau. I have done as you commanded me."
26:28 Isaac was utterly astonished and said, "Who is this who hunted, caught game and brought it to me? I ate from all of it before you came, and I blessed him. He will be blessed, he and all his offspring forever."
26:29 When Esau heard the words of his father Isaac, he cried out with a loud and very bitter cry, and said to his father, "Bless me, me too, father!"
26:30 Isaac said to him, "Your brother came with trickery and took your blessings." Esau said, "Now I know why he was named Jacob. Look, he has tripped me up these two times. He took my birthright, and now he has taken my blessing."
26:31 He said, "Have you not left a blessing for me, father?" Isaac answered Esau, "Look, I have made him your lord, / and I have given him all his brothers as his servants. / With plenty of grain, wine and oil I have made him strong. / So what can I do for you now, my son?"
26:32 Esau said to Isaac his father, "Do you have only one blessing, father? Bless me, me too, father!"
26:33 Esau raised his voice and wept. Isaac answered him, "Look, away from the dew of the earth your home will be, / and away from the dew of the sky above.
26:34 By your sword you will live, / and you will serve your brother. / But when you become great / and shake his yoke off your neck, / then you will commit a sin that is wholly for death, / and your offspring will be uprooted from under the sky."
26:35 Esau kept threatening Jacob because of the blessing with which his father had blessed him. He said in his heart, "Now let the days of mourning for my father come, and I will kill my brother Jacob."
## Jacob Sent Away
27:1 What Esau, her older son, had said was told to Rebekah in a dream. Rebekah sent and called Jacob, her younger son, and said to him,
27:2 "Look, Esau your brother is planning to take revenge on you by killing you.
27:3 So now, my son, listen to my voice. Get up and run away to Laban my brother, to Haran. Stay with him a few days, until your brother's anger turns back and he lets go of his anger toward you and forgets all you have done. Then I will send and bring you back from there."
27:4 Jacob said, "I am not afraid. If he wants to kill me, I will kill him."
27:5 But she said to him, "Do not let me be robbed of both my sons in one day."
27:6 Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Look, you know that my father has grown old and cannot see, because his eyes are dull. If I leave him, it will be wrong in his eyes, because I am leaving him and going away from you both. My father will be angry and curse me. I will not go. Only if he sends me will I go."
27:7 Rebekah said to Jacob, "I will go in and speak to him, and he will send you."
27:8 Rebekah went in and said to Isaac, "I am sick of my life because of the two daughters of Heth whom Esau has taken as wives. If Jacob takes a wife from among the daughters of the land like these, why should I go on living? For the daughters of the land of Canaan are evil."
27:9 Isaac called his son Jacob, blessed him, warned him and said to him,
27:10 "Do not take a wife from any of the daughters of Canaan. Get up, go to Mesopotamia, to the house of Bethuel your mother's father, and take a wife there from the daughters of Laban your mother's brother.
27:11 May God Almighty bless you, make you grow and make you many, so that you become a gathering of nations. May he give the blessings of my father Abraham to you and to your offspring after you, so that you inherit the land where you are a foreigner, and all the land the Lord gave to Abraham. Go in peace, my son."
27:12 Isaac sent Jacob away, and he went to Mesopotamia, to Laban son of Bethuel the Aramean, brother of Rebekah, Jacob's mother.
## Isaac Comforts Rebekah
27:13 After Jacob had set out to go to Mesopotamia, Rebekah's spirit grieved after her son, and she wept.
27:14 Isaac said to Rebekah, "My sister, do not weep over my son Jacob. He goes in peace, and in peace he will come back.
27:15 God Most High will guard him from all evil and be with him. He will not abandon him all his days.
27:16 For I know well that his ways will go straight in everything, wherever he goes, until he comes back to us in peace and we see him in peace.
27:17 Do not be afraid for him, my sister. He is upright in his path. He is a blameless man, he is faithful, and he will not be lost. Do not weep."
27:18 Isaac comforted Rebekah about her son Jacob, and blessed him.
## The Dream at Bethel
27:19 Jacob left the Well of the Oath to go to Haran, in the first year of the second week of the forty-fourth jubilee. He came to Luz on the mountain, that is, Bethel, on the first day of the first month of this week. He reached the place at evening and turned off the road toward the west that night, and he slept there, because the sun had set.
27:20 He took one of the stones of that place and set it under that tree. He was traveling alone. And he slept.
27:21 That night he dreamed. Look: a stairway set on the earth, its top touching the sky. Look: the angels of the Lord were going up and coming down on it. And look: the Lord was standing on it.
27:22 He spoke with Jacob and said, "I am the God of Abraham your father and the God of Isaac. The land you are sleeping on I will give to you and to your offspring after you.
27:23 Your offspring will be like the sand of the earth, and you will grow to the west and to the east, to the north and to the south. In you and in your offspring all the families of the nations will be blessed.
27:24 Look, I will be with you and guard you everywhere you go. I will bring you back to this land in peace, because I will not leave you until I have done all I have told you."
27:25 Jacob slept, and he said, "Truly this place is the house of the Lord, and I did not know it." He was afraid and said, "How fearsome this place is! It is nothing but the house of the Lord, and this is the gate of heaven."
27:26 Jacob got up early in the morning. He took the stone he had put by his head and set it up as a standing stone for a sign, and he poured oil on its top. He named that place Bethel, though at first the place was called Luz.
27:27 Jacob made a vow to the Lord: "If the Lord is with me and guards me on this road I am traveling, and gives me bread to eat and clothes to wear, and I come back to my father's house in peace, then the Lord will be my God. This stone that I have set up as a standing stone for a sign in this place will be the house of the Lord. And of everything you give me I will give a tenth to you, my God."
## Leah and Rachel
28:1 He picked up his feet and went to the land of the east, to Laban, Rebekah's brother. He stayed with him and served him for Rachel his daughter one week of years.
28:2 In the first year of the third week he said to him, "Give me my wife, for whom I have served you seven years." Laban said to Jacob, "I will give you your wife."
28:3 Laban made a feast. He took Leah, his older daughter, and gave her to Jacob as a wife, and he gave her Zilpah his slave woman as her slave woman. Jacob did not know, for he thought she was Rachel.
28:4 He went in to her, and look, it was Leah. Jacob was angry with Laban and said to him, "Why have you done this? Did I not serve you for Rachel, and not for Leah? Why have you wronged me? Take your daughter, and I will go, because you have done evil to me."
28:5 For Jacob loved Rachel more than Leah. Leah's eyes were weak, but her form was very beautiful. Rachel's eyes were beautiful, her form was beautiful, and she was very lovely.
28:6 Laban said to Jacob, "It is not done this way in our country, giving the younger before the older." It is not right to do this, because this is how it is decreed and written on the heavenly tablets: no one is to give his younger daughter before the older one. The older is given first, and after her the younger. The man who does this, they bring up guilt against him in heaven. No one who does this thing is righteous, because this deed is evil before the Lord.
28:7 And you, command the Israelites not to do this thing. Let them not take or give the younger before they give the older, because it is very evil.
28:8 Laban said to Jacob, "Let the seven days of this one's feast pass, and I will give you Rachel, so that you serve me another seven years, grazing my sheep as you did in the first week."
28:9 On the day when the seven days of Leah's feast had passed, Laban gave Rachel to Jacob, so that he would serve him another seven years. He gave Rachel Bilhah, Zilpah's sister, as her slave woman.
28:10 He served seven more years for Rachel, because Leah had been given to him for nothing.
## The Children Born in Haran
28:11 The Lord opened Leah's womb. She conceived and bore Jacob a son, and he named him Reuben, on the fourteenth day of the ninth month, in the first year of the third week.
28:12 But Rachel's womb was closed, because the Lord saw that Leah was hated and Rachel was loved.
28:13 Again Jacob went in to Leah. She conceived and bore Jacob a second son, and he named him Simeon, on the twenty-first day of the tenth month, in the third year of this week.
28:14 Again Jacob went in to Leah. She conceived and bore him a third son, and he named him Levi, on the first day of the first month, in the sixth year of this week.
28:15 Again he went in to her, and she bore a fourth son. He named him Judah, on the fifteenth day of the third month, in the first year of the fourth week.
28:16 With all this, Rachel was jealous of Leah, because she herself did not bear. She said to Jacob, "Give me children." Jacob said to her, "Have I kept the fruit of your womb from you? Have I abandoned you?"
28:17 When Rachel saw that Leah had borne Jacob four sons, Reuben, Simeon, Levi and Judah, she said to him, "Go in to Bilhah my slave woman. She will conceive and bear a son for me."
28:18 He went in to her, and she conceived and bore him a son, and he named him Dan, on the ninth day of the sixth month, in the sixth year of the third week.
28:19 Jacob went in again, a second time, to Bilhah. She conceived and bore Jacob another son, and Rachel named him Naphtali, on the fifth day of the seventh month, in the second year of the fourth week.
28:20 When Leah saw that she had become barren and was not bearing, she too was jealous of Rachel, and she too gave her slave woman Zilpah to Jacob as a wife. She conceived and bore a son, and Leah named him Gad, on the twelfth day of the eighth month, in the third year of the fourth week.
28:21 He went in to her again, and she conceived and bore him a second son, and Leah named him Asher, on the second day of the eleventh month, in the fifth year of the fourth week.
28:22 Jacob went in to Leah. She conceived and bore Jacob a son, and he named him Issachar, on the fourth day of the fifth month, in the fourth year of the fourth week. And she gave him to a nurse.
28:23 Jacob went in to her again. She conceived and bore two, a son and a daughter. She named the son Zebulun and the daughter Dinah, on the seventh day of the seventh month, in the sixth year of the fourth week.
28:24 The Lord was kind to Rachel and opened her womb. She conceived and bore a son, and she named him Joseph, on the first day of the fourth month, in the sixth year of this fourth week.
## Jacob's Wages
28:25 In the days when Joseph was born, Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wives and my sons, and let me go to my father Isaac and make a house for myself. For I have completed the years I served you for your two daughters, and I will go to my father's house."
28:26 Laban said to Jacob, "Stay with me for your wages. Graze my flock for me again and take your wages."
28:27 They agreed between them that he would give him as his wages every lamb and kid born dark and spotted and white. That would be his wages.
28:28 All the sheep gave birth to spotted young, every kind of marking, mottled and dark. And the sheep kept giving birth to young like themselves. All the marked ones went to Jacob, and the unmarked ones to Laban.
28:29 Jacob's possessions grew very great. He came to own cattle and sheep, donkeys and camels, and male and female servants.
28:30 Laban and his sons were jealous of Jacob. Laban took his sheep back from him and watched him with evil intent.
## Jacob Leaves Laban
29:1 After Rachel bore Joseph, Laban went to shear his sheep, which were three days' journey away from him.
29:2 Jacob saw that Laban was going to shear his sheep. He called Leah and Rachel and spoke to their hearts, so that they would come with him to the land of Canaan.
29:3 For he told them how he had seen everything in a dream, and all that had been said to him, that he should return to his father's house. They said, "We will go with you to every place you go."
29:4 Jacob blessed the God of Isaac his father and the God of Abraham his father's father. He got up and put his wives and his children up on the animals, and took all his possessions. He crossed the river and came to the land of Gilead. Jacob hid his plan from Laban and did not tell him.
29:5 In the seventh year of the fourth week Jacob turned toward Gilead, in the first month, on the twenty-first day. Laban chased after him and caught up with Jacob on Mount Gilead in the third month, on the thirteenth day.
29:6 But the Lord did not let him harm Jacob, because he appeared to him in a dream at night. And Laban spoke with Jacob.
29:7 On the fifteenth of those days Jacob made a feast for Laban and for all who had come with him. That day Jacob swore to Laban, and Laban also to Jacob, that neither would cross Mount Gilead to the other with evil intent.
29:8 He made a heap there for a witness. That is why that place is called the Heap of Witness, after this heap.
## The Land of the Rephaim
29:9 Before that, people used to call the land of Gilead the land of the Rephaim, because it was the land of the Rephaim. The Rephaim were born there, giants whose height was ten cubits, nine, eight, down to seven cubits.
29:10 They lived from the land of the Ammonites as far as Mount Hermon, and their royal cities were Karnaim and Ashtaroth, Edrei, Misur and Beon.
29:11 The Lord destroyed them because of their evil deeds, for they were very cruel. The Amorites lived in their place, evil and sinful. Today there is no people that has done all their sins in full, and they no longer have long life on the earth.
## Esau, and Gifts for Isaac and Rebekah
29:12 Jacob sent Laban away, and he went to Mesopotamia, the land of the east. Jacob returned to the land of Gilead.
29:13 He crossed the Jabbok in the ninth month, on the eleventh day. That day Esau his brother came to him, and they made peace with each other. Esau went from him to the land of Seir, but Jacob lived in booths.
29:14 In the first year of the fifth week of this jubilee he crossed the Jordan and lived on the far side of the Jordan. He grazed his sheep from the Salt Sea to Beth-shan, to Dothan, and as far as Akrabbim.
29:15 He sent his father Isaac some of all his possessions: clothing, food, meat, drink, milk, oil, cheese, and some dates from the valley.
29:16 And to his mother Rebekah too, four times a year: between the turns of the months, between plowing and harvest, between autumn and the rains, and between winter and spring, to Abraham's tower.
29:17 For Isaac had come back from the Well of the Oath and gone up to the tower of his father Abraham, and he lived there apart from his son Esau.
29:18 For in the days when Jacob went to Mesopotamia, Esau took as his wife Mahalath, daughter of Ishmael. He gathered all his father's flocks and his wives, went up and lived on Mount Seir, and left his father Isaac alone at the Well of the Oath.
29:19 Isaac went up from the Well of the Oath and lived in the tower of Abraham his father, on the mountain of Hebron.
29:20 From there Jacob sent everything he sent to his father and his mother, from time to time, everything they needed. And they blessed Jacob with all their heart and with all their soul.
## Dinah and the Men of Shechem
30:1 In the first year of the sixth week he went up to Salem, east of Shechem, in peace, in the fourth month.
30:2 There they carried Dinah, Jacob's daughter, off into the house of Shechem son of Hamor the Hivite, the ruler of the land. He lay with her and defiled her. She was a little girl, twelve years old.
30:3 He begged her father and her brothers to give her to him as a wife. Jacob and his sons were angry at the men of Shechem, because they had defiled Dinah their sister. They spoke with them with evil intent, deceived them and tricked them.
30:4 Simeon and Levi came into Shechem without warning and carried out judgment on all the men of Shechem. They killed every man they found in it and left not one in it. They killed them all in torment, because they had dishonored Dinah their sister.
## The Law Against Marrying Outside Israel
30:5 Let it never again be done from now on, that a daughter of Israel is defiled. For judgment was decreed in heaven against them, that they should wipe out all the men of Shechem with the sword, because they had done a disgraceful thing in Israel.
30:6 The Lord handed them over into the hands of Jacob's sons, to wipe them out with the sword and carry out judgment on them, and so that it would never again be done in Israel that an Israelite virgin is defiled.
30:7 If there is a man in Israel who wants to give his daughter or his sister to any man who is of the offspring of the nations, he must surely die. They are to stone him with stones, because he has done a disgraceful thing in Israel. And the woman they are to burn with fire, because she has defiled the name of her father's house, and she is to be uprooted from Israel.
30:8 Let no immoral woman and no uncleanness be found in Israel through all the days of the generations of the earth, for Israel is holy to the Lord. Every man who has defiled it must surely die. They are to stone him with stones.
30:9 For this is how it is decreed and written on the heavenly tablets about all the offspring of Israel: whoever defiles it must surely die, and they are to stone him with stones.
30:10 For this law there is no limit of days, no pardon and no atonement of any kind. The man who has defiled his daughter must simply be uprooted from the midst of all Israel, because he has given some of his offspring to Molech and sinned by defiling it.
30:11 And you, Moses, command the Israelites and charge them solemnly not to give any of their daughters to the nations, and not to take any of the daughters of the nations, for this is detestable before the Lord.
30:12 That is why I wrote for you in the words of the law all the deeds of the Shechemites that they did to Dinah, and how Jacob's sons spoke, saying, "We will not give our daughter to a man who has a foreskin, for that would be a disgrace for us."
30:13 It is a disgrace for Israel, for those who give and for those who take from the daughters of the nations, for this is unclean and detestable for Israel.
30:14 Israel will not be clean of this uncleanness if it has a wife from the daughters of the nations, or if it has given any of its daughters to a man from any of the nations.
30:15 For it is plague on plague and curse on curse, and every judgment, plague and curse will come. If he does this thing, or if he shuts his eyes to those who commit uncleanness, who defile the Lord's sanctuary and who profane his holy name, then the whole people together will be judged for all the uncleanness and this profanation.
30:16 There will be no favoritism and no partiality, and no receiving from his hand of fruit, offerings, burnt offerings, fat or the smell of a pleasing fragrance, to accept it. And so it will be for every man or woman in Israel who defiles the sanctuary.
## Levi Chosen for the Priesthood
30:17 That is why I have commanded you to bear this testimony to Israel: see how it went for Shechem and its sons, how they were handed over into the hands of two of Jacob's sons, who killed them in torment. It was counted to them as righteousness, and it is written down for them as righteousness.
30:18 The offspring of Levi were chosen for the priesthood and to be Levites, to serve before the Lord as we do, always. Levi and his sons are blessed forever, because he was zealous to carry out righteousness, judgment and vengeance on all who rose up against Israel.
30:19 So they write up for him, as a testimony on the heavenly tablets, blessing and righteousness before the God of all.
30:20 We remember the righteousness that the man did in his life, at all the seasons of the year. For a thousand generations they will record it, and it will come to him and to his descendants after him. He has been written on the heavenly tablets as a friend and a righteous man.
30:21 I have written all this account for you and commanded you to tell the Israelites not to sin, not to break the ordinances and not to break the covenant decreed for them, but to do it and be recorded as friends.
30:22 But if they break them and walk in all the ways of uncleanness, they will be written on the heavenly tablets as enemies. They will be wiped out of the book of the living and written in the book of those who will be destroyed, with those who will be uprooted from the earth.
30:23 On the day Jacob's sons killed Shechem, a record went up for them in heaven that they had done righteousness, uprightness and vengeance on the sinners, and it was written for a blessing.
30:24 They brought Dinah their sister out of Shechem's house. They captured everything that was in Shechem: their sheep, their cattle, their donkeys, all their goods and all their herds. And they brought it all to Jacob their father.
30:25 He spoke with them about their having killed the city, because he was afraid of those who lived in the land, the Canaanites and the Perizzites.
30:26 The terror of the Lord was on all the cities around Shechem, and they did not set out to chase Jacob's sons, because terror had fallen on them.
## Back to Bethel
31:1 On the first day of the month Jacob spoke to all the people of his household: "Make yourselves clean and change your clothes. Let us get up and go up to Bethel, where I made a vow to him on the day I ran away from Esau my brother, because he has been with me and brought me into this land in peace. Put away the foreign gods that are among you."
31:2 They melted down the foreign gods, and what was in their ears and on their necks. The idols that Rachel had stolen from Laban her father she gave, all of them, to Jacob. He burned them, broke them to pieces and destroyed them, and hid them under the oak in the land of Shechem.
31:3 On the first day of the seventh month he went up to Bethel. He built an altar at the place where he had slept, and he set up a standing stone there. He sent word to his father Isaac to come to him, to his sacrifice, and to his mother Rebekah.
31:4 Isaac said, "Let my son Jacob come, and let me see him before I die."
## Jacob Brings Levi and Judah Home
31:5 Jacob went to his father Isaac and his mother Rebekah, to the house of his father Abraham. He took two of his sons with him, Levi and Judah, and came to his father Isaac and his mother Rebekah.
31:6 Rebekah came out of the tower to the tower's gate to kiss Jacob and embrace him, because her spirit came back to life when she heard, "Look, Jacob your son has come." And she kissed him.
31:7 She saw his two sons and recognized them. She said to him, "Are these your sons, my son?" She embraced them, kissed them and blessed them: "Through you the offspring of Abraham will be honored, and you will be a blessing on the earth."
31:8 Jacob went in to Isaac his father, to the room where he was lying, and his two sons were with him. He took his father's hand, bent down and kissed him. Isaac hung on the neck of Jacob his son and wept on his neck.
31:9 The darkness left Isaac's eyes, and he saw the two sons of Jacob, Levi and Judah. He said, "Are these your sons, my son? They look like you."
31:10 He told him that they were truly his sons: "You have truly seen that they are truly my sons."
31:11 They came close to him. He turned and kissed them and embraced both of them together.
## Isaac Blesses Levi
31:12 A spirit of prophecy came down into his mouth. He took Levi by his right hand and Judah by his left hand.
31:13 He turned to Levi first and began to bless him first. He said to him, "May the Lord of all, he who is the Lord of all the ages, bless you and your children for all the ages.
31:14 May the Lord give you and your offspring very great greatness, for glory. / From all flesh may he bring you and your offspring near to himself, / to serve in his sanctuary like the angels of the presence and like the holy ones. / Like them the offspring of your sons will be, for glory, greatness and holiness, / and may he make them great for all the ages.
31:15 They will be rulers, princes and chiefs of all the offspring of Jacob's sons. / They will speak the word of the Lord in righteousness, / and they will judge all his judgments in righteousness. / They will tell my ways to Jacob / and my paths to Israel. / The blessing of the Lord will be given in their mouth, / to bless all the offspring of the beloved.
31:16 Your mother called you by your name, Levi, / and rightly she called your name. / You will be joined to the Lord / and be the companion of all Jacob's sons. / Let his table be yours; eat from it, you and your sons. / May your table be full for every generation, / and may your food never run short for all the ages.
31:17 May all who hate you fall before you, / and may all your enemies be uprooted and perish. / Whoever blesses you, blessed! / And may every nation that curses you be cursed!"
## Isaac Blesses Judah
31:18 To Judah he said, "May the Lord give you strength and power / to trample all who hate you. / Be a ruler, you and one of your sons, over Jacob's sons. / May your name and the name of your sons go out and travel through every land and region. / Then the nations will fear before your face, / all the nations will tremble, / and every people will tremble.
31:19 In you will be the help of Jacob, / and in you the salvation of Israel will be found.
31:20 When you sit on the throne of the honor of your righteousness, / there will be great peace for all the offspring of the sons of the beloved. / Whoever blesses you, blessed! / And all who hate you, trouble you and curse you / will be uprooted and destroyed from the earth and be cursed."
## A Night with Isaac
31:21 He turned and kissed him again and embraced him, and he rejoiced greatly, because he had truly seen the sons of Jacob his son.
31:22 He came out from between his feet, fell down and bowed to him, and he blessed them. He rested there near Isaac his father that night, and they ate and drank with joy.
31:23 He had Jacob's two sons sleep, one on his right and one on his left, and it was counted to him as righteousness.
31:24 That night Jacob told his father everything: how the Lord had shown him great mercy, how he had made all his ways go well, and how he had protected him from all evil.
31:25 Isaac blessed the God of his father Abraham, who had not withdrawn his mercy and his righteousness from the son of his servant Isaac.
31:26 In the morning Jacob told his father Isaac about the vow he had made to the Lord and the vision he had seen: how he had built an altar and everything was ready for making the sacrifice before the Lord as he had vowed, and that he had come to set him on a donkey.
31:27 Isaac said to Jacob his son, "I cannot come with you, because I am old and cannot bear the road. Go in peace, my son, for I am a hundred and sixty-five years old. I can no longer make the journey. Set your mother on a donkey, and let her go with you.
31:28 I know, my son, that you came because of me. Blessed be this day on which you have seen me alive, and I too have seen you, my son.
31:29 Prosper, and carry out the vow you made. Do not put off your vow, because you will be called to account for the vow. So now hurry to carry it out, and may he who made all things be pleased, he to whom you made the vow."
31:30 He said to Rebekah, "Go with Jacob your son." So Rebekah went with Jacob her son, and Deborah with her, and they came to Bethel.
31:31 Jacob remembered the prayer with which his father had blessed him and his two sons, Levi and Judah. He rejoiced and blessed the God of his fathers Abraham and Isaac.
31:32 He said, "Now I know that I have an eternal hope, and my sons too, before the God of all." This is how it is decreed about the two of them, and they record it for them as an eternal testimony on the heavenly tablets, just as Isaac blessed them.
