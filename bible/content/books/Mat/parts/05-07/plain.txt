## Jesus teaches on the mountain
5:1 Jesus saw the crowds and climbed the mountain. Once he was seated, his disciples came to him.
5:2 Then he opened his mouth to teach them. He said:
## The blessed
5:3 "Blessed are those who are poor in spirit, because the kingdom of heaven is theirs.
5:4 Blessed are the mourners, because they will be comforted.
5:5 Blessed are the gentle, because they will inherit the earth.
5:6 Blessed are those who are hungry and thirsty for righteousness, because they will be given their fill.
5:7 Blessed are the merciful, because mercy will be shown to them.
5:8 Blessed are those whose hearts are pure, because they will see God.
5:9 Blessed are the peacemakers, because they will be called sons of God.
5:10 Blessed are those who have suffered persecution on account of righteousness, because the kingdom of heaven is theirs.
5:11 "You are blessed when people insult you and persecute you, and tell lies about you, saying every evil thing, on account of me.
5:12 Be glad and celebrate, because your reward in heaven is great. People persecuted the prophets who lived before you in the same way.
## Salt for the earth, light for the world
5:13 "You are the salt of the earth. But if salt goes flat, what can make it salty again? From then on it is worth nothing. It is thrown outside, and people walk all over it.
5:14 You are the light of the world. No one can hide a city that stands on a mountain.
5:15 Nobody lights a lamp only to set it under a grain basket. It goes on the lampstand, where it shines for everyone in the house.
5:16 Your light must shine like that in front of people, so that they see the good you do and give glory to your Father who is in heaven.
## Jesus and the Law
5:17 "Do not suppose I came to do away with the Law or the Prophets. I came not to do away with them but to fulfill them.
5:18 Amen, I tell you: until heaven and earth pass away, not one iota or one tiny hook of a letter will ever drop out of the Law, until all things have come about.
5:19 So if anyone loosens even one of the smallest of these commands, and teaches people that way, he will be called least in the kingdom of heaven. But if anyone keeps them and teaches them, he will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.
5:20 I tell you, if your righteousness does not overflow far beyond the righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees, there is no way you will enter the kingdom of heaven.
## Anger
5:21 "You have heard what the people of long ago were told: 'You must not murder,' and 'Whoever murders must stand trial.'
5:22 But I myself tell you: everyone who is angry with his brother must stand trial. Whoever says to his brother, 'Raca,' will answer to the Sanhedrin. Whoever says, 'You idiot!' will be liable to the fire of Gehenna.
5:23 "Suppose, then, you are presenting your offering at the altar, and there it comes to mind that your brother holds something against you.
5:24 Leave the offering where it is, before the altar, and go. Make peace with your brother first, and then come and present your offering.
5:25 "Be quick to make friends with the man who is suing you, while you are still on the road with him. If you don't, he may turn you over to the judge, the judge may turn you over to the guard, and you will end up in prison.
5:26 Amen, I tell you, you will not come out of there until you have paid off the last cent.
## Lust
5:27 "You have heard what was said: 'You must not commit adultery.'
5:28 But I myself tell you: anyone who looks at a woman so as to lust after her has, in his heart, already committed adultery with her.
5:29 If your right eye trips you up, pull it out and fling it away from you. You are better off losing a single part of yourself than having your whole body thrown into Gehenna.
5:30 And if your right hand trips you up, cut it off and fling it away from you. You are better off losing a single part of yourself than having your whole body go off into Gehenna.
## Divorce
5:31 "And it was said, 'A man who divorces his wife must hand her a certificate of divorce.'
5:32 But I myself tell you: if a man divorces his wife for any cause except sexual sin, he makes her an adulteress. And if a man marries a woman whose husband has divorced her, he commits adultery.
## Oaths
5:33 "Again, you have heard what the people of long ago were told: 'You must not swear falsely, but you must pay to the Lord what you promised under oath.'
5:34 But I myself tell you: do not take oaths at all. Do not swear by heaven, since it is God's throne,
5:35 or by the earth, since it is the stool for his feet, or by Jerusalem, since it is the great King's city.
5:36 Do not even swear by your own head. You do not have the power to turn a single hair white or black.
5:37 When you speak, say 'Yes, yes' or 'No, no.' Whatever goes past these has its source in the evil one.
## Do not pay back evil
5:38 "You have heard what was said: 'Eye for eye, and tooth for tooth.'
5:39 But I myself tell you: do not set yourself against the evil person. Instead, when someone slaps your right cheek, turn the other one to him as well.
5:40 When someone wants to sue you for the tunic you wear, give up your outer robe to him too.
5:41 When someone drafts you into service for one mile, go with him for two.
5:42 If someone asks you for something, give it. If someone wants to borrow from you, do not turn your back on him.
## Love your enemies
5:43 "You have heard what was said: 'You must love your neighbor and hate your enemy.'
5:44 But I myself tell you: keep loving your enemies, and pray for the people who persecute you.
5:45 That is how you will become sons of your Father who is in heaven. For he lets his sun come up over bad people and good people alike, and he sends rain on righteous and unrighteous people.
5:46 For if you love the people who love you, what reward is there in that? Don't the tax collectors do that very thing?
5:47 And if you say hello only to your brothers, what are you doing that goes beyond anyone else? Don't the Gentiles do just that?
5:48 So you are to be perfect, the way your heavenly Father is perfect.
## Giving in secret
6:1 "Take care not to put your righteousness on show in front of people, so that they will watch you. If you do, your Father who is in heaven has no reward for you.
6:2 So whenever you give to the poor, do not have a trumpet blown ahead of you. That is what the hypocrites do in the synagogues and the lanes, to win praise from people. Amen, I tell you, they are already paid in full.
6:3 Instead, when you give to the poor, keep your left hand from knowing what your right hand does.
6:4 That way your gift stays hidden, and your Father, who sees what is hidden, will repay you.
## Praying in secret
6:5 "When you pray, you must not act like the hypocrites. They are fond of standing up to pray in the synagogues and at the corners of the main streets, to put themselves on view for people. Amen, I tell you, they are already paid in full.
6:6 As for you, when you pray, go into the storeroom of your house and close the door behind you. Pray to your Father, who is there in the hidden place. And your Father, who sees what is hidden, will repay you.
6:7 "When you are praying, do not chatter on like the Gentiles. They imagine that all their talking will get them a hearing.
6:8 So do not imitate them. Your Father knows the things you have need of before you ever ask him.
## The prayer Jesus taught
6:9 You, then, should pray like this: 'Our Father, who is in heaven, may your name be honored as holy.
6:10 May your kingdom come. May your will be done, as in heaven, so also on earth.
6:11 Give us today our bread for the coming day.
6:12 And forgive us the debts we owe, just as we ourselves have forgiven the people who owe us.
6:13 And do not bring us into testing, but rescue us from the evil one.'
6:14 "If you forgive people their wrongs, you too will be forgiven by your heavenly Father.
6:15 If you do not forgive people, then neither will your Father forgive your wrongs.
## Fasting in secret
6:16 "When you fast, do not take on a gloomy look like the hypocrites. They make their faces hard to recognize, so that people will recognize that they are fasting. Amen, I tell you, they are already paid in full.
6:17 As for you, when you are fasting, rub oil into your hair and wash your face,
6:18 so that your fasting is not on show to people, only to your Father, who is in the hidden place. And your Father, who sees in that hidden place, will repay you.
## Treasure in heaven
6:19 "Do not pile up treasures for yourselves on earth. Here moths and rust eat them away, and thieves dig through the wall and steal them.
6:20 Instead, pile up treasures for yourselves in heaven. There no moth or rust eats them away, and no thieves dig through and steal.
6:21 Wherever your treasure is, that is where your heart will be.
6:22 "Your body's lamp is the eye. If your eye is sound, light will fill your whole body.
6:23 But if your eye is evil, darkness will fill your whole body. And if the light inside you is really darkness, what a darkness that is!
6:24 "No one can be a slave to two masters. He will either hate the first and love the second, or stick by the first and look down on the second. You cannot be a slave both to God and to wealth.
## Stop worrying
6:25 "So I say to you: stop worrying about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink, or about your body, what you will put on. Doesn't life mean more than food, and the body more than clothing?
6:26 Take a good look at the wild birds. They do no sowing, no reaping, no gathering into barns, but your heavenly Father feeds them. Don't you matter far more than they do?
6:27 Who among you can add even one cubit to the length of your life by worrying?
6:28 As for clothing, why are you worried about it? Learn from the lilies out in the fields, how they grow. They do no work, and they spin no thread.
6:29 Yet I tell you, Solomon with all his glory never wore anything to match one of them.
6:30 God dresses the grass of the field like that, grass that is here today and tossed into the oven tomorrow. Won't he do far more to clothe you, you people with so little faith?
6:31 So stop worrying. Stop asking, 'What are we going to eat?' or 'What are we going to drink?' or 'What will we put on?'
6:32 The Gentiles go hunting for all these things. But your heavenly Father knows you need every one of them.
6:33 Seek God's kingdom and his righteousness before anything else, and you will receive all these things in addition.
6:34 So do not fret over tomorrow. Tomorrow can do its own fretting. Today's trouble is enough for today.
## Judging others
7:1 "Do not judge, so that you are not judged.
7:2 The verdict you hand down on others is the verdict you will get. The measure you use is the one that will be used to measure out to you.
7:3 Why do you stare at the splinter in your brother's eye, while you do not notice the beam in yours?
7:4 Or how will you tell your brother, 'Here, I'll pull that splinter out of your eye,' when look, there's a beam in your own?
7:5 Hypocrite! Get the beam out of your own eye first. Then you will see well enough to pull the splinter from your brother's eye.
7:6 "Do not give what is holy to the dogs. Do not toss your pearls down in front of pigs, or the pigs will stomp on them and then turn around and rip you apart.
## Ask, look, knock
7:7 "Keep asking, and it will be given to you. Keep looking, and you will find. Keep knocking, and it will be opened for you.
7:8 All who ask receive, those who look find, and for the one who knocks it will be opened.
7:9 Is there a man among you who would hand his son a stone when the boy asks for bread?
7:10 Or would hand him a snake when he asks for a fish?
7:11 You are evil, and yet you know how to give your children good gifts. So think how much more your Father who is in heaven will give what is good to the people who ask him!
7:12 "So then, whatever you want people to do for you, do the same for them. That is the Law and the Prophets.
## Two gates, two roads
7:13 "Go in by the narrow gate. For the gate is wide, and the road that leads to ruin is roomy, and many people are going in through it.
7:14 How narrow the gate is, and how cramped the road leading to life! Not many find it.
## Trees and their fruit
7:15 "Be on your guard against false prophets. They come to you dressed up as sheep, but underneath they are wolves out for prey.
7:16 You will know who they are from their fruit. Does anyone gather grapes from thorns, or figs from thistles?
7:17 That is how it is: every good tree produces good fruit, and a rotten tree produces bad fruit.
7:18 A good tree is not able to produce bad fruit, and a rotten tree is not able to produce good fruit.
7:19 Any tree that fails to produce good fruit is being cut down and thrown into a fire.
7:20 So then, it is from their fruit that you will know them.
## Not everyone who says "Lord"
7:21 "Not all who call me 'Lord, Lord' will get into the kingdom of heaven. The one who will get in is the one who does what my Father in heaven wants.
7:22 When that day comes, many will say to me, 'Lord, Lord, wasn't it by your name that we prophesied, and by your name that we threw out demons, and by your name that we did many mighty works?'
7:23 Then I will declare to them openly, 'I never knew you. Leave me, you who practice lawlessness!'
## Two builders
7:24 "So then, anyone who listens to these words of mine and acts on them will be like a sensible man, who built his house on bedrock.
7:25 Down came the rain, in came the floods, and the winds blew and battered that house. Yet it did not fall, because its foundation had been laid on bedrock.
7:26 But anyone who listens to these words of mine and does not act on them will be like a foolish man, who put up his house on sand.
7:27 Down came the rain, in came the floods, and the winds blew and struck that house. It fell, and what a fall it was!"
## The crowds are astounded
7:28 When Jesus finished these words, the crowds were astounded at his teaching.
7:29 For he taught them as one who held authority, not as their scribes did.
