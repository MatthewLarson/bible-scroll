## A question about divorce
19:1 When Jesus finished these words, he moved on from Galilee and came into the territory of Judea on the far side of the Jordan.
19:2 Great crowds went after him, and he healed them in that place.
19:3 Some Pharisees came to him to test him. They said, "Is a man permitted to divorce his wife on any ground at all?"
19:4 He answered, "Have you not read that the Creator made them 'male and female' at the start?
19:5 And he said, 'That is why a man will leave his father and his mother and stick close to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.'
19:6 So they are no longer two, but one flesh. What God has yoked together, then, no human being should separate."
19:7 They said to him, "Why then did Moses order a man to hand her a divorce document and send her away?"
19:8 He said to them, "Moses let you divorce your wives in view of your hard-heartedness. But from the beginning it has not been that way.
19:9 I tell you that anyone who divorces his wife, except for sexual sin, and marries someone else commits adultery. And the man who marries a divorced woman commits adultery."
19:10 The disciples said to him, "If that is the man's position with his wife, it is not worth getting married."
19:11 But he said to them, "This saying is not something everyone can make room for. Only those who have been given it can.
19:12 There are eunuchs who were born that way, straight from their mother's womb. There are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by people. And there are eunuchs who made themselves eunuchs because of the kingdom of heaven. Let anyone who can make room for this make room for it."
## Jesus and the children
19:13 Next, little children were brought to him so he could lay his hands on them and pray. The disciples, though, scolded them.
19:14 Jesus said, "Leave the children alone, and do not stop them from coming to me. The kingdom of heaven is for people like them."
19:15 And after he laid his hands on them, he traveled on from there.
## The rich young man
19:16 Then a man came to him and said, "Teacher, what is the good I should do, so that I may have eternal life?"
19:17 He said to him, "Why are you asking me about the good? Only One is good. But if you want to enter into life, obey the commandments."
19:18 "Which ones?" the man asked him. Jesus said, "'You must not murder. You must not commit adultery. You must not steal. You must not give false testimony.
19:19 Honor your father and mother.' And, 'You are to love your neighbor as yourself.'"
19:20 The young man said to him, "I have obeyed every one of these. What am I still missing?"
19:21 Jesus said to him, "If you wish to be perfect, go and sell what belongs to you and give it to the poor. You will have treasure in heaven. And come, follow me."
19:22 But when the young man heard that word, he left in grief. He owned a great deal of property.
19:23 Then Jesus said to his disciples, "Amen, I tell you: a rich person will get into the kingdom of heaven only with difficulty.
19:24 I say it again: a camel could get through a needle's eye more easily than a rich person could get into the kingdom of God."
19:25 The disciples were stunned when they heard it. "Who can be saved, then?" they said.
19:26 Jesus looked straight at them and said, "From the human side this is impossible. But with God, anything is possible."
19:27 Then Peter spoke up. "Look at us," he said. "We left it all behind and followed you. What will there be for us, then?"
19:28 Jesus said to them, "Amen, I tell you: you who have followed me, when the world is born again and the Son of Man takes his seat on the throne of his glory, you too will sit on twelve thrones and judge the twelve tribes of Israel.
19:29 And everyone who left homes, brothers, sisters, father, mother, wife, children or farms because of my name will get back a hundred times as much, and will inherit eternal life.
19:30 But many of the first will be last, and the last will be first.
## The workers in the vineyard
20:1 "Here is what the kingdom of heaven is like. A man who ran a household went out as soon as it was morning to hire workers for his vineyard.
20:2 He settled with the workers on one denarius for the day's work, and sent them off to his vineyard.
20:3 Around the third hour he went out and saw others standing in the market square with nothing to do.
20:4 He told them, 'You too, go to the vineyard. I will pay you what is fair.'
20:5 And off they went. Around the sixth hour, and again around the ninth, he went out and did the same.
20:6 Near the eleventh hour he went out and found still more people standing there. He asked them, 'Why have you stood here the whole day with nothing to do?'
20:7 'No one has hired us,' they told him. He said, 'Then you go to the vineyard too.'
20:8 At evening the vineyard's owner told his foreman, 'Call the workers in and give them their pay. Start with the last ones and finish with the first.'
20:9 The eleventh-hour workers came up, and each one was paid a denarius.
20:10 Then the first ones came. They thought they would be paid more, but they too were each paid one denarius.
20:11 As they took it, they muttered complaints against the man who ran the household.
20:12 'These latecomers put in one hour,' they said, 'and you have put them on the same level as us. We carried the whole day's load and the burning heat!'
20:13 He replied to one of them, 'Friend, I am not cheating you. Was a denarius not the deal you made with me?
20:14 Take your money and be on your way. It is my wish to give this last man just what I gave you.
20:15 Am I not permitted to do as I wish with my own things? Or is your eye evil because I am good?'
20:16 That is how it will be: the last first, and the first last."
## Jesus speaks of his death a third time
20:17 On his way up to Jerusalem, Jesus took the twelve disciples off by themselves. As they walked, he told them,
20:18 "Listen. We are on our way up to Jerusalem. There the Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and scribes, and they will sentence him to death.
20:19 They will turn him over to the Gentiles, who will mock him, whip him and crucify him. On the third day he will be raised."
## A mother's request
20:20 Then the mother of Zebedee's sons came up to him with her sons. She bowed down to him, wanting to ask him for something.
20:21 "What do you wish?" he asked her. She answered, "Give the word: let these two sons of mine sit beside you in your kingdom, one on your right and one on your left."
20:22 Jesus answered, "You have no idea what you are asking for. Can you drink the cup that I am soon to drink?" "We can," they told him.
20:23 He told them, "My cup you will drink, yes. But the seats at my right and my left are not mine to hand out. They go to the ones my Father has prepared them for."
20:24 The other ten heard about it and were annoyed at the two brothers.
20:25 But Jesus called them over and said, "You know how it is with the nations: their rulers act as lords over them, and their important men use their power over them.
20:26 That is not how it will be with you. No, if anyone among you wishes to become great, he will be your servant.
20:27 And if anyone among you wishes to be first, he will be your slave.
20:28 The Son of Man is like that. He did not come to have people serve him. He came to serve, and to give his life as a ransom in place of many."
## Two blind men near Jericho
20:29 As they were on their way out of Jericho, a big crowd came after him.
20:30 And there by the road sat two blind men. Hearing that Jesus was going past, they cried out, "Lord, show us mercy, Son of David!"
20:31 The crowd sternly told them to hush. But they cried out more loudly still, "Lord, show us mercy, Son of David!"
20:32 Jesus came to a stop and called to them. "What would you like me to do for you?" he said.
20:33 "Lord," they said, "we want our eyes to open."
20:34 Jesus' heart went out to them, and he touched their eyes. At once they could see again, and they followed him.
## The king comes to Jerusalem
21:1 They were getting close to Jerusalem. When they came to Bethphage, by the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent off two disciples
21:2 with these words: "Go to the village facing you. As soon as you get there you will find a donkey tied up, and a colt with her. Set them loose and lead them to me.
21:3 Should anyone question you, tell him, 'The Lord has need of them.' He will send them straight away."
21:4 This has happened so that what was spoken through the prophet would be fulfilled:
21:5 "Tell the daughter of Zion: 'Look, your king is coming to you. He is gentle, and he rides on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a work animal.'"
21:6 The disciples went off and carried out Jesus' instructions.
21:7 They led the donkey and the colt back and laid their cloaks over them, and Jesus sat on top of them.
21:8 The great mass of the crowd laid their own cloaks down on the road. Others were cutting branches off the trees and laying them on the road.
21:9 The crowds walking in front of him and the ones coming behind were crying out, "Hosanna to the Son of David! Blessed is the one who comes in the Lord's name! Hosanna in the highest!"
21:10 As he came into Jerusalem, the entire city was shaken. "Who is this?" people were asking.
21:11 The crowds kept saying, "This man is the prophet, Jesus, the one from Nazareth in Galilee."
## Jesus in the temple
21:12 Jesus went into the temple and threw out everyone who was selling and buying in the temple. He turned over the money changers' tables and the chairs of the dove sellers.
21:13 "Scripture says, 'My house will be called a house of prayer,'" he told them, "but you are turning it into a cave for bandits."
21:14 In the temple, blind and lame people came up to him, and he healed them.
21:15 The chief priests and the scribes saw the wonders he was doing. They saw the children crying out in the temple, "Hosanna to the Son of David!" And they were furious.
21:16 "Do you hear what they are saying?" they asked him. "Yes," Jesus told them. "Did you never read this: 'From the mouths of children and babies at the breast you have made praise complete'?"
21:17 He left them behind and went outside the city to Bethany. He stayed the night there.
## The fig tree
21:18 Early the next day, on his way back into the city, Jesus felt hungry.
21:19 He spotted a fig tree beside the road and walked over to it, but found nothing on it, only leaves. "May fruit never come from you again!" he said to it. And right then the fig tree dried up.
21:20 The disciples were astonished at the sight. "How did the fig tree dry up all at once?" they said.
21:21 Jesus answered them, "Amen, I tell you: if you have faith and do not waver, you will do more than this thing with the fig tree. You could even tell this mountain, 'Be lifted up and hurled into the sea,' and it would happen.
21:22 When you pray and believe, you will receive everything you ask for."
## By what authority?
21:23 He came into the temple, and as he taught, the chief priests and the people's elders approached him. "On what authority do you do these things?" they asked. "Who handed you this authority?"
21:24 Jesus answered, "I too have one question to put to you. If you give me your answer, I in turn will tell you on what authority I do these things.
21:25 The baptism of John: what was its source? Heaven, or human beings?" They talked it over among themselves. "Suppose we say, 'From heaven.' Then he will ask us, 'So why did you not believe him?'
21:26 But suppose we say, 'From human beings.' We fear the crowd, since everyone treats John as a prophet."
21:27 So their answer to Jesus was, "We do not know." He replied, "Then I am not telling you either on what authority I do these things.
## The two sons
21:28 "Now tell me what you think. A man had two children. He came to the first and said, 'Child, today go and work in the vineyard.'
21:29 'I do not want to,' he answered. Afterward he regretted it and went.
21:30 Then the man came to the second and said the same. 'Yes, sir, I will,' he replied. But he never went.
21:31 Which of the two did the father's will?" "The first," they said. Jesus told them, "Amen, I tell you: tax collectors and prostitutes are going ahead of you into the kingdom of God.
21:32 John came to you walking the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him. The tax collectors and the prostitutes did believe him. But you, though you saw it, did not even regret it later and come to believe him.
## The tenants of the vineyard
21:33 "Hear another parable. A man who was master of a household planted a vineyard. He set a hedge around it, dug a pit in it for a winepress and put up a tower. Then he leased it out to farmers and left the country.
21:34 As the season for the fruit drew close, he sent his slaves to the farmers to receive his crop.
21:35 The farmers took hold of his slaves. One they beat, one they killed, and one they stoned.
21:36 He sent another group of slaves, bigger than the first, and they dealt with them in just the same way.
21:37 Last, he sent them his own son. 'My son they will respect,' he said.
21:38 But the farmers, seeing the son, said among themselves, 'Here is the heir! Come on, let us kill him, and his inheritance will be ours.'
21:39 They grabbed him, cast him outside the vineyard and killed him.
21:40 Now then, when the vineyard's master comes, what will he do with those farmers?"
21:41 They told him, "He will destroy those evil men in an evil way, and lease the vineyard to other farmers, who will pay him the fruit when each season comes."
21:42 Jesus said to them, "Have you never read in the Scriptures: 'A stone that the builders threw out has become the head of the corner. From the Lord this came about, and in our eyes it is a marvel'?
21:43 That is why I tell you: the kingdom of God will be taken from you, and it will be given to a nation that yields its fruit.
21:44 Anyone who falls on this stone will be shattered, and anyone it falls on, it will scatter like chaff."
21:45 The chief priests and the Pharisees heard his parables and understood that he meant them.
21:46 They were looking for a way to seize him, but they feared the crowds, who took him for a prophet.
## The wedding feast
22:1 Once more Jesus answered them, speaking in parables:
22:2 "The kingdom of heaven is like a king who put on a wedding for his son.
22:3 He sent his slaves out to summon the invited guests to the wedding, and they did not want to come.
22:4 He sent still more slaves with this message: 'Tell the invited guests, "My meal is ready. My bulls and my grain-fed animals are butchered, and it is all prepared. Come to the wedding!"'
22:5 But they did not care. Off they went, one to his own field and one to his trading.
22:6 The others grabbed his slaves, abused them and put them to death.
22:7 This made the king angry. He sent his armies, wiped out those murderers and set fire to their city.
22:8 Then he told his slaves, 'The wedding is all ready, but the invited guests did not deserve it.
22:9 Go out to where the roads leave town, and call everyone you come across to the wedding.'
22:10 The slaves went out onto the roads and brought in everyone they found, evil people and good people alike. The wedding hall filled up with diners.
22:11 The king came in to see the diners, and he noticed a man there who had not put on clothes for a wedding.
22:12 'Friend,' he said to him, 'how did you come in here with no wedding clothes?' The man was speechless.
22:13 Then the king told the servants, 'Bind his feet and hands and throw him out into the darkness outside. There people will weep and grind their teeth.'
22:14 For the called are many, but the chosen are few."
## Taxes for Caesar
22:15 Then the Pharisees went off and made a plan to catch him out with his own words.
22:16 They sent him their own disciples, with the Herodians, to say, "Teacher, we know you are honest. You teach God's way truthfully, and you do not care what anyone thinks, because you are not impressed by anyone's position.
22:17 So give us your view. Is it permitted to pay the head tax to Caesar, or not?"
22:18 Jesus saw through their wickedness. "Hypocrites," he said, "why do you put me to the test?
22:19 Let me see the coin you pay the tax with." They handed him a denarius.
22:20 He asked them, "Whose image is this, and whose name is written here?"
22:21 "Caesar's," they answered. Then he told them, "So pay back to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God."
22:22 His answer astonished them, and they left him alone and went off.
## A question about the resurrection
22:23 On that day some Sadducees came to him. They say there is no resurrection. They put a question to him:
22:24 "Teacher, Moses said, 'If someone dies childless, his brother must marry his widow and give his brother descendants.'
22:25 Well, there were seven brothers among us. The first one took a wife and then died. He had no descendants, so he left his wife to his brother.
22:26 So too with the second and the third, all the way to the seventh.
22:27 And last of all the woman died.
22:28 So at the resurrection, which of the seven will she be wife to? All of them had married her."
22:29 Jesus answered them, "You have gone astray. You do not know the Scriptures, and you do not know God's power.
22:30 In the resurrection, men do not take wives and women are not given as wives. They are like God's angels in heaven.
22:31 As to the dead being raised, have you never read the words God spoke to you:
22:32 'I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob'? God is not God of dead people but of living ones."
22:33 The crowds who heard it were amazed by his teaching.
## The great commandment
22:34 The Pharisees heard that he had muzzled the Sadducees, and they met together in one place.
22:35 And one of them, a lawyer, put a question to him as a test:
22:36 "Teacher, which commandment in the law is the great one?"
22:37 He told him, "'Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your being and with all your mind.'
22:38 This commandment is the great one, and the first.
22:39 A second one is like it: 'You are to love your neighbor as yourself.'
22:40 The whole law hangs from these two commandments, and the Prophets too."
## Whose son is the Messiah?
22:41 With the Pharisees still gathered there, Jesus put a question to them.
22:42 "What is your view of the Messiah? Whose son is he?" "David's," they answered.
22:43 He asked them, "So how can David, in the Spirit, call him 'Lord'? He says,
22:44 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand, until I set your enemies down beneath your feet."'
22:45 So if David calls him 'Lord,' in what way is he David's son?"
22:46 Nobody could say a single word in reply. And from that day on, no one dared to question him again.
## Moses' seat
23:1 Then Jesus addressed the crowds and his disciples.
23:2 "On Moses' seat the scribes and the Pharisees have sat down.
23:3 So whatever they say to you, do it and keep it. But do not act the way they act. They say things and do not do them.
23:4 They bundle up heavy burdens and load them on people's shoulders. But they will not budge those burdens even with their own finger.
23:5 All their deeds they do so that people will look at them. They widen their phylacteries and make their tassels bigger.
23:6 They love to recline in the top place at dinners and to sit in the front seats in the synagogues.
23:7 They love being greeted in the market squares and having people call them 'Rabbi.'
23:8 But you must not be called 'Rabbi.' Your Teacher is one, and you are all brothers.
23:9 Do not call anyone on earth your 'father,' either. Your Father is one: the heavenly Father.
23:10 And do not be called 'leaders,' for your Leader is one, the Messiah.
23:11 The one who is greater among you will be your servant.
23:12 Anyone who raises himself up will be brought low, and anyone who brings himself low will be raised up.
## Seven woes
23:13 "But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! You lock up the kingdom of heaven right in front of people. You do not go in yourselves, and when others are on their way in, you do not let them enter.
23:14 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
23:15 "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! You go around sea and dry land to make one convert, and once he is made, you turn him into a son of Gehenna twice as bad as you.
23:16 "Woe to you, blind guides! This is what you teach: 'If someone takes an oath by the sanctuary, that counts for nothing. But if he takes an oath by the gold of the sanctuary, he owes it.'
23:17 Foolish and blind! Which counts for more: the gold, or the sanctuary that made the gold holy?
23:18 You also say, 'If someone takes an oath by the altar, that counts for nothing. But if he takes an oath by the offering lying on it, he owes it.'
23:19 Blind! Which counts for more: the offering, or the altar that makes the offering holy?
23:20 A person who takes an oath by the altar, then, is swearing by the altar and by all that lies on it.
23:21 A person who takes an oath by the sanctuary is swearing by it and by the One who lives in it.
23:22 And a person who takes an oath by heaven is swearing by God's throne and by the One seated on it.
23:23 "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! You pay a tithe on mint and dill and cumin, but you have let go of the heavier things in the law: justice, mercy and faithfulness. These you should have done, and not let go of the others.
23:24 Blind guides, filtering out the gnat and gulping down the camel!
23:25 "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! You wash the outside of the cup and the plate, while inside they are filled with what you have grabbed and with your lack of self-control.
23:26 Blind Pharisee, start by washing the inside of the cup and the plate, so that its outside will be clean as well.
23:27 "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! You resemble tombs painted with whitewash. From outside they look lovely, but inside they are crammed with dead men's bones and every sort of impurity.
23:28 That is how you are, too. Outside, people see you as righteous. Inside, you are crammed with hypocrisy and lawlessness.
23:29 "Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! You put up tombs for the prophets and adorn the memorials of righteous people.
23:30 And you say, 'Had we lived in our fathers' days, we would not have been their partners in the blood of the prophets.'
23:31 So you give evidence against yourselves: you are the sons of the men who murdered the prophets.
23:32 You, then, fill up your fathers' measure!
23:33 Snakes! Offspring of vipers! How will you flee from the sentence of Gehenna?
23:34 Listen: because of this I am sending you prophets, wise men and scribes. Some of them you will kill and crucify. Others you will whip in your synagogues and hunt from one town to the next.
23:35 The result will be that all the righteous blood poured out on the earth will come on you, from the blood of Abel the righteous to the blood of Zechariah son of Berechiah, whom you murdered between the sanctuary and the altar.
23:36 Amen, I tell you: it will all fall on this generation.
## Jerusalem, Jerusalem
23:37 "Jerusalem, Jerusalem, killer of the prophets, stoner of those sent to her! How many times I wanted to gather your children close, the way a bird gathers her young under her wings. And you would not.
23:38 Look, your house is abandoned to you, deserted.
23:39 I tell you, from now on you will never see me until you say, 'Blessed is the one who comes in the Lord's name!'"
