## Herod and the death of John
14:1 Around that time Herod the tetrarch heard what was being said about Jesus.
14:2 He said to his court servants, "This is John the Baptist! He himself is raised from the dead. That is why the powers work in him."
14:3 Herod had arrested John earlier. He had him bound and locked away in prison on account of Herodias, who was the wife of Philip, Herod's brother.
14:4 For John had kept telling him, "The law does not allow you to have her."
14:5 Herod wished to put John to death, but he feared the crowd. To them John was a prophet.
14:6 At Herod's birthday celebration, the daughter of Herodias danced in the middle of the gathering, and Herod was delighted with her.
14:7 So he gave her his word, under oath, that he would give her anything she asked for.
14:8 Her mother had coached her, so she said, "Give me John the Baptist's head, right here, on a platter."
14:9 This grieved the king. Yet he had sworn, and the guests at his table had heard it, so he gave the order for it to be given to her.
14:10 He sent men to the prison, and they cut off John's head there.
14:11 The head came in on a platter. It was handed to the girl, and she brought it to her mother.
14:12 John's disciples came, carried away his corpse and buried him. Then they went to Jesus with the news.
## Five loaves for five thousand
14:13 Hearing the news, Jesus left that place in a boat and went off alone to a lonely spot. But the crowds heard where he had gone, and they came after him on foot from the towns.
14:14 As he stepped out of the boat he saw a huge crowd. His heart went out to them, and he healed the ones among them who were sick.
14:15 Toward evening the disciples came to him. "There is nothing out here," they said, "and it is already late. Dismiss the crowds, so they can go off to the villages and buy something to eat."
14:16 Jesus said to them, "There is no need for them to leave. Feed them yourselves."
14:17 "All we have here," they told him, "is five loaves and two fish."
14:18 He said, "Bring them over here to me."
14:19 He told the crowds to recline on the grass. He took the five loaves and the two fish, raised his eyes toward heaven and said the blessing. He broke the bread and handed the loaves to the disciples, and the disciples handed them to the crowds.
14:20 Everyone ate until they were full. And they gathered the leftover pieces: twelve full baskets.
14:21 The men who ate numbered about five thousand, apart from women and children.
## Walking on the sea
14:22 At once he made the disciples get into the boat and cross ahead of him to the far shore, until he could send the crowds home.
14:23 Once the crowds were gone, he climbed the mountain to pray in private. Evening fell, and he was there on his own.
14:24 But the boat was already many stadia out from land, and the waves were tormenting it, because the wind was blowing against it.
14:25 During the night's fourth watch, he came toward them, walking on the sea.
14:26 The disciples saw him walking on the sea and panicked. "It's a phantom!" they said, and they shrieked in fear.
14:27 Right away Jesus called out to them, "Courage! I am. Stop being afraid."
14:28 Peter answered, "Lord, if it is really you, give me the order to come to you across the waters."
14:29 "Come," Jesus said. Peter climbed down from the boat and walked on the waters, and he came toward Jesus.
14:30 But seeing how strong the wind was, he took fright. He started to go under, and he shouted, "Lord, save me!"
14:31 At once Jesus stretched out his hand and grabbed hold of him. "You with so little faith," he said to him, "why did you waver?"
14:32 As the two of them climbed up into the boat, the wind dropped.
14:33 The ones in the boat bowed down before him and said, "You really are God's Son."
## Healing at Gennesaret
14:34 They made the crossing and came ashore at Gennesaret.
14:35 The local men knew who he was, and they sent messengers through the whole district around. People carried to him everyone who was ill,
14:36 and they pleaded with him just to let them touch the tassel on his cloak. Everyone who touched it was made completely well.
## Washed hands and the tradition of the elders
15:1 Then a group of Pharisees and scribes arrived from Jerusalem. They approached Jesus with a question:
15:2 "Why do your disciples go against the tradition of the elders? They do not wash their hands when they eat a meal."
15:3 He answered, "And why do you go against God's command because of your tradition?
15:4 For God said, 'Honor your father and mother,' and, 'Anyone who speaks evil of father or mother must be put to death.'
15:5 But you teach this: suppose a man says to his father or mother, 'Whatever support you could have had from me is an offering.'
15:6 Then he is free from honoring his father. In this way you have cancelled God's word because of your tradition.
15:7 Hypocrites! Isaiah hit the mark when he prophesied about you:
15:8 'This people gives me honor with their lips, / but their heart keeps far away from me.
15:9 Their worship of me is empty; / the teachings they teach are human commands.'"
## What makes a person unclean
15:10 He called the crowd over and told them, "Hear me, and take this in.
15:11 What enters a person's mouth does not make him unclean. It is what leaves the mouth that makes a person unclean."
15:12 The disciples came up to him and said, "Did you know the Pharisees took offense when they heard what you said?"
15:13 He replied, "Any plant that was not planted by my heavenly Father will be uprooted.
15:14 Let them be. They are blind guides of blind people. When one blind man leads another, both of them will fall into a ditch."
15:15 Peter spoke up: "Tell us what this parable means."
15:16 He said, "Even now, you too fail to understand?
15:17 Don't you see? Everything that enters the mouth moves on into the belly and is thrown out into the latrine.
15:18 But what leaves the mouth comes out of the heart, and those things make a person unclean.
15:19 For it is from the heart that evil plans come out: murders, adulteries, acts of sexual immorality, thefts, false testimony, slanders.
15:20 Those are the things that make someone unclean. Eating without washing your hands does not make anyone unclean."
## The Canaanite woman
15:21 From there Jesus went out and retreated into the area around Tyre and Sidon.
15:22 Just then a Canaanite woman from those borderlands came out, shouting again and again, "Take pity on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter has a demon, and she is in a terrible state."
15:23 He gave her no answer at all. His disciples came and pleaded with him: "Send her off. She keeps shouting behind us."
15:24 His answer was, "I have not been sent to anyone but the sheep of the house of Israel that are lost."
15:25 But she came and bowed down before him. "Lord," she said, "help me!"
15:26 He replied, "It is not a fine thing to take the bread meant for the children and throw it to the little dogs."
15:27 "True, Lord," she said, "but the little dogs do eat some of the scraps that drop from their owners' table."
15:28 Then Jesus said to her in reply, "Oh, woman, your faith is great! May it happen for you just as you want." Her daughter was healed from that very hour.
## Healing on the mountain and four thousand fed
15:29 Leaving there, Jesus came to the shore of the Sea of Galilee. He climbed up the mountain and was sitting there.
15:30 Crowd after crowd came to him, and they had with them people who could not speak, people who could not see, people who could not walk, people with twisted limbs, and many others. They set them down at his feet, and he healed them.
15:31 The crowd was astonished to see speechless people talking, twisted limbs made sound, lame people walking about and blind people seeing. And they gave glory to the God of Israel.
15:32 Jesus summoned his disciples. "My heart goes out to this crowd," he said. "They have been staying with me three days now, and they have nothing left to eat. I won't send them off without food. They might collapse on the road."
15:33 His disciples said, "Out here in the wilderness, where would we find so many loaves, enough to fill up a crowd this size?"
15:34 Jesus asked, "How many loaves have you got?" "Seven," they answered, "and some little fish."
15:35 He gave orders for the crowd to recline on the ground.
15:36 Then he picked up the seven loaves and the fish, gave thanks and broke them. He kept handing them to the disciples, and the disciples handed them to the crowds.
15:37 Everyone ate until they were full. And they gathered the leftover pieces: seven big baskets, full.
15:38 The men who ate numbered four thousand, apart from women and children.
15:39 Then he dismissed the crowds, stepped into the boat and came to the district of Magadan.
## The signs of the times
16:1 Pharisees and Sadducees approached him. They wanted to put him to the test, so they asked him to show them a sign out of heaven.
16:2 He replied, "When evening comes you say, 'Good weather: the sky is red,'
16:3 and at dawn, 'Bad weather today: the sky is red and dark.' You can tell what the face of the sky means. The signs of the times, though, you cannot read.
16:4 A generation that is evil and adulterous keeps hunting for a sign. It will be given no sign except the sign of Jonah." Then he left them behind and went away.
## The yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees
16:5 The disciples crossed to the far shore, and they had forgotten to take any loaves along.
16:6 Jesus told them, "Look out! Keep yourselves clear of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees."
16:7 They talked it over among themselves and said, "We didn't take any loaves."
16:8 Jesus knew what they were saying. "You with so little faith," he said, "why are you arguing among yourselves that you have no loaves?
16:9 Haven't you caught on yet? Have you forgotten the five loaves and the five thousand, and how many baskets you picked up?
16:10 Or the seven loaves and the four thousand, and how many big baskets you picked up?
16:11 How can you miss that I was not talking to you about loaves? Keep yourselves clear of the yeast of the Pharisees and Sadducees!"
16:12 Then it dawned on them. He had not said to keep clear of the yeast in loaves, but of what the Pharisees and Sadducees taught.
## Peter's confession
16:13 Jesus came into the area around Caesarea Philippi, and there he put a question to his disciples: "What are people saying about the Son of Man? Who is he?"
16:14 They answered, "Some say John the Baptist. Others say Elijah. Still others say Jeremiah, or one of the prophets."
16:15 He said to them, "But you yourselves, who do you say I am?"
16:16 Simon Peter gave the answer: "You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God."
16:17 Jesus answered, "You are blessed, Simon Bar-Jonah, because flesh and blood did not uncover this for you. My Father in heaven did.
16:18 And I in turn tell you this: you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not be stronger than it.
16:19 To you I will give the keys of the kingdom of heaven. Anything you bind on earth will stand bound in heaven, and anything you loose on earth will stand loosed in heaven."
16:20 Then he warned the disciples: they were to tell no one that he was the Messiah.
## The way of the cross
16:21 From then on Jesus began to make clear to his disciples what had to happen. He had to go to Jerusalem. There he would suffer much at the hands of the elders, the chief priests and the scribes. He would be killed, and on the third day he would be raised.
16:22 Peter pulled him aside and started to rebuke him. "Heaven spare you, Lord!" he said. "This will never happen to you!"
16:23 He turned around and said to Peter, "Go, get behind me, Satan! You are a snare in my path. Your thoughts are not on God's things but on human things."
16:24 Then Jesus said to his disciples, "Anyone who wishes to come behind me must say no to himself, pick up his cross and follow me.
16:25 Anyone set on saving his life will lose it. But anyone who loses his life because of me will find it.
16:26 What good will it do a person to win the whole world and pay for it with his life? What could a person hand over to buy his life back?
16:27 The Son of Man is about to come in his Father's glory, with his angels, and then he will pay back each one for the way he has acted.
16:28 Amen, I tell you: among those standing here are some who will never taste death before they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom."
## The transfiguration
17:1 Six days later Jesus took Peter, James and James's brother John along, and brought them up onto a high mountain, away from everyone.
17:2 Before their eyes he was transformed. His face blazed like the sun, and his clothes turned as white as light.
17:3 Suddenly the disciples saw Moses and Elijah, in conversation with him.
17:4 Peter responded, "Lord, it's a fine thing that we are here! If you want, I will put up three booths here, one for you, one for Moses and one for Elijah."
17:5 He had not finished speaking when a cloud full of light cast its shadow over them, and a voice came out of the cloud: "This is my dearly loved Son; in him I take delight. Listen to him!"
17:6 Hearing it, the disciples threw themselves face down, overcome with fear.
17:7 Jesus came over, laid a hand on them and said, "Get up. Stop being afraid."
17:8 They raised their eyes and saw nobody there except Jesus, alone.
## Elijah has come
17:9 On their way down the mountain Jesus gave them an order: "Do not tell the vision to anyone until the Son of Man has been raised from the dead."
17:10 The disciples put a question to him: "Then why do the scribes say Elijah has to come first?"
17:11 He replied, "Yes, Elijah is coming, and he will set everything right again.
17:12 But I tell you, Elijah came already. They did not know him for who he was, and they treated him any way they pleased. In the same way the Son of Man is about to suffer at their hands."
17:13 Then the disciples saw that he had been talking to them about John the Baptist.
## A boy set free, and faith like a seed
17:14 As they reached the crowd, a man came up to Jesus and dropped to his knees in front of him.
17:15 "Lord," he said, "take pity on my son. He is moonstruck and suffers badly. Again and again he falls into the fire, and again and again into the water.
17:16 I brought him to your disciples, and they were not able to cure him."
17:17 Jesus replied, "Oh, faithless and warped generation! How long must I be among you? How long must I endure you? Bring him to me here."
17:18 Jesus rebuked him, and the demon came out of him. The boy was healed from that very hour.
17:19 Afterward the disciples came to Jesus when he was alone and asked, "Why weren't we able to throw it out?"
17:20 He told them, "Because your faith is so small. Amen, I tell you: if your faith is the size of a mustard seed, you will tell this mountain, 'Go over there from here,' and over it will go. There will be nothing you cannot do."
17:21 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
## He speaks again of his death
17:22 While they were coming together in Galilee, Jesus told them, "The Son of Man is about to be handed over into human hands.
17:23 They will put him to death, and on the third day he will be raised." The disciples were deeply grieved.
## The Temple tax and the coin in the fish
17:24 When they got to Capernaum, the men who collected the double drachma came to Peter. "Your teacher pays the double drachma, doesn't he?" they asked.
17:25 "Yes," Peter said. He went into the house, but before he could speak, Jesus asked him, "Simon, how does it seem to you? When the kings of the earth collect duties or head tax, whom do they take it from? Their own sons, or strangers?"
17:26 "From strangers," Peter replied. Jesus said to him, "So the sons go free.
17:27 Still, we do not want to trip them up. Go down to the sea and cast a fishhook. Pull out the first fish that comes up. Open its mouth and you will find a stater. Take it and pay them with it, for me and for you."
## Who ranks highest
18:1 Just then the disciples came up to Jesus with a question: "So who ranks highest in the kingdom of heaven?"
18:2 He called over a little child and had the child stand in the middle of them.
18:3 Then he said, "Amen, I tell you: if you do not turn around and become like the little children, you will not get into the kingdom of heaven at all.
18:4 So anyone who makes himself low like this child, that is the one who ranks highest in the kingdom of heaven.
18:5 And anyone who takes in one child like this for my name's sake is taking me in.
## Snares
18:6 "But if anyone trips up one of these little ones who believe in me, he would be better off with a donkey's millstone hung from his neck, sunk in the open sea.
18:7 Woe to the world because of its snares! Snares have to come, yet woe to the person through whom the snare comes!
18:8 If your hand or your foot trips you up, cut it off and fling it away from you. Better to go into life maimed or limping than to keep two hands or two feet and be thrown into the eternal fire.
18:9 If your eye trips you up, pull it out and fling it away from you. Better to go into life with one eye than to keep both eyes and be thrown into the fire of Gehenna.
## The straying sheep
18:10 "Be careful not to look down on a single one of these little ones. I tell you, their angels in heaven are always looking on the face of my Father in heaven.
18:11 [The oldest manuscripts do not have this verse.]
18:12 How does it seem to you? Suppose someone has a hundred sheep, and one of them strays off. Won't he leave the ninety-nine on the hillsides and set out to search for the straying one?
18:13 And if he manages to find it, amen, I tell you, that one sheep gives him more joy than the ninety-nine that never strayed.
18:14 In the same way, your Father in heaven does not will that even one of these little ones be lost.
## When a brother sins
18:15 "If your brother sins against you, go and correct him, with just the two of you there. If he listens to you, you have won your brother back.
18:16 If he will not listen, bring along one or two more with you, so that every statement may stand on the word of two or three witnesses.
18:17 If he ignores them, tell the church. If he ignores the church as well, then regard him as you regard the Gentile and the tax collector.
18:18 Amen, I tell you: anything you bind on earth will stand bound in heaven, and anything you loose on earth will stand loosed in heaven.
18:19 Again, amen, I tell you: if two of you here on earth are of one mind about any matter they ask for, my Father in heaven will make it happen for them.
18:20 For wherever two or three have come together in my name, I am right there in the middle of them."
## How many times to forgive
18:21 Peter came up to Jesus then and asked, "Lord, if my brother keeps sinning against me, how many times do I forgive him? Up to seven times?"
18:22 Jesus answered him, "Not up to seven times, I tell you, but up to seventy-seven times.
## The slave who would not forgive
18:23 "That is why the kingdom of heaven is like a human king who decided to go over the books with his slaves.
18:24 As he started the reckoning, they brought him a man who owed him ten thousand talents.
18:25 He had nothing to pay with, so the master gave orders to sell him, and his wife and children, and everything he owned, and to put that toward the debt.
18:26 The slave threw himself down and bowed low before him. 'Give me time,' he said, 'and I will pay it all back to you.'
18:27 The master's heart went out to that slave. He let him go and forgave him the loan.
18:28 But on his way out, that slave came across a fellow slave who owed him a hundred denarii. He grabbed him by the throat and started choking him. 'Pay up, if you owe anything!' he said.
18:29 His fellow slave threw himself down and pleaded with him: 'Give me time, and I will pay it back to you.'
18:30 He would not. Instead he went off and had him thrown into prison until he paid what he owed.
18:31 The other slaves saw what took place and were very upset. They went to their master and laid out the whole story.
18:32 His master summoned him. 'Evil slave!' he said. 'I forgave you that entire debt because you pleaded with me.
18:33 Didn't you have to show mercy to your fellow slave too, the same way I showed mercy to you?'
18:34 His master was furious and handed him over to the torturers until he paid back everything he owed.
18:35 My heavenly Father will do the same to you, unless each one of you forgives his brother from the heart."
