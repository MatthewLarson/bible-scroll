## A man with a skin disease made clean
8:1 As he came down from the mountain, many crowds followed him.
8:2 Just then a man with a skin disease came up and bowed low before him. "Lord," he said, "if you want to, you are able to make me clean."
8:3 Jesus stretched out his hand, touched him and said, "I want to. Be made clean." His skin disease was cleansed at once.
8:4 Then Jesus said to him, "Be careful not to say a word to anyone. Go and let the priest see you. Then bring the gift Moses ordered, as evidence to them."
## A centurion's faith
8:5 As Jesus went into Capernaum, a centurion came up and pleaded with him.
8:6 "Lord," he said, "my servant is laid up at home, paralyzed, in dreadful pain."
8:7 Jesus said to him, "I myself will come and heal him."
8:8 The centurion answered, "Lord, I am not good enough for you to come in under my roof. Only say a word, and my servant will get well.
8:9 I am a man under someone's authority myself, and I have soldiers under me. I say 'Go!' to this one, and off he goes. I say 'Come!' to another, and he comes. I say to my slave, 'Do this,' and he does it."
8:10 Jesus was amazed at what he heard. He said to the people following him, "Amen, I tell you, I have not met faith this great in anyone in Israel.
8:11 I tell you, many will come from the sunrise and the sunset and lie down to eat with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.
8:12 But the sons of the kingdom will be thrown out into the darkness outside. There people will weep and grind their teeth."
8:13 Then Jesus told the centurion, "Go on your way. As you have believed, so let it happen for you." The servant was healed in that same hour.
## Healing at Peter's house
8:14 Jesus came to Peter's home and saw Peter's mother-in-law laid up in bed and feverish.
8:15 He touched her hand. The fever let go of her, and she got up and began serving him.
8:16 As evening came on, people brought him many who had demons in them. With a word he threw the spirits out, and he healed everyone who was ill,
8:17 so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet would be fulfilled: "He himself took our weaknesses and carried our diseases."
## The cost of following Jesus
8:18 Seeing large crowds around him, Jesus gave the order to go over to the far shore.
8:19 One scribe came up to him and said, "Teacher, wherever you go, I will follow you."
8:20 Jesus said to him, "Foxes have their holes, and the birds in the sky have places to roost, but the Son of Man does not have anywhere to rest his head."
8:21 Another, one of the disciples, said to him, "Lord, allow me first to go off and bury my father."
8:22 But Jesus told him, "Follow me. Let the dead bury their own dead."
## Jesus calms a storm
8:23 He stepped into a boat, and his disciples went after him.
8:24 All at once a great storm shook the sea, and the waves were covering the boat. But he was sleeping.
8:25 They went to him and woke him up. "Lord, save us!" they said. "We are going down!"
8:26 He said to them, "Why are you so timid, you with so little faith?" Then he got up and spoke sternly to the winds and the sea, and a great calm came.
8:27 The men were astonished. "What sort of person is this," they said, "that even the winds and the sea do what he says?"
## Two men with demons
8:28 He came to the far shore, to the land of the Gadarenes. Two men who had demons came out from among the tombs to meet him. They were so dangerous that no one was able to get past on that road.
8:29 Suddenly they screamed, "What is there between us and you, Son of God? Did you come here to torment us ahead of time?"
8:30 A long way off from them, a big herd of pigs was grazing.
8:31 The demons kept pleading with him: "If you are throwing us out, send us off into the herd of pigs."
8:32 He told them, "Go!" They came out and went off into the pigs. Then the whole herd charged down the cliff into the sea, and they died in the waters.
8:33 The herdsmen ran away. They went into the town and told the whole story, and also what had become of the men who had demons.
8:34 Then the entire town turned out to meet Jesus. When they saw him, they urged him to move away from their territory.
## A paralyzed man forgiven
9:1 He got into a boat, went back across the water, and came home to his own town.
9:2 Just then people were bringing him a man who was paralyzed, stretched out on a cot. Jesus saw their faith and told the paralyzed man, "Courage, child! Your sins are forgiven."
9:3 Then some of the scribes said within themselves, "He is blaspheming!"
9:4 Jesus knew their thoughts. He said, "Why are you thinking evil things in your hearts?
9:5 Which is the easier thing to say: 'Your sins are forgiven,' or 'Get up and walk around'?
9:6 But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority to forgive sins here on earth..." Then he said to the paralyzed man, "Get up, pick up your cot, and go to your house."
9:7 The man got up and went off to his house.
9:8 The crowds saw it and were struck with fear. They praised God for giving people authority like this.
## Jesus calls Matthew
9:9 Moving on from there, Jesus saw a man sitting at the tax office. His name was Matthew. Jesus told him, "Follow me," and he stood up and followed him.
9:10 Later, as Jesus lay at the table in the house, many tax collectors and sinners arrived and joined him and his disciples at the meal.
9:11 The Pharisees saw it and kept asking his disciples, "Why is your teacher eating with the tax collectors and sinners?"
9:12 He heard it and said, "People who are strong do not need a doctor. Sick people do.
9:13 Go and find out what this means: 'Mercy is what I want, not sacrifice.' I did not come to call righteous people. I came to call sinners."
## A question about fasting
9:14 Next, the disciples of John came to him with a question. "We fast a great deal, and so do the Pharisees," they said. "Why do your disciples not fast?"
9:15 Jesus answered them, "The groom's friends cannot grieve as long as he is with them, can they? But days are coming when the groom will be taken away from them. Then they will fast.
9:16 Nobody puts a patch of new cloth that has not been shrunk onto an old coat. The patch would pull loose from the coat, and the tear would get worse.
9:17 Nor do people put new wine in old wineskins. Otherwise the skins split open, the wine spills out, and the skins are lost. Instead, they put new wine into fresh skins, and both are kept safe."
## An official's daughter and a woman who touched his cloak
9:18 He was still telling them these things when an official came up and bowed down before him. "My daughter died just now," he said. "But come and place your hand on her, and she will live."
9:19 Jesus stood up and followed the man, along with his disciples.
9:20 Then a woman came up from behind, one who had had a flow of blood for twelve years, and touched the tassel of his cloak.
9:21 She kept telling herself, "Just let me touch his cloak, and I will be saved."
9:22 Jesus turned around and looked at her. "Courage, daughter!" he said. "Your faith has saved you." From that hour the woman was saved.
9:23 Jesus came into the official's house. He saw the flute players and the crowd in an uproar,
9:24 and said, "Clear out! The little girl has not died. She is asleep." But they kept laughing at him.
9:25 Once the crowd was thrown out, he went in and took hold of her hand, and the little girl rose up.
9:26 The story of this went out into that whole land.
## Two blind men see
9:27 When Jesus moved on from there, two blind men came after him, shouting, "Have mercy on us, Son of David!"
9:28 He went into the house, and the blind men came up to him. Jesus asked them, "Do you believe I can do this?" "Yes, Lord," they told him.
9:29 Then he touched their eyes. "Let it happen for you in keeping with your faith," he said.
9:30 Their eyes were opened. Jesus gave them a fierce warning: "Make sure nobody finds out."
9:31 But they went out and talked about him everywhere in that land.
## A man who could not speak
9:32 As they were leaving, people brought him a man who had a demon and could not speak.
9:33 Once the demon was thrown out, the man who could not speak began to talk. The crowds were astonished. "Never has anything like this appeared in Israel!" they said.
9:34 But the Pharisees kept saying, "He throws out the demons with the help of the ruler of the demons."
## Sheep without a shepherd
9:35 Jesus traveled around all the towns and the villages. He taught in their synagogues, announced the good news of the kingdom, and cured every kind of disease and illness.
9:36 As he looked at the crowds, his heart went out to them. They were battered and lying helpless, like sheep that have no shepherd.
9:37 Then he told his disciples, "There is a big harvest, but there are only a few workers.
9:38 So plead with the Lord of the harvest to send workers out into his harvest."
## The twelve apostles
10:1 Jesus summoned his twelve disciples and put unclean spirits under their authority, so that they could drive them out and cure every kind of disease and illness.
10:2 Here are the names of the twelve apostles: first Simon, the one called Peter, and his brother Andrew; James son of Zebedee and his brother John;
10:3 Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas, and Matthew the tax collector; James son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus;
10:4 Simon the Cananaean, and Judas Iscariot, the one who also handed him over.
## Sent to Israel's lost sheep
10:5 Jesus sent these twelve out with orders. "Do not go off on a road to the Gentiles," he told them, "and do not go into a Samaritan town.
10:6 Instead, go to the sheep of the house of Israel that are lost.
10:7 On your way, announce: 'The kingdom of heaven has drawn near.'
10:8 Cure those who are ill, raise the dead, make clean those with skin disease, throw out demons. You received for free, so give for free.
10:9 Do not get gold or silver or copper to carry in your belts,
10:10 no bag for the road, no two tunics, no sandals, no staff. A worker has earned his food.
10:11 When you come into a town or a village, find out who in it is worthy, and stay there until you move on.
10:12 When you go into the house, greet the household.
10:13 If the house is worthy, let your peace settle on it. But if it is not worthy, let your peace come back to you.
10:14 If anyone does not receive you or hear what you say, then as you leave that house or that town, shake its dust off your feet.
10:15 Amen, I tell you, on judgment day the land of Sodom and Gomorrah will have an easier time than that town.
## Sheep among wolves
10:16 "Look, I myself am sending you like sheep into the middle of a pack of wolves. So be sharp like snakes and pure like doves.
10:17 Watch out for people. They will hand you over to councils and whip you in their synagogues.
10:18 Because of me you will even be taken before governors and kings, as evidence to them and to the Gentiles.
10:19 When they hand you over, do not worry about how to speak or what to say. When that hour comes, the words you should say will be given to you.
10:20 It is not you who will be doing the talking. The Spirit of your Father will be talking in you.
10:21 One brother will hand another over to be killed, and a father will hand over his child. Children will rise up against their parents and have them executed.
10:22 Everyone will hate you because of my name. But whoever holds out to the end, that person will be saved.
10:23 When they hunt you down in this town, escape to the next. For, amen, I tell you, you will surely not finish the towns of Israel until the Son of Man comes.
## Do not be afraid
10:24 "A disciple does not rank above the teacher, and a slave does not rank above his master.
10:25 The disciple should be content to become like his teacher, and the slave like his master. If they have named the head of the house Beelzebul, how much more will they do it to the members of his house!
10:26 "So do not fear them. Nothing is covered up that will not be uncovered, and nothing is secret that will not become known.
10:27 Whatever I say to you in the dark, tell it in the light. Whatever you hear in your ear, shout it from the rooftops.
10:28 And do not fear the people who kill the body but have no power to kill the soul. Instead, fear the One who can destroy soul and body alike in Gehenna.
10:29 Two sparrows sell for a small coin, don't they? Yet not one of them falls to the ground without your Father.
10:30 As for you, even the hairs on your head have all been counted.
10:31 So stop being afraid. You matter more than a great many sparrows.
10:32 "So everyone who openly declares for me in front of people, I too will declare for that person in front of my Father in heaven.
10:33 But whoever denies me in front of people, I too will deny that person in front of my Father in heaven.
## Not peace but a sword
10:34 "Do not imagine that I came to cast peace upon the earth. I did not come to cast peace, but a sword.
10:35 I came to set a man at odds with his father, a daughter with her mother, and a daughter-in-law with her mother-in-law.
10:36 Those in a man's own house will be his enemies.
10:37 "Someone who loves father or mother over me is not worthy of me. Someone who loves son or daughter over me is not worthy of me.
10:38 And someone who does not take hold of his cross and come along behind me is not worthy of me.
10:39 The one who has found his life will lose it, and the one who has lost his life because of me will find it.
## Whoever takes you in
10:40 "Whoever takes you in takes me in, and whoever takes me in takes in the One who sent me.
10:41 Whoever takes in a prophet in the name of a prophet will get a prophet's reward. Whoever takes in a righteous person in the name of a righteous person will get a righteous person's reward.
10:42 And whoever gives one of these little ones only a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple, amen, I tell you, he will not lose his reward at all."
