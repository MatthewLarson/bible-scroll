## The Temple will fall
24:1 As Jesus came out of the Temple and was going on his way, his disciples came to him. They wanted to show him the Temple buildings.
24:2 But he answered them, "You see all these things, don't you? Amen, I tell you, here not one stone will be left on a stone. Every one will be knocked down."
## The first labor pains
24:3 Jesus was sitting on the Mount of Olives when the disciples came to him by themselves. "Tell us," they said, "when will this happen? What sign will mark your coming and the completion of the age?"
24:4 Jesus answered them, "Be on your guard, so that no one leads you astray.
24:5 Many will come using my name. They will say, 'I am the Messiah,' and they will lead many people astray.
24:6 You are going to hear of wars and reports of wars. Take care not to panic. This has to happen, but the end is not here yet.
24:7 One nation will rise up against another, and one kingdom against another. There will be famines and earthquakes in place after place.
24:8 All these things are only the start of the labor pains.
24:9 "Then they will hand you over to suffering, and they will kill you. All the nations will hate you because of my name.
24:10 Many will be tripped up then. People will hand each other over and hate each other.
24:11 Many false prophets will rise up and lead many people astray.
24:12 Lawlessness will multiply, and because of it the love of many will go cold.
24:13 But whoever holds out to the end, that person will be saved.
24:14 And this good news of the kingdom will be proclaimed in the whole inhabited world, as testimony to all the nations. And then the end will come.
## The abomination and the great suffering
24:15 "The prophet Daniel told of 'the abomination of desolation.' When you see it standing in a holy place (the one who reads this should think about what it means),
24:16 then those who are in Judea must run for the hills.
24:17 Whoever is up on the roof must not go down to get things out of the house.
24:18 Whoever is out in the field must not go back home for his coat.
24:19 In those days, woe to women who are expecting, and to mothers nursing babies!
24:20 Pray that you do not have to run away in winter or on a Sabbath.
24:21 Then there will be great suffering. Nothing like it has happened from the start of the world until now, and nothing like it ever will.
24:22 Unless those days were made shorter, no one at all would be saved. But those days will be made shorter for the sake of the chosen.
24:23 "Then if someone tells you, 'See, the Messiah is here!' or 'Here he is!' do not believe them.
24:24 For false messiahs and false prophets will rise up. They will give great signs and wonders, so that even the chosen would be led astray, if that were possible.
24:25 Listen: I have told you in advance.
24:26 "So if people say to you, 'He's out in the wilderness!' do not go out there. If they say, 'He's in the back rooms!' do not believe it.
24:27 Lightning flashes out in the east and lights up the sky all the way to the west. The coming of the Son of Man will be like that.
24:28 Where there is a dead body, that is where the vultures flock together.
## The Son of Man comes
24:29 "Right after the suffering of those days, 'the sun will turn dark, the moon will not shed its glow, the stars will fall from the sky, and the powers in the heavens will be shaken.'
24:30 And at that time the sign of the Son of Man will show itself in the sky. Then all the tribes of the earth will mourn. They will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of the sky, with power and much glory.
24:31 He will send his angels out with a great trumpet, and they will gather together his chosen from the four winds, from the farthest edge of the sky to its other edge.
## The lesson of the fig tree
24:32 "Learn this parable from the fig tree. When its branch is already soft and it pushes out its leaves, you know that summer is close.
24:33 In the same way, when you see all these things, know that it is near, right outside the doors.
24:34 Amen, I tell you, this generation will not be gone before all these things happen.
24:35 Heaven and earth will be gone, but my words will never be gone.
## No one knows the day
24:36 "As for that day and hour, no one knows it. The angels of heaven do not know, and the Son does not know. Only the Father knows.
24:37 The days of Noah show what the coming of the Son of Man will be like.
24:38 Before the flood came, people went on eating and drinking. Men took wives and parents gave their daughters in marriage, until the very day Noah went into the ark.
24:39 They had no idea what was happening until the flood came and carried them all off. The coming of the Son of Man will be the same.
24:40 Then there will be two men out in a field. One is taken along, and one is left behind.
24:41 Two women will be grinding at the millstone. One is taken along, and one is left behind.
24:42 So stay awake. You do not know the day when your Lord is coming.
24:43 "You can be sure of this: if the master of the house had known during which watch of the night the thief would come, he would have stayed awake. He would not have let him dig his way into the house.
24:44 For this reason you also need to be ready. The Son of Man comes at an hour you are not counting on.
## The faithful slave and the evil slave
24:45 "Who, then, is the slave who is faithful and sensible? His master puts him over the household staff, to hand out their food when it is time.
24:46 That slave is blessed whose master finds him doing this when he comes.
24:47 Amen, I tell you, the master will put him over everything he owns.
24:48 But what if that slave is evil and tells himself, 'My master is delayed,'
24:49 and he starts beating the other slaves, and eating and drinking with men who get drunk?
24:50 That slave's master will show up on a day he is not looking for him, at an hour he does not know.
24:51 He will cut him in two and give him his share with the hypocrites. There people will weep and grind their teeth.
## The ten young women
25:1 "When that time comes, the kingdom of heaven will be compared to ten young women. They took their own lamps and went out to meet the groom.
25:2 Five of them were foolish and five were sensible.
25:3 The foolish ones brought their lamps, but they brought no oil along.
25:4 The sensible ones carried flasks of oil as well as their lamps.
25:5 The groom was delayed. They all got sleepy, and they slept.
25:6 "In the middle of the night someone shouted, 'Look! The groom! Go out and meet him!'
25:7 Then all those young women got up and got their lamps ready.
25:8 The foolish ones said to the sensible ones, 'Share some of your oil with us. Our lamps are going out!'
25:9 But the sensible ones answered, 'There might not be enough for us and for you. You had better go to the sellers and buy your own.'
25:10 They went off to buy it, and while they were gone the groom came. The ones who were ready went in with him to the wedding feast, and the door was closed.
25:11 "After that the rest of the young women came too. 'Lord, Lord,' they called, 'open up for us!'
25:12 But he answered, 'Amen, I tell you, I do not know you.'
25:13 So stay awake, since you know neither the day nor the hour.
## The talents
25:14 "It is like a man who was about to travel abroad. He called in his own slaves and handed his property over to them.
25:15 He gave five talents to one, two to another, and one to another, to each according to what he could handle. Then he left the country.
25:16 Right away the slave who got the five talents went and did business with them, and he made five talents more.
25:17 The one with two did the same and made two more.
25:18 But the slave who got one talent went away, dug in the ground, and hid his master's silver.
25:19 "Much time went by. Then the master of those slaves came and went over the accounts with them.
25:20 The slave who had gotten five talents came up and brought five more talents. 'Master,' he said, 'you handed me five talents. Look, I have made five more talents with them.'
25:21 His master said to him, 'Well done! You are a good and faithful slave. You were faithful over a little, so I will put you over much. Come in to your master's joy!'
25:22 "The slave with the two talents came up too. 'Master,' he said, 'you handed me two talents. Look, I have made two more talents with them.'
25:23 His master said to him, 'Well done! You are a good and faithful slave. You were faithful over a little, so I will put you over much. Come in to your master's joy!'
25:24 "Then the slave who had gotten one talent came up too. 'Master,' he said, 'I knew you were a hard man. You harvest where you never planted, and you collect where you never scattered.
25:25 I was afraid, so I went off and hid your talent in the ground. Here, you have what is yours.'
25:26 His master answered, 'You evil and lazy slave! You knew that I harvest where I never planted and collect where I never scattered?
25:27 Then you should have put my silver with the bankers. When I came, I would have gotten back what is mine, with interest.
25:28 Take the talent away from him, then, and give it to the slave who has the ten talents.
25:29 Everyone who has something will be given more, and he will have plenty. But the one who has nothing will lose even what he has.
25:30 As for this useless slave, throw him out into the darkness outside. There people will weep and grind their teeth.'
## The sheep and the goats
25:31 "When the Son of Man comes in his glory and all the angels come with him, then he will take his seat on the throne of his glory.
25:32 All the nations will be brought together in front of him. He will divide people from one another, the way a shepherd divides the sheep from the goats.
25:33 The sheep he will stand at his right hand, and the goats at his left.
25:34 "Then the King will tell those at his right, 'Come here, you whom my Father has blessed. Receive what you inherit: the kingdom made ready for you from the time the world was founded.
25:35 For when I was hungry, you gave me food. When I was thirsty, you gave me a drink. When I was a stranger, you took me in.
25:36 When I had no clothes, you dressed me. When I was sick, you took care of me. When I was in prison, you came to me.'
25:37 "Then the righteous will answer him, 'Lord, when did we ever see you hungry and give you food? Or thirsty and give you a drink?
25:38 When did we see you as a stranger and take you in? Or without clothes and dress you?
25:39 And when was it that we saw you sick or in prison and came to you?'
25:40 The King will answer them, 'Amen, I tell you, every time you did it for one of these brothers of mine, even the least of them, you did it for me.'
25:41 "Then he will also tell those at his left, 'Go away from me, you who are under a curse, into the eternal fire made ready for the devil and his angels.
25:42 For when I was hungry, you gave me no food. When I was thirsty, you gave me nothing to drink.
25:43 When I was a stranger, you did not take me in. When I had no clothes, you did not dress me. When I was sick and in prison, you did not take care of me.'
25:44 "Then they too will answer, 'Lord, when did we ever see you hungry or thirsty, a stranger or without clothes, sick or in prison, and not serve you?'
25:45 He will answer them, 'Amen, I tell you, every time you did not do it for one of these, even the least of them, you did not do it for me.'
25:46 So these people will go off to eternal punishment, and the righteous will go to eternal life."
