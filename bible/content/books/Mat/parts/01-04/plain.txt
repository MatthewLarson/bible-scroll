## The family history of Jesus
1:1 The book of the family history of Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham.
1:2 Abraham fathered Isaac, Isaac fathered Jacob, and Jacob fathered Judah and his brothers.
1:3 Judah fathered Perez and Zerah with Tamar. Perez fathered Hezron, and Hezron fathered Ram.
1:4 Ram fathered Amminadab, Amminadab fathered Nahshon, and Nahshon fathered Salmon.
1:5 Salmon fathered Boaz with Rahab. Boaz fathered Obed with Ruth, and Obed fathered Jesse.
1:6 Jesse fathered David the king. David fathered Solomon with Uriah's wife.
1:7 Solomon fathered Rehoboam, Rehoboam fathered Abijah, and Abijah fathered Asaph.
1:8 Asaph fathered Jehoshaphat, Jehoshaphat fathered Joram, and Joram fathered Uzziah.
1:9 Uzziah fathered Jotham, Jotham fathered Ahaz, and Ahaz fathered Hezekiah.
1:10 Hezekiah fathered Manasseh, Manasseh fathered Amos, and Amos fathered Josiah.
1:11 Josiah fathered Jeconiah and his brothers. This was at the time the people were carried off to Babylon.
1:12 After they were carried off to Babylon, Jeconiah fathered Shealtiel, and Shealtiel fathered Zerubbabel.
1:13 Zerubbabel fathered Abiud, Abiud fathered Eliakim, and Eliakim fathered Azor.
1:14 Azor fathered Zadok, Zadok fathered Achim, and Achim fathered Eliud.
1:15 Eliud fathered Eleazar, Eleazar fathered Matthan, and Matthan fathered Jacob.
1:16 Jacob fathered Joseph, the husband of Mary, and from her was born Jesus, the one called the Messiah.
1:17 So there were fourteen generations in all from Abraham to David, fourteen from David to the carrying off to Babylon, and fourteen from the carrying off to Babylon to the Messiah.
## Mary's child and Joseph's dream
1:18 Now Jesus Christ's beginning came about this way. His mother Mary was promised in marriage to Joseph. But before they had come together as husband and wife, she was discovered to be pregnant from the Holy Spirit.
1:19 Her husband Joseph was a righteous man. He did not want to make a public example of her, so he made up his mind to end the marriage in secret.
1:20 He was still thinking this over when, look, an angel of the Lord showed himself to him in a dream. "Joseph, son of David," he said, "don't be afraid to take Mary, your wife, into your home, because the child conceived in her comes from the Holy Spirit.
1:21 She will have a son. Name him Jesus, for he is the one who will save his people from their sins."
1:22 All this has happened so that what the Lord spoke through the prophet would be fulfilled:
1:23 "Look, the virgin will be pregnant and will have a son, and they will give him the name Immanuel." The name is translated "God is with us."
1:24 Joseph rose from sleep and followed the order the angel of the Lord had given him. He took his wife into his home,
1:25 but he did not sleep with her until she had a son. And he named him Jesus.
## Magi from the east
2:1 Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea while Herod was king, and look, magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem.
2:2 "Where is the newborn king of the Jews?" they asked. "We saw his star at its rising, and we have come to bow down to him."
2:3 The news alarmed King Herod, and the whole city of Jerusalem along with him.
2:4 He gathered all the people's chief priests and scribes and kept asking them, "Where will the Messiah be born?"
2:5 They answered, "In Bethlehem of Judea. That is what was written through the prophet:
2:6 'And you, Bethlehem, land of Judah, / are in no way the least among the leaders of Judah. / For a leader will come out of you, / one who will shepherd my people Israel.'"
2:7 Then Herod called the magi in private and questioned them closely about how long ago the star had shown itself.
2:8 He sent them on to Bethlehem with these orders: "Go and look hard for the child. Once you have found him, report back to me, so that I can come and bow down to him too."
2:9 They listened to the king and set off. And look, the star they had seen at its rising was going in front of them. It came and stood above the spot where the child was.
2:10 At the sight of the star they rejoiced with a very great joy.
2:11 Inside the house they saw the child and Mary, his mother. They threw themselves to the ground and bowed down to him. Then they opened their treasure chests and brought out gifts for him: gold, frankincense and myrrh.
2:12 Warned by God in a dream not to go back to Herod, they withdrew to their own country by another road.
## Escape to Egypt
2:13 After they had left, look, an angel of the Lord showed himself to Joseph in a dream. He said, "Get up! Take the child and his mother and run away to Egypt. Stay there until I give you word. Herod is about to hunt for the child to destroy him."
2:14 Joseph got up, and that night he took the child and his mother and withdrew to Egypt.
2:15 He stayed there until Herod's death, so that what the Lord spoke through the prophet would be fulfilled: "Out of Egypt I called my son."
2:16 Herod saw that the magi had tricked him, and he flew into a rage. He sent men to kill every boy in Bethlehem and in all the country around it, from two years old down, going by the time he had worked out from the magi.
2:17 Then the word spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled:
2:18 "A cry was heard in Ramah, / sobbing and loud wailing. / Rachel is weeping for her children. / She would not be comforted, because they are gone."
## Back to the land of Israel
2:19 But once Herod was dead, look, an angel of the Lord showed himself in a dream to Joseph in Egypt.
2:20 He said, "Get up! Take the child and his mother and go into the land of Israel. The ones who wanted the child's life have died."
2:21 Joseph got up, took the child and his mother, and entered the land of Israel.
2:22 But he heard that Archelaus now ruled Judea in place of his father Herod, and he was afraid to go back there. Warned by God in a dream, he withdrew instead to the region of Galilee.
2:23 He came and settled in a town named Nazareth, so that what was spoken through the prophets would be fulfilled: "He will be called a Nazarene."
## John in the wilderness
3:1 In those days John the Baptist arrived on the scene in the Judean wilderness, preaching.
3:2 His message was, "Repent, because the kingdom of heaven has drawn near."
3:3 John is the one Isaiah the prophet spoke about when he said, "A voice is shouting in the wilderness: / 'Get the Lord's road ready. / Make the paths for him straight.'"
3:4 John himself had a garment of camel hair and a leather belt tied around his waist. He lived on locusts and wild honey.
3:5 At that time Jerusalem kept going out to him, and all Judea, and all the country along the Jordan.
3:6 As they confessed their sins out loud, he baptized them in the Jordan River.
3:7 He saw many Pharisees and Sadducees coming for his baptism, and he said to them, "You offspring of vipers! Who warned you to run from the coming anger?
3:8 So produce fruit worthy of repentance.
3:9 Don't get the idea of telling yourselves, 'Our father is Abraham.' Let me tell you: God can raise up children for Abraham from these very stones.
3:10 The ax already lies against the root of the trees. Any tree that fails to produce good fruit is being cut down and thrown into a fire.
3:11 I baptize you in water, for repentance. But the one coming after me is stronger than I am. I am not good enough even to carry his sandals. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and fire.
3:12 He holds his winnowing fork in his hand. He will clean out his threshing floor. His wheat he will gather into the barn, but the chaff he will burn up with a fire no one can put out."
## Jesus is baptized
3:13 At that time Jesus arrived at the Jordan from Galilee. He came to John to be baptized by him.
3:14 John tried to stop him. "I'm the one who needs baptism from you," he said, "and you are coming to me?"
3:15 But Jesus answered him, "Allow it for now. Doing it this way is fitting for us, to fulfill all righteousness." Then John allowed him.
3:16 Once Jesus had been baptized, he came straight up from the water. And look, the heavens opened, and he saw God's Spirit coming down like a dove and landing on him.
3:17 And look, a voice out of the heavens said, "This is my dearly loved Son; in him I take delight."
## Tested in the wilderness
4:1 Next the Spirit led Jesus up into the wilderness, where the devil was to put him to the test.
4:2 He fasted for forty days and forty nights. After that he was hungry.
4:3 The tester came up to him. "If you are God's Son," he said, "order these stones to turn into loaves of bread."
4:4 But he answered, "It is written, 'People will not live by bread alone, but by every word that comes out of God's mouth.'"
4:5 Then the devil took him along into the holy city and set him on the high corner of the Temple.
4:6 "If you are God's Son," he said to him, "throw yourself down. For it is written, 'He will give his angels orders about you,' and, 'They will hold you up on their hands, so your foot does not hit a stone.'"
4:7 Jesus told him, "It is also written, 'You must not put the Lord your God to the test.'"
4:8 Once more the devil took him along, this time to a very high mountain. He showed him all the world's kingdoms and their glory,
4:9 and said to him, "I will give you all of these, if you will fall down and bow down to me."
4:10 Then Jesus told him, "Go away, Satan! For it is written, 'It is the Lord your God you must bow down to, and him alone you must serve.'"
4:11 At that the devil left him. And look, angels came and took care of him.
## Light rises in Galilee
4:12 Jesus heard that John had been handed over, and he withdrew into Galilee.
4:13 He left Nazareth behind and came to make his home in Capernaum by the sea, in the territory of Zebulun and Naphtali,
4:14 so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet would be fulfilled:
4:15 "Land of Zebulun, land of Naphtali, / on the road by the sea, across the Jordan, / Galilee of the nations:
4:16 the people who sat in darkness / saw a great light, / and for those who sat in the land and shadow of death, / a light rose up."
4:17 From then on Jesus began to preach. His message was, "Repent, because the kingdom of heaven has drawn near."
## The first disciples
4:18 Walking along the shore of the Sea of Galilee, he saw two brothers: Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother. They were throwing a net into the sea, since they fished for a living.
4:19 He told them, "Come after me, and I will make you fishermen who catch people."
4:20 Right away they dropped their nets and followed him.
4:21 He went on from there and saw another pair of brothers, James son of Zebedee and John his brother. They were in the boat with Zebedee their father, fixing their nets. He called them,
4:22 and right away they left the boat and their father and followed him.
## Teaching and healing
4:23 He traveled all over Galilee. He taught in their synagogues, announced the good news of the kingdom, and cured every kind of disease and illness among the people.
4:24 Word about him went out through all Syria. People brought him everyone who was sick, those gripped by all kinds of diseases and pains, people with demons, people who were moonstruck, and people who could not move, and he healed them.
4:25 Huge crowds followed him from Galilee, from the Decapolis, from Jerusalem and Judea, and from across the Jordan.
