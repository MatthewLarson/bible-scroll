## The plot against Jesus
26:1 When Jesus finished all these words, he told his disciples,
26:2 "You know that the Passover comes in two days, and the Son of Man is being handed over to be crucified."
26:3 Then the chief priests and the people's elders met together in the courtyard of the high priest. His name was Caiaphas.
26:4 They made a plan to seize Jesus by a trick and kill him.
26:5 But they kept saying, "Not at the festival. We don't want an uproar to break out among the people."
## A woman pours oil on Jesus at Bethany
26:6 Jesus had come to Bethany and was in the house of Simon, the man with the skin disease.
26:7 A woman came to him holding an alabaster jar of perfumed oil that cost a great deal. While he lay at the table, she poured it over his head.
26:8 Seeing it, the disciples grew angry. "What is this waste for?" they said.
26:9 "Someone could have sold it for a lot of money and given that to the poor."
26:10 Jesus knew this and said to them, "Why are you making trouble for the woman? What she has done for me is a beautiful work.
26:11 For you have the poor among you at all times. Me, though, you do not have at all times.
26:12 By pouring this oil over my body, she has gotten me ready to be buried.
26:13 Amen, I tell you, wherever this good news is proclaimed in the whole world, people will also tell the story of what she did, so that she is remembered."
## Judas agrees to hand Jesus over
26:14 Then Judas Iscariot, as he was called, one of the Twelve, went off to the chief priests.
26:15 "What will you give me," he asked, "if I hand him over to you?" They weighed out thirty silver coins and gave them to him.
26:16 After that, he kept watching for his chance to hand Jesus over.
## The Passover meal
26:17 It was the first day of the Festival of Unleavened Bread. The disciples came up to Jesus and said, "Where would you like us to get the Passover ready for you to eat?"
26:18 He said, "Go into the city to So-and-so and tell him, 'The Teacher says: My time is close. I will hold the Passover with my disciples at your place.'"
26:19 Following Jesus' instructions, the disciples made the Passover ready.
26:20 In the evening he was at the table, reclining with the twelve disciples.
26:21 During the meal he said, "Amen, I tell you, one of you will hand me over."
26:22 Deeply hurt, they began to say to him, one by one, "Lord, it isn't me, is it?"
26:23 He replied, "Someone who has dipped his hand in the bowl along with me, he is the one who will hand me over.
26:24 The Son of Man goes his way, just as it stands written about him. But woe to the man through whom the Son of Man is handed over! Better for that man never to have been born."
26:25 Judas, the one who was handing him over, answered, "Rabbi, it isn't me, is it?" He told him, "You said it."
## Bread and cup
26:26 As they ate, Jesus took a loaf of bread and said the blessing. He broke it and handed it to the disciples. "Take it and eat," he said. "This is my body."
26:27 He also took a cup and gave thanks. He passed it to them and said, "Every one of you, drink from it.
26:28 For this is my blood of the covenant. It is being poured out for many, so that sins can be forgiven.
26:29 I tell you, from this moment I will not drink this produce of the vine until that day, the day I drink it new with you in the kingdom of my Father."
26:30 After singing the hymn, they left for the Mount of Olives.
## Jesus predicts Peter's denial
26:31 Then Jesus said to them, "Every one of you will be tripped up because of me tonight. For Scripture says, 'I will strike the shepherd, and the flock's sheep will be scattered.'
26:32 But after I have been raised, I will get to Galilee ahead of you."
26:33 Peter spoke up: "Everyone else may be tripped up because of you, but I will never be tripped up."
26:34 Jesus said, "Amen, I tell you: tonight, before any rooster crows, you will deny me three times."
26:35 Peter said to him, "I will never deny you, not even if I have to die with you!" And every one of the disciples talked the same way.
## Praying in Gethsemane
26:36 Then Jesus came with them to a spot named Gethsemane. He told the disciples, "Stay seated here until I have gone over there to pray."
26:37 He brought Peter and Zebedee's two sons with him. He began to feel grief and deep distress.
26:38 He told them, "My soul is crushed with grief, enough to kill me. Wait here and stay awake with me."
26:39 He moved on a short way and threw himself face down to pray. "My Father," he said, "if it can be done, let this cup go past me. Even so, not what I want, but what you want."
26:40 He returned to the disciples. They were asleep. He said to Peter, "Were you not strong enough to keep awake with me for one hour?
26:41 Stay awake and pray that you do not come into testing. The spirit is eager, the flesh weak."
26:42 Again he went off, a second time, and prayed: "My Father, if there is no way for this to go past me without my drinking it, then may your will be done."
26:43 He came again and found them sleeping once more. They could not keep their eyes open.
26:44 He left them there and went away again. For the third time he prayed, and he said the same thing again.
26:45 Then he came to the disciples and said to them, "Go on, sleep now and rest. Look, the hour has come near. The Son of Man is being handed over into the hands of sinners.
26:46 Get up, let's go. Look, the man handing me over has come close."
## The arrest
26:47 He was still talking when Judas came, one of the Twelve. With him was a big crowd carrying swords and clubs. They came from the chief priests and the people's elders.
26:48 The man handing him over had agreed on a sign with them. "Whoever I kiss, that's him," he said. "Grab him."
26:49 Judas went straight up to Jesus. "Hello, Rabbi!" he said, and he kissed him.
26:50 Jesus said to him, "Friend, get on with what you came for." Then the others stepped up, put their hands on Jesus and held him fast.
26:51 And look, one of the men with Jesus reached out his hand and drew his sword. He struck the high priest's slave, slicing off his ear.
26:52 Jesus told him, "Return your sword to where it belongs. Everyone who takes the sword will be destroyed by the sword.
26:53 Or do you suppose I am unable to ask my Father? He would place more than twelve legions of angels beside me right now.
26:54 But then how would the Scriptures be fulfilled? They say it must happen this way."
26:55 In that same hour Jesus spoke to the crowds. "Am I a bandit, that you come out with swords and clubs to take me? Day by day I sat teaching in the Temple, and you did not lay a hand on me.
26:56 All this has happened so that the writings of the prophets would be fulfilled." Then the disciples all abandoned him and ran off.
## Before the Sanhedrin
26:57 The men who had seized Jesus brought him away to the high priest Caiaphas. The scribes and the elders were meeting there.
26:58 Peter kept following from far back, as far as the courtyard of the high priest. Once inside, he sat down among the officers to see how it would end.
26:59 The chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin were looking for false testimony against Jesus, testimony that would let them put him to death.
26:60 Many false witnesses came forward, yet they found nothing. At last two men stepped up
26:61 and said, "This man said, 'I am able to tear down God's sanctuary and build it in three days.'"
26:62 The high priest rose to his feet. "You answer nothing?" he said to him. "What are these men testifying against you?"
26:63 Jesus stayed silent. The high priest said to him, "I put you on oath before the living God: tell us, are you the Messiah, the Son of God?"
26:64 Jesus answered, "You said it. But I tell you, from now on you will see the Son of Man sitting at the Power's right hand and coming on the clouds of the sky."
26:65 Then the high priest tore his clothes. "He has blasphemed!" he said. "Why should we need witnesses anymore? You have just heard the blasphemy yourselves.
26:66 What is your opinion?" They answered, "He is liable to the death penalty."
26:67 Then they spat in his face and punched him, and some slapped him,
26:68 saying, "Be a prophet for us, Messiah! Who struck you?"
## Peter denies Jesus three times
26:69 Peter, meanwhile, sat outside in the courtyard. One of the slave girls came up to him and said, "You too were with Jesus the Galilean."
26:70 He denied it with all of them listening. "I have no idea what you mean," he said.
26:71 Out at the gateway another girl caught sight of him. "This man was with Jesus the Nazarene," she told the people there.
26:72 Again he denied it, this time with an oath: "I don't know who the man is!"
26:73 A little later those who were standing around came over and said to Peter, "You really are one of them. The way you talk shows it."
26:74 Then he began to curse and to swear, "I do not know who the man is!" At that moment a rooster crowed.
26:75 Peter remembered what Jesus had said: "Before any rooster crows, you will deny me three times." He went outside and cried bitterly.
## Handed over to Pilate
27:1 At daybreak all the chief priests and the people's elders reached a decision against Jesus: he must be put to death.
27:2 They tied him up and took him away. Then they handed him over to the governor, Pilate.
## The death of Judas
27:3 Judas, the one who had handed him over, saw that Jesus had been sentenced. Then he regretted it. He took the thirty silver coins back to the chief priests and elders
27:4 and said, "I sinned when I handed over innocent blood." They answered, "Why should we care? That's for you to see to."
27:5 He flung the silver into the sanctuary and went off. Then he went and hanged himself.
27:6 The chief priests took the silver. "The law does not allow us to put it in the Temple treasury," they said, "because it is the price of blood."
27:7 After talking it over, they spent it on the Potter's Field, to be a graveyard for strangers.
27:8 That is how the field came to be named the Field of Blood, as it still is today.
27:9 Then the word spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled: "And they took the thirty silver coins, the price of the one who was priced, the value some of Israel's sons put on him,
27:10 and they paid them out for the Potter's Field, just as the Lord had instructed me."
## Jesus before Pilate
27:11 Jesus stood before the governor, who questioned him: "So you are the King of the Jews?" "You are the one saying it," Jesus answered.
27:12 The chief priests and elders brought charges against him, but he answered nothing.
27:13 Pilate said to him, "How much they are testifying against you! Don't you hear it?"
27:14 Jesus did not reply to him with even one word, and the governor was very surprised.
27:15 At the festival the governor used to set one prisoner free for the crowd, whichever one they wanted.
27:16 Right then there was a famous prisoner in their hands, called Jesus Barabbas.
27:17 When the people had come together, Pilate asked them, "Which one shall I set free for you? Jesus Barabbas, or Jesus, the one they call the Messiah?"
27:18 For he knew it was jealousy that made them hand Jesus over.
27:19 While he was sitting on the judge's bench, word came from his wife: "Have nothing to do with that righteous man. Because of him I suffered many things today in a dream."
27:20 The chief priests and elders, though, talked the crowds into asking for Barabbas and having Jesus destroyed.
27:21 The governor responded, "Of these two, which one do you wish me to set free for you?" "Barabbas!" they said.
27:22 Pilate said to them, "Then what am I to do with Jesus, the one called the Messiah?" Every one of them said, "Have him crucified!"
27:23 He asked, "What evil has he done, then?" But they only screamed louder, "Have him crucified!"
27:24 Pilate saw that he was accomplishing nothing; in fact, an uproar was beginning. So he took water and washed his hands where the crowd could see. "I am clean of this man's blood," he said. "That's for you to see to."
27:25 All the people answered, "His blood on us, and on our children!"
27:26 So Pilate freed Barabbas for them. Jesus he had whipped, and then he handed him over for crucifixion.
## The soldiers mock the King
27:27 After that, the soldiers who served the governor led Jesus into the Praetorium and called the whole cohort together around him.
27:28 They stripped him and threw a scarlet military cloak around him.
27:29 They wove thorny branches into a crown and placed it on his head, and they put a reed in his right hand. Kneeling in front of him, they made fun of him, saying, "Hail, King of the Jews!"
27:30 They spat on him. Then they took the reed and kept hitting him on the head with it.
27:31 When they were done making fun of him, they pulled the cloak off him and dressed him in his own clothes. Then they marched him off to be crucified.
## The crucifixion
27:32 On their way out they came across a man from Cyrene. His name was Simon. They drafted him to carry Jesus' cross.
27:33 They arrived at a spot named Golgotha, which is to say, Skull Place.
27:34 They gave him wine mixed with gall to drink. He tasted it and would not drink.
27:35 They crucified him and then shared out his clothes by throwing lots.
27:36 Then they sat down there to guard him.
27:37 Above his head they fixed a written notice giving the charge: "This is Jesus, the King of the Jews."
27:38 Two bandits were crucified alongside him at the same time, one on his right and one on his left.
27:39 Those walking past kept reviling him. They shook their heads
27:40 and said, "You who tear down the sanctuary and build it in three days, save yourself! If you are God's Son, come down from the cross!"
27:41 The chief priests made fun of him too, along with the scribes and elders. They said,
27:42 "Others he saved. Himself he cannot save. King of Israel, is he? Then he should come down off the cross right now, and we will put our faith in him.
27:43 He has put his trust in God, so God should get him free now, if he takes delight in him. After all, he said, 'I am God's Son.'"
27:44 Even the bandits crucified beside him threw the same taunts at him.
## The death of Jesus
27:45 From the sixth hour until the ninth hour, darkness lay over the whole land.
27:46 Around the ninth hour Jesus shouted with a great cry, "Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?" That is, "My God, my God, why have you abandoned me?"
27:47 Hearing it, some of those standing there said, "This man is calling Elijah."
27:48 Right away one of them ran off for a sponge. He soaked it with sour wine, stuck it on a reed, and held it up for him to drink.
27:49 But the rest kept saying, "Hold on! Let's watch and see if Elijah is coming to save him."
27:50 Then Jesus gave another loud cry and let his spirit go.
27:51 And look, the curtain of the sanctuary was split from top to bottom into two pieces. The earth quaked and the rocks broke apart.
27:52 Tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who slept in death were raised.
27:53 After his resurrection they left the tombs and entered the holy city, where many people saw them.
27:54 The centurion and the men guarding Jesus with him saw the earthquake and everything that was happening. They were very frightened and said, "This man really was God's Son!"
27:55 Many women were also there. They watched from far off. These women had followed Jesus from Galilee, serving him.
27:56 One of them was Mary Magdalene. Others were Mary the mother of James and Joseph, and the mother of Zebedee's sons.
## The burial
27:57 Evening came, and so did a rich man from Arimathea. Joseph was his name. He too had been made a disciple of Jesus.
27:58 This man went to Pilate with a request for the body of Jesus, and Pilate gave orders to release it.
27:59 So Joseph received the body and wound clean linen around it.
27:60 The tomb was new, his own, carved out of the rock, and he laid the body there. He rolled a great stone up against the door of the tomb and went away.
27:61 Mary Magdalene was there, and the other Mary. They sat across from the tomb.
## A guard at the tomb
27:62 On the following day, which comes after the Day of Preparation, the chief priests and Pharisees met together with Pilate.
27:63 "Sir," they said, "we recall that that deceiver said, while he was alive, 'After three days I will be raised.'
27:64 So command that the tomb be kept secure until the third day. If not, his disciples might come and steal him and then tell the people, 'He has been raised from the dead.' And the last deception would be worse than the first."
27:65 "Take a guard," Pilate said to them. "Go and secure it as you see fit."
27:66 So they went off and made the tomb secure. They sealed the stone, and the guard was with them.
## He has been raised
28:1 When the Sabbath was over, as light was breaking toward the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to see the tomb.
28:2 Suddenly the ground shook hard. An angel of the Lord had come down out of heaven. He went to the stone, rolled it away, and sat down on it.
28:3 To look at, he was like lightning. His clothing was white like snow.
28:4 Terror of him made the guards tremble, and they were like corpses.
28:5 But the angel spoke to the women: "As for you, do not be afraid. I know you are searching for Jesus, the one who was crucified.
28:6 He isn't here. He has been raised, exactly as he said. Come and look at the spot where he was lying.
28:7 Hurry off now and say to his disciples, 'He has been raised from the dead. He is going on ahead of you to Galilee, and there you will see him.' Remember, I have told you."
28:8 They hurried from the tomb, frightened and full of joy at once, and ran to bring the news to his disciples.
28:9 And look, Jesus was there in their path. "Rejoice!" he said. They came close, held on to his feet, and bowed down before him.
28:10 Jesus told them, "Don't be frightened. Go, take word to my brothers that they should leave for Galilee. That is where they will see me."
## The guards' story
28:11 As the women went, some men from the guard came into the city and gave the chief priests a report of everything that had gone on.
28:12 The chief priests got together with the elders and worked out a plan. They gave the soldiers a good number of silver coins
28:13 and told them, "Your story is this: 'His disciples came at night and stole him while we slept.'
28:14 If the governor hears about it, we will win him over and see that you have nothing to worry about."
28:15 So they took the silver coins and acted as they had been taught. This story has spread among Jews right up to today.
## Sent to all the nations
28:16 The eleven disciples made their way to Galilee, to the mountain Jesus had appointed for them.
28:17 When they saw him, they bowed down. Yet some wavered.
28:18 Jesus came up to them and spoke: "I have been given all authority, in heaven and on earth.
28:19 Go, then, and make all the nations into disciples, baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
28:20 teaching them to keep everything I have commanded you. And look: I myself am with you every day, until the completion of the age."
