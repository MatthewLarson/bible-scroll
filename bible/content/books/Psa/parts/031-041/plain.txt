## Into your hand
31:0 For the music director. A psalm of David.
31:1 I take shelter in you, LORD; / never let me end up in shame. / Rescue me, because you are righteous.
31:2 Turn your ear toward me; / be quick to pull me out. / Become a strong rock for me, / a house of strongholds, to save me.
31:3 For you are my cliff and my stronghold. / For the sake of your name, lead me and guide me.
31:4 You will bring me out of the net / that they hid to catch me, / for you are my safe place.
31:5 Into your hand I place my spirit. / You have ransomed me, LORD, faithful God.
31:6 I hate those who devote themselves to empty nothings, / but as for me, I trust the LORD.
31:7 I will be glad and celebrate in your loyal love, / for you saw how I was suffering; / you knew me in my deep troubles.
31:8 You did not hand me over to the enemy; / you set my feet in a wide-open place.
## Wasting away
31:9 Be kind to me, LORD, for I am in trouble. / Grief has worn out my eye, / my whole self and my body too.
31:10 Sorrow has used up my life, / and groaning my years. / Through my guilt my strength has stumbled, / and my bones have worn thin.
31:11 Every foe I have has made me a disgrace, / and to my neighbors, very much so; / those who know me are afraid of me. / When people see me outside, they run.
31:12 I am forgotten like a dead man, out of mind; / I have become like a smashed pot.
31:13 For I hear many people whispering, / "Terror all around!" / They meet together against me; / they are planning to take my life.
## My times are in your hand
31:14 But I, LORD, I rely on you. / I say, "You are my God."
31:15 My times are in your hand. / Rescue me from the hand of my enemies, / from those who hunt me down.
31:16 Shine your face on your servant; / save me in your loyal love.
31:17 LORD, I have called to you; do not let me be shamed. / Let shame fall on the wicked; / let them be silenced, down to Sheol.
31:18 Let lying lips be struck dumb, / lips that speak against the righteous / with arrogance, pride and scorn.
## How great your goodness is
31:19 How great your goodness is, / which you have hidden away for those who fear you, / and done for those who shelter in you, / with all humanity watching!
31:20 You tuck them away in the secret place of your face, / away from men's schemes; / you keep them in a shelter, / safe from tongues that quarrel.
31:21 Blessed be the LORD, / for he has shown me the wonder of his loyal love / in a city ringed by siege.
31:22 As for me, I said in my panic, / "I have been cut away from before your eyes." / Yet you heard me pleading aloud / as I called out to you for help.
31:23 Love the LORD, every one of his faithful! / The LORD keeps safe those who stay true, / but he pays back in full / whoever behaves with pride.
31:24 Stay strong; let your hearts stand firm, / all who wait for the LORD.
## The joy of being forgiven
32:0 Of David. A maskil.
32:1 Happy is the one whose rebellion is lifted away, / whose sin is covered over.
32:2 Happy is the person the LORD does not charge with guilt, / whose spirit holds no trickery.
## When I said nothing
32:3 As long as I said nothing, my bones were wearing out / with my roaring the whole day.
32:4 For your hand weighed on me by day and by night. / My sap was turned, as in the droughts of summer. Selah
32:5 Then I let you know my sin; / I did not cover up my guilt. / I said, "I will confess my rebellious acts to the LORD." / And you, you lifted away the guilt of my sin. Selah
## A hiding place
32:6 So let every faithful person pray to you / at the time for finding you. / Surely when the great waters flood, / they will not reach him.
32:7 You are a place for me to hide; / from trouble you keep me safe; / with cries of rescue you ring me round. Selah
32:8 "I will make you wise and show you / the road you should walk. / My eye will be on you as I advise you.
32:9 Do not be like a horse or a mule / that has no sense. / It takes bit and bridle, its gear, to hold it in; / otherwise it will not come near you."
32:10 Pains are many for the wicked; / but the one who trusts the LORD, loyal love wraps him around.
32:11 Be glad in the LORD and celebrate, you righteous! / Shout for joy, all you whose hearts are straight!
## A new song
33:1 Shout for joy, you righteous, in the LORD! / For the upright, praise is lovely.
33:2 Give thanks to the LORD on the lyre; / play for him on a ten-string harp.
33:3 Sing him a new song; / play your best, and raise the shout.
33:4 For the LORD's word is upright, / and he does all his work faithfully.
33:5 He loves righteousness and fair judgment; / the LORD's loyal love fills the earth.
## By his word
33:6 The heavens were made by the LORD's word, / all their starry army by the breath of his mouth.
33:7 He heaps up the sea's waters like a mound; / he lays the deeps away in storerooms.
33:8 Let all the earth fear the LORD; / let all who live in the world tremble before him.
33:9 For he said it, and it was; / he gave the order, and there it stood.
33:10 The LORD breaks up the plans of the nations; / he blocks what the peoples intend.
33:11 The LORD's plan stands forever, / what his heart intends, from generation to generation.
## The nation he chose
33:12 Happy is the nation whose God is the LORD, / the people he chose as his own inheritance.
33:13 From the heavens the LORD looks; / he sees all humanity.
33:14 From his settled home he peers out / at all the earth's people.
33:15 He shapes the hearts of them all; / he understands everything they do.
33:16 A king does not win safety by having a big army; / a warrior is not rescued by sheer strength.
33:17 The horse is a lie when you hope for victory; / for all its great strength, it cannot get anyone away.
## The eye of the LORD
33:18 Look: the LORD's eye is toward those who fear him, / who hope for his loyal love,
33:19 to rescue them from death / and keep them living through famine.
33:20 We wait for the LORD with all our being; / our helper and our shield, that is he.
33:21 It is in him our hearts are glad, / because it is his holy name we rely on.
33:22 LORD, let your loyal love rest on us, / as our hope is set on you.
## Taste and see
34:0 Of David, when he changed his behavior in front of Abimelech, who drove him out, and he went away.
34:1 At every time I will bless the LORD; / his praise will never leave my mouth.
34:2 My whole self boasts in the LORD; / let the lowly hear and rejoice.
34:3 Make the LORD great with me; / let us lift high his name together.
34:4 I looked for the LORD, and he answered me; / he rescued me from everything I feared.
34:5 They looked to him and their faces shone; / their faces will never be red with shame.
34:6 Here was a poor man who called out; the LORD heard, / and from all his troubles he saved him.
34:7 The angel of the LORD pitches camp around those who fear him, / and he pulls them free.
34:8 Taste and see: the LORD is good! / Happy is the one who takes shelter in him.
34:9 Fear the LORD, you his holy ones, / for those who fear him never go short.
34:10 Young lions run short and go hungry, / but those who seek the LORD do not go without anything good.
## Come, children
34:11 Come here, children, and hear me out; / I will teach you how to fear the LORD.
34:12 Is there anyone who wants life, / who longs for days of seeing good?
34:13 Keep your tongue away from evil / and your lips from words of deceit.
34:14 Turn aside from evil and do what is good; / look for peace and run after it.
34:15 The LORD's eyes turn toward the righteous, / and his ears toward their cry for help.
34:16 The LORD sets his face against evildoers, / to wipe their memory from the earth.
34:17 They cry, and the LORD is listening; / out of all their troubles he pulls them.
34:18 Near to the broken-hearted is the LORD; / he saves those whose spirits are crushed.
34:19 Many are the evils that strike a righteous person, / but out of all of them the LORD rescues him.
34:20 He keeps watch over all his bones; / not a single one gets broken.
34:21 Evil will kill the wicked; / and the haters of the righteous will be found guilty.
34:22 The LORD buys back the lives of his servants; / no one who takes shelter in him will be found guilty.
## Fight for me, LORD
35:0 Of David.
35:1 LORD, take up my case against those who take up cases against me; / fight those who fight me.
35:2 Grab the small shield and the large one, / and stand up to help me.
35:3 Draw out the spear and the battle-axe / to face those who chase me. / Tell my soul, "I am the one who saves you."
35:4 Let those who are after my life / be shamed and disgraced. / Let those who plot to hurt me / be driven back and made red-faced.
35:5 Let them be like chaff blown by the wind, / as the LORD's angel pushes them on.
35:6 Let their path turn dark and slippery, / as the LORD's angel chases them.
35:7 Though I gave them no reason, they hid their net for me; / for no reason they dug a pit to take my life.
35:8 Let ruin take him by surprise; / let his own hidden net catch him; / into it, into ruin, let him fall.
35:9 Then my whole self will be glad in the LORD / and thrilled with his rescue.
35:10 Every bone in me will say, / "LORD, who is like you? / You rescue the poor from those too strong for them, / the poor and needy from those who rob them."
## Evil for good
35:11 Violent witnesses stand up; / they question me about things I know nothing of.
35:12 They pay me back evil for good; / I am left like a parent robbed of children.
35:13 But when they were sick, I dressed in sackcloth; / I bowed my soul low with fasting, / and my prayer kept coming back to my own chest.
35:14 As for a friend, as for my own brother, I walked about; / bowed and dark with grief, I mourned as for a mother.
35:15 But when I stumbled, they were glad and gathered; / the beaten-down gathered against me, people I did not know. / They tore at me and did not stop.
35:16 Like godless men who mock for a cake of bread, / they ground their teeth at me.
35:17 Lord, how long will you just look on? / Bring my life back from their ravages, / my only life from the young lions.
35:18 I will thank you in the great gathering; / I will praise you among a crowd of people.
## Do not let them gloat
35:19 Do not let my enemies who lie about me / make merry over me; / do not let those who hate me for no reason / wink at me.
35:20 For they do not speak peace; / against the quiet people of the land / they plot deceitful words.
35:21 Their mouths gape wide at me. / They say, "Aha! Aha! We saw it with our own eyes!"
35:22 LORD, you have seen it; do not stay silent. / Lord, do not stay far from me.
35:23 Stir! Wake up to judge my case, / my God and my Lord, to plead my cause.
35:24 Judge me by your righteousness, LORD my God, / and do not let them make merry over me.
35:25 Do not let them say in their hearts, "Aha, we got our wish!" / Do not let them say, "We have swallowed him whole."
35:26 Let all who are happy at my trouble / be shamed and red-faced together. / Let those who make themselves big against me / be dressed in shame and disgrace.
35:27 Let those who want me proved right / shout and be glad. / Let them say at all times, "Great is the LORD, / who takes pleasure in his servant's well-being!"
35:28 Then my tongue will tell of your righteousness, / of your praise, the whole day.
## Rebellion's oracle
36:0 For the music director. Of David, the LORD's servant.
36:1 Rebellion speaks like a prophet to the wicked / deep inside his heart. / He has no dread of God / before his eyes.
36:2 In his own eyes he flatters himself / too smoothly to find his guilt and hate it.
36:3 His mouth speaks trouble and lies; / he has given up being wise, being good.
36:4 Lying in bed, he works out trouble; / he takes his stand on a road that is no good; / evil he does not refuse.
## Your loyal love in the heavens
36:5 LORD, your loyal love is in the heavens, / your faithfulness reaches the clouds.
36:6 Like the mountains of God is your righteousness; / your judgments, the great deep. / Humans and animals alike you keep safe, LORD.
36:7 How precious your loyal love is, God! / The children of Adam take shelter / in the shadow of your wings.
36:8 They have their fill of the rich food of your house; / and from your river of delights you give them drink.
36:9 For with you is the spring of life; / in your light we see light.
36:10 Keep on giving your loyal love to those who know you, / your righteousness to those whose hearts are straight.
36:11 Do not let the foot of the proud come at me, / or let wicked hands make me a wanderer.
36:12 There the evildoers have fallen; / they are thrown down and cannot get up.
## Do not burn up inside
37:0 Of David.
37:1 Do not burn up inside over people who do evil; / do not envy those who do wrong.
37:2 For they will soon dry up like grass / and wilt like green plants.
37:3 Trust the LORD and do good; / live in the land and graze on faithfulness.
37:4 Find your delight in the LORD, / and he will give you what your heart asks for.
37:5 Roll your way onto the LORD; / lean on him, and he himself will act.
37:6 He will make your righteousness shine out like light, / your just cause like the noon sun.
37:7 Go quiet before the LORD and wait for him with longing. / Do not burn up inside over someone whose way goes well, / over the man who makes his plots come true.
37:8 Let go of anger and drop your rage. / Do not burn up inside; it only leads to evil.
37:9 For evildoers will be cut off; / those who wait for the LORD, they will take possession of the land.
37:10 Just a little longer, and the wicked will be gone. / You will study the spot where he was, and he is not there.
37:11 But the humble will take possession of the land / and delight in great peace.
## The wicked plot
37:12 The wicked man schemes against the righteous / and grinds his teeth at him.
37:13 The Lord laughs at him, / for he can see his day on its way.
37:14 The wicked pull out swords and string their bows / to knock down the poor and the needy / and slaughter people who walk straight.
37:15 But the sword they hold will end up in their own heart, / and their bows will snap.
37:16 The little that a righteous person has is better / than the riches of many wicked people.
37:17 For the wicked will have their arms broken, / while the LORD props up the righteous.
37:18 The LORD keeps track of the days of the wholehearted; / their inheritance will last forever.
37:19 When times are evil, they will have no shame; / when famine comes, they will eat their fill.
37:20 But the wicked will be lost; / the LORD's enemies, like the splendor of the fields, / are finished; in smoke they are finished.
## Borrowing and giving
37:21 The wicked man takes a loan and never repays, / but the righteous man shows kindness and gives.
37:22 For those he blesses will take possession of the land, / but those he curses will be cut off.
37:23 It is the LORD who makes a man's steps firm, / and he delights in his way.
37:24 He may fall, but he will not be thrown flat, / because the LORD is holding his hand.
37:25 I was a young man, and now I am old too, / and I have not seen a righteous man left on his own, / or his offspring hunting for food.
37:26 All day long he is generous and lends, / and his offspring turn into a blessing.
37:27 Turn aside from evil and do good, / and you will have a home forever.
37:28 For the LORD loves justice / and will not abandon his faithful ones. / They are kept safe forever, / but the wicked's offspring is cut off.
37:29 The righteous will take possession of the land / and live in it forever.
## The law in his heart
37:30 The mouth of the righteous murmurs wisdom, / and his tongue talks of justice.
37:31 The law of his God is in his heart; / his steps never slide.
37:32 The wicked man watches for the righteous / and looks for a chance to kill him.
37:33 The LORD will not leave him in his hand, / nor declare him guilty at his trial.
37:34 Wait for the LORD and keep to his way, / and he will lift you up to take possession of the land. / You will see it when the wicked are cut off.
37:35 I have seen a wicked man, ruthless, / spreading himself out like a lush tree in its native soil.
37:36 But he passed on, and look, he was gone. / I searched for him, but he could not be found.
37:37 Watch the wholehearted and look at the upright, / for the man of peace has a future.
37:38 But rebels will be destroyed all together; / the future of the wicked will be cut off.
37:39 The rescue of the righteous comes from the LORD, / their fortress when trouble comes.
37:40 The LORD came to help them and set them free; / he frees them from the wicked and brings them safe, / because they ran to him for shelter.
## Your arrows in me
38:0 A psalm of David. To bring to remembrance.
38:1 LORD, not in your fury, do not rebuke me; / not in your rage, do not discipline me.
38:2 For your arrows have come down into me, / and your hand has come down on me.
38:3 Your indignation has left no whole place in my flesh; / my sin has left no peace in my bones.
38:4 For my guilty deeds have climbed above my head; / as a heavy load they outweigh me.
38:5 They reek and run, these wounds of mine, / because I was a fool.
38:6 I am twisted and utterly bent double; / I walk in mourning black from morning to night.
38:7 For my loins are full of scorching pain; / there is no whole place in my flesh.
38:8 I am numb and crushed completely; / I roar because my heart groans.
## My friends stand back
38:9 Lord, everything I long for lies open before you; / my groans are no secret to you.
38:10 My heart is racing; my strength has deserted me. / The light of my eyes, even that is no longer with me.
38:11 My friends and companions keep their distance from the blow that struck me; / my relatives stay a long way off.
38:12 People hunting my life set out traps; / those looking for my harm speak of disaster / and murmur lies the whole day.
38:13 But I, like a deaf man, hear nothing; / like a man who cannot speak, I keep my mouth shut.
38:14 I am like someone who hears nothing, / with no answer to give back.
## I wait for you
38:15 For I have set my hope on you, LORD; / you, Lord my God, will answer.
38:16 For I said, "Or else they will make merry over me; / when my foot gives way, they lord it over me."
38:17 For I am about to stumble, / and my pain never leaves my sight.
38:18 For I tell about my guilt; / my sin fills me with worry.
38:19 But my enemies are alive and strong; / those who hate me falsely are many.
38:20 Those who pay back evil for good / are my accusers, since I run after good.
38:21 Do not abandon me, LORD; / my God, do not keep away from me.
38:22 Hurry to help me, / Lord, my salvation.
## A muzzle on my mouth
39:0 For the music director. For Jeduthun. A psalm of David.
39:1 I said, "I will watch how I go, / and keep my tongue from sin. / My mouth will wear a muzzle / while the wicked man stands before me."
39:2 I was silent, completely quiet; / I held still, though it did no good, / and my pain was stirred up.
39:3 My heart was burning inside me; / while I groaned, a fire blazed. / My tongue spoke up:
## A puff of air
39:4 "LORD, show me my end, / how many days I am measured out. / Let me know what a passing thing I am.
39:5 Look, you have made my days handbreadths; / my span is nothing before you. / Every human being, even standing firm, / is nothing but a puff of air. Selah
39:6 A person walks about, only a shadow-shape; / all their uproar is only a puff of air. / He heaps things up, not knowing who will haul them in.
39:7 And now, Lord, what am I waiting for? / My hope, it is in you.
39:8 Rescue me from all my rebellious acts. / Do not make me something fools jeer at.
39:9 I have fallen silent; my mouth stays shut, / because this is your doing.
39:10 Take your blow off me; / the attack of your hand has finished me.
39:11 You punish a person with rebukes for guilt, / and like a moth you melt away what he holds dear. / Every human being is only a puff of air. Selah
## A foreigner with you
39:12 LORD, hear my prayer; / turn your ear to my call for help; / do not keep silent at my tears. / For I am a foreigner with you, / a lodger, as all my fathers were.
39:13 Take your eyes off me, so I can brighten up / before I go away and am no more."
## A new song in my mouth
40:0 For the music director. Of David. A psalm.
40:1 I waited and waited for the LORD; / he bent down to me and heard my cry for help.
40:2 He lifted me out of the roaring pit, / out of the muddy swamp. / He stood my feet up on a rock / and made my steps steady.
40:3 He gave my mouth a new song, / praise for our God. / Many will see it and be afraid, / and they will trust the LORD.
40:4 Happy is the man who has set his trust on the LORD, / who has not turned to the arrogant / or to those who drift off after lies.
40:5 You have done so much, LORD my God: / your wonders, your plans for us. / Nothing can be set next to you. / I would tell them, I would speak of them, / but they are too many to count.
## Ears you have dug
40:6 Sacrifice and grain offering you did not want; / ears you have dug for me. / Burnt offering and sin offering you did not ask for.
40:7 Then I said, "Look, I have come. / The scroll of the book has it written about me.
40:8 To do what you want, my God, is my delight; / your law is in my very core."
## I did not keep it hidden
40:9 I have brought the good news of righteousness / to the great gathering. / Look, I do not hold back my lips, / LORD, as you know.
40:10 Your righteousness I have not buried in my heart; / of your faithfulness and your rescue I have spoken; / I have not kept your loyal love and your faithfulness / hidden from the great gathering.
40:11 LORD, you will not shut off your compassion from me; / your loyal love and faithfulness will stand guard over me always.
## Troubles past counting
40:12 For troubles beyond counting have closed in around me. / My guilty deeds have caught up with me, and I cannot see. / They outnumber the hairs on my head, / and my heart has deserted me.
40:13 LORD, may it please you to rescue me! / LORD, hurry to my help!
40:14 Let those who are after my life, to sweep it away, / be shamed and red-faced together. / Let those who want to hurt me / be driven back and disgraced.
40:15 Let those who say to me, "Aha! Aha!" / be stunned because of their shame.
40:16 Let all who seek you / celebrate and be glad in you. / Let those who love your rescue / say at all times, "Great is the LORD!"
40:17 As for me, I am poor and needy; / may the Lord keep me in mind. / You are my helper and my rescuer; / my God, do not be late.
## Happy is the one who cares
41:0 For the music director. A psalm of David.
41:1 Happy is the one who pays attention to the weak; / when the bad day comes, the LORD will get him out.
41:2 The LORD will guard him and keep him alive; / he will be called happy in the land. / Do not hand him over to the greed of his enemies.
41:3 The LORD will hold him up on his sickbed; / when he is ill, you make over his whole bed.
## My close friend
41:4 As for me, I said, "LORD, be kind to me; / heal my very self, because I have sinned against you."
41:5 My enemies say evil things about me: / "When will he die, and his name be lost?"
41:6 If one comes to visit, he speaks empty words; / his heart collects trouble, / and he goes outside and talks.
41:7 Together they whisper against me, all who hate me; / they think up harm for me, and say,
41:8 "Something vile has been poured into him; / now that he is lying down, he will not get up again."
41:9 Even the man I was at peace with, the one I trusted, / who ate my bread, / has raised his heel high against me.
41:10 But you, LORD, be kind to me and raise me up, / so that I can pay them back.
41:11 This is how I know you take delight in me: / my enemy raises no shout of triumph over me.
41:12 As for me, in my integrity you have held me up, / and you set me before your face forever.
## The end of Book One
41:13 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, / from the ages past to the ages to come. / Amen and amen.
