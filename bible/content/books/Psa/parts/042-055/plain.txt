## Thirsting for God far from the temple
42:0 For the music director. A maskil of the sons of Korah.
42:1 Like a deer that craves the water channels, / my soul craves you, O God.
42:2 My soul is thirsty for God, for the living God. / When will I come / and appear before the face of God?
42:3 Day and night my tears have become my bread, / as they say to me all day long, "Where is your God?"
42:4 I call these things to mind, / pouring out my soul within me: / how I used to pass along in the throng / and lead them slowly up to the house of God, / with the sound of shouting and thanks, / a crowd keeping the feast.
42:5 Why are you so bowed down, my soul? / Why do you groan within me? / Wait for God, for I will thank him again / for the saving help of his face.
## Deep calls to deep
42:6 My God, my soul is bowed down within me. / That is why I call you to mind / from the Jordan country and the peaks of Hermon, / from Mount Mizar.
42:7 Deep calls out to deep / at the sound of your water shafts. / Every breaker of yours, every wave, / has passed over me.
42:8 The LORD gives orders for his loyal love by day, / and in the night his song is beside me, / a prayer to the God of my life.
42:9 I will say to God, my rock, / "Why have you forgotten me? / Why must I go about in black, / with the enemy pressing me?"
42:10 With a crushing in my bones my foes mock me, / as all day long they say to me, "Where is your God?"
42:11 Why are you so bowed down, my soul? / Why do you groan within me? / Wait for God, for I will thank him again, / the saving help of my face, and my God.
## Send your light
43:1 Give judgment for me, O God, / and take up my case against a nation without loyalty. / From the man of deceit and wrong / set me free.
43:2 For you are the God of my stronghold. / Why have you pushed me away? / Why must I go about in black, / with the enemy pressing me?
43:3 Send your light and your faithfulness. / They will guide me; / they will bring me to your holy mountain / and to the places where you dwell.
43:4 Then I will come to the altar of God, / to God, the gladness of my joy, / and I will thank you on the lyre, / O God, my God.
43:5 Why are you so bowed down, my soul? / Why do you groan within me? / Wait for God, for I will thank him again, / the saving help of my face, and my God.
## What you did in the old days
44:0 For the music director. Of the sons of Korah. A maskil.
44:1 O God, with our own ears we have heard; / our ancestors have recounted to us / the work you did in their days, / in the days of long ago.
44:2 It was your hand that drove nations out, / and them you planted. / You brought harm on peoples, / and them you let spread out.
44:3 It was not by their sword that they took the land, / and their own arm did not save them. / It was your right hand and your arm / and the light of your face, / because you delighted in them.
44:4 You yourself are my King, O God. / Give the order for Jacob's victories.
44:5 Through you we gore our foes; / in your name we stamp down our attackers.
44:6 I will not rely on my bow; / my sword is not what saves me.
44:7 No, you rescued us from our enemies, / and you brought shame on those who hate us.
44:8 All day long we have made our boast in God, / and we will thank your name for all time. Selah
## But now you have rejected us
44:9 But now you have pushed us away and disgraced us. / You do not go out anymore at the head of our armies.
44:10 You make us turn our backs to the foe, / and those who hate us take plunder for themselves.
44:11 You have made us like sheep kept for meat / and among the nations you have scattered us.
44:12 You sell your people for no money at all; / their price did not make you richer.
44:13 You make us a disgrace to our neighbors, / something to laugh at and sneer at for those around us.
44:14 You make us a saying of scorn among the nations, / a shaking of heads among the peoples.
44:15 My dishonor faces me all day long, / and the shame of my face has covered me over,
44:16 at the voice of the one who taunts and slanders, / before the face of the enemy who takes revenge.
## We did not forget you
44:17 All this came on us, / yet we did not forget you / or play false with your covenant.
44:18 Our hearts did not slip backward; / our feet did not leave your path.
44:19 But you crushed us down where jackals live / and covered us with deep shadow.
44:20 Had we forgotten our God's name / or spread our palms to a strange god,
44:21 would God not search it out? / For he knows what is hidden in the heart.
44:22 No, it is on your account that we are killed all day long; / we are looked on as a flock for slaughter.
44:23 Get up! Why do you sleep, Lord? / Wake up! Do not push us away for good.
44:24 Why do you hide your face / and forget how we suffer and are pressed down?
44:25 For our life has sunk down to the dust; / our belly sticks to the earth.
44:26 Get up, come to our help, / and ransom us for your loyal love's sake.
## A song for the king's wedding
45:0 For the music director. On "Lilies." Of the sons of Korah. A maskil. A love song.
45:1 My heart bubbles over with a good word. / I speak my work to the king; / my tongue is the pen of a swift scribe.
45:2 You are more handsome than any human being; / charm flows over your lips. / So God has blessed you for all time.
45:3 Strap your sword on your thigh, mighty one, / your splendor and your majesty.
45:4 In your majesty, ride and win, / for the sake of truth and of humble justice. / Let your right hand show you deeds that strike awe.
45:5 Your arrows are sharp. / Peoples fall beneath you. / They strike the heart of the king's enemies.
45:6 Your throne, O God, is for ever and ever. / A scepter of fairness is your royal scepter.
45:7 You have loved justice and hated wrong. / So God, your God, has anointed you / with the oil of joy, more than your fellows.
45:8 Your clothes are all myrrh and aloes and cassia. / From palaces of ivory, the music of strings makes you glad.
45:9 Among the ladies you prize are daughters of kings. / At your right the queen takes her place, / dressed in gold of Ophir.
## Listen, daughter
45:10 Hear, daughter, and see; bend your ear. / Forget your own people and your father's household.
45:11 The king will crave your beauty. / Because he is your lord, bow low before him.
45:12 Daughter Tyre comes with a gift; / the rich among the people will court your favor.
45:13 The king's daughter is all splendor inside the palace; / her clothing is of gold mesh.
45:14 In bright woven garments she is brought to the king; / the virgins behind her, her friends, / are being brought to you.
45:15 They are brought with joy and celebration; / they go into the palace of the king.
45:16 Your sons will take your fathers' place. / You will set them up as princes in all the land.
45:17 I will keep your name in memory through all generations, / so peoples will give you thanks for ever and ever.
## God is our refuge
46:0 For the music director. Of the sons of Korah. On alamoth. A song.
46:1 God is a shelter and strength for us, / a help in troubles, readily found.
46:2 So we will have no fear when the earth changes place, / when mountains slip into the heart of the seas.
46:3 Its waters may roar and churn; / the mountains may quake as the sea swells up. Selah
## The city of God
46:4 A river! Its channels gladden the city of God, / the holy place where the Most High dwells.
46:5 God is inside her; she will not totter. / God helps her at the turning of the morning.
46:6 Nations were in uproar; kingdoms shook. / He raised his voice; the earth melted.
46:7 The LORD of Armies is with us; / the God of Jacob is a high fort for us. Selah
## Come and see
46:8 Come, look at the LORD's deeds, / the havoc he has placed on the earth.
46:9 He puts an end to wars as far as the earth reaches. / The bow he breaks, the spear he cuts in two, / the wagons he burns with fire.
46:10 "Let go, and know that I am God. / I will be raised high among the nations; / I will be raised high on the earth."
46:11 The LORD of Armies is with us; / the God of Jacob is a high fort for us. Selah
## God has gone up
47:0 For the music director. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.
47:1 All peoples, clap your hands. / Raise a shout to God with the sound of joy.
47:2 For the LORD Most High is awesome, / a great King over the whole earth.
47:3 He brings peoples down under us, / nations beneath our feet.
47:4 He chooses our inheritance for us, / Jacob's pride, whom he loved. Selah
47:5 God has gone up with a shout of joy, / the LORD with the sound of the ram's horn.
47:6 Sing praise to God, sing praise! / Sing praise to our King, sing praise!
47:7 For God is King of all the earth; / sing praise with a maskil.
47:8 God rules as King over the nations; / God has taken his seat on his holy throne.
47:9 The nobles of the peoples have gathered / the people of the God of Abraham. / For God owns the shields of the earth; / he is lifted very high.
## The city of the great King
48:0 A song. A psalm. Of the sons of Korah.
48:1 The LORD is great, and much to be praised / in our God's city, on his holy mountain.
48:2 Lovely and lofty, the delight of all the earth, / is Mount Zion, the heights of Zaphon, / the town of the great King.
48:3 God, in her strongholds, / has shown that he is a high fort.
## The kings who came and fled
48:4 See, the kings gathered; / together they marched on.
48:5 They took one look and were stunned. / They were filled with dread and rushed away.
48:6 Shaking took hold of them there, / pain like a woman's in childbirth.
48:7 With a wind from the east / you break the ships of Tarshish.
48:8 What we had heard, we have now seen / in the city of the LORD of Armies, / in the city of our God: / God will keep it secure forever. Selah
## Walk around Zion
48:9 Within your temple, O God, / we have pondered your loyal love.
48:10 Your praise, O God, is as wide as your name; / it stretches to the ends of the earth. / Your right hand is full of what is right.
48:11 Mount Zion rejoices; / the daughters of Judah are glad / because of your rulings.
48:12 Circle Zion, walk all around her. / Count up her towers.
48:13 Set your heart on her outer walls; / pass through her fortresses, / so you can tell it to the generation after you,
48:14 that this is God, our God, for ever and ever. / He will lead us until we die.
## A riddle for the whole world
49:0 For the music director. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.
49:1 Listen to this, all peoples. / Turn your ears here, everyone who lives in the world,
49:2 common folk and great alike, / rich and needy together.
49:3 My mouth will speak words of wisdom, / and what my heart turns over will bring insight.
49:4 I will turn my ear toward a saying; / with the lyre I will lay open my riddle.
## No one can buy back a life
49:5 Why should I be afraid in evil days, / when the wrongdoing of those at my heels closes around me,
49:6 people who lean on their money / and make a boast of all their riches?
49:7 No one can ever buy back a brother / or give God a ransom for him.
49:8 The price for their lives is too high; / one must drop the idea for good,
49:9 so that he might go on living for all time / and not look on the Pit.
49:10 Indeed, one sees wise people die. / Fool and dullard perish together / and leave what they own to others.
49:11 Inside, they think their houses will last forever, / their homes for all generations; / they named whole lands after themselves.
49:12 Yet a man in his splendor does not stay even a night; / he is like the animals that are silenced.
## Death will shepherd them
49:13 This is the path of the foolishly sure, / and of those who follow them, pleased with their words. Selah
49:14 Like sheep they are herded toward Sheol; / Death will be their shepherd. / By morning the upright will be their masters. / Their form will waste away in Sheol, / far from any lofty home.
49:15 But God will ransom my life / from the hand of Sheol, for he will take me. Selah
49:16 Do not be frightened when a man gets rich, / when his household's splendor swells,
49:17 for he takes none of it with him when he dies; / his splendor will not follow him down.
49:18 In his life he told himself how blessed he was, / and people praise you when you treat yourself well,
49:19 but he will join the generation of his ancestors, / who will not see light ever again.
49:20 A human in splendor, but without understanding, / is like the animals that are silenced.
## God comes to judge his people
50:0 A psalm of Asaph.
50:1 The God of gods, the LORD, has spoken. / He summons the earth / from where the sun rises to where it goes down.
50:2 From Zion, beauty made perfect, / God shines out.
50:3 Our God comes, and he will not keep quiet. / A fire eats up what is before him, / and around him a storm rages hard.
50:4 He summons the heavens on high / and the earth, for the judging of his people:
50:5 "Bring together to me those loyal to me, / who cut my covenant over a sacrifice."
50:6 And the heavens make his justice known, / for God is the one who judges. Selah
## I do not need your bulls
50:7 "Hear me, my people, and let me speak. / Israel, I am bringing charges against you. / God, your God, am I.
50:8 I do not scold you over your sacrifices; / your burnt offerings are in front of me all the time.
50:9 I will not take a young bull from your household, / nor goats from your pens.
50:10 Every wild creature in the forest belongs to me, / and the animals on a thousand hills.
50:11 I know each bird on the mountains; / the creatures that stir in the countryside are with me.
50:12 Suppose I were hungry: I would not say so to you, / for the world and everything in it is mine.
50:13 Do I feed on the flesh of strong bulls? / Do I drink goats' blood?
50:14 Sacrifice a thank offering to God, / and pay the Most High what you vowed.
50:15 Cry out to me on the day of distress; / I will set you free, and you will give me glory."
## To the wicked
50:16 But God says to the wicked: / "What business do you have listing my rules / and carrying my covenant in your mouth?
50:17 You hate to be taught a lesson; / you toss my words over your shoulder.
50:18 If you see a thief, you are happy to join him; / and with adulterers is your share.
50:19 You turn your mouth loose for evil, / and your tongue harnesses deceit.
50:20 You sit around talking against your brother; / you smear your own mother's son.
50:21 You did all this, and I said nothing. / You imagined I was the same as you. / Now I will rebuke you / and set it all out in front of your eyes.
50:22 Understand this, you who forget God, / or I will rip you to pieces, and no one will save you.
50:23 Whoever brings a thank offering as his sacrifice gives me honor, / and whoever prepares the way, / I will let him see God's rescue."
## Wash me
51:0 For the music director. A psalm of David, when Nathan the prophet came to him, after David had come to Bathsheba.
51:1 Be gracious to me, O God, as your loyal love is. / As your compassion is great, / wipe out my rebellions.
51:2 Wash me again and again of my wrongdoing; / make me pure from my sin.
51:3 I myself know my rebellious acts; / my sin stands before me all the time.
51:4 You, you alone, are the one I sinned against; / what is evil in your eyes, I did it. / So you are in the right when you speak, / and clear when you pass judgment.
51:5 Look, I was born in guilt, / and in sin my mother conceived me.
51:6 Look, faithfulness deep inside is what you want; / in the hidden place you make wisdom known to me.
51:7 Take away my sin with hyssop, and I will be pure. / Wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.
51:8 Let me hear joy and celebration; / let the bones you crushed leap for joy.
51:9 Turn your face away from my sins; / wipe out every wrong I have done.
## Create a pure heart
51:10 Create a pure heart for me, O God, / and make a steady spirit new inside me.
51:11 Do not fling me away from before you; / and your holy spirit, do not take it from me.
51:12 Bring back to me the joy of being saved by you, / and let a generous spirit hold me up.
51:13 I will teach your ways to those who rebel, / and sinners will come back to you.
51:14 Save me from the guilt of blood, O God, / God of my rescue, / and my tongue will shout for joy about your justice.
51:15 Lord, open my lips, / and my mouth will make your praise known.
51:16 You take no pleasure in a sacrifice, or I would bring one. / A burnt offering does not delight you.
51:17 A broken spirit: that is the sacrifice for God. / A heart broken and crushed, O God, / you will not look down on.
## Build the walls
51:18 Be good to Zion, as it pleases you; / build the walls of Jerusalem.
51:19 Then sacrifices offered rightly will please you, / burnt offering and whole offering. / Then young bulls will go up on your altar.
## The big man and the olive tree
52:0 For the music director. A maskil of David, when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul, "David has gone to Ahimelech's house."
52:1 Why boast of your evil, you hero? / The loyal love of God is there all the day.
52:2 Your tongue plots ruin, / like a sharpened razor, you worker of deceit.
52:3 You love wrong over good, / falsehood over telling the truth. Selah
52:4 You love every word that swallows people up, / you deceitful tongue.
52:5 God too will pull you down forever. / He will seize you and drag you from your tent / and tear up your roots from the land of the living. Selah
52:6 Those in the right will see it and fear, / and they will laugh at him:
52:7 "Look at the man who did not take God as his stronghold, / but trusted in his great wealth / and grew strong in his ruinous schemes."
52:8 But I am a leafy olive tree in God's house; / I have put my trust in God's loyal love for ever and ever.
52:9 I will give you thanks for all time for what you have done. / I will hope in your name, for it is good, / in front of those who are loyal to you.
## The fool says there is no God
53:0 For the music director. On mahalath. A maskil of David.
53:1 "There is no God," the fool says to himself. / They have gone rotten, and their injustice is detestable. / Not one of them does good.
53:2 From heaven God leans out and looks at the human race, / to see whether any act wisely, / whether any seek God.
53:3 Every one has slid back; all alike have turned sour. / No one does good, no, not a single one.
53:4 Will they never learn, those who do wrong? / They devour my people as they would eat bread, / and they do not call on God.
53:5 There they shook with dread, / where no dread had been. / For God has scattered the bones of your besieger. / You have put them to shame, because God has thrown them away.
53:6 Who will send Israel's saving help out of Zion? / When God brings his people back from captivity, / Jacob will be jubilant, Israel glad.
## Save me by your name
54:0 For the music director. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David, when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "David is hiding among us, isn't he?"
54:1 By your name, O God, rescue me; / by your power win my case.
54:2 God, listen to my prayer; / turn your ear to what my mouth says.
54:3 For outsiders have stood up against me, / and brutal men are hunting for my life. / They give God no place before their eyes. Selah
54:4 Look, God is my helper; / the Lord is among those who hold up my life.
54:5 He will turn the evil back on those who spy on me. / By your faithfulness, put an end to them.
54:6 I will offer you a sacrifice of my own free will; / I will thank your name, LORD, because it is good.
54:7 For he has pulled me out of every distress, / and my eye has gazed on my enemies.
## If only I had wings
55:0 For the music director. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David.
55:1 Bend your ear to my prayer, O God; / do not hide yourself when I beg for favor.
55:2 Listen closely and answer me. / I wander restless in my complaint, and I groan
55:3 at the voice of the enemy, / at the pressure of the wicked. / For they topple trouble onto me, / and in their anger they hold a grudge against me.
55:4 My heart twists in pain within me; / and death's terrors have dropped on me.
55:5 Fear and shaking enter me; / shuddering wraps itself over me.
55:6 And I said, "Who will give me wings like a dove's? / I would fly off and settle down.
55:7 Look, I would run far away; / I would spend the night in the wilderness. Selah
55:8 I would hurry to a place of escape, / away from the rushing wind and the storm."
## Violence in the city
55:9 Confuse them, Lord, split their speech, / for in the city I see violence and quarreling.
55:10 They make their rounds of it on its walls by day and night, / and evil and misery are in the middle of it.
55:11 Destruction lives within it; / harm and deceit never move out of its public square.
## It was you, my friend
55:12 If an enemy insulted me, / I could stand it. / If one who hated me made himself big against me, / I could hide from him.
55:13 But it was you, a man of my own rank, / my trusted friend, someone I knew.
55:14 We used to share sweet secrets together; / in the house of God we walked with the crowd.
55:15 Let death come on them by surprise; / let them go down to Sheol alive, / for evil lives where they lodge, inside them.
55:16 But I, I will call out to God, / and the LORD will rescue me.
55:17 At evening, at morning, at midday / I pour out my complaint and groan, / and he hears my voice.
55:18 He has bought back my life and kept it whole / from the fight waged against me, / though many stood against me.
55:19 God will hear and humble them, / the one enthroned from ancient days. Selah / For them there is no change of heart, / and they do not fear God.
55:20 He reached out his hands against those at peace with him; / he defiled his covenant.
55:21 The butter-talk of his mouth was smooth, / but his heart was set on war. / Softer than oil were his words, / and they were swords out of their sheaths.
## Throw your load on the LORD
55:22 Throw your load on the LORD, / and he will provide for you; / never will he let the righteous be toppled.
55:23 But you, O God, will bring them down / to the well of the Pit. / Men of blood and deceit / will not reach half their days. / But I will trust in you.
