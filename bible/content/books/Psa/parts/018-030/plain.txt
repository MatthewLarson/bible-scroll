## The King's Song of Rescue
18:0 For the music director. Of David, the LORD's servant, who spoke the words of this song to the LORD on the day the LORD rescued him from the grip of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.
18:1 He said: / I love you, LORD, my strength.
18:2 The LORD is my high cliff, my fortress, / the one who gets me free. / My God, my rock, I take shelter in him, / my shield and the horn that saves me, / my high tower.
18:3 To the LORD, the one worthy of praise, I call, / and I come out safe from my enemies.
18:4 Death's ropes closed around me; / the floods of ruin filled me with terror.
18:5 The ropes of Sheol were wound around me; / death's snares met me head-on.
18:6 When I was hemmed in, I called the LORD; / to my God I cried for help. / He heard my voice in his temple; / my cry for help came before him, into his ears.
## God Comes Down in the Storm
18:7 The earth heaved and quaked; / the base of the mountains shuddered. / They heaved, for his anger was burning.
18:8 Up went smoke from his nose, / and a fire that eats from his mouth; / coals flared up from him.
18:9 He bent the sky low and came down, / storm-darkness beneath his feet.
18:10 Riding a cherub, he flew; / he swooped on the wind's wings.
18:11 He made darkness his hiding place, / his shelter all around him, / dark water and heavy cloud.
18:12 From the glare in front of him / his clouds swept past, / with hail and fiery coals.
18:13 In the sky the LORD thundered; / the Most High gave out his voice, hail and fiery coals.
18:14 He let his arrows fly and scattered them; / he shot lightning in plenty and threw them into panic.
18:15 The channels of water showed; / the world's footings lay uncovered / at your rebuke, LORD, / at the gust of breath from your nose.
18:16 From the heights he stretched out his hand and seized me; / he pulled me from great waters.
18:17 He snatched me away from my strong enemy, / from the people who hated me, / since they were stronger than I was.
18:18 They met me head-on in my day of disaster, / but the LORD became my staff to lean on.
18:19 He led me out where there was room; / he pulled me free, since he took pleasure in me.
## Clean Hands
18:20 The LORD treated me as my righteousness deserved; / he paid me back for my clean hands.
18:21 I have stayed on the LORD's roads; / I did not do wrong and leave my God.
18:22 All his rulings stand before me; / I have not put his rules away from me.
18:23 With him I have been wholehearted, / and I have guarded myself against my own guilt.
18:24 So the LORD repaid me as my righteousness deserved, / for my clean hands before his eyes.
18:25 With the loyal you show yourself loyal; / with the wholehearted man you show yourself wholehearted.
18:26 With one who keeps himself pure you are pure, / but with the twisted you twist and turn.
18:27 A people pushed down you rescue, / but haughty eyes you bring low.
18:28 You light my lamp, LORD; / my God makes my darkness bright.
18:29 By your help I rush at a war party; / by my God's help I vault a wall.
18:30 God's way is flawless. / The LORD's word is refined like metal. / He is a shield for everyone who takes shelter in him.
18:31 Who is God, if not the LORD? / Who is a rock, if not our God?
## Trained for Battle
18:32 God is the one who wraps me in strength / and makes my way whole.
18:33 He makes my feet like a doe's / and stands me firm on my high places.
18:34 He teaches my hands to fight; / my arms can draw a bronze bow.
18:35 You handed me your rescue as a shield; / your right hand holds me up, / and your gentleness makes me great.
18:36 You widen the ground under my stride; / my ankles do not wobble.
18:37 I ran after my enemies and caught them; / there was no turning back till I made an end of them.
18:38 I smashed them, and they could not get up; / they fell under my feet.
18:39 You wrapped me in strength for battle; / you bent my attackers low beneath me.
18:40 You gave me my enemies' necks; / the ones who hated me I wiped out.
18:41 They cried for help, and there was no rescuer; / they cried to the LORD, and he gave them no answer.
18:42 I pounded them fine like dust before the wind; / like the mud of the lanes I emptied them out.
18:43 You rescued me from the feuds of the people; / you made me chief over the nations. / A people I never knew serve me.
18:44 The moment their ears hear, they do what I say; / sons of foreign lands fawn on me.
18:45 Sons of foreign lands wilt; / they come trembling out of their fortresses.
## The LORD Is Alive
18:46 The LORD is alive! Blessed is my rock! / Let the God who saves me be raised high!
18:47 This is the God who grants me revenge, / who brings nations down beneath me,
18:48 who frees me from my enemies. / Over my attackers you raise me; / from the man of violence you snatch me.
18:49 That is why I will give you thanks among the nations, LORD; / to your name I will make music.
18:50 He makes great the rescues of his king; / he shows loyal love to his anointed, / to David and his line for all time.
## The Sky and the Law
19:0 For the music director. A psalm of David.
19:1 The heavens keep recounting God's glory; / the sky's dome reports what his hands have made.
19:2 One day gushes out talk to the next; / one night passes knowledge to the next.
19:3 No speech, no words; / no voice of theirs is heard.
19:4 Yet their line has run out over the whole earth, / their words to the world's edge. / There he has set up a tent for the sun,
19:5 and the sun comes out like a bridegroom from the wedding room; / like a warrior it delights to run the track.
19:6 From the sky's far edge it sets out, / and its loop runs round to the other edge; / nothing hides from its heat.
19:7 The LORD's law is flawless; it brings the soul back to life. / The LORD's decree can be trusted; it gives wisdom to the simple.
19:8 The LORD's orders are right; they make the heart glad. / The LORD's command is clear; it makes the eyes shine.
19:9 The fear of the LORD is clean; it stands forever. / The LORD's rulings are truth; every one of them is right.
19:10 You should crave them more than gold, / more than a mass of fine gold; / they are sweeter than honey, / than what drips from the comb.
19:11 Through them your servant is also warned; / the one who keeps them gains much in the end.
19:12 Who notices his own slips? / Clear me of faults I cannot see.
19:13 Hold your servant back from arrogant sins too; / do not let them rule over me. / Then I will be whole / and clear of a great offense.
19:14 May what my mouth says / and what my heart murmurs / win your favor, LORD, / my rock and my redeemer.
## A Prayer for the King Before Battle
20:0 For the music director. A psalm of David.
20:1 In your day of trouble, may the LORD answer you; / may the name of Jacob's God set you high, out of reach.
20:2 From the holy place may he send you help; / from Zion may he prop you up.
20:3 May he remember all your grain offerings / and find your burnt offering rich. Selah
20:4 May he give you what your heart wants / and carry out every plan you make.
20:5 Let us cheer at your rescue / and lift our flags in the name of our God. / May the LORD do all you ask.
20:6 Now I know it: the LORD has rescued his anointed. / He answers him from his holy sky / with the mighty deeds of his right hand that save.
20:7 These have chariots, and those have horses, / but we call on the name of the LORD our God.
20:8 They have sunk to their knees and fallen, / but we have gotten up and stand tall.
20:9 LORD, save! / May the King answer us on the day we call.
## Thanks for the King's Victory
21:0 For the music director. A psalm of David.
21:1 LORD, the king is glad because of your strength; / and at your rescue, how wildly he rejoices!
21:2 You have given him what his heart longed for; / you have not held back what his lips asked. Selah
21:3 You go out to meet him with blessings of good things; / on his head you set a crown of refined gold.
21:4 Life is what he asked of you, and you gave it: / long days, on and on forever.
21:5 Your rescue makes his glory great; / you lay splendor and majesty on him.
21:6 For you make him a blessing forever; / you cheer him with joy before your face.
21:7 The king relies on the LORD; / the Most High's loyal love keeps him from tottering.
21:8 Your hand will track down all your enemies; / your right hand will track down those who hate you.
21:9 You will make them like a fiery furnace / at the time you show your face. / The LORD in his anger will swallow them up, / and fire will eat them.
21:10 You will wipe their fruit from the earth / and their seed from among humankind.
21:11 They stretched out evil against you; / they thought up a plot, but they cannot carry it out.
21:12 You will make them show you their shoulders; / with your bowstrings you will take aim at their faces.
21:13 Rise up, LORD, in your strength! / We will sing and make music about your might.
## Why Have You Abandoned Me?
22:0 For the music director. On "The Doe of the Dawn." A psalm of David.
22:1 My God, my God, why have you abandoned me? / You stay far from rescuing me, / from the words I roar out.
22:2 My God, all day I call, and you give no answer; / all night, and there is no quiet for me.
22:3 But you, the Holy One, / sit on Israel's praises as your throne.
22:4 Our ancestors trusted you; / they trusted, and you got them out safe.
22:5 They called to you and escaped; / they trusted you and were never shamed.
22:6 But I am a worm and not a man, / a thing for people to insult, / dismissed with contempt by the crowd.
22:7 All who see me make fun of me; / they sneer with their lips and shake their heads:
22:8 "Roll it onto the LORD! Let the LORD get him free! / Let the LORD snatch him away, since he takes such delight in him!"
22:9 Yet you are the one who pulled me out of the womb; / you taught me trust at my mother's breasts.
22:10 Onto you I was flung from the womb; / since I was in my mother's belly, you have been my God.
22:11 Don't keep your distance! Trouble is close, / and no one is here to help.
22:12 A herd of bulls is all around me; / the mighty ones of Bashan ring me in.
22:13 Their jaws gape at me, / a lion ripping prey and roaring.
22:14 I am poured out like water, / and all my bones have pulled apart. / My heart is like wax, / melting deep inside me.
22:15 My strength has dried out like a piece of broken clay; / my tongue is glued to my gums. / You are setting me down in death's dust.
22:16 For dogs are all around me; / a gang of evildoers has closed in on me. / Like a lion, they go for my hands and feet.
22:17 Every one of my bones I can count. / They look at me; they feast their eyes on me.
22:18 They hand out my clothes among them, / and over my clothing they throw lots.
22:19 But you, LORD, don't be distant! / You who are my strength, hurry to my help!
22:20 Snatch my life away from the sword, / my one and only life from the dog's paw.
22:21 Save me from the lion's mouth! / From the horns of the wild oxen you have answered me.
## I Will Tell Your Name
22:22 I will spread the news of your name to my brothers; / where the people gather, I will sing your praise.
22:23 Praise him, you who fear the LORD! / Every one of Jacob's line, give him glory! / Every one of Israel's line, be in awe of him!
22:24 He did not treat the poor man's misery as nothing; / he did not shrink from it in disgust. / He did not hide his face from him; / when the man called to him for help, he listened.
22:25 You are the source of my praise in the great crowd of worshipers; / in front of those who fear him I will pay what I vowed.
22:26 The humble will eat until they have enough; / the people who look for the LORD will praise him. / Let your hearts live on forever!
22:27 The far corners of the earth will remember / and come back to the LORD; / every clan of the nations will bow low in front of you.
22:28 Royal power is the LORD's; / he is the ruler of the nations.
22:29 All the rich of the earth have eaten and bowed down; / all who go down to the dust will kneel before him, / the one who could not keep himself alive.
22:30 A line of descendants will serve him; / the Lord will be told of to the next generation.
22:31 They will come and announce his righteousness / to a people still to be born: / He did it!
## The LORD My Shepherd
23:0 A psalm of David.
23:1 The LORD is my shepherd; / I will never be in need.
23:2 In meadows of fresh grass he has me lie down; / to waters of rest he guides me.
23:3 He brings my life back. / He steers me on the right tracks / for the sake of his name.
23:4 Even if I go through a valley of deep shadow, / I am not afraid of harm, for you are with me. / Your club and your staff, they reassure me.
23:5 You lay out a table for me / where my enemies can see it. / You have made my head rich with oil; / my cup is brimming.
23:6 Surely good things and loyal love will chase after me / all the days of my life, / and I will come back to the house of the LORD / for many long days.
## The King of Glory Comes In
24:0 Of David. A psalm.
24:1 To the LORD belong the earth and all that fills it, / the world and those who live there.
24:2 He is the one who laid its base upon the seas / and made it stand fast upon the streams.
24:3 Who can climb the LORD's mountain? / Who can take a stand in his holy place?
24:4 Someone whose hands are clean and whose heart is pure, / who has not lifted up his soul to what is empty / and has not sworn to a lie.
24:5 He will carry home a blessing from the LORD / and righteousness from the God who rescues him.
24:6 This is the company of those who seek him, / who search for your face, O Jacob. Selah
24:7 Gates, lift your heads high! / Rise up, you age-old doors, / so that the King of glory can come in!
24:8 Who is this King of glory? / The LORD, strong and a warrior, / the LORD, a warrior in battle.
24:9 Gates, lift your heads high! / Lift them, you age-old doors, / so that the King of glory can come in!
24:10 Who is he, this King of glory? / The LORD of Armies, / he is the King of glory. Selah
## From A to Z: Teach Me Your Ways
25:0 Of David.
25:1 To you, LORD, I raise my soul.
25:2 My God, I rely on you; do not let me be disgraced; / do not let my enemies crow over me.
25:3 None of those who wait for you will be disgraced; / the disgraced will be those who betray for no reason.
25:4 LORD, let me know your ways; / train me in your paths.
25:5 Make me walk in your faithfulness and teach me, / since you are my God who rescues; / it is you I wait for all day.
25:6 LORD, call to mind your compassion and your acts of loyal love; / they go back to ancient times.
25:7 The sins I did when young and my acts of rebellion, do not remember; / remember me in keeping with your loyal love, / because you are good, LORD.
25:8 The LORD is good, and he is honest; / so he shows sinners which way to go.
25:9 He leads the lowly by his justice; / he teaches the lowly the way he goes.
25:10 Every path the LORD takes is loyal love and faithfulness / to those who keep his covenant and his decrees.
25:11 Because of your name, LORD, / pardon my guilt; it is huge.
25:12 Is there someone who fears the LORD? / The LORD will show him the path to pick.
25:13 He himself will rest in good things, / and his descendants will take possession of the land.
25:14 The LORD shares his secrets with those who fear him; / he lets them know his covenant.
25:15 I keep my eyes turned to the LORD at all times, / for he is the one who will free my feet from the net.
25:16 Face me and show me grace; / I am on my own and beaten down.
25:17 The troubles of my heart keep spreading; / lead me out of my tight places.
25:18 Look at how I suffer and toil, / and carry away all my sins.
25:19 Look at my enemies; there are so many, / and they hate me with a violent hate.
25:20 Keep watch over my life and pull me out; / let me not be disgraced, since I have come to you for shelter.
25:21 Let integrity and honesty stand guard over me, / for I am waiting for you.
25:22 God, ransom Israel / from all its troubles.
## Judge Me, LORD
26:0 Of David.
26:1 Give judgment for me, LORD; / my way of life has been one of integrity. / In the LORD I have put my trust, and I will not slip.
26:2 Examine me, LORD, and put me to the test; / refine my kidneys and my heart.
26:3 I keep your loyal love where my eyes can see it, / and I walk about in your truth.
26:4 I have not sat down with worthless liars, / and with two-faced people I do not go.
26:5 The crowd of evildoers I hate; / with the wicked I will not take a seat.
26:6 In innocence I wash my hands, / and I circle your altar, LORD,
26:7 raising my voice in a song of thanks / and telling over every wonder you have done.
26:8 LORD, I love the home that is your house, / the place your glory makes its home.
26:9 Do not gather my soul in with sinners, / my life with men of bloodshed,
26:10 men with wicked schemes in their hands, / their right hands loaded with bribes.
26:11 But I, I keep walking in my integrity; / ransom me and show me grace.
26:12 My foot has found level ground; / where the people gather, I will bless the LORD.
## The LORD Is My Light
27:0 Of David.
27:1 The LORD is my light and my rescue; / who is there for me to fear? / The LORD is the stronghold that guards my life; / who is there to make me tremble?
27:2 When evil men closed in on me to eat my flesh, / my attackers and my enemies, / it was they who tripped and went down.
27:3 If a camp of soldiers sets up around me, / my heart will not fear; / if a war rises up against me, / even so, I keep my trust.
27:4 I have asked the LORD for one thing, / and it is the thing I go after: / to stay in the LORD's house as long as I live, / to look at how lovely the LORD is, / and to search out answers in his temple.
27:5 On the day of trouble he will tuck me into his shelter; / he will hide me in the hidden part of his tent; / he will set me up on a rock out of reach.
27:6 So now I will hold my head high / over the enemies who ring me in. / In his tent I will offer sacrifices with shouts of joy; / I will sing; I will play music for the LORD.
## Do Not Hide Your Face
27:7 Listen, LORD, to my voice as I call; / show me grace and answer me.
27:8 My heart repeats your word to me: "Seek my face!" / So your face, LORD, is what I will seek.
27:9 Do not keep your face hidden from me; / do not push your servant aside in anger. / You have been my help; / do not drop me, do not walk away from me, / God who rescues me.
27:10 Even if my father and my mother abandon me, / the LORD will gather me in.
27:11 LORD, show me your way; / guide me on flat, even ground, / because of those who watch me with hate.
27:12 Do not give me up to the greed of my foes, / for lying witnesses stand up to accuse me, / and a witness who breathes out violence.
27:13 If I had not trusted that I would see the LORD's goodness / in the land of the living...
27:14 Wait for the LORD! / Be strong, and may your heart grow brave. / Yes, wait for the LORD!
## My Rock, Do Not Be Deaf
28:0 Of David.
28:1 LORD, it is you I call. / My rock, do not turn a deaf ear to me, / or else, if you keep quiet toward me, / I will end up like those going down into the pit.
28:2 Listen to the sound of my pleading / when I shout to you for help, / when I raise my hands / toward the inner room of your holy place.
28:3 Do not haul me away along with the wicked / and the people who make trouble, / who say "Peace" to their neighbors / with evil in their hearts.
28:4 Give them what matches their work, / what matches the evil of their actions; / give them what matches the work of their hands; / return on them what they have earned.
28:5 They do not notice the LORD's deeds / or what his hands have made, / so he will pull them down and not rebuild them.
28:6 Blessed is the LORD! / He has heard the sound of my pleading.
28:7 The LORD is my strength and my shield; / on him my heart relied, and help came. / So my heart dances with joy, / and I will thank him out of my song.
28:8 The LORD is strength for them; / he is a fortress of rescue for his anointed.
28:9 Rescue your people! Bless your inheritance! / Shepherd them, and carry them for all time.
## The Voice of the LORD

29:1 Give the LORD, you sons of the gods, / give the LORD glory and strength.
29:2 Give the LORD the glory of his name; / bow low to the LORD in holy splendor.
29:3 The LORD's voice is over the waters. / The God of glory sends out thunder; / the LORD is over the vast waters.
29:4 The LORD's voice has power; / the LORD's voice has majesty.
29:5 The LORD's voice breaks cedars; / the LORD smashes the cedars of Lebanon.
29:6 He sets Lebanon jumping like a calf, / and Sirion like a wild ox's young.
29:7 The LORD's voice carves out flames of fire.
29:8 The LORD's voice sets the wilderness writhing; / the LORD makes the wilderness of Kadesh writhe.
29:9 The LORD's voice makes the does go into labor / and strips the forests bare, / and in his temple everyone says, "Glory!"
29:10 At the Flood the LORD took his throne; / the LORD sits as King forever.
29:11 Strength to his people the LORD will give; / the LORD will bless his people with peace.
## Mourning Turned to Dancing
30:0 A psalm. A song for the dedication of the house. Of David.
30:1 I will lift you high, LORD, for you drew me up, / and you did not give my enemies a reason to cheer over me.
30:2 LORD my God, I shouted to you for help, / and you made me well.
30:3 LORD, you lifted my life out of Sheol; / you gave me life again, so I did not go down into the pit.
30:4 Play music for the LORD, you his loyal ones; / give thanks as you call his holy name to mind.
30:5 His anger is over in a moment; / his favor is for life. / At evening, weeping comes to spend the night, / and at morning, a cry of joy.
30:6 As for me, in my comfortable days I said, / "I will never totter."
30:7 LORD, when you were pleased with me, / you set strength on my mountain. / Then you hid your face, / and I fell into panic.
30:8 I called to you, LORD; / I begged the Lord for mercy:
30:9 "What good is my blood to you, / my going down into the pit? / Can dust give you thanks? / Can it announce how faithful you are?
30:10 Listen, LORD, and show me grace! / LORD, come and help me!"
30:11 My mourning song you changed into a dance for me; / you untied my sackcloth and belted me with joy,
30:12 so that glory may make music to you and never go quiet. / LORD my God, I will give you thanks for all time.
