## Book One (Psalms 1–41)
## Two roads
1:1 Happy is the person / who does not walk by the advice of the wicked, / does not stand on the road of sinners, / does not sit where mockers sit.
1:2 Instead, the law of the LORD is his delight, / and he murmurs over his law day and night.
1:3 He will be like a tree set out by channels of water, / that gives its fruit at the right time, / and its leaves do not wither. / All that he does turns out well.
1:4 The wicked are not like this. / No, they are like the husks the wind drives off.
1:5 That is why the wicked will not stand when judgment comes, / nor sinners in the company of the righteous.
1:6 For the LORD knows the road of the righteous, / but the road of the wicked will be lost.
## The LORD's anointed king
2:1 Why do the nations gather in an uproar, / and the peoples murmur over empty plans?
2:2 The kings of the earth station themselves, / the rulers sit down together, / against the LORD and against his anointed one:
2:3 "Let us tear off their straps / and fling their ropes away from us!"
2:4 Seated in the heavens, he laughs. / The Lord makes fun of them.
2:5 Then he speaks to them in his anger. / In his hot fury he throws them into panic:
2:6 "I myself have put my king in place / on my holy mountain, on Zion."
2:7 I will repeat what the LORD decreed. / He said to me, "You are my son. / This day I have fathered you.
2:8 Ask it of me, and I will hand you the nations to inherit, / and the far ends of the earth to hold as your own.
2:9 You will crush them with an iron rod; / you will shatter them like a jar from the potter."
2:10 So then, kings, show good sense. / Rulers of the earth, learn your lesson.
2:11 Serve the LORD in fear, / and be glad with trembling.
2:12 Kiss the son, or he will grow angry / and you will be lost on your road, / for his anger is quick to catch fire. / Happy are all who shelter in him.
## Morning prayer in danger
3:0 A psalm of David, when he ran away from his son Absalom.
3:1 LORD, how my enemies have grown in number! / So many rise up against me.
3:2 Many say about me, / "There is no rescue for him in God." Selah
3:3 But you, LORD, are the shield that covers me; / you are my honor; you raise my head.
3:4 I called aloud to the LORD, / and from his holy mountain he answered me. Selah
3:5 I lay down and fell asleep; / I woke up, for the LORD was holding me.
3:6 I will not fear the people by the tens of thousands / who have taken their places all around me.
3:7 Rise up, LORD! Rescue me, my God! / You have hit all my enemies across the jaw; / you have smashed the teeth of the wicked.
3:8 Rescue is the LORD's! / Let your blessing rest on your people. Selah
## Evening prayer in peace
4:0 For the music director. With stringed instruments. A psalm of David.
4:1 When I call, answer me, God of my right! / In my tight place you made room for me. / Show me favor and listen to my prayer.
4:2 You men of standing, how long will my honor be put to shame? / Will you love what is worthless and chase after what is false? Selah
4:3 Understand that the LORD has picked out his loyal one for himself. / When I call to him, the LORD will hear.
4:4 Be shaken, but do not sin. / Talk it over in your heart as you lie in bed, and keep quiet. Selah
4:5 Bring sacrifices made in the right way, / and put your confidence in the LORD.
4:6 Many are saying, "Who will show us anything good?" / LORD, raise the light of your face above us.
4:7 The gladness you gave my heart is greater / than theirs in the season their grain and new wine run over.
4:8 I lie down in peace, and at once I sleep, / because you alone, LORD, settle me in safety.
## Prayer at daybreak
5:0 For the music director. For the flutes. A psalm of David.
5:1 LORD, hear my words; / take note of my sighing.
5:2 Listen closely to my voice as I call for help, / my King and my God, / because I am praying to you.
5:3 LORD, at daybreak you will hear my voice. / At daybreak I set out my plea for you, and I keep watch.
5:4 You are no God who enjoys what is wicked. / Evil may not live with you as a guest.
5:5 The proud cannot stand in front of your eyes. / You hate everyone who does wrong.
5:6 You bring to ruin those who speak lies; / the LORD detests the man of bloodshed and deceit.
5:7 As for me, by the greatness of your loyal love I will enter your house. / Toward your holy temple I will bow, in fear of you.
5:8 Guide me in your righteousness, LORD, / because of my watchful enemies; / make your road level before me.
5:9 Nothing they say can be trusted; / deep inside they are pure ruin; / their throat is a grave left open; / their tongues are slippery with flattery.
5:10 Hold them guilty, God. / Let their own schemes trip them up. / Thrust them away for all their crimes, / for they are rebels against you.
5:11 But let all who shelter in you be glad. / Let them always shout for joy. / Put a covering over them, / and let those who love your name take delight in you.
5:12 You yourself, LORD, bless the one who does right. / Your favor goes around him like a great shield.
## A sick man's plea
6:0 For the music director. With stringed instruments. On the sheminith. A psalm of David.
6:1 LORD, not in your anger, please, do not rebuke me; / not in your rage, do not discipline me.
6:2 Show me favor, LORD, for I am wasting away. / Heal me, LORD, for my bones are in terror.
6:3 My soul too is in deep terror. / And you, LORD, how long?
6:4 Come back, LORD; pull my life free. / For the sake of your loyal love, save me.
6:5 For among the dead no one calls you to mind. / In Sheol, who gives you thanks?
6:6 My groaning has tired me out. / Night after night I make my bed swim; / I melt my couch with my tears.
6:7 My eye is dim from grief; / it has grown old because of all who are against me.
6:8 Leave me, every one of you who does wrong! / The LORD has heard the voice of my crying.
6:9 The LORD has heard my plea for grace; / the LORD will receive my prayer.
6:10 Let all my enemies be shamed and filled with terror. / Let them turn back, shamed in an instant.
## The innocent man's appeal
7:0 A shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD about the words of Cush, a Benjaminite.
7:1 LORD my God, in you I have taken shelter. / Save me from all who hunt me, and pull me free,
7:2 or else, like a lion, he will tear me to pieces, / carrying me off with no one to save me.
7:3 LORD my God, if this is what I have done, / if there is crookedness in my hands,
7:4 if I have done harm to the one at peace with me, / or stripped my enemy without cause,
7:5 then let the enemy chase me and catch me. / Let him trample my life into the ground / and settle my honor in the dust. Selah
7:6 Rise up, LORD, in your anger. / Lift yourself high against my raging foes. / Wake up for my sake! You have commanded that justice be done.
7:7 Let the nations gather around you in assembly, / and over them take your place again on high.
7:8 Let the LORD be judge over the peoples. / Give me justice, LORD, as my right deserves, / and as the innocence within me deserves.
7:9 Make an end, please, of the evil that the wicked do, / and set the righteous on firm ground. / Righteous God, you test hearts and kidneys.
7:10 God holds my shield; / he rescues those whose hearts are straight.
7:11 God is a judge who does right, / a God who shows his fury every day.
7:12 If one does not turn back, / he will sharpen his sword; / he has bent his bow and made it ready.
7:13 He has readied weapons of death against him, / and he makes his arrows burn.
7:14 See how he goes into labor with wrong: / he conceives trouble, and what he bears is a lie.
7:15 He dug a pit and hollowed it out, / and into the ditch he made he has fallen.
7:16 The harm he does turns back onto his own head; / his violence comes down on the top of his skull.
7:17 I will thank the LORD as his justice deserves; / I will make music to the name of the LORD Most High.
## The glory of God and of humans
8:0 For the music director. On the gittith. A psalm of David.
8:1 LORD, our Lord, how mighty your name is through all the earth! / You have placed your splendor over the heavens.
8:2 Out of the mouths of children and babies at the breast / you have founded a strong defense because of your foes, / to put a stop to the enemy and the one bent on revenge.
8:3 When I see your skies, the work of your fingers, / the moon and the stars you have fixed there,
8:4 what is a mere mortal, that you remember him, / a son of man, that you look after him?
8:5 You made him only a little less than God; / with glory and majesty you crown him.
8:6 You set him as ruler over what your hands have made; / everything you have placed beneath his feet:
8:7 sheep and cattle, every one, / and even the wild animals of the field,
8:8 the birds of the air, the fish of the sea, / everything that crosses the roads through the seas.
8:9 LORD, our Lord, how mighty your name is through all the earth!
## The judge of the nations
9:0 For the music director. On muth-labben. A psalm of David.
9:1 With all my heart I will thank the LORD; / all your wonders I will tell.
9:2 I will rejoice and be jubilant because of you; / I will make music to your name, Most High.
9:3 As my enemies retreat, / they trip and are lost in front of you.
9:4 For you have carried out justice for me and settled my case; / you sat down on the throne, judging fairly.
9:5 You have scolded the nations and wiped out the wicked; / their name you have erased for all time.
9:6 The enemy has come to an end: ruins forever. / You pulled up their cities by the roots; / the memory of them has perished with them.
9:7 But the LORD remains on his throne forever; / he has made his seat firm for judging.
9:8 He himself will judge the world in the right way; / he will govern the nations with fairness.
9:9 So the LORD will be a high tower for the downtrodden, / a high tower in times of distress.
9:10 Those who know your name rely on you, / because you never leave those who look for you, LORD.
9:11 Make music to the LORD, who lives in Zion! / Announce his deeds among the peoples.
9:12 For the one who avenges blood has remembered them; / he has not forgotten the outcry of the afflicted.
9:13 Show me favor, LORD! / See what I suffer from those who hate me, / you who raise me from the gates of death,
9:14 so that I can recount every praise of yours / in the gates of Daughter Zion. / I will be glad that you saved me.
9:15 Down into the pit they made the nations sank; / in the net they set in hiding their own foot was caught.
9:16 The LORD has shown who he is: he has done justice. / By what his own hands did the wicked man is snared. Higgaion. Selah
9:17 The wicked will go back to Sheol, / every nation that forgets God.
9:18 The needy will not be forgotten for good; / the hope of the humble will not be lost forever.
9:19 Rise up, LORD! Do not let mere humans grow strong. / Let the nations be put on trial in front of you.
9:20 Set fear over them, LORD. / Let the nations learn that they are mere mortals. Selah
## Why do you stand far off?
10:1 LORD, why keep standing at a distance? / Why hide away when times are hard?
10:2 The wicked in his arrogance hounds the poor. / May they be trapped by the schemes they have invented.
10:3 The wicked man brags of what his soul craves. / Greedy for gain, he curses and spurns the LORD.
10:4 With his nose held high the wicked does not ask after him. / "There is no God" sums up all his plans.
10:5 His ways hold firm at all times. / Your judgments are high above him, out of his view. / At all his foes he snorts.
10:6 He tells himself, "Nothing will move me. / For all time to come I am one who never meets disaster."
10:7 Cursing fills his mouth, with tricks and abuse; / under his tongue sit trouble and wrong.
10:8 He sets an ambush by the farm villages. / In secret places he kills the innocent. / His eyes peer out for the helpless.
10:9 He crouches in hiding like a lion in its lair; / he crouches to grab the afflicted; / he grabs the afflicted and hauls him off in his net.
10:10 The victim is crushed and bows down; / the helpless fall into his powerful claws.
10:11 He tells himself, "God forgets. / He has turned his face away; he never looks."
10:12 Rise up, LORD! God, raise your hand! / Do not forget the afflicted.
10:13 What makes the wicked treat God with contempt / and say in his heart, "You will not look into it"?
10:14 You have seen it, yes, you! You watch trouble and sorrow / so as to take it into your own hand. / The helpless leaves it all to you. / You have been the orphan's helper.
10:15 Smash the arm of the wicked; / as for the evil man, hunt down his wickedness till nothing is found.
10:16 The LORD is king always and forever. / Nations have perished from his land.
10:17 The longing of the humble you have heard, LORD. / You steady their hearts; / you bend your ear to them,
10:18 to give justice to the orphan and the oppressed, / so that no mortal from the earth will spread terror again.
## Fly to your mountain?
11:0 For the music director. Of David.
11:1 In the LORD I have found shelter. / So how can you tell me, / "Fly off to your mountain, bird!"?
11:2 "See, the wicked draw back the bow; / they have notched their arrow on the string, / to shoot from the darkness at those whose hearts are straight.
11:3 When the foundations are smashed, / what is a righteous man to do?"
11:4 In his holy temple is the LORD; / in the heavens is the LORD's throne. / His eyes look on; / his eyelids examine the human race.
11:5 The LORD puts the righteous to the test; / but the wicked and the lover of violence / he hates with all his being.
11:6 On the wicked he will rain down traps, / fire and sulfur; / a wind of searing heat is what their cup will hold.
11:7 The LORD is just, and he loves acts of justice; / those who are upright will look upon his face.
## Pure words in a lying world
12:0 For the music director. On the sheminith. A psalm of David.
12:1 Come and save, LORD, for the loyal one has come to an end; / the faithful have disappeared from among humankind.
12:2 Each one tells empty things to his neighbor; / they speak with slippery lips, and with a divided heart.
12:3 Let the LORD cut away every slippery lip, / every tongue that speaks big words,
12:4 who say, "Our tongues will make us strong. / Our lips are on our side. Who can be our master?"
12:5 "The afflicted are ruined and the needy groan, / so now I will get up," says the LORD. / "I will set in safety the one who gasps for it."
12:6 The LORD's sayings are sayings without impurity, / silver smelted in a crucible in the ground, / refined seven times.
12:7 You, LORD, will keep them safe; / you will protect him from this generation for all time.
12:8 The wicked parade on every side / when worthlessness is raised high among people.
## How long?
13:0 For the music director. A psalm of David.
13:1 LORD, how long will you forget me? Forever? / How long will your face stay hidden from me?
13:2 How long must I keep turning plans over in my mind, / with grief in my heart day by day? / How long will my enemy be raised above me?
13:3 Look, and answer me, LORD my God. / Brighten my eyes, or I will fall asleep in death,
13:4 and my enemy will say, "I got the better of him," / and my foes will cheer when I am shaken.
13:5 As for me, it is in your loyal love that I trust. / Let my heart be glad because you save.
13:6 To the LORD I will sing, / for he has been generous to me.
## The fool who says there is no God
14:0 For the music director. Of David.
14:1 "There is no God," the fool says to himself. / They have gone rotten; what they do is detestable. / Not one of them does good.
14:2 From heaven the LORD leans out and looks at the human race, / to see whether any act wisely, / whether any seek God.
14:3 Every one has strayed; all alike have turned sour. / No one does good, no, not a single one.
14:4 Will they never learn, all those who do wrong? / They devour my people as they would eat bread, / and they do not call on the LORD.
14:5 There they shook with dread, / because God stands with the circle of the righteous.
14:6 You make a mockery of what the poor man plans, / but the LORD is where he takes shelter.
14:7 Who will send Israel's rescue out of Zion? / When the LORD brings his people back from captivity, / Jacob will be jubilant, Israel glad.
## Who may be the LORD's guest?
15:0 A psalm of David.
15:1 LORD, who is welcome as a guest in your tent? / Who may settle on your holy hill?
15:2 One who walks with a whole heart and does what is right, / and speaks the truth inside his heart.
15:3 His tongue does not go around spreading gossip. / He does no wrong to his companion. / He does not load disgrace on someone close to him.
15:4 A rejected man is worthless in his eyes, / but he honors those who fear the LORD. / He swears to his own cost and does not go back on it.
15:5 He lends his silver without charging interest, / and takes no payment to harm the innocent. / The one who does these things will not be toppled, ever.
## My good share
16:0 A miktam of David.
16:1 Keep watch over me, God, / for I shelter in you.
16:2 I said to the LORD, "You are my Lord. / Nothing good is mine beyond you."
16:3 As for the holy ones who are in the land, / and the noble ones, all my delight is in them.
16:4 Many pains await those who rush after another god. / I will not pour drink offerings of blood to them, / nor will I speak their names.
16:5 The LORD is the share I was given, and my cup. / You are the one who keeps my lot safe.
16:6 My boundary lines have landed in lovely places; / yes, the land I hold delights me.
16:7 I will bless the LORD, who has advised me; / at night, too, my inner self trains me.
16:8 I have placed the LORD in front of me at all times; / he is at my right hand, so I will not be moved.
16:9 That is why my heart is happy and my honor celebrates; / even my flesh will settle down in safety.
16:10 For you will not leave my life to Sheol; / you will not allow your loyal one to see the Pit.
16:11 You let me know the path of life. / Before your face is joy to the full; / in your right hand are delights that never end.
## Hide me in the shade of your wings
17:0 A prayer of David.
17:1 Hear the right, LORD; / pay attention to my loud cry; / listen to my prayer, from lips that do not deceive.
17:2 Let the judgment on me come from before you; / let your eyes see what is fair.
17:3 You have tested my heart and come to me by night. / You have put me through the fire, and you find nothing. / I have made up my mind: my mouth will not overstep.
17:4 As for what humans do: guided by the word of your lips, / I have kept clear of the robber's paths.
17:5 My steps have kept a firm hold on your tracks; / my feet have not slid.
17:6 It is you I call on, God, for you will answer me. / Bend your ear to me; listen to my words.
17:7 Make your acts of loyal love wonderful, / you who rescue with your right hand / those who run for shelter from their attackers.
17:8 Guard me like the pupil of the eye. / Hide me in the shade of your wings
17:9 from the wicked who have ruined me, / my enemies who press in around me, hungry for my life.
17:10 They have closed up their fat; / with their mouths they say proud things.
17:11 Now they have hemmed in our steps on every side; / they keep their eyes set on knocking us down to the ground.
17:12 He looks like a lion that craves to rip its prey, / like a young lion sitting in its hiding places.
17:13 Rise up, LORD! Step out to face him; make him bow low. / With your sword, pull my life free from the wicked,
17:14 from men, LORD, by your hand, / from men of this fleeting world, who take their share while they live. / You fill their belly with what you have stored up; / their sons have their fill, / and they leave what remains to their little ones.
17:15 But I, in the right, will look upon your face. / When I wake up, I will be filled with the sight of your form.
