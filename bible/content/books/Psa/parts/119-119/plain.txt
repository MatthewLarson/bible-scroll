## Alef: The Blameless Way
119:1 Happy are the blameless in their way, / those who walk by the LORD's law.
119:2 Happy are those who guard his decrees; / with all the heart they seek him.
119:3 Yes, they do nothing unjust; / in his ways they walk.
119:4 You yourself gave your orders, / for them to be kept very closely.
119:5 If only my ways stood firm, / so that I keep your rules!
119:6 Then I will not be ashamed / when I look to all your commands.
119:7 I will give you thanks with a straight heart / as I learn the rulings of your righteousness.
119:8 Your rules I will keep; / do not abandon me completely.
## Bet: A Young Man's Path
119:9 How will a young man make his path clean? / By keeping it as your word says.
119:10 With all my heart I have sought you; / keep me from wandering away from your commands.
119:11 In my heart I have treasured your saying, / so I do not sin against you.
119:12 Blessed are you, LORD; / teach me your rules.
119:13 My lips recount / every ruling your mouth has spoken.
119:14 I am glad in the way of your decrees, / as one is glad over all wealth.
119:15 I will ponder your orders / and look closely at your paths.
119:16 In your rules I will take my delight; / your word I will not forget.
## Gimel: A Foreigner on the Earth
119:17 Treat your servant kindly; / let me live, and I will keep your word.
119:18 Uncover my eyes, and I will look on / the wonders that come out of your law.
119:19 I am only a foreigner on the earth; / do not keep your commands hidden from me.
119:20 My soul is crushed by its longing / for your rulings in every season.
119:21 You have scolded the arrogant, the cursed ones, / who drift away from your commands.
119:22 Roll scorn and contempt off me, / because I have guarded your decrees.
119:23 Officials sit and talk against me, / but your servant ponders your rules.
119:24 Yes, your decrees are my joy; / they are the people I go to for advice.
## Dalet: Stuck in the Dust
119:25 My soul sticks to the dust; / give me life by your word.
119:26 I laid out my ways, and you answered me; / teach me your rules.
119:27 Give me insight into the way of your orders, / so I can ponder your wonders.
119:28 My soul drips away with grief; / raise me up by your word.
119:29 Remove the way of falsehood from me, / and favor me with your law.
119:30 The way of faithfulness is the one I chose; / your rulings I have placed in front of me.
119:31 I stick to your decrees, LORD; / do not put me to shame.
119:32 I run the way of your commands, / because you open my heart wide.
## He: Teach Me the Way
119:33 LORD, teach me the way of your rules, / and I will guard it to the last.
119:34 Make me understand, so I can guard your law / and keep it with the whole heart.
119:35 Make me walk the trail of your commands, / for that is where my delight is.
119:36 Bend my heart toward your decrees, / and not toward dishonest profit.
119:37 Make my eyes pass by without looking at emptiness; / in your way give me life.
119:38 Make your saying stand for your servant, / the saying that is for those who fear you.
119:39 Make the disgrace I fear pass away from me, / because your rulings are good.
119:40 Look how I yearn for your orders! / By your righteousness give me life.
## Waw: An Answer for the Mocker
119:41 And let your acts of loyal love come to me, LORD, / your salvation, by your saying.
119:42 And I will have a word to answer the one who mocks me, / because I rely on your word.
119:43 And do not strip the word of truth from my mouth altogether, / for I have hoped for your ruling.
119:44 And I will keep your law at all times, / forever and ever.
119:45 And I will move about with room to spare, / because I have searched out your orders.
119:46 And I will talk about your decrees in front of kings, / and I will feel no shame.
119:47 And I will take my joy in your commands; / I love them.
119:48 And I will raise my hands toward your commands, the ones I love, / and I will ponder your rules.
## Zayin: Comfort in Misery
119:49 Call to mind the word you gave your servant, / since you made me hope in it.
119:50 What comforts me in my misery is this: / your saying has given me life.
119:51 The arrogant have made fun of me past all measure; / still I have not swerved from your law.
119:52 I have called to mind your rulings of old, LORD, / and I am comforted.
119:53 A scorching anger comes over me at the wicked, / who leave your law behind.
119:54 Songs, that is what your rules have been to me, / in the house where I live as a guest.
119:55 At night I have thought of your name, LORD, / so I will keep your law.
119:56 This is what has been mine: / I have guarded your orders.
## Chet: The LORD My Share
119:57 My share is the LORD. / I have said I will keep your words.
119:58 With all the heart I have pleaded before your face; / show me favor by your saying.
119:59 I took stock of my ways, / and I turned my feet back toward your decrees.
119:60 I was quick, I did not hold back, / in keeping your commands.
119:61 Ropes of the wicked wrapped around me; / I did not forget your law.
119:62 In the middle of the night I rise to give you thanks / for the rulings of your righteousness.
119:63 I am a partner of all who fear you / and of those who keep your orders.
119:64 Your loyal love, LORD, fills the earth; / teach me your rules.
## Tet: Good to Be Brought Low
119:65 Good is what you have done for your servant, LORD, / by your word.
119:66 Good discernment and knowledge, teach me these, / since I have trusted your commands.
119:67 I used to wander off, before I was brought low; / now I keep your saying.
119:68 Good are you, and you do good; / teach me your rules.
119:69 The arrogant plaster falsehood over me; / I, with all the heart, guard your orders.
119:70 Their heart has gone dull like fat; / as for me, your law is my delight.
119:71 Being brought low did me good; / it was so I would learn your rules.
119:72 Good for me is the law your mouth has spoken, / more than gold and silver by the thousand.
## Yod: Your Hands Made Me
119:73 Your own hands made me and shaped me; / make me understand, and I will learn your commands.
119:74 May those who fear you look at me and be glad, / because I have waited in hope for your word.
119:75 LORD, I know your rulings are righteousness itself, / and you were faithful when you brought me low.
119:76 Please let your loyal love come and comfort me, / by your saying to your servant.
119:77 Let your acts of compassion reach me, and I will live, / for your law is all my delight.
119:78 May the arrogant be ashamed, since they bent me crooked with a lie; / as for me, I will ponder your orders.
119:79 May those who fear you come back to me, / and those who know your decrees.
119:80 Make my heart blameless in your rules, / so that I will not be ashamed.
## Kaf: A Skin Bottle in the Smoke
119:81 My soul is spent with longing for your salvation; / for your word I wait in hope.
119:82 My eyes are spent watching for your saying, / as I say, "When will you comfort me?"
119:83 I am like a skin bottle in the smoke; / your rules I have not forgotten.
119:84 How many days does your servant have left? / When will you bring my pursuers to judgment?
119:85 The arrogant have dug pits for me, / which is not in keeping with your law.
119:86 Every one of your commands is faithful. / People hunt me down with a lie; help me!
119:87 A little more and they would have finished me in the land, / but I did not let go of your orders.
119:88 By your loyal love give me life, / and I will keep the decree your mouth has spoken.
## Lamed: Your Word Stands Forever
119:89 Forever, LORD, your word / is set fast in the heavens.
119:90 Your faithfulness runs from generation to generation; / you made the earth firm, and there it stands.
119:91 Today they stand by your rulings, / for everything is your servant.
119:92 Without your law as my delight, / I would have been lost in my misery.
119:93 I will never forget your orders, / because through them you gave me life.
119:94 I belong to you; rescue me, / since I have searched out your orders.
119:95 The wicked have waited for me, to make an end of me; / I pay close attention to your decrees.
119:96 Everything complete, I have seen, comes to an end; / your command reaches very wide.
## Mem: How I Love Your Law
119:97 How I love your law! / The whole day long I ponder it.
119:98 More than my enemies, your command makes me wise, / since it is mine forever.
119:99 More than all my teachers I have gained insight, / for I ponder your decrees.
119:100 More than old men I understand, / because I have guarded your orders.
119:101 From every evil path I have held back my feet, / so I could keep your word.
119:102 From your rulings I have not swerved, / because you are the one who taught me.
119:103 How smooth your saying is to my palate, / smoother than honey in my mouth!
119:104 From your orders I learn to understand; / that is why I hate every path of lies.
## Nun: A Lamp for My Foot
119:105 A lamp for my foot is your word, / and a light for my trail.
119:106 I have sworn, and I will make it stand: / I will keep the rulings of your righteousness.
119:107 I have been brought down very low; / LORD, give me life by your word.
119:108 Please take pleasure, LORD, in the freewill offerings of my mouth, / and teach me your rulings.
119:109 My life is in my palm all the time, / yet I have not forgotten your law.
119:110 The wicked put out a snare for me, / yet from your orders I did not wander.
119:111 I have taken your decrees as my inheritance forever, / for they are what makes my heart glad.
119:112 I have bent my heart toward doing your rules, / forever, to the last.
## Samekh: My Hiding Place
119:113 People of divided mind I hate, / and your law I love.
119:114 My hiding place and my shield are you; / for your word I have waited in hope.
119:115 Turn aside from me, you who do evil, / and let me guard the commands of my God.
119:116 Prop me up by your saying, so I may live; / do not shame me in what I hope for.
119:117 Hold me steady, and I will be saved, / and I will keep my eyes on your rules always.
119:118 You toss aside everyone who wanders from your rules, / for their trickery is falsehood.
119:119 Like slag you put an end to all the wicked of the earth; / that is why I love your decrees.
119:120 My body bristles with dread of you, / and your rulings make me afraid.
## Ayin: Stand Surety for Your Servant
119:121 Justice and righteousness are what I have done; / do not hand me over to my oppressors.
119:122 Stand surety for your servant, for his good; / keep the arrogant from crushing me.
119:123 My eyes are spent watching for your salvation, / for the saying of your righteousness.
119:124 Deal with your servant by your loyal love, / and teach me your rules.
119:125 Your servant is what I am; make me understand, / and I will know your decrees.
119:126 It is time to act for the LORD; / they have made your law void.
119:127 So I love your commands / beyond gold, beyond refined gold.
119:128 So I hold that all your orders about everything are right; / every path of lies I hate.
## Pe: Your Decrees Are Wonders
119:129 Wonders are what your decrees are; / so my soul guards them.
119:130 The doorway of your words lets in light, / making the simple understand.
119:131 My mouth I opened wide, and I gasped, / for I yearned for your commands.
119:132 Face toward me and show me favor, / as is right for those who love your name.
119:133 Set my footsteps firm in your saying; / let no evil be master over me.
119:134 Ransom me from people who crush me, / and I will keep your orders.
119:135 Shine your face on your servant, / and teach me your rules.
119:136 My eyes pour down channels of water, / because they do not keep your law.
## Tsade: You Are Righteous
119:137 Righteous you are, LORD, / and your rulings are straight.
119:138 The decrees you commanded are righteousness / and faithfulness, through and through.
119:139 My zeal has been the end of me, / because my enemies forgot your words.
119:140 Your saying is refined all the way through, / and your servant loves it.
119:141 Small I am, and scorned; / your orders I have not forgotten.
119:142 Your righteousness is a righteousness that lasts forever, / and truth is what your law is.
119:143 Distress and hard pressure have caught up with me; / my delight is your commands.
119:144 Righteousness forever is what your decrees are; / make me understand, so that I live.
## Qof: Calling Before Dawn
119:145 I have called out with the whole heart: answer me, LORD; / I will guard your rules.
119:146 To you I have called; save me, / so I can keep your decrees.
119:147 I am up ahead of the dawn, crying for help; / for your word I wait in hope.
119:148 My eyes are ahead of the night watches, / as I ponder your saying.
119:149 Listen to my voice by your loyal love; / LORD, give me life by your ruling.
119:150 Those who chase after vile plans have come close; / from your law they are far away.
119:151 Near are you, LORD, / and every one of your commands is truth.
119:152 From of old I have known, from your decrees, / that you laid their foundation forever.
## Resh: See My Misery
119:153 See my misery and rescue me, / for your law I have not forgotten.
119:154 Take up my case and buy me back; / by your saying give me life.
119:155 Far from the wicked is salvation, / because they never look for your rules.
119:156 Many are your acts of compassion, LORD; / by your rulings give me life.
119:157 Many are those who hunt me and oppose me; / from your decrees I have not swerved.
119:158 I have watched the traitors, and I am disgusted, / because they have not kept your saying.
119:159 See that I have loved your orders; / LORD, by your loyal love give me life.
119:160 Truth is the whole of your word, / and every ruling of your righteousness is forever.
## Shin: Great Peace
119:161 Officials have hunted me without cause, / yet it is your word my heart fears.
119:162 I am glad over your saying / like someone who carries off a great haul of plunder.
119:163 Falsehood I hate and find disgusting; / your law I love.
119:164 Seven times in a day I have praised you / for the rulings of your righteousness.
119:165 Peace in plenty belongs to those who love your law; / there is no stumbling block for them.
119:166 LORD, I look forward to your salvation, / and your commands I carry out.
119:167 My soul has kept your decrees, / and I have loved them deeply.
119:168 I have kept your orders and your decrees; / every way I take lies open before you.
## Tav: A Lost Sheep
119:169 May my cry come close before you, LORD; / by your word make me understand.
119:170 May my prayer for mercy reach your presence; / by your saying pull me free.
119:171 Let praise bubble up from my lips, / because you teach me your rules.
119:172 Let my tongue sing back your saying, / since every one of your commands is righteousness.
119:173 May your hand be there to help me, / because your orders are what I have chosen.
119:174 I have craved your salvation, LORD, / and your law is all my delight.
119:175 May my soul live, so it can praise you; / may your rulings come to my help.
119:176 I have wandered like a sheep that is lost; come looking for your servant, / for your commands I have not forgotten.
