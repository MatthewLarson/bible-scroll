## Book Five (Psalms 107–150)
## Psalm 107: Let the redeemed say so
107:1 Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; / his loyal love is forever.
107:2 Let those the LORD has redeemed say it, / the ones he redeemed out of the foe's grip,
107:3 gathering them from the lands, / from sunrise and from sunset, / from the north and from the sea.
## Lost in the wilderness
107:4 They strayed in the wilderness, on a wasteland road; / a city to settle in they never found.
107:5 Hungry, and thirsty as well, / their life was fading out inside them.
107:6 Then they cried to the LORD, hemmed in as they were, / and out of their tight places he pulled them free.
107:7 He set them on a straight road / that took them to a city to settle in.
107:8 Let them give the LORD thanks for his loyal love, / and for the wonders he does for human beings.
107:9 For he has given the craving soul its fill, / and filled up the hungry soul with good.
## Prisoners in the dark
107:10 Others were sitting in the dark and deep shadow, / held prisoner by misery and iron,
107:11 because they had rebelled against God's words / and scorned the plan of the Most High.
107:12 He humbled their hearts through toil; / they stumbled, and no helper came.
107:13 They cried to the LORD, hemmed in as they were, / and out of their tight places he saved them.
107:14 Out of darkness and deep shadow he led them, / and he snapped their ropes.
107:15 Let them give the LORD thanks for his loyal love, / and for the wonders he does for human beings.
107:16 For he has broken bronze gates to pieces / and chopped iron bars in two.
## Fools at death's gates
107:17 Fools they were: for their rebel ways / and for their wrongdoing they suffered.
107:18 Their throats turned away from every food, / and they reached the gates of death.
107:19 They cried to the LORD, hemmed in as they were, / and out of their tight places he saved them.
107:20 He sent his word to heal them / and let them escape from their pits.
107:21 Let them give the LORD thanks for his loyal love, / and for the wonders he does for human beings.
107:22 Let them sacrifice thank offerings / and tell of his deeds with shouts of joy.
## Sailors in the storm
107:23 Others took ships down to the sea / and traded on the wide waters.
107:24 They were the ones who saw what the LORD does, / his wonders down in the deep.
107:25 He spoke and stood up a storm wind, / and it lifted the waves high.
107:26 Up to the heavens they climbed, down to the depths they dropped; / their nerve melted in the danger.
107:27 They lurched and swayed like a drunk man, / and all their skill was swallowed up.
107:28 They cried to the LORD, hemmed in as they were, / and out of their tight places he brought them.
107:29 He brought the gale down to a hush, / and its waves fell silent.
107:30 How glad they were that all was still! / And he steered them to the port they longed for.
107:31 Let them give the LORD thanks for his loyal love, / and for the wonders he does for human beings.
107:32 Let them lift him high in the gathering of the people / and praise him where the elders sit.
## The God who turns the land around
107:33 He makes rivers into wilderness / and springs of water into parched ground,
107:34 orchard country into salt land, / because the people living there are evil.
107:35 He makes the wilderness into a pond of water, / and dry country into springs of water.
107:36 He settles hungry people there, / and they set up a city to live in.
107:37 They seed the fields and plant vines, / and they reap a crop of fruit.
107:38 He blesses them, and they become very many; / he keeps their cattle from becoming few.
107:39 Then they dwindle and are bent low / by crushing trouble and sorrow.
107:40 He pours scorn on the nobles / and sends them wandering in an empty waste with no road.
107:41 But he sets the needy high, out of reach of misery, / and makes their clans as many as a flock.
107:42 The upright watch and rejoice, / and all wrongdoing must shut its mouth.
107:43 Who is wise? Let him take note of these things, / and let them think over the LORD's many acts of loyal love.
## Psalm 108: My heart is firm
108:0 A song. A psalm of David.
108:1 My heart is firm, God. / I will sing; I will play music, and my glory too.
108:2 Wake up, harp and lyre! / I will wake the dawn.
108:3 I will thank you among the peoples, LORD, / and I will make music to you among the nations.
108:4 For your loyal love is great, higher than the heavens, / and your faithfulness reaches the clouds.
108:5 Rise up high over the heavens, God! / And your glory over all the earth!
108:6 So that the ones you love may be rescued, / save with your right hand, and answer me!
108:7 God spoke in his holiness: / "I will celebrate. I will split up Shechem, / and measure out the Valley of Succoth.
108:8 Gilead is mine; Manasseh is mine. / Ephraim is the helmet on my head; / Judah is my ruler's staff.
108:9 Moab is my washbasin. / On Edom I throw my sandal. / Over Philistia I raise the war cry."
108:10 Who will bring me into the fortified city? / Who will lead me as far as Edom?
108:11 Have you not thrown us off, God? / God, you no longer march out with our armies.
108:12 Give us help against the enemy, / for rescue by human beings is empty.
108:13 With God we will do mighty things, / and he himself will stamp on our foes.
## Psalm 109: They curse, but you will bless
109:0 For the music director. Of David. A psalm.
109:1 O God, the one I praise, / do not keep quiet.
109:2 For wicked mouths and deceiving mouths / have opened wide against me. / They talk to me with a tongue of lies.
109:3 They have ringed me with words of hatred / and attacked me for nothing.
109:4 In return for my love they act as my accusers, / and I? I am all prayer.
109:5 They lay evil on me in place of good, / and hate in place of my love.
## Set a wicked man over him
109:6 Set a wicked man over him; / at his right hand may an accuser take his stand.
109:7 When he is tried, may he walk out condemned, / and may his very prayer turn into sin.
109:8 Let his days be few; / let another man take over his charge.
109:9 Let his sons become orphans / and his wife a widow.
109:10 Let his sons drift, always drifting, and beg; / let them go searching from their ruins.
109:11 Let the moneylender set a trap for all he has, / and let outsiders loot the fruit of his work.
109:12 Let no one keep showing him loyal love; / let no one take pity on his orphans.
109:13 Let what comes after him be cut down; / by the next generation let their name be erased.
109:14 Let his ancestors' guilt be kept in the LORD's memory, / and his mother's sin never be erased.
109:15 Let them stay before the LORD always, / and may he cut their memory off from the earth.
109:16 For he did not remember to show loyal love. / He chased down the poor and the needy / and the crushed in heart, to kill them.
109:17 He loved cursing, and it has come home to him; / he had no delight in blessing, and it has stayed far from him.
109:18 He wore cursing like his robe; / it ran like water into his insides / and like oil into his bones.
109:19 Let it be the coat he pulls around himself, / the belt he ties on day after day.
109:20 This is my accusers' pay from the LORD, / the pay of the ones who speak harm against my soul.
## But you, LORD
109:21 But you, LORD, my Lord, / act for me for the sake of your name. / Your loyal love is good: pull me free.
109:22 For I am poor and needy, / and inside me my heart has been run through.
109:23 Like a shadow as it stretches out, I am gone; / I am shaken out like a locust.
109:24 My knees buckle from fasting, / and my flesh has wasted, with no fat on it.
109:25 I have turned into their insult; / they see me and toss their heads.
109:26 Help me, LORD, my God! / Save me in keeping with your loyal love,
109:27 so that they will know this is your hand: / you, LORD, you did it.
109:28 They curse, and you bless. / They have risen up, and they are put to shame, / and your servant will rejoice.
109:29 Let my accusers wear humiliation for clothes, / and let their shame wrap them like a robe.
109:30 I will give the LORD great thanks out loud; / in the middle of many people I will praise him.
109:31 For he takes his stand at the needy one's right hand, / to save him from those who pass judgment on his life.
## Psalm 110: Sit at my right hand
110:0 Of David. A psalm.
110:1 The LORD declares to my lord: / "Sit at my right hand, / until I set your enemies down / as a stool for your feet."
110:2 From Zion the LORD will send out the scepter of your strength. / Rule, right among your enemies!
110:3 Your people are freewill offerings / on the day you call up your army. / In holy splendor, out of the dawn's womb, / the dew of your young years is yours.
110:4 The LORD has taken an oath, and he will not go back on it: / "You are a priest forever, / after the pattern of Melchizedek."
110:5 The Lord stands at your right side; / he shatters kings when the day of his anger comes.
110:6 He brings judgment among the nations; the land is full of bodies. / He shatters the head across the broad land.
110:7 On the way he drinks from a stream; / that is why he will raise his head.
## Psalm 111: Great are the LORD's works
111:1 Praise the LORD! / I will give the LORD thanks with all my heart, / in the close circle of the upright, and in the community.
111:2 The LORD's works are great, / searched out by everyone who takes pleasure in them.
111:3 What he does is splendor and majesty, / and his righteousness stands for all time.
111:4 He has set up a memorial of his wonders; / gracious and compassionate is the LORD.
111:5 He hands out food to those who fear him; / he keeps his covenant in mind forever.
111:6 He has told his people how strong his works are, / giving them the nations as their inheritance.
111:7 The works of his hands are faithfulness and justice; / all his orders can be trusted.
111:8 They are propped firm for ever and ever, / done with faithfulness and with honesty.
111:9 He sent his people redemption; / he laid down his covenant for all time. / His name is holy and to be feared.
111:10 Wisdom starts with the fear of the LORD; / good insight belongs to all who do them. / His praise stands for all time.
## Psalm 112: Happy is the one who fears the LORD
112:1 Praise the LORD! / Happy is the person who fears the LORD, / who delights deeply in his commands.
112:2 His seed will be a power in the land; / a generation of upright people will be blessed.
112:3 There is wealth and plenty in his house, / and his righteousness stands for all time.
112:4 In the darkness a light rises for the upright: / gracious, compassionate and righteous.
112:5 Good comes to the person who is kind and lends; / he manages his affairs with justice.
112:6 Surely he will never be moved; / the righteous will live on in memory forever.
112:7 He does not fear bad news; / his heart is firm, it trusts in the LORD.
112:8 His heart is propped firm; he has no fear, / and at last he will look down on his foes.
112:9 He has scattered, he has given to the needy. / His righteousness stands for all time; / his horn is raised high with honor.
112:10 The wicked one will see this and fume; / he will grind his teeth and melt away. / The craving of the wicked will be lost.
## The Egyptian Hallel (Psalms 113–118)
## Psalm 113: Who is like the LORD our God?
113:1 Praise the LORD! / Praise, servants of the LORD! / Praise the name of the LORD!
113:2 Let the name of the LORD be blessed / from this moment on and for all time.
113:3 From the sun's rising to its setting, / the LORD's name deserves praise.
113:4 High over all the nations is the LORD; / over the heavens, his glory.
113:5 Who is like the LORD our God, / the one who sets his seat on high,
113:6 who bends down low to look / at the heavens and the earth?
113:7 He lifts the poor up out of the dust; / he raises the needy from the garbage heap
113:8 to give them a seat among nobles, / among the nobles of his people.
113:9 The barren woman of the house he seats / as a glad mother of children. / Praise the LORD!
## Psalm 114: When Israel went out from Egypt
114:1 When Israel went out from Egypt, / Jacob's family from a people who babbled a foreign tongue,
114:2 Judah became his holy place, / Israel his domain.
114:3 The sea saw and ran; / the Jordan turned backward.
114:4 The mountains leapt like rams, / the hills like young sheep.
114:5 What is it, sea, that makes you run? / Jordan, why turn backward?
114:6 Mountains, why leap like rams? / Hills, like young sheep?
114:7 Writhe, earth, before the face of the Lord, / before the face of Jacob's God,
114:8 who changes rock into a pond of water / and flint into a fountain.
## Psalm 115: Not to us
115:1 Not to us, LORD, not to us, / but to your own name give glory, / because of your loyal love and because you are faithful.
115:2 Why must the nations say, / "So where is their God?"
115:3 Our God is in the heavens; / everything he wants, he does.
115:4 Their idols are silver and gold, / something human hands have made.
115:5 A mouth they have, but they cannot speak; / eyes they have, but they cannot see.
115:6 Ears they have, but they cannot hear; / a nose they have, but they cannot smell.
115:7 With their hands they cannot touch; / with their feet they cannot walk; / they make no sound in their throat.
115:8 The ones who make them will end up like them, / everyone who trusts in them.
115:9 Israel, put your trust in the LORD! / He is their help and their shield.
115:10 House of Aaron, put your trust in the LORD! / He is their help and their shield.
115:11 You who fear the LORD, put your trust in the LORD! / He is their help and their shield.
115:12 The LORD has remembered us; he will bless. / He will bless the house of Israel; / he will bless the house of Aaron.
115:13 He will bless those who fear the LORD, / the small along with the great.
115:14 May the LORD add to your number, / to you and to your children.
115:15 Blessed are you by the LORD, / maker of heaven and earth.
115:16 The heavens are the LORD's own heavens, / and the earth is his gift to human beings.
115:17 The dead do not praise Yah, / nor any who go down to the silent place.
115:18 But we, we will bless Yah / from this moment on and for all time. / Praise the LORD!
## Psalm 116: The cup of rescue
116:1 I love, because the LORD hears / my voice, my pleas for mercy.
116:2 For he has turned his ear toward me, / and as long as I live I will call.
116:3 Death's ropes closed around me; / the straits of Sheol found me; / I found trouble and grief.
116:4 And I called on the LORD's name: / "Please, LORD, rescue my life!"
116:5 The LORD is gracious, and he is righteous; / our God is tender with mercy.
116:6 The LORD guards the simple; / I was down low, and he saved me.
116:7 Come back, my soul, to your resting place; / the LORD has repaid you with good.
116:8 For you have rescued my life from death, / my eyes from tears, / my feet from stumbling.
116:9 I will walk about in the LORD's presence / in the lands of the living.
116:10 I kept trusting, even when I said, / "I am bowed down very low."
116:11 I said when I was in a panic, / "Every human being lies."
116:12 What will I give back to the LORD / for all the good he has paid out to me?
116:13 I will raise the cup of rescue / and call on the LORD's name.
116:14 My vows to the LORD I will pay / right in front of all his people.
116:15 The death of his loyal ones / weighs heavily in the LORD's eyes.
116:16 Please, LORD, for I am your servant; / I am your servant, your servant girl's son. / You have loosened my chains.
116:17 To you I will sacrifice a thank offering / and call on the LORD's name.
116:18 My vows to the LORD I will pay / right in front of all his people,
116:19 in the courts of the LORD's house, / right in the middle of you, Jerusalem. / Praise the LORD!
## Psalm 117: All you nations
117:1 All you nations, praise the LORD! / All you peoples, applaud him!
117:2 For his loyal love is mighty over us, / and the LORD's faithfulness is forever. / Praise the LORD!
## Psalm 118: His loyal love is forever
118:1 Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; / his loyal love is forever.
118:2 Let Israel say, / "His loyal love is forever."
118:3 Let the house of Aaron say, / "His loyal love is forever."
118:4 Let those who fear the LORD say, / "His loyal love is forever."
## Out of the tight place
118:5 From the narrow place I called on Yah; / Yah answered me and set me in the open.
118:6 With the LORD for me, I have no fear. / What can a human being do to me?
118:7 The LORD is for me, as my helper, / and I will look down on those who hate me.
118:8 Sheltering in the LORD is better / than relying on people.
118:9 Sheltering in the LORD is better / than relying on nobles.
118:10 Every nation closed in around me; / in the LORD's name I cut them down.
118:11 They closed in, they closed in all around me; / in the LORD's name I cut them down.
118:12 They swarmed around me like bees; / they flickered out like a fire in thornbushes; / in the LORD's name I cut them down.
118:13 You shoved me, shoved me, to make me fall, / but the LORD helped me.
118:14 My strength and my song is Yah, / and he has become my rescue.
118:15 The sound of joyful shouts and rescue / is in the tents of the righteous: / "The right hand of the LORD acts with might!
118:16 The right hand of the LORD lifts high; / the right hand of the LORD acts with might!"
118:17 I will not die, for I will live / and tell what Yah has done.
118:18 Yah has trained me with hard discipline, / but to death he did not give me up.
## The gate of the LORD
118:19 Open the gates of righteousness for me; / I will enter them and give Yah thanks.
118:20 This is the gate that belongs to the LORD; / through it the righteous will enter.
118:21 I will thank you, for you answered me / and have become my rescue.
118:22 A stone that the builders threw out / has become the head of the corner.
118:23 From the LORD this came about; / in our eyes it is a marvel.
118:24 This is the day the LORD has acted; / let us shout for joy and be glad in it.
118:25 LORD, please, save us now! / LORD, please, make us prosper now!
118:26 Blessed is the one who comes in the LORD's name! / We bless you, all of you, from the LORD's house.
118:27 The LORD is God, and he gave us light. / Tie the festival offering with cords / to the horns of the altar.
118:28 You are my God, and I will thank you; / my God, I will lift you high.
118:29 Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; / his loyal love is forever.
