## Psalm 102: A prayer of the afflicted
102:0 A prayer of a person in misery, when he faints away and pours out his troubled thoughts before the LORD.
102:1 LORD, listen to my prayer, / and let my cry for help reach you.
102:2 Do not hide your face from me / on a day when trouble presses me. / Bend your ear toward me. / On a day when I call, hurry and answer me.
102:3 For my days have run out in smoke, / and my bones are scorched like a hearth.
102:4 Like a plant, my heart is beaten down and dried up, / for I have forgotten to eat my food.
102:5 With all the noise of my groaning, / my bones stick to my flesh.
102:6 I am like the owl of the open wilderness; / I have become like an owl among the rubble.
102:7 I stay awake all night; / I have become like a lone bird on a roof.
102:8 My enemies insult me the whole day; / those who sneer at me use me in their curses.
102:9 For I have eaten ashes as if they were bread, / and I have mixed my drink with weeping,
102:10 because of your rage and your anger; / you picked me up and flung me away.
102:11 My days are a shadow stretching out at evening, / and I dry up like a plant.
## Zion will be built again
102:12 But you, LORD, sit on your throne forever, / and your name is remembered age after age.
102:13 You will get up and show Zion compassion, / for it is the time to be kind to her. / Yes, the hour you set has come.
102:14 For your servants take delight in her stones, / and they feel tenderness for her dust.
102:15 The nations will be in awe of the LORD's name, / and all the kings on earth, of your glory.
102:16 For the LORD has built Zion; / he has been seen in his glory.
102:17 He has turned toward the prayer of those stripped bare, / and he has not looked down on their prayer.
102:18 Let this be put in writing for the next generation; / a people that will be created will praise Yah.
102:19 For he bent down to look from his holy heights; / the LORD looked from the heavens down to the earth,
102:20 to hear the moaning of the prisoner, / to unbind the children of death,
102:21 so that people may tell of the LORD's name in Zion / and of his praise in Jerusalem,
102:22 when the peoples are gathered as one, / and the kingdoms too, to serve the LORD.
## Short days, endless years
102:23 On the road he has brought my strength low; / he has made my days short.
102:24 I said, "My God, do not take me away / when my days are only half done; / your years go on through generation after generation."
102:25 Long ago you set the earth on its foundations; / your hands made the heavens.
102:26 They will be lost, but you will stand. / All of them will wear thin like a coat; / you will change them like clothing, and they will be gone.
102:27 But you are he, / and your years will never run out.
102:28 Your servants' children will settle down and stay, / and their descendants will stand firm in your presence.
## Psalm 103: Bless the LORD, my soul
103:0 Of David.
103:1 Bless the LORD, my soul; / all that is deep inside me, bless his holy name.
103:2 Bless the LORD, my soul, / and forget none of the good he has done.
103:3 He pardons every wrong you have done; / he heals every sickness you have.
103:4 He ransoms your life from the Pit; / he sets loyal love and tender mercy on you like a crown.
103:5 He fills your years with good things, / and like the eagle, you grow young again.
103:6 The LORD does acts of justice / and sets things right for all the downtrodden.
103:7 He let Moses know his ways; / he let the Israelites know his deeds.
103:8 Compassionate and gracious is the LORD, / slow to anger and great in loyal love.
103:9 He will not go on accusing without end; / he will not nurse his anger for all time.
103:10 He has not done to us what our sins have earned, / or paid us back to match our guilt.
103:11 For like the height of the heavens over the earth, / his loyal love stands strong over those who fear him.
103:12 As distant as the sunrise is from the sunset, / that is how far he has put our rebellions from us.
## As a father feels for his children
103:13 The way a father feels tenderly for his children / is the way the LORD feels for those who fear him.
103:14 For he knows what we are made of; / he keeps in mind that we are dust.
103:15 A human being's days are like grass; / like a wildflower in the field, so he blossoms.
103:16 A wind blows across it, and it is not there; / its spot no longer recognizes it.
103:17 But the loyal love of the LORD rests on those who fear him / from long ago and on forever, / and his righteousness reaches the children of their children,
103:18 for those who guard his covenant / and keep his orders in mind so as to do them.
## All his works, bless him
103:19 In the heavens the LORD has set his throne firm, / and his royal power is over everything.
103:20 His angels, bless the LORD, / you mighty in strength who do what he says, / who obey when his word is spoken.
103:21 Bless the LORD, all his armies, / you who serve him and do what pleases him.
103:22 Bless the LORD, all he has made, / everywhere under his rule. / Bless the LORD, my soul.
## Psalm 104: The world God made
104:1 Bless the LORD, my soul. / LORD my God, how very great you are! / You have dressed yourself in splendor and majesty.
104:2 You wrap light around you like a cloak; / you spread out the heavens like a tent curtain.
104:3 He sets the rafters of his rooms upstairs in the waters; / he turns the clouds into his chariot; / he goes walking on the wind's wings.
104:4 He makes the winds into his messengers / and a blazing fire into his servants.
## Waters put in their place
104:5 He founded the earth on its firm bases; / it will not totter for ever and ever.
104:6 The deep was the robe you put over it; / water stood higher than the mountains.
104:7 When you scolded them, they ran; / at the noise of your thunder they hurried away.
104:8 Up the mountains, down the valleys they went, / to the spot you had fixed for them.
104:9 You drew a line they will not pass over, / and they will not turn back to cover the earth.
104:10 He sends springs out into the stream valleys; / they make their way between the mountains.
104:11 They water every creature of the open country; / the wild donkeys drink until their thirst is broken.
104:12 Next to them live the birds of the heavens; / from the thick leaves they raise their voices.
104:13 He waters the mountains from his rooms upstairs; / the earth has its fill from the fruit of what you do.
## Food from the ground
104:14 He makes green grass sprout for the herds, / and crops for the work of human hands, / so that bread comes up out of the ground,
104:15 and wine that makes people glad at heart, / so that faces glow with oil, / and bread that keeps people's hearts strong.
104:16 The LORD's trees have all the water they want, / the cedars of Lebanon that he planted.
104:17 The small birds build nests there; / the pine trees are the stork's house.
104:18 The tall mountains are for the ibex; / the cliffs are where the rock badgers hide.
## Night and day, land and sea
104:19 He made the moon to keep the appointed times; / the sun knows where it sets.
104:20 You lay down the dark, and night comes; / then every forest creature is on the move.
104:21 The young lions roar after their prey; / they ask God for their food.
104:22 The sun comes up; they gather themselves in / and settle down in their lairs.
104:23 Then a person heads out to his work / and toils until evening.
104:24 LORD, how many things you have made! / You made every one of them with wisdom. / The earth is filled with what you own.
104:25 And this sea, so big and broad on every side: / in it are crawling things past counting, / living creatures small and great alike.
104:26 The ships sail back and forth there, / and Leviathan, which you shaped to sport in it.
## All of them wait on you
104:27 They all wait on you / to hand them their food in its season.
104:28 You give, and they collect it. / You open your hand, and they eat their fill of good things.
104:29 You hide your face, and they panic. / You gather back their breath, and they die / and go back to their dust.
104:30 You send your breath out, and they are created; / you make the surface of the ground new.
104:31 Let the LORD's glory be forever; / let the LORD be glad in what he has made.
104:32 One look from him at the earth, and it shakes; / one touch on the mountains, and they pour out smoke.
104:33 As long as I live I will sing to the LORD; / I will make music for my God for as long as I am here.
104:34 May my musing be sweet to him; / I will be glad in the LORD.
104:35 Let sinners be finished off from the earth, / and let the wicked be gone for good. / Bless the LORD, my soul. / Praise the LORD!
## Psalm 105: He keeps his covenant in mind
105:1 Give thanks to the LORD; call out his name. / Let the peoples know his deeds.
105:2 Sing to him, make music for him; / speak of every wonder he has done.
105:3 Make your boast in his holy name; / let those who seek the LORD be glad at heart.
105:4 Seek the LORD and his strength; / look for his face at all times.
105:5 Call to mind the wonders he worked, / his marvels, and the rulings from his mouth,
105:6 you seed of Abraham, his servant, / you sons of Jacob, whom he chose.
105:7 He, the LORD, is our God; / his rulings reach the whole earth.
105:8 He keeps his covenant in mind for all time, / the word he gave as a command to a thousand generations,
105:9 what he agreed with Abraham, / his sworn word to Isaac.
105:10 He set it up for Jacob as a rule, / for Israel as a covenant for all time,
105:11 saying, "I will give you the land of Canaan, / the share of your inheritance."
105:12 When they were men who could be counted, / very few, foreigners living there,
105:13 they went about from one nation to the next, / from a kingdom to a different people.
105:14 He did not let anyone wrong them; / he scolded kings because of them:
105:15 "Hands off my anointed ones! / Don't hurt my prophets."
## Joseph sent ahead
105:16 Then he called a famine down on the land / and snapped every staff of bread.
105:17 He had sent someone on before them; / Joseph was sold off to be a slave.
105:18 They tormented his foot with fetters; / his neck went into iron,
105:19 until the time his word came to pass; / the LORD's saying refined him like metal.
105:20 A king sent word and untied him, / a ruler of nations, and let him go.
105:21 He put him in charge of his household / and made him ruler of everything he had,
105:22 to tie up his officials as he wished / and to make his elders wise.
105:23 So Israel went into Egypt, / and Jacob stayed as a foreigner in Ham's land.
105:24 He made his people bear many children / and grow mightier than their enemies.
105:25 He turned their hearts toward hating his people, / toward scheming against his servants.
## Signs in the land of Ham
105:26 He sent his servant Moses / and Aaron, the one he had chosen.
105:27 They set out his signs among them / and wonders in Ham's land.
105:28 He sent darkness, and it grew dark, / and they did not defy his word.
105:29 He changed their water into blood / and made their fish die.
105:30 Frogs swarmed all over their land, / even in their kings' bedrooms.
105:31 He spoke, and the swarm came on, / and gnats in all their borders.
105:32 Instead of rain he sent them hail / and flames of fire across their land.
105:33 He beat down their grapevines and their figs / and smashed the trees all through their borders.
105:34 He spoke, and the locusts came, / and the hoppers, too many to count.
105:35 They ate every plant in their land; / they ate the fruit of their fields.
105:36 He struck every firstborn in their land, / the first fruit of all their manhood.
## Out of Egypt and into the land
105:37 He led them out carrying silver and gold, / and in his tribes not one person tripped.
105:38 Egypt rejoiced as they went out, / since the fear of them had fallen on it.
105:39 He stretched a cloud over them for shelter, / and a fire to light up the night.
105:40 They asked, and in came the quail; / he gave them their fill of bread from heaven.
105:41 He split open a rock, and the waters poured out; / a river ran through the parched places.
105:42 For he kept in mind his holy word / and Abraham his servant.
105:43 So he led his people out rejoicing, / his chosen ones out with glad cries.
105:44 He handed them the nations' lands, / and they took possession of what the peoples had toiled for.
105:45 This was so that they would keep his rules / and guard his laws. / Praise the LORD!
## Psalm 106: We have sinned with our ancestors
106:1 Praise the LORD! / Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; / his loyal love is forever.
106:2 Who is able to put the LORD's mighty acts into words, / or to make all his praise be heard?
106:3 Happy are those who hold to justice, / the one who does what is right in every season.
106:4 LORD, keep me in mind with the goodwill you show your people; / come and see to me with your rescue,
106:5 so that I may look on the good things your chosen ones receive, / and be glad with your nation's joy, / and boast together with your own inheritance.
## At the sea
106:6 We have sinned along with our ancestors. / We have gone wrong. We have done evil.
106:7 Our ancestors in Egypt paid no attention to your wonders. / Your many acts of loyal love slipped from their minds, / and they were defiant at the sea, at the Sea of Reeds.
106:8 Still he saved them, for the sake of his name, / to show everyone his strength.
106:9 He scolded the Sea of Reeds, and it went dry; / he walked them through the deeps as though it were the wilderness.
106:10 He rescued them from the grip of the one who hated them; / he redeemed them out of the enemy's power.
106:11 The water closed over their foes; / not one of them was left.
106:12 Then they put their trust in his words / and sang praise to him.
## In the wilderness
106:13 They were quick to forget what he had done; / they would not wait for his plan.
106:14 In the wilderness they craved and craved; / they tried God in the barren land.
106:15 He gave them what they demanded, / but he sent a thinning sickness down their throats.
106:16 They grew jealous of Moses in the camp, / and of Aaron, the LORD's holy one.
106:17 The earth split open and swallowed Dathan; / it closed over the community of Abiram.
106:18 A fire was lit in their community; / flames ate up the wicked.
106:19 They made a calf at Horeb / and bowed low to something cast in metal.
106:20 They traded their glory / for the shape of an ox that feeds on grass.
106:21 They forgot God, the one who rescued them, / the doer of great things in Egypt,
106:22 of wonders in Ham's land, / of fearsome acts at the Sea of Reeds.
106:23 He talked of wiping them out, / and he would have, had not Moses, his chosen one, / stood in the broken wall facing him / to turn back his rage from destroying them.
106:24 They rejected the land worth wanting; / they did not trust his word.
106:25 They complained inside their tents / and would not listen to the LORD's voice.
106:26 So he lifted his hand to them in an oath / that he would bring them down in the wilderness,
106:27 and their children too, among the nations, / scattering them through the lands.
106:28 They tied themselves to the Baal of Peor / and ate the sacrifices of the dead.
106:29 They made him angry with what they did, / and a plague burst out on them.
106:30 But Phinehas rose and gave judgment, / and the plague was held back.
106:31 It was reckoned to him as righteousness, / for generation after generation, for all time.
106:32 At the waters of Meribah they stirred up his anger, / and Moses came to harm on their account,
106:33 for they defied his spirit, / and he let careless words slip from his lips.
## In the land
106:34 They did not wipe out the peoples / the LORD had spoken to them about.
106:35 They mixed in among the nations / and picked up their practices.
106:36 They served their idols, / and those became a trap for them.
106:37 They offered up their own sons / and their daughters to demons.
106:38 They poured out blood that was innocent, / the blood of their own sons and daughters / that they offered for Canaan's idols, / and the land was defiled with bloodshed.
106:39 They became unclean through their works / and were unfaithful like a prostitute in their practices.
106:40 The LORD's anger blazed against his people, / and he found his own inheritance abhorrent.
106:41 He put them in the power of the nations, / and people who hated them became their masters.
106:42 Their enemies crushed them down, / and they were brought low under their hand.
106:43 Again and again he pulled them out, / but they went on defying him with their schemes, / and they sank under their own guilt.
## Gather us from the nations
106:44 Still he looked on when trouble came to them, / as he heard their pleading cry.
106:45 He kept his covenant in mind on their behalf / and changed his mind, so many were his acts of loyal love.
106:46 He let them find pity / in the eyes of all who had taken them captive.
106:47 Rescue us, LORD our God, / and gather us from the nations, / to thank your holy name / and to boast in praising you.
## The blessing that closes Book Four
106:48 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, / from the ages past to the ages to come. / And let all the people say, "Amen!" / Praise the LORD!
