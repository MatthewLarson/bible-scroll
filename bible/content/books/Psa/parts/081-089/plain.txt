## Shout at the festival
81:0 For the music director. On the gittith. Of Asaph.
81:1 Ring out your joy to God, our strength! / Raise a shout to the God of Jacob!
81:2 Take up a song, beat the tambourine, / play the pleasant lyre and the harp.
81:3 At the new moon blow the ram's horn, / at the full moon, when our feast day comes.
81:4 For it is a rule for Israel, / a ruling from the God of Jacob.
81:5 He made it a decree in Joseph / when he went out over the land of Egypt. / I hear a speech I had never known:
## God's speech at the feast
81:6 "I lifted the load from his shoulder; / his palms let go of the basket.
81:7 When trouble came, you called, and I pulled you out. / I answered you, hidden in the thunder. / At the waters of Meribah I put you to the test. Selah
81:8 Listen, my people: I am giving you a warning. / Israel, if only you would listen to me!
81:9 No strange god is to be among you; / never bow low to a god from abroad.
81:10 I am the LORD your God, / who brought you up from the land of Egypt. / Open your mouth as wide as you can, and I will fill it.
81:11 But my people paid no heed to my voice; / Israel was not willing to have me.
81:12 So I left them to the stubbornness of their hearts; / they walked by their own schemes.
81:13 Oh, if my people were listening to me, / if Israel were walking in my ways,
81:14 in no time I would humble their enemies, / and against their attackers I would turn my hand.
81:15 The LORD's haters would come cringing to him, / and their time would last forever.
81:16 He would feed him with the best of the wheat; / I would fill you with honey from the rock."
## God judges the gods
82:0 A psalm of Asaph.
82:1 God takes his stand in the assembly of El; / among the gods he passes judgment:
82:2 "How long will you give crooked rulings / and show the wicked favor? Selah
82:3 Judge for the weak and the orphan; / put the poor and the penniless in the right.
82:4 Help the weak and the needy escape; / snatch them from the grip of the wicked.
82:5 They do not know, and they do not understand; / they wander about in the dark. / Every foundation of the earth totters.
82:6 I said, 'You are gods, / and sons of the Most High, every one of you.'
82:7 Yet you will die as humans die; / you will fall like any one of the princes."
82:8 Stand up, God! Be the judge of the earth, / for you will take all the nations as your own.
## The nations plot
83:0 A song. A psalm of Asaph.
83:1 God, give yourself no rest! / Do not be mute, do not keep still, O God!
83:2 See: your enemies are roaring, / and the ones who hate you hold their heads high.
83:3 Against your people they scheme with cunning; / they put their heads together against the ones you shelter.
83:4 They have said, "Come, we will wipe them out so they are no nation; / Israel's name will not be called to mind again."
83:5 They have put their hearts together in one plan; / they are making a pact against you:
83:6 Edom's tents and the Ishmaelites, / Moab and the Hagrites,
83:7 Gebal and Ammon and Amalek, / Philistia with those who live in Tyre.
83:8 Even Assyria is linked with them; / they have lent their arm to Lot's descendants. Selah
## Do to them as before
83:9 Treat them like Midian, / like Sisera, like Jabin at the Kishon River,
83:10 who were wiped out at Endor / and became manure on the soil.
83:11 Treat their noblemen as you treated Oreb and Zeeb, / and all their chiefs like Zebah and Zalmunna,
83:12 who said, "Let God's grazing lands / become our own possession."
83:13 My God, set them whirling like a thistle wheel, / like straw blown in front of the wind.
83:14 Like fire that burns through a forest, / like a flame that sets the hills on fire,
83:15 hunt them down that way with your gale, / and throw them into panic with your storm wind.
83:16 Let disgrace fill their faces, / so they will search for your name, LORD.
83:17 May they be ashamed and dismayed for all time; / may they be humiliated and die out,
83:18 and let them learn that you, you whose name is the LORD, / you alone are Most High above all the earth.
## Longing for God's house
84:0 For the music director. On the gittith. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.
84:1 How dearly loved are your dwellings, / LORD of Armies!
84:2 My being yearns, and even wastes away, / for the LORD's courtyards. / My heart and my body ring out with joy / to the living God.
84:3 The little bird, too, has found a house, / the swallow has a nest of her own / to set her chicks in: / your altars, LORD of Armies, / my King and my God.
84:4 Happy are the ones who live in your house; / they go on praising you. Selah
## The pilgrims' road
84:5 Happy is anyone who finds strength in you, / who carries the highways in the heart.
84:6 They cross the Valley of Baca / and turn it into a spring; / the early rain also wraps it in blessings.
84:7 They walk on, from strength to strength, / until each one presents himself to God in Zion.
84:8 LORD God of Armies, hear my prayer; / turn your ear to me, God of Jacob. Selah
84:9 See, God, the one who is our shield; / turn your eyes to the face of your anointed.
84:10 One day in your courtyards is better than a thousand. / I choose to stand at the threshold of my God's house / rather than to live in the tents of the wicked.
84:11 The LORD God is sun and shield; / grace and glory are what the LORD gives. / He keeps nothing good back / from those who walk wholeheartedly.
84:12 LORD of Armies, / happy is anyone who puts trust in you!
## Turn us again
85:0 For the music director. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.
85:1 LORD, you were pleased with your land; / you turned Jacob's fortunes back.
85:2 You carried off the guilt of your people / and put a cover over all their sin. Selah
85:3 You gathered in all your rage; / you turned back from the heat of your anger.
85:4 Turn us around, God of our rescue; / break off your irritation with us.
85:5 Is your anger at us to last forever? / Will you drag your anger on from generation to generation?
85:6 Is it not you who will turn and bring us back to life, / so your people will be glad in you?
85:7 Let us see your loyal love, LORD, / and give us your saving help.
## Listening for peace
85:8 I will listen for what God, the LORD, says. / He speaks of peace to his people and to his loyal ones; / only, they must not go back to foolishness.
85:9 Yes, his rescue is close to those who fear him, / so that glory will make its home in our land.
85:10 Loyal love and faithfulness have met; / righteousness and peace have kissed.
85:11 Faithfulness sprouts out of the earth, / and righteousness leans out of heaven to look down.
85:12 Yes, the LORD will hand out good things, / and our land will give its crops.
85:13 Righteousness goes out in front of him / and turns his footprints into a path.
## A poor man's prayer
86:0 A prayer of David.
86:1 Bend your ear down, LORD, answer me, / because I am poor and in need.
86:2 Guard my life, for I am one of your loyal ones. / You, my God, rescue your servant, / the one who relies on you.
86:3 Show me grace, Lord, / for I keep calling to you the whole day.
86:4 Fill your servant's soul with gladness, / for I raise my soul to you, Lord.
86:5 You, Lord, are kind and ready to pardon; / your loyal love is great toward everyone who calls to you.
86:6 LORD, open your ears to my prayer; / take note of my voice when I plead with you.
86:7 When my day of distress comes, I call on you, / for you will give me an answer.
## None like you
86:8 Lord, among the gods not one is like you, / and nothing is like the things you do.
86:9 Every nation that you made will come / and bow low in front of you, Lord, / and give glory to your name.
86:10 You are great, and you work wonders. / You are God, you alone.
86:11 Teach me, LORD, your way; / I will walk by your truth. / Make my heart one, / so that it fears your name.
86:12 With my whole heart I will give you thanks, Lord my God, / and give glory to your name for all time.
86:13 For great is your loyal love toward me; / you have pulled my life out of the lowest Sheol.
## A sign of your goodness
86:14 God, arrogant men have stood up against me, / and a band of brutal men is hunting for my life. / They give you no place before their eyes.
86:15 But you, Lord, are a God compassionate and gracious, / slow to anger, great in loyal love and faithfulness.
86:16 Face me and show me grace; / hand your strength to your servant, / and rescue your servant girl's son.
86:17 Do something for me that shows your goodness, / so the people who hate me will see and be shamed, / for you, LORD, have been my help and my comfort.
## Zion, mother of nations
87:0 Of the sons of Korah. A psalm. A song.
87:1 What he founded stands on the holy mountains.
87:2 The gates of Zion are what the LORD loves, / more than all Jacob's dwellings.
87:3 Splendid things are being said of you, / O city of God. Selah
87:4 "I will call Rahab and Babylon to mind / as ones who know me. / See, Philistia, and Tyre, with Cush: / 'This one was born there.'"
87:5 And about Zion they will say, / "One after another was born in her," / and the Most High himself will make her firm.
87:6 The LORD will count, when he writes the peoples down: / "This one was born there." Selah
87:7 Both singers and pipers: / "Every spring of mine is in you."
## A cry from the pit
88:0 A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah. For the music director. On mahalath leannoth. A maskil of Heman the Ezrahite.
88:1 LORD, God of my rescue, / by day I have cried out; at night I am in front of you.
88:2 Let my prayer get through to you; / bend your ear down to my loud cry.
88:3 My whole self has had its fill of trouble; / my life has touched Sheol.
88:4 People already count me with those going down into the pit; / I am like a man with no strength,
88:5 turned loose among the dead, / like the fallen who lie in the grave, / those you no longer remember, / who are cut off from your hand.
88:6 You have laid me in the bottom of the pit, / in dark places, down in the deeps.
88:7 Your fury has settled its weight on me, / and with all your breaking waves you have pressed me down. Selah
88:8 You have put distance between me and those who know me; / you have made me abhorrent to them. / I am locked in, and I will not get out.
88:9 My eye has worn out from misery. / I have called you, LORD, every single day; / I have stretched my palms out toward you.
## Do the dead praise you?
88:10 Is it for the dead that you will do a wonder? / Will the shades get up and give you thanks? Selah
88:11 Will anyone tell of your loyal love in the grave, / or of your faithfulness in Abaddon?
88:12 Will the dark know your wonder, / or the land of forgetting your righteousness?
88:13 As for me, LORD, I shout to you for help, / and in the morning my prayer is there to meet you.
88:14 Why, LORD, do you push my soul away? / Why keep your face hidden from me?
88:15 From boyhood I have been crushed and near to dying. / I carry the terrors you send; I am numb.
88:16 Your rages have rolled over me; / your terrors have put an end to me.
88:17 Like water they swirl around me the whole day; / they have closed around me from every side at once.
88:18 You have put lover and neighbor far away from me; / my companions are the dark.
## I will sing of loyal love
89:0 A maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.
89:1 The LORD's acts of loyal love I will sing forever; / generation after generation, my mouth will tell of your faithfulness.
89:2 For I have said, "Loyal love will stand built forever; / in the heavens you make your faithfulness firm."
89:3 "I have made a covenant with the one I chose; / I have taken an oath to David, my servant:
89:4 'I will make your offspring firm for all time / and build your throne for generation after generation.'" Selah
## None like the LORD
89:5 The heavens give thanks for your wonder, LORD, / yes, for your faithfulness, in the gathering of the holy ones.
89:6 Who up in the skies is the LORD's equal? / Who among the sons of the gods is like the LORD,
89:7 a God dreaded in the inner council of the holy ones, / greatly feared above all who stand around him?
89:8 LORD, God of Armies, who is as strong as you, LORD? / Your faithfulness is all around you.
89:9 You are master of the sea when it swells; / when its waves lift up, it is you who hushes them.
89:10 You crushed Rahab like one cut down in battle; / your powerful arm sent your enemies flying.
89:11 The heavens belong to you; the earth belongs to you as well. / The world and everything in it, you laid its foundations.
89:12 North and south: you made them. / Tabor and Hermon ring out with joy at your name.
89:13 Yours is an arm with power in it; / your hand has strength, your right hand is held high.
89:14 Your throne rests on righteousness and justice; / loyal love and faithfulness come out to meet you.
89:15 Happy the people who know the shout of worship! / They walk, LORD, by the light from your face.
89:16 They are glad in your name the whole day, / and your righteousness raises them high.
89:17 You are the splendor of their strength; / through your favor our horn is lifted high.
89:18 Our shield is the LORD's own; / our king belongs to Israel's Holy One.
## The promise to David
89:19 Back then you spoke in a vision to your loyal ones. You said, / "I have laid help on a warrior; / I have raised up a chosen one out of the people.
89:20 I have found David my servant / and anointed him with my holy oil.
89:21 My hand will hold steady beside him, / and my arm will give him strength.
89:22 No enemy will trick him; / no man of evil will beat him down.
89:23 His attackers I will smash before his eyes, / and the ones who hate him I will cut down.
89:24 He will have my faithfulness and my loyal love at his side; / through my name his horn will rise.
89:25 His hand I will place on the sea, / his right hand on the rivers.
89:26 He will cry out to me, 'You are my Father, / my God, the rock of my rescue.'
89:27 I, for my part, will make him firstborn, / most high over the kings of the earth.
89:28 For all time I will guard my loyal love toward him; / my covenant with him can be relied on.
89:29 I will set his offspring in place for all time, / and his throne for as many days as the heavens have.
## If his sons forsake my law
89:30 If his sons turn their backs on my law / and do not live by my rulings,
89:31 if they profane my rules / and do not keep my commands,
89:32 I will call their rebellion to account with the rod, / and their guilt with blows.
89:33 Yet my loyal love I will not break off from him; / I will not let my faithfulness turn into a lie.
89:34 My covenant I will not profane; / what has passed my lips I will not alter.
89:35 I have taken one oath by my own holiness: / never will I lie to David.
89:36 His offspring will go on for all time, / his throne like the sun in front of me.
89:37 Like the moon it will stay fixed for all time, / and the witness in the skies is faithful." Selah
## But you have rejected him
89:38 Yet you yourself have cast aside and refused; / you have raged at your anointed one.
89:39 You have disowned the covenant of your servant; / you have profaned his crown down to the dirt.
89:40 You have knocked holes in all his walls; / you have made a rubble heap of his forts.
89:41 Every traveler on the road has robbed him; / he is something for his neighbors to mock.
89:42 You have raised his attackers' right hand; / you have made every one of his enemies glad.
89:43 You even bend back the flint edge of his sword, / and you have not held him up in the fight.
89:44 You have stopped his brightness / and flung his throne to the earth.
89:45 You have made his youth short; / you have wrapped shame around him. Selah
## How long, LORD?
89:46 How long, LORD? Will you stay hidden for good? / Is your anger going to burn on like fire?
89:47 Remember how short my lifetime is. / For what nothingness did you create all the children of Adam?
89:48 What man is there who lives on and never sees death, / who gets his life away from the hand of Sheol? Selah
89:49 Where, Lord, are your first acts of loyal love, / the ones you swore to David on your faithfulness?
89:50 Remember, Lord, the disgrace your servants bear, / how I carry in my chest all the many peoples,
89:51 the insults your enemies have thrown, LORD, / the insults they have thrown at the footprints of your anointed.
## Blessing at the end of Book III
89:52 Blessed be the LORD forever. / Amen and amen.
