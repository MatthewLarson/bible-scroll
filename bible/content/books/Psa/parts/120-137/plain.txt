## Among people who hate peace
120:0 A song of the ascents.
120:1 To the LORD I called when trouble came, / and he answered me.
120:2 LORD, snatch my life away from lips that lie, / from a tongue that deceives.
120:3 What will he give you, and what more will he add, / you deceiving tongue?
120:4 A warrior's arrows, sharpened, / with coals of broom-wood!
120:5 How bitter for me! I live as a stranger in Meshech; / I have settled among the tents of Kedar.
120:6 I have lived far too long / next to one who hates peace.
120:7 I am peace itself; but when I speak, / they are ready for war.
## The guard who never sleeps
121:0 A song for the ascents.
121:1 I raise my eyes toward the mountains: / where is my help to come from?
121:2 My help is from the LORD, / maker of heaven and earth.
121:3 May he never let your foot slide; / may the one who guards you never doze.
121:4 Look, the one who guards Israel / does not doze and does not sleep.
121:5 The LORD is your guardian. / The LORD is the shade beside your right hand.
121:6 In the daytime the sun will not strike you, / or the moon in the night.
121:7 The LORD will guard you from every harm; / he will guard your very life.
121:8 The LORD will guard your going out and your coming in / from this moment on and for all time.
## Standing in Jerusalem's gates
122:0 A song of the ascents. Of David.
122:1 I was full of joy when they told me, / "Let's go to the LORD's house."
122:2 Our feet were standing / in your gates, Jerusalem!
122:3 Jerusalem, built up like a city / whose parts are joined into one.
122:4 There the tribes went up, the tribes of Yah, / by a decree given to Israel, / to thank the LORD's name.
122:5 For that is where thrones were placed for judging, / thrones that belong to David's house.
122:6 Ask for peace for Jerusalem: / "May those who love you live at ease.
122:7 Let there be peace on your ramparts, / quiet in your strongholds."
122:8 For my brothers' and my friends' sake, / let me speak: "Peace be in you!"
122:9 Because of the house of the LORD our God, / I will seek what is good for you.
## Eyes on the master's hand
123:0 A song of the ascents.
123:1 Toward you I have raised my eyes, / you whose seat is in the heavens.
123:2 See how slaves keep their eyes on their master's hand, / how a slave woman's eyes are on her mistress's hand. / That is how our eyes are on the LORD our God, / until he is gracious to us.
123:3 Be gracious to us, LORD, be gracious to us! / We have been fed contempt until we are full.
123:4 Far too long we have been fed full / with the jeering of the comfortable, / the contempt of the proud.
## If the LORD had not been with us
124:0 A song of the ascents. Of David.
124:1 Were it not the LORD who stood with us, / so let Israel say,
124:2 were it not the LORD who stood with us / when human beings rose against us,
124:3 then they would have gulped us down alive / in the heat of their anger against us.
124:4 Then the waters would have flooded over us; / a torrent would have run up over our necks.
124:5 Then over our necks would have gone / the waters, swollen with pride.
124:6 The LORD be blessed! / He did not hand us over to their teeth like torn meat.
124:7 Like a bird, our life got loose / from the bird-catchers' trap. / The trap snapped, / and we got loose.
124:8 Our help is in the name of the LORD, / maker of heaven and earth.
## Like the mountains around Jerusalem
125:0 A song of the ascents.
125:1 People who trust the LORD are like Mount Zion: / it will never totter; it sits firm for all time.
125:2 Jerusalem: mountains are all around her, / and the LORD is all around his people / from this moment on and for all time.
125:3 The rod of the wicked will not stay / on the share of land the righteous drew by lot. / Otherwise the righteous might reach out / and turn their hands to wrong.
125:4 LORD, be good to the good, / to people who are straight in heart.
125:5 As for those who bend off onto their twisting paths, / the LORD will march them away with the people who do evil. / Peace on Israel!
## Sowing in tears
126:0 A song of the ascents.
126:1 When the LORD turned Zion's fortunes back again, / we were like people dreaming.
126:2 Our mouths overflowed with laughter then, / our tongues with ringing cries. / Then people in the nations said, / "The LORD has done something great with them."
126:3 The LORD has done something great with us, / and we were glad.
126:4 LORD, turn our fortunes back again, / like the stream beds in the Negev.
126:5 Whoever sows with tears / will reap with a ringing shout.
126:6 He goes and goes, weeping, / carrying the seed bag. / He will come, yes, come home with a ringing shout, / carrying his sheaves.
## If the LORD does not build
127:0 A song of the ascents. Of Solomon.
127:1 If the LORD does not build a house, / its builders have worked on it for nothing. / If the LORD does not guard a city, / the watchman has stayed awake for nothing.
127:2 For nothing you get up early / and sit down late to rest, / eating bread earned with pain. / Just so, he gives the one he loves sleep.
127:3 Look: sons are an inheritance the LORD gives, / and the fruit of the womb is wages paid.
127:4 Sons born when a man is young / are like arrows in the hand of a fighter.
127:5 Happy is the man / who has filled his quiver full of them! / They will not be put to shame / when they argue with enemies in the gate.
## The table of one who fears the LORD
128:0 A song of the ascents.
128:1 Happy is each one who fears the LORD, / each one walking in his ways.
128:2 What your own hands have toiled for, you will eat. / Happy are you! It will go well for you.
128:3 Your wife: a vine heavy with fruit / deep inside your house. / Your children: young olive plants / set all around your table.
128:4 Look! For that is how a man will be blessed, / a man who fears the LORD.
128:5 May the LORD bless you from Zion; / may you look on the good of Jerusalem / every day of your life.
128:6 And may you see children born to your children. / Peace on Israel!
## Plowed, but not beaten
129:0 A song of the ascents.
129:1 Over and over they have attacked me since I was young, / now let Israel say it,
129:2 over and over they have attacked me since I was young, / yet they have not won out over me.
129:3 Across my back the plowmen plowed; / they cut their furrows long.
129:4 The LORD is in the right: / he has cut through the ropes of the wicked.
129:5 Let them be shamed and driven back, / all who hate Zion.
129:6 Let them be like grass on a flat roof / that dries out before it can be pulled.
129:7 It never fills the reaper's hand, / or the lap of the one who ties sheaves.
129:8 No one passing by calls out, / "The LORD's blessing on you!" / "We bless you in the LORD's name!"
## From the depths
130:0 A song of the ascents.
130:1 From the depths I have called you, LORD.
130:2 Lord, listen to my voice. / May your ears be alert / to the sound of my pleading.
130:3 If you were to keep watch over guilt, Yah, / Lord, who would remain standing?
130:4 Yet forgiveness is found with you, / so that people will stand in awe of you.
130:5 I have waited for the LORD; my soul has waited; / I have put my hope in his word.
130:6 My soul looks for the Lord / more than the watchmen look for the morning, / the watchmen for the morning.
130:7 Wait in hope for the LORD, Israel! / For loyal love is with the LORD, / and redemption is with him in plenty.
130:8 And he is the one who will redeem Israel / from all its guilty deeds.
## Like a weaned child
131:0 A song of the ascents. Of David.
131:1 LORD, my heart has not grown tall; / my eyes have not climbed high. / I have not gone walking among great things / or among wonders beyond me.
131:2 Instead I have leveled my soul and hushed it, / like a weaned child lying on its mother. / My soul lies on me like that weaned child.
131:3 Israel, wait in hope for the LORD / from this moment on and for all time.
## David's oath
132:0 A song of the ascents.
132:1 LORD, remember for David / all the pain he took on himself,
132:2 he who swore an oath to the LORD / and made a vow to the Mighty One of Jacob:
132:3 "I will not set foot in the tent of my house; / I will not climb onto the couch that is my bed;
132:4 I will not let my eyes sleep / or my eyelids doze,
132:5 until I find the LORD a place, / a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob."
132:6 Listen: in Ephrathah we heard about it; / in the fields of Jaar we came upon it.
132:7 Let us come into his dwelling; / let us bow low at the footstool for his feet.
132:8 Up, LORD, to your place of rest, / you and the ark of your strength!
132:9 May your priests wear righteousness like clothes, / and your faithful ones shout for joy.
132:10 For the sake of David your servant, / do not turn your anointed one away.
## The LORD's oath
132:11 The LORD swore to David in truth, / and he will not go back on it: / "From the fruit of your body / I will set one on your throne.
132:12 If your sons keep my covenant / and my decrees, which I will teach them, / their sons as well, for all time to come, / will sit on your throne."
132:13 Zion is the place the LORD has chosen; / he has longed to make it his home:
132:14 "This is my place of rest forever and ever. / Here I will make my home, for I have longed for it.
132:15 Her food I will surely bless; / her needy I will fill with bread.
132:16 Her priests I will make wear salvation like clothes, / and her faithful ones will shout and shout for joy.
132:17 There I will make a horn spring up for David. / I have set a lamp in order for my anointed one.
132:18 His enemies I will dress in shame, / but his crown will gleam on him."
## Brothers together
133:0 A song of the ascents. Of David.
133:1 Look how good it is, how lovely, / when brothers live together, all as one!
133:2 It is like good oil on the head / that flows down onto the beard, / Aaron's beard, / that flows down over the neck of his robes.
133:3 It is like Hermon's dew coming down / on the hills of Zion. / There is where the LORD has ordered the blessing: / life for all time.
## Bless the LORD by night
134:0 A song of the ascents.
134:1 Now then, bless the LORD, / all you who serve the LORD, / who stand in the LORD's house night after night.
134:2 Raise your hands toward the sanctuary, / and bless the LORD.
134:3 May the LORD bless you from Zion, / maker of heaven and earth.
## Chosen as his treasure
135:1 Praise the LORD! / Praise the name of the LORD! / Praise, you servants of the LORD,
135:2 who stand in the LORD's house, / in the courtyards of our God's house.
135:3 Praise the LORD! The LORD is good. / Make music to his name, because that is a delight.
135:4 For Yah chose Jacob for himself, / Israel to be his own private treasure.
135:5 I myself know it: the LORD is great, / and our Lord is above every god.
135:6 Everything the LORD wants, he does, / in the heavens and on the earth, / in the seas and in all the ocean depths.
135:7 He lifts the clouds from the edge of the earth, / makes lightning bolts for the rain, / and lets the wind out of his storerooms.
## From Egypt to the land
135:8 It was he who struck Egypt's firstborn, / from humans to animals.
135:9 He sent signs and wonders into the middle of you, Egypt, / on Pharaoh and all his officials.
135:10 It was he who struck many nations / and put powerful kings to death:
135:11 Sihon king of the Amorites, / Og king of Bashan, / and all the kingdoms of Canaan.
135:12 He gave their land as an inheritance, / an inheritance to Israel his people.
## Idols cannot speak
135:13 LORD, your name lasts forever; / LORD, people will call you to mind age after age.
135:14 For the LORD will judge his people / and show his servants pity.
135:15 The nations' idols are silver and gold, / something human hands have made.
135:16 A mouth they have, but they cannot speak; / eyes they have, but they cannot see.
135:17 Ears they have, but they cannot hear; / not even breath is in their mouths.
135:18 The ones who make them will end up like them, / everyone who trusts in them.
135:19 House of Israel, bless the LORD! / House of Aaron, bless the LORD!
135:20 House of Levi, bless the LORD! / You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD!
135:21 From Zion may the LORD be blessed, / the one who makes his home in Jerusalem. / Praise the LORD!
## His loyal love is forever
136:1 Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, / for his loyal love is forever.
136:2 Give thanks to the God of gods, / for his loyal love is forever.
136:3 Give thanks to the Lord of lords, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:4 to him who does great wonders, he alone, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:5 to him who made the heavens with skill, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:6 to him who stretched the earth flat on top of the waters, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:7 to him who made the big lights, / for his loyal love is forever:
136:8 the sun to govern the day, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:9 the moon and stars to govern the night, / for his loyal love is forever;
## Out of Egypt, into the land
136:10 to him who struck Egypt in its firstborn, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:11 and led Israel out of their midst, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:12 with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:13 to him who cut the Sea of Reeds into pieces, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:14 and took Israel across through the middle, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:15 and shook Pharaoh and his army off into the Sea of Reeds, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:16 to him who guided his people in the wilderness, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:17 to him who struck great kings, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:18 and killed majestic kings, / for his loyal love is forever:
136:19 Sihon king of the Amorites, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:20 and Og king of Bashan, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:21 and gave their land as an inheritance, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:22 an inheritance to Israel his servant, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:23 who remembered us when we were brought low, / for his loyal love is forever,
136:24 and tore us free from our foes, / for his loyal love is forever;
136:25 who gives food to every living thing, / for his loyal love is forever.
136:26 Give thanks to the God of the heavens, / for his loyal love is forever.
## By the rivers of Babylon
137:1 Beside the rivers of Babylon, / there we sat; we even wept, / as we remembered Zion.
137:2 On the poplar trees in that land / we hung up our lyres.
137:3 For there the ones who took us captive / asked us for words of song, / our tormentors asked for joy: / "Sing for us from Zion's songs!"
137:4 How can we sing the LORD's song / on foreign soil?
137:5 If I forget you, Jerusalem, / let my right hand forget.
137:6 May my tongue cling to my palate / if I fail to remember you, / if I fail to lift Jerusalem up / above the best of my joys.
## Remember, LORD
137:7 Remember, LORD, against the people of Edom / the day of Jerusalem, / those who kept saying, "Tear it bare! Tear it bare, / right down to the foundation in it!"
137:8 Daughter Babylon, you who will be laid waste, / happy is the one who pays you back / in full for what you dealt out to us.
137:9 Happy is the one who grabs your babies / and shatters them on the rock.
