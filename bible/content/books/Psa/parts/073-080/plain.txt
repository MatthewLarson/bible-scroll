## Psalm 73: Almost slipping
73:0 A psalm of Asaph.
73:1 God really is good to Israel, / to people whose hearts are pure.
73:2 But me, my feet had almost turned aside; / my steps had all but slid out from under me.
73:3 For I was jealous of the boasters / as I watched the wicked thrive.
73:4 There are no pangs in their dying; / their bodies are fat.
73:5 Human trouble passes them by; / they are not struck down with everyone else.
73:6 So they wear pride around their necks; / violence wraps them like clothing.
73:7 Their eye looks out from fat; / the fancies of their heart spill over.
73:8 They jeer and speak of evil; / from on high they talk of crushing others.
73:9 They put their mouths up in the sky, / and their tongues stroll over the earth.
73:10 That is why his people turn this way / and drink their fill of water.
73:11 They ask, "How does God know? / Is there knowledge up with the Most High?"
73:12 Look at them, the wicked! / Carefree forever, their riches keep growing.
73:13 For nothing, then, I kept my heart clean / and washed my hands, clear of guilt.
73:14 I have been struck the whole day long, / and I am rebuked morning after morning.
73:15 If I had said, "I will talk this way too," / look, I would have broken faith with your children's generation.
## In God's holy places
73:16 So I set my mind to understand this, / but it looked like hard labor to me,
73:17 until I came into the holy places of God / and gave thought to where they end up.
73:18 You really have put them where the ground is slick; / you throw them down into ruins.
73:19 How they are turned to waste in an instant! / They come to an end, wiped out by terrors.
73:20 Like a dream to someone who has woken, / Lord, when you get up, you will scorn their image.
73:21 When my heart turned sour / and I was stung to my inmost parts,
73:22 I was stupid; I knew nothing. / I was a dumb animal with you.
73:23 And still I am with you, all the time; / you have taken hold of my right hand.
73:24 Your advice leads me on, / and after that you will take me in glory.
73:25 Who do I have in the heavens? / Having you, I take no delight in anything on earth.
73:26 My body and my heart are used up. / The rock of my heart, and my share, is God forever.
73:27 For look, those far from you will be lost; / you put an end to all who leave you like a prostitute.
73:28 And me? Drawing near to God is my good. / I have made the Lord GOD my shelter, / so that I may recount all your works.
## Psalm 74: The burned sanctuary
74:0 A maskil of Asaph.
74:1 God, why have you pushed us away for good? / Why is your anger smoking at the flock you pasture?
74:2 Remember your community, the one you bought long ago, / the tribe you redeemed as your inheritance, / Mount Zion, where you made your home.
74:3 Lift your feet and come to the ruins that never end; / in the holy place, the enemy has wrecked it all.
74:4 Your foes roared in the middle of your meeting place. / They set up their own signs to be the signs.
74:5 It was like someone raising axes high / in a tangle of trees.
74:6 And now its carved panels, every one, / they hammer down with hatchet and bar.
74:7 They set fire to your sanctuary. / They made the home of your name unholy, razing it to the ground.
74:8 They said to themselves, "Let's crush them, all of them." / They burned up all of God's meeting places across the land.
74:9 We see none of our signs. / There is no prophet now, / and no one with us knows for how long.
74:10 Until when, God, will the foe jeer? / Will the enemy spurn your name for all time?
74:11 Why do you draw your hand back, your right hand? / Pull it out from inside your cloak, and destroy!
## You split the sea
74:12 Yet God is my king from ages past, / doing saving deeds in the middle of the earth.
74:13 You split the Sea by your strength. / You shattered the heads of the dragons on the waters.
74:14 You crushed the heads of Leviathan. / You fed it to a people, to the desert dwellers.
74:15 You cut open spring and torrent. / You dried up rivers that never stop.
74:16 Day belongs to you, and night belongs to you too. / You set up the light and the sun.
74:17 You laid down every border of the earth. / Summer and winter, you shaped them.
## Do not forget
74:18 Remember this, LORD: the enemy jeers, / and a people of fools spurns your name.
74:19 Do not give your turtledove's life to a wild animal; / do not forget your poor ones' lives for all time.
74:20 Pay attention to the covenant, / for the land's dark corners are filled with places where violence lives.
74:21 Do not let the oppressed go back humiliated; / may poor and needy people praise your name.
74:22 Stand up, God, and plead your own case! / Remember the insults a fool throws at you all day.
74:23 Do not forget the noise your foes make, / the roar of those who stand against you, rising and rising.
## Psalm 75: The cup in the LORD's hand
75:0 For the music director. On "Do Not Destroy." A psalm of Asaph. A song.
75:1 We thank you, God, we thank you, / and your name is close by. / They tell the story of your wonders.
75:2 "When I seize the appointed time, / I myself will judge with fairness.
75:3 The earth and all who live on it are melting; / I myself have made its pillars firm. Selah
75:4 I tell the boasters, 'Stop your boasting,' / and the wicked, 'Do not raise your horn.
75:5 Do not raise your horn to the sky, / or speak arrogance with a stiff neck.'"
75:6 For not from where the sun comes out, nor from where it sets, / and not from the wilderness, does anyone's rise come.
75:7 No, God is the one who judges. / This one he puts low, and that one he raises.
75:8 For the LORD holds a cup in his hand, / its wine foaming, full of spiced mix, / and he pours from it. / Yes, its dregs they will drain and drink, / all the wicked of the earth.
75:9 But I will keep speaking out for all time; / I will make music to the God of Jacob.
75:10 "I will chop off every horn the wicked have, / and the horns of the righteous will rise."
## Psalm 76: God's lair in Zion
76:0 For the music director. With stringed instruments. A psalm of Asaph. A song.
76:1 God has made himself known in Judah; / his name stands great in Israel.
76:2 His lair was in Salem, / and his den in Zion.
76:3 There he smashed the bow's fire-arrows, / shield and sword and war. Selah
76:4 Shining with light are you, / glorious beyond the hills of prey.
76:5 The brave-hearted were stripped bare; they drowsed into their sleep. / Not one of the fighting men could find his hands.
76:6 From your rebuke, God of Jacob, / chariot and horse sank into a deep sleep.
76:7 You are fearsome, you alone. / Who can stand in front of you the moment your anger flares?
76:8 From the heavens you made your verdict heard; / the earth was afraid and went quiet,
76:9 when God stood up for judgment, / to rescue all the lowly of the land. Selah
76:10 Even human rage will bring you praise; / you will strap on what remains of the fury.
76:11 Vow to the LORD your God, and pay what you vow. / All who are around him, bring a gift to the Awesome One.
76:12 He clips the spirit of princes; / the kings of the earth hold him in awe.
## Psalm 77: A sleepless night
77:0 For the music director. On Jeduthun. Of Asaph. A psalm.
77:1 I lift my voice to God, and I cry out; / I lift my voice to God, and he hears me.
77:2 When distress came, I searched for the Lord. / All night my hand reached out and never went limp. / My soul would take no comfort.
77:3 I call God to mind, and I moan. / I pour out my complaint, and my spirit sinks. Selah
77:4 You held my eyes from shutting. / I was shaken, and I said nothing.
77:5 I went over the days of old times, / years of ancient ages.
77:6 I recall my song in the night. / I muse with my own heart, / and my spirit digs for answers:
77:7 "Will the Lord cast off for all time, / and never be kind again?
77:8 Is his loyal love gone for good? / Is his word finished, generation after generation?
77:9 Has God forgotten how to show favor? / Or has anger made him lock up his compassion?" Selah
77:10 Then I said, "This is what wounds me: / the right hand of the Most High has changed."
## The waters saw you
77:11 I will bring to mind the LORD's deeds; / I will remember your wonder of long ago.
77:12 I will murmur over everything you have done, / and turn your deeds over in my mind.
77:13 God, your road runs through holiness. / Who is a god as great as God?
77:14 You are God, the one who does wonders. / Among the peoples you have shown how strong you are.
77:15 Your arm redeemed your people, / the descendants of Jacob and Joseph. Selah
77:16 The waters caught sight of you, God; / the waters saw you and twisted in labor; / the deeps themselves quaked.
77:17 Clouds streamed with water. / The thunderheads let out a roar. / Your arrows went flying everywhere.
77:18 Your thunder rumbled in the wheel of the storm. / Lightning bolts lit up the world. / The earth shivered and shook.
77:19 Your road was in the sea, / your trail in the vast waters, / yet no one could make out your footprints.
77:20 Like sheep you guided your people / through Moses and Aaron.
## Psalm 78: Tell the next generation
78:0 A maskil of Asaph.
78:1 Listen, my people, to my teaching. / Turn your ears to what my mouth says.
78:2 I will open my mouth in a parable. / I will bubble out riddles from ancient times,
78:3 what we heard and came to know, / what our ancestors recounted to us.
78:4 We will not keep them hidden from their children. / To a generation still to come we will recount the LORD's praises, / his strength, and the wonders he has worked.
78:5 He set up a decree in Jacob / and placed a law in Israel. / He commanded our ancestors / to make them known to their children,
78:6 so that a later generation might know them, / the children who would be born, / and they would stand up and recount them to their own children,
78:7 so they would set their confidence in God, / not forgetting what God has done, / and would keep his commands,
78:8 and not be like their ancestors, / a generation that was stubborn and defiant, / a generation that did not set its heart right, / whose spirit did not stay true to God.
## They forgot
78:9 Ephraim's sons, archers fitted out with bows, / turned around on the day of war.
78:10 God's covenant they did not keep, / and they would not walk by his law.
78:11 They forgot what he did, / and the wonders he let them see.
78:12 Before their ancestors' eyes he worked wonders, / in the land of Egypt, in the open country of Zoan.
78:13 He split the sea open and took them across. / He made the waters stand up like a mound.
78:14 He guided them with the cloud in the day, / and with a fire's light all through the night.
78:15 He broke rocks open in the wilderness / and let them drink as if from the deeps, in plenty.
78:16 He drew running streams from a crag / and sent water down like rivers.
## Can God set a table?
78:17 But they kept adding sin against him, / defying the Most High in the parched land.
78:18 In their hearts they tried God / by asking for food for their appetite.
78:19 They spoke against God. / They said, "Is God able to lay out a table in the wilderness?
78:20 Yes, he hit a rock, and water flowed out, / and torrents flooded over. / But is he able to give bread as well? / Can he get meat ready for his people?"
78:21 So the LORD heard, and he was enraged. / Fire was lit against Jacob, / and anger went up against Israel.
78:22 For they had no faith in God, / no trust in his rescue.
78:23 He commanded the clouds overhead, / and he threw open heaven's doors.
78:24 He made manna fall like rain on them for food. / He gave them heaven's grain.
78:25 Each one ate the bread of the mighty ones; / he sent them supplies until they had their fill.
78:26 He started an east wind moving in the sky, / and by his strength he steered in the south wind.
78:27 Meat fell on them like rain, thick as dust, / flying birds as many as the sand of the seas.
78:28 He brought them down in the middle of the camp, / all around where they lived.
78:29 So they ate and stuffed themselves full; / he had brought them their craving.
78:30 They were not yet done with their craving; / the food was still in their mouths,
78:31 when God's anger went up against them. / He slew their strongest / and brought Israel's young men to their knees.
## They sought him and lied to him
78:32 Through all of this they kept on sinning, / and they had no faith in his wonders.
78:33 So their days he finished off in emptiness, / and their years in sudden terror.
78:34 When he slew them, then they looked for him; / they came back and searched hard for God.
78:35 They recalled that God was their Rock, / and God Most High their redeemer.
78:36 But they sweet-talked him with their mouths, / and with their tongues they lied to him.
78:37 Their hearts were not firm with him, / and they did not keep true to his covenant.
78:38 Yet he is compassionate; / he makes atonement for guilt and does not destroy. / Many times he turned his anger back / and did not rouse all his fury.
78:39 He bore in mind that they were flesh, / a wind that blows away and never comes back.
## The signs in Egypt
78:40 How many times they defied him in the wilderness, / and caused him pain in the wasteland!
78:41 They turned back and tried God, / and they hurt the Holy One of Israel.
78:42 They did not keep his hand in mind, / nor the day he paid their ransom from the foe,
78:43 how he placed his signs in Egypt, / his wonders in the open country of Zoan.
78:44 He changed their Nile channels to blood; / they could not drink from their streams.
78:45 He let loose the swarm on them, and it ate them up, / and frogs, which brought them to ruin.
78:46 He handed their harvest to the grub, / and the fruit of their toil to the locust.
78:47 He killed their grapevines with the hail, / their fig-sycamores with sleet.
78:48 He gave over their cattle to the hail, / and their livestock to the fire-bolts.
78:49 He let his blazing anger loose on them, / outrage and fury and trouble, / a squad of angels bringing disaster.
78:50 He leveled a road for his anger. / He did not hold back their souls from death, / and handed their life to the plague.
78:51 He struck every firstborn in Egypt, / the first fruit of their manhood in the tents of Ham.
## Into the land
78:52 He moved his people out like sheep / and steered them like a flock through the wilderness.
78:53 He brought them on securely, and they did not fear. / As for their enemies, the sea covered them.
78:54 He brought them into his holy territory, / to this mountain, which his right hand gained.
78:55 He pushed out nations ahead of them / and allotted them their inheritance with a measuring line, / and he had the tribes of Israel live in their tents.
## Shiloh abandoned
78:56 But they tried God Most High and defied him; / his decrees they did not keep.
78:57 They turned away and broke faith, as their ancestors had. / They warped like a bow that cannot be trusted.
78:58 By their high places they provoked him, / and by their idols they roused his jealousy.
78:59 God heard it and flew into a rage, / and he wholly rejected Israel.
78:60 He left the tabernacle of Shiloh behind, / the tent where he had made his home among humankind.
78:61 He let his strength be taken captive, / his beauty fall into the foe's hand.
78:62 He handed his people to the sword, / and he raged at his own inheritance.
78:63 The fire ate its young men, / and its young women heard no wedding praise.
78:64 Its priests went down under the sword, / and its widows did not weep.
## Judah, Zion and David
78:65 Then the Lord woke, like someone who had been asleep, / like a hero letting out a shout after wine.
78:66 He struck his enemies from behind; / he laid on them a disgrace that never ends.
78:67 He turned down Joseph's tent; / Ephraim's tribe was not his choice.
78:68 He chose Judah's tribe instead, / Mount Zion, the one he loved.
78:69 He built his sanctuary like the lofty heights, / like the earth he grounded forever.
78:70 Then he chose his servant David; / he took him from the pens where the sheep were kept.
78:71 He brought him from walking behind the ewes with lambs / to be shepherd over Jacob, his people, / over Israel, his inheritance.
78:72 He shepherded them in the wholeness of his heart / and led them by the know-how of his hands.
## Psalm 79: The nations have come in
79:0 A psalm of Asaph.
79:1 God, the nations have come in, into your inheritance. / They have made your holy temple unclean; / they have turned Jerusalem into piles of rubble.
79:2 The corpses of your servants they have handed / to the birds of the sky to eat, / the flesh of your loyal ones to the wild beasts of the land.
79:3 Their blood they spilled like water / on every side of Jerusalem, / and there was nobody to bury them.
79:4 We have become a disgrace to our neighbors, / something to laugh at and sneer at for those around us.
## How long?
79:5 Until when, LORD? Is your anger going to last forever? / Will your jealousy blaze on like a fire?
79:6 Pour out your fury on the nations that have not known you, / and on kingdoms that never call on your name,
79:7 for they have eaten Jacob up / and turned his pasture-home into waste.
79:8 Do not remember against us the guilt of those before us. / Hurry, let your compassion come out to meet us, / for we are sunk very low.
79:9 Come to our aid, God who saves us, / because of the glory of your name. / Rescue us, and make atonement for our sins / on account of your name.
79:10 Why should the nations say, "Where is their God?" / Let the nations come to know, while we watch, / the vengeance for your servants' blood that was spilled.
79:11 May the prisoner's sighing reach your presence. / With your great arm, keep alive the children of death.
79:12 Repay our neighbors sevenfold, right into their laps, / for the scorn with which they scorned you, Lord.
79:13 And we, the people who are yours, the flock you pasture, / will thank you for all time. / Generation after generation, we will recount your praise.
## Psalm 80: Shepherd of Israel
80:0 For the music director. On "Lilies of the Testimony." Of Asaph. A psalm.
80:1 Shepherd of Israel, give ear! / You who drive Joseph along like sheep, / you who sit on the cherubim as your throne, shine out!
80:2 In front of Ephraim, Benjamin and Manasseh, / rouse your strength, / and come to rescue us.
80:3 God, bring us back. / Make your face shine, and we will be rescued.
80:4 LORD God of Armies, / how long will you smoke with anger at your people's prayer?
80:5 You have given them tears to eat as bread, / and made them drink tears by the measure.
80:6 You turn us into a quarrel for our neighbors, / and our enemies mock among themselves.
80:7 God of Armies, bring us back. / Make your face shine, and we will be rescued.
## The vine from Egypt
80:8 A vine from Egypt you dug up and moved. / You threw out nations, and you planted it.
80:9 You made room in front of it; / it struck its roots down and filled the land.
80:10 Its shadow spread over the mountains, / and its boughs over God's own cedars.
80:11 It stretched its limbs out to the Sea / and its young shoots as far as the River.
80:12 Why have you torn down its walls, / so that every traveler on the road plucks at it?
80:13 A wild hog from the woods chews it down, / and whatever moves in the field grazes on it.
80:14 God of Armies, please turn around! / Gaze down from the heavens and see. / Come and tend this vine,
80:15 the root that your right hand set in the ground, / and the son you made strong for yourself.
80:16 It has been burned in the fire, hacked down. / They are lost at the rebuke of your face.
80:17 May your hand be over the man of your right hand, / over the son of man you made strong for yourself.
80:18 And we will not slip away from you. / Keep us alive, and your name is the one we will call on.
80:19 LORD God of Armies, bring us back. / Make your face shine, and we will be rescued.
