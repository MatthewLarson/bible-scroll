## Thanks in front of the gods
138:0 Of David.
138:1 I will thank you with all my heart; / in front of the gods I will make music for you.
138:2 I will bow low toward your holy temple / and thank your name for your loyal love and your faithfulness, / for you have made your word great, / even above all your name.
138:3 The day I called out, you answered me; / you put boldness in me, strength in my soul.
138:4 LORD, all the kings of the earth will thank you, / for they have heard what your mouth has said.
138:5 They will sing of the paths the LORD takes, / because the LORD's glory is great.
138:6 High as the LORD is, he looks on the lowly, / while he knows the proud from a distance.
138:7 If I walk where trouble is all around, you keep me alive. / You send out your hand against my enemies' rage, / and your right hand saves me.
138:8 The LORD will carry it through for me. / LORD, your loyal love lasts forever; / the works of your hands, do not let them fall.
## You know me
139:0 For the music director. Of David. A psalm.
139:1 LORD, you have probed me, and you know me.
139:2 You yourself know my sitting down and my getting up; / from a distance you grasp what I am thinking.
139:3 You sift through my walking and my lying down; / every path I take is familiar to you.
139:4 There is not a word on my tongue yet, / and there, LORD, you know all of it.
139:5 From behind and from the front you close me in, / and you have put your hand on me.
139:6 Knowing like this is a wonder beyond me; / it stands too high, and I cannot get up to it.
## Nowhere to hide
139:7 Where could I go to get away from your spirit? / Where could I run from your face?
139:8 Should I climb to the heavens, you are there; / should I make Sheol my bed, here you are.
139:9 Should I take up the wings of the dawn / and make my home where the sea ends,
139:10 there too your hand would lead me on, / and your right hand would grasp me.
139:11 And if I say, "Darkness will surely crush me, / and the light around me will be night,"
139:12 darkness itself is not too dark for you; / night gives light like the day; / dark and light are alike.
## Made in the womb
139:13 It was you who made my kidneys; / you wove me together in my mother's womb.
139:14 I thank you, for I am a wonder, made in awesome ways. / Your works are wonders, / and my soul knows it through and through.
139:15 My bones were not hidden from you / when I was being made in a hidden place, / embroidered in the lowest parts of the earth.
139:16 Your eyes saw me as a shapeless lump, / and in your scroll all of them were written: / days were shaped / when not one of them had yet come.
139:17 To me, God, how dear your thoughts are! / How massive the count of them!
139:18 Should I count them, there are more than the sand. / I come awake, and I am with you still.
## Against God's enemies
139:19 God, if only you would put the wicked to death! / You bloodstained men, stay away from me!
139:20 They talk of you to work their schemes; / your enemies take it up for something empty.
139:21 Those who hate you, LORD, do I not hate them? / Those who rise against you, do I not find them sickening?
139:22 I hate them with a hatred that holds nothing back; / they have become enemies of mine.
139:23 Probe me, God, and know my heart; / put me to the test, and know my restless thoughts.
139:24 See whether there is a hurtful way in me, / and lead me along the everlasting road.
## Snakes' tongues and hidden traps
140:0 For the music director. A psalm of David.
140:1 Pull me free, LORD, from the evil man; / guard me from the man of violence.
140:2 They work out evils in their hearts; / all day long they stir up wars.
140:3 They whet their tongues like a snake; / under their lips lies the venom of the viper. Selah
140:4 LORD, keep me out of the wicked one's hands; / from the man of violence protect me, / from those who have made plans to push my feet aside.
140:5 Proud men have hidden a trap for me; / they have laid out ropes as a net by the trail; / they have set lures for me. Selah
140:6 I say to the LORD, "You are my God." / Turn your ear, LORD, to my cries for mercy.
140:7 LORD, my Lord, my strong salvation, / you have put a cover over my head / on the day the weapons clash.
140:8 LORD, do not hand the wicked what they crave; / do not help his scheme along, / or they will hold themselves high. Selah
140:9 The heads of those who ring me round: / let the harm their own lips speak bury them.
140:10 Let hot coals rain down on them; / let him make them fall into the fire, / into deep holes from which they cannot get up.
140:11 Do not let the man with the slandering tongue take root in the land; / let evil chase the man of violence down, thrust after thrust.
140:12 I know that the LORD will plead the case of the poor / and win the rights of the needy.
140:13 Yes, the righteous will give thanks to your name; / the upright will make their home before your face.
## An evening prayer
141:0 A psalm of David.
141:1 LORD, I have called you: come to me fast! / Turn your ear to my voice as I cry to you.
141:2 Let my prayer be set up as incense in front of you, / my hands raised high as the evening grain offering.
141:3 LORD, post a guard for my mouth; / stand watch over the door of my lips.
141:4 Do not bend my heart toward any evil thing, / to carry out wicked acts / alongside men who do wrong. / Let me not taste their dainty food.
141:5 If a righteous man hits me, it is loyal love; / if he scolds me, it is oil on my head, / and my head will not refuse it. / But my prayer still stands against their wicked acts.
141:6 Their judges are flung down the face of the cliff, / and they will hear my words, how pleasant they are.
141:7 Like someone plowing and splitting the soil, / so our bones have been strewn at the mouth of Sheol.
141:8 Yet my eyes are on you, LORD, my Lord; / I take shelter in you; do not empty out my life.
141:9 Guard me from the jaws of the trap they have laid for me, / and from the lures of men who do wrong.
141:10 Let the wicked drop into their own nets, all of them at once, / while I go on past.
## A prayer in the cave
142:0 A maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer.
142:1 I raise my voice and cry to the LORD; / I raise my voice to the LORD and beg for grace.
142:2 In front of him I pour out my worry; / my distress I lay out in front of him.
142:3 When my spirit sinks within me, / you are the one who knows my track. / On the path where I go / they have set a hidden snare for me.
142:4 Look to my right side and see: / there is nobody who knows me. / Every way of escape has failed me; / no one asks after my life.
142:5 I have cried out to you, LORD. / I said, "You are my place of shelter, / my allotted share in the land of the living."
142:6 Pay attention to my shout, / for I have sunk very low. / Pull me free from the ones hunting me, / for they are stronger than I am.
142:7 Lead me out of the prison, / so that I can thank your name. / The righteous will crowd around me, / for you will be good to me.
## No one alive is in the right
143:0 A psalm of David.
143:1 LORD, hear what I pray; / turn your ear to my begging for grace. / Answer me by your faithfulness, by your righteousness.
143:2 Do not go to trial with your servant, / for no living person is in the right in your presence.
143:3 The enemy has chased me down; / he has ground my life into the dirt. / He has put me in dark places / like the dead of long ago.
143:4 So my spirit sinks within me; / inside me my heart is stunned.
143:5 I call to mind the days from long ago; / I turn over in my mind every deed of yours; / I murmur over the work of your hands.
143:6 I hold my hands wide toward you; / my soul reaches for you like weary land. Selah
## Show me the way
143:7 Be quick, LORD, answer me; / my spirit is used up. / Do not hide your face from me, / or I will become like those going down into the pit.
143:8 Make me hear your loyal love in the morning, / for I have put my trust in you. / Let me know the road to walk on, / for to you I raise my soul.
143:9 LORD, pull me free from my enemies; / I have hidden myself in you.
143:10 Teach me how to do what you want, / for you are my God. / May your spirit, which is good, guide me / in a land that is level.
143:11 LORD, for the sake of your name, give me life; / by your righteousness lead my soul out of distress.
143:12 In your loyal love silence my enemies; / bring to ruin all who press hard on my soul, / for I am your servant.
## The king's new song
144:0 Of David.
144:1 Blessed is the LORD, my rock, / who teaches my hands to fight a war, / my fingers to do battle,
144:2 my loyal love and my fortress, / my high tower, the one who gets me free, / my shield, the one I take shelter in, / who beats down peoples beneath me.
144:3 LORD, what is a human being, that you know him, / a son of man, that you keep him in mind?
144:4 A human is like a puff of air; / his days are a shadow that slips past.
144:5 LORD, bend your sky low and come down; / touch the mountains and make them smoke.
144:6 Flash your lightning and scatter them; / shoot your arrows and throw them into panic.
144:7 Stretch out your hands from on high; / snatch me up and pull me out of the great waters, / from the grip of sons of foreign lands,
144:8 whose mouths speak what is empty, / and whose right hand is a right hand that lies.
144:9 God, a new song I will sing you; / on the ten-string harp I will make music for you,
144:10 the one who hands kings their rescue, / who frees David his servant / from the deadly sword.
144:11 Snatch me up and pull me out / from the grip of sons of foreign lands, / whose mouths speak what is empty, / and whose right hand is a right hand that lies.
## A happy people
144:12 Then our sons will be like young plants / grown tall while they are young, / our daughters like corner columns / carved to shape a palace.
144:13 Our storerooms will be full, / giving out one kind of food after another. / Our flocks will multiply by thousands, / by tens of thousands in our open fields.
144:14 Our cattle will be well loaded. / There will be no breach and no going out, / and no cry of grief in our squares.
144:15 Happy is the people who have it like this! / Happy is the people whose God is the LORD!
## Every day I will bless you
145:0 A praise of David.
145:1 I will raise you high, my God, the King; / I will bless your name for ever and ever.
145:2 Every day I will bless you / and praise your name for ever and ever.
145:3 The LORD is great, and much to be praised; / there is no searching out his greatness.
145:4 Each generation will praise your works to the next / and report your mighty deeds.
145:5 On the splendor and glory of your majesty / and the accounts of your wonders I will muse.
145:6 People will speak of the power of your awesome acts, / and your greatness I will tell out.
145:7 They will bubble over with the memory of your great goodness / and ring out with joy over your righteousness.
145:8 Gracious and compassionate is the LORD, / slow to anger and great in loyal love.
145:9 The LORD is good to everyone, / and his compassion rests on all his works.
## Your kingdom for all ages
145:10 LORD, everything you have made will thank you, / and the ones loyal to you will bless you.
145:11 They will tell of your kingdom's glory / and talk about your strength,
145:12 to make his mighty deeds known to human beings, / and the glory and splendor of his kingdom.
145:13 Your kingdom is a kingdom for all ages, / and your rule lasts through every generation. / God is faithful in his words and loyal in all his works.
145:14 The LORD gives support to all who are falling / and raises all who are bent double.
145:15 All eyes wait in hope for you, / and you hand them their food when it is time.
145:16 You open your hand / and fill every living thing with what it longs for.
145:17 In all his ways the LORD is righteous, / and loyal in all his works.
145:18 Near is the LORD to everyone who calls to him, / to everyone who calls to him truly.
145:19 He carries out the wishes of those who fear him; / he hears them when they cry for help, and he saves them.
145:20 The LORD keeps watch over everyone who loves him, / and he will wipe out every one of the wicked.
145:21 The LORD's praise is what my mouth will speak; / let every creature of flesh bless his holy name / for ever and ever.
## Do not trust in princes
146:1 Praise the LORD! / My soul, praise the LORD!
146:2 I will praise the LORD as long as I live; / I will make music for my God for as long as I am here.
146:3 Do not rely on nobles, / on a son of man, who has no rescue in him.
146:4 His breath leaves, he goes back to his ground; / that same day his schemes are gone.
146:5 Happy is the person who has the God of Jacob as his help, / who rests his hope on the LORD his God,
146:6 maker of heaven and earth, / of the sea and everything in them, / who keeps faith for all time;
146:7 who wins justice for those crushed by wrong, / who hands bread to the hungry. / The LORD unties the prisoners.
146:8 The LORD gives sight to the blind; / the LORD raises those bent double; / the LORD loves the righteous.
146:9 The LORD keeps watch over foreigners; / he helps the orphan and the widow back up, / but he bends the road of the wicked out of shape.
146:10 The LORD will be king forever, / your God, Zion, for generation after generation. / Praise the LORD!
## He counts the stars
147:1 Praise the LORD! / Making music to our God is good; / it is sweet, and praise is what fits.
147:2 The LORD builds Jerusalem; / he gathers Israel's scattered outcasts.
147:3 He is the healer of those whose hearts are broken; / he bandages their wounds.
147:4 He counts how many stars there are; / he calls each of them by a name.
147:5 Our Lord is great, and his strength is vast; / there is no counting his understanding.
147:6 The LORD helps the lowly back up; / he throws the wicked down to the dirt.
147:7 Answer the LORD with a song of thanks; / play the lyre for our God.
147:8 He wraps the heavens in cloud; / he makes rain ready for the land; / he makes the mountains sprout grass.
147:9 He gives the beasts their food, / and the raven's chicks what they cry out for.
147:10 The horse's might is not what he enjoys; / the legs of a man do not win his favor.
147:11 The LORD is pleased with those who fear him, / with those who wait in hope for his loyal love.
## Jerusalem, praise your God
147:12 Jerusalem, lift up the LORD's praise; / Zion, praise your God.
147:13 He has made the bolts of your gates firm; / he has blessed your children within you.
147:14 He sets peace as your border; / he feeds you full with the best of the wheat.
147:15 He sends out his saying to the earth; / his word comes running, fast.
147:16 He lays down snow like wool / and strews frost like ash.
147:17 He flings his ice like bits of bread; / who can stand up to his cold?
147:18 He sends his word, and they melt; / he stirs his wind, and water runs.
147:19 He tells his words to Jacob, / his rules and his rulings to Israel.
147:20 No other nation has he treated like this, / and as for rulings, they have not known them. / Praise the LORD!
## Praise from the heavens
148:1 Praise the LORD! / Praise the LORD from the heavens; / up in the heights, praise him!
148:2 Praise him, all you his angels; / praise him, all you his armies.
148:3 Praise him, sun and moon; / praise him, every star of light.
148:4 Praise him, you heavens of the heavens, / and you waters over the heavens.
148:5 Let them praise the LORD's name, / for he gave the order, and they were created.
148:6 He made them stand for ever and ever; / he set a rule, and it will not be broken.
## Praise from the earth
148:7 Praise the LORD from the earth, / you sea monsters and every ocean deep,
148:8 fire and hail, snow and haze, / the storm wind that carries out his word,
148:9 mountains and every hill, / fruit trees and every cedar,
148:10 wild beasts and all cattle, / creatures that creep and birds on the wing,
148:11 kings of the earth and every people, / officials and every judge on earth,
148:12 young men, and young women too, / old people with the young.
148:13 Let them praise the LORD's name, / for his name, and his alone, stands high; / his splendor is over earth and heavens.
148:14 He has lifted up a horn for his people, / a praise for all his loyal ones, / for the children of Israel, a people near to him. / Praise the LORD!
## A new song and a sword
149:1 Praise the LORD! / Sing the LORD a new song, / his praise in the gathering of his loyal ones.
149:2 Let Israel take joy in the one who made it; / let Zion's children celebrate their King.
149:3 Let them praise his name in the dance, / play for him on tambourine and lyre.
149:4 For the LORD is pleased with his people; / he makes the lowly beautiful with victory.
149:5 Let the loyal ones triumph in honor; / let them ring out with joy upon their beds.
149:6 God's lofty praises in their throats, / and a double-edged sword in their hand,
149:7 to bring vengeance on the nations / and punishments on the peoples,
149:8 to tie up their kings in fetters / and their great men in iron chains,
149:9 to carry out on them the sentence that is written. / This is the glory of all his loyal ones. / Praise the LORD!
## Let everything that breathes praise
150:1 Praise the LORD! / Praise God in his holy place; / praise him in the dome of his power.
150:2 Praise him for his feats of strength; / praise him in keeping with how vast his greatness is.
150:3 Praise him with a blast on the ram's horn; / praise him with harp and lyre.
150:4 Praise him with tambourine and dancing; / praise him on strings and reed pipe.
150:5 Praise him with cymbals that ring out; / praise him with cymbals of the loud shout.
150:6 Let every breathing thing praise Yah! / Praise the LORD!
