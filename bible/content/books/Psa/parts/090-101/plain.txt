## Psalm 90: Moses' prayer
90:0 A prayer of Moses, the man of God.
90:1 Lord, you yourself have been our home / in one generation after another.
90:2 Before the mountains were born, / before you brought the earth and the world to birth, / from the ages past to the ages to come, you are God.
90:3 You turn a human being back into powder / and say, "Go back, you children of Adam."
90:4 For a thousand years, as you see them, / are like yesterday after it has gone by, / like one shift of the night watch.
90:5 You flood them away; they are only a sleep. / In the morning they are like grass that sprouts again.
90:6 In the morning it blooms and shoots up; / by evening it wilts and dries out.
## Teach us to count our days
90:7 For we are used up by your anger, / and your rage leaves us in terror.
90:8 You have set our guilt out in front of you, / what we kept hidden, in the light of your face.
90:9 For all our days turn away under your fury; / we bring our years to an end like a low moan.
90:10 The days of our years add up to seventy, / or eighty for the strong. / What they boast of is hard work and grief; / it passes swiftly, and off we fly.
90:11 Who knows how strong your anger is? / Your fury runs as deep as the fear of you.
90:12 Teach us, then, to count our days, / so that we may bring in a wise heart.
## Turn back, LORD
90:13 Turn back, LORD! How long? / Change your mind about your servants.
90:14 At daybreak, fill us with your loyal love, / and we will cheer and celebrate through all our days.
90:15 Give us as many days of gladness as the days you humbled us, / as the years we saw harm.
90:16 Show your servants what you do, / and their children your splendor.
90:17 Let the kindness of the Lord our God be on us. / Give the work of our hands firm footing for us; / the work of our hands, give it firm footing.
## Psalm 91: In the shadow of the Almighty
91:1 Whoever lives in the hiding place of the Most High / will spend the night in the shadow of the Almighty.
91:2 This is what I say to the LORD: / "My shelter and my fortress, / my God, the one I trust!"
91:3 For he himself will pull you free from the bird-catcher's trap, / from the plague that brings ruin.
91:4 With his long feathers he will cover you over; / beneath his wings you can take shelter. / A shield and a wall, that is his faithfulness.
91:5 You need not fear what terrifies at night, / or an arrow in flight by day,
91:6 or the plague that creeps through the dark, / or the scourge that ravages at noon.
91:7 Though a thousand drop at your side / and ten thousand on your right, / it will not reach you.
91:8 You will just look on with your own eyes / and watch the wicked get their payback.
## The Most High your home
91:9 For you, LORD, are my shelter! / You have made the Most High your home.
91:10 Harm will not be let loose on you; / no stroke of disease will come near your tent.
91:11 For he will give his angels orders about you, / to guard you on every road you take.
91:12 Their hands will hold you up, / so your foot does not hit a stone.
91:13 Lion and cobra you will walk upon; / young lion and serpent you will stamp down.
91:14 "Because he has fastened his heart on me, I will get him out. / I will set him high and safe, because he knows my name.
91:15 He will cry out to me, and I will give him an answer. / I am at his side in hard times. / I will pull him out, and I will bring him honor.
91:16 I will fill him with long life / and let him look on my saving help."
## Psalm 92: A song for the Sabbath
92:0 A psalm. A song for the Sabbath day.
92:1 It is a good thing to thank the LORD, / to make music to your name, Most High,
92:2 to tell of your loyal love at daybreak / and of your faithfulness through the nights,
92:3 on the ten-string and on the harp, / with the low hum of the lyre.
92:4 LORD, what you have done fills me with gladness; / over the work of your hands I shout for joy.
92:5 How great your works are, LORD! / How very deep your plans run!
## The wicked spring up like grass
92:6 A stupid person does not know it; / a fool cannot make sense of this:
92:7 the wicked may spring up like grass, / and all who do wrong may blossom, / but only to be wiped out forever.
92:8 You stay on high forever, LORD.
92:9 For look at your enemies, LORD! / Look: your enemies will be lost; / all who do wrong will be scattered.
92:10 But you have lifted my horn like a wild ox's; / I am soaked in fresh oil.
92:11 My eyes have looked down on those who lie in wait for me; / my ears have heard about the evil men who rise against me.
## The righteous like a palm tree
92:12 The righteous will sprout like the date palm; / like a cedar in Lebanon they will grow tall.
92:13 Planted in the LORD's house, / they will bud in the courtyards of our God.
92:14 In old age they will still yield fruit; / full of sap and fresh they will be,
92:15 to make known that the LORD is straight, / my rock, with no wrong in him.
## Psalm 93: The LORD is King
93:1 The LORD is King! / He is dressed in majesty. / The LORD is dressed, with strength tied around him as a belt. / Surely the world is set firm; it will not totter.
93:2 Your throne has stood firm from the start; / you have been since the ages past.
93:3 The rivers have raised, LORD, / the rivers have raised their voice; / the rivers raise their pounding.
93:4 More than the sounds of the vast waters, / the majestic breakers of the sea, / majestic on high is the LORD.
93:5 Your decrees can be trusted completely. / Holiness is fitting for your house, LORD, / for days without end.
## Psalm 94: God who takes vengeance
94:1 God who takes vengeance, LORD, / God who takes vengeance, shine out!
94:2 Stand tall, Judge of the earth! / Give the proud what their deeds have earned.
94:3 How long will the wicked, LORD, / how long will the wicked gloat?
94:4 They gush out insolent talk; / all who do wrong brag about themselves.
94:5 They grind down your people, LORD; / they bring your inheritance low.
94:6 Widow and foreigner they kill; / the fatherless they murder.
94:7 And they say, "Yah does not see; / the God of Jacob pays no attention."
## Will he not hear?
94:8 Take note, you brutes among the people! / Fools, when will you have any sense?
94:9 Will the one who planted the ear not hear? / Will the one who shaped the eye not see?
94:10 Will the one who disciplines nations not rebuke, / the one who teaches people knowledge?
94:11 The LORD knows human thinking, / and that it is no more than a puff of air.
94:12 Happy is the one you train, Yah, / the one you teach from your law,
94:13 giving him calm in bad days / until the pit for the wicked is dug.
94:14 For the LORD will not let go of his people; / he will not walk away from his inheritance.
94:15 Judgment will turn back to what is right, / and all whose hearts are straight will follow it.
## Who will stand with me?
94:16 Who will stand up on my behalf against evildoers? / Who will take his place beside me against those who do wrong?
94:17 Had the LORD not been there to help me, / my soul would almost have settled in the silent place.
94:18 The moment I said, "My foot has slid," / your loyal love, LORD, kept me up.
94:19 When my troubled thoughts pile up inside me, / your comforts delight my soul.
94:20 Can a throne that brings ruin be joined with you, / one that shapes misery into law?
94:21 They gang up on the life of the righteous / and declare innocent blood guilty.
94:22 But the LORD has become a high tower for me, / and my God the rock I run to for shelter.
94:23 He has brought their wrongdoing back upon them; / for their evil he will destroy them; / the LORD our God will destroy them.
## Psalm 95: Come, let us bow low
95:1 Come, let us cry out with joy to the LORD; / let us raise a shout to the rock that saves us.
95:2 Let us come to meet him with thanks; / with songs let us raise a shout to him.
95:3 For a great God is the LORD, / a great King over all the gods.
95:4 In his hand are the earth's deep places, / and the peaks of the mountains belong to him.
95:5 To him belongs the sea; he is the one who made it, / and the dry ground was shaped by his hands.
95:6 Come in, let us bow low and bend down; / let us drop to our knees before the LORD who made us.
95:7 For he is our God, / and we are the people he pastures, the sheep his hand tends. / Today, if you would only listen to his voice!
## Do not make your hearts hard
95:8 "Do not make your hearts hard, as at Meribah, / like that day in the wilderness at Massah,
95:9 when your ancestors put me on trial; / they tested me, even after seeing my work.
95:10 Forty years I felt disgust at that generation, / and I said, 'This is a people with straying hearts; / they do not know my ways.'
95:11 That is why I swore in my anger, / 'They will never come into my place of rest.'"
## Psalm 96: A new song for all the earth
96:1 Sing a new song to the LORD; / sing to the LORD, all the earth.
96:2 Sing to the LORD; bless his name. / Bring the good news of his saving help each day.
96:3 Recount his glory to the nations, / his wonders to every people.
96:4 For the LORD is great, and praised to the highest; / he is more to be feared than all the gods.
96:5 Every god of the peoples is a nothing-god, / but the LORD made the heavens.
96:6 Before him go splendor and majesty; / his sanctuary is full of strength and beauty.
## Give the LORD what is his
96:7 Give the LORD, you clans of the peoples; / give the LORD glory and strength.
96:8 Give the LORD the glory of his name. / Carry a gift and come into his courts.
96:9 Bow low to the LORD in holy splendor; / all the earth, shake in his presence!
96:10 Say among the nations, "The LORD is King!" / Surely the world is set firm; it will not totter. / He will govern the peoples with fairness.
96:11 May the heavens be glad, may the earth celebrate; / let the sea thunder with everything that fills it.
96:12 Let the open country cheer, and all that is in it; / then all the forest trees will shout for joy
96:13 before the LORD, because he is coming, / because he is coming to judge the earth. / He will judge the world in the right way, / and the peoples with his faithfulness.
## Psalm 97: Cloud and fire around the King
97:1 The LORD is King! / May the earth celebrate; / may the many islands be glad.
97:2 Cloud and dense darkness are all around him; / his throne rests on righteousness and justice.
97:3 Fire walks out ahead of him / and sets his foes ablaze on every side.
97:4 His lightning flashes make the world bright; / the earth sees it and writhes.
97:5 Like wax, the mountains melt away in the LORD's presence, / in the presence of the Lord of all the earth.
97:6 The skies make his righteousness known; / all the peoples have seen his glory.
## Zion hears and is glad
97:7 Let everyone who serves a carved image be ashamed, / those who boast of nothing-gods; / all you gods, bow down to him!
97:8 Zion heard and was glad, / and the daughters of Judah celebrated / because of your rulings, LORD.
97:9 For you, LORD, are the Most High over the whole earth; / you stand far higher than all the gods.
97:10 You who love the LORD, hate what is evil. / He keeps watch over the lives of his loyal ones / and pulls them out of the hands of the wicked.
97:11 Light is scattered like seed for the righteous, / and gladness for those whose hearts are straight.
97:12 Be glad in the LORD, you righteous ones, / and give thanks as you call his holy name to mind.
## Psalm 98: The victory of the King
98:0 A psalm.
98:1 Sing a new song to the LORD, / for he has worked wonders. / His right hand brought him victory, / his holy arm.
98:2 The LORD has made his saving help known; / before the nations' eyes he has uncovered his righteousness.
98:3 He has called to mind his loyal love / and his faithfulness toward the house of Israel. / Every far end of the earth has seen / how our God saves.
## Let the whole world shout
98:4 Raise a shout to the LORD, all the earth! / Burst into song; cry out with joy; make music!
98:5 Make music to the LORD with the lyre, / with the lyre and the sound of melody.
98:6 To the sound of trumpets and ram's horn, / raise a shout before the King, the LORD.
98:7 Let the sea thunder with everything that fills it, / the world and those living in it.
98:8 Let the rivers clap palm to palm; / let the mountains ring out with joy together
98:9 before the LORD, for he is coming to judge the earth. / He will judge the world in the right way, / and the peoples with fairness.
## Psalm 99: Holy is he
99:1 The LORD is King! Let the peoples tremble. / He sits on the cherubim as his throne; let the earth shake.
99:2 The LORD in Zion is great, / and high above all the peoples is he.
99:3 Let them give thanks to your name, so great and so fearsome. / Holy is he!
99:4 Strong King, lover of justice, / you are the one who set fairness firmly in place; / you have done justice and right in Jacob.
99:5 Lift high the LORD our God; / bow low at the footstool for his feet. / Holy is he!
## Moses, Aaron and Samuel
99:6 Moses and Aaron stood among his priests, / Samuel among those who called on his name; / they cried out to the LORD, and he answered them.
99:7 In a pillar of cloud he would speak to them; / they kept his decrees, the rule he handed them.
99:8 LORD our God, you answered them. / You were a God who forgave them, / and who paid them back for what they did.
99:9 Lift high the LORD our God; / bow low toward his holy mountain, / for holy is the LORD our God.
## Psalm 100: Through his gates with thanks
100:0 A psalm for the thank offering.
100:1 Raise a shout to the LORD, all the earth!
100:2 Serve the LORD with joy; / come before his face with glad shouts.
100:3 Know this: the LORD, he is God. / He is the one who made us; we belong to him, / his people, the flock he pastures.
100:4 Come through his gates with thanks, / into his courtyards with praise. / Thank him; bless his name.
100:5 For the LORD is good; his loyal love is forever, / and his faithfulness lasts from generation to generation.
## Psalm 101: The king's promise
101:0 Of David. A psalm.
101:1 Loyal love and justice: of these I will sing. / To you, LORD, I will make music.
101:2 I will keep my mind on the blameless way. / When will you come to me? / I will walk with a whole heart inside my own house.
101:3 I will not put anything worthless in front of my eyes. / The deeds of those who swerve away, I hate; / they will not stick to me.
101:4 A twisted heart will depart from me; / evil will be a stranger to me.
## No liar in my house
101:5 Anyone who slanders a neighbor in secret, / him I will destroy. / The one with haughty eyes and a swollen heart, / him I cannot bear.
101:6 I will keep my eyes on those in the land who are faithful, / so they can live with me. / Whoever walks the blameless way / is the one who will serve me.
101:7 He will not live inside my house, / the one who works deceit. / The one who speaks lies / will not last in my sight.
101:8 Morning by morning I will destroy all the wicked of the land, / cutting off from the city of the LORD all who do wrong.
