## What we heard, saw and touched
1:1 What was from the beginning, what we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, what we gazed at and our hands touched: this is what we speak of, the word of life.
1:2 The life was made visible. We have seen it, and we testify to it, and we announce to you the eternal life that was with the Father and was made visible to us.
1:3 What we have seen and heard we announce to you as well, so that you too may share fellowship with us. The fellowship we have is with the Father, and with his Son Jesus Christ.
1:4 We are writing these things so that our joy will be filled up.
## God is light
1:5 Here is the message we heard from him and now pass on to you: God is light, and in him there is no darkness, not the least bit.
1:6 If we say we have fellowship with him and yet walk around in the darkness, we are lying, and we are not practicing the truth.
1:7 But if we walk around in the light, just as he himself is in the light, then we share fellowship with each other, and the blood of Jesus his Son makes us clean from every sin.
1:8 If we say, "We have no sin," we are leading ourselves astray, and the truth is not in us.
1:9 If we confess our sins, he is faithful and he is just: he will forgive us our sins and make us clean from all unrighteousness.
1:10 If we say, "We have not sinned," we are making him a liar, and his word is not in us.
## An Advocate with the Father
2:1 My little children, my reason for writing these things to you is that you not sin. Yet if someone does sin, we have an Advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ, who is righteous.
2:2 He himself is the atoning sacrifice where our sins are concerned. And it does not stop with ours: it is for the whole world.
## Knowing him means keeping his commands
2:3 Here is how we know that we have come to know him: by keeping his commands.
2:4 The one who says, "I have come to know him," but does not keep his commands is a liar, and the truth is not in that person.
2:5 But anyone who keeps his word, in that person the love of God has truly been made complete. This is how we know that we are in him:
2:6 whoever says he remains in him ought to walk the same way that one walked.
2:7 Beloved, it is no new command I am writing to you. It is an old command, one you have had from the beginning. That old command is the word you heard.
2:8 And yet it is a new command I am writing to you, and that is true in him and in you: the darkness is passing away, and the real light already shines.
2:9 Anyone who claims to be in the light while hating his brother is in the darkness right up to now.
2:10 The one who loves his brother remains in the light, and there is nothing in him to trip over.
2:11 But the one who hates his brother is in the darkness and walks around in the darkness. He has no idea where he is headed, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.
## Why I am writing to you
2:12 Little children, I am writing to you because your sins have been forgiven for the sake of his name.
2:13 Fathers, I am writing to you because you have come to know the one who is from the beginning. Young men, I am writing to you because you have won the victory over the evil one. Children, I have written to you because you have come to know the Father.
2:14 Fathers, I have written to you because you have come to know the one who is from the beginning. Young men, I have written to you because you are strong, and the word of God remains in you, and you have won the victory over the evil one.
## Do not love the world
2:15 Do not love the world, or what is in the world. If anyone loves the world, the Father's love is not in him.
2:16 Everything in the world, the craving of the flesh, the craving of the eyes, the bragging about what one has, does not come from the Father. It comes from the world.
2:17 The world is passing away, and its craving too; but whoever does what God wills remains for all time.
## The last hour
2:18 Children, it is the last hour. Just as you heard that antichrist is coming, so now many antichrists have arisen. That is how we can tell it is the last hour.
2:19 They went out from among us, but they were never really from us. Had they been from us, they would have remained with us. Their leaving happened so that it would be made visible that none of them are from us.
2:20 As for you, you have an anointing, which comes from the Holy One, and every one of you knows.
2:21 My reason for writing to you is not that you are ignorant of the truth. It is that you know it, and that no lie has its source in the truth.
2:22 Who is the liar, except the one who denies that Jesus is the Messiah? The antichrist is this: the one who denies the Father and the Son.
2:23 Anyone who denies the Son is without the Father as well. Anyone who confesses the Son has the Father too.
2:24 You, then: what you heard from the beginning must remain in you. If what you heard from the beginning remains in you, then you also will remain in the Son and in the Father.
2:25 He made us a promise, and this is it: eternal life.
2:26 What I have written to you here concerns the people who are leading you astray.
2:27 And you? The anointing you received from him remains in you, so you do not need anyone to teach you. His anointing teaches you about all things; it is true, not a lie. So, just as it taught you, remain in him.
## God's children
2:28 So now, little children, remain in him. Then, if he appears, we will have freedom to speak and will not be shamed away from him at his coming.
2:29 If you know he is righteous, you can be sure that everyone who does righteousness has been born from him as well.
3:1 Look what sort of love the Father has given us: we are to be called God's children, and we are! That is why the world does not know us: it did not know him.
3:2 Beloved, God's children is what we are now. What we are going to be has not yet been made visible. We know that if he appears, we will be like him, since we will see him just as he is.
3:3 And everyone who has this hope set on him makes himself pure, just as that one is pure.
3:4 Everyone who commits sin also commits lawlessness. Sin is lawlessness.
3:5 And you know that he appeared in order to take sins away, and in him there is no sin.
3:6 Anyone who remains in him does not sin. Anyone who sins has neither seen him nor known him.
3:7 Children, let no one lead you astray. The one who does righteousness is righteous, just as that one is righteous.
3:8 The one who commits sin is from the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. This is why the Son of God appeared: to undo the works of the devil.
3:9 Everyone who has been born from God does not commit sin, because his seed remains in him. He cannot sin, because he has been born from God.
3:10 This is how God's children and the devil's children are out in the open: anyone who does not do righteousness is not from God, and neither is the one who does not love his brother.
## Love each other
3:11 The message you heard from the beginning is this: we are to love each other.
3:12 Not as Cain was: he was from the evil one, and he slaughtered his brother. And why did he slaughter him? Because what he did was evil, and what his brother did was righteous.
3:13 Do not be amazed, brothers, if the world hates you.
3:14 We know that we have moved out of death and into life, because we love the brothers. The one who does not love remains in death.
3:15 Everyone who hates his brother is a killer, and you know that no killer has eternal life remaining in him.
3:16 Here is how we have come to know love: that one laid down his life for our sake. We too are bound to lay down our lives for the brothers.
3:17 But if someone has enough to live on in this world, and sees his brother in need, and locks his heart against him, how can God's love remain in him?
3:18 Little children, our love must not be a matter of words or of the tongue, but of deeds and of truth.
3:19 This is how we will know that we are from the truth, and how we will calm our heart before him:
3:20 even if our heart accuses us, God is greater than our heart, and he knows everything.
3:21 Beloved, if our heart does not accuse us, we have freedom to speak with God,
3:22 and we receive from him anything we ask for, since we keep his commands and do the things that are pleasing in his sight.
3:23 His command is this: we are to believe the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and to love each other, just as he told us to.
3:24 Whoever keeps his commands remains in him, with him remaining in that person. We know that he remains in us from this: from the Spirit he has given us.
## Test the spirits
4:1 Beloved, do not trust every spirit. Put the spirits to the test, to find out if they come from God. Many false prophets have gone out into the world.
4:2 Here is how you recognize the Spirit of God: any spirit that confesses Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh is from God,
4:3 and any spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God. This is what belongs to the antichrist. You have heard that it is coming, and now it is in the world already.
4:4 Little children, you are from God, and you have won the victory over them. For greater is the one in you than the one in the world.
4:5 They are from the world. For that reason what they say comes from the world, and the world listens to them.
4:6 We are from God. The one who knows God listens to us; the one who is not from God does not listen to us. From this we recognize the Spirit of truth and the spirit that leads astray.
## God is love
4:7 Beloved, let us love each other. Love has its source in God, and everyone who loves has been born from God and knows God.
4:8 Whoever does not love has never known God, for God is love.
4:9 God's love was made visible among us in this: God has sent his only Son into the world so that we may live through him.
4:10 This is where love is found. It is not that we have loved God; it is that he himself loved us, and that he sent his Son to be the atoning sacrifice where our sins are concerned.
4:11 Beloved, if that is how God loved us, we are bound to love each other too.
4:12 No one has ever gazed at God. If we love each other, God remains in us, and his love in us has been made complete.
4:13 We know that we remain in him, and he in us, from this: he has given us a share of his Spirit.
4:14 We ourselves have gazed at this, and we testify that the Father has sent the Son as the Savior of the world.
4:15 If anyone confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God remains in that person, and that person in God.
4:16 We ourselves have come to know the love that God has among us, and we have believed it. God is love. Whoever remains in love remains in God, and God remains in that person.
4:17 Love has been made complete with us in this, so that we will have freedom to speak on the day of judgment: as that one is, so are we in this world.
4:18 Fear does not exist in love. Complete love throws fear out, since fear carries punishment with it. Whoever is afraid has not been made complete in love.
4:19 We love because he loved us first.
4:20 If someone says, "I love God," and hates his brother, he is a liar. For the one who does not love his brother, whom he has seen, is not able to love God, whom he has never seen.
4:21 This is the command we have from him: whoever loves God is to love his brother too.
## Faith that wins the victory
5:1 Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Messiah has been born from God. And everyone who loves the parent also loves the one born from him.
5:2 We know that we love God's children from this: when we love God and do what he commands.
5:3 Loving God means this: keeping his commands. And his commands are not heavy.
5:4 For all that is born from God wins the victory over the world. And the victory that has won over the world is this: our faith.
5:5 Who is the one who wins the victory over the world, if not the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?
5:6 He is the one who came through water and blood: Jesus Christ. He came not in the water only, but in the water and in the blood. The Spirit is the one testifying, because the Spirit is the truth.
5:7 For those who testify are three:
5:8 the Spirit, the water and the blood; and the three agree as one.
5:9 If we receive the testimony of people, God's testimony is greater. For this is the testimony of God: he has testified about his Son.
5:10 Whoever believes in the Son of God has this testimony within him. Whoever does not believe God has made him a liar, by not believing the testimony that God has given about his Son.
5:11 The testimony is this: God has given us eternal life, and the life is found in his Son.
5:12 Whoever has the Son has the life; whoever does not have the Son of God does not have the life.
## So that you may know
5:13 I have written these things to you so that you will know that you have eternal life: to you who believe in the name of the Son of God.
5:14 And this is the freedom we have before him: if we ask anything in line with his will, he hears us.
5:15 And if we know that he hears us, whatever we ask, then we know that we already have the requests we have made of him.
5:16 If anyone sees his brother committing a sin that is not headed toward death, he should ask, and he will give him life. This is for those who sin in a way not headed toward death. There is sin headed toward death. I am not saying he should make a request about that.
5:17 All unrighteousness is sin, and there is sin not headed toward death.
5:18 We know that everyone who has been born from God does not sin. Instead, the one born from God keeps himself safe, and the evil one does not touch him.
5:19 We know that we are from God, while the world, all of it, lies in the evil one's hands.
5:20 And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us a mind able to know the one who is real. And we are in the one who is real, in his Son Jesus Christ. This one is the real God and eternal life.
5:21 Little children, guard yourselves against idols.
