## Greeting
1:1 From Paul and Timothy, slaves of Christ Jesus, to all the saints in Christ Jesus who live in Philippi, with overseers and deacons.
1:2 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
## Thanks and a prayer
1:3 Each time you come to mind, I thank my God.
1:4 In every one of my prayers for you all, I always pray with joy,
1:5 for your partnership in the good news, from the first day right up to now.
1:6 I am persuaded of this very thing: the one who started a good work in you will go on completing it until the day of Christ Jesus.
1:7 It is fair for me to think this about every one of you, since I carry you in my heart. In my chains, and as I defend the good news and make it stand firm, you all have a share with me in grace.
1:8 God is my witness: I long for every one of you with the tender heart of Christ Jesus.
1:9 This is what I ask in prayer: that your love will keep growing, more and more, with true knowledge and every kind of insight,
1:10 so you can tell what matters most, and be sincere and give no one cause to stumble when the day of Christ comes,
1:11 filled with the fruit that righteousness bears, which comes through Jesus Christ, so that God will have the glory and the praise.
## Chains that spread the good news
1:12 Brothers, I want you to understand: my circumstances have, if anything, moved the good news forward.
1:13 My chains are now seen for what they are, chains for Christ, by the whole praetorium and by everyone else.
1:14 Most of the brothers have gained confidence in the Lord from my chains, and they are daring to speak the word far more freely, with no fear.
1:15 Some do proclaim Christ because they are jealous and want a fight. Others do it with good intentions.
1:16 Some announce Christ to push themselves forward, not honestly. They imagine they can make my chains harder to bear.
1:17 Others act from love. They know I have been put here to defend the good news.
1:18 So what? Just this: one way or another, for show or for real, Christ is being announced, and I rejoice at that. Yes, and I will go on rejoicing,
1:19 for I know this will end in my salvation, through your prayers and the help that the Spirit of Jesus Christ supplies.
1:20 It is what I eagerly watch for, and what I hope: I will not be put to shame in anything. With complete freedom of speech, now as always, Christ will be shown to be great in my body, by my living or by my dying.
1:21 For me, living is Christ and dying is gain.
1:22 But if I go on living in the flesh, that brings me fruit for my labor. So which would I choose? I will not say.
1:23 I am squeezed from both sides. I long to leave and be with Christ, since that is much, much better.
1:24 But for your sake it is more needed that I stay on in the flesh.
1:25 Being sure of that, I know I will stay, and stay alongside all of you, so that you will move forward and find joy in your faith.
1:26 Then, when I am with you again, you will have even more reason to boast in Christ Jesus because of me.
## Stand firm together
1:27 Just one thing: live as citizens worthy of the good news of Christ. Then whether I come and see you or am away, I will hear this about you: that you stand firm in one spirit, fighting side by side with one soul for the faith of the good news,
1:28 and that nothing your opponents do startles you. That is a plain sign to them of their destruction, but of your salvation, and it comes from God.
1:29 For you have been given this as a gift, on Christ's behalf: not only believing in him, but suffering for his sake too.
1:30 You are in the same contest you once watched me fight, and now hear that I am still fighting.
## The mind of Christ
2:1 So then: if being in Christ gives any encouragement, if love brings any comfort, if the Spirit gives any fellowship, if there is any warmth of heart and pity,
2:2 then make my joy full: think the same way, hold on to the same love, be one in soul, and think the one thing.
2:3 Do nothing to push yourselves forward or to puff yourselves up. Instead, with a lowly mind, rank others above yourselves.
2:4 Do not each keep an eye on your own concerns, but each on other people's too.
2:5 Think this way among yourselves, the way that is also in Christ Jesus:
2:6 Being in the form of God, / he did not count equality with God / as something to exploit.
2:7 No, he emptied himself, / taking a slave's form, / coming to be in the likeness of human beings.
2:8 Found in outward shape as a human being, / he made himself low / and became obedient all the way to death, / yes, death on a cross.
2:9 That is why God also raised him to the heights / and gave him, as a gift, the name / that is above every name,
2:10 so that, at the name of Jesus, / every knee should bend, / of those in heaven, on earth and under the earth,
2:11 and every tongue confess, / "Jesus Christ is Lord," / for the glory of God the Father.
## Lights in the world
2:12 So, my dear ones, you have always obeyed, and not just as when I was there. Now that I am away, do so even more. With fear and trembling, work out your own salvation.
2:13 For the one at work in you is God. He works in you both the wanting and the doing, for the sake of what pleases him.
2:14 Do everything without complaining under your breath or picking arguments,
2:15 so that you will be blameless and pure, God's children with no flaw, in the middle of a warped and twisted generation. Among them you shine out like lights in the world,
2:16 holding out the word of life. That will be my pride on the day of Christ: proof that I did not run for nothing and did not work hard for nothing.
2:17 Yet even if I am being poured out as a drink offering over the sacrifice and priestly service of your faith, I rejoice, and I rejoice together with all of you.
2:18 And you, in the same way, rejoice too, and rejoice together with me.
## Timothy and Epaphroditus
2:19 In the Lord Jesus, I hope to send Timothy to you quickly. Then I can take heart too, when I find out how you are doing.
2:20 There is no one with me who shares my heart as he does, no one who will so honestly worry about your affairs.
2:21 All the others are after what is theirs, not what is Jesus Christ's.
2:22 You know how he has proved himself. Like a child beside his father, he has worked like a slave with me for the good news.
2:23 He is the one I hope to send, then, the moment I can see what is going to happen to me.
2:24 And I am convinced in the Lord that I will come quickly myself as well.
2:25 Still, I have decided I must send Epaphroditus to you. He is my brother, my fellow worker and my fellow soldier. He is also your messenger, the one you sent to serve my needs.
2:26 He has been homesick for every one of you, and very troubled, because you heard he had been ill.
2:27 And ill he was, nearly to the point of death. But God showed him mercy, and not him alone but me too, so I would not have one sorrow piled on another.
2:28 So I am sending him all the sooner, so that you can see him again and rejoice, and so that I can have one sorrow fewer.
2:29 Receive him, then, in the Lord, with every joy, and hold people of his kind in honor,
2:30 for he came near to death for the sake of Christ's work. He gambled with his life to fill in what was missing in the service you sent me.
## No confidence in the flesh
3:1 As for the rest, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. Writing the same things to you again does not tire me, and it keeps you safe.
3:2 Look out for the dogs. Look out for the bad workers. Look out for the mutilation.
3:3 We are the circumcision: we worship by the Spirit of God, we take pride in Christ Jesus, and we do not rely on the flesh,
3:4 not that I lack grounds for confidence in the flesh. If anyone else thinks he can rely on the flesh, I can do it more:
3:5 I was circumcised when I was eight days old. I am from the nation of Israel and the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew whose parents were Hebrews. When it came to the law, I was a Pharisee.
3:6 As for zeal, I persecuted the church. As for the righteousness the law speaks of, I had become blameless.
3:7 But the things that were profits to me, these I have come to count as loss, because of Christ.
3:8 More than that: I count everything as loss, because knowing Christ Jesus my Lord is worth so much more. Because of him I have lost everything, and I count it all as dung, so that I may gain Christ
3:9 and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own, the kind that comes from the law, but the one that comes through faith in Christ, the righteousness that is from God and is based on faith.
3:10 I want to know him: the power of his rising, and what it is to share his sufferings, taking on the form of his death,
3:11 in the hope that somehow I will arrive at the resurrection out from among the dead.
## Running for the prize
3:12 I do not mean that I have already got it, or that I have already been made perfect. But I keep running after it, to see if I can catch hold of it, since Christ caught hold of me.
3:13 Brothers, I do not count myself as having caught hold of it. Just one thing: I put out of mind the things behind me, and I reach out for the things in front of me,
3:14 and I run hard toward the mark, after the prize: God's call upward in Christ Jesus.
3:15 So let those of us who are mature think this way. If you see something differently, God will show you that too.
3:16 Only, let us stay in line with the point we have already reached.
3:17 Brothers, all of you copy me, together. Watch the people who walk this way, since you have us as your model.
3:18 For many are walking about, people I have often told you of, and now I say it weeping: they are the enemies of Christ's cross.
3:19 They will end in ruin. Their god is the belly. They take pride in what should shame them. Their thinking is on earthly things.
3:20 But we belong to a commonwealth in heaven, and from there we are eagerly waiting for a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ.
3:21 He will refashion the body we have in our low state, to share the form of his body of glory, by the power that makes him able even to put all things under himself.
## Stand firm, think the same way
4:1 And so, my brothers, my loved ones, how I miss you! You are my joy and my victory wreath. This is how to stand firm in the Lord, my dear ones.
4:2 Euodia, I appeal to you, and Syntyche, I appeal to you: think the same way in the Lord.
4:3 Yes, and I ask you as well, my true yokefellow: help these women. They fought at my side in spreading the good news, together with Clement and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the book of life.
## Joy and peace
4:4 Rejoice in the Lord at all times. Again I will say it: rejoice!
4:5 Let all people come to know your gentle fairness. The Lord is near.
4:6 Worry about nothing. In everything, tell God what you are asking for, by prayer and by pleading, and with thanks.
4:7 Then God's peace, greater than anything the mind can reach, will stand guard over your hearts and your thoughts in Christ Jesus.
4:8 As for the rest, brothers: all that is true, all that deserves respect, all that is right, all that is pure, all that is pleasing, all that people speak well of, any virtue, anything that earns praise: keep thinking these over.
4:9 The things you learned from me and took in, heard from me and saw in me: do them. Then the God of peace will be with you.
## Thanks for the gift
4:10 In the Lord I rejoiced greatly that now, at last, your thinking of me has burst into bloom again. You were thinking of me all along, but the right moment never came.
4:11 It is not that I am short of anything. Whatever situation I am in, I have learned to be content with it.
4:12 I know how to live low, and I know how to live with more than enough. In every single thing and in all things I have been let into the secret: how to eat my fill and how to go hungry, how to have plenty and how to go without.
4:13 I have strength for everything in the one who makes me strong.
4:14 All the same, it was kind of you to become partners with me in my hardship.
4:15 You know it yourselves, Philippians: in the early days of the good news, when I set out from Macedonia, not one church went into partnership with me in a matter of giving and receiving, only you.
4:16 Even when I was in Thessalonica, you sent something for my needs, once and then a second time.
4:17 It is not the gift I am after. What I am after is the fruit that keeps adding up in your account.
4:18 I have been paid in full, and more than enough. I am filled up, now that I have received from Epaphroditus what you sent: a soothing smell, a sacrifice that God accepts and that pleases him.
4:19 And my God will fill every need you have, to match his wealth, in glory, in Christ Jesus.
4:20 Glory belongs to our God and Father for ever and ever. Amen.
## Greetings
4:21 Give my greeting to every one of the saints in Christ Jesus. The brothers with me send you greetings.
4:22 All the saints send you greetings, and above all those who belong to Caesar's household.
4:23 May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
