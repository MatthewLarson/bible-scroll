# Hostile review: the Adams chart's marks (2026-11-01)

Eight agents surveyed the chart in strips (1,151 marks); two separate agents then reviewed every mark (0–575, 576–1150)
against crops of the scan and the site's own Bible texts. They wrote fixes; the lead applied them.

## Findings

- Captions that misread the chart: Josephus cited where the chart does not (pillars before the Flood); Job "great-grandson"
  of Esau where the chart says grandson; "490 years" for the chart's "490 days"; 150 bishops for 350; the Kaaba "half a mile"
  around for a quarter; Leo VI for Leo V; "in the time of Jesus" added to the Hebrew dress picture.
- The chart's own errors stated as fact (Ussher "Archbishop of Dublin", Eusebius baptizing Constantine, a line of Scott's given
  to Byron): reworded as the chart's claim.
- Wrong topics: fall-of-jerusalem (586 BC) on AD 70 marks; dating (Revelation's date) on the Christian-era panels; joseph (son of
  Jacob) on Mary's husband; word-of-god (the Logos) on Bible-printing marks; holy-war on the Crusades; and others.
- References: Genesis 19 for Lot as Abraham's nephew; Genesis 1:1 on a panel of years; Miriam without Numbers 26:59.
- Wikipedia titles that land on the wrong person or page (Obed, Enoch, Jared, Charles VI and VII of France, and others).
- Boxes missing their subject (Melchizedek note and six others); 13 duplicate or empty repeat labels.
- Style: B.C./A.D. and "Usher" outside quotations of the chart.

## Fixes applied

All 247 fixes applied (226 changed, 13 deleted, 12 fields removed), plus Vespasian's topic (rome, not the 586 BC fall). The page's
"Using this page" now says the captions' dates and numbers are the chart's own, with the linked passage giving the Bible's.
1,138 marks remain. Full build: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
