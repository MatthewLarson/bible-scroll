# Ignatius to the Smyrnaeans: brief for the Romans and Smyrnaeans writer (second letter)

Book id `ISm` (works.json `ignatius-smyrnaeans`, proposed in `content/proposals/works/works.json`, tier `early`). 13 chapters, 28
verses plus `1:0`, the greeting ("to the church of God the Father and of the beloved Jesus Christ ... which is in Smyrna of
Asia"). Written from Troas (12:1, "I am writing to you from there by Burrhus"; Lake's introduction, vol. 1 OCR lines 8694-8698,
from Eusebius), after Ignatius had stayed in Smyrna, to its church (Polycarp was its overseer; Ignatius's letter To Polycarp is not
a book on the site). Its first half is the strongest of the letters against those who said Christ suffered
"only in appearance" (2:1; Lake's introduction: "docetism (δοκεῖν), which regarded the sufferings, and in some cases the life,
of Jesus as merely an appearance", lines 8742-8745); its second half holds the most debated verses in Ignatius: the
thanksgiving as "the flesh of our Savior" (7:1), "the catholic church" (8:2), and nothing without the overseer (8:1-2).

**One writer, two letters**: you write this after Ignatius to the Romans (`brief-iro.md`, which has the shared reading list
and your book.json duties). Own both book.json and sources.json files and the new `docetism` core.

## Parts
None: `content/books/ISm/` flat, 1:0-13:2. Sections: the greeting (1:0); the faith of the Smyrnaeans, a creed in Ignatius's
words (1:1-2: of David's line according to the flesh, Son of God, truly born of a virgin, baptized by John, truly nailed under
Pontius Pilate and Herod the tetrarch); he truly suffered and truly rose, not "in appearance" (2); in the flesh after the
resurrection, "take hold of me, handle me and see that I am not a bodiless demon", he ate and drank with them (3); beasts in
human form, "near the sword is near God" (4); those who deny him, "flesh-bearing" (5); judgment even on heavenly beings who do
not believe in Christ's blood; they care nothing for love, the widow, the orphan, the oppressed, the prisoner, the hungry (6);
they abstain from the thanksgiving and prayer (7); follow the overseer, the thanksgiving under him, the catholic church (8);
it is good to know God and the overseer (9); Philo and Rheus Agathopus (10); the church in Antioch at peace, send an envoy (11);
greetings from Troas by Burrhus (12); the households, "the virgins who are called widows", Tavia, Alce, Daphnus, Eutecnus (13).

## Text and witnesses
- Base text: Lake's Greek (`texts/orig/ISm.json` `grc`, with `fixed`: 7 slips corrected in 6 verses, among them 7:1 πρυσευχῆς and 2:1
  "το δοκεῖν"), the short (middle) recension. Witnesses as brief-iep.md (G, g, L, A, S), and for this letter two more (Lake,
  lines 8895-8903): **C**, the Sahidic fragment ("part of the epistle to the Smyrnaeans, from MS. Borg. 248 in the Museo
  Nazionale at Naples", published 1883 and by Lightfoot in 1885), and **B**, the Berlin papyrus (P. 10581, "of the 5th century",
  published 1910 by Schmidt and Schubert), which "contains Smyrnaeans iii. 3-xii. 1". Say in the chapter pages of 3-12 that a
  witness a thousand years older than the others is there, and give its readings where Lake does.
- Lake's footnotes (`texts/src/ISm.app.json`, 25, most of them B's readings): enter as variants those that change the meaning,
  witnesses exactly as Lake gives them. Among them: 1:1 (A and Theodoret omit θεοῦ, followed by Lightfoot: "Jesus Christ, the
  God"); 3:2 (πνεύματι GLC, αἵματι A); 4:1 (several, and "here and elsewhere Bg read τῷ δοκεῖν against G which has τὸ δοκεῖν");
  5:2; 6:2 (λελυμένου); 7:2 (πρέπον οὖν); **8:2** (ἤτω B, ἔστω Gg; Ἰησοῦς Χριστός BA, Χριστὸς Ἰησοῦς GL; πράσσετε or
  πράσσεται); 9:1-2; 10:1 (B has Γάϊον, Gaius, for Ῥέον; Lake: "transcriptionally more probable"), 10:2 (ἐλπίς or πίστις);
  11:2-3; 12:1 (the spelling of Burrhus); 13:1 (πατρός LA, πνεύματος G); 13:2 (Ταουΐας or Γαουΐας).
- English check texts: Roberts and Donaldson's shorter column (`texts/en/ISm.json`) and Lake's English on his facing pages (vol.
  1 OCR from line 12976), with his footnote on the love feast at 8:2 (line 13526, brief-apfathers2.md, Terms).
