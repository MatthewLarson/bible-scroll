# Ephesians: brief for the part writers

Read tools/brief-nt.md, brief-pauline.md and brief-rom.md first. Book id `Eph`. 6 chapters, 155 verses (23, 22, 21, 32, 33, 24). Lenses H C L J T. Two parts:

| Part | Chapters | Verses | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01-03 | 1-3 | 66 | The greeting ("in Ephesus" is missing from P46, Sinaiticus and Vaticanus first hands: a full variant), the great blessing 1:3-14 (one Greek sentence: chosen, adoption, redemption, the mystery, the seal), the prayer (1:15-23), made alive by grace (2:1-10), Jew and Gentile one new humanity (2:11-22), Paul's stewardship of the mystery (3), the prayer (3:14-21). **Owns book.json** |
| 04-06 | 4-6 | 89 | Unity, gifts (4:7-16; Ps 68:18), old and new self, the Spirit grieved (4:30), light and darkness, the household code (5:21-6:9: wives, husbands, children, parents, slaves, masters), the armor of God (6:10-20), Tychicus (6:21-24) |

**Setting (H):** Ephesus and the temple of Artemis (Acts 19 is on the site), the Roman household and the household-code tradition (Aristotle, *Politics* 1.1253b; Philo; Josephus, *Against Apion* 2.199-217: verify before citing; Colossians 3:18-4:1 is parallel but not yet on the site: use prose), Paul in prison (Rome? Caesarea? Ephesus?). **C (full weight):** authorship, Pauline or deutero-Pauline (vocabulary, long sentences, dependence on Colossians, ecclesiology, 2:20 and 3:5, the missing personal greetings, "in Ephesus" omitted): fairly give Lincoln, Best, Schnackenburg, Perkins, Barth, Hoehner, O'Brien, Arnold, Thielman, Wright, Witherington; the encyclical theory (Col 4:16 "from Laodicea"; Marcion's "Laodiceans"); Colossians-Ephesians relationship. If authorship is disputed, book.json says "traditionally Paul" and labels the question; the Plain follows the text. **L:** the long sentences (1:3-14; 1:15-23; 3:1-7), prayer reports, the household code, the armor image. **J:** Isa 52:7, 57:19 (2:17), Ps 68:18 (4:8), Ps 4:4 (4:26), Zech 8:16 (4:25), Gen 2:24 (5:31) and the "great mystery", Deut 5:16 (6:2-3), Isa 11:4 and 59:17 (armor), the dividing wall and the Temple warning inscription (Josephus: verify), Jewish readings of election, the Gentiles in "the commonwealth of Israel" (supersession question). **T:** Chrysostom's homilies on Ephesians, Augustine and Pelagius, Jerome, Aquinas, Luther, Calvin (1:4), Wesley, Orthodox (theosis, recapitulation in Irenaeus), Catholic (marriage as sacrament via *mysterion*/*sacramentum*, 5:32).

**Places for full treatment:** 1:1; 1:3-14 (*proorizo*, *eklegomai*, "in him"); 1:10 (*anakephalaiosasthai*, recapitulation); 1:13-14 (seal, *arrabon*); 1:22-23 (head, *pleroma*); 2:1-10 (2:8 "this is not from yourselves": *touto* neuter; faith or grace?); 2:14-18; 2:20 (apostles and prophets as foundation); 3:5, 3:9-10; 4:8-10 (descended to the lower parts of the earth: the descent into Hades); 4:11 (pastors and teachers: one office or two; apostles today?); 4:30; 5:14 (a hymn quotation); 5:18-20; 5:21-33 (headship and submission; *kephale*; 5:21 participle; 5:32); 6:1-4; 6:5-9 (slavery); 6:10-20 (the powers: *archai*, *exousiai*, *kosmokratores*; spiritual warfare).

**Cards (plan 8 to 10):** 1:3-5 (election), 2:8-9 (grace and faith), 2:20 (foundation), 4:8-10 (the descent), 4:11-12 (offices), 5:21-33 (headship: complementarian and egalitarian, Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Reformed, Baptist: cite each tradition's own documents only if verified), 5:32 (marriage as sacrament), 6:5-9 (slavery), 6:12 (spiritual warfare), 4:5 (one baptism).
