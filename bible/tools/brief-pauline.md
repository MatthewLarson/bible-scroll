# Paul's letters, 1 Corinthians to Ephesians: shared brief for 1Co 2Co Gal Eph

Read tools/brief-nt.md first (rules for every New Testament book), then tools/brief-rom.md (Romans is the model for a Pauline letter: its lenses, book.json, deep dives and cards) and content/books/Rom/ for form. nt lane: write only in `content/books/<id>/...`; shared additions in `content/proposals/nt/`. Romans is on the site: quote its Plain exactly where a verse is repeated (Gal 3 and Rom 4 on Abraham; Gal 2:16 and Rom 3:20, 28; 1 Cor 12 and Rom 12; Rom 6 and Col; Eph and Col are parallel, Colossians not yet written: use prose).

**Lenses (fixed, same as Romans): H History and setting, C Critical, L Literary, J Jewish reading, T Church tradition.** Each book.json gives its own `desc`.

**Terms (lead decisions, matching Romans; read terms.json and proposals/nt/terms.json first).** "brothers" for *adelphoi* (Romans decision; note the debate at the first use in each book); "By no means!" for *me genoito*; "faith in Jesus Christ"/"faith of Jesus Christ" (*pistis Christou*): follow Rom 3:22 as decided there, but Gal 2:16 and 3:22 and Phil-type places get a full variant/note with both readings; "justify/righteous" (*dikaioo/dikaiosyne*): follow Romans (read it first and match); "flesh" (*sarx*), "Spirit", "grace", "gospel", "church" (*ekklesia*) as the nt terms say; "saints/holy ones" as the terms say; "Christ" with "the Christ" note; "Lord"; "the world/the age" (*kosmos/aion*). Greek text: SBL base per brief-nt.md, variants against NA28, THGNT, Byzantine and the TR. Reading level 8th grade. Drift audit in the head only.

**Audience.** The cards "who it was for, and why" matter most here: each letter has a named, specific audience and occasion. The settings must say, for every section, what problem Paul answers and what the first readers would have heard (Corinth's honor and patronage culture, the Galatian crisis over circumcision, the Ephesian audience and the "in Ephesus" omission). Do not turn a local instruction into a universal one without a note, and do not hide the Jewish setting of Galatians and Ephesians (the J lens).

**Fairness.** The women's passages (1 Cor 11:2-16, 14:33-36), same-sex terms (1 Cor 6:9; *arsenokoitai*, *malakoi*), slavery (1 Cor 7:21; Eph 6:5-9), divorce (1 Cor 7), the Lord's Supper (11:17-34), baptism for the dead (15:29), speaking in tongues, justification and the "works of the law", election (Eph 1), submission (Eph 5:21-33), and the armor (Eph 6) are the places that need traditions cards with every tradition side by side (TRADITIONS in build.js), sourced to the churches' own teachers, confessions and documents. Never rank them. Label Claude's own theories `spec`.

**Hostile-review priorities** (for later): invented sources or quotations, drift toward the NIV/ESV, papyrus and codex claims without a source, flattening disputed authorship (Ephesians; 2 Cor 6:14-7:1 and 2 Cor 10-13 partition theories; 1 Cor 14:34-35 as an interpolation, the Western displacement and Bezae/Claromontanus), unfair summaries of any tradition.

## Lead decisions (2026-10-29)
- *naos* in Paul (1 Cor 3:16-17; 6:19; 2 Cor 6:16; Eph 2:21): "temple". The content/terms.json "sanctuary" rule covers Matthew, Mark, Luke and Acts only (John already uses "temple" for *naos*). Each book keeps a short note at first use saying the Greek is *naos* (the inner house of the god) and that Paul is using the image of the believers as the place where God lives. Do not change it to "sanctuary".
- "brothers" for *adelphoi* with the gloss, as in Romans and Acts.
