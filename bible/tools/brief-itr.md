# Ignatius to the Trallians: brief for the Smyrna writer (second letter)

Book id `ITr` (works.json `ignatius-trallians`, proposed in `content/proposals/works/works.json`, tier `early`). 13 chapters, 29
verses plus `1:0`, the greeting ("to the holy church which is in Tralles of Asia", ἐν Τράλλεσιν τῆς Ἀσίας). Written from Smyrna
(12:1, "I greet you from Smyrna"; Lake's introduction, vol. 1 OCR lines 8690-8694), after the Trallians' overseer Polybius visited
Ignatius there (1:1). Its first chapters are on the overseer, the elders and the deacons ("without these it is not called a
church", 3:1); its center is the strongest statement in this batch against those who said Christ suffered "only in appearance"
(9-11; Lake's introduction: "docetism (δοκεῖν), which regarded the sufferings, and in some cases the life, of Jesus as merely an
appearance", lines 8742-8745).

**One writer, two letters**: you write this after Ignatius to the Magnesians (`brief-img.md`, which has the shared reading list
and your duties). Own both book.json and sources.json files.

## Parts
None: `content/books/ITr/` flat, 1:0-13:3. Sections (from the Greek and Lake's English, vol. 1 OCR 11085-11688): the greeting,
"in the fullness, in the apostolic manner" (1:0); Polybius their overseer at Smyrna (1); be subject to the overseer as to Jesus
Christ and to the council of elders as to the apostles; the deacons "of the mysteries of Jesus Christ ... not deacons of food and
drink" (2); respect the deacons as Jesus Christ, the overseer as a type of the Father, the elders as God's council: "without these
it is not called a church"; the overseer's bearing; "I am not one to give you orders like an apostle" (3); he must not be puffed
up; the ruler of this age (4); he will not write of heavenly things to "babes"; the places of the angels and the gatherings of the
rulers (5); Christian food only, "a foreign plant, which is heresy"; deadly poison in honeyed wine (6); within the altar, pure (7);
faith the flesh of the Lord, love the blood of Jesus Christ; "Woe to the one through whom my name is blasphemed" (8); Jesus Christ
of David's family, of Mary, truly born, ate and drank, truly persecuted under Pontius Pilate, truly crucified and died, in the
sight of those in heaven, on earth and under the earth; truly raised (9); if he suffered only in appearance, why am I in chains?
(10); the offshoots that are not the Father's planting; branches of the cross; the head and the members (11); greetings from
Smyrna; the elders to refresh the overseer (12); the love of the Smyrnaeans and Ephesians; the church in Syria, "the last of them";
farewell (13).

## Text and witnesses
- Base text: Lake's Greek (`texts/orig/ITr.json` `grc`, with `fixed`: 11 slips corrected in 9 verses, and the restored **11:2**: the
  digital file ran it into 11:1; both scans begin section 2 at εἰ γὰρ ἦσαν), the short (middle) recension, witnesses G, g, L, A, S.
  Known and not fixed: 3:3 ὤν and the second ὤν of 7:2 (the participle; read ὢν); 9:2 νεκρῶν. or νεκρῶν, (the scans differ).
- Lake's footnotes (`texts/src/ITr.app.json`, 9): enter as variants those that change the meaning, witnesses exactly as Lake gives
  them. Above all:
  - **6:2**: "The text is clearly corrupt" (Lake): οἱ ἑαυτοῖς παρεμπλέκουσιν "seems to be the text implied by the translations of
    SA, but G(L) read οἱ καιροὶ παρεμπλέκουσιν and g has καὶ τὸν ἰὸν προσπλέκοντες", and Lightfoot's καὶ ἰῷ παρεμπλέκουσιν ("for they
    even mingle poison with Jesus Christ"). Lake prints οἱ ἑαυτοῖς (the digital file's accent on οἳ is now corrected to his page).
    A full variant entry; translate Lake's text and say in a note that it is uncertain. The second 6:2 note (κακῇ L, om. SA,
    κἀκεῖ τὸ ἀποθανεῖν G) too.
  - **3:3**: "The text is here confused and corrupt in all the authorities. Lightfoot prefers to read οὕτως φείδομαι, and adds ἀλλ᾽
    οὐχ ἱκανὸν ..." (the scans; the Beta Code is garbled in app.json: read Lake's page): a variant and a note.
  - **7:1**: "The text is doubtful. A omits θεοῦ" (ἀχωρίστοις θεοῦ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ); Lake's English footnote offers "from our God Jesus
    Christ" (line 11429): a variant, and the brief-apfathers2.md rule on ὁ θεός of Jesus (translate exactly, say the reading is
    uncertain).
  - 1:1, 2:2, **9:2** (κατὰ τὸ ὁμοίωμα ὃς καί G; "qui et secundum similitudinem" L; SA perhaps ὡς καί), 12:3 (Lightfoot: περίκειμαι
    "impossible", with Bunsen's οὗπερ ἔγκειμαι), 13:3 (A and g "may you be found").
- English check texts: Roberts and Donaldson's shorter column (`texts/en/ITr.json`) and Lake's English on his facing pages, with his
  English footnotes (vol. 1 OCR): 3:2 "Or, possibly, 'is his power'" (line 11215); 4:2 τὸ ζῆλος "the jealousy of the devil", or
  "Ambition is not obvious" (11326-11329, with "cf. the letter to the Romans v. 3"); 7:1 (11429).
