# 1 Corinthians: brief for the part writers

Read tools/brief-nt.md, brief-pauline.md and brief-rom.md first. Book id `1Co`. 16 chapters, 437 verses. Lenses H C L J T. Five parts, written at once (no part can see another's files):

| Part | Chapters | Verses | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01-04 | 1-4 | 91 | Greeting and thanks; factions (Paul, Apollos, Cephas, Christ); the cross as foolishness and power; wisdom and the Spirit; servants and stewards. **Owns book.json** |
| 05-08 | 5-8 | 86 | The man with his father's wife; lawsuits; "all things are lawful"; the body and prostitutes (6:9-11 vice list, *malakoi*, *arsenokoitai*); marriage, divorce, the unmarried (7), slavery (7:21), food offered to idols (8) |
| 09-11 | 9-11 | 94 | Paul's rights and the athlete; Israel in the wilderness (10); idol meat; head coverings (11:2-16); the Lord's Supper (11:17-34) |
| 12-14 | 12-14 | 84 | Gifts, one body; the hymn of love (13); tongues and prophecy; women silent (14:33-36) |
| 15-16 | 15-16 | 82 | The creed (15:3-8), resurrection, baptism for the dead (15:29), the resurrection body; the collection, travel plans, greetings, *maranatha* (16:22) |

**Setting (H):** Corinth, rebuilt as a Roman colony in 44 BC, the Isthmian games, patronage and status, Erastus (Rom 16:23), Gallio's inscription at Delphi (Acts 18:12-17) for the date (written from Ephesus, about AD 53-55; Acts is on the site), Chloe's people (1:11), Stephanas, Fortunatus and Achaicus (16:17), the earlier letter (5:9), the letter from Corinth (7:1). **C:** unity of the letter (the partition theories), possible interpolations (11:3-16? 14:34-35), the Corinthian slogans (6:12; 7:1; 8:1, 4: where Paul may be quoting), P46, Sinaiticus, Vaticanus, Claromontanus, Bezae. **L:** rhetoric (Mitchell's deliberative rhetoric, Winter), the shape of 8-10, 13 as the center of 12-14. **J:** Paul and Torah (5:7-8 Passover; 9:9 Deut 25:4; 10 the exodus; 14:21 Isa 28:11-12), the Shema in 8:6. **T:** Chrysostom's homilies, Augustine, Aquinas, Luther, Calvin, Orthodox readings (theosis, the liturgy), the Reformation debates on 11:23-29 and 15.

**Places for full treatment:** 1:18-2:5; 2:6-16; 3:10-17 (builder, fire, the temple; the purgatory debate at 3:15); 4:6; 5:5; 6:9-11; 6:12-20; 7 (divorce and remarriage; 7:1, 7:14, 7:21, 7:36-38 betrothed or daughter, 7:29-31); 8:6; 9:24-27; 10:1-13; 10:16-17; 11:2-16 (head coverings, *kephale*, "authority on her head", "because of the angels"); 11:17-34 (real presence, memorial, sacrifice); 12:3; 12:13; 13 (*agape*: see terms); 14:2-19 tongues; 14:34-35 (the Western tradition moves it after verse 40: Codex Fuldensis, Bezae; editors who see an interpolation: Fee, Payne; weigh fairly); 15:3-8 (the creed; Gal 1:18-19), 15:12-34, 15:29, 15:35-58, 15:51 (variants "we shall all sleep"); 16:22 *marana tha* ("Our Lord, come" or "Our Lord has come"; Didache 10:6 parallel).

**Cards (plan 12 to 16):** 3:15 (purgatory), 5:1-5 (church discipline), 6:9-11, 7:10-16 (divorce), 7:1-9 and 7:25-40 (celibacy), 8-10 (idol meat), 11:2-16, 11:17-34, 12:27-31 (gifts, cessationism), 14:34-35 (women), 15:29, 15:20-28 (the Son subject to the Father), 16:1-4 (the collection and giving).
