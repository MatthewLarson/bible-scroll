# Philippians: brief for the writer

Read tools/brief-nt.md, brief-pauline.md, brief-pauline2.md and brief-rom.md first. Book id `Php`. 4 chapters, 104 verses (30, 30, 21, 23). Lenses H C L J T. One part, flat folder `content/books/Php/`, one writer, owns book.json.

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1:1-11 | Greeting to the saints "with the overseers and deacons" (1:1), thanksgiving for their partnership (*koinonia*), prayer |
| 1:12-30 | Paul's chains advance the gospel, the praetorium (1:13), rival preachers, "to live is Christ and to die is gain" (1:21-24), stand firm |
| 2:1-11 | Unity and humility; the Christ hymn (2:5-11): form of God, did not count equality something to grasp, emptied himself, slave, death on a cross, the name above every name (Isa 45:23) |
| 2:12-30 | "Work out your salvation with fear and trembling, for God is at work in you" (2:12-13), lights in the world, poured out as a drink offering, Timothy and Epaphroditus |
| 3 | "Beware of the dogs", the true circumcision, Paul's credentials (3:5-6), loss and gain, righteousness "through faith in Christ" (3:9), pressing on, citizenship in heaven (3:20) |
| 4 | Euodia and Syntyche and the "true yokefellow", rejoice, "the peace of God", whatever is true, "I can do all things", the gift from Philippi, Caesar's household (4:22) |

**Setting (H):** Philippi as a Roman colony (Acts 16 is on the site: Lydia, the slave girl, the jailer; Plain is the model), veterans and Latin public life, *politeuma* and citizenship (1:27; 3:20), the gift carried by Epaphroditus; where Paul was imprisoned (Rome, Ephesus or Caesarea: views and their dates; the praetorium and "Caesar's household" for each). **C:** unity of the letter (the partition theories: two or three letters joined at 3:1/3:2 and 4:10-20; views, fairly), the hymn as pre-Pauline or Paul's own (Lohmeyer, Martin, Käsemann, Hurtado, Fee, Bockmuehl, Hellerman: verify), the opponents of chapter 3. **L:** the friendship-letter and family-letter forms, joy vocabulary, the hymn's structure, the "mind" (*phroneo*) thread. **J:** Paul's Jewish credentials (3:5-6, "Hebrew of Hebrews", "as to the law a Pharisee", "blameless"), "the dogs", "the circumcision" (3:2-3: who is meant; the misuse of 3:2 against Jews; Paul within Judaism readings), Isa 45:23 at 2:10-11 (YHWH's oath said of Jesus), the drink offering (Num 15, 28), the Philippi without a synagogue (Acts 16:13 "place of prayer"). **T:** Chrysostom's homilies on Philippians, Augustine, Aquinas, Luther, Calvin, the kenotic christologies (Thomasius, Gore, Bulgakov) and the councils (Chalcedon), Catholic and Orthodox readings of 1:23 and the saints, Wesley on 2:12-13.

**Places for full treatment:** 1:1 (overseers and deacons: plural overseers in one city; the church order debate); 1:6; 1:13 (the praetorium); 1:19 (salvation or deliverance; Job 13:16 in the Greek); 1:21-24 (depart and be with Christ: the intermediate state); 1:29; 2:5 ("which was also in Christ Jesus": supply "is yours in Christ"? the two readings); **2:6-11** (*morphe*, *harpagmos* res rapta or res rapienda, *ekenosen*, "being found in appearance", "even death on a cross" as Paul's addition?, "God highly exalted him", "Jesus Christ is Lord": deep dive); 2:12-13; 2:17; 3:2-3; 3:5-6; **3:9** (*pistis Christou*: the Plain follows Rom 3:22's decision, with the full note and both readings, per brief-pauline.md); 3:10-11; 3:12-16 (perfection: Wesleyan Christian perfection and others); 3:19-21 (citizenship; the resurrection body); 4:2-3 (two women leaders; the "yokefellow" possibly named Syzygos; Clement and the book of life); 4:13; 4:15-18 (giving, the "fragrant offering"); 4:22.

**Variants to weigh (check each in texts/orig and texts/auto; name only witnesses verified this session):** 1:14 "the word" / "the word of God" / "of the Lord"; 1:16-17 order (the Byzantine reverses the verses); 2:4; 3:3 "worship by the Spirit of God" / "worship God in spirit"; 3:12 "or am already justified" (a Western addition); 3:16 the Byzantine addition "let us mind the same thing"; 4:13 "through Christ who strengthens me" (Byzantine) / "through him who"; 4:23 "with your spirit" / "with you all. Amen".

**Cards (plan 5 to 7):** 1:1 (church order: overseers, elders, deacons, bishops: Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, Lutheran, Presbyterian, Baptist and congregational, `top: elders`); 1:21-24 (`top: intermediate-state`: soul sleep, conscious presence, purgatory); 2:6-8 (the self-emptying: what Christ laid aside; Chalcedonian and kenotic readings, `top: kenosis`); 2:12-13 (`top: free-will` or `justification`: synergy, monergism); 3:9 (`top: justification`, pointing to the Romans card); 3:12-15 (Christian perfection, `top: holiness`); 4:3 (women's leadership, `top: women-in-ministry`, pointing).
