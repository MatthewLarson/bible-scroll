# 2 Peter: brief for the writer

Read tools/brief-nt.md, brief-pauline.md, brief-pauline2.md and brief-general.md first. Book id `2Pe`. 3 chapters,
61 verses. Lenses H C L J T. One part, flat folder `content/books/2Pe/`, one writer, owns book.json. Jude is published
and is the model for 2:1-3:3: read content/books/Jud/ in full (plain.txt, notes, variants, book.json and the
`jude-and-2-peter` topic) before you write a verse. 1 Peter is being written at the same time.

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1:1-2 | "Simeon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ", to those with "a faith as precious as ours" |
| 1:3-11 | His divine power; "sharers in the divine nature" (1:4); the ladder of virtues (faith ... love); make your calling and election sure |
| 1:12-21 | Peter's farewell (his "tent" soon put off, as the Lord showed him); eyewitnesses of the majesty on the holy mountain; the prophetic word "like a lamp shining in a dark place"; no prophecy "of one's own interpretation" |
| 2:1-22 | False teachers (Jude's material, reworked: the angels cast into Tartarus, Noah, Sodom and Lot, Balaam, the dog returns to its vomit, Proverbs 26:11) |
| 3:1-13 | "This is now the second letter"; the scoffers: "where is the promise of his coming?"; the world once destroyed by water; "one day is like a thousand years" (Psalm 90:4); the day of the Lord like a thief; the elements dissolved; new heavens and a new earth |
| 3:14-18 | "Our beloved brother Paul" and "his letters", "hard to understand", which the ignorant twist "as they do the other Scriptures"; grow in grace |

**Setting (H):** the farewell testament genre (Jacob, Moses, Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs: verify), the scoffers
and the delay of the coming ("since the fathers fell asleep": the first Christian generation dead?), Epicurean denial
of providence (Neyrey: verify), the readers (perhaps the same as 1 Peter, 3:1). **C:** authorship and date, at full
weight: the case against Peter (the dependence on Jude; the collection of Paul's letters as "Scripture"; "the fathers"
dead; the different Greek from 1 Peter (Jerome's explanation of two secretaries: verify); thin early attestation: first
named by Origen (verify), doubted by Eusebius (verify); a testament written in Peter's name, perhaps AD 80-130) and the
case for Peter or a secretary (Michael Green, Kruger: verify each), with the majority view named as the majority. Never
flatten. **L:** the farewell form, the sorites of virtues (1:5-7), the "Asian" style (Bauckham's commentary on 2 Peter's
style: verify), the rare words. **J:** the Watchers (1 Enoch 10; Tartarus in 1 Enoch 20:2 Greek: verify), Noah "a herald
of righteousness" (Jewish tradition of Noah preaching: Josephus, *Antiquities* 1.74; Sibylline Oracles 1: verify), Lot
"righteous" (Wisdom 10:6: verify), Balaam son of Bosor (2:15; the variant Beor; Balaam's donkey; Jewish Balaam
traditions), the world's destruction by fire (the Stoic *ekpyrosis*; Jewish texts: 1QH, the Life of Adam and Eve 49:3,
Sibylline Oracles: verify each; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 34:4; 66:15-16 Plain English Translation), Psalm 90:4 (quote the Plain English Translation) and its Jewish
use (Jubilees 4:30: verify). **T:** the canon (2 Peter's late acceptance; Calvin's doubts about Peter as writer while
holding the book canonical (verify); Luther), 1:4 and theosis (Athanasius, *On the Incarnation* 54: verify; Orthodox
teaching; the Catechism 460 quoting 2 Peter 1:4: verify; Wesley; Protestant hesitations), 1:20-21 and the authority
to interpret (the Catholic Magisterium and *Dei Verbum*: verify; the Reformation's Scripture interpreting Scripture).
