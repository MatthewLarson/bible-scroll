# Joel: brief for the writer

Read tools/brief-twelve.md first. Book id `Jol`, 3 chapters in English (4 in Hebrew), 73 verses (20, 32, 21). Prophets lane. One part, flat folder `content/books/Jol/` (like Lam). The writer owns book.json. One review.md.

**Numbering.** English 2:28-32 = Hebrew 3:1-5; English chapter 3 = Hebrew chapter 4. Verify in texts/orig/Jol.json; explain in notes at 2:28 and 3:1 and in book.json `facts`.

**Lenses:** H, C, J, X, F.
- H: a locust plague and drought; the date: pre-exilic (9th or 8th century) or post-exilic (5th-4th century, the common critical view: Temple and priests lead, no king, exile at 3:2, Greeks at 3:6, borrowing from other prophets). Give the evidence for each; do not settle it.
- C: unity (1-2 and 3), the locust poem and army poem (2:1-11), the "northern one" (2:20), Joel's dense quotation of and by other prophets (Exodus 10; Isaiah 13; Ezekiel 30; Obadiah 15, 17; Amos 1:2; 9:13; Micah 4:3), Joel in the Twelve.
- J: Rashi, Ibn Ezra, Kimhi, the Targum; 2:12-17 and fast days; Joel in the haftarot (verify from a printed source or leave out); the valley of Jehoshaphat and the Messianic age in Talmud and midrash.
- X: Acts 2; Romans 10:13; Revelation 6:12, 9:7-11, 14:14-20; Jerome, Ephrem, Cyril, Theodoret, Chrysostom, Luther, Calvin, Pusey; Joel 2:12-18 as the Ash Wednesday reading; Pentecostal and charismatic use.
- F: the day of the LORD as future (2:30-32; 3), the valley of decision, the final war and restoration: dispensational, amillennial and Jewish messianic readings side by side.

**Places for full treatment.**
- 1:1 Joel son of Pethuel (the Greek "Bethuel"). 1:2-4 the four locust words (Plain English Translation: the cutting, swarming, hopping and stripping locust; notes give the debate: stages of one insect, four kinds, or poetry; Exodus 10:4-15; Amos 4:9; 7:1; Psalm 78:46; Deuteronomy 28:38-42; Revelation 9). 1:5-12 the drunkards, the vine, the grain and drink offerings cut off; 1:13-14 priests in sackcloth, the solemn assembly; 1:15 "Alas for the day! the day of the LORD is near" (Isaiah 13:6; Ezekiel 30:2-3; Obadiah 15; Zephaniah 1:7, 14); 1:16-20 drought, fire, the animals cry.
- 2:1-11 the trumpet in Zion; locusts as an army or an army like locusts; 2:10 earth and heaven shake. 2:12-14 "Even now, return to me" (creed of Exodus 34:6, match the Exodus Plain English Translation; "Who knows? He may turn and relent": Jonah 3:9; 2 Samuel 12:22); "rend your hearts, not your clothes". 2:15-17 the assembly, the priests' prayer (Psalm 79:10; 115:2). 2:18-27 the answer; 2:20 *hatsefoni* "the northern one" (locusts, an army, an apocalyptic foe); 2:23 *hammoreh litsedaqah* "the early rain for your vindication" vs "the teacher of righteousness" (Vulgate, Targum; Qumran use: check CD and 1QpHab before saying anything); 2:25 "I will repay you for the years the locust has eaten" (its use in prosperity preaching is a modern use: label).
- 2:28-32 (Hebrew 3:1-5) the Spirit poured on all flesh, sons and daughters, old and young, male and female servants; compare Acts 2:17-21 against the Greek of Joel (Acts has "in the last days", "and they will prophesy", "signs below"); sun darkened, moon to blood; "everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be saved" (Romans 10:13; topic names-of-god); "on Mount Zion... those who escape" (Obadiah 17).
- 3:1-8 (Hebrew 4:1-8) the nations judged in the Valley of Jehoshaphat ("the LORD judges"; a symbolic name or a real valley: give views), the children sold to the Greeks (*hayyevanim*), the Sabeans of 3:8; Tyre, Sidon, Philistia (Amos 1:6, 9; Ezekiel 27:13; 2 Chronicles 21:16-17; the dating argument).
- 3:9-17 swords from plowshares (the reversal of Isaiah 2:4, Micah 4:3), the sickle and winepress (Revelation 14:14-20), *'emeq hecharuts* "the valley of decision" (or of threshing), the LORD roars from Zion (3:16 = Amos 1:2, align). 3:18-21 the mountains drip wine, the spring from the LORD's house (Ezekiel 47; Zechariah 14:8; Revelation 22:1), Egypt and Edom, "Judah will be inhabited forever"; 3:21 is hard in Hebrew, Greek and Syriac.
