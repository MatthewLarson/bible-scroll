**Present tense and sin (3:6-9; 5:18 against 1:8-10).** Decide one rendering for the habitual present and keep it:
"does not keep on sinning" (ESV-like readings) is interpretation; "does not sin" with a note giving the readings
(habitual sin, a different kind of sin, an ideal, the believer's new nature, perfectionism (Wesley's *A Plain Account of
Christian Perfection*: verify)) keeps every view open. The brief decides: **literal "does not sin" / "cannot sin"** with
the full note at 3:6 and a deep dive on the chapter page, because the Greek tense does not settle the question and the
site lets the reader see the tension 1 John itself sets up.
