# Brief: add "Where Christians differ" to published books of the Bible Scroll

Matt wants readers to see how different Christian traditions read the Bible (Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, Methodist, Baptist, Pentecostal, Anabaptist and others), especially where they conflict. The site supports this with `traditions` entries, shown as "Where Christians differ" cards on chapter pages (chapters.json) and on the book's introduction (book.json). Read the "traditions" section of bible/STYLE.md and the "Where Christians differ" and "Books outside the 66" sections of tools/agent-writer.md.

## Your job
For each of your books:
1. Read book.json and every chapter page, including its lenses (the X lens is the Christian reading), its notes and its settings, so what you add agrees with what is there and does not repeat it word for word.
2. Find the passages where Christian traditions disagree in a way that matters for reading the text. Old Testament examples: the image of God and original sin (Gen 1–3); the protoevangelium (Gen 3:15) and Mary; Melchizedek and the Eucharist (Gen 14); Abraham's faith and justification (Gen 15:6); the Sabbath and the Lord's Day (Exo 20, Deu 5); which commandments are which (the numbering of the Ten Commandments differs: Catholic and Lutheran vs Reformed, Orthodox and others); images and icons (Exo 20:4); the food laws and the Christian; tithing; the Day of Atonement and the atonement; the land promise and modern Israel (dispensationalist vs covenant theology vs Catholic); holy war; women leaders (Deborah); the prophet like Moses (Deu 18); the Davidic covenant; the kingship of Saul and Samuel's ghost (1 Sa 28, prayers for and with the dead); the canon (Esther's place; the Greek Additions; 1–2 Chronicles and 1 Esdras). For Revelation: the millennium (premillennial, amillennial, postmillennial), the rapture, the 144,000 and the identity of the woman of chapter 12.
3. Write each card in the chapter where the passage is (`traditions` in that chapter of chapters.json; book-wide questions go in book.json `traditions`). Each card is one question with two or more views, and a `top`: the id of the topic page the question belongs to (for example `baptism`, `mary`, `justification`, `communion-of-saints`, `sabbath`). When the same question comes up in another book, use the same `top` so the cards link to each other and are listed on that topic page; write the card for what this passage adds rather than repeating the other card. State each tradition's answer as its own teachers state it, cite catechisms, confessions and named theologians (Catechism of the Catholic Church, Augsburg Confession and Luther, Westminster Confession and Calvin, the Thirty-Nine Articles, Wesley, the Baptist Faith and Message or the 1689 London Confession, the Orthodox fathers and modern Orthodox theologians), say where a tradition is itself divided, and never rank them. Keep each answer to two or three sentences in 8th-grade English. Only use source keys that exist in content/sources.json or the book's parts' sources.json; add a key to content/sources.json (re-read it first, edit a few lines at a time) if you need a real published work that is missing. Before adding a key, search content/sources.json and the book's parts' sources.json for the same work under another key (for example `westminster`, `ccc`, `augsburg`, `trent`, `bfm-2000`, `london-1689`) and reuse it; never add a second key for a work that already has one, and never add a shared key that a part already defines with different wording.
4. A book usually needs 2–8 cards. Do not add a card where all traditions agree, or where the disagreement is between scholars rather than churches (that belongs in the notes and views).
5. Write the book name in every reference to another book. Quotes of any book on the site must use its Plain wording; the build checks. Name writings outside the 66 books as content/works.json does.

## Files
- In part folders, edit that part's chapters.json. Edit book.json only for book-wide questions.
- Revelation is imported: put its chapter cards in content/overlays/Rev.json under `traditions_by_chapter` ({"<chapter>": [cards]}) and book-wide cards under `traditions` and run `node tools/import-rev.js`; never edit revelation/ files.
- Run `node tools/build.js --check <id>` until it reports 0 errors.
- Report each book's cards (chapter and question) and anything you were unsure of.
