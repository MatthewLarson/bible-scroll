# Colossians: brief for the writer

Read tools/brief-nt.md, brief-pauline.md, brief-pauline2.md, brief-rom.md and brief-eph.md first. Book id `Col`. 4 chapters, 95 verses (29, 23, 25, 18). Lenses H C L J T. One part, flat folder `content/books/Col/`, one writer, owns book.json.

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1:1-14 | Greeting from Paul and Timothy, thanksgiving, Epaphras, prayer, "rescued from the authority of darkness" |
| 1:15-20 | The Christ hymn: image of the invisible God, firstborn of all creation, all things created in him, head of the body, firstborn from the dead, all the fullness, peace through the blood of his cross |
| 1:21-2:5 | Reconciled; "filling up what is lacking in Christ's afflictions" (1:24); the mystery "Christ in you" |
| 2:6-23 | The "philosophy": the elements of the world, the fullness of deity bodily (2:9), circumcision made without hands and buried with him in baptism (2:11-12), the record of debt nailed to the cross, the powers disarmed (2:15), food, festivals, new moons and Sabbaths "a shadow" (2:16-17), the worship of angels (2:18), "do not handle" |
| 3:1-17 | Seek the things above, put to death, put off and put on, "neither Greek nor Jew", the word of Christ, psalms, hymns and spiritual songs |
| 3:18-4:1 | The household code (parallel Eph 5:22-6:9: Ephesians' Plain English Translation is the model where the Greek matches) |
| 4:2-18 | Prayer, Tychicus (= Eph 6:21-22) and Onesimus, the greetings (Aristarchus, Mark "the cousin of Barnabas", Jesus called Justus, Epaphras, Luke "the beloved physician", Demas, Nympha and the church in her house), the letter from Laodicea (4:16), Archippus, "Remember my chains" |

**Setting (H):** Colossae, Laodicea and Hierapolis in the Lycus valley; the earthquake under Nero (Tacitus, *Annals* 14.27, on Laodicea: verify, and whether Colossae is named); Epaphras as founder; Paul's imprisonment (Rome, Ephesus, Caesarea: the same views as Philemon and Philippians); the tie to Philemon (the same names). **C (full weight):** authorship (Paul, a coworker such as Timothy during Paul's life, or a later disciple: style and vocabulary, the realized eschatology of 2:12 and 3:1, the cosmic christology; Lohse, Schweizer, Dunn, Moo, Wright, Sumney, MacDonald: verify each); the relation to Ephesians (Colossians is generally taken as earlier and used by Ephesians: views) and to Philemon; the "Colossian philosophy" (Jewish mysticism, ascetic visionary Judaism, Gnostic, pagan, Pythagorean, a syncretism: Lightfoot, Dibelius, Francis, Arnold, Dunn: views); the hymn of 1:15-20 as pre-Pauline (views). **L:** the hymn's two strophes, the "all" (*panta*) repetitions, the vice and virtue lists, the household code form. **J:** Wisdom christology (Prov 8:22-31 and Wis 7:25-26 as Wisdom "image"; Sirach 24: Plains exist for Proverbs; check for Wisdom and Sirach), circumcision "made without hands" (Deut 10:16; 30:6), festivals, new moons and Sabbaths (Hos 2:11; 1 Chr 23:31; Ezek 45:17), the "worship of angels" (objective: worshipping angels; subjective: joining the angels' worship, as at Qumran: Francis), "the elements of the world" (the topic exists from Galatians), Jewish readings and Messianic Jewish practice on 2:16. **T:** the Arian controversy over "firstborn of all creation" (Arius, Athanasius), Chrysostom's homilies on Colossians, Theodore of Mopsuestia, Aquinas, Luther, Calvin, the infant-baptism argument from 2:11-12 (Reformed covenant theology) and the Baptist reply, the Catholic reading of 1:24 (redemptive suffering, *Salvifici Doloris* 1984: verify), Seventh-day Adventist and Sabbatarian readings of 2:16.

**Places for full treatment:** 1:2 (the saints and faithful brothers); 1:7 ("on our behalf" or "on your behalf": variant); 1:12; 1:13-14 (1:14 "through his blood", Byzantine addition from Eph 1:7: variant); **1:15-20** (*eikon*, *prototokos*: rank or first created, *en auto*, thrones and dominions, "in him all things hold together", *pleroma*, "reconcile all things": universalism readings); 1:23 "proclaimed in all creation"; **1:24**; 1:26-27; 2:2 (one of the most tangled variants in Paul: "the mystery of God, Christ"); 2:8 (*philosophia*, *paradosis*, *stoicheia*); **2:9** ("the whole fullness of deity bodily", *theotes*); **2:11-12** (circumcision of Christ, baptism, faith in the working of God); 2:14 (*cheirographon*, the record of debt); 2:15 (*apekdysamenos*: stripped off the powers, or himself; triumph procession); **2:16-17** (shadow and body); **2:18** (*thelon en tapeinophrosyne*, worship of angels, *embateuon*: "entering" the visions; the "not" variant: "things he has not seen"); 2:20-23; 3:1-4; 3:5 ("greed, which is idolatry"); 3:9-11 (old and new self; "Scythian", "barbarian"); 3:16; **3:18-4:1**; 4:3; 4:10 (Mark the cousin of Barnabas; Acts 15:37-39 reconciled); 4:14 (Luke the physician: the tradition of Luke's authorship of Luke and Acts); 4:15 (Nympha or Nymphas, "her house" or "his house" or "their house": variant); **4:16** (the letter from Laodicea: lost, Ephesians, or Philemon; the apocryphal Epistle to the Laodiceans: check works.json, propose if missing); 4:17; 4:18 (the signature).

**Cards (plan 6 to 8):** 1:15 (firstborn: Arius, Nicene, Jehovah's Witnesses as a non-church reading only if labeled outside the traditions; `top: son-of-god`); 1:24 (`top: suffering`: Catholic redemptive suffering, Orthodox, Lutheran and Reformed); 2:11-12 (`top: baptism`: infant baptism and covenant, believer's baptism, baptismal regeneration); 2:16-17 (`top: sabbath`, pointing to Romans 14:5); 2:18 (angels and saints: `top: communion-of-saints`, only if a real dividing line exists); 3:18-19 (`top: headship`, pointing to Ephesians 5); 3:22-4:1 (`top: slavery`, pointing to Ephesians 6 and Philemon); 4:16 (canon and lost letters, `top: canon`, only if the churches really differ).
