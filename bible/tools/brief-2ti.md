# 2 Timothy: brief for the writer

Read tools/brief-nt.md, brief-pauline.md, brief-pauline2.md, brief-rom.md and brief-1ti.md first (1 Timothy is being written at the same time and owns the shared core `pastoral-letters`, the person core `timothy`, and the term entries *epiphaneia*, *eusebeia*, *pistos ho logos*: follow brief-pauline2.md's renderings; contribute to those topics through your topics.json). Book id `2Ti`. 4 chapters, 83 verses (18, 26, 17, 22). Lenses H C L J T. One part, flat folder `content/books/2Ti/`, one writer, owns book.json.

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1 | Greeting; Timothy's tears; the faith of Lois and Eunice; "fan into flame the gift of God, which is in you through the laying on of my hands" (1:6); "not a spirit of fear"; do not be ashamed; "he abolished death and brought life and immortality to light"; "guard the good deposit" (*paratheke*); all in Asia turned away, Phygelus and Hermogenes; Onesiphorus ("May the Lord grant him to find mercy on that day", 1:16-18) |
| 2 | Entrust to faithful people who will teach others (2:2: the chain of teaching); soldier, athlete, farmer; "Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, from the seed of David" (2:8); the saying "if we died with him... if we deny him, he will deny us; if we are faithless, he remains faithful" (2:11-13); "rightly handling the word of truth" (2:15); Hymenaeus and Philetus, "the resurrection has already happened"; "the Lord knows those who are his" (Num 16:5); vessels of honor; the Lord's slave must not quarrel |
| 3 | The last days: lovers of self; "creeping into households and capturing weak women"; Jannes and Jambres (3:8); persecutions at Antioch, Iconium and Lystra; "the sacred writings" from childhood; "All Scripture is God-breathed and useful" (3:16-17) |
| 4 | Preach the word; itching ears; "I am already being poured out as a drink offering... I have fought the good fight... the crown of righteousness" (4:6-8); Demas, Crescens, Titus, Luke, Mark, Tychicus, the cloak and the scrolls and parchments at Troas (4:13), Alexander the coppersmith, "at my first defense no one came to my side", rescued "from the lion's mouth" (4:17); greetings to Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus; Erastus, Trophimus sick at Miletus; Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, Claudia |

**Setting (H):** Paul in chains in Rome expecting death (1:16-17; 4:6-8, 16-18), the second Roman imprisonment of tradition (1 Clement 5; Eusebius *History* 2.22 and 2.25 on Paul's death under Nero: verify), Timothy in Ephesus or nearby (4:12, 19), the persecution of Nero (Tacitus *Annals* 15.44: verify; the topic `nero` exists). **C:** authorship as for the Pastorals (point to `pastoral-letters`, do not repeat), plus the special case of 2 Timothy: the most personal of the three, the "fragment hypothesis" (Harrison: genuine notes in 4:6-22 and 1:15-18 built into a later letter: verify), the "testament" or farewell form (Acts 20:17-38 on the site; the Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs: check works.json), and the reading of the letter as genuine by those who accept 2 Timothy but not 1 Timothy and Titus (Murphy-O'Connor, Prior: verify). **L:** the farewell-testament form, the athletic and military images, the "trustworthy saying" (2:11-13) as a hymn, the vice list of 3:2-5. **J:** the Jewish Scriptures as "the sacred writings" (3:15: what "Scripture" meant then, the books Timothy learned, the Septuagint), 2:19 (Num 16:5 in the Greek; Isa 26:13? and Sir 17:26 for the second line: verify), Jannes and Jambres (Exod 7:11-22; the names in the Damascus Document 5:18-19 as "Yohanah and his brother" and the Targum Pseudo-Jonathan on Exod 1:15 and 7:11; the Jannes and Jambres book; Pliny *Natural History* 30.2.11 names Iannes: verify each), Ps 22:21 for "the lion's mouth" (Plain exists), Ps 62:12 / Prov 24:12 behind 4:14 (Plains exist; quote exactly), 1 Kgs 2:1-9 and Deut 31-33 as farewell models. **T:** 1:6 and ordination; 1:16-18 as a prayer for the dead (Onesiphorus taken as dead by many: the Catholic and Orthodox use, the Protestant reply that he may be alive); 2:2 and apostolic succession (Irenaeus *Against Heresies* 3.3, which names Linus, cf. 4:21: verify); 2:12-13 and perseverance; 2:20-21 vessels; 3:16 and inspiration and inerrancy (Chicago Statement 1978, *Dei Verbum* 11, the Orthodox and Lutheran views: verify); 4:8 and rewards; Linus as the first bishop of Rome after the apostles (Irenaeus: verify).

**Places for full treatment:** 1:5 (Lois and Eunice; Acts 16:1 Plain); **1:6**; 1:9-10; 1:12-14 (*paratheke*: what Paul has entrusted to God, or what God entrusted to Paul); **1:16-18**; **2:2**; 2:8; **2:11-13**; 2:15 (*orthotomeo*); 2:17-18; 2:19; 2:20-21; 2:26; 3:1-5; 3:6-7 (the "weak women": who; the letter's view of women and its local setting); **3:8**; 3:15; **3:16-17** (*pasa graphe theopneustos*: "All Scripture is God-breathed and useful" or "Every God-breathed Scripture is also useful"; what "Scripture" meant then; a deep dive); 4:3-4; **4:6-8**; 4:10 (Galatia / Gaul variant; Demas, "loving this present age"); 4:11 (Luke and Mark; Col 4:10, 14 and Phm 24 are being written at the same time: list the pair); 4:13 (the scrolls and *membranai*: the codex question; verify any claim); 4:14 (wish or prediction: "will repay" / "may the Lord repay": variant); 4:16-18; 4:19-21 (names; Linus; Claudia and Pudens: later legends, labeled).

**Variants (verify each):** 1:11 "and teacher of the Gentiles" (Byzantine "of the Gentiles"); 2:3 "share in suffering" / "you therefore endure"; 2:14 "before God" / "before the Lord"; 2:19; 3:16 (no variant of substance in the key clause: say so if so); **4:10** "Galatia" / "Gaul"; 4:14 "will repay" / "may he repay"; 4:22 "the Lord be with your spirit" / "the Lord Jesus Christ", "Amen".

**Cards (plan 5 to 7):** 1:6 / 2:2 (ordination and apostolic succession, `top: elders`: Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, Lutheran, Reformed, Baptist and Pentecostal); 1:16-18 (`top: purgatory` or `communion-of-saints`: prayer for the dead); 2:12-13 (`top: perseverance`); **3:16-17** (`top: scripture-and-tradition`: the inspiration and authority of Scripture; inerrancy and infallibility; the canon each church means by "Scripture"); 4:6-8 (rewards and merit, `top: justification`, only if a real dividing line exists).
