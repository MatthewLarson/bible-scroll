# 2 Corinthians: brief for the part writers

Read tools/brief-nt.md, brief-pauline.md and brief-rom.md first; also brief-1co.md (settings and terms shared). Book id `2Co`. 13 chapters, 257 verses. Lenses H C L J T. Three parts:

| Part | Chapters | Verses | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01-05 | 1-5 | 98 | Affliction in Asia, the change of plans, the painful visit and letter (2:1-4), the ministry of the new covenant (3: veil, Moses, "the letter kills"), treasure in clay jars (4), the earthly tent (5:1-10), "new creation" and reconciliation (5:17-21). **Owns book.json** |
| 06-09 | 6-9 | 73 | Open hearts, "do not be yoked with unbelievers" (6:14-7:1, a possible interpolation), Titus and joy (7), the collection (8-9, the cheerful giver) |
| 10-13 | 10-13 | 86 | Paul against the "super-apostles", his boasting, the fool's speech, the lists of sufferings, the man caught up to the third heaven (12:1-10), the thorn in the flesh, the third visit, the closing benediction (13:14) |

**Setting (H):** Paul after Ephesus (the affliction "in Asia" 1:8), Macedonia, Titus, the crisis in Corinth after 1 Corinthians (the painful visit, the tearful letter), Aretas at Damascus (11:32), the collection for Jerusalem. **C:** the unity of 2 Corinthians (partition theories: chapters 10-13 as the "severe letter", 6:14-7:1 an interpolation, 8 and 9 two letters; defenders of unity: Harris, Garland, Barnett; Thrall's ICC argues that chapters 1-9 are one letter and 10-13 a later, separate letter: give each fairly), the opponents ("super-apostles": views), Paul's boasting. **L:** irony and the fool's speech, the sufferings catalogs, the Moses typology in chapter 3. **J:** Moses's veil (Exod 34; Exodus is on the site and is the model), "new covenant" (Jer 31), "the letter kills", the third heaven (merkavah tradition: label as a comparison only), the thorn. **T:** Chrysostom, Augustine, Aquinas, Calvin, Luther, Wesley, Orthodox (theosis, "from glory to glory"), Catholic teaching on 5:10.

**Places for full treatment:** 1:20-22 (seal, *arrabon*); 2:14-17 (the triumphal procession); 3:6, 3:14-18 (the old covenant read, the veil; supersession question); 4:4 ("the god of this age"); 5:1-10 (the intermediate state); 5:14-15; 5:16; 5:17 ("new creation"); 5:21 (atonement); 6:14-7:1; 8:9; 9:6-15; 11:2-3; 11:22-29 (39 lashes: Deut 25:3), 11:32-33; 12:2-4 (the man in Christ: Paul himself? views), 12:7 (the thorn: illness, opponent, temptation); 13:14 (the benediction and the Trinity).

**Cards (plan 8 to 12):** 4:4, 5:1-10, 5:21 (atonement: penal substitution, Christus Victor, theosis), 5:19 (reconciliation), 6:14-7:1 (separation), 9:6-8 (giving and tithing), 12:7, 3:6 and 3:14 (old covenant: supersession, fulfillment, Paul within Judaism), 13:14 (Trinity).
