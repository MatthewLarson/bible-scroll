# Obadiah: brief for the writer

Read tools/brief-twelve.md first. Book id `Oba`, 1 chapter, 21 verses. Prophets lane. One part, flat folder `content/books/Oba/`. One writer, who owns book.json. One review.md. Note density up to one per verse or more, as for Jude. One-chapter rules: in prose cite "Obadiah 10"; in `r` lists use bare "1:10".

**Lenses:** H, C, J, X, F.
- H: Edom's betrayal when Jerusalem fell in 586 BC (vv. 10-14; the commonest dating; Psalm 137:7; Lamentations 4:21-22; Ezekiel 25:12-14 and 35; Jeremiah 49:7-22; Malachi 1:2-5), with the earlier option (Edom's revolt under Jehoram, 2 Kings 8:20-22; 2 Chronicles 21:8-17, 9th century) labeled, and the later Nabataean takeover.
- C: the relation of vv. 1-9 to Jeremiah 49:7-22 (borrowing either way, or a shared source), composition (vv. 1-14 and 15-21; later additions), the place in the Twelve (catchwords with Amos 9:12 and Joel 3:19).
- J: Edom as Esau and later Rome in the midrash (verify any citation); Obadiah as an Edomite convert (Bavli Sanhedrin 39b: verify); Rashi, Ibn Ezra, Kimhi, the Targum; haftarah use (verify or leave out).
- X: Jerome, Cyril, Theodoret, Luther, Calvin; Esau/Edom as a type of the world or of the church's enemies; "saviors on Mount Zion" (v. 21) read of the apostles or Christ (labeled reception); Herod the Idumean is not predicted by Obadiah; the warning that anti-Edom rhetoric was sometimes turned on Jews (topic anti-judaism).
- F: the day of the LORD (v. 15), the return of the exiles (vv. 17-20), "the kingdom will be the LORD's" (v. 21).

**Places for full treatment.**
- v. 1 "Obadiah" ("servant of the LORD", a common name), "a vision", "a messenger sent among the nations" (Jeremiah 49:14). vv. 2-4 "you who live in the clefts of the rock" (*chagve sela'*; Sela, Petra? debated), "Who will bring me down to the ground?" (Jeremiah 49:16; Isaiah 14:13-15). vv. 5-9 grape gatherers and thieves (Jeremiah 49:9 in reverse order), "Esau ransacked" (v. 6), "Your allies sent you to the border... those who eat your bread set a trap under you" (v. 7, hard Hebrew), the wise of Edom, Teman, "Mount Esau".
- vv. 10-14 "Because of the violence done to your brother Jacob" (Genesis 25-36; Numbers 20:14-21; Deuteronomy 23:7; Amos 1:11), the eight "do not" lines (vv. 12-14): the Hebrew can be a prohibition or "you should not have"; weigh and state; gloat, boast, enter the gate, loot, stand at the crossroads, hand over survivors.
- vv. 15-16 "The day of the LORD is near for all the nations... as you have done it will be done to you" (Joel 3:4, 7; Habakkuk 2:8, 16); "you drank on my holy mountain" (the cup: Jeremiah 25:15-29; 49:12; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 51:17-23; Psalm 75:8).
- vv. 17-21 Zion's deliverance; Jacob a fire, Esau stubble (v. 18; Isaiah 10:17); the lists of lands (Negev, Shephelah, Ephraim, Samaria, Gilead, Zarephath, Sepharad: Sepharad uncertain, Sardis or elsewhere; the later Jewish use of "Sefarad" for Spain is a reception, labeled); the obscure "exiles of this host" (v. 20); v. 21 "saviors/deliverers will go up on Mount Zion to rule Mount Esau" (Judges 3:9, 15; Nehemiah 9:27; the Greek and Targum), and "the kingdom will be the LORD's".
