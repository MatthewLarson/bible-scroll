# 1 Thessalonians: brief for the writer

Read tools/brief-nt.md, brief-pauline.md, brief-pauline2.md and brief-rom.md first. Book id `1Th`. 5 chapters, 89 verses (10, 20, 13, 18, 28). Lenses H C L J T. One part, flat folder `content/books/1Th/`, one writer, owns book.json.

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1 | Greeting from Paul, Silvanus and Timothy; faith, love, hope; "you turned to God from idols" (1:9-10); "the anger to come" |
| 2:1-16 | Paul's conduct among them (gentle as a nurse or "infants", 2:7), working night and day, as a father; the word of God received; 2:14-16 "the Jews who killed the Lord Jesus... God's anger has come on them at last" |
| 2:17-3:13 | Torn away, Satan hindered us, Timothy sent from Athens, the good report, the prayer |
| 4:1-12 | Holiness and sexual conduct (4:3-8: "each of you know how to control his own *skeuos*": vessel = body or wife), brotherly love, quiet lives and work |
| 4:13-18 | Those who have fallen asleep; the Lord's descent, the archangel's voice, the trumpet, the dead in Christ first, "caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air" |
| 5:1-11 | Times and seasons, "peace and security" (5:3), the thief in the night, children of light, the armor of faith, love and hope |
| 5:12-28 | Respect leaders, "rejoice always, pray without ceasing", do not quench the Spirit, test everything, "spirit and soul and body" (5:23), the holy kiss, "I put you under oath that this letter be read to all the brothers" |

**Setting (H):** Thessalonica, a free city and the capital of Macedonia; Acts 17:1-10 on the site (Jason, the politarchs, "turning the world upside down", "another king, Jesus": Acts' Plain is the model); the short stay; Paul writing from Corinth about AD 50 (Acts 18:5; the Gallio inscription for the date: verify); the imperial cult and the slogan "peace and security" (*pax et securitas*: views, verify); the persecution from "your own countrymen" (2:14). **C:** probably the earliest surviving letter of Paul (consensus: say who dissents, e.g. those who date Galatians earlier); unity (the partition theories: Schmithals; views); **2:13-16 as an interpolation** (Pearson's case after AD 70 and the replies: verify each; the Plain translates the text and the variants file notes that no manuscript lacks it); Paul's eschatology and its development (did he expect to be alive, "we who are alive"?). **L:** the long thanksgiving (1:2-3:13), the triad faith-love-hope (1:3; 5:8), the consolation-letter form at 4:13-18. **J:** "turned from idols to serve a living and true God" (Jewish missionary language), the "anger to come", Isa 59:17 behind 5:8 (Plain exists; quote exactly), the day of the Lord (Amos 5:18; Joel 2: Plains exist), the trumpet and the archangel (Dan 12:1; the topic `michael` exists), **2:14-16 and its later use against Jews** (honestly, as brief-nt.md's fairness section asks; "the Jews" as the Judean persecutors; the "Paul within Judaism" readers; the punctuation of 2:14-15 debated by some), Jewish purity and sexual ethics (4:3-8; Lev 18). **T:** Chrysostom's homilies, Augustine on 5:17 ("pray without ceasing" in his letter to Proba: verify) and the Jesus Prayer in the Orthodox tradition (Hesychasm; *The Way of a Pilgrim*: verify), the rapture readings (Darby, Scofield; the historic premillennial, amillennial and postmillennial readings), the trichotomy debate (5:23), the "tradition" language.

**Places for full treatment:** 1:1 (Silvanus = Silas); 1:3; 1:9-10; **2:7** (*nepioi* "infants" against *epioi* "gentle": one letter in Greek; a full variant with the editions and witnesses verified this session; the NA28 changed to "infants": check what SBL and THGNT print); 2:12; **2:14-16** (variant/notes, J and C lenses, a deep dive); 2:18 (Satan); 3:3 ("destined for this"); 4:3-8 (*skeuos*); 4:9; 4:11-12 (work: Thessalonian idleness? connect to 2 Thess 3 by reference); **4:13-18** (*koimao* "fall asleep" and the soul; *parousia*; *apantesis*; *harpazo*; "the word of the Lord" in 4:15: a saying of Jesus? a prophecy?; 1 Cor 15:51-52 Plain exists: quote exactly); **5:1-11** ("peace and security"; the thief); 5:12-13 (leaders: *proistamenoi*); 5:19-22 (prophecy, test everything); **5:23** (spirit, soul and body: trichotomy and dichotomy); 5:27.

**Cards (plan 4 to 6):** 4:13-18 (`top: rapture`: pretribulation, midtribulation, prewrath, posttribulation; amillennial and Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran and Reformed readings of the "meeting"; never rank), 4:13-14 (`top: intermediate-state`: soul sleep, conscious presence, prayer for the dead: pointing if Philippians covers it), 5:23 (human nature: tripartite and bipartite views, Orthodox and Catholic, `top` must be an existing id: check `image-of-god`, or skip), 5:19-21 (`top: spiritual-gifts`: continuationist and cessationist), 2:14-16 only if a real divergence among the churches exists (otherwise a deep dive and notes; do not invent a card).
