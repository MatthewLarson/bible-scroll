Part of [[bible-site]]. Published in canonical order through Job by 2026-10-23; v22 added Tobit; v23 (2026-10-24) added Psalms; v26 (2026-10-24) added Proverbs and 1 Maccabees. Ecclesiastes next.

Terms (content/terms.json is authoritative): torah 'law', chuqqim 'rules', edot 'decrees'; 'innah 'dishonor'; nokhri 'foreigner', zar 'outsider', ger 'foreigner who lives among you'; hesed 'loyal love'; kana' (Niphal) 'humble oneself'; seganim 'deputies' (sarim 'officials'); Neh 5:19/13:31 "My God, remember me for good".

Text policies: Leningrad base; TAHOT type-X words are reconstructions (variants only). Names in each verse's Hebrew form, with a note. Ketiv in names only when it has other support (Greek or a parallel list), unless a parallel list in the book shows the Qere is meant. Parallel passages (Chronicles/Kings, Ezra 2/Neh 7) reuse the site's Plain English Translation where the Hebrew matches. In 2 Sam 11 to 1 Kgs 2:11 and all of 2 Kings the Vaticanus Greek is kaige; the Lucianic is the better Old Greek. Esther: the Greek Additions and Alpha Text are variants and notes, never the Plain English Translation.

Safety: a writer brief that asked agents to write out recalled NIV/ESV text tripped an API safeguard; the drift audit compares in the head and logs only verse/outcome/reason.

Bible Atlas (2026-10-23): artifacts block iframes and outside images, so the site draws its own sketch maps. content/atlas/<id>.json holds per-chapter places/people/events and coordinates, harvested by agents with the MCP tools (bible-atlas.com is blocked from Bash). content/atlas/basemap.json is built by tools/basemap.js from Natural Earth 10m (raw.githubusercontent nvkelso/natural-earth-vector). Chapter pages show "Places and people in this chapter": a map plus Bible Atlas links. Every new book needs an atlas harvest before publishing.

Canon (Matt, 2026-10-23): three tiers. (1) the 66; (2) books some churches hold as Scripture (deuterocanon, Orthodox, Ethiopian incl. 1 Enoch, Jubilees), labeled with who accepts them; (3) outside every Bible (works.json tiers other/early/lost), full "not widely accepted" treatment. works.json marks every mention by tier. X books need book.json `canon` (validated in build). Traditions cards: `traditions` [{q, top, views:[[tradition, answer, keys?]], s}], names fixed in TRADITIONS (incl. Ethiopian Orthodox); `top` links same question across books. Atlas rule: patriarch linked where tribe/nation/land meant is dropped.

Atlas layers (2026-10-24): content/atlas/<id>.json is the raw Bible Atlas harvest and is never hand-edited; content/atlas/corrections/<id>.json holds per-chapter drop/unmap/add with a reader-facing why; content/atlas/points.json fixes a Bible Atlas point that is wrong in every book. Psalms lead decisions (letter names, Yah, Selah, "the Greek" = critical text, earlier psalm is the model for shared Hebrew) are at the end of tools/brief-psa.md; reuse the pattern for later poetry books. Proverbs lead decisions are at the end of tools/brief-pro.md: 'eshet chayil is "a woman of strength" in Proverbs but Ruth 3:11 keeps "a woman of worth" (deliberate split, paired with Boaz 2:1).

"Write the Prophets books" = Isaiah–Malachi; "Write the New Testament books" = Matthew–Jude; Non-canonical thread = outside the 66.
